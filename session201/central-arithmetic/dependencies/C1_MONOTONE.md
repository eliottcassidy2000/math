## 2. Strict growth and target cutoff

The physical moves are

    O(D,E,U,d)=(D,0,2U,d),
    M_L(D,E,U,d)=(gD−(L mod2)E+LU, D−[L=1]E, QD+BU, D),

where L>=1 and g,Q,B are the positive gadget constants. They satisfy g>=1,Q>=1,B>=1. Direct subtraction gives

    F(Ov)−F(v)=U+E>0,
    F(M_L v)−F(v)
       =(g+Q−2)D+(1+[L=1]−(L mod2))E+(L+B−1)U>0.    (1)

Every displayed coefficient is nonnegative and L+B−1>=1. These facts include the singleton boundary and the exceptional L=1 endpoint subtraction.

Also

    F(M_L v)=(g+Q−1)D+([L=1]−(L mod2))E+(L+B)U>=LU. (2)

Indeed D>=E; the only negative endpoint coefficient is −1 when L>=3 is odd, and then g+Q−1>=1. All other terms are nonnegative.

**Theorem.** All physical sources with F<=N are exactly enumerated by beginning at the singleton, trying O and every 1<=L<=floor(N/U) at each visited state, and keeping a successor only when its F<=N. Duplicate profiles may be merged with one predecessor recipe.
