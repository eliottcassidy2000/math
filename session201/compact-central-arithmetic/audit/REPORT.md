Therefore it maps each complete finite rewrite component to another component. It also reverses boundary positions while preserving membership in the binary alphabet, so the property of exposing a binary boundary is invariant.

Consequently dagger acts as an involution on the components retained after the reviewed boundary-absorption reduction. If W and P are chosen representatives of paired components, their matrices satisfy `Q M_W=(Q M_P)^T`. Thus
