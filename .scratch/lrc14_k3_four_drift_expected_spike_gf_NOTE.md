## 0. Why the resolving denominator is septimal

The fact `7|D` is structural, not merely a census pattern.  Suppose `7` does
not divide `D`, fix a residue `r mod D`, and write its `L/D=M` lifted cells as
`j=r+Dt`.  For a body speed `f`, put `P=L/f`.  Modulo `P`, this fibre visits a
coset of the subgroup generated by `D`, of size

```text
h=P/gcd(P,D).
```

Since `v_7(P)>=1` but `7` does not divide `D`, one has `7|h`.  The speed's
cyclic half-open danger block has length `P/7`; on the coset lattice it
therefore contains exactly `h/7` visited residues, each repeated `M/h`
times.  That speed removes exactly `M/7` cells from the fibre.  Six body
speeds remove at most `6M/7`, leaving a safe address above every `r mod D`.
Hence

```text
7 not|D  =>  S_D=Z/DZ.
```

The full row census confirms this on all `96,235/96,235` nonseptimal
body/divisor rows.  Since the k=3 support cutoff is strictly below one,
every one of the 26,970 support-hard rows must have `7|D`.  This lemma is the
reason `q=D/7` is universally available below.

## 1. The missing coordinate is spike support, not ceiling volume

Fix a projected-support row with resolving denominator `D`, and put
`q=D/7`.  Every resolving `D` in the complete 26,970-row universe is
divisible by seven.  If `d|D` and `g=gcd(d,q)`, then `d/g|7`, hence

```text

```

In the second case a literal strict `d`-mask hits any `q`-fibre at most
once.  Granting it one point in *every* fibre is therefore a valid upper
relaxation.  Let `c` be the number of these uniform-granted masks.

```text

```

Thus all `m=4-c` spike masks have the same expected support `q/7`, even
though their ceiling capacities and phase laws differ.

## 2. Expected-spike theorem

Let

```text
N_c(S_D) = #{b mod q : lambda_q(S_D,b)>c}.
```

After the `c` uniform masks receive their full one-point-per-fibre grant,
every one of these `N_c` fibres must be hit by at least one whole-fibre
spike.  If `T` is the sum of the `m` spike support sizes, a cover forces
`T>=N_c` at every common phase in the compact aligned-safe carrier `R_A`.
Its measure is at least `55/91`.

The strict masks make `{T>=N_c}` an open phase event.  For `N_c>0`, Markov
and compact/proper-open separation give

```text
55/91 < mu(T>=N_c) <= E(T)/N_c = m*q/(7*N_c).
```

Consequently every cover must satisfy the strict condition

```text
N_c=0  OR  55*N_c < 13*(4-c)*q.                    (E)
```

The zero-demand exception is essential, including `c=4`; the displayed
ratio proof is not applied as `0/0`.  Equality in (E) is impossible, not
admissible.

## 3. Exact `c=3` denominator refinement

For `c=3` there is one spike denominator `d|q`.  Write

```text

```

On open phase cells, the spike support size is exactly

```text

```

The event that this one spike covers `N_3` heavy fibres has measure
strictly above `55/91` precisely when

```text

```

Indeed `r/7>55/91` is equivalent to `13r>55`, hence to `r=5` or `6`.
The capacity equality in (S3) is admissible: its event has measure `1` or
`r/7>55/91`.  This is different from equality in the Markov inequality
(E), which is killed by compact/open separation.
