THE UNKNOTTING NUMBER OF 11n102 IS 2

arXiv:2606.12431v1 [math.GT] 15 May 2026

TYE LIDMAN

We prove that the unknotting number of the knot 11n102 is 2. It is known, see e.g. KnotInfo [LM26],
that the unknotting number is 1 or 2, so we will assume for a contradiction it is 1. By the Montesinos
trick [Mon73], the branched double cover of 11n102 is half-integral surgery on some knot J in S 3 . Up
to mirroring, the knot 11n102 is the determinant 3 Montesinos knot M (−2/3, 1/3, 2/7) [LM26]; with this
orientation, the branched double cover Y is the Seifert fibered space S 2 (0; −2/3, 1/3, 2/7) [Mon73], which
has H1 = Z/3. We can alternatively write this manifold as S 2 (1; 1/3, 1/3, 2/7), where our conventions mean
that in the keychain link surgery diagram, the central circle has surgery coefficient 1 and the three other
components have surgery coefficients 3, 3, 7/2. From this, it is easy to check that the associated plumbing
bounding Y is positive-definite. Consequently, the Heegaard Floer homology of Y is readily computed using
the algorithms of Némethi [N0́5] or Ozsváth-Szabó [OS03b]: there are two spinc structures with HFred = 0
and d = 1/6, while the third has HFred = F2 and d = −1/2. In principle, Y could be either +3/2-surgery or
−3/2-surgery on J. The mod 2 d-invariants of Y agree with those of +3/2-surgery on the unknot and not
−3/2 surgery [OS03a, Proposition 4.8], so the Ni-Wu formula [NW15, Proposition 1.6] implies that Y must
be +3/2 surgery on J. (The sign of the surgery coefficient can also be deduced by computing the linking
form of Y .) In fact, the d-invariants of Y agree with L(3, 2), so in Ni-Wu’s notation we have V0 (J) = 0 and
hence Vs (J) = H−s (J) = 0 for all s ≥ 0 [NW15, Theorem 2.5, Lemma 2.7]. Because the surgery is positive,
we have from [NW15] and Gainullin’s work [Gai17, Corollary 14, Proposition 15] that HFred (Y, si ) ∼
