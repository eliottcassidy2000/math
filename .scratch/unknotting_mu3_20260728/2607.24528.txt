3. The merger K2ad
Although the merger Kn allows us to prove Feige’s conjecture (Conjecture 1.2),
the bound obtained from Kn is not sharp for arbitrary δ (Conjecture 1.1). In recent
work, Ming, Ramdas, Shen, Wang, and Waudby-Smith [MRS` 26] studied whether
Kn can be improved as a merger.
A merger G is said to dominate a merger F if G ď F pointwise, and to strictly
dominate F if the inequality is strict at some point. A merger is admissible if it is
not strictly dominated by any other merger.
For n “ 2, they constructed a merger K2ad , which is the unique admissible merger
among those that dominate K2 . This is particularly relevant here, since Lemma 2.2
shows that a smaller merger may yield a stronger lower-tail bound. We now recall
their construction.
