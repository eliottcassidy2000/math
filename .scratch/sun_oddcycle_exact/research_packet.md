## Promotion candidate: exact pair-incidence decomposition

Let `f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4` be any four integer-valued atom maps on labelled
address sets.  For a target `n`, put

```text
L_s = {(i_1,i_2): f_1(i_1)+f_2(i_2)=s},
H_t = {(i_3,i_4): f_3(i_3)+f_4(i_4)=t}.
```

Define the labelled pair-incidence graph `Gamma_n` with left vertices all
low-pair addresses occurring in a representation, right vertices all
high-pair addresses occurring in a representation, and an edge

```text
(i_1,i_2) -- (i_3,i_4)
```

exactly when their four atom values sum to `n`.

Then, with `ell_s=|L_s|` and `h_t=|H_t|`, there is an exact labelled
decomposition

```text
Gamma_n = disjoint_union_s K_(ell_s,h_(n-s)).          (A)
```

Consequently

```text
a(n)                 = |E(Gamma_n)| = sum_s ell_s h_(n-s),
beta_1(Gamma_n)       = sum_s (ell_s-1)(h_(n-s)-1),
#C4(Gamma_n)          = sum_s C(ell_s,2) C(h_(n-s),2),
#odd cycles(Gamma_n)  = 0.                              (B)
```

Here empty factors are omitted.  Formula `(A)` is immediate: equality fixes
the complementary pair sum, and every low/high address with complementary
sums makes an edge.  The remaining formulas are the standard exact formulas
for complete bipartite components.

This is a universal theorem, not a finite extrapolation.  It says the
intrinsic graph generated by the most natural `2+2` factorization has no odd
cycles for structural reasons.  The correct first observable is edge count;
the first genuine collision observables are codegrees, cycle rank and even
four-cycles.

## What happens if one insists on representation-overlap odd cycles

Let the overlap graph have the representations themselves as vertices, with
an edge when two representations share at least one role index.  At
`N-1,N,N+1` its triangle counts are respectively

```text
6, 0, 2.
```

Every one of the eight triangles is a clique generated by three or more
representations sharing one *single* coordinate.  There is no Berge triangle
with three distinct shared role-coordinate vertices.  The stricter
two-role-exchange graph (edge iff exactly two role indices agree) has

```text
(vertices,edges,odd cycles) = (89,0,0), (0,0,0), (67,1,0).
```

Odd exchange cycles nevertheless exist in other Sun fibres, so their absence
at the large neighbours is not a universal parity law.  In the canonical
domain the first is the triangle at `n=2`

```text
(2,3,6,7), (2,3,5,8), (2,4,5,7).
```

After requiring every higher atom to be positive (`x>=4,y>=6,z>=8`), the
first is the finite-exact triangle at `n=73`

```text
(2,8,6,8), (9,7,6,8), (12,5,6,8),
```

coming from the elementary repeated pair-sum identity

```text
C(2,2)+C(8,4) = C(9,2)+C(7,4) = C(12,2)+C(5,4) = 71,
71 + C(6,6)+C(8,8) = 73.
```

The search enumerated all 156,043 all-positive-higher tuples through 10,000;
only the prefix through 72 is needed for the stated minimality.  This boundary
shows why overlap odd cycles are multiplicity shadows, unlike THM-466's
tournament odd cycles.

## The lawful Rédei analogy is fixed-point parity, not OCF

THM-4037 already supplies an exact graph-theoretic comparison.  For an odd
modulus `m`, take the complete-period modular representation fibre and apply
the coordinate reflections

```text
iota_(2r)(u)=2r-1-u,  r=1,2,3,4.
```

Make the reflection graph whose vertices are modular representations and
whose edges join every nonfixed representation to its reflected partner.
Because this is an involution, the graph is a matching together with at most
one isolated fixed vertex.  It has no cycle of any length.  Its vertex parity
is the isolated-vertex indicator, and THM-4037 identifies that indicator as

```text
R_m(t) odd  <=>  32768*t+3453 = 0 mod m.
```

This is the exact shared mechanism with the repo's Rédei involution work:
pair nonfixed objects and read the fixed layer.  The mechanisms separate at
the next step.  For tournaments, Grinberg--Stanley OCF makes intrinsic
directed odd-cycle collections determine the full 2-adic Hamiltonian-path
tower.  For Sun fibres, the natural reflection and pair-incidence graphs are
acyclic, so no analogous odd-cycle tower follows.  Height and pair-block
addresses, rather than higher odd-cycle digits, are the missing coordinates.

## Connection contract and frontier consequence

```text
source: role-labelled Sun representation fibre
target: labelled low/high pair-incidence graph Gamma_n
map: representation -> its low/high address edge
preserves: exact equality, representation count, pair addresses, pair sums,
           pair-fibre multiplicities
destroys if labels are forgotten: roles, numeric heights, Pascal carry data
odd-cycle verdict: identically zero because Gamma_n is bipartite
needed sidecars: role labels and heights for coverage; codegrees/even cycles
                 for multiplicity; Pascal carry state for normal-form routes
cheapest hostile: N-1,N,N+1, all of which make Gamma_n a forest
```

THM-4036 has an exact graph reading:

```text
sum_(n<=X) a(n)^2 = sum_(n<=X) |E(Gamma_n)|^2,
```

the number of ordered pairs of representation edges, summed over targets.  In
fact the complete-bipartite decomposition gives the exact per-target ledger

```text
a(n)^2
 = a(n)                                                   identical edge
 + sum_s ell_s h_s(h_s-1)                                shared low endpoint
 + sum_s h_s ell_s(ell_s-1)                              shared high endpoint
 + sum_s ell_s(ell_s-1)h_s(h_s-1)                        disjoint, same block
 + 2 sum_(s<t) ell_s h_s ell_t h_t,                      different blocks
```

where `h_s` abbreviates `h_(n-s)`.  The fourth term is exactly four times
the number of `C4`s.  THM-4036's successful mechanism is therefore edge-pair
energy, not odd-cycle collections.  A useful next Sun experiment should bound
these five ledgers separately, especially the different-block term that no
within-component cycle statistic sees.  A useful LRC transfer should likewise
use a symmetric repair-compatibility graph and retain owner/phase labels; odd
cycles become relevant only after an intrinsic non-bipartite relation is
proved.

At the two represented hostiles this ledger is particularly sharp:

```text
N-1: a^2=7,921 = 89 identical + 7,832 different-block;
N+1: a^2=4,489 = 67 identical + 2 shared-high + 4,420 different-block.
```

Every within-block cycle term vanishes.  Hence even replacing odd cycles by
four-cycles would discard essentially the entire local second moment at these
targets; the block-address sidecar is load-bearing.

The cheapest promotion candidate is the universal theorem `(A)--(B)` plus
the exact hostile table.  It is short, proof-complete, and directly repairs a
tempting but false OCF-style analogy.
