## 1. Exact signed-endpoint identities

Let `G` be the positive-length safe set of an eleven-runner body, put
`m=mu(G)`, and let `0<alpha<1` below:

```text
A=D_p union D_q,                 alpha=mu(A),
A_t={x: tx mod 1 lies in A},     L=mu(G\A_t).
```

Cancel the identified `0=1` contributions in the signed periodic endpoint
words

```text
E(G)={(e,sigma_e)},              E(A)={(f,tau_f)},
```

where left and right endpoints have signs `+1,-1`.  With
`B_2(x)={x}^2-{x}+1/6`, one has

```text
C_t=Cov(1_G,1_(A_t))
   =(1/(2t)) sum_(e,f) sigma_e tau_f B_2({f-te}),
L=m(1-alpha)-C_t.                                       (1)
```

Indeed, the nonzero Fourier coefficient of a step word is its signed endpoint
sum divided by `2*pi*i*n`; pullback by `t` keeps precisely the frequencies
divisible by `t`.  Multiplying the two endpoint sums and using

```text
sum_(k!=0) exp(2*pi*i*k*x)/k^2 = 2*pi^2 B_2({x})
```

gives `(1)`, including its sign and factor `1/(2t)`.

The corresponding sampled-grid discrepancy is

```text
disc_t(G)=sum_(k!=0)|Ghat(kt)|^2
 =(1/(2t^2)) sum_(e,e') sigma_e sigma_e'
                            B_2({t(e-e')}).              (2)
```

If `G subset A_t`, then `C_t=m(1-alpha)`.  Cauchy--Schwarz and
`sum_(k!=0)|Ahat(k)|^2=alpha(1-alpha)` therefore prove the sufficient
noncontainment certificate

```text
disc_t(G)<((1-alpha)/alpha)m^2.                          (3)
```

If `G` has `r` positive-length circular arcs, the total endpoint variation
gives

```text
disc_t(G)<=r^2/(3t^2).                                  (4)
```

There is also a sometimes strictly sharper integer boundary.  Define

```text
N_t(y)=#{0<=j<t:(j+y)/t lies in G},       theta={tm/alpha}.
```

If `L=0`, then `N_t` is integer-valued and supported on `A`.  Among integer
functions on a set of mass `alpha` with the same mean, the second moment is
minimized by the two adjacent occupancies.  Parseval consequently gives

```text
disc_t(G)>=((1-alpha)/alpha)m^2
             +alpha theta(1-theta)/t^2.                 (5)
```

The extra term vanishes exactly when `tm/alpha` is integral.  Equations
`(1)--(5)` are unrestricted finite-union identities and implications.  The
full paired step words are the sharp retained object because

```text
L=(1/t) integral_(A^c) N_t(y)dy.                         (6)
```

Power alone forgets on which side of the labelled obstruction the occupancy
lies.
