## 2. The verifier repair is one exact Farkas row

The inherited status generator has an early branch at the first positive
threshold `t` for which no one of the sixteen status columns is good.  If the
tail demand at `t` is positive, the corresponding inequality has zero left
side for every nonnegative status mixture and positive right side.  In the
five-equality-row Farkas normalization, its exact certificate is therefore

```text
thresholds=(t),   alpha=(1),   z=(0,0,0,0,0),              (8)
```

with sixteen zero column slacks and contradiction `-demand<0`.

The promoted legacy auditor instead reconstructs every earlier nontrivial
threshold before checking a certificate.  It consequently rejects the
smaller but already sufficient generator certificate `(8)`.  The companion
does not change either promoted file.  Its local wrapper accepts `(8)` only
when all of the following hold:

1. `t` is the first positive zero-good threshold;
2. the stored certificate is exactly `(8)`;
3. the demand is positive;
4. all sixteen rational slacks are exactly zero; and
5. the recomputed contradiction is exactly `-demand`.

Every other certificate is delegated byte-for-byte to the legacy full-table
auditor.  The exact `z_1=234` census is

```text

legacy certificates:  191,718, across the other 380 rows. (9)
```

## 3. Memory-bounded exact terminal certificates

For each fixed pair of low labels, the terminal routine materializes only its
clean cells in `Z/LZ`, audits a sample against the inherited literal predicate,
and discards the array after all needed high divisors have been processed.
If `C` clean cells are projected modulo a divisor `d|L`, one residue class can
contain at most `L/d` cells.  Hence their support has the rigorous lower bound

```text
ceil(C/(L/d)).                                               (11)
```

Whenever `(11)` is greater than `ceil(d/7)`, strict complete-cell cardinality
closes the case without constructing its residue set.  Otherwise the routine
computes the exact distinct residues and applies the same strict test.  Only
if that test fails may it call the inherited max-gap fallback.

On the `74` residual bodies the result is

```text
zero-high scalar cases:          9,028;
one-high translated cases:   1,245,093;
coarse-cardinality closes:    1,162,904;
exact-cardinality closes:        82,189;
max-gap cases:                         0;
failed one-high cases:                 0.                 (12)
```

Thus `(11)` plus exact residue cardinality accounts for every one-high case;
there is no unit-phase or max-gap rescue hidden in `(12)`.  Seventy of the
seventy-four bodies also have a positive duplicate-two-high gap.  Their
smallest positive gap is

```text
13057/124176780 at body (1,6,9,10,12,14).                   (13)
```

This proves the split `(2)` inside the projected relaxation.
