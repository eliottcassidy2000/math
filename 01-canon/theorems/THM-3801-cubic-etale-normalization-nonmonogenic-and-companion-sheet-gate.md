# THM-3801 -- a cubic survivor must be nonmonogenic with one companion per branch

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**  Let `k` be
an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.  Let `X=Spec B` be an
integral normal affine surface with

```text
B*=k*,                                                               (1)
```

and let

```text
f=(A,C):X -> A2=Spec R,                  R=k[A,C],                    (2)
```

be dominant and etale, with

```text
[Frac(B):Frac(R)]=3.                                                 (3)
```

Let `K=Frac(B)` and let `S` be the integral closure of `R` in `K`.  Then:

1. `S` is finite flat of rank three over `R`, and `X` is an open subscheme
   of `Xbar=Spec S` over `A2`;
2. the cubic algebra `S/R` is not generated by one element;
3. every irreducible component of the discriminant has tame decomposition
   type `(2,1)`: one ramified double sheet, omitted from `X`, and one reduced
   unramified companion sheet whose generic point lies in `X`;
4. the discriminant divisor is reduced, its square class is nontrivial, and
   the Galois closure of `K/Frac(R)` has group `S3`; and
5. for a branch component `Gamma=V(g)`, its visible companion `F_g` is the
   single reduced divisor

```text
div_X(g(A,C))=F_g,                     [F_g]=0 in Pic(X).             (4)
```

The word *single* in `(4)` is a generic codimension-one assertion.  The
companion may lose finitely many closed points when `X` is cut out of its
finite completion; no properness of `F_g -> Gamma` is claimed.

## 1. The finite flat cubic completion

Every element of `S` is integral over `R`, hence also integral over `B`.
Normality of `B` gives `S subset B`.  Thus there is a factorization

```text
X --j--> Xbar=Spec S --pi--> A2,                 f=pi o j.            (5)
```

The first map is birational.  It is quasi-finite because each of its fibres
lies inside a fibre of the quasi-finite map `f`, while `pi` is finite.
Normalization-form Zariski Main therefore makes `j` an open immersion.

A normal Noetherian surface is Cohen--Macaulay.  At every point of `Xbar`,
the finite local map to the regular surface `A2` has equal dimensions and
zero-dimensional fibre.  Miracle flatness makes `pi` flat.  Hence

```text
S is finite locally free of rank 3 over R.                           (6)
```

The trace splitting and Quillen--Suslin give

```text
S=R direct_sum M,                M=ker Tr_(S/R) ~= R^2.               (7)
```

Choose a basis `(u,v)` of `M`.  For `T=xu+yv`, define the binary index form

```text
I(x,y)=det_R(1,T,T^2) relative to the basis (1,u,v).                 (8)
```

It is a homogeneous binary cubic.  Translation of `T` by an element of `R`
does not change `(8)`, and

```text
S=R[T]
iff I(x,y) in R*=k* for some x,y in R.                              (9)
```

If `delta` is the discriminant of the trace pairing in the basis `(1,u,v)`,
then

```text
disc(1,T,T^2)=I(x,y)^2 delta.                                      (10)
```

Thus the binary cubic `(8)`, rather than a guessed global root, is the
intrinsic generator sidecar.

## 2. Why a global cubic generator is impossible

Suppose `S=R[t]`.  Since both sides of the resulting monic presentation
have rank three, after translating by `Tr(t)/3` there are `p,q in R` with

```text
S=R[t]/(t^3+pt+q).                                                   (11)
```

The relative differential module and different are

```text
Omega_(S/R)=S dt/((3t^2+p)dt),
Different_(S/R)=(3t^2+p).                                           (12)
```

On the open `X`, the map to `A2` is etale, so `3t^2+p` has no zero.  It is
a regular function on the affine variety `X`, hence a unit of `B`.  By `(1)`,

```text
3t^2+p=lambda in k*.                                                (13)
```

But `(13)` gives a quadratic equation for `t` over `Frac(R)`, contradicting
that `t` generates the cubic field `K`.  This proves assertion 2 and, by
`(9)`, the exact index-form obstruction

```text
I(R^2) intersection k*=empty.                                      (14)
```

The full depressed-cubic trace packet explains the mechanism.  In the power
basis `(1,t,t^2)`,

```text
Tr(1)=3,       Tr(t)=0,       Tr(t^2)=-2p,
Tr(t^3)=-3q,                  Tr(t^4)=2p^2,                         (15)

delta=-4p^3-27q^2.                                                  (16)
```

Inside `S`, the pullback of the discriminant factors exactly as

```text
delta=(3t^2+p)^2(-3t^2-4p).                                        (17)
```

The squared factor is the ramified sheet and the last factor is its
unramified companion.  Formula `(17)` is the cubic analogue of THM-3784's
different/codifferent packet.  The contradiction in `(13)` says that this
packet cannot be globally coordinatized by one element on a constant-unit
etale open.

This does **not** say that finite flat cubic algebras over `R` are always
monogenic.  Section 5 gives a normal finite-flat counterexample.  Local
monogenic presentations also remain available after localization, where
new nonconstant units invalidate `(13)`.

## 3. The branch and quadratic-resolvent grammar

Fix an irreducible factor `g` of the discriminant `delta` from `(10)` and
localize at the DVR `R_(g)`.  Because the residue characteristic is zero,
the normalized cubic extension is tame.  Its ramification data satisfy

```text
sum_i e_i f_i=3.                                                     (18)
```

At least one `e_i` exceeds one.  Every corresponding ramified prime is
absent from the etale open `X`.

If all primes over `g` were ramified, then `g(A,C)` would have no zero on
`X`.  It would be a unit of `B`, hence a scalar by `(1)`, contradicting the
injectivity of `R -> B` and the nonconstancy of `g`.  Therefore an unramified
prime must also occur.  The only ramified partition of three with an
unramified summand is

```text
(e,f)=(2,1) plus (1,1).                                             (19)
```

The second prime is unique.  Its generic point must belong to `X`; otherwise
again `g(A,C)` would have no zero there.  Tameness gives

```text
ord_g(delta)=sum_i f_i(e_i-1)=1.                                   (20)
```

This proves assertions 3 and 5.  In the finite completion the generic
pullback is

```text
pi^*Gamma=2E_g+Fbar_g,                                              (21)
```

where `E_g` is ramified and deleted, while `F_g=Fbar_g intersection X`
is reduced and has residue degree one.  Restricting `(21)` to `X` gives
`(4)`.  In particular every branch companion is birational to its branch
curve and has trivial Picard class.

Equation `(20)` for every factor says that `delta` is squarefree.  The
branch locus is nonempty: otherwise the connected finite cover `Xbar -> A2`
would be etale, contrary to the trivial etale fundamental group of affine
space.  Hence `delta` is a nonconstant nonsquare in `Frac(R)`.  The quadratic
resolvent field

```text
Frac(R)(sqrt(delta))                                               (22)
```

is nontrivial, and the normal closure of the irreducible cubic field
`K/Frac(R)` has group `S3`.  In particular a cyclic cubic normalization is
impossible: its codimension-one inertia would have type `(3)` and no
unramified companion, violating `(1)` component by component.

## 4. The nodal arm curve consumes too many companion sheets

Now specialize to `k=C` and the cubic pseudo-plane of THM-3785,

```text
Y=Spec k[r,z,e]/(r^2e-z^3+c^3r),             c in k*,                (23)
Pic(Y)=Z/3[L],                     L=V(r,z) ~= A1_e.                  (24)
```

Suppose a Darboux pair `(A,C)` has surface degree three and has the minimal
nodal arm profile of THM-3790,

```text
(A,C)|L=(e^2,e^3-e).                                                 (25)
```

Let

```text
N(A,C)=C^2-A(A-1)^2,                    Gamma=V(N).                  (26)
```

The curve `Gamma` is irreducible and nodal.  The restriction in `(25)` is
its normalization: generically

```text
e=C/(A-1),                                                         (27)
```

and the two points `e=1,-1` lie over its node `(1,0)`.  Thus the affine line
`L` is already one complete residue-degree-one sheet over `Gamma`; it is not
a vertical or contracted curve.

THM-3790 proves the reduced generic pullback law

```text
div_Y(N(A,C))=L+D,                 D effective and nonzero,          (28)
[L]=1,                            [D]=2 in Pic(Y)=Z/3.               (29)
```

No component of `D` equals `L`, and quasi-finiteness makes every component
of `D` dominate `Gamma`.  If `Gamma` were a discriminant component, Section
3 would allow exactly one visible unramified prime above it.  The affine line
`L` already uses that residue-degree-one budget, while any component of `D`
would be a second visible unramified prime.  This contradicts `(19)`.  Hence

```text
N does not divide delta.                                             (30)
```

The Picard packet gives the same failure anatomy.  A cubic branch companion
must be one reduced principal divisor of class zero.  In `(28)`, class zero
is obtained only by cancellation between the distinct visible pieces of
classes one and two; neither the affine-line sheet `L` nor the residual
divisor `D` is the single class-zero companion demanded by `(4)`.

Consequently the finite cubic completion is unramified over the generic
point of `Gamma`.  If `D_i` are the irreducible components of `D`, then the
visible residue-degree ledger is

```text
1+sum_i [k(D_i):k(Gamma)] <=3,
sum_i [k(D_i):k(Gamma)] <=2.                                     (31)
```

If a component in `(31)` has residue degree one, its normalization is an
open subset of the affine-line normalization `A1 -> Gamma`.  At each of its
intersections with `L` over `e=+/-1`, it realizes the local nodal branch
opposite to the one carried by `L`; the two node points may be handled by
different components of `D`.  Such a component is the whole affine line
precisely when no point of its completion curve is deleted.  Thus `(31)`,
not an unsupported claim that every affine line on `Y` is a ruling fibre,
is the exact affine-line classification available from the cubic
normalization.

This excludes the natural attempt to use the nodal arm image itself as the
simple branch curve of a degree-three completion.  It does not exclude a
different discriminant curve or an intrinsically nonmonogenic `S3` cubic
algebra satisfying `(14),(19),(31)`.

## 5. Sharp hostiles and the surviving binary-cubic lane

The constant-unit hypothesis is load-bearing.  The monogenic map

```text
G_m x A1 -> A2,                    (u,v) |-> (u^3,v),                 (32)
```

is etale of degree three; its derivative `3u^2` is a nonconstant unit.

Nor may one promote the monogenic obstruction to all finite flat cubic
algebras.  Let

```text
R=k[a,d],
S_0=R[omega,theta]/(omega^2-a theta,
                    theta^2-d omega,
                    omega theta-ad).                                (33)
```

It is the normal third-Veronese invariant ring

```text
S_0=k[s^3,s^2t,st^2,t^3]=k[s,t]^(mu_3),
(a,omega,theta,d)=(s^3,s^2t,st^2,t^3),                              (34)
```

and is finite free of rank three over `R` with basis
`(1,omega,theta)`.  Its trace discriminant and binary index form are

```text
delta_0=-27a^2d^2,
I_0(x,y)=a x^3-d y^3.                                                (35)
```

Since `(35)` vanishes at `(a,d)=(0,0)`, it never represents a unit of `R`;
`S_0/R` is genuinely nonmonogenic.  Its cubic field is cyclic and its two
branch axes are totally ramified, so deleting ramification makes `ad` a
nonconstant unit.  This is the exact hostile predicted by Section 3: binary
cubic algebras exist, but the first easy nonmonogenic one fails the
constant-unit companion-sheet law.
