**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

jointly by the Keller derangement-character obstruction and the LRC carry
torsor.  The intended theorem separates three levels: a character whose
nonzero support consists of derangements; a genuinely fixed branch of the

fixed branch, while explicitly showing that separate carry marginals, full
