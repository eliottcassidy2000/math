# THM-4673 — `N = 2` minimal models are never classically free

## Setting

Let `V^univ_c` be the universal `N = 2` superconformal vertex superalgebra (NS sector), with generators:
- `J` (weight 1);
- `G^±` (weight 3/2, odd, `J_0`-charge `±1`);
- `L`.

`gr_F V^univ` is the supercommutative polynomial algebra on the jets:
- `j_m` for `J_(-1-m)`;
- `psi^±_m` for `G^±_(-3/2-m)` (odd);
- `l_m` for `L_(-2-m)`.

The Li degree is the subscript `m`, and `d` raises it by one.

For `c = c_k = 3k/(k+2)`, let `V_k = V^univ / I` be the simple quotient. Its pieces are:
- `R_V = gr^0_F V_k`, with defining ideal `I_0 = grI ∩ (Li degree 0)`;
- the arc ideal `J = (I_0)_d`;
- the map `pi : gr_F V^univ / J -> gr_F V_k`.

## Part B (all `k >= 1`): `V_k` is not classically free

Let `u_k = G^-_(-k-3/2) ... G^-_(-5/2) G^-_(-3/2)|0>`.
1. **`u_k ∈ I`.** By Adamović (CITED), the maximal submodule of the vacuum module at `c_k` is generated by `u_k`. In
   particular `u_k` is in it.
2. **Its symbol lies in `grI`.** `u_k` is a single PBW monomial (distinct odd modes in decreasing order), so its symbol
   is `±psi^-_k ... psi^-_0`. This is nonzero in the jet superalgebra and lies in `grI`.
3. **`J` contains no monomial in `psi^-` letters only.**
   - In Li degree 0, the only monomials in `psi^-` letters alone are `1` and `psi^-_0`, since `(psi^-_0)^2 = 0`. Neither
     lies in `I_0`: `I` starts above weight 3/2. So every element of `I_0` is a combination of monomials each
     containing a letter other than a `psi^-`.
   - This property is preserved by multiplication, and by `d`: differentiating a non-`psi^-` letter gives a
     non-`psi^-` letter, and differentiating another letter leaves it in place.
   - So every element of `J` is a combination of such monomials, and `psi^-_k ... psi^-_0 ∉ J`.

Hence `0 != psi^-_k ... psi^-_0 ∈ ker pi`. `□`

## Part A (`k = 1`, after Hao Li): the weight-4 kernel, explicitly

1. **The state is zero in `V`.** In `V_(sqrt3 Z)`, `e^beta(z) e^beta(w) = (z-w)^3 :e^beta(z)e^beta(w):`. So
   `G^+_(n) G^+ = 0` for `n >= -3`. In particular `G^+_(-5/2) G^+_(-3/2)|0> = 0` in `V`.
2. **Its symbol lies in `grI`.** The symbol is `psi^+_1 psi^+_0 ∈ grI`.
3. **`J_(4,2,1) = 0`.** The only charge-2 monomial in weight `<= 4` is `psi^+_1 psi^+_0`; `psi^+_0 psi^+_0 = 0`. The
   pieces of `J` that could reach `(4, 2, 1)` are `d J_(3,2,0)` and `x J_(4 - wt x, 2 - chg x, 1 - li x)`. Every
   source block is zero:
   - `J_(3,2,0)` sits inside the span of `(psi^+_0)^2 = 0`;
   - the charge-2 blocks of weight 2 or 3, Li 1, are empty;
   - `psi^+_0 ∉ I_0`, since `G^+ != 0`;
   - `psi^+_1 ∉ J`, since `J` in weight `5/2`, Li 1 is `d J_(3/2,1,0) = 0`;
   - charge-3 sources have weight `>= 9/2`.
4. **Conclusion.** `psi^+_1 psi^+_0` and its conjugate `psi^-_1 psi^-_0` span `ker pi` in weight 4. Through weight 10
   (FINITE-EXACT) they generate the whole kernel as a differential ideal. `□`

## Mechanism and reading

- **Odd squares have no differential consequences.** `(psi_0)^2 = 0` is automatic for an odd variable, so the arc
  space of the `C_2`-superscheme cannot see the high-order vanishing of `G^- G^-` products. That vanishing is what
  makes the vertex algebra smaller.
- **Contrasts.**
  - Even lattice: `V_(sqrt2 Z) = L_1(sl2)` is classically free (van Ekeren-Heluani).
  - `N = 1` minimal models are classically free exactly for `(p,q) = (2,4k)` (Hao Li).
- **Supersymmetric QFT (corrected after audit 2).** `V_k` is the holomorphic `N = 2` vertex algebra of the IR fixed
  point of the 2d `N = (2,2)` Landau-Ginzburg model `W = x^(k+2)`. The theorem says `V_k` is not a free quantization of
  the arc space of its own `C_2`-superscheme.
  - This is **not** a statement about the `Qbar_+`-cohomology of the half-twisted LG model. That is a different chiral
    algebra, in the twisted frame `L_0 - J_0/2`, containing sectors paired with right-moving chiral primaries; its
    `q^0` part is `Jac(W)`.
  - Nor is it about the arc space of `Crit(W)`.
  - An earlier version conflated these.
- **Higher `k`.** HYP-9295 records the finer pattern: for `k >= 2` the first failure is at weight `k+3`, Li 1, charge
  `±2`, below the weight `(k+1)(k+3)/2` of `u_k`.
