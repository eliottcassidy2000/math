# THM-4673 — the `c = 1` `N = 2` minimal model is not classically free

## Setting

Let `V^univ` be the universal `N = 2` superconformal vertex algebra at `c = 1`, with generators `J` (weight 1),
`G^+-` (weight 3/2, odd, `J_0`-charge `+-1`) and `L`.

`gr_F V^univ` is the supercommutative polynomial algebra on the jets:
- `j_m` for `J_(-1-m)`;
- `psi^+-_m` for `G^+-_(-3/2-m)` (odd);
- `l_m` for `L_(-2-m)`.

The Li degree is the subscript `m`, and `d` raises it by one.

The simple quotient is `V = V^univ / I = V_(sqrt3 Z)`. Its pieces are:
- `R_V = gr^0_F V`, with defining ideal `I_0 = grI ∩ (Li degree 0)`;
- the arc ideal `J = (I_0)_d`;
- the map `pi : C[J_infty X_V] = gr_F V^univ / J -> gr_F V`.

## Proof

1. **The state is zero in `V`.** In `V_(sqrt3 Z)`, `e^beta(z) e^beta(w) = (z-w)^3 :e^beta(z)e^beta(w):`. So
   `G^+_(n) G^+ = 0` for `n >= -3`. In particular `u := G^+_(-5/2) G^+_(-3/2)|0> = G^+_(-2) G^+ = 0` in `V`, i.e.
   `u ∈ I`.
2. **Its symbol lies in `grI`.** `u` is a single PBW monomial of Li degree 1, so its symbol is `psi^+_1 psi^+_0`, and
   `psi^+_1 psi^+_0 ∈ grI`.
3. **`J` is zero in weight 4, charge 2.** A monomial of charge 2 contains at least two `psi^+`, of total weight at
   least 3. In weight `<= 4` the only charge-2 monomial is `psi^+_1 psi^+_0`, in weight 4 and Li degree 1;
   `psi^+_0 psi^+_0 = 0`.

   The pieces of `J` that could reach `(weight 4, charge 2, Li 1)` are:
   - `d J_(3,2,0)`;
   - `x J_(4 - wt x, 2 - chg x, 1 - li x)` for the jet variables `x`.

   Every candidate source block is zero:
   - `J_(3,2,0)` sits inside the span of `psi^+_0 psi^+_0 = 0`.
   - The charge-2 blocks of weight 2 or 3, Li 1, contain no monomials.
   - The charge-1 blocks `(3/2, 1, 0)` and `(5/2, 1, 1)` are zero:
     - `psi^+_0 ∉ I_0`, since `G^+ != 0` in `V`;
     - `psi^+_1 ∉ J`, since `J` in weight `5/2`, Li 1 is generated by `d` of `J_(3/2,1,0) = 0`.
   - Charge-3 sources have weight `>= 9/2`.

   Hence `J_(4,2,1) = 0`.
4. **Conclusion.** `psi^+_1 psi^+_0` is a nonzero element of `ker pi` in weight 4. The charge-conjugate argument gives
   `psi^-_1 psi^-_0`. `□`

## Mechanism and reading

- **No differential consequence for an odd square.** For an odd variable `psi_0^2 = 0` is automatic, so it has no
  differential consequences: `d(psi_0^2) = 0` identically. The arc space of the `C_2`-superscheme therefore cannot see
  the third-order vanishing of the `e^beta e^beta` OPE. The vertex algebra does see it: the vanishing comes from
  `beta^2 = 3` being odd and `> 1`.
- **Comparison with even lattices.** For an even lattice the bosonic relation `x_0^2 = 0` does propagate:
  `V_(sqrt2 Z) = L_1(sl2)` is classically free (van Ekeren-Heluani).
- **Supersymmetric QFT.** `V` is the chiral algebra of the 2d `N = (2,2)` Landau-Ginzburg model with `W = x^3`, the
  derived critical locus `Crit(x^3)` of S43's note. So the half-twisted LG chiral algebra is not a "free"
  quantization of its classical limit.
- **Contrast.** `N = 1` minimal models are classically free exactly for `(p,q) = (2,4k)` (Hao Li).
- **Higher `k`.** HYP-9295 records the pattern for `c = 3k/(k+2)`: first failure at weight `k+3`, which is 4 for
  `k = 1`.
