# THM-1242 — seven-wall pair-beat sunflower law

At a sum/difference beat of one defining pair among seven walls, those two
danger masks coincide, leaving six master-clock obligations, all containing
zero.  On a common reduced clock `Q>=2`, with
`A(Q)=2ceil(Q/14)-1`, the candidate uniform escape threshold is

```text
B7(Q)=6A(Q)-4=12ceil(Q/14)-10 <= Q.
```

Seven walls are exactly critical: for eight walls the analogous threshold
already exceeds the first modulus in every ceiling shell by one.  In the
mixed-period setting, nontrivial quotient periods do not suffice: at `q=15`,
the seven speeds `{1,2,3,4,5,7,16}` yield six masks whose nonzero petals
partition all fourteen nonzero residues, so their union is the whole clock.

This reservation does not claim a useful pair beat exists on every protected
needle or that the mixed-clock sunflower is incompatible with an LRC cover.
