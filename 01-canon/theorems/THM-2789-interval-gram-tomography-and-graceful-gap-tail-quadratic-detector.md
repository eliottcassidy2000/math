# THM-2789 -- interval Gram tomography and graceful gap-tail quadratic detector

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: prove that the pairwise Gram matrix of a finite family of
intervals on a line, together with the ambient length, reconstructs the
complete multiset of incidence columns by the Helly identity and Boolean
Möbius inversion.  Apply this to the consecutive-ones gap matrix of a
graceful labeling to detect and count every gap-tail column from quadratic
data.  Record the sharp losses: the Gram matrix forgets column order and the
marked attachment rank, and the interval identity fails for circular arcs
without a retained cut.

Until the proof, exact companion, independent hostile audit, and status
promotion land, this reservation is not a proved theorem or dependency.
