# THM-842 — B retains the apex-relative sheet

On THM-828's 58 size-nine defect cells, the B gap-contraction face is the
unique B3 role that descends to literal `Q` cell by cell.  Its 58 target `Q`
cells and 58 target endpoint-coupled `bar P` cells are all distinct.  The
jointly well-defined A/C endpoint carrier instead has exactly 29 doubleton
fibres:

```text
carrier                    cells     fibre profile
{Q(Au),Q(Cu)}                 29       29 x 2
{bar P(Au),bar P(Cu)}         29       29 x 2
Q(Bu)                         58       58 x 1
bar P(Bu)                     58       58 x 1
(endpoint carrier,B carrier) 58       58 x 1.             (1)
```

This is not chirality loss.  Each endpoint-deck companion has the same defect
`D` and the same complete upper skew word `J`, not merely the same sign.  The
lost bit is an affine sheet generated by reflection after complementing every
nonapex coordinate.  Endpoint deletion turns this sheet into ordinary
complement-reflection, which `Q` already quotients; B transports it as a
distinct sheet and therefore retains it.

## Exact face geometry

For a target cell `(a,b)` of `X_(n-1)`, the three literal maps are

```text
A_n(u)[a,b] = u[a,b],
B_n(u)[a,b] = u[a+1,b],
C_n(u)[a,b] = u[a+1,b+1].                                (2)
```

Thus A deletes fixed-path vertex `n`, C deletes vertex `1`, and B contracts a
path gap.  B is not deletion of any one tournament vertex.  All maps commute
with complement, while reflection satisfies

```text
A sigma = sigma C,   C sigma = sigma A,   B sigma = sigma B. (3)
```

Equation (3) proves that B descends to `Q` alone and A/C descend only as an
unordered endpoint pair.  The exact audit sharpens this: on the 58 source
cells, rolewise literal-Q descent counts are `(A,B,C)=(0,58,0)`.  All three
roles descend after the coarser `bar P` projection, but A and C retain only
the 29 cells in (1); B retains 58.

Every restriction of THM-832's `V` has rank four.  The eleven occupied
sectors have eleven distinct A images, eleven B images, and eleven C images,
with

```text
d_A=sigma(d_C),  d_B=sigma(d_B).                         (4)
```

Therefore the endpoint collapse is nonlinear base/path loss, never defect-
coordinate loss.

## The apex-relative antipode

Let `a_n` be the apex bit `(n,1)` and define

```text
theta_n(u)=sigma_n(u xor (FULL_n xor a_n)).               (5)
```

On the 58 canonical source representatives, `theta_9` is fixed-point-free and
has 29 two-element orbits.  These orbits are exactly the endpoint-Q and
endpoint-`bar P` fibres in (1).  Since `D` is reflection-fixed and apex-zero,
`theta` preserves `D`; complement and reflection each negate the skew word,
so their composite preserves the entire `J` and `eta`.

The decisive functor identities are

```text
A theta_9 = sigma_8 kappa_8 C,
C theta_9 = sigma_8 kappa_8 A,
B theta_9 = theta_8 B.                                   (6)
```

The endpoint faces delete the distinguished apex context, converting `theta`
to the ordinary `sigma kappa` action already killed by `Q`.  B contains the
apex and transports the affine antipode itself.  In all 29 companion pairs,
the two B-Q values differ.  This proves the last two rows of (1) structurally,
not merely by a fibre histogram.

There is also an orientation warning.  Within every witness,
`P^->(Au)=P^->(Cu)`, while across companions these ordered values reverse.
The legitimate complement-line multiset `bar P` has 29 doubletons even though
an independently oriented ordered tuple can misleadingly show 58 values.

## Chirality action

Its first nonzero layer distribution is

```text
tau=5:44, tau=6:12, tau=7:2.                             (7)
```

Relative to upper `eta`, B preserves 20 and reverses 38.  It never erases the
sign.  Preservation sectors are

```text

```

the other five occupied sectors reverse it.

A and C each preserve 31, reverse 23, and erase four.  Their first-layer
histogram is `tau5:25,tau6:18,tau7:11,none:4`; unlike B, their action varies
inside sectors.  Endpoint `bar P` plus both A/C signs still gives only

```text
50 cells = 42 singletons + 8 doubletons.                  (8)
```

Upper `D`, the full upper `J`, and endpoint chirality therefore cannot repair
the 29-fold endpoint-deck collapse.  B carries a genuinely transverse affine
sheet bit.

## Repaired path cards versus induced tournaments

For deletion position `j`, the repaired fixed-path card is

```text
R_j(u)[a,b]=u[a+1_(a>=j),b+1_(b>=j)].                    (9)
```

An internal card also needs the shortcut seam
`s_j=u[j+1,j-1]`; when the inherited shortcut points against the fixed path,
`R_j` alone is not a literal presentation of the induced tournament in the
inherited order.  B is different from every internal `R_j` as a coordinate
map, although accidental equalities occur on individual bank elements.  Among
58 sources, the numbers with `B(u)=R_j(u)` for `j=2,...,8` are

```text
0,0,1,2,1,0,0.                                          (10)
```

For repaired-card Q or `bar P`, position orbits under reflection have

```text
{1,9}: 29 cells, 29 x 2,
{2,8}: 46 cells, 34 x 1 + 12 x 2,
{3,7}: 58 singletons,
{4,6}: 58 singletons,
{5}:   58 singletons.                                   (11)
```

The complete position-marked repaired deck modulo reversal has 58 singleton
values.  The seven shortcut bits form only 22 words and do not alter the
fibre counts in (11).

For the intrinsic induced-tournament card, delete the vertex in the adjacency
matrix, find a directed Hamiltonian path, re-encode in the size-eight chart,
and atlas-lookup its merged node.  The exact position-orbit node census is

```text
{1,9}: 49 cells = 41 x 1 + 7 x 2 + 1 x 3,
{2,8}: 45 cells = 33 x 1 +11 x 2 + 1 x 3,
{3,7}: 55 cells = 52 x 1 + 3 x 2,
{4,6}: 55 cells = 52 x 1 + 3 x 2,
```

The ordered nine-card node sequence modulo reversal is injective.  But its

the bare upper-node partition of THM-834.  The central induced node alone has
that same partition.  Hence neither central deletion nor the entire unmarked
deletion deck adds any node information on this bank; path position does.

## Boundaries and Tournament Analysis

Bare-sector continuation is false even though (4) has rank four.  The missing
sector `1026286` has `d_B=001946` with 4,144 B-kernel bases despite empty A/C
support, while the other three punctures have post-B masses `4,504,4` and die
only at raw S2.  Sending bare `D` to `d_B` therefore creates false stalks.

Use nine information carriers as Tournament Analysis vertices, from bare D
through endpoint, B, and ordered-deck carriers.  The pairwise observable is
the number of unordered source pairs separated.  Switching from raw retention
to retention per logarithmic cell cost flips 28 of 36 edges.  Both gauges are
transitive with score histogram `0,...,8`, no directed triangle, singleton
SCCs, and one Hamiltonian path.

The challenged assumption is that continuation vertices must be runners,
tournament vertices, or unmarked isomorphism classes.  Here the correct
vertices include face roles, affine sheets, path positions, seam tokens, and
proof obligations.  This theorem preserves only the finite defect-bank
relations stated above.  It preserves no LRC gap, owner, wall schedule, or
loneliness predicate and proves no implication for LRC(14). ∎
