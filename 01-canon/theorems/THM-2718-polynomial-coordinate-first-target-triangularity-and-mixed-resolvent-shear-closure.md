lemma and its THM-2696 graph specialization.  The intended result says that
if one target of a planar constant-Jacobian pair is already a polynomial

closes every mixed target `(A,B+H(A,d))` and in fact every restricted second
polynomial target paired with `A`.  No theorem, arbitrary first-coordinate
