# THM-3077 -- pointed norm-augmentation lift and relation-carry obstruction

**PROVED CANDIDATE + VERIFIED-EXACT; INDEPENDENT AUDIT REQUESTED.**

THM-3073 proves that a separated resultant is a weighted norm of its lower
and normal resultants.  Its scalar remembers magnitude and one relation but
can erase torsion phase.  The missing object is neither another scalar norm
nor an untyped choice of root.  It is a canonical finite lift torsor carrying
one modular **carry**.

## 1. Weighted norm plus augmentation

Work first over a field `K`.  Let

```text
T=(G_m)^r,                 r>=2,
w=(w_1,...,w_r),           w_i>=1,
W=sum_i w_i.                                                  (1)
```

Let `Delta G_m` be the diagonal subtorus and let

```text
A=T/Delta G_m                                                (2)
```

be the augmentation torus.  Define

```text
N_w(x)=product_i x_i^w_i,
F_w=(N_w,pi): T -> G_m x A.                                  (3)
```

Then `F_w` is a finite isogeny of degree `W`, with exact kernel

```text
ker F_w=Delta mu_W.                                          (4)
```

If `char K` does not divide `W`, it is finite etale.  On `q`-torsion,

```text
ker(F_w|T[q])=Delta mu_gcd(q,W).                              (5)
```

### Proof

The character lattice of `A` is

```text
X^*(A)={m in Z^r: sum_i m_i=0}.                              (6)
```

The pullback lattice for `(3)` is generated by `(6)` and the weight row
`w`.  Modulo `(6)`, all standard basis vectors become one generator, and
`w` becomes multiplication by `W`.  Hence the lattice cokernel is `Z/W`,
which proves finiteness, degree, and `(4)`.  Intersecting the diagonal kernel
with `T[q]` gives `(5)`.

This is dimension-minimal.  Any algebraic target receiving a generically
finite map from the `r`-torus has dimension at least `r`; after the scalar
norm, at least `r-1` sidecar dimensions are needed.  The augmentation quotient
supplies exactly that many.  It removes the continuous kernel but not the
diagonal torsion in `(4)`.

## 2. The lift torsor is the preserved object

For any scheme `U` and datum

```text
y:U -> G_m x A,                                              (7)
```

define

```text
Lift_w(y)=U fibre_product_(G_m x A,F_w) T.                   (8)
```

This construction commutes with base change.  It is canonically a
`mu_W`-torsor in the fppf topology, and etale when `W` is invertible on `U`.
A phase-preserving realization is exactly a section of `(8)`; if sections
exist, they form a torsor under `mu_W(U)`.  The global obstruction is its
class

```text
[Lift_w(y)] in H^1_fppf(U,mu_W).                             (9)
```

Thus the torsion killed by the scalar norm has not disappeared from the
mathematics.  It survives canonically as the ambiguity of the lift torsor.

After a common determinant-line trivialization, choose the first coordinate
and write

```text
r_i=x_i/x_1,             2<=i<=r.                           (10)
```

Then

```text
x_1^W=N_w(x) product_(i=2)^r r_i^(-w_i),
x_i=r_i x_1.                                                (11)
```

The ratios in `(10)` are not gauge-invariant physical observables under
independent line rescalings.  Intrinsically they are relative determinant
lines, or equivalently the augmentation class `(2)`.  The common line gauge
is part of the typing.

## 3. Pointed paths and the exact carry

Over `C`, `F_w` is a covering map.  Every path in `G_m x A`, together with
one initial lift, has a unique lift through `(3)`.  In the coordinates `(10)`,
the lift is the branch of the `W`th root in `(11)` selected by the initial
point.

For a closed target path, put

```text
c=wind(N_w)-sum_(i=2)^r w_i wind(r_i) mod W.                 (12)
```

The lifted path closes if and only if `c=0`.  In general its endpoint is its
initial point multiplied diagonally by

```text
exp(2 pi i c/W).                                             (13)
```

Indeed the integer before reduction in `(12)` is `W wind(x_1)` for every
closed source loop.  Conversely, divisibility by `W` reconstructs
`wind(x_1)` and then every `wind(x_i)` from `(10)`.  This proves both
necessity and sufficiency.

There is an exact endpoint hostile.  If `q|W`, take

```text
x_i(t)=exp(2 pi i t/q),       0<=t<=1, all i.                (14)
```

The norm and every augmentation ratio have the same endpoints at `t=0,1`,
but the norm winds `W/q` times and the lifted endpoint is the nontrivial
diagonal element `zeta_q`.  Endpoint data erase it; continuation and `(12)`
recover it.

There is also a canonical local normalization.  Let the coefficient ring be
an `I`-adically complete `Q`-algebra with topologically nilpotent ideal `I`.
If the norm and ratios lie in `1+I`, then

```text
x_1=exp((log N_w-sum_(i=2)^r w_i log r_i)/W)                (15)
```

is the unique lift congruent to one.  Equation `(15)` is a carrier.  Without
a point or a proved equality among physical pointed factors, it is not
automatically one physical input factor.

## 4. Minimal endpoint sidecars

For the two-block THM-3073 norm, write

```text
R=S^a T^b,             r=T/S,             W=a+b.            (16)
```

The exponent matrix of `(R,r)` is

```text
[ a   b ]
[-1   1 ],                                                   (17)
```

with determinant `W`.  Hence `(R,r)` has exactly the diagonal `mu_W`
ambiguity.

A different monomial endpoint sidecar `S^alpha T^beta` has determinant

```text
Delta=a beta-b alpha.                                       (18)
```

Together with the norm it is injective on `q`-torsion if and only if

```text
gcd(Delta,q)=1.                                              (19)
```

Such a sidecar exists if and only if

```text
gcd(a,b,q)=1.                                                (20)
```

Thus the symmetric relative line is canonical but not always `q`-optimal.
For example, a relative line can lose a prime dividing `a+b` even when an
asymmetric pointed character would preserve it.  The loss is unavoidable
exactly when a prime dividing `q` divides every norm weight.  In general
rank, a `q`-invertible character completion of the norm row exists precisely
when

```text
gcd(q,w_1,...,w_r)=1.                                       (21)
```

At `k=14`, both GMC exponent pairs hit the unavoidable case for `C7` on the
unrestricted two-input phase torus:

```text
THM-3069: (a,b)=(14,13!),
THM-3063: (a,b)=(182,12!),
gcd(a,b,91)=gcd(a+b,91)=7.                                 (22)
```

No single endpoint monomial complement repairs the full `C91` pair.  Norm
plus the relative line preserves the `C13` components but loses diagonal
`C7`; a point or based lift restores it.  This is not a new obstruction on
the literal physical slices of THM-3063/3069: there the normal-resultant phase
has fixed positive orientation (and `(-1)^14=1` in the one-normal case), so
`r=T/S` already recovers the variable base phase.  The diagonal hostile
applies when both input phases are genuinely variable, as in a proposed
transport of independently labelled sidecars.

## 5. The model tropical flag becomes a carry spectrum

At one model suspension stage of THM-3069, set

```text
a=k,                    b=(k-1)!,
r_k=U_k/R_(k-1),        W_k=k+(k-1)!.                       (23)
```

The exact upper-triangular **model carrier** satisfies

```text
R_k=R_(k-1)^k U_k^((k-1)!)
   =R_(k-1)^W_k r_k^((k-1)!).                              (24)
```

Therefore `(R_k,r_k,c_k mod W_k)` with a reference lift reconstructs
`R_(k-1)` by `(11)--(12)`, and then `U_k=r_kR_(k-1)`.  Iterating turns the
augmented model phase torus of the lexicographic tropical flag into a
mixed-radix relation-and-carry inverse.  In the literal physical carrier
`U_k>0`, so its phase lane is fixed and the phase reconstruction is simpler.
The attachment's three-coordinate proof state is now literal:

```text
symbolic state       = augmentation class [x],
algebraic defect     = weighted norm N_w(x),
continuation sidecar = mu_W carry and reference lift.       (25)
```

Equation `(24)` is exact for the model/leading carrier, not for the finite
physical resultant before THM-3069's exponentially small remainder.  A
physical reconstruction needs a nonzero carrier-to-physical continuation
which tracks that remainder.  It is still fixed-low and lexicographic.

## 6. LRC and JC leverage

For a cyclic `p`-profile, choosing one covariant phase pointer and moving it
to zero gives the pointed Fourier identity

```text
hat x(0)=p x_0-sum_(eta!=0) hat x(eta).                     (26)
```

The pointer value and all augmentation modes reconstruct the entire profile.
THM-2543 supplies an anchored lane and an augmentation lane, but explicitly
lacks one common physical atom/root selector joining them.  Equation `(26)`
identifies that covariant pointer cospan as the realization debt; it does not
construct it.  THM-2548's rule to pair charges before taking the full norm is
the additive order-of-operations analogue.

THM-3048 gives a different no-pointer route.  Whole-table parallel holonomy
can force all pointed determinant factors to agree, making the unpointed norm
a perfect prime power with its constant-one root.  A perfect-power product
alone is insufficient: `(1+t)^p` times `p-1` unit factors is a perfect `p`th
power but does not identify equal physical factors.  One needs a point or the
proved transitive pointed-factor equality.

For JC, an even-`W` lift carries one diagonal `mu_2` torsor.  The quartic
`V4` obstruction is a rank-two `F2` plane, so one determinant/root line cannot
replace it.  THM-2962 preserves the full plane by genuine pullback.  THM-3072
is the finite common-atom analogue: all three conjugate `C2` views reconstruct
the flag module, while incomplete separate views lose a sector.  These are
blueprints for the missing realization, not a new Keller or Jacobian closure.

## 7. Zero and physicality boundaries

If any `x_i=0`, the augmentation ratios and the etale Kummer lift cease to be
defined.  The correct replacement is the normal-cone kernel-to-cokernel and
arc-barcode data of THM-2983/2985.  Other sharp boundaries are incompatible
determinant-line gauges, a root chosen only after summation, and separate
sidecars with no common atom or whole-table transport.

Top exterior power or determinant retains only the norm.  A full regular
representation can retain every phase, but without a cyclic vector/common
atom it still supplies no root origin.  No formal reconstruction here makes
an abstract section physical.
