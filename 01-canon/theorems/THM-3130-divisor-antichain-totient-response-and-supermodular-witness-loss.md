# THM-3130 -- divisor-antichain totient response and supermodular witness loss

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-3055 identifies the safe points on the distinguished `q`-grid with an
upset of unhit divisors and compresses that upset to its minimal antichain.
THM-3129 says that, universally, no upset inequality can be discarded from
the divisor-poset Hasse cone.  These facts are compatible: one *fixed* upset
can be evaluated from principal tails by signed inclusion-exclusion, even
though principal-tail positivity cannot replace the full family of upset
inequalities.

This theorem makes that response formula exact, factors its totient weights,
and identifies the operation law under inserting another speed.

## 1. Principal response tails

Fix `q>=2`, and let `Div(q)` be ordered by divisibility.  For any response
`r:Div(q)->R` and any `m|q`, put

```text
R_q(m)=sum_(m|d|q) r(d).                                      (1)
```

If `U` is a nonempty upset and `M=Min(U)`, then

```text
U=union_(m in M) {d:m|d|q}.                                  (2)
```

Intersections of the principal sets in `(2)` are again principal:

```text
intersection_(m in A) {d:m|d|q}
 ={d:lcm(A)|d|q}.                                             (3)
```

Ordinary inclusion-exclusion therefore gives the response identity

```text
sum_(d in U) r(d)
 =sum_(empty != A subset M)(-1)^(|A|+1) R_q(lcm(A)).          (4)
```

Equation `(4)` is an identity of coefficient vectors, not only of their
totals.  It holds over any commutative coefficient group where the displayed
finite sums make sense.

## 2. Closed totient tails

Specialize to `r(d)=phi(d)`.  Write

```text
q=product_p p^a_p,             m=product_p p^b_p,             (5)
```

where `0<=b_p<=a_p`.  Multiplicativity and
`sum_(j=0)^a phi(p^j)=p^a` give

```text
T_q(m):=sum_(m|d|q)phi(d)
       =product_(p^a || q) t(p,a,b_p),                        (6)

t(p,a,0)=p^a,
t(p,a,b)=p^a-p^(b-1)                 for 1<=b<=a.             (7)
```

For a nonempty speed set `S`, let `U_q(S)` and
`M_q(S)=Min(U_q(S))` be the THM-3055 unhit upset and antichain.  Then its
grid-witness count is the closed formula

```text
|W_q(S)|
 =sum_(empty != A subset M_q(S))
    (-1)^(|A|+1) T_q(lcm(A)).                                 (8)
```

If `U_q(S)` is empty, take `M_q(S)` empty and interpret the sum in `(8)` as
zero.  This includes the terminal case in which some speed is divisible by
`q`.

For example, `q=12,S={2}` has `M={3,4}` and

```text
|W_12({2})|=T_12(3)+T_12(4)-T_12(12)=8+6-4=10.              (9)
```

## 3. Exact speed-insertion loss

Adjoin one speed `v`, and put `g=gcd(v,q)`.  THM-3055 says that the lost
divisors are

```text
L_v(S)=U_q(S) intersection Div(g).                           (10)
```

Only minimal generators dividing `g` can contribute.  Define

```text
M_g(S)={m in M_q(S):m|g}.                                    (11)
```

Applying `(2)--(8)` inside `Div(g)` gives the exact loss

```text
Delta_v(S):=|W_q(S)|-|W_q(S union {v})|
 =sum_(d in L_v(S))phi(d)
 =sum_(empty != A subset M_g(S))
    (-1)^(|A|+1) T_g(lcm(A)).                                (12)
```

The empty `M_g(S)` gives zero loss.  In the control `(9)`, adjoining a speed
with gcd `6` loses the layers generated by `3`, of mass
`T_6(3)=4`.

## 4. Supermodularity and the death barcode

Let

```text
F_q(S)=|W_q(S)|                                               (13)
```

on nonempty finite speed sets.  If `S subset T`, then
`U_q(T) subset U_q(S)`, so `(10)` gives

```text
Delta_v(T)<=Delta_v(S).                                      (14)
```

Equivalently,

```text
F_q(S union {v})-F_q(S)
 <=F_q(T union {v})-F_q(T).                                  (15)
```

Thus `F_q` is decreasing and **supermodular**.  The direction matters:
witness *loss* has diminishing returns, while the negative increment of a
decreasing function has increasing returns.

For an ordered speed list `v_1,...,v_k`, define the death time

```text
tau(d)=min{i:d|v_i},                 d|q, d>1,                (16)
```

with `tau(d)=infinity` if the layer is never hit.  Every denominator layer
is a bar of multiplicity `phi(d)`, and after any nonempty prefix

```text
|W_q({v_1,...,v_j})|
 =sum_(d|q,d>1) phi(d) 1_(tau(d)>j).                         (17)
```

This divisor-death barcode is the exact operation-level object behind
`(12)--(15)`.  It records grid witnesses only; it is not the continuous safe
component barcode of the full loneliness profile.

## 5. Sharp normalized-transport hostile

It is tempting to normalize the surviving totient layers to probability one
and regard speed insertion as upward stochastic transport on `Div(q)`.  This
is false already for `q=6`.

Start with `S={1}`.  Then

```text
U_6(S)={2,3,6},                    total weight=1+2+2=5.      (18)
```

Adjoin `v=2`, leaving

```text
U_6(S union {2})={3,6},            total weight=2+2=4.       (19)
```

On the principal upset `X={2,6}` the normalized masses are

```text
mu_S(X)=3/5,             mu_(S union {2})(X)=1/2,            (20)

mu_(S union {2})(X)-mu_S(X)=-1/10.                          (21)
```

Upward Strassen/Hasse transport would make every upset mass nondecreasing,
so `(21)` rules it out.  Raw insertion is a death operation, not a
mass-preserving displacement.  A cemetery state can conserve lost mass, but
such a state is an added observer and not a canonical LRC witness.

## 6. Relation to upset-facet irredundancy and scope

Equation `(4)` does not contradict THM-3129.  It uses alternating signs; a
negative higher-intersection term prevents the principal responses from
implying the sign of an arbitrary upset response.  Minimal antichains give
exact **state/evaluation compression**, while the universal monotone-current
cone still has one irredundant facet for every nontrivial upset.

The theorem concerns the fixed `q`-grid at threshold `1/q`.  It does not
control safe arcs between grid points, their Haar measure, endpoint owners,
the maximum loneliness value, or tightness.  It supplies no LRC(14) ledger
decrement.  Its useful payload is an exact response calculus and operation
law for the grid sidecar.
