# THM-3036 -- marked modular `S4` Schreier incidence and `V4` boundary

**PROVED CANDIDATE + VERIFIED-EXACT; AWAITING INDEPENDENT HOSTILE AUDIT.**

This note starts from proved THM-2968 and THM-2971.  It isolates exactly what
the marked quotient

```text
Gamma=PSL_2(Z)=C2*C3 -> S4
```

does on the two six-sheet sets, and separates three objects that are easy to
conflate:

1. the six-state generator Schreier graph;
2. the factor-orbit incidence quotient of the Bass--Serre tree;
3. the local `V4` stabilizer-extension frame.

Only (2), after forgetting multiplicity, is a partial cube.  Only (3) is a
literal `V4`.  Neither is a faithful finite model of the Farey flank.

## 1. Marked quotient and common labels

Use the THM-2968 generators

```text
a=(2 3),                         b=(0 2 1),
a^2=b^3=1,                       <a,b>=S4.                (1)
```

Label the edge sheets by

```text
0=01, 1=23, 2=02, 3=13, 4=03, 5=12,                     (2)
```

and use THM-2968's matching order and orientation gauge for the oriented
four-cycles.  The exact induced permutations are

```text
edge:        a=(2 4)(3 5),          b=(0 2 5)(1 3 4),
orientation: a=(0 1)(2 5)(3 4),    b=(0 2 5)(1 3 4).     (3)
```

Thus the two actions agree on the ternary generator.  The binary generator
has type `1^2 2^2` on edges and `2^3` on orientations.  This is the exact
graph-level form of THM-2968's central sign twist.

The loopless underlying edge graph consists of two `b`-triangles joined by
the two edges `2--4` and `3--5`; it has eight edges and degree sequence
`2,2,3,3,3,3`.  The orientation graph consists of the same two triangles
joined by the bridge `0--1`: its other two `a`-edges, `2--5` and `3--4`,
coincide with `b`-edges.  It has seven simple edges and degree sequence
`2,2,2,2,3,3`.

Both contain triangles.  Therefore neither six-state Schreier graph is
bipartite or a partial cube, even after loops and repeated colors are
discarded.

## 2. The discriminant cover is `C3*C3`, not a ternary tree

Let `epsilon:Gamma->C2` count the parity of the `a` letters.  Reidemeister--
Schreier gives

```text
Gamma^+=ker(epsilon)=<b,c | b^3=c^3=1>=C3*C3,
c=aba.                                                      (4)
```

Its image is `A4`.  In the common gauge,

```text
c=(0 4 3)(1 5 2)                                          (5)
```

on both six-sheet sets.  Hence the two colored actions restrict literally
to the same `A4`-set, as THM-2971 predicts.

The loopless simple graph generated by `b,c` is

```text
K_(2,4) plus the disjoint edges 2--5 and 3--4.             (6)
```

It has ten edges, degrees `3,3,3,3,4,4`, and four visible triangles.  It is
still not a partial cube.  Passing to the discriminant cover removes the odd
binary discrepancy; it does not turn the six sheets into a tree.

There is an exact prime asymmetry in the free-factor grammar.  The index-two
cover replaces the one binary factor by a second, conjugate ternary factor.
It does **not** replace `C2` by a `V4` translation:

```text
C2*C3  --discriminant kernel-->  C3*C3.                   (7)
```

## 3. The Bass--Serre incidence shadows

For a generator action, replace the six states by its factor orbits and keep
one incidence edge for each state.  This is the finite quotient of the
appropriate Bass--Serre tree.  Its exact incidence matrices are

```text
edge C2/C3 quotient:
    [1 0]
    [0 1]
    [1 1]
    [1 1],

orientation C2/C3 quotient:
    [1 1]
    [2 0]
    [0 2],

common C3/C3 quotient:
    [1 2]
    [2 1].                                                (8)
```

The entries are intersection multiplicities of factor orbits.  After
replacing each positive entry by one, the three simple graphs are

```text
edge:        C4 with one leaf at each of two opposite vertices;
orientation: P5;
cover:       C4.                                          (9)
```

All three graphs in (9) are partial cubes.  This is the strongest correct
partial-cube survivor.  It is also sharply lossy: the doubled end links in
the orientation matrix and the alternating `1,2,1,2` weights on the common
square are erased.  Those multiplicities are part of the six-sheet action.
Indeed `P5` is a tree, `C4=Q2`, and attaching either leaf to the first graph
uses a fresh hypercube coordinate at the attachment vertex, so the displayed
embeddings are isometric rather than merely bipartite.

The common weighted square has abstract automorphism group `V4`, acting
regularly on its four factor-orbit vertices, if interchange of the two `C3`
factors is allowed.  This gives a genuine abstract `V4` torsor at the
**four factor-orbit nodes**, not at the six sheets.

## 4. Two different `V4` frames

Let

```text
N={1,(01)(23),(02)(13),(03)(12)}                           (10)
```

be the normal quartic `V4`.  On either six-sheet set it has three orbits

```text
{0,1}, {2,3}, {4,5},                                      (11)
```

and every sheet stabilizer in `N` has order two.  Thus the six sheets are
not an `N`-torsor.  The quotient by `N` is the common three-block frame

```text
S4/N=S3,                                                   (12)
```

which remembers the matching/inverse-pair permutation but forgets the
within-pair central twist distinguishing (3).

There is nevertheless a second, exact local `V4`.  Put

```text
d=(01)(23),                H=<d>.
```

`H` is the selected common sheet stabilizer after restriction to `A4`.
Its centralizer is `D=C_S4(d)=D8`.  The three order-four subgroups of `D`
containing `H` are exactly

```text
normal quartic V4 N,
selected edge stabilizer V4_edge,
selected orientation stabilizer C4.                       (13)
```

They meet pairwise in `H`, and their images are the three nonzero lines of

```text
D/H = D8/C2 = V4.                                         (14)
```

Equation (14) is a theorem-grade `V4` frame: it classifies the three local
order-four extensions of the common discriminant-cover stabilizer.  It is
not a `V4` action on the six sheets and it is not the normal quotient (12).

In the chosen modular factor frame the normalizer of the unordered pair
`{<b>,<aba>}` is the selected `V4_edge`.  Its action on the four weighted-
square nodes has image only `C2` in the edge lift, while in the orientation
lift it realizes the full regular `V4`.  The exact table is in the companion
transcript.  This is a small graph shadow of the THM-2968 central twist:
the same root relabellings acquire one extra factor-orbit reflection only in
the orientation descent.

## 5. Sharp hostiles to the proposed analogies

### Farey / THM-2056

The modular word `ab` is parabolic and has infinite order in `C2*C3`; in the
`S4` quotient it has order four.  Hence

```text
(ab)^4=1                                                   (15)
```

on both six-sheet graphs while it is a nontrivial reduced modular word.
After the discriminant cover the reduced word `b(aba)` has infinite order in
`C3*C3`, but its `A4` image has order two.  Thus `(b aba)^2` also collapses.
These are short, explicit reasons the finite Schreier graphs cannot retain
the Farey flank, Bass--Serre path, or THM-2056 Gram-owner cross term.  The
binary/ternary common object is a finite congruence quotient, not the fraction
tree itself.

### Tournament on six vertices

The `S4` action on unordered pairs of the six states has orbit sizes `3,12`.
Its directed orbitals have sizes `6,24`, and both are self-paired, so no
`S4`-invariant orientation survives.

The discriminant cover produces one sharp positive survivor.  Under `A4`, the
six directed opposite pairs remain one self-paired orbital of size six, but
the 24 directed incident pairs split into two inverse orbitals of size 12.
Choosing either one gives an `A4`-invariant chiral orientation of the
octahedral incident graph `L(K4)`:

```text
outdegree=indegree=2,
three antipodal/opposite pairs remain ties,
all eight octahedral triangular faces are directed cycles.                 (16)
```

Thus there is no full `A4`-invariant tournamentâ€”the three opposite pairs are
an unavoidable self-paired orbitalâ€”but there is a canonical orientation up
to global reversal on every nonopposite pair.  This is the exact repaired
meaning of a size-six tournament analogy.  It is an oriented octahedron with
three ties, not a tournament.  The marked `b,c` generator graph in (6) is a
proper ten-edge subgraph of that twelve-edge orbital graph.

The directed `b`-triangles plus involutory `a`-edges before the cover are only
a partially colored relation.  Treating involution edges as arbitrary ties
loses the complement bit.

### Gracefulness

Exact exhaustive controls find graceful labellings for both pre-cover
loopless graphs:

```text
edge:        (0,2,1,4,7,8),
orientation: (0,6,5,2,3,7).                              (17)
```

The common `A4` graph (6) is not graceful.  Hence gracefulness neither
distinguishes the two complements nor survives the discriminant restriction.
It is an existential labelling property, not a preserved action invariant.

### Feuerbach / external geometric origins

THM-2606 explains how Feuerbach tangency can select an origin on a separate
four-state affine sign torsor.  No such origin occurs in (2)--(14).  Importing
it would be an additional labelled section; the Schreier graph does not
construct it.
