# THM-2416 -- zero current or bounded sideband

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for the proposed post-THM-2410/THM-2408 dichotomy: an eligible
full-coordinate step packet either has nonzero mean, or its oriented-jump
Prony sequence forces a nonzero physical Fourier sideband at some
`1 <= X <= L-1`, where `L` is the number of effective jump nodes.  No
mathematical claim or proved dependency is asserted until the proof,
sharp boundary, exact companion, and independent audit are installed.
