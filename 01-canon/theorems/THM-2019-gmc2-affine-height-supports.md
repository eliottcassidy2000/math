# THM-2019 — reserved affine-height closure

For a finite monomial support `Z^a Zbar^b`, suppose all support points obey
`a+b=lambda*(a-b)+delta`. Every charge-balanced word of length `m` then has
the same total degree `delta*m`, hence the same Wick factorial. The candidate theorem
deduces NC2 from the Duistermaat–van der Kallen toral nullcone theorem. This includes
arbitrary coefficients and arbitrarily many colliding primitive circuits.
