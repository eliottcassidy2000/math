# THM-3233 -- fraction-free exceptional quotient-tail renormalization

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-3231 computes the second-order strict transform after a simple pivot
wall.  Its formula contains more than one surviving coordinate: it gives a
new pair of series on which the same fraction-free operator acts again.  The
whole exceptional tower is therefore a renormalized copy of the original
recurrence, driven by the unremoved reciprocal-quotient tail.

## 1. Operator covariance

For a series `f`, write `f_j=[z^j]f` and

```text
P_1(f,g)=f_0g_1-f_1g_0,

E(f,g)=z^(-2){f_0^2g-[f_0g_0+P_1(f,g)z]f}.              (1)
```

The numerator in `(1)` has zero constant and linear coefficients, so `E`
is a formal series over every commutative coefficient ring.  Two covariance
identities are load bearing.  For coefficient-ring scalars `c,d` and a
series `h`,

```text
E(cf,dg)=c^2d E(f,g),                                    (2)

E(hf,hg)=h_0^2 h E(f,g).                                 (3)
```

Indeed `P_1(cf,dg)=cdP_1(f,g)` and
`P_1(hf,hg)=h_0^2P_1(f,g)`; substitution in `(1)` proves both identities.
Equation `(3)` is stronger than scalar homogeneity: a common series factor
survives as one common factor, with only its constant term squared.

## 2. The exceptional input pair

Work over `A[a][[z]]`, where `A` is an integral domain, `a` is an
indeterminate, and

```text
w=a+zv,             v_0,t_0 in A^*,
x=E(w,t),           y=E(x,w).                            (4)
```

Since `v_0` is a unit, put

```text
q=t/v,                    r=S^3q,
(Sf)_j=f_(j+1).                                             (5)
```

THM-3231 gives `a^2|y` coefficientwise and the exact exceptional pair

```text
x|_(a=0)=alpha v,           alpha=t_0v_0,

(y/a^2)|_(a=0)=beta vr,     beta=-t_0v_0^3.               (6)
```

Thus the old shifted row and the new strict-transform row have the common
unit series factor `v`; after removing it, their projective content is the
pair `(1,r)`.

## 3. All-depth exceptional conjugacy

Starting with the last two rows in `(4)`, define

```text
R_(-1)=x,             R_0=y,
R_(n+1)=E(R_n,R_(n-1))                    (n>=0).          (7)
```

Let

```text
A_(-1)=0,             A_0=2,
A_(n+1)=2A_n+A_(n-1),                                      (8)
```

and define the renormalized quotient `Rtilde_n=R_n/a^(A_n)`.  The quotient
is integral coefficientwise.  If `P_0=0,P_1=1` are the Pell numbers, then

```text
A_n=2P_(n+1),

(A_(-1),A_0,A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4,A_5)
 =(0,2,4,10,24,58,140).                                  (9)
```

Now define a second fraction-free tower and its scalar gauge by

```text
F_(-1)=1,             F_0=r,
F_(n+1)=E(F_n,F_(n-1)),

kappa_(-1)=t_0v_0,    kappa_0=-t_0v_0^3,
kappa_(n+1)=kappa_n^2 kappa_(n-1) v_0^2.                 (10)
```

Then, for every `n>=-1`,

```text
Rtilde_n|_(a=0)=kappa_n v F_n.                           (11)
```

Consequently `A_n` is the universal extracted `a`-exponent.  It is the
actual coefficientwise `a`-adic order of `R_n` exactly when `F_n!=0`; if
`F_n=0`, then `ord_a(R_n)>A_n` (with infinite order when `R_n=0`).

### Proof

The two base cases are `(6)`.  If `(11)` holds at `n,n-1`, equations
`(2),(3)` give

```text
E(kappa_n vF_n,kappa_(n-1)vF_(n-1))
 =kappa_n^2 kappa_(n-1)v_0^2 v E(F_n,F_(n-1)),           (12)
```

which is the next case.  Before specialization, `(2)` also shows that the
next universal extracted power of `a` supplied by the known divisibilities is
`2A_n+A_(n-1)`.  This proves integrality, `(8)`, and `(11)` by induction.
The closed form `(9)` is the Pell recurrence.

This is an equality on the exceptional divisor of the normalized tower.  It
does not identify the generic rows away from `a=0` with the `F` tower.

## 4. First-live depth and the next sharp boundary

Over the fraction field of `A`, let

```text
m=min{k>=3:q_k!=0}.                                      (13)
```

If the set is empty, the first exceptional row is zero.  Otherwise `(6)`
gives

```text
ord_z((y/a^2)|_(a=0))=m-3.                              (14)
```

Thus exceptional division converts the first unremoved quotient coefficient
into an anchored contact depth; it does not erase delayed contact.

The next renormalized row is governed by

```text
F_1=E(r,1)=r_1Sr-r_0S^2r,

[z^j]F_1=r_1r_(j+1)-r_0r_(j+2).                        (15)
```

Equation `(15)` gives a complete sharp trichotomy.

1. If `r_0!=0`, then `F_1=0` exactly when

   ```text
   r=r_0/(1-lambda z),        lambda=r_1/r_0.             (16)
   ```

   A nonzero geometric quotient tail is therefore an exact terminal hostile.
2. If `r_0=0,r_1!=0`, then

   ```text
   F_1=r_1Sr                                                   (17)
   ```

   has nonzero constant `r_1^2`: a one-level delayed split returns as a unit.
3. If `r_0=r_1=0`, then `F_1=0`.  A delay of at least two levels is the exact
   double-zero collapse boundary for the first successor.

For example, with an arbitrary quadratic `Q_2(z)`, the four quotient words

```text
Q_2,
Q_2+z^3/(1-lambda z),
Q_2+z^4/(1-lambda z),
Q_2+z^5                                                   (18)
```

respectively give: no first exceptional row; a nonzero geometric row whose
successor is zero; a delayed simple row whose successor has a unit constant;
and a delayed double row whose successor is zero.  These controls rule out
both "nonzero once means persistent" and "zero pivot means dead forever."
