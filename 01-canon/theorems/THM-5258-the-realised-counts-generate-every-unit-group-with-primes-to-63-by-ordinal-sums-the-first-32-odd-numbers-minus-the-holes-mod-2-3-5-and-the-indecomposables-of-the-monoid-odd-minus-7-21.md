# THM-5258 — residues by ordinal sums, and the indecomposables of odd ∖ {7, 21}

**(A)** Packet RESIDUES.md §1–2: ordinal sums multiply counts; a finite abelian group is generated by a set iff the
set's image spans each `G/qG`; the components (two bits at 2; `q`-th power residue classes; `(x^(p−1) − 1)/p mod p`)
do not depend on the exponents beyond `e_p ≥ 2`; the ranks are full in the three cases. Non-unit residues use the
realised primes `3, 5, 11, …, 61` and, for the 7-lane, the line `L_3` whose quotients `(1 + 3·2^s)/7` run through every
class modulo every `7^k` with the other primes avoided.

**(B)** Let `M` be the odd numbers other than 7, 21 under multiplication. `n ∈ M` is decomposable iff `n = ab` with
`a, b ∈ M`, `a, b > 1`. Primes `p ≠ 7` are indecomposable; a composite coprime to 7 splits into two factors in `M`
(neither 7 nor 21). For `n = 7m`: a factorisation `7k · (m/k)` needs `7k ∈ M`, i.e. `k ∉ {1, 3}`, and `m/k > 1` in
`M`; for `m` prime (≥ 5) the divisors are `1, m`, so `7p` is indecomposable; `49` (`k = 7`, `m/k = 1`) and
`63 = 7·9` (`k = 3`: 21 ∉ M; `k = 9`: `m/k = 1`) are indecomposable; `343 = 7·49` (`k = 49`: `m/k = 1`; `k = 7`:
`7 ∉ M`) is indecomposable; `7^4 = 49·49` and every other `7^v m` with `v ≥ 2` decompose, and `7m` with `m` composite
coprime to 7 decomposes as `(7p)(m/p)` for a prime `p | m`, `m = 3^j` (`j ≥ 2`) as `63·3^(j−2)`, `m = 3p` as `(7p)·3`.
∎ In `t = (H − 1)/2`: `7H ↦ t′ = 7t + 3`.
