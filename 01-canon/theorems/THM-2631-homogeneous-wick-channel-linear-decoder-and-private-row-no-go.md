# THM-2631 -- reserved candidate

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for the homogeneous Gaussian/Wick channel-decoder theorem.  The
intended statement proves that a universal scalar linear combination of
moment polynomials can recover one level-tagged balanced channel only when
that moment level contains exactly one channel; degree grading prevents all
other moment levels from helping.  Its nonnegative form is the private-row
criterion.  The exact two-atom cancellation from MISTAKE-211 is the sharp
hostile, and nonlinear resultants/cumulants/confluent determinants and
THM-2022's whole-face Frobenius mechanism remain outside the no-go.  This
reservation proves no new NC2 or GMC(2) conclusion.
