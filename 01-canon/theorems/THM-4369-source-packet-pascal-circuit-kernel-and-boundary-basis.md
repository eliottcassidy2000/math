# THM-4369 -- Source-packet Pascal circuit kernel and boundary basis

**PROVED ELEMENTARY RELATIVE TO THM-4368 + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY
AUDITED. THIS IS A FINITE PACKET-KERNEL THEOREM. IT DOES NOT ASSERT A NEW
BRACKET OBSTRUCTION, SEAM ENTRY, `JC(2)`, OR `DC(2)`.**

## 1. Statement

Fix a THM-4368 diagonal intercept `ell>=2` and put

```text
s=ceil(ell/2),       rho=ceil(ell/3),       f=floor(ell/2)=ell-s.       (1)
```

For positive boundary coordinates `(u,v)`, write

```text
tau=u+v-1,       n0=u+s-1,       N=v.                                  (2)
```

By THM-4368, the type is source-realizable exactly when

```text
v>=rho,       u+s-1>=v,       tau>=f.                                  (3)
```

For `R>=f`, let `S_(ell,R)` be the finite set of pairs satisfying `(3)` and
`tau<=R`. Let `M_(ell,R)` be the free abelian group with basis symbols
`e_(u,v)` for these types.

The theorem is:

1. The complete kernel of both the expanded-packet map and the complete
   `q=0` diagonal generating-stream map (equivalently, by THM-4368, any or all
   complete fixed-`q` streams) is freely generated by the local source-
   internal Pascal circuits

   ```text
   C_(u,v)=e_(u,v)-e_(u,v+1)+e_(u+1,v),                  (4)
   ```

   where `(u,v) in S_(ell,R)`, `tau<R`, and `u+s-1-v>=1`.
2. The quotient has a canonical integral boundary basis: the types with
   `tau=R` or `u+s-1=v`. It has rank `R-rho+1`, and its image is an explicit
   principal polynomial module.
3. Reduction to that boundary basis has a closed signed-ballot formula.
4. On the full positive triangular prefix `tau<=R`, all local Pascal cells
   give the kernel, of rank `T(R-1)`. Under THM-4368's address, a parent is
   rewritten into two consecutive addresses in the next triangular block.
5. If individual source monomials rather than packet types are retained, a
   basis of the full kernel is obtained by adding the within-type fibre
   differences from THM-4368.

The inheritance pass was:

- closest proved mechanism: THM-4368's exact packet polynomial and triangular
  address;
- canonical hostile: its valid `(u,v)=(3,6)` packet whose reciprocal leaves
  the source cone;
- corrected near miss: an ambient Pascal cell based on the wall `n0=N` also
  leaves the source cone and is not a source relation;
- least-used sidecar: the full integral relation module among packet types,
  rather than only equality of two normalized packets.

The live board was

```text
triangular address | Pascal cell | Bernstein boundary | source wall
ballot first passage | packet fibre | honest three-circuit.              (5)
```

## 2. Packet and trace are the same kernel problem

Put `w=x^2t`. THM-4368's common expanded packet for type `(u,v)` is

```text
P_(u,v)=x^(2n0-ell)t^n0(1+x^2t)^N
       =x^(2s-ell)t^s G_(u,v)(w),

G_(u,v)(w)=w^(u-1)(1+w)^v.                                  (6)
```

Its normalized `q=0` trace is

```text
F_(u,v)(z)=(-1)^(u-1)z^(u-1)(1-z)^(v-1).                    (7)
```

They obey

```text
G_(u,v)(w)=(1+w)F_(u,v)(-w).                                (8)
```

Variable substitution and multiplication by the displayed nonzero common
factors are injective over `Z`. Therefore the `P`, `G`, and `F` maps from the
free type module have the same integral kernel.

The polynomial identity

```text
1-(1+w)+w=0                                                 (9)
```

gives `(4)`. If its parent is source-internal, increasing `v` reduces
`u+s-1-v` by one and increasing `u` raises it by one, so both children remain
in `(3)`. If the parent is on `u+s-1=v`, the `v` child leaves `(3)`; this is
why only the declared internal cells are admitted.

## 3. Ambient triangular kernel

First ignore `(3)` and use every positive `(u,v)` with `tau<=R`. There are
`T(R)` basis types and `T(R-1)` local parents. Use `(4)` to replace each
type below `tau=R` by its two children. Because children lie one level higher,
this terminates and leaves only the `R` top types

```text
(u,R+1-u),                 u=1,...,R.                     (10)
```

After removing the common factor `(1+w)`, their images are

```text
w^(u-1)(1+w)^(R-u),       u=1,...,R.                      (11)
```

Ordered by `u`, the coefficient matrix of `(11)` in
`1,w,...,w^(R-1)` is upper unitriangular. Hence the top images are an integral
basis. The local relations are independent because each has unit coefficient
on its unique lowest-level parent, and the reduction proves that they span the
kernel. Thus

```text
rank(image)=R,       rank(kernel)=T(R)-R=T(R-1).          (12)
```

This is an integral statement, not merely a rank calculation over a field.

## 4. Source boundary basis and exact image

At a fixed layer `tau`, `(3)` is the consecutive interval

```text
ceil((tau+2-s)/2)<=u<=tau+1-rho,             f<=tau<=R.  (13)
```

Every nonboundary source type is the parent of `(4)`, so repeated reduction
terminates on

```text
B_(ell,R)={(u,v) in S_(ell,R): tau=R or u+s-1=v}.         (14)
```

Put

```text
h=floor((R+1-s)/2).                                      (15)
```

The lower wall part of `(14)` has

```text
u=1,...,h,             v=u+s-1,
```

and the top part has

```text
u=h+1,...,R+1-rho,     v=R+1-u.                          (16)
```

Thus the boundary has exactly one type for each
`u=1,...,R+1-rho`, hence `R-rho+1` types.

Every source packet has the factor `(1+w)^rho`. After removing it, a boundary
type maps to

```text
H_(u,v)(w)=w^(u-1)(1+w)^(v-rho).                         (17)
```

In increasing `u`, this polynomial has valuation `u-1` and initial
coefficient one at `w^(u-1)`. Its degree is at most `R-rho`. The square
coefficient matrix in `1,w,...,w^(R-rho)` is therefore upper unitriangular.
Consequently

```text
image(G)=(1+w)^rho Z[w]_(<=R-rho),
image(F)=(1-z)^(rho-1) Z[z]_(<=R-rho),                  (18)

rank(image)=R-rho+1,
rank(kernel)=|S_(ell,R)|-(R-rho+1).                      (19)
```

The relations `(4)` have one unit pivot for every nonboundary type, so their
number is `(19)` and they freely generate the integral kernel.

For reference,

```text
|S_(ell,R)|=
 sum_(tau=f)^R [tau+2-rho-ceil((tau+2-s)/2)].             (20)
```

## 5. Signed-ballot boundary normal form

The terminating rewrite also has an explicit first-passage form. For an
internal parent define

```text
d=u+s-1-v>=1,                  H=R-tau>=1.               (21)
```

A `v` step has coefficient `+1` and changes `d` by `-1`; a `u` step has
coefficient `-1` and changes `d` by `+1`.

For a boundary point (`d=0` or `H=0`), the boundary normal form is simply the
delta vector on that point. For an internal parent, use the following two
families.

For every `r` with

```text
d<=r<H,                       r=d (mod 2),               (22)
```

put `j=(r-d)/2`. The coefficient at the wall endpoint

```text
(u+j,v+r-j)
```

is

```text
(-1)^j (d/r) C(r,j).                                      (23)
```

These are the paths whose first contact with `d=0` occurs at time `r`.

For every integer `j` with `0<=j<=H` and `d+2j-H>=0`, the top endpoint
`(u+j,v+H-j)` has coefficient

```text
(-1)^j B(d,H,j),                                         (24)

B(d,H,j)= (d/H)C(H,j),                  if d+2j-H=0,
          C(H,j)-C(H,j+d),              if d+2j-H>0,     (25)
```

where an out-of-range binomial coefficient is zero. The first line counts
first contact exactly at the top; the second is the reflection-principle
count for paths remaining strictly above the wall. Equations `(23)--(25)`
give the unique boundary normal form because `(17)` is a basis.

## 6. Lifting from packet types to individual monomials

THM-4368 gives every monomial realization of a type. With `N=v` and
`n0=u+s-1`, they are indexed by

```text
max(0,ell-2N)<=e<=min(N,n0-N),                            (26)

c=N-e,       a=2N+e-ell,       b=n0-N-e.
```

Choose one base monomial in each nonempty type fibre. This is a noncanonical
section of the type aggregation map, not a source operation. On the free
module of all individual realizers in `S_(ell,R)`, take:

1. every difference between a nonbase realizer and its base realizer; and
2. the circuits `(4)` lifted through the chosen base realizers.

The first family is a basis of the kernel of aggregation by packet type. The
second maps to the already proved type-kernel basis. Together they form an
integral basis of the full monomial-to-expanded-packet kernel. Its rank is

```text
sum_((u,v) in S) (mu(v,u+s-1)-1)
 + |S|-(R-rho+1),                                        (27)
```

where `mu` is THM-4368's exact fibre size. The split depends on the chosen
base representatives, but the kernel and its rank do not.

## 7. Natural-address circuits and sharp controls

For `k>=0`, put `T(k)=k(k+1)/2`. THM-4368's address is

```text
Addr(u,v)=T(tau-1)+u.                                    (28)
```

The full prefix `tau<=R` is exactly the integer interval `1,...,T(R)`. A
local parent at address `T(tau-1)+u` has children at

```text
T(tau)+u,                  T(tau)+u+1.                   (29)
```

Thus every Pascal circuit rewrites one old address into two consecutive
addresses in the next triangular block.

At `ell=10`, the source-valid types

```text
(u,v)=(2,4),(2,5),(3,4),       addresses 12,17,18        (30)
```

give the local relation

```text
e_(2,4)-e_(2,5)+e_(3,4).                                 (31)
```

One choice of source-monomial representatives `(a,b,c,e)` is

```text
(0,0,2,2),       (0,1,5,0),       (0,1,2,2),            (32)
```

respectively. Their expanded packets, and hence their complete traces, obey
`(31)`.

No relation supported on one or two distinct packet types exists: the two
boundary valuations are

```text
ord_(w=0) G_(u,v)=u-1,             ord_(w=-1) G_(u,v)=v.
```

Thus proportional nonzero packets have the same `(u,v)`. Hence `(31)` is an
honest support-minimal three-circuit.

The adjacent ambient parent `(u,v)=(1,5)` at address 11 is a necessary
hostile. Its children have addresses 16 and 17, but `(1,6)` is not source-
realizable because `n0=5<N=6`. An ambient triangle is not automatically a
source triangle.

For the source prefix `(ell,R)=(10,8)`, there are ten types, image rank five,
and kernel rank five. Its boundary addresses are

```text
11,23,31,32,33.                                          (33)
```

The ballot normal form of `(2,4)` is

```text
e_(2,6)-2e_(3,6)+3e_(4,5)-e_(5,4).                      (34)
```

This is the precise tournament analogy: three-term circuits occur on a
triangular address grid, but the source wall deletes some cells and fixed or
missing edges cannot be freely oriented. No tournament invariant or theorem
is transferred.
