# THM-3892 -- reserved

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**  This namespace is reserved for the
all-degree classification of homogeneous binary-cubic coefficient rows whose
discriminant is a single pure power.  The intended theorem identifies their
dehomogenizations exactly with constant squarefree cubics acted on by a
polynomial `GL_2` matrix of constant determinant, using finite-etale
triviality and the three pairwise determinant units.  No mathematical claim
is made by this stub, and it has no proved dependencies until a proof
candidate is written and independently audited.
