# THM-3430 -- nonlinear wrap linearization and canonical vertical torsion

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.**

## 1. Statement and connection contract

Let `K` be a field of characteristic zero, let `d>=2`, and fix

```text
P_d=ax+b+g(x)z^d in K[x,z],             a in K*, 0!=g.    (1)
```

Write

```text
D_d=Jac(P_d,-),
C_d=K[x,z]/D_d(K[x,z]),
W_d=(C_d)_(d-1 mod d),                                  (2)
```

where `W_d` is the wrap target sector.  Introduce a separate variable `w`
and the linear model

```text
P_1=ax+b+g(x)w,
C_1=K[x,w]/Jac(P_1,K[x,w]).                              (3)
```

Identify the two abstract polynomial rings generated by the Hamiltonians,
writing their common generator as `T`.  Then there is a filtration-preserving
`K[T]`-module isomorphism

```text
W_d ~= C_1.                                               (4)
```

Let

```text
S=rad(g),             N=deg(S),
c=g/S,                E=deg(c)=deg(g)-N.                 (5)
```

For constant nonzero `g`, use `S=1`; then `N=E=0`.  The split CRT diagram,
the one-root theorem THM-3422, and faithful descent give the complete wrap
response

```text
W_d ~= K[T]^N direct-sum K[x,c^(-1)]/K[x]                (6)
```

noncanonically, where `T` acts on the localization quotient by multiplication
by `ax+b`.  The torsion submodule itself is canonical:

```text
tors_(K[T])(W_d) ~= K[x,c^(-1)]/K[x]                     (7)
```

as a filtered module, with the displayed model noncanonical only through
the compatible rootwise cyclic choices in THM-3422 and their descent.

Over a faithful splitting field `K'/K`, write

```text
g=gamma product_(i=1)^N (x-alpha_i)^e_i,
beta_i=a alpha_i+b,
Pr_(T-beta_i)=K'[T,(T-beta_i)^(-1)]/K'[T].               (8)
```

Then

```text
W_d tensor_K K'
 ~=K'[T]^N direct-sum
   direct_sum_(i:e_i>1) Pr_(T-beta_i),                   (9)

tors(W_d) tensor_K K'
 ~=direct_sum_(i:e_i>1) Pr_(T-beta_i).                  (10)
```

Thus every repeated geometric root contributes exactly one wrap Prüfer arm,
independently of `d`; simple roots contribute only generic/free rank.

## 2. The wrap transition forgets `d`

THM-3418 gives the exact wrap presentation

```text
W_d ~= colim_(k>=0) A_k,
A_k=K[x]/(g^(k+1)),                                      (11)
```

whose transition from stage `k` to stage `k+1` is induced by

```text
L_n(q)=(d/(an))gq'-(1/a)g'q,
n=(k+1)d.                                                (12)
```

Substituting the wrap exponent cancels `d` exactly:

```text
L_((k+1)d)(q)
 =[1/(a(k+1))]gq'-(1/a)g'q.                              (13)
```

For the linear model `(3)`, the unique sector is its wrap sector.  This case
is outside THM-3418's `d>=2` statement, so derive it directly.  One has

```text
Jac(P_1,qw^(k+1))
 =a(k+1)q w^k+[(k+1)g'q-gq']w^(k+1).                    (13a)
```

At exponent zero, `Jac(P_1,q)=-gq'`; differentiation is onto `K[x]`, so the
first target stage is `K[x]/(g)`.  Equation `(13a)` then inductively gives
the stages `K[x]/(g^(k+1))` and precisely the transition `(13)`, just as in
the affine-modification tower used by THM-3412.  The exact induction identity
is

```text
L_((k+1)d)(g^(k+1)h)=g^(k+2)h'/[a(k+1)],                (13b)
```

and surjectivity of differentiation shows that the next relation ideal is
exactly `(g^(k+2))`.  The action of the abstract Hamiltonian generator is
also identical:

```text
T[q]_k=[(ax+b)q]_k+[gq]_(k+1).                           (14)
```

Equations `(11)--(14)` identify the filtered diagrams, not only their
colimits as vector spaces.  They prove the `K[T]`-linear isomorphism `(4)`.
This is the load-bearing step: generic rank equality alone would not recover
the integral torsion.

## 3. Faithful descent of the complete rootwise response

The CRT identity proved in Section 4 and THM-3422 give, over a finite normal
splitting field `K'/K`,

```text
W_d tensor_K K'
 ~=direct_sum_i W_(d,i)
 ~=K'[T]^N direct-sum
   direct_sum_(i:e_i>1) Pr_(T-beta_i).                   (15)
```

This split formula alone does not license a direct-sum claim over `K`; the
free section and the torsion descent must both be exhibited.

First put `c=g/S`.  At the `i`th root, the canonical free ray in the
THM-3422 wrap chain begins at `y_i^(e_i-1)` (and at `1` when `e_i=1`).  The
stage-zero classes

```text
f_j=[c x^j z^(d-1)] in W_d,             0<=j<N,           (16)
```

restrict modulo `y_i^e_i` to

```text
f_j |_(alpha_i)=kappa_i alpha_i^j y_i^(e_i-1),
kappa_i in (K')*.                                         (17)
```

The coefficient matrix is a nonzero diagonal matrix times the Vandermonde
matrix `(alpha_i^j)`.  It is invertible.  Consequently the `K[T]`-submodule
`F` generated by the `f_j` satisfies

```text
F tensor_K K'=K'[T]^N,                  F~=K[T]^N,         (18)
```

and is exactly the sum of the one-root free rays after splitting.  This is
the promised free section; in particular it is defined before any choice of
split primary coordinates.

Now let `Tau=tors_(K[T])(W_d)`.  Torsion commutes with the extension to
`K'`.  Indeed, one inclusion follows from flatness.  Conversely, if an
element after extension is killed by `p(T) in K'[T]`, it is killed by the
nonzero norm multiple `Norm_(K'/K)(p)(T) in K[T]`; flatness of the kernel of
that multiplication map puts it in `Tau tensor_K K'`.  Hence

```text
Tau tensor_K K'
 =direct_sum_(i:e_i>1) Pr_(T-beta_i).                    (19)
```

Let `F_kW_d` denote the image of the `k`th stage in `(11)`.  THM-3422 gives
the exact rootwise window

```text
(F_kW_d intersect Tau) tensor_K K'
 ~=direct_sum_(i:e_i>1)
   K'[T]/(T-beta_i)^((k+1)(e_i-1)).                       (20)
```

Factor `c` over `K` and apply the elementary-divisor theorem over the PID
`K[T]` (using `T=ax+b`).  Formula `(20)` says exactly that, noncanonically,

```text
F_kW_d intersect Tau ~= K[x]/(c^(k+1)).                  (21)
```

The rootwise wrap transitions are injective and advance the `i`th primary
length by exactly `e_i-1`.  Primary by primary, compatible unit rescalings
therefore put every inclusion into the denominator form

```text
K[x]/c^(k+1) -> K[x]/c^(k+2),            [r] |-> [cr].   (22)
```

Their filtered colimit is therefore

```text
Tau ~= colim_k K[x]/c^(k+1)
    ~= K[x,c^(-1)]/K[x],                                  (23)
```

with `T` acting by multiplication by `ax+b`.  Equivalently, this follows
from the classification of divisible primary modules over `K[T]`: `(19)`
has one geometric Prüfer copy at every root of `c`, exactly as the
localization quotient does.  The submodule `Tau` and its stage filtration
are canonical; `(21)--(23)` use noncanonical compatible cyclic generators.

After extension, `(18)` and `(19)` are complementary and exhaust `(15)`.
Faithful flatness therefore gives

```text
W_d=F direct-sum Tau,                                     (24)
```

which proves `(6)--(10)` over the original field.  In particular,

```text
dim_K(F_kW_d intersect Tau)=(k+1)E,                       (25)
```

the successive layer has dimension `E`, and the arm at `alpha_i` has slope
`e_i-1`.  Since `a!=0`, distinct geometric roots have distinct values
`beta_i`; there are no primary-support collisions.

This proof deliberately does **not** invoke THM-3412 for arbitrary `g`.
For the linear model, its gradient pair is

```text
(P_(1,x),P_(1,w))=(a+g'w,g).                              (26)
```

The first entry is a unit modulo `g` precisely when `g'` is nilpotent modulo
`g`, equivalently when every geometric root of `g` is repeated (with the
constant case understood separately).  Thus THM-3412 is a consistency check
in the all-repeated-root range, but is not a dependency here.  The examples
`g=x` and `g=x^2(x-1)` are sharp hostiles to the false arbitrary-`g`
unimodularity shortcut.

## 4. Split-root CRT diagram and rootwise theorem

The degree-collapse proof does not require splitting.  There is nevertheless
an illuminating independent rootwise audit.  Work over `K'` as in `(8)` and
put

```text
y_i=x-alpha_i,
u_i=product_(j!=i)(x-alpha_j)^e_j,
g=gamma y_i^e_i u_i.                                    (27)
```

At stage `k`, the Chinese remainder theorem gives

```text
A_k=direct_sum_(i=1)^N A_(k,i),
A_(k,i)=u_i^(k+1)K'[x]/(g^(k+1))
       ~=K'[y_i]/(y_i^(e_i(k+1))).                       (28)
```

For `n=(k+1)d`, direct differentiation gives the exact intertwining identity

```text
L_n^g(u_i^(k+1)p)
 =u_i^(k+2)L_n^(gamma y_i^e_i)(p).                       (29)
```

The `T-beta_i` action intertwines as well:

```text
(T-beta_i)[u_i^(k+1)p]_k
 =[a y_i u_i^(k+1)p]_k
  +[gamma y_i^e_i u_i^(k+2)p]_(k+1).                    (30)
```

Thus the entire split wrap diagram is the direct sum of the one-root wrap
diagrams from THM-3422.  A simple root gives `K'[T]`; a repeated root gives
`K'[T] direct-sum Pr_(T-beta_i)`.  This proves the split input `(15)` used by
the descent in Section 3 and explains geometrically why the global
Kummer-period coupling disappears only at the wrap boundary.

## 5. Nonwrap hostile and a limited surviving gauge

For `1<=sigma<d`, the stage is `K[x]`, not `K[x]/(g^(k+1))`, and its exponent
is `n=sigma+kd`.  Neither the cancellation `(13)` nor the CRT decomposition
`(28)` applies.  The failure is real: in the split profile

```text
(d,sigma;e_1,e_2)=(4,2;3,1),                             (31)
```

the chosen repeated root is locally resonant, but the other-root monodromy
is nontrivial.  THM-3422's exact first-window theorem gives dimension `N-1`,
not the `N` predicted by independent root channels.

There is a useful but strictly one-way survivor.  At a chosen root write

```text
g=gamma y^e u,
d divides sigma e_j for every other root alpha_j.        (32)
```

Then `h=u^(sigma/d)` is a polynomial over the splitting field, and the
subspaces

```text
h u^k K'[y] subset K'[x]                                 (33)
```

form a transition- and `T-beta`-stable subdiagram isomorphic to the one-root
sector.  The exact intertwining is

```text
L_(sigma+kd)^g(h u^k p)
 =h u^(k+1)L_(sigma+kd)^(gamma y^e)(p).                  (34)
```

If also `e>1` and `d|sigma(e-1)`, put
`q=sigma(e-1)/d>=1`.  Its torsion endpoint has the explicit identity

```text
tau=[h y^(q-1)z^(sigma-1)],
(T-beta)tau
 =D_d(h y^q z^sigma/sigma).                              (35)
```

Filtered-colimit exactness makes this one-root subdiagram injective.  For
example `(d,sigma;e_1,e_2)=(2,1;3,2)` gives `h=x-alpha_2` and a genuine
embedded Prüfer arm.  Equation `(33)` is not exhaustive: without the wrap
quotients, it gives no full nonwrap decomposition and no torsion-free
complement.
