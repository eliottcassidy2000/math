# THM-2746 -- the C3 quotient has an A4 translation defect

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-2743 treats affine lifts of the full `S3` quotient and finds the
transitive `S4` extension as a compatibility class invisible on either free
factor separately.  The other live quartic-monodromy branch has a different
prime anatomy.  Its matching quotient is only `C3`, and its binary generator
lives inside the invisible `V4` kernel.  That binary translation already
creates the same rank-one charged leakage.

## 1. The marked cyclic quotient and its affine lifts

Let

```text
G=C2*C3=<s,t | s^2=t^3=1>,             V=F_2^2,            (1)
```

and retain THM-2595's order-three matrix

```text
T=[0 1].                                                     (2)
  [1 1]
```

Consider the different linear quotient

```text
rho(s)=I,                      rho(t)=T.                     (3)
```

Thus the image of `(3)` is `C3`, not `S3`.  An affine lift is determined by

```text
sigma_a(v)=v+a,                tau_b(v)=Tv+b,                (4)
```

with `a,b in V`.  In characteristic two the binary relation imposes no
condition on `a`, while

```text
I+T+T^2=0                                                   (5)
```

makes every `b` admissible.  Hence there are exactly `16` affine cocycles.

A translation of the chosen torsor origin by `v` adds

```text
delta_v(s)=0,                 delta_v(t)=(I+T)v.             (6)
```

The map `I+T` is invertible, so there are four coboundaries and each cocycle
has a unique gauge with `b=0`.  Consequently

```text
H^1(G,V) isomorphic to V,              |H^1|=4,              (7)
```

with class represented exactly by `a`.

The restriction map reveals a sharp contrast with THM-2743:

```text
H^1(C2,V)=V,                 H^1(C3,V)=0,
H^1(G,V) -> H^1(C2,V) is an isomorphism.                    (8)
```

Thus the `A4` class below is already visible on the binary face.  It is not
the locally trivial free-factor co-occurrence class that produces `S4` in
THM-2595/2743.

## 2. The image is C3 or A4

If `a=0`, then `sigma_a=1`; the image is the cyclic group generated by
`tau_b`, with orbit partition `1+3` on `V`.

Suppose `a!=0`.  Then `sigma_a` is translation by a nonzero vector, hence a
fixed-point-free double transposition on the four torsor points.  Conjugating
it by `tau_b` produces translations by `Ta` and `T^2a`.  These are the three
nonzero elements of `V`, so the image contains the full translation group and

```text
<sigma_a,tau_b>=V semidirect <T>=A4.                         (9)
```

It has order `12` and acts transitively.  All three nonzero cohomology classes
are conjugate under the marked `C3` action.  Every one of the `16` raw lifts
also satisfies

```text
(sigma_a tau_b)^3=1.                                       (10)
```

Thus the nonzero lifts realize the spherical triangle presentation
`(2,3,3)` of `A4`; the zero class is its degenerate `C3` quotient.

## 3. A general orbit-contingency formula

The relevant projector calculation has a useful form beyond this four-point
case.  Let a permutation `tau` act on a finite set `Omega`, with orbits
`O_1,...,O_m` of sizes `n_i`, and let `Pi` be orthogonal averaging onto the
functions constant on each orbit.  For an involutive permutation `sigma`, put

```text
Q=I-Pi,                 M=Q sigma Pi,
a_ij=|sigma(O_i) intersection O_j|.                         (11)
```

Then over the standard real Euclidean permutation module, or over the
rational module after scalar extension to the reals,

```text
||M||_HS^2 = m-sum_(i,j) a_ij^2/(n_i n_j),
||[sigma,Pi]||_HS^2 = 2||M||_HS^2.                          (12)
```

Moreover

```text
M=0  iff  sigma permutes the tau-orbit blocks.               (13)
```

To prove `(12)`, scalar-extend if necessary and use the normalized orbit indicators
`u_i=1_(O_i)/sqrt(n_i)`.  The compression `Pi sigma Pi` has matrix

```text
<u_i,sigma u_j>=a_ij/sqrt(n_i n_j).                          (14)
```

Since `sigma` is orthogonal,

```text
||Q sigma Pi||_HS^2
 =||sigma Pi||_HS^2-||Pi sigma Pi||_HS^2,
```

which is `(12)`.  The two off-diagonal blocks of the commutator are adjoints
with orthogonal supports.  Finally, `M=0` says that `sigma` preserves the
orbit-constant partition algebra; a permutation automorphism must permute its
atoms, proving `(13)`.  The Hilbert--Schmidt language here uses the standard
positive-definite form.  The algebraic zero criterion itself is valid over
any field whose characteristic does not divide the order of `tau`.

## 4. The exact A4 projector defect

Apply `(11)--(13)` to `Omega=V`, `tau=tau_b`, and `sigma=sigma_a`.  The
`C3` orbit sizes are `(1,3)`.  In the zero class the binary move is the
identity, so `M=0`.

In every nonzero class, the binary translation moves the unique `C3` fixed
point into the three-cycle and has contingency matrix

```text
                   [0 1]
(a_ij) =           [1 2].                                  (15)
```

Therefore

```text
||M||_HS^2 = 2-(1/3+1/3+4/9)=8/9.                          (16)
```

The fixed space of `tau_b` is two-dimensional.  The constant vector is
killed by `M`, while its orthogonal fixed contrast is not, so

```text
rank M=1,                    rank[sigma_a,Pi]=2.              (17)
```

Equations `(15)--(17)` hold on all `12` raw lifts representing the three
nonzero cohomology classes.

### 4.1 The primitive leakage vector is the `V4` character

The Reynolds denominators are powers of three, so the whole operator reduces
modulo two.  Equivalently,

```text
Pi_2=I+tau+tau^2.                                           (18)
```

There is a more precise integral statement.  Let `p` be the unique fixed
point of `tau_b`, and put

```text
w_p=3e_p-sum_(x!=p)e_x.                                    (19)
```

For every `a in V`, including `a=0`, direct projection gives

```text
z_a=(3/4)M_(a,b)w_p
   =2e_(p+a)-e_(p+Ta)-e_(p+T^2a) in Z[V].                 (20)
```

The factor `3/4` is an **integral renormalization of this particular image
vector**, not a scalar to be reduced modulo two.  For `a!=0`, `z_a` is a
primitive generator of the rational image line.  Reducing that primitive
vector gives

```text
bar(z_a)(p+x)=det(a,x),                  x in V.            (21)
```

Indeed `Ta,T^2a` are the other two nonzero vectors, and the determinant
functional vanishes on `0,a` and equals one on those other two.  Since every
element of `GL_2(F2)` has determinant one, this alternating pairing is
canonical and invariant.  Consequently

```text
V -> W_p,
a |-> bar(z_a),

W_p={f in F2^V : f(p)=0, sum_(x!=p) f(x)=0}              (22)
```

is an `F2`-linear `C3`-equivariant isomorphism.  It is literally the truth-
table realization of

```text
V isomorphic_to Hom(V,C2),
a |-> chi_a,                 chi_a(x)=det(a,x).            (23)
```

In the gauge `b=0`, the three nonzero vectors are

```text
0011, 0101, 0110,                                             (24)
```

which are also the three mod-two image lines of `(I-Pi_2)sigma_a Pi_2`.
Thus the projector does not merely have the same representation type as the
quartic character plane: after the marked `C3` fixed point chooses the
origin, `(20)--(23)` give the exact self-duality map.

Now assume there is an **actual connected `V4` torsor** over the regular
locus `U` of a full Galois resolvent normalization.  THM-2655 dualizes its
monodromy to the canonical equivariant injection

```text
i:Hom(V,C2) -> H^1_et(U,mu_2).                             (25)
```

Only under that geometric torsor hypothesis may `(23)` be composed with
`i`.  If moreover `U=Xbar\D` has a smooth projective rational
`C3`-equivariant SNC completion, with boundary-class map

```text
delta:C=Z^(Irr D) -> P=Pic(Xbar),
K=ker(delta), L=im(delta), L_sat=P intersection (L tensor Q),
```

the Kummer localization row and torsion-freeness of `P` give

```text
H^1_et(U,mu_2) isomorphic_to ker(delta mod 2),
0 -> K/2K -> ker(delta mod 2) -> (L_sat/L)[2] -> 0.       (26)
```

For completeness, the last arrow sends `x mod 2C`, with `delta(x)=2p`, to
`p+L`; its kernel is `K/2K`, and the torsion of `P/L` is `L_sat/L`.
Therefore `(20)--(26)` put the scaled leakage plane into the exact boundary
kernel whenever the actual torsor and completion exist.

They still do **not** determine its integral source.  Since the plane is
irreducible under `C3`, it may lie wholly in `K/2K` (unit relations) or map
injectively to `(L_sat/L)[2]` (Picard saturation).  The finite marked fibre
and its projector see the common standard plane but not this dichotomy; the
integral presentation `delta` is the required sidecar.

### 4.2 A compatible reflection completes `A4` to `S4`

The binary generator `sigma_a` above is a vertical deck translation.  It is
not the order-two reflection which inverts the `C3` quotient.  Fix `tau_b`
and ask instead for an affine involution `j` satisfying

```text
j tau_b j=tau_b^(-1).                                     (27)
```

Equation `(27)` forces `j` to fix the unique `tau_b`-fixed point and to act
as one of the three transpositions on its three-cycle.  Hence there are
exactly three such `j` for each `b`; every one has cycle type `1+1+2`, is an
odd permutation outside `A4`, and satisfies

```text
<A4,j>=S4.                                                (28)
```

The companion enumerates all affine linear parts and translations: it finds
`12` reflection rows over the four gauges and order `24` in every completion.
Thus an actual `C2` reflection on a completion can be connected to this root-
fibre projector only if it lifts compatibly to the marked torsor.  At the
finite-fibre level such a lift is already an `S4` completion; the internal
`A4` involution supplies no reflection on the base or on `delta`.  An abstract
semilinear reflection of the standard plane is not a geometric lift.

## 5. The two live quartic branches need two coordinates

After THM-2633, the live transitive degree-four monodromy types are `A4` and
`S4`.  THM-2598 identifies their matching quotients as `C3` and `S3`.
Together with THM-2743, the marked affine anatomy is now:

| branch | matching quotient | binary linear shadow | source of affine class | binary permutation | off-diagonal rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| `A4` | `C3` | identity | nonzero `H^1(C2,V)` translation | double transposition | 1 |
| `S4` | `S3` | transposition | class trivial on both factors, nontrivial jointly | transposition | 1 |

The equality of the final rank and norm is informative but insufficient:
projector leakage alone does **not** distinguish `A4` from `S4`.  The linear
shadow of the binary generator, or equivalently whether the class is local
on `C2` or only a free-factor compatibility defect, is the required second
coordinate.

For a marked quartic root fibre, the pair

```text
(binary linear shadow, rank((I-Pi_3)sPi_3))                  (29)
```

therefore distinguishes the two live affine lift mechanisms from their
intransitive quotient controls.  This is a finite monodromy statement.  It
does not construct the marked action from a polynomial Keller map, preserve
Jelonek ownership, constrain the normalized resolvent divisor, or exclude
either `A4` or `S4`.  It proves no part of JC(2) or DC(2) beyond that exact
finite interface.

## 6. LRC interpretation and stopping boundary

The `A4` row gives a useful warning for any attempt to couple a binary
transporter to an equal ternary corolla.  A binary move can act trivially on
the ternary quotient labels and still create charged amplitude through an
invisible translation of the underlying four-state torsor.  Quotient action
alone therefore cannot certify flatness; one must retain the affine origin
class or an equivalent owner/phase sidecar.

Conversely, no common four-state torsor has been identified between the
physical LRC binary carrier and THM-2721's three-arm corolla.  The theorem
does not supply a lawful involution, endpoint current, owner label, or
positive charged coefficient on that carrier.  It excludes no LRC row and
does not prove LRC(14).
