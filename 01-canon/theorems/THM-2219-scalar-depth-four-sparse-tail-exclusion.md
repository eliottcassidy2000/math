# THM-2219 -- every scalar depth-four profile is empty

## 1. Capacity criterion on the primitive carrier

Use

```text
D_a={t in R/Z:||at||<1/14},
C_H={t in R/Z:||Ht||>1/7}.                           (1)
```

In the scalar `5+3` branch, normalize the thirteen-unit guard on

```text
N=13^5=371293.                                       (2)
```

The signed primitive unit group is

```text
G_5=(Z/NZ)^*/{+/-1},
|G_5|=6*13^4=171366,                                 (3)
```

and is generated by `2`. A blocker of depth four is safe at every primitive
residue: after dividing by `13^4`, its normalized residue has distance at
least `1/13>1/14`. It therefore places no further restriction on this
carrier.

For two shallower blocker masks `A_u,A_v`, let

```text
R_(u,v)=C_H\(A_u union A_v),
W_(u,v)=|R_(u,v)|,                                   (4)

C_(u,v)(q)=|R_(u,v) intersection D_q|               (5)
```

on either the signed carrier or its doubled full-residue normalization.
If five unit terminal dangers cover `R_(u,v)`, then

```text
W_(u,v)
 <=sum_(i=1)^5 C_(u,v)(q_i)
 <=Top_5(C_(u,v)).                                   (6)
```

For singleton residual rows `C_u,C_v`,

```text
C_(u,v)(q)<=min(C_u(q),C_v(q)),                     (7)
```

so any strict upper bound for the top five entries of the meet row
contradicts (6). This is the capacity criterion of THM-2216.

## 2. Exact cyclic-correlation engine

Put

```text
B=|G_4|=6*13^3=13182,
e=r+jB,             0<=r<B, 0<=j<13.                (8)
```

Write `g(e)` for the guard bit and `d(e)` for the terminal-danger bit. For
a terminal exponent `q`, define its root-fibre row

```text
h_q(r)=sum_(j=0)^12 g(r+jB)d(q+r+jB).                (9)
```

Exact root counting gives

```text
h_q(r) in {0,1,2}.                                  (10)
```

The unrestricted guard capacity is `F(q)=sum_r h_q(r)`. If `b_lambda` is
the periodic danger row of a blocker of depth `lambda`, the singleton
capacity for owner phase `a` is

```text
C_(lambda,a)(q)
 =F(q)-sum_r h_q(r)b_lambda(r+a).                   (11)
```

Thus every required singleton row is one cyclic correlation. The owner
periods at depths one, two, and three are

```text
13182,       1014,       78.                        (12)
```

The companions compute (11) over the finite field of order

```text
5767169=11*2^19+1                                   (13)
```

with primitive root `3`. The full transform has length `2^19`; the three
short transforms have lengths `2^15,2^11,2^8`. Each length is sufficient
for the corresponding linear convolution and divides the field order
minus one. Every coefficient is at most the signed guard mass

```text
122405<5767169,                                     (14)
```

so its canonical finite-field residue is the exact integer coefficient.
No floating-point arithmetic or rounding occurs.

The guard-fibre weight

```text
H(r)=sum_j g(r+jB)                                  (15)
```

is always nine or ten, with histogram

```text
#{H=9}=9415,              #{H=10}=3767.             (16)
```

These weights give complete exact residual tables without expanding all
thirteen sheets.

## 3. The profile `(2,2,4)`

There are `1014` depth-two owner classes and hence

```text
binom(1014+1,2)=514605                               (17)
```

unordered pairs, including all repeated-owner diagonals. For every pair,
(7) gives the simpler one-owner bound

```text
Top_5(C_(u,v))
 <=min(Top_5(C_u),Top_5(C_v)).                      (18)
```

The first companion checks every pair in exact full-residue normalization.
All margins

```text
W_(u,v)-min(Top_5(C_u),Top_5(C_v))                  (19)
```

are positive. The unique minimum is

```text
(u,v)=(508,1015),
W=177888,                bound=174358,
margin=3530.                                        (20)
```

A definition-level replay at this pair gives actual pair-row top five

```text
(29662,29648,29644,29620,29612),                    (21)
```

with sum `148186` and actual margin `29702`. The script also supplies an
independent fixed-threshold Hellinger table over the same complete universe;
its minimum certificate margin is `4876`. Hence `(2,2,4)` is empty.

## 4. The sparse-tail mechanism for `(1,1,4)`

Depth one has `B=13182` owner classes, so the unordered universe contains

```text
binom(B+1,2)=86889153                                (22)
```

pairs. Direct storage of every pair row is unnecessary.

Let `L_a` be the guard weight removed by owner `a`, let

```text
J(delta)=#{r:b_1(r)=b_1(r+delta)=1},                (23)
```

and let `T_(a,b)` count intersection phases with `H(r)=10`. Exact
inclusion-exclusion gives

```text
W_(a,b)
 =122405-L_a-L_b+9J(b-a)+T_(a,b).                  (24)
```

The nine-sheet floor obtained by omitting `T_(a,b)>=0` has global minimum

```text
87478.                                               (25)
```

Choose

```text
theta=17495,                5theta=87475.            (26)
```

If a pair has no label at which both singleton capacities exceed `theta`,
then its meet top five is at most `5theta`, and (25) closes it with margin
at least three.

Moreover `C_a(q)<=F(q)`, so only the `6294` labels with `F(q)>theta` can
enter a tail. Exact short correlations over all those labels produce only

```text
1835 triples (q,a,C_a(q)) with C_a(q)>theta.         (27)
```

Form pair records only among owners occurring at the same `q`. This creates

```text
178781 occurrences on 177699 distinct owner pairs,  (28)
```

rather than all `86,889,153` pairs. For each such pair, compute the exact
weight (24), sort its shared high meet entries, and pad to five entries by
`theta`. Every padded bound is strict except two:

```text
owner labels (1,12):       W=87708, padded bound=88025,
owner labels (6,14280):    W=87865, padded bound=88653. (29)
```

The complete sparse pair digest is

```text

6ee285a058cbc997c5cdaa0a56be4476.                   (30)
```

For the two exceptions, exact full singleton rows give meet top-five sums

```text
(1,12):       86050,       meet margin=1658,
(6,14280):    86803,       meet margin=1062.         (31)
```

Independently, the integral-ceiling Hellinger certificate at threshold
`17000` gives

```text
(1,12):       bound=86742, margin=966,
(6,14280):    bound=87371, margin=494.               (32)
```

Definition-level actual pair rows have still larger margins `9528` and
`9293`. Hence `(1,1,4)` is empty.

## 6. Consequence and scope

The six legal deepest-depth-four profiles are

```text
(1,1,4), (1,2,4), (1,3,4),
(2,2,4), (2,3,4), (3,3,4).                          (41)
```

Sections 3--5 close the first four. THM-2215 closes `(2,3,4)`, and THM-2213
closes `(3,3,4)`. Therefore every scalar survivor has

```text
lambda_3>=5.                                         (42)
```

There are `1140=binom(20,3)` legal profiles through depth nineteen.
THM-2198/2204/2205/2207 remove the four with deepest depth at most three,
and this theorem with THM-2213/2215 removes the next six. Thus `1130`
profiles remain before later independent exclusions. THM-2223 removes
another `21`, temporarily leaving

```text
1109 scalar valuation profiles at depths 5..19.      (43)
```

The later uniform clause-state bound THM-2224 subsumes those `21` and removes
all `455` profiles with `lambda_1>=6`. In the combined current ledger this
leaves `1130-455=675` scalar profiles, all with

```text
lambda_1<=5,                 lambda_3>=5.             (44)
```

This is a large exact pruning of the scalar finite ledger, not a proof that
the remaining profiles or LRC(14) are empty. The successful mechanism is
labelled correlation sparsity: a global residual floor pays for all
nonshared tails, while exact meet or Hellinger data are retained only on
the sparse shared-tail support.
