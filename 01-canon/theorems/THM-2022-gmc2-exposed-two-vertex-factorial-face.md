# THM-2022 — reserved exposed-face theorem

Write each monomial as a support point `(q,h)`, where `q=a-b` is charge and
`h=(a+b)/2` is Wick height. Suppose an affine functional `h-lambda*q` is maximized
at exactly two nonzero-coefficient monomials of opposite charge, and every other
support point has deficit strictly greater than `1`. Along multiples of the primitive
return length the exposed pair gives a unique channel. The proposed proof shows that
one off-face factor gains at most one power of multinomial entropy but loses more than
one factorial power; sublinear channels are summable and linear channels are
superfactorially small.
