# THM-2862 -- modular level three/four and the two six-point lifts

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: identify the level-three and level-four projective modular
quotients with the marked `A4/C3` and `S4/S3` affine quartic rows, then
compare the natural six-point action on `P^1(Z/4)` with the six-edge action
on the four level-four complements.  Promotion requires exact group and
cycle censuses, a proof that both six-actions have the same three-block
`S3` quotient but are nonisomorphic, the precise stabilizer and parity
sidecars, and a reproducible standard-library companion.

The reservation proves no graph-quartic realization, Keller exclusion,
Jacobian or Dixmier conjecture case, LRC current, canonical tournament, or
identification of a concrete Farey/Pythagorean carrier with either finite
action.
