# THM-3138 -- left-factorial resonance Newton-slope separation

**SUPERSEDED.**

The intended exact-quadratic lane was `d=r+2=p+1` with `p` prime.  It no
longer needs a Kurepa or left-factorial hypothesis.  If `p` is odd, then
`d=p+1` is even and `d>=4`, so THM-3140 excludes the resonance uniformly by
its `2`-adic Bessel-residual argument.  If `p=2`, then `d=3` is prime and is
already excluded by THM-3131.  This retirement is scoped to that reserved
factorial-window application; it makes no assertion about broader forms of
Kurepa's conjecture.
