# THM-2704 -- the split even-Faber prime-23 curve has generic genus 89

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**  The scope is
the nonzero-`lambda` split
**even-Faber** subchart only: the eleven allowed odd seeds
`E1,E3,...,E21` are set to zero.

## 1. Statement

Let `C_(c,d,e,w,eta)` be the weighted projective closure of

```text
f2(t,v,zeta)=0,
zeta f1(t,v,zeta)^4=eta t^23,                eta!=0,   (1)
```

where `f1,f2` are the exact scaled flux polynomials frozen by the companion.
Then:

1. at `c=d=e=w=eta=1`, the curve is geometrically integral and its
   normalization has genus `89`;
2. the same conclusions hold on a nonempty Zariski-open subset of the
   five-parameter family;
3. on that open set the degree-`23` function `t` has fixed-fibre order word
   `4^5,1^3`, twenty-three simple poles at infinity, and ramification degree
   `222`, of which `15` lies over `t=0`, zero at infinity, and `207`
   elsewhere.

Consequently no nonconstant rational Keller trajectory can lie in this
generic coefficient locus.  Special coefficient strata, constant-`t`
branches, `y=0`, the third flux, and the odd-seed bank remain outside this
conclusion.

## 2. Weighted complete intersection and arithmetic genus

Give `(h,t,v,zeta)` weights `(1,1,2,3)`.  Weighted homogenization makes

```text
F2=0,                         deg F2=6,
zeta F1^4-eta t^23=0,         deg F1=5, deg H=23.      (2)
```

The only singular points of `P(1,1,2,3)` are the `v` and `zeta` coordinate
points.  The values of `F2` there are respectively

```text
-1190488992,                     15944049,             (3)
```

so the curve avoids both uniformly.  At `c=d=e=w=1`, exact Groebner bases
prove that the surface `{F2=0}` is smooth in `h=1` and on all three weighted
infinity patches; exact factorization also makes it integral.  Smoothness is
therefore nonempty open.  On this locus the two equations in (2) are a
regular sequence in the smooth ambient locus.  The curve is lci and
Gorenstein, so weighted adjunction applies:

```text
omega_C=O_C(6+23-7)=O_C(22),
deg O_C(1)=6*23/(1*1*2*3)=23,
deg omega_C=506,
p_a(C)=254.                                           (4)
```

Independently, the weighted Hilbert count is
`h^0(O(22))-h^0(O(16))=458-204=254`.

## 3. Exact pencil base and the five forced cusps

On the smooth surface the second equation is the pencil generated by

```text
zeta F1^4,                         t^23.               (5)
```

The companion checks that there is no base point on `h=t=0`.  In `h=1`, its
reduced base support is exactly

```text
zeta=0, G3(v)=0:               three points,
F1=0, L5(v)=0:                 five points,            (6)
```

where `G3,L5` are squarefree and coprime.  The local base ideals are
`(t^23,zeta)` and `(t^23,x^4)`.  Exact Jacobian gcds give the member equations

```text
zeta=unit*t^23                         at G3,
x^4=unit*t^23                          at L5.           (7)
```

Thus the three new points are smooth.  Since `gcd(4,23)=1`, each old point is
one analytically irreducible branch with

```text
delta_(4,23)=(4-1)(23-1)/2=33.                       (8)
```

The five forced cusps contribute total delta `165`.  Bertini makes a general
pencil member smooth away from the base support.  Once geometric integrality
is supplied below, (4) and (8) give the generic normalization genus

```text
254-5*33=89.                                          (9)
```

This fixed-place statement is uniform in `c,d,e,w`: every parameter-bearing
term of `f1,f2` contains a positive power of `t`, while the unit and Jacobian
gcds at `G3,L5` are parameter-independent.  Hence for every `eta!=0` the five
old points retain the analytic type `(4,23)`; no semicontinuity assumption is
being substituted for the exact local calculation.

## 4. Geometric integrality and birational elimination

At the all-one specialization, the invariant eliminant

```text
R(t,v)=Res_zeta(f2,zeta f1^4-t^23)                   (10)
```

has `553` terms, bidegree `(46,23)`, constant `v`-leading coefficient, and
one exact factor over `Q`.  Its `zeta`-subresultant degrees are

```text
5,2,1,0.                                              (11)
```

The penultimate member is linear and its coefficients have `v`-degree at
most `11<23`; they cannot both vanish generically on `(R)`.  Hence the common
`zeta` root has degree one over `Q(R)`, so (10) is birational to (1).
The resultant occurs to exponent one.  Thus it also cannot conceal a second
affine curve component: a component with one-dimensional `(t,v)` image would
contribute another resultant factor, while a component with zero-dimensional
image is impossible because the coefficient of `zeta^2` in `f2` is the
nonzero constant `15944049`.  The exact finite infinity fibre below excludes
a component contained in `h=0`.

Irreducibility over `Q(t)` makes arithmetic monodromy transitive on the prime
number `23` of sheets.  The normal geometric monodromy group has equal block
sizes `1` or `23`.  Over the algebraic closure, local inertia at `t=0` is the
nontrivial word `4^5,1^3` from (7), and lies in geometric monodromy.  Singleton
orbits are impossible.  Thus the all-one curve is geometrically integral.
Geometric integrality is open on the proper flat regular-sequence locus.

## 5. Exact all-one normalization by the q-plane

The physical fourth root is already rational on (1): after normalizing the
nonzero constants,

```text
zeta=q^4t^3,
q f1(t,v,q^4t^3)+t^5=0,
f2(t,v,q^4t^3)=0.                                    (12)
```

Eliminating `v` gives an irreducible `75`-term polynomial `F(t,q)` of
bidegree `(15,23)`, with Newton polygon

```text
conv{(0,3),(15,0),(15,23)}.                           (13)
```

Its three edge polynomials are squarefree, and Pick's theorem gives `158`
interior lattice points.  The `v`-subresultant degrees are `3,2,1,0`; after
removing the sole content `72024584016q`, the linear member is
`A(t,q)v+B(t,q)`.  Exact factorization gives

```text
Res_t(A,B)=unit*q^5*(99q^4-640)*Q69(q),              (14)
```

where `Q69` is irreducible and squarefree.  Four resultants coprime to `Q69`
certify that each of its roots has two distinct finite `v` preimages, a
transverse `(A,B)` collision, and nonzero plane Hessian.  Hence they are
sixty-nine ordinary nodes.  The `t`-leading coefficients of `A` and `B` have
`q`-degree at most `9<69`; their shared factor is `99q^4-640`, while both are
coprime to `Q69`.  Thus the quartic in (14) is the `t=infinity` collision and
every `Q69` collision is finite.  Completeness is the exact identity

```text
gcd(Res_t(F,F_t),Res_t(F,F_q))=unit*q^30*Q69^2.       (15)
```

Every known ordinary node contributes at least order two to both local
resultant factors.  Thus the exact exponent `Q69^2` in (15) leaves no room
even for an additional singular point sharing one of the same `q`-values.
The `q=0` factor and the quartic in (14) belong to the already squarefree
toric boundary, so no other torus or boundary singularity remains.  The
all-one normalization therefore has

```text
g=158-69=89.                                          (16)
```

Comparing (16) with (4) shows that the five cusps (8) are the only intrinsic
all-one singularities.  It also supplies the exact integrality witness used
in the open-locus argument of Sections 3--4.  The all-one point need not be
identified with the abstract Bertini-generic parameter to prove that the
nonempty opens intersect.

## 6. Infinity and Riemann--Hurwitz

Put `s=1/t`, `v=V/s^2`, `zeta=S/s^3`.  The infinity face is

```text
Binf(V,S)=0,                       S Ainf(V,S)^4=eta.  (17)
```

Its generic resultant has content `11^30`, `1416` terms, degree `23` in `V`,
one irreducible factor over `Q(c,d,e,w,eta)`, and constant leading coefficient

```text
2^25*3^21*7^5*11^40.                                  (18)
```

At the all-one point it is irreducible modulo `17`; an exact Jacobian
resultant is coprime to it.  Hence twenty-three transverse infinity points
occur on a nonempty open locus.  There `s` is a uniformizer, all poles of `t`
are simple, and infinity ramification is zero.

For the connected genus-`89`, degree-`23` map,

```text
deg Ram(t)=2*89-2+2*23=222.                           (19)
```

The fixed fibre contributes `5*(4-1)=15`, infinity zero, and all other finite
fibres together contribute `207`.

## 7. Uniform singularity budget and exact boundary

Every integral flat specialization retaining (1) with `eta!=0` has arithmetic
genus `254`; the uniform local calculation after (9) gives five forced cusps
of total delta `165`.  A rational normalization must therefore acquire at
least

```text
254-165=89                                             (20)
```

additional delta.  Thus a genus-zero survivor must become reducible/nonflat,
leave this chart, or enter a proper genus-drop/discriminant locus carrying at
least `89` extra singularity units.

This is not a uniform closure.  In particular, it does not address:

1. the eleven odd split Faber coefficients;
2. exceptional reducible or high-delta parameter strata;
3. constant-`t` and split `y=0` branches;
4. the retained third flux; or
5. arbitrary degree-twenty-two Keller pairs, `JC(2)`, or `DC(2)`.

The local fixed and infinity fibres alone also do not imply positive genus:
Riemann--Hurwitz would allow genus zero with `29` further ramification units.
The positive-genus mechanism is global adjunction plus the exact singularity
audit, not the neutralized Kummer cover.
