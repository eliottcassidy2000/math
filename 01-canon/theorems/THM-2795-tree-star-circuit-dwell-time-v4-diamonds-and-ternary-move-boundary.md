# THM-2795 -- tree star-circuit dwell time, V4 diamonds, and ternary move boundary

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: identify the vertex-star vectors of a tree as one minimal
binary circuit.  A vertex order is its simple closed prefix walk in the cut
cube, and each edge difference is exactly that coordinate's dwell time;
gracefulness means the dwell times are `1,...,m`.  Adjacent swaps replace
one prefix by the opposite corner of an affine `V4` diamond, while
three-place rotations give the local `C3/S3` permutohedral move.  Exact
small-tree hostiles will show that the graceful subset is already
disconnected at `P4`, so the binary/ternary moves are structure, not a
Graceful Tree proof or a literal modular-group action.

Until proof, replay, independent hostile audit, and promotion, this is not a
proved theorem or dependency.
