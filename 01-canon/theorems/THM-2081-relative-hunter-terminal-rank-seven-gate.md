# THM-2081 -- relative Hunter on the terminal guard complement

Put

```text
D_q={t in R/Z:||qt||<1/14},
E_h={t in R/Z:||ht||<1/7},
C_h=(R/Z) minus E_h.                                    (1)
```

Let `Q={q_1,...,q_7}` be seven distinct positive speeds. Define

```text
I_i=measure(D_(q_i) intersect E_h),
A_i=D_(q_i) intersect C_h,
w_ij=measure(A_i intersect A_j).                        (2)
```

Let `tau_h(Q)` be the maximum total weight of a spanning tree of the complete
graph on the seven labels with edge weights `w_ij`, and put

```text
Delta_h(Q)=2/7-sum_i I_i.                               (3)
```

## 1. The relative Hunter gate

If

```text
G_Q={t:||q_it||>=1/14 for every i} subset E_h,           (4)
```

then

```text
Delta_h(Q)>=0,
tau_h(Q)<=Delta_h(Q).                                   (5)
```

More sharply,

```text
measure(G_Q minus E_h)>=tau_h(Q)-Delta_h(Q).             (6)
```

Thus either a negative `Delta`, or `tau>Delta`, contradicts terminal guard
containment and supplies positive-measure safe time outside the guard.

### Proof

Condition (4) is equivalent to

```text
C_h subset union_i A_i.                                 (7)
```

Every `D_q` has measure `1/7`, so

```text
sum_i measure(A_i)=sum_i(1/7-I_i)=1-sum_i I_i.          (8)
```

Hunter's pointwise forest inequality on any spanning tree `T` gives

```text
measure(union_i A_i)
 <=sum_i measure(A_i)-sum_(ij in T)w_ij.                (9)
```

Choose a maximum spanning tree. Since `measure(C_h)=5/7`, equations
(7)--(9) imply

```text
5/7<=1-sum_i I_i-tau_h(Q),                              (10)
```

which is exactly (5). Without assuming (7), subtract the right side of (9)
from `measure(C_h)` to get

```text
measure(C_h minus union_i A_i)
 >=5/7-[1-sum_i I_i-tau_h(Q)]
 =tau_h(Q)-Delta_h(Q).                                  (11)
```

The set on the left is `G_Q minus E_h`, proving (6). QED.

The first inequality in (5) is the seven-leaf scalar wall already visible in
THM-2080: the seven independence baselines sum to `2/7`. The second inequality
is the missing collision invoice. It charges only danger-comb intersections
that occur **outside** the guard, precisely where they waste capacity needed
to cover `C_h`.

## 2. Exact projective data

THM-2080 computes each `I_i` exactly. The new edge is the three-frequency
atom

```text
w_ij=measure(D_(q_i) intersect D_(q_j) intersect C_h).  (12)
```

Its endpoints lie on the rational arrangement generated by

```text
k/q_i plus-or-minus 1/(14q_i),
l/q_j plus-or-minus 1/(14q_j),
m/h plus-or-minus 1/(7h).                              (13)
```

Testing one midpoint of every arrangement cell therefore computes (12)
exactly. Common dilation of `(h,q_i,q_j)` is Haar-invisible, so the edge is a
projective three-frequency invariant. Unlike the global pair weight
`measure(D_(q_i) intersect D_(q_j))`, it retains the guard location. This is
why THM-1221's global seven-wall tree cannot simply substitute for `tau_h`.

## 3. Complete terminal-height-24 rank-seven shell

Retain the rank-seven terminal conditions from THM-2073/2077/2078:

```text

|Q|=7,
Q hereditarily primitive and divisor-complete through 14,
h positive odd,
6h<16 max(Q).                                           (14)
```

There are exactly `131` such cores and `4,120` allowed core/guard pairs. The
companion evaluates every `I_i`, all restricted edges (12), and an exact



Thus this relative-tree certificate independently reproves the rank-seven

## 4. Honest all-height target

The theorem does not prove

```text
tau_h(Q)>Delta_h(Q)                                     (19)
```

for every unbounded hereditary divisor-complete rank-seven terminal. The
finite replay suggests (19) has substantial room, but a height cutoff cannot
be inferred from it. A scale-free proof must classify or bound the projective
three-frequency edges (12), including their common-gcd and endpoint phases.

THM-2083 subsequently closes the relation-free part of this target, and
THM-2085 makes its bound explicit: relative-tree failure forces a nonzero
integer relation of coefficient height at most `57` on some triple
`(h,q_i,q_j)`. Thus only finitely many coefficient templates, rather than the
whole projective three-frequency space, remain.

THM-2087 retains the graph carrier implicit here: the pairs without such a
short relation cannot contain a spanning tree. Hence the short-relation graph
contains a complete cut of at least six indexed relations, forcing either a
bounded guard ratio or a guard-anchored height-`6498` two-anchor star.

The useful next split is exact:

```text
sum I_i>2/7       -> mixed-overlap star closes;
sum I_i<=2/7      -> restricted collisions must satisfy tau<=Delta.        (20)
```

This is a smaller residual than raw guard containment: the second branch
retains a seven-vertex weighted graph and one scalar deficit rather than the
whole circle arrangement.

## 5. Assumption challenge and Tournament Analysis

The challenged assumption is that graph vertices should be runners or global
danger combs. The faithful vertices are the **restricted events**
`A_i=D_(q_i) intersect C_h`. This quotient preserves the exact predicate
`C_h subset union A_i`; it destroys all geometry inside the guard, which is
irrelevant to the cover, while retaining outside intersections as edge
weights.

Orienting edges by larger `w_ij` produces a priority tournament, but Hunter
needs a maximum undirected spanning tree and its actual rational weight.
Score order, SCCs, and Hamiltonian paths do not determine that weight. The
faithful carrier is the weighted graphic matroid on the seven restricted
events, with `Delta_h(Q)` as its capacity sidecar. QED.
