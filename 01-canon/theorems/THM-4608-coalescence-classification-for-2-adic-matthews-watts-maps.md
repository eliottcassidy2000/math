# THM-4608 — coalescence classification on Z_2

## Setting

* Every Matthews–Watts map on `Z_2` has the form `T(x) = (m_0 x + r_0)/2` for even `x` and `(m_1 x + r_1)/2` for odd `x`, with:
  * `m_0, m_1` odd and positive;
  * `r_0` even and `r_1` odd.
* Its debt group is generated by `m_1/m_0`, so its rank is at most 1.
* It contracts (`Λ = ½ ln(m_0 m_1/4) < 0`) iff `m_0 m_1 ∈ {1, 3}`.
* Let `y` be Haar and `e ∈ Z ∖ {0}`.

## Statement

**(a) Rank zero, `m_0 = m_1 = 1`.**
* `y` and `y + e` merge almost surely iff `s_0 := r_1 − r_0` divides `e`. Then `P(no merge by T)` decays geometrically.
* Otherwise they never merge.

**(b) The Collatz class, `{m_0, m_1} = {1, 3}`.**
* `y` and `y + e` merge almost surely iff `s/3^(v_3 s)` divides `e`, where `s := r_0 + r_1`. Then

      c T^(−1/2) ≤ P(no merge by T) ≤ C T^(−1/2) (log T)²,

  with the upper bound at sketch level (THM-4581 4).
* Otherwise they never merge.

**(c) Expanding maps, `m_0 m_1 ≥ 5`.**
* `q(e) → 0` as `|e| → ∞` (THM-4607).
* For `m_0 = 1` and `r_1 = 1` (the `px + 1` maps), `q(e) < 1` for every `e` (THM-4606).

**Summary.**
* On `Z_2`, every accessible integer translate coalesces almost surely iff the map contracts.
* Among the contracting maps, the coalescence tail is geometric in rank zero and diffusive in rank one.
* Up to affine conjugacy, `3x + 1` is the only 2-adic map whose translates coalesce at the diffusive rate.

## Proof

**Conjugacies.**
* A translation `x ↦ x + c` maps orbits of `y, y + e` to orbits of `y + c, y + c + e`. It preserves the offset and Haar measure, and conjugates `T` to a map of the same class.
* **(a)** With `c = −r_0` (even), `T` becomes `y/2` (even `y`) and `(y + s_0)/2` (odd `y`).
* **(b), `m_0 = 1`, `m_1 = 3`.** With `c = −r_0`, `T` becomes `y/2` and `(3y + r_0 + r_1)/2`.
* **(b), `m_0 = 3`, `m_1 = 1`.** With an odd `c = −r_1` (this swaps the parity classes), `T` becomes `y/2` on even `y` and `(3y + r_0 + r_1)/2` on odd `y`.
* Then `x ↦ s x` (`s` odd) conjugates `(·)/2, (· + 1)/2` and `(·)/2, (3· + 1)/2` to the maps with constant `s`. It rescales offsets by `s`.
* So the pair `(y, y + e)` for the map with constant `s` is the pair `(y', y' + e/s)` for the map with constant 1.

**Accessible offsets.**
* **(a)** If `s_0 | e`, the `x + 1` chain from the integer `e/s_0` is absorbed almost surely with a geometric tail (THM-4606 statement 4). Its offset moves `e ↦ e/2` or `(e ± 1)/2` into `{0, ±1}`, and from `±1` it is absorbed with probability 1/2 per step.
* **(b)** If `s/3^(v_3 s) | e`, then `e/s ∈ Z[1/3]`, and THM-4581 3′ gives almost-sure absorption. The tail is THM-4581 4.

**Non-accessible offsets.**
* If `e/s` has an odd prime `ℓ ∤ 3` in its denominator (for (a), any odd prime), the offset keeps `v_ℓ < 0` forever:
  * the additive terms of the pair chain are `ℓ`-integral;
  * the multipliers `1/2` and `3/2` are `ℓ`-units.
* So `e ≠ 0` at every time. A meeting with nonzero debt is null (THM-4581 3(i)), so there is no merge.

**(c)** THM-4607. Its departure rule, that both coins give `min(m_0, m_1)/2` at a flip from zero debt, is checked exactly in `z2_step_table.py`. ∎

## Reading

* **Why `d = 2` closes.**
  * The debt group of a 2-adic map is generated by one ratio, so its walk is at most one-dimensional and recurrent.
  * Contraction plus accessibility then suffices, by THM-4581's argument transported through the conjugacies.
  * For larger digit bases the rank can reach `d − 1`, and contraction no longer suffices (THM-4609).
* **Collatz singled out.**
  * Among all 2-adic Matthews–Watts maps, the `3x + s` class is exactly the contracting class of rank one.
  * Within it, `3x + 1` is the representative for which every integer offset is accessible.
  * Coalescence of its translates sits at the boundary between exponential (rank 0) and failing (expanding) behaviour: the critical `T^(−1/2)` tail.
