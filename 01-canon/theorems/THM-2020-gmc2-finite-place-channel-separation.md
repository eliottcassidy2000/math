# THM-2020 — reserved finite-place channel certificates

For fixed support the moment ideal is defined over the integers and is finitely
generated; any complex two-sided nullcone point therefore has an algebraic one.
At a nonarchimedean place a finite channel sum with a unique least valuation cannot
vanish. Legendre's formula turns factorial valuations into an exact carry/digit-sum
observable.

The first application is the support `P=a Z^6+b Zbar^2+c Zbar^18`: after its two
first-return contributions cancel at `m=4`, the three `m=8` channels have 3-adic
valuations `5,4,5`, so the middle channel survives.
