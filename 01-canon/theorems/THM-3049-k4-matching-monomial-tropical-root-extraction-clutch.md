# THM-3049 -- tropicalizing the three matching monomials exposes both clutches

**PROVED CANDIDATE + VERIFIED-EXACT; AWAITING INDEPENDENT HOSTILE AUDIT.**

## 1. Inheritance and statement

[THM-3045](THM-3045-k4-edge-isotypic-binary-ternary-integral-clutch.md)
computes the exact integral quotient left by the rational
`1+[22]+[31]` decomposition of the six-edge lattice of `K4`:

```text
Z^E/(L_0+L_22+L_31)  ~=  F2[M] + F3.                         (1)
```

The closest amplitude-side mechanism is
[THM-2290](THM-2290-context-selected-colored-pair-kernel-is-hafnian-complete.md):
an endpoint-coloured pair kernel is exact only before its matching-fibre
hafnian contraction forgets selectors and permits phase cancellation.  The
four-vertex positive carrier in Krenn--Gu--Soltesz uses the three disjoint
perfect matchings of `K4`.  The new point is that its three **uncontracted
matching monomials** carry precisely the quotient `(1)` after tropicalization.

Let

```text
E={01,02,03,12,13,23},
M={01|23,02|13,03|12}.                                      (2)
```

For nonzero edge weights `w_e` define

```text
A_m = product_(e in m) w_e.                                (3)
```

Equivalently, `(3)` is an `S4`-equivariant monomial map

```text
mu:(G_m)^E -> (G_m)^M.                                     (4)
```

Its map on cocharacter lattices, and hence on values under any additive
valuation, is

```text
lambda_m=x_e+x_e_bar.                                      (5)
```

Then the exact clutch map is

```text
Psi(x)=(lambda mod2, sum_m lambda_m mod3),                 (6)
ker Psi=L_0+L_22+L_31,
coker(ker Psi -> Z^E)=F2[M]+F3.                            (7)
```

The `S4` action permutes the binary coordinates through
`S4/V4=S3` and fixes the ternary coordinate.

## 2. Proof on the cocharacter lattice

The three pairs in `(2)` partition `E`.  Consequently the matrix of `(5)`
contains a `3 x 3` identity submatrix, so it is onto, and its kernel is the
rank-three lattice generated by the opposite-edge differences

```text
n_m=e_e-e_e_bar.                                           (8)
```

The first component of `(6)` therefore records exactly whether each matching
sum is even.  If it vanishes, write

```text
a_m=lambda_m/2.                                            (9)
```

The positive matching lattice is `Z^M`.  Its rational constant and `A2`
augmentation summands have integral sum

```text
Z(1,1,1)+{a:sum_m a_m=0},                                 (10)
```

of index three, with quotient `sum_m a_m mod3`.  Since multiplication by
two is invertible modulo three, that quotient is equivalently
`sum_m lambda_m mod3`.  Pulling `(10)` back through `(5)`, and adjoining
the kernel `(8)`, gives exactly `L_0+L_22+L_31`.  This proves `(6)--(7)`.

Every sheet relabelling permutes the three matchings, while their product

```text
product_m A_m = product_e w_e                               (11)
```

is invariant.  Equivariance and the `S4/V4=S3` action follow directly.

## 3. The exact root-extraction interpretation

Let the value group be `Z`.  Equation `(5)` gives two independent necessary
and sufficient **value-lattice** divisibility tests:

```text
v(A_m) is twice an integer for every m
    iff lambda mod2=0;                                     (12)

v(product_m A_m) is three times an integer
    iff sum_m lambda_m mod3=0.                             (13)
```

Thus the binary clutch obstructs choosing integral valuations for the three
pairwise square roots, while the ternary clutch obstructs choosing an
integral valuation for a cube root of the total matching monomial.  Both
vanish exactly when all three rational edge-isotypic projectors of THM-3045
are integral.

This statement is deliberately about cocharacters/valuations.  It is not a
field-root criterion.  Over `Q_7`, take matching monomials `(3,1,1)`, realized
by putting weight `3` on one edge and weight `1` on the other five.  Every
valuation is zero, so `(6)` vanishes, but `3` is neither a square nor a cube
modulo `7`.  Unit and residue-field data are the missing sidecar.

The two prime pieces are independent.  Cocharacters with matching sums

```text
(2,0,0)  give (kappa,tau)=(000,2),
(1,1,1)  give (kappa,tau)=(111,0).                         (14)
```

Hence neither the square-root obstruction nor the cube-root obstruction
determines the other.

## 4. Why this does not tropicalize the full coloured amplitude

For one inherited colouring, Krenn--Gu--Soltesz sum the products `(3)` over
the whole matching fibre.  Valuation does not commute with that sum when
leading terms cancel.  Give every edge the same endpoint colour, so all three
perfect matchings induce the same colouring.  The two exact weight choices

```text
(A_1,A_2,A_3)=(1,1,1),       (1,1,-2)                     (15)
```

are realizable by edge weights and have identical zero valuation vector and
identical zero clutch.  Their contracted amplitudes are respectively `3`
and `0`.  Thus `(6)` neither determines the amplitude nor even its
nonvanishing.

Nor may one erase endpoint-colour selectors before applying the theorem.
THM-2290 gives exact selector-erasure and phase-erasure collisions.  The
preserved object here is only

```text
three labelled matching monomials -> their cocharacter vector.             (16)
```

The operation destroys units, phases, addition inside a colouring fibre,
and every affine/Keller/LRC owner coordinate.  No theorem about arbitrary
coloured graphs, a tournament, a quartic source cover, or `LRC(14)` follows.
