# THM-3205 -- odd-primary lambda algebra engine and Toda gate family

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + FINITE-EXACT; INDEPENDENT IMMUTABLE AUDIT
PENDING.**

This is the repository's first object in unstable homotopy theory.  It is here
because its arithmetic engine is a continuant: the whole odd-primary input to
the theorem `pi_n(S^2) != 0` is one `tridiag(1,-2,1)` determinant, which
THM-3204 identifies as the parabolic point of the Chebyshev family.

## 1. The algebra and two load-bearing conventions

For an odd prime `p`, `Lambda` is the `F_p`-algebra on `lambda_i` (`i>=1`,
internal degree `2(p-1)i-1`, odd) and `mu_j` (`j>=0`, degree `2(p-1)j`, even),
with admissible monomials `nu_(i_1)...nu_(i_l)` characterized by

```text
i_(t+1) <= p i_t - 1   if nu_(i_t) = lambda_(i_t),
i_(t+1) <= p i_t       if nu_(i_t) = mu_(i_t),                        (1)
```

Adem-type relations rewriting each inadmissible adjacent pair, and a
differential `d` of internal degree `-1` and length `+1`.  Two conventions are
not determined by the displayed formulas and are fixed here by internal
consistency.

**(C1) The self term is omitted.**  In each relation the summand `j=k` returns
the left-hand side itself with coefficient `a(k,k)=-1`.  Reading the sum as
written would give `2 mu_0 lambda_2 = -mu_1 lambda_1`.  Omitting it gives
`mu_0 lambda_2 = -mu_1 lambda_1`, which is the identity the source itself uses.
The engine omits the self term.

**(C2) Leibniz carries the Koszul sign.**

```text
d(xy) = d(x)y + (-1)^|x| x d(y),                                      (2)
```

so only `lambda` factors flip the sign.  This is forced: with the unsigned rule
`d^2 != 0` already on `mu_2`, where `d^2(mu_2)=4 lambda_1^2 mu_0`.  The
companion records **3640** explicit unsigned failures against **0** signed
failures on 4150 normalized words.  No computation displayed in the source is
affected, because in every one of them `d` is applied to the right of an
even-degree (`mu`) prefix only; but any extension of those computations needs
`(2)`.

With `(C1)` and `(C2)` the engine reproduces verbatim, at `p=3,5,7,11,13`, all
eleven identities displayed in the source:

```text
mu_0 lambda_1=0,  mu_0 mu_1=0,  mu_0 lambda_2=-mu_1 lambda_1,
mu_0 mu_2=-mu_1 mu_1,           d lambda_1=0,   d mu_1=-lambda_1 mu_0,
d lambda_2=-2 lambda_1^2,       d mu_2=-lambda_2 mu_0-2 lambda_1 mu_1
                                       +mu_1 lambda_1,
d(mu_2 lambda_1)=-2 lambda_1 mu_1 lambda_1+mu_1 lambda_1^2,
d(mu_1 lambda_2)=lambda_1 mu_1 lambda_1-2 mu_1 lambda_1^2,
d(mu_1 mu_2 lambda_1)=lambda_1 mu_1^2 lambda_1
                      -2 mu_1 lambda_1 mu_1 lambda_1+mu_1^2 lambda_1^2. (3)
```

It also passes two controls the source does not state: `d^2=0` on 4150 words,
and confluence of the rewriting (leftmost-first equals rightmost-first
reduction on 4356 words).

## 2. The right stratification: index sum and lambda count

For a monomial `w` write `S(w)` for the sum of its indices, `l(w)` for its
length, and `q(w)` for the number of `lambda` letters.  Every relation and
every term of `d nu_k` preserves the index sum, and `d` raises both `l` and `q`
by one.  Hence

```text
S is a d-invariant;   l-q is a d-invariant;   q increases by 1.        (4)
```

Define the **excess** `e(w)=S(w)-l(w)`, so `e` drops by one at each step.
Inside the left ideal `Lambda*lambda` no letter can be `mu_0`: a `mu_0` forces
the next index to be `<=0`, which cascades to an impossible `lambda_0` before
the word can end in a `lambda`.  Therefore every index is `>=1` and `e>=0`.

**Lemma A (no incoming differential).** The `q=1` part of `Lambda*lambda(n)`
receives no differential, because its source would have `q=0` and every word of
`Lambda*lambda` has `q>=1`.

So on the `q=1` strata the `E^2`-term of the spectral sequence
`E^1(n)=Lambda*lambda(n) => (p) pi_*(S^(2n+1))` is a **kernel**, not a
subquotient.  This is sharper and simpler than a raw bidegree count, which at
`p=3` is polluted by strata with `q = 1 + 2(p-1)`.

## 3. Excess one is the unique square stratum

Fix `n=2` (so `S^5`) and `q=1`.  A word is then `mu_(a_1)...mu_(a_k)
lambda_(a_(k+1))` with all `a_t>=1`, `a_1<=2`, and `(1)`.

* `e=0`: all indices are `1`, the stratum is the single Toda word
  `alpha_(k+1)=mu_1^k lambda_1`, and the target stratum is empty, so every
  Toda element is a cycle.
* `e=1`: exactly one index equals `2`, the rest are `1`.  The stratum is

```text
u_0=mu_1^k lambda_2,
u_i=mu_1^(k-i) mu_2 mu_1^(i-1) lambda_1        (1<=i<=k),             (5)
```

  of size `k+1`, and the target (`q=2`, `e=0`) stratum is

```text
v_i=mu_1^(k-i) lambda_1 mu_1^i lambda_1        (0<=i<=k),             (6)
```

  also of size `k+1`.  **The source and target have equal dimension: this is
  the unique square stratum.**  The companion verifies that `(5)` and `(6)` are
  the complete strata, not merely subsets.
* `e>=2`: the target is strictly larger than the source (section 5).

## 4. The excess-one gate, strengthened

**Theorem B.** For `1<=k` and every odd prime `p`, in the basis `(5)`,`(6)`,

```text
d(u_0)=v_1-2v_0,
d(u_i)=v_(i+1)-2v_i+v_(i-1)      (1<=i<=k-1),
d(u_k)=v_(k-1)-2v_k,                                                 (7)
```

so `d^1` is the matrix `tridiag(1,-2,1)` of size `k+1`: precisely THM-3204's
parabolic point `alpha=-2`.  Therefore, by THM-3204 `(4)` and Theorem 1,

```text
det d^1=U_(k+1)(-1)=(-1)^(k+1)(k+2),                                  (8)

integral invariant factors = (1,...,1,k+2),  coker = Z/(k+2) cyclic.  (9)
```

Combining with Lemma A:

```text
E^2 = 0                       if p does not divide k+2,
E^2 = F_p . z_k               if p divides k+2,                      (10)

z_k = sum_(i=0)^k (i+1) u_i.                                        (11)
```

*Proof of `(11)`.* `tridiag(1,-2,1)x=0` is the discrete Dirichlet problem
`x_(i-1)-2x_i+x_(i+1)=0` with `x_(-1)=x_(k+1)=0`; its solutions are affine,
`x_i=B(i+1)`, and the right boundary condition is `B(k+2)=0`, which holds in
`F_p` exactly when `p | k+2`.  The matrix is symmetric, so the cokernel is
detected by the same functional `v_i -> i+1`. QED

Three things in `(9)--(11)` are new relative to the source's Lemma 3, which
asserts only that the displayed map is an isomorphism iff `k+2 != 0 mod p`.

1. **The gate is exactly one-dimensional, and it is a genuine `E^2`-term.**
   Lemma A removes the possibility of a larger kernel being cut down or of an
   image spoiling the count.
2. **The obstruction is cyclic.**  Integrally it is `Z/(k+2)`, never a higher
   `p`-rank; this is THM-3204 Theorem 1 applied to a unit sub-diagonal.
3. **The class is explicit, with a nonzero leading term.**  In `z_k` the
   coefficient of `u_k = mu_2 mu_1^(k-1) lambda_1` is `k+1 = -1 mod p`.  The
   source's Proposition 2 needs precisely such a class,
   "`C mu_2 mu_1^(k-3) lambda_1 + ...` with `C != 0 mod p`"; `(11)` supplies it
   canonically instead of assuming it, and pins `C=-1` up to scale.

Finally, `S=k+2` is the index sum of the stratum, so the gate reads in
invariant form:

```text
the excess-one Toda gate fires exactly when p divides the index sum S. (12)
```

**Why parabolic.** The diagonal entry is `a(2,1)=binom(p-2,1)=p-2 = -2`,
carried by the `-2 lambda_1 mu_1` term of `d mu_2`.  Both off-diagonal entries
are `+1`: the lower one is `b(2,1)=-(p-1)=1`, the `mu_1 lambda_1` term of
`d mu_2`; the upper one is the product `(-1)(-1)` of `d mu_1=-lambda_1 mu_0`
with the rewrite `mu_0 mu_2=-mu_1 mu_1`.  The continuant discriminant is
therefore `(-2)^2-4(1)(1)=0`, and in the presentation's own structure
constants `(p-2)^2-4 = p^2-4p = 0 mod p` **identically in `p`**.  The
Adem-type structure constants of `Lambda` place the excess-one continuant on
THM-3204's parabolic locus for every odd prime; the congruence gate
`p | k+2` is the signature of that degeneration, not a coincidence of small
primes.

## 5. The gate does not repeat at higher excess

For `e=2` the source stratum has one index `3` or two indices `2`, and the
target (`q=2`, `e=1`) is strictly larger; the same holds for `e=3,4`.

**FINITE-EXACT.** For `e in {2,3,4}`, `p in {3,5,7,11,13}`, and every
`delta=l-1` from `1` up to the point where the stratum first exceeds `120`
words, the differential

```text
(q=1, excess e) -> (q=2, excess e-1)                                 (13)
```

is **injective**; the `E^2` term vanishes.  Universe, filters and the exact row
counts are printed by the companion, and a positive control confirms that the
same machinery does find the one-dimensional kernel at `e=1`, `p=5`, `k=3`.

So the determinant gate of section 4 is not the first member of a tower.  The
mechanism needs a square stratum, and excess one is the only square one.
Whether `(13)` is injective for all `e>=2`, all `delta` and all `p` is **OPEN**;
the dimension gap is the obvious reason to expect it, not a proof.
