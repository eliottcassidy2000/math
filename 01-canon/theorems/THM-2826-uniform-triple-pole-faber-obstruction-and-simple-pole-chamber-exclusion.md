# THM-2826 -- the triple-pole obstruction is uniform in Faber degree

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: for every normalized reduced Faber degree
`m=4R-2`, `R>=7`, prove that a finite response point with local valuation
pair `(ord V,ord M)=(3,1)` cannot occur in the nonsplit polynomial
exact-square-prefix chart.  Replace THM-2823's degree-twenty-six finite
resultants by a uniform response-remainder formula, a mod-three pure-face
cycle, and THM-2760's all-degree exact-prefix coprimality theorem.

Proposed balanced-response corollary: in the THM-2796 normal form
`V=vSDT^2`, `M=SET`, every simple pole part has local pair `(3,1)`;
therefore every balanced passport containing a simple pole part, and in
particular the chamber `h>N/2`, is excluded in every normalized reduced
degree covered by the theorem.  Balanced passports with all pole parts at
least two and charts outside the inherited exact-square-prefix setting
remain open.

Until a proof, exact companion, and independent hostile audit are installed,
this reservation is not a proved result or proved dependency.
