# THM-3533 -- the newest Keller different is reduced; coordinates add index squares

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT.**

Retain the fixed sporadic Keller map `F`, the degree-`3^n` function-field
tower `K_n/K_0` of THM-3531, and THM-3530's pairwise distinct raw image
primes

```text
P_0=L,             closure(F(V(P_j)))=V(P_(j+1)),
deg(V(P_j)->V(P_(j+1)))=1.                              (1)
```

Put `A=Q[a,b,c]`, and let `B_n` be the integral closure of `A` in `K_n`.
It is finite over `A`.  At a height-one prime `p` of `A`, write

```text
delta_n(p)=v_p(Disc(B_n/A))                             (2)
```

for the discriminant exponent after localizing at the DVR `A_p`.  Equation
`(2)` is the basis-independent codimension-one discriminant divisor of the
normalization.  It is more information than the rational trace-discriminant
square class of THM-3531.

## 1. Exact newest-prime multiplicity

For every `n>=1`, at the newest prime `p_(n-1)=(P_(n-1))`,

```text
delta_n(p_(n-1))=1.                                    (3)
```

Thus the newest component occurs reduced in the normalization discriminant
divisor, not merely with odd parity.

For an element `theta in B_n localized at p_(n-1)` that is primitive for
`K_n/K_0`, let `m_theta` be its monic degree-`3^n` minimal polynomial and put

```text
i_(theta,n)=length_(A_p)(B_(n,p)/A_p[theta]).           (4)
```

Then

```text
v_(P_(n-1))(Disc(m_theta))=1+2 i_(theta,n).             (5)
```

The multiplicity is one exactly when the primitive power order is locally
maximal.  Such locally maximal integral primitive elements exist at every
fixed depth, so the minimum in `(5)` is attained.  Arbitrary primitive
elements can nevertheless have larger odd multiplicity.

## 2. The one-step reciprocal cubic has different exponent one

At the generic point of `V(L)`, the functions `c`,

```text
T=4-3bc,                  S=27ac^2-9bc+8               (6)
```

are units.  THM-2473's inverse cubic in `x` is

```text
Lx^3+Tx-2c=0.                                            (7)
```

The two escaping roots are made integral by `u=1/x`.  Their reciprocal core
is

```text
G(u)=L+Tu^2-2cu^3,
Disc_u(G)=-4L(T^3+27c^2L)=-4LS^2.                     (8)
```

The leading coefficient is a unit, so monic normalization changes the
discriminant only by a unit.  Equation `(8)` has valuation one.  Modulo `L`,

```text
G(u)=u^2(T-2cu),                                       (9)
```

and the two factors are coprime.  Hensel splitting gives one unramified
linear branch and one ramified quadratic branch.  The latter is tame in
characteristic zero.  Equivalently, the order generated by `u` has
discriminant exponent one; the field exponent is positive and differs from
it by twice an index length, forcing index zero and different/discriminant
exponent one.

This ramification belongs to the finite normalization at infinity.  It does
not contradict `det J_F=-2`: the affine polynomial morphism is etale at every
affine source point.

## 3. Exactly one simple branch reaches the newest prime

Let `p=p_(n-1)`.  By THM-3530, `P_(n-1)` is distinct from
`P_0,...,P_(n-2)`.  Its generic point is outside

```text
S_(F^(n-1))=union_(j=0)^(n-2) V(P_j).                  (10)
```

Near that point, `F^(n-1)` is proper.  It is also quasi-finite and etale, so
it is finite etale there.  Consequently `K_(n-1)/K_0` is unramified at `p`.

Iterating THM-3530's degree-one restrictions gives

```text
deg(V(L)->V(P_(n-1)) under F^(n-1))=1.                 (11)
```

Hence among the primes `q` of `B_(n-1)` over `p`, exactly one lies on
`L(q)=0`.  Finite etaleness and `(11)` give

```text
e(q/p)=f(q/p)=1,              v_q(L)=1.                (12)
```

At this unique `q`, Section 2 gives relative discriminant exponent one for
`K_n/K_(n-1)`.  Every other prime over `p` avoids `V(L)`; the one-step map is
finite etale there and contributes zero.  Local discriminant transitivity is

```text
delta_n(p)
 =3 delta_(n-1)(p)
  +sum_(q|p) f(q/p) delta(K_n/K_(n-1);q).               (13)
```

The first term is zero by `(10)`, and `(12)` leaves one summand `1*1`.  This
proves `(3)` at every depth.

At an old prime `P_j`, the preceding cover is already nonproper there and
`L(q)` may have poles on escaping valuations.  Neither term in `(13)` then
vanishes.  THM-3531 controls their parity cancellation, but the present
argument does not supply an all-component positive-multiplicity recursion.

## 4. Primitive-order discriminants and the index square

Let `R=A_p` and let `S_n` be its integral closure in `K_n`.  It is a finite
free `R`-module of rank `N=3^n`.  If `theta` is integral and primitive, then
`R[theta]` is another free rank-`N` lattice.  Trace Gram matrices give

```text
Disc(R[theta]/R)
 =Disc(S_n/R) Index_R(S_n:R[theta])^2.                 (14)
```

Over a DVR, the index valuation is the module length in `(4)`.  Substituting
`(3)` into `(14)` proves `(5)`.

The equality case is nonempty.  The residue field `kappa(p)` is infinite.
After henselization, `S_n/pS_n` is a product of finite separable residue
algebras and one factor `kappa(q)[epsilon]/(epsilon^2)` from the single tame
quadratic branch.  Each factor is monogenic.  Translating their generators
by distinct constants makes their minimal polynomials pairwise coprime, so
the product is monogenic.  Lift a generator to `S_n`; Nakayama gives

```text
S_n=R[theta].                                           (15)
```

Local primitivity implies `K_0(theta)=K_n`, and `(15)` makes the index zero.
This proves attainment of multiplicity one without naming an affine
coordinate.

## 5. Why primitivity alone cannot determine multiplicity

At level one, the reciprocal generator `u` in `(8)` has `L`-discriminant
valuation one.  The element `theta=Lu` is still integral and primitive.  The
power-basis change

```text
(1,u,u^2) -> (1,Lu,L^2u^2)                             (16)
```

has determinant `L^3`, so

```text
v_L(Disc(Lu))=1+2*3=7.                                 (17)
```

More generally, multiplying a degree-`N` primitive element by a base
uniformizer shifts its monic discriminant valuation by `N(N-1)`.  Multiplying
a degree-`N` eliminant polynomial by a scalar shifts it by `2N-2`.  Both are
even shifts here, but neither preserves exact positive multiplicity.  Thus an
unspecified "primitive discriminant" has no unique multiplicity: the integral
order and polynomial normalization are required sidecars.

For a prescribed coordinate one must additionally prove full degree and
separability, an integral or reciprocal chart at the newest valuation, and
local index zero.  The literal `x/y/z` theorems through depth six do not
automatically provide this all-level sidecar.
