# THM-4629 — the twisted transfer law and the near-returns by congruence class

## Setting

`T(n) = n/2` (`n` even), `(3n+1)/2` (`n` odd); `tau(n)` the number of `T`-steps from `n` to `1`, `omega(n)` the number of odd steps among them (`tau + omega` is the number of steps of the Collatz map `C(n) = 3n+1` or `n/2`); `theta = log2 3`; `u = log2 n`. For `zeta = e^(2 pi i/m)` and the character `chi(n) = zeta^(alpha tau(n) + beta omega(n))`, the relations `tau(n) = 1 + tau(Tn)` and `omega(n) = omega(Tn) + [n odd]` give `chi(n) = zeta^alpha chi(Tn)` (`n` even) and `chi(n) = zeta^(alpha+beta) chi(Tn)` (`n` odd). The *class mean* `m^((j,k))_(r,rho)(u)` is the mean of `chi` over the `n` in a log-bin around `2^u` with `n = r (mod 2^j)`, `n = rho (mod 3^k)`; `W_(j,k)` is the space of functions on `Z/2^j x Z/3^k` (the mean functions that depend on `n` only through these digits). The Collatz functional `(-1)^tau` is the case `m = 2`, `(alpha, beta) = (1, 0)`; `(-1)^omega` is `(0, 1)`; `(-1)^(tau+omega)` is `(1, 1)`; the trivial character is HYP-9230's class density.

## Statement

1. **(Exact identities; PROVED.)** For `r` even, halving maps the class `(r, rho)` in the bin at `u` onto the class `(r/2 mod 2^(max(j-1,0)), rho 2^(-1) mod 3^k)` in the bin at `u - 1`; for `r` odd, the odd step maps the class `(r, rho)` onto the class `((3r+1)/2 mod 2^(max(j-1,0)), c~(rho) mod 3^(k+1))` in the bin at `u + theta - 1`, where `c~(rho) = (3 rho' + 1)/2` with `rho'` the odd representative of `rho` modulo `2 3^k`. Hence, up to bin-edge terms,
   `m^((j,k))_(r,rho)(u) = zeta^alpha m^((j-1,k))_(r/2, rho/2)(u-1)` (`r` even), `= zeta^(alpha+beta) m^((j-1,k+1))_((3r+1)/2, c~(rho))(u+theta-1)` (`r` odd),
   with the lumping identities (a class mean is the average of its two 2-adic or three 3-adic lifts). Each step consumes one binary digit and each odd step produces one ternary digit: the hierarchy of exact identities closes at no finite level. (For `j = 0` the class is the whole bin and the two branches enter with weight `1/2` each.) In the 2-adic direction the identities are exact and complete: the class `n = 0 (mod 2^a)` carries `zeta^(alpha a) m(u - a)`, so the mean function depends on every binary digit, and the level-`(j-1)` means are the averages of the level-`j` means.
2. **(Closed models and their spectrum; PROVED.)** Let `M(s)` on `W_(j,k)` be the transfer operator obtained from 1 by reading the `(k+1)`-level mean of the odd branch through its reduction to level `k`. This is the compression `P_(j,k) M_exact P_(j,k)` of the exact operator to the class means modulo `2^j 3^k`: in the 2-adic direction it is exact (the exact identities close on the level-`j` means, since the level-`(j-1)` means are their averages; the exact operator itself raises the 2-adic depth by one, so `W_(j,k)` is not invariant under it, but the level-`j` means never see the discarded part), and its only hypothesis is the 3-adic closure `H_k` that the class means modulo `3^(k+1)` are determined by their reductions modulo `3^k` at the scales considered:
   `(Mv)(r, rho) = zeta^alpha e^(-s) (1/2)[v(r/2, rho/2) + v(r/2 + 2^(j-1), rho/2)]` (`r` even), `= zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1)) (1/2)[v(s_r, c(rho)) + v(s_r + 2^(j-1), c(rho))]` (`r` odd), `s_r = (3r+1)/2 mod 2^(j-1)`, `c(rho) = c~(rho) mod 3^k` (for `j = 0` both branches with weight `1/2`). Then for every `j >= 0`, `k >= 0` and every character,
   `det(I - M(s)) = [1 - (1/2) zeta^alpha e^(-s) - (1/2) zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1))] prod_(i=1)^(k) [1 - ((1/2) zeta^alpha e^(-s))^(2 3^(i-1))]`.
   The first factor is the scalar law; the other factors vanish only at `|e^(-s)| = 2`, i.e. `Re s = -ln 2`, at the frequencies `f = alpha/m - l/(2 3^(i-1)) (mod 1)`. So every finite 3-adic closure of the hierarchy, at any 2-adic depth, has exactly the slow spectrum of the scalar law, and its extra modes decay by the factor `1/2` per octave.
3. **(Resonances of the scalar law; PROVED for the model.)** A mode `e^(su)`, `s = 2 pi i f + w`, solves the scalar law iff `zeta^alpha e^(-s) + zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1)) = 2`. The two terms have modulus `1` at `w = 0`, so a root with `w` near `0` requires both near `1`: `f = Q/m` with `Q = alpha (mod m)` and `Q theta = P + m theta_f` with `P = -beta (mod m)`, `theta_f` small. At such `f` the equation is `e^(-w) + e^(2 pi i theta_f) e^(w(theta-1)) = 2`, HYP-9230's root equation with `theta_f` in place of `theta`, and its root continued from `w(0) = 0` is
   `w = 2 pi i theta_f/(2 - theta) - 4 pi^2 theta_f^2/(2 - theta)^3 + O(theta_f^3)`:
   drift `f_eff - f = theta_f/(2 - theta) + O(theta_f^2)` (`1/(2-theta) = 2.4094`, the half drift of HYP-9258), decay per octave `exp(-4 pi^2 theta_f^2/(2-theta)^3) = exp(-552.2 theta_f^2)` to relative accuracy `2%` for `|theta_f| <= 0.002`, off by the factor `2` at `|theta_f| = 0.02` (the Gaussian failure recorded in HYP-9230's audit A2; `4 pi^2/(2-theta)^3 = 2 pi^2 x 27.97` with `27.97 = 2/(2-theta)^3` the Gaussian variance already in HYP-9230, and the linear drift is in the Codex note, so the expansion is new only as a joint derivation). ## Proof

**1.** For `n = 2m` with `n = r (mod 2^j)`, `n = rho (mod 3^k)`: `m` ranges over all integers with `m = r/2 (mod 2^(j-1))`, `m = rho 2^(-1) (mod 3^k)`; the bin shifts by exactly `-1`. For `n` odd with the same congruences, `n` is determined modulo `2^j 3^k` and modulo `2 3^k` (hence `rho'`), `n = n_0 + 2^j 3^k t`, and `(3n+1)/2 = (3n_0+1)/2 + 3^(k+1) 2^(j-1) t`: the image is one class modulo `2^(j-1) 3^(k+1)`, every element of it in the shifted bin arises once, and `log2((3n+1)/2) = u + theta - 1 + log2(1 + 1/(3n))`. The lumping identities are the equal density of lifts. ∎

**2.** (a) *The compression.* Under `H_k` the odd-branch value `v^((k+1))(c~(rho))` is `v(c(rho))`; the even branch and the 2-adic lumping are exact; so the displayed `M` is the system of identities of 1 closed by `H_k`, i.e. the compression `P_(j,k) M_exact P_(j,k)` (the exact operator raises the 2-adic depth by one on both branches and lowers the 3-adic depth by one on the odd branch, so `W_(j,k)` is not invariant under it; the compression discards only what the level-`j` means never see). (b) *3-adic filtration.* Let `V_i subset W_(j,k)` be the functions of 3-adic depth `<= i` (depending on `rho mod 3^i`). Both branches preserve `V_i`: halving because `rho/2 mod 3^i` depends on `rho mod 3^i`; the odd branch because `c(rho) mod 3^i` depends only on `rho mod 3^(i-1)` (the three lifts of a class modulo `3^(i-1)` have odd representatives differing by `3^(i-1)(a + 3b)`, `a in {0,1,2}`, and `(3n+1)/2` then differs by `3^i(a + 3b)/2 = 0 mod 3^i`). On the graded piece `V_i / V_(i-1)` (`i >= 1`) the odd branch is therefore zero and `M` acts as `zeta^alpha e^(-s) E_j (x) Pbar_i`, where `E_j v(r) = [r even] (1/2)(v(r/2) + v(r/2 + 2^(j-1)))` on functions of `Z/2^j` (`E_0 = 1/2`) and `Pbar_i` is the permutation `rho -> rho/2` on the characters of exact order `3^i`. (c) *The two factors.* Sylvester's identity `det(I - XY) = det(I - YX)` with `E_j = iota rho^* A_j` (`iota` the indicator of even classes, `A_j` the average over the two lifts, `rho^*` the pullback along `r -> r/2`) gives `A_j iota rho^* = E_(j-1)` (the two lifts of an even class have even parity and halve to the two lifts of its half), so `E_j` has the nonzero spectrum of `E_1 = [[1/2, 1/2], [0, 0]]`, namely `{1/2}` once. Since `2` is a primitive root modulo every `3^i`, `Pbar_i` is one cycle of length `phi(3^i) = 2 3^(i-1)` and its eigenvalues are all the roots of unity of that order; hence the graded piece contributes `prod_eta (1 - (1/2) zeta^alpha e^(-s) eta) = 1 - ((1/2) zeta^alpha e^(-s))^(2 3^(i-1))`. (d) *Depth 0.* On `V_0 = W_(j,0)` both branches act, `M = K_j rho_j^* A_j` with `K_j` the diagonal of the two branch weights and `rho_j` the image map; Sylvester again gives `A_j K_j rho_j^* = M_(j-1)` (the two lifts of a class modulo `2^(j-1)` share its parity and are sent to the two lifts of its image), down to `M_1 = [[a', a'], [b', b']]`, `a' = zeta^alpha e^(-s)/2`, `b' = zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1))/2`, whose eigenvalues are `a' + b'` and `0`: the factor `1 - a' - b'`. (e) The determinant over an invariant filtration is the product over the graded pieces. ∎ (The sympy check of all 30 cases is `collatz_parity_mixed_levels_20261013.out`; at `k = 1, 2` the factors are `(1 - a/2)(1 + a/2)` and `(1 -+ a/2)^2 (1 -+ a/2 + a^2/4)` as displayed in `collatz_parity_3adic_hierarchy_20261013.out`.)

**3.** Substituting the mode gives the equation; `|zeta^alpha e^(-s)| = e^(-Re w)` and `|zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1))| = e^(Re w (theta-1))`, so `w -> 0` forces both terms to `1`: `alpha/m - f in Z` and `(alpha+beta)/m + f(theta-1) in Z`, i.e. `f = Q/m` with `Q = alpha (mod m)` and `Q theta = -beta (mod m)`, which is the displayed pair. ## Scope and boundary

- Statement 1 is an exact theorem about the integers; statement 2 is a theorem about the compressions of the exact operator (exact in the 2-adic direction, closed in the 3-adic direction); statement 3 is about the scalar law. The step from the law to the integers is the 3-adic closure (the class means modulo `3^(k+1)` determined by their reductions modulo `3^k` at the scales considered, for every `k`), and statement 2 shows that closing at any finite 3-adic depth cannot prove or modify the law: the remaining content is the 3-adic closure at infinite depth, whose precise form is the Remark below. In the 2-adic direction nothing is open: the identities are exact, and the mean function depends on every binary digit (the class `n = 0 (mod 2^a)` carries `zeta^(alpha a) m(u - a)`, for the Mercator line the global line times `(1.0012 e^(-0.0226 i))^a`). The data agree with the law's frequencies to `0.1%` and with its decays to `0.006` per octave for 24 of the 25 lines of amplitude above `0.02`; the 3-adic class dependence at `2^29` is below `0.7%`.
- *Remark (PROVED; added after the audit was launched and then checked adversarially by the auditor, finding 11 of the note's section 11).* For a continuous solution `v` on `Z_3` of the exact 3-adic mode equation (the infinite-depth 3-adic hierarchy at 2-adic depth `0`: a statement about the model, not about the integers) with martingale differences `d_k = (E_k - E_(k-1)) v`, the eigen-equation `v = M(s) v` is upper triangular, `(I - a' P) d_k = b' d_(k+1) o c` for `k >= 1` (the odd step `c` maps depth-`k` classes onto depth-`(k+1)` classes and preserves zero means, so `d_(k+1) o c` is a depth-`k` difference; halving commutes with the conditional expectations), hence for `Re s > -ln 2`: `||d_k||_inf <= rho(s) ||d_(k+1)||_inf` with `rho(s) = e^(Re s (theta - 1))/(2 - e^(-Re s))`. On `Re s = 0`, `rho = 1` and continuity forces `d_k = 0`: no non-constant continuous mean function carries an undamped mode. For `Re s < 0` the eigenfunction is constant whenever `||d_k|| = o(rho(s)^(-k))`, e.g. for 3-adically Holder mean functions of exponent above `log_3 rho(s)` (`0.00047` at the Mercator line, where `rho = 1.000517`; `0.13` at the `2.455` line). The open boundary of statement 2 is thus the mean functions rougher than this.

## Reading

Every step of the inverse tree adds one to `tau` and the two step sizes `1` and `log2(3/2)` in `log2 n` have an irrational ratio with good rational approximations. A near-return `2^P ~ 3^Q` with `Q` odd and `P` even brings an orbit back to nearly its starting size after an odd number of steps and flips the parity of `tau`; the signed transfer amplifies exactly those returns, the odd-step count hears the returns with `Q` even and `P` odd, and the Collatz-map count those with both odd: the three parities of `(tau, omega)` partition the near-returns of `log2 3`, and the characters modulo `m` refine the partition by congruence class. The deterministic structure of the tree does not change the law: Terras's 2-adic periodicity is exact and reproduced by it (a class modulo `2^a` carries the global line shifted by `a` octaves), and the 3-adic classes are silent at every finite closure, their only contribution being modes decaying by half per octave. The parity of the total stopping time is therefore locked to the Mercator comma (`53` fifths against `84` octaves) at every scale computation can reach, and its ordinary mean, proved to vanish in the limit, is predicted to do so at the rate `N^(-0.0018)`.
