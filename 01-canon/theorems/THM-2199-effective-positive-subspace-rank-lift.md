# THM-2199 -- effective positive-subspace safe floor and rank lift

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Candidate statement under audit: every rational subspace of `Q^13` of
dimension at least two that contains a positive integer row has
`1/14`-safe Haar mass at least `182^(-13)`. A zero-Haar row in that
subspace should then have a relation outside the subspace annihilator of
height at most `78*182^13`, making THM-2196's carry-lock rank ladder
effective.
