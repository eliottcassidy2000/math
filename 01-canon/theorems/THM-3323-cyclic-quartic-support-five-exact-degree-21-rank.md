# THM-3323 -- cyclic-quartic support-five exact degree-21 rank

**PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.**

Retain THM-3321's cyclic-quartic eigenvector

```text
g=A*zbar+B*z^2+C*z*zbar^2+D*z^3*zbar+E*zbar^4
```

and moment forms `M_m=<g^m>` for `m=3,6,...,21`.  Let
`R=Q[A,B,C,D,E]`.  The degree-21 multiplication map is

```text
Phi_21 : direct_sum_(d=3,6,...,21) R_(21-d) -> R_21,
         (h_d) |-> sum_d h_d M_d.                         (1)
```

It has `13972` source rows and `12650` target columns.

## 1. Universal rank ceiling

First retain arbitrary forms of degrees `3,6,9,12,15` in five variables.
Regular sequences form a nonempty open subset of their irreducible parameter
space: `(A^3,B^6,C^9,D^12,E^15)` is one witness.  Rank in a fixed degree is
generically maximal, so every specialization has quotient dimension at least
the complete-intersection coefficient

```text
[t^21] (1-t^3)(1-t^6)(1-t^9)(1-t^12)(1-t^15)/(1-t)^5
 =1705.                                                    (2)
```

Now specialize to the five moment forms and add `M_18`.  Its degree-21
multipliers form `R_3`, of dimension `35`, but `M_3` is a nonzero member of
the kernel because `M_3*M_18` already lies in the ideal generated by the first
five forms.  Thus `M_18` adds rank at most `34`.  The scalar multiple of
`M_21` adds rank at most one.  Consequently

```text
dim_Q (R/(M_3,...,M_21))_21 >=1705-34-1=1670,
rank_Q(Phi_21) <=12650-1670=10980.                         (3)
```

No generic-Hilbert-series assertion for seven forms is used.

## 2. Exact sparse rank and rational lift

