# THM-3842 -- the nonlinear cubic is an eightfold pullback of the rational tower

**PROVISIONAL PROOF CANDIDATE + VERIFIED-EXACT; AWAITING INDEPENDENT HOSTILE
AUDIT.**  Work over an algebraically closed field `k` of characteristic zero.
The theorem identifies an exact common cubic beneath two apparently different
Jacobian-construction lanes.  The identification is a finite base change, not
a target coordinate change; its lost eight-sheet address is load-bearing.

Let

```text
K=k(A,C),                         M=Frac(S),                         (1)
```

where `S/k[A,C]` is the THM-3811 nonlinear cubic algebra and `omega in S` is
its marked root.  Its characteristic polynomial is

```text
f(T)=T^3-CT^2+7A^2T+3A^3-A^2C^2.                                (2)
```

Write `Delta(A,C)` for THM-3811's intrinsic discriminant.  Define

```text
tau=omega-C/3,

p=(C^2-21A^2)/6,
u=(81A^3-27A^2C^2+63A^2C-2C^3)/54.                              (3)
```

Then

```text
tau^3-2p tau+2u=0,                                                (4)

disc_T(f)=A^2 Delta=4(8p^3-27u^2).                               (5)
```

Put

```text
K_0=k(p,u),
L_0=K_0[tau]/(tau^3-2p tau+2u).                                  (6)
```

The polynomial in `(6)` is irreducible and

```text
[K:K_0]=8,
M=K L_0,
[M:K]=[L_0:K_0]=3,
[M:L_0]=8.                                                       (7)
```

Thus `L_0/K_0` is literally the `m=2` THM-3774 cubic tower, and the
THM-3811 cubic is its degree-eight scalar extension.  The square `A^2` in
`(5)` is the marked-root index square; it is not a new ramification
component.

## 1. The trace shift and index square are exact

Substitute `T=tau+C/3` in `(2)`.  The quadratic term vanishes and direct
collection gives

```text
f(tau+C/3)=tau^3+(7A^2-C^2/3)tau
 +3A^3-A^2C^2+(7/3)A^2C-(2/27)C^3,                              (8)
```

which is `(4)` with `(3)`.  The trace shift is unique: the trace of `omega`
is `C`, so `omega-C/3` is the unique translate of the marked root having
trace zero.

For a cubic order, the discriminant of the power basis generated by a
marked element is the square of its binary-index value times the intrinsic
trace discriminant.  In the THM-3811 binary index form,

```text
I(1,0)=-A.                                                       (9)
```

Consequently `disc(f)=A^2 Delta`.  Independently, the standard depressed-
cubic formula applied to `(4)` gives

```text
disc(tau^3-2p tau+2u)=4(8p^3-27u^2),                            (10)
```

proving `(5)`.  This explains exactly why the marked polynomial can look
more singular than the intrinsic cubic extension: `A=0` is an index divisor
of even multiplicity, not an odd discriminant prime.

The coefficient map

```text
Phi:A2_(A,C) --> A2_(p,u)                                      (11)
```

is generically finite.  Its Jacobian is

```text
J(Phi)=A(42A^2C-49A^2-9AC+2C^3)/6,                             (12)
```

so it is neither a symplectic target coordinate change nor an etale cover.

## 2. The coefficient field has exact degree eight

Fix `p` and work over `F=k(p)`.  The first equation in `(3)` presents `K`
as the function field of the smooth projective conic

```text
C^2-21A^2=6p.                                                   (13)
```

It has the two `F`-rational points at infinity

```text
infinity_+=[1:sqrt(21):0],       infinity_-=[1:-sqrt(21):0].    (14)
```

On `(13)`, the second equation in `(3)` is equivalently

```text
54u=-567A^4+81A^3-162pA^2+(21A^2-12p)C.                        (15)
```

The function `A` has a simple pole at each point in `(14)`.  In `(15)` the
term `-567A^4` is the unique order-four term at both points; every other
term has pole order at most three.  Hence

```text
div_infinity(u)=4 infinity_+ +4 infinity_-.                     (16)
```

There are no other poles because `(15)` is regular on the affine conic.
The degree of the rational function `u` on this conic is therefore eight,
and

```text
[F(A,C):F(u)]=8.                                                 (17)
```

This is exactly `[K:K_0]=8`.  As a hostile algebraic control, eliminating
`C` from `(3)` gives a degree-eight polynomial in `A` with leading
coefficient `11907`; `(16)` proves that no eliminant factor was being counted
extraneously.

THM-3811 proves that `(2)` is irreducible over `K`.  Equation `(4)` is
therefore irreducible over `K`, and a fortiori over its smaller coefficient
field `K_0`.  Thus `L_0` in `(6)` is a cubic field, it is linearly disjoint
from `K` over `K_0`, and `(7)` follows.

One immediate target-field consequence is stronger than a symplectic
calculation.  Every birational rational target change of `(p,u)` still
generates exactly `K_0`; it cannot recover `A,C`, whose joint field has index
eight over `K_0`.  The exact matching `(3)` therefore cannot be upgraded to
a target transformation.  A construction that uses the nonlinear packet
must retain an eight-sheet coefficient sidecar.

## 3. Six branch addresses collapse to the tower cusp




## 4. A marked symplectic residue distinguishes the affine packets

There is also an obstruction that does not merely count coefficient fields.
For a symplectic function field with a cubic discriminant squareclass and a
chosen odd discriminant valuation, take the Poincare residue

```text
eta=Res_Gamma(symplectic_form/discriminant).                      (28)
```

Changing the discriminant representative by a square multiplies `eta` by a
square in the branch function field.  Consequently the divisor of `eta`
modulo two on the branch normalization is an invariant of the **marked**
symplectic cubic packet.

For the tower cusp `D_0=8p^3-27u^2`, `(20)` gives

```text
eta_0=Res(dp wedge du/D_0)=-dr/(18r^2),                          (29)
```

whose divisor is `-2[r=0]` and is even.  For the nonlinear packet, exact
substitution of `(18)` gives

```text

 =(3q^2+7)^4 dq /
  (2q^3(q-3)^3(q+1)^3(q+2)^3(3q^2-7)^2).                       (30)
```

Its odd divisor is

```text
[q=0]+[q=3]+[q=-1]+[q=-2],                                     (31)
```

which is nonzero.  Hence no symplectic birational field isomorphism carrying
the tower's distinguished affine discriminant valuation to the nonlinear
affine discriminant valuation can identify the two cubic extensions.  This
is a marked statement: an unclassified birational map that sends the affine
branch to a different boundary valuation is not excluded.

The finite map `Phi` evades the obstruction exactly as it must.  Along the


The multiplier is not a square on the branch; together with the ramification
divisor of `(21)` it supplies exactly the parity change allowed under this
finite pullback.  Thus the degree-six collapse and the non-symplectic
Jacobian are two faces of the same lost branch address.
