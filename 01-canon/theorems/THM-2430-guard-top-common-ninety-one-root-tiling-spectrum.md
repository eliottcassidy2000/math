# THM-2430 -- the blocker-free guard-top stalk has a finite tiling spectrum

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-2427 reduces the deep-`c_3` side to seven valuation types. Six have
`t=5`; in three of those, no low blocker lies in the septimal top
layer:

```text
t=5,                       b=0.                         (1)
```

The thirteen-root capacity estimate alone has one unit of slack:

```text
guard capacity 4 + five ordinary capacities 2 =14.
```

That slack disappears if the septimal and thirteen-root operations are
composed before either is collapsed to a scalar count. The resulting
`91`-point stalk is an exact tiling. Its complete affine type bank is
finite and small enough to enumerate twice.

## 1. The typed `b=0` residual

Retain THM-2427's primitive scalar-cover notation and assume

```text
M=max(nu_7(H),nu_7(q_1),...,nu_7(q_5)),

nu_7(c_3)>M,                    nu_7(H)=M,

t=#{i:nu_7(q_i)=M}=5,

b=#{j<=2:nu_7(c_j)=M}=0.                              (2)
```

Put

```text
N_7=7^(M+1),

u_0=H/7^M,                     u_i=q_i/7^M.             (3)
```

Every `u_i`, including `u_0`, is a unit modulo `91`: division removes
the exact top septimal power, while all six original speeds are
thirteen-units.

THM-2427 has

```text
W=2+t+b=7>k.
```

Therefore its seven-bin inequality proves more than scalar coverage:
on every generic inherited `N_7`-orbit, the top words alone cover every
point. Because `b=0`, those top words are exactly

```text
E_H, D_(q_1),...,D_(q_5).                              (4)
```

No top blocker occurs in (4). Low blockers may still be present, but
they are not needed for this top-word cover.

## 2. Compose the two root operations

Choose the generic parent `y` from THM-2427 Section 3, so in particular
the thirteen inverse roots

```text
x_h=(y+h)/13,                         h in F_13,          (5)
```

avoid every relevant endpoint and inherited null set. For
`r in F_7`, retain the seven top-bin representatives

```text
x_(h,r)=(y+h)/13+r/N_7.                                  (6)
```

Multiplication by `7^M` gives

```text
z_(h,r)
 =7^M(y+h)/13+r/7.                                      (7)
```

The differences generated by `7^M/13` and `1/7` form the whole
`91`-torsion group: the first is a unit direction modulo thirteen and
zero modulo seven, while the second is zero modulo thirteen and a unit
direction modulo seven. Hence

```text
{z_(h,r):h in F_13,r in F_7}

is one translate of (1/91)Z/Z.                           (8)
```

Equations (3), (6), and (7) identify the top masks exactly:

```text
1_(E_H)(x_(h,r))=1_(E_(u_0))(z_(h,r)),

1_(D_(q_i))(x_(h,r))=1_(D_(u_i))(z_(h,r)).               (9)
```

Thus (4) covers all `91` points in (8).

For a `91`-unit speed, a strict guard arc of length `2/7` meets a
generic `91`-grid in exactly `26` points. A strict ordinary arc of
length `1/7` meets it in exactly `13` points. Consequently the total
incidence in (9) is

```text
26+5*13=91.                                               (10)
```

Coverage plus (10) forces an exact one-fold partition:

```text
E_(u_0) disjoint union D_(u_1) disjoint union ... disjoint
union D_(u_5)

= the full common 91-root stalk.                           (11)
```

At positive `M`, (8) is the `T_(7^M)` quotient stalk. The operation
does not preserve the labelled valuation-zero low blocker, the owner,
or the unscaled base address.

ordinary word has length `13`.



## 4. The thirteen-root slack vanishes

On a generic fixed thirteen-root slice, let

```text
g in {3,4}
```

be the guard root count and let `s` be the number of ordinary masks
having one rather than two roots. Before using (11), coverage permits
exactly

```text
(g,s,excess)=(3,0,0), (4,1,0), or (4,0,1),                 (20)
```

because the total incidence is

```text
g+10-s=13+excess.                                           (21)
```

The full `91`-tiling (11) is disjoint on every slice, so the
excess-one alternative is impossible when `b=0`. Thus every slice has
one of the exact types

```text
(g,s,excess)=(3,0,0) or (4,1,0).                            (22)
```

Only the `r=0` slice inherited from (5) is guaranteed to be physically
blocker-free; (22) on the other six slices is a statement about the
top-word tiling, not about absence of low blockers.

Both alternatives in (22) are sharp at one common physical phase.
At `y=1/2`,

```text
H=1, (q_i)=(2,3,5,11,19)
```

gives guard size `4`, one singleton ordinary mask, and an exact
partition. Also at `y=1/2`,

```text
H=2, (q_i)=(3,5,9,11,19)
```

gives guard size `3`, five two-root ordinary masks, and an exact
partition.

```text

```


## 7. Frozen tilings have distinct endpoint evolutions

For integer lifts `u=(u_0,...,u_5)`, define the labelled root word

```text

 ={z in Z/91Z:
   ||u_i(theta+z)/91||<w_i/14},

w_0=2,                     w_i=1 for i>0.             (25)
```

Its labelled endpoint events occur at

```text

 =91(n +/- w_i/14)/u_i-z                  modulo 91,  (26)
```

with the label, site, side, and entry/exit sign retained. The word is
constant between consecutive events, so one initial labelled word and
the ordered signed schedule (26) reconstruct its base evolution. The
affine atlas forgets the integer lifts and hence forgets (26).

`91`. At



```text

 =83(11/83+z)
```

The two packets therefore have the same labelled support word and the

next wall.

## 8. Consequence and scope

For every THM-2427 residual satisfying `t=5,b=0`, the continuous-looking
root-capacity problem is now the finite `34`-type bank (17), with the
ratio restriction (19) and exact slice law (22).

This does not remove a valuation type or any of the `165` scalar
profiles. Indeed every finite root type is locally physical, and
THM-2427 supplies a positive-chamber full-stalk hostile. A successful
continuation must use data discarded by the affine tiling:

```text
cross-base translate flow,
owner and low-blocker state,
the labelled valuation-zero speed,
or a same-shell endpoint/reference phase.                     (32)
```

THM-2421's endpoint-event word and THM-2420's reference mechanism are
possible consumers of the finite bank, but neither is automatically
present on this quotient stalk. There is no ledger decrement and no
proof of LRC(14).
