# THM-2432 -- reserved guard-top pair-cage exclusion

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

This identifier is reserved for auditing two proposed exclusions in the
THM-2427 residual list: the positive-depth types `(2,0,0,2)` and
`(2,5,0,7)`. The intended mechanism first forces, respectively, an exact
lower one-fold partition or an exact top one-fold partition, then turns
pairwise collision into containment under the deep blocker and applies an
adapted two-source comb-escape lemma.

No theorem, dependency, residual deletion, row decrement, or LRC(14)
conclusion is claimed by this stub.
