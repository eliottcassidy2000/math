# THM-2093 -- the dyadic global star is finite

Retain Branch II of THM-2090. Thus a hypothetical depth-four obstruction has

```text
S={2h_0,4h_1,8h_2,H,V_1,...,V_7,x,y},
H=16h,                 V_i=32q_i,                       (1)
```

where `h_0,h_1,h_2,h,x,y` are odd, `Q={q_1,...,q_7}` is primitive, and one
terminal speed `q=q_*` makes `(H,V_*)` an independent pair of coefficient
columns. Put

```text
Q_0=91^6=567869252041.                                  (2)
```

For every other coordinate `S_k`, THM-2090 supplies a primitive relation

```text
A_k H+B_k V_*+C_k S_k=0,
C_k!=0,       max(|A_k|,|B_k|,|C_k|)<=Q_0.              (3)
```

Dividing the relation originally supplied by THM-2090 by its coefficient
gcd makes it primitive without increasing its height.

## 1. The exact valued deletion flag

Let `nu_2` denote the two-adic valuation. The displayed coordinates in (1)
have the exact initial profile

```text
nu_2(x)=nu_2(y)=0,
nu_2(2h_0)=1,  nu_2(4h_1)=2,  nu_2(8h_2)=3,
nu_2(H)=4,     nu_2(V_i)>=5.                            (4)
```

Consequently, if `d` is the primitive parameter covector of the saturated
two-plane and `c_(2^k)(d)` is its coordinate-evaluation word modulo `2^k`,
then for `1<=k<=5`

```text
supp c_2(d)    ={x,y},
supp c_4(d)    ={x,y,2h_0},
supp c_8(d)    ={x,y,2h_0,4h_1},
supp c_16(d)   ={x,y,2h_0,4h_1,8h_2},
supp c_32(d)   ={x,y,2h_0,4h_1,8h_2,H}.                (5)
```

Deleting the displayed support in the `2^k` line leaves a row whose gcd has
two-adic valuation exactly `k`. For `k<5`, the next guard supplies a retained
coordinate of exact valuation `k`; for `k=5`, the retained row is `32Q` and
`gcd(Q)=1`.

At the prime two, the first line of (5) is a weight-two word in THM-2069's
rank-two evaluation code. Hence

```text
d_min(C_2)<=2,
B_(2,2)>0,                                               (6)
```

and the binary coefficient matroid is not cosimple. This is the precise
code-cogirth form of the original two-tail seam. It is not merely a parity
analogy: deleting exactly `{x,y}` makes the remaining specialized row even.
The higher lines of (5) are a lift of that cocircuit shell over
`Z/2^k Z`, information which the binary code alone forgets.

## 2. Forced private-coefficient staircase

Take one of the five outer coordinates

```text
S_k=2^t u,       u odd,       0<=t<=3,                  (7)
```

where the two tails have `t=0` and the three earlier guards have
`t=1,2,3`. Put

```text
g=gcd(h,q).                                              (8)
```

Since `h` is odd, `g` is odd and

```text
gcd(H,V_*)=gcd(16h,32q)=16g.                            (9)
```

Equation (3) implies `16g|C_k 2^t u`. Therefore

```text
2^(4-t) * g/gcd(g,u) divides C_k.                       (10)
```

In particular, write

```text
C_k=2^(4-t)c_k.
```

Then (3) has the normalized odd-coordinate form

```text
A_k h+2B_k q+c_k u=0,
g/gcd(g,u) divides c_k,
A_k == c_k (mod 2),                                    (11)
```

and

```text
coordinate      forced divisor of C_k     bound on |c_k|
x,y              16                        floor(Q_0/16)
2h_0              8                        floor(Q_0/8)
4h_1              4                        floor(Q_0/4)
8h_2              2                        floor(Q_0/2). (12)
```

Thus the five private pivots pay a combined factor `2^14`.

There is also an intrinsic arithmetic-matroid reading. In a saturated
coefficient lattice `L`, let `L_0` be generated by the two independent
anchor columns. The primitive relation (3) generates the integer kernel of
the three columns, so

```text
|C_k|=order([rho_k] in L/L_0).                          (13)
```

Hence (10) is an order statement in the anchor quotient, and the two odd
tails force `16|[L:L_0]`. A coefficient-height atlas which forgets the
divisibility in (10) overcounts every outer fiber.

## 3. The cut tree bounds the common anchor gcd

Root the six-edge spanning tree from THM-2087/2090 at `q=q_*`. Every oriented
edge from a parent `q_u` to a child `q_v` carries

```text
a_e h+b_e q_u+c_e q_v=0,
b_e c_e!=0,       max(|a_e|,|b_e|,|c_e|)<=57.           (14)
```

For a prime `p`, put `e_p=nu_p(g)`. Along a root-to-vertex path, (14) gives

```text
nu_p(q_v)>=e_p-sum_(edges on the path) nu_p(c_e),        (15)
```

where at each edge `c_e` denotes the coefficient of the newly reached child
(interchanging `b_e,c_e` when the stored orientation is reversed). Indeed,
if `h` and the parent are divisible by `p^E`, then the other side of (14) is
divisible by `p^E`, so the child is divisible by `p^(E-nu_p(c_e))`.

Because `gcd(Q)=1`, for every prime `p` some terminal speed has valuation
zero. A tree path has at most six edges, so (15) yields

```text
e_p<=6 floor(log_p 57).                                 (16)
```

In particular no prime greater than `57` divides `g`, and, with

```text
Lambda_57=lcm(1,2,...,57)=164249358725037825439200,
```

one has the uniform bound

```text
g divides Lambda_57^6,
gcd(H,V_*)=16g<=16 Lambda_57^6.                         (17)
```

The sharper template-specific exponent is the maximum child-coefficient
valuation cost of a root path in (15); (16) is the coefficient-free envelope.

## 4. Clearing the star denominators

Let

```text
D_0=gcd(H,V_*)=16g,
H=D_0 a,       V_*=D_0 b,       gcd(a,b)=1,
L=lcm_(k notin {H,V_*}) |C_k|.                          (18)
```

For every nonanchor coordinate, (3) gives `D_0|C_k S_k`, hence
`D_0|L S_k`. The same divisibility is immediate for the anchors. A
hypothetical counterexample is primitive, so Bezout over all thirteen
coordinates gives

```text
D_0 divides L.                                          (19)
```

Put `R=(L/D_0)S`. This is a common integer multiple of `S`, so `M(R)=M(S)`.
It has the integral two-coordinate presentation

```text
R=a u+b z,                                               (20)
```

whose coefficient columns are

```text
(u_H,z_H)=(L,0),       (u_V,z_V)=(0,L),
(u_k,z_k)=(-A_k L/C_k,-B_k L/C_k).                      (21)
```

Consequently

```text
L_*=max_k sqrt(u_k^2+z_k^2)
   <=sqrt(2) Q_0 L
   <=sqrt(2) Q_0^12,                                    (22)
```

because there are eleven private coefficients and
`L<=product |C_k|<=Q_0^11`.

We use the following presentation-invariant form of THM-2053. If integer
vectors `u,z` span a positive rational two-plane and `(a,b)` is primitive,
then

```text
sqrt(a^2+b^2)>=91 max_i sqrt(u_i^2+z_i^2)
   implies M(a u+b z)>=1/14.                            (23)
```

For completeness, (23) does not require `(u,z)` to be a saturated basis.
The column lattice has a primitive positive evaluation after dividing the
coordinate gcd. Adjacent normalized columns expose a full-support repeated
projection exactly as in THM-2053, giving two-torus margin at least `1/13`.
The primitive geodesic `(at,bt)` lies within normal distance
`1/(2sqrt(a^2+b^2))`, so the loss is at most
`L_*/(2sqrt(a^2+b^2))<=1/182`. This proves (23).

Since `S` is assumed to be a strict obstruction, (22)--(23) force

```text
sqrt(a^2+b^2)<91 sqrt(2) Q_0^12.                       (24)
```

## 5. Uniform finite terminal for the no-pair branch

Equations (17), (18), and (24) bound both anchors. For a nonanchor,
equation (3) gives

```text
S_k<=Q_0(H+V_*)
    <=Q_0 sqrt(2) D_0 sqrt(a^2+b^2)
    <182 D_0 Q_0^13.
```

Using `D_0<=16 Lambda_57^6`, every coordinate satisfies the single uniform
bound

```text
max(S)<2912 Lambda_57^6 Q_0^13
      =2912*lcm(1,...,57)^6*(91^6)^13.                  (25)
```

The right side is a 296-digit integer. The symbolic form in (25) is the
useful certificate: it depends only on the already proved relation heights,
not on a hidden template enumeration.

Thus THM-2090's rank-eleven, restriction-rank-two, no-pair global-star branch
is no longer potentially unbounded. Together with THM-2092, every no-pair
branch of the rank-seven terminal pipeline is contained in an explicit finite
box. What remains includes the separate THM-2087 bounded guard-ratio branch
and the finite discharge itself. Finiteness is a reduction, not an LRC(14)
proof.

## 6. Assumption challenge and Tournament Analysis

The challenged assumption is that the useful residue object is only the
binary evaluation code. Its support `{x,y}` correctly finds the first fragile
deletion, but it destroys the four higher valuation layers in (5). Conversely,
raw coefficient height forgets the forced private divisors in (10). The
faithful carrier is the nested valued support flag together with the marked
anchor pair and cut-tree coefficients.

Candidate tournament vertices were runners, the five valuation layers,
private relations, deletion obligations, and tree paths. The only honest
binary order is containment of the five supports in (5). It is transitive,
with score histogram `(0,1,2,3,4)`, no directed cycle, singleton SCCs, and
one Hamiltonian path. That fingerprint records depth but destroys the
private-coefficient orders, root-path valuation costs, and owner addresses.
Tournament Analysis is therefore a scheduling sidecar here; the valued
arithmetic matroid is the proof carrier. QED.
