# THM-2120 -- the Fourier-neutral wall and vanishing scalar-filter tree margin

This refines THM-2099 rather than superseding it. THM-2099 supplies the exact
finite-fiber spectrum and already disproves a universal strict tree gate with
a dyadic family below budget. The new content here is the global Fourier
formula, its exact mod-seven neutrality law, a complete equality graph, and a
vanishing-margin family that passes two scalar terminal filters but is caught
by the newer clock and content sidecars.

Let `g,f,f' in Z^2` be integer characters on `T^2`, with `g` rationally
independent of each terminal character, and put

```text
w(g;f,f')=measure{X:
 ||f.X||<1/14, ||f'.X||<1/14, ||g.X||>1/7}.            (1)
```

## 1. Independent terminal characters

Assume `f,f'` are independent. Divide the determinant relation by the gcd of
its coefficients and choose its sign uniquely as

```text
A g+B f+C f'=0,        A>0, BC!=0, gcd(A,B,C)=1.       (2)
```

The weight depends only on `(A,|B|,|C|)`, not on the index of the lattice
generated by the original rows.

For `x,y in [-1,1]`, define

```text
N_A(u)=#{0<=j<A:||(j-u/14)/A||>1/7}.                  (3)
```

Then

```text
w(A,B,C)=1/(196A) integral_[-1,1]^2 N_A(Bx+Cy) dxdy.  (4)
```

Indeed, the image of `X -> (g.X,f.X,f'.X)` is the connected two-torus cut
out by primitive relation (2). Projection to `(f,f')` is surjective, and its
guard fiber consists of the `A` equally weighted values

```text
g=(-B(f.X)-C(f'.X)+j)/A,       0<=j<A.                 (5)
```

If the raw determinants have common gcd `d_0`, each value in (5) occurs
`d_0` times in the original finite fiber; the multiplicity cancels after
normalization. This proves (4) in nonsaturated cases as well.

Put `P=|B|`, `Q=|C|`, `[z]_+=max(z,0)`, and

```text
F_(P,Q)(t)=1/(2PQ)[
 (t+P+Q)_+^2-(t-P+Q)_+^2
 -(t+P-Q)_+^2+(t-P-Q)_+^2].                           (6)
```

This is the area of `{(x,y) in [-1,1]^2:Px+Qy<=t}`. Summing the guard strips
in (4) gives the finite rational formula

```text
w(A,B,C)=1/49-1/(196A) sum_(n in Z)
 [F_(P,Q)(14n+2A)-F_(P,Q)(14n-2A)].                   (7)
```

Only finitely many summands meet `[-P-Q,P+Q]`.

Haar orthogonality on the relation torus also gives the absolutely convergent
Fourier form

```text
w(A,B,C)
 =5/343-sum_(m!=0)
   [sin(2 pi mA/7)/(pi mA)]
   [sin(pi mB/7)/(pi mB)]
   [sin(pi mC/7)/(pi mC)].                            (8)
```

Every term is `O(|m|^-3)`. In particular,

```text
7|ABC  implies  w(A,B,C)=5/343,                       (9)
```

where `7|ABC` means at least one coefficient is divisible by seven. The zero
and first positive layers are

```text
w(1,1,1)=0,              w(1,1,2)=1/392.              (10)
```

The first identity is THM-2097's pair-rigidity boundary.

THM-2099's unwrapped specialization (9) is valid only before wraparound. For
the root indexed by `j`, the correct general outside condition is

```text
dist(Bx+Cy-14j,14A Z)>2A.                             (11)
```

Formula (4) averages (11) over `j=0,...,A-1`; overlapping strips therefore
count distinct guard roots rather than forming one union. For example
`(A,B,C)=(1,13,1)` has positive-area strips near `13x+y=14` that the
unwrapped formula misses.

## 2. Dependent terminal characters

If `f=a u`, `f'=b u` for primitive `u`, positive specialization permits
`a,b>0`, and transversality makes `(u,g)` surjective. Thus the terminal phase
and guard phase are independent, and

```text
w(g;f,f')=(5/7)rho(a,b)>0,                            (12)
```

where `rho(a,b)=measure(D_a intersect D_b)`. Hence a dependent transverse
pair never lies in THM-2098's zero graph.

## 3. Exact rank-eight equality family

Take

```text
g=(0,1),             c_i=(1,7i),       0<=i<=7.        (13)
```

The primitive direction `d=(2,1)` specializes them to

```text
h=1,
q_i=2+7i=(2,9,16,23,30,37,44,51),                    (14)
```

which are positive and distinct. For `i<j`,

```text
7(j-i)g+c_i-c_j=0                                     (15)
```

is primitive. Its guard coefficient is divisible by seven, so (9) gives

```text
w_ij=5/343                                             (16)
```

on every edge. Every spanning tree has seven edges and therefore

```text
tau=7(5/343)=5/49.                                    (17)
```

This is an exact equality witness at the universal strict target
`tau>5/49`, with a complete positive graph rather than a numerical artifact.
THM-2099's dyadic family already falls below the same target; the present
family identifies a symmetric neutral wall. The family `(1,7Mi)` gives
infinitely many such templates.

It is not an LRC counterexample: near the guard boundary all eight terminal
phases align, and moving into the guard complement leaves a mixed safe point.
That fact is precisely what pair averages forget. THM-2114 now excludes the
same equality row twice: its mod-seven finite-ring needle is `(1,1)`, and its
all-maximum-bases gate sees that every spanning tree is maximum. For the
latter, the exact point

```text
(x,y)=(3/50,47/175)
```

lies in the guard complement and has active terminal set exactly `{0,1}`;
this is disconnected in some maximum tree, contradicting equality under a
hypothetical cover. The neutral row is therefore a sharp pair certificate
boundary, not a residual geometric obstruction.

## 4. No uniform margin off the neutral locus

For

```text
g=(0,1),         c_i=(1,Ni),         0<=i<=7,          (18)
```

write `w_A=w(A,1,-1)`. If `A=7m+r`, direct root counting in (4) gives

```text
w_A=5/343                              if r=0,
w_A=5/343+(2r-7)/(343A)                if 1<=r<=6.     (19)
```

When `N=1 mod 7`, Kruskal's algorithm chooses two distance-six, three
distance-five, and two distance-four edges, giving

```text
tau_N=5/49+17/(1470N) -> 5/49.                        (20)
```

Thus deleting exact neutrality still leaves no uniform positive pair margin.

This obstruction survives two scalar terminal filters that were previously
used as arithmetic sidecars. Put

```text
L=lcm(2,...,14)=360360,       N=1+kL,
g=(0,1),                      c_i=(1,Ni),
d=(7,1).                                             (21)
```

The specialization is

```text
h=1,       Q_k={7,7+N,...,7+7N}.                     (22)
```

For every `2<=m<=14`, `N=1 mod m`, so `Q_k mod m` is represented by the
consecutive block `{7,...,14}` and contains a multiple of `m`. Thus `Q_k` is
divisor-complete through fourteen. Moreover `gcd(7,N)=1`. After deleting any
one of the eight terms, an adjacent indexed pair remains, and the gcd of that
pair is `gcd(7+iN,N)=gcd(7,N)=1`. Every one-deletion is therefore primitive.

The pair relation at distance `j-i` has coefficients
`(A,B,C)=(N(j-i),1,-1)`. Formula (20) consequently gives

```text
tau(Q_k)-5/49=17/[1470(1+kL)] -> 0.                  (23)
```

There is no uniform positive tree gap even on the scalar divisor-complete,
one-deletion-primitive, odd-guard locus. This is not a live-cover locus: all
coefficient characters are primitive, contrary to THM-2114's necessary
rank-at-most-twelve 13-content blocker. The construction proves only that
these scalar filters cannot create a coefficient-free pair margin.

In the quotient presentation of THM-2104, take `p=g=(0,1)` and `q=(1,0)`.
Every `c_i=Ni p+q` has quotient coefficient one, so its `2`-, `3`-, and
`5`-adic valuations are constant. THM-2104 independently supplies a small-
prime clock escape for the whole family, and THM-2105's third-clock carrier
test also fails. Thus (23) is a hostile control for the **pair certificate**,
not a difficult cover family: the clock and content sidecars see exactly what
the vanishing graph margin does not.

## 5. Failure anatomy and next carrier

At the guard-boundary phase `g.X=1/7`, all characters in (13) have the same
terminal phase. A simpler strict escape is the THM-2104 third-clock point. For
`c_i=(1,7Mi)`, take

```text
X=(x,y)=(1/6,1/2).                                    (24)
```

Then `||g.X||=1/2`, while

```text
c_i.X=1/6+7Mi/2 in {1/6,2/3} mod 1,
```

so every terminal distance is at least `1/6>1/14`. Thus the equality family
has an explicit mixed safe point; it is not a cover or an LRC counterexample.
The pair spectrum integrates the common mod-seven fiber away. A lawful
rank-eight continuation must retain at least one of

```text
the common mod-seven character coset,
the full center configuration on each guard fiber,
higher small-clock/quotient-valuation data,
higher intersection/multiplicity data,
or equality conditions in Hunter's forest inequality.                 (25)
```

The weighted graph remains useful, but every edge ties in (16); any
tournament tie-break is cosmetic and destroys the alignment responsible for
the safe cell.
