# THM-4889 — the Hamiltonian-path spectrum by order and by value, on the levels of the metagraph

Notation as in the packet: `S(n)`, `Ss(n)` (strong), `N(n) = S(n) − S(n−1)`, `max H(n)` (A038375), `f(n)` (Busch), `n(h)`, `m(h) = min{n : max H(n) ≥ h}`, `B(h) = max{n : f(n) ≤ h}`, `W(h)`, `Λ(m)` = the greatest number of cyclic triangles of an `m`-tournament, `φ_n(k)` / `ψ_n(k)` = least / greatest count at level `k` of order `n`, `φ(k) = min_n φ_n(k)`.

**(1) The spectra.** `|S(n)|` = 2, 3, 7, 19, 77, 320, 1,520, 7,663, 43,911 for `n = 3..11`; `|Ss(n)|` = 1, 1, 4, 13, 67, 297, 1,482, 7,590, 43,775; `|N(n)|` = 1, 1, 4, 12, 58, 243, 1,200, 6,143, 36,248. The odd values below `max H(n)` not attained are 7, 21 and: 35, 39 (order 6); 63, 107, 119, 149, 161–169, 173, 177–187 (order 7); 611, 615, 617, 619, 623, 625, 635, 647, 655 (order 8); 157 values in `[2883, 3355]` (order 9); 208 values in `[14685, 15743]` (order 10); 3,635 values in `[80453, 95093]` (order 11). The solid prefix is 0.733, 0.323, 0.921, 0.858, 0.933, 0.846 of the maximum at orders 6–11 (HYP-9452 (1)'s constant 0.85 holds at 8–10 and fails at 11). The classes above the first gap lie at levels `≥ Λ(n) − 1` (orders 8, 10), `≥ Λ(n) − 2` (order 9) or `≥ Λ(n) − 3` (order 11).

**(2) Least orders.** For the 43,911 values with `n(h) ≤ 11`: `n(h) = m(h)` for all but 392, which are exactly the 2 + 16 + 9 + 157 + 208 top-band values of orders 6–10, and for them `n(h) = m(h) + 1`. `N(n) ⊆ Ss(n)` at every order (43,910 values), and `n_s(h) = n(h)` (least strong order = least order) for every `h ∈ S(11)`.

**(3) Strong windows.** For odd `h < 225` (complete from orders ≤ 10, since `f(11) = 225`): 109 values, all odd numbers in `[3, 223]` but 7 and 21; every `W(h)` is an interval of length 1–4 (14, 27, 58, 10 values); `e(h) = B(h)` for 90 values and `B(h) − 1` for 47, 55, 67, 77, 79, 83, 127, 129, 131, 133, 137, 139, 141, 143, 149, 151, 157, 163, 173. For odd `h < 375` (complete from orders ≤ 11): 184 values, all intervals (lengths 14, 27, 84, 59), `e(h) = B(h)` for 139 and `B(h) − 1` for 45.

**(4) Level floors.** *Lemma.* Cyclic triangles lie inside strong components and `h` is multiplicative over them; a strong component with `k_i` cyclic triangles has order `≤ k_i + 2` (Moon). Hence `φ(k) = min(φ_s(k), min_{k = Σ k_i} Π φ_s(k_i))`, `φ_s` the strong floor by level, attained at order `≤ k + 2`. ∎ From the census: `φ(0..9) = 1, 3, 5, 9, 11, 15, 23, 33, 41, 51` (exact), all attained by strong tournaments; `φ(10..17) ≤ 69, 97, 107, 141, 171, 213, 227, 321` (the census minima, unchanged at orders 9–11; `φ_11(k) = φ_10(k)` for all `k ≤ 21`). At every order `≤ 11`, `φ_n(k)` is nondecreasing in `k`; `ψ_n(k)` is not monotone from order 8 on. The floor falls with `n` at fixed `k` and can fall late (level 20: 621 at orders 8, 9; 467 at order 10; levels 22–29 fell again at order 11).

**(5) Zone law and top-level separation.** `φ_n(Λ(n−1) + 1) > max H(n−1)` with `(φ, max H)` = (9, 5), (23, 15), (51, 45), (263, 189), (891, 661), (4923, 3357), (20479, 15745) at `n = 5..11`; consequently every value of `S(n−1)` occurs at order `n` only at levels `≤ Λ(n−1)`, and level `Λ(n−1)` always carries one (the padded extremal `(n−1)`-tournament). `φ_n(Λ(n)) > ψ_n(Λ(n) − 1)`: (41, 37), (171, 159), (621, 613), (3159, 3109), (14877, 14733), (91395, 89357) at `n = 6..11` — at order 11 every one of the 1,223 regular tournaments has more Hamiltonian paths than every non-regular 11-tournament.

**(6) Gradients.** Over all (class, arc) flips of orders 6–9 (840; 9,576; 192,640; 6,895,296): a flip with `Δc3 > 0` has `Δh > 0` in 98.7, 97.6, 98.0, 98.2 % of cases; `max |Δh|/h` = `2^{n−2}` at level 0 (the extreme-arc reversal, THM-4749 (4)) and 0.44, 0.16, 0.27, 0.09 at the top level; `h`-preserving flips are 12.6, 6.8, 3.1, 1.2 % of all. Over all classes and vertex deletions: `1 ≤ h(T)/max_v h(T − v) ≤ 4.09, 4.27, 4.38, 5.35` (orders 6–9; the identity `h(T) = Σ_v #(paths ending at v) ≤ Σ_v h(T − v)` gives the bound `n`), and the least ratio among classes with a value new at the order is 1.727, 1.690, 1.646, 1.681.

**What is new.** The exact order-10 and order-11 spectra (the repository had samples), the level resolution, the least-order rule with the band exception made exact, the strong-window interval law, the level floor `φ(k)` as an extremal function and its exact initial segment, the zone law and top-level separation, the sign law of the flip gradient and the deletion-gradient bounds. Known and used, not claimed: Rédei, Moon (cyclic triangles; deletion), Busch, THM-1370 (padding, multiplicativity, insertion), THM-4744 (chains, inventories), THM-4861 (least orders of the Fermat primes), A038375.
