# THM-3980 -- the canonical split cusp atlas cannot algebraize on a domain

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT / AWAITING INDEPENDENT HOSTILE AUDIT.** Work over
an algebraically closed field `k` of characteristic zero and fix

```text
n>=2,                         6L^2=1.                         (1)
```

Inside `k[x,t]`, put

```text
z=1+x^n t,          p=zt,          y=x^(n-1)zt^2,
B_n=k[x,z,p,y],      X_n=Spec(B_n),      K=Frac(B_n).          (2)
```

As in THM-3973,

```text
D=V(x,z,p) isomorphic to A1_y,
L_1=V(x,z-1,y) isomorphic to A1_p                              (3)
```

are the two disjoint components of `V(x)`; `D` is the omitted boundary and
`L_1` lies in the retained source plane. In the two completed factors there
are exact Darboux pairs

```text
(A_D,C_D) on Dhat,                    (A_L,C_L) on Lhat        (4)
```

with boundary values `(y^2,y^3)` and `(p,0)`. They glue to an exact pair

```text
(Ahat,Chat) in Bhat_n=Bhat_D times Bhat_L,
dAhat wedge dChat=dx wedge dt.                                (5)
```

The conclusion is a precise local--global boundary:

```text
Ahat,Chat are not in the diagonal image of K,                 (6)
```

and neither can be realized by an element of any **integral** `K`-algebra
that embeds compatibly in the two completed fraction fields. Nevertheless
they are algebraic over `K` in the reducible algebra `K times K`. Thus
“nonalgebraization” here means no algebraization on a connected integral
model; it does not mean transcendence in the product completion.

## 1. Uniform completed charts

The height-`n` determinantal relations are

```text
z(z-1)=x^n p,            p(z-1)=xy,
zy=x^(n-1)p^2.                                                (7)
```

They imply

```text
z(z-1)^2=x^(n+1)y,                                       (8)
B_n/(x)=k[y] times k[p].                                  (9)
```

The first factor has `z=0,p=0`; the second has `z=1,y=0`.
Consequently the `x`-adic completion splits:

```text
Bhat_n=Bhat_D times Bhat_L.                                (10)
```

At the generic point of either component, `x` is a uniformizer. The maps

```text
K -> Frac(Bhat_D),                 K -> Frac(Bhat_L)       (11)
```

are injective: they factor through the corresponding DVR and its separated
completion. This elementary injectivity will make the pole comparison below
global rather than merely formal.

## 2. The boundary color has a rational exact cusp coordinate

In the `D` factor, `z-1` is a unit. Define

```text
q_D=z/x^(n+1)=y/(z-1)^2 in Bhat_D.                         (12)
```

Since `t=(z-1)/x^n` in `K`, one has the exact identity

```text
eta=dx wedge dt=x^(-n) dx wedge dz
   =x dx wedge d q_D.                                      (13)
```

Now set

```text
A_D=q_D^2+2Lx,
C_D=q_D^3+3Lx q_D.                                        (14)
```

In the coordinates `(x,q_D)`,

```text
det partial(A_D,C_D)/partial(x,q_D)=6L^2x=x.              (15)
```

Equations `(13)--(15)` prove

```text
dA_D wedge dC_D=eta,             (A_D,C_D)|D=(y^2,y^3).   (16)
```

This is a rational normal form in `K`, but it is regular only on the
boundary completion. Since `z` is a unit on `L_1`, its exact pole ledger is

```text
ord_L(q_D)=-(n+1),
ord_L(A_D)=-2(n+1),
ord_L(C_D)=-3(n+1).                                      (17)
```

The two terms in each of `(14)` have different orders, so no cancellation
is hidden in `(17)`.

## 3. The retained arm has an ordinary exact chart

In the `L_1` factor, `z` is a unit and `t=p/z` is regular. Put

```text
A_L=t+2Lx,                         C_L=-x.                (18)
```

Then

```text
dA_L wedge dC_L=eta,              (A_L,C_L)|L_1=(p,0).   (19)
```

Both entries are regular on `Lhat`. On the other color, however,

```text
ord_D(A_L)=ord_D(t)=-n,            ord_D(C_L)=1.          (20)
```

Thus neither chart by itself is a global regular repair: the cusp chart
pays poles on `L_1`, while its arm counterpart pays a pole on `D`.

## 4. The exact mixed-sign Hensel glue

Put

```text
q=2z-1,                   q^2=1+4x^n p.                   (21)
```

Let `W in Bhat_n` be the unique square root of the right side congruent to
one modulo `x`. Modulo `x`, the element `q` is `-1` on `D` and `+1` on
`L_1`; hence

```text
W|D=-q,                    W|L_1=+q.                      (22)
```

Therefore

```text
e_D=(1-q/W)/2,             e_L=(1+q/W)/2                 (23)
```

are the two Hensel idempotents. Define

```text
Ahat=e_D A_D+e_L A_L,
Chat=e_D C_D+e_L C_L.                                    (24)
```

Since `de_D=de_L=0`, equations `(16)` and `(19)` give `(5)` immediately.
The construction is canonical after choosing the displayed chart on each
color and the square root congruent to one.

## 5. Why the canonical split section is not rational

Suppose `R in K` had completed image `Ahat`. Its `D` component would equal
`A_D`. By the injectivity in `(11)`, this forces

```text
R=A_D in K.                                                (25)
```

But the `L_1` expansion of that rational function has order `-2(n+1)` by
`(17)`, whereas the prescribed component `A_L` is regular. This is a
contradiction. The same argument for `Chat` uses

```text
ord_L(C_D)=-3(n+1),                ord_L(C_L)=1.          (26)
```

This proves `(6)`. In particular, no finite Taylor truncation sequence that
stabilizes to elements of `B_n` can equal the canonical split formal pair.

The square root gives the same obstruction in one line. In the field `K`,

```text
W^2=q^2  implies  (W-q)(W+q)=0  implies  W=+q or W=-q.    (27)
```

Either global sign makes one idempotent in `(23)` zero and the other one;
it selects one pole-bearing chart. The mixed sign `(22)` exists only because
the completed divisor is disconnected.

## 6. Exact algebraic scope: split, not transcendental

It would be false to call `(24)` nonalgebraic over `K`. Inside the product
of completed fraction fields,

```text
(Ahat-A_D)(Ahat-A_L)=0,
(Chat-C_D)(Chat-C_L)=0.                                  (28)
```

Since `A_D!=A_L`, the first entry reconstructs

```text
e_D=(Ahat-A_L)/(A_D-A_L).                                (29)
```

Thus

```text
K[Ahat] isomorphic to K times K,                          (30)
```

and the same conclusion follows from `Chat`. This algebraic envelope is
reducible. If an element `alpha` of an integral `K`-algebra embedded
compatibly into both completed fraction fields had image `Ahat`, then
`(28)` would give

```text
(alpha-A_D)(alpha-A_L)=0.                                (31)
```

The domain property would force `alpha=A_D` or `alpha=A_L`, contradicting
the opposite completed component. Therefore the split section has no
connected integral algebraization, even after passing to an algebraic field
extension. Its only immediate algebraic home is the disconnected envelope
`K times K`.

## 7. Scope and relation to the live counterexample lane

THM-3978 found the same mechanism for a linear submersion: two completed
colors choose incompatible invariant integration constants. THM-3979 shows
that the seed-matching cusp problem is formally soluble to all orders. The
present theorem isolates a canonical all-height solution for which the
failure of global termination is completely visible: it is the mixed-sign
idempotent itself.

This does **not** prove that every formal Darboux pair uses `(23)`, nor that
every polynomial pair must reproduce `(14)` and `(18)`. A different global
first coordinate could couple the two colors before completion and avoid
this split section. Such pairs, finiteness, and `JC(2)` remain open.

As a hostile boundary, the centered quadrature used in THM-3979 is a
different formal gauge; its algebraization is not decided by the split
quadratic calculation `(28)`. The theorem therefore does not transfer the
present nonalgebraization claim to arbitrary formal solutions.
