# THM-2875 -- all-order initial Pascal ladder and support-square range

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

No mathematical claim is established by this reservation.  The intended
target is a rigorously scoped initial-range theorem for

```text
n |-> L(d_n W^m)/L(d_n W),                 m>=2,
```

when `W` is a nonzero nonnegative adjacent-difference cone element.  Promotion
requires the fully polarized marked-permutation kernel proof, exact separation
of the still-open high-row regime, a reproducible exact companion and stored
transcript, and hostile controls for signed coefficients and failure of the
sufficient weight-order gate.
