# THM-3875 -- normalized forest support cannot descend below score two

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**  The statement is
internal to the repository's paid-row forcing semantics.  It proves a search
barrier, not an Arithmetic-Kakeya bound and not a certificate accepted by an
external verifier.

## 1. Forest port-space and one-round barrier

Let `U` be a finite nonempty vertex set, `u=|U|>0`, and let `F` be a
simple forest on `U` (so no parallel live edges are admitted).  Work over
`Q`.  To every forest edge and every ground half-edge at a vertex attach a
nonzero label `(s,d)` with `s != 0`.  Let `R` be the row space in
`(Q^2)^U` generated by

```text
ground half-edge at v:       (s,d) at v,
forest edge vw:              (s,d) at v and -(s,d) at w.
```

For `v in U`, define its port space

```text
P_v = R intersect Q^2_v,
```

where `Q^2_v` is the coordinate plane supported only at `v`.  Then `P_v` is
exactly one of

```text
0,
a one-dimensional line spanned by a label with s != 0,
Q^2_v.
```

In particular `P_v` cannot be the vertical line `s=0`.  Hence, if the
distinguished direction `(0,1)` belongs to `P_v`, then `P_v=Q^2_v`.

Apply this to a paid forcing instance after the invertible change

```text
(a,b) -> (s,d)=(a+b,a-b).
```

If `F_1` is the full first simultaneous firing set, then
`Q^2_v subset R` for every `v in F_1`.  Projecting away `F_1` cannot create a
new fireable vertex: any projected singleton at `w` lifts to a row supported
on `F_1 union {w}`, and subtracting the already available coordinate planes
`Q^2_v`, `v in F_1`, gives the same singleton at `w` before projection.
Therefore closure has at most one nonempty round.

If forcing succeeds, every vertex fires in that round and

```text
R = (Q^2)^U,
rank R = 2|U|.
```

Assume every active nonzero row comes from a separately charged slot.  The
number of paid charged slots is at least the active-row count, hence at least
`2|U|`; zero rows produced by projection remain paid waste.

## 2. Proof of the port-space lemma

Root the component of `F` containing `v` at `v` and prune leaves toward the
root.  At a non-root leaf `w`, let `e` be its parent edge, with label line
`L_e`, and let `S_w` be the span of the ground labels already available at
`w` (original ground half-edges plus child edges enabled by earlier pruning).
To make the output at `w` vanish, the scalar coefficient on `e` can be chosen
arbitrarily exactly when

```text
L_e subset S_w;
```

otherwise that coefficient is forced to zero.  Thus an enabled child branch
acts at its parent as one more available ground line, namely its own edge
label line.  Induction gives that the root port space is the span of a subset
of the original nonvertical label lines.  In a two-dimensional vector space
such a span is zero, one of those nonvertical lines, or all of `Q^2`.  This
proves the classification.

The argument handles disconnected forests componentwise, missing/zero edges,
arbitrary ground-seed multiplicity, duplicate labels, and initial wildcards.
After wildcard columns are removed, a live–wild edge is just a ground
half-edge at its live endpoint; a wild–wild edge is a zero row and paid waste.

Integral forcing is no loophole.  The proof uses the rational relaxation, so
any instance that succeeds by integer combinations also succeeds in the
rational game and is subject to the same lower bound.  Conversely, all
positive controls below clear denominators to nonzero integral multiples of
the distinguished direction, as in THM-2850.

## 3. `k=1` corollaries and exact scope

For the verifiable construction with one dimension, the graph is a path and
each nonzero `f_1(e)` supplies exactly one charged physical edge.  Strict and
loose rule-(1) coincide because the suffix set is a singleton.  Removing
initial `T` leaves a forest.  Therefore every successful normalized `k=1`
instance has score at least `2`, for arbitrary length, finite label set,
wildcards, and seed list.

For the intuitive legal-slot construction with one dimension, merge slots
identify adjacent path vertices.  Their equivalence classes are intervals;
the quotient of the remaining labelled boundary slots is a subgraph of a
path.  Seeds push forward to ground half-edges, and any row that becomes zero
still counts in `m+r`.  Hence the same floor applies.  This is a genuine
same-`H` regime: at one dimension the repeated inner object is the base
vertex, so there is no unverified prefix variation.

The proof does **not** cover:

- `k>=2`, where grid cycles and suffix multiplicities are the actual descent
  resource;
- non-adjacent or cross-suffix identifications;
- a quotient with a cyclic/parallel live multigraph;
- the literal `k>=2` loose rule, where one charged entry creates many rows;
- labels with `a+b=0`; or
- the private benchmark verifier and the certificate-to-`AK(s)` implication.

## 4. Equality and hostile boundaries

Three positive equality controls were replayed independently by exact
rational row-span membership:

```text
merge-free path:             u=6, paid=12, all six fire in round 1;
path with one wildcard:      u=6, paid=12, all live vertices fire in round 1;
adjacent-merge quotient:     u=4, paid=8,  all four classes fire in round 1.
```

Two hostile controls show that both main hypotheses are essential.

1. If a vertical label is allowed, a one-vertex seed `(1,-1)` fires at score
   `1`.  This is excluded by the canonical normalization `a+b!=0`.
2. A live cycle defeats the port classification.  Take a triangle
   `1-2-3-1` with normalized slopes `0,1,2` on the oriented edges
   `1->2`, `2->3`, `3->1`, and ground seeds of slopes `2` at vertex `2`
   and `0` at vertex `3`.  These slopes come from allowed integer labels
   `(1,1)`, `(1,0)`, and `(3,-1)`.  The combination with coefficients

   ```text
   edge12 + 2 edge23 + edge31 - seed2 + seed3
   ```

   is supported only at vertex `1` and is vertical there.  These are
   coefficients for projectively normalized `(s,d)=(1,rho)` rows; for the
   displayed raw integer labels, clearing row scales gives coefficients
   `(1,4,1,-1,1)`.  Its port space is
   exactly that vertical line, not `Q^2`.  Vertex `1` fires first; after it is
   removed, vertices `2` and `3` fire together.  Thus this arbitrary paid-row
   graph has

   ```text
   paid=5, u=3, score=5/3, trace=(1),(2,3), ranks=5,4.
   ```

   Deleting the first `d` column drops rank by one, while deleting its
   matching `s` column drops no additional rank.  This is precisely the
   `p=0` transition forbidden by the forest lemma.  The triangle is a hostile
   algebraic control, **not** asserted to be a constructible benchmark graph.
