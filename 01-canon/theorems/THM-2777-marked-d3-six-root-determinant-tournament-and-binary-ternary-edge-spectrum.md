# THM-2777 -- a marked `D3` frame gives a genuine six-root tournament

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

No mathematical claim is established by this reservation.  The intended
target marks one even-sign body diagonal `h` and one chamber of its
orthogonal `A2` subsystem, then orients the six positive `D3` root lines by
the sign of `det(alpha,beta,h)`.  Promotion must prove tie-freeness, declare
the chamber/orientation gauge, classify the resulting tournament and its
weighted determinant spectrum, and identify exactly the three determinant-
two opposition pairs and three determinant-three `A2` spanning-tree pairs.

oriented-matroid switching class, not a canonical tournament, and supplies
no graceful, modular-monodromy, Keller, LRC(14), or JC(2) conclusion.
