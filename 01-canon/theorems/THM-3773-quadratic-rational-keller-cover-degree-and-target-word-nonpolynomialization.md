# THM-3773 -- the quadratic rational near miss cannot be polynomialized

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + PENDING INDEPENDENT AUDIT.**  THM-3758 pays the
generic time-form debt but fails a one-step target shear because two
components have opposite principal parts.  Alternating rational shears might
seem able to trade that divisor debt between the two outputs.  They cannot:
the rational pair already has irreducible cover degree two, and every
birational target word preserves it.

Let `k` be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.  Retain all
of THM-3758's admissible parameters

```text
a0,a1,beta in k*,               gamma in k,
delta=2a1 gamma-beta a0^2 !=0,                         (1)
A=a0+a1z,
B=gamma+beta a0z+(beta a1/2)z^2,
z=XT,
Q=XA+X^2B.                                             (2)
```

For any `kappa in k*`, put

```text
L=Q,
C=a1+2beta L,
Y=A+2XB,
P0=-kappa/(2L) [z+Y/C].                               (3)
```

THM-3758 proves `J(P0,Q)=kappa`.  The new assertion is

```text
[k(X,T):k(P0,Q)]=2.                                   (4)
```

Consequently, let `Phi=(R,S)` be any birational rational target map with

```text
k(R,S)=k(p,q),             J_(p,q)(R,S)=eta in k*.    (5)
```

Then `R(P0,Q)` and `S(P0,Q)` cannot both belong to `k[X,T]`.  In particular,
no finite word generated by

```text
(p,q) |-> (p,q+F(p)),          (p,q) |-> (p+G(q),q),  F,G in k(t),       (6)
```

and invertible affine symplectic target changes can turn `(P0,Q)` into a
polynomial pair.

## 1. The primitive parameter leaves a quadratic equation

Define the rational target coordinate

```text
w=-2LP0/kappa=z+Y/C.                                  (7)
```

On the generic `Q`-fibre, THM-3758's quadratic equation is

```text
Y^2=A^2+4BL.                                          (8)
```

Substituting `Y=C(w-z)` gives the following equation for `z` over `k(L,w)`:

```text
E(z)=2beta L C z^2
     -2C(a0+Cw)z
     +C^2w^2-a0^2-4gamma L=0.                         (9)
```

Its leading coefficient is nonzero in `k(L,w)`.  Let
`D(w)=disc_z(E)`.  A second discriminant collapses to the parameter invoice

```text
disc_w(D)=-256 beta delta L^2 C^4 !=0.                (10)
```

Thus `D` is a degree-two squarefree polynomial in `w` over `k(L)`.  A
nonconstant squarefree polynomial in the UFD `k(L)[w]` is not a square in
its fraction field.  The quadratic criterion applied to `(9)` therefore
shows that `E` is irreducible over `k(L,w)`.

There is no hidden larger recovery problem.  Equation `(7)` gives
`Y=C(w-z)`, while

```text
X=2L/(Y+A),                       T=z/X.               (11)
```

Hence

```text
k(X,T)=k(L,w,z),                 k(P0,Q)=k(L,w).       (12)
```

Irreducibility of `(9)` proves `(4)`.  In characteristic zero this quadratic
extension is separable and hence Galois.

For the normalized integral row `(a0,a1,beta,gamma)=(1,-2,-1,0)`, equation
`(9)` becomes

```text
4L(1+L)z^2
+4(1+L)[1-2(1+L)w]z
+4(1+L)^2w^2-1=0,                                    (13)
```

an explicit degree-two control over the rational target plane.

## 2. Every birational symplectic target word preserves the obstruction

Substitution of `(P0,Q)` into `(5)` does not change the generated subfield:

```text
k(R(P0,Q),S(P0,Q))=k(P0,Q).                           (14)
```

The chain rule and `(3)` give

```text
J(R(P0,Q),S(P0,Q))=eta kappa in k*.                  (15)
```

If both functions in `(14)` were polynomials, they would therefore form a
planar Keller map of function-field degree two by `(4)`.  THM-1330 records
the classical obstruction: a quadratic function-field extension is Galois,
and Campbell's Galois Keller theorem forces such a Keller map to be
invertible, hence to have degree one.  This contradiction proves the claim.

Each generator in `(6)` is birational, has target Jacobian one, and has an
inverse of the same type with the sign reversed.  Finite compositions and
affine symplectic changes therefore satisfy `(5)`, proving the announced
all-word nonpolynomialization result.  The argument is stronger than a
bounded coefficient census and stronger than a two-shear DVR obstruction:
it closes every finite rational target word at once.  **QED.**

## 3. What the degree remembers

The three successive gates are now distinct:

```text
rational time exactness     passed by P0;
vertical target shear       fails by THM-3758's opposite coefficients;
arbitrary target word       fails because the rational cover degree is two. (16)
```

The field degree is the information that componentwise Laurent expansions do
not record.  Conversely, the vertical coefficients are the information lost
by looking only at the generic quadratic extension.  Both sidecars are
needed to describe why the family is close but cannot be repaired.

The conclusion is scoped to birational target operations.  A nonbirational
rational operation need not preserve `(14)` or the response.  It also does
not classify arbitrary rational Keller pairs or arbitrary source
modifications.  For counterexample construction, the lesson is positive and
sharp: a useful rational seed must have function-field degree at least three
and must solve the vertical equalizer before alternating target words can
plausibly expose a polynomial counterexample.
