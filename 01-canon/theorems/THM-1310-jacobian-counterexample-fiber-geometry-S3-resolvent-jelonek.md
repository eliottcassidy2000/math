## Statements (all proved by explicit symbolic computation, frozen in results)

**(1) The conic pair.** F is affine in z; eliminating z and clearing the spurious
factors u^k, the fiber of F over (a,b,g) is, for x ≠ 0, the solution set of

    C1:  3a·x² = u·(bx − s)                    [u = 1+s]
    C2:  a·x³ + g·u³ = x·u·(u+1)

with y = s/x and z reconstructed linearly. The plane {x=0} maps to
(z + 4y², y, 0), covering the g = 0 plane once.

**(2) The fiber cubic and the TRACE LAW.** Res_s(C1, C2) = a·x³·N(x) with

    N(x) = L·x³ + (4 − 3bg)·x − 2g,
    L(a,b,g) = 27a²g² − 18abg + 16a + b³g − b²   (irreducible quartic).

The x²-coefficient of N vanishes IDENTICALLY: **the three preimages of every
target satisfy x₁ + x₂ + x₃ = 0** — an exact conservation law of the
counterexample. (At the collision target (−1/4, 0, 0): N = −4x(x−1)(x+1), roots
{0, ±1}, sum 0.)

**(3) Field degree exactly 3.** The degree-1 subresultant of (C1, C2) in s gives
s (hence y, z) as a rational function of x over ℚ(a,b,g) ⟹
ℂ(x,y,z) = ℂ(a,b,g)(x) and N is the minimal polynomial of x: **deg F = 3.**

**(4) The S₃ pin via the resolvent −L.** disc_x(N) = Δ = −4·Q²·L with

    Q(a,b,g) = 27ag² − 9bg + 8.

L appears to the FIRST power: Δ is not a square in ℚ(a,b,g) (UFD exponents; the
odd exponent persists over ℂ(a,b,g)), and N is generically irreducible ⟹
**arithmetic AND geometric monodromy = S₃**, with quadratic resolvent field
ℚ(a,b,g)(√(−L)). This proves what HYP-8070 measured (7-prime empirical S₃) and
what Campbell's theorem forces a priori (non-Galois minimal stratum).

**(7) The design equations (the generative core).** With B = F − Az,
A = (u³, 3u²x, −x³): B₁ = uW, B₂ = y + 3xW, x³B₁ + u³B₃ = R, where

    W = y²(4 + 3xy),   R = x·u·(u+1).

The two "miracle cancellations" in the fiber elimination ARE these identities:
the whole map is generated by one plane function W and one residue R. In the

wt(x,y,z) = (1,−1,−2), wt(F) = (−2,−1,1). For the general template weight system

random (1,−1,−3) instances). **k = 3 is the unique ℂ*-equivariant degree.**
