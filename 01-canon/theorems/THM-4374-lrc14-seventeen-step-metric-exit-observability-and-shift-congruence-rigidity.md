### Corollary (equality is the only output-compatible forward-shift congruence)

`W_17(P)=W_17(P')`, and Theorem B gives `P=P'`.

Here an **exact state** means an output-emitting deterministic quotient whose
kernel is a forward-shift congruence.  Therefore:

This is an obstruction to noninjective operation compression, not to LRC.
