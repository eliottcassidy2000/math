# THM-2549 -- chronology is not target activity

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + PROOF-GRAPH STOPPING THEOREM.**

The live chain now contains two facts which must be held simultaneously.

```text
positive selected empty head
  + arbitrarily late positive owner-to-word handoff
  = genuine same-base chronology on all 165 rows;

every currently constructed positive later factor on that common base
  = a positive-depth base-thirteen pullback
  = neutral under the old target action.                         (1)
```

Thus lack of chronology is no longer the exact obstruction.  The missing
coordinate is the action which makes the later leg a target leg.  The old
target charge lives in an ancestry sheet; it does not move to the future base
merely because that base is sampled later.

This theorem makes that boundary algebraic, proves the positive chronological
packet uniformly over the finite live ledger, and translates the current
knowledge state into the Hall language of THM-2545.  The Hall table below is a
**proved-field table**, not the unknown physical arrival table.

## 1. The positive-depth future algebra

Put

```text
T(y)=13y mod 1.
```

For each atomic role `i`, let

```text
y_i=w_i x mod 1,                         w_i in Z,             (2)
```

and let the old THM-2365 target co-shift act factorwise by

```text
tau_theta:y_i -> y_i-theta_i/13,
theta_i in F_13.                                               (3)
```

Equation (3) is not a common translation of `x`; its role-by-role typing is
load-bearing.  Let `A_+` be the finite Boolean algebra generated by periodic
Boolean step functions of finitely many coordinates

```text
Phi(T^(L_1)y_1,...,T^(L_d)y_d),
L_i>=1.                                                        (4)
```

Products such as a future owner followed by its terminal word are included:
their factors simply occur at two positive depths.  Complements, finite
unions, labelled atom refinements, and finite real or complex linear
combinations of these generators will be called the positive-depth
future-pullback algebra.

### Future-pullback neutrality theorem

Every element of `A_+` is pointwise fixed by every old target co-shift (3).
Indeed, on the `i`-th argument of (4),

```text
T^(L_i)(y_i-theta_i/13)
 =T^(L_i)y_i-13^(L_i-1)theta_i
 =T^(L_i)y_i                 mod 1.                           (5)
```

Each generator is fixed, so every Boolean or linear expression in the
generators is fixed.  This is an all-depth identity; no limiting argument is
involved.

The same generators are constant on the old predecessor-root fibre.  Write

```text
x=(z+r)/13,                  r in F_13.                        (6)
```

Then

```text
T^(L_i)(w_i x)
 =13^(L_i-1)w_i z+13^(L_i-1)w_i r
 =13^(L_i-1)w_i z             mod 1.                         (7)
```

Thus every element of `A_+` is independent of `r`.  Equations (5) and (7)
are distinct statements: (5) concerns the factorwise target action, while
(7) concerns a common physical predecessor fibre.

Both boundaries are sharp at depth zero.  The Boolean root cell

```text
delta_0(y)=1_[0,1/13)(y)                                    (8)
```

obeys

```text
delta_0((z+r)/13)=1_(r=0)                                   (9)
```

away from null endpoints and is moved to a different root cell by every
nonzero shift `y -> y-theta/13`.  Hence positive depth, not periodicity or
Booleanity alone, is the exact source of the collapse.

## 2. No future-algebra target-root selector

The preceding invariance has a categorical consequence.  Suppose a partial
later-root selector generated by `A_+` is encoded by pairwise disjoint fields

```text
B_s in A_+,                         s in F_13,
B_partial in A_+,
```

where `partial` is fixed by the target action.  Genuine target-root covariance
would require, up to the harmless global sign convention,

```text
tau_theta B_s=B_(s+theta),
tau_theta B_partial=B_partial.                              (10)
```

But every `B_s` is fixed by (5).  Equation (10) therefore makes all thirteen
root fields equal.  Since they are pairwise disjoint, all of them are zero:

```text
B_s=0                         for every s in F_13.            (11)
```

Consequently an `A_+`-measurable equivariant partial selector can select only
the fixed cemetery label.  In particular, adding more future-pullback factors,
more terminal words, more delays, or more Boolean refinements cannot by itself
create the later categorical root map `b` required in THM-2545 equation (4).

This is a definability no-go, not a claim that the physical target selector is
empty.  A field which retains the ancestry sheet, or one which carries a new
future-scale target action, lies outside `A_+` and is not ruled out.

## 3. Where the old charge went: the ancestry sheet

For one atomic role and `L>=1`, write its natural-extension coordinate as

```text
y=(z+a)/13^L,
z=T^L y,                         a in Z/13^L Z.               (12)
```

The old target action gives

```text
y -> y-theta/13,
z -> z,
a -> a-13^(L-1)theta             mod 13^L.                  (13)
```

Thus old charge is not annihilated globally; it is pushed entirely into the
ancestry sheet `a`.  A genuine target action on the future base would be

```text
z -> z-theta/13,                                           (14)
```

whose pullback is instead

```text
y -> y-theta/13^(L+1).                                    (15)
```

Equations (13) and (15) are different group actions.  A future factor can
carry target charge in either of two honest ways:

1. retain enough of `a` to transport the old action; or
2. construct the new action (14) and an intertwiner relating it to the
   selected head.

Calling the future base “the later target” without one of these sidecars
forgets exactly the coordinate on which the charge acts.

## 7. Sharp boundary and next theorem

1. A positive common-ancestry field retaining the sheet `a` in (12)--(13),

Any of these would create information absent from `A_+`.  A further delayed
owner, word, collision colour, Fourier mode, or horizontal transfer without
