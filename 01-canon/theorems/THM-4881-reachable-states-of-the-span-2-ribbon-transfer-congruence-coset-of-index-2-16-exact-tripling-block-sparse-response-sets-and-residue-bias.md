# THM-4881 - the reachable set of the span-2 ribbon transfer

Setting (THM-4880): `y(u) in Z^14` is the reduced state of a span-2 ribbon word, `y(empty) = e_1`, `H(u) = y_2(u)`,
`y_1 = y_2` on nonempty words, and the letters act by the integer matrices `A_{--}, A_{1-}, A_{-2}, A_{12}` of
`span2_intrep.txt`. `Y_n` is the set of states of words of length `n`.

**(A) Congruence coset.** Let `y_ref = (1, 1, 0, 2, 0, ..., 0)` and let `L` have the echelon basis `2e_1 + 2e_2, 2e_3,
..., 2e_12, 4e_13 + 4e_14, 8e_14`. Then `Y_n subset y_ref + L` for every `n >= 7`. Equivalently: `H` odd; `y_3, ...,
y_12` even; `y_13 = 0 (mod 4)`; `y_14 = y_13 (mod 8)`. `L` is exactly the lattice generated by the differences of
`Y_n` for every `n >= 5` (index `2^16` in `{d : d_1 = d_2}`); levels 3 and 4 lie in cosets of proper sublattices
(indices 128 and 16384). *Proof.* `L` is the Z-span of the differences of `Y_7`; `A_m L subset L` and `A_m y_ref -
y_ref in L` for all four letters (finite exact check); induction from level 7.

**(B) Exact tripling.** `A_B = A_{-2} A_{1-}` (the block `1-.-2`, and likewise `A_{1-} A_{-2}`) has characteristic
polynomial `x^13 (x - 3)` and rank 4; hence `A_B = 3P + N` with `P` idempotent of rank one, `PN = NP = 0`,
`N^13 = 0`, and `H(u B^k) = 3^{k-13} H(u B^13)` for every word `u` and `k >= 13`. In every tested case the tripling is
exact from `k <= 3`: `H(B^k) = 1, 1, 5, 15, 45, 135, ...`; `H(1-.-2.12.B^k) = 1, 11, 33, 95, 285, 855, ...`. The
block's first vertex is a singleton strong component and its second continues a chain of Moon's 2-blocks (THM-4744,
factor `2^(2-1) + 1 = 3` per block); the residual `H(uB) - 3H(u)` is unbounded (THM-4880 (H1)), so this is
multiplicative structure only.

**(C) Sparse, saturated response sets.** For the last vertex `t` of `u`, `(h, e, R) = (H(u), e_t(u), R_t(u))` is read
off `A_{--} y(u)`. Over all span-2 words of length `<= 20` with `H <= 99`, and for every odd `h <= 99`, the set
`{R : (h, e, R) occurs, R <= 4h}` has density 0.03-0.17 among the parity-admissible integers of `[h, 4h]` (e.g.
`h = 9`: 8 of 28; `h = 63`: 6 of 190; `h = 99`: 34 of 298) and is identical at lengths 16 and 20 for all 47 attained
`h`. Every root of every tournament of orders `<= 8` gives denser but still sparse sets (17-56 % for `h <= 45`). Hence
the near-join induction (`N = h + 2R` with dense `R`-sets, HYP-9453 (2)) is not the mechanism of the ribbon tiling
law; the covering of every odd number `<= 100,000` except 7, 21, 47 (THM-4880) is collective across `h`.

**(D) Residues and exponential sums.** On the solid window `[49, d_n]` of level `n <= 13` the counts `H(u)` are
equidistributed modulo 7, 8, 16 (deviation `<= 0.03` at `n = 13`) and biased modulo 3, 5, 9 (deviation 0.21, 0.16,
0.13 at `n = 13`, growing with `n`). The normalised exponential sums `|sum_{|u| = n} e(theta H(u))| / 4^(n-1)` decay
geometrically at `1/7, 1/8, 1/16, 3/11, 5/13` and at irrational `theta` (e.g. `theta = 0.1234...`: 0.062, 0.031,
0.003 at `n = 9, 11, 13`) and grow at `1/3` (0.190, 0.265, 0.324) and `1/5` (0.165, 0.186, 0.227). Multiplicities
`r_n(N)` on the window: medians 79, 108, 100, 304, 232 for `n = 9..13`; `(1/n) E log H = 0.386 ... 0.443` for
`n = 8..13`.

**(E) Proximality.** The two largest eigenvalue moduli are 1.839 / 0.737 (`1-`), 2.288 / 1.255 (`-2`), 2.563 / 1.346
(`12`), 3.000 / 0.000 (`1-.-2`), 4.745 / 0.604 (`1-.12`), 5.236 / 1.576 (`-2.12`).

Consequence for the search for a collective invariant (HYP-9474): no local invariant survives - quotient and digit
systems (THM-4880 (G), (H)), arithmetic progressions ((H2)), dense response sets ((C)), dense extension sets (THM-4880
note, section 9) - and the surviving formulation is distributional: an integer-scale local limit law for the linear
random walk `y -> A_a y`, whose major arcs are the rationals with denominators `3^i 5^j`.
