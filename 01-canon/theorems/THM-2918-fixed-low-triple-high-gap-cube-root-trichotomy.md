# THM-2918 -- fixed-low-triple high-gap cube-root trichotomy

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

No mathematical claim is established by this reservation.  The intended
target fixes any three low factorial slots, adjoins one exponent tending to
infinity, and identifies the scaled cubic-null equations with a three-sheet
cube-root torsor in the positive-definite quadratic quotient field.  Promotion
requires a uniform finite factorial-ratio estimate, a proof that exactly one
sheet enters the positive coefficient quadrant, and a sign-blind nonzero
quartic-remainder certificate on all three sheets.  It will claim only local
branches at the natural cube-root scale, not global branch exhaustion or
arbitrary-support GMC.
