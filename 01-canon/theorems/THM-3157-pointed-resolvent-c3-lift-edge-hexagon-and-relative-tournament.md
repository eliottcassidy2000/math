# THM-3157 -- pointed resolvent C3 lift, edge hexagon, and relative tournament

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

This file reserves the namespace for a local pointed quartic-resolvent theorem.
Given a four-sheet set, a chosen sheet, and an oriented order-three generator
on the three matching-resolvent roots, the intended statement lifts that
generator uniquely through the chosen sheet and combines it with edge
complementation to produce a six-cycle on the six edge sheets.  It also records
the resulting uniquely selected relative tournament and the exact obstruction
to forgetting the point or orientation.

The intended theorem is conditional/local.  It does not identify edge
complementation with quartic monodromy, does not construct the missing global
Keller resolvent torsor, and does not assert a Keller-map exclusion.
