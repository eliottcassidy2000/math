# THM-2577 -- the word-depth collision law is independent of the packet clock

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved to prove, on the canonical typed row and for every packet clock with
positive prescribed-word return, that the first owner-normalized collision
depth is `3` for the `{a}` word and `5` for the `{b}` and `{a,b}` words.

The intended mechanism is support-theoretic rather than an extrapolation in
the clock: opposite danger/safe status at the deepest blocker appearing in
the word keeps the pushed supports disjoint through its `13`-adic depth, while
an exact finite interval-image inclusion at the next push forces positive
overlap for every nonzero returned subpacket.  The audit must reproduce these
image identities independently, distinguish essential support from pointwise
boundary conventions, and retain the typed-row/non-cover scope.  No temporal
intertwiner, physical target current, scalar-row exclusion, or LRC(14)
conclusion is reserved.
