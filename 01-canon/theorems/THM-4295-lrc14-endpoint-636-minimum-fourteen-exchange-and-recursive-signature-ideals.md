# THM-4295 -- LRC(14) endpoint-636 minimum-fourteen exchange and recursive signature ideals

## 1. Statement

Retain THM-4287's labelled thirty-speed pool, threshold `1/14`, ordered
421-mask joint deck `E`, repaired 9,006-mask carrier `C_0`, and residual
`U_4287`:


There are three complementary conclusions.

1. For each of the 101 labelled body failures of `C_0` at endpoint 636, a
   candidate rank-eight mask responds exactly when it is disjoint from that
   body and active at that obligation's own ordered row. The exact minimum
   number of appended masks covering all 101 obligations is fourteen.
2. There is a selected downstream-optimal fourteen-mask witness `A` such that
   `C_14=C_0+A` closes every live row in the complete `U_4287` layers at
   endpoints `636,635,634,633`. It first fails on the complete endpoint-632
   layer, with 37 bodies. One of these, `1d106401` at `(256,632)`, admits no
   active disjoint rank-eight mask at all. Thus no rank-eight append-only
   extension of `C_14` can close that row.
3. Three separate recursive signature-ideal surgeries produce common decks
   on `36`, `21`, and `54` current rows. Their ideals are pairwise disjoint.
   Joining these three common-deck nodes with the separate ten-row carrier
   node removes exactly 118 rows from `(1)` and leaves

The word *minimum* in conclusion 1 is relative to the exact row-aware,
rank-eight, append-only carrier operation. The zero-response conclusion in 2

## 2. Exact row-aware minimum fourteen

THM-4287 leaves 64 failures on `(100,636)` and 37 on `(256,636)`. For an
obligation `(p,B)` define its response predicate

```text
R(m;p,B)  iff  |m|=8, m intersect B=empty, and m is active at p.   (3)
```

Activity in `(3)` is evaluated at `p`, not intersected across the two rows.
Adding a mask cannot destroy an old cover, and every mask already in `C_0`
has empty response on these failures. Hence the minimum augmentation problem
is exactly the set-cover problem on `(3)`.

Exhausting all `C(30,8)=5,852,925` masks gives


class cover. An independently written SciPy/HiGHS binary integer model on the
same frozen atlas returns objective `14` and MIP gap `0`. Thus fourteen is
necessary and sufficient.

This consumer distinction is load-bearing. Requiring every appended mask to
be active at both hostile rows would solve a stricter common-deck problem, not
the carrier problem `(3)`.

## 7. Validity gates, mechanism, and boundary

- **Types:** `K` is a carrier node. `H19`, `H294`, and `H372` are three
  different common-deck nodes. Their union exists only at the proof-graph
  consumer. No classifier, response atlas, carrier, or rebuilt deck is
  substituted for another.
- **Quantifiers:** every count is over the frozen labelled pool, the exact
  THM-4287 residual, all labelled nine-bodies, and all labelled rank-eight
  candidates. Pairs remain ordered with `q<r`.
- **Activity:** carrier response is row-aware. Signature surgery requires a
  full common-activity intersection over its entire ideal. Neither consumer
  is silently strengthened or weakened into the other.
- **Negative result:** the body `(8)` is the minimal witness for the failed
  continuation. The first failed implication is “a lower carrier failure can
  be repaired by another rank-eight append.” The strongest survivor is the
  exact closure of the ten rows in `K`. The missing coordinate is a new
  certificate operation, not a larger append budget.
- **Equality boundary:** the detached endpoint-636 carrier audit, all three
  rebuilt-deck audits, and the zero-response audit have no equalities in their
  stated cells. The best candidate for `(8)` has a strictly negative margin.
- **Reproducibility:** wall and incidence enumeration is exact. Floating-point
  solvers see only frozen binary response matrices; independent solvers agree
  on the two load-bearing integer objectives. Solver-selected witnesses and
  timing statistics are not canonical.

The mechanism is consumer-aware exchange. First quotient only by the exact
predicate the next consumer preserves; then use the next boundary layer to
select among cardinality-minimal witnesses. When one obligation has empty
response, change operations rather than increasing the budget. Recursive
signature ideals supply such an orthogonal operation because they rebuild a
common deck after deleting the target's inactive joint signature.

## 8. Scope and non-consequences

- The 9,020-mask carrier is a finite sufficient certificate on ten frozen
  rows. It is not a physical orbit witness and is not a uniform carrier.
- Failure of the carrier at `(256,632)` does not imply that the pair is
  dangerous for LRC(14); it proves only absence of this rank-eight append-only
  body-cover certificate.
- The three recursive ideals are complete only inside `U_4287`; no claim is
  made about rows already removed or about pairs outside the frozen pool.
- The proof union does not create one common deck on 118 rows.
- No semantic arrival, exclusive owner, or global termination theorem is
  obtained.
- LRC(14) remains open.
