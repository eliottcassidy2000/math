# THM-2622 -- reserved candidate

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for the finite affine-holonomy theorem behind both the LRC
`C_13` ancestry loss and the quartic `V_4` resolvent loss.  For a finite
abelian deck torsor, cyclic parallel sections should be the fixed points of
one affine holonomy `x -> A x+c`, hence should be empty or a coset of
`ker(I-A)`.  The intended specializations are the fixed-action `0/13`
spectrum for `C_13` and the `0/1/2/4` spectrum for
`V_4 semidirect S_3 = S_4`, including the distinction between invisible
double-transposition translation and visible linear resolvent monodromy.
No LRC or Jacobian consequence is proved by this reservation.
