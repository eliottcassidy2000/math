# THM-4110 -- sparse reciprocal phase graph saturation and AP13 torsion tariff

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.** THM-4105 uses every
reciprocal commutator to recognize one physical time. The sparse question has
an exact answer: connectedness is not enough, and its failure is measured by
a finite lattice index.

## 1. Phase graph and physical orbit

Let

```text
v=(v_1,...,v_r) in Z_(>0)^r,       gcd(v_1,...,v_r)=1,       r>=2,       (1)
```

and let `Gamma` be a finite connected loopless graph on `{1,...,r}`. Work on
the circle `T=R/Z`. Define

```text
K_Gamma(v)={theta in T^r:
              v_j theta_i-v_i theta_j=0 mod 1 for every ij in E(Gamma)}, (2)

P_v={t v mod 1:t in T}.                                                   (3)
```

THM-4105 says that imposing `(2)` on the complete graph gives exactly `P_v`.
For a sparse graph, `P_v` is still contained in `K_Gamma(v)`, but equality can
fail by finitely many torsion sheets.

For a spanning tree `T_0` put

```text
I_(T_0)(v)=product_(i=1)^r v_i^(deg_(T_0)(i)-1).             (4)
```

Then the exact sheet formula is

```text
boxed:
|K_Gamma(v)/P_v|
 = I_Gamma(v)
 = gcd_(T_0 spanning tree of Gamma) I_(T_0)(v).              (5)
```

Equation `(5)` gives the **order** of the finite quotient. Except in the
explicit AP13 case below, it does not claim to determine its invariant-factor
decomposition.

## 2. Weighted-incidence proof

For an oriented edge `ij`, let

```text
a_ij=v_j e_i-v_i e_j in Z^r.                                (6)
```

Let `L_Gamma` be the lattice generated by these edge rows and let

```text
H_v={a in Z^r:a dot v=0}.                                    (7)
```

Characters of `T^r` are indexed by `Z^r`. Therefore

```text
K_Gamma(v)=L_Gamma^perp,       P_v=H_v^perp,
dual(K_Gamma(v)/P_v)=H_v/L_Gamma.                            (8)
```

Connectedness gives `rank(L_Gamma)=r-1` and rational span `v^perp`, so its
saturation is `H_v`. Thus the order in `(5)` is the gcd of all maximal minors
of the edge-row matrix.

Take `r-1` edge rows. A nonzero maximal minor occurs exactly when those edges
form a spanning tree `T_0`. If column `k` is deleted, weighted incidence gives

```text
|det A_(T_0,hat k)|
 = (product_(ij in T_0) v_i v_j)/(product_(i!=k) v_i)
 = v_k product_i v_i^(deg_(T_0)(i)-1)
 = v_k I_(T_0)(v).                                          (9)
```

For a fixed tree, primitivity makes the gcd over `k` equal to `I_(T_0)(v)`.
Taking the gcd over all spanning trees proves `(5)`. **QED.**

The scope `T=R/Z` is load-bearing. Over a divisible torsion-free group such as
the additive reals, a finite lattice index need not create extra solutions.
The arbitrary-abelian-group descent in THM-4105 remains correct only because
there the full commutator lattice equals `H_v`, rather than having finite
index in it.

## 3. Primewise connected-dominating criterion

For a prime `p`, put

```text
C_p={i:p does not divide v_i}.                               (10)
```

Taking `p`-adic valuations in `(5)` gives

```text
nu_p(I_Gamma)
 = min_(T_0) sum_i (deg_(T_0)(i)-1) nu_p(v_i).               (11)
```

Hence `p` does not divide `I_Gamma` exactly when there is a spanning tree in
which every `p`-divisible vertex is a leaf. Such a tree exists exactly when

```text
Gamma[C_p] is connected,
and every vertex outside C_p has a neighbour in C_p.         (12)
```

Indeed, `(12)` lets one take a tree on `C_p` and attach every other vertex as
a leaf. Conversely, deleting the `p`-divisible leaves from a witnessing tree
leaves a connected tree on `C_p`, and each deleted leaf was attached to it.
Therefore

```text
boxed:
I_Gamma=1
 iff C_p is a connected dominating set for every prime p.   (13)
```

The witnessing tree may depend on `p`. For example, on the triangle with
`v=(2,3,5)`, the three tree indices are `2,3,5`; their gcd is one even though
no single tree has index one. Replacing the gcd in `(5)` by an arbitrarily
selected tree is therefore false.

## 4. Tree boundary

If `Gamma` itself is a tree, `(5)` reduces to

```text
I_Gamma=product_i v_i^(deg_Gamma(i)-1).                      (14)
```

For `r>=3` and distinct positive speeds, this equals one exactly when speed
`1` is present and every other vertex is a leaf: `Gamma` is the star centred
at speed `1`. For `r=2`, the one edge is already the complete graph and a
primitive pair has index one.

Thus a spanning tree can reconstruct all **labels** and still fail to
synchronize all **phases**. Internal-vertex speeds are the exact torsion
tariff.

## 5. AP13: the 64-sheet hostile

Take

```text
v=(1,2,...,13),
ij in E_2  iff  nu_2(i)!=nu_2(j).                            (15)
```

This is the complete `53`-edge unequal-`v_2` graph used by the informative
pair lane of THM-4106. It is connected, but its reciprocal phase equations
are not saturated.

Root any spanning tree of `E_2` at speed `1`. Since `v_1=1`, `(4)` becomes

```text
I_(T_0)=product_(x!=1) speed(parent(x)).                     (16)
```

Each of the six nonunit odd vertices

```text
3,5,7,9,11,13                                               (17)
```

must have an even parent, contributing at least `2` to `(16)`. Hence every
tree index is divisible by `2^6`. Equality is attained uniquely by

```text
1--{2,4,6,8,10,12},
2--{3,5,7,9,11,13}.                                         (18)
```

All even children must have parent `1` and all six nonunit odd children must
have parent `2`, so equality has no alternative. Thus `(5)` gives

```text
boxed: I_(E_2)(1,...,13)=64.                                (19)
```

The quotient structure is also explicit. Put `t=theta_1`. The first six
edges in `(18)` force

```text
theta_e=e t                     for e even,                  (20)
```

while the other six force

```text
theta_o=o t+epsilon_o/2,
epsilon_o in {0,1},             o in {3,5,7,9,11,13}.        (21)
```

Every packet `(20)--(21)` satisfies all `53` unequal-`v_2` equations: on an
odd-even edge the extra term is an even multiple of `epsilon_o/2`. The six
bits are independent, and only the all-zero packet lies in `P_v`. Therefore

```text
boxed: K_(E_2)(v)/P_v isomorphic to (Z/2Z)^6.                (22)
```

Adding the six same-shell edges

```text
1--3, 1--5, 1--7, 1--9, 1--11, 1--13                       (23)
```

kills the bits one by one. Six are also necessary among reciprocal edge
constraints: each new edge supplies at most one linear equation on the
elementary two-group in `(22)`. This is an exact missing-sidecar count, not a
heuristic complexity estimate.

## 6. Consequence for the live LRC proposal

THM-4106 proves that a connected unequal-`v_2` owner graph can reconstruct a
mixed primitive speed row from directed pair summaries. THM-4110 proves the
first failed implication:

```text
lossless row reconstruction + connected reciprocal edge graph
  -/-> one common physical phase.                            (24)
```

On AP13, even the **complete** informative graph retains the `64` sheets in
`(22)`. The backlog task must therefore use a saturated commutator graph, or
carry the six same-shell bits explicitly, before testing loneliness.

The incoming THM-4100 component compiler does not remove this obstruction.
It begins with one actual interval `J` in one time coordinate and ends with
one actual survivor, so all reciprocal commutators vanish tautologically.
Conversely, zero commutator defect records physicality but not clearance:
on `{1,...,8,93,94,95}`, both `t=0` and the safe `t=11/49` are physical.
Any attempted composition must additionally retain `J`, `P(c,d)`, the parity
gap, component ancestry, endpoint owner, and clock. This is a generated test,
not a theorem dependency.

## 8. Scope

THM-4110 counts physical phase sheets on the circle. It does not identify the
quotient group in general, does not transfer pairwise owner maxima to one
arrival time, and does not say that a physical packet is `1/14`-safe. The
ABC radical statements in THM-4108 operate on primitive additive relations
and do not change this two-adic lattice index. **LRC(14) remains OPEN.**
