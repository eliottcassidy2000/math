# THM-3398 -- all-sheet atomized coset covers and affine cochains

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for an all-`q` extension of THM-3395's small-sheet star-cochain
criterion.  Beyond seven sheets, one owner may fire several multiplication-
kernel cosets at the same phase.  The intended statement retains each such
coset as a distinct owner-labelled atom, requires their footprints to cover
the common sheet source, and glues their strict tooth intervals by one
complete integral affine cochain.  It must handle repeated owners, prove the
generalized-CRT and real-line Helly sufficiency without a circular-wrap
shortcut, and retain the core/deleted-grid sidecars needed for body-relative
claims.

Promotion requires an independent proof audit, ordinary/optimized exact
replays, the strict `q=7`/`q=8` boundary controls, repeated-owner hostiles,
and an explicit distinction between nonempty cover and reconstruction of the
full cover locus.  This reservation itself proves nothing, changes no ledger,
and has no proved dependencies.
