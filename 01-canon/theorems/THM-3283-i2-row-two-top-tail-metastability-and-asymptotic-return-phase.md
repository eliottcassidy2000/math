# THM-3283 -- I2 row-two top-tail metastability and asymptotic return phase

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for the exact finite-difference classification of the single
top-tail ray obtained from the bank-`I2`, support-`(1,b)` reset by deleting
`n+4` largest poles at widths `b=2n` and `b=2n+1`.  The intended theorem
records its long finite metastable interval, first returned-pole sign change,
and asymptotic leading-phase boundary.  It does not classify arbitrary
deletions, arbitrary `I2` tails, other selector rows, or the Gaussian Moment
Conjecture.  No statement in this stub is a proved dependency.
