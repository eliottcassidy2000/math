# THM-1244 — slowest-spoke component handoff debt

Let `G` be a complete `c`-safe gap covered by the six strict danger combs of
`c<d1<...<d6`, and let `t1` be THM-1240's centered `(c,d1)` sum beat.  The
reserved target takes `K` to be the closed `d1`-safe component containing
`t1` and proves

```text
|K| >= (6d1-c)/(7d1(c+d1)) > 5/(14d1).
```

Since `d2,...,d6` cover `K` and each of their teeth is shorter than
`1/(7d1)`, a minimal tooth-component cover should require at least three
components and hence two strict handoffs.  Those handoffs are intended to
produce a rank-at-most-two labelled forest with explicit gcd/address overlap
credit, which can be inserted into the positioned Hunter inequality of
THM-1237.  The namespace is reserved now; no final inequality is claimed
until strict endpoint, repeated-owner, containment, and minimal-cover cases
have been audited.
