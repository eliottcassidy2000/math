# THM-2776 -- one long wall and the signed-component imbalance

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

No mathematical claim is established by this reservation.  The intended
target is a complete determinant classification for frames consisting of
one long wall `h=(1,...,1)` and `k-1` type-`B_k` roots.  Promotion must prove
the signed-component factorization, its zero boundary, the exact role of
two-primary square components, and the sharp path extremizer.  It must not
infer graceful-label existence, a modular action, a global arrangement
cokernel, or an LRC(14) consequence from a selected-frame determinant.
