# THM-2843 -- four-slot projective divisibility and resolvent reduction

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for the proposed exact reduction of a four-slot factorial-moment
counterexample to a real two-plane on which the restricted quadratic
divides both the cubic and the next moment form, together with its finite
complete-intersection multiplication determinant and the sharp boundary
showing that the resulting `2 x 3` resolvent count does not canonically
carry a `C_2 * C_3` modular action.  No mathematical claim is made by this
reservation.
