# THM-2121 -- effective Fejer localization

Let

```text
B_i(beta)=1_{||v_i beta+e_i/2||<1/14},
v_i in Z_{>0},       e_i in {0,1},       i=1,...,n,
N(beta)=sum_i B_i(beta),                 H(beta)=N(beta)-1,
V=max_i v_i.                                             (1)
```

Here `n>=1`.

The open bands `B_i` cover the circle almost everywhere exactly when
`H>=0` almost everywhere. A **strict safe cell** means a connected component
of the set where every displayed circle distance is strictly greater than
`1/14`. It is a maximal nonempty open interval `I` on which `H=-1`.

For an integer `K>=1`, put

```text
F_K(x)=1/K |sum_(j=0)^(K-1) exp(2 pi i jx)|^2
      =1/K [sin(pi Kx)/sin(pi x)]^2,                    (2)
```

with the continuous value `K` at integers. This is the normalized Fejer
kernel: it is nonnegative, has integral one, and is the squared modulus of a
degree-`K-1` trigonometric polynomial.

We prove:

> **Effective localization.** If a strict safe cell has circular length
> `delta` and midpoint `beta_0`, then
>
> ```text
> integral_T H(beta) F_K(beta-beta_0) d beta <0          (3)
> ```
>
> for every integer `K` satisfying `K delta>n`.

Moreover every strict safe cell has

```text
delta>=1/(14V^2).                                       (4)
```

Consequently `K_0=14nV^2+1` always suffices.

## 1. The endpoint-spacing floor

The endpoints of the `i`th band solve

```text
v_i beta+e_i/2 = integer +/-1/14,
```

so every endpoint has the form

```text
(14a-7e_i+sigma)/(14v_i) mod 1,
a in Z,       sigma in {-1,1}.                          (5)
```

Take two distinct endpoint representatives in `[0,1)`, from speeds `v,w`.
Their difference is a nonzero integer divided by `14vw`; hence

```text
|x-y|>=1/(14vw)>=1/(14V^2).                            (6)
```

The same calculation across the circle cut uses `y+1` and is unchanged.
Every component of the complement of the endpoint set is bounded by two
consecutive distinct endpoints, and every band indicator is constant on that
component. By the strict definition, a safe cell is exactly one of these
components, proving (4).

This argument is deliberately arithmetic. A generic finite union of intervals
has no uniform width floor; the denominators `14v_i` are load-bearing.

## 2. A quantitative Fejer tail

Write `rho=delta/2`. On the circle, for `rho<=||x||<=1/2`, (2) and
`|sin(pi Kx)|<=1` give

```text
F_K(x)<=1/[K sin^2(pi||x||)].                           (7)
```

Concavity of sine on `[0,pi/2]` gives the chord bound

```text
sin(pi t)>=2t,              0<=t<=1/2.                 (8)
```

Therefore the Fejer mass outside the centered interval of length `delta` is

```text
eta_K
 <=2 integral_rho^(1/2) dt/(4Kt^2)
 =1/(Kdelta)-1/K
 <1/(Kdelta).                                           (9)
```

No asymptotic convergence theorem is needed; (9) is a finite explicit bound.

## 3. Negative Toeplitz quadratic

On `I`, one has `H=-1`; everywhere else, `H<=n-1`. Using (9),

```text
integral H F_K
 <=-(1-eta_K)+(n-1)eta_K
 =-1+n eta_K
 <-1+n/(Kdelta).                                       (10)
```

Thus `Kdelta>n` proves (3). Combining (4) with

```text
K_0=14nV^2+1
```

gives `K_0 delta>n`, so the uniform conclusion follows.

To make the Toeplitz form explicit, take

```text
c_j=K^(-1/2) exp(-2 pi i j beta_0),       0<=j<K.       (11)
```

Then `|sum_j c_j exp(2 pi i j beta)|^2=F_K(beta-beta_0)`.
With the Fourier convention of THM-2115, (3) is the quadratic form of the
`K by K` Toeplitz section generated by `hat H(j-k)`. Hence that finite section
is not positive semidefinite.

Conversely, a negative quadratic against any nonnegative trigonometric square
is impossible if `H>=0` almost everywhere. Since `H` is integer-valued and
locally constant away from finitely many endpoints, negativity implies a
positive-measure union of open safe cells. Thus the finite certificate is
equivalent to positive-measure cover failure, not merely necessary for it.

## 4. A finite decision list

There are at most

```text
2 sum_i v_i <=2nV                                      (12)
```

distinct endpoints in (5). Sort them around the circle and take the midpoint
of every consecutive pair. There are at most `2nV` candidate centers. Testing
the single order `K_0` quadratic (3) at those centers decides whether an open
safe cell exists:

```text
open safe cell
 iff some candidate midpoint has a negative K_0-Fejer quadratic.            (13)
```

The forward implication uses the midpoint of an endpoint atom contained in a
safe cell. The reverse implication is the nonnegativity observation after
(11).

## 5. Exact impact on THM-2117 and the finite-box route

THM-2117's distinguished safe cell has

```text
n=8,       delta=51/448945.                            (14)
```

The cell-specific theorem therefore permits

```text
K=floor(8/delta)+1=70423.                              (15)
```

Its maximum speed is `V=7070`, so the row-independent fallback is

```text
14*8*7070^2+1=5598308801.                              (16)
```

The size gap between (15) and (16) is expected: (4) forgets all endpoint
geometry except the largest denominator. The importance is finiteness, not
numerical efficiency.

THM-2112 supplies an explicit whole-row height box in the live rank-seven
ratio lane. Whenever that box bounds the transverse half-fiber speeds, (12)
and `K_0` turn the full Toeplitz test into a finite audit as well. This closes
HYP-2974's logical ``some finite degree exists'' gap with a `V`-dependent
bound. It does **not** prove the much stronger empirical hope that a modest
absolute degree such as 280 works throughout the structured residual bank.

## 6. Boundary and assumption challenge

If the only safe points are band endpoints, then `H>=0` almost everywhere and
every integral against a trigonometric square is nonnegative. THM-1185 shows
that this is a structural, not quantitative, blindness. No choice of Fejer
degree detects a boundary-only lonely point. Endpoint owners, clocks, and the
finite-ring/toothpick carriers remain necessary.

The challenged assumption was that Fejer convergence is only qualitative or
that a useful bound needs smoothness. Here `H` is discontinuous, but the exact
rational endpoint grid supplies the missing modulus of localization.

## 7. Tournament Analysis

Candidate vertices were runners, bands, endpoints, safe atoms, Fourier modes,
Toeplitz minors, and proof obligations. The faithful vertices for the finite
decision (13) are endpoint atoms decorated by their midpoint and length.
Orienting two atoms from wider to narrower, with circular endpoint order as
the tie Hamiltonian path, preserves testing priority. It destroys the signed
Toeplitz quadratic and therefore cannot certify (3); the midpoint/length and
moment evaluation must remain as sidecars. Frequency vertices alone are also
insufficient by THM-2117. QED.
