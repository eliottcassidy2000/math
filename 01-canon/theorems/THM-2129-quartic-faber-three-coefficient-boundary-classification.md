# THM-2129 -- quartic three-coefficient boundary classification

Reserved for the audited all-degree boundary theorem. The intended carrier is
the ordered triple of consecutive coefficients, not a scalar resultant. The
candidate exceptional point is the boundary quartic

```text
z^4-2z^2+1=(z^2-1)^2,
```

so promotion must retain THM-2102's proper-square first-defect quotient and
must not claim quartic source-fiber closure.
