# THM-2438 -- reserved Poisson--Newton and harmonic-incidence bridge

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

This identifier is reserved for an audit of two proposed exact operation
bridges.  The first is the exponential-binomial identity
`sum Delta^k a_0 x^k/k! = exp(-x) sum a_n x^n/n!`, whose application to
the two central Pascal halves is expected to produce the weak and strict
halves of ternary zero-drift words.  The second identifies the reciprocal
mass of an integer support with the limiting mean number of its divisors,
and hence with the mean marked multiplicative scar.

No transform theorem, central-trinomial formula, harmonic convergence
criterion, A014574 consequence, operation-graph conclusion, or LRC(14)
consequence is claimed by this stub.
