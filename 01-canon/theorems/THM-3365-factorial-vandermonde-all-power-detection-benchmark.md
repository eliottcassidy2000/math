# THM-3365 -- Vieta-Jacobian Vandermonde factorial benchmark

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

The all-order factorial formula, its two independent exact evaluations, and
the toric loss ledger have passed independent audits.  This is a detection
benchmark inside the three-variable factorial problem, not a proof of `FC(3)`.

## 1. Inheritance and connection contract

[THM-3018](THM-3018-factorial-conjecture-as-a-simplex-moment-problem.md)
identifies the factorial functional with exponential measure on the positive
orthant and, for homogeneous polynomials, with a simplex moment.
[THM-3304](THM-3304-fourier-dirichlet-kernel-and-alternating-quintic-hfc3-exclusion.md)
and
[THM-3311](THM-3311-alternating-sextic-hfc3-exclusion.md)
use the alternating factor

```text
V=(x-y)(y-z)(z-x),                                      (1)
```

but do not evaluate all powers of the base cubic.  The corrected near miss is
that a product of three linear forms need not have any odd-moment cancellation:
`xyz` is the minimal hostile.  Alternation, not factor count, is load-bearing.

The exact connection is

| field | contract |
|---|---|
| source | an ordered root triple `(x,y,z)` |
| target | the elementary-symmetric coefficient triple `(e1,e2,e3)` |
| map | the nonlinear Vieta transfer `E=(x+y+z,xy+yz+zx,xyz)` |
| preserved | the unordered cubic and its discriminant square |
| destroyed | root order and the sign of the Jacobian |
| sidecar | an `S3` ordering/orientation lift |
| cheapest test | a transposition reverses the determinant, while its square remains positive on distinct real roots |

## 2. The nonlinear transfer has the alternating cubic determinant

Let

```text
E(x,y,z)=(e1,e2,e3)
        =(x+y+z,xy+yz+zx,xyz).                          (2)
```

Its Jacobian is

```text
DE=[1       1       1;
    y+z     x+z     x+y;
    yz      xz      xy],                                (3)
```

and direct expansion gives

```text
J=det(DE)=(x-y)(x-z)(y-z)=-V.                           (4)
```

Thus `J^2` is the cubic discriminant and vanishes exactly on the union of the
three root-collision hyperplanes.  A permutation `sigma in S3` sends

```text
J(x_(sigma(1)),x_(sigma(2)),x_(sigma(3)))
 =sgn(sigma)J(x,y,z).                                   (5)
```

Every alternating polynomial is divisible by `J`.  Consequently every
alternating polynomial of total degree at most three is exactly `kappa J` for
some scalar `kappa`.  This is the maximal scope in which determinant
alternation alone fixes the whole polynomial.

## 3. Exact all-power factorial moments

Let `L:C[x,y,z]->C` be the factorial functional

```text
L(x^a y^b z^c)=a!b!c!.                                  (6)
```

The candidate theorem is the closed formula

```text
L(J^(2m+1))=0,                                           (7)

L(J^(2m))=((2m)!)^2 (3m)!/m! >0       for every m>=0.   (8)
```

For a real nonzero scalar `kappa`, the second line becomes the strictly
positive number `kappa^(2m)` times (8).  For complex `kappa` it remains
nonzero but is not an ordered positivity statement.

The integral identity

```text
L(f)=integral_(R_+^3) f(x,y,z)e^(-x-y-z) dx dy dz        (9)
```

proves (7): transposing two variables preserves the measure and reverses
`J^(2m+1)`.

For (8), `J^(2m)` is symmetric, so split the orthant into its six ordering
chambers.  On `0<=x<=y<=z`, put

```text
a=x,       b=y-x,       c=z-y.                          (10)
```

The Jacobian of (10) is one,
`|J|=bc(b+c)`, and `x+y+z=3a+2b+c`.  Integrating `a` and expanding the last
factor gives the independent finite sum

```text
L(J^(2m))
 =sum_(j=0)^(2m) binom(2m,j)
    (2m+j)!(4m-j)!/2^(2m+j).                            (11)
```

Set `n=2m`.  The beta identity

```text
(n+j)!(2n-j)!
 =(3n+1)! integral_0^1 t^(n+j)(1-t)^(2n-j) dt           (12)
```

turns the binomial sum into

```text
(3n+1)!/4^n integral_0^1 [t(1-t)(2-t)]^n dt.            (13)
```

After `u=1-t` and `n=2m`, this is

```text
(6m+1)!/2^(4m) integral_0^1 u^(2m)(1-u^2)^(2m) du
 =(6m+1)! Gamma(m+1/2)(2m)!
   /[2^(4m+1)Gamma(3m+3/2)].                            (14)
```

The two duplication identities

```text
Gamma(m+1/2)m!=2^(-2m)sqrt(pi)(2m)!,
(3m)!Gamma(3m+3/2)=2^(-6m-1)sqrt(pi)(6m+1)!             (15)
```

reduce (14) exactly to (8).

## 4. Simplex normalization and the FC(3) benchmark

Let

```text
Delta_2={(x,y):x>=0,y>=0,x+y<=1},
z=1-x-y,                     dA=dx dy.                  (16a)
```

Thus `area(Delta_2)=1/2`, and the normalized area average is twice the
displayed integral.  Because `J^(2m)` is homogeneous of degree `6m`, the polar
decomposition in THM-3018 yields

```text
L(J^(2m))
 =(6m+2)! integral_(Delta_2) J^(2m) dA.                 (16)
```

Therefore

```text
integral_(Delta_2) J^(2m) dA
 =((2m)!)^2(3m)!/[m!(6m+2)!].                           (17)
```

At `m=1`, these normalizations are

```text
L(J^2)=24,
integral_(Delta_2)J^2 dA=1/1680,
average_(Delta_2)J^2=1/840.                             (18)
```

This is a sharp `FC(3)` detection benchmark.  Symmetry kills the entire odd
subsequence, but the first even test detects the nonzero polynomial with the
large margin `24`.  Thus `J` is not an `FC(3)` counterexample: it fails the
hypothesis already at power two.  Any proposed three-variable detector that
sees only odd powers is incomplete.

The example also complements THM-3311.  There, an alternating sextic has a
three-parameter symmetric cofactor and complex cancellation survives two even
moments.  Here the degree-three sign cell is one-dimensional, so no cofactor
can cancel the positive base integral.

## 5. Exact toric bouquet audit and graph-ideal boundary

The six monomials in the expansion of `V` have the ordered exponent
configuration

```text
A_V=(210,201,120,102,021,012),
c_V=(-1,1,1,-1,-1,1),           A_V c_V=0.              (19)
```

The vector `c_V` gives the toric binomial

```text
t_2 t_3 t_6-t_1 t_4 t_5.                               (20)
```

It is a Graver element.  Indeed, a conformal subrelation would select equally
many columns from

```text
P={201,120,012},       N={210,102,021}.                  (21)
```

No singleton columns agree.  If two columns on each side agreed in sum, the
omitted columns would agree because `sum(P)=sum(N)=(3,3,3)`.  Hence only the
full three-against-three relation is possible.  But `c_V` is not a circuit:

```text
210+012=201+021=(2,2,2)                                 (22)
```

is already a four-column relation.

An integral Gale matrix for `A_V` has rows

```text
(2,2,3), (-2,-1,-2), (-1,-2,-2),
(1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1).                              (23)
```

No row is zero and no two rows are proportional.  Therefore every bouquet is
a singleton: the bouquet ideal is `I_(A_V)` itself.  The general bouquet map
of Petrovic--Thoma--Vladoiu is an integer-kernel bijection by their Theorem 1.9
and preserves circuits and Graver bases by their Theorem 1.11, but on this
bare Vandermonde support it gives no compression at all.  Under their
stable/non-mixed hypothesis, Theorem 3.7 also transports minimal Markov,
indispensable, reduced-Grobner, and universal Grobner data.  Those data still
do not include polynomial coefficients or an external moment functional.

This distinction is literal.  The signed **linear** polynomial `V` is not the
toric binomial (20).  Replacing its alternating coefficients by all plus signs
keeps exactly the same `A_V`, toric ideal, Gale matrix, and bouquet graph, but
the resulting polynomial `S` satisfies

```text
L(V)=0,                L(S)=12.                          (24)
```

Thus the alternating coefficient character and the weights `a!b!c!` must be
retained as separate sidecars.  The hostile `xyz` adds a second loss: it is
also a product of three linear forms, yet

```text
L((xyz)^n)=(n!)^3 !=0                                   (25)
```

for odd and even `n`.

The graph-ideal results of Garcia-Marco--Marquez-Corbella--Tatakis provide a
different audit.  Their Theorem 3.2 says that a graph `G` is UMG if and only
if its toric ideal `I_G` is generalized robust.  Their Theorem 5.2 says that,
for bipartite `G`, one minimal-generator degree -- equivalently one even
length for all chordless cycles -- implies that `G` is MG.  The matrix `A_V`
is not a graph edge-incidence matrix, so neither result applies directly.
More generally, those generator properties do not imply finite-state support,
factorial positivity, or Vieta alternation.  A future graph model must specify
its incidence matrix and then separately transport the sign character and
factorial observer.

## 6. Verified companion and scope

The exact companion constructs (3) and (4) by sparse polynomial arithmetic,
evaluates `L(V^r)` directly for `0<=r<=12`, compares (11) with (8) through
`m=64`, cross-multiplies the beta/duplication identities, checks all six

the exponent kernel, Graver minimality, four-support circuit hostile, Gale
matrix, and singleton bouquet partition.  It uses no floating point or
assertion-sensitive validation.

This theorem proves only the Vieta-Jacobian benchmark and the stated all-order
moments.  It does not prove `HFC(3)`, full `FC(3)`, a bouquet preservation
theorem for factorial moments, a tournament orientation, or any LRC
consequence.
