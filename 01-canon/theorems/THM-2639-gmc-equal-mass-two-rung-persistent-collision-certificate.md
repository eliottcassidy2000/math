# THM-2639 -- two nonlinear rungs defeat a permanently collided face

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-2631 proves that one scalar return row cannot linearly decode any of its
channels unless that row is singleton.  The first important question left by
that theorem is whether a face with no singleton at any positive level can
still admit an elementary finite certificate.  The answer is yes.

The operative coordinate is not an extra linear row.  It is multiplication
inside the ideal generated by two return polynomials of different degrees.

## 1. Free equal-mass two-ray faces

Let `q=(q_1,q_2,q_3)` be three distinct nonzero integer charges of both signs,
and put

```text
S={r in N^3:q dot r=0}.                                  (1)
```

Write `B_m={r in S:|r|=m}` for its mass-`m` channel fibre.

After permuting the support, suppose the two primitive extreme rays of the
real balanced cone are

```text
v=(a,0,c),                 w=(0,b,d),                    (2)
```

where `a,b,c,d>0`, and assume

```text
S=N*v direct-sum N*w,       a+c=b+d=ell.                 (3)
```

Thus the balanced semigroup is free on its two primitive rays and the rays
have equal total mass.  Distinctness of the first two charges forces `a!=b`:
if `a=b`, then equal mass gives `c=d`, and the two primitive balance equations
give `q_1=q_2`.

For torus coefficients `alpha,beta,gamma in C*`, define

```text
f(u)=alpha*u^(q_1)+beta*u^(q_2)+gamma*u^(q_3),
T_m=CT(f^m),
x=alpha^a gamma^c,          y=beta^b gamma^d.             (4)
```

By (3), a balanced vector of mass `m` exists exactly when `m=k*ell`, and the
complete level is

```text
B_(k*ell)={i*v+(k-i)*w:0<=i<=k}.                         (5)
```

In particular,

```text
|B_(k*ell)|=k+1.                                         (6)
```

Every positive return level is collided.  There is no singleton from which a
THM-2631 linear decoder can restart.

The freeness hypothesis is checkable directly in this three-charge setting.
The lattice index of the two primitive rays in the saturated balanced lattice
is

```text
gcd(v cross w)=gcd(bc,ad,ab)=gcd(a,b).                   (7)
```

Indeed, after dividing `q` by its content, `v cross w` is the primitive
normal to the balanced plane times precisely this lattice index.  Primitivity
of the rays says `gcd(a,c)=gcd(b,d)=1`.  Any prime power common to
`bc,ad,ab` is then common to `a,b`, and the reverse divisibility is immediate.

The mass-`ell` lattice segment has exactly `gcd(a,b)+1` points.  Hence, under
the primitive-ray and equal-mass hypotheses,

```text
S is free on v,w
 iff gcd(a,b)=1
 iff B_ell={v,w}.                                        (8)
```

The minimal hostile in the exact companion has charges `(1,6,-4)`, primitive
rays `(4,0,1),(0,2,3)`, common mass `5`, and index `2`; its first slice also
contains `(2,1,2)`.  Equal mass alone is therefore not enough.

## 2. The two-rung Bezout identity

The first two nonempty constant terms have the exact form

```text
T_ell=A*x+B*y,
T_(2ell)=C*x^2+D*x*y+E*y^2,                              (9)

A=binom(ell,a),              B=binom(ell,b),
C=binom(2ell,2a),
D=(2ell)!/(a!*b!*(c+d)!),    E=binom(2ell,2b).           (10)
```

Direct expansion in `Z[x,y]` gives

```text
B^2*T_(2ell)
 -[E*B*y+(D*B-E*A)*x]*T_ell
 =Delta*x^2,                                             (11)

Delta=C*B^2-D*A*B+E*A^2.                                (12)
```

Thus simultaneous vanishing of the first two rungs is impossible on the
coefficient torus as soon as `Delta!=0`.  In fact the coefficient has a
uniform sign.

## 3. The binomial determinant is strictly negative

Swap the two rays if necessary so that

```text
a<b,                    c>d,                    h=b-a=c-d>0. (13)
```

Put

```text
K=binom(2ell,ell),
N_1=binom(2a,a)binom(2c,c),
M=binom(c+d,c),
N_2=binom(2b,b)binom(2d,d).                              (14)
```

Cancelling factorials in (10) gives three normalized identities:

```text
C/A^2=K/N_1,              D/(A*B)=K/M,
E/B^2=K/N_2.                                             (15)
```

Consequently

```text
Delta/(A^2 B^2)=K*(1/N_1-1/M+1/N_2).                    (16)
```

The two product comparisons are

```text
binom(2c,c)/M
 =product_(i=1)^h (2d+h+i)/(d+i) >=2^h,

M/binom(2d,d)
 =product_(i=1)^h (2d+i)/(d+i) <2^h.                    (17)
```

Also `binom(2a,a)>=2^a` and `binom(2b,b)>2^b`; the second is strict because
`b>=2`.  Therefore

```text
N_1>=2^(a+h)M=2^b M>=4M,
N_2>2^(b-h)M=2^a M>=2M.                                 (18)
```

Equations (16)--(18) imply

```text
1/N_1+1/N_2<1/M,
Delta<0.                                                 (19)
```

Combining (11) and (19),

```text
(T_ell,T_(2ell)) != (0,0) on (C*)^3.                    (20)
```

This proves a finite, DvdK-free noncancellation theorem on a face for which
every nonempty return row has multiple channels.

## 4. A horizontal affine Wick face

Take

```text
P=alpha*Z^6+beta*Z^6*W^8+gamma*Z^6*W^24.                (21)
```

Its charges are `(6,-2,-18)`.  Every balanced term in `P^m` has both Wick
exponents equal to `6m`, so with

```text
f=alpha*u^6+beta*u^(-2)+gamma*u^(-18),
C_m=CT(f^m),                                             (22)
```

one has

```text
E[P^m]=(6m)!*C_m.                                        (23)
```

The primitive balanced rays have common mass four, and their semigroup is
free.  The two-rung identity specializes to

```text
C_8-7*alpha*(alpha^2*gamma+9*beta^3)*C_4
 =-224*alpha^2*beta^6.                                  (24)
```

The right side is nonzero on the coefficient torus.  Hence `E[P^4]` and
`E[P^8]` cannot both vanish, although the channel counts at levels `4j` are
exactly `j+1` for every `j>=1`.

This example is deliberately different from THM-2631's
`alpha Z^6+beta W^2+gamma W^18`.  The charge semigroup is the same, but the
horizontal lift (21) makes the Wick factorial common across each balanced
row.  That common factorial is the sidecar that transfers the Laurent ideal
identity without changing its coefficients.

## 5. Boundary and scope

Three boundaries are load-bearing.

1. If the two primitive equal-mass rays have lattice index greater than one,
   the first slice contains interior channels and formulas (9)--(10) are
   incomplete.  The `(1,6,-4)` hostile detects this immediately.
2. If the support charges are not distinct, `a=b` may occur.  The family
   `a=b=1,c=d` has `Delta=0`; injective support indexing cannot be dropped.
3. For a general Gaussian support, balanced channels of one mass need not
   share a Wick factorial.  Equation (24) transfers to (21), not to an
   arbitrary off-face polynomial with the same charges.

The result does not replace THM-2022's whole-face Frobenius/DvdK mechanism on
arbitrary supports.  It proves no higher-dimensional GMC statement and no
LRC theorem.  It does suggest a precise formalization extension: first prove
the ring identity (11), then the natural-number binomial inequality (19), and
finally instantiate the horizontal support (21).  No such Lean theorem is
claimed here.
