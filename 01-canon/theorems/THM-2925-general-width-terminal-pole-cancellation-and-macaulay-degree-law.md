# THM-2925 -- general-width terminal-pole cancellation and Macaulay degree law

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

No mathematical claim is established by this reservation.  The intended
target is a general Laurent-pole proof that every order-`m` mean-difference
factorial tensor of width `M` is cleared by

```text
[prod_(r=1)^(M-1)(n+r)^(m-1)](n+M)^(m-2),
```

with scaled degree at most `(m-1)M-1`, and hence that the fixed
`20Q+10C+6F` Macaulay minor has degree at most `58M-36`.  Promotion
requires a complete terminal-residue cancellation proof, careful control at
infinity, exact hostile checks, and an independent audit.  The theorem will
not assert uniform minor nonvanishing, Gregory--Newton positivity, or
arbitrary-width SFC(4).
