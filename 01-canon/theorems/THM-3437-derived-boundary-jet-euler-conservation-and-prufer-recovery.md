# THM-3437 -- derived boundary-jet Euler conservation and Prüfer-presence/Tate-module recovery

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.**

## 1. Exact statement

Use the notation and hypotheses of THM-3433 and THM-3436.  Thus `K` has
characteristic zero,

```text
P=a x+b+g(x)z^d,       a!=0, d>=2,
```

`K'/K` is a finite normal extension which splits the nonconstant polynomial
`g` and contains the `d`th roots of unity, and

```text
g=gamma product_j (x-alpha_j)^(e_j),
N=deg(rad(g)).                                           (1)
```

Fix a repeated root `alpha_i`, a sector `1<=sigma<=d`, and put

```text
beta_i=a alpha_i+b,
lambda=P-beta_i,
A=K'[lambda],       R_q=A/(lambda^q),       q>=1,
M=Cbar_(sigma-1)=C_(sigma-1) tensor_K K'.                (2)
```

Let `epsilon_(i,sigma)` be the selected-root bit

```text
epsilon_(i,sigma)=1
 iff d divides sigma(e_i-1)
     and d divides sigma e_j for every j!=i;             (3)
epsilon_(i,sigma)=0 otherwise.
```

Then the derived Artin packet is concentrated in homological degrees zero
and one, and its homology is free over `R_q`:

```text
Tor_0^A(R_q,M) ~= R_q^(N-1+epsilon_(i,sigma)),
Tor_1^A(R_q,M) ~= R_q^epsilon_(i,sigma),
Tor_n^A(R_q,M) =0                         for n>=2.      (4)
```

Consequently the derived Euler rank does not jump:

```text
rank_Rq Tor_0-rank_Rq Tor_1=N-1.                         (5)
```

The transition `R_(q+1)->R_q` may be lifted between the standard two-term
resolutions by the identity in degree zero and multiplication by `lambda` in
degree one.  Under these maps, the inverse limits of `(4)` are

```text
lim_q Tor_0^A(R_q,M) ~= K'[[lambda]]^(N-1+epsilon),
lim_q Tor_1^A(R_q,M) ~= K'[[lambda]]^epsilon.            (6)
```

Thus ordinary completion is the first line of `(6)` and still discards every
`lambda`-divisible Prüfer arm.  The second line is the `lambda`-adic Tate
module of the selected arm and therefore recovers exactly its presence, not
the Prüfer module itself.  Under the different, canonical kernel inclusions

```text
M[lambda^q] -> M[lambda^(q+1)],
```

one instead has

```text
colim_q Tor_1^A(R_q,M) ~= Pr_lambda^epsilon.             (6a)
```

It does **not** recover the THM-3433 filtration slope `e_i-1` or its DeathBar
intercept; those require the original stage filtration as an additional
sidecar.

## 2. Proof

The standard free resolution

```text
0 -> A --lambda^q--> A -> R_q -> 0                       (7)
```

shows immediately that higher Tor vanishes and that

```text
Tor_1^A(R_q,M)=ker(lambda^q:M->M).                       (8)
```

THM-3433 classifies all geometric primary torsion of `M`.  At the support
`lambda=0` it is exactly one copy of

```text
Pr_lambda=A[lambda^(-1)]/A                              (9)
```

when `(3)` holds, and it is zero otherwise.  The boundary values `beta_j`
are pairwise distinct because `a!=0`, so torsion at another root has no
`lambda`-power kernel.  The torsion-free quotient has none by definition.
Hence `(8)` is zero when `epsilon=0`.  When `epsilon=1`, it is generated by
the class of `lambda^(-q)`:

```text
Pr_lambda[lambda^q]
 =span_K'{lambda^(-q),...,lambda^(-1)}
 ~=A/(lambda^q)=R_q.                                    (10)
```

This proves the degree-one and higher lines of `(4)`.  THM-3436 gives

```text
M/lambda^q M ~= R_q^N          if epsilon=1,
M/lambda^q M ~= R_q^(N-1)      if epsilon=0,             (11)
```

which is the degree-zero line.  Subtraction gives `(5)`.

For the transition in `q`, the square

```text
A --lambda^(q+1)--> A
| lambda             | identity
v                    v
A --lambda^q-------> A                                  (12)
```

is a chain map lifting `R_(q+1)->R_q`.  On `(10)` it sends a principal part
`u` to `lambda u`; after the identification

```text
R_q -> Pr_lambda[lambda^q],
f mod lambda^q |-> f lambda^(-q),                       (13)
```

this is exactly coefficient truncation `R_(q+1)->R_q`.  All transition maps
in `(4)` are therefore surjective.  Both towers satisfy the Mittag--Leffler
condition, so their first derived inverse limits vanish.  Their inverse limits
are the power-series modules `(6)`; no derived-limit correction remains.
Separately, the inclusions of the principal-part kernels in `(10)` exhaust
`Pr_lambda`, proving `(6a)`.  These are different transition systems: replacing
the inverse-limit map by inclusion would change the object rather than refine
the same limit.
**QED.**

## 4. Boundaries

- The root must be repeated.  At a simple root the factor `H` in
  `lambda=(x-alpha_i)H` is not a unit along the vertical component, so the
  THM-3436 Artin-freeness input is unavailable.
- The result is geometric over `K'`.  Galois descent repackages conjugate
  supports, but no canonical split coordinate over `K` is asserted.
- Formula `(10)` detects one Prüfer arm, not its inherited stage filtration.
  All multiplicities `e_i>1` give the same unfiltered `Tor_1` packet.
- Replacing the entire all-iterate family by a finite prefix loses both limit
  statements, even though each individual formula `(4)` remains valid.
