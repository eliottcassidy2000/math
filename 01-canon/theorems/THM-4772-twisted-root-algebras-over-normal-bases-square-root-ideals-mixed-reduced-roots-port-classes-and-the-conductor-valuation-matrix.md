# THM-4772 — Twisted root algebras over normal bases

## Statement

`R` Noetherian normal, `2 in R^x`, `K = Frac R`; `b_1,...,b_n in R\{0}` with `F_2`-independent
squareclasses; `A = R[u]/(u_i^2 - b_i)`; for `z in F_2^n`, `b^z = prod_{z_i = 1} b_i` and `c_z` the
divisorial ideal with `v_p(c_z) = floor(v_p(b^z)/2)` at every height-one prime `p`.

1. **Normalization.** `A' := integral closure of A in K ⊗ A = ⊕_z c_z^{-1} u_z`.
2. **Mixed reduced roots.** `v_p(c_z) - sum_{i in z} v_p(c_{e_i}) = floor(#{i in z : v_p(b_i) odd}/2)`.
3. **Port classes** (`R` Dedekind). `c_z^{-1}u_z` is free iff `[c_z] = 0`; the Steinitz class of `A'` is
   `-sum_z [c_z]`.
4. **Conductor.** `(A : A') = ⊕_z d_z u_z`, `v_p(d_z) = max(0, max_{z'}(v_p(c_{z'}) - v_p(b^{z∧z'})))`;
   for the fibre product `B = ⊕ R b^{e(z)} u_z` of two complementary packets,
   `v_p((B:A')_z) = max(v_p(b^{e(z)}), max_{z'}(v_p(b^{e(z⊕z')}) - v_p(b^{z∧z'}) + v_p(c_{z'})))`.
5. **Compatibility bank.** `x in B` is in `(B:A')` iff `x·c_{z'}^{-1}u_{z'} ⊂ B` for all `z'`; the tests
   with `c_{z'} = R` reduce to CONTINUATION D2's single-root tests; mixed reduced roots give tests
   not implied by single ones (e.g. `b = (15,21,55)`: scalars need `15 | a`).

## Proof

See the source note §1–§3: the sign group acts by `R`-algebra automorphisms, `2 in R^x` makes the
isotypic projections integral, `f u_z` is integral iff `f^2 b^z in R`, and divisorial ideals are
determined by valuations. The conductor is a sign-stable submodule, hence componentwise, and the
component condition is `f b^{z∧z'} in (R : c_{z'}^{-1}) = c_{z'}`.

## Mechanism, boundary, hostiles

- The additive carry in (2) is what the bare multiplicative monoid (BOUNDARY B4) cannot see; twists
  with shared odd primes make the normalization strictly larger than the product of single reduced
  roots, and the test bank must grow accordingly.
- Non-UFD base: the obstruction to a global reduced root is an ideal class, not a missing factor; the
  total module can be free while every port is not.
- Prime 2: `(u+v)/2` is integral for `u^2 = 6, v^2 = 10` over `Z`; the theorem does not extend to
  residue characteristic two without the classical wild-ramification analysis.
- Nothing about rational points; THM-4773 handles the point-level question for the hourglass.
