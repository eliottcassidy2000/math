# THM-2659 -- three nonlinear rungs defeat a free three-ray collision face

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

THM-2639 proves that two return rungs suffice on a free equal-mass two-ray
face even though no return row is singleton.  The next question is whether
the mechanism survives a genuinely two-dimensional channel simplex.  The
answer is yes in the following exact example, and the third rung is
necessary.

## 1. A free equal-mass three-ray semigroup

Use the four distinct charges

```text
q=(24,9,4,-6).                                           (1)
```

For a channel `r=(r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4)` put

```text
q dot r=24r_1+9r_2+4r_3-6r_4.                           (2)
```

The three primitive balanced rays are

```text
v_1=(1,0,0,4),
v_2=(0,2,0,3),
v_3=(0,0,3,2).                                           (3)
```

Each has mass five.  More strongly, every nonnegative integral solution of
`q dot r=0` has a unique expression

```text
r=i v_1+j v_2+k v_3,             i,j,k in N.             (4)
```

Indeed, reducing (2) modulo two gives `2|r_2`; write `r_2=2j`.
Reducing the resulting equation modulo three gives `3|r_3`; write
`r_3=3k`.  Equation (2) then forces

```text
r_4=4r_1+3j+2k,                                        (5)
```

which is (4) with `i=r_1`.  Uniqueness is immediate.  Equivalently, the
four maximal minors of the ray matrix have absolute values

```text
6,24,9,4,                 gcd(6,24,9,4)=1.              (6)
```

Thus the rays generate the saturated balanced lattice as well as the real
cone.

The mass of (4) is `5(i+j+k)`.  Hence the nonempty return levels are exactly
the multiples `5n`, and their complete channel fibres are

```text
B_(5n)={i v_1+j v_2+k v_3:i+j+k=n},
|B_(5n)|=binom(n+2,2).                                  (7)
```

Every positive return level is collided; the first already has three
channels.  No private-row linear decoder is available at any rung.

## 2. The first three return polynomials

Let

```text
f(u)=alpha u^24+beta u^9+gamma u^4+delta u^(-6),
x=alpha delta^4,
y=beta^2 delta^3,
z=gamma^3 delta^2,                                      (8)
```

and write `T_m=CT(f^m)`.  The channel description (7) and multinomial
weights give

```text
T_5=5(x+2y+2z),                                         (9)

T_10=15(3x^2+24xy+56xz+14y^2+168yz+14z^2),            (10)

T_15=455(x^3+18x^2y+66x^2z+33xy^2+660xyz+99xz^2
         +11y^3+495y^2z+396yz^2+11z^3).                (11)
```

These are the complete rows, not selected subchannels.

## 3. Exact three-rung Bezout certificate

Assume first that the coefficient tuple lies in the torus, so `x,y,z` are
nonzero.  If `T_5=0`, then

```text
x=-2y-2z.                                                (12)
```

Put `t=y/z`.  Substituting (12) into (10)--(11) gives

```text
T_10/z^2=-30 Q(t),       Q(t)=11t^2-16t+43,             (13)

T_15/z^3=1365 C(t),      C(t)=3t^3-169t^2-182t+23.     (14)
```

The following identity is direct in `Z[t]`:

```text
(-157251t^2+8616505t+23433513) Q(t)
 +(576587t+48544) C(t)
 =1008757571.                                           (15)
```

Equivalently,

```text
Res_t(Q,C)=1008757571=127*7942973 !=0.                  (16)
```

Therefore `Q` and `C` have no common complex root.  Equations (12)--(16)
show

```text
(T_5,T_10,T_15)!=(0,0,0) on (C*)^4.                    (17)
```

In fact the three homogeneous forms have no common nonzero projective zero.
The chart `z=0` gives from (9) `x=-2y`, after which (10) is `-330y^2`, so
it is also empty away from the origin.

## 4. The third rung is sharply necessary

The first two rungs can vanish together on the coefficient torus.  The
quadratic in (13) has

```text
disc(Q)=-1636!=0,        Q(0)=43,        Q(-1)=70.       (18)
```

It therefore has two complex roots, neither `0` nor `-1`.  For either root
`t`, the projective point

```text
(x:y:z)=(-2t-2:t:1)                                    (19)
```

lies in the torus and satisfies `T_5=T_10=0`.  Identity (15) says exactly
that `T_15` is nonzero at both points.  Thus this example is not a disguised
two-rung theorem: three is the minimal number of initial return rows.

## 5. Horizontal Gaussian Wick realization

Let the Wick functional on `C[Z,W]` be

```text
L(Z^A W^B)=1_(A=B) A!,                                  (20)
```

and take

```text
P=alpha Z^24+beta Z^24 W^15+gamma Z^24 W^20
   +delta Z^24 W^30.                                    (21)
```

The charge `Z-degree minus W-degree` of the four terms is exactly (1).
Every term in `P^m` has `Z`-degree `24m`.  A channel `r` has

```text
W-degree=24m-q dot r.                                   (22)
```

Consequently precisely the balanced channels survive, and every survivor
has the same Wick factorial:

```text
L(P^m)=(24m)! T_m.                                      (23)
```

Combining (17) and (23), for `alpha,beta,gamma,delta in C*`,

```text
at least one of L(P^5), L(P^10), L(P^15) is nonzero.     (24)
```

This is a horizontal Gaussian moment certificate on a face whose channel
Hilbert function grows quadratically and has no singleton at any positive
level.

## 6. Boundaries and scope

Four qualifications are load-bearing.

1. The theorem is an explicit three-ray example, not an all-range theorem
   for every free equal-mass three-ray face.  A uniform resultant sign or
   nonvanishing law in the ray parameters remains open.
2. Freeness is essential to (7) and therefore to the complete formulas
   (9)--(11).  An equal-mass cone of lattice index greater than one has
   interior channels already on its first slice.
3. The horizontal support (21) is what makes the factorial common across
   each row.  A support with the same charge semigroup but unequal Wick
   heights does not inherit (15) coefficientwise.
4. The coefficient-torus hypothesis cannot simply be deleted.  If
   `delta=0`, all four charges remaining in (21) are positive, so every
   positive moment vanishes for the unrelated null-cone reason.

This theorem neither replaces THM-2022 on arbitrary two-dimensional
supports nor proves a higher-dimensional Gaussian Moment Conjecture.  Its
gain is a new finite nonlinear mechanism beyond the two-ray Bezout identity:

```text
quadratically growing permanent channel collision
  + three complete return rungs
  -> exact unit ideal on projective channel space.       (25)
```
