# THM-4389 -- weight-thirteen row-ten stability of the elliptic carrier

**PROVED FINITE-ROW RELATIVE TO THM-4308/4315/4380/4385 + VERIFIED-EXACT +
INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.** This theorem uses the fixed source-normal finite-row
chart. It proves no chart entry, seam entry, all-row lift, polynomial
termination, Keller pair, `JC(2)`, or `DC(2)`; all remain open.

## Result in one sentence

The first omitted weight-thirteen face splits anisotropically: the coefficient
of `p^2 y^3` is tangent through row ten, while the coefficient of `p^5 y`
deforms THM-4385's single elliptic curve into a non-isotrivial elliptic pencil.
Thus the fixed elliptic target and its simultaneous-sign quotient are
weight-twelve-cap-specific, although the genus-one double-cover architecture
persists one weight farther.

## 1. Exact universe and inheritance pass

Adjoin to THM-4308's residual-weight-at-most-twelve source exactly the two
weight-thirteen monomials

```text
a p^5 y + b p^2 y^3,              a=[p^5 y]H, b=[p^2 y^3]H.       (1)
```

These are the `A,B` channels of THM-4226; lower-case letters are used here to
avoid collision with the source coordinate `A(x,t)`. Under
`p=t(1+x^2t), y=xtp`, their complete changes to the source rows are

```text
dG_7 = a x,
dG_8 = (6a+b)x^3,
dG_9 = 5(3a+b)x^5,
dG_10=10(2a+b)x^7,
dG_11=5(3a+2b)x^9,
dG_12=(6a+5b)x^11,
dG_13=(a+b)x^13.                                             (2)
```

The closest proved mechanism is THM-4380's complete row-ten bracket/depth
compiler. The canonical hostile is THM-4385's warning that its elliptic curve
was extracted only after imposing a weight-twelve cap. The corrected near miss
is that preserving genus one is weaker than preserving the same elliptic curve
or the same deck involution. The least-used sidecar is the labelled distinction
between the two weight-thirteen monomials: forgetting it would miss the
tangent/normal split below.

```text
  -707284001250 Phi^2 a+6793915500000 Phi eta^2
```


## 5. A non-isotrivial elliptic pencil, not one fixed sign quotient

Fix `a` and homogenize `(8)`:

```text
C_a: H_3(P,e)-c a P^2T-L(P,e)T^2=0,                        (15)
```

where `H_3-L=D_0` at `T=1`. The rational point `O=[0:0:1]` persists. Put
`r=e/P` and retain THM-4385's polynomials

```text
A(r)=353642000625r^3+6793915500000r^2
     +50541940696500r+7231154026500,
B(r)=91584545734393856r+631918028977864704.                 (16)
```

On the function field, `(15)` is the quadratic

```text
A(r)P^2-c a P-B(r)=0.                                       (17)
```

Completing the square with `Y=2A(r)P-ca` gives

```text
Y^2=4A(r)B(r)+c^2a^2.                                      (18)
```

So the ratio `r` remains a degree-two elliptic coordinate for every smooth
fibre, but its deck transformation is now

```text
P -> ca/A(r)-P.                                             (19)
```

Only at `a=0` is `(19)` simultaneous sign change. Algebraically, the total
parity operation exchanges the fibres `C_a` and `C_-a`; it is not elliptic
negation on a fixed nonzero fibre.

The pencil is genuinely non-isotrivial. Modulo `7`, the quartic in `(18)` is

```text
q_a(r)=r^4+r^3+3r^2+2r+(2+a^2).                            (20)
```

For a binary quartic with invariants `I,J`, put
`Delta_flat=4I^3-J^2`. Exact reduction gives

```text
I=-2a^2-1,       J=-3,
Delta_flat=3a^6+a^4-3a^2+1                    in F_7[a].    (21)
```

The last polynomial has no root in `F_7`. At `a=0` and `a=1`, respectively,

```text
(I,J,Delta_flat,I^3/Delta_flat)=(6,4,1,6), (4,4,2,4).       (22)
```

Both fibres therefore have good reduction at `7`, while their (fixed
nonzero multiple of) `j`-invariants differ. Hence `C_0` and `C_1` are not
geometrically isomorphic and the pencil is non-isotrivial.

There is a useful strengthening over `Q`: every rational `a` gives a smooth
fibre. If `v_7(a)>=0`, `(21)` is a unit because it has no residue root. If
`v_7(a)<0`, the term `3a^6` has uniquely least valuation. Thus
`Delta_flat(a)!=0` in both cases. Over an algebraically closed
characteristic-zero base the degree-six discriminant does have finitely many
algebraic exceptional parameters; only the generic and rational-parameter
claims are made here.

The `b` direction is stronger: it does not change `(15)--(18)` at all. It is
an exact cylinder direction through row ten, although it changes selected
row lifts.
