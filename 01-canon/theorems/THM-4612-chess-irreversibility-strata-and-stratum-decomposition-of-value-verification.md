# THM-4612 — irreversibility strata and the stratum decomposition of value verification

## Setting

A state `s` of chess is (placement, side to move, castling rights, en-passant square, half-move clock, repetition record). `G_inf` is the game without the move-count and repetition rules, infinite play counting as a draw; `v(s) in {+1, 0, -1}` is its value from the mover's view (Zermelo). Rule-chess differs from `G_inf` only at cursed wins; a trap in `G_inf` certifies at least a draw in rule-chess.

For a state `s` put `N(s)` = number of pieces on the board (kings included), `P(s)` = number of pawns, `A(s)` = the sum over pawns of the number of ranks still to travel (a White pawn on rank `r` contributes `8-r`, a Black pawn `r-1`), `C(s)` = number of castling rights still held. `Phi(s) = (N, P, A, C)` with the lexicographic order. The **stratum** of `s` is the triple (material signature with bishop colours, pawn placement with colours, castling rights).

## Statement

1. **(The potential.)** For every legal move `s -> s'`, `Phi(s') <= Phi(s)`, with equality iff the move is a non-capturing move of a knight, bishop, queen, king or rook that forfeits no castling right; equality holds iff `s'` lies in the stratum of `s`. The `Phi`-decreasing moves are exactly captures, pawn moves (pushes and promotions), castling, and king/rook moves that forfeit a castling right.
2. **(The 118 bound.)** Every game contains at most 118 moves that are pawn moves or captures, at most 122 `Phi`-decreasing moves, and visits at most 123 strata. The per-file lemma behind it: the two pawns that start on one file make at most 10 moves in all if neither of them ever captures (at most 4 if neither is captured).
3. **(Stratum decomposition of verification.)** Let `L` assign a value in `{+1, 0, -1}` (mover's view) to every legal state of `G_inf`. Then `L = v` iff
   (T) `L` is correct on terminal states (checkmate `-1`; stalemate and dead positions `0`);
   (B) at every non-terminal state `L(s) = max over legal moves of -L(s')`, successors in lower strata being read from strata verified earlier (the strata form a finite DAG under `Phi`);
   (R) inside each stratum there is a ranking `rho` into the naturals with: at every state with `L(s) = +1`, some move leads to a state `s'` with `L(s') = -1` that is a terminal or a lower-stratum state or satisfies `rho(s') < rho(s)`; at every non-terminal state with `L(s) = -1`, every move leads to a state `s'` with `L(s') = +1` that is a lower-stratum state or satisfies `rho(s') < rho(s)`.
   Equivalently: the states labelled `+-1` inside a stratum are the least fixed point of the win/loss operator relative to the boundary labels. This is Pavlov's capture-quiet verification theorem with the material class refined to the stratum: pawn pushes and castling-right losses move from "quiet" to "boundary".
4. **(One-sided spuriousness.)** If `L` satisfies (T) and (B) only, then `L(s) = v(s)` for every state with `v(s) != 0`; `L` may label a drawn state `+1` (a two-state cycle with labels `(+1, -1)` is consistent). An all-draw labelling is not consistent in any class containing a mate.
5. **(The pawn mask is a stratum invariant and is digital topology.)** On a stratum the pawn placement `P` is constant, hence so is the multilayer visibility graph (rook rays, bishop rays, queen rays, knight moves, king steps, pawn attacks conditioned on `P`). Components of the rook-ray graph on the empty squares are the 4-connected components of the complement of `P`; components of the bishop-ray graph on colour `c` are the diagonal-step components of (colour `c`) minus `P`, and depend only on `P intersect (colour c)`; components of the queen-ray graph are the 8-connected components. Ray distances are not preserved.
6. **(Value symmetries.)** `sigma` = rank reflection `(f, r) -> (f, 9-r)` composed with the colour swap, the swap of the side to move, the swap of the two sides' castling rights and the reflection of the en-passant square is an involution on legal states with `v(sigma s) = v(s)`, i.e. `V(sigma s) = -V(s)` in White's view. It fixes the board of the initial position. On states without castling rights the file reflection `rho` preserves `v`, and `{1, rho, sigma, rho sigma}` is a Klein four-group; `rho sigma` (180-degree rotation with colour swap) is not a symmetry of states with castling rights (it sends a king with rights to the d-file). On pawnless states without castling rights all eight board isometries composed with the colour swap negate `V`.

## Proof

**1.** A capture lowers `N`. A non-capturing pawn move keeps `N`, lowers `P` at promotion and otherwise lowers `A` by one or two. A non-capturing non-pawn move keeps `N, P, A` and lowers `C` iff it is a castling move or a king/rook move from a square still carrying a right. No coordinate ever rises: pieces are never added (promotion exchanges a pawn for a piece, keeping `N`), pawns never move backwards, rights are never regained. Equality therefore holds exactly for the listed moves, and those are exactly the moves that keep material, pawn placement and rights, i.e. the stratum. The en-passant flag appears only after a double push, which lowered `A`. ∎

**2.** *Per-file lemma.* Two pawns on one file never pass each other. If neither captures, both stay on the file. If neither is captured they end adjacent (White on rank `r`, Black on `r+1`) after `(r-2) + (7-(r+1)) = 4` moves. If one is captured, say the Black pawn on rank `r_b` while the White pawn is on `r_w <= r_b - 1`, the Black pawn has made at most `7 - r_b <= 6 - r_w <= 4` moves and the White pawn makes at most 6 in the whole game; symmetrically in the other order; if both are captured the count is smaller still. Hence at most 10. The exhaustive single-file model in `strata_census.py` (single and double steps, an outside capture of either pawn at any moment) returns 10 and 4.
*Count.* Let `k` be the number of starting files on which at least one of the two pawns captures during the game. Those `k` pawns are distinct and each captures at least once, so captures by non-pawns number at most `30 - k`. Pawn moves on the other `8-k` files are at most 10 each (lemma) and at most 12 on each of the `k` files. So pawn-moves-or-captures are at most `10(8-k) + 12k + 30 - k = 110 + k <= 118`. A castling right is lost at most 4 times by a move that is neither a pawn move nor a capture. Hence at most 122 `Phi`-decreasing moves and at most 123 strata. ∎ (Attainment of 118 and the resulting 5898.5 / 8848.5 move lengths are Labelle's, CITED.)

**4 first.** Induction on the DTM `d` of a state with `v(s) = +-1`. `d = 0`: a mate, correct by (T). If `v(s) = +1` with DTM `d`, some move reaches `s'` with `v(s') = -1` and DTM `d-1`; by induction `L(s') = -1`, so the maximum in (B) is `+1`. If `v(s) = -1` with DTM `d`, every move reaches a state with `v = +1` and DTM at most `d-1`, all labelled `+1` by induction, so `L(s) = -1`. The two-state cycle shows over-claiming is consistent; a class containing a mate forces the mate's predecessors to `+1`, so an all-draw labelling violates (B) there. ∎

**3.** `v` satisfies (T), (B), (R) with `rho = DTM`. Conversely, induct along the stratum DAG, assuming `L = v` on all lower strata. Within the stratum, first show `L(s) = +-1` implies `v(s) = L(s)` by induction on `rho(s)` (lower-stratum and terminal successors are base cases): at `L(s) = +1` the move guaranteed by (R) reaches a state already known to have `v = -1`, so `v(s) = +1`; at `L(s) = -1`, `s` is a mate or every move reaches a state known to have `v = +1`, so `v(s) = -1`. Next, if `L(s) = 0` but `v(s) != 0`, statement 4 (which uses (T) and (B) only, with the lower strata as verified boundary) gives `L(s) = v(s) != 0`, a contradiction. Hence `L = v` on the stratum. ∎

**5.** Constancy: a move changes `P` only by moving or capturing a pawn, both `Phi`-decreasing (statement 1). A ray between two empty squares is a chain of single steps through its interior squares, which are empty; conversely a single step is a ray. So ray connectivity equals step connectivity for the orthogonal (rook), diagonal (bishop) and eight-direction (queen) step graphs on the complement of `P`. A diagonal ray between squares of colour `c` passes through squares of colour `c` only, so pawns of the other colour never obstruct it. A rook crosses an open rank in one ray move but seven steps, so distances differ. ∎ (Checked on 300 random masks for all three piece types.)

**6.** `sigma` maps legal placements to legal placements: pawn directions reverse with the colour swap and the rank reflection together, so every pawn move, capture, double push and en-passant capture of `s` corresponds to one of `sigma s`; king, knight, bishop, rook and queen moves are reflection-invariant; castling of one side on the back rank corresponds to castling of the other side on the opposite back rank (kings stay on the e-file, rooks on the a- and h-files); checks, mates, stalemates and dead positions correspond. So `sigma` is a bijection of the game graphs exchanging the roles of the players, whence `v(sigma s) = v(s)`. The board of `s0` is fixed by the rank reflection with colour swap. The file reflection preserves all move rules except castling (it moves the king to the d-file), so it is a symmetry exactly on states without castling rights, and there `rho` commutes with `sigma` and the group is Klein four; `rho sigma` is the rotation. Without pawns and rights the whole dihedral group of the board acts, and composing with the colour swap negates `V`. ∎

## Scope and boundary

- Statements 3-4 are for `G_inf`. Rule-chess needs the half-move clock in the state; then the same proof goes through with `DTZ`-type rankings (not carried out here).
- Statement 2 is an inequality; the attainment of 118 is cited, not re-derived.
- Statement 5 concerns components only. Attack relations (which squares are controlled) are a different layer; the certificate machinery of THM-4613 uses the full move generator, not the mask.
- The KPvK stratum census: 331,352 states, 48 strata of 6,822-7,004 states (every stratum passes (T), (B), (R) with `rho = DTM` in `verify_strata.py`, 2 s in all), boundary classes KQvK (368,452 states, longest mate 20 plies), KRvK (399,112; 32 plies), KBvK, KNvK (all draws); KPvK has 124,960 white-to-move wins, 38,368 white-to-move draws, 70,420 black-to-move draws, 97,604 black-to-move losses, longest win 38 plies to the promotion boundary.

## Reading

The potential turns the "material universes" of the owner's thread into a well-founded coordinate with three more layers below material (pawn count, pawn advancement, castling rights). The reversible part of chess is what happens on one stratum: a fixed pawn mask and the non-pawn pieces moving. That is where fortresses live (THM-4613), where Pavlov-style verification is cheapest (two-king models in KPvK), and where the third pasted note's visibility atlas is literally a constant. The one-sided spuriousness statement locates the only circularity danger of self-consistent tablebase checking: a cycle of mutually supporting win labels, which a ranking excludes; a draw can never be claimed by mistake by a locally consistent labelling.
