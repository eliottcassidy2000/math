# THM-2965 -- modular signed-pair complements and the V4 compound shadow

## 1. Inheritance and nonoverlap

THM-2951 proves that for the full signed-pair group

```text
W=(C2^3) semidirect S3
```

there is no equivariant linear map from the fifth compound of the real
six-dimensional three-pair carrier to its balanced middle sector.  Its sharp
hostile keeps norm and fifth-compound energy while changing the V4 trace
frame.  THM-2595 supplies a different binary/ternary phenomenon:
`H^1(C2*C3,F2^2)=F2`, although both free-factor restrictions vanish.  That
class changes the affine quotient image from a point stabilizer `S3` to a
transitive `S4`.

The present theorem holds the affine `S4` quotient action fixed and asks how
it lifts through the central global conjugation of the three signed pairs.
The answer is a second, distinct bit.  Exactly one of the two lifts admits a
linear fifth-to-balanced symmetry shadow.  No edge label, primitive point,
Keller graph, or LRC current is identified.

## 2. The two complements

Let

```text
U=E_1 (+) E_2 (+) E_3,                 dim_R E_i=2,
E_i=<e_(i,0),e_(i,1)>.
```

Write an element of `W=F2^3 semidirect S3` as `(delta,sigma)`, acting by

```text
e_(i,b) |-> e_(sigma(i),b+delta_i).                  (1)
```

The global pair flip

```text
z=((1,1,1),1)
```

is central.  On oriented half-systems `epsilon in F2^3`, it is global
conjugation, hence trivial on the four complement classes

```text
Omega=F2^3/<(1,1,1)>.
```

Therefore

```text
W/<z> = AGL(2,2) = S4.                                 (2)
```

Put

```text
p(delta)=delta_1+delta_2+delta_3 mod2,
s(sigma)=sign(sigma) in F2.
```

Then `(2)` has exactly two subgroup complements:

```text
H_0=ker p,
H_1=ker(p+s).                                          (3)
```

Indeed, `H_0` has order `24`, avoids `z`, and maps isomorphically to the
quotient; moreover `W=<z> x H_0`.  Any other complement is the graph of a
homomorphism `S4=H_0 -> <z>=C2`.  Since `S4_ab=C2`, there are exactly the
trivial and sign graphs.  They are the two normal, nonconjugate subgroups in
`(3)`.

Thus `H_0` is the even-pair-flip lift, while `H_1` satisfies

```text
number of within-pair flips mod2 = sign(pair permutation).     (4)
```

The latter is the orientation-preserving signed-axis group
`Aut(Q8)=S4` from THM-2887.

## 3. The complement-sensitive compound representations

Set

```text
L=Lambda^5 U,                  dim L=6,
B=E_1 wedge E_2 wedge E_3,     dim B=8.                (5)
```

The determinant of `(delta,sigma)` on `U` is `(-1)^p(delta)`, since a
permutation of the two-dimensional blocks has positive determinant.  Hence

```text
Lambda^5 U = det(U) tensor U*.                         (6)
```

In the balanced basis

```text
b_epsilon=e_(1,epsilon_1) wedge e_(2,epsilon_2)
          wedge e_(3,epsilon_3),                       (7)
```

the same element acts by its cube-vertex permutation and the wedge-reordering
sign `(-1)^s`.

Write the real irreducibles of `S4` as `1,sgn,2,3,3sgn`.  Exact character
decomposition gives

```text
H_0:
  L = 1 (+) 2 (+) 3,
  B = 2 sgn (+) 2(3sgn),
  Hom_(H_0)(L,B)=0;                                   (8)

H_1:
  L = sgn (+) 2 (+) 3,
  B = 1 (+) sgn (+) 3 (+) 3sgn,
  dim Hom_(H_1)(L,B)=2.                               (9)
```

Thus THM-2951's full-`W` no-go already persists on the natural even-flip
complement `H_0`, but changes sharply after the synchronized lift `H_1` is
supplied as extra structure.

## 4. The two intertwiners and their exact loss

Using `(6)`, let `x_(i,b)` be the basis dual to `e_(i,b)` after a temporary
volume choice.  Define face--cube incidence and nonincidence by

```text
A(x_(i,b))    = sum_(epsilon_i=b)  b_epsilon,
Abar(x_(i,b)) = sum_(epsilon_i!=b) b_epsilon.          (10)
```

Condition `(4)` identifies the source determinant sign with the target wedge
sign, so both maps are `H_1`-equivariant.  Neither is `H_0`-equivariant: an
odd pair permutation with no pair flip acts without sign on `L` and with sign
on `B`.  There are exactly two `H_1` orbitals on a face/cube-vertex pair, and
`(9)` therefore shows that `A,Abar` span the full Hom space.

As functions on `F2^3`, their columns satisfy

```text
1_(epsilon_i=b)=(1+(-1)^b chi_i)/2.
```

Consequently

```text
rank A=rank Abar=4,
rank(A+Abar)=1,
rank(A-Abar)=3.                                       (11)
```

The total reachable coefficient subspace is exactly the constant and first
Walsh layers.  Quadratic and cubic coefficient layers are destroyed.

Global conjugation acts on coefficient characters by

```text
z chi_S=(-1)^|S| chi_S.                               (12)
```

For the four-coordinate real trace and oriented anti-trace maps,

```text
trace(c)_epsilon     =c_epsilon+c_(epsilon+111),
anti_trace(c)_epsilon=c_epsilon-c_(epsilon+111),
```

one has

```text
A rho_L(z) = -rho_B(z) A,
rank(trace o A)=1,
trace o (A-Abar)=0,
rank(anti_trace o A)=3.                               (13)
```

Hence real trace symmetrization retains at most the constant line.  The new
rank-three first-Walsh channel is `z`-odd anti-trace data, not THM-2950's four
real traces.  No member of the two-dimensional Hom family chooses one
half-system or reconstructs the full trace frame.

There is a second essential boundary.  Equivariance in `(10)` concerns the
finite symmetry representations; it does not intertwine multiplication
compounds.  For

```text
M=diag(2,2,3,3,5,5),
```

the fifth-compound weight omitting one `2` is `900/2=450`, whereas every
balanced third-compound weight is `2*3*5=30`.  Thus

```text
A (Lambda^5 M) != (Lambda^3 M |_B) A.                 (14)
```

This explicit hostile prevents the new representation Hom space from being
silently used as a compound-operator or physical-amplitude intertwiner.

## 5. Why the primes two and three differ

The exact modular correspondence is

```text
PSL2(Z)=C2*C3 -> H_i -> W -> W/<z>=S4 -> Sym(Omega),   i=0,1.  (15)
```

The companion exhibits the semantic tuple convention `(delta,sigma)`:

```text
s_0=(000,(0,2,1)) in H_0,
s_1=(111,(0,2,1))=z s_0 in H_1,
t  =(011,(1,2,0)) in H_0 intersect H_1.               (16)
```

On `Omega`, `s_0` and `s_1` induce the same involution, `t` has order three,
and either marked pair generates the same quotient `S4`.  Nevertheless

```text
Hom_<s_0,t>(L,B)=0,
dim Hom_<s_1,t>(L,B)=2.                               (17)
```

The prime asymmetry is literal.  Since `z` is central of order two,

```text
(z s_0)^2=1,                 (z t)^3=z!=1.            (18)
```

The binary generator can absorb the central conjugation bit without changing
its local order.  The ternary generator cannot: its central twist has order
six.  Thus the `C2` and `C3` free factors are two faces of the same quotient
object but are not interchangeable when one asks for an oriented lift.

This is the minimal hostile to the claim that the binary and ternary faces,
or even their full action on the four affine half-system classes, determine
the oriented compound shadow.  Quotienting to `Omega` erases precisely the
reflection--conjugation synchronization bit.

The bit differs from THM-2595's affine cocycle:

```text
THM-2595: H^1(C2*C3,F2^2), changing the affine quotient image;
THM-2965: Hom(S4,C2), changing a central oriented lift while fixing
          the affine S4 quotient action pointwise.                (19)
```
