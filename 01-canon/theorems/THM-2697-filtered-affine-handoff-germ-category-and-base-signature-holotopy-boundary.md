## 1. The ambient local affine groupoid is `BS(1,13)`-modeled

Put

```text

A_k(x)=x+k/R,         C(x)=x/p,          D=C^(-1).
```

On the universal cover `R`, `A=A_1` and `C` generate the faithful affine
model of `BS(1,13)`:

```text

D A_k = A_(13k) D.                                      (1)
```

On the circle, `A^R=identity`, so this is not literally the universal-cover
Baumslag--Solitar group.  The forward transformations form the endomorphism
category `C_R semidirect N`, where `N` acts on `C_R` by multiplication by

an etale affine **germ groupoid**, a `BS(1,13)`-modeled quotient.  Chronology
uses only the forward endomorphism subcategory generated by `D` and the
translations.  Full germs and forward chronology must not be conflated.

Every forward affine word has the normal form

```text

```

```text
T_(m,l) o T_(n,k)=T_(m+n,l+13^m k mod R).                (3)
```

Equation (3) is associative.  It is the exact source/target arithmetic of a
local affine handoff.  In particular, the mixed map in the unnumbered

### Proof

Both relations in (1) are literal affine identities.  Moving every
translation left across every `D` gives (2), and a direct substitution gives
(3).  Uniqueness holds on the universal cover because the slope determines
`n` and the intercept determines `k`; on the circle forward quotient `k` is
determined modulo `R`.  A full local germ additionally remembers its selected
inverse branch.  The inverse-branch charts make every germ invertible, while
the forward part alone is a category rather than a group.  This distinction
is essential.
