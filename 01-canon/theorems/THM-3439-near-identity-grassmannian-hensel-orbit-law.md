# THM-3439 -- near-identity Grassmannian Hensel orbit law

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.**

The proof below is complete and has passed an independent derivation and
replay audit.

## 1. Exact statement

Let `p` be an odd prime, let

```text
n>=2,       1<=k<n,       c>=1,       a>=c,
```

and put `R_a=Z/p^a Z`.  Let

```text
U=I+p^c E in GL_n(Z_p)                                    (1)
```

and write `Ebar` for `E mod p`.  Scalar multiplication of `U` is invisible
on every Grassmannian, so `(1)` also covers a scalar times a near-identity
matrix after removing that scalar.

Let `Gr_k(R_a)` denote the rank-`k` direct summands of `R_a^n`.  Reduction
gives

```text
pi_(a,c): Gr_k(R_a) -> Gr_k(R_c).                         (2)
```

Assume that `Ebar` has no invariant `k`-plane in `F_p^n`.  Then `U` preserves
every fibre of `(2)`, and on every such fibre:

```text
fibre size       = p^(k(n-k)(a-c)),
every orbit size = p^(a-c),
number of orbits = p^((k(n-k)-1)(a-c)).                  (3)
```

For `a>c`, the action is transitive on every fibre if and only if

```text
k(n-k)=1,
```

equivalently `(n,k)=(2,1)`.  Thus regular transitivity is special to
`P^1`; higher-dimensional projective or Grassmannian fibres split into an
explicit bank of equally long cycles.

The first-carry condition is exact.  If `Ebar` does have an invariant
`k`-plane, then there is a base point of `Gr_k(R_c)` for which **every** one
of the `p^(k(n-k))` lifts to level `c+1` is fixed by `U`.  Consequently,

```text
Ebar has no invariant k-plane
  iff U acts freely on every depth-c fibre at level c+1
  iff U acts freely on every depth-c fibre at every level a>c.             (4)
```

For `k=1`, irreducibility of the characteristic polynomial of `Ebar` is a
convenient sufficient certificate.  In that case every depth-`c` fibre of
`P^(n-1)(R_a)` splits into

```text
p^((n-2)(a-c))                                             (5)
```

cycles of length `p^(a-c)`.

The least-used sidecar is therefore the orbit label in the quotient bank of

## 3. Fibre cardinality

Fix `W_c in Gr_k(R_c)`.  Choose a complement and lift the resulting basis to
`R_a^n`.  In these coordinates, every lift of `W_c` is uniquely the graph of

```text
X: R_a^k -> R_a^(n-k),       X=0 mod p^c.                (6)
```

There are `p^(a-c)` choices for each of the `k(n-k)` entries of `X`, proving
the first formula in `(3)`.  Since `U=I mod p^c`, it fixes `Gr_k(R_c)` and
therefore preserves every fibre.

## 4. First-carry linearization and freeness

For `t>=0`, the odd-prime binomial valuation gives

```text
U^(p^t)=I+p^(c+t) E_t,       E_t=E mod p.                (7)
```

Indeed, after raising `I+p^(c+t)F` to the `p`th power, every term of degree
at least two lies modulo `p^(c+t+2)`, while the linear term is
`p^(c+t+1)F`.  This is the point at which `p` odd is used.

Take an exponent

```text
0<j<p^(a-c),       j=p^t q,       p does not divide q.   (8)
```

Then `t<=a-c-1`, and `(7)` gives

```text
U^j=I+q p^(c+t)E        mod p^(c+t+1).                  (9)
```

Suppose `U^j` fixed a direct summand `W in Gr_k(R_a)`.  Choose a basis matrix
`V` for `W` and a left inverse for `V`.  There is an `A in GL_k(R_a)` with

```text
U^j V=V A.
```

Reduction modulo `p^(c+t)` and application of the left inverse show
`A=I mod p^(c+t)`.  The next layer of `(9)` therefore says

```text
q Ebar Vbar=Vbar C
```

for some `C`.  Hence the reduction `Wbar` is an invariant `k`-plane for
`Ebar`, contrary to the hypothesis.  No exponent in `(8)` fixes a point.

Taking `t=a-c` in `(7)` gives

```text
U^(p^(a-c))=I mod p^a.                                  (10)
```

Thus every orbit has exactly `p^(a-c)` points.  Dividing the fibre cardinal
from Section 3 proves all of `(3)` and the transitivity boundary.

## 5. Necessity and the fixed first layer

Conversely, suppose `Wbar` is an invariant `k`-plane for `Ebar`.  Lift a
basis and complement so that `Wbar` is the first coordinate plane.  The
lower-left `(n-k) x k` block of `E` is then zero modulo `p`.

At level `a=c+1`, every lift over the coordinate base plane has graph
`X=p^cY`.  In the fractional-linear graph action

```text
X |-> (C+D X)(A+B X)^(-1),                              (11)
```

the lower-left block `C=p^c E_21` vanishes modulo `p^(c+1)`, while every
product containing both `p^c` and `X` is divisible by `p^(2c)` and hence by
`p^(c+1)`.  Formula `(11)` is therefore exactly `X |-> X`.  All first-layer
lifts are fixed, proving `(4)`.

## 6. Sharp boundaries and controls

1. **The prime two.**  Let `E mod 2` have irreducible polynomial
   `T^2+T+1`, and put `U=I+2E`.  Although `E` has no eigenline,

   ```text
   U^2=I+4(E+E^2)=5I mod 8.
   ```

   Hence its action on a four-point depth-one fibre of `P^1(Z/8)` has two
   2-cycles rather than one 4-cycle.  The odd-prime hypothesis is sharp.
2. **An invariant plane.**  A diagonal first carry fixes its coordinate
   planes, and Section 5 makes the whole first lifting fibre fixed.  A single
   nonconstant orbit elsewhere cannot repair the universal claim.
3. **Irreducibility is sufficient, not necessary.**  The theorem needs only
   the absence of an invariant plane of the selected dimension.  Other
   invariant dimensions are irrelevant to that `Gr_k` statement.
4. **Freeness is not transitivity.**  In dimension three, a projective fibre
   has `p^(2(a-c))` points but an orbit has only `p^(a-c)`.
5. **No LRC transport.**  A Grassmannian orbit label is not a physical clock,
   half-twist owner, or current.  This theorem supplies no LRC(14) decrement.

For the Fibonacci matrix packet, remove the scalar from `G^18` and take
`p=17,c=1,n=2,k=1`.  Its nonsquare first-carry discriminant is precisely the
no-eigenline certificate, so `(3)` recovers the regular `17^(a-1)` fibre.
This is a specialization, not a dependency or a converse identification.
