# THM-2769 -- Full-S4 pair-sum affine divisor-parity hostile

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Reserved for an explicit one-parameter monic quartic whose generic splitting
group is `S4`, whose translated pair-sum product and six-edge discriminant are
squares, but whose Kummer boundary has a nonzero weight-two divisor-parity
row.  Promotion requires the complete algebraic proof, an exact no-`assert`
normal/optimized/stored companion, and an independent hostile audit.

This stub proves nothing and is not a dependency.  The intended result is a
non-Keller affine boundary hostile showing that field monodromy is
insufficient; it is not a Jacobian counterexample.
