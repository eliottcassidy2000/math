# THM-2810 -- factorial faithfulness forbids bounded radial carriers

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: for the factorial functional `L(s^n)=n!`, use the exact
factorial Hankel determinant to prove that its algebra annihilator is zero.
Consequently `L` factors through no proper polynomial-algebra quotient and
has no tower-wide finite-dimensional multiplicative, linear-state, Prony, or
periodic exponent carrier.  Record `s^a(1-s^N)` as the sharp cyclic alias and
the characteristic-`p` truncation as the exact boundary.

This is a no-go for fixed bounded carriers, not a refutation of effective
HYP-8765, a replacement for THM-2022's characteristic-`p` whole-face
preserver, a global de-factorialization theorem, or a new proof of GMC(2).

Until a proof is written, exactly replayed, independently hostile-audited,
and this status is promoted, this file is not a proved result or proved
dependency.
