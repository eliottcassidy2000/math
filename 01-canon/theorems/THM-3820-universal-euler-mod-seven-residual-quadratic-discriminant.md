# THM-3820 -- reserved universal residual-discriminant namespace

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

This namespace is reserved for the proposed coefficient-free identity for the
universal Hamiltonian critical resultant of the nodal carrier
`A=e^2-z/3+r g(e)`.  After the Euler/mod-seven change of variables

```text
t=e^7,       Y=G/e^3,       Z=(eD-G)/e^3,
```

the residual should be quadratic in `t`, and its quadratic discriminant
should equal an explicit square times the discriminant of the source critical
quadratic in `u=re`.  No degree-six-or-higher carrier closure is claimed here.
