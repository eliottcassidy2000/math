# THM-3576 -- higher-exponent Belyi Keller collision tower

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**  Reserved for a proposed family of
weight-`(1,-n)` rational Keller pairs whose polynomial observables land on
the smooth non-`A2` targets

```text
c^n e=b(b-beta),                 n>=2.
```

The candidate uses an exact polynomial solution of
`P+n tP'=(1+t)^(n-1)` and is expected to have generic degree
`n(n-1)+1`.  The degree-seven member appears to have alternating monodromy.
No Keller identity, maximal observable ring, image statement, monodromy
classification, or planar Jacobian consequence is asserted until the proof
and an exact companion pass independent audit.
