# THM-3546 -- invariant graph Keller descent criterion

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**  No mathematical claim is made here and
no proved theorem may depend on this file.  The intended scope is the exact
normal/tangential determinant factorization for a polynomial Keller map
carrying one polynomial graph to another, and the criterion under which a
collision on those graphs would descend to a planar Keller counterexample.
