# THM-2337 -- expiration-word residue invisibility and first Bockstein sidecar

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

This namespace is reserved for the exact comparison between the full
atomic relation address

```text
r_full=u+13^k beta+m e_(c_3)-v
```

and its bare-endpoint projection

```text
r_base=u+m e_(c_3)-v,
```

where `k=lambda_j+1` is the expiration depth and `beta` is the labelled
terminal-word harmonic.  The intended theorem should prove
`r_full=r_base (mod 13^k)`, then retain the divided difference

```text
(r_full-r_base)/13^k = beta
```

as the first Bockstein/jet sidecar.  The proof, exact hypotheses, quotient
typing, sharpness example, convergence boundary, and companion checks have
not yet been written or independently audited.  This stub is not a proved
dependency.
