# THM-2866 -- positive factorial-difference semiring and cubic Pascal-response ladder

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**

Let

```text
L(s^m)=m!,                 f_m=s^m/m!,
d_m=f_(m+1)-f_m.                                      (1)
```

The load-bearing theorem is that, for every finite nonzero
nonnegative adjacent-difference cone

```text
W=sum_j mu_j d_j,                 mu_j>=0,             (2)
```

the response ratio

```text
S_n(W)=L(d_n W^3)/L(d_n W)                            (3)
```

is strictly increasing:

```text
S_(n+1)(W)>S_n(W),                 n>=0.               (4)
```

This is the fourth-order analogue of THM-2830's quadratic-response
Pascal ratio.  It is not obtained by assuming a stronger raw
coefficientwise total-positivity statement.  Full polarization produces a
four-index cyclic kernel, and all four choices of the distinguished atom
are essential.

The payoff is global.  The factorial four-slot harmonic reduction of
THM-2848 has two remaining branches.  Equation `(4)` proves that the
first one is uniformly empty:

```text
F^o!=0
```

on the mean-zero three-space of every four-slot factorial support.  Only
the shared cubic--quartic multipole-line branch remains.

## 1. The positive multiplication semiring

Two elementary identities explain why adjacent differences reproduce
the same prefix-plus-tooth shape under multiplication:

```text
f_a f_b=binom(a+b,a)f_(a+b),

f_a d_j
 =binom(a+j,a-1) f_(a+j)
  +binom(a+j+1,a) d_(a+j),                            (5)

d_p d_q
 =binom(p+q,p) f_(p+q)
  +(p+q+2)!/[(p+1)!(q+1)!] d_(p+q+1).                (6)
```

The first coefficient in `(5)` is zero when `a=0`.  Thus these identities
show that the cone
generated by the `f_a` and `d_j` with nonnegative coefficients is
multiplicatively closed.  This is the exact algebraic version of the
prefix/ladder self-similarity: a product creates a positive lower stalk
and a positive higher tooth.

For three atoms, put

```text
P=p+q+r,
E_2=pq+pr+qr,                    E_3=pqr,

B(p,q,r)=2(P+1)^2+P E_2-E_3,

T(p,q,r)=P! B(p,q,r)/[(p+1)!(q+1)!(r+1)!],
M(p,q,r)=P!/(p!q!r!),
N(p,q,r)=(P+3)!/[(p+1)!(q+1)!(r+1)!].                (7)
```

Every quantity in `(7)` is strictly positive.  For `B`, note that
`P E_2-E_3` is a polynomial with nonnegative coefficients.  One more
application of `(5)--(6)` gives the exact three-atom law

```text
d_p d_q d_r
 =T(p,q,r) f_(P+1)+M(p,q,r)d_P+N(p,q,r)d_(P+2).      (8)
```

Also

```text
T(p,q,r)=L(d_p d_q d_r).                              (9)
```

The missing `d_(P+1)` coefficient in `(8)` is a genuine cancellation,
not a suppressed term.

## 2. Polarize the response ratio

Write

```text
H_n(k)=L(d_n d_k)=binom(n+k,n)>0.                     (10)
```

Since

```text
f_(P+1)=1+sum_(k=0)^P d_k,
```

equation `(8)` gives

```text
G_n(p,q,r):=L(d_n d_p d_q d_r)

 =T(p,q,r) sum_(k=0)^P H_n(k)
  +M(p,q,r)H_n(P)
  +N(p,q,r)H_n(P+2).                                  (11)
```

Let

```text
D_n=L(d_nW),                  R_n=L(d_nW^3).          (12)
```

Expanding `(2)` and symmetrizing the four dummy indices gives

```text
R_(n+1)D_n-R_nD_(n+1)

 =1/4 sum_(p,q,r,s) mu_pmu_qmu_rmu_s
      K_n(p,q,r,s),                                   (13)
```

where

```text
K_n(p,q,r,s)
 =sum_(x in {p,q,r,s})
   [G_(n+1)(the other three)H_n(x)
    -G_n(the other three)H_(n+1)(x)].                 (14)
```

Repeated labels are retained with their multiplicities in `(14)`.
The Pascal row ratio

```text
H_(n+1)(k)/H_n(k)=(n+k+1)/(n+1)                      (15)
```

turns each summand arising from an output tooth `d_k` into

```text
H_n(k)H_n(x)(k-x)/(n+1).                              (16)
```

Consequently `(11)` splits the cyclic kernel exactly as

```text
K_n=K_n^T+K_n^M+K_n^N,                                (17)
```

with the three pieces contributed by the three terms in `(8)`.  We prove

```text
K_n^T>=0,                 K_n^M>=0,                 K_n^N>0. (18)
```

This will make every polarized coefficient in `(13)` strictly positive.

## 3. The middle-tooth component

Fix four nonnegative labels `x_1,...,x_4` and put

```text
P=x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4.                                    (19)
```

After cancelling a common positive factorial, the `M` component is

```text
sum_i w_i^M(P-2x_i),                                  (20)

w_i^M=(n+x_i)!(n+P-x_i)!.                             (21)
```

If `x<=y` are two of the four labels, then `x+y<=P`, and

```text
w^M(x)/w^M(y)
 =product_(r=0)^(y-x-1)
   (n+P-y+1+r)/(n+x+1+r)
 >=1.                                                 (22)
```

Thus `w^M(x)` is antitone on the four observed labels.  Pairwise
covariance, or the rearrangement inequality, gives

```text
sum_i w_i^M x_i/sum_i w_i^M
 <=(x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4)/4=P/4.                           (23)
```

Equations `(20)--(23)` prove

```text
K_n^M>=0,                                              (24)
```

with equality exactly when `P=0`.

## 4. The prefix component

For a distinguished label `x_i`, let `B_i` denote `B` of the other
three labels and define

```text
q_i=(x_i+1)B_i,
F_i=(n+x_i)!(n+P-x_i+1)!,
w_i^T=q_iF_i.                                         (25)
```

The hockey-stick identities

```text
sum_(k=0)^S H_n(k)=H_(n+1)(S),

sum_(k=0)^S kH_n(k)
  =[(n+1)S/(n+2)]H_(n+1)(S)                           (26)
```

show that, up to one common positive factor,

```text
K_n^T
 =sum_i w_i^T[alpha P-(alpha+1)x_i],
alpha=(n+1)/(n+2).                                    (27)
```

The factorial factor `F_i` is antitone on the four labels.  Indeed, for
`x<=y`,

```text
F(x)/F(y)
 =product_(r=0)^(y-x-1)
   (n+P-y+2+r)/(n+x+1+r)
 >=1.                                                 (28)
```

Therefore weighted pairwise covariance gives

```text
mean_(qF)(x)<=mean_q(x).                               (29)
```

It remains to bound the much smaller polynomial weight `q`.  Let
`m_lambda` denote the monomial symmetric polynomial in the four
`x_i` with exponent partition `lambda`.  Exact expansion gives

```text
sum_i q_i(P-3x_i)

 =2m_1
  +(8m_2+4m_11)
  +(6m_3+4m_21+12m_111)
  +[4(m_31-m_22)+4m_211+32m_1111]
  +[2(m_311-m_221)+8m_2111].                          (30)
```

Every displayed term is nonnegative.  The only two differences have the
explicit square certificates

```text
m_31-2m_22
 =sum_(i<j)x_ix_j(x_i-x_j)^2>=0,                      (31)

m_311-m_221
 =sum_({x,y,z} subset {x_1,...,x_4})
   xyz[x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-xz-yz]>=0.                      (32)
```

The first term `2m_1=2P` makes `(30)` strict when `P>0`.  Hence

```text
mean_q(x)<P/3,                         P>0.            (33)
```

Combining `(29)` and `(33)`,

```text
sum_i w_i^T(P-3x_i)>0,                 P>0.            (34)
```

Finally,

```text
alpha P-(alpha+1)x
 =[(alpha+1)/3](P-3x)+[nP/(3(n+2))].                  (35)
```

Equations `(27),(34),(35)` prove

```text
K_n^T>=0,                                              (36)
```

again with equality exactly when `P=0`.

## 5. The high-tooth component

After cancelling its common positive factorial, the `N` component is

```text
sum_i w_i^N(P+2-2x_i),                                (37)

w_i^N
 =(x_i+1)(P-x_i+3)(n+x_i)!(n+P-x_i+2)!.              (38)
```

If every `x_i<=(P+2)/2`, all summands in `(37)` are
nonnegative and at least one is positive.

Otherwise there is one unique large label `d>(P+2)/2`.  Let the other
three labels sum to `y=P-d` and put

```text
h=d-y>=3.                                             (39)
```

The coefficient in `(37)` is `-(h-2)` at `d`, while it is at least
`h+2` at every small label.  If `x` is one of the small labels and
`r=y-x>=0`, direct cancellation in `(38)` gives

```text
w^N(x)/w^N(d)

 >=(x+1)(d+3)/[(y+3)(d+1)].                           (40)
```

Indeed, before the final lower bound the exact ratio is

```text
(x+1)/(d+1) * (d+r+3)/(y+3)
 * (n+x)!(n+d+r+2)!/[(n+d)!(n+x+r+2)!].
```

The remaining factorial quotient pairs intervals of length `d-x`; each
numerator argument exceeds its denominator partner by `r+2`.
Summing `(40)` over the three small labels and using

```text
sum_small(x+1)=y+3
```

gives

```text
sum_small w^N(x)/w^N(d)
 >=(d+3)/(d+1)>1.                                     (41)
```

Thus the positive part of `(37)` is strictly larger than
`(h+2)w^N(d)`, while its negative part has magnitude only
`(h-2)w^N(d)`.  This proves

```text
K_n^N>0.                                              (42)
```

Equations `(17),(24),(36),(42)` prove `K_n>0`.
Every occupied quadruple in `(13)` has positive coefficient, so

```text
R_(n+1)D_n-R_nD_(n+1)>0.                              (43)
```

Since `D_n>0`, equation `(43)` is exactly the ratio theorem `(4)`.

The nonnegative-cone hypothesis is load-bearing.  For the signed
combination

```text
W=3d_0-d_1
```

one has exactly

```text
L(d_0W)=2,        L(d_1W)=1,
L(d_0W^3)=18,     L(d_1W^3)=-27.
```

Thus `S_0(W)=9>S_1(W)=-27`; no signed-cone extension of `(4)` is being
claimed.

## 6. The quartic endpoint mismatch

Take any four-slot factorial support

```text
0<=a_0<a_1<a_2<a_3.                                   (44)
```

Use only its top-three mean-zero face.  Put

```text
a=a_1,                  b=a_2,                  c=a_3,

U=f_b-f_a=sum_(i=a)^(b-1)d_i,
V=f_c-f_b=sum_(j=b)^(c-1)d_j.                         (45)
```

The choice of the top face is load-bearing: `a>=1`, so

```text
U(0)=V(0)=0.                                          (46)
```

Define

```text
g_0=L(U^2),             g_1=L(UV),             g_2=L(V^2),

A_0=L(U^4),             A_1=L(U^3V),           A_2=L(U^2V^2),
A_3=L(UV^3),            A_4=L(V^4).                    (47)
```

For `H=xU+yV`, the binary quadratic and quartic are

```text
Q=g_0x^2+2g_1xy+g_2y^2,

F=A_0x^4+4A_1x^3y+6A_2x^2y^2+4A_3xy^3+A_4y^4.       (48)
```

If

```text
F=Q(r_0x^2+2r_1xy+r_2y^2),                            (49)
```

the endpoint coefficients force

```text
r_0=A_0/g_0,                       r_2=A_4/g_2,

r_1^(left) =(2A_1g_0-A_0g_1)/g_0^2,
r_1^(right)=(2A_3g_2-A_4g_1)/g_2^2.                  (50)
```

Thus divisibility requires the endpoint mismatch

```text
J=
 (2A_1g_0-A_0g_1)g_2^2
 -(2A_3g_2-A_4g_1)g_0^2
 =0.                                                  (51)
```

Under the positive gauge `U->lambda U,V->mu V`, this mismatch scales by
`lambda^5mu^5`; swapping the oriented endpoints reverses its sign.

Integration by parts is valid without a boundary term because of `(46)`:

```text
g_0=2L(UU'),                  A_0=4L(U^3U'),
g_2=2L(VV'),                  A_4=4L(V^3V').          (52)
```

Consequently

```text
2A_1g_0-A_0g_1
 =4[L(U^3V)L(UU')-L(U^3U')L(UV)],                    (53)

2A_3g_2-A_4g_1
 =4[L(UV^3)L(VV')-L(V^3V')L(UV)].                    (54)
```

For a fixed positive cone `W`, and any other positive cone
`Y=sum_i eta_i d_i`,

```text
L(YW^3)/L(YW)
```

is a positive weighted average of the strictly increasing values
`S_i(W)` from `(3)--(4)`.

Now

```text
supp(U')=[a-1,b-2],                  supp(V)=[b,c-1].
```

Every index in `V` is strictly larger than every index in `U'`.
Applying `(4)` with `W=U` to `(53)` gives

```text
2A_1g_0-A_0g_1>0.                                    (55)
```

Likewise

```text
supp(U)=[a,b-1],                    supp(V')=[b-1,c-2].
```

Every index in `U` is at most every index in `V'`.  Applying `(4)` with
`W=V` to `(54)` gives

```text
2A_3g_2-A_4g_1<=0,                                    (56)
```

with equality exactly when

```text
b=a+1,                         c=b+1.                 (57)
```

Equations `(51),(55),(56)` prove the strict orientation

```text
J>0.                                                  (58)
```

Therefore `Q` does not divide `F` on the top-three face of any four-slot
support.

The restriction to `a>=1` is essential only for the boundary-free form
of `(52)`.  If `a=0`, then `U(0)=-1` and

```text
L(U^2)=U(0)^2+2L(UU'),
L(U^4)=U(0)^4+4L(U^3U').                              (59)
```

We do not use or claim `(52)` on that bottom face.

## 7. Global harmonic consequence

In the three-dimensional mean-zero space of THM-2848, the condition

```text
F^o=0
```

is equivalent to the ternary quartic `F` being divisible by the positive
quadratic `Q`.  Such a divisibility would survive restriction to every
real two-plane, in particular the top-three coordinate face `(45)`.
Equation `(58)` excludes that restriction.  Hence, uniformly for every
four-slot factorial support,

```text
F^o!=0.                                                (60)
```

Combining `(60)` with THM-2848, the first-window four-slot bad locus now
has only one remaining branch:

```text
some cubic Maxwell multipole line
equals some quartic Maxwell multipole line.            (61)
```

The finite exponent scans for `F^o!=0` are superseded by `(60)`.  This
theorem does not exclude `(61)`, prove arbitrary four-slot SFC, supply a
new DvdK-free proof of GMC(2), or close the planar Jacobian conjecture.
