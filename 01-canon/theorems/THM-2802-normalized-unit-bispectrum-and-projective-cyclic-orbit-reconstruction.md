# THM-2802 -- a unit cycle is reconstructed projectively by its bispectrum

**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.**

Proposed scope: over a splitting field for `C_p`, prove that the normalized
Fourier bispectrum

```text
B_f(k,l)=fhat(k) fhat(l)/(fhat(k+l) fhat(0))
```

of a group-algebra unit is a complete invariant up to nonzero scalar and
cyclic translation.  Equality makes the Fourier ratio a character.  Apply
the invariant to the full-spectrum THM-2790 endpoint cycles and compare it
with THM-2791/2792's two-point semantic unit, retaining the exact
origin/frame/dihedral and physical-allocation boundaries.  The intended
result is coefficient/projective reconstruction, not a positive physical
intertwiner, semantic attachment, row exclusion, or LRC(14).

Until a proof is written, exactly replayed, independently audited, and this
status is promoted, this file is not a proved result or proved dependency.
