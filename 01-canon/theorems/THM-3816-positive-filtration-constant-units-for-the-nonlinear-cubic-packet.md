# THM-3816 -- a positive filtration closes the ambient-unit gate

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY HOSTILE-AUDITED.**  Let `k` be
any field, put `R=k[A,C]`, and give

```text
S=R*1 direct_sum R*omega direct_sum R*theta                         (1)
```

the THM-3811 Delone--Faddeev multiplication

```text
omega^2=-7A^2+C omega-A theta,
omega theta=3A^2-AC^2,
theta^2=3AC-C^3+(C^2-3A)omega-7A theta.                            (2)
```

Then

```text
S*=k*.                                                             (3)
```

In particular `(3)` applies over the algebraically closed characteristic-zero
field used in THM-3811.

## 1. The split positive filtration

For a monomial `A^iC^j`, set

```text
wt(A^iC^j)=2i+j,
wt(omega)=wt(theta)=2.                                             (4)
```

Use the free splitting `(1)` to define `F_nS`: the scalar coefficient has
weight at most `n`, while the `omega` and `theta` coefficients have weight at
most `n-2`.  This is an exhaustive, separated, nonnegative filtration, and

```text
F_0S=k.                                                            (5)
```

Every term on the right of `(2)` has weight at most the weight of its left
side.  Retaining the weight-four pieces gives the associated-graded table

```text
omega^2=-7A^2-A theta,
omega theta=3A^2-AC^2,
theta^2=(C^2-3A)omega-7A theta.                                   (6)
```

Because the filtration is split on `(1)`, `gr S` remains a free rank-three
`k[A,C]`-module with basis `(1,omega,theta)` and multiplication `(6)`.

## 2. The associated graded ring is a domain

Let `K=k(A,C)`.  In `gr S tensor_R K`, the first relation in `(6)` recovers

```text
theta=-(omega^2+7A^2)/A.                                          (7)
```

Thus the three-dimensional generic algebra is generated by `omega`.  Its
characteristic polynomial is

```text
f_0(T)=T^3+7A^2T+3A^3-A^2C^2.                                    (8)
```

This polynomial is irreducible over `K`.  Indeed, substitute `T=Aq`; after
removing the nonzero factor `A^2`, `(8)` becomes

```text
H(q)=Aq^3+7Aq+3A-C^2.                                             (9)
```

The reciprocal polynomial is

```text
X^3H(1/X)=(3A-C^2)X^3+7AX^2+A.                                  (10)
```

In the UFD `k[A,C]`, equation `(10)` is Eisenstein at the prime `A`: the
leading coefficient reduces to `-C^2`, every lower coefficient is divisible
by `A`, and the constant coefficient has `A`-valuation exactly one.  This
argument works in every characteristic.  Gauss's lemma, reciprocal change of
variable, and the nonzero scaling `T=Aq` prove the irreducibility of `(8)`.

Consequently

```text
(gr S) tensor_R K = K[T]/(f_0)
```

is a field.  Since `gr S` is free over the domain `R`, it injects into this
localization.  Hence

```text
gr S is a domain.                                                  (11)
```

## 3. Leading degrees force constant units

In a filtered ring with domain associated graded, nonzero leading forms do
not cancel under multiplication.  Therefore

```text
deg(uv)=deg(u)+deg(v)                                              (12)
```

for nonzero `u,v in S`.  If `uv=1`, equations `(5),(12)` and nonnegativity
force `deg(u)=deg(v)=0`.  Thus `u,v in k`; conversely every nonzero scalar is
a unit.  This proves `(3)`.
