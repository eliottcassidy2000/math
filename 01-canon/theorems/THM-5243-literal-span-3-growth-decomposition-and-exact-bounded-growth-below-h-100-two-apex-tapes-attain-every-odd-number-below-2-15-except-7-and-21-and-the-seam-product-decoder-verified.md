# THM-5243 — the growth decomposition for literal span-3 words, exact bounded growth below H = 100, two-apex coverage below 2^15, and the seam decoder

Notation as in the packet. A literal span-3 word is a first vertex followed by letters `ℓ ⊆ {1, 2, 3}`: the new vertex beats the older vertices at the gaps in `ℓ` and loses to every other older vertex. Letters are coded by bits (`7 = {1,2,3}`, `6 = {2,3}`, `0 = ∅`).

**(1) Decomposition and sealing.** Let `v` be the new vertex and `G` the set of vertices it beats (`|G| ≤ 3`, among the last three). In a Hamiltonian path of `T_W + v`, `v` is last (its predecessor is any in-neighbour), first (its successor lies in `G`), or sits between `w → v → u` with `u ∈ G`, `w ∉ G`. Deleting `v` gives, respectively, a Hamiltonian path of `T_W` ending at an in-neighbour of `v`, one starting in `G`, or — according as `w → u` or `u → w` — a Hamiltonian path of `T_W` in which some `u ∈ G` has a predecessor outside `G`, or a two-path cover `(F₁, F₂)` with `tail(F₁) = w`, `head(F₂) = u`. The last two cases are exactly the bridging transitions of the profile transfer from two-fragment states: `Z_ℓ(W) = Σ_s x_s b_ℓ(s)`. Hence `H(Wℓ) = e_ℓ + s_ℓ + Z_ℓ` with `e_ℓ, s_ℓ ≤ H(W)`. Sealing: after three consecutive empty letters every earlier vertex beats every later one (no back arc reaches past the window), so every Hamiltonian path traverses the earlier vertices first and `H(W' 0 0 0 X) = H(W')·H(0 0 0 X)`. ∎

**(2) Exact maxima.** Over all words of length `n ≤ 7` the maximum of `H(Wℓ)/H(W)` is 1, 3, 5, 11, 23, 39, 39, 39, the maximisers for `n ≥ 5` being `0^(n−3) 7 6 0` with `ℓ = 6`: a transitive run, a vertex beating the last three, a vertex beating the second and third last, a sink; `H(W) = 1` and the next vertex (beating the two predecessors of the sink) bridges 38 two-covers. The maximum of `Z_ℓ/H` is 38 at `n = 6, 7`. The transfer states with `H ≤ 40` form a closed set of 16,794 states (breadth-first search closes at depth 13), so every word of any length with `H(W) ≤ 40` has `H(Wℓ) ≤ 39 H(W)`, and `max H(Wℓ) = 39H`, `max Z = 38H` at every attained `H ≤ 40` (by sealing `W'` and appending `7 6 0`, `{2,3}`); the same holds with `H ≤ 100` (packet §5).

**(3) Obstructions.** The reachable span from the one-vertex state has dimension 117 of 119; the LP `h A_ℓ − ψ_ℓ ≤ K h` with `ψ_ℓ` in the annihilator is infeasible for all `K` (so span and positivity cannot see the lemma). With `c_s = max x_s/H(x)` over words of length ≤ 7 (largest 14), the cone `{x ≥ 0 : x_s ≤ c_s H(x)}` is not invariant under the letters: 478 pairs `(ℓ, s)` admit `x` in the cone with `(A_ℓ x)_s > c_s H(A_ℓ x)` (largest excess 56). A certificate must use correlations between coordinates, i.e. the ages of fragment heads and tails.

**(4) Two apexes.** Spine `0 < … < m−1` transitive, apex `A` beating the spine vertices in `S_A`, apex `B` those in `S_B`, either orientation between `A` and `B`. All `2^21` tapes with `m = 10` attain every odd `N < 2^14` except 7, 21 (19,876 distinct values, maximum 69,485, first miss above 21 is 27,141); all `2^23` tapes with `m = 11` every odd `N < 2^16` except 7, 21 (63,113 values, maximum 227,295, first miss 77,533); with `|S_B| ≤ 3` and `m = 12` (2,449,408 tapes) every odd `N < 2^16` except 7, 21 (141,026 values, maximum 611,117, first miss 117,345). The one-apex sub-family (`S_B = ∅`) gives 1,605 of the 32,766 values below `2^16`; a second apex beating 1, 2, 3 spine vertices is needed for 19,467, 11,615, 79 of them; `A → B` suffices for every one of them. Every odd `N ≤ 33` other than 7, 21 is attained with spine 4.

**(5) The seam decoder.** The formula (★) of `PRODUCT.md` agrees with brute-force Hamiltonian-path counts of the literal tournaments (tape `TT_k → TT_t → TT_r`, sentinel, head, marks `b` backward on the left block and `a` forward on the right block) for all `k, r ≤ 5`, `t ≤ 2` (1,922 cases; controls 801, 803). For all `k, r ≤ 11` the remainders `C` of the factor pairs sharing a product form at most three consecutive odd values (the record `P = 812,130`, `k = r = 10`), although up to 20 pairs share a product. The family attains 222,796 of the 524,288 odd numbers below `2^20` (`k, r ≤ 11`, `t ≤ 12`), a density between 0.42 and 0.46 in every dyadic range from 256 on. Run in reverse the decoder lists every `(k, r, t, a, b)` for a target: 1,623,871 has 11 realisations, 262,145 has 75, 99,999 and 1,000,001 none.

**What is new.** The decomposition and sealing identity, the exact maxima and the exact lemma below `H = 100` (HYP-9802 (ii) gave 39 at lengths ≤ 7 as an observation), the two obstructions, the two-apex coverage (UNIFYING.md asked for the overlap lemma; the one-apex half was known), and the seam windows/density.
