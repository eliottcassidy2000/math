# THM-5243 — the growth decomposition for literal span-3 words, exact bounded growth below H = 100, two-apex coverage below 2^17, and the seam decoder

Notation as in the packet. A literal span-3 word is a first vertex followed by letters `ℓ ⊆ {1, 2, 3}`: the new vertex beats the older vertices at the gaps in `ℓ` and loses to every other older vertex. Letters are coded by bits (`7 = {1,2,3}`, `6 = {2,3}`, `0 = ∅`).

**(1) Decomposition and sealing.** Let `v` be the new vertex and `G` the set of vertices it beats (`|G| ≤ 3`, among the last three). In a Hamiltonian path of `T_W + v`, `v` is last (its predecessor is any in-neighbour), first (its successor lies in `G`), or sits between `w → v → u` with `u ∈ G`, `w ∉ G`. Deleting `v` gives, respectively, a Hamiltonian path of `T_W` ending at an in-neighbour of `v`, one starting in `G`, or — according as `w → u` or `u → w` — a Hamiltonian path of `T_W` in which some `u ∈ G` has a predecessor outside `G`, or a two-path cover `(F₁, F₂)` with `tail(F₁) = w`, `head(F₂) = u`. The last two cases are exactly the bridging transitions of the profile transfer from two-fragment states: `Z_ℓ(W) = Σ_s x_s b_ℓ(s)`. Hence `H(Wℓ) = e_ℓ + s_ℓ + Z_ℓ` with `e_ℓ, s_ℓ ≤ H(W)`. Sealing: after three consecutive empty letters every earlier vertex beats every later one (no back arc reaches past the window), so every Hamiltonian path traverses the earlier vertices first and `H(W' 0 0 0 X) = H(W')·H(0 0 0 X)`. ∎

**(2) Exact maxima.** Over all words of length `n ≤ 9` the maximum of `H(Wℓ)/H(W)` is 1, 3, 5, 11, 23, 39, 39, 39, 39, 39, attained for `n ≥ 5` by `0^(n−3) 7 6 0` with `ℓ = 6`: a transitive run, a vertex beating the last three, a vertex beating the second and third last, a sink; `H(W) = 1` and the next vertex (beating the two predecessors of the sink) bridges 38 two-covers (37 at `n = 5`, where it can also be prepended). The maximum is attained by 24, 24, 48, 192, 1,536 pairs `(W, ℓ)` at `n = 5, …, 9`, and the maximum of `Z_ℓ/H` (38 at `n = 6..9`) by the same pairs; up to isomorphism they are exactly the sealed gadgets `T_(W′) ⇒ B`: `B` the seven-vertex block of `0 0 7 6 0` followed by `ℓ = 6` (`TT_6` plus a vertex beating ranks 1 and 3, `H(B) = 39`), `T_(W′)` any literal span-3 tournament on `n − 5` vertices (empty at `n = 5`), every arc from `W′` into `B`, so `H(W) = H(W′)` and `H(Wℓ) = 39·H(W′)`; the classes number 1, 1, 1, 2, 4 (the literal tournaments on 0–4 vertices; for `n ≤ 7` only transitive `W′`, i.e. `T_W = TT_(n+1)` with the new vertex beating ranks `n − 4` and `n − 2`, rank 1 = source). Verified by comparing canonical forms (nauty's `labelg`) of all maximisers with those of all `T_(W′) ⇒ B`, after the block structure had been predicted from the length-8 listing (packet, PREREGISTERED.md addendum). The pair multiplicity comes from the bits of the first two letters that address gaps not yet present and from the three spellings of the block (`3·8^(n−6)` pairs for `n ≥ 8`).

**(2′) The constant (PROVED).** For the transitive tournament `TT_m` (ranks `1 = source … m = sink`) plus a vertex `u` beating exactly the ranks `r` and `r + 2`, `r + 2 < m`, `H(TT_m + u) = 5·2^(m−r−2) − 1`. Proof: deleting `u` from a Hamiltonian path leaves an increasing prefix `P` (ending at a vertex beating `u`, i.e. outside `S = {r, r+2}`) and an increasing suffix `Q` (starting in `S`), the two forming a partition of the ranks, `Q = ∅` when `u` is last (allowed, since the sink is outside `S`) and `P = ∅` when `u` is first (needs `r = 1`). If `Q` starts at `r + 2` then `{1, …, r+1} ⊆ P` and the `m − r − 2` ranks above `r + 2` are split freely: `2^(m−r−2)` paths, `max P ∉ S` automatically. If `Q` starts at `r` then `{1, …, r−1} ⊆ P`, the `m − r` ranks above `r` are split freely except that `max P ≠ r + 2` (2 subsets) and `P ≠ ∅` (one more subset when `r = 1`): `2^(m−r) − 2 − [r = 1]` paths. With `1` for `u` last and `[r = 1]` for `u` first the total is `2^(m−r−2) + 2^(m−r) − 1 = 5·2^(m−r−2) − 1`. ∎ (Brute force agrees for all `m ≤ 11`, receipt `tt_plus_vertex`.) The block `B` is the case `m = 6`, `r = 1`: `H(B) = 39`; for transitive `W′` the maximiser `T_(W′) ⇒ B = TT_(n+1) + u` is the case `m = n + 1`, `r = n − 4`, so 39 = 5·2³ − 1 at every `n ≥ 5`, and `Z = 38` is the `Q`-starts-at-`r` count `2^(m−r) − 2 = 30` plus the `Q`-starts-at-`r+2` count 8 for `r ≥ 2`. A smaller `r` (a deeper pair) is unavailable to a literal word with transitive `T_W`: the vertex added at step `j` beats at most three older vertices, so it is beaten by at least `j − 3` of them, and the last three added vertices — the only ones the new vertex may beat — have ranks at least `n − 4`, `n − 3`, `n − 2`. Over all `S` within the last six ranks the maximum of `H(TT_m + u)` is 51 at `S = {m−5, m−4, m−3}` (receipt `tt_plus_vertex`), but that pattern is not realisable (rank `n − 2` for the last vertex forces it to beat the previous one, rank `n − 3` for the previous one forbids that); whether 39 bounds every transitive `T_W` at every length was checked here only to length 9 — it is PROVED for every `T_W`, transitive or not, by SHARP_GROWTH.md (THM-5253 (A)). The transfer states with `H ≤ 40` form a closed set of 16,794 states (breadth-first search closes at depth 13), so every word of any length with `H(W) ≤ 40` has `H(Wℓ) ≤ 39 H(W)`, and `max H(Wℓ) = 39H`, `max Z = 38H` at every attained `H ≤ 40` (by sealing `W'` and appending `7 6 0`, `{2,3}`); the same holds with `H ≤ 100` (packet §5).

**(3) Obstructions.** The reachable span from the one-vertex state has dimension 117 of 119; the LP `h A_ℓ − ψ_ℓ ≤ K h` with `ψ_ℓ` in the annihilator is infeasible for all `K` (so span and positivity cannot see the lemma). With `c_s = max x_s/H(x)` over words of length ≤ 7 (largest 14), the cone `{x ≥ 0 : x_s ≤ c_s H(x)}` is not invariant under the letters: 478 pairs `(ℓ, s)` admit `x` in the cone with `(A_ℓ x)_s > c_s H(A_ℓ x)` (largest excess 56). A certificate must use correlations between coordinates, i.e. the ages of fragment heads and tails. (UPDATE: the lemma is proved by a combinatorial injection using fragment ages — the tail deletion of SHARP_GROWTH.md, THM-5253 (A) — not by a linear potential; THM-5253 (D) shows that history-refined box cones fail for every history length tested.)

**(4) Two apexes.** Spine `0 < … < m−1` transitive, apex `A` beating the spine vertices in `S_A`, apex `B` those in `S_B`, either orientation between `A` and `B`. All `2^21` tapes with `m = 10` attain every odd `N < 2^14` except 7, 21 (19,876 distinct values, maximum 69,485, first miss above 21 is 27,141); all `2^23` tapes with `m = 11` every odd `N < 2^16` except 7, 21 (63,113 values, maximum 227,295, first miss 77,533); with `|S_B| ≤ 3` and `m = 12` (2,449,408 tapes) every odd `N < 2^16` except 7, 21 (141,026 values, maximum 611,117, first miss 117,345); with `|S_B| ≤ 3` and `m = 13` (6,193,152 tapes) every odd `N < 2^17` except 7, 21 — indeed every odd `N < 2^18` except 7, 21 and 256,521 (401,873 values, maximum 1,904,361); below `2^17` overlap 0/1/2/3 is needed by 3,185/40,638/21,635/76 values and `A → B` suffices for all of them. The one-apex sub-family (`S_B = ∅`) gives 1,605 of the 32,766 values below `2^16`; a second apex beating 1, 2, 3 spine vertices is needed for 19,467, 11,615, 79 of them; `A → B` suffices for every one of them. Every odd `N ≤ 33` other than 7, 21 is attained with spine 4.

**(5) The seam decoder.** The formula (★) of `PRODUCT.md` agrees with brute-force Hamiltonian-path counts of the literal tournaments (tape `TT_k → TT_t → TT_r`, sentinel, head, marks `b` backward on the left block and `a` forward on the right block) for all `k, r ≤ 5`, `t ≤ 2` (1,922 cases; controls 801, 803). For all `k, r ≤ 11` the remainders `C` of the factor pairs sharing a product form at most three consecutive odd values (the record `P = 812,130`, `k = r = 10`), although up to 20 pairs share a product. The family attains 222,796 of the 524,288 odd numbers below `2^20` (`k, r ≤ 11`, `t ≤ 12`), a density between 0.42 and 0.46 in every dyadic range from 256 on. Run in reverse the decoder lists every `(k, r, t, a, b)` for a target: 1,623,871 has 11 realisations, 262,145 has 75, 99,999 and 1,000,001 none.
