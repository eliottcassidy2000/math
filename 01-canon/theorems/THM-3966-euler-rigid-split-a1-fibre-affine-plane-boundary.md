**RESERVED / UNPROVED EMPTY STUB.** This reserves the theorem ID and filename
for the proposed general boundary lemma: if a smooth affine surface has
`Cl(X)=Z^n`, scalar units, and Euler characteristic `1+n`, then any dense
affine-plane open has a pure boundary of exactly `n` prime curves, and
rational-unibranch boundary control forces them to be pairwise disjoint
copies of `A1`. It contains no usable theorem until boundary purity, the
Euler comparison, characteristic-zero descent, and hostile examples have
