## 1. Exact source, target, and map

Work over the characteristic-zero exceptional quartic field `K` of
THM-3703. Its restriction compiler has

```text
N=K[x],
D=1+x^2 Q(x),
B=(D-1)(D+2)^2,
C=xD(D+2),
E=Q(D+3),
C^2E=B(B+4).                                           (1)
```

Put

```text
T=K[b,c,e]/(c^2e-b(b+4)),
gamma_0:T -> N,             (b,c,e) |-> (B,C,E),
S=gamma_0(T)=K[B,C,E] subset N.                        (2)
```

The normal direction in this theorem is fixed once and for all: keep `x`
fixed and replace `Q(x)` by `Q(x)+s`, with `s^2=0`. Because the compiler
identity in `(1)` holds for every `Q`, this gives a well-defined algebra map

```text
gamma_s=gamma_0+s gamma_1:T -> N[s]/(s^2).              (3)
```

Thus `gamma_1` is a `gamma_0`-derivation. Direct differentiation gives

```text
gamma_1(B)=3x^2D(D+2),
gamma_1(C)=2x^3(D+1),
gamma_1(E)=2(D+1).                                     (4)
```

Here and below

```text
dS={f'(x):f in S} subset N,             E_x=N/dS.       (5)
```

The quotient in `(5)` is a `K`-vector-space quotient, not an ideal quotient.
THM-3703's monic Apery basis has 89 degree gaps; differentiating it shifts
those gaps by one and proves

```text
dim_K E_x=89.                                           (6)
```

Pullback of Kahler forms along `(3)`, evaluation on the ordered tangent pair
`(d/dx,d/ds)` at `s=0`, and reduction modulo `(5)` define a well-typed
`K`-linear map

```text
tau:Omega^2_(T/K) -> E_x.                               (7)
```

For a decomposable target form the map is

```text

 =[(gamma_0(P))' gamma_1(Q)
   -(gamma_0(Q))' gamma_1(P)] mod dS.                   (8)
```

For `H dP wedge dQ`, the right side of `(8)` is multiplied by
`gamma_0(H)`. This construction through Kahler pullback is important:
`tau` is independent of polynomial representatives in `K[b,c,e]`, even
though the raw operation `gamma_1` does not descend from `T` to the image
algebra `S`.

Let the retained normalization fibre be ordered as `x=(-1,0,1)` and define

```text

```

The main conclusion is


Here


is the seminormal generator of THM-4381, and `[r']` denotes its class in
`E_x`.

## 2. Cutoff-free upper bound at the retained triple
