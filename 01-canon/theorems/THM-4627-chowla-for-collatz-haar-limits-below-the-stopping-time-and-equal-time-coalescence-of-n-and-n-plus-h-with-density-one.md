# THM-4627 — Chowla for Collatz

## Setting

`T(n) = n/2` (`n` even), `(3n+1)/2` (`n` odd). `sigma(n)` is the stopping time (least `k` with `T^k(n) < n`), `kappa(n)` Terras's coefficient stopping time (least `k` with `3^(a_k) < 2^k`, `a_k` the number of odd steps among the first `k`), which depends only on `n mod 2^k` and equals `sigma(n)` outside a finite set in each residue class; `tau(n)` the number of `T`-steps to `1`, `omega(n)` the number of odd steps to `1`; `s = (-1)^sigma`, `t = (-1)^tau`, `lam = (-1)^omega`. A functional `f` of the orbit is *stopping-time measurable* if `f(n) = F(w_1(n), ..., w_kappa(n)(n))` for a bounded `F` on finite parity words (any bounded value when `kappa = infinity`); `kappa(n) <= k` is a condition on `n mod 2^k`. Functionals defined with `sigma` in place of `kappa` differ from these on a set that is finite in each residue class modulo `2^k` at each depth `k` (hostile pair `1, 5`: congruent modulo `4`, `kappa = 2` for both, `sigma(1) = infinity`, `sigma(5) = 2`; `sigma = kappa` for all `2 <= n <= 2^20`, audit check; equality for all `n >= 2` is Terras's open conjecture). The codex repair [incoming_cylinder_prime_repair_20261013.md](../../05-knowledge/results/incoming_cylinder_prime_repair_20261013.md) keeps actual-`sigma` functionals and retains the exceptional set as the explicit height `H_k = max ceil(c/(2^j - 3^a))` over the words of depth `j <= k` with `3^a < 2^j` (`H_k < 3^k`): with the periodic coefficient-stopping models `f_k^*, g_k^*` and their full-period product mean `R_k`, `|N^(-1) sum f(a_1 n + b_1) g(a_2 n + b_2) - R_k| <= ||f|| ||g|| [P_1(k) + P_2(k) + (4 2^k + 4 H_k)/N]`, `P_i(k)` the Haar probability of `kappa(a_i y + b_i) > k`; the limit is taken as `N -> infinity` first and `k -> infinity` second, and a free horizon `k ~ log_2 N` is not justified without the height term.

## Statement

1. Let `f, g` be stopping-time measurable and bounded, and `a_1, a_2 >= 1`, `b_1, b_2 >= 0` integers with `a_1 b_2 != a_2 b_1`. Then `(1/N) sum_(n <= N) f(a_1 n + b_1) g(a_2 n + b_2)` converges as `N -> infinity`, to the limit over `k` of the averages over a full period `n mod 2^k` of the depth-`k` truncations (`f_k = f` if `sigma <= k`, else `0`), and `|(1/N) sum - period average at depth k| <= 4 ||f|| ||g|| (2^s P(kappa > k) + 2^k/N)` where `2^s` is the largest power of two dividing `a_1 a_2`. For `f = g = s` and `a_1 = a_2 = 1`, `b_1 = 0`, `b_2 = h`, writing `m = lim E[s]` and `m_odd = 1 + 2m`: the limit correlation is `-m_odd` for odd `h`, `+m_odd` for `h = 2 mod 4`, and `3/4 + (1/4) lim E[s(n)s(n+h) | n = 3 mod 4]` for `h = 0 mod 4`.
2. Fix `h >= 1`. (a) `M_h(K) = {n : exists k <= K with T^k(n) = T^k(n+h) and equal numbers of odd steps up to k}` has natural density equal to the Haar probability `mu_h(K)` of the same event for `(y, y+h)`, `y` Haar on `Z_2`. (b) `mu_h(K) -> 1` as `K -> infinity`. (c) Hence for `n` in a set of density one the orbits of `n` and `n+h` coincide from a common time on with equal odd-step counts; for such `n > 2^K` the merge precedes either orbit reaching `1`, so `tau(n) = tau(n+h)` and `omega(n) = omega(n+h)` whenever either side is finite, and `tau(n) < infinity iff tau(n+h) < infinity`. (d) If the orbits of all `n <= N` reach `1`, then `liminf_N (1/N) sum_(n <= N) t(n)t(n+h) >= 2 mu_h(K) - 1` for every `K`; so the correlations of `t` and of `lam` along `(n, n+h)` tend to `+1`.
3. (FINITE-EXACT.) To `N = 2^28`: `E[s] = -0.234140`, `m_odd = 0.53172`; the integer correlations of `s` equal `-0.53172` at every odd lag `<= 39`, `+0.53172` at every lag `2 mod 4`, and `0.724, 0.696, 0.744, 0.805, 0.746, 0.685, 0.726, 0.868, 0.718, 0.677` at lags `4, 8, ..., 40`; the depth-`26` periodic truncation gives `-0.5390` at odd lags with `P(kappa > 26) = 0.0155`. To `N = 2^26`, stopping when either orbit reaches `1`: `P(merge before 1) = 0.510, 0.419, 0.330, 0.360, 0.341, 0.302, 0.317, 0.284` and `C_t = 0.508, 0.417, 0.327, 0.357, 0.338, 0.298, 0.314, 0.281` at lags `1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 16`; merged pairs have equal `tau` (an identity: equal `tau` is an equal-time coincidence at or before `1`) and correlation exactly `1`, unmerged pairs correlation `-0.003` to `-0.005`; `P(merge)` at lag `1` rises `0.478, 0.495, 0.510` over `2^20, 2^23, 2^26`. Class-wide merge by depth `26` (exact over `2^26` residues): `0.421, 0.306, 0.198, 0.229, 0.211, 0.162, 0.174, 0.131`; at lag `1` the first-merge masses are `1/8` (depth 3), `1/16, 1/16, 1/32, 3/128, 1/64, ...`. The break density `P(tau(n) != tau(n+1))` falls `0.555 -> 0.481` from `2^16` to `2^28`, parity flips are half the breaks, the longest run of equal `tau` below `2^28` has length `120`, and the commonest nonzero differences are `+-19, +-38, +-27, +-46, +-8, +-57`.

## Proof

**1.** The parity word of `m` up to depth `k` depends only on `m mod 2^k` (Terras), so `f_k(a n + b)` is a function of `(a n + b) mod 2^k`, hence of `n mod 2^k`: the integer average of `f_k(a_1 n + b_1) g_k(a_2 n + b_2)` over `n <= N` differs from the average over a full period by at most `2^k/N` times the sup norm. `|f - f_k| <= ||f|| 1_(kappa(a n + b) > k)`; as `n` ranges over a period, `a n + b` ranges over the coset `b + a Z/2^k`, which is a single class modulo `2^s` and uniform on it, so the density of `{n : kappa(a n + b) > k}` is at most `2^s P(kappa > k)` (the Haar measure of the no-descent cylinders), which tends to `0` by Terras (`kappa` is finite almost everywhere, and `P(kappa > k) >= P(sigma > k)` up to the finite exceptional sets). The period averages are Cauchy in `k` by the same bound, so the limit exists and the error bound follows. For the lag laws: even `n` have `sigma = 1`, `s = -1`, so `m = -1/2 + m_odd/2`. Odd `h`: one member of the pair is even with `s = -1`, the other is uniformly distributed among the odd residues, giving `-m_odd`. `h = 2 mod 4`: both even gives `+1` on half the pairs; both odd forces exactly one member `= 1 mod 4` (`sigma = 2`, `s = +1`), so the product is `s` of the `3 mod 4` member, whose mean is `E[s | 3 mod 4] = 2 m_odd - 1` (since `m_odd = (1 + E[s | 3 mod 4])/2`); the total is `1/2 + (2 m_odd - 1)/2 = m_odd`. `h = 0 mod 4`: both even (`+1`, half), both `1 mod 4` (`+1`, a quarter), both `3 mod 4` (a quarter, mean `E[s(n)s(n+h) | n = 3 mod 4]`). ∎

**2.** (a) Let the parity words of `n` and `n+h` up to step `k` have `a` and `a'` odd steps and constants `c, c'`, so `T^k(n) = (3^a n + c)/2^k`, `T^k(n+h) = (3^(a')(n+h) + c')/2^k`. Equality with `a = a'` is the condition `c = 3^a h + c'` on the two words, which holds for the whole class of `n mod 2^k` or not at all. Equality with `a != a'` is a nontrivial linear equation in `n`, satisfied by at most one `n` per class and step; these form a set of density zero for fixed `K`. Hence the density of `M_h(K)` exists and equals the Haar probability of the class-wide event, which is `mu_h(K)` for the pair `(y, y+h)`. (b) THM-4581 with `u = v + h`: `e = h in Z[1/3]`, `k = 0`; for almost every `y` the orbits of `y` and `y + h` meet at equal Terras time with `y + h` making exactly `0` fewer odd steps than `y`, i.e. with equal odd-step counts; the events `M_h(K)` increase to this almost-sure event, so `mu_h(K) -> 1`. (c) For `n in M_h(K)` with `n > 2^K`, `T^j(n) >= n/2^j > 1` and likewise for `n+h` for all `j <= K`, so neither orbit has reached `1` when they coincide at time `k <= K`; from then on the orbits are identical, so `tau(n) = k + tau(T^k n) = tau(n+h)` and the odd-step counts agree before `k` (by the merge condition) and after `k` (identical orbits). The density of `M_h(K) cap (2^K, infinity)` is `mu_h(K)`, and `K` is arbitrary. (d) On `M_h(K) cap (2^K, N]` the product `t(n)t(n+h)` equals `1`; elsewhere it is at least `-1`; so the average is at least `mu_h(K) - (1 - mu_h(K)) - o(1)`. The same for `lam`. ∎

**3.** Computation; see the scripts. The integer merge census stops when either orbit reaches `1` (a first version counted two orbits resting at `1` as a merge and was discarded).

## Scope and boundary

- Statement 1 concerns ordinary averages at every cutoff; no logarithmic weighting is involved. The limits are typically nonzero: the stopping-time parity is `2`-adically locally constant, which is the opposite of the situation for the Liouville function.
- Statement 2(d) is conditional on reaching `1`; 2(a)-(c) are unconditional. The merge inside the final cycle (equal phase) would give equal parity but not equal `tau`; density-one merges happen at bounded depth, before that.
- The rate at which `mu_h(K) -> 1` and the rate at which the integer break density decays are not proved here (HYP-9258); THM-4581 states `c T^(-1/2) <= P(no merge by T) <= C T^(-1/2) (log T)^2`, both bounds at sketch level.
- Nothing here bears on whether orbits reach `1`.

## Reading

Below the stopping time, Collatz statistics along affine forms are exact Haar computations because the relevant events are periodic; the Chowla manuscript's central lemma (bounded independence fooling residue tests beyond the joint period) is the price paid for the absence of periodicity in multiplicative number theory, and Collatz does not pay it. Beyond the stopping time the natural functionals are locally constant along lags because the orbits of `n` and `n+h` merge, a theorem of the repository's own thread; the only Chowla-flavoured question left is the one-point statistic: `tau mod 2` is not equidistributed in log-bins at any computed scale, its bin mean being locked to the Mercator comma `3^53 ~ 2^84` (the signed resonance law: the Codex note collatz_signed_resonance_pair_mass_20261013b.md and THM-4629, where the general character law and the silence of every finite deterministic truncation are proved), while its ordinary mean is `o(1)` (Codex, from THM-4581) at a rate the model puts at `N^(-0.0018)`.
