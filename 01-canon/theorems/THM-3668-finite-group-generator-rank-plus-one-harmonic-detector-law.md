# THM-3668 -- finite-group harmonic detection costs generator rank plus one

**PROVED.**  This theorem isolates the group-theoretic mechanism behind the
three-twist LRC detector.  Fourier diagonalization is unnecessary.

## 1. Statement

Let `Gamma` be a nontrivial finite group and let

```text
d=d(Gamma)                                           (1)
```

be the minimum cardinality of a generating set.  For a complex mask
`mu:Gamma->C`, use left convolution

```text
(C_mu f)(x)=sum_(s in Gamma) mu(s)f(s^(-1)x).       (2)
```

Assume `mu` is nonzero and mean zero:

```text
sum_s mu(s)=0.                                      (3)
```

Then

```text
ker C_mu=C*1      implies      |supp(mu)|>=d+1.     (4)
```

Conversely, choose a minimal generating tuple

```text
g_1,...,g_d                                        (5)
```

and arbitrary positive real weights

```text
p_0,...,p_(d-1)>0,
S=p_0+...+p_(d-1).                                  (6)
```

The mask

```text
mu=p_0 delta_e+sum_(i=1)^(d-1)p_i delta_(g_i)
   -S delta_(g_d)                                   (7)
```

has exactly `d+1` support sites, has mean zero, and satisfies

```text
ker C_mu=C*1.                                       (8)
```

Thus `d+1` is the exact minimum support.

## 2. Lower bound from a proper support-difference subgroup

Let `T=supp(mu)` have `r<=d` points and choose `s_0 in T`.  Form

```text
H=<s_0^(-1)s:s in T>.                               (9)
```

The group `H` is generated by at most `r-1<d` elements, so the definition of
`d(Gamma)` makes it a proper subgroup.  Choose a nonconstant left
`H`-invariant function `f` on `Gamma`; for example, the indicator of `H` is
constant under left multiplication by `H` and is nonconstant because
`H!=Gamma`.

Every support point is `s=s_0h` with `h in H`.  Hence

```text
f(s^(-1)x)=f(h^(-1)s_0^(-1)x)=f(s_0^(-1)x)          (10)
```

for all `s in T`.  Equations (2)--(3) give

```text
(C_mu f)(x)=f(s_0^(-1)x)sum_s mu(s)=0.              (11)
```

So the kernel contains a nonconstant function, contradicting (4).  This
proves the lower bound.

## 3. Positive construction by the maximum-modulus principle

Let `f` satisfy `C_mu f=0` for the mask (7).  The convolution equation is

```text
S f(g_d^(-1)x)
 =p_0 f(x)+sum_(i=1)^(d-1)p_i f(g_i^(-1)x).         (12)
```

Choose `y` where `|f|` attains its maximum `M`; this is where finiteness is
used.  Substitute `x=g_d y` in (12).  The left side has modulus `SM`, while
the right side is a positive weighted sum of `d` values of modulus at most
`M`.  Equality in the triangle inequality forces

```text
f(g_d y)=f(g_i^(-1)g_d y)=f(y),       1<=i<d.       (13)
```

Every point obtained from `y` still has the same maximal value, so (13) can
be iterated.  The propagating left multipliers are

```text
g_d,             g_i^(-1)g_d, 1<=i<d.              (14)
```

They generate `Gamma`, because

```text
g_i=g_d(g_i^(-1)g_d)^(-1).                          (15)
```

In a finite group, the semigroup generated by (14) equals the subgroup they
generate: every inverse is a positive power.  Thus the propagation orbit of
`y` is all of `Gamma`, and `f` is constant.  Constants are in the kernel by
(3), proving (8).

## 4. Representation-theoretic form

For any nontrivial irreducible unitary representation `rho`, the matrix

```text
p_0 I+sum_(i=1)^(d-1)p_i rho(g_i)-S rho(g_d)        (16)
```

is invertible.  Indeed, a kernel vector would make a positive weighted sum
of unit vectors attain the maximal possible norm.  Triangle-equality
rigidity would make that vector fixed by every generator, contradicting
nontrivial irreducibility.  Equation (16) is the nonabelian analogue of
THM-3665's nonvanishing Fourier multipliers.

## 5. Boundaries and transfers

- For `Gamma=F_p^d`, (4)--(8) recover THM-3665's exact `d+1` law.
- For the LRC target plane, `d=2`, so three twist sites are optimal.
- The construction is an operation-compatible finite observer: its
  zero equation is harmonicity on a strongly connected weighted Cayley
  digraph.
- On an infinite group, a bounded harmonic function need not attain its
  supremum, and nontrivial Poisson boundaries can occur.  The proof therefore
  gives no conclusion about amenability, soficity, or the recently opened
  non-sofic frontier.
- The theorem controls the kernel of one convolution operator.  It does not
  assert simple spectrum, a minimal separating codebook, or rigidity of the
  full operator centralizer; THM-3665 and THM-3667 show those are separate
  questions even in the abelian target plane.

**QED.**
