# THM-1266 -- closest-return leaf rank and the paid-star obstruction

chronological owner word.  The proved paper core is as follows.  A closest
equal-owner pair has distinct internal owners, hence between two and six

THM-1253 invoice.  Therefore closest-return rank is a valid well-founded
