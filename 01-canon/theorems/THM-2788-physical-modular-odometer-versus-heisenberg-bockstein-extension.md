# THM-2788 -- the physical odometer is the other central extension

**RESERVED / PROOF-COMPLETE CANDIDATE + VERIFIED-EXACT / AWAITING
INDEPENDENT AUDIT.**

THM-2779 constructs the carry-suppressed Heisenberg action on the
`169`-point digit carrier.  THM-2782 then finds a physical `+13` semantic arm
segment on that same digit quotient, but its coefficient changes after the
thirteenth arm, when the address has moved by `169`.

The apparent tension has an exact group-theoretic explanation.  There are
two nonisomorphic odd-prime groups acting on the same `p^2` points:

```text
carry-suppressed successor + dilation  -> H_p;
actual odometer successor + dilation  -> M_(p^3).
```

They have the same alternating commutator, so the determinant and central
spectra in THM-2779 cannot distinguish them.  The physical group has one
additional Bockstein coordinate: its low successor has order `p^2`, not
`p`.  At full address depth this becomes a lower-central tower.  The
THM-2782 high-sheet hostile is exactly its next commutator digit, not
unstructured sheet noise.

## 1. Two actions on the same two-digit carrier

Let `p` be prime and write

```text
Omega_p=Z/p^2Z,                 n=v+p w,       v,w in F_p.       (1)
```

Define four permutations:

```text
O:n -> n+1,                                      actual odometer;
X:n -> (1+p)n,             X(v,w)=(v,w+v),      dilation/shear;
Y(v,w)=(v+1,w),                                  carry-suppressed shift;
Z:n -> n+p,                Z(v,w)=(v,w+1).       high-digit shift. (2)
```

The actual and suppressed successors differ precisely at the carry wall:

```text
O(n)=Y(n)                  for v!=p-1,
O(n)=ZY(n)                 for v=p-1.                         (3)
```

The suppressed group has presentation

```text
H_p=<X,Y,Z |
     X^p=Y^p=Z^p=1, Z central, [X,Y]=Z>,                     (4)
```

where `[A,B]=ABA^(-1)B^(-1)`.  The physical two-digit group is

```text
M_(p^3)=<O,X |
     O^(p^2)=X^p=1, XOX^(-1)=O^(1+p)>.                       (5)
```

In `(5)`,

```text
Z=O^p,                         [X,O]=O^p=Z.                  (6)
```

Both actions are faithful and both groups have order `p^3`.  For odd `p`,
however, they are not isomorphic:

```text
H_p element orders:       1^1, p^(p^3-1);
M_(p^3) element orders:   1^1, p^(p^2-1), (p^2)^(p^2(p-1)).
                                                                    (7)
```

At `p=13`,

```text
H_13:    1^1,13^2196;
M_2197:  1^1,13^168,169^2028.                              (8)
```

At `p=2` the two generated permutation groups coincide with `D_8`; their
marked successor sections remain different, but the odd-prime exponent
distinction disappears.

## 2. Same commutator, different power/Bockstein coordinate

For both groups,

```text
Z(group)=derived subgroup=<Z> isomorphic C_p,
group/<Z> isomorphic F_p^2.                                  (9)
```

Use quotient coordinates `(a,b)` represented by `X` and the low successor.
For odd `p`, the `p`-power map from the quotient to the center is

```text
q_H(a,b)=0,
q_M(a,b)=b,                                                  (10)
```

because every element of `H_p` has exponent `p`, while

```text
(O^bX^a)^p=Z^b.                                              (11)
```

The distinction can be read directly from extension cocycles.  Choose the
sections

```text
s_H(a,b)=Y^bX^a,
s_M(a,b)=O^bX^a,                  0<=a,b<p.                  (12)
```

For `u=(a,b)` and `v=(c,d)`, normal-form multiplication gives

```text
c_H(u,v)=a d,
c_M(u,v)=a d+floor((b+d)/p)                    in F_p.       (13)
```

Their alternating parts agree:

```text
c_H(u,v)-c_H(v,u)
 =c_M(u,v)-c_M(v,u)
 =a d-c b.                                                    (14)
```

Their difference is the standard carry cocycle

```text
c_M-c_H=floor((b+d)/p),                                      (15)
```

the Bockstein of the second quotient coordinate.  It is not a coboundary:
restriction to the `b`-axis is split `C_p x C_p` for `H_p` and nonsplit
cyclic `C_(p^2)` for `M_(p^3)`.

Equivalently, for odd `p`,

```text
H^2(F_p^2,F_p)
 isomorphic wedge^2(F_p^2)^* direct-sum beta((F_p^2)^*),

[H_p]      =(omega,0),
[M_(p^3)] =(omega,beta(e_2^*)).                             (16)
```

Thus the determinant/commutator sees `omega` and necessarily misses the
physical carry Bockstein.

## 3. The common carrier degree does not identify the extensions

Both groups act faithfully on `p^2` points, and

```text
mu_perm(H_p)=mu_perm(M_(p^3))=p^2.                           (17)
```

Indeed, an action of a `p`-group on fewer than `p^2` points has only fixed
or `p`-point orbits.  Its image on each `p`-point orbit lies in a cyclic
Sylow `p`-subgroup of `S_p`; the product image is abelian and kills the
nontrivial commutator subgroup.  Hence it cannot be faithful.

Every faithful action of exact degree `p^2` is transitive, with a core-free
order-`p` stabilizer.  For odd `p` the two groups differ again:

```text
H_p:       p+1 minimal transitive action classes;
M_(p^3):  one minimal transitive action class.                (18)
```

For `M_(p^3)`, the `p^2-1` elements of order dividing `p` form `p+1`
order-`p` subgroups: the center and `p` noncentral complements over the
`X`-line.  Those `p` complements are conjugate and core-free.  The natural
action `(2)` is their coset action.  At `p=2`, `D_8` has the two reflection
classes already classified in THM-2779.

Inside `Sym(Omega_p)`, the exact overlap for odd `p` is

```text
H_p intersect M_(p^3)=<X,Z>,                order p^2.        (19)
```

Thus the two actions agree on the shear and central direction while
disagreeing on the physical low successor.

## 4. Their join is the carry-wall wreath product

The discrepancy itself has a simple physical carrier.  Put

```text
C=O Y^(-1).                                                   (20)
```

In digit coordinates, `C` increments `w` only on the column `v=0`.
Conjugating by `Y` gives the `p` disjoint column translations

```text
C_r=Y^r C Y^(-r),                  r in F_p.                  (21)
```

They commute, each has order `p`, and

```text
B=<C_r:r in F_p> isomorphic F_p^(F_p),
product_r C_r=Z,
product_r C_r^r=X.                                           (22)
```

Since `O=CY`,

```text
<H_p,M_(p^3)>=B semidirect <Y>
             isomorphic C_p wreath C_p,
|<H_p,M_(p^3)>|=p^(p+1).                                    (23)
```

The missing datum between the two lifts is therefore not another scalar
central phase.  It is the one-column carry function.  Modulo the constant
line `<Z>`, its translates span the `(p-1)`-dimensional augmentation
module.  Over the complex character basis, the delta column has every
`C_p` Fourier character nonzero.

For `p=13`, `(23)` identifies an exact twelve-dimensional charged carry
sidecar adjacent to the central coefficient spectrum.  It does not identify
that sidecar with a root, owner, endpoint, or physical current.

## 5. The full physical tower

The physical address in THM-2782 has six digits, not two.  For an odd prime
and `k>=2`, act on

```text
Omega_(p,k)=Z/p^kZ
```

by the same formulas

```text
O:n->n+1,                       X:n->(1+p)n.               (24)
```

The principal unit `1+p` has exact order `p^(k-1)` modulo `p^k`.  Hence

```text
G_(p,k)=<O,X>
       isomorphic C_(p^k) semidirect C_(p^(k-1)),
|G_(p,k)|=p^(2k-1).                                           (25)
```

Put

```text
Z_r=O^(p^r),                       r=0,...,k.                (26)
```

Then

```text
[X,Z_r]=Z_(r+1),                    0<=r<=k-1,
gamma_(r+1)(G_(p,k))=<Z_r>=<O^(p^r)>,   1<=r<=k,
Z(G_(p,k))=<Z_(k-1)>,
class(G_(p,k))=k.                                            (27)
```

Here `Z_k=1`, so the last displayed lower-central term is
`gamma_(k+1)=1`; the formula deliberately starts at `gamma_2`, since
`gamma_1=G_(p,k)` is not the cyclic odometer subgroup.

In particular `Z_1=O^p` is central only after truncating to `k=2`.
Upstairs it has the next commutator

```text
[X,Z_1]=Z_2=O^(p^2).                                        (28)
```

Reduction modulo `p^2` gives a surjection

```text
G_(p,k) -> G_(p,2)=M_(p^3)
```

with

```text
|kernel|=p^(2k-4).                                           (29)
```

For `p=13,k=6`,

```text
|G_(13,6)|=13^11=1,792,160,394,037,
|kernel(G_(13,6)->G_(13,2))|=13^8=815,730,721,
Z(G_(13,6))=<O^(13^5)>.                                     (30)
```

This is the graded carrier that the depth-two Heisenberg shadow forgets.

## 6. THM-2782 detects the second commutator

Let `F_tau(n)` denote the clock-one raw semantic coefficient constructed in
THM-2782 on the physical address orbit.  Its arm step is

```text
n->n+13=Z_1 n.                                               (31)
```

After quotienting modulo `169`, this is the central order-thirteen direction
shared by `H_13` and `M_2197`.  On the full address `Z/13^6`, however,
`Z_1` has order `13^5`.

THM-2782 proves at its selected base `n_1`, for `tau=3,12`,

```text
F_tau(n_1)=c_1,
F_tau(n_1+169)=0,

c_1=790161473087466480.                                     (32)
```

But `n_1+169=Z_2 n_1`, and by `(28)`,

```text
Z_2=[X,Z_1].                                                 (33)
```

Consequently the second lower-central difference is explicitly nonzero:

```text
(F_tau after Z_2-F_tau)(n_1)=-c_1.                          (34)
```

The high digit in THM-2782 is therefore not merely load-bearing; its first
observed failure is exactly the next commutator digit of the physical
odometer tower.

There is also a necessary terminology boundary.  The thirteen samples
`F_tau(Z_1^j n_1)`, `0<=j<13`, form a first-digit or polyphase spectrum of
one lifted segment.  They are not a genuine character decomposition of the
full physical `Z_1` orbit until invariance under `Z_2` is proved.  THM-2782
already states the corresponding failure of cyclic equivariance; `(33)`
identifies its group-theoretic source.
