# THM-3386 -- canonical divergence remembers the minimal polynomial, not the resultant

**PROVED + VERIFIED-EXACT + INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.**

## 1. Setup and result

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field, put `R=K[x,z]`, and let

```text
P=f(x)+g(x)z,                     0!=g in K[x],          (1)
D_P(q)=P_x q_z-g q_x=Jac(P,q).                           (2)
```

Since `D_P(P)=0`, the cokernel

```text
C_P=R/D_P(R)                                             (3)
```

is a `K[P]`-module.  Assume the gradient ideal `(P_x,g)` is the unit
ideal.  Choose any polynomial Bezout row

```text
A P_x+B g=1                                             (4)
```

and define

```text
m=A_x+B_z,                    mu=[m] in C_P.             (5)
```

The class `mu` is independent of the row.  Indeed two rows differ by
`(hg,-hP_x)`, whose divergence is `-D_P(h)`.

For nonconstant `g`, let

```text
E_g=K[x]/(g),
ev_(f,g):K[T] -> E_g,              F |-> F(f mod g).    (6)
```

Its kernel has a unique monic generator `chi_(f,g)`.  Equivalently, `chi`
is the minimal polynomial of multiplication by `f mod g` on the finite
`K`-space `E_g`.  For constant `g`, set `E_g=0` and `chi=1`.

Then, under the identification `K[T]=K[P]`,

```text
Ann_(K[P])(mu)=(chi_(f,g)(P)),
K[P] mu ~= K[T]/(chi_(f,g)(T)).                         (7)
```

In particular `mu` is pure vertical torsion,

```text
dim_K K[P]mu=deg chi,
mu tensor_(K[P]) K(P)=0.                               (8)
```

## 2. The canonical localized primitive

First suppose `g` is nonconstant and invert it:

The elements `P,x` are algebraically independent: in any proposed relation,
the top `P`-degree term produces a nonzero top `z`-coefficient divisible by
the corresponding power of `g`.

```text
S=R[g^(-1)]=K[P,x,g^(-1)],
z=(P-f(x))/g(x).                                       (9)
```

With `P` held fixed,

```text
D_P=-g(x) partial_x,                 ker(D_P:S->S)=K[P]. (10)
```

For the kernel statement, differentiation in the rational field
`K(P)(x)` leaves only `K(P)`.  Its intersection with
`K[P,x,g^(-1)]` is `K[P]`: a nonconstant denominator in `P` cannot divide
a power of the nonzero polynomial `g(x)`.

Put

```text
h=-A/g in S.                                            (11)
```

Adding the syzygy `(hg,-hP_x)` to `(A,B)` gives the localized row
`(0,1/g)`, whose divergence is zero.  The row-independence calculation
therefore gives the exact primitive identity

```text
D_P(h)=m.                                               (12)
```

This is a generic primitive only.  Whether a multiple of it returns to the
polynomial ring is the entire integral problem.

## 3. The annihilator is one evaluation kernel

For `F in K[T]`, equations `(10)--(12)` give

```text
F(P)mu=0
 iff there is q in R with D_P(q)=F(P)m
 iff q-F(P)h belongs to K[P]
 iff F(P)h belongs to R.                               (13)
```

Now `F(P)h=-A F(P)/g`.  Reducing `(4)` modulo `g` shows that `A` is a
unit in `R/(g)`, with inverse `P_x`.  Hence

```text
F(P)h in R
 iff g divides F(P)
 iff g divides F(f)
 iff F belongs to ker(ev_(f,g)).                       (14)
```

The middle equivalence uses `P=f mod g`.  Combining `(13)--(14)` proves
`(7)`.  If `g` is constant, the row `(0,g^(-1))` has zero divergence;
then `mu=0` and the convention `chi=1` proves the same statement.

This proof also explains why generic exactness cannot be promoted directly
to integral exactness: the missing information is exactly the finite
evaluation algebra `K[x]/(g)`.

## 4. Geometric collision formula

Over an algebraic closure, write

```text
g=c product_alpha (x-alpha)^(m_alpha).                 (15)
```

Gradient unimodularity is equivalent to

```text
m_alpha>=2 and f'(alpha)!=0 for every alpha.            (16)
```

For a simple root, one can choose `z` so that both `g` and `P_x` vanish.
For a repeated root, `P_x(alpha,z)=f'(alpha)`, proving `(16)` in both
directions.

Chinese remainders split `E_g` into the local factors
`Kbar[u]/(u^(m_alpha))`.  At such a factor,

```text
f(alpha+u)-f(alpha)=u times a unit,                     (17)
```

so the local minimal polynomial is
`(T-f(alpha))^(m_alpha)`.  The minimal polynomial on a direct sum is the
least common multiple of the local ones.  Therefore, writing

```text
M_b=max{m_alpha : g(alpha)=0 and f(alpha)=b},           (18)
```

one obtains the exact Galois-stable formula

```text
chi_(f,g)(T)=product_(b in f(Z(g))) (T-b)^(M_b)
             in K[T].                                  (19)
```

Coincident root values thus combine by maximum multiplicity.  The
characteristic polynomial, equivalently the corresponding resultant up to
its leading scalar, uses the sum of the local multiplicities instead.  This
is the sharp collision boundary.

## 5. Controls and minimal hostiles

The following packets distinguish every clause.

```text
constant:       g=1                         chi=1;
one root:       f=x, g=x^2                  chi=T^2;
equal collision:
                f=x^2, g=(x^2-1)^2         chi=(T-1)^2,
                                             char=(T-1)^4;
unequal collision:
                f=x^2, g=(x-1)^2(x+1)^3    chi=(T-1)^3,
                                             char=(T-1)^5;
nonsplit/Q:     f=x, g=(x^2+1)^2           chi=(T^2+1)^2;
distinct values:
                f=x, g=x^2(x-1)^3          chi=T^2(T-1)^3. (20)
```

In the equal collision, `(P-1)mu` is nonzero but `(P-1)^2mu=0`.
In the unequal collision, powers one and two survive and power three kills.
These are the cheapest exact refutations of a summed-multiplicity
annihilator.

## 6. Relation to the existing response split

THM-3326 proves that the unit-response class `theta=[1]` is free over
`K[P]` when `g` has at least two geometric roots.  The present theorem says
that the canonical divergence class `mu` is nevertheless torsion in every
branch.  Thus the two responses separate cleanly:

```text
multiple roots:       K[P]theta is free,
                      K[P]mu is vertical torsion.       (21)
```

In particular a relation `A(P)mu=B(P)theta` in a multi-root branch forces
`B=0`: multiply by `chi(P)` and use freeness of `theta`.

This is also the integral information discarded by the punctured generic
connection in THM-3348.  It repairs the localization trap recorded in
`MISTAKE-374`: generic vanishing of `mu` does not provide an integral
polynomial Jacobian mate.

THM-2063 already proves the linear-in-one-source-variable Keller stratum
tame.  Consequently `(7)` is a response-module theorem, not a new case of
`JC(2)`.  It does not assert that all of `C_P` is finite, cyclic, or split.
