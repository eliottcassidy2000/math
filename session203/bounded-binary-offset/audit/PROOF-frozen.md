# Bounded binary offsets permit only quadratically logarithmic parent sets

Status: **RESERVED / UNREVIEWED PROOF CANDIDATE**. This is an all-order
obstruction to a specified constructive strategy, not a spectrum theorem.
The twelve-template occupancy lemma and every unconditional eventual odd

## 1. Scope and inherited physical interface

The actual source orbit starts at t=(D,E,U,d)=(1,0,2,1), with chronological
operations

    O(D,E,U,d)=(D,0,2U,d),
    Z(D,E,U,d)=(D-E+U,d,2U-d,d),
    J(D,E,U,d)=(D-E+U,D-E,D+U,D).

Every recipe is an actual source. Appending the final closing J produces
an actual C1 tournament with Hamilton-path count F=D-E+U. We use the
physical compiler in `session201/central-arithmetic/PROOF.md`, Sections2--3.
No arbitrary integer point of a source cone is declared physical.

In nonnegative physical coordinates

    (a,f,e,p)=(D-E-d,U-D+E-d,E,d-E),
    D=a+2e+p, E=e, U=a+f+2e+2p, d=e+p,

we have d>=1 and D odd. Define three exact sidecars

    ell=a+p=D-2E,
    h=f-d=U-D+E-2d,
    c=f+d=U-D+E.

The first is positive odd, the second is odd, and the third is positive.
The relevant exact identities are

    F(O s)=F(J s)=2F(s)-ell(s),
    F(Z s)=2F(s)+h(s),
    h(Z s)=h(s), h(J s)=-D(s), h(O s)=a+2f+p,
    c(Z s)=c(s), c(J s)=D(s), c(O s)=2U-D>=U.       (1)

We also use the exact profile identity JZ=OJ. Its interpretation is equality
of numerical profiles of two actual constructions, not graph isomorphism.

Closest proved mechanism: the Session202 exact +/-1 binary-child
classification. Its hostile parent25 remains relevant. The corrected near
miss is inferring a bounded binary carry scheme from dense finite target
coverage. The least-used sidecar here is c=f+d, which, like h, is Z-invariant.
Concepts: physical source cores; source rebasing; boundary offsets; rewrite
normal forms; sparse exponential families. The new result treats every
fixed bound on the binary offset, not just +/-1.

## 2. Two bounded-core lemmas

Fix an integer C>=1 and let

    B_C={actual numerical source profiles with U<=4C}.

This is finite, with actual recipes retained. U increases by at least one
under every operation, so it can be generated by a finite complete search.
Its size is denoted b_C. Section4 gives an explicit polynomial bound in C,
using source-readout growth and the profile rewrite JZ=OJ.

**Lemma 1 (bounded D).** If D(s)<=C, then s has the same numerical profile
as b.O^i for some b in B_C and i>=0.

Strip the final O letters from an actual recipe. If nothing remains, take
b=t. Otherwise the remaining last letter is Z or J. At a Z output,

    2D_new-U_new=2(D-E)+d>=3d>=3.

At a J output,

    2D_new-U_new=D-2E+U=ell+U>=3.

Thus the remaining core has U<=2D-3<=2C-3 and belongs to B_C. The retained
O suffix preserves D. This is a physical inverse of an actual recipe.

**Lemma 2 (bounded c).** If c(s)<=C, then s has the numerical profile of
one of

    b.Z^i,                 b.O^i.J,                  (2)

with b in B_C and i>=0.

Strip final Z letters, which preserve c. The base is either the seed, an O
output, or a J output. The seed belongs to B_C. At an O output c_new>=U_old,
so U_old<=C and U_new<=2C; this base also belongs to B_C. At a J output
D_old=c_new<=C. Apply Lemma1 to its predecessor to write the base as b.O^r.J.
Restoring Z^k and repeatedly using JZ=OJ gives b.O^(r+k).J. This proves(2).

## 3. Normal forms for bounded binary offsets

**Lemma 3.** Every actual source satisfying either ell<=C or |h|<=C has
the numerical profile of b followed by one of the six suffix forms

    O^i Z^j,           Z^i J O Z^j,           O^i J J O Z^j,
    O^i J,             Z^i J,                O^i J J,       (3)

where b belongs to B_C and i,j>=0. The second exponent is unused in the
last three forms. This is containment, not an assertion that every displayed
choice has a bounded sidecar.

*Proof for ell.* The seed is in B_C. At an O output, ell_new=D_old, so
Lemma1 applies and gives b.O^i. At a Z output,

    ell_new=2a+f+d >= U_old/2.

Therefore U_old<=2C and U_new<=4C, and the whole output is in B_C. At a J
output ell_new=c_old; apply Lemma2 to the predecessor. Its two possibilities
give b.Z^i.J and b.O^i.JJ. These are all contained in(3).

*Proof for negative h.* Strip final Z letters. The seed has h=-1. An O
output has h_new=a+2f+p>0, since a+p is positive odd. Thus any other base
must be a J output, with D_old=-h<=C. Lemma1 and JZ=OJ put the restored
source in b.O^i.J. The seed branch t.Z^j is covered by the first form of(3).
There is no h=0 case, since h is odd.

*Proof for positive h.* Strip final Z letters. Neither seed nor J output
has positive h, so the source has form q.O.Z^j, where

    a(q)+2f(q)+p(q)=h<=C.                              (4)

If E(q)=0, then D(q)=a(q)+p(q)<=C. Lemma1 gives the first form in(3).
If E(q)>0, an actual recipe for q ends in Z or J, since O gives E=0.
For a Z predecessor r, a(q)=2a(r)+f(r)+d(r)>=U(r)/2. By(4), U(r)<=2C
and U(q)<=4C. Thus q belongs to B_C, again giving the first form.
For a J predecessor r, f(q)=0 and a(q)+p(q)=c(r)<=C. Lemma2 puts r
in b.Z^i or b.O^i.J. Adding the final J.O.Z^j gives the second or third
form in(3), respectively. Every transformation used retained an actual
recipe and exact numerical profile. QED.

The rewrite JZ=OJ is essential to the count below: keeping its two exponents
independent would incorrectly obscure the stronger quadratic-logarithmic
bound, although a cubic-logarithmic upper bound would still be valid.

## 4. All-order sparsity, including arbitrary source rebasing

For N>=3 define the exact integer length bound

    L_N=max{l>=0: 3^(l+1)<=N*2^l}.

Every source with F<=N has every actual recipe length at most L_N. Indeed
F(t)=3 and, coefficient by coefficient on the nonnegative physical cone,

    F=(2,1,3,3)y,
    F(O s)=F(J s)=(3,2,6,5)y >=(3/2)F(s),
    F(Z s)=(4,3,5,5)y >=(3/2)F(s).                      (5)

In each normal form(3), every free exponent is consequently at most L_N.
Thus the number of distinct actual source profiles with F<=N and either
ell<=C or |h|<=C is at most

    b_C [3(L_N+1)^2+3(L_N+1)] <=6 b_C (L_N+1)^2.        (6)

Repeated profiles only reduce the count. Equation(6) follows from the
all-order normal forms, not finite observed sparsity.

**Theorem.** Fix C>=1. Let P_C be the set of odd n>4C for which there exist
ANY actual source s with F(s)=n, ANY nonempty append word w, and ANY integer
r with |r|<=C satisfying

    F(s.w)=2n+r.                                        (7)

Then, for every N>=3,

    # {n in P_C:n<=N} <=6 b_C (L_N+1)^2 = O_C((log N)^2). (8)

In particular P_C has density zero and cannot contain a tail of the odds.
The choice of s can depend arbitrarily on n and r, so this includes arbitrary
target-preserving source rebasing before the append operation.

Proof. If |w|>=2, (5) gives F(s.w)>=(9/4)n>2n+C because n>4C. Hence w
has one letter. Equation(1) then forces ell<=C or |h|<=C. Apply(6) and
map each represented profile to its scalar F. QED.

For fixed C, at most O_C((log N)^2) parent counts through N can support even
one such bounded-offset append move. This rules out a scalar binary
constructor with uniformly bounded additive carry in this interface,
including a different bounded offset chosen for every target. It is stronger
than failure of the exact +/-1 tree. It does not exclude a constructor that
replaces part of the source word, changes the parent count before rebasing,
uses offsets growing with n, uses a larger multiplication base, or changes
the C1 interface. In particular it does not refute the twelve-template
occupancy conjecture, which is free to rebuild both source indices.

### 4.1 A polynomial core bound and growing-carry consequence

Let Fib_m be the Fibonacci numbers with Fib_0=0, Fib_1=1. Then

    b_C <= Fib_(2 L_(8C)+3)-1.                           (9)

Indeed F<=2U<=8C on B_C, so every recipe has length at most L_(8C).
Repeatedly replace JZ by OJ. Each replacement reduces the number of Z
letters, so it terminates at a word of the same length containing no JZ,
with the same actual numerical source profile. No uniqueness or confluence
claim is required. The number a_l of length-l words avoiding JZ satisfies

    a_0=1, a_1=3, a_l=3a_(l-1)-a_(l-2)=Fib_(2l+2).

The subtracted words are exactly the extensions by Z of the length-(l-1)
words ending in J; these are in bijection with arbitrary admissible words
of length l-2. Summing gives sum_(l=0)^L a_l=Fib_(2L+3)-1, proving(9).
This counts profile representatives from above and does not require distinct
normal words to represent distinct profiles.

Put lambda=(3+sqrt(5))/2 and

    kappa=log(lambda)/log(3/2).

Since L_(8C)<=log_(3/2)(8C/3), the Fibonacci formula makes (9)
b_C=O(C^kappa), with an absolute implied constant. Consequently the exact
bound(8) is uniformly

    O(C^kappa (1+log N)^2).                             (10)

The constant in(10) is independent of C and N. The restrictions n>4C and
C>=1 still apply.

For a quantitative consequence, let N be a positive multiple of four and
1<=C<N/8. If every odd n in (N/2,N] admitted a move(7), then necessarily

    N/4 <= 6 (Fib_(2 L_(8C)+3)-1) (L_N+1)^2.            (11)

In particular any such interval-covering bound C=C(N) must satisfy

    C(N) = Omega(N^(1/kappa)/(1+log N)^(2/kappa)).        (12)

This says more than an unbounded correction is required: even a uniformly
polylogarithmic binary offset cannot support that construction. Equation(11)
is the rounding-explicit version; (12) is its asymptotic consequence. It
remains an obstruction to append-only binary induction, not to rebuilding
both parameters of a compact central form or to general tournament surgery.
