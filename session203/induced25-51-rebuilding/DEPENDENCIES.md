# Dependencies and provenance

1. **CITED: Busch's strong-order minimum.** For a strong n-vertex tournament, n at least 3, the minimum Hamiltonian-path count is f(3q)=3 times 5^(q-1), f(3q+1)=5^q, and f(3q+2)=9 times 5^(q-1). In particular H is at least 5^((n-1)/3). The weaker inequality alone suffices here. Since 5^8=390625 exceeds 51^3=132651, a strong H51 tournament has order at most 8. Reference: A. H. Busch, Electron. J. Combin. 13 (2006), N3, Corollary 1. The inherited repository citation and formula are frozen in dependencies/THM-4744_BUSCH_PROVENANCE.md, section Setting. That file's other statements are not used, and this release does not claim to reprove Busch's theorem.
2. **PROVED elementary facts.** SCC multiplicativity and induced monotonicity are justified in session203-induced51-audit/REVIEW.md. These facts plus Busch give the all-order reduction, including arbitrary singleton padding and the factorization 51=3 times 17.
3. **FINITE-EXACT, independently audited: complete representatives through order 8.** The frozen bank and full-adjacency growth checker are in session194-weak-parent-certificate/. The checker exhausts all 62,423 one-vertex extension presentations and proves equality with the bank inductively at every order. Its original normal/optimized C++ receipts are retained. The inherited independent audit is dependencies/SESSION194_COMPLETENESS_AUDIT.md; only its bank-completeness review is needed here, not its separate H41/H67 weak-parent theorem.
4. **FINITE-EXACT, independently audited: new H51 subset census and repair.** session203-induced51-audit/check.py uses ending-at-vertex DP and independent literal simple-path DFS. check_repair.py uses literal permutations. Normal and optimized receipts agree. GROWTH_REPLAY.json independently replays dependency 3 on the exact frozen bank. The audit records the exact source hash, all 12 strong H51 matrices, and every induced-count histogram.

The code tests the finite universe after a proved order reduction. It does not extrapolate a bounded search to all tournament orders. The eventual odd spectrum remains open.
