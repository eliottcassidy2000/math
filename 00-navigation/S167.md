# Session 167: recurring structures and constructive bridges

This session develops several precise connections between earlier threads. Its constructions are not a complete classification of tournament or metagraph structure, and none proves the open tournament Hamiltonian-path spectrum conjecture.

1. [Octonion transport and covers](../05-knowledge/results/session167_recurring_structures_20261103/octonion/NOTE.md). Left multiplication on signed octonion units produces a 128-element permutation transport group and a concrete tower of regular covers whose composite is nonregular. The 16-sheet cover over an earlier tournament quotient is a formal observation-state extension, not a realization by tournament classes.
2. [Honeycomb return laws](../05-knowledge/results/session167_recurring_structures_20261103/honeycomb/NOTE.md). One colored honeycomb covers both arithmetic reflection states and an actual pinned three-arc tournament cube; a positive square-sum cycle family exhibits the two-lap synchronization. Pinned states are not the blue/black complement-line metagraph or unmarked classes, and the Collatz comparison is a finite capacity obstruction, not a conjugacy or convergence result.
3. [Divisor lattices, aliquot trees, and cactus graphs](../05-knowledge/results/session167_recurring_structures_20261103/aliquot/NOTE.md). Marked divisor grids and finite rooted trees embed into equal-profile flip-class fibers; cactus graphs embed via the ternary switch exactly when they have no five-cycle. The aliquot examples are finite certificates, not a general termination theorem. Arithmetic weights and directed marks remain necessary data.
4. [Cubic norm and guard dynamics](../05-knowledge/results/session167_recurring_structures_20261103/norm/NOTE.md). A cubic norm yields finite descent roots for each positive integral shell and describes the norm-two nonnegative states, with modular periodicity results. These are endpoint-state classifications, not uniqueness or classification of all tournaments realizing those states.
5. [Ternary profile switch and constraint compiler](../05-knowledge/results/session167_recurring_structures_20261103/ternary/TERNARY_CONSTRAINTS.md). Three strong order-six classes share a complete path-cover profile and form a triangle in the single-arc-flip class graph; ordinal composition transports capacity constraints, finite Ramsey coloring spaces, and cellular clocks into equal-profile families. This does not improve a Ramsey bound or solve the geometric fence problem.

Each packet states inherited sources, proof and computation boundaries, and reproduction commands. The independent controls are retained alongside the relevant certificates. In particular, the simple flip-class graph is distinct from the repository's blue/black complement-line metagraph, and graph-cover state labels need not have physical tournament realizations.
