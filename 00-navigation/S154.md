# Session 154: spectrum gadgets and arithmetic constraints

The conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd tournament Hamiltonian-path counts remains open. These packets develop specific constructions, exact constraints and inspirations; they do not prove that conjecture.

- [Frontier review](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/frontier/REPORT.md) pins the prior source state and distinguishes verified structural results from open closure.
- [Feedback-core gap expansion](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/inspiration/README.md) gives an exact inclusion-exclusion formula and fixed-template recurrence.
- [Sparse adaptive closure](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/closure/NOTE.md) bounds specific transported and finite-branch families, not unrestricted gadgets.
- [Triangle interfaces](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/interfaces/README.md) gives a uniform-module identity and finite nonuniform replacement examples, without claiming an invariant bank.
- [Seven-adic coverage](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/golden/SEVEN_ADIC.md) lifts a specific transported family; [general prime-power analysis](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/padic_general/GENERAL.md) includes 19-adic coverage and a 13-adic obstruction; [the refinement](../05-knowledge/results/session154_gadgets_20261103/padic13/REFINEMENT.md) identifies the exact higher 13-adic image and 19-adic fibre count. These are congruence results, not exact target coverage.

Proof scopes, provenance and reproduction commands appear in each packet. Search and finite calculations do not establish an all-order spectrum induction.
