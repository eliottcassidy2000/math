## Read first: every cyclic quotient converse is visible in every fixed ambient

The [unified cyclic theorem](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/cyclic_ambient_synthesis/PROOF.md), with [independent review](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/cyclic_ambient_synthesis_audit/REVIEW.md), separates C3[A,B,C] from its opposite quotient for **arbitrary nonempty child tournaments and every fixed ambient tournament**. No child-size, parity, transitivity, common-profile or outer strong-connectivity restriction remains. Physical child sets and names stay fixed. Target interiors may change if their complete ordered path-cover profiles agree with the source profiles.

The actual ports have fixed edge x→y and are uniform within each child. Both ports receive the core’s original exterior incidences. The theorem is about complete augmented-tournament responses, not independently relabeled graphs or products of endpoint marginals.

Three exhaustive branches prove the ambient conclusion:

1. **A singleton child:** all 61 singleton-containing triples in the [five-type classification](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/five_profile_types/PROOF.md) have a query with augmented profile difference (2,0,…) modulo four. The [five-type audit](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/five_profile_types_audit/REVIEW.md) retains complete canonical data.
2. **Nontrivial children, with an odd child or a pair:** the other 63 nontrivial type triples have a query with difference (4,0,…) modulo eight. The [odd-profile lift](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/cyclic_odd_profile_lift/PROOF.md) and [audit](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/cyclic_odd_profile_lift_audit/REVIEW.md) explicitly handle the odd-total-order correction and preserve the unsafe-query boundary.
3. **Three even children of order at least four:** the [local modulo-sixteen theorem](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/even_cyclic_mod16/PROOF.md) and [all-cover ambient companion](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/even_cyclic_mod16_ambient/PROOF.md) prove the special cyclic query has difference (8,0,…) modulo sixteen. See the distinct [local](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/even_mod16_audit/LOCAL_REVIEW.md) and [ambient](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/even_mod16_audit/AMBIENT_REVIEW.md) reviews.

In each branch every higher augmented cover-coordinate difference vanishes in the stated modulus. Outer module substitution therefore multiplies the surviving Hamilton difference by an odd first coefficient. A nonzero local scalar gap alone is never treated as an ambient certificate. The special modulo-sixteen identity does not contradict the retained [failure of general modulo-sixteen transfer](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/mod8_transfer/SHARPNESS.md).

### Original-module families and the remaining highest-node frontier

The synthesis also detects every root-containing XOR family of modules of the **original cyclic source**, including nested or overlapping proper selections inside its children. Complete profile monotonicity and [converse-complement duality](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/monotone_dual_queries/PROOF.md) give a fixed two-query battery {z,Jz}. For one fixed source and ambient, the baseline-negative member works for every such family target; which member has that sign may depend on the ambient.

This is **source-relative detection, not all-pairs injectivity among arbitrary family outputs**. It excludes exterior changes and arbitrary sequences using newly created modules.

The [highest-changed-node reduction](../05-knowledge/results/session188_structural_bridges_20261104/highest_quotient_reduction/PROOF.md) already excludes highest changed linear quotients. Section 7 of the new synthesis excludes highest changed C3 quotients as well, restoring other disjoint changes by exact equal-profile substitution. Thus every highest changed quotient in a still-possible original-module collision must be **prime of order at least five**. There is no prime four-name exception. If all prime quotients in the original strong-module tree are triangles, every original-module target different from its source is distinguishable. Earlier frozen packets retain their historical scope statements; this synthesis supplies the stronger current C3 boundary.
