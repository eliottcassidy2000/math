# Session 149: tournament spectrum and class symmetry

[Research packet and reproduction guide](../05-knowledge/results/session149_tournament_spectrum_20261103/README.md).

The global Hamiltonian-path count spectrum remains open. An exact finite decision procedure follows from existing strong-tournament minima and component factorization; a separate one-apex family has an explicit count formula and a finite membership test. Exact cyclic-block generating functions provide additional constructions.

The class-census work proves two-adic divisibility refinements from Davis's formula and gives exact small converse diagrams. Converse has fixed classes; the recorded complete pairing is artificial and encoding-dependent. The toothpick half-turn has just one fixed segment, obstructing a literal equivariant injection from tournament classes under converse. The even-graph bridge gives a different equinumerosity, not a natural pairing.

All proofs, citations, executable checks, prior-art credits, and scope limits are in the linked packet. Finite calculations do not establish global classification or novelty.
