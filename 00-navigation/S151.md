# Session 151: spectrum history and metagraph structure

The conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamiltonian-path counts remains open. The survey is targeted, not a review of every prior record, and no literature-priority claim is asserted.

- [Spectrum history and proof frontier](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/spectrum_history/HISTORY.md) compares prior constructions, exact reductions, failed extrapolations, and the finite certificate through 100,001.
- [Provenance audit](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/provenance/AUDIT.md) separates inherited work, proofs, computations, and unresolved priority.
- [Ordinal-pair insertion](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/proof_routes/RESULT.md) proves a new scoped arithmetic route and states a sufficient prime-lane condition; it does not establish that condition.
- [Ternary compiler target](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/strategy/TERNARY_COMPILER.md) gives a sufficient finite-state induction condition; the required offset gadgets and state bank remain open.
- [Metagraph structure](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/metagraph/STRUCTURE.md) gives an exact finite procedure, parity and multiplicity consequences, and limitations on closed-form classification.
- [Colored incidence](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/incidence/INCIDENCE.md) expresses multiplicities through marked-path orbits and filtered double cosets.
- [Overlap and path walk](../05-knowledge/results/session151_spectrum_metagraph_20261103/bridges/OVERLAP_AND_PATH_WALK.md) links chord-complement edges to path counts and shows closed-side monotonicity fails.

Each packet gives proof scope, prior-art credits and reproducible small checks. Complete all-order spectrum and efficient closed-form metagraph classification remain unresolved.
