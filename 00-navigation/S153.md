# Session 153: flexible gadgets and new viewpoints

The conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamiltonian-path counts remains open. The results here give construction targets, exact identities, and scoped obstructions; they do not prove the conjecture.

- [Adaptive attachments](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/gadgets/README.md): independently checked representative-specific three-vertex exclusions and two-vertex positive examples.
- [Ordinal-sum ear composition](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/cuts/README.md): an exact boundary-aware identity and a limited reverse-connector obstruction.
- [Finite interface certificate](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/strategy/finite_certificate.md) and [word-interface inverse](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/strategy/word_interface.md): a conditional descent plan and the limits of a one-feedback-vertex family.
- [Twin towers and recognizable series](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/connections/automata.md): an exact fixed-template generating function and sparse-family obstruction.
- [Weighted affine-bank obstruction](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/rewriting/NOTE.md): a spectral-radius test for a specific finite-state model, not for arbitrary adaptive gadgets.
- [Strong-component series and tropical order](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/species/SPECIES_TROPICAL.md): a credited reformulation of prior fixed-count formulas.
- [Sevenfold automorphism bound](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/cycles/RESULT.md): a sharp 175-path threshold with a finite circulant verification.
- [Apex permanent inversion](../05-knowledge/results/session153_gadgets_20261103/mobius/NOTE.md): an exact partition-lattice formula whose signed cancellations do not prove either gap.

Each packet states its assumptions, source credits, checks and limitations. Finite searches are not exhaustive beyond their stated scope.
