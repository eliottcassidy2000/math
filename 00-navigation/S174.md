2026-11-03. Five independently audited packets and a cross-packet synthesis. Universal proofs and finite certificates are distinguished throughout. No enormous-integer primality certificate, historical novelty claim, or resolution of the global Hamilton-path spectrum problem is asserted.

The synthesis proves that every uniform cyclic wrapper A → A* → Z → A, with nonempty tournaments A and Z, excludes Hamilton-path counts 7 and 21. The singleton-feedback case has the quadratic norm (F_A(2)−F_A(1))²+2F_A(1)² and hence even valuations at primes where −2 is a nonsquare. This closes a constructor family, not the global spectrum question.

## Scope and next target

The three meanings of spectrum here are distinct: complex polynomial roots, modular residues, and integer Hamilton-path counts. Any bridge must specify its map and preserved predicate. To seek 7 or 21 constructively, change the mirror relation, uniform cyclic intermodule orientation, or the architecture itself; changing the feedback tournament within the proved class cannot work.
