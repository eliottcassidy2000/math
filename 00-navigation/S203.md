### Positive constructions and scoped obstructions

- [Paired O/Z carriers](../session203/staggered-paired-carriers/README.md) supply
  both staggered residues on four explicit infinite orbits. Their
  [menu-density bound](../session203/carrier-menu-density/README.md) is below 1.5%
  of odd bases. The separate finite-seed obstruction concerns full nine-coordinate
  states, not the pair (B,s). [Eight synchronization tests](../session203/synchronized-grammar/README.md)
  decide all-word synchronization of an already suitable pair, not seed existence.
- [Arbitrary-module equal-row switches](../session203/arbitrary-module-row-switch/README.md)
  and [prescribed positive even differences](../session203/free-additive-difference/README.md)
  are genuine physical constructions. Neither freely rebases a target or proves coverage.
- [Induced H25-to-H51 ancestry is impossible](../session203/induced25-51-rebuilding/README.md),
  while one old-arc reversal plus one birth gives a physical repair for the displayed
  parent. This does not exclude binary induction beginning above 25.
- [One-sided multipliers](../session203/one-sided-multipliers/README.md) permit
  a=1 or a=2^m+1, not an unrestricted ternary ladder. The
  [uniform-incidence curvature theorem](../session203/uniform-incidence-curvature/README.md)
  excludes nonzero even affine multipliers only under its stated bit-cube hypotheses.
- From a fixed finite seed bank, [bounded-offset O/Z/J/K1/K2 lifts](../session203/binary-lift-obstruction/README.md)
  have only O(log M) outputs. The [fixed one-port digital-context obstruction](../session203/digital-context-obstruction/README.md)
  excludes one context decoding the entire O/Z orbit, not multiple ports or
  target-dependent contexts. The [two displayed colored repairs](../session203/two-root-colored-contexts/README.md)
  attain equality only on ordered color blocks; this is not a classification of all two-port contexts.
- [Sharp Z-defect equality](../session203/sharp-z-defect/README.md) is attainable
  at start t exactly when t is an ordinary Hamilton-path count. Its
  [complete graph normal form](../session203/sharp-defect-normal-form/README.md)
  allows one optional flip at an interior-SCC vertex and gives O_t(log^2 X) support.
  More generally, for each fixed positive integer K,
  [all nontrivial positive-start sources with D<=K](../session203/bounded-defect-sparsity/README.md)
  have O_K(log^(2K+1) X) distinct B values. Thus a staggered construction covering
  every sufficiently large odd base must allow unbounded Z defect. This does not refute F(8,137).
