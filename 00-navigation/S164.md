# Session 164: compositional structures and physical interfaces

This session develops several exact constructions and abstractions from the preceding tournament, ternary-return and triple work. It does not establish that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamilton-path counts. A scalar count, a modular residue, a full path-cover profile and a reusable physical observer state remain distinct objects.

- [Literal endpoint gluing and Gaussian signs](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/triple_gluing/NOTE.md) composes endpoint-reversal tournaments, identifies a missing relative-conjugation bit and gives an exact shared-prime carry. A Cassini-selected branch yields a positive square-sum triple family; the arithmetic guard does not automatically transfer to ternary tournament returns.
- [Canonical Boolean carriers and gates](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/boolean_gates/NOTE.md) orient the cut carrier along the original tournament, prove a deletion-minor lower bound and a linear-forest support rule, and attain the arbitrary-modulus escape-density bound in an all-strong family. [Closed observer operations](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/boolean_gates/CLOSED_OBSERVER.md) repeat two six-vertex fixed-port operations with commuting observer maps, not necessarily isomorphic reordered graphs.
- [Nonuniform head operators](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/nonuniform_operator/NOTE.md) act on entire path-cover polynomials, produce sharp strong interface witnesses, and show that a fixed finite-dimensional linear observer cannot capture all repetitions on all transitive tails. This does not rule out nonlinear or restricted-family encodings.
- [Profile algebra](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/profile_algebras/PROFILE_ALGEBRA.md), [guard algebra](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/profile_algebras/GUARD_ALGEBRA.md), and the [pointed profile module](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/profile_algebras/POINTED_PROFILE_ALGEBRA.md) compose ordinal joins, triangle wrappers and moving guards. Their exact finite quotients and family-specific modular obstructions do not classify arbitrary tournaments. The [canonical returns](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/profile_algebras/CANONICAL_RETURNS.md) give a reusable multiplier 91 and strong sources covering every residue modulo 7^k; multiplier 91 means output count 273 from the initial triangle, not 91.
- [Complementary guards](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/complementary_guard/COMPLEMENTARY_OFFSETS.md) produce literal strong scalar steps 5→17→53→161 and an all-order two-sided family, without proving marked ternary-bank closure. The [universal guard lift](../05-knowledge/results/session164_emergent_20261103/complementary_guard/UNIVERSAL_GUARD_LIFT.md) extends any strong source into a canonical guarded bank with an explicit rooted-cover correction; it does not promise an unchanged count or an exact 3H step for arbitrary inputs.

Notes give source credits, proofs, scope and reproducible controls. Finite tests corroborate general proofs, except where a finite closed-state table is itself the stated certificate. No claim of novelty against the complete literature is made.
