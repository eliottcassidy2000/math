# S193: triangulating tournament-spectrum methods

This session combines constructive and structural approaches toward the conjecture that the positive odd Hamiltonian-path spectrum omits only 7 and 21. The conjecture remains open: the results below do not yet give a universal target construction.

## Complementary proof components

- [Exact power windows](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/power_windows/PROOF.md): weighted cut responses and binary padding realize all 135 odd numbers in [2^k−115, 2^k+153] by strong tournaments for every k≥13. The width is fixed.
- [Rooted merge envelope](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/single_triangle/PROOF.md): a root-avoiding decomposition and tagged path-cover injection prove the sharp rooted response bound. Combined with the earlier one-triangle formula, it proves the exact deletion minimum in that entire sector at every order. The multiple-triangle sector remains open.
- [Multiple-triangle bound](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/multiple_triangles/PROOF.md): an exact odd-cycle projection gives a strict bound whenever a reverse arc joins the relevant extreme components; the remaining case has both sides weak and no such arc.
- [Prime-atom induction](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/prime_induction/PROOF.md): ordered joins reduce the desired monoid to prime and seven-times-prime targets plus finite seeds. A physical infinite family combines an unconditional upper ternary branch with a lower branch conditional on a smaller factor; no universal rearming follows.
- [One-arc join synthesis](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/one_arc_joins/PROOF.md): an independently checked finite cover through 2^20 and exact one-apex family reduction. The source-reported 2^32 result remains unaudited here.
- [Block divisibility and escape](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/block_divisibility/PROOF.md): bounded apexes preserve explicit module divisors, while two apexes can remove a divisor carried by the H13 core. The named mixed library remains too sparse for all prime atoms.
- [Modular synchronization](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/modular_sync/PROOF.md): for every fixed modulus, actual one-apex words synchronize admissible residues with square-root-scale size approximation; residue mixing and limiting size laws hold jointly. The modulus-dependent error does not force exact equality.
- [Rooted-clock profiles](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/profile_substitution/PROOF.md): complete path-cover polynomial transfer composes with affine symplectic substitution and realizes the physical two-level fan. Infinite linear continuation rank and an intact-module divisibility obstruction explain why a bounded scalar state cannot suffice for that calculus.
- [Triangle-module rearming](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/triangle_rearming/PROOF.md): exact powers of three survive bounded edits/births, with sharp examples and a two-birth escape. This does not give a universal ternary return.
- [Response rigidity and surgery](../05-knowledge/results/session193_triangulated_proof_20261105/response_rigidity/PROOF.md): depth-three suffix measurements reconstruct two-apex attachment words up to neutral leading padding; fixed append macros cannot realize a closed binary or ternary affine grammar. A quartic compiler retains all effects of independent surgeries.

Each note states its hypotheses and limitations and includes a reproducible verifier. The all-order claims were independently reviewed; finite calculations remain identified as such.
