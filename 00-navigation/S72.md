**INHERITED / CITED:** tournaments and automorphism-sign-even graphs have
matching isomorphism-type counts; the signed Walsh basis, affine quotient
criterion and ordinary tournament arc-flip gap four are already established.
Sign-even does **not** mean that every vertex has even degree.

**PROVED interfaces:** the affine-Fourier operation envelope simultaneously
tracks invariant convolution, reversal, and quotient averaging. Cut-switching
retains sign-even Eulerian Fourier graphs and has unnormalized arc-flip gap
eight for n>=4. The complementary cycle-space quotient is literal score
parity; its weighted pair-flip chain has gap 2(n-1) for n>=3. Tiny one-state
quotients have no asserted positive gap. These are weighted operations, not
Boolean adjacency or arbitrary object bijections.

**Positive realization:** the exact Walsh basis transports the probability
cone rather than identifying its coefficients with state probabilities. The
explicit four-vertex cone and converse calculation is independently audited.

**Boundary:** inherited symmetry obstructions persist. Equal counts, equal
spectral gaps and exact linear coordinates do not establish a natural labelled
bijection, a positive coordinate identification or a universal finite carrier.
Finite computations verify their stated ranges only. LRC(14) remains open.
