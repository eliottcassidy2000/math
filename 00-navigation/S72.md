**INHERITED / CITED:** tournaments and automorphism-sign-even graphs have equal
isomorphism-type counts. The signed Walsh basis and ordinary arc-flip gap four
are inherited. Sign-even is not the Eulerian vertex-degree condition.

**PROVED interfaces:** invariant affine-Fourier kernels and quotient averaging
share an exact operation carrier. Cut-switching retains sign-even Eulerian
modes and has unnormalized arc-flip gap eight for n>=4. The complementary
cycle-space quotient is literal score parity, with gap 2(n-1) for n>=3.
Loops and multiplicities matter; tiny one-state cases have no positive gap claim.

Reset/action terminal observables can have smaller linear rank than the number
of distinct states. Supported pair laws give an incidence-cycle kernel and an
exact positive-law cone; a native five-vertex example is nonsimplicial.
Reversible test-return protocols instead recover conjunctions and full linear
state resolution when their guard predicates separate states.

For full products of cyclic frames, relative pair phases reconstruct the
common-shift orbit. Sparse tests suffice exactly when every nonempty
prime-power layer is connected. This is not a theorem about arbitrary group
actions: a genuine triple of native Paley-seven markings has identical named
pair types but a differing common-out guard (and F8 rank two versus three).

**Boundary:** exact linear coordinates are not positive state masses; terminal
responses are not same-sample trajectories; pairwise information need not glue.
Contextual/Hankel minimality and the factorial-moment finite-carrier obstruction
are inherited/standard mechanisms, not a universal finite-carrier theorem.
Finite certificates remain scoped. LRC(14) and the proposed all-order native
continuations remain open.
