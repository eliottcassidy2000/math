# Session 158: creative tournament constructions

The session treats earlier ideas as starting points for new constructions. Proofs, finite checks, and scope limits are recorded separately in the linked notes. None proves that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamilton-path counts, and novelty against the full literature has not been established.

- [Binary state bank and density obstruction](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/binary_bank/NOTE.md): exact membership for strong tournaments made transitive by deleting one vertex, and a positive-density omission bound within that restricted family. The [FRACTRAN compiler](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/binary_bank/FRACTRAN.md) realizes the count in a word-specialized program; it is not a fixed universal interpreter.
- [Cyclic transitive blocks](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/cyclic_blocks/NOTE.md): an exponential generating function, explicit strong families with arbitrarily large feedback vertex number, and a 3-adic family. High feedback is a property of the exhibited witnesses, not a lower bound for all witnesses of their counts.
- [Weighted carriers and cyclic substitution](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/carrier/NOTE.md): terminal-pair carriers relate 7, 28, and 126 to ordered path-cover profiles. Arbitrary cyclic substitution produces strong tournaments. The [two-bin extension](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/carrier/extension.md) gives a multiplicative state and an infinite 7-divisible strong family, including 133 and 8197. It does not prove infinitely many prime cofactors.
- [Square-sum crossing intervals](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/square_overlap/NOTE.md): a two-edge formula, a strong 13-path witness, a 265-path square-sum witness, and exact square feasibility. The [Zeckendorf code](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/square_overlap/ZECKENDORF.md) injects integers into actual counts, while [contiguous chains](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/square_overlap/CONTIGUOUS.md) extend the product to arbitrary finite index sets and give strong contiguous-run witnesses. These are constructions, not spectrum coverage.
- [Reflection-sandwich metagraph gadget](../05-knowledge/results/session158_creative_20261103/reflection/NOTE.md): a self-converse line whose second endpoint is strong, with an exact quadratic count and a length-resolved fixed-cycle bijection. The portal-parity recovery is restricted to the stated path-end portals.

All linked notes describe their proofs, dependencies, controls, and reproducible checkers. In particular, finite checks are controls rather than substitutes for the stated general arguments.
