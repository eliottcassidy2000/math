# Session 200: arithmetic definability and physical fibers

**ACTIVE.** Explore how ideas around Carmichael's conjecture and Diophantine definability of rings of integers may advance physical source-pair coverage for the tournament Hamiltonian-path spectrum. The full {7,21} spectrum remains open.

The emphasis is on constructive transfers: physical source recipes, exact arithmetic fibers and useful reductions. Neither an outside theorem's status, an abstract existential encoding nor bounded checks alone establish universal coverage.

Starting points: [Session 199 guide](S199.md), [C2 source inverse](../session197/bilinear-c2-inverse/PROOF.md), [C3 source templates](../session197/c3-two-source/PROOF.md), [physical adjoint](../session199/physical-adjoint/PROOF.md) and [balanced sources](../session198/balanced-source-normal-form/PROOF.md). The attached paper is being read for methods and context.

## Reviewed checkpoints

- [Physical Diophantine compiler](../session200/diophantine-source-compiler/PROOF.md): finite target-specific quadratic systems over characteristic-zero fields decode to actual C1/C2 tournament witnesses. This is not a single formula defining the unbounded spectrum.
- [Height-bounded lifting](../session200/height-bounded-source-lifting/PROOF.md): synchronized bounded physical witnesses give an exact target-specific prime-field criterion; incompatible local witnesses do not imply one global witness.
- [Sharp dyadic word fibers](../session200/sharp-dyadic-fibers/PROOF.md): the attached manuscript's parity mechanism yields all-order residue results for physical labelled words, not graph-class multiplicity or exact-target coverage.
- [Carmichael-style source fibers](../session200/carmichael-fibers/PROOF.md): represented nonbalanced counts admit a physical descent to a balanced parent below half the size, with a jointly realized auxiliary parameter. Finite singleton-fiber observations are not an all-order multiplicity theorem.
- [Signed fiber repair](../session200/signed-fiber-repair/PROOF.md) and [physical block transport](../session200/signed-block-transport/PROOF.md): extend exact signed-response constructions and exhibit real equal-count C2 presentations; prescribing the positive target simultaneously remains open.
- [Root-weighted height](../session200/root-weighted-height/PROOF.md): a bounded-root-change construction specification and prime bound; uniform solvability is still open.
