# Session 196: A000568 and creative spectrum approaches

2026-11-05. **The full tournament Hamiltonian-path spectrum remains open.** A000568 counts unlabeled tournaments by order; the spectrum concerns the distinct Hamiltonian-path counts H among them. This session connects those questions through automorphism orbits and develops new physical constructions and restricted classifications. No finite census is presented as global coverage.

## A000568 and symmetry

- [Hamilton-path orbit mass and rigidity](../session196/symmetry/PROOF.md): the automorphism group acts freely on the Hamiltonian paths, and the sum of H(T)/|Aut(T)| over order-n isomorphism classes is 2^((n−1)(n−2)/2). Every tournament with H=p or p² for prime p≥5 is rigid.
- [First symmetric 7p stratum](../session196/orbit-spectrum/PROOF.md): the least H=7p, p prime, admitting a nontrivial automorphism is 4,151=7·593. Exactly two strong symmetric classes attain it; they are a converse pair of order 10. At each n≥10 there are exactly 2(n−9) symmetric classes with that H. The proof includes finite exact certificates and does not assume the global 7 or 21 gaps.
- [Path-moment refinement](../session196/sequence-structure/PROOF.md): a higher-order controlled asymptotic expansion and explicit conditional-expectation construction. The exact second-moment formula and 2/n concentration rate are inherited from earlier THM-589 work, and stronger extremal asymptotics exist. These results do not establish spectrum support.

## New constructions and restrictions

- [Vertex cloning and two-piece synthesis](../session196/spectral-moves/PROOF.md): a matching number exactly determines the maximum number of clone runs; binary-affine growth occurs precisely when the root-triangle incidence graph is a nonempty star. A physical two-piece formula reduces unit offsets to an arithmetic condition on rooted data. Fixed finite banks of cloning templates with ordered joins cannot cover all prime counts.
- [Unit determinants and sign switching](../session196/clone-determinants/PROOF.md): a physical compiler changes the sign of a unit-offset interface while preserving strongness, but changes the count and template. A complete deletion-one slack classification supplies a concrete limitation; deletion count five yields a positive contrast.
- [Equal-response five-times window](../session196/global-induction/PROOF.md): an exact all-order descendant classification through 5H for a specified rooted state, allowing arbitrary gadget sizes and operation depth. It restricts a particular recursive architecture, not the full tournament spectrum.

## Low-cycle route

- [Two-apex low-cycle automaton](../session196/lowcycle/PROOF.md): exact cycle and path transfers and a minimal 32-state cycle machine. Within this defined class, for one or two Hamiltonian cycles, the exact arbitrary-order image through 501 has gaps 7, 21, 67. The word 12301 realizes H=67 with four Hamiltonian cycles, so the exclusion is not for the full two-apex class.
- [Cycle-preserving deletion](../session196/lowcycle/DELETION.md): a separately reviewed finite-state certificate gives an all-order recursive deletion for the same one/two-cycle subclass and a second proof that 67 is absent there.

The low-cycle general gluing metadata discussed during this session was not used as a theorem: its exact permitted proof source was not available. The automaton and deletion claims are confined to their stated subclass. Its validation file specifies that assertions must remain enabled and that one verification script uses SymPy.

## Remaining proof gap

The sign-switch compiler does not produce both binary children from the same count, and the two-piece determinant equation has not been solved for arbitrary targets. The prime-rigidity and low-cycle results constrain potential witnesses but do not construct every required prime or 7p atom. A new all-order physical construction or a proof of a sufficiently broad recursive realization principle is still needed. All linked packets were independently reviewed and include reproducible checks.
