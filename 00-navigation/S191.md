# S191: tournament spectrum and growth

Session 191 develops constructions and structural boundaries for Hamiltonian-path counts H in tournaments. The global conjecture that the positive odd spectrum omits only 7 and 21 remains open. The exclusions of 7 and 21 are inherited, not new here.

## Constructions and interfaces

- [Two-port ternary fan](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/ternary_fan/PROOF.md): explicit strong tournaments with counts N=5+7·2^k and 3N+2, 3N+4, preserving a physical unit-start vertex. This is not recursive closure.
- [Second-generation obstruction](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/ternary_fan/SECOND_GENERATION.md): one of four branches has no fixed-core same-clock realization; the other three have scalar witnesses. The lower continuation can retain physical ports.
- [Unit-start boundary](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/ternary_fan/UNIT_START_BOUNDARY.md): strong unit-start outputs of order at least three have exactly one Hamiltonian cycle; no H=13 tournament has a unit start or end.
- [Cycle-crossing fan](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/ternary_fan/CYCLE_CROSSING.md) and [rerooted continuation](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/ternary_fan/REROOTED_CONTINUATION.md): a different explicit fan changes the number of Hamiltonian cycles from one to three, and its upper child has two physical continuations through strong states. No universal rearming theorem is claimed.
- [Two-apex grammar](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/two_apex/TWO_APEX.md): an exact 36-state counting transfer has minimal rational linear continuation dimension 17. An independently checked finite scan realizes every odd value through 230519 except 7 and 21. This is finite evidence, not an all-order proof.
- [Bounded-complexity sparsity](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/two_apex/SPARSITY.md): bounded feedback-arc or finite clock-template strong atoms remain sparse even after arbitrary order joins. The restriction does not include unbounded pattern complexity or recursive substitution.
- [One-apex boundaries](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/one_apex/PROOF.md): exact order-specific extremal counts, local swap costs and affine single-run classification. H=13 occurs globally but not with feedback vertex number at most one.

## Size and structure

- [Deletion-growth proof](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/PROOF.md), [all roots](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/ALL_ROOTS.md), and [extended census](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/EXTENDED_CAP128.md): exact minima through order 12, and Δ(T,v)≥130 for every vertex of every strong tournament of order at least 12. The all-order conclusion is a lower bound, not a formula for exact minima at all orders.
- [Interval-chain law](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/INTERVAL_CHAINS.md) and [equality cases](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/CHAIN_EQUALITIES.md): exact growth and minimizing marked classes within that family, not all strong tournaments.
- [Neutral extensions](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/NEUTRAL_EXTENSIONS.md): a parent with k ordered strong components has exactly m! binom(m+k,k) count-preserving extensions by m named vertices.
- [Obstruction layers](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/obstruction_layers/PROOF.md) and [next layer](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/obstruction_layers/ADDENDUM.md): all-threshold classifications near the maximal order of minimal induced obstructions to H≤B. These are not a spectrum-completeness proof.
- [Random growth](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/random_growth/PROOF.md) and its [rate](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/random_growth/RATE_EXTENSION.md): normalized H converges almost surely to 1 for any coupling with uniform tournament marginals; inherited moments are explicitly credited.

The linked notes give exact hypotheses, reproduction commands, and the separation between proved statements, finite computation and open questions. Source credits for deletion growth appear in its [lineage note](../05-knowledge/results/session191_tournament_growth_20261104/deletion_growth/SOURCE_LINEAGE.md).
