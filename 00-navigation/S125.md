## A genuine rooted-forest correspondence

[Rooted path forests, tilings and numeration](../05-knowledge/results/session125_rooted_forest_numeration_20261031/README.md)
gives explicit inverse maps between fixed-length legal Chung–Graham words,
odd-length monomer/domino tilings and rooted forests of a path. Nonzero digits
count forest edges; a digit2 contributes one edge, not two. Total forest count
and an individual forest's numerical rank are different quantities.

Right-appending0 adds a rooted singleton at the right and implements the
canonical Beatty shift. A leading zero adds a singleton at the left while
preserving numerical value. Reading direction is retained: the forest's LSD
state need not equal the numeral's MSB Beatty state. Ordinary numerical
successor does not mean adding a graph vertex in this carrier.

The exact Schur/Green vertex-arrival formula retains named boundary response.
A pathP3 has8 rooted forests; attaching a leaf at its endpoint gives21, while
attaching at the middle gives20. A scalar count cannot replace the port data.

No assertion of coherent all-order nice paths, new number fields, arbitrary
prescribed-path embedding, or global tournament spectrum completeness is made.
