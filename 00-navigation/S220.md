# Session220: binary envelopes and witness selection

**Checkpoint 1 published; proof research continues. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

[Research index and proofs](../05-knowledge/results/session220_binary_envelopes_and_selectors_20261107/README.md).

- The three-core inverse and fixed join bank share one exact binary quadratic carrier.
- Fixed-length maxima reduce to one-run words; arbitrary fixed-bit minima reduce to minimum cut. Sharp square-root deficits retain their parity oscillation.
- Any ordinary finite-state transducer selecting valid fixed-bank binary witnesses has only polylogarithmic output support, even with asynchronous transitions and an unreported bank label.
- Exact interval hulls do not prove occupancy; output-only proofs and unbounded-register constructions remain outside the selector obstruction.

No all-target proof, positive-density theorem, or unrestricted finite coverage extension is claimed.
