5. [Strong one-apex modular synthesis](../05-knowledge/results/session179_niche_methods_20261104/spectrum/REPORT.md): every residue modulo every odd q is realized by a strongly connected one-apex tournament on at most 3q² vertices. Exact count gaps remain compatible with modular coverage. [Independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session179_niche_methods_20261104/spectrum/AUDIT_REPORT.md).

The synthesis proves two scope-preserving interfaces: compiling a matched valuation prefix into a strict-descent certificate, and normalized separation after primitive-domain reduction. It does not claim an implemented cross-packet speedup.
