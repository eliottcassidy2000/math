# S190: constructive interfaces and finite graph certificates

**Reviewed checkpoints, 4 November 2026.** The retained packets provide boundary-signature transfer, short planar Hamiltonian interfaces, and an exact tournament/even-graph Walsh coupling. They do not supply a general Hamiltonicity algorithm, a natural tournament/graph bijection, or a revalidation of the uploaded papers' global theorems.

## Boundary-state patch transfer

The [constructive synthesis](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/paper_patch/attached_papers/constructive_paper_synthesis.md) separates three source methods: ring-coloring states in planar coloring, endpoint states in hypohamiltonian patch replacement, and bridge-aware equal-length cycle moves. The precise source statements, versions, page references, and limitations are retained there.

Its derived patch lemma assumes common labeled terminals, disjoint patch/exterior edge sets, no other shared vertices, and **at least two exterior-private vertices**. Equal full fragment signatures and the specified one-way vertex-deletion inclusions transfer hypohamiltonicity. Signatures retain terminal degrees and endpoint pairings; closed cycle components are excluded. Planarity is not needed for this lemma.

The [independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/paper_patch/session190-papers-audit/PATCH_AND_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) checks the Figure 2 H-to-H1 replacement from Durias et al.: exactly 14 full signatures on each patch, 34 versus 68 actual spanning linear forests, all terminal-deletion inclusions, and every new internal deletion. Two old deletion anchors suffice and one does not, for the displayed labeling. Complete fragment and deletion witnesses are retained, with separate original and independent checkers.

## Short planar Hamiltonian interfaces

The [planar proof](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/planar/session190-planar/PROOF.md) and [independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/planar/session190-planar-audit/PLANAR_INTERFACE_AUDIT.md) give exact live degree-and-pairing dimensions 6, 26, and 100 at three, four, and five ordered planar ports. Sparse planar unit probes recover every coordinate through five ports. The dimension lower bound concerns factorization of the formal response operator or a linear/bilinear gluing representation, not unrestricted decoding of arbitrary real encodings.

At six ports, the first nonpermutation block is the disjoint union of K2 and K3, with determinant -2; the full ranks are 365 over the rationals and 364 over F2. This is a formal response-kernel result, not a claimed single-graph realization or a Hamiltonicity test by parity. The same packet retains the complete face-stellation counterexample family separating coloring reducibility from Hamiltonian preservation, and all 124 deletion cards for six finite square-sum graphs. Its all-order square-sum corollary remains explicitly dependent on the cited existence result, with the Q2 convention exception.

## Tournament/even-graph operators

The [Walsh-coupling proof](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/bijection/session190-bijection/NOTE.md) and [independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/bijection/session190-bijection-audit/AUDIT.md) use **automorphism-even** to mean that every graph automorphism has positive edge-direction-reversal sign. This is not a statement that the automorphism group has even order.

The normalized signed invariant basis is orthogonal; its entrywise square is a canonical doubly stochastic coupling of unlabelled tournament and automorphism-even graph types. It determines the full-flip return law but loses information: at order four a transitive tournament and a strong tournament have the same coupling row and return law, yet Hamiltonian-path counts 1 and 5. The exact path-complement Walsh formula preserves Fourier parity, mixes Fourier degrees, and has its own stationary weights. The full-flip exit-rate identity retains its factor 2. Existing basis and free marked-path results are credited; no rank-free natural bijection, reconstruction theorem, or new global enumeration method is claimed.

Complete exact outputs are retained for the independent census through order six, including all 4,164 full labelled path-Walsh entries only through order four. Larger class-level checks are not described as full labelled-matrix checks.

## Group actions and path-complement edges

The [group-action proof](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/groups/session190-groups/PROOF.md) and [independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session190_constructive_links_20261104/groups/session190-groups-audit/AUDIT.md) distinguish planar cyclic/dihedral symmetry from the five finite rotation-group families in three dimensions. Paley7 has ordinary group C7 semidirect C3 of order 21 and signed extension AGL(1,7) of order 42; the 7/21/42 orbit diagram is not a Hamiltonian-spectrum exclusion argument.

On the actual named path-complement edge carrier, ordinary stabilizers are trivial and signed stabilizers are trivial or C2, with the stated n>=3 boundary. The all-order edge-orbit formulas retain their factors of two and do not count a collapsed simple support graph. The minimum faithful real dimension six and binary dimension three for the order-21 group retain their different-field hypotheses. The Fermat-prime discussion explicitly depends on the inherited prime-degree affine theorem. No five-to-five correspondence or new global Hamiltonian-spectrum conclusion is claimed.
