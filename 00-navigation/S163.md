# Session 163: ternary returns, triples and interfaces

The central open step remains a source-closed construction of all three ternary branches, not just arithmetic expressions for their counts. This session combines literal tournament constructions with a targeted survey of triple analogies. It does not prove that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamilton-path counts; novelty against the full literature is not asserted.

- [Strong source returns](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strong_returns/NOTE.md) give literal 1→0 and 2→0 branch patterns from marked seeds and preserve them under even-b mixed suffixes. [Two-vertex returns](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strong_returns/TWO_EARS.md) continue both paths with full observer state tripled; their finite offset exclusions concern only the named induced sources.
- The [moving-guard lemma](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strong_returns/MOVING_GUARD.md) gives an indefinitely repeatable, bounded two-vertex strong 3× return on two guarded source families. The [first-step refinement](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strong_returns/FIRST_STEP.md) allows scalar +2 and +4 on explicit sources, but proves this attachment cannot produce those marked offsets with the same observer port; under its strong-source hypotheses it cannot repeat such small scalar offsets consecutively. Neither result supplies all-branch closure.
- [Later-return obstructions](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strong_returns/LATER_RETURNS.md) isolate six absent full states in one pinned 65-atom language and prove a crossing-rewiring bound for fixed internal blocks. These are scoped obstructions, not global impossibility theorems.
- [Nonuniform interfaces](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/nonuniform/NOTE.md) give arbitrary-modulus resilience bounds, including heterogeneous primewise capacity-two matching. The [marked escape analysis](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/nonuniform/MARKED_ESCAPE.md) gives a quadratic interface test and literal escape, while separate exhaustive checks rule out specified one-vertex exact-state returns only for five fixed induced sources.
- [Triple strands](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/strands/NOTE.md) connect Collatz residue/exponent strands, Pell families, Pythagorean triples and Fibonacci hypotenuses without claiming Collatz convergence. The [triple codec](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/triple_codec/NOTE.md) links marked Gaussian-factor addresses and divisor-balance shapes to a guarded normalization in a rational triple chart; that arithmetic return is not a tournament-source return.
- The [source metanalysis](../05-knowledge/results/session163_ternary_triples_20261103/survey/META_LEDGER.md) maps earlier experiments to possible levers and obstacles. Its cyclic transitive-block mod-3 result excludes the 1 mod 3 branch for that family, not for arbitrary strong tournaments.

Each packet states its hypotheses, source credits and reproducible controls. An arithmetic analogy, an encoded integer, a scalar target count and a reusable physical observer state are different kinds of evidence.
