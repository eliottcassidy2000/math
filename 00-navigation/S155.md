# Session 155: spectrum constructions and corrections

The conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd tournament Hamiltonian-path counts remains open. The results below distinguish congruence coverage, finite certificates and family-specific obstructions from an exact global classification.

- [Four-prime mixed-prefix orbit](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/prefix31/NOTE.md) proves a congruence orbit at all powers of 7, 13, 19 and 31, conditional on the inherited literal gadget construction. The [three-prime proof](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/prefix19/NOTE.md) and independent audits are preserved.
- [Sparse exact-output family](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/sparse/NOTE.md) shows that this named bank misses almost all prime-lane targets in each fixed reduced class, without restricting other tournament families.
- [Fixed return lattice](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/returns/NOTE.md) and [mixed-return certificate](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/returns/MIXED.md) establish scoped formal-state obstructions, not global impossibility.
- [Two-apex witnesses and clone slots](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/primes/README.md) provide independently checked finite examples and a general clone-count identity; the larger census remains exploratory.
- [Research writeup corrections](../06-writeups/research-document-for-mathematicians.md) replace a false extension to tied digraphs, qualify modular-rank certification, correct gap arguments and Egyptian-fraction criteria, and give verified tied-digraph examples. The finite checks are in [the correction packet](../05-knowledge/results/session155_spectrum_20261103/corrections/check.py).

Each packet records its dependencies, proof scope and reproduction commands. None establishes global prime-lane coverage.
