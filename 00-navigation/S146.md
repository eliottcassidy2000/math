# Session 146: carrier dimension and prime-pair machines

**PROVED, scoped:** The full fixed source-oblivious attachment-response space on seven named old vertices has rational dimension 2668, or 2667 varying coordinates for normalized laws. At eight vertices it has dimension 13028, or 13027 varying coordinates. The upper bounds come from the existing pooled-parity carrier; independently verified modular rank certificates and lifting yield the lower bounds. Higher-order lower bounds now give 56296 ≤ d(9) ≤ 65908; equality for n ≥ 9 remains open. These results do not establish minimality for a restricted S/B-only language, source-dependent operations, conditioning, or shared-source products.
