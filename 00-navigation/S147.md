# Session 147: square-sum graph structure

**PROVED and FINITE-EXACT:** For the simple square-sum graph on positive integers, the given three groups are exactly the components induced by deleting 13, 14 and 15 from G18; their sizes are 6, 4 and 5. Edges 1–3 and 4–5 and nonedge 2–4 share a triangle-free common-neighbor obstruction. The pair 2,4 has distance 4 for 14 ≤ n ≤ 76 and 3 for n ≥ 77, and is disconnected for n ≤ 13. The G18 edge 4–5 is a bridge but not the unique maximum of ordinary edge betweenness. G30 is the first graph in the nested family containing a triangle.
