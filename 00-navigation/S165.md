# Session 165: geometry, representations and categories

This session composes tournament path-cover structures with algebraic geometry, representation theory and category theory. It separates formal algebraic or categorical closure from physical tournament realizability. None of the results settles the conjectured odd Hamilton-path spectrum, and novelty against the full literature is not asserted.

- [Strong Zeckendorf codes](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/geometric_carry/STRONG_ZECKENDORF.md) use literal guarded returns to build injective strong codes, with exact 2-adic separation and a Fibonacci-dimensional limit set. Infinite addresses are 2-adic limits, not infinite-tournament counts. The [integral carry geometry](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/geometric_carry/GEOMETRIC_CARRY.md) models Gaussian carry and a two-valued rational-circle correspondence; localization, specialization and arithmetic normalization are kept distinct.
- [Guard-state geometry](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/guard_geometry/NOTE.md) proves a dense algebraic orbit and an exact integral matrix-algebra description while exhibiting a forbidden projective region of the physical closure. Algebraic density does not imply positive-word or tournament-state reachability.
- [Slot representations](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/slot_representation/NOTE.md) turn path-cover composition into genuine symmetric-group representations and moving guards into Young-lattice dynamics. The [uniform Plancherel theorem](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/slot_representation/UNIFORM_PLANCHEREL.md) supplies a quadratic rate for growing tournaments, with sharp order even among strong tournaments. The group acts on slots, not on tournament vertices.
- [Guard adjunctions and blue tilings](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/guard_adjunctions/NOTE.md) relate divisibility observations to closure and marked tilings, while preserving the distinction between static guards and dynamics. [Universal denominator codes](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/guard_adjunctions/UNIVERSAL_CODES.md) realize any finite closure system and characterize prime-register cost; they do not simulate arbitrary FRACTRAN transitions.
- [FI path-cover interfaces](../05-knowledge/results/session165_geometry_representation_20261103/fi_interfaces/FI_INTERFACES.md) construct a symmetric monoidal closed formal category and distinguish shared-slot and disjoint-slot products. Not every formal interface is claimed to arise from a tournament.

Each packet states its source credits, exact hypotheses and reproducible controls. Finite computations support the stated proofs, except where a closed finite certificate is part of the proof itself.
