# Session 194: closing the {7,21} spectrum

2026-11-05. **The full Hamiltonian-path spectrum remains open.** This session combined constructive ternary fans, structural deletion bounds, exact finite certificates, and tests of reusable interfaces. The results below were independently reviewed; finite checks support but do not replace the all-order proofs.

## New constructions

- [Endpoint-matched ternary fans](../session194/ternary-fans/PROOF.md): nine infinite families realize both 3N+2 and 3N+4 for specified parents. A concrete induced-birth fan supplies 9,929,145,737 = 7 × 1,418,449,391 and prime 9,929,145,739, with trial-division certificates. These families do not prove all target atoms.
- [A twelve-value window](../session194/two-apex/WINDOW15.md): for every k ≥ 8, all twelve odd integers from 15·2^k−13 to 15·2^k+9 occur as counts of strong tournaments. The fixed width does not close the spectrum.
- [Two-sided dyadic returns](../session194/two-sided-padding/PROOF.md): exact source/sink padding classification and an explicit new infinite family, together with a lacunarity barrier for fixed finite banks.

## Structural advances and barriers

- [Sharp weak-deletion ratio](../session194/weak-deletion/STRONGER_RATIO.md): if T is strong and T−v is not strong, H(T) ≥ 3H(T−v). Equality is characterized and the constant is sharp. This closes the weak-deletion sector of the separate rooted-minimum problem, not the full spectrum.
- [Module barrier](../session194/module-barrier/PROOF.md): a nontransitive input retained as a module cannot produce 3H+2 or 3H+4. Constructions that break or replace the module are outside this theorem.
- [Endpoint floor](../session194/endpoint-floor/PROOF.md): an induced strong core with every endpoint count at least q forces every positive endpoint count in the ambient tournament to be at least q. This explains why the ternary fan cannot simply be rearmed while retaining its core.
- [Induced ancestry](../session194/induced-ancestry/ANCESTRY.md): there is no induced chain H13 ⊂ H41 ⊂ H127, by an all-order reduction and exhaustive finite certificate. Each number is attainable separately, and the alternative chain 13→29→75→127 exists.
- [Weak-parent certificate](../session194/weak-parent-certificate/PROOF.md): no strong one-vertex child of a weak H41 parent has count 125 or 127, and no such child of a weak H67 parent has count 203 or 205. This does not exclude those target counts by other constructions.
- [Two-apex periodic counters](../session194/two-apex/PROOF.md): an exact all-prefix certificate rules out two fixed affine digit branches of suffix length at most six in the stated two-apex model, not arbitrary constructions.

## Verification and remaining gap

Each linked folder contains its verifier and necessary input data. Independent cross-checks included exact path enumeration, subset dynamic programming, normal and optimized Python replay, and an exhaustive 62,423-extension isomorphism-bank completeness check. The ancestry negative certificate checks 624,640 final extension masks. Checkers and proofs specify their precise scope.

The key unresolved step is an all-order construction covering every required prime and 7p atom, or an alternative proof of the entire spectrum. Neither fixed-width windows nor isolated large atoms establish this. The strong-deletion structural sector and reusable ternary return also remain open. Session 193 independently checked one-arc coverage through 2^20; its separate reported 2^32 figure was not independently verified in that session.
