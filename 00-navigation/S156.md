# Session 156: feedback states and spectrum constructions

The conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd tournament Hamiltonian-path counts remains open. These results provide exact families, finite witnesses and structural constraints, not global coverage.

- [Feedback-two clone families](../05-knowledge/results/session156_spectrum_20261103/interfaces/NOTE.md) give exact path-count formulas and finite prime-lane examples; the [root-0 analysis](../05-knowledge/results/session156_spectrum_20261103/interfaces/BRIDGE.md) gives a scoped quotient obstruction.
- [One-apex exact family](../05-knowledge/results/session156_spectrum_20261103/exact/NOTE.md) provides 7-divisible counts and verified prime-quotient examples, without an infinitude claim.
- [Feedback-state bank](../05-knowledge/results/session156_spectrum_20261103/states/NOTE.md) gives an exact finite-state path transfer, a bounded-feedback cutoff, a regular strongness language and a finite witness bank through 301. It is not an all-order spectrum classification.
- [Physical return analysis](../05-knowledge/results/session156_spectrum_20261103/returns/NOTE.md) gives an exact seven-macro predecessor criterion, 65-atom necessary exclusions and two actual source identities. It does not prove a closed return bank.

Each packet distinguishes all-order proofs from finite tests, states its inherited dependencies and includes reproduction code.
