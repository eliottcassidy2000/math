# Session 198: realizable source-pair coverage

**ACTIVE.** The goal is to prove that physically realizable source pairs cover every remaining odd Hamiltonian-path target. The full {7,21} spectrum is open; neither finite coverage nor an algebraic solution with unrealizable profiles is a proof.

Starting points:

- [Session 197 guide](S197.md), including finite coverage and independently reviewed checkpoints.
- [Complete strong two-cycle source normal form and inverse](../session197/bilinear-c2-inverse/PROOF.md).
- [Strong three-cycle source templates and inverse](../session197/c3-two-source/PROOF.md).
- [Physical rooted-profile inverse](../session197/lowcycle/backward-profile-inverse/README.md).
- [Source signatures and pruning](../session197/source-signature/PROOF.md).

The work will pursue actual constructive inverse descents, source realizability and possible obstructions, with independent checks before promoting claims. A proof of coverage must handle all remaining odd targets and demonstrate physical source realizations, not just congruence coverage or a bounded census.

## Independently reviewed advances

- [Physical three-move grammar and descent](../session198/bilinear-descent/PROOF.md): actual source recipes admit short certificates and a growth-controlled inverse. This descends known representations; existence for every target remains open.
- [Sharper three-cycle inverse](../session198/source-coverage/PROOF.md): reduce eight scalar forms to five and sharpen the sufficient source cutoff. Exact support agrees in the tested range.
- [All-modulus constructions](../session198/source-pair-modular/): physical two- and three-cycle families hit every parity-compatible residue for every modulus. This does not imply exact integer coverage.
- [Physical source obstructions](../session198/pair-obstructions/PROOF.md) and [universal-transport limits](../session198/universal-source-transport/PROOF.md) rule out particular shortcuts without ruling out target-dependent reconstruction.
- [Primitive branching and rebasing](../session198/primitive-branching-barrier/PROOF.md) shows why a supplied representation may not yield binary children by source extensions and gives explicit alternative presentations.
- [Target 43 replacements](../session198/target43-replacement/PROOF.md) demonstrate physical target-dependent rebuilds that produce 85 and 87 even though extending the original pair cannot, with infinite replacement families. This is not an all-target descent.
