# Session 148: square-sum vertex growth

Status: finite exact computations and inherited formulas, not a claim of a
new prime or supersingular equivalence. See the [independent audit](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/finite_audit/README.md)
for the simple-graph definition, birth and edge counts, an n=15 oriented
Hamilton-path witness, precise 8/10 neighborhoods, reproduction, and hostile
controls. Later packets may be added without altering this frozen audit.

## Growth and path-cover mechanisms

The [growth packet](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/growth/GROWTH.md)
proves degree thresholds, Pell cancellation families, a cumulative edge
formula and an exact two-path-cover insertion recurrence. It distinguishes
finite tests from all-order proofs and supplies normal and optimized replays.

**Literature status:** OEIS A090461 credits Gerbicz (2018) with a proof
that every n >= 25 has a square-sum Hamilton path and every n >= 32 has a
cycle. This is a cited external result, not a proof audited in this packet;
the linked original forum post was inaccessible during this session. See
[OEIS A090461](https://oeis.org/A090461).

## Orientations and prime alignment

The [orientation packet](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/oriented/REPORT.md)
separates bidirected edges, arbitrary edge orientations and guarded tournament
extensions; it includes independent finite checks and attributes inherited
results. The [prime-alignment packet](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/prime_alignment/README.md)
compares three explicit indexing conventions and checks 25 finite witnesses.
It records the repeated 71 and missing 67 without assuming a correction.

**Concurrent source reconciliation:** The cited global threshold predates
this session and is already recorded in
[THM-4851, square-sum prehistory](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4851-the-prehistory-of-the-square-sum-numbers-unique-cycles-at-32-and-33-terminal-spectrum-with-the-hole-at-39.md).
The present finite searches and witnesses do not independently prove the
all-order Gerbicz result, and this session does not claim it as new.

## Pell cancellation plateaus

The [Pell plateau addendum](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/pell_plateaus/PELL_PLATEAUS.md)
proves equal birth counts on both sides of each cancellation center. The first
two square cancellation centers are 9 and 25, producing plateaus 1,1,1 and
2,2,2 respectively.
It uses the known Pell families in the growth packet; the observed arithmetic
overlap with the G15 path is not asserted to be causal or structural.

## Unused edges and prime-shell comparison

The [cycle-basis packet](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/cycle_basis/FUNDAMENTAL_CYCLES.md)
shows that unused edges of a fixed Hamilton path give fundamental-cycle
coordinates, with orientation incidences distinguished from distinct
orientations. The [shell comparison](../05-knowledge/results/session148_square_sum_20261103/shell_bridge/README.md)
finds matching component/shell sizes but different graph edge counts, so the
specified square-sum graphs are not isomorphic under any bijection.
