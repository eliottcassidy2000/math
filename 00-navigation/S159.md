# Session 159: construction tools and niche bridges

This session develops exact tournament constructions and translates them into several other mathematical languages. It does not settle the conjecture that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd path counts. Novelty relative to the full literature is not asserted; individual notes identify the relevant earlier repository results and dependencies. Proofs and finite controls are distinguished in each note.

- [Positive path-cover operator](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/profile_operator/NOTE.md): the full cover profile transforms under triangle substitution by a nonnegative operator. It also has an exact marked-arc tropical version and a finite mod-9 certificate for one strong family.
- [Strong square-sum encoding and heaps](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/heaps/NOTE.md): a strong tournament encodes each nonnegative integer injectively through square-sum attachments, building on S158. A separate weighted independence polynomial connects the earlier family to heaps, continued fractions, and a Riordan array; that aggregate is not one tournament's count.
- [Automatic binary bank](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/automatic/NOTE.md): a 2-regular state, Mahler equation, and quadratic algebraic irrational series for the S158 one-apex bank. The block proof of modular universality is an alternative to the result already proved in S157, not exact integer coverage.
- [Odd-cycle path-cover profiles](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/cycle_profiles/NOTE.md): the cycle-packing identity gives positive profile polynomials, sharp 2-adic gates, and a Smith-form interpolation lattice. Lattice membership does not characterize tournament realizability.
- [Parity-filtered cycle packings](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/parity_code/NOTE.md): a Fourier projection counts top-degree odd-cycle packings in disjoint-endpoint reversal gadgets, with explicit crossing formulas. The constrained code is not claimed to be a general delta-matroid.
- [Transfer and FRACTRAN](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/transfer_knots/NOTE.md): a strong interval-chain family has a rational generating function, an exact mod-7 restriction, and a fixed 13-fraction program for its recurrence. This restriction applies only to that family.
- [Branched fraction and Lagrange skeletons](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/lagrange/NOTE.md): a positive weighted-walk continued fraction and a Lagrange inversion formula lead to explicit iterated strong gadgets and an all-depth finite exponential formula.
- [Odd-cycle gas determinant](../05-knowledge/results/session159_constructions_20261103/odd_cycle_gas/NOTE.md): a labeled gas expansion refines earlier triangle-block path-count generating functions to the full odd-cycle packing polynomial, with a general-base determinant form.

The linked notes include checkers, hypotheses and prior-source credits. Computations are finite controls except where a finite state table is itself the stated induction certificate.
