# S189: prime-nine frontier and dyadic separation

**Reviewed snapshot, 4 November 2026.** This index covers thirteen independently reviewed publication checkpoints. The necessary frontier for a nontrivial full named-response collision from simultaneous XOR of **original source modules** is now: **every highest changed quotient is prime of order at least nine**. Separately, every fixed source admits an explicit sufficiently dyadic uniform transitive inflation whose original-module XOR family is separated. Neither result proves unrestricted named-response injectivity.
