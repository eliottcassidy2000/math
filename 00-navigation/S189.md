# S189: prime-nine frontier and dyadic separation

**Reviewed proof snapshot, 4 November 2026. The complete size-29 extension archive is still uploading; the earlier C3 and size-28 archives are complete.** A nontrivial full named-response collision obtained by simultaneous XOR of **original source modules** requires **at least 30 old vertices**. Every highest changed quotient is prime of order at least nine and has no transitive child, so each such node contains at least 27 physical vertices. The finite boundary results exclude entire sources of 27, 28 and 29 old names, including separately treated singleton and two-vertex exteriors. They do not claim arbitrary-child or arbitrary-fixed-ambient prime-nine closure. Separately, every fixed source admits an explicit sufficiently dyadic uniform transitive inflation whose original-module XOR family is separated. Unrestricted named-response injectivity remains open.
