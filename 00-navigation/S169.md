# Session 169: lonely-runner walls and collision geometry

This session reconnects earlier lonely-runner, planar-Jacobian and tournament-interface ideas through explicit examples and structural tests. It does not resolve the Lonely Runner Conjecture, the Jacobian conjecture, or the exact odd tournament Hamiltonian-path spectrum.

1. [Quadratic guard collision lattice](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/quadratic/NOTE.md): six literal guard states generate an index-64 integral quadratic sampling lattice, giving an optimal six-state collision test over characteristic zero and every odd characteristic. This is a local algebraic test, not a Keller-map construction or a Jacobian-conjecture proof.
2. [Runner guard geometry](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/runner_guards/NOTE.md): marked guard arcs encode exact runner threshold states and carry winding on a retained product torus. Boundary atoms require their own OR rule; coordinate marks and an external event schedule are part of the construction.
3. [Nine-dimensional collision bank](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/collision/COLLISION_BANK.md): twelve selected physical states form an optimal-size, radius-four test for the full query interface, with explicit radius-three blind directions. Ambient branch velocities are not thereby physical tournament deformations. The accompanying [historical metanalysis](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/collision/META_LEDGER.md) traces source ideas, lost coordinates and next constructive obstacles.
4. [Torus transport and winding](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/torus/NOTE.md): formal signed-unit transport, marked arithmetic honeycomb winding and scheduled tournament bit circuits are compared with their necessary sidecars. The arithmetic covering is bipartite only when its layer is retained, or under the stated disjoint-residue condition after forgetting it.
5. [Timed runner wall movies](../05-knowledge/results/session169_runner_jacobian_20261103/runner_movie/NOTE.md): rational wall cells and retained boundary and cut atoms encode exact specified runner instances. Their externally timed threshold lifts have a two-lap bound, and a reflection-compatible unshifted movie has a one-lap cancellation. These are not autonomous tournament dynamics or a new general lonely-runner case.

The notes include inherited source credits, exact proofs, finite certificates, and reproduction instructions. The independent checks retain the limitations of the interfaces: event geometry is not the same as formal transport, local collision completeness is not global invertibility, and a physical source return still requires its own construction.
