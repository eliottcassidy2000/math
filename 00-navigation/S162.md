# Session 162: MRB and square-sum constructions

This session uses MRB alternating-series ideas and square-sum graphs to build and analyze tournament witnesses. It does not prove that 7 and 21 are the only missing positive odd Hamilton-path counts. The notes distinguish exact path counts, weighted or marked observables, and family-specific obstructions; novelty against the full literature is not asserted.

- [Square-sum matched chains](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/matched_square/NOTE.md) realizes the earlier 33→51→69 and 257→387→517 chains on explicitly labelled square-sum-supported graphs, and proves an all-padding existence statement. Only the final member reverses every eligible square-sum edge on the chosen set; the two earlier states omit specified eligible edges.
- [Square-path transfer](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/square_transfer/NOTE.md) converts the gaps of actual square-sum Hamilton paths into interval gadgets and exact strong source counts. The [prime-lane obstruction](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/square_transfer/PRIME_OBSTRUCTION.md) proves a 3-divisibility barrier for the stated transferred family and gives exact 3-adic weights with their interval scope. The [run-number barrier](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/square_transfer/MODULE_BARRIER.md) extends this obstruction to arbitrary one-hole modular substitutions; a nonuniform attachment escapes it, reproducing an earlier 133 witness.
- [MRB-weighted carrier](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/mrb_weighted/RESULT.md) gives an exact weighted wrap identity and an explicit strong 145-path tournament. Its marked-path derivative and integer-token construction give a rigorous interval for the MRB series; marked/token counts and signed weights are not ordinary tournament path counts.
- [MRB defect transform](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/cycle_transform/NOTE.md) diagonally transforms odd-cycle packing coefficients and derives an exact signed edge-response identity. A checked example creates more odd cycles while decreasing the Hamilton-path count; positivity of the transform is not a spectrum-realization theorem.
- [MRB star filters](../05-knowledge/results/session162_mrb_square_20261103/mrb_star/NOTE.md) provide a weighted-cut formula for actual strong tournaments and a finite-difference projector. The carrier excludes 35 even though an explicit strong 35-path tournament exists, so it cannot alone cover the spectrum.

Each note states its dependencies, proofs, and finite controls. The familiar 7 and 21 exclusions are inputs where cited; the remaining global inclusion is open.
