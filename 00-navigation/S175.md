## Research packets

[Fixed-target A000568 projective quotient](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/a000568/README.md) and [independent orbit audit](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/orbits/README.md). 3. [Uniform cyclic-module classification](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/obstructions/PROOF.md) and [audit](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/obstructions/AUDIT.md). 4. [Nonuniform interfaces and orphan paths](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/interface/PROOF.md) and [independent reviewer audit](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/interface/REVIEWER_AUDIT.md). Endpoint-sensitive edge responses, the correction to legal-slot insertion, and 1,536 exactly checked seven-vertex interface states. Finite response components and explicit strong witnesses are verified; no unbounded interval coverage or new global attainment is claimed.
5. [Finite clone-library obstruction](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/clone_obstruction/PROOF.md) and [independent reviewer audit](../05-knowledge/results/session175_tournament_spectrum_20261104/clone_obstruction/REVIEWER_AUDIT.md). For K fixed quotient templates of order at most d, arbitrary transitive inflation, reversal and ordered sums produce at most Kd floor(log_2(N−1))^d prime counts through N. Thus their share of primes tends to zero. Singleton quotient components are treated separately. The theorem does not obstruct growing interface complexity or richer internal modules.

A000568 counts tournament isomorphism classes; H counts Hamilton vertex words; H/|Aut| counts path orbits. These are distinct targets. The enumeration quotient does not recover Hamilton witnesses.

## Reproduction and remaining frontier

Each packet includes source, exact receipts and independent review. Normal and optimized mathematical outputs agree; timing fields may differ. Run scripts from their packet directory and follow each README's commands. The independent orbit checker imports its sibling enumeration packet. The synthesis audit checks metrics, links and small spectra. No software was installed for this session.

The remaining infinite obligations are strong witnesses for every odd prime other than 7, and every 7p with p an odd prime other than 3. Known carry witnesses complete the multiplicative reduction, not the missing constructions. The new clone obstruction explains why a fixed finite library of transitive inflations cannot settle the prime lane. A justified next route is increasing nonuniform interface complexity with a proved endpoint-sensitive recurrence and overlapping strong response intervals.
