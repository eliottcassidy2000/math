# Session 152: two lists, recurrences and ear responses

The lists are compared as given; shared integers do not by themselves establish a map between their mathematical contexts. The tournament Hamiltonian-path spectrum conjecture remains open.

- [Ear-image polynomial](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/ear/README.md) explains the ten-element list as the nonconstant cut image of one order-five parent, including multiplicities and a stateful recurrence.
- [Ear-cut reciprocity](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/recursions/RESULT.md) derives general quadratic and self-converse identities, including even multiplicities in odd order.
- [Integral directed-cut carrier](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/cut_induction/README.md) constructs exact cut weights by max flow and proves strong-parent growth, while recording why those properties alone do not yield interval coverage.
- [Ear-response generating function](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/ear_gf/README.md) gives a variable-elimination recurrence for the fixed cut carrier, with its limits.
- [Square-sum comparison](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/square_sum/NOTE.md) gives graph edge-birth generating functions and distinguishes them from Hamiltonian-path existence.
- [Additive semigroups](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/number_theory/NOTE.md) gives a deliberately chosen additive interpretation, with Apéry sets and generating functions, not a claimed natural correspondence.
- [Historical contexts](../05-knowledge/results/session152_sequences_20261103/history/HISTORY.md) traces intersections and differences among ear images, square-sum orders, PRIMEGAME and Ogg primes, with scope and source caveats.

The finite calculations and cited results do not establish an all-order spectrum induction or a structural equivalence among these contexts. Each packet states its dependencies and reproduction commands.
