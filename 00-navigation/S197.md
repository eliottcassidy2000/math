# Session 197: overnight tournament spectrum research

**ACTIVE.** This is a working index for the extended overnight session. The full {7,21} Hamiltonian-path spectrum remains open. All finite ranges below are restricted to the stated construction classes and are not all-order spectrum proofs.

## Reviewed checkpoints so far

- [Bounded-backward-span frontier transfer](../session197/ribbon/PROOF.md): exact physical transfer and arbitrary-depth bounded-output certificates; span 2 misses 7, 21 and 47 through 1,001, while span 3 realizes 47.
- [Logarithmic bounded-span witnesses](../session197/ribbon-compression/PROOF.md): for fixed span, a count h>1 admits a representative of order O(log h), with explicit constant and a finite verification consequence.
- [Paired ternary fan](../session197/paired-fan/PROOF.md): both 3H+2 and 3H+4 are physically realized from a common infinite family, with separate port choices; this is not a universal returning grammar.
- [Returning three-port clock](../session197/ternary-clock/PROOF.md): actual tournaments with H_m=60·3^m−1 and an indefinitely reusable 3H+2 birth. A pure-clone 3H+4 counterpart is ruled out in the stated form.
- [Two-port synthesis](../session197/two-port/PROOF.md) and [two-sided gateway](../session197/two-sided/PROOF.md): exact physical interfaces, and explicit H49→H149/H151 escapes beyond the older one-root operation. Large gateway certificates can be regenerated as directed in REPRODUCE.md.
- [Complete H67 classification](../session197/lowcycle/PROOF.md): every H67 tournament has one of six seven-vertex strong cores; the minimum Hamiltonian-cycle count among strong examples is three.
- [Rooted low-cycle gluing](../session197/lowcycle/ROOTED.md), [Fibonacci enumeration](../session197/lowcycle/FIBONACCI_CLASS.md), and [serial obstructions](../session197/lowcycle/SERIAL_OBSTRUCTIONS.md): exact scoped recursive results; they do not prove a global low-cycle classification.
- [Bounded-slack inverse](../session197/prime-atoms/PROOF.md), [factorwise bridge](../session197/prime-atoms/FACTOR_BRIDGE.md), and [sharpening](../session197/prime-atoms/SHARPENING.md): all-order profile reduction and finite factorwise certificates for specified cloning interfaces, not universal prime coverage.
- [Positive path-polynomial coordinates](../session197/path-polynomials/PROOF.md) and [universal contexts](../session197/path-polynomials/UNIVERSAL_CONTEXTS.md): exact positive central-factorial representations; positivity of algebraic factors does not imply all positive states are physically realizable.
- [Automorphism and parity wildcard](../session197/orbit-wildcard/PROOF.md): exact context divisor identities and a verified 14-vertex symmetric 7p example with two moving seven-orbits and no nontrivial module.

## Additional verified coverage and structural tools

- [Arithmetic inverse and finite coverage](../session197/lowcycle/arithmetic-inverse/README.md): explicit strong tournaments with at most two Hamiltonian cycles realize every odd H from 3 through 10,000,001 except 7, 21 and 67. The 67 case is realized by the classified three-cycle cores above. This is finite coverage, not an all-order spectrum proof.
- [Low-cycle two-port compiler](../session197/lowcycle-two-port/PROOF.md) supplies a physical finite-state construction and exact bounded closure; [pair-entry bridge](../session197/pair-entry-bridge/PROOF.md) reduces the full strong two-cycle class to a single physical pair attachment and rooted continuations.
- [Clone matching and sparsity](../session197/ribbon-clone-matching/PROOF.md) identifies the exponential base from crossing matching rank, and shows that cloning from finitely many fixed strong seeds yields only polylogarithmically many distinct counts up to X. This is a restriction on that particular grammar.
- [Minimum-cover polynomial factorization](../session197/path-polynomials/MINIMUM_COVER_POLYNOMIAL.md) and [endpoint reciprocity](../session197/path-polynomials/ENDPOINT_RECIPROCITY.md) give independently checked structural identities for clone profiles and rooted path responses.
- [Primitive cancellation](../session197/path-polynomials/PRIMITIVE_CANCELLATION.md) gives an explicit coprime-parameter physical family with a prime count and a 7p count, escaping a fixed-divisor obstruction in a smaller bank. Its outputs still obey restrictive congruences; no infinite-prime or full-coverage claim is made.


## Later structural and inverse results

- [Exact two-cycle, two-feedback-vertex spectrum](../session197/fixed-two-c2-spectrum/PROOF.md): a complete regular-language and arithmetic classification for this restricted class. It does not classify all strong two-cycle tournaments.
- [The 533 boundary](../session197/lowcycle/feedback533/README.md): every strong H=533 tournament with at most two Hamiltonian cycles needs at least three feedback vertices. A [ten-vertex three-cycle witness](../session197/lowcycle/feedback533-c3/README.md) attains H=533 with two feedback vertices. [The 821 threshold](../session197/fixed-two-821/PROOF.md) is four cycles within the two-feedback-vertex class.
- [Rooted backward inverse](../session197/lowcycle/backward-profile-inverse/README.md): an exact physical profile-membership test and witness reconstruction, with logarithmic recursion depth. Choosing a suitable profile for an arbitrary scalar target remains open.
- [A global induced-extension obstruction](../session197/thirteen-to-twentyseven/PROOF.md): no H=13 tournament is an induced subtournament of an H=27 tournament. Thus an append-only 13→27 step cannot be repaired merely by changing ports; this does not rule out rebuilding or other constructions.
- [Clone symmetry and rigidity](../session197/converse-clone-lengths/README.md): a sharp finite-or-all threshold for self-converse clone lengths, plus eventual rigidity for unit clone clocks. [The unit-boundary twin theorem](../session197/unit-boundary-twin/PROOF.md) identifies a literal-cloning obstruction for a specified fixed-mask return.
- [A three-cover cyclic gate](../session197/path-polynomials/THREE_COVER_GATE.md) gives an exact restricted inverse and a strong self-converse 16-vertex example H=37,050,461=7·5,292,923. It is a construction within one gate, not a universal 7p theorem.

Further independent audits and constructive searches are ongoing. Checkpoints include verifier code and their review notes; named inherited mechanisms are credited in each proof.
