# Session 197: overnight tournament spectrum research

**ACTIVE.** This is a working index for the extended overnight session. The full {7,21} Hamiltonian-path spectrum remains open. All finite ranges below are restricted to the stated construction classes and are not all-order spectrum proofs.

## Reviewed checkpoints so far

- [Bounded-backward-span frontier transfer](../session197/ribbon/PROOF.md): exact physical transfer and arbitrary-depth bounded-output certificates; span 2 misses 7, 21 and 47 through 1,001, while span 3 realizes 47.
- [Logarithmic bounded-span witnesses](../session197/ribbon-compression/PROOF.md): for fixed span, a count h>1 admits a representative of order O(log h), with explicit constant and a finite verification consequence.
- [Paired ternary fan](../session197/paired-fan/PROOF.md): both 3H+2 and 3H+4 are physically realized from a common infinite family, with separate port choices; this is not a universal returning grammar.
- [Returning three-port clock](../session197/ternary-clock/PROOF.md): actual tournaments with H_m=60·3^m−1 and an indefinitely reusable 3H+2 birth. A pure-clone 3H+4 counterpart is ruled out in the stated form.
- [Two-port synthesis](../session197/two-port/PROOF.md) and [two-sided gateway](../session197/two-sided/PROOF.md): exact physical interfaces, and explicit H49→H149/H151 escapes beyond the older one-root operation. Large gateway certificates can be regenerated as directed in REPRODUCE.md.
- [Complete H67 classification](../session197/lowcycle/PROOF.md): every H67 tournament has one of six seven-vertex strong cores; the minimum Hamiltonian-cycle count among strong examples is three.
- [Rooted low-cycle gluing](../session197/lowcycle/ROOTED.md), [Fibonacci enumeration](../session197/lowcycle/FIBONACCI_CLASS.md), and [serial obstructions](../session197/lowcycle/SERIAL_OBSTRUCTIONS.md): exact scoped recursive results; they do not prove a global low-cycle classification.
- [Bounded-slack inverse](../session197/prime-atoms/PROOF.md), [factorwise bridge](../session197/prime-atoms/FACTOR_BRIDGE.md), and [sharpening](../session197/prime-atoms/SHARPENING.md): all-order profile reduction and finite factorwise certificates for specified cloning interfaces, not universal prime coverage.
- [Positive path-polynomial coordinates](../session197/path-polynomials/PROOF.md) and [universal contexts](../session197/path-polynomials/UNIVERSAL_CONTEXTS.md): exact positive central-factorial representations; positivity of algebraic factors does not imply all positive states are physically realizable.
- [Automorphism and parity wildcard](../session197/orbit-wildcard/PROOF.md): exact context divisor identities and a verified 14-vertex symmetric 7p example with two moving seven-orbits and no nontrivial module.

Additional reviewed checkpoints:

- [Complete unique-cycle structure](../session197/lowcycle/C1_COMPLETE.md): every strong tournament with exactly one Hamiltonian cycle has a strong bridge. Combined with the earlier rooted grammar, this removes the bridge qualification from its Fibonacci class counts. It is not a classification of the two-cycle case.
- [Returning three-port ternary clock](../session197/ternary-clock/PROOF.md): actual tournaments realize H_m=60·3^m−1 and the physical birth H→3H+2 indefinitely; the opposite digit is not supplied by the same pure-clone mechanism.
- [Entropy and cycle obstructions](../session197/entropy-bridge/PROOF.md) and [opposed-binary sparsity](../session197/opposed-binary/PROOF.md) constrain specified construction languages, not the full spectrum.
- [Strong H15 classification](../session197/prime-atoms/H15_CLASSIFICATION.md) gives all strong cores and clone outcomes; [prime-cube rigidity](../session197/orbit-wildcard/PRIME_CUBES.md) extends the earlier prime/square symmetry results.
- [Positive universal whole-module contexts](../session197/path-polynomials/UNIVERSAL_CONTEXTS.md) and [matching bandwidth](../session197/path-polynomials/CONSEQUENCES.md) give exact positive profile operators, while leaving realizability of arbitrary positive states open.
