# Session 166: planar ideas and recurring metagraph structures

This session builds exact bridges from planar-Jacobian, unit-distance and local-to-global ideas to specified tournament-state and metagraph constructions. It does not give a complete classification of all tournaments or all metagraph patterns, prove the planar polynomial Jacobian conjecture, or improve the Hadwiger–Nelson bounds.

- [Physical interpolation banks](../05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/collision_banks/NOTE.md) give dimension- and word-length-optimal polynomial tests in the literal C/D guard language, including a six-marked-state collision test. The map germs are an explicit analogy to collision geometry, not physical tournament deformations or a Keller-map construction.
- [Local reversibility and monodromy](../05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/local_monodromy/NOTE.md) distinguish erased information, hidden covering sheets and restricted physical images. The graph covers are formal observation-state extensions; rigid coloring alone does not give a global inverse.
- [Reflection voltages](../05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/reflection_voltages/REFLECTION_VOLTAGES.md) classify the specified fixed-flag quotient and its local transport. This is not a realization theorem for every abstract lift or a classification of the whole metagraph.
- [Shared-root unit diamonds](../05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/unit_diamonds/NOTE.md) classify their injective plane realizations and develop exact rigidity and coloring properties. The Moser spindle also occurs as an induced subgraph of a tournament single-arc-flip class graph. That graph is not the repository's blue/black complement-line metagraph.
- [Cartesian flip graphs](../05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/cartesian_flip/NOTE.md) give induced product and strong-wrapper mechanisms, plus a certified order-eight strong realization of the rigid nontraceable G5 chain. Its minimal-order argument uses exhaustive searches only through order seven; nontraceability concerns the graph of tournament classes, not Hamilton paths inside the tournaments.

The notes credit inherited and external results, state exact hypotheses and include reproducible controls. Finite computations serve as controls unless a finite exhaustive certificate is explicitly part of the claim. No literature-priority claim is made for the new compositions.
