# Session 161: source matching and transported responses

This session builds on the earlier [apex-wedge carrier](../05-knowledge/results/apex_wedge_constructor_20261103/CARRIER.md), [full-profile response](../05-knowledge/results/apex_wedge_constructor_20261103/PROFILE.md), and [wedge geometry](../05-knowledge/results/apex_wedge_constructor_20261103/GEOMETRY.md). Equal jumps do not imply equal source counts. None of the constructions establishes matching at arbitrary prescribed counts or the complete tournament path-count spectrum.

- [Matched literal sources](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/matched_sources/NOTE.md) give explicit strong +18, +130 and −126 transitions, with actual starting and ending counts and the same old edge changed. The [chain addendum](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/matched_sources/CHAIN.md) constructs the exact labelled chains 33→51→69 and 257→387→517. Their intermediate graphs are literally the same, not merely equal in count.
- [Shared-center fans](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/matched_sources/FAN.md) give all-order profile and jump formulas for a prescribed sequence of edge reversals. A complete seven-vertex labelled search from one specified source finds no third +18 move; it does not rule out other sources or orders.
- [Carry fingerprints](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/carry_fingerprint/NOTE.md) give full-profile response polynomials and a sign threshold for balanced +2 jumps, together with an infinite family whose absolute starting counts are explicitly certified. The finite linear certificate and imported source automaton have their stated scope.
- [The q-response family](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/q_response/NOTE.md) supplies an explicit parametrized family and a recurrence for its transported jumps. Its sources generally have different absolute counts.
- [Wedge centers](../05-knowledge/results/session161_source_matching_20261103/lattice/WEDGE_CENTERS.md) describe modular solvability, paired lattice structure and marked Boolean source-presentation parity counts. These are response-lattice facts, not automorphism-group or isomorphism-class counts.

Each note includes its proof, dependencies, explicit scope and reproducible controls. Finite computations test general arguments unless the note identifies a finite certificate as part of the proof. Novelty against the full literature is not asserted.
