3. [Uniform-context information](../05-knowledge/results/session176_tournament_information_20261104/information/PROOF.md). Ordered path-cover profiles characterize all uniform one-hole contexts and are closed under uniform substitution. Scalar H is insufficient: two H=15 modules yield 123 and 135 in the same wrapper. Nonuniform attachment lies outside this equivalence.
4. [Strong unit gates and feedback circuits](../05-knowledge/results/session176_tournament_information_20261104/spectrum/PROOF.md). Strong pairs differing by one edge have H=6·2^r±1 at every order r+4. Arbitrary-module replacement changes the increment to 2H(A). An exact growing-feedback streaming circuit includes all endpoint and orphan cases; it does not establish interval coverage.
5. [Constructor resource tradeoff](../05-knowledge/results/session176_tournament_information_20261104/resource/PROOF.md). A deduplicated library of K strong cores of order at most d, under transitive inflation, reversal and ordered sums, supplies at most K floor(log₂(N−1))^d prime outputs through N. This is a limitation of the specified constructor language, not global attainment.
