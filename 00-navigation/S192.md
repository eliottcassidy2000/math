# S192: unexpected approaches to tournament spectrum

This session develops several distinct routes toward the Hamiltonian-path spectrum and size-dependent tournament structure. The global conjecture that the positive odd spectrum omits only 7 and 21 remains open. The notes distinguish all-order proofs, finite certificates and proposals.

- [Dyadic residue programming](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/dyadic/PROOF.md): a fixed-exterior switch bank programs every odd residue modulo 2^k while an independent two-ended selector controls size to O_k(√H). The modulus-dependent error prevents an exact-coverage conclusion. [Arithmetic/dynamics approaches](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/dyadic/APPROACHES.md) include a conditional ternary induction and failed transfers, with inheritance credited.
- [Weighted-cut geometry](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/weighted_cuts/PROOF.md): exact path-count increments under a vertex birth are positive weighted directed cuts. Pinned min-cuts give integral certificates, and the orientation point's permutohedron face records strong components. [Even-path continuation](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/weighted_cuts/EVEN_PATH_CONTINUATION.md) gives a positive path expansion and a circulation explanation.
- [Interval-cycle matching model](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/interval_cycles/PROOF.md): exact odd-cycle collections of interval chains form a weighted interval model, yielding negative real roots for their full collection polynomial. This is a family-specific theorem; one arc reversal can destroy real-rootedness.
- [Fixed-apex tensor flags](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/tensor/PROOF.md): arbitrary mixed blocks share a tensor flag, while Jordan terms give polynomial counters in block repetition. Periodic families are constructed; arbitrary targets are not claimed.
- [Symplectic compiler](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/symplectic/PROOF.md): every two-apex word and arbitrary ordered joins admit integral four-dimensional alternating-sign realizations. All positive odd 139-smooth values except 7 and 21 have realizations in this fixed dimension; rank two cannot realize any H=13 tournament.
- [Boundary probability laws](../05-knowledge/results/session192_surprising_approaches_20261105/boundary_limits/PROOF.md): fixed-apex random attachments have boundary-controlled nondegenerate limits, unlike uniform random tournaments. The limiting distributions are not claims about the distinct integer spectrum.

Each linked note supplies exact hypotheses and executable checks. The new work builds on prior results identified within the individual notes; no general literature-priority claim is made.
