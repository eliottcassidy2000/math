# Degenerate-dimension boundary clarification

During the independent pre-publication audit, Section 3 was clarified: the projective automorphism iff requires k≥2. At k=0 the point set is empty; at k=1 it is a singleton. Every power acts trivially in either case. The coefficient-monoid iff and all fixed-count formulas are unchanged. This clarification is included before the first publication; no earlier published claim is silently replaced.

Section 6 also corrects the subgroup generated by the four center-65 gaps from 12Z to 24Z. The integer-multiplication obstruction is unchanged. The verifier now checks the gcd explicitly, together with the empty/singleton dimension boundaries.
