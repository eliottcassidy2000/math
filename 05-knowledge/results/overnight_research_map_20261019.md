## The theorem/status map

- **Square sums:** the consecutive seed 482–3362–6242 occurs in a square-sum Hamiltonian cycle on [1,N] exactly for N≥6242. Compatible lifts also preserve a large simultaneous family of prescribed paths.
- **Graceful reconfiguration:** every acyclic fixed upper-tail choice has a connected completion fiber under two-color exchanges. Arbitrary fixed-color fibers can disconnect; prescribed tree shape is not preserved.
- **Collatz:** guarded expansion/reset banks have uniform descent heads, and a symbolic word-length bound rules out zero-head failures below an enormous explicit length. Arbitrary source entry and unrestricted zero-head remain open.
- **Seven-thin Gold/balance:** height at most two is settled by very good pairs. Height-three rigid examples have no good pair but strict Gold certificates. Matching certificates extend the exchange method; no universal exchange-capacity theorem is proved.
- **Planar source flows:** the full two-exponent family has an algebraic/transcendental classification, individual algebraic degrees, Galois groups, and radical classification. Finite-word and closure conclusions depend essentially on the generating family. None supplies a global Jacobian-conjecture reduction.

The common mechanism is controlled repair: preserve exactly the state needed by the next argument, prove admissibility after the move, and separately prove that every required input reaches that state. Local exchange, exact algebraic motion, and global coverage are distinct obligations.

## 1. Square sums and graceful trees: what an exchange may release

The [seeded threshold theorem](seeded_optimal_threshold_6242_20261019/OPTIMAL_THRESHOLD_THEOREM.md) combines protected insertions/reversals for 6242≤N≤128452 with an [eventual transition construction](seeded_eventual_cycle_theorem_20261019/SEEDED_INTERVAL_THEOREM.md) for N≥128453. Both seed edges survive, and the ranges meet without a gap. Necessity is immediate because the seed includes 6242; sufficiency is the substantive construction.

The transferable invariant is positional parity shared by compatible interval paths, rather than their entire internal ordering. Certified cuts release selected edges while preserving coverage and connectivity. The [finite-cut retention theorem](square_sum_finite_cut_retention_20261019/FINITE_CUT_RETENTION.md) gives |K′|≥Q|K|−|D| after a factor-Q lift, where D records copies actually destroyed by cuts. The [all-size path theorem](square_sum_all_size_connected_paths_20261019/ALL_SIZE_CONNECTED_PATHS.md) supplies 2,303 pairwise disjoint triples simultaneously with the seed for every N≥6,294,221. Disjointness is among the triples, not necessarily between triples and seed. Longer prescribed paths still require compatible inputs; retention alone does not manufacture them.

The analogous graceful invariant is matroid closure, graphically the connected-component partition. The [rank-staircase theorem](graceful_upper_tail_two_color_connectivity_20261019/UPPER_TAIL_TWO_COLOR_CONNECTIVITY.md) proves nonempty, two-color-connected completion fibers when fixed difference colors form an acyclic upper tail. With r free colors the diameter is at most 2^(r−1) for r≥1. Closure-preserving double exchanges keep the untouched connecting choices valid.

Freezing arbitrary already-correct edges overconstrains this mechanism. The [arithmetic counterexample](graceful_fixed_color_counterexample_20261019/ARITHMETIC_FIXED_COLOR_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md), on labels 0,…,12 with free colors 6,9,11, has five completions split into components of sizes three and two. Global connectivity may require releasing a common fixed edge. Because exchanges may change abstract tree shape, neither result proves the Graceful Tree Conjecture.

## 2. Seven-thin Gold: a sufficient structure, its failure, and its replacement

Seven-thin means that each vertex has at most seven incomparable neighbors. Weak Gold asks for a two-comparison strategy with E≥t₁+t₂ along every answer path, where E is the initial extension count and tᵢ the successive survivor counts; the convention permits repeated or determined comparisons.

The [good-pair synthesis](good_pair_gold_synthesis_20261019.md) combines attributed Zaguia insertion arguments with the Gold half-crossing calculation. The [height-two theorem](seven_thin_height_two_very_good_gold_20261019.md) gives a very good pair in every non-total seven-thin poset of height at most two, including isolated vertices. Ambient degree budgets force nearby incidence rows; their directed differences are chains. This structural conclusion is stronger than merely observing a balanced comparison in a uniform induced subposet.

It fails at height three. A [rigid ten-point witness](height_three_seven_thin_no_good_pair_20261019.md) has 11,971 extensions and no good pair in either orientation or the dual. Nevertheless, comparisons (3,5) then (2,4) have cells (3281,3281,1899,3510) and minimum Gold margin 2,128. Ordinal sums preserve the normalized margin and absence of good pairs, producing arbitrarily large rigid examples, with growing height. Failure of good-pair existence is not failure of Gold.

The [conditional exchange theorem](conditional_gold_exchange_and_minimal_obstruction_20261019.md) and [matching refinement](gold_module_reduction_and_triple_exchange_20261019.md) identify the exact replacement obligation. If the first heavy branch has b extensions, and a legal exchange graph joins opposite outcomes of the second comparison, a matching of size ν proves Gold whenever

    ν ≥ 2b−E.

Fractional matchings also work, provided each extension has incident weight at most one. Overlapping exchange domains cannot simply be added.

This distinction is visible on the second no-good-pair seed Q′: E=7249, b=4710, so ν≥2171 is needed. Every collection of label involutions literally swapping the second pair is bounded by positional capacity ν≤1338. Allowing all three-cycles meeting either endpoint gives a certified matching of size 2206, with an equal-size vertex cover establishing optimality for that graph. The certified heavy margin is 35. Thus bounded-support exchanges succeed where the entire fixed-pair-involution method fails. This is an exact example, not a theorem that support-three exchanges always suffice.

The open hypothesis **H7** says every non-total seven-thin poset has a good pair or a comparison in [2/5,3/5]. A sufficient, narrower target restricts to posets with no proper non-total module and transitive strict majority order. Module restriction has equal extension fibers, so internal probabilities and strategies transfer exactly. Lexicographic minimality by vertices and then incomparable pairs, together with the classical cyclic-majority Gold criterion, proves that this restricted hypothesis would imply seven-thin Gold. Neither hypothesis is established. Proper chain modules are not eliminated, and contracting them is not justified.

## 3. Ambient structure and finite morphology certificates

The [one-slack antichain theorem](saturated_antichain_very_good_pair_20261019.md) gives a new ambient restriction: an m-antichain with t common incomparable vertices and degree bound m+t contains a very-good-pair clique of size at least ⌈m/2⌉, for m≥3. Only one additional incomparability per antichain vertex remains; splitting lower and upper defects proves the claim directly.

Consequently a hypothetical seven-thin balance or weak-Gold counterexample has width at most six, and every antichain S of size 3 through 6 has at most 6−|S| common incomparable neighbors. These are necessary conditions, not complete morphology exclusions.

The root-approved [two-slack theorem](two_slack_antichain_boundary_20261019.md) reduces the defect graph, absent a very good pair inside the maximum antichain, to paths and cycles. A maximum antichain of size m≥5 whose members have degree at most m+1 guarantees an ambient very good pair when both exterior parts are antichains. A concrete 5–6–5 layered example has no very good pair inside its unique maximum six-antichain, but has them on the lower boundary. Thus even choosing an extremal antichain does not locate the needed pair. Arbitrary exterior attachments remain the obstacle; the full seven-thin width-six theorem is open.

For ordinary balance, maximum-span roots in the strict majority order give finite core morphologies (l,u,c). The [exact projection lemma](canonical_maxspan_transfer_20261019/canonical_maxspan_transfer_20261019.md) preserves common-neighbor data for tied maximum-span pairs. It does not automatically preserve exclusive-neighbor counts or extension probabilities. Those require separate saturation/slack arguments and nonnegative boundary-generator lifts. A feasible relaxed cone mixture is not automatically an ambient poset.

The [mixed-(2,2,4) exclusion](mixed22_c4_exact_exclusion_20261019/mixed22_c4_exact_exclusion_20261019.md) is completed after fresh four-way replay and exact set-equality assembly: 1,165,395 degree-valid cores split into 950,128 analytic exclusions and 215,267 residual cores, with 2,902,976 generator-certified orders. This c=4 proof does not use the six-thin theorem. Together with the earlier c≤3 exclusions, it closes the full mixed-(2,2) branch for any maximum-span root.

The [canonical (2,4,1) exclusion](canonical_mixed24_c1_exclusion_20261019/canonical_mixed24_c1_exclusion_20261019.md) covers 91,604 degree-valid cores and 415,884 certified residual orders. The completed [canonical (3,3,1) exclusion](canonical_mixed33_c1_exclusion_20261019/canonical_mixed33_c1_exclusion_20261019.md) has a fresh complete three-phase replay covering all 80,914 residual cores and 515,442 certified projected orders. Both canonical roots maximize span and then common-neighbor count; neither theorem excludes every maximum-span root of its morphology. Short-span reductions explicitly use Peczarski's established six-thin theorem. The remaining catalogue has 25 any-root triples, or 23 canonical-root triples after these two extra restrictions. These are morphology counts, not counts of posets or independent conjectures. Full seven-thin balance and Gold remain open.

Two resolved obstacles in the canonical-(3,3,1) proof expose what exterior information a relaxation must retain. For the specified 540-extension core and order, seven independently weighted boundary generators genuinely make every forward comparison exceed 2/3. Yet actual coefficients factor as U_u V_v; the witness violates a rank-one determinant identity. The [factorized-boundary certificate](factorized_boundary_exclusion_20261019/factorized_boundary_exclusion_20261019.md) excludes every normalized nonnegative rank-one model for this core/order, without degree-slack assumptions: 20 positive multiplier terms combine the comparison margins into a coefficientwise nonpositive polynomial. Independently, the [one-slack exterior split](one_slack_exterior_split_20261019/one_slack_exterior_split_20261019.md) uses the degree-six common vertex: its single remaining incomparability forces all lower exterior vertices below it, or all upper exterior vertices above it. Each resulting 112-generator cone has a three-row integer certificate with weights 2,1,2 up to ordering and margins at most −2. Actual data belong to a union of these cones, not their convex hull. These fixed-core results resolve the first obstacle; complete catalogue coverage is the separate obligation discharged by the terminal replay. The valid relaxed witness was never a poset counterexample.

The second obstruction shows that factorization is still insufficient. An [exact rank-one array](factorized_boundary_feasibility_20261019/factorized_boundary_feasibility_20261019.md) on a different 408-extension core gives total mass 948 and all 14 forward probabilities above 2/3, with minimum 211/316. Yet its positive long-word increments have zero ancestors. The [degree-cap theorem](boundary_increment_caps_20261019/boundary_increment_caps.md) proves U_(wq)≤s_A(q)U_w and the suffix dual by deletion/reinsertion, hence λ_child≤(7−d_Q(q))λ_parent. Five comparison rows and 18 cap rows give strictly negative residuals on all 133 generators, excluding this core/order for every actual exterior. Independent caps on both sides suffice; allocation branching is unnecessary. The progression is therefore from independently weighted generators, to factorized coefficients, to degree-capped word trees. These are necessary exterior constraints, not a realizability characterization, and each exact obstruction diagnoses a relaxation rather than a poset counterexample.

## 4. Collatz: compression can destroy the useful bound

The [bank theorem](collatz_bank_reset_20261019.md) treats fully guarded expansion-bank words followed by reset-bank words, with contracting total slope. Bounded expanding centers C and reset fixed points P bound the normalized carry by C+P. The [modular refinement](collatz_modular_bank_head_20261019.md) yields head 107 for the specified banks; another lawful reset removes it on the refined lawful cylinder.

But [genuine adjacent macroreturns](collatz_unbounded_return_centers_20261019.md) have compressed centers tending to infinity as slopes approach one from above, although every lower prefix stays above its source. The uncompressed expansion/reset factorization retains its bounded-center argument. Compression preserves an endpoint affine formula while losing the quantitative hypothesis.

The [zero-head length obstruction](collatz_zero_head_length_obstruction_20261019.md) now proves descent for every guarded contracting word in the specified banks with nonempty expansion and reset segments and Terras length

    N < 918644030413102796640970006656455.

This is a symbolic necessary-length obstruction to a counterexample, not enumeration to that bound. Universal zero-head remains open. More importantly, no theorem forces arbitrary Collatz sources into these guarded cylinders; even universal zero-head would leave that source-entry gap.

## 5. Planar flows: complete algebraic locus, carefully bounded scope

For characteristic-zero K and nonzero scalar time, the [two-exponent theorem](planar_two_exponent_flow_classification_20261019.md) classifies actual source-coordinate flows of

    H=x^k t^m(1+x²t),   m≥2, k≥0, a=k−2m.

Both coordinates are algebraic exactly when a=0, a>0 divides m, or a=−b with b≥2 a proper divisor of m+1. Otherwise each coordinate is transcendental. The rational cases are exactly a=0 and (m,k)=(3,4); these maps are nonpolynomial. Exactness of the time differential, not rationality of the invariant curve alone, controls algebraicity.

The [positive](planar_positive_divisor_galois_classification_20261019.md) and [negative](planar_negative_divisor_galois_classification_20261019.md) classifications complete individual degrees and radicals. For a>0, put r=m/a: each coordinate generates degree n=m+a+1. For a=−b, put r=(m+1)/b: each generates degree n=m−b. Negative r=2 cases are Kummer, with geometric C_n and arithmetic C_n⋊Gal(K(ζ_n)/K). Otherwise geometric groups are A_n when the relevant pair (a,r), or (b,r), is even-even, and S_n otherwise; the notes give exact arithmetic square classes. Radical cases are precisely the rational locus, positive quartic (2,5), negative r=2 family, and negative cubic (5,8).

The [incidence-tree proof](polynomial_critical_injectivity_incidence_tree_20261019.md) removes classification-of-finite-simple-groups dependence from group identification. Distinct critical values give simultaneous single branch cycles; their support-incidence graph is a tree. Two adjacent supports meet once, producing a commutator 3-cycle; primitivity then forces A_n. Classical covering theory, Lüroth, and base-change facts remain explicit inputs. Generic-target monodromy alone is insufficient: the source-flow proofs separately establish disjoint translated branch loci and fixed-time linear disjointness.

The [quintic connection](planar_quintic_s5_petersen_resolvent_20261019.md) is exact: S5 acts faithfully on ten root pairs as on the Petersen graph; the same five labels define K5, and the discriminant extension leaves A5, abstractly the icosahedral rotation group. In the E→∞ specialization V⁵−z⁵, the pair-sum resolvent is Y¹⁰+11z⁵Y⁵−z¹⁰, with 11=φ⁵−φ⁻⁵. This is a genuine permutation/resolvent/Lucas connection, not a spatial symmetry claim about the flow.

Finally, [finite affine-x words](planar_affine_x_rational_word_rigidity_20261019.md) are polynomial only at identity, while their [source t-adic closure](planar_source_closure_formal_shears_20261019.md) contains every polynomial source shear. Enlarging to all x-dependent Hamiltonians c+t²R permits [three rational nonpolynomial factors](planar_full_source_group_polynomial_intersection_20261019.md) to realize every such shear already as a finite word. Generator class and topology change the answer. These source-chart theorems establish algebraic motion and exact obstructions; they do not prove that arbitrary Keller maps enter the chart or settle planar JC.
