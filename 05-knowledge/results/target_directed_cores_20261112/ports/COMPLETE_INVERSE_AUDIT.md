## 1. Finite universe and safe pruning

For a strong unique-start core with state
`(h,e,s,p,f,t)=(H,E1,S2,P2,E2,S3)`, the exact ready target at outer-word
integer `x≥0` is

    Q(x)=h g(x)+(s+p)x+e·parity(x)+s+p−e−f−1.

The word is written least-significant bit first and ends1 when nonempty.
The empty word has `x=0`. The tail automaton gives `g(x)≥1`: for a nonempty
legal word, replacing the `last` state at every bit supplies an accepted
history; the empty history also contributes1. Thus `Q(x)≥Q(0)`. Moreover

    Q(0)−(p−f)=h−e+s−1≥0,

because `e≤h` and `s≥1`. Swapping the two path slots shows `f≤p/2`.
Therefore any core in a target realization has `p≤2Q`, and `P2≥2^n`
gives core order `n≤floor(log2(2Q))`. The latter inequality simply counts
every vertex subset as an ordered pair of induced subtournaments, each
of which has at least one Hamilton path.

Every intermediate ancestor can also be restricted to `p≤2Q`. For a
funnel tail of length `l`, with the inherited coefficients, one has

    p'=2(L3·t+B2·p−D2·f)
       ≥(L3+2B2−D2)p.

Here `t≥p/2` follows by cutting the root-containing path in an ordered
two-slot cover just before its root and retaining the original-slot bit.
For a nonempty tail ending1, `B2≥2^(l−1)`, `D2≤2^l−1`, and the last1
gives `L3≥2^l+3^(l−1)`. Consequently

    p'≥(2^l+3^(l−1)+1)p,    l>0;
    p'≥2p,                  l=0.

These are exactly the safe skip conditions in the parent's C++ enumerator.
In particular discarded ancestors cannot later return below the bound.
