`H(D)=35` and `P_D(2)=676` certified in [PROOF](PROOF.md). For every

    M_(a,b)=D^b → TT_a.

    h=35^b,       P=676^b·2^a,       c=P−2h.

The chart2 source and its retained-predecessor RNR exit are therefore

    H=1386P−13h,       s=q=15h,       F=2592P−129h,

35 is coprime to `2·676`; also `3∤h`. The only remaining failure in(P)

    2^2519=35^2519=676^2519=1,     2365^2519=−1  (mod5039)
