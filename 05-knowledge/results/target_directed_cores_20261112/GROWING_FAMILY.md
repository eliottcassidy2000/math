## 1. A common actual module behind positive and negative offsets

For a nonempty tournament M retain the pair

    h=H(M),  f=P_M(2),  c=f−2h.

Here P(2) counts ordered two-slot spanning path covers, allowing empties.
Ordinal join multiplies both h and f coordinatewise. The three exact
[module charts](ports/PROOF.md) have whole counts

    462f−5h,       1386f−13h,       4158f−37h.          (1)

Each next chart is three times the preceding count plus2h, and the
literal replacement retains M and its two physical ports. This changes
the core while retaining the same defect h; it is unit-ready only at h=1.

Incoming [REPAIR23](../surplus_selector_20261113/REPAIR23.md) instead reads
the same actual pair as23h+60f. Its prefix guard and marked repair give
a different physical continuation. Thus an actual SCC-product bank can
serve both observers, with the component word retained for reconstruction.
Equality of either scalar does not identify the other rooted responses.

In our middle chart, choose its already existing predecessor x as active.
The [exact port calculation](ports/PORT_ESCAPE.md) is

    (H,s,q,F)=(1386f−13h,15h,15h,2592f−129h).

The three actual births RNR produce the balanced state (B,u,u,B), where

    u=3978f−157h,       B=14346f−703h.                (2)

The complete entry gate is

    gcd(h,f)=1,  3 does not divide h,
    5039 does not divide3978f−157h.                  (3)

## 2. Doubling a module removes the exceptional residue

**Ordinal-square theorem.** Let D be any actual nonempty tournament with

    h0=H(D), f0=P_D(2), gcd(h0,f0)=1,
    3 does not divide h0.                                      (4)

For every a,b≥0 with a+2b>0, form

    M_(a,b)=D→...→D→TT_a          (2b copies of D).

Its pair is h=h0^(2b), f=f0^(2b)2^a. Substitution in the middle chart
gives an actual strong source at the exact target

    N_(a,b)=1386 f0^(2b)2^a−13 h0^(2b).             (5)

Every such source passes(3), and hence has the physical continuation(2)
into the reusable controller.

Proof. The first two gates follow immediately from(4). If b>0 and5039
divides exactly one of h0,f0, coprimality makes the other a unit. In(2)
one term of u then vanishes modulo5039 and the other does not. This
already excludes the bad residue. In the remaining case, including b=0,
h and f are units modulo5039. The prime5039 is
7 modulo8, and the exact modular certificates are

    2^2519=1,     (157/3978)^2519=2365^2519=−1  (mod5039).

For b=0 the ratio f/h is2^a. For b>0 in this remaining case, h0 and f0
are units and f/h=(f0/h0)^(2b)2^a. In either case it has2519th power1
and therefore cannot equal
2365. Both h and3978 are invertible modulo5039, so the last gate follows.
This is an all-parameter group argument, not a sampled divisibility claim.
The module products are actual ordinal joins and the exterior is the
literal middle chart, completing the graph-level proof.

The actual final root-child pair is primitive as well. With q,F from
the predecessor state, B−u=2(2F−q). Since u is odd, gcd(B,u)=1 implies
gcd(u,q,2F−q)=1, exactly the retained pair's inherited entry content.

This is the precise doubles/squares transfer: ordinal doubling squares
the response ratio in a finite multiplicative group. Lost information is
the internal module geometry; the necessary sidecar is the actual module
word and its pair(h0,f0). The theorem uses no claim that a formal feasible
pair is realized by a tournament. If f0/h0 itself is a quadratic residue,
one may use b copies, without the factor2 in the exponent.

## 3. An explicit family permits every core depth

The independently counted seven-vertex module

    D=(8,5,9,18,71,31,47),   (h0,f0)=(35,676)

has35^2519=1 modulo5039, and676=26². Thus every b, even or odd, is legal:

    N_(a,b)=1386·676^b·2^a−13·35^b,
    a,b≥0,  a+b>0.                                  (6)

The strong source has7b+a+11 vertices and defect35^b at its old ready
port y. Rerooting at x and applying RNR gives7b+a+14 vertices and the
primitive balanced state(2). At b=0 this recovers the middle462 clock;
at b=1 it recovers the earlier H35 family. Unbounded b is the new source
supply. The exponent5039 gate, including the actual port pair, is
independently checked in PORT_ESCAPE.

This family is **not contained in finitely many rays A_i2^k+B_i**, with
fixed positive integers A_i and integer offsets B_i. Fix b and let a
increase. Write C_b=1386·676^b and d_b=13·35^b. If B_i≠−d_b, then

    v2(C_b2^a−d_b−B_i)=v2(d_b+B_i)

for all sufficiently large a. Equality with A_i2^k would force k to be
fixed, allowing at most one such a. Therefore some B_i must equal−d_b
for each b. The infinitely many distinct d_b make a finite atlas impossible.
The same proof applies to(5) when h0>1.

This does not escape every sparsity theorem. For fixed D with h0>1,
both parameters are O(log X) below X, so at most O((log X)²) parameter
pairs occur. A finite union of these families still has density zero.

## 4. Exact target-directed selectors, with honest negative answers

For the explicit family(6), use35^b≤676^b and a≥0 to obtain

    N≥1373·676^b.

Thus only finitely many b satisfy1373·676^b≤N. For each, test whether

    (N+13·35^b)/(1386·676^b)

is a positive integer power of two2^a, and exclude a=b=0. Return every
successful(a,b), its actual module word, and the chart/port/birth recipe.
The test is necessary and sufficient for membership in(6). It does not
decide the entire Hamilton spectrum. `growing_family.py` implements it.

More generally define the entered module language

    E={1386P_M(2)−13H(M) : M nonempty and satisfies(3)}.

For an order-n module, splitting each
Hamilton path at every cut gives f≥(n+1)h, and all vertex bipartitions
give f≥2^n. In particular f≥2h. Therefore at a target N∈E,

    f≤2N/2759,      n≤floor(log2(2N/2759)),
    h≤N/[1386(n+1)−13],      h≡−853N (mod2772).       (7)

The congruence uses even f and13·853≡1 modulo2772. Enumerating actual
tournaments through the stated finite n bound, computing(h,f) and testing
(3), gives a terminating complete membership algorithm for E. An SCC bank
can compress that enumeration while retaining one actual component word.
No efficient implementation of this unrestricted enumerator is claimed.

Unlike the explicit family, E allows all actual module types. Equations(7)
make it an exact target-directed language rather than an unbounded search
without a stopping rule. A rejection is only from E: it is not a missing
tournament count. The next proof obligation is coverage by such entered
languages, or an actual selective conversion between witnesses at a
prescribed target. Merely passing their arithmetic congruences is insufficient.

The incoming [induced-spectrum theorem](../growing_t_returns_20261113/containment/PROOF.md)
shows why old-arc changes belong in that operation language: the first five
405-ray dyadic ready returns cannot be made solely by adding vertices,
even after arbitrary same-count preparation. Our selective881 split and
the direct bridge across the k17 interface hole explicitly leave that
induced-inclusion restriction.
