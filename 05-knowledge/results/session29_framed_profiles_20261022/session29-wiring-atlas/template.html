Why the cut is intrinsic
In a strong tournament, one-outgoing-arc cuts with both sides of size at least two are nested. The size-5 cut therefore recovers the two blocks intrinsically, rather than depending on a drawing.
δ(A) + δ(B) = δ(A∩B) + δ(A∪B) + |A∖B| |B∖A|
δ counts outgoing arcs. At longer lengths, cuts of sizes 5, 10, …, 5(r−1) recover the address chain.
