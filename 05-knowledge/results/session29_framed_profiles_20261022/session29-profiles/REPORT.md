## Loop1: repair the proposed hierarchy

Hypothesis from the supplied inspiration: small motif counts refine scores, and more overlap resolves the remaining information. First hostile: C3->singleton and singleton->C3 have the same D3 (one cyclic, three transitive), but different scores (0,2,2,2) and (1,1,1,3). They are converse, so this particular hostile must not be advertised as a merged-class separation. Root's five-vertex C3 ordinal-sum position examples supply merged D3 hostiles.

PROOF: c3(T)=binom(n,3)-sum_v binom(d+(v),2). Each transitive triple has one unique source, which counts one pair of out-neighbors. Thus adding triangle count within a fixed score class contributes zero information.

PROOF (profile downward identity): for f<=k<=n,

sum_{|U|=k} N_U(T) N_F(U) = binom(n-f,k-f) N_F(T).

Count pairs X subset Y with |X|=f, |Y|=k and T[X]~F. Consequently D_k determines every smaller profile, but not generally the full score sequence.

PROOF (score-moment interface): M_j=sum_v binom(d+(v),j) is determined by D_{j+1}, by counting sources with j selected out-neighbors. If g_d is the number of score-d vertices, then

g_d=sum_{j=d}^{n-1} (-1)^(j-d) binom(j,d) M_j.

This follows by substituting M_j=sum_e binom(e,j)g_e and the binomial theorem. It identifies the missing coordinate: low profiles supply truncated score moments, not the full degree histogram. Keeping scores as an explicit sidecar makes the corrected hierarchy (scores,D_k) monotone in k.

PROOF (overlap budget): let a configuration consist of a fixed finite list of induced motifs, with specified intersection requirements and compatible orientations. If every realization has a union of at most K vertices, its number is sum_{|U|<=K} c_U N_U(T), where c_U is the number of covering configurations inside U. Group realizations by their union. Thus aggregated overlap does not exceed its union-profile order. Global persistent vertex identities or additional markings are different data and must be retained explicitly.

Test: pairs of distinct directed triangles sharing an edge have union4. At n5 masks8 and10 have equal scores and D3, but edge-sharing triangle-pair counts3 and2. So overlap can genuinely refine the lower-order profile, while being exactly computable from D4. Both controls are needed: 'overlap adds nothing' would be false without the fixed union-order qualifier.

All n<=6 iso classes were generated by complete permutation orbits: counts1,1,2,4,12,56. For n=3,4,5,6, numbers of D_{n-1} values are1,3,11,52. Scores have2,4,9,22 values. At n5 D4 has11 values but there are12 classes; the surviving pair is A,B, with different H. At n6 D4 has36 values and D5 has52. These are finite counts, not asymptotic claims.

## Loop2: repair reconstruction and obtain a constructive family

Hypothesis: perhaps retaining the full proper deck plus scores fixes the triangle-level loss. Refuted by A,B above: same scores and D4, H15 versus13. H is invariant under converse, so this counterexample survives merged classification. The problem is genuinely global, not a missing low-order triangle correlation.

Let w in {A,B}^r and T_w=w1 join ... join wr, where all arcs go from earlier to later blocks. Both A,B are strongly connected: a nonstrong tournament on five vertices can have at most one nontrivial SCC; any remaining singleton SCC forces a source or sink somewhere at an extreme, contrary to their minimum score1 and maximum3. Equivalently this follows by direct reachability verification.

1. SCC uniqueness shows T_w~T_v iff w=v. Automorphisms fix each SCC, hence |Aut(T_w)|=3^a, where a=#A.
2. Scores in position i are the core scores plus5(r-i), independent of its letter. Thus all words share the score multiset.
3. For any induced subset of size<=4, its intersections with the blocks have sizes k_i<=4; the selected tournament is the ordered join of those induced block tournaments. Each block has the same profiles through4. Sum over the composition (k_i) to obtain identical D_{<=4} for every word. In fact (D_{<=k},join) has an exact truncated convolution law for every k.
4. Every Hamiltonian path traverses the SCCs in order. Therefore H(T_w)=15^a13^(r-a), producing r+1 distinct values, and sum_w H(T_w)=28^r.
5. Both cores are self-converse (explicit permutation check), so T_w^op~T_reverse(w). Hence merged nodes correspond to binary words modulo reversal; palindromes are exactly the self-converse nodes in this family. The number is (2^r+2^ceil(r/2))/2.
6. A and B differ by one arc and are nonisomorphic, hence d_iso(A,B)=1. Applying repository THM2183's proved equal-factor-order join metric product repeatedly gives d_iso(T_w,T_v)=Hamming(w,v). After converse merging the distance is min(Hamming(w,v),Hamming(w,reverse(v))). This is an exact structured map preserving distance, not just a numerical analogy.

## Generating functions and metagraph mass

Here z records the number of five-vertex blocks, not the number of vertices; substitute z=x^5 for vertex-order GFs. Include the empty word at r=0.

Unmerged class GF =1/(1-2z).
Merged class GF =1/2[1/(1-2z)+(1+2z)/(1-2z^2)].
Unmerged H-weighted GF =1/(1-28z).
Merged H-weighted GF (H counted once per merged node) =1/2[1/(1-28z)+(1+28z)/(1-394z^2)].

Reason: weighted Burnside for reversal. For even r=2m the palindromic H-sum is (15^2+13^2)^m=394^m; for odd r it is28*394^m. In general replace letter weights by p,q to obtain 1/2[1/(1-(p+q)z)+(1+(p+q)z)/(1-(p^2+q^2)z^2)].

Fixed-spine tiling mass for an unmerged class is H/|Aut|: the automorphism action on Hamiltonian paths is free, and path order supplies the spine labels. Thus core masses are5 and13, NOT15 and13. For T_w the mass is5^a13^(r-a). Total mass over unmerged words is18^r. A nonpalindromic merged node has twice this mass, a palindromic node has this mass. Therefore actual total merged-node mass is still18^r, with GF1/(1-18z). The one-representative mass GF, which is a DIFFERENT statistic, is1/2[1/(1-18z)+(1+18z)/(1-194z^2)].

Neither self-converse nor these counts implies that every incident line is blue, nor that the family is closed under fixed-spine complement. Complete metagraph wiring remains open. These formulas provide constraints for a precisely specified subfamily, not A000568 as a whole.

## Root extension: independently audited blue/black incidence budgets

The parent derived a colour refinement, independently checked here by mirror_check.py. On the Hamiltonian-path orbits under Aut(A), reflection fixes1 of5 tiles; for B it fixes3 of13. Choose any anti-isomorphism phi and send path p to phi(reverse(p)); all possible phi give the same map on Aut-orbits. The independent script checks all3 anti-isomorphisms of A and the unique anti-isomorphism of B, not a cherry-picked choice.

A word can have a mirror-fixed tile only if it is palindromic. For its paired SCC positions, choose a tile on the first side and the mirrored partner is forced; in a middle SCC choose a fixed tile. Hence, summing over the whole word family, the blue (symmetric-tile) incidence counts are b_(2m)=18^m and b_(2m+1)=4*18^m, with GF(1+4z)/(1-18z^2). Black incidences are18^r-b_r. These are boundary-inclusive incidence/stub counts, not internal edge counts: the complement partner can leave the family. In particular one may not divide these counts by2 to claim edges contained in the subfamily.

No nonempty word is a point of symmetry in the all-blue sense. For a nonpalindrome all its tiles are asymmetric. For a palindrome of positive length, each paired block contributes squared mass5^2 or13^2 to its total versus5 or13 to its fixed tiles; an unpaired middle contributes5 versus1 or13 versus3. Thus total exceeds fixed count in every case.

Normal and python -O mirror tests pass for all binary words of lengths1 through8, alongside all seed automorphism and anti-isomorphism checks. The formulas and no-all-blue assertion are proved for every r, not inferred from that finite check.
