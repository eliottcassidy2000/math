## Proof review and failure boundaries

1. **Cycle law:** the affine translation is in the image of g^t-1 iff
   it has a fixed point. Outside the image, no proper p-power iterate
   has a fixed point before the whole permutation becomes identity.
   This establishes uniform cycles, rather than assuming semiregularity.
   The geometric-sum valuation2j and the kernel size p^r are in the
   actual ramified ring. No finite-field substitution is made.
2. **Moments:** E|Stab| is a sum over group elements; higher-moment
   convergence uses the nontrivial-event bound, not a false independence
   of group-element fixed sets. The action has order N^(3/2) and index
   at least16N/25. The conclusion is for the named subgroup only.
3. **Border norm:** x and Kx are orthogonal because K is real skew.
   This orthogonality is required for the squared budget; arbitrary
   nonsymmetric matrices do not inherit the argument. The zero-matrix
   case is separate. Interlacing supplies the lower bound.
4. **Full rigidity and primeness:** Gluck addresses Aut(T), not merely
   A_k. Each clone cut retains a nontrivial point stabilizer, so a
   regular subset avoids every forbidden cut. For the actual626 graph,
   the UNIQUE regular-deletion root is checked directly and refinement
   from that intrinsic root becomes discrete. This certifies every
   graph automorphism without assuming affine normality.
5. **Converse:** an even-order tournament admits no vertex fixed by an
   anti-automorphism, by outdegree/indegree parity. Uniqueness of the
   regular card forces such a fixed vertex. The source/sink and clone
   distinctions are retained; small clone children test the failure of
   a broader all-extensions chirality claim.
6. **Metagraph:** the selected legal Hamilton path is retained. The
   matrix error is2 times its skew adjacency, hence norm less than4.
   Re-marking is explicitly permutation conjugation. Other black moves,
   arbitrary relation edits, or path-free reset operations are not given
   zero cost. The distance theorem is conditional on a route existing.
7. **Limits:** prime and rigid are certified for the constructed child,
   not for every later complement. Spectral quasirandomness does not
   itself reconstruct joint ports. Coherent distinguishing cuts across
   ring reductions, all-size integral recovery and A000568 remain open.
8. **Universal extra group:** eta has valuation2k-1 and annihilator
   delta R_k. The difference between two distinct residue fibres is
   a unit, allowing division by its residue; same-fibre differences
   do not change. These are the two exhaustive cases preserving arcs.
   Affine intersection has dimension2, not1: both constant and linear
   fibre laws are already affine. The larger group has order p^(3k+p-2).
9. **Enlarged cycle sum:** constancy on nonidentity fibre-group cosets
   follows from the p-power iterate formula and fixed-point counts.
   The nonzero residue-translation branch needs k>=2. Its k=1 hostile
   is retained rather than silently included in the recurrence.
10. **Full625 completeness:** stable cells give upper bounds on orbits;
    the clock supplies the matching25-element orbit of vertex1 after
