agree.** This is a complete coefficient-box result, with no speed-height
census. The separate zonotope lemma is needed to extend it to coefficients
larger than eighteen.

For every primitive sorted coefficient magnitude pattern `p=(p_1,p_2,p_3)`
satisfying

```text

at least two p_i are nonzero,
gcd(p_1,p_2,p_3)=1,
```


Let `v` be a signed permutation of a pattern, with both signs occurring, and
put `R=3/14`. For primitive positive distinct odd ternary-unit speeds `w`
satisfying `v.w=0`, every complete raw carrier lies on a defect line with
integer defect in

```text

   if every v_i is a unit modulo three;
```

```text

```

For `P_delta(v)={e in [-R,R]^3: v.e=delta}`, define its projected scalar width

```text
T_delta(v,w)=max_e (w cross e)_i/v_i
              -min_e (w cross e)_i/v_i,
```

choosing any nonzero `v_i`. It is coordinate independent because differences
of image points are multiples of `v`. The inherited open-interval residue
count proves

```text

```

Define



The map retains every allowed defect and the complete affine scalar interval.

## Why a finite rational calculation proves (1)

For fixed `v,delta`, the width `T_delta(v,w)` is a maximum of finitely many
linear functions of `w`, hence convex and positively homogeneous. A convex
function on `W_v` has a maximum at a vertex, by expressing each point as a
convex combination of vertices. Boundary points with equal or zero speeds are
admissible for this upper bound.

The main implementation eliminates one nonzero coefficient coordinate from
the plane equation, then clips an exact rational square by the two remaining
coordinate inequalities. This produces the vertices of both the error slice
and the normalized speed polygon. It runs every isolated sign and every
coordinate permutation; actual-zero coefficients are handled by a simultaneous
cyclic coordinate rotation to a nonzero elimination pivot. A second nonzero
coordinate supplies the projected scalar, so no division by zero occurs.

The complete defect range is recomputed from each pattern's actual norm. It
reaches `|delta|=11` in this box; the smaller norm-twenty defect range is not
reused. This is essential for completeness.

Every full-support result is compared with the independently organized
cube-edge vertex compiler in the frozen norm-twenty producer, whose compiler
function accepts arbitrary coefficient patterns. Every support-two result is
also checked by the exact rectangle formula. For `v=(p,-q,0)`, `0<p<q`, this is

```text
A_(p,q)=[2Rp |D| +sum_(delta in D) min(2pR,R(p+q)-|delta|)]/(3pq).
```

The coefficient universe itself is generated by sorted combinations and
independently by a full ordered Cartesian cube followed by normalization.
The two universes agree exactly.
