# Attributed Gold corollaries from known very-good-pair theorems

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: direct synthesis of cited structural results with the local good-pair Gold theorem; no novelty claim.

Every finite non-totally-ordered poset whose cover graph is a forest has weak Gold.

Indeed, Zaguia's Theorem 6 states that such a poset has a very good pair. The [good-pair Gold synthesis](good_pair_gold_synthesis_20261019.md) proves that every poset with a very good pair has weak Gold. Composing these two statements proves the corollary. This uses the synthesis's convention allowing determined or repeated second comparisons, and imposes no thinness bound. [Primary source: Zaguia, Theorem 6, printed page 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.00809v3).

The same primary paper, immediately after Definition 5 on printed page 3, records very-good-pair existence for width-two posets and for non-total semiorders and N-free orders. Those stated classes likewise inherit weak Gold from the same synthesis. These are attributed corollaries, not new structural proofs or claims that the cited paper itself states Gold. Total orders are excluded throughout.

These corollaries are independent of the bounded K4 deformation experiment. The previously verified structural statements, rather than an enumeration of small examples, supply their all-size hypotheses.
