# A finite synchronization geometry for each acyclic length chart

**PROVED using a CITED classical theorem; reconstruction after environment rollback.**
Chan–Pak and an effective practical all-chart compiler remain unestablished.

## 1. Physical input and provenance

Fix a marked tournament, a selection O and r unselected ports inducing an
acyclic tournament. Inflate port i to a nonempty transitive module of length
m_i. The inherited walk-content kernel gives the exact signed defect

    F(m)=delta(m)−1 = sum_j c_j product_i b_ji^(m_i),               (1)

with rational c_j and positive integer b_ji; collect identical base vectors
and discard zero coefficients. Include−1 as an all-one base term. This extends
algebraically to m∈Z^r, without asserting negative-length physical objects.

The finite expansion follows because between repeated free-module runs a
compressed path must consume a fixed vertex. Acyclicity bounds each run count
uniformly. Each lift factor j!S(m,j) is a finite sum of positive-base powers.
Cyclic free ports may alternate without a uniform run bound, so no such
finite compiler is asserted there. The kernel is inherited from
[THM-3121](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3121-path-cover-walk-content-substitution-kernel.md)
and [Session288 stable tensor](../../session288_twin_lengths_20261112/stable/PROOF.md).
Session288 already gives an effective one-clock theorem and tests arbitrary
specified rays. The new consequence treats the full multivariable locus.

## 2. Torus theorem transferred exactly

**CITED:** Laurent's1984 theorem implies that the intersection of an algebraic
subvariety of a characteristic-zero torus with a finitely generated subgroup
is a finite union of intersections with torus cosets lying in the variety.
Source inspected: J.-H. Evertse, [*Points on Subvarieties of Tori*](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~evertse/00-torus.pdf),
Theorem B, pp.1–2; reference24: M. Laurent, *Equations diophantiennes
exponentielles*, Invent. Math.78(1984),299–327. We use this classical theorem,
not claim a new proof of it. It is a qualitative structure statement here.

Define Phi:Z^r→(Q_>0)^s by Phi(m)_j=product_i b_ji^(m_i).
Its image Gamma is generated by its r column vectors. The equation F=0 is
the inverse image of X={x:sum_j c_j x_j=0} in the torus. Apply Laurent to
its finitely many irreducible components if needed. The nonempty preimage
of each coset is a translate a+L of an integer subgroup. Since the cosets
lie in X, this proves equality, not merely containment:

    {m∈Z^r:F(m)=0} = finite union_t(a_t+L_t).                       (2)

The identically-zero polynomial gives the whole lattice. The map preserves
exact unit-defect equality, not merely congruence or a normalized ratio.
It does not supply effective exceptional anchors, new physical skeletons,
or arbitrary odd coefficient values.

## 3. An elementary finite direction menu and checkable certificates

Set chi_j(v)=product_i b_ji^(v_i). For an integer lattice L with basis
v_1,...,v_d, group j by equal signatures(chi_j(v_1),...,chi_j(v_d)). Then

    F(a+L)≡0 iff sum_(j in B)c_j chi_j(a)=0 for every signature class B. (3)

Distinct characters of an abelian group are linearly independent: translate a
minimal relation by a group element distinguishing two characters and subtract
a scalar multiple of the original, producing a shorter relation. Applying
this to F(a+v) proves(3). `verify.py` checks supplied cosets with exact fractions.

For every partition Pi of the term indices, define L_Pi by chi_j(v)=chi_k(v)
within each part. Factoring the integer bases turns this into integer linear
constraints for each prime p:

    sum_i (v_p(b_ji)−v_p(b_ki))v_i=0.                              (4)

Hence L_Pi is a saturated lattice, explicitly computable. For any zero coset
a+L in(2), use its signature partition Pi. Equation(3) proves the larger
coset a+L_Pi also vanishes. Consequently the whole locus is a finite union
of translates of a finite computable direction menu. There are at most
Bell(s) partitions before identifying equal lattices. This is neither an
assertion of efficiency nor an algorithm for all exceptional translates.

Full rank means all clocks are free; intermediate rank allows synchronized
motion; rank0 is an isolated length choice. These ranks may coexist in one
chart, so one isolated point does not make its entire chart locked.

## 4. Rational size growth within a fixed chart

Use the saturated lattices from§3 in(2) and impose m_i≥1. Each intersection
is the integer-point set of a rational affine polyhedron; it is Presburger-
definable and semilinear. Finite union preserves this property. Thus its
multivariate length generating series is rational. Equivalently use rational
polyhedral generating functions and finite inclusion–exclusion.

Under total-size grading n=n_fixed+sum m_i, each coefficient is finite, and
this sequence is eventually quasipolynomial. The theorem does not give its
period or threshold. It counts parameter vectors, not automatically distinct
tournaments. If the skeleton is the canonical marked twin quotient (no
unselected twins), expansion is injective up to its finite automorphism group.
For every group element the fixed vectors add linear equal-length equations;
Burnside therefore gives the same rationality for marked isomorphism types.
No blind sum over overlapping/noncanonical charts is licensed.

A fixed-chart size series is NOT a Hamiltonian-count spectrum series. New
cores can keep adding new charts without bound; no finite global atlas is
proved. This distinguishes a concise recursion from uniform target coverage.

## 5. Positive and hostile examples

1. **Inherited physical gate:** Session288 gives F(a,b)=2^a−2^b.
Its exact positive locus is a=b. With four fixed vertices its parameter
series is z^6/(1−z²), not a free quadrant.
2. **Formal, not asserted physical:** F=(2^a−2)(3^b−3) vanishes exactly
on a=1 or b=1. Both singleton directions at(1,1) work, but their joint
motion fails at(2,2). Its series is2z²/(1−z)−z².
3. **Model carry, not asserted physical:** 2^a+2^b=2^c for positive
integers forces a=b,c=a+1. If a<b, division by2^a yields an odd number
larger than1 on the left and a power of2 on the right. Thus the locus is
(1,1,2)+t(1,1,1),t≥0, with series z^4/(1−z³). The nonzero anchor cannot
be recovered from its direction alone.

## 6. Effective and global boundaries

An **unimplemented research goal** is a practical complete affine-lattice
compiler including offsets, using bases and coefficient bit sizes as well
as support. Support cardinality alone cannot bound offsets:2^m−2^N=0 has
two terms and an arbitrarily large root. We do not claim that effectiveness
of S-unit techniques is globally open; our cited-theorem application supplies
no implementation of all offsets.

**OPEN seed-descent program:** find seed-preserving changes of canonical chart
with a well-founded target-aware rank, covering all required odd coefficient
rays and their absolute low heads. Neither(2) nor a free-clock tensor proves
this. A finite chart family can have a beautiful complete grammar and still
miss infinitely many scalar targets.
