## Inheritance and live board

Anchor: the proposed Collatz hexagonal representation, with Hadwiger--Nelson
as a test of what the representation preserves. Niche: native shared-tail
response kernels. Wildcard: prime multipliers and residue precision. LRC(14)
remains the repository's open anchor; no bound for it is promoted here.

The closest proved mechanism is **SF5** in
[source floors that survive refinement](../collatz_source_refinement_floor_20261005.md):
a decidable, source-labelled binary inverse family with bounded native clocks.
The canonical hostile is the positive5n+1 cycle13->33->83->13, augmented
by the actual tournament81/27 pair. The corrected near miss is turning a
proper basin or a measure-one ambient assertion into universal integer
coverage. The least-used relevant sidecars are the ORIGINAL integer
embedding inside a2-adic conjugacy, and the shared EVEN tails hidden when
one passes to an odd-only tree.

Six live concepts, revisited after the finite growth count and then after
the native-LCA correction:

1. **Sources:** root5 witnesses are integers with replayable paths; no
   anonymous binary-tree node is allowed to stand for every integer.
2. **Operations/clocks:** ordinary C, shortcut T, odd U, and macro depth
   differ. Their native edge expansions overlap before the odd LCA.
3. **Geometry:** polynomial ball growth is a capacity constraint; macro
   drawings may shrink, or use hyperbolic geometry, without being unit grids.
4. **Joint ports:** J4 derives native meetings from LCA and exponents;
   J5 counts the shared tail once. Independent marginals lose this response.
5. **Holonomy/phase:** palette compatibility is a fixed-point equation;
   arithmetic branch precision grows with the requested future horizon.
6. **Weighted observation:** a tree can be contractible while its native
   path Gram rank doubles at every odd depth. Geometry/topology alone
   does not supply uniform target-specific evaluation bounds.

The positive change in direction was from a global drawing obstruction to
an exact native expansion and recurrence. No further global compression
is inferred merely because the recurrence is short.

## Three principles with explicit maps

**First: compression is relative to the future questions.** Let R be a
representation of states under specified legal operations. An exact
deterministic update exists on R precisely when equality of R states is
preserved by every legal update (with legality itself observable), and
every requested output is constant on those fibres. Necessity follows
by applying an update/output to equal representations; sufficiency defines
the quotient update/output by any representative. This elementary criterion
is already a recurring repository mechanism, not a new universal theory.

Here the exact census is **nine false accepts among24 parity-accepted
transports**. The surviving15 fix a
frame. In arithmetic, equal roots mod3^k can have different next roots mod3^k.
In supplied odd trees, by contrast, LCA plus exponent-labelled edges does
derive pair meetings without storing a separate quadratic table.

**Second: identify overlaps before summing responses.** For nested halving
tails, union uses max(a,b), intersection uses min(a,b), and J5 is an exact
source-dependent generating recurrence. Its Gram determinant is a product
of positive stage increments. This is a positive theorem, not only a
warning about loss. If attachments do not have nested overlaps, max/min
does not replace full incidence data; the actual81/27 control remains one
such test of information loss in tournament summaries.

**Third: quantify the capacity of the proposed carrier.** Exponentially
many distinguishable integer sources cannot fit in quadratically many
lattice positions with bounded edge stretch and bounded fibres. A finite
tree's trivial homotopy type likewise does not bound its source path Gram
rank. These are different inequalities involving different observables;
their common use is to state exactly which resource must grow.

## Where this can advance the open targets

**Collatz:** J1--J6 give exact reusable certificates within an inverse family.
They do not prove universal entry. A new return controller should first
add genuine sources outside SF5, retaining the two native meeting clocks,
and prove a well-founded dependency order. Already7 converges while SF5's
strictly monotone recognizer rejects it. Covering even this known source
is a useful first extension test; density alone cannot replace coverage.

**Hadwiger--Nelson:** a candidate six-palette construction can use the
complete fixed-frame test, then certify every required Euclidean unit edge.
Functional arrows alone are a three-colourable skeleton. A finite quotient
with high chromatic number or a folded drawing is insufficient without
metric realizability and ownership of all distinct points. The externally
cited six-colour lower bound remains CITED, not locally built or improved.

**Tournament composition:** look for a restricted attachment language whose
overlap sets are genuinely nested, so the max/min recurrence is transferable.
Supply vertices, pairwise observable, ties/gauge and an actual source-preserving
map before calling an arithmetic network a tournament. Full native complement
and arbitrary re-marking still require their own closure proof. The current
weighted stability frontier remains the inherited3..9 and n>=42;10..41 is OPEN.

**LRC(14):** preserve the actual runner phases and owner/clock data before
constructing any constraint graph. The star Gram control and J7 both reject
topological simplicity as the missing weighted estimate. A useful transfer
would identify a specific native LRC response operator and bound its selected
source-evaluation norm; no such bound is supplied by this packet.

The protocol therefore ends with exact new calculations and explicit proof
obligations, rather than treating shared trees, sevens, or symmetries as a
proof of one of the major conjectures.
