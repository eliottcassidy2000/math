# Session27 exploration: preserve the operation, then test the invariant

This is a record of actual hypothesis/testing loops, not a claim that every numerical analogy became a theorem. The anchor was repunit rarity and prior-work recovery; the niche was modular/lattice structure; the wildcard was the four attached papers and the observability mechanisms they suggested.

## Six live concepts

1. Object versus observable: H, degree vectors, theta series, and restricted theta are different maps.
2. Composition and measure: ordinary classes, labelled objects, automorphism weights, and strong cores require different counts.
3. Context and response: an incomplete scalar can become faithful when evaluated on controlled extensions.
4. Dependency beyond pairs: linear spans and ordered path covers retain data absent from a graph or a marginal.
5. Integral normalization: rank, signature, root count, modular weight, vacuum shift and Lie dimension are not interchangeable labels.
6. Finite versus uniform evidence: a complete finite audit can be a theorem in its stated universe without certifying an infinite geometric claim.

## Loop1: repair the meaning of repunit rarity

The archive initially mixed repunit vertex orders, trace values, Hamiltonian counts and Collatz digits. The search recovered older March trace/necklace work, the first H63 realizations, later family constructions, and the corrected October empirical study. The report preserves this lineage rather than presenting the latest thread as the beginning.

The first explanatory guess, small feedback-arc complexity, had already failed a prior audit as a universal explanation. The robust replacement is strong-component factorization. It extends to automorphism-weighted counts because automorphisms fix the ordered condensation. This proves comparable polynomial degrees for labelled and unlabelled fixed-H populations.

The hostile255 versus257 prevents turning three examples into a universal repunit law. The conclusion remains measure-specific:31,63,127 are relatively rare compared with33,65,129 as vertex order grows. Strong-class rarity as H grows is a different question.

## Loop2: convert the24/248/1728 cluster into typed maps

The genuine identities are theta/eta character identities and root decompositions. E8 rank8 gives240 roots and8 oscillator states, producing248 at the first character coefficient. The Leech lattice character has constant term24 and is j-720, not the normalized monstrous j-744. Ordinary Golay Construction A has48 roots and is not rootless Leech.

This did not prove a tournament modular form. The native fixed-H series is rational, giving a precise obstruction to its being a positive-weight holomorphic level-one modular form. That obstruction is much narrower than old prose saying no modular structure could ever exist.

The Heegner list and self-dual E8 root datum are legitimate imported structures. Their names do not identify a period, a tournament-to-elliptic-curve map, or an explanation of the global H gaps. The {7,21}->{8,24} proposal still owes a map: the familiar QR-code length rule q->q+1 instead pairs7 with8 and23 with24.

## Loop3: audit the paper's observation space, not only its coefficients

The Niemeier paper suggested that a suitable Fourier coefficient should distinguish lattices on a restricted locus. An independent primary-source check showed the opposite on the hyperelliptic locus: all five equal-root-count pairs coincide in every genus. A concrete rank-four coefficient11520 then refuted its proposed vanishing hypothesis directly.

The positive survivor is important: genus5 Jacobian restriction distinguishes the pairs by a known theorem. The failure was a transfer from ambient Fourier data to a restricted geometric evaluation, not absence of all useful separation.

The96-dimensional papers exhibited another distinct failure: a formal theta candidate and a genuine Gram matrix do not establish a minimum. Exact arithmetic certified the supplied old lattice's evenness, determinant and positive definiteness. Bounded short-vector tests did not certify extremality. The earlier wrong q6 coefficient and symmetric-versus-symplectic counting step were separately identified.

## Loop4: retain dependence and obtain an exact gluing criterion

Correcting the orthogonal incidence probability did not by itself construct a lattice. The next question was which finite forbidden-set data would suffice. Grouping inclusion-exclusion by the full span poset gives an exact avoidance formula and a positive-probability realizability test.

A cheap hostile made the missing coordinate visible: two forbidden triples have the same K3 orthogonality graph but span dimensions2 and3, and yield60 versus61 avoiding maximal spaces out of80. Pairwise compatibility is insufficient. The span-sensitive formula restores exactly that lost information, but the actual short-vector forbidden set of the96-dimensional candidate remains an unfulfilled input.

## Loop5: move from a scalar to directed continuation experiments

The first tentative one-vertex insertion polynomial failed on a two-vertex core. Deleting an inserted vertex can leave a missing core arc; an insertion-only calculation omits bypasses. Ordered spanning two-path covers repair the formula.

The corrected polynomial exposes a converse current. One probe observes a divergence vector and can miss a nonzero circulation. All vertex-transitive cores are converse-blind to one probe. The triangle supplies an exact witness, not a picture.

Two ordered probes recover the entire two-path-cover matrix and both endpoint vectors. A tempting shortcut saying their antisymmetric response equals a signed twice-deleted-core H was immediately refuted at order two. The correct two-port formula includes endpoint potentials. Zero-mask calibration removes those potentials and makes full two-cover tomography possible.

Finite tests through six vertices support universal two-port reconstruction, but no all-order proof was found. The next loop deliberately changed the probe family rather than extrapolating those tests.

## Loop6: isolate an arc by cancellation, then discard almost all numerical precision

The key construction places every vertex exceptu along a marked alternating core/base path. A signed sum over its cross-edge orientations cancels every Hamiltonian path missing one varied edge. Only four endpoint placements survive. Averaging the final endpoint edge yields an exact±2 contrast and proves full response faithfulness using k-1 base vertices.

The next refinement fixes that endpoint edge instead of averaging it. The contrast becomes0 or2, halves the query count, and works modulo every integerM>=3. The usual parity bit is useless because every tournament has odd H.

A final precision reduction keeps just the second bit of H. Its XOR across the query family recovers the arc. The odd-cycle collection formula supplies an independent proof: all cycles omitting a varied edge cancel; the even direct closure is excluded; the only odd survivor closes throughu using the target arc.

Thus context, not a large scalar range, restores information. This is a proved finite representation of every labelled tournament and its isomorphism/converse quotient. It is not a proof that two ports always suffice, or that evaluating exponentially many H queries is fast.

## Loop7: test the actual score-lattice map at its best shell

A vague warning that scores lose information became an exact hostile. Reversing one directed triangle in Paley7 preserves regularity but changes H. The same one-vertex attachment gives two eight-vertex tournaments at the identical E8 root, not merely the same shell, with H657 and643.

The first score-to-H failure was then located structurally at order five. Scores determine triangles, and through order four triangles determine H. At order five the pair has the same four triangles but two versus one five-cycles. This identifies the lost cycle statistic.

An incoming concurrent S52 proposal supplied a second legitimate score map: the25-vertex lift(s;70) has Lorentzian norm-2c3. We checked its algebra and coordinate membership independently, preserving the concurrent attribution. Its transitive null vector is the classical cannonball/Weyl vector. Nontransitive scores are timelike and cannot use the same null quotient. Padding the E8 hostile also shows the Lorentzian point is not faithful for H.

## Loop8: recover1729's real recurrence before inventing a new one

The broader historical pass recovered Ramanujan's reversible recurrence and the associated arithmetic-geometry literature. The negative-index identity produces1729 exactly. A naive tensor-cube proof uses27 dimensions, while reciprocal eigenvalue structure uses only seven modes.

Primary-source checking was decisive: Hirschhorn already published that seven-check argument. The session therefore treats it as recovered mathematics, not a new discovery. The written uniform theorem explains the2d+1 annihilator over arbitrary commutative rings, including degenerate roots, while respecting its sharpness boundary.

The rank43 ADE collision in another paper has a related but distinct finite-difference mechanism. Its rank-mass polynomial has a fifth-order zero at1 and the remaining D-family defects cancel. This proves the collision; a separate complete finite census establishes its minimal rank. The shared number43 with the Heegner list supplies no additional implication.

## Terminology and what it commits us to

- “Converse current” means the explicit antisymmetric ordered-cover matrix and its endpoint-adjusted divergence, not a physical electrical network.
- “Context-faithful second bit” means the finite XOR experiment proved above; the bare bit alone does not classify anything.
- “Spectrum-indecomposable” describes an attained H-value that cannot split into two attained nontrivial strong H-values. It is not ordinary primality.
- “Span-sensitive avoidance” means the exact Möbius formula retaining linear dependence, not a graph-only clique count.

The productive common principle is to specify the experiment and the lost coordinate. It does not establish that these subjects are isomorphic, that all mathematics is a tournament, or that a common small integer is a universal organizing cause.
