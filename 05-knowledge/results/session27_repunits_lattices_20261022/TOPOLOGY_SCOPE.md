# Smale and the information lost before topology begins

Status: PROVED elementary obstruction; CITED topology; user's intended name remains unconfirmed.

The phrase “Smale h curve order theorem” is provisionally interpreted as Smale's h-cobordism theorem. No claim is made that this is certainly the intended theorem. The question was sent to the user without blocking other research.

A simply connected smooth h-cobordism of dimension at least six is diffeomorphic to a product. Its hypotheses concern a manifold cobordism and homotopy-equivalent boundary inclusions, not just equal counts or homology. Smale's original high-dimensional Poincare work uses handle and Morse-function structure; its Theorem A concludes homeomorphism to a sphere for smooth homotopy spheres of dimension at least five, not equivalence of all smooth structures. Source: Stephen Smale, *Generalized Poincare's Conjecture in Dimensions Greater Than Four*, Annals of Mathematics 74 (1961), 391–406, [primary paper](https://math.uchicago.edu/~shmuel/tom-readings/Smale,%20PC.pdf).

## A minimal obstruction to an unmarked topological transfer

Let U send a tournament to the geometric realization of its underlying undirected graph. For every tournament on n vertices, U(T) is the same complete graph K_n. Thus every invariant factoring through U is constant at fixed n.

At n=3, the transitive tournament has exactly one directed Hamiltonian path, whereas the directed triangle has exactly three. Both underlying realizations are the same circle-shaped one-dimensional simplicial complex K_3. Therefore H(T) does not factor through U, nor through any ordinary homotopy invariant of U(T).

This is a proof about that specific forgetful map. It does not exclude a directed topological construction, an orientation-marked complex, a higher-dimensional realization, or a future geometric bridge. Such a bridge must retain a sidecar that distinguishes the two orientations. A tournament graph is not automatically a high-dimensional manifold or an h-cobordism.

The useful methodological comparison is narrower: algebraic cancellation can be necessary for a geometric cancellation without furnishing the geometric witnesses. In this session the Niemeier restriction audit supplies a concrete mathematical warning: ambient Fourier coefficients cannot be selected term by term after restricting to a geometric locus. The tournament attachment-response lane similarly retains entry and exit ports that are lost by the scalar H. These are distinct examples of a lost-coordinate problem, not consequences of Smale's theorem.

## Cheap independent control

For the transitive arcs 0->1, 0->2, 1->2 the sole Hamiltonian word is (0,1,2). For arcs 0->1, 1->2, 2->0 the three words are (0,1,2), (1,2,0), (2,0,1). The underlying unordered edge set is identical. This checks the claimed minimal hostile without any numerical approximation.
