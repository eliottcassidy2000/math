## Shared measurements in the ETH manuscript

The [OpenAI source at7ef5c0c](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/7ef5c0cc5ca4b0e072b7a050fca09ab24bc6fa3a/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/main_full_version.tex),
especially sections2.3–2.5 and3, replaces clause inputs by parameterized
linear forms. At selected parameter values, cross-group dependence
factors through shared bounded integer measurements. Fixing those
measurements makes the weighted sum factor; interpolation recovers the
original count. The paper claims deterministic subexponential fixed-width
CNF counting with polynomial space. Its full construction is not audited
here. The exact local lesson is to condition on **joint** summaries, with
their ranges and coefficient bit lengths charged, before factorization.

That principle has a direct elementary instance in our P3 resolvent and
minimal response theorem: retained pairings support exactly the chosen
future determinant tests. It does not make other future observables free.
In particular the repeated singleton returns of a fixed Collatz source
cannot be replaced by independent samples of a summary distribution.

If the external counting claim is accepted, it refutes ETH but does not
imply P=NP. A polynomial reduction that enlarges n to n^c need not retain
a subexponential bound in the original n. Nor can any algorithm list all
unlabelled tournament classes faster than the output size. Scalar
A000568 counts already have the repository's exact partition and operator
recurrences; importing a generic SAT encoding is not automatically faster.

## Separating a shared tensor leg

The [matrix-multiplication manuscript atfd4aeeb](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Matrix-Multiplication-Nine-Fourths-October-2-2026/build/paper.tex)
starts with blocks independent on two tensor legs but sharing the third.
Its finite construction uses Fourier projection and a squared mismatch
weight to separate the remaining labels, retaining an auxiliary dot
product. The paper then uses tensor characters, interpolation and
polynomial-multiplication growth inequalities toward exponent9/4.
The site reports verification; no full proof build was attempted here.

The relevant research obligation is concrete: separating one shared port
can introduce an auxiliary factor, and its cost must survive composition.
The tournament control has identical cyclic triples but automorphism
orders81 and27 and binary attachment-orbit counts176 and512. Its old
checker was replayed; it still defeats a summary that loses the group
action. Shared labels cannot be silently replaced by a direct sum.

## Exact Fourier controllers retain live resources

The [pinned DFT submission0c57432](https://github.com/bigospeedrun/verifier/blob/0c574321e757332ca225f7696c31d7617dfdfdf6/submissions/dft/halitziyakartal-halitziyav4-logception/Submission.lean)
exports an all-length exact-arithmetic DFT statement. Its header credits
Sussman's engine and the integer-multiplication constructions; the changed
controller retains entrances, exits, scalar frames, fresh helper jobs and
their residual ranks, then applies a strict tensor power. Those details
are part of the cost proof, not optional annotations. The site reports
Lean verification; this session read the exported source but did not
rebuild its import graph or audit axioms. Exact complex arithmetic here
must not be confused with bit complexity for integer multiplication.

Our current transfer is a testable design rule: a re-marking or queue
restart must transport live state and pay its actual resource cost. The
new parser potential pays8/5 at reset; the sharper incoming potentials
pay3/2 or4. These are different valid potentials, not interchangeable
physical prices. None permits a free restart.

## Conditional Subset Sum and moving a boundary

[Bills's pinned refinementbf1f50f](https://github.com/yannouli/subset-sum-conditional-refinement/blob/bf1f50f066f2c71a53293791f693f505a67a1540/paper.tex)
explicitly retains unverified inherited algorithmic and continuous-moment
hypotheses. Its local improvement moves a certificate boundary because
the old lower-bound row has multiplier exactly zero. The affected
integrals change by explicit constant factors; scalar verification alone
does not verify the inherited algorithm. This is a useful example of
deleting an unused constraint **after inspecting the certificate**.

For our Selmer bridge, a bound on a formal group model is similarly
conditional until an arithmetic source, local conditions and compatible
maps are supplied. For the Collatz lane, exact first-return moments do
not pay the pointwise restart debt. The cheap decisive test for every
proposed deletion is to reinstate the lost operation and ask whether
its certificate still applies.

## A concrete algorithm improvement from this session

The bounded two-port determinant distribution is a convolution of product
multiplicities. [P5](TWO_PORTS.md) implements it by a single integer
polynomial square, with a rigorously sufficient carry-free base, followed
by Mobius inversion for primitive lattice content. It counts the full
L=50 universe of104,060,401 cuts and agrees with direct small-box
enumeration. No unverified asymptotic manuscript is needed for this
improvement. If all cuts themselves are requested, the output cost
returns; the histogram intentionally forgets their labels and clocks.

The next algorithmic experiments should compare **the same output**:
bounded paired-group histograms, clock-invariant histograms, or literal
attachment representatives. Counting, finding one witness, and emitting
every isomorphism class have different output obligations.
