  couplings, legal orbit sums recover every invariant Gram coordinate.
  Type(1,3) has exactly two coordinates; type(1,3,1,3) has ten. An explicit
  infinite unipotent clock loses a coordinate under every future query.
  This does not cover cross-block-only or native sign-cut operations.
- [Two port groups and recurrences](TWO_PORTS.md): fixed dJ,eJ generate
  precisely H_s+H_(s n) for s|gcd(d,e), n>=1. In particular every H_n
  is attainable with arbitrary integer weights. Full joint-port Gram
  data propagate by `(I+GE)^-1 G`; lattice and clock sidecars remain.
- [Minimum rational response carrier](MINIMAL_RATIONAL_PORTS.md):
  determinant plus the joint port Gram matrix is exactly the future

- A packed polynomial square counts 104,060,401 weighted cuts at L=50

- [Ternary parser proof](TERNARY_TERMINATION.md): n(8/5)^Q contracts
