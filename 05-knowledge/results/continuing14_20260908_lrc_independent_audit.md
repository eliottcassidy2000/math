## 2. Independent analytic check of the three consumers

### Marginal attainment and strict chambers

Write d_i = gcd(t,u_i). Since 7 divides t and d_i is prime to seven,
7d_i divides t. Each individual open danger set on a translated t-grid has
cardinality at most t/7. If seven dangers cover all t points, equality in
the union bound forces both attainment of every marginal and pairwise
disjointness of all seven sets. This is an exact equality argument; it
does not assume a generic phase.

On a quotient n-grid, an individual p-danger set with a = gcd(n,p) reduces
to an n/a-grid with multiplicity a. Its open interval has integer length
n/(7a). Off its endpoint phases the count is n/7; at an endpoint it loses
one point on the reduced grid, hence a points on the original quotient
grid. Such a phase is impossible in a full unsafe zero-budget cover.

Every boundary of a component of a pair intersection is an individual
danger boundary. Consequently a hypothetical unsafe phase lies in an open
chamber of the pair-intersection count, and a positive minimum over these
chambers excludes that pair. Some other inadmissible individual endpoints
may lie inside a chamber; retaining them can only weaken this test. The
argument therefore pays the endpoint restriction without claiming positive
intersection at every strict wall or replacing open dangers by closed ones.

### Quotient typing and full zero cuts

For an actual strict edge with speeds Dp,Dq and gcd(p,q)=1, let
e = gcd(t,D). Then

    e = gcd(d_i,d_j),  n = t/e,
    d_i = e*gcd(n,p),  d_j = e*gcd(n,q).

Multiplication by D takes the original t-grid to an n-grid with multiplicity
e. This proves the native quotient and margin roles used by both banks.
The audit directly derives all t/gcd(a,b) from the full allowed alphabets:
their set is exactly the same 1,085 declared clocks. Thus no quotient clock
is supplied by analogy, omitted, or silently replaced by a nearby clock.

For a seven-position word, permit an edge when some compatible strict ratio
has zero ordinary minimum, or respectively zero chamber minimum. Under an
unsafe cover every actual strict edge belongs to both possible graphs.
The actual strict graph is connected by the entry hypothesis. A disconnected
possible graph therefore contradicts an unsafe cover. This uses an entire
cut, including cuts without an isolated vertex. It neither forces an
arbitrary possible edge to be actual nor presumes compatible choices on
different edges can be realized simultaneously.

### Distinct-speed slots

Suppose a margin class a has m_a positions and no internal chamber-zero
edge. Every actual a-speed must then have a neighbor outside this class in
the actual connected graph. For each fixed neighbor speed v of margin b,
the complete oriented bank gives at most R_ab possible a-speed values rv.
Choose one external neighbor for each a vertex. The assignment from a
distinct a-speed to its chosen (neighbor, ratio) slot is injective: equal
slots produce equal speeds. Therefore

    m_a <= sum_(b != a) m_b R_ab.

The right side may overcount coincidences between slots with different
neighbors. That weakens the bound and is safe. There is no use of
simultaneous attainability of the full bank. Distinct actual speeds and
absence of internal edges are load-bearing hypotheses.

Oriented ratios, rather than phases, are the counted objects. A coprime
p<q ratio with equal margins contributes p/q and q/p, two different speed
ratios. With unequal margin roles, exactly one orientation has the requested
role; its reciprocal belongs to the opposite role. The independent code
retains the actual fraction sets and recovers every reported count from
those sets. This rules out counting multiple phase witnesses as extra
slots or stopping after the first usable ratio.

## 5. Divisibility transport: full sets, not only counts

Let t divide T and assume the full allowed divisor alphabet is unchanged.
Fix margins a,b and e=gcd(a,b). A ratio compatible at T remains compatible
at t: each reduced margin divides t/e, so its gcd with the smaller quotient
retains that margin. A translated T/e-grid is a disjoint union of T/t
translated t/e-grids. A zero intersection on the larger grid gives a zero
intersection on each subgrid. If a subgrid phase were a pair endpoint
phase, the larger grid would also contain that endpoint; hence an open
larger-grid phase gives open subgrid phases.

This proves inclusion of the **entire oriented ordinary ratio set** and of
the **entire oriented chamber ratio set** at T inside the corresponding
sets at t. Connectivity and the slot upper bounds can only weaken under
this refinement, while the full profile-word domain remains the same.
All three exclusions thus transport upward under these paid hypotheses.

The referee explicitly checks both actual fraction-set inclusions for
every ordered allowed margin pair across all **1,055** lawful refinement
pairs in the declared universe. Count monotonicity alone would not verify
this stronger transport claim. Mere numerical growth in t, a changed
alphabet, or reusing a bank at an unproved quotient is outside its scope.
