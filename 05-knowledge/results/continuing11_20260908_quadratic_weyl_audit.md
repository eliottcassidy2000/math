## 2. Both paid hypotheses, all components and every affine pole

Put u=x-1, w=1+u^2t, L=(u+1)w-4, y=uw. Then

    F=uwL,
    G=((1-2u)w+2)/(3u^2w^2), J(F,G)=1.

The rational mate proves the source gradient nonzero away from u=0
and w=0, its only affine pole divisors. On u=0 the actual source
derivative F_x is -3. On w=0, u is a unit and F_t=-4u^3 is nonzero.
These cases cover every source point, proving the polynomial gradient
ideal is the unit ideal without a numerical search.

The rational coordinates give

    F=(1+1/u)y^2-4y,
    u=y^2/(F-y^2+4y),
    C(x,t)=C(F)(y), D_F(y)=-u y^2.

This nonzero derivation has constants exactly C(F), since a rational
function of y with zero derivative in characteristic zero is constant.
This pays the full constant field, not only constants in a selected chart.

The zero fibre has exactly the three source components E_u={u=0},
E_w={w=0}, E_L={L=0}. They are reduced, irreducible and pairwise disjoint.
For the two nontrivial factors, primitive linearity in t and

    gcd(u^2,1)=1,
    gcd(u^2(u+1),u-3)=1

prove irreducibility. Their disjointness follows from w|u=0=1,
L|u=0=-3, and L|w=0=-4. These identities also prove comaximality
of the component ideals in the actual source ring.

There is no other reducible affine fibre. For c!=0, no u factor is
omitted when passing to u!=0, because F-c restricts to -c on u=0.
In the valid (u,w) chart its discriminant as a quadratic in w is

    4u[(c+4)u+c].

It has an odd-order zero at u=0 for every c!=0; for c=-4 it is
the nonzero linear polynomial -16u, also nonsquare. Primitivity and
Gauss's lemma therefore give irreducibility in the original source.
Equivalently the source-t discriminant is this expression times u^4,
which has the same square class. This accounts for every target value.

G has only the two affine polar divisors E_u and E_w; it is regular
on E_L because u,w are units there. In the actual boundary chart, put

    M=2-r-rb(1-r)^2,
    F_inf=(1-r)M(M-4),
    G_inf=[2+(3r-2)M]/[3(1-r)^2M^2].

Direct substitution verifies these identities. Along the whole D,
F=-4, F_r=4, and G=-1/6. This independently proves global submersion
and absence of a hidden pole on D. It also explains why irreducibility
of all other fibres was asserted only on U0: globally D is an additional
component over -4.

## 3. The complete scalar tuple and exact unit order

I independently recovered the scalar parts in the same parameter g=F:

    pp_(E_u)(G)=9/g^2+4/g,
    pp_(E_w)(G)=(32/3)/g^2+4/g,
    pp_(E_L)(G)=0.

At E_u the calculation first imposes w=1+u^2t and then takes u->0
with t fixed. Holding w independent there would leave the actual source
chart and is invalid. At E_w, (u,w) is a valid chart with u invertible.
The independent source checks the full rational remainders after
subtracting the displayed scalar parts; their denominators remain units
at the relevant generic points. Thus both orders and all lower principal
coefficients are checked, not only the highest pole.

The polynomial

    P2=g^2G=L^2[(1-2u)w+2]/3

has D_F P2=g^2. Conversely gG has a genuine simple pole on E_w and
is regular on E_L. A polynomial primitive of g would differ from gG
by H(F), using the paid rational constant field. Cancellation at E_w
forces H to have a simple pole at zero, which is uncancelled at E_L.
A higher pole already fails at E_w. Hence g theta is nonzero while
g^2 theta is zero, and the full C[g] annihilator is (g^2).

## 4. Entire torsion, cyclicity and the exact left annihilator

The complete torsion theorem now applies with all hypotheses paid.
Since zero is the only reducible affine fibre and it has three components,

    tors_(C[g])(C_F) = W tensor J,
    W=C^3/C(1,1,1), J=g^-1 C[g^-1].

Multiplication by g discards nonnegative powers in J, and nabla is d/dg.
Use E_L to choose representatives with third coordinate zero and put

    A=(9,32/3,0), B=(4,4,0), theta=A g^-2+B g^-1.

The determinant of A,B,(1,1,1) is -20/3, so A and B are independent
in the actual component quotient W. In particular the regular component
E_L is essential: discarding it and quotienting only the two polar
components would incorrectly kill the entire B direction as diagonal.
Zero principal part does not make a component disposable.

The following actual Weyl operations recover both first levels:

    g theta=A/g,
    (g nabla+2)theta=B/g.

More explicitly, the first-level classes of E_u and E_w are respectively

    -(3/5)g theta+(8/5)(g nabla+2)theta,
    (3/5)g theta-(27/20)(g nabla+2)theta.

Repeated differentiation generates every negative power in both arms,
so theta generates the entire torsion, not just a chosen finite packet.
Its own j-th derivative has order j+2; that statistic alone would not
have established two-arm generation.

Let `A1=C<g,nabla>/(nabla g-g nabla-1)`. The exact annihilator is the
**left** ideal `A1 g^2`, consisting of operators Q g^2. It is not a
two-sided ideal or a commutative-polynomial ideal being silently reused.
The PBW normal form with derivatives on the left and g powers on the
right reduces every P uniquely modulo this left ideal to

    P=a(nabla)+b(nabla)g.

Applied to theta, its coefficient in the B direction is
`a(nabla)(g^-1)`. Since

    nabla^j(g^-1)=(-1)^j j! g^(-j-1),

these vectors are linearly independent in characteristic zero, so a=0.
The remaining A coefficient similarly forces b=0. Conversely g^2 kills
theta. This proves both injectivity and surjectivity of

    A1/(A1 g^2) -> tors_(C[g])(C_F), [P] -> P theta.

The proof covers arbitrary finite Weyl operators, not a bounded-degree
matrix computation. It does not identify the nontorsion part of C_F
with this cyclic module.
