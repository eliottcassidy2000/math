# Exact target transitions, changing cores, and positive Collatz debt

**PROVED:** the scoped constructions and obstructions below.
**FINITE-EXACT:** the supplied computations in their explicit universes.
**OPEN:** Chan–Pak, ordinary positive Collatz, and universal reachability
in the literal-extra Collatz graph. This packet does not promote a
constructor obstruction to nonattainment in all tournaments.

The useful change of question is from “how can one fixed family supply
every next digit?” to “which actual module responses supply this target,
and what information must survive a change of family?” The paired norm
family cannot supply any target7p with prime p≠7. Releasing its equality
constraint gives an exact factor selector and real7p constructions.
Separately, an incoming ternary fork now enters an existing two-clock
family by a fully specified change of core. These are new interfaces;
their unbounded coverage and renewal are still separate obligations.

## 1. A target selector that escapes the norm obstruction

For independent nonempty tournament modules M,N, put

    (h,p)=(H(M),P_M(2)), (j,q)=(H(N),P_N(2)),

where P(2) counts covers in two ordered path slots, including empty slots.
[POLARIZED §§1–2](POLARIZED.md) proves a literal three-apex-arc repair
followed by two root-child births, with output

    Y=36pq+6hq+18pj−hj=(6p+h)(6q+3j)−4hj.

All module interiors and the designated root star survive the three
flips. The output is balanced, with an exact necessary and sufficient
primitivity test. The physical examples include347,371=7·53 and5299=7·757.
The nontransitive family4728·4096^t+571 is always divisible by7 and has
primitive balanced ports. No infinitude of prime cofactors is asserted.

For any prescribed Y, enumerate odd h,j with191hj≤Y, factorY+4hj,
and retain the ordered factors with the specified size and mod12 guards.
Each resulting response pair has a finite physical-module test, with
module orders O(log Y). This is a complete inverse for this constructor,
not merely a congruence search. The sharp boundY<39971 forces both
modules to have order at most8. The inherited complete bank then gives
**205 distinct outputs, including34 primes and9 values7p with p prime**
in that interval, at all possible module orders in this construction.
The constructor misses881 already at its arithmetic inverse.

The old norm branch is now better understood, too.
[NORM_CHAMBER](history/NORM_CHAMBER.md) proves:

- every nontrivial Pell unit leaves the physical cone x≥14h;
- a prime target has at most one arithmetic candidate in that cone;
- inert-prime valuations are even, so7|Y implies49|Y;
- a primitive norm output contains no inert prime factor.

Thus the prime branch has a finite, unusually rigid test, while the7p
branch requires a different interface. Polarization supplies one.

## 3. A physical handoff, a consumed resource, and an exact refill

[The core handoff](targets/PROOF.md) retains an arbitrary nonempty K
and changes the incoming source frame into the two-clock frame Π:

    60P_K(2)−H(K) -> 180P_K(2)+H(K).

Its actual old-to-new vertex map uses two deletions, three insertions,
three fixed arc reversals and three module-incidence reversals. The root
name survives; its star changes. Its full new rooted state needs P_K(3),
showing concretely why H and P2 alone are insufficient for every next
operation. A finite inverse supplies a smaller source and a construction
address for every target accepted by any Π[TT_j,K] chart.

The concrete combined path is

    239 -> 721=7·103 -> 1081 -> 1603 -> 3241.

The first step is the core handoff. The second is one inherited arc flip
realizing(3H−1)/2. At the next step the final dyadic site disappears:
[CLOCK_BOUNDARY](collatz/CLOCK_BOUNDARY.md) proves an exact deficit18,
so the graph has1603 rather than the arithmetic continuation1621. A
native birth then restores the chart at3241. The reusable two-step
boundary/refill circuit realizes3H−2; it does **not** continue consecutive
minus-Collatz steps for free. With an arbitrary retained module the
deficit is18H(M), and the refill is2H+35H(M).

An automatic return to the original source is also excluded at small
counts: any transitive-clock count that equals60P_L(2)−H(L) must be at
least28441. Thus721 and1081 cannot return through that source equation,
even with a different module L. The restriction is to this return chart.

## 4. A simultaneous Collatz result with its legal domain retained

[The debt proof](collatz/PROOF.md) considers U(x)=3x+1 and the extra
odd inverse V(x)=(x−1)/3, each followed by at least one halving. Every
nonempty such word with positive intermediates and#V≥#U strictly
decreases its source. It follows that every odd-containing cycle in the
literal-extra graph has moreU thanV actions. Pure even backtracking
cycles and endpoint-positive words with negative intermediates are
explicitly excluded.

For every r,q≥1 it also gives the exact legal source lift

    n=2·4^r q+(4^r−1)/3 -> 4^r(6q+1) -> 6q+1 -> 2q -> q,

using2r+3 native edges and peak3n+1. The extra odd inverse is not a
genuine reverse ordinary-Collatz edge. Ordinary Collatz plus ALL genuine
reverse edges still has the same reachability-to1 question as ordinary
Collatz. The larger union graph has an inherited descent theorem; the
literal-extra graph alone remains open. These objects are not conflated.

## 5. How the strategy changes next

The old-work transfers are recorded with their maps and losses in
[HISTORY](history/HISTORY.md): Gold/Pell positive chambers, Berggren
absorber host conditions, ordered Stern flanks, and square-sum named
ports. Each yielded a specific theorem or a missing response coordinate,
rather than a numerical analogy alone.

[INCOMING](INCOMING.md) connects the results to the concurrent bounded
mixed-sign descent, matched-core Collatz edge, and fixed-frame singleton
obstructions. The remaining joint obligation is a **physical transition
system with a proved return rule**: choose responses from the exact
target, carry enough ordered-cover information through core changes,
and either replenish consumed sites or charge their replacement to a
well-founded descent. The scalar mixed-sign descent is already available
in the incoming work. What is missing is a construction that executes
its needed moves with those interfaces, for every remaining target.

Neither the norm inverse, the new factor inverse, the single compensated
pair, nor the clock refill proves that universal statement. This packet
supplies concrete entry tests, actual transitions, and sharp failure
boundaries with which to attack it.
