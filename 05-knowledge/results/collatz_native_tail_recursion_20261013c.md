## 2. Every strict native lift pays coefficient one

Write \(U(n)=\operatorname{oddpart}(3n+1)\), stopping at the first 1, and
\(E(n)=\lfloor\log_2(n+1)\rfloor\). Let a supplied actual nonempty word
\(w=(a_1,\ldots,a_\ell)\) take an odd source \(n>1\) to \(h\), with

\[
2(h+1)\le n+1,\qquad
U_w(x)=\frac{Px+B}{Q},\quad P=3^\ell,\quad Q=2^A,
\quad A=\sum_i a_i.                                      \tag{1}
\]

All proper prefix states are greater than 1. The source need not be paid,
and the word need not end at its first half exit. Since \(B>0\), (1) implies

\[
(Q-2P)n\ge2B+Q>0,
\quad\text{so }Q>2P.                                    \tag{2}
\]

For every integer \(t\ge0\), put \(n_t=n+Qt\) and
\(b_t=v_2(h+Pt)\). The exact actual word at that source is

\[
w_t=(a_1,\ldots,a_{\ell-1},a_\ell+b_t),\qquad
h_t=\operatorname{oddpart}(h+Pt).                        \tag{3}
\]

The head valuations remain exact: changing the source by \(Q\) preserves
its head cylinder, and the last numerator has been multiplied by precisely
\(2^{b_t}\) relative to its odd part. Every preterminal state increases
with the source, so no earlier ROOT is introduced. Also

\[
2(h_t+1)\le2(h+Pt+1)\le n+Qt+1.                        \tag{4}
\]

The last inequality follows from (1) and (2). At every **strict lift**
\(t\ge1\), we have \(E(n_t)\ge A\), while the number \(\delta_t\) of odd
valuation letters in (3) is at most \(\ell\le A\). Equation (4) gives
\(E(h_t)\le E(n_t)-1\), and therefore

\[
\delta_t\le A\le2E(n_t)-1
\le E(n_t)^2-E(h_t)^2.                                  \tag{5}
\]

Thus **every strict coarse native lift pays coefficient one**, without
any additional height-cut computation or a child ROOT premise. The exact
word cylinder has modulus \(2Q\); (3) uses the coarser modulus \(Q\) and
retains its variable final valuation. This canonical coarse choice need not
be as broad as every optimized annular gate in the incoming packet, which
can sometimes lower the final-valuation threshold further.

The original source \(t=0\) is deliberately excluded from (5). More
generally, any half-band source failing an integer rate \(c\ge1\) must have
\(\delta\ge c(2E(n)-1)+1\); in particular \(A\ge2E(n)\).
For a fixed coarse native cell, at most its one member below \(Q\) can
fail coefficient one **once the actual half-band condition holds**.
The infinitely many possible words still leave an all-source obligation.
No density statement or backward transfer from a larger lift grounds that
exceptional source.

## 3. Recursive payment needs surplus, not just a pass/fail bit

For any actual segment \(n\xrightarrow{u}h\), define its signed surplus

\[
S_c(n,u)=c\{E(n)^2-E(h)^2\}-o(u).
\]

For consecutive actual segments, exact charge additivity gives

\[
S_c(n,uv)=S_c(n,u)+S_c(h,v).                            \tag{6}
\]

Hence a first exit with deficit \(d>0\) is repaired precisely when the
supplied continuation has surplus at least \(d\), provided the combined
endpoint remains a half-band endpoint of the original source. Knowing only
that the continuation is paid does not supply this required margin. The
continuation need not itself cross a half band relative to its own start.

The least inherited hostile supplies exact numbers: its first exit has
surplus \(-4\); the following \((2,2)\) segment has surplus
\(3(24^2-23^2)=141\); the combined source-owned receipt has surplus 137.
Starting a new invoice at the intermediate source would lose the \(-4\).
This is a checked composition law, not independence of two recursion calls.

## 4. A completed subfamily with constant rank and odd charge

Now supply a strict ROOT word \(z\) at the **actual endpoint** \(h\) in
(1). A nonempty actual forward word always has \(3\nmid h\): its last
numerator is 1 modulo 3 before division by a power of two. Consequently

\[
P\mid h(2^r-1)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
r\equiv0\pmod{R},\qquad R=2\,3^{\ell-1}.               \tag{7}
\]

This is the exact order of 2 modulo \(3^\ell\); it follows from
\(v_3(4^j-1)=1+v_3(j)\). In particular every permitted \(r\) is even.
For \(k\ge0\), set \(r=Rk\) and

\[
N_k=n+Q\frac{h(2^{Rk}-1)}P
    =\frac{Qh2^{Rk}-B}{P}.                            \tag{8}
\]

The integer parameter in (3) is exactly \(h(2^{Rk}-1)/P\). Therefore

\[
N_k\xrightarrow{(a_1,\ldots,a_{\ell-1},a_\ell+Rk)}h
\xrightarrow{z}1.                                    \tag{9}
\]

All sources are positive, all proper prefix states stay above 1, and the
supplied suffix stops at first ROOT. This proves (9) for every \(k\),
including the original source at \(k=0\). For \(k>0\), the first arrow
also satisfies (5). Because \(Rk\) is even, the complete odd charge is
exactly constant, as is the number \(\ell+|z|\) of odd steps. The total
valuation cost grows by \(Rk\); these clocks are not interchangeable.

These are infinite **grounded subfamilies**, not grounding of every
parameter in the coarse native progression. This distinction is exact:
membership in (8) requires
\((Px+B)/(Qh)\) to be a power of two whose exponent is divisible by \(R\).
The `recognize` API decides that condition on a supplied finite integer.

Inflating by \(Ri\) and then by \(Rj\) is exactly inflation by
\(R(i+j)\). It produces the same source and the same valuation word, so
flattening these nested addresses is a valid certificate compression.
The intermediate decomposition of \(i+j\) is intentionally forgotten;
the seed, cut, terminal \(h\), and supplied ROOT word are retained.

No astronomical source needs expansion. `member_word` retains the large
terminal exponent as one integer. For any positive modulus \(m\), the
reader first computes

\[
u=(Qh\,2^{Rk}-B)\bmod Pm,
\qquad N_k\bmod m=(u/P)\bmod m.                       \tag{10}
\]

The division is exact. Reducing modulo \(m\) before division would be
invalid in general, especially when 3 divides \(m\). `materialize` is a
separate optional operation with an explicit bit cap. No unsupported ROOT
claim is accepted merely because a materialization was refused.

## 5. A larger recursion address can name a smaller source

Let an authenticated nonempty prefix \(v\), of length \(j\), take
\(m>n\) to the seed \(n\) of (8). Prepend it to \(w\), retaining the
same child \(h\) and suffix \(z\). The new prefix has length
\(\ell+j\), hence its canonical period is \(3^jR\). Let its factory
be \(M_k\). Direct affine substitution gives

\[
U_v(M_k)=N_{3^j k}<M_k\qquad(k\ge0).                  \tag{11}
\]

To check strictness, write \(U_v(x)=(p_vx+b_v)/q_v\).
The seed inequality \(m>n\) forces \(q_v>p_v\), and the inverse
difference \((q_v-p_v)y-b_v\) is positive at \(y=n\) and increasing.
Every \(N_{3^jk}\ge n\) therefore preserves the strict inequality.
Actual valuations and first-hit safety follow from (9) for the prepended
family. The API `extend_family` authenticates the supplied descent once;
it does not discover one or assume a child completion.

This is the precise recursion connection: a recursive call is licensed by
an authenticated decreasing **source**, not by the appearance of its index.

claimed. Nor does a count like 20 out of 1024 replace a source guard.
