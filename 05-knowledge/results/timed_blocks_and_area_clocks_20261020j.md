## 5. The polygon idea preserves a precise predicate

The [runner packet](runner_area_clock_20261020j/README.md) proves that an
increasing circle homeomorphism F preserves the closed1/n separation
predicate for every pair iff F(x+1/n)=F(x)+1/n. The perimeter-to-angle map
of a regular m-gon has this property exactly when n divides m. Uniform
perimeter speed is constant swept-area speed about its centre. Thus a
regular n-gon, n>=3, gives an exact same-time reformulation for n runners;
a square handles n=2.

The common clock is indispensable. Keeping starts and average speeds0,1,2
but changing one runner's private clock lowers maximum clearance from1/3
to3/10. Half-turn orientations also lose the needed metric: two literal
three-point configurations have the same tournament but different answers
to the1/3 observer threshold. Gaps and the marked observer remain sidecars.

Fresh incoming [polygon transport](polygon_areal_transport_20261021/REPORT.md)
independently obtains the same threshold theorem and extends it to rational
thresholds and general radial curves. Its LCM criterion makes the useful
tournament refinement explicit: choose m divisible by both n and2 to
preserve the critical threshold and half-turn ties simultaneously.
This is a shared result, not two independent advances in coverage.

The incoming [THM-4737, Kepler polygon and vertex times](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4737-kepler-polygon-reformulation-of-the-lonely-runner-conjecture-observer-angular-momentum-vertex-times-and-the-relation-torus.md)

## 6. A concrete boundary-support exclusion for Hadwiger--Nelson

The [conic packet](conic_boundary_support_20261020j/README.md) proves an
all-size geometric extension rule. Any finite exterior on one ellipse
preserves an old six-colouring if every exterior vertex sees at most two
distinct old colours, allowing at most two vertices to see a third.
The proof combines the maximum internal degree4, degree choosability,
and the at-most-two-common-unit-neighbours condition. A failed colouring
must therefore have a vertex seeing four old colours, or at least three
vertices each seeing three. These necessary contacts are not sufficient
for forcing.

Incoming [circle-port palettes](circle_ports_polygon_palettes_20261021/README.md)
supplies the finer circle case: internal graphs are paths or cycles,
with the unit circle giving paths and even6-cycles. Its actual-port list
certificate and our conic certificate fit the same recursive deletion
test. Neither a prescribed blocked palette nor a harmless geometric
carrier constructs a six-colour equality tester.
