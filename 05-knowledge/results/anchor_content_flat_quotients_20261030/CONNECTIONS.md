# The 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 archaeology: maps and boundaries

This is a bounded inheritance pass, not a claim to have classified every
occurrence of these numbers in the repository. Bare numeral hits and theorem
IDs were not treated as mathematical connections. Current proofs and repairs
take precedence over the historical sources linked below.

## One exact diagram contains all four numbers

The concurrent [signed/unsigned quotient](../session111_scale_connections_20261030/SIGN_QUOTIENT.md)
now supplies the strongest literal connection. The same5000-element additive
normalizer acts on the24 orientation coordinates in two ways:

| action retained | kernel | image |
|---|---:|---:|
| coordinate permutation **and** orientation flips | 25 | 200 |
| coordinate permutation only | 100 | 50 |

Thus `5000=25*200=100*50`. The extra kernel has two independent phase bits:
global negation and conjugation, the latter flipping odd-valuation layers.
Signs on the orbits of1 and delta distinguish the four lifts of each unsigned
action. This is an exact two-bit recovery theorem for an *action*, not a claim
that two bits classify the resulting tournament. The published producer was
replayed; [incoming_sign_receipt.json](incoming_sign_receipt.json) verifies all
200 effective signed actions,50 unsigned images and rank-two sign kernel.

The concurrently proved [universal closure, U6–U7](../universal_ramified_closure_20261030/PROOFS.md)
strengthens the boundary: at ramified depth k>=2, even allowing all unsigned
color-preserving vertex permutations adds exactly these two phases to the
universal directed-color group. At k=1 there are independent fibre reflections
instead. It also separates the universal kernel, fixed by every bank, from
possible extra automorphisms of an individual tournament. We inherit these
scoped claims; we do not extend them through arbitrary native complements.

For our compiler this dictates an exact interface. A relabeling is a signed
affine action on edge bits. Dropping its translation loses legality of the
Hamilton-edge equations. Restore the two global phases while still inside
this ramified colored carrier, or carry the literal signed edge map once
native complements leave it. The Collatz counterpart is the elementary
hostile `(m,b)=(1,0)` versus `(1,1)`: identical multipliers but only the first
is the identity. The transferable rule is to restore the action kernel before
testing a return or legal continuation; the two underlying groups are not
being identified.

## A useful older connection that is not a clock

[THM-781, Hamilton-path inverse metagraph fibre](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-781-hamiltonian-path-inverse-metagraph-fibre.md)
identifies the ordinary presentation fibre as `H(T)/|Aut(T)|`. Its seven-vertex
example has `175/7=25` presentations. This is a genuine *forgetful fibre*, not
25 independent structures. A converse-merged pair doubles an ordinary fibre
when the class is non-self-converse; one must check that condition before
announcing50. In particular the displayed heptagon's merged fibre is25.

[THM-3457, four-point line-metric preorder atlas](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3457-four-point-line-metric-turnpike-preorder-atlas.md)
has a different25: the distance preorders of four ordered points on a line,
with dimension counts10,10,4,1. Reversal gives14 types, while arbitrary
labelings give300. The K4 edge incidence is useful, but tie and order data
remain separate. There is no established bijection to the 25 Hamilton-path
presentations. What transfers is the requirement to name the observer before
counting its fibres.

## The q=50 LRC sidecar: exact overlaps rather than summed families

[THM-4178, divisor-complete anchor triple exchange](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4178-q50-divisor-complete-anchor-triple-exchange.md)
retains the full labelled failure atoms, not just safe-set masses. The formula
`M_50(R)=sum_(F subset R) w_50(F)` and Boolean inversion recover every overlap.
Its different anchor rows cannot simply be added because their bodies overlap.

The source-to-target map in our tournament experiment is:

    labelled failure atoms → intersections of affine bank images;
    safe-set mass          → exact binary-rank cardinality;
    deletion-deck inversion→ inclusion–exclusion on the union.

The preserved predicate is exact membership in the union. The destroyed data
are which witness/anchor/base supplied membership. Retaining the intersection
coefficients is the necessary sidecar. The cheapest hostile test is the
five-vertex normalizer count52 versus full-class count12. This is a transfer
of an incidence-algebra mechanism, not an identification of LRC and tournaments.

claimed group isomorphism: one fibre has25 coefficient lifts, while the named

the method of retaining the shared residue in a recursive potential.

The reusable move is to distinguish *paying for a return* from *counting a
description*. A legal return has a clock cost; two descriptions of the same
object have an overlap cost. Both can be represented exactly, but neither
cost disappears merely because the observer forgot its witness.
