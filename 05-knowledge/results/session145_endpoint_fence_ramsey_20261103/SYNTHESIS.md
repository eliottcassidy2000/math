## 3. The geometric target narrowed to an exact theorem

For fences, ends are supported, crossings and collinear overlaps are excluded,
and each bounded field has area at most one. Splitting at allowed endpoint
contacts is part of the model. A pseudoforest split graph reduces its bounded faces to cycles and gives total
area at most n/4, with equality exactly at disjoint unit squares. A T junction
can split one original fence across several roles, so the product cactus
argument cannot be exported without an endpoint-only hypothesis.

In the endpoint-only product problem, the cactus optimum at twelve edges is
`6+3 sqrt(3)`. The known theta(5,5,2) construction attains
`(89+26 sqrt(13))/16`, so shared cycle edges matter. Under the specified common
two-edge separator and interior-chord hypotheses, a fixed-side cyclic-polygon
cap plus AM-GM proves this larger value optimal and forces the shared path to
straighten. That is the strongest geometric closure here. Other theta
embeddings, nonconvex chord placements, and the unrestricted product maximum
remain separate obligations.

## 4. What the Ramsey and knot lanes actually deliver

The four sparse Ramsey architectures have exact avoiding maxima
`2(k-1),3(k-1),4(k-1),5(k-1)`, with sharp red/blue blocks K2,K3,K4,K5. They are
far below known unrestricted witness orders, so a successful unrestricted
construction cannot stay inside those sparse classes. If an r-factor
attachment-polynomial support is explicitly used as the non-green graph,
its degeneracy gives the necessary bound `n<=2r(k-1)`. This is a real
conditional transfer, not a constructor and not an unrestricted Ramsey bound.

For knots, the loop polynomial and contextual matching minimum are complete
for the stated component observer. The closures of the one-crossing and
three-crossing two-strand braids have the same matching and component count
but Fox 3-coloring counts three and nine. Matching is therefore not a
knot-sensitive complete state. Preserve an actual tangle/diagram class before
asking for a stronger topological transfer.

## 5. The nine-label lane is a classification, not numerology

The prior labels are `174,285,396,417,528,639,741,852,963`. Their mean is 555,
which is not one of them, ruling out all magic permutations and nonconstant
affine repairs. Their Lo Shu rank layout has seven line sums 1665 and one
1584. The explicit Latin decomposition identifies the entire obstruction:
the nonlinear V-squared coefficient.

On those nine states, all maps that are magic in that placement form the
three-dimensional space `c+pU+qV`; the increasing chamber is exactly
`p>0,q>2p`. Order and trace maps are distinct, and the fixed-trace polynomial
criterion explains the exceptional cubic and the next new mode in degree
seven. No acoustical, medical, curve-action, Ramsey, or knot theorem follows
from this finite numerical correspondence.
