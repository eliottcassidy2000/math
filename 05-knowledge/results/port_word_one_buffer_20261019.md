# One whole core as a boundary-cone buffer

2026-10-19 UTC. Separate strengthening of the five-seam B8/B9 theorem. A single neighboring core on each nonempty side improves the common balance bound to

    5872/12875 = 9/20 + 313/51500.

This is a statement for actual B8/B9 core words, or for exteriors containing a whole such neighboring core with arbitrary further nonnegative profile weights. It is not a stronger bound for completely arbitrary unbuffered exterior posets.

## 1. Finite one-buffer cones

Use the five-prefix/four-nonempty-suffix core matrices N8,N9 and the four-by-five seam matrices K_g from the base note. Let the incoming row weight before a left neighboring core of type s be x>=0. After that core and its seam to the chosen central block, the central prefix weight is

    l = x N_s K_g.

Therefore it is in the nonnegative cone generated by the nonzero rows of N_s K_g. Union these generators over the two core types and five seams, and include (1,1,1,1,1) for an empty left exterior.

Dually, for right exterior suffix weight y>=0, the central suffix weight is

    r = K_g N_s y.

It belongs to the cone generated by the nonzero columns of K_g N_s. Union over core and seam types and include (1,1,1,1)^T for an empty right exterior. No assumption of uniform internal-core restrictions is made: x and y retain all earlier/later context effects.

These are automatically invariant outer cones under further legal updates. Indeed any nonnegative row x mapped by N_s K_g is a nonnegative combination of that product's rows; the column statement is dual. This is an exact inclusion proof, not an empirical stabilization or a limit argument.

After primitive integer normalization and duplicate removal, there are 36 left rays and 15 right rays. The central marked-event probability is the bilinear ratio l C_s r/(l N_s r), so the 540 generator pairs per central core suffice.

## 2. Exact bounds

The one-buffer corner probability ranges are

    B8: [5872/12875, 58366/115755],
    B9: [759/1622, 33199/64887].

Both imply balance at least 5872/12875. All lower and upper deficits

    12875 l C_s r − 5872 l N_s r,
    7003 l N_s r − 12875 l C_s r

are nonnegative. With the saved primitive rays their minimum values are 0 and 102875 for B8, and 285367 and 465617 for B9.

Both lower endpoints use left ray (17,32,19,32,19) and right ray (5,13,13,5). The left ray is row 1 of N8 K_matching-swap, after scaling. The right ray comes from a star seam with a suitable whole-core suffix column; its sources are recorded in the JSON. These rays are outer-cone generators and need not themselves be realized by finite exterior words, so sharpness over actual contexts is not claimed.

Thus every block in every allowed five-seam B8/B9 word has the stronger bound, including the first, last and single-block cases. The graph, degree and endpoint-witness statements are unchanged. This is one buffer application only, with no iteration or word enumeration.
