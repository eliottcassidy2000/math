# Independent proof audit of equal-rank exchanges

24 October2026. **PASS**, after the author's explicit rank, endpoint and
empty-maximum clarifications. The optimized finite checker independently
replayed297,967 controls, including15,477 equal-rank endpoint pairs.

The exchange lemma is valid with the declared order direction. A minimal
element of A\B has no strict predecessor in A: a predecessor in A\B violates
minimality, and one in B would force the element into B. Removing it therefore
preserves the upset property. Every strict successor of a maximal element of
B\A lies in A and cannot be the removed element, since that element is outside
B. Adding it preserves the upset property and lowers each endpoint difference
by one. This gives termination and the exact shortest exchange count.

The rank condition is now separately declared. Its interpretation as a fixed
margin requires the stated nonzero exact integer increment and fixed helper
contribution; unequal component weights do not inherit a two-component route.

The inherited neutral-fibre lemma gives both within-fibre adjustment and the
attained minimum boundary bill. Paying helper cost, changed visible component
cost and that exact bill in one event is therefore sufficient. Including the
largest neutral component cost makes the preceding within-fibre adjustments
legal, including heavy neutral components. The supplied endpoint helpers are
required to lie in their accepted A(T), and a same-T helper reset is counted
as one quotient edge. Empty maxima are explicitly zero.

The uniform bound follows without an additional quotient-connectivity
assumption because the equal-rank exchange lemma provides the visible route
and actual compatible sections exist at every required visible upset.
Section existence remains a substantive hypothesis. The theorem correctly
does not identify an aggregate charge table with shared-port feasibility,
or an atomic packet event with a native metagraph microedge.
