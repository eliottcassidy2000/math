# Root audit of separator sections and exact source-fixed resets

Status: PASS, independently reviewed proof and implementation.

The count recurrence checks each factor in its assigned bag and counts each
slot charge exactly once, at its highest bag. Running intersection implies
that child interiors are disjoint conditional on the complete separator
record. Therefore both the product of counts and the gluing of full witnesses
are valid. The code rejects a broken running-intersection tree and mismatched
charge types. Integer margin coordinates remain exact.

The two-arrival recurrence applies the same decomposition to two complete
records and pays each owned slot's replacement cost once. Its minimum is
attained. It does NOT by itself compose arbitrary minimum-distance edges:
different minima could use disconnected members of an intermediate fibre.
The proof explicitly retains the actual source record or requires a separate
within-fibre connectivity theorem. The nonmetric three-fibre hostile detects
that omitted hypothesis.

The conditional coset theorem is sufficient, not necessary. Its offsets and
subgroups depend on the SAME separator record; summing unconstrained marginal
charge sets is not allowed. A genuine section of each child coset supplies
actual compatible records, and the finite group sum provides a charge split.
This is a reusable finite-width compiler, not a claim of polynomial time at
arbitrary width, or a native record-supply theorem at every tournament order.

The independent literal-enumeration controls compare all exact charges,
counts and attained pairwise costs. The normal and optimized final compiler
replays pass307,767 checks. An earlier local replay saw a stale frozen receipt
while two new hostile controls were being added; the final receipt includes
both controls, and neither recurrence was changed to fit the old output.

The source-specific native reset independently compares the occupancy/charge
dynamic program to all9880 actual triples at every debt. The fixed six-arrival
core sequence has layer sizes1,3,8,3,3,1, hence216 full helper trajectories.
Two-pass bottleneck/shortest-path optimization and independent enumeration
agree: minimum peak103, and minimum total415 among routes at that peak.
The exact helper endpoint records, twelve shared-owner guards, physical
current rows and actual source clocks are retained. A greedy legal selection
also attains peak103 but total427, so local cheapest choice does not certify
the globally least total. The previous exhibited path had peak114/total433.

The claim is exact for this fixed core sequence and its supplied full menus.
It does not replace the uniform120 bound by103 or optimize over all possible
core detours. The reset replays pass30,886 checks in normal and optimized
Python. `native/AUDIT.md` independently authenticates the geometric and
resource carrier used here; the compiler does not erase that dependency.
