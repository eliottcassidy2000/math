# Independent audit of the shielded-reservoir boundary theorem

24 October2026. **PASS** after three explicit full-record clarifications.
This is a proof/scope audit; native authenticity and finite charge coverage
remain the stated independently checked dependencies.

The extension formula is correct: after the actual core and control records
are fixed, subtract their charges and choose the supplied reservoir section.
Its constant occupancy supplies the required remaining margin, and the full
configuration-level shielding hypothesis supplies every cross-boundary
predicate, including higher-arity ones. The theorem does not derive
shielding from separate resource counts or from matching charge labels.

The bound2*min(rho,|R|-rho) follows from the symmetric difference of two
rho-subsets. The final wording explicitly assigns two fixed actual full
records to each reservoir slot. This is necessary: a same-bit kernel change
cannot otherwise be omitted from the cost. With fixed records, weighting
the symmetric difference by actual slot-clock distances gives the stated
largest-cost upper bound. Core and control costs are added independently;
a large neutral or correlated control component is not made free.

Prescribed endpoint configurations now explicitly require legality, occupancy
rho, the required charge and shielding. Reusing a deterministic section
returns the literal full reservoir only when the repeated core/control state
has the same total target charge. Strict temporal nonreturn remains outside
scope; later use of an own released option does not manufacture simultaneous
capacity.

The variable-occupancy hostile is correct. In the four distinct elements of
C2 x C2, the sum of two distinct labels cannot be zero, whereas occupancies1
and3 each cover the group. Thus adjacent-rank coverage cannot be inferred.
