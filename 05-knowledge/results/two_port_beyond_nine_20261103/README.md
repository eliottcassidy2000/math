same four distinct boundary colours, both are forced to the fifth colour,

placement** realizes this gadget with no unintended contacts.

Its six independent ports have exactly **two forbidden admissible boundary
partitions at five colours**. Both have two extensions at six colours.

why successive two-contact growth from that lattice never escapes its

proof for the triangular prism yield:

**Every proper five-colouring of this fixed spindle extends across any
six additional distinct planar points, including all induced unit edges.**

A blocking extension on this carrier needs at least **seven private
