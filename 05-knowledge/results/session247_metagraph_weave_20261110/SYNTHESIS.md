## 1. Invisible topology becomes an explicit arithmetic family

Session166/170's equal-path-cover-profile edges were built to carry
metagraph topology without changing H. Uniform module substitution cannot
see their bits. An asymmetric marked root can.

The new boundary-run formula retains five pointed responses E,S,D,A,B.
For an ordinal array of modules, it gives the exact H polynomial and every
mixed switch interaction. The criterion Delta S=Delta A=0 kills all pair
interactions, leaving a real affine cube of literal internal arc reversals.
This is an operation on tournaments, not on formal cube interiors.

Two explicit families result:

- The inherited six-vertex masks18/22, marked by root mask5, give H=41/43
  at depth1 and the affine cube5605,5635,6005,6035 at depth2.
- A seven-vertex edge recovered from the old metagraph atlas, masks568/696,
  marked by root mask53, has H=393/399 at depth1 and
  410445,411051,412729,413335 at depth2. It extends to every dimension.
  From dimension2 onward the gcd of all normalized increments is1.

Primitive affine cubes of every dimension already existed in THM-5117
(Session245), including the proof that gcd1 can coexist with exponential
spacing. We do not claim that general feature as new. Our different source
is a full-profile-invisible internal arc-flip edge; the new general results
are the interface charge and observation-degree laws.

The second family repairs a real obstruction in the first. Define the
module charge as gcd_c(c!pc(c)), equivalently the fixed divisor of its
integer-valued path-slot polynomial. The old six-vertex pair has charge3;
the new seven-vertex pair has charge1. Its complete profile is
(101,585,915,570,162,21,1).

This is where the niche connection matters. The old polynomial-address
reflection warned that a primitive polynomial can still have a fixed local
divisor. The old path-cover walk kernel supplies the exact factorial
coefficients. Combined with marked incidence, they yield a physical theorem:
if k p-charged modules meet q arbitrary exceptional vertices, at least
k−2q charges survive in every path skeleton, so p^max(0,k−2q) divides H.
The statement holds with any quotient tournament between the modules, not
only an ordinal product.

The same skeleton proof gives an exact observation theorem: q marked
vertices see at most2q-way interactions among profile-invisible module
bits. That degree bound is sharp for every q. For one root, a physical
second difference16 is exhibited; ordinal products give the sharp
2q-degree examples.

The construction now has physical vertices, explicit response laws,
arbitrary affine dimension, and a charge-free primitive-increment variant.
It still lacks small enough weights and controlled absolute baselines.
Primitive binary weights can have large holes: the second family's first
weights are303 and1142. This is the remaining issue, not a hidden
assumption that gcd1 fills an interval.

## 2. Conserved charges allow dense interactions instead of forbidding them

The earlier shared-core exchange approach either cancelled mixed
interactions or bounded every Boolean edge. The new transfer lane instead
fixes the number of selected vertices. On a k-subset slice,

    W_ij -> W_ij+alpha_i+alpha_j,
    a_i -> a_i+(k−1)alpha_i/2

does not change the physical count. A dense row-potential background is
therefore removable as an exact coordinate gauge. With several conserved
block charges, dense block interactions can also become constants.

For a residual matrix, the new safe-swap criterion uses the largest and
smallest k−1 row-difference sums. If the safe-pair graph is connected, its
token graph connects every physical cut in the charge slice with H-steps
of size at most2. Consequently the slice fills the odd interval between
its actual extrema. This certifies permitted moves, not merely a lattice
or rank condition.

An actual five-cycle core with H=15 has dense W entries8 and10, yet all
its two-token swaps are safe and its strong seven-vertex closures fill
89,91. Formal dense models have arbitrarily wide intervals, but no growing
physical realization is claimed.

The metagraph connection is conserved incidence margins with port identity
retained. A shared count does not identify continuations, and a gauge change
of a response does not assert a second tournament with those transformed
coordinates. The source and both limitations are explicit in the proof.

## 3. Only three window orbits would be enough

Fresh concurrent work reports full deletion windows, taking a union over
all actual realizers of a base count h: every odd in[3h,5h) for
237<=h<=1023, and every odd in[3h,9h) for365<=h<=1023. We use those reports
as conditional inputs, without re-verifying their census.

The new scale-circle theorem solves the numerical gluing exactly. For
count multiplier p and window[alpha h,beta h), phase seeds
`a_1<...<a_q<p a_1` tile the odd tail precisely when

    alpha a_(i+1)<=beta a_i, and alpha p a_1<=beta a_q.

For p=3 and the narrow window, three phases are necessary and the explicit
seeds237,343,495 satisfy every seam. If the full window property can be
continued at all base counts3^t times these three seeds, every odd>=711
follows. Together with the inherited finite low-count spectrum, this would
prove Chan–Pak. Two phases cannot work in this exact model because
(5/3)^2<3.

A single wide rail from365 would suffice above1095, but its seam is critical:
exact tripling is required unless a correction is separately paid. The
three-phase version has growing positive overlap margins and therefore
allows bounded affine errors under an explicit seam-debt test.

This reduces the physical continuation obligation to specified sparse
orbits. It does not prove their continuation. A window is a family of
witnesses, potentially using a different base tournament for each output.
There is no assertion of one representative realizing its entire window.

### A concrete next interface, not just an analogy

The marked response formula gives a useful test for a proposed rail step.
For a fixed rooted T, let h=H(T) and F=H(T plus its root)=E+S+D, with
pointed responses E,B as defined in marked/PROOF.md. Append a directed
triangle after T in ordinal order, retaining the root. If the root beats
respectively3,2,1,0 vertices of that triangle, the new counts are exactly

    3F,
    3F+3h−E+3B,
    3F+4h−2E+6B,
    3F+3h−3E+9B.

These follow from the one-root boundary formula and the triangle's four
pointed states. They exhibit the information that a window-transport proof
must retain. Bare tripling gives only one residue class; the other cuts
are controlled by joint E,B responses, not by F alone. A promising next
search is therefore for enough endpoint-response diversity inside the
three designated base-count families to pay the missing residue classes
while satisfying the scale seams.

## 4. The carry work is useful infrastructure, not a new headline

The one-apex inverse was already known. The carry lane rewrites its exact
target equation as a nonnegative budget with two endpoint states. Zero bits
increase a future denomination without spending budget; one bits spend the
current exact denomination. It records the exact child-set union and the
necessary nonterminal source inequality2R+H<=N.

This may help choose a physical witness once a rail or selector supplies
an appropriate source. It does not produce an all-target source family and
is explicitly a reorganization rather than a claim to supersede the older
inverse algorithms.

## 5. The research direction after this session

The pieces are complementary, not yet one completed construction:

1. **Marked metagraph modules** supply actual affine families and show how
   to remove a fixed-divisor charge.
2. **Conserved-charge cuts** can fill intervals despite dense interaction,
   provided a physical safe-swap graph is connected.
3. **Scale-circle gluing** says exactly which recurring intervals would
   cover all targets and how much seam error they tolerate.
4. **Exact budget numeration** selects inside a supplied physical family
   without replacing equality by projective density.

The next high-value theorem is a physical window-propagation mechanism on
the three specified rails, retaining endpoint/marking information. Another
is a growing charge-slice selector on the charge-free marked family with
controlled baselines. Neither follows formally from the other's algebra;
that missing map is now sharply identified.

No new global spectrum cutoff is claimed. No inherited proof was promoted
because it looked plausible. The saved new-formula checks are small and
scoped; universal statements rest on the written proofs, and conditional
applications retain their hypotheses. The session has ended.
