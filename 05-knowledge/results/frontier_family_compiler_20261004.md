## 2. A self-contained coarse-family compiler

For a nonempty positive valuation wordw of lengthj, write

    P=3^j, Q=2^A, A=sum(w),
    F_w(n)=(P*n+B)/Q.

Let `r=(-B)*P^(-1) modQ` be the least positive odd coarse residue, and put
`e=(Pr+B)/Q`. Then for every integerk>=0,

    n=r+Qk,
    U^j(n)=oddpart(e+Pk).                                (1)

The firstj-1 valuations are exactly the displayed letters. The last is the
displayed last letter plus `h(k)=v2(e+Pk)`. To prove this, invert the nominal
endpoint alongw. The total congruence ensures each inverse is integral;
all intermediate inverse numerators are odd. Every intermediate target
before the terminal nominal value is therefore odd, fixing its preceding
valuation exactly. The nominal terminal value may be even, yielding the
extra divisionh. Positivity follows from the positive forward affine carries.

SupposeQ>P. Define

    k_min=max(0, floor((e-r)/(Q-P))+1).                    (2)

**PROVED.** For everyk>=k_min, the family in(1) strictly descends. Indeed,

    (r+Qk)-(e+Pk)=(r-e)+(Q-P)k>0,

and taking the odd part only decreases the positive nominal endpoint. The
compiler checks the least covered boundary by independent literal replay,
including its possibly larger final valuation. This proof needs no assertion
about unobserved family members reaching1.

IfQ<=P, the compiler withholds this unrefined nominal-contraction conclusion.
It does not claim that every individual source fails to descend. For example
the coarse word(1,2) coversn=3 mod8; source3 reaches1 while27 reaches31.
One extra terminal division selects a paid subfamily3 mod16. The withheld
coordinate is an actual valuation, not an arbitrary sign or phase.

The exact fixed-last-valuation cylinder has modulus2Q; the coarse family
permits all extra terminal divisions and has modulusQ. This broader guard
is inherited from the boundary compiler, now stored explicitly on a learned
frontier instead of remaining only a point route.

## 3. Pull back a family without losing the original source

Let a checked exact head have data(p,q,b), mappingn to`x=(pn+b)/q`. A tail
family has data(P,Q,B), residue r and its parameter threshold. Composition
has data

    (Pp, Qq, Pb+Bq),
    r'=(q*r-b)*p^(-1) mod(qQ).                           (3)

The pulled coarse guardn=r'+qQk ensures both the exact head and the tail
guard. If `x0=(p*r'+b)/q`, the tail parameter is

    t=(x0-r)/Q+p*k.                                     (4)

The compiler retains `t>=tail.k_min` and independently applies(2) to the
composed data. These are different obligations. For instance word(2) has
coarse residue1, nominal endpoint1 andk_min=1. Prefixing(1) produces the
unpaid nominal word(1,2), witnessing27->41->31. Prefixing(4) gives residue5;
its k=0 boundary has another root proof, but the inherited tail threshold
still requiresk>=1 for that particular composed rule. The implementation
retains the threshold rather than silently replacing the derivation.

Source object: a grounded discovered tail plus exact stored heads. Target:
coarse cylinders with affine nominal endpoints, boundary thresholds and
extra-valuation functions. The map preserves actual transitions and guarded
descent; it does not preserve unconditional home across all free parameters.
A rooted endpoint certificate is a separate sidecar.

## 6. Complete selected members at an already rooted hub

Letz be a supplied positive odd hub not divisible by3, accompanied by its
actual root certificate. Seek parameters satisfying

    e+Pk=2^h z,
    2^h z=e modP,
    k=(2^h z-e)/P>=k_min.                               (6)

HereP=3^j ande is a ternary unit: reducingQe=Pr+B modulo3 proves this. The
order of2 modulo3^j is `L=2*3^(j-1)`. An elementary proof starts with4=1+3:
cubing a number whose difference from1 has exact3-adic valuationm increases
that valuation tom+1. Successive ternary lifts therefore choose a unique
exponent digit amongh,h+L_current,h+2L_current. This computes the unique
solutionh0 moduloL to(6), without expanding2^h.

Choose the least h*=h0 modL large enough to meet the inequality in(6).
Ifz=1, also require the resulting last exponent to be at least3, excluding
a terminal root self-return. Then for every integert>=0,

    h=h*+Lt,
    n=(Q*2^h*z-B)/P,

is a completed member of the learned family. Its exact prefix word isw with
h added to the last valuation. Backward integrality gives every guarded
inverse edge; attach that word to the supplied hub AST. The positive prefix
cannot meet1 early and then leave it; the root last-letter condition excludes
the only root self-return. Thus the total first-hit odd rank isj plus the
hub's rank, with ordinary rank increased byA+h+j. These are infinitely many
constructed completed members, not a claim that every free k is completed.
