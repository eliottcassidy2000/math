## Proof directions and hostile controls

* JE1 uses positivity of every summand, not signed cancellation. The
  no-rise condition concerns every earlier/later threshold pair, not
  merely adjacent thresholds. A reconstructed code includes all arcs.
* JE2 counts ordered marked pairs, including diagonal ones. The rigid
  pair stabilizer justifies the weighted unrooted sum. It does not identify
  these counts with the unweighted A000568 sequence.
* JE3/JE4 use the exact strong-component start-budget law. A small J does
  not generally dominate the deletion count: the known four-vertex
  J=2<q=3 hostile remains valid. The two-start exception has three
  returning marks and cannot be generalized to arbitrary source size.
* RP1's repair is absorption of a known wrapper at a different physical
  mark. Its scalar law is credited; neither integer inversion nor the
  mod8 guard substitutes for an actual graph inverse.
* JR1 has a proved finite-dimensional container before interpolation.
  The exceptional empty module is not evaluated by the positive-m
  formula without its separate boundary table.
* JR2 distinguishes arbitrary modular boundary weights from actual
  tournament sources. Its minimality concerns only bulk insertion and
  the declared observations. JR4 demonstrates a complement instability
  of a genuine weighted port kernel, not an unproved same-size pair of
  physically realizable tournaments.
* JR3's equal-chart sources have different orders. Size supplies the
  missing parity inside the guarded family. At19 the polynomial decoder
  needs the orbit relation; equal numbers alone provide no decoder.
