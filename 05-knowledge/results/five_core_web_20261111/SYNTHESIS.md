## 4. Binary shells now have exact addresses and exact borrow terms

For `0<=n<2^k`, let rev_k reverse all k bits, including leading zeroes.
The exact new shell identity is

    g(2^k+n)−g(n)=2^k−rev_k(n)−topbit(n).

Every even jump from0 through2^k occurs; each interior jump has exactly two
inverse addresses. Through a core with deletion count d and linear slope
`LB+i`, the jump spectrum is exactly

    (LB+i)2^k + 2d*{0,...,2^(k−1)}.

The five spacings are2,6,10,18,18. Distinguishing the H9 cores therefore
requires the association of a jump to its baseline, not its marginal set.
The joint `(n,rev_k(n))` points have exact occupancy in every dyadic rectangle
whose two depths sum to at most k.

Continuing the probe gives the internal-bit correction. If a zero bit at
position k separates upper prefix p and lower k-bit suffix s, changing it
to1 changes g by

    2^k−rev_k(s)−topbit(s)−p−(p mod2),       k>=1.

The prefix penalty is precisely what a leading-bit-only extrapolation loses.
The complete inverse of the322 inherited small-parent holes through2^24−1
finds3,560 H9 witnesses. Some require both flanks: the smallest possible
short flank reaches13. A real internal-bit move connects the two distinct
small-parent holes42223 and40685. Thus scalar support cannot simply be
propagated through repeated bit deletion.

## The remaining proof obligation, after this session

A sufficient completion is still the explicit five-core statement: for
every positive odd N other than7,21, find a bank core, cut and nonnegative
addresses A,B with the inherited exact compiler value N. The existing
all-order exclusion of7 and21 is a separate, already proved result.

The most promising recovered methodology is **joint defect avoidance**:

1. Use shell or physical cut moves to generate several legal, correlated
   candidates for a prescribed N, retaining both addresses and the cut.
2. Prove that the candidates cannot all fail, or give a target-preserving
   rewrite that decreases a proper height containing the full address and
   borrow data.
3. When switching to a new source, transport its marked response. The
   H13 family proves that preserving the scalar count or relabelling the
   graph is not enough to return to the five-core source class.

The new formulas make this obligation explicit and testable. They do not
yet supply the uniform no-simultaneous-failure lemma or a terminating
target-preserving rewrite. Neither finite coverage, module classification,
golden spectra, marginal jump fullness nor local intervals closes that gap.
