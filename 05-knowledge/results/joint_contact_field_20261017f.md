    v_i^2+Z_i v_i+Z_i/conjugate(Z_i)=0.

## 6. Reusable construction, limitations, and next target

Before adding another contact, test its reduction at every known small
conjugation-fixed place. A simple split contact polynomial over the existing
residue field preserves that place and its colouring bound, even when the
new global squareclass is independent. Apply the criterion repeatedly to
avoid spending time enlarging a field whose entire graph is already
four-colourable. This is an application of the inherited residue-colouring
lemma, with explicit Hensel data as the additional supplier.

The next meaningful geometric target is a *shared golden-coordinate*
attachment whose boundary relation is proved on the same marked ports.
The new golden edge supplies that interaction, but its28 occurrences do not
restrict the relation. Replacing this by a mere no-retraction test, a large
six-core, or a high extension degree would repeat an already closed route.
A next candidate must either obstruct the surviving graph-colouring quotient
or provide certified incompatible boundary extensions when glued at literal
shared vertices. Both are stronger requirements than adding independent
contact equations.
