## Comparisons after the first pull and probes

1. Incoming Session61 subsumes the simple hexagon-to-Fano and fixed-channel
   parity proposals. Continue at eight points, variable channel transport,
   and the actual planar metric instead of repeating those proofs.
2. Caldero–Germoni Proposition9 supplies the classical projective completion
   to S(3,4,8). Earlier repository cardinality/code work already knows its
   affine and extended-Hamming descriptions. The new application must give
   the actual conic marking and retain the cube basis.
3. The metric collision probe and affine design probe use different maps:
   collapsing opposite cube vertices can give the seven-point A2 flower;
   deleting the affine origin gives a Fano plane. They cannot be interchanged.
4. Ordered frame transport and unordered transport differ by a local
   coefficient system. A rotation of the three channels can remove hidden
   cycle obstructions rather than merely relabel them.
