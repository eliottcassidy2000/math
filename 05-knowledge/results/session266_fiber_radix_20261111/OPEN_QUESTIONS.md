# Next constructive questions

All questions below remain OPEN unless an exact partial result is stated.

1. **Complete the fast inventory interface.** Load the existing below625
   ordinary strong inventory into the all-order compiler, preserving exact
   automorphism and self-converse data. Design canonical augmentation that
   adds each new strong class once, instead of re-enumerating every padded
   order. The finite algorithm is known; efficiency and the unified data
   interface are the next tasks.
2. **Refine the smooth leading law to full polynomials.** The six irreducible
   scalar atoms determine maximal degree and leading coefficient. Lower terms
   see strong classes at reducible totals. Can those be organized by a bounded
   excess/defect grammar rather than tabulated separately for each h?
3. **Consume the signed atlas without entering a hole.** The raw code step
   sends H3 to forbidden H7, so a universal implementation is impossible.
   Is there a natural first-return or decorated partial operation which stays
   in the realizable support and preserves enough tournament state to iterate?
   A sampled numerical return is not a construction.
4. **Choose Collatz chords intrinsically.** An orbit segment supplies a path,
   not a tournament. Find an arithmetic comparison of nonadjacent points with
   a stated orientation and tie rule, and prove which part of its path-cover
   response is controlled. Arbitrary chord completion is already understood.
5. **Find a stronger puncture invariant.** Binary carry geometry completely
   controls parity of each path-cover completion fiber. Identify a natural
   refinement modulo4 or an odd prime that retains the needed cycle or
   automorphism data; universal oddness alone does not extend to those moduli.
6. **Remove singleton separators electrically.** Adjacent nonsingleton SCCs
   meet through a rank-one interface rather than an articulation. Find a
   small exact two-port composition state extending the additive defect law.
   The unseparated case is not proved by ordinary resistance addition.
7. **Retain state under scalar factorization relations.** The smooth monoid
   has exact elasticity5/2, but equal H can hide different padding dimensions,
   sizes and interfaces. Seek useful positive intertwiners between the
   63² and49*3⁴ families, or prove a sidecar obstruction to such intertwiners.

The full support question “all odd H except7,21?” remains Chan–Pak. The weighted
signed-edge atlas repackages this exact question; it does not settle Collatz
convergence or the completeness of its known cycles.
