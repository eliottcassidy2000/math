## 1. Precise theorem and the retained-sheet consumer

Let W be the Coxeter group on four generators with chain labels `3,3,5`,
all other pairs commuting, and each generator an involution. Let W act
transitively on D>=2 letters. For any Coxeter generator s,

    |Fix(s)| / D <= 4/15.                              (1)

This is sharp: the conjugation action on its sixty reflections is transitive,
and every reflection fixes exactly sixteen of those sixty labels.

Suppose, in addition, that the action comes from a hypothetical Keller map
whose whole irreducible nonproperness set has normalization A1 and whose
affine singularities are exactly three unibranch cusps and N>=3 ordinary
nodes, with no others, with actual meridians generating
an H4 quotient and all meridians involutive. Let k be the number of sheets
actually retained along the smooth part of the curve. Then `1<=k<D`, and
retained sheets are pointwise fixed by their positive meridian. Consequently

    k<=|Fix(s)|<=4D/15.                                (2)

Let n_i>=0 be the three actual cusp fibre cardinalities, and omega_j the
actual deleted-sheet overlap at the j-th node. The original Euler formula is

    1=-2k+sum_i n_i+sum_j omega_j,
    omega_j>=D-2k.                                   (3)

Since (2) makes D-2k positive, N>=3 gives

    1 >= 3D-8k >= 13D/15 > 1,                         (4)

a contradiction. This does not require full fixed-set retention, individual
cusp injection inequalities, local cusp generation, or a faithful action.
A (2,5) cusp is unibranch and causes no change to (3). Its fibre cardinality
is only used through nonnegativity.

Thus the specified actual H4 whole-support package cannot have involutive
meridian monodromy in any degree. The [actual mixed-cusp supplier](planar_jc48_sep08_mixed_cusp_braid.md)
is now independently audited. Its literal curve has the required complete
singularity inventory and normalization A1. An abstract action alone is
not a Keller map.

The closest proved mechanisms are the exact fixed-coset trace in

and the actual Euler ledger in
[the three-cusp support theorem](planar_jc48_sep08_three_cusp_closure.md).
The hostile is replacing retained subsets by full fixed sets. Here that

The live concepts are actual retention, reflection conjugacy, stabilizer

## 3. Arbitrary subgroups and fixed cosets

Let T be the sixty-element conjugacy class of reflections. For an arbitrary
proper subgroup H<W, put K=<H intersect T>. Then K is a proper reflection
subgroup and

    K intersect T = H intersect T,                     (5)

because K is contained in H and contains every element of H intersect T.
The classification, or the exact closed-set computation below, therefore
gives `|H intersect T|<=16`. H itself need not be generated by reflections.
The empty intersection is allowed and only strengthens the bound.

Every transitive action is the coset action W/H for the stabilizer H of
one letter. A coset gH is fixed by s precisely when `g^-1 s g in H`.
Every element of H intersect T has exactly |C_W(s)| conjugators. Dividing
by |H| and then by D=|W|/|H| gives

    |Fix(s)|/D = |H intersect T|/|T| <=16/60=4/15.     (6)

This is a direct counting identity. A kernel of the coset action does not
alter it. D>=2 ensures H is proper. If an Artin H4 meridian tuple is
involutive, its representation factors through the displayed Coxeter
presentation by imposing the four square relations. The direction is
from W onto the actual image; no reverse identification with an arbitrary
finite image is assumed. Transitivity and (6) survive such a quotient.
This proves (1), then (2)--(4) prove the stated geometric obstruction
whenever its actual supplier is available.

## 5. Sharpness, lost data and the actual application boundary

The natural conjugation action on the sixty reflections is a sharp positive
control for (1): each reflection fixes itself and fifteen orthogonal root
lines, for sixteen fixed labels. It is transitive but cannot be the stated
Keller monodromy. Even with all sixteen fixed sheets retained, (3) would

valid abstract group action from an actual affine-source Euler package.

and nonreflection stabilizer elements. Those quantities are not reconstructed

uses only the information that survives, with a positive coefficient of the
number of nodes. No multiplication of a negative lower bound by N occurs.
