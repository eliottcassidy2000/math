## The fundamental object and the live portfolio

The useful object is a **guarded proof implication with an original source,
reconstructible words, clock costs, and a named terminal**. A smaller child,
a continuous shape, or a positive transition weight records only part of it.
Different representations become useful when they retain the coordinates
needed for their next operation.

Anchor: ground the inherited child obligations. Niche: source-specific ranks
that survive hidden-state refinement. Wildcard: simplex/sphere/cube sections
and the arithmetic structure of their boundary. The board is **marked ray /
native guard / ordered carry / paid credit / common rank / ROOT terminal**.

The closest proved mechanism is the
[existing common-future certificate integration](collatz_fundamental_integration_20261009.md).
The hostile is its closed, ungrounded 1,949-exponent component. The corrected
near miss is substituting a slope, abstract absorption, or positive measure
for actual terminal discharge. The least-used sidecars recovered here are
the constant marker and simultaneous real/2-adic coordinates. The older
[adaptive credit account](adaptive_credit_potential_20261004.md) provides a
second concrete mechanism: an affine interface can be checked against the
same source and budget, rather than inventing a new potential at every switch.

\qquad

## 2. The geometric triple has two precise meanings

The [geometry packet](simplex_sphere_cube_geometry_20261009.md) separates
polarity, chart changes, and historical bit-count identities.

First, the same marked ray \((x,n,1)\) can be normalized by its 1-, 2-, or
infinity norm. It then lies respectively in a triangle, a positive sphere
octant, or three cube boundary patches. Dividing the first two coordinates
by the last recovers current \(x\) and original \(n\) exactly. These are
compatible views of one arithmetic monitor. Erasing the marker is a genuine
loss: states \((3,5)\) and \((9,15)\) have the same unmarked ray but
different next Collatz valuations.

Second, choosing probabilities among three available proof steps gives a
different simplex. Its square-root coordinates lie on a sphere orthant.
Independent binary choices at several states form a cube. The
[policy geometry packet](collatz_receipt_flow_geometry_20261009.md) uses
these probability spaces; they are not being identified with integer rays.

Polarity is a third, separate operation. A regular simplex is dual to a
reflected/scaled simplex, the Euclidean unit ball is literally self-polar,
and a cube is dual to a crosspolytope. A square is combinatorially self-dual;
a cube in dimension at least three is not. A simplex minimizes the number
of vertices of a full-dimensional polytope, but there is no unqualified
theorem making a sphere the universally most complex shape.

The productive relation is therefore not a ranking of shapes. It is a
collection of exact changes of representation, each with its own retained
data and admissible operations.

## 3. Positivity becomes useful through a common rank

For a finite authenticated proof system, let \(\mathcal P_i\) be the
allowed probability-row polytope at state \(i\). A nonnegative rational
rank with \(h_{\rm ROOT}=0\) and
\[
h_i\ge1+\max_{p\in\mathcal P_i}\sum_jp_jh_j                \tag{1}
\]
certifies termination under arbitrary history-dependent choices in those
polytopes. It gives \(\mathbb E\tau\le h_{\rm source}\), a geometric
tail, and a deterministic strictly decreasing-rank proof path. Actual
receipt words are retained when compiling that path to a ROOT word.

This condition survives finite or countable hidden-state refinement if
every conditional projected row remains in its old certified polytope and
the ROOT fibre consists exactly of genuine completed proofs. The pulled-back
rank is the same certificate. Support alone does not suffice; inserting
arbitrarily long hidden waits can invalidate the row condition.

The sharp warning is already one nonterminal state with an authenticated
exit probability \(p_t=1/(t+2)^2\): all probabilities are positive and
every frozen policy terminates, but adaptive survival tends to \(1/2\).
Thus pointwise positivity and a source-specific quantitative guarantee are
different assertions. On the actual 27/31 receipt graph, a supplied ROOT
word for 27 is still needed before the rank compiles ROOT(31). A closed
unrooted component cannot acquire a terminal by changing its weights.

## 4. Why the real boundary and the arithmetic boundary must be kept together

The [boundary-fibre packet](collatz_boundary_fibres_20261011.md) proves
\[
\frac{(n,n,1)}{2n+1}\longrightarrow(1/2,1/2,0)
\]
along every positive source sequence tending to infinity. Every initial odd valuation
occurs along such a sequence. Hence this one real boundary point cannot
determine the branch. Retaining the integer's 2-adic coordinate replaces
it by a fibre containing every odd 2-adic address and makes the coupled
Terras dynamics continuous.

For a word with matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}P&0&B\\0&Q&0\\0&0&Q\end{pmatrix},
\]
setting the marker to zero removes exactly the ordered carry \(B\).
The words \((1,3)\) and \((3,1)\) have multiplier \(9/16\), but carries
5 and 11 and disjoint native cells \(19,29\pmod{32}\). This realizes
[THM-4604's trace/carry distinction](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4604-collatz-words-are-the-reducible-locus-carries-are-the-extension-cocycle-and-traces-only-hear-the-tuning.md)
as a geometric boundary operation. The identities
\(\lambda\mapsto\lambda^2\) and \(J\mapsto J^2-2\), with
\(J=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}\), correctly describe that multiplier quotient;
they cannot supply the missing carry or guard.

The boundary has a second consequence. Actual Mersenne monitors
\[
n_k=4^k-1,\qquad x_k=T^k(n_k)=6^k-1
\]
approach the current-axis ray with both 2-adic addresses equal to \(-1\).
That limiting state is fixed by the coupled map. Consequently no continuous
rank on this compact closure can decrease by a uniform positive amount on
every positive nonterminal forward state. Source-dependent ranks, ranks
singular at the boundary, nonuniform drift, and authenticated child moves
remain possible. This is an obstruction to a proof ansatz, not to Collatz.

## 5. The next concrete interface: carry-sensitive credit and native reachability

The [run-credit interface](collatz_run_credit_interface_20261011.md)
combines the run family with the recovered account
\(E(x,k)=(x+5)(9/8)^k\). Every run move may earn one credit with the
sharp uniform ratio \(57/64\). Together with the inherited H move earning
two credits and the G move spending one, the potential proves payment
against the unchanged original source and finite episodes.

The carry makes the sharp difference. For \(r=4\), awarding two credits
would give
\[
\frac{E(m_4,k+2)}{E(n,k)}=1+\frac9{16(n+5)}>1,
\]
equal to \(513/512\) at 283. At the zero-marker limit this tends to 1.
The real boundary is therefore exactly where the failed credit award
appears neutral. This is a concrete proof-level connection between the
geometry, the affine carry, and the older adaptive controller.

Arithmetic reachability remains independent. A run child is 15 modulo 16,
which blocks the older H/G native guards. From a zero-credit start, the
H/G/R library in fact separates into H/G episodes and pure run episodes.
A compatible potential alone does not create a mixed native word. The
interface packet records the stronger five-letter guard separation and
the precise pre-funded entry exception.

An actual inverse \(G_5\) move can fund the entry neutrally, but adding it
without another budget creates the exact loop
\(283\to251\to283\). This is certificate recycling, with no net payment.
Thus the next missing object is an authenticated **strictly paid bridge
between the native components**, or a separately justified bound on neutral
recycling. We have an explicit interface inequality and native residues to
test, rather than an appeal to positivity or a new shape.

There is a useful positive change of granularity. Bundle the two native
steps in the pre-funded ray into a **new atomic action**, with zero credit
change. For \(x=251+288t\), \(t\ge0\), its child is
\(m=223+256t=(8x-1)/9<x\), and its authenticated receipt is
\[
x\xrightarrow{(1,2,1)}425+486t\xleftarrow{(1^4,3)}m.
\]
The all-height potential interface is
\[
\frac{m+5}{x+5}=\frac89+\frac4{9(x+5)}\le\frac{57}{64}<1.
\]
Thus the compound move is a valid zero-credit strict-descent import even
though its separate first G would be forbidden from an unfunded state.
The original controller checks payment at action endpoints, so its recursive
replacement principle permits this declared change of atomic action. This
does not retroactively make the old two-action execution legal. A positive
credit award would instead give \(1+1/[2(x+5)]>1\).

The benefit here is proof compression and safe scheduling, not coverage:
these inputs already have the smaller \(G_1\) child \(167+192t\), and
their residue 8 modulo 9 is absent from the old five-letter output phases.
An operation can therefore improve how a proof is packaged without yet
connecting the components that obstruct completion.

## Recommended proof targets and falsifiable hypotheses

1. **One grounded port before a larger bank.** Produce a supplied ROOT
   suffix for one of the 36 symbolic ports, or a source-owned receipt into
   a genuinely grounded bank. More internal edges alone do not improve
   the component's terminal status.
2. **A strict native bridge.** Search outside the saturated H/G/A/B/L
   transition classes for an authenticated operation that enters or leaves
   the run component and satisfies the old credit interface. Reject neutral
   loops and retain all first-hit guards. The barrier proved above gives
   exact excluded residues and identifies what a new operation must change.
3. **A rank that survives refinement.** For that declared bridge library,
   find one coarse row-polytope rank satisfying (1), and authenticate every
   concrete transition and ROOT fibre. Only then refine arithmetic states
   while preserving projected rows.

An **OPEN hypothesis**, not a claimed theorem, is that a useful controller
can use ranks indexed by arithmetic boundary cells, with explicit growth
near negative-cycle shadows and paid transitions between those cells.
The multiplier quotient suggests how to choose the growth scale; carries
and native cells decide whether the transitions exist. A finite collection
of such rules would require an actual exhaustive guard cover and verified
terminal bank. None has been obtained here.
