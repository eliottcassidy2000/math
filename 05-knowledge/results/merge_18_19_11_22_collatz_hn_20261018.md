## 2. Hadwiger–Nelson: the residue-colouring screen of the six-colour field

### 2.1 The conjugation-fixed places of `F = Q(ζ15, √-11, w)` (PROVED)

Complex conjugation `c` acts by `ζ -> ζ^-1`, `j -> -j`, `w -> w`. Lemma R (THM-4558) bounds `χ(G(F)) <= κ(q)` at every
`c`-fixed prime that is inert over `F⁺` with residue field `F_(q²)`; ramified fixed primes give 2 or 3.
- **No ramified prime is `c`-fixed.**
  - 2 and 37 are not fixed in `Q(ζ15)`: `⟨2⟩`, `⟨7⟩ ∌ -1` in `(Z/15)^×`.
  - The primes over 3 and 5 split in `K = Q(ζ15, j)` into conjugate pairs.
  - 11 is not fixed in `Q(ζ5)`, since `11 ≡ 1 (mod 5)` splits completely and `±1` are not primitive fifth roots.
- **An unramified prime `p` has a `c`-fixed prime of `F` above it iff three conditions hold.**
  - `p ≡ 14 (mod 15)`: this makes the prime `c`-fixed in `Q(ζ15)`, where `c` acts as Frobenius on `F_(p²)`.
  - `(p/11) = -1`: then `√-11 ∉ F_p`, so `c` fixes each prime of `K` above it.
  - The cubic `w^3 + 2w^2 - 4w - 4` has a root in `F_p` (residue `F_(p²)`), or is irreducible mod `p` (residue
    `F_(p^6)`, bound `κ(p^3)`).
- **The list** (`fixed_places_golden_field.py`):
  - residue `F_(p²)` at `p = 29, 239, 479, 569, 809, 1019, ...`;
  - residue `F_(p^6)` at `p = 149, 359, 659, ...`.
- clock_duality's G2 had already found the conjugation-fixed 29-place of `K`. Here it extends to `F` because `w ≡ 3` is
  a simple root of the cubic mod 29.

### 2.2 κ(29), and the conclusion for the six-colour programme

- **Upper bound (PROVED).** `κ(29) <= 7`, by an explicit, verified 7-colouring of `G_29 = Cay(F_841, μ_30)`
  (`kappa_29_colouring_k7.txt`, from `tabucol_gq.c`).
- **Lower bound (PROVED).** `κ(29) >= 5`: Hoffman's bound is 4.31. The Delsarte/Schrijver LP over the norm circles gives
  the same 195.2 bound on α, so symmetry does not sharpen it (`delsarte_gq.py`).
- **Evidence that κ(29) = 7 (HEURISTIC).** Tabu search with 6 colours stalls at 232–233 conflicts in three independent
  runs of `2·10^7` iterations each. 7-colourings are found in every run. Controls: 5-colourings of `G_19` are found at
  once (κ(19) = 5, decalion89); with 5 colours `G_23` stalls at 246 conflicts. A proof of `κ(29) >= 7` needs
  `α(G_29) <= 140` or an UNSAT certificate. Neither is attempted: decalion89 needed about 470 kissat-hours for κ(13) on
  169 vertices.
- **The other places (PROVED).** Every other fixed place has `q >= 149` (residue `F_(149^6)`) or `q >= 239`. By Hoffman
  with the Weil bound `|λ| <= 2√q`, `κ(q) >= 1 + (q+1)/(2√q) > 8.7` for `q >= 239`.

**Consequence.**
- If `κ(29) >= 7`, no residue colouring caps `G(F)` below 7 colours.
- So the field of clock_duality's six-colour construction is not closed by the known local technique. Conditionally on
  the heuristic, the programme survives the screen that S42 asked for ("a difference-forcing layer, or a no-go
  theorem").
- If instead some later computation gives `κ(29) <= 6`, then every unit-distance graph in `F` is 6-colourable, and the
  programme in `F` is dead. The decisive open computation is therefore exactly `κ(29) ∈ {5, 6, 7}`.

### 2.3 Why 19 appears in the six-colour construction (PROVED; explains THM-4690(5))

Let `F' = Q(ζ5, √-11, w)`, the subfield of `F` without `√-3` (`nineteen_place_subfield.py`).
- **The fixed 19-places of `F'`.** `19 ≡ -1 (mod 5)` makes the primes above 19 in `Q(ζ5)` conjugation-fixed with `c`
  acting as Frobenius, and `(-11/19) = -1` keeps them fixed in `Q(ζ5, √-11)`. Mod 19, the cubic in `h = w + 3` factors
  as `(h^2 + 11)(h - 7)`. Its only `F_19` root is 7, so exactly the four primes of type `h ≡ 7` are fixed in `F'`. The
  check: 12 primes above 19, 4 of them `c`-stable, all with `w ≡ 4`.
- **Lemma R on `F'`.** `χ(G(F')) <= κ(19) = 5`. This extends `rhombus_overlap_elliptic_20261011.md` G2's colouring of
  `Q(ζ5, √-11)` mod 19 by the cubic.
- **Lemma R.1 on `F'`.** Every unit vector of `F'` is a unit at these four places.
- **Where the coefficients sit.** `b, c ∈ Q(ζ5, √-11)` (audit F2) have simple poles there. So **no path of unit steps
  from `F'` joins two coefficient copies**, and in `G(F')` the copies lie in different components. This is
  THM-4690(5)'s separation at the `h ≡ 7` primes: `u` and `u_2` lie in `F'`, so they are units there automatically.
- **What `√-3` does.** The Moser spindle needs `√-3`. Since `19 ≡ 1 (mod 3)`, adjoining `√-3` splits each fixed 19-place
  of `F'` into a conjugate pair. In `F`, no 19-place is fixed (24 primes, 0 stable). The only small fixed place left is
  the 29-place of §2.2.
- **Connectors.** A connector between copies must therefore use unit steps that genuinely involve `√-3`. The
  K-directions `conj(γ)/γ` of THM-4690 are of this kind. If clock_duality's B2 (`Γ = ∩` of the local rings at the fixed
  primes) extends to `F`, such walks exist (CONDITIONAL).

So the 19 in the cubic recipe is the prime whose conjugation-fixed places make the golden coefficient field
5-colourable. The construction escapes that cap only through `√-3`.

## 3. Collatz: the clock tower in the energy ladder, and a conditional density exponent

### 3.1 The tower in the exact ideal energies (KNOWN values; the denominator pattern FINITE-EXACT)

The S41 audit (`a4_ideal_ladder.out`) and clock_duality (`root_clock.json`) computed the exact ideal energies
`E_0 = 34/7`, `E_1 = 32898/9709`, `E_2 = 572764898734162/222399981598543`, and more. Their reduced denominators are

    7 = (2^6 - 1)/9,   9709 = (2^18 - 1)/27 = 7·19·73,   222399981598543 = (2^54 - 1)/81,

that is, `(2^(L_d) - 1)/3^(d+2)` with `L_d = ord_(3^(d+2))(2) = 2·3^(d+1)`, the 3-adic clock tower of the 2026-10-06
mod-18/19 session (`nineteen_place_subfield.out`).
- **Mechanism (PROVED, clock_duality 3c).** The depth-`d` band coefficients have exact period `2·3^(d+1)` in the binary
  band index, because `B(x) = 4x + 1` has order `3^(d+1)` modulo `3^(d+1)` and its iterate multiplies sizes by
  `4^(3^(d+1)) = 2^(2·3^(d+1))`. In R7's case `d = 1`: order 9 modulo 9 and factor `4^9 = 2^18`. Summing one period
  against the geometric band weights, the denominator of `E_d` divides a fixed multiple of `2^(L_d) - 1`. That its
  reduced form is exactly `(2^(L_d) - 1)/3^(d+2)` is FINITE-EXACT for `d <= 2`.
- **Where 19 enters.** 19 enters at `d = 1` because `ord_19(2) = 18 = ord_27(2)`.
- **Merge with the l2 ledger** (`eighteen_band_l2.py`). Running R7's band method with squares instead of absolute
  values reproduces `E_1 = 32898/9709` and R7's `155896/87381`.

The exact clock-lag split of the first cross term `E_1 - E_0 = -99820/67963 = -1.4687403` is new. Pair sums over common
preimages, per `N`:

| Lag | Value |

apart.
