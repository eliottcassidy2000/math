# Primary-source verification

Fresh checks on 20 October 2026.

1. Janusz Gwozdziewicz, *Injectivity on one line*, arXiv:alg-geom/9305008v1, 19 May 1993, Theorem 1.1, PDF page 1. Original PDF opened successfully: https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9305008v1. The field is algebraically closed of characteristic zero; the map is polynomial k^2 to k^2 with nonzero constant Jacobian; injectivity on one affine line implies polynomial automorphism. No properness or global injectivity assumption is missing.

2. Avinash Sathaye and Jon Stenerson, *On Plane Polynomial Curves*, author text dated 10 September 2014, Section 3.1, printed pages 8-9, especially items (4) and (5). Original author-hosted PDF opened successfully: https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/papnew.pdf. Item (4) gives initial coordinate bounds c+1 and later-entry bounds c-1 for nonprincipal sequences. Item (5) gives occurrence of primitive generators. Section 3.1 immediately precedes these with the characteristic-zero one-place-at-infinity scope. The bounds are directly the ones needed here, not estimates guessed from low-conductor examples.

3. S. S. Abhyankar and Avinash Sathaye, *Uniqueness of plane embeddings of special curves*, Proceedings AMS 124 (1996), 1061-1069, Section 2, printed page 1062. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6. The indexed original PDF paragraph was retrieved directly through a targeted search at https://scispace.com/pdf/uniqueness-of-plane-embeddings-of-special-curves-omujaliiec.pdf. It identifies the first characteristic entries with the actual coordinate pole orders, then describes triangular power subtraction until a nonprincipal pair or a constant coordinate is reached. Whole-PDF opening at that mirror and at the AMS endpoint failed; no downloaded or byte-identical PDF is claimed. The quoted mathematical paragraph was available from the indexed original and independently agrees with the frozen predecessor audit.

No literature novelty claim is made. The uniqueness theorem in source 3 is not used, only its general coordinate-order paragraph.
