## 1. A complete binary return tree is sparse

Let B2(h)=3h+2 and B4(h)=3h+4. Put x=(h+1)/2. Then these two operations
are exactly

    x -> 3x,       x -> 3x+1.

Consequently a depth-k descendant of h0 has the fixed ternary prefix
(h0+1)/2 followed by k digits from{0,1}. There are exactly2^k descendants,
with minimum3^k(h0+1)-1 and maximum3^k(h0+2)-2. Distinct depths occupy
disjoint intervals. If X>=h0 and N(X) counts all such descendants at most X,
then

    N(X) <= 2 ((X+1)/(h0+1))^(log_3 2) - 1.

The same power bound holds for a fixed finite union of starting sources.
It gives a convergent sum of reciprocals of the represented positive
integers, by grouping them into dyadic intervals. Euler's elementary
divergence of the prime reciprocal series therefore shows that infinitely
many ordinary primes, and infinitely many values7p, remain outside any
such finite union. No prime-number-theorem assumption is needed. Removing
the finitely excluded7 and21 does not affect this argument. Alternatively,
the explicit central-binomial proof in
[GRAMMAR G5](../../source_selection_closure_20261103/GRAMMAR.md#g5-singular-choices-cannot-have-a-fixed-budget)
gives the required prime-count lower bound directly.
For a short self-contained justification of the reciprocal comparison:
convergence of the prime reciprocals would bound all finite Euler products
`product_(p<=X)(1-1/p)^(-1)`, whereas unique factorization makes that product
at least `sum_(n<=X)1/n`, which is unbounded. Guards removing forbidden
return words can only decrease the binary-tree count.

This bound concerns the declared B2/B4 output language. It does not bound
all outputs of an unrelated constructor used to realize those returns.
In particular the incoming219-key retained-mark reader has unbounded
suffix words; it is not a finite bank of fixed suffixes or fixed count
trees. Its exact inverse can accept other targets. Acceptance at all
required targets is still an independent obligation.

## 2. Adding the third letter gives exactly six required entries

Let A be the positive odd integers other than7 and21. Consider the three
unary maps M0(h)=3h, B2 and B4, allowing a move only when its output is in A.

**Theorem.** The unique minimal seed set whose closure is A is

    {1,23,25,63,65,67}.                                      (S1)

Minimality is with respect to these three unary maps, with1 supplied as a
seed rather than generated by an empty construction. It is not a theorem
that those six values already have compatible tournament interfaces.

Proof. Every positive odd N>1 has exactly one positive odd predecessor:

    N=0 mod3: (N/3, M0),
    N=2 mod3: ((N-2)/3, B2),
    N=1 mod3: ((N-4)/3, B4).

The predecessor is strictly smaller. For allowed N, it is forbidden
exactly at23,25 (predecessor7) and63,65,67 (predecessor21). Otherwise
repeated descent ends at1. This proves sufficiency by induction. Each
listed value has no allowed predecessor, proving necessity.

There is an exact tree interpretation. Set m=(h-1)/2. The three maps
become m->3m+1,3m+2,3m+3, the full ternary tree on nonnegative integers.
Forbidden7 and21 have addresses3 and10; the latter is a child of the
former. Removing these two vertices leaves precisely six components,
rooted at(S1). In bijective-base-three words these roots are

    empty, 32, 33, 311, 312, 313.

The component of1 has the two surviving first branches1 and2. Thus the
seven surviving boundary cylinders have prefix code
`1,2,32,33,311,312,313`, with Kraft sum1. This is an arithmetic entry
classification, not a relation between seven physical tournament ports.

For a general finite forbidden set in this rooted b-ary tree, the required
new seeds are the allowed children of forbidden vertices, together with
the original root if it survives. If the forbidden vertices avoid the root
and form c components, their number is1+(b-1)|F|+c. Here b=3,|F|=2,c=1.

## 3. Multiplication changes the minimal entry problem

Using arbitrary multiplication and B2/B4, the exact minimal seeds are

    {1,3,23,63,67}.                                         (S2)

This scalar grammar was already proved, without its minimality formulation,
in [balanced conditional C3](../../balanced_ternary_returns_20261103/conditional/NOTE.md#c3-the-arithmetic-descent-and-its-exact-exceptions).
Multiplication by the seed3 supplies M0, while25=5*5 and65=5*13 remove two
entries from(S1). Both5 and13 are built from1 and3. Conversely3,23,67 are
prime and have no allowed B2/B4 predecessor;63 has no proper factorization
into allowed integers and no B2/B4 predecessor. They and1 are mandatory.

If both M0 and arbitrary multiplication are primitive operations, the
minimal seeds reduce further to{1,23,63,67}, since M0 supplies3. These three
seed statements concern different operation alphabets and must not be mixed.

The multiplicatively irreducible elements of A other than1 are exactly

    {odd primes p !=7} union {7p : p odd prime, p!=3}
    union {63,343}.                                       (S3)

For proof, write n=7^a m with gcd(m,7)=1. The a=0 case gives ordinary
primes; a=2 gives the atom49, included as7p with p=7; a=3 gives343;
higher a factors through49 and343. For a=1, a prime m>=5 gives7m;
m=9 gives63; every other allowed composite m either splits off an
ordinary prime while leaving an allowed7-factor, or is3^k with k>=3,
which splits off3. Any additional m in the a>=2 cases factors separately.
Necessity follows because a proper factorization of any displayed atom
would require a forbidden factor7 or21, or would factor a prime.

This is the factorization mechanism of **PROVED** THM-4094, not a new
completeness theorem. SCC order joins realize multiplication; their
finite63/343 carry witnesses and the two infinite strong lanes are
sufficient. An inverse target in a prime lane can still require a
composite predecessor outside both lanes.

## 4. What a source-compatible proof would actually need

Here is a sufficient, explicitly **CONDITIONAL** constructor criterion.
Let C be a decidable class of finite named interfaces, each carrying an
actual tournament and enough joint data to execute its declared operations.
Assume:

1. Actual seed interfaces in C have every value in(S1).
2. For every I in C and d in{0,2,4} with3H(I)+d in A, a terminating
   constructor supplies a legal finite operation certificate to a child
   I' in C with H(I')=3H(I)+d.
3. The certificate tracks physical names, retained ports, every changed
   incidence, guards and any permitted discards; the child is accepted
   with its actual output type rather than a reset scalar representative.

Then the unique decreasing inverse in section2 constructs every member of A.
No extra arithmetic lemma remains. One can replace the universal single
class by typed subfamilies and proved entry maps, but those maps must
actually return a source accepted by the next requested letter. A weaker
scalar statement `there exists a source of this H for each separate arrow`
does not establish this condition.

This distinction has exact inherited controls. H35 is attained yet its
1/2-ready fibres are empty at every order; a different two-birth mechanism
does realize107 and109. Both facts are in
[balanced conditional C1](../../balanced_ternary_returns_20261103/conditional/NOTE.md).
At H31, a legal neutral native change alters which positive branch is
available although both sources have continuation gcd1;
[source-selection B5](../../source_selection_closure_20261103/BRANCH.md#b5-a-neutral-native-edge-repairs-the-two-positive-branches-from-a)
retains the actual port maps. Thus an integer predecessor, a primitive
gcd, a complete inverse algorithm, and source acceptance are different
predicates. The earlier finite target decider does not prove that every
allowed target is accepted; see
[coverage axiom boundary](../../coverage_machine_induction_20261103/axiom/AXIOM.md).

The newer multiport evaluator from incoming commit b38b531963,
`session181_multiport_defect_20261104/multiport/PROOF.md`, explicitly carries
`H=H(A)(h-J_W)+P_A(2)F_W` and defect `H(A)J_W`. It enlarges the available
interfaces but does not discharge the entry/return quantifiers above.
Incoming90746ce8ef/981b732965 likewise supplies three complete binary

## 5. The third letter is available, but its input/output type matters

An elementary actual tripler is the order join U=T->C3. For old ports r,s,
write H,E1,S1,D,E2,S2,P2,W2 for the standard endpoint and ordered two-slot
data. Directly splitting coloured paths at the ordered join gives

    H'=3H, P2'=12P2, D_r'=3D_r,
    E1_r'=0, S1_r'=3S1_r,
    E2_r'=3E2_r, S2_r'=12S2_r, W2_rs'=3W2_rs.

Thus end-defect E2-H triples and zero end-defect survives. Nevertheless U
is weak. The scalar multiplier has not returned to a strong input chart.
If future edits and births leave C3 as a separate unchanged terminal SCC,
every resulting H remains divisible by3, so neither3H+2 nor3H+4 is possible.
The spectator SCC must be absorbed or the source changed. This is an
operation-local obstruction, not an impossibility of a strong tripler.

Indeed the concurrent construction packet now supplies a **strong** tripler.
For C2(B,a), append x beating only old a, then y beating only x and losing
to every original vertex. Transport the auxiliary mark to x. The output
has S_x(2)=H; its joint invariant G_(w,x)=F_w makes the next C2 multiply
both(h,F_w) by3. Strongness is preserved for strong B. This is an actual
zero-offset channel, independently checked in `audit_c2.py`; it replaces
the weak-join route when its entry assumptions hold.

The alternate newborn y carries ancestor information:

    E_x(C2,1)=h(B),
    S_y(C2,2)-H(C2)=h(B)-E_a(B,1).

Thus using y at the next step gives
`3H(C2)+2(h(B)-E_a(B,1))`. The companion marked-end debt is
`3F_w(C2)+2(F_w(B)-J_old)`, where J_old counts the appropriate two
ending-slot covers from the construction packet. That debt is at least
E_w(B,1)>0 for strong B. These are carried ancestor counters, not free
choices of constant offset.

There is also an inherited all-marking obstruction. C2 is the converse
of the standard reset in [READY section4](../../two_entry_funnels_20261103/READY.md#4-re-marking-cannot-make-the-standard-reset-reusable).
For a **strong input of order at least4**, its C2 output has no vertex
with start-defect1 or2. Consequently another C2 at any auxiliary cannot
produce either positive offset. The hypothesis matters: C3 input produces
the strong H9 rows(26,20,25,2,8); repeating at x gives27, while y gives31.
This exception was independently checked by permutation enumeration.
The larger typed grammar needs a different digit macro or actual entry
transport; arbitrary auxiliary selection alone does not solve it.

The existing1537279 child supplies a concrete large entry failure. Its
induced B14 has H80319, minimum end-defect2960 at32 and minimum
start-defect616 at7. Neither polarity has a zero-defect vertex. Therefore
re-marking this exact suffix does not enter the C2 zero-offset chart;
the smallest C2 scalar output on it is242189, greater than3*80319.
This does not forbid a different count-preserving conversion or another
macro. Its named values are saved in `c2_audit.json`.
