# The normalized Gewirtz exact cover has no additive or congruence obstruction

This is a limitation on proof methods, not a sharply transitive set.
Let G be the full Gewirtz automorphism action on 56 vertices and D
the derangements in this action. For a permutation g let P_g be its
56×56 integral permutation matrix. Then the following holds:

    J−I belongs to the integral linear span of {P_g : g in D}.

In particular every system of weight tests given by integer linear
forms on a permutation matrix, including contradicting-subset or
weighted intersection divisibilities with coefficients in any
Z/m, is incapable of ruling out an exact cover. This holds even when
the normalized cover is constrained to use the identity and 55
derangements. It says nothing about simultaneous nonnegativity,
zero-one coefficients, or constraints involving products of chosen
permutations.

## Exact model

The attached standard-library replay constructs the 21 projective
points of PG(2,4), with the field generator satisfying a²+a+1=0.
It takes the PSL₃(4) orbit of a hyperoval (six points meeting each
line in zero or two points) and gets 56 blocks. Edges are disjoint
blocks. Our permutations are generated by the six elementary
transvections x_i←x_i+c x_j for (i,j)=(0,1),(1,2),(2,0), c∈{1,a},
the field automorphism, and duality by passing to the six disjoint
lines and identifying line covectors with points. This is the
[standard hyperoval model](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/math/srg/rk3/srgw.pdf).
The script checks adjacency preservation, evaluates the order 80640
of the generated group, and verifies the suborbits of a vertex
stabilizer have sizes 1,10,45. In fact the result holds for this
explicit subgroup of actual automorphisms, so no independent
upper bound on the full automorphism order is necessary.

## The binary certificate reaches the rational upper bound

The adjacency A has parameters (56,10,0,2), checked directly.
Thus A²=8I−2A+2J and its eigenvalues are 10,2,−4 with
multiplicities 1,35,20 (from the dimension and trace equations).
The full commuting matrix space over Q has dimension
1+35²+20²=1626, since a commuting matrix preserves the three
eigenspaces. All permutation matrices of automorphisms commute
with A, and those of derangements have zero diagonal.

The diagonal map on the commuting space has rank 56. To see this,
average the permutation matrices over the stabilizer of each
vertex. The resulting diagonal vectors, assembled as columns, are
exactly the columns of

    I + A/10 + (J−I−A)/45.

The suborbit sizes give these entries by orbit-stabilizer. The
matrix is invertible: its eigenvalue on constants is 3, and on
each of the two other eigenspaces it is 1+λ/10+(−1−λ)/45,
nonzero for λ=2,−4. Thus the rational rank of the derangement
matrix span is at most 1626−56=1570.

Direct binary elimination finds 1570 independent flattened
derangement matrices. Since the rational rank is also 1570,
the integral span is saturated at 2 in its rational subspace:
the elementary divisors of a generating matrix are all odd,
as follows by comparing its ranks mod 2 and over Q. The computation
does not require a rational basis search.

## Class sums remove all other possible denominators

For a conjugacy class consisting of derangements, the sum of
its permutation matrices has constant entries on adjacent cells
and on nonadjacent distinct cells, by the rank-three action. If
the class has size k and its elements send e vertices to adjacent
ones, these two entries are ke/560 and k(56−e)/2520 respectively.

The replay obtains classes with (k,e)=(280,20),(2520,8),(5760,14).
Let their matrices be U,V,W respectively; their pairs of entries
are (10,4),(36,48),(144,96). Thus

    −10U−V+W=8(J−I).
    2U+V=56(J−I).

The first relation shows that J−I belongs to the rational span
and that its class in the rational saturation modulo the integral
span has order dividing eight. The 2-saturation above says that
the class must be zero, proving the assertion. The second
relation gives a simple fractional nonnegative exact cover
of all off-diagonal cells by only two derangement classes.
Neither relation produces a 0–1 exact cover.
