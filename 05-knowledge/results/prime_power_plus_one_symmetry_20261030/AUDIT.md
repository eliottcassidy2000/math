**Directions.** In G5, H-invariance and r-converse transport are iff.
The smallest base multiplier p cannot preserve because its group contains
negation. When its order has more than one factor2, p cannot itself be
a reverser either; the exact power2^a is retained. The converse of a
symmetry threshold is not claimed without its probability assumptions.

**Quotient losses.** G1 records group orders, not full action types.
Scalar count, group order and unmarked joint-port carrier are distinct.
The root-of-unity map retains the primitive root and replaces differences
by ratios. Forgetting that map would confound preserving and reversing
Frobenius. The general moment recursion retains its growing moment index;
a fixed finite vector of moments is not claimed closed under composition.

**Weighted tail review.** The tilted orbit mass is
rho_i=g_m w_i H_i^(s-1)/M. IID gives equal w_i on an orbit and w_i<=a^m.
For s>=1 this is at most g_m^s a^m. This is the crucial missing bound
for rare high stabilizers. The exponent d counts cycle IDENTIFICATIONS,
not moved vertices. The bottom collision scales force the whole upper
error to vanish. Higher fixed moments provide uniform integrability for
every lower fixed moment, including0<s<1. No moment order growing with
depth or arbitrary return-weight functional is covered.

**Actual consequence.** The upper-symmetry event has probability o(1),
