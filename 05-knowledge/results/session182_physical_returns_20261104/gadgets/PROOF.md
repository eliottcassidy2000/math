## An actual one-birth tripler

Let a multiport tournament M(A;B,W) have A=TT2 with physical vertices a→b. Add x with b→x→a, with root r→x and x→B. All old edges remain unchanged. Thus the prefix becomes the actual directed triangle C3 on a,b,x; the suffix and actual port set W are unchanged. If B is strong and W is nonempty, both source and output are strong.

The general multiport formula is

H(M(A;B,W))=H(A)(h−J_W)+P_A(2)F_W.

Here H(TT2)=1, P_TT2(2)=4, H(C3)=3, and P_C3(2)=12. The one birth therefore multiplies H by exactly3 for every suffix and every physical port set. The marked-root defect multiplies from J_W to3J_W. These claims do not require J_W=0.

More generally a prefix A that is an ordinal sum L→TT2→R admits the same local replacement. Hamilton counts and empty-allowed ordered two-path-cover counts each multiply under ordinal sums: a path is the concatenation of its block paths, and each of the two slots splits independently into the block slots. Therefore the replacement multiplies both H(A) and P_A(2) by3, giving the same exact tripling. Empty L or R are permitted with the usual empty-product convention. Further disjoint TT2 blocks permit further triples. This supplies multiplication, not the missing additive digits.

## The module obstruction is all-order and physical

If a tournament contains a homogeneous directed-triangle module C={a,b,x}, cyclically rotating its three physical vertices and fixing all other vertices is an actual automorphism of order3. Its action on Hamilton paths is free: no ordered list of all distinct physical vertices can be fixed by this nonidentity rotation. Consequently its Hamilton-path count is divisible by3.

Thus **no amount of external editing, external births or deletions, or changes in the root's other ports can produce3H+2 or3H+4 while preserving that homogeneous triangle module**. The obstruction holds for arbitrary external tournament size and does not assume strongness. A successful correction must break the triangle's internal cycle or its uniform external incidences, delete one of its vertices, or otherwise destroy this actual symmetry. This is an explicit graph automorphism, not an identification with F49/F343 observer phases or inherited81/27 automorphisms.
