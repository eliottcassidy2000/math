# Theorem targets with decisive tests

**OPEN targets, not conclusions of the proved packet.** P1–P6 are useful
because they localize the missing properties. Replacing a hard conjecture
with its own exact feasibility problem is not an algorithmic advance.

## 1. Native clocked rounding with target control

A suitable next theorem would give, for the native source/clock controller,
a family of rounding maps R_E with all of the following explicit properties:

1. Inputs are feasible finite local measures on the actual level-E states
   and allowed transitions, retaining source residue, entry phase, ports
   and any lifts required at a precision increase.
2. Output is a legal realized transition certificate with the same designated
   source and the required terminal condition.
3. A specified potential or objective loses at most a proved factor/defect
   smaller than the available slack, uniformly in E.
4. The local measures exist for every required actual source. Their
   denominator or support-face obstruction is certified, not assumed.

This is more demanding than ordinary marginal consistency. P4 supplies an
exact obstruction test once the actual finite equality system is built;
P3 supplies rounding only after the constraint-preserving operation is
proved. A chart with fixed Collatz valuation is increasing, so P1 already
descends covers of every degree. **The unresolved step is changing charts
with the original source retained.**

The cheapest next experiment is the seven open E12 price families in the
terminal-fees packet. Extract the local constraints with their actual
lifted source ports. Compare the maximal-support face and Smith two-part
before and after identifying entry clocks. A quotient that changes the
minimum cover-denominator exponent or accepts a native-infeasible terminal
condition has already failed, before any global convergence claim.

A second test uses the28 already certified LRC cores: keep each winding
chart and each boundary owner, then compare actual first-arrival
certificates with the proposed local marginals. Point components and
boundary equalities need explicit atoms. Positive mass alone does not
ensure that median rounding retains a designated point; a strict majority
would, but constructing that majority uniformly is a new obligation.

**Mechanism for a major advance:** a uniform, source-preserving theorem
with a genuinely favorable cost bound could turn finite local certificates
into global controls. Current P5 only supplies a factor approaching two
for nonnegative convex unary costs; point indicators have unbounded loss.
The native potentials have not been proved to meet a sufficient closure or
cost hypothesis. Neither E11 success nor a large finite census proves
uniformity or excludes exceptional Collatz sources. The same machinery
must continue to admit the known5x+1 cycle.

## 2. Collision-safe descent for graceful and square-sum labels

The signed equation layer is now completely classified by P2. Selecting
edge labels and keeping vertex/edge labels distinct is the remaining
problem. A useful theorem would identify a **constructible attachment
invariant** that guarantees simultaneous collision-free median or
alternative rounding while retaining every prescribed edge label.

A basic sufficient condition illustrates the required information. For
each pair of vertices u,v, require all values in the u-fibre to lie
strictly below all values in the v-fibre, or the reverse. Their medians
are then distinct. This condition is transparent but too restrictive:
it chooses a global order and offers no reason that every tree admits
a compatible lift. Claiming that the lift exists would assume the hard
part.

The next candidate is a joint-order/transversal sidecar: retain the order
relations between fibre supports together with Hall-type constraints
for the possible chosen representatives. A theorem would have to prove
that representatives satisfying those constraints can also preserve
all signed edge equations and the entire edge-label permutation.
Ordinary Hall feasibility alone is insufficient because the edge
equations couple the choices.

**Cheapest decisive test:** exhaust the smallest tree attachments with
two shared ports, compare the exact feasible labelings with the proposed
sidecar, and include the cyclic-permutation median collapse(1,1,1).
Also include square edges(1,3),(2,7),(3,1), whose coordinate median(2,3)
has sum5, despite all original sums being squares. A proposed operation
must either preserve a fixed square label per edge or justify how label
changes remain in the allowed relation.

**Mechanism for a major advance:** an inductively constructible,
collision-safe attachment invariant for all trees would address the
actual missing part of a graceful-label construction. The signed-incidence
gauge or the two-primary solvability theorem alone does not do so.

## 3. Marked low-precision tournament recursion

The inherited second-bit XOR detector already recovers every labelled
core arc using a family of contextual attachments. This yields a serious
lower-bound constraint on any proposed smaller carrier: preserving all
those named responses entails retaining the core orientation.

A useful next theorem should therefore specify a restricted attachment
language before minimizing memory. One candidate target is a recurrence
for the joint response module modulo2^h under a fixed family of strong
atom attachments, with:

- actual named ports, orientation gauge and admissible re-markings;
- exact stabilizer action, including orbit multiplicities;
- converse compatibility on that same representation;
- a proof of which contexts distinguish states at precision h.

Odd tournament automorphism orders permit division by the group order
modulo2^h, but they do not provide that order. The81/27 pair remains a
mandatory control. Equal cyclic triples, equal H, or equal scalar
residues cannot replace group and port data.

**Cheapest decisive test:** restrict the contextual XOR experiments to
the actual strong-atom attachment language and compute their span.
If the restricted probes still recover every arc, abandon an
orientation-forgetting carrier for that language. If they do not,
construct the first separating legal continuation before accepting
the quotient. Track the full marked response through a recurrence,
not just the current response value.

The inherited OCF truncation
H mod2^h = sum_(j<h) 2^j alpha_j is an exact finite precision formula,
not a new result. Carries mean it is not an inverse formula for the
alpha_j. An eventual A000568 speedup needs efficient class construction,
canonical augmentation or exact orbit enumeration in addition to
Hamiltonian statistics. This packet supplies no new term or asymptotic
enumeration bound.

## 4. A general obstruction theory worth pursuing

P4b gives a complete invariant for the **minimum two-adic part of a finite
cover degree** in any finite local constraint model. It can be generalized
prime by prime using Z_(p). This suggests recording a local cover profile

    e_p = minimum v_p(D) over satisfying finite cover degrees D.

Each coordinate is computable from the same maximal support face and
Smith data. The minima for different primes are not asserted to be
simultaneously attained by one smallest cover, and neither connectedness
nor automorphism data is encoded.

There is a small exact warning: take a cyclic permutation with one2-cycle
and one3-cycle, used as the closing edge after two identity edges of a
triangle. It has no fixed point, hence no base assignment, but it admits
covers of degrees2 and3. Every primewise minimum e_p is zero, even though
degree1 is impossible. Matching around the triangle forces constant value
multiplicities on each permutation cycle; conversely disjoint unions of
the two lifts realize every2a+3b>0. Thus even the
entire primewise profile cannot replace the actual semigroup or a
constraint-preserving rounding theorem. This contrasts with the positive
P3 median/XOR cases and is checked in the native controls.

The late incoming Session119 gives a complementary arithmetic warning:
two seven-step Collatz words have the same cyclic Smith group of order139
but different ordered-carry membership. Both have vanishing two-adic
denominator obstruction. Keep the right-hand-side coordinate and the
odd-primary conditions when testing native arithmetic controllers.

A concrete next question is whether the native paired-port recursion
updates these support faces and Smith presentations without expanding
all states. The required theorem must preserve the actual tuple support,
not merely the rank of an unconstrained equality matrix. The face-hostile
in P4 is the first control; a cyclic q-state triangle provides exact
test values e_p=v_p(q). Marked-source costs form an additional layer.

This would give a reusable obstruction calculus across arithmetic,
graph covers and constraint systems. It would not by itself imply
Sha finiteness, Fermat-prime exhaustion, Collatz convergence or ETH
failure: each requires a valid encoding and a separately proved
complexity or uniformity theorem.
