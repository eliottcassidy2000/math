## Exact consequence and proof check

Let `T` be the quotient of the free group on `a,b,c,d` by fixedness
of all four entries under each of the six displayed chronological
Hurwitz words. Root independently read the full note and source,
checked the prefix/core pairs, and reconstructed the quotient argument.

With `e=bcb^-1`, the three cusp cores are exactly the braid relations
on `(b,c)`, `(b,d)`, and `(a,e)`. The node cores are commutation
of `(e^-1ae,b)`, `(a,d)`, and `(e,bdb^-1)`; conjugation by `b^-1`
turns the last relation into `[c,d]=1`. Every full word has the literal
form prefix, core, inverse prefix. There is no replacement of access
paths by separately chosen conjugates. A square fixes a pair if and
only if it commutes; a cube fixes it if and only if it satisfies the
braid relation. These elementary group facts hold without permutation
or finite-order assumptions.

In `B3=<p,q | pqp=qpq>` set `a=c=d=q`, `b=p`.
The first two cusp relations are immediate. The identity
`e=pqp^-1=q^-1pq` transports the braid relation on `(q,p)` to
the third cusp pair `(q,e)`. Direct use of the defining relation gives
`e^-1qe=p`, so the first node compares `p` with itself, and the
other nodes compare `q` with itself. Both `p` and `q` are generator
images; the resulting map `T -> B3` is surjective.

For nonabelianity and infinitude, the explicit matrices
`p=((1,1),(0,1))`, `q=((1,0),(-1,1))` satisfy the defining
relation and fail to commute. The identity
`p^n=((1,n),(0,1))` for all integers `n` follows by multiplication
or induction, not from extrapolating the twenty displayed finite checks.
The matrix image is infinite. Hence the literal fixedness relations
cannot force cyclicity or equality of all four generators.

The second hostile is independently decisive for the proposed
cusp-generation shortcut. In `S4` take
`a=d=(34)`, `b=(23)`, `c=(12)`, so `e=(13)`.
The first node is the disjoint pair `(14),(23)`; the second is an
equal pair; the third is the disjoint pair `(12),(34)`. All three
cusp pairs share one transposition label and therefore braid. Their
respective images each generate an `S3` fixing one label, whereas the
full image generated by adjacent transpositions is `S4`. If any one
of the corresponding cusp subgroups were the whole group `T`, its
image in every quotient would equal the full image. This explicit
proper-image control disproves that assertion. It also has `a!=c`,
so the particular substitution used to obtain `B3` is not claimed to
