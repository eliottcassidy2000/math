# Independent review of the elementary derivative bad-prime theorem

20 October 2026. Verdict: PASS for the exact stated scope.

The reviewer derived the positive and negative cases independently before inspecting the unified quotient proof. For |c|>=2 and odd prime ell not dividing c+1, the quotient (c^ell+1)/(c+1) is positive, odd, and greater than one. Its polynomial values modulo c and c+1 show the required coprimality. Every prime factor therefore gives c^ell=-1 but c!=-1, forcing exact order 2ell; distinct ell cannot share such a prime. The special values c=-1,1,0 are correctly separated. No prime-distribution theorem or unproved Kummer compatibility is needed for infinitude.

The odd reduction kernel at prime powers preserves the 2-primary part of the order. If an odd-order unit h carried a nonzero ring residue x to -x, raising h to its odd order would give x=-x, impossible in an odd ring. Thus the orbit-pair construction covers nonunits as well as units. Complete graphs are sign-odd through a vertex transposition, independently of their even vertex degrees.

The proof does not establish density, all desired residue-class refinements, or a no-go theorem for arbitrary nonlinear dilation rules. The separate affine obstruction is invoked only with its stated prime and type-bijection scope.

Independent standard-library trial division checks 89 admissible pairs with c from -20 through 20 and ell in {3,5,7}; it verifies least prime factors, exact-order divisors, and disjoint order parameters. Normal and optimized receipts agree. These finite controls supplement, not replace, the elementary proof.
