# Exact crossings, spectral guards, and the cost of separating scales

This session carries two ideas from the external work into the Chan–Pak construction: keep the full compatibility relation at a seam, and certify the error or retained data before passing between scales. The tournament proofs are self-contained apart from the cited standard results. Neither external theory is a reduction to Chan–Pak.

## External reading and prior repo connections

- The [Yang–Mills group's November 10 assessment](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/YM) validates the October 28 construction as a route from Wilson lattice theory to continuum correlations and Wightman reconstruction for simple compact gauge groups. Its general infinite-volume step uses subsequences. Uniqueness and a mass gap are not asserted. This extends the perspective of the repo's [earlier reflection-positivity margin and rotation notes](../pythagorean_form_conic_graphs_rp_margin_20261018.md); in the tournament model the finite Gram matrix and tails can be written down explicitly.
- The [OpenAI no-five-coloring formalization](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/158.md) addresses arbitrary plane color classes. [Shukla's November paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06269) gives a different kind of result: explicit five-chromatic finite witnesses in prescribed small disks, e.g. 773 vertices in radius 3/2. [Haugland's sevenfold construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04542) gives a structured five-chromatic witness with no Moser spindle. [Koshelev–Kupavskii–Voronov](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.18907v1) exploit labeled finite compatibility to remove exceptional pairs in Euclidean three-space. The distinction among an obstruction, an explicit witness and a complete boundary relation connects to the repo's [six-color port work](../six_colour_interface_profile_20261015f.md).

## Tournament results

1. **[An exact bounded-checksum interface](CHROMATIC_INTERFACE.md).** Finite lists of normalized leading coefficients exhaust any fixed checksum budget at sufficiently high scales. Their intersections give the persistent adjacent odd pairs `59·2^j+{37,39}` and `117·2^j+{97,99}`, from literal host/core switches. The complete lists through checksum 99 have no three-entry run at a common large clock.
2. **[Absolute crossings and their exact obstruction](CHECKSUM_CROSSING.md).** With two transitive cores every non-power-of-two high coefficient is attainable, but has just one possible low checksum. Changing orders only moves a terminal part across the seam. A cyclic middle core gives three further adjacent pairs, including `1281(2^g+1)−2` and `1281(2^g+1)`. The displayed pair cannot be freely recentered by a common prefix.
3. **[Square-root cost of separating the scales](HEIGHT_TARIFF.md).** All carry-free separated high/low words with a changing binary host and arbitrary independent ordinal sums of one-apex cores yield only Θ(sqrt X) distinct counts through X. This includes the growing common-quotient reservoirs, not merely a fixed menu. It says precisely why the new exact adjacent pairs do not grow to universal coverage by this grammar alone.
4. **[A reflection-positive seam](YANG_MILLS_BOUNDARY.md).** Centered converse gluing yields two rank-two Gram channels with square principal determinants and exponentially controlled boundary limits. A shared first active shell ties the two absolute counts together and rules out infinitely many proposed profiles satisfying the elementary screens.
5. **[A spectral carry filter and real third-slot switch](SLOT_SPECTRAL.md).** The odd-cycle scale polynomial gives an exact bounded-base-4 feasibility test for its coefficient relaxation. Matching the first k slot observations quantizes the next difference by a factorial. Two literal strong order-eight cores agree at H=345 and Q_2=5640 but differ by exactly 720 at Q_3. A three-visit host reads this as a 2880 change in H. The shift is real but is not a unit step.
6. **[Fixed-H tightness](FIXED_COUNT_TIGHTNESS.md).** The reduced strong-component bank is finite at fixed H, and singleton SCCs account for every dyadic scaling of Q_2. An explicit finite 2-adic precision is therefore sufficient for exact identification if H was preserved as an integer. No indefinite increasing affine Q_2 update at fixed H exists except dyadic scaling.
7. **[Unit-distance projection obstruction](CHROMATIC_PROJECTION.md).** Although a designated unit-distance graph can embed among selected odd cycles, an extra universal cycle strictly distorts its selected hard-core odds whenever it is connected and needs at least four colors. Exact incidence alone does not preserve the weighted marginal.

The next constructive task is to use a low-block carry with its full interface preserved, or to move outside the one-apex ordinal alphabet so richer profiles can pay the square-root tariff. The exact +2 families are useful controls for either route. Chan–Pak remains open.
