The initial completion theorem allowed a global coupled factor while claiming that a resource matching, with any independent edge decorations, was sufficient. This is false without an edgewise hypothesis: two uniquely available distinct resources can carry incompatible aggregate charges.

The producer repaired section 2 to require that every remaining nonresource constraint factor edgewise after fixing shared coordinates. Its matching iff and independent-decoration conclusion are now correct in that scope. A coupled guard remains an additional full condition; the repaired text explicitly routes it to a valid factor join tree or retained residual-state tables. The audit includes a matching-success/global-guard-failure hostile.

The raw labelled K and converse actions commute and act freely for n >= 3. Each full demand block consumes its actual four-port orbit. Removing every orbit meeting the occupied remainder is necessary even if the remainder contains only one member and is not closed. These facts give the exact matching reduction. Nonclosed or multi-orbit demands require the stated richer configuration model.

The laminar proof is sound: after removing unavailable resources, processing neighborhoods by increasing cardinality leaves at most d(S)-1 previously selected resources in S. Repeated neighborhoods count with multiplicity. The independent tests include empty neighborhoods, so a demand with no resource is not silently dropped. ## 3. Resource carrier: raw lifts versus original normalized tilings

Two different notions must not be conflated:

- A raw resource is a literal owner-labelled matrix/path K/converse orbit.
- An original metagraph tiling resource is a normalized F/R word orbit. It has unit capacity even when several raw owner/automorphism lifts represent it. Full lifts are witness decorations, not new tiling capacity.

The producer added section 2a to state this distinction and its normalized-resource matching specialization explicitly. For n >= 3, F is fixed-point-free and R fixes the outermost clock-pair position, so FR is also fixed-point-free; R-fixed words give normalized tiles of size two, while free words give size four. Raw four-port closure alone therefore does not establish pure-black weight two. The raw matching theorem is valid for its stated carrier. Applying it to the original metagraph tiling inventory requires the normalized resource quotient and its occupied-resource quotient, with the full owner lifts retained above each edge. The finite three-source minimum is in this normalized original tiling carrier, not a statement about a different raw-labelled multiplicity convention.

Thus neither positive construction manufactures capacity through hidden lifts, and the carrier distinction does not weaken either positive certificate.
