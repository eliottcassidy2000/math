# Charge-neutral workspace cannot erase a projected atomicity barrier

## 1. Two different reasons a multi-slot assembly can be frozen

The new incoming `guarded_coverage_20261023/PORTS.md` proves a four-layer
workspace theorem when injection is the only changing cross-slot constraint.
Actual new resources then let one slot park while another moves. Its native
realization appends two rigid eight-owner blocks; this changes the source and
provides distinct normalized resources, not extra credits from hidden lifts.

Our large circuits are frozen for a different reason. Their complete pinned
binary menus have just two assignments preserving both target margins and the
full original dyadic current bank. All resource alternatives are already
mutually disjoint. More vacancy does not itself pay an electrical defect.
The following elementary projection criterion makes the distinction precise.

## 2. Projection obstruction theorem

Let X be an original s-slot state space, with a guarded subset A. Let W be any
enlarged state space and let pi:W -> X retain the s actual slot choices. Assume:

1. Every legal enlarged state w projects into A. In particular, all original
   shared pins, margin conditions and current equations are still satisfied
   after projection. Actual owner coordinates are not silently identified.
2. Each permitted enlarged event changes at most b coordinates of pi(w).
3. Desired endpoints project to a and c in A.

Then every enlarged legal route projects to a walk in the graph on A joining
pairs at Hamming distance at most b (with repetitions allowed). Consequently,
if a and c lie in different components of that graph, no enlarged route exists.

Proof: apply pi to each successive state. Hypothesis 1 places each projected
state in A, and hypothesis 2 places every nonconstant consecutive pair on an
allowed edge. This is exactly the asserted projected walk. QED.

In particular, if A={0^s,1^s}, any enlarged route between these alternatives
must contain an event changing all s projected slot choices. Arbitrarily many
extra neutral coordinates, resources, kernel decorations or waiting steps do
not lower this bound while hypotheses 1 and 2 remain true.

The statement is independent of the number or geometry of the fibers of pi.
It is a necessary obstruction, not a sufficient lifting criterion: projected
connectivity alone does not supply compatible native events or resources.

## 3. Conditional consequence for the audited circuits

For the six complete pinned H256 families constructed in this session,
A={0^s,1^s} for s=6,8,10,12,14,16. The same accepted set describes preservation
of the entire original dyadic bank, by the audited exponent saturation.
Any proposed workspace extension that retains these original binary choices
and whose legal states project to these same guarded menus must therefore
retain atomic support s. This includes charge-neutral auxiliary decorations
when their deletion leaves the original margins and current signature intact.

This is NOT a claim that every tournament source extension admits pi with
these properties. Appending blocks changes the ambient tournament and may
change its metagraph, critical group and target identities. Such an extension
must supply a separately authenticated projection before this theorem applies.
Nor does the theorem exclude additional genuine path alternatives outside the
original two-option slot menus, or compensating auxiliary charges. Those
change the projected state space or its legal guard condition and deserve
independent investigation with their actual inventory and integer clocks.

## 4. Why the incoming Gray-cycle repair escapes the obstruction

In the original Gray-cycle assignment, the obstruction is unit resource
capacity. During the incoming buffer repair, two slots may have the same base
port in different new layers. They are injective as enlarged resources, but
forgetting the layer need not give an injective original assignment. Thus
hypothesis 1 fails if A means original injective assignments. There is no
contradiction: new workspace relaxes the projected capacity obstruction.

For our mutually disjoint binary circuit supplies, projection of neutral
resource layers does not relax the electrical equation. To defeat that barrier,
a proposed repair needs one of the following explicit changes:

- an event with the full required projected support;
- genuinely new projected path alternatives with retained shared coordinates;
- extra charged slots whose full row contributions cancel the intermediate
  defect, with distinct available normalized resources;
- a different ambient guard together with a proved, stated comparison map.

The third option is the natural continuation of this session's successful
multi-source cancellation. Its charge budget must be accounted for at every
physical owner, not only in a scalar total. A borrowed compensator must return
with its original resource and current ledger restored; a hidden lift is not
an additional port.
