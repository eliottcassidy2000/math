## 6. Pairwise consistency can conceal a genuine current obstruction

The sixteen-slot mod 8 family has 196 accepted states, but EVERY pair of literal slot choices has all four possible values. Its pairwise closure therefore contains 65536 states. Pairwise resource and owner tests cannot replace the actual current predicate.

A concrete witness is the three accepted masks 0,1806,6500. Their coordinatewise majority is 260, which still preserves target margins but violates the mod 8 current guard. Every 2-CNF relation is majority-closed, and existential auxiliary bits preserve this property. Thus no 2-SAT representation retaining these visible literal choice bits can describe that mod 8 relation.

The stronger H256 relation is much simpler to describe: all bits must agree. It is one free complementary implication-component pair, and flipping it changes all sixteen slots. Increasing guard precision therefore simplifies the Boolean description while increasing the required atomic move size. Observation complexity and move complexity are different invariants.

The concurrent binary-assembly theorem is valid after its interface is authenticated. Our exact H256 families fit it after their global relation has been certified; the larger H8 families do not. The 10- and 12-slot cases also have majority obstructions, despite their accepted-state counts being powers of two.

## 8. A conditional all-size supply rule

Let G be the relevant finite paired-charge group and suppose N actual binary source options, one at each of N distinct source owners outside the target pair, are already jointly compatible in their pins, lifts and disjoint resources. Assume the two targets have equal paired charge. If binom(N,k)>|G|, two k-subsets have equal charge sum. Cancel their intersection to obtain disjoint positive and negative source sets. These give two injective packet alternatives with the same margins and owned current signature.

For |G|=2048, fourteen compatible options suffice by binom(14,7)=3432. This is the threshold of this particular counting criterion, not a claimed optimal threshold. With distinct labels, sixty-five options instead force a disjoint pair-sum collision and hence a four-source relation. A witness-retaining finite-group dynamic program constructs the collision without searching all signed assignments.

The theorem is conditional on authenticated native supply. It constructs compatible alternatives, not a route from every prescribed starting assignment or a global remainder. The group and its size can change with tournament order, so fourteen is not an order-independent theorem about every metagraph.
