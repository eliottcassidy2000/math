# Wave 24 pre-registration (mac-mini, written before any wave-24 program was run)

Registered: 2026-11-14T23:10:34Z

Setting. The *invertible root-change language*: a one-apex source word (any O/Z word), then any sequence of
invertible blocks J and J O v Z (v any O/Z word, unbounded length). In letters: after the first J, a Z may not
follow a J, and a J may not follow an O. No singular block (J O^k followed by J, J O..O) ever occurs, so by
THM-5120 no reset can occur and no state becomes p-divisible after the source.

Heuristic used for the predictions. Pressure equation sum_b c_b^(-s) = 1 with c_J = 1.84 (tribonacci) and
c_b ~ kappa 2^n for the 2^(n-2) blocks of length n: s* ~ 1.17 (kappa = 3) to 1.24 (kappa = 2). The Poincare
series diverges at s = 1, so the language is supercritical, with multiplicity exponent about 0.15-0.25.

- **P51 (coverage).** The invertible language has per-window miss counts that decrease on the dyadic windows
  [2^16, 2^17) ... [2^21, 2^22), and at most 0.1% of the odd numbers of [2^21, 2^22) are missed.
- **P52 (super-approximation).** The group generated by the invertible blocks (J, JOZ, JOOZ, JOZZ, JOOOZ, JOZOZ,
  JOZZZ) maps onto G_p = {g in GL_3(F_p) : det g in <2>} for every odd prime p. Test: exact BFS at p = 7
  (|G_7| = 16,892,064); a proof for all odd p via a unipotent element plus the subgroup structure of SL_3(p).
- **P53 (local law washes out).** For the invertible language on [2^21, 2^22), the ratio (mean multiplicity over
  odd N prime to p) / (overall mean) lies in [0.80, 1.00] at p = 3 and in [0.93, 1.02] at p = 5, 7, 11, 13
  (11-block orbit: 0.405, 0.672, 0.930, 0.958, 0.973), and the mean multiplicity of primes is at least half the
  overall mean (11-block orbit: 0.164).
- **P54 (prime exponent).** For the invertible language, the fitted growth exponents of the overall mean
  multiplicity and of the prime mean multiplicity (windows 2^16 ... 2^22) differ by at most 0.03 (11-block orbit:
  0.38 versus 0.25).
- **P55 (L^2 local limit).** The normalized variance V(X) of r(N)/(local model) over odd N in [X, 2X), the local
  model being the product of the empirical class factors mod 3, 5, 7, 9, decreases from 2^17 to 2^21.

## Addendum (registered 2026-11-14T23:12:58Z, after the mode-1 runs to 2^22 and the mode-2 miss list at 2^20, before any mode-2
## window statistics were printed)

Mode 2 = empty source: the word starts like a block (J, or O v Z then J ...), so the initial state (1,1,1,1) is
acted on only by invertible letters-blocks. Every source state then has E = 1 or is (1,0,2,1): no state is
p-divisible at any odd p. If the block group maps onto G_p (P52) and the walk mixes, H mod p is the e1-coordinate
of a uniform nonzero vector of F_p^3, so the class 0 is DEPLETED: P(H = 0 mod p) = (p^2-1)/(p^3-1).

- **P56 (exact local law without a source).** In mode 2 on [2^21, 2^22), the ratio (mean multiplicity over odd N
  prime to p)/(overall mean) is within 0.01 of p^3/(p^3-1) = 1.0385, 1.0081, 1.0029 at p = 3, 5, 7, and within
  0.005 of 1 at p = 11, 13.
- **P57 (coverage without a source).** Mode 2 misses no odd N in [2^21, 2^24).
- **P58 (source law).** In mode 1 the class-0 excess is the source's p-divisible mass: the ratio equals
  (1 - pi_p) p^3/(p^3-1) with pi_p the by-value share of words whose source state is p-divisible; in particular
  the mode-1 ratio at p = 3 stays in [0.94, 0.97] on every window [2^k, 2^(k+1)), 16 <= k <= 23.

## Addendum 2 (registered 2026-11-14T23:35:12Z, before the spectral program was written)

- **P64 (uniform gap of the GL_3 walk).** Let P_p be the transition operator of the random walk on F_p^3 \ {0}
  that applies one of the 16 invertible blocks with |w| <= 5 (J, OZ, OOZ, OZZ, and the 4 + 8 blocks of lengths 4
  and 5), uniformly at random. For every odd prime p <= 43 (p = 3 included), the second-largest eigenvalue modulus of
  P_p is at most 0.60. For comparison, the rank-one digit operators of HYP-9660 (B) reach 0.932 (at modulus 7).
