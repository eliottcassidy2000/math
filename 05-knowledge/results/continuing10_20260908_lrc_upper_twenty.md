For each margin pair (a,b), the producer enumerates all compatible strict
atlas ratios p<q, with e=gcd(a,b), n=t/e and

    {e gcd(n,p),e gcd(n,q)}={a,b}.

It takes the exact minimum over all real translates of the intersection count
for the literal open sets ||px||<1/14 and ||qx||<1/14, then minimizes over those
ratios and multiplies by e. All walls and open chambers are retained. Positional
minimum spanning trees use these weights; disconnected possible graphs have
no actual connected realization. The already proved theorem gives safe-grid
credit M_t(d)-E_t(d), with

    E_t(d)=sum_i d_i ceil(t/(7d_i))-t.

No maximum tree on merely possible edges is used. Native edge ratios and
their minimizing phases need not coexist: every individual lower bound is
uniform, which is exactly the sufficient direction the inherited proof needs.

## Result and failure boundary

Every word at every selected clock has strictly positive credit. The minimum
over the entire declared universe is 133, attained at

    t=11952,
    d=(1,6,8,8,9,36,48),
    E=56, M=189.

One minimum tree has five zero-weight edges and the edge between the 1-margin
and 9-margin positions with weight 189. Thus the positive result does not
require every pair to have a positive overlap. Positional connectivity forces
the remaining expensive connection. This explains why a preliminary analytic
uniform bound, six times the smallest compatible component-bound edge weight
minus an unrestricted marginal excess cap, failed at all twenty clocks: its
edge floor is zero. That failure concerns the coarse certificate only.

For a physical hypothetical failure, the CITED proper-six supplier gives a
safe phase for A/t; its t lifts preserve those six clearances. The inherited
sheet map turns the complementary B/g grid into a translated t-grid, to which
the positive minimum-tree credit applies. Therefore these twenty clocks are
excluded in precisely the same connected-complement scope as continuing8.

After the separately proved 7200 deletion and these twenty exclusions, the
necessary array contains **7,625 clocks, maximum 11,935**, with canonical hash

    51c00db52c18732ec6128e4d9e1ed197813ab107bfd3dec7dc7ace9c5c3c67c7.

No other clock was tested or removed by this result. In particular it is not
a monotonicity theorem in t and does not address disconnected complements.
The connection source is the actual connected graph; the target is a weighted
positional tree. The map retains margins and uniform overlap lower bounds,
loses actual ratios and shared phases, and pays for that loss by taking the
minimum over every compatible edge and every possible spanning tree.
