## 4. Unrestricted binary-fibre connectivity:20 slots and45 clocks

Let n be80 or81, E the other n-20 binary sources, and k the TOTAL number at V.
No old-core/helper occupancy is fixed. Every E switch costs at most7 clocks.
Fix a total charge q and any two full accepted endpoint records.

For5<=k<=n-5, normalize each endpoint as follows. Write r for its V occupancy
inside R and J for its V subset of E.

* If r<5, move5-r currently selected E slots to U and replace the reservoir
  by any grade5 subset of the necessary residual charge.
* If r>15, move r-15 currently unselected E slots to V and use grade15.
* Now r lies in[5,15]. Until r reaches r*=max(5,k-|E|), turn one unselected
  E slot to V and choose a reservoir subset at grade r-1 with the exact debt.

Every replacement exists by the full grade table. The number of E slots
available in each instruction follows from total cardinality. Pins and
capacity are retained because every option is an authenticated disjoint
record in the same inventory.

For an initial r<5 repair, changed reservoir slots are at most r+5 and
changed E slots at most5-r. The support is at most10 and clock work at most

    2(r+5)+7(5-r)=45-5r <=45.

Complementation proves the same upper bound when r>15. A consecutive-grade
step changes at most19 reservoir slots and one E slot, hence at most20 slots
and2*19+7=45 clocks. It is one simultaneous accepted event, not a sequence of
individually legal single-slot changes.

At the common grade r*, if r*>5 then J=E is forced and the two reservoir
records can be changed directly (at most20 slots/40 clocks). If r*=5,
connect the two outside subsets by ordinary two-slot exchanges. At each
arrival choose the grade5 residual charge section. Such an event changes
at most10 reservoir and two outside slots, costing at most20+14=34 clocks.
Reverse the second endpoint's normalization to recover its **full** record.

For k<=4, use a minimum-cost grade-k witness of charge q as an intermediate
record. At k<=3 the direct endpoint cost is already at most2*3*7=42, so a
two-step witness route also satisfies that bound. For k=4, the old28-source
grade4 table supplies every charge using at most4*4=16 clocks, while any
endpoint's selected slots have cost at most28. Thus each hub edge costs
at most44 and changes at most8 slots. (The new exact full-supply DP improves
the worst grade4 hub weight to10, but is unnecessary for45.) For n-k<=4,
apply the same argument to the complemented records.

reservoir adjustment gives at most52 events. The W exchange adds at most

## 5. Boundaries and next question

The earlier fixed28 optimum8/22 is stronger on that smaller state space.
The new result removes the fixed helper occupancy and covers the complete
three-target menus on85 sources. It does not make20/45 optimal, create entry
from zero W population, or solve all-order native supply.

Capacity is one at each arrival. A released option may later be reused.
Strict temporal nonreturn prevents changed auxiliaries from returning and
is not covered. Arbitrary new moving shared-owner constraints require
conditional charge sections, not this unrestricted table. The recent literal
176/368 coupling is separately probed in the conditional-band discussion.

Statistical source weights are not changed by the route theorem. Weighted
rates are treated in `WEIGHTS.md`; connectivity alone gives no uniform gap
for an externally supplied chain with arbitrarily small conductances. No
Collatz ROOT positivity or geometric six-colour forcing follows without a
predicate-preserving map to those native objects.
