# Scoped next proof targets

These are OPEN or further research tasks, not work still running at closeout.

1. **Other Monster prime normalizers and Paley actions.** Starting from the verified7A quotient, determine the multiplier image and power fusion for other Monster primes p≡3 mod4. Do not assume every normalizer has a full AGL(1,p) quotient or an orientation-reversing coset. Retain the centralizer kernel and the chosen coset action.
2. **Endpoint-preserving all-order square-sum construction.** Ordinary existence is already known. Ask instead which prescribed ports or marked edges survive the Gerbicz gluing, and whether the finite path-cover transfer gives a uniform invariant supporting a simpler proof of that stronger property.
3. **Arrival-degree generating functions.** Each fixed-degree polynomial is explicit. Further questions concern asymptotics and recurrence structure of their coefficient totals, not another guess from the first few values. The12j temptation already fails at degree3.
4. **Prime-support square graphs.** The Monster support is a15-vertex tree and Baby support gives two components. This is a verified finite arithmetic feature, not yet a theorem forced by moonshine. Any generalization must retain the actual square-sum relation rather than ordinally relabel primes.

Across all lanes, the next target must preserve the specific incidence, orientation, endpoint, level or collision coordinate carrying the property. Aggregate totals alone have explicit counterexamples in this packet.
