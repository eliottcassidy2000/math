# Prime wheels and source-valid recursive machines

**PROVED IN NOTE where labelled E7–E9; CITED recovery elsewhere.**
The maps below preserve specified information. No identification of primes,
totients, numeral words and tournament counts is assumed.

## E7 The requested 99 ray is an actual tournament family

Use the six-vertex tournament with adjacency bit rows

    B = [40,17,3,6,13,30],       O={0,3}.

Bit j of row i means i -> j. Exact endpoint counts are

    H(B)=37,     W_03=14,     U_0=46,     U_3=53.

Thus delta=23 and U(O)=99. The inherited tail law, equation (0) of
[PROOFS.md](PROOFS.md), gives

    H(tail(B,O,k))=99*2^k+23,       k>=1,                  (7)

on k+7 vertices. Actual counts at k=1,...,6 are
221,419,815,1607,3191,6359. The first two were also checked by path
backtracking; the others by an independent physical-graph DP.
The complete ordinary-base search first finds this exact offset/slope ray
at order six. This is minimum base order within the selected-set tail
construction; no claim of minimum order for each individual number follows.
At k=0 the number is 122, which is even and cannot be a tournament path
count. The k>=1 condition is essential.

The first 25 primes are indeed the primes below 100. Their exact role for
(7) is a finite exponent wheel. For odd p not dividing 99, a divisor can
occur precisely when -23/99 lies in the subgroup generated by 2 modulo p;
if it does, its exponents form one congruence class modulo ord_p(2).

|p|ord_p(2)|Forbidden k class|
|---:|---:|---:|

The other 14 primes below 100 never divide a term with k>=1:
2,3,7,11,23,31,41,43,47,71,73,79,89,97. In particular 3 and 11 divide
the coefficient 99 but not the offset 23. All classes use the same k;
their avoidance probabilities are not independent. Generalized CRT and
inclusion-exclusion give the exact period and number of surviving classes:

    L = 428468040,
    survivors = 226472946,
    density = 5392213/10201620.

These are densities of **exponents surviving this finite wheel**, not of
primes or tournament counts. The exact calculations, including incompatible
CRT intersections, are in `wheel.py`; 5,000 direct exponent tests agree.

There is an explicit infinite hostile to mistaking wheel survival for
primality. N_2=419 is prime and ord_419(2)=418. Set

    M=lcm(L,418)=8140892760.

Every exponent k=2+Mt with t>=1 avoids all 25 small primes but has 419 as
a proper divisor of N_k. Such N_k are still valid tournament counts by
(7). Thus the prime wheel and the graph construction are compatible and
measure different predicates. The number 25 enters as the chosen sieve
cutoff, not as a necessary graph parameter.

For k>=5 the binary expansion is the regular language

    1100011 0^(k-5) 10111,

and N_(k+1)=2N_k-23. This arithmetic recursion is physically implemented
by one further tail vertex. It is a source-valid recursion on this marked
family, not an operation on an arbitrary tournament with the same H.

## E8 A natural register and a precise Zeckendorf obstruction

For any stable port of E2, keep the vector

    p_j(a)=P_j(TT_a),      p_j(a+1)=j*(p_j(a)+p_(j-1)(a)),

with p_0(a)=0 for a>=1. This nonnegative integer register has upper/lower
triangular transfer matrix depending on vector convention, diagonal
1,2,...,n. Its output is U(a)=sum u_j p_j(a). Adding one clone to the same
transitive block realizes each register step as an actual graph operation.
The defect functional p_1 is invariant. This is a finite way to manage
infinitely many seeds while retaining a valid source.

For example c=b*3^a-1 obeys the **legal** update c -> 3c+2 within its
certified family. The binary families obey c -> 2c-3, 2c-1, or 2c-29 as
appropriate. Their domains are marked families, not all scalar seeds.
The mixed family needs both its 2^a and 3^a coordinates; its current c
alone does not supply a justified new port or transition.

**Inflation-only Zeckendorf obstruction (PROVED).** Consider a compiler
whose outputs, on a repeated-zero input phase, remain in a fixed
one-port transitive-inflation template with block size A+qk and tail size
T+rk, q,r nonnegative integers. A finite set of templates and a finite
control state are allowed: pass to a repeated phase to fix the template.
Suppose it must output H=2F_(mk+b)+1 for a fixed m>=1 on that phase, as
would exact compilation of the Zeckendorf inputs 1 followed by zeros.
This is impossible.

Indeed each P_j(TT_a) is an integer linear combination of 1^a,...,j^a.
Equation (3), followed by a tail, expresses the output as a linear
combination of powers of the nonnegative integer bases j^q and 2^r*j^q
(plus a constant). Its characteristic roots are therefore integers.
The target sequence has the distinct roots 1, phi^m and (-1/phi)^m,
with nonzero coefficients, where phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2. The last two roots
are not integers. Distinct exponential sequences are linearly independent
by the Vandermonde determinant, so equality for every k is impossible.
The constant-output boundary q=r=0 also fails. QED.

This restriction is specific to an inflation-only template with additive
size counters. It does not rule out multiple interacting cyclic modules,
source replacement, extra arithmetic registers, or more general graph
operations. It complements, rather than extends beyond its assumptions,
the inherited [inverse65 Zeckendorf obstruction](../inverse65_zeckendorf_20261103/ZECKENDORF.md):
there, a prefix-correct append-only 65-symbol compiler is impossible, and
a final-only compiler's eventual zero cycle must have length at least 12.
That lower bound is not a statement about all tournament algorithms.

The native [Zeckendorf register](../zeckendorf_guard_automaton_20261003.md)
uses (X,Y) -> (Y+d,X+Y+2d) with the previous-digit guard. The missing
shift register Y is exactly the kind of extra coordinate that a scalar
projection loses. Here the positive integer eigenvalues identify why
this particular successful inflation mechanism cannot supply Fibonacci
growth on a zero phase. An additional operation would need to change
that spectral restriction and preserve the endpoint conditions as well.

## E9 Equal counts and equal defects still lose the next operation

Reverse every arc in the base B of E7, retaining O={0,3}. Its endpoint
state is

    (H,W(O),U(O))=(37,14,117),

whereas the original gives (37,14,99). Thus even the pair (H,delta) is
insufficient to predict the tail count. The resulting rays
23+99*2^k and 23+117*2^l have no common point with integer k,l>=1: equality
would make 11/13 a power of two. This is an exact hostile to replacing
the current source by an equal-H or equal-defect source without a new
certificate for the operation.

Conversely, distinct descriptions need not represent distinct marked
sources. The ternary90 family is contained in ternary30 because
90*3^a-1=30*3^(a+1)-1. At their first overlap, ternary30 with size2 and
ternary90 with size1 have the same (H,W,U)=(47,46,269) and are isomorphic
as selected tournaments. In the constructors' emitted base labels, the
isomorphism is (0,2,3,4,5,1,6). The extra port lies in the same transitive
module, so subsequent block growth gives the same family. This repeated
presentation adds no new coefficient coverage. After removing it, the
three ternary orbits have factors b in {30,50,78}.

|Catalan and Collatz carries|A word's power gap and numerator to its reduced denominator; compare inflation coefficients to actual defect|An exact integer equation|A raw power gap omits its carry numerator. |Natural descent|Actual marked sources and permitted moves to a weighted state graph|The supplied source, port and exact count update|Scalar greedy descent may ask for an unavailable branch. The [Carmichael recovery](../root_change_carmichael_chamber_20261107/CARMICHAEL_MAP.md)
distinguishes the totient conjecture from Carmichael numbers and Carmichael
lambda. Ford's [1999 primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9907204)
proves every totient multiplicity k>=2 occurs; it does not settle the
absence of multiplicity one. Neither assertion supplies tournament seed
coverage. The useful transfer is the disciplined analysis of fibres and
the rigidity left after pairing symmetries. E9 supplies concrete controls
on both under-distinguishing and over-counting source states.

The [Catalan recovery](../catalan_elliptic_20260921_catalan.md)
uses Delta=2^K-3^L and the actual gate
|Delta|/gcd(B,|Delta|) dividing the parameter. Its negative seven-cycle has
raw gap -139 and still has integral vertices: unit raw gaps are sufficient
for that integrality gate, not necessary. Mihailescu's theorem about 8
and 9 is the classical perfect-power result, as described in
[Bilu's Bourbaki exposition](https://numdam.org/item/SB_2002-2003__45__1_0/).
Here the occurrence of 2^a and 3^a comes from ordered path partitions,
P_2 and P_3; primality of 2 and 3 is not used in that counting mechanism.

For natural recursion, the inherited [source-preserving descent](../spectrum_descent_jacobian_20261102/DESCENT.md)
and [inverse65 membership algorithm](../inverse65_zeckendorf_20261103/README.md)
already distinguish full-state membership from scalar coverage. For
automata, [the TM/CA/FRACTRAN comparison](../lenses_tm_ca_fractran_tournaments_20261101.md)
records the need for priority guards and the loss of tournament identity
under a linear-automaton projection. E5 adds an exact path-language map;
it does not import the computational universality of those other models.
