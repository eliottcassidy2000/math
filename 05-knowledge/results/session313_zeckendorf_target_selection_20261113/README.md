# Session 313: From exact symbolic addresses to physical target selection

1. [Weighted prefix selection](PREFIX_SELECTION.md). The optimal-marking theorem generalizes from regular ternary trees to arbitrary weighted prefix trees. The signed Plücker address chart survives Fibonacci branching and the typed Zeckendorf unit markers. Greedy optimization alone does not decide exact targets.
2. [Fibonacci conflict boundary](FIBONACCI_CONFLICT.md). Two types per chosen nonadjacent digit give a gapless Jacobsthal rank decoder. It cannot be made into an arbitrary long full odd-cycle conflict path: a tournament with triangle-free full conflict graph has only $K_1,K_2$ components and counts $3^a5^b$. This is an all-order structural obstruction, not a failed search.
3. [Independent physical switches](ADDITIVE_PORTS.md). Separated transitive-spine edits necessarily interact. Holding the incoming response fixed in nontransitive blocks removes this interaction and gives independently selectable physical switches at arbitrary lengths, plus a pure $+2$ offset over any chain of transitive companion modules. A second, 19-path core gives a backwards exact selector in a sparse 19-adic family. [Switch replay](switch_replay.py) checks the local profiles and all two-block choices against direct subset DP.
4. [Physical marking](PHYSICAL_MARKS.md) and [modular bypass](MODULAR_BYPASS.md). Two probes realize arbitrary pointwise mark profiles. But a Hamilton path sees at most two old leaves per new vertex, so unused arcs can destroy graph symmetry while path packets retain free group actions. The result gives an all-depth divisibility lower bound. A uniform recursive construction realizes either nonzero residue modulo three, with sharp cost within its uniform-tile grammar.

## A literal Plücker chart in the physical count

Fix the first switch of the 19-path block bank. Its actual count differences, ordered by the remaining bits from right to left, have 19-adic valuation equal to the length of the common prefix. These same addresses are also in real count order. To see this, divide the increments in the [formula](ADDITIVE_PORTS.md) by $19^{n-2}$: they are $130+1128\sum_{t=0}^{i-2}(256/19)^t$ before the terminal site and the analogous expression with 152 at the terminal site. Each increment is greater than twice the preceding one, since the new geometric term dominates the entire preceding geometric sum and the constant. Hence the highest differing bit determines the real order. Taking columns $(1,H)$ gives positive minors. Prefix classes are intervals in that order, so the tropical exchange identity from [Session 312](../session312_cluster_pythagorean_symmetry_20261113/CLUSTER_CHART.md) holds with no valuation cancellation. This time the coordinate is the **actual Hamiltonian path count**, not a fabricated address coordinate.

There is a reason the same construction does not directly use the original prime 3. For any tournament, $Q(2)\equiv Q(-1)=(-1)^{|T|}H\pmod3$, since all exponents in the path-slot polynomial have the parity of the order. Thus the block valuation split $p\mid H,\ p\nmid Q(2)$ used for this address construction is impossible at $p=3$. The modulo-three bypass instead exploits mixed order parities in cyclic joins.

The practical dividing line is now explicit: a gapless numeral language, independent physical switches, and a prescribed modular guard are three different things. We obtained all three in their stated domains, but have not combined them into a physical interval covering all sufficiently large odd integers. Chan–Pak remains open.
