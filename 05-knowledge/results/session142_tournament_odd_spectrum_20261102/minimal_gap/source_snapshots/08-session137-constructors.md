# Actual-source cones, dyadic faces, and the sealing barrier

**Status: PROVED structural statements, with standalone exact controls.**
Every positive odd tournament Hamiltonian-path count except 7 and 21 being
attained remains **OPEN**. No literature-priority claim is made. Counts are
raw directed Hamiltonian vertex words, not automorphism orbits.

## 0. Inheritance and scope

An actual source consists of a nonempty tournament A and an external marked
vertex x. Its cut S is the set of vertices of A beaten by x. We use

    h=H(A), F=H(A+x),
    q=# ordered covers (P,Q) of A, Q nonempty, first(Q) in S,
    g=# Hamiltonian paths of A ending in S,
    C=(F-h)/2, L=(q-F+h-g)/2, t=h-g.

P may be empty. The source state is v=(C,L,g,t), and

    (h,F,q,g)=(g+t,2C+g+t,2C+2L+g,g).

The inherited weighted-cut theorem proves

    C=sum_(u in S,v outside S,u->v) b_uv,
    L=sum_({u,v} subset S) b_uv,                 b_uv positive integers.

Its exact source is `session124_tournament_cut_response_20261031/README.md`;
the displayed L identity is in `square_sum_spectrum_overnight_20261031b/README.md`,
Section 5. Hence every actual v is a nonnegative integer vector. This is a
necessary condition, NOT a realization criterion.

The universal four-state fresh-block compiler, its source convention and its
path decomposition are inherited from
`session134_constructive_spectrum_frontier_20261101/constructor/PROOF.md`,
Sections 1-2. That proof explicitly left general entrywise positivity of the
conjugated matrix unasserted, verifying only its chosen finite bank. Section 1
below closes precisely that gap. The genuine radix-80 ray on L=g=0 in that
session's `radix_probe/PROOF.md` is the positive control for Section 3: its
parent height grows, so it is not excluded.

The inheritance pass retained four live objects: the full actual source orbit,
its convex relaxation, the zero-interior face, and the original marked port
under re-marking. The hostile near miss is treating a positive vector or an
H-neutral padding as an interchangeable source. The repair always retains
actual graph recipes. No arbitrary positive vector is used as an actual input.

## 1. The actual-source cone and lattice are saturated

Let V be the set of all actual source vectors, over every nonempty parent.
Let e_C,e_L,e_g,e_t denote the coordinate vectors.

**Theorem 1.**

1. The integer group generated by V is Z^4.
2. The closed conical hull of V is R_+^4.
3. The closed convex hull of V is

       {v>=0 : g+t>=1}.

4. If a real linear map M:R^4->R^4 sends EVERY actual source state to an
   actual source state, then every entry of M is a nonnegative integer.
   Moreover, with ell=(0,0,1,1),

       ell M = ell (mod 2).

Universal means all actual nonempty marked sources, not only strong parents
or a chosen seed orbit. Part 4 does not claim its converse.

**Actual witnesses.** Let L_n be the transitive chain 0->1->...->n-1.
The following recipes and states are exact:

| Parent and cut | v=(C,L,g,t) |
|---|---|
| L_1, empty | e_t |
| L_1, full | e_g |
| L_2, first vertex | e_C+e_t |
| L_2, full | e_L+e_g |
| L_n, first vertex, n>=2 | (2^(n-2),0,0,1) |
| L_n, full, n>=1 | (0,2^(n-1)-1,1,0) |

For a singleton first cut, q=2^(n-1), g=0, L=0: each later chain
vertex independently belongs to P or Q. For the full cut, every ordered
cover except Q empty is counted, so q=2^n-1 and F=h=g=1.

The first four witnesses give e_C and e_L by integer differences, and give
e_g,e_t directly. The last two families, normalized by their growing first
or second coordinate, approach e_C,e_L. Nonnegativity of actual states then
proves the conical-hull statement.

For the convex hull, actual sources satisfy g+t=h>=1. To realize unbounded
multiples of the g and t axes, take B to be an ordinal sum of k directed
triangles, so H(B)=3^k. The empty cut on B gives 3^k e_t. The parent
B->v with cut {v} gives 3^k e_g (include k=0 by the one-vertex witnesses).
Convex combinations therefore supply every point (0,0,g,t) with g+t>=1:
first interpolate the height between consecutive powers of 3, then mix the
two axes. Add any desired C,L in the closure by mixing in the two transitive
spike families with coefficients C/2^k and L/(2^k-1), tending to zero.
The other coordinates converge to the original g,t. This proves part 3.

For part 4, M e_g and M e_t are integer vectors because they are actual.
The other columns are the differences M(e_C+e_t)-M e_t and
M(e_L+e_g)-M e_g, hence are integer too. Every column is nonnegative:
for C,L use the limits of the actual spike families, and for g,t use the
actual axes directly. Finally h is odd for every actual source. Applying
this to the same four witnesses makes the C,L entries of ell M even and
the g,t entries odd, proving the parity identity.

**Consequences.** Every universal homogeneous linear necessary inequality
on source states is a nonnegative combination of coordinate nonnegativity.
More generally every valid affine halfspace contains the entire displayed
closed convex hull. Thus no polyhedral linear source relaxation can recover
the missing arithmetic realization conditions inside it.

Four calibration sources determine a linear map but do not certify that it is
universal. For example the map

    (C,L,g,t) -> (C,L,g,3t-2C)

sends the four small calibration states to actual states (the first becomes
3e_t), yet sends the next actual C-ray point (2,0,0,1) to a vector with t=-1.
Conversely (C,L,g,t)->(C+4t,L,g,t) is nonnegative integer and parity-preserving,
but sends the actual (1,0,0,1) to the nonrealizable state (5,0,0,1) below.
These are separate hostiles to finite calibration and to cone sufficiency.

Every fixed local tournament G on old port x and fresh vertices, re-marking
any fresh vertex and orienting A toward all fresh vertices, is source-universal
by the inherited compiler theorem. Its conjugate in (C,L,g,t) is therefore
entrywise nonnegative integer for EVERY G, at EVERY order. No finite-bank
sampling or extrapolation is needed. The inherited capped-semiring fixed
point now applies to any finite bank of such recipes, not only a bank whose
positivity has separately been checked.

## 2. An exact arithmetic face: unit height

**Theorem 2.** Among actual states with h=1 and L=g=0, precisely

    v=(0,0,0,1), or v=(2^k,0,0,1), k>=0

occur.

If h=1 then A is transitive: a directed triangle would have three Hamiltonian
paths and tournament path insertion injects them into paths of A. A tournament
with no directed triangle is transitive. L=0 and positivity of b_uv force
|S|<=1. The empty cut gives C=0. A nonempty singleton cut cannot select the
last chain vertex because g=0. If r>=1 vertices follow the selected vertex,
q=2^r and C=q/2=2^(r-1). Earlier vertices contribute a unique prefix and do
not change q. The recipes in Section 1 establish the converse.

A useful hostile is the formal nonnegative state (5,0,0,1), with F=11.
It satisfies the ordinary parity and positivity guards but is NOT an actual
source at this height/face. The number 11 itself IS attained: the one-apex
word 1100 has actual state (5,1,0,1). Thus this is a source-state obstruction,
not a disguised use of the unrestricted H=7 exclusion.

If a universally lawful linear operation preserves this face and h=1,
its C action is a C+b with a,b nonnegative integers. If a>0 and it works
on every 2^k, then b=0 and a is a power of 2: for large k, a2^k+b cannot
remain a power of two when b>0, and b=0 forces a to be a power of two.
A right-local induced operation cannot have a=0 on this unbounded family,
because it cannot decrease F. Consequently no nonzero affine digit, at ANY
word length or variable radix, preserves this whole unit-height face.

## 3. Every fixed-height zero-interior face is dyadic

For an attained odd h, define

    D_h={C>0 : (C,0,0,h) is an actual source state}.

**Theorem 3.** There is an explicit finite bank of actual positive integers
B_h such that

    D_h = union_(b in B_h) {b*2^k : k>=0}.

One may form B_h by enumerating sources of height h on parents with at most
h+2 vertices and retaining L=g=0,C>0. A smaller inherited core-order bound
may replace h; h+2 is a sufficient elementary bound, not a sharp claim.
Thus for every finite height bank K, the actual outputs 2C+h from L=g=0,
h in K, have only O_K(log X) distinct values at most X. This rules out a
universal fixed-finite-height compiler on this face, even with arbitrary
phases, unbounded words or variable radices.

**Proof.** Again L=0 forces S empty or singleton. Take S={v}. A Hamiltonian
cycle in a nontrivial strong component supplies a Hamiltonian path ending
at each of its vertices; the singleton case is immediate. Since paths of an
ordinal SCC sum concatenate, g=0 says v is not in the final SCC. Write

    A=P -> B -> Q, v in B, Q nonempty.

Let E(U) count ordered two-path covers with both paths allowed empty.
Then E(U->W)=E(U)E(W), by independently restricting the two ordered paths
to the two blocks. Also E(singleton)=2. Since Q of the q-cover must start
at v, all P vertices lie in its first path. Restriction to B is exactly the
local q_B(v) cover; its second path already contains v, so the two suffix
fragments in Q may be empty independently. Hence

    h=H(P)H(B)H(Q),
    q=H(P)q_B(v)E(Q), C=q/2.                 (1)

Delete every unselected singleton SCC before B. Delete singleton SCCs after
B, except keep one if no nonsingleton SCC follows B, so Q stays nonempty.
Keep v if B itself is a singleton. All nonsingleton SCCs are retained.
The reduced parent has its original nonsingleton core and at most two
singleton vertices. Its height is h; it still has L=g=0,C>0. Each deleted
suffix singleton removed a factor 2 from q, while deleted prefix singletons
changed neither h nor q. Thus C=b2^k for this actual reduced base b.

For the elementary size bound, each nontrivial strong component of order n_i
has at least n_i Hamilton paths, and n_i>=3. If their counts are h_i, then
sum n_i<=sum h_i<=product h_i=h (one component is immediate; induction uses
a+b<=ab for a,b>=3). With no core the order is zero. This proves the stated
finite base universe. Conversely appending any number k of unselected sink
vertices to the parent, all beating x, preserves h,L,g=0 and doubles C per
vertex. It constructs every point b2^k from its actual base. QED.

The exact minimal dyadic base banks at the first attained heights are

    h=1: {1};   h=3: {3};   h=5: {5,6,7,9,16}.

These are **FINITE-EXACT bases plus the all-order reduction**, not new global
spectrum intervals. For the prime heights 3 and 5 the nonsingleton core is
one strong block B, of order at most h. Its possible bases are h (a selected
singleton after B, followed by a sink), q_B(v) (v in B followed by a sink),
and E(B)/2 (a selected singleton before B). Enumerating every labelled strong
block through orders 3 and 5 is complete by the order bound; removing bases
that are positive powers of two times a smaller base gives the displayed
lists. `face_bases.py` records actual adjacency recipes for each retained base.

More generally, a fixed nonzero affine offset cannot carry an unbounded
actual dyadic ray into a finite-height face bank. If A2^k+d belongs to a
finite union of rays b_j2^ell for infinitely many k, with A>0 fixed, then
some j and some bounded integer ell-k recur infinitely. The equation
(A-b_j2^(ell-k))2^k=-d then forces d=0. This is an all-length obstruction to
constant-offset carries on these face banks, not an exclusion of phase
systems with unbounded height or positive L.

The same proof may use the inherited logarithmic core bound from
`session133_fractran_spectrum_refinement_20261101/core_reduction/THEORY.md`
to make the base search much smaller; that bound is not required for finiteness
or the O(log X) consequence. The radix-80 family of S134 does not contradict
this result: its height t=(16*80^k+63)/79 tends to infinity.

## 4. Every right-local history retains its original-port cost

A right-local operation adds fresh vertices dominated by the current
unmarked parent. It may keep the old mark or re-mark one fresh vertex. All
local edges and the re-marking are fixed independently of the source. The
inherited S0,S1 and all fresh-block migrations are included. Operations that
re-mark an arbitrary OLD parent vertex, reverse old edges, or attach fresh
vertices with nonuniform old-parent neighborhoods are NOT included.

**Collapse lemma.** Every finite right-local word, however often it changes
the mark, has a fixed local tournament on the ORIGINAL port x and the set U
of all new vertices; every original A vertex dominates U. This follows
inductively because original A always remains in the unmarked parent.
The local tournament is independent of A and its original cut.

For nonempty U put

    a=H(U),
    b=# ordered covers (P,Q) of U, P nonempty, last(P)->x,
    c=H(U)-# Hamiltonian paths of U starting at an out-neighbor of x,
    d=H(U+x)-H(U).

Q may be empty. The inherited one-port path decomposition gives the terminal
full count, irrespective of the final mark:

    F_word=a F+b q+c g+d h.                    (2)

All four coefficients are nonnegative integers, a>=1. For d use induced
Hamilton-path monotonicity. For U empty the identity word has a=1,b=c=d=0.

**Sealing dichotomy.** Either

* some u in U beats the original x, in which case b>=1 and F_word>q; or
* x dominates all U, in which case b=c=d=0 and F_word=H(U)F.

Indeed a singleton first path P=(u), with an arbitrary Hamiltonian path of
U-u as Q, proves b>=1 in the first case. The converse is immediate. In the
second case the full graph is (A+x)->U, giving the claimed product.

**High-cost exact reachability.** If q>=N, every right-local word whose
terminal count is at most N has count F times an attained integer. When
arbitrary finite local blocks are allowed, the converse holds: every such
attained multiple is realized by taking (A+x)->U and re-marking a vertex
of U. Thus the entire bounded-output reachability set is exactly

    {F*s<=N : s is an attained tournament H-value}.

This is an exact structural reduction, not an efficient spectrum oracle.
For a restricted recipe bank the necessary multiplicative condition remains
valid, while the factor U must additionally arise from a sealing word of that
bank.

For a target that has no factorization into two attained factors greater
than one, every successful construction prefix with 1<F<N must have q<N.
Otherwise sealing would force just such a factorization. In particular this
applies to prime targets, and to the inherited prime/7-prime atom lanes when
the all-order H=7 exclusion makes them multiplicatively indecomposable.
This is a strict source-cost gate for an exact inverse, not a claim that an
eligible predecessor exists.

**Neutral-padding obstruction.** Appending S1 preserves F,h and sends
q->2q+h. After r such actual global-sink vertices,

    q_r=2^r(q+h)-h -> infinity.

Therefore every attained F-fibre contains marked sources from which a fixed
nonmultiple N of F is impossible under ALL right-local words, including
adaptive words of unbounded length. In particular no source-independent
2F+/-1 lift works uniformly for every actual source when F>1. This is stronger
than a finite affine-digit search and is not a congruence obstruction on N
alone: another source with the same F may work.

The minimal illustrative pair starts with transitive parent 0->1 and
x->0, 1->x, giving (h,F,q,g)=(1,3,2,0). S0 attains 5. One H-neutral S1
instead gives (1,3,5,1), still F=3. Thereafter target 5 is impossible by
q>=5 and 3 not dividing 5. All graphs and the successful S0 child are
retained by the checker.

If a fixed word promises F_word=B F+d0 on EVERY actual source, then d0=0
and B=H(U): apply it to the unbounded-q F=1 transitive full-cut family to
force b=0, then compare actual F=1 and3. This excludes universal scalar
affine digits even with re-marking; it does not exclude carefully restricted
actual profiles, such as S134's radix-80 ray.

## 5. Safe overflow semantics for arbitrary finite recipe banks

At a target cap N, keep exact actual states (h,F,q,g) with F<=N and q<N.
Replace every state with q>=N by the single tag overflow(F). This is a
right-congruence for capped terminal H under right-local words, by the
sealing dichotomy. In particular no unsafe monotonicity claim about the
CURRENT q under re-marking is needed.

For a generator with b>0, an overflow input necessarily overshoots. For a
zero-b generator that keeps the mark, F'=aF and q'=E(U)q+q_U h>=q,
so the result remains overflow(aF). For a zero-b generator re-marking fresh
z, the old full graph T=A+x is an ordinal prefix of U. Therefore its entire
new state is exactly

    F * state(U-z,z),

with the empty local parent allowed as the algebraic local unit. This is a
literal factorization of path/cover counts, not a fabricated state. Compute
it from F and the finite local graph, then tag overflow only if needed.
On a fine input use the exact inherited compiler. Discard F>N; retain an
actual recipe witness for every first reached state.

There are finitely many fine states (1<=h<=F<=N,0<=g<=h,0<=q<N), plus at
most N overflow tags. Closure therefore decides every target up to N for
EVERY finite bank of actual right-local recipes, over all word lengths.
This is an alternative exact quotient to the now-general nonnegative-matrix
saturation of Section 1. The two quotients need not have equal state counts,
and neither proves universal acceptance. As a finite implementation control,
the inherited 32-matrix bank at N=173 has 6,234 positive-saturation states
versus 2,592 source-cost states (85 overflow tags), with the identical exact
all-depth output set and the same two missing odds 7 and 21. This is a smaller
quotient for that bank/cutoff, not a new unrestricted coverage result.

## 6. Checks, hostiles and remaining boundary

`check.py` is a standalone standard-library consumer. It independently counts
Hamilton paths and ordered covers on explicit graphs, checks the actual
axis witnesses, all small-source/compiler instances, the original-port
collapse, the scalar overflow quotient against the inherited positive
saturation closure, and fake-positive/mutated-transfer controls. It does not
import a graph atlas or use an arbitrary depth cutoff for a negative claim.
`migration.py` is the inherited small compiler copied unchanged solely for
replay; its theorem is an explicit dependency, not inferred from the controls.

The source-realization problem is still nonlinear and arithmetic despite its
full convex cone. The strongest actionable repair is to retain actual source
ancestry and allow unbounded height or positive interior L when escaping the
dyadic face; and to alter the original-port architecture when a target is not
a multiple of a high-cost source F. Neither repair is proved universally
successful. Exact realization of all allowed odd integers remains OPEN.
