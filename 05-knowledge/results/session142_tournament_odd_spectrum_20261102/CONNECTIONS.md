# Typed connections and boundaries

## Tournament -> arithmetic target monoid

- **Source:** actual tournament, decomposed into its ordered strong components.
- **Map:** take each component's ordinary Hamiltonian-path count, then multiply.
- **Preserved:** exact total H, oddness and attained-value status.
- **Destroyed:** component adjacency, endpoint response and realization cost.
- **Sidecar:** literal adjacency certificates and component ordering.
- **Test:** the frozen strong witnesses give49,63,343 by two exact counters.

The reverse map exists for a list of already realized factors by order join.
A factorization in the *candidate* target monoid alone supplies no factor
witness. This is exactly why the two prime lanes remain existence problems.

## Polynomial response -> guarded Boolean spectrum

- **Source:** a tournament's Boolean orientation coordinates.
- **Map:** extend its Hamiltonian-path enumerator multiaffinely to rational
  orientation weights.
- **Preserved:** literal path-count polynomial at Boolean points.
- **Destroyed:** Booleanity when the domain is enlarged to rational points.
- **Sidecar:** every orientation coordinate must remain0 or1.
- **Test:** one-edge responses5+4t and19+4t attain7 and21 at t=1/2, although
  the true tournament spectrum excludes them.

A nonzero derivative, an invertible linear response, an integral inverse
outside the positive cone, or a primitive response lattice does not recover
that sidecar. Jacobian methods can organize response charts but do not by
themselves give actual tournament realization.

## Recovered polynomial identity -> no tournament shortcut

The inherited three-variable polynomial and its exact determinant/collision
certificate are rechecked as algebra. Source attribution and external formal
claims are separate. No map from that polynomial's fibers to actual
Hamiltonian-path witnesses has been supplied. Shared terminology or a
constant determinant is not the missing realization theorem.

## Automorphism action -> actual prime-lane search constraint

- **Source:** an actual tournament and its action on Hamiltonian vertex words.
- **Map:** decompose into free automorphism orbits, retaining the strong core.
- **Preserved:** exact divisibility |Aut(T)| divides H(T).
- **Destroyed:** explicit paths and construction coordinates.
- **Sidecar:** cited strong-order floor, fixed singleton padding, and actual
  induced cyclic modules; no vertex-transitivity assumption is inserted.
- **Test:** prime H>3 forces trivial automorphism group; the explicit H4151
  witness certifies the sharp symmetric7p branch with group order7.

## Actual marked constructor -> operation-language obstruction

- **Source:** a real parent tournament with a marked external vertex.
- **Map:** append a singleton and either keep the mark or move it to the
  fresh vertex, recording(C,L,g,t) after each operation.
- **Preserved:** exact graph reconstruction and count update F'=2F+I.
- **Destroyed by scalar F:** the eligibility coordinate I and the next
  state's ability to continue a binary-growth history.
- **Sidecar:** all four nonnegative marked coordinates and the precise
  operation word; arbitrary old-vertex re-marking is not in this alphabet.
- **Test:** both mark choices have the same immediate scalar output; the
  R choice cannot sustain three binary steps when F>=5; the named neutral
  S1/reset steps enter an invariant set with no future binary growth. An unbounded
  operation language can therefore have only logarithmic output growth.

This obstruction explains why a Jacobian or formal inverse on the state
space does not itself create legal tournament branches. It leaves a concrete
research obligation rather than claiming that all richer constructors fail.


## Hamiltonian cuts -> module amplification

A Hamiltonian vertex word with k-1 cut positions maps to an unordered cover
by k nonempty directed paths. It preserves every internal path and loses at
most k! run orders. Restoring ordered marked quotient runs gives the exact
k!W_k kernel. The sidecar is the homogeneous port and the cyclic/inactive
SCC distinction. The decisive consequence is size-dependent amplification
at every cyclic port, even with varying contexts; it does not constrain
nonhomogeneous S0/R1 attachments.

The inherited seven-parent inverse yields an explicit block word and cut.
Reconstruction gives all0/1 adjacency entries. Independent counting then
