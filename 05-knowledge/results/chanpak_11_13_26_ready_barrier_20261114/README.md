# The terminal-seven route is not universal; three exact ways to go beyond it

**Chan–Pak remains open.** This checkpoint rules out a specific
universal strategy, not the underlying target. It also gives
genuinely different literal constructions.

- **[The terminal-seven obstruction](READY_LINE_OBSTRUCTION.md).**
  The prime cofactor 25,733 has no physical ready source at any
  terminal width of the length-seven corridor. Exact arithmetic
  leaves only width-power 4 and state \((59,410)\).
  The [complete ready-root classification](READY_CLASSIFICATION.md)
  proves the cone for every ready graph, not just positive bridges:
  the root has one outgoing arc, and its signed profile is the
  negative of a literal Hamiltonian end count. The exact rooted
  census on the 12 possible strong \(H=59\) cores rules out the
  required response. But `python alternate_target.py` verifies
  180,131 paths on the explicit 15-vertex two-hub tournament
  `1002303022110`. This is a construction obstruction, not a spectrum gap.
- **[11, 13, and 26](BILATERAL_GLUE.md).** A real five-vertex
  rooted core has \(H=11,R_+=13\) and makes the ready state
  \((11,26)\), not attainable on the terrace. At corridor length
  11 a particular ray reaches the retained axis; length 13
  kills the transverse axis; length 26 is nonsingular and sends
  the transverse axis into the retained one. The exact mixed
  difference is a shifted tribonacci term:
  \(t^t C_q t=T_{q-30}\). In particular two source changes
  add independently at 26, 29, and 30. The actual splice accepts
  arbitrary ready cores on both sides. A complement-lookup
  inverse recovers the prime 5,611,326,148,061 at length 26.
  Holding both longitudinal coordinates fixed still limits
  the possible residues, so this repair does not close coverage.
- **[A nonlinear ordinary-target route](CUT_BALANCE.md).**
  Balancing two binary contexts by bit reversal lets one literal
  arc flip change the count by any fixed positive even amount.
  At each fixed gap the construction has \(\Theta(\sqrt X)\)
  distinct baselines, with a monotone exact inverse in
  \(O((\log N)^2)\) counting evaluations.
  It yields prime/7-times-prime examples in both orders:
  1,517,507 and 1,517,509, then 3,354,407 and 3,354,409.
  The natural 11-to-13 reflection instead adds both radial
  contributions with the same sign. At any fixed gap it has only
  finitely many absolute pairs, even with ordinal reservoirs.
  The change to a balance equation, not another depth choice,
  is what makes the latter route grow.

Run `python ready_class.py`, `python ready_line_replay.py`,
`python alternate_target.py`, `python bilateral.py`, and
`python replay_cut_balance.py` for the separate exact replays.
The three smaller imported Python files are pinned dependencies
from the earlier literal-prefix proof.

**Proof frontier.** Do not seek a physical point on every
terminal-seven line: one of those lines does not have one.
A viable covering argument must switch construction families
as well as change a source. The strongest concrete next tests
are absolute target selection in the two-source splice with
changing longitudinal values, and overlap of the nonlinear
ordinary repair ranges with other exact families. Neither
modular matching nor arbitrarily many existing witnesses
establishes those overlaps.
