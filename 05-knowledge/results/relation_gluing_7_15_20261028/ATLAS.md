# A typed atlas of seven, five, fifteen, and composition

This is a bounded recovery of relevant current packets, supplemented by
a reproducible random sample, not a census proving that any vocabulary
is the repository's dominant theme. The incoming correction MISTAKE-693
is especially relevant: a substring census had mistaken `checked` for
`Hecke`. We use explicit mathematical maps rather than word frequency.

## Inheritance and research board

**Anchor:** the requested arithmetic Fano example and exact composition as
size changes. **Niche:** integral quotient modules and coefficient change.
**Wildcard:** Eisenstein norm-seven charts, six-label symplectic geometry,
and turning old relations into the next scale's objects. LRC(14) remains
the repository's open near-term target; its integral kernel obstruction
is included as a test of transfer, not treated as solved.

Closest proved mechanism: Session56's actual compatible-assignment gluing
and the corrected square-class map Q(sigma(n)). Canonical hostile:
atoms 2 and 4 share a prime, so a formal square total need not be the actual
sigma. Corrected near miss: the Fano configuration has seven three-point
circuits but fourteen circuits in total. Least-used useful sidecars:
the full integer presentation and the quotient-module family for all
subsets, rather than a single global rank or cokernel.

The six live concepts were: (1) prime-power choices and arithmetic fusion;
(2) codes and finite projective geometry; (3) LCA/interface gluing;
(4) integer torsion and coefficient change; (5) labelled group actions;
(6) generation, descent and scale. Three meaningful board revisions were:

- The fifteen-prime extension made the old duad/pentad geometry an
  arithmetic carrier, while identifying the symplectic form as a choice.
- The integer lift separated kernel-born from torsion-born Fano lines;
  this connected signed incidence and boundary discriminants and reduced
  the decorated symmetry group to 2.
- The collision2/4 produced a fusion law rather than another exclusion.
  This clarified why Liouville composes with no defect while sigma does
  not, and why a finite parity model is not full arithmetic memory.

T3c adds a genuine scale map: codewords of Hamming[7,4,3] become points
of PG(3,2), after a basis is chosen. The old arithmetic group operation
is xy/gcd(x,y)^2. Its fifteen nonidentity elements map to the fifteen
new prime labels. Three old dependencies sum to zero iff the corresponding
new three-prime product has square sigma. This explains the passage7->15
without conflating code coordinates and codewords.

## 5. Gaussian five and Eisenstein seven have a separate exact mechanism

**Source:** corrected [THM-4839 / unit Cayley graphs](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4839-unit-cayley-graphs-toroidal-maps-petersen-klein-four-belyi-pythagorean-frey.md),

**Preserved:** the labelled quotient Cayley adjacency. **Lost:** the

Five here is a residue-field size, whereas five in a pentad is a spread
size. The Eisenstein quotient and the Fano chart meet only through a
specified incidence marking, not by identifying fields of characteristic
seven and two. The classical regular-map and modular-cover interpretations
of THM-4839 remain useful adjacent structure; they do not assert that
every arithmetic code comes from a triangle-group quotient.

## 13. The two grids become a Selmer coordinate

**Source:** corrected [THM-4825 grid account](../two_grids_ring_or_ideal_class_13_37_20261027.md)

**Map:** an element alpha with even ideal valuations gives a fractional

the remaining square obstruction comes from a unit or a nonprincipal
ideal class. **Lost by ideal parity alone:** both of these coordinates.
**Hostiles:** -1 in the Eisenstein field, and 2 in Q(sqrt(-d)) for
d=5,13,37. The latter maps to the centred-grid class(2,1+sqrt(-d)).
**Status:** T7 proves the classical exact sequences and the elementary
hostiles. The class-number-two list is inherited, not recomputed here.

A stronger literature connection is [Dummit–Voight, Theorem 6.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.00092):
under the paper's number-field setup, the 2-Selmer signature image is
maximal totally isotropic for the real and 2-adic Hilbert-symbol pairing.
This is a genuine arithmetic source of binary orthogonality. It is
**CITED**, not proved or implemented in this session. A symmetric form
in characteristic two need not be alternating; no automatic identification
with our chosen doily form follows. A transfer would need an actual field,
its local symbols and an isometry on the specified image.

The repo's [finite Selmer growth packet](../bsd_selmer_growth_20261013a.md)
also distinguishes finite torsion from divisible growth. Its binary
Monsky matrices apply to a specified elliptic-curve family, not our
sigma-prime bank. Its stronger BSD consequences explicitly rely on a
supplied manuscript; those consequences are not imported here. The
classical number-field Selmer group in T7 and an elliptic curve's Selmer
group are related descent constructions, not interchangeable groups.

## Stopping rules and next decisive experiments

The all-size module, coefficient-change, fusion and projective-width
statements have proofs. The explicit fifteen-prime and symmetry/spread
claims have complete finite witnesses. Further numeric coincidences
without maps were not promoted.

- For a new arithmetic bank, compare all subset integer modules before
  claiming its binary automorphism group is arithmetic symmetry.
- For graceful or square-sum construction, test a local extension on
  actual positive, injective label domains; incidence rank alone is closed
  as a route to an existence proof.
- For tournament transfer, identify actual affine flip coordinates and
  quotient decorations before importing the Hamming interface bounds.
- For a proposed universal prime realization of binary configurations,
  separate the finite witness search from unproved prime-value assertions
  for quadratic polynomials. No all-r arithmetic realization is claimed.

The resulting program is to study presentations, coefficient changes,
extensions and exact composition. It has several demonstrated common
mechanisms and several precise open realization problems, rather than
one theorem claimed to settle every domain.
