# Exact interfaces, grammar, and target-sensitive certificates

Status and reproduction scope are in [README.md](README.md). Symbols M2 here
always mean the integer matrix of ordered two-path spanning covers, allowing
singleton paths, with ordered distinct start/end pairs. No integer state is
identified merely because its rational Fourier sectors agree.

## I. Closure under legal futures is the correct equivalence

Let X be a finite set, f_a partial operations, and O a family of rational-valued
observations. Add an illegal cemetery state, extend each operation by sending
an undefined input to the cemetery, and observe the cemetery indicator.
For words w, write f_w for sequential operation, and set

    x ~ y iff o(f_w(x)) = o(f_w(y)) for every w and every o in O.   (1)

**Elementary theorem (classical behavioural quotient mechanism).** Relation(1)
is the coarsest operation-stable equivalence refining the observations. The
unital Q-algebra generated by all o composed with f_w consists exactly of all
functions constant on its equivalence classes. Its dimension is the number of
classes. It is closed under pullback by every f_a.

**Proof.** Prefixing a future by a gives stability. Any stable equivalence that
preserves O preserves every future observation, so refines(1). Every generator
is constant on(1). Conversely for distinct classes C,D choose a generator
h_CD with h_CD(C)!=h_CD(D). The product over D!=C of

    (h_CD-h_CD(D))/(h_CD(C)-h_CD(D))

is the indicator of C. These indicators span all class-constant functions.
Pullback preserves sums and products and simply prepends an operation. QED.

This theorem is a specification, not an efficient algorithm on unbounded X.
Nor does it identify the algebra with the span of unmultipled observations.
It requires same-source products; independently averaged observations are
different functions. Finally Q-interpolation may use forbidden denominators:
the corresponding Z-lattice and positive cone must be tracked separately.

This gives four questions to ask of a proposed recurrence state:

1. Does it determine each legal continuation?
2. Does it preserve the required joint products rather than just marginals?
3. Does it preserve integer realizability, if the question is about torsion?
4. Does its evaluation functional give positive weight to the sources that
   the target quantifier ranges over?

These are not mutually equivalent. A proof of one is not a proof of the others.

## II. A classical grammar, with a new finite observer experiment

**Classical input, CITED.** Shelor, *On Tournaments Having A Unique Hamiltonian
Circuit* (May1977), sections 2--5, gives a bijective maximum-outdegree insertion
construction and the recurrence counting these tournaments. The author's
[paper text](https://paperzz.com/doc/7419910/shelor--m.r.%3B--1977-.on-tournaments-having-a-unique-hamil...)
was inspected; this is a mirror of the primary manuscript, not a new theorem
attributed to the repository. The linked CiteSeer PDF and NCSU endpoint did
not yield readable files. The construction is complete for n>=4, starting at
the unique strong four-tournament. Garey's count is F_(2n-6).

Here is our notation for its ports. The unique cycle gives predecessor pred.
TypeA has one maximum-outdegree vertex a; typeB has two, a0->a1. Every new
vertex v beats all old vertices except its chosen parent p, which beats v.

| Source | Letter | p | Child type |
|---|---|---|---|
|A|a|pred(a)|A|
|A|b|a|B|
|B|c|pred(a0)|A|
|B|d|a0|B|
|B|e|a1|B|

The new cycle inserts v immediately after p. Thus counts obey
`A'=A+B, B'=A+2B`, starting `(A,B)=(0,1)` at order 4.

**Independent canonicalization argument (PROVED).** An isomorphism of two
unique-directed-cycle tournaments maps the unique cycle to the unique cycle,
preserving its direction. Once both cycles have order 0,...,n-1, it is a cyclic
rotation. Thus the least upper-triangle bit string among rotations is a full
isomorphism certificate in this class. It does not quotient by reflection:
reflection corresponds to the converse, which is separately checked.
The generator explicitly rejects duplicate certificates and verifies one HC
by exact subset DP for every generated object. The two/three-child counts
match the classical census at every tested order. No gentourng census or
graph-isomorphism heuristic is needed for the extension to12.

### The observer, without losing the gluing lattice

For nonempty U, let H_U(s,t) count directed Hamilton paths of T[U] from s to t.
Then

    M2[(a,b),(c,d)] = sum_(empty<U<V) H_U(a,c) H_(V\U)(b,d).   (2)

This follows by naming the vertex set of the first of the two disjoint paths.
We implement the recurrence adding the last vertex; the audit independently
uses the inherited prepend recurrence and, at small orders, literal cutting
of every permutation. These are integer counts. For n<=12 a crude total bound
`n!*(n-1)` fits in signed64-bit arithmetic.

Order pairs as `(i,j),(j,i)` for each i<j. Swapping both run names leaves M2
unchanged. After grouping the first and second pair orders its blocks are
`[[X,Y],[Y,X]]`. Therefore

    det M2 = det(X+Y) det(X-Y).                            (3)

Equation(3) is valid over Q, hence as an equality of integer determinants.
It does **not** identify integer cokernels. Our120 independent native
determinants check(3), and the hostile below checks the actual Smith form.

### A concrete failure of a plausible recurrence state

At order 5, the growth words d and e have the same typeB and determinant+1.
Their cycle-normalized upper-triangle codes are respectively

    d: 1000100111,     e: 1000100101.

After the common e operation:

| Word | Native Smith factors | det plus | det minus |
|---|---|---:|---:|
|de|1 repeated30|1|1|
|ee|1 repeated26,0 repeated4|16|0|

Thus `(type, current determinant)` is not stable under continuation. The
vanishing determinant of ee does not create torsion: its cokernel is free
of rank4. Replacing this statement by a torsion claim would already be false.
These matrices are regenerated, not read from a fitted sequence.

### The proposed language and its proved enumeration

Let L be the classical grammar words that avoid be and ee. Define three
productive states: A, B0 (typeB with no pending bad e), B1 (typeB reached by
b or e). The transitions, with omitted moves illegal, are

    A:  a->A,  b->B1
    B0: c->A,  d->B0, e->B1
    B1: c->A,  d->B0, e->FB.

Here FB and FA are legal rejected states of the corresponding types; their
future transitions retain legality but never recover acceptance. The initial
state is B0. This is an exact model of pattern avoidance by inspection of
the last symbol, not a theorem about arbitrary-order M2.

The transfer matrix on `(A,B0,B1)` is

    C = [[1,0,1],[1,1,1],[1,1,0]].

With initial row `(0,1,0)` and terminal column `(1,1,1)^T`, elementary matrix
inversion gives

    V(x) = (1+x)/(1-2x-x^2+x^3).                         (4)

The numerator and denominator are coprime. Thus the scalar sequence has
minimal constant-coefficient recurrence order 3, and minimal stationary linear
realization dimension 3. Cayley--Hamilton gives the recurrence in README.
Its combinatorial meaning is an exact count of a well-defined subfamily of
unique-cycle tournaments at all orders, whether or not the determinant
conjecture holds outside the tested range.

If only acceptance matters, merge all failures into one absorbing state:
A,B0,B1 and failure give four states. They are minimal: the empty word
separates failure from each productive state; a separates A from B0 or B1;
e separates B0 from B1. If legality must also be reported, keep FA,FB and
illegal X. The empty word separates the three output kinds; a separates
FA from FB. This proves the six-state minimum for the three-valued output
language. The audit prints distinguishing words for every pair.

**FINITE-EXACT:** on every generated tournament through n=12, acceptance
equals unimodularity of M2. Both sectors have determinant+/-1 on accepted
words; every rejected word has det minus=0 and nonzero det plus of magnitude
greater than1. No all-order determinant identity is asserted. A sufficient
induction would prove the three productive-sector transitions and persistence
of a native kernel after be or ee. That is the precise remaining obligation.

### Why the full A000568 problem has a different scale

**Elementary obstruction (PROVED).** No fixed finite matrix C over C and fixed
vectors u,v satisfy `a_n=u C^n v` for all tournament class counts a_n. Any such
matrix sequence is bounded by a polynomial times an exponential. But each
isomorphism class has at most n! labelings, so

    a_n >= 2^(n(n-1)/2)/n!.

Its nth root is unbounded. This is stronger than any numerical failure of a
small fitted recurrence but weaker than the inherited non-P-recursiveness
result. It only excludes stationary finite linear transfer; growing state,
nonstationary weights, nonlinear recurrences and symbolic cycle indices remain
available. The small grammar and the full enumeration therefore demand
different kinds of closure.

## III. A positive functional must see the target sources

### Closed LRC thresholds: keep endpoint atoms

For positive integer speeds V and rational `0<delta<1/2`, set

    Safe(V,delta) = {t mod1: ||v*t||>=delta for every v in V}.

Each individual safe set is a finite union of closed arcs. Define the
owner-tagged atomic measure

    nu = sum_(v in V) sum_(0<=k<v) (delta_((k+delta)/v)
                                      +delta_((k-delta)/v)),

where locations are reduced modulo 1 and repeated locations retain their
owner multiplicity. Let b_v indicate the *open* danger condition
`||v*t||<delta`. Then

    Safe(V,delta) nonempty iff
    nu[ product_(v in V)(1-b_v) ] > 0.                  (5)

**Proof.** A nonempty safe set is a proper closed finite union of intervals
and points, because0 is unsafe. A boundary point of any nonempty component
is a boundary point of at least one individual safe set, hence belongs to
the support of nu. Conversely a positive summand in(5) is itself a safe
time. Every summand has nonnegative integer weight. QED.

This is a reformulation using elementary endpoint geometry, not a new LRC
proof or a claim of a new decision algorithm. Its atom budget grows with
`sum V`; computing positivity uniformly is still the problem. Reflection
pairs all endpoint atoms: neither0 nor1/2 is an endpoint for delta=1/q,q>=3.
Thus the integer in(5) is even, but evenness alone never proves positivity.

For V=1,...,q-1 and delta=1/q, the safe times are precisely j/q with gcd(j,q)=1.
Indeed the q points0,t,...,(q-1)t must have pairwise circular distance at
least1/q. Their q gaps sum to1, forcing all gaps equal1/q; the converse follows
by permuting residues. For q=14 this gives six points. Each has exactly two
owners, from v*j=+/-1 mod14, giving atomic value12 despite zero Lebesgue
measure. Full diffuse moment information cannot distinguish a null safe set
from an empty one. The atom changes the functional, not the underlying orbit.

### Positive integers in a Collatz word space

Use odd source n=2m-1 and weights

    w(m)=1/(2*4^floor(log2 m)),    m>=1.

Each dyadic shell has mass `2^(-k-1)`, so the weights sum to1. These are the
inherited Elias source weights, not a new probability distribution. If E_L
is the set of sources not reaching the specified root by horizon L, then

    mu(E_L) -> mu(intersection E_L).

Since every source has positive weight, the limit vanishes iff every source
eventually reaches the root. This is continuity from above on a probability
space; it is an exact equivalence, not a decay estimate.

For accelerated 5x+1, the three sources 13,33,83 cycle and never reach1.
Their indices m are 7,17,42, with weights1/32,1/512,1/2048. Thus

    mu(E_L) >= 69/2048 for every L.                     (6)

A source-preserving bijection pushes these atoms forward and preserves(6).
Conjugacy also preserves the period of the cycle. Passing to a diffuse
generic measure or forgetting source labels is not such a bijection. This
is why a reframe that removes the 5x+1 counterexample must expose the precise
changed predicate or lost source before being transferred to 3x+1.

The LRC and Collatz measures are not identified: one is a finite endpoint
measure constructed from a speed row, the other a countable measure on
arithmetic inputs. The transferred principle is the positive atom criterion
for the actual target quantifier, with the relevant source maps retained.
