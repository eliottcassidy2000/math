# A quartic algebraic, nonrational source Hamiltonian flow

2026-10-19. **PROVED:** for H=x⁵t²(1+x²t), every nonzero scalar time of the source t-adic Hamiltonian flow has both coordinate images algebraic but individually nonrational over K(x,t), over any characteristic-zero field K. The field generated by both coordinate images over K(x,t) has degree exactly four. This is a concrete flow classification for one Hamiltonian; it does not classify all monomial carriers or settle planar JC.

## 1. Source existence and the rational invariant coordinates

Work in original source coordinates x,t, with {A,B}=A_xB_t−A_tB_x. Set

    q=1+x²t,   u=x²t,   E=H=x⁵t²q.

Let δ={−,E}. Then

    δx=2x⁵t+3x⁷t²,
    δt=−5x⁴t²−7x⁶t³.

Thus δ raises source t-order by at least one on K[x][[t]]. For every λ∈K its scalar exponential C_λ=exp(λδ) exists continuously, with inverse C_(−λ). Set X=C_λ(x), T=C_λ(t), v=C_λ(u)=X²T. All these are specified elements of the original source completion. No choice of an arbitrary algebraic root is substituted for this exponential.

The invariant E is fixed, and

    E=xu²(1+u),
    x=E/[u²(1+u)],   t=u⁵(1+u)²/E².                (1)

Consequently u,E are algebraically independent and K(x,t)=K(u,E). These are birational function-field coordinates, not global polynomial affine coordinates. The source completion embeds in K(x)((t)), which supplies a common field for comparing algebraicity and rationality.

## 2. A polynomial time coordinate

Direct differentiation gives

    δu=−xE=−E²/[u²(1+u)].

For the polynomial

    P(z)=z³/3+z⁴/4,   P′(z)=z²(1+z),

one therefore has the exact source polynomial identity

    δP(u)=−E²,   δ²P(u)=0.

Apply the continuous source exponential to P(u). Since it is an algebra homomorphism, this proves

    P(v)=P(u)−λE².                                 (2)

In particular v is algebraic of degree at most four over K(x,t). The construction of C_λ already supplies the relevant branch; no implicit-function assertion at the singular value u=0 is required. Equation (2) alone is not claimed to select this branch uniquely.

## 3. Irreducibility at every nonzero scalar time

Assume λ≠0. Regard u as transcendental over K. In K(u)[V], the polynomial P(V)−P(u) vanishes at V=u with derivative P′(u)=u²(1+u)≠0. Hence the prime V−u occurs with multiplicity exactly one. Therefore

    −[P(V)−P(u)]/λ

is not a square in K(u,V): a square rational function has even valuation at every irreducible prime. It follows that

    λW²+P(V)−P(u)

is irreducible as a quadratic polynomial in W over K(u,V). Its coefficients, viewed in the UFD K(u)[V], have no common nonunit divisor because λ is a nonzero scalar. Gauss's lemma makes it irreducible in K(u)[V,W]. Now view it as a polynomial in V over K(u)[W]. Its leading V coefficient is the nonzero scalar 1/4, so it is again primitive; a second application of Gauss's lemma proves irreducibility over K(u,W)[V]. Finally rename the independent variable W as E.

Thus the polynomial

    P(V)−P(u)+λE²                                  (3)

is irreducible of degree four over K(u,E)=K(x,t). The source element v from (2) is a root, so

    [K(x,t,v):K(x,t)]=4.                            (4)

The argument works for every nonzero scalar λ over every characteristic-zero field; it makes no assumption that K is algebraically closed or embeds into the complex numbers.

## 4. Each source coordinate is algebraic and nonrational

Apply the source exponential to the identities behind (1). In the common fraction field,

    X=E/[v²(1+v)],   T=v⁵(1+v)²/E².                (5)

All denominators here are nonzero: E≠0, and v is the image of the nonzero element u under a source automorphism, while 1+v is the image of 1+u. Equations (5) show X,T∈K(x,t,v), hence both are algebraic. Conversely v=X²T, so

    K(x,t,X,T)=K(x,t,v)

and the joint coordinate extension has degree exactly four.

If X belonged to K(x,t), the relation

    Xv³+Xv²−E=0

would be a nonzero polynomial of degree at most three over K(x,t) satisfied by v, contradicting (4). Thus X is nonrational.

To treat T separately, put C=P(u)−λE². The monic quartic relation is

    v⁴+(4/3)v³−4C=0.

Polynomial division gives

    v⁵(1+v)² ≡ (4C+16/81)v³+(8C/3)v²
                    +(4C/9)v−16C/27
                  modulo V⁴+(4/3)V³−4C.           (6)

If T belonged to K(x,t), subtracting E²T from the right side of (6) would give a polynomial of degree less than four over K(x,t) annihilating v. This polynomial is nonzero because C=P(u)−λE² is nonzero and its v² coefficient is 8C/3. Again this contradicts (4). Thus T is nonrational as well.

The inverse source exponential exists, but neither this fact nor the birational coordinate change (1) makes C_λ a rational plane map. At nonzero time its two coordinate images lie in a genuine degree-four algebraic extension. Its formal Jacobian is one, as for every source Hamiltonian exponential, but it is not a polynomial or rational Keller-map counterexample.

## 5. A concrete three-way comparison

For the shared shape H=t²q R(x,t), the following basic examples are now proved:

- R=x⁴: H=x⁴t²q=u²(1+u). [Packet 124](planar_common_u_rational_flows_20261019.md) gives an explicit rational nonpolynomial flow.

All times here are nonzero and K has characteristic zero. These examples establish three distinct scalar-time outcomes. They do not classify other exponents, arbitrary polynomial R, or compositions with different invariants. The x⁵ carrier has boundary weight −5 and lies outside the earlier Newton cone.
