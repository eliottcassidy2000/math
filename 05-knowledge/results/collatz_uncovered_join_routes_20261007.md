# Guarded inverse families and paid exits at a bounded selector's frontier

2026-10-07. **PROVED:** exact guarded smaller dependencies, their first-hit
certificate transport, and infinite arithmetic progressions missed by the
declared baseline. **FINITE-EXACT:** the finite comparison below. **OPEN:**
universal paid coverage and Collatz. A smaller dependency is not itself a
completed ROOT certificate. No priority claim is made for inverse iteration,
affine word carriers, sibling equivalence, or cylinder lifting.

The baseline **B8** is exactly
`adaptive_boundary_selector_20261004.select(n,8,'adaptive',word_cap=128,phase_depth=2)`
with no learned rows or supplied child certificates. Its eight units are
**macro actions**, not eight odd steps. The existing171-row core bank, virtual
contraction recognition, all eligible siblings, and repeated112/12/1 rules
remain enabled. This is a particular inherited selector, not the union of
every proved family in the repository.

Two new rules turn some of its pending sources into strict smaller-source
obligations:

| Source | Smaller child | Checked relationship |
|---|---|---|
| \(n=91+162t\) | \(h=71+128t=(64n-73)/81\) | \(U_{1122}(h)=n\) |
| \(n=111+4374t\) | \(h=103+4096t=(2048n-2067)/2187\) | \(U_{1121112}(h)=4n+1\) |

Here and throughout \(t\) is a nonnegative integer, \(U\) is the accelerated
odd Collatz map, and a string denotes its **exact ordered valuations**. The
second source family consists entirely of multiples of3; such a source has
no odd predecessor. Its useful relation is a common future through the
sibling \(4n+1\), rather than an actual predecessor of \(n\).

The following narrower progressions are missed by B8 **at every height**:

\[
 n=91+81\,2^{38}t,
 \qquad
 n=111+2187\,2^{28}t.                                      \tag{1}
\]

Both are accepted by the new rules. They are disjoint modulo3, so the increase
in the set of sources with a paid dependency has natural density at least
\(1/(81\,2^{38})+1/(2187\,2^{28})>0\) among all positive integers. This is
**relative coverage**, not a claim that all members already have ROOT proofs.

## 1. A general inverse-word-to-sibling compiler

Let \(w\ne\varnothing\) be a positive valuation word with carrier

\[
 U_w(x)=(Px+B)/Q,\qquad P=3^r,\quad Q=2^A.
\]

Fix \(k\ge0\), put \(S(n)=4n+1\), and define

\[
 C=Q4^k,\qquad D=Q(4^k-1)/3-B,
 \qquad h=(Cn+D)/P.                                      \tag{2}
\]

Assume \(C<P\). The exact all-height source conditions are

\[
 n>1\text{ odd},\quad Cn+D\equiv0\pmod P,
 \quad Cn+D>0,\quad (P-C)n>D.                            \tag{3}
\]

There is one odd source class modulo \(2P\), with a finite initial cut from
the strict inequalities. The compiler returns its least allowed member and
period. It makes no inference from slope alone when the intercept is positive.

**Legality proof.** Equation(2) gives the formal endpoint \(S^k(n)\), an odd
positive integer. The total divisibility by \(3^r\) forces each reverse
division by3: the later inverse numerators inherit the appropriate factors
because powers of2 are units modulo powers of3. Each reverse numerator has
the form \(2^a y-1\), so every integral inverse state is odd. Alternatively,
reduce the full forward identity modulo each remaining power of3 and work
backwards. Starting from the positive \(h\), all forward states are positive;
their oddness makes every declared valuation exact. Thus
\(U_w(h)=S^k(n)\), with no intermediate ROOT: ROOT would remain1, whereas
the endpoint is greater than1.

For \(k=0\), the common-future receipt has source word empty and child word
\(w\), meeting at \(n\). For \(k>0\), let
\(a=v_2(3n+1)\). Since \(3S^k(n)+1=4^k(3n+1)\), the actual words

\[
 \text{source }(a),\qquad \text{child }w(a+2k)             \tag{4}
\]

meet at \(U(n)\). The join may itself beROOT, but neither route is padded
after an earlier ROOT. Conditions(3) prove \(0<h<n\).

This is a sufficient compiler domain requiring \(C<P\), not a completeness
claim for all possible affine joins. Within that domain, (3) is the exact
positive paid family.

### The two concrete rows

For \(w=1122,k=0\), the carrier is \((P,Q,B)=(81,64,73)\), giving the
first row. Its least positive paid source is91.

For \(w=1121112,k=1\), the carrier is
\((P,Q,B)=(2187,512,2579)\), so \(D=-2067\), giving the second row. At its
least source, the two actual paths are

\[
111\xrightarrow{1}167,
\qquad
103\xrightarrow{1121112}445\xrightarrow{3}167.
\]

Both rows have negative affine intercept and contraction coefficient less
than1. Positivity is checked at the least member and increases thereafter.
In the second row \(n\equiv3\pmod9\); the simple obstruction
\(3\nmid U(x)\) for any odd integer \(x\) proves that this is a genuine
asynchronous join, not an inverse route into \(n\).

2. Exact valuations of \(x+1,x+5,11x+19,3x+1\) are fixed with one extra bit.
   This fixes repeated-block lengths and their128-letter caps. The
   to-ROOT special branch stays false because the fixed first valuation has
   an endpoint greater than1 at the seed, increasing with the source lift.
3. Every virtual row is absent for a demonstrated finite reason. Fix
   \(j=v_2(x+1)-1\). If its proposed chart has the wrong \(j\), that rejection
   is permanent. Otherwise the native dyadic residue fails, and its full
   modulus is retained. No claim of an unbounded failed search is used.
4. Every possible sibling list is fixed by its dyadic prefix. The only

   the seed original source, and its affine coefficient against that source

5. Each actual source prefix has coefficient greater than1 and positive

This exhausts the branches reached by the unmodified policy. The script
checks the policy's actual code/data, the fixed constants, and all retained

## 3. Inspect the retained frontier before declaring its obligation unpaid

The first two rules alone do **not** improve a ROOT1-only ascending completion
count: their three finite children71,103,6471 are themselves unavailable to
that policy. This is a useful separation between a paid dependency and a
grounded certificate. A further bounded probe gives a constructive repair.

At111, B8 has already checked the18-letter word

\[
 v=(1,1,1,2,1,2,1,1,1,1,1,3,1,1,1,4,2,2)
\]

and stopped at325. Its carrier is

\[
 F_v(n)=\frac{387420489n+617087321}{134217728}.
\]

The stored frontier325 is \(S(81)\). The child81 already has a certificate
under the unchanged ROOT1-only baseline. More generally, the exact native
guard \(n\equiv111\pmod{2^{30}}\) yields

\[
\begin{aligned}
n&=111+1073741824t,\\
x=F_v(n)&=325+3099363912t,\\
h=(x-1)/4&=81+774840978t
 =\frac{387420489n+482869593}{536870912}<n.             \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]

Here \(n-h=30+298900846t>0\), and \(x\equiv5\pmod8\). For the actual
\(a=v_2(3x+1)\ge3\), the source word \(v(a)\) and child word \((a-2)\)
meet at the same endpoint. All sources in(5) are in the B8-pending cylinder
111mod\(2^{28}\). Thus this is a third all-height added guard, of density
\(2^{-30}\); it is disjoint from the91 progression in(1).

Every source prefix through \(v\) grows relative to the original source.
For odd \(t\), the final exponent is exactly3 and
\(3^{19}>2^{30}\), so even the common endpoint remains above the source.
This half is a genuine smaller-child reroute before any ordinary descent.
At \(t=0\), the final exponent is4 and the source first descends at its
19th odd edge; the authenticated child81 supplies its completed suffix.

**General frontier rule.** Given an authenticated pending prefix
\(U_w(n)=x\), retain every odd sibling ancestor \(h\) with
\(x=S^k(h)\), \(k\ge1\), and \(0<h<n\). If
\(a=v_2(3x+1)\), use the source word \(w(a)\) and child word \((a-2k)\).
The native sibling chain guarantees \(a-2k\ge1\). If \(h=1\), the child
word is instead empty, and the source's last edge ends atROOT; the compiler
does not pad the root with its self-return. Every other receipt has a one-edge
actual child suffix. All alternatives are retained, rather than discarding
an available child because a different smaller child is not yet certified.

This uses no additional *discovery prefix* before the stored frontier. The
receipt still has **one additional source edge**: its exponent is checked by
the integer identity, and it can be derived from the supplied child's first
edge plus \(2k\). It is not counted as part of the original eight actions or
called a free unobserved proof. No claim compares its runtime with a nine-macro
policy or with a fully saturated observed-edge graph.

### Grounded completion under the same seed bank and policy

The next control processes requested odd sources in increasing order. It
starts from the declared supplied seed certificates, accepts only an
available strictly smaller dependency, retains all advertised alternatives,
and compiles an actual first-hit source word. Each resulting word is replayed
before entering the certificate cache. It makes one ascending pass; it is
neither a least fixed point of all known common-future edges nor an optimal
search over source orders.

The seed27, when present, has the same explicit41-letter first-hit word in
both runs, printed in the output and independently checked before use.
It is not used in the ROOT1-only comparison. With suffix memory enabled,
only actual suffixes of already authenticated seed or compiled certificates
are added, retaining their parent certificate and cut position as provenance.
This includes values above the requested universe; none is an extra assumed
seed. The baseline and refinement use identical memory rules and seed banks.

omitted sources have no proofs, or that every member of the all-height rows

is still guard coverage and grounding, not the local receipt substitution.

**A second pass is a checked stopping result, not another source of gains.**
Without suffix memory, ascending order already gives the least grounded
closure for the frozen strict-smaller-dependency relation. With memory, a
later proof can in principle authenticate an earlier unprocessed suffix, so
a second pass is meaningful. The script feeds only each first pass's already
authenticated certificate dictionary into an otherwise identical second
pass. The original assumed seed bank remains1/27; the rest have the first
pass's proved provenance. For all three policies, the **entire** dictionary
is unchanged, including the exact first-hit word at every key, not just the
requested-source subset. Its respective cardinalities are22964,22965,23418,
including certified suffix values above32767. Determinism then makes these
finite caches fixed points of this particular continuation operator. Mere
reordering or another identical pass supplies no further completion here;
the next useful change must add a rule, a checked observation, or a justified
certificate. This is not closure over every possible Collatz receipt.

## 5. The remaining obligation

The least pending source remains27;703 also remains pending. Reversing the
valid \(71\to91\) receipt cannot certify a paid dependency from71 to91,
because the child would be larger than its retained source. A cycle of
undischarged point assumptions is rejected rather than treated as ROOT.

If a proved family of guarded rules were shown to give every positive odd
\(n>1\) a strictly smaller positive odd child with a checked common-future
receipt, ordinary strong induction fromROOT would finish Collatz. This note
adds two families and a reusable way to certify their **relative** novelty;
it does not establish that remaining covering lemma. A concrete next target
is a growing family through a source with higher3-adic valuation such as27,
or a structural guard-cover theorem for an unbounded inverse-word grammar.
