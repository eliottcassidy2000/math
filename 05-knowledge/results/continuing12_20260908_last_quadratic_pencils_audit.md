## 4. Geometric generic integrality and the constants field

Set g=F-s and v=(1+x²t)/x. Then

    C(x,t)=C(g)(v,x), with x³=g/[v(dv+p)],
    D_F(v)=3g.

The inverse source expression t=v/x-1/x² proves that this presentation
recovers the full field; it does not assert that v alone generates it.
Over an algebraic closure of C(g), the right side has simple poles at
v=0 and v=-p/d. Therefore it is not a cube. Since this field contains
all cubic roots of unity, X³-g/[v(dv+p)] is irreducible. Thus the generic
curve is geometrically integral and the extension over C(g)(v) has
degree exactly three.

In particular C(g) is algebraically closed in C(x,t). To spell out the
step used here, an algebraic element outside C(g), in characteristic
zero, would generate a nontrivial separable constant extension. Tensoring
that extension with the algebraic closure splits it into more than one
factor. It cannot be a subalgebra of the geometrically integral generic
field after the same base change. Hence there is no such element.

Any D_F-constant is algebraic over C(g): otherwise the full field, of
relative transcendence degree one, would be algebraic over C(g) together
with that transcendental constant. A derivation vanishing on this base
vanishes on its algebraic extension in characteristic zero. That
contradicts D_F(v)=3g!=0. Combining the two facts gives

    ker(D_F:C(x,t)->C(x,t))=C(g).

This is the exact constants hypothesis of the component-jet theorem.
Replacing geometric integrality by rational generic fibres would lose
this example.

## 5. Genus and all actual source fibres

For any c!=0, the cubic cover of the v-line is

    x³=c/[v(dv+p)].

The divisor orders are -1 at v=0, -1 at v=-p/d, and +2 at infinity.
All three points are fully ramified in the degree-three cover; there are
no other branch points. Riemann-Hurwitz gives

    2*genus-2=3*(-2)+3*(3-1)=0.

Thus the smooth projective completion has genus one. Independently, the
quadratic-in-t discriminant of g-c is

    x⁵(p²x³+4dc).

After removing the square x⁴, its double-cover model is
eta²=x(p²x³+4dc). This quartic has four distinct roots, since its
discriminant is -27p⁴(4dc)⁴!=0. The double-cover branch count again
gives genus one. Both arguments apply to every nonzero complex c and
to the geometric generic fibre.

The same discriminant has odd x-valuation five and hence is not a square
in C(x). The source polynomial g-c is primitive in t: its leading and
middle coefficients have only x=0 as a possible common root, while its
constant there is -c!=0. Gauss's lemma therefore proves irreducibility
of every nonzero source fibre.

The zero source fibre has exactly three components:

    E_x: x=0,
    E_w: 1+x²t=0,
    E_L: d(1+x²t)+px=0.

The last two are primitive linear polynomials in t, with constant values
1 and d at x=0, so are irreducible and separated from E_x. On E_w,
x is a unit and L=px!=0, separating E_w and E_L. Source submersion
also ensures reducedness. No extra component of the original source
