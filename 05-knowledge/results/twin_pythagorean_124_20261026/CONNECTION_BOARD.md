## Precise stopping boundaries

The natural twin/tree correspondence is closed as a theorem, but counting
infinitely many selected vertices is exactly the unresolved twin-prime
question. Finite conic incidence and branch parity do not count those lifts.
The centre abc family has no height/radical excess to exploit; the squared
family pays an additional norm-support factor. Forgetting prime weights
does not preserve the aliquot collision. A successful next number-theoretic
step would need a lift-counting estimate or additional arithmetic invariant
that survives these explicit losses. These are research targets, not a
claim that an open problem has been solved or background work scheduled.
