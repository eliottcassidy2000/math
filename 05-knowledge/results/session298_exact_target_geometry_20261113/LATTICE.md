# Finite legal repair geometry

## A conductor theorem for already renewable repairs

Suppose a finite strongly connected directed graph of marked interface states has a verified physical translation x↦x+w for each edge, w a nonnegative integer, with guards closed under legal paths from a seed at v. Assume some directed cycle has positive weight. Let d be the gcd of weights of all directed cycles. For any terminal t and any fixed path v→t of weight q, the increments at t are all q mod d and include every sufficiently large number of that residue.

Proof: Every closed walk decomposes into cycles. Conversely for a cycle at u, fix routes v→u→v and compare the closed walk with and without the cycle. Their weights differ by that cycle weight. Thus the monoid of actual v→v weights has gcd d; a finite set of positive actual walk weights generates that gcd (take the simple cycles and their two routed walks, omitting zero). After dividing by d, these generate a numerical semigroup: modulo one generator a, nonnegative combinations reach all residues because addition in a finite cyclic group eventually supplies inverses, and the subgroup generated is all residues. Choose an actual nonnegative combination b_i in each residue; beyond max b_i every value is a b_i plus a nonnegative multiple of a. Append the fixed path to t. Appending any fixed t→v path proves every actual increment at t has the same residue. If all cycle weights vanish then all v→t path weights coincide; there is no positive conductor mechanism.

The hypothesis of physical translation is substantive. The R^kNR operation above changes size, scale and endpoint state, and is not a finite-state additive repair loop merely because its closed formula is affine in 2^k. Primitive signed span (a lattice computation) is necessary but cannot substitute for nonnegative walks or preservation of guards.

## Related carriers (not identifications)

* [Graph Hodge/Kirchhoff port ledgers](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4739-kirchhoff-and-hodge-laws-of-the-tournament-metagraph-intransitivity-currents-second-order-determination-and-the-consecutive-start-port-ledgers.md) motivate separating differences of a global scalar (which telescope around a true closed walk) from increments of a marked coordinate on a family of different witnesses. The latter need a defined lift, not just nonzero periods in an abstract graph.
* [Planar Jacobian response fibre](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2230-planar-jacobian-response-fiber-and-exact-target-shear-quotient.md): a quotient which removes nuisance coordinates may preserve an exact target while concealing the coordinate needed for the next lift. Here the unrooted H17 target and even the unrooted isomorphism type stay fixed, yet the named internal port crosses a defect wall. This does not imply transport of Keller conditions.
* [Hadwiger–Nelson and Collatz geometry](../session140_collatz_hexagonal_geometry_20261102/README.md): the distinction between an abstract lift and a bounded local lattice realization is sharp; a local gadget or finite residue corridor need not scale globally. The conductor proof works because its cycles come with repeatable physical witnesses, a condition not provided by merely embedding a residue graph.
* [Algebraic Hodge and cycle lifting](../../../07-reflections/hodge-conjecture-cycle-lifting-lrc14-codex-s232.md): retained invariants can define a necessary fiber without producing integral representatives in it. The proved analog here is the legal-loop certificate above, not a result on algebraic classes.

The geometric question is not whether a pattern looks straight in a convenient projection. It is whether the projected repair direction has physical representatives that compose, and whether a finite collection of those representatives generates the required positive cone and residue classes.
