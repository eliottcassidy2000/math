## Mathematical proof audit

- **Types and clocks:** c is a five-adic affine identity height; R is mean
  per controller period, R/A is per original valuation clock. It is not a
  return probability or an actual integer orbit theorem. Entry charge is
  omitted once and its state persists; it is never reset.
- **Quantifiers:** the suffix domination chooses one suffix which works at
  every depth for a fixed entry. An arbitrary positive word is allowed;
  no bound on its length is assumed. Finite-time error explicitly depends
  on its clock. The all-clock<=14 result is finite only in controllers.
- **Cosets:** the ambient affine group need not be commutative or normal.
  Intersecting right cosets of the same subgroup are equal. The canonical
  suffix supplies a genuine representative at all depths.
- **Equality boundary:** arbitrary-controller empty entry need not maximize;
  the five-letter hostile has mean369/200 at a suffix versus5/8 empty.

  all-maximizer completion requires a!=b, hence a primitive word.

  changing-generator induction, a geometric tail proves every threshold

  phase strictly below1/2, eliminating hidden folds.

  banks may have irrational clearance; the explicit convergent witness
