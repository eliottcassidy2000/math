The abstract explicitly states the lexicographic-product reduction to a complete bidirected palette of size chi(H). The elementary tournament proof needed here is supplied in P1; no uninspected technical lemma is a dependency.

The abstract reports a quadratic rather than linear neighborhood scale and a dominating-set bound in terms of fractional dichromatic number. Its proof was not audited in this session; none of P1–P4, R1–R4, A1–A3 or L1–L3 depends on it. It is context for future comparisons, not a claimed transfer.

Anti-blocking duality, the interval Helly property and clique encodings are classical mechanisms. We give their short required proofs and do not claim them as new general theorems. The weighted P7 certificate and the stated applications are self-contained; a literature priority audit remains separate.
