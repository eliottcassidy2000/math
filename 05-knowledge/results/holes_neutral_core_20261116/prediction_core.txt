Pre-registered predictions, klein-2026-11-16-core, written 2026-11-16 before neutral.py runs.  A "neutral choice" of a
tournament is a balanced arc a -> b (M(a, b) = M(b, a), THM-5267 (A)): reversing it preserves the count.  It is trivial
if the reversed tournament is isomorphic to the original, nontrivial otherwise (a count-preserving move to another
class of the same fibre F_h).  Fibres are taken over all classes of orders <= 8.

N-1 (the first two odd numbers)  The fibres F_1 (transitive tournaments) and F_3 (padded 3-cycles) have no balanced arc at
     all; F_5 has balanced arcs (the chords of the strong order-4 tournament) but no nontrivial neutral move.
N-2 (sterile-only fibres)  The strong classes of counts 11, 13, 47, 67 admit no nontrivial neutral move (orders <= 8).
N-3 (growth)  Among odd h <= 125, at least 60 % of the fibres with h >= 23 have a nontrivial neutral move, and the fibre
     with the most nontrivial neutral edges has h >= 60.
N-4 (the hard small counts)  Every fibre in {1, 3, 5, 9, 11, 13, 17, 19, 27, 35} has no nontrivial neutral move.
N-5 (neutral-rich cores)  The strong class of order 7 with the most balanced arcs is not the Paley tournament; at order 8
     some strong class has at least 8 balanced arcs.
N-6 (neutral choices enrich the response set)  Over odd h <= 125 with nonempty fibre, the Spearman correlation between the
     number of nontrivial neutral edges of F_h and the size of its response set B(h) (orders <= 8) exceeds 0.5.
