# Session 133 concept board

Anchor: actual FRACTRAN counters and the tournament H-spectrum.
Niche: exact bounded-H strong cores and finite induced-obstruction recognition.
Wildcard: one-apex binary words, odd-cycle conflicts, and positive matrix streams.

1. Burnside arithmetic: refine A000568 engine, then S/M/strong/colored variants.
   Preserve exact divisions and first-eligible control guards, not just outputs.
2. SCC words: positive lag recurrence -> actual simultaneous FRACTRAN stream.
   Inherited fixed-H generating functions; new compiler is not a novelty claim
   about the mathematical counting formula.
3. Strong budget: classical Busch bound + exact small inventory -> all-order
   finite reduction. A core-size optimum is not a minimum realizing order.
4. Local obstructions: hereditary H<=21, finite minimal induced forbidden list.
   A proved order bound must precede any claim of exhaustive completeness.
5. One-apex and adjacency encodings: keep the full ordered word/adjacency.
   Arbitrary independence graphs and arbitrary scalars need not be realizable.
6. Open spectrum completeness: exclusions7,21 already proved and credited.
   The remaining target is realization of every other odd integer. Finite
   witnesses and the existence of a universal evaluator do not discharge it.

Session124 finite constructor frontier, Session132 arithmetic compiler.
