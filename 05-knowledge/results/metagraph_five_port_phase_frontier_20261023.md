## 2. Converse turns the repair into a lossless parity triple

The converse of rooted type 66 is 457; type 204 is self-converse. None of
the fourteen successful pairs is closed under converse. Within the same
raw-coordinate menu, the minimum converse-closed separating set therefore
has three elements, attained by {66,457,204}.

    E = c_66 + c_457,   O = c_66 - c_457,   S = c_204.

    E >= |O|,   E = O modulo 2,   S >= 0,

index-two image lattice of this change of basis. Cone membership is only

This supplies a concrete even/odd triple, with reversal, integer recovery

## 3. The all-order rule transports flags before forgetting them

For a directed Hamiltonian path P, the target K_P(T) keeps the path arcs
and reverses the others. Inside a selected subset X, retain the forest
F_X of **actual consecutive path arcs** whose endpoints lie in X. Then

    (K_P(T)[X], F_X, root) = U(T[X], F_X, root),

where U keeps F_X and reverses every other local arc. U is a relabelling-
equivariant involution and commutes with deletion, provided deletion never
inserts a shortcut. Thus source flag histograms transform by a permutation;
forgetting the forest afterward gives the actual target motif records.
The proof and independent audit are in [the flag packet](metagraph_flag_transport_20261023/README.md).

At local size k there are

    2^(binom(k,2)-(k-1)) * 3^(k-1)

labelled admissible path-order states. Every one embeds in a global path
on at most 2k-1 vertices. At k=5 the 5,184 states give 676 unrooted and
3,360 rooted flag types. The transport is exact at every size; a fixed
small marginal need not separate all larger tournaments.

The [new cyclic hostile](metagraph_five_census_hostile_20261023/README.md)
makes this boundary sharp for the current observer. Two actual order-23
vertex-transitive tournaments have identical full rooted-five vertex
records. Ordinary repeated vertex refinement remains blind at every
depth. Their transitive-six counts are 1,173 and 1,265. Adding a common
dominating transitive prefix extends this obstruction to every order
at least 23, without claiming that the padded tournaments are strong.
The transitive-six difference remains exactly 92 after every such padding,
by the convolution TT6(TT_k -> T)=sum_r binom(k,6-r) TTr(T).
The next scale is therefore six-vertex incidence or retained overlaps,
not simply more rounds of the same vertex summaries.

There is also a structural obstruction at **every fixed motif size** k.
At prime order p, cyclic tournaments have at least
2^((p-1)/2)/(p-1) isomorphism classes. Their full k-census has at most
(binom(p,k)+1)^t_k values, where t_k is the fixed number of k-tournament
types. The former grows exponentially in p and the latter polynomially.
Hence sufficiently large prime orders have a collision. Vertex transitivity
turns equality of the global census into equality of every singly rooted
record, and regularity makes every ordinary refinement round blind.
Every prime cyclic tournament is strong. The packet supplies the prime
isomorphism and root-orbit arguments, so this is a proof, not an extrapolation
from the order-23 experiment. It does not cover actual-path flags or
multi-vertex overlap observers.

There is a constructive repair for the explicit pair: retaining **one
actual distinguished vertex**, rather than merely an unmarked histogram
of vertex-rooted counts, makes both order-23 tournaments discrete after

becomes discrete. Thus the endpoints already owned by an actual port are

The counting proof also gives a necessary growth scale in its specified
format. Using t_k<=2^(k(k-1)/2), cutoffs
k(p)<=(sqrt(2)-epsilon)*sqrt(log2 p) still collide for every sufficiently
large prime. A complete singly rooted motif/ordinary-refinement signature
on prime-cyclic tournaments therefore requires liminf k(p)/sqrt(log2 p)
at least sqrt(2). This is an information bound for that observer, not a
complexity lower bound for tournament enumeration or marked-port methods.

## 4. Cross-energy and a three-bit phase close the named electrical family

Let A be the root-reduced Laplacian, u=e_a-e_b and v=e_c-e_d. A balanced
four-terminal switch has

    A' - A = h (u v^T + v u^T).

For h=2^j s with s odd, the matrices agree through modulus 2^j. Their
first potentially different dyadic form is q_j; its difference is the
cross-form alpha tensor beta + beta tensor alpha, where alpha=u.x and
beta=v.x modulo two on the common lift space. It is nonzero exactly when
these two functionals are independent. Every diagonal value stays fixed.
See [proof and literal certificates](metagraph_linking_lifts_20261023/README.md).

For the native order-eight switch A_t=A+2t(uv^T+vu^T), capacities allow
exactly t=-1,0,1,2. The t=2 counterfeit preserves the full half-form and
the rank of the quarter-form, yet changes its marked radical. The highest
cyclic 2-primary factors are respectively 32,256,64,8. Ranks and counts
alone forget which potentials survive.

One saved native harmonic potential has residues (1,2,1,4) modulo eight
at these four terminals. Its defect on A_t is zero modulo eight iff 4
divides t. Thus only the native t=0 passes among the feasible choices.
Modulus four cannot reject t=2 for any potential because A_2-A is divisible
by four. This is an exact minimum within binary modular harmonic tests for
this **one-parameter family**, not all feasible port switches.

The limitations are productive. A small fixed-blue-path switch defeats
the entire binary tower but is detected by one ternary potential. Two
nonisomorphic trees with identical labelled degrees defeat all finite
critical-group/linking invariants. Electrical data need structural port
incidence even when every prime is retained.

## 5. What survives from Hall physics, QCD, and Bott work

The [recovery and source ledger](metagraph_hall_bott_20261023/README.md)
gives the precise transfers and primary references.

- **Fractional Hall:** inverse integral matrices pair marked charges.
  For the graph lattice, ker(A mod m) maps isomorphically to the m-torsion
  of coker(A) by x -> Ax/m. The phase of port i->j is (x_i-x_j)/m. This
  yields the tested nodewise conservation guard. It supplies no physical
  Hall fluid or conductance without physical and charge data.
- **A concrete return of 105:** the native order-256 charge has self-link
  105/256. Multiplying its generator by 27 gives self-link 1/256 because
  105*27^2=1 mod256. The invariant is its unit squareclass, here 1 mod8;
  the numerator105 depends on the generator. The old Goeritz and
  factorial-block work explains why retaining this unit matters.
- **QCD/root algebra:** an arc i->j maps to e_i-e_j; for three vertices,
  zero total flow characterizes the directed cycle. Matrix units retain
  composable paths through [E_ij,E_jk]=E_ik. But every strongly connected
  tournament generates the same sl_n, so forgetting the grading and
  distinct-vertex ownership destroys the distinctions we need.
- **Bott/stabilization:** the old marked-corner device is reusable.
  Adding a unimodular lattice block leaves the finite linking pairing
  unchanged; an even E8 block also preserves the discriminant quadratic
  form. Hence stable data omit actual coordinates. The occurrence of an
  eighth dyadic layer is not itself Bott periodicity.

For odd lattices the naive half-energy does not descend to a quadratic
form without a characteristic choice. The current port-phase guard uses
the bilinear pairing and avoids that extra obligation. The algebraic
connection is useful precisely because its lost data are visible.
