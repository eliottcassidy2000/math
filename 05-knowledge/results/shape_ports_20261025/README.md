The useful common research question is **which operation can be transported
without losing the target predicate?** This is a classification of proof
obligations, not a theorem that the named conjectures imply one another.

## What changed when the lanes met

- The low-order detour audit turned a missing filter into an explicit
  obstruction. Its lesson applies directly to the planar lane: a statement
  about one normalized chart must retain its entry hypotheses.
- The pointed-C3 collapse changes the actual weighted object while retaining
  cost and cut connectivity. It is a precise transition between the owner
  and endpoint languages, rather than an unrecorded clone merger.
- The finite-polytope obstruction explains why passing from a triangle to a
  circle may enlarge the available symmetries. Covariance gives an exact
  transition for an already fixed operator; it does not supply a missing
  arithmetic action or positivity certificate.
- The planar same-support hostile shows that preserving shape and semiring
  operations still loses determinant cancellations. This supplies an exact
  first test for every proposed Newton/F1 transfer.
- Bounded proof search places formalizations of the named conjectures in a
  common verification framework. A conditional fast search algorithm does
  not supply proofs, bounds on their lengths, or logical equivalences.
