# Moore graph spines and exact tile placements

**Status (2026-11-24):** Explicit for the diameter-two Moore graphs of degrees 2, 3 and 7; degree 57 remains open. The degree-57 graph-spine statements assume both the graph and, where specified, a spanning path. No spanning path for that hypothetical graph has been deduced.

- [Tile packing and the two arrow conventions](TILE-PACKING.md): if the path is made of graph edges, the free graph chords are exactly 6 (Petersen) and 126 (Hoffman–Singleton), not 15 and 175. Undirected girth five turns the Moore identity into an exact packing by disjoint five-cell pluses and two-chord forks. Petersen has just two fork cells.
- [The affine arrows explain unique routes](AFFINE.md): two same-side cohorts meet at a unique slope, while the ±1 and ±2 steps partition the missing mixed contacts. The symmetric affine scaffold explains why 5 works and why replacing it by a larger field does not give degree 57. Its cross rule also produces explicit graph Hamilton paths and cycles; the restricted whole-cohort family alone has 1,472,800 undirected Hamilton cycles.
- [Metagraph consequences](TILE-PACKING.md): every even-order direct graph-spine presentation is black. Petersen's two direct sparse ordinary classes have no blue line even after rerooting, lie at distance four from transitive, and are two native black moves apart. A restricted full-family replay proves the analogous reroot obstruction for every alternating whole-cohort Hoffman–Singleton spine. The earlier [compact blue encodings](../moore57_compact_blue_routes_20261124/) use a different decoder.
- [Hamilton paths versus tournaments](moore57-hamilton-path.md): a hypothetical degree-57 graph has a path through at least 3138 vertices by an exact shifted spectral calculation and rotations; this is not a spanning assertion. Once a graph spine is chosen, the [two completed tournaments](moore-spine-chords.md) have directed girth three, and their directed triangle counts differ by exactly (d−1)².
