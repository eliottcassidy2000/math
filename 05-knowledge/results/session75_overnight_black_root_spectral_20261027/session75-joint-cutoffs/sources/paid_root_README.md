## R1. Correct native affine coordinate and exact forcing price

Seeds s are positive odd integers, s not 5 mod 8. Let

\[
 a=\begin {cases}2&s=1\pmod 8,\\1&\text {otherwise},\end {cases}
 \quad (3s+1)/2^a=B^j(y),\quad B(x)=4x+1,
 \quad P(s)=y,\quad t=a+2j.
\]

Here t is the **native integer braid clock**, not the number of fully odd
Collatz steps, and not that count plus its valuations. Put X=3s+1, Y=3y+1.
Directly substituting B^j(y)=4^j y+(4^j−1)/3 gives

\[
 \boxed {2^tY=3X+2^a.} \tag {1}
\]

This corrects the displayed edge identity in the inherited actual-clock
note, where a constant was three too large. At s=3,y=1,a=1,t=3 the corrected
identity is 32=30+2. Its support bound remains valid, since3X<2^tY; its
inverse compiler already used the literal map. The correction is applied at
the source and logged, not left as a conflicting new version.

Define the actual-branch contraction and its loss parameter

\[
 b_s=\frac {3X}{3X+2^a},\quad d_s=1-b_s^2,\quad
 \lambda_s=\frac {1-b_s}{1+b_s}
 =\frac {2^a}{18s+6+2^a}. \tag {2}
\]

For an Eisenstein vector e in Q(omega), omega²+omega+1=0, with
Q(x+y omega)=x²−xy+y², the rational two-port identity is

\[
 e'=b_se+f,\quad r=d_se-b_sf,
 \qquad Q(e')+Q(r)/d_s=Q(e)+Q(f)/d_s. \tag {3}
\]

The proof is direct Hermitian polarization; rational coefficients commute
with the Eisenstein form. It is the earlier elementary contraction dilation
applied to the **native** affine coordinate.

Now use the real source transport, which multiplies a charge by
epsilon=(-1)^t=(-1)^a. To represent e'=epsilon e in (3) requires
f=(epsilon−b_s)e, hence the exact forcing cost is

\[
 \frac {Q(f)}{d_s}=
 \begin {cases}
 \lambda_s Q(e)&epsilon=+1,\\
 \lambda_s^{-1}Q(e)&epsilon=-1.
 \end {cases} \tag {4}
\]

If each source retains its complete path sign as a sidecar, the sign-gauged
source charge is constant along its path. It then pays lambda_s Q(e) at
every step. The signs have **not** been discarded: they must be restored in
the actual terminal synthesis. Under the constant-charge gauge the second
port r equals f, so this is an honest payment identity, not a claim of
decay of the transported charge itself.

## R2. A sharp per-clock payment bound on the actual frontier

Use the unchanged forward-closed 627-seed frontier V, maximum63665, from
`04-computation/experiments/first_arrival_20261018/V_closed_Y1000.txt`.
Its minimum excluded primitive seed is 1009. **PROVED, with this finite
frontier authenticated by the checker:** every exterior native edge satisfies

\[
 \boxed {\lambda_s\le t_s/9139.} \tag {5}
\]

Proof: for a=1, lambda_s=1/(9s+4), and t_s≥1. For a=2,
lambda_s=2/(9s+5), and t_s≥2. Thus every s≥1015 obeys (5).
The only smaller exterior primitive seeds are1009 and 1011; their exact
lambda/t values are1/27258 and 1/27309. At1015 the value is 1/9139,
so the local per-clock constant is sharp. Membership of all smaller
primitive seeds and these three edge evaluations are finite exact checks.

Let A_N(s) be the **original** source charge and mu_s=Q(A_N(s)), including
zero-charge source labels. For each source stop at first V entry or any
earlier finite prefix. If T_s is its actual elapsed clock, then

\[
 \mathcal F_{N}=\sum_s\mu_s\sum_{x\text { on its exterior prefix}}\lambda_x
 \le\frac1{9139}\sum_s\mu_sT_s.
 \tag {6}
\]

If every prefix has T_s≤h, this gives F_N≤(h/9139)S_N, where
S_N=sum_s mu_s is the unchanged full source energy. In particular this
specific forcing term is paid by S_N through clock 9139, uniformly in N.
This is **not** a ROOT-current energy bound through clock 9139: the coherent
terminal readout is a separate cost. No universal-entry assumption is used;
positive clocks make every finite cutoff computable, with moving sources
retained. The local sharp constant does not assert aggregate sharpness for
the complete prescribed family of A_N(s).

For a word of d native edges, T_i=t_1+...+t_i, its exact carry is

\[
 2^{T_d}X_d=3^dX_0+C_d,\quad
 C_d=\sum_{i=1}^d3^{d-i}2^{T_{i-1}+a_i}>0.
\]

Moreover

\[
 \prod_i b_i=\frac {3^dX_0}{2^{T_d}X_d},\qquad
 \sum_i\lambda_i\le\tfrac12\log\left(1+\frac {C_d}{3^dX_0}\right)
 \le\frac {C_d}{2\cdot3^dX_0}. \tag {7}
\]

The first inequality is -log b=2 atanh(lambda)≥2lambda. This converts
forcing into a source/carry budget; it does not bound that budget independently
of depth. **OPEN PF:** control (6) uniformly in depth for the actual source
family, retaining nonarrived prefixes, without presupposing entry.

## R3. Actual source refinement allocates each coherent pair once

For a declared finite first-arrival cutoff, retain each arrived source's
tuple (s,u_s,p_s,t_s,d_s,A_N(s)): terminal, exterior entry port, complete
integer clock, native depth and original charge. Set
v_s=(-1)^(t_s)A_N(s), and use the odd-index coordinate m_s=(s−1)/2.
For dyadic q define

\[
 W_{u,r}^{(q)}=\sum_{u_s=u,\ m_s=r\bmod q}v_s,
 \qquad E_q=\sum_{u,r}Q(W_{u,r}^{(q)}).
\]

This is an explicit temporary source partition of the **same** signed
current. It does not replace the full source by a random lift or erase
entry ports from the stored records. It is a Nyquist statement; no
all-frequency contraction is asserted.

Let beta(x,y)=Q(x+y)−Q(x)−Q(y). Then

\[
 \Delta_q:=E_q-E_{2q}
 =\sum_{u,r\bmod q}\beta(W_{u,r}^{(2q)},W_{u,r+q}^{(2q)}). \tag {8}
\]

Thus every pair of distinct named sources with the same terminal appears
exactly once, at q=2^(v_2(m_s−m_t)). This is its least common ancestor in
the binary tree read from low bits. Its sign still uses the exact clocks;
its two entry ports are recoverable from the pair address. There are no
cross-terminal interactions and no reuse of a pair at another refinement.

When q separates all sources, E_q=S_arrived≤S_N. Hence

\[
 E_1=S_{\rm arrived}+\sum_k\Delta_{2^k}
 \le S_N+\mathcal D^+,
 \qquad\mathcal D^+=\sum_k\max(\Delta_{2^k},0). \tag {9}
\]

The absolute/positive part is taken **after** summing a whole level, retaining
its actual cancellation. A pairwise absolute-value bound is weaker.
This is a finite algebraic identity, not a new general martingale theorem.

**OPEN dyadic-debt target:** for every N≥4096 and every finite native
first-arrival depth cutoff d, prove D^+_(N,d)≤S_N/2. This would imply the
inherited ET bound sum_u Q(W_u)≤(3/2)S_N. Through the already proved
source-norm estimate and marked-row inequality, it would give the inherited
large-source ROOT energy bound 15N for N≥2^14. Arrival of every source is a
separate obligation; the denominator continues to include moving sources.

Changing the modulus resolves *which pairs own the debt*. It does not
make the terminal sum contractive: summing the refined bins restores exactly
(8). The earlier adaptive alias-extraction lemma remains useful when a
certified energy budget is available, but is not a payment for (9).
For exact native branch queries, division still consumes precision: on a
fixed word of total clock T, retaining s modulo2^(T+r), r≥3, preserves its
bit guards and endpoint modulo2^r. This observation precision is not time.

## T1. Exact exception-cycle minimum for three transitive modules

For a tournament T and total vertex order pi, let D_pi contain precisely
the backward pairs. Put

\[
 \beta(D)=|E(D)|-|V(D)|+\#\text {components}(D),\quad
 \beta_{\min}(T)=\min_\pi\beta(D_\pi),
\]

counting isolated vertices in the component count. Let C3[T_a,T_b,T_c]
be a cyclic triple of transitive tournaments, with 1≤a≤b≤c after using
cyclic symmetry and converse. **PROVED for all a,b,c:**

\[
 \boxed {\beta_{\min}(C3[T_a,T_b,T_c])=\max(a-2,0)(b-1).} \tag {10}
\]

The minimum *number of backward edges* is ab. These are different
optimization objectives.

**Upper bound.** If a=1 use a whole-block order to make the backward graph
a star and isolated vertices. For a≥2 split the smallest block A into
A_1 of size 1 and A_2 of size a−1, preserving its internal transitive order.
Order the vertices as A_1,B,C,A_2. The backward graph is the disjoint union
K_(1,c) and K_(a−1,b). Its cycle rank is (a−2)(b−1). The named vertices
and every attached port are preserved; only the observer order changes.

**Lower bound.** Restrict the largest module to b named vertices. Deleting
vertices cannot increase the cycle space, so it suffices to bound
C3[T_a,T_b,T_b]. It has ab² directed triangles, each consisting of one
vertex from each part. Every triangle requires a backward edge. Each edge
is in at most b such triangles, so the backward graph has at least ab edges.
If it has at least ab+1, then beta≥ab+1−(a+2b)+1=(a−2)(b−1).

In the equality case of exactly ab edges, every triangle has precisely one
backward edge, and every backward edge attains the maximum triangle
incidence. Thus there are no within-part backward edges, and no backward
two-edge path whose vertices use all three parts: such a path would put
two backward edges in one directed triangle. A connected component cannot
therefore meet all three parts. There are at least two components, giving
beta≥ab−(a+2b)+2=(a−2)(b−1). For a=1 the nonnegative lower bound suffices.
The bound ab on backward edges is attained by a whole-block order choosing
the smallest pair as the backward interface. QED.

The checker exhausts 59,083 interleavings for all 20 sorted triples with
1≤a≤b≤c≤4, plus all 720 labelled orders for the 2+2+2 control. Sorting labels
within a clone part merely relabels its cross-edge defect graph and deletes
internal defects, so the interleavings suffice for the cycle minimum.

### Consequences and the necessary scope boundary

- Balanced blocks of size t require(t−1)(t−2) cycles on 3t vertices: quadratic
  debt relative to a total-order baseline. A universal linear rank-exception
  budget cannot cover a class closed under arbitrary transitive cloning of C3.
- Thin triples with a≤2 admit a forest frame even when b,c are arbitrarily
  large. For example C3[T_2,T_5,T_c] has beta_min=0 for every c≥5. The value 5
  is not special here; the structural threshold is the smallest block size 2.
- At sizes3,3,4 the edge minimum is 9 while the cycle minimum is 2, attained
  with 10 backward edges. A minimum-edge order has only the K_(3,3) defect
  and cycle rank 4. Minimizing edge count can miss the best cycle budget.
- These three-transitive-module families never have minimum cycle rank 1:
  the first positive value of (10) is 2. Paley7's exact value 1 is a different
  small atom. It has no proper nontrivial homogeneous module, checked on all
  119 candidate subsets; a join/C3 substitution tree cannot make it a
  zero-exception leaf-free composite. This is a finite structural distinction,
  not an identification with prime untouchability.
- This does **not** prove that these cloning operations are generated by
  the original four native return maps. The native-only all-word linear
  budget remains OPEN. It refutes the stronger clone-closed claim.

## T2. Reroute the baseline, rather than storing every cyclic block as errors

Use a named rooted expression tree with ordered-join and directed-three-cycle
nodes, transitive leaves/blocks, a marked Hamilton path, and a sparse exception
graph D. Keep every external port's actual vertex address. This describes the
family in (10) with zero exceptions and O(n log n) combinatorial bits, even
when its total-order exception debt is quadratic. Arbitrary supplied weights
and clocks have their own representation costs.

Arc queries use the least common ancestor and the selected quotient arc;
there are no ties. Re-marking a legal Hamilton path changes neither the tree
nor D. Full converse reverses join order and cyclic orientation. For native
path complement the exact update is

\[
 (B,D,P)\longmapsto(B^{\rm op},D\triangle E(P),P). \tag {11}
\]

Thus one complement of a zero-exception hierarchical baseline costs at most
n−1 named exceptions, regardless of its rank-relative debt. This is a proved
one-step statement. Repeated complements add at most n−1 each; a uniform
linear bound after arbitrary sequences requires a legal reparsing theorem
that is still **OPEN**. If future operations change modules, old port addresses
and path data must survive the parse. The inherited module-shattering hostile
still applies; a fixed unmarked tree is not asserted to be operation closed.

## A1. The {7,21} connection has an exact source-algebra realization

There is a useful exact connection before invoking any gap analogy. In the
same Eisenstein ring as the native ROOT charges, put alpha=3+omega. Then

\[
 Q(\alpha)=7,\quad (1-\omega)\alpha=4-\omega,
 \quad Q(4-\omega)=21.
\]

Multiplying the actual charge A=x+y omega by alpha uses the integer matrix

\[
 M=\begin {pmatrix}3&-1\\1&2\end {pmatrix},\qquad\det M=7,
 \quad Q(MA)=7Q(A). \tag {12}
\]

The source norm shells {0,1,3} become exactly {0,7,21}. This map commutes
with addition, the native sign, source refinement, and every fixed labelled
transport. All energy and dyadic-debt inequalities scale by 7 and hence have
the same normalized content. The original physical source weights are
recovered by dividing the new quadratic form by 7.

The important sidecars are exact. If MA=(x',y'), its image satisfies
2x'+y'=0 mod 7; M is an index-seven embedding, not a unimodular change over Z.
The original ROOT readout2x−y becomes

\[
 2x-y=(5x'-y')/7. \tag {13}
\]

Keeping instead the untransformed readout2x'−y' would change the target
direction. The conjugate element2−omega has trace5 and norm 7, with polynomial
z²−5z+7. These are exact algebraic appearances of 2,5,7, but untouchability
is not a predicate preserved by this map. Repeated norm-seven twists merely
rescale existing debt; they do not pay it.

A second exact {7,21} diagram uses the same divisor chain {1,2,4}:
sigma_1(4)=7 and sigma_2(4)=21, equivalently Phi_3(2)=7 and Phi_3(4)=21.
The weights changed from first to second powers. This is not an equality
between ordinary aliquot fibres and tournament path counts.

The inverse ROOT compiler makes the cyclotomic identity operational. Its
already proved branch formula is

\[
 s_j=\frac {2^a(3u+1)4^j-(2^a+3)}9,\qquad
 j=3-a-u\pmod3.
\]

The least next lift preserving the divisibility guard is j→j+3, so

\[
 s_{j+3}=64s_j+7(2^a+3),\quad
 7=\frac {4^3-1}{9}=\frac {\Phi_3(4)}3,
 \quad\Phi_3(4)=Q(4-\omega)=21.
\]

These are exactly the inherited offsets35 and 49. The clock increases by 6,
not by zero, even though its parity returns. Thus7 and 21 share a genuine
three-step/prime-norm mechanism with the native inverse compiler. The5 in
35 comes from the a=1 branch term 2+3; no untouchability predicate is used.
The old compiler is credited; the norm factorization supplies the connecting
map, rather than a new claim for its affine formulas.

## A2. Missing fibres: what transfers, and what does not

Recover [the audited aliquot inversion](../aliquot_power_124_20261026/aliquot/README.md)
and [S57's distinctions](../s57_untouchables_root27_20261026/README.md).
For m>1 every s(n)=m has n≤(m−1)², giving a complete finite inverse test.
Here a divisor sieve independently recovers

| m | Complete aliquot preimages |
|---:|---|
|2|empty|
|5|empty|
|7|8|
|21|18,51,91|

Thus 2 and 5 are proved prime untouchables; their being the **only** prime
untouchables is OPEN. The familiar distinct-prime Goldbach strengthening
would imply 5 is the only odd untouchable: see
[Pollack, exercise 8.26, page 272](https://www.pollack-math.net/NABDofficial.pdf#page=285).

There is a revealing difference between a missing subgrammar and an empty
full fibre. For distinct primes p,q, s(pq)=1+p+q. Target7 is missed by this
subgrammar because6 only has the prime decomposition3+3; on the diagonal,
s(p²)=1+p rather than 1+2p. But the higher prime-power atom8 repairs7.
For target5 the analogous4=2+2 also fails, and the complete inverse cutoff
rules out the other families. Target21 is already repaired off diagonal:
21=1+3+17=1+7+13, explaining sources51 and 91; 18 supplies another shape.

For tournaments, [THM-1370, H-spectrum omission](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-1370-h-spectrum-omits-7-21-all-n.md)
already proves raw H omits7 and 21 at every order, with no new priority claim.
Strong-component multiplication and the strong-order floor explain the gaps:
7 is between the strong-order floors 5 and 9. For 21, the complete small
spectra exclude it through order six, and the floor at order seven is 25;
its nontrivial factorization would require the unavailable factor 7.
The separate claim for
tc=H/|Aut| is only proved through order 11 in
[THM-4790](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4790-presentation-fibres-7-and-21-absent-through-eleven-vertices.md).
One cannot silently drop the automorphism denominator and use the H proof.

The useful analogy is **inverse-image emptiness with a complete source cutoff
or a certified atom reduction**. Its implementations differ: divisor closure
and a height bound for aliquot fibres; strong-component factorization and a
path-count floor for H. For tc the growing automorphism denominator is a
missing scale control. Moreover tc(C3)=1, so adjoining ordered C3 components
gives arbitrarily large neutral extensions. Raw source size is not a complete
inverse cutoff in that setting. No number map here transports untouchability
to a Hamilton-spectrum omission; 7 and 21 are explicitly touchable.
Nor is the missing H-factor 7 a ban on every multiple of 7: the inherited
order-eight example with H=63 already refutes that extrapolation. The useful
research question is which new indecomposable source shapes can repair a
subgrammar's gap, subject to the actual guards and lower bounds.
