# Three different 27s: dimension, a Collatz itinerary, and divisibility

**PROVED** for the bank and frame in [FILLER-JOINT](FILLER-JOINT.md);
**FINITE-EXACT** for the input tables and Collatz trace. Neither Collatz
termination nor an all-prime inverse follows.

## The 27-dimensional bank contains 27's long itinerary

Let \(H_X\) be the set of distinct physical starts in \([X,2X]\) from
unrestricted ordinal multiplicities of the 28 fixed alternatives, with
the same eight-site frame. Write \(c_{28}=\log2/(27!\prod_j\log R_j)\).
The old count is \(|H_X|\sim c_{28}(\log X)^{27}\): the 28 independent
multiplicities in one fixed-width log-height slice leave 27 dimensions.

**Dyadic distribution.** For every fixed \(K\ge1\) and every odd
\(u\bmod2^K\),
\[
 \#\{h\in H_X:h\equiv u\bmod2^K\}
 \sim \frac{c_{28}}{2^{K-1}}(\log X)^{27}.
\]
The iterated map \(T(n)=n/2\) for even \(n\) and \(T(n)=(3n+1)/2\) for
odd \(n\) associates each word of \(K\) parities to just one residue
modulo \(2^K\). This follows by lifting one bit at a time: after \(j\)
fixed parities, changing \(n\) by \(2^j\) changes \(T^j(n)\) by an odd
integer \(3^{o_j}\). See also the
[Bernstein–Lagarias conjugacy](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1996-060-x).
Thus every odd-start itinerary of fixed length is represented, with
the same power 27 and the indicated fraction of the leading constant.

For \(27=3^3\), the ordinary map takes **111** steps to reach 1, with
peak 9232. Every smaller positive start reaches 1 in at most 23 steps,
by the finite trace. The halving-accelerated map \(T\) uses 70 steps,
41 of them odd. Its word therefore holds on exactly the residue
\(h\equiv27\bmod2^{70}\). In particular that word is carried by an
asymptotic fraction \(2^{-69}\) of the bank at each dyadic height scale.
The integer outcome is not inherited: for \(h=27+2^{70}t\),
\[
 T^{70}(h)=1+3^{41}t.
\]
One real dynamical property does survive at all such \(h\ge27\).
The first iterate strictly below its own start comes at shortcut
step 59, or ordinary step 96. Up through shortcut step 58 the exact
trace checks \(T^j(27)\ge27\) and \(3^{o_j}\ge 2^j\), where \(o_j\)
counts odd branches. At step 59 both inequalities reverse:
\(T^{59}(27)=23\) and \(3^{37}<2^{59}\). Now
\(T^j(h)=T^j(27)+(3^{o_j}/2^j)(h-27)\), so each comparison lifts
in the asserted direction. Extra odd-step states of the ordinary
map never cause an earlier descent.

### Proof of the distribution and its scope

The finite bank has \(D_jR_j\equiv1\) or \(3\bmod8\) and \(4\mid Q_j\)
for all 28 alternatives. The subgroup generated by the pairs
\((D_j,R_j)\) modulo \(2^K\), for \(K\ge3\), is exactly
\[
 G_K=\{(D,R):D,R\text{ odd},\ DR\equiv1\text{ or }3\bmod8\}.
\]
Indeed the first alternatives of types 0, 1, 2 give the pairs
\((7,5),(1,3),(5,7)\bmod8\), generating all eight allowed pairs.
The squares of types 0 and 2 have independent binary lift vectors
\(((D^2-1)/8,(R^2-1)/8)\bmod2=(0,1),(1,0)\). Successive squaring
propagates these independent directions through every higher level:
\((1+2^j a)^2\equiv1+2^{j+1}a\bmod 2^{j+2}\) for \(j\ge3\).
This fills the kernel over mod 8. For \(K<3\) reduce the result.

For large \(X\), every contributing word is long enough that \(Q\)
vanishes modulo \(2^K\). The height residue is then \(11D+20R\).
For a fixed odd output \(u\), writing \(D=11^{-1}(u-20R)\) fixes
\(D\bmod8\) independently of odd \(R\), since \(20R\equiv4\bmod8\).
Exactly half the odd \(R\)'s meet the defining condition of \(G_K\).
The equal fibers give \(1/2^{K-1}\).

To turn group sizes into height counts, put \(E=2^{K-2}\) for \(K\ge3\).
The exponent vectors in \((\mathbb Z/E)^{28}\) map onto \(G_K\), with
constant fiber size. In each exponent congruence class, write \(e=a+Eb\)
with \(0\le a_j<E\), \(b_j\ge0\). Its \(R\)-products have the same
thin-strip asymptotic as in FILLER-JOINT, but with log weights
\(E\lambda_j\) and origin shifted by \(\lambda\cdot a\), so each
class has weight \(E^{-28}\). Eventual order preservation for the
physical heights, also from FILLER-JOINT, changes only \(O(1)\) points
at the two endpoints in total, not \(O(1)\) per congruence class.

**Growing clocks.** In fact there is an effective \(\eta>0\) for this
bank such that the same equivalent is uniform in \(u\) and
\(3\le K\le\eta\log_2\log X\). To see this, the fixed-log Matveev bound
used in FILLER-JOINT also gives
\(\|q\lambda_1/\lambda_2\|\gg q^{-B}\) for some \(B\ge1\).
Among the convergent denominators choose the largest \(q\le\sqrt N\).
The next denominator is \(\gg\sqrt N\) and at most \(O(q^B)\), so
\(q\gg N^{1/(2B)}\). Every full block of \(q\) rotation points is
within \(1/q\) of a translated equally spaced grid, after a permutation;
a leftover block costs at most \(q\) points. Thus the interval
discrepancy is \(O(N^{-\delta})\) uniformly in interval and phase for
some \(\delta>0\). In the thin-strip proof, after division by \(E\),
the scale is \(S=\log(X/20)/E+O(1)\) and the strip width is \(w/E\)
where \(w=\log2\). Summing the two-axis count over the other 26
coordinates changes its main term by at most \(O(S^{27-\delta})\):
for residual size \(U\), the rotation error is \(O((1+U)^{1-\delta})\),
and bounded residual and Riemann-sum errors are \(O(S^{26})\).
The main term is \(\asymp S^{27}/E\), so the relative error is
\(O(E S^{-\delta})\), uniformly over all cosets. Choose
\(\eta<\delta/(1+\delta)\) (and \(\eta<1\)). Summing uniform
relative errors over the appropriate cosets preserves the bound;
the \(O(1)\) physical endpoint discrepancy is negligible.
This controls some growing prefixes, not arbitrary depth at fixed scale.
Indeed the \((\log X)^{27}\) total count prevents shattering all
odd-start words beyond \(27\log_2\log X+O(1)\) bits.

## A constructive Collatz selector with the same mod-77 phase

Fix \(K\ge6\). On a single layout with \(D,Q,d\) fixed,
one can prescribe both \(R\bmod77\) (any unit) and \(h\bmod2^K\)
(any residue \(27\bmod64\)). This is independent of the asymptotic
argument and uses the first signed move of the prior max-gap selector.

Take \(N=2^{K-6}\) and quota \(16(N+1)\) for each of the five types
\((0,2,6,11,13)\). Prefix 13 fixed first alternatives of type 8.
Each of the five ratios is \(1\bmod16\); their quotas make their
first-alternative products in \(D\) and \(R\) neutral modulo 64, and
\(Q=0\bmod64\). Thus the five-type part makes no change to the
primer's \(h\equiv27\bmod64\), regardless of which copies are exchanged
(the coefficient of \(R\) is \(20\)).
Use baseline \((0,0,0,N,0)\) and the small phase anchors from FILLER-JOINT
to choose \(R\bmod77\) first. Then apply \(k\) times the signed
change \(v=(0,0,2,-1,0)\), for \(0\le k<N\), without leaving the quotas.
Its ratio \(g=P(v)\) is \(1\bmod77\) and \(17\bmod32\). Repeated
squaring gives \(v_2(g^{2^j}-1)=4+j\), so these powers fill all
\(1\bmod16\) classes modulo \(2^{K-2}\). The affine formula
\(h_k=C+20R_0g^k\) gives all the required binary lifts. The script
constructs \(k\) bit by bit and checks all 60 mod-77 phases for the
full 70-bit 27 itinerary. This uses an exponentially large quota in
\(K\); it is an existence and modular inverse, not an efficient compact
tournament for \(K=70\), nor the relative height bound at that same depth.

A smaller practical witness for the 70-bit prefix (without prescribing
modulo 77) uses 96 copies each of types
\((0,1,2,3,5,6,7,8,11,13)\), 13 fixed first copies of type 8,
and second-copy counts \((72,75,53,47,4,80,11,95,41,51)\).
It has order 8,767. A lattice search found the copy counts; the
certificate only needs direct modular powers. All these types also
have \(3\nmid D\), so the start is not divisible by 3.

**The sevenfold exit gives a second 27:28.** In the ordinary map \(F\),
27 and \(189=7\cdot27\) first join at 142, at steps 8 and 3 respectively.
The common suffix has 103 further steps, so 189 uses 106 ordinary
steps, with 38 odd branches and 68 halvings. For any physical
\(h=27+2^{70}z\), the paired end \(7h\) follows that second itinerary,
since it agrees with 189 modulo \(2^{68}\). The exact lifted endpoints
of the two itineraries are
\[
 F^{111}(h)=1+27\cdot3^{38}z,\qquad
 F^{106}(7h)=1+28\cdot3^{38}z.
\]
Their excesses over 1 are in the ratio **27:28** when \(z>0\).
The two return readings themselves are coprime: if \(U=3^{38}z\),
then \(28(1+27U)-27(1+28U)=1\). Here the 27 is \(3^{41-38}\); the 28 is \(7\cdot2^{70-68}\).
Unlike the small base orbits, the lifted trajectories do not meet at
the aligned phases when \(z>0\). So the parity word also exposes
exactly which data a finite Collatz merge forgets.

## A different occurrence: almost every start has a factor of 27

Eight generators (both alternatives of types 4, 9, 10, 12)
have \(3\mid D,Q\), while the other 20 have \(D,Q\) units mod 3.
Every generator has \(3\mid R\). In a long word, therefore,
its bad factors contribute at least their total multiplicity to
\(v_3(h)\), since \(h=11D+42Q+20R\). In particular a prime start
\(h>3\) can use only the 20 good alternatives. Its candidate count
drops from dimension 27 to 19, even before primality testing.

More precisely put \(c_{20}=\log2/(19!\prod_{\text{good }j}\log R_j)\).
The sharp dyadic counts for \(v_3(h)=0,1,2\) are respectively
\[
 (1+o(1)),\quad(4+o(1)),\quad(13+o(1))
 \qquad\text{times }c_{20}(\log X)^{19}.
\]
For sufficiently large \(X\) every word has \(27\mid R\), so only
\(11D+42Q\) matters here. The four bad types have valuation pairs
\((2,1),(2,3),(1,1),(1,1)\). With no bad factors \(h\) is a unit.
With one bad type 10 or 12, \(v_3(h)=1\) (four choices);
one type 9 gives valuation 2 (two choices). One type 4 gives
valuation 2 exactly when the number of good factors is even:
\(h/9\equiv2(D_{\rm good}+Q_{\rm good})\bmod3\), and each good
factor has \(Q_j\equiv-D_j\bmod3\). It contributes one effective
choice (two alternatives times a half). Two bad factors give
valuation 2 exactly when both are among types 10 and 12; there are
\(\binom{5}{2}=10\) multisets of four alternatives. All other cases
vanish modulo 27. Fixed bad multiplicities merely shift the log-height
origin; parity of the sum of good multiplicities splits the same
thin-strip asymptotic in half, by taking exponent cosets modulo 2.

Thus the **nonmultiples of 27** number
\((18+o(1))c_{20}(\log X)^{19}\). A fraction
\(1-O((\log X)^{-8})\) of the original dimension-27 bank starts
is divisible by the literal integer 27. Yet restricting to
the 20 good alternatives still gives the same dyadic uniformity
proof: the three mod-8 generators and both lifts survive. The
dyadic law is also independent of each fixed valuation \(v_3=0,1,2\).
Indeed all good factors satisfy \(D_jR_j\equiv3\bmod8\), so fixing
good-length parity fixes the class of \(DR\bmod8\). A frozen bad
multiset only translates that class. With \(D\bmod8\) determined by
\(h\), each such class allows exactly one odd \(R\bmod8\), giving
equal fibers as before. In particular the bank can exhibit the
entire 27 itinerary without divisibility by 3. The binary itinerary and ternary obstruction
are separate controls, not two readings of the same congruence.

## Two useful analogies, not identifications

* **Ternary cube.** A transitive three-vertex filler has third reading
  \(3^3=27\): each vertex chooses one of three ordered slots and each
  chosen slot has one Hamilton path. On the directed triangle the very
  same 27 assignments survive, but three monochromatic assignments
  now have weight 3 rather than 1, giving \(33\). This is a small exact
  example of what changes when slot labels are kept but the internal
  path counts are forgotten.
* **28 bitangents, 27 lines.** Blow up a general point off the lines
  on a smooth cubic surface. Its 27 lines and the new exceptional
  curve give the 28 bitangents of the resulting degree-two model's
  branch quartic. In reverse, distinguish one lift of one bitangent;
  exactly one lift of each of the other 27 is disjoint from that
  distinguished curve and descends to a line after contraction.
  Indeed each pair of lifts sums to the anticanonical class, of
  intersection 1 with the distinguished curve. See
  [Dolgachev, Classical Algebraic Geometry, §9.1.7](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/idolga/wp-content/uploads/sites/1334/2024/08/CAG.21.pdf).
  Both this and the bank use a single constraint on 28 inputs.
  In the surface it is a geometric incidence with a marked curve;
  in the bank the removed degree of freedom is additive log-height.
  There is no asserted incidence-preserving map or shared cause.
