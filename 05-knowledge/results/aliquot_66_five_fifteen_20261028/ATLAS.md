This is a research map, not a claim that all occurrences of a numeral have
one cause. The criterion for a usable bridge is: **source, target, map,

The source history supplies candidates; current corrections and exact
formulas decide which candidates survive.

## 3. The history of five, including corrected March and June readings

The March [Five as the Bridge Between Worlds](../../../07-reflections/five-as-bridge.md)
and [Five Platonic Tournaments](../../../07-reflections/the-five-platonic-tournaments.md)
proposed a broad sphere/plane/tournament identification. The latter's
current warning and the June [angle-defect reflection's repair](../../../07-reflections/the-angle-defect-spine-five-solids-five-lattices-and-the-seventh-prime.md)
must override their historical bodies. The valid shared formula is the
curvature test `1/2+1/3+1/m−1`, positive at m5, zero at6, negative at7.
It does not identify tournament moduli with a hyperbolic surface.

The five Platonic solids have Schläfli types
`{3,3},{3,4},{4,3},{3,5},{5,3}`. The five follows from p,q>=3 and
`1/p+1/q>1/2`, with existence supplied by the familiar solids. The
reflection groups have three types A3,B3,H3; dual solids share a symmetry
group. The golden ratio enters H3 coordinates and the pentagon through
quadratic field Q(sqrt5). It is not an isomorphism between that field and
the imaginary order of discriminant−20 used by the idoneal construction.

Three exact repairs connect this old material to present work:

1. **Five orbits, fifteen labelled objects.** Set partitions of four
   labelled elements have block-shape counts1,4,3,6,1, totaling15. Their
   S4-orbit set has five elements, the five integer partitions of4. Here
   the5/15 relation is an actual quotient, and the fibre sizes are
   explicitly nonuniform.
2. **Five-point involutions.** A5 has15 double transpositions: choose its
   fixed point in5 ways and pair the other four points in3 ways. Their
   icosahedral meaning and the marked-sixth-point map are detailed below.
3. **Fermat rigidity.** For odd prime p, every circulant tournament on p
   vertices has only translation automorphisms iff p−1 is a power of2.
   Use **THM-4644 / [Fermat-prime rigidity](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4644-fermat-primes-rigid-circulant-tournaments-wieferich-orbit-explosion.md)**,
   with its Akin/Alspach/Burnside attribution and MISTAKE-608 correction.
   The five *known* Fermat primes are3,5,17,257,65537. Their count is not a
   proof there are exactly five. Intersecting the fixed Ogg list with all
   Fermat primes gives exactly3,5,17 by inspection; hypothetical later
   Fermat primes cannot lie in the finite Ogg list.

The least-used prior sidecars were the marked point and the action kernel.
For example S5 and the full icosahedral symmetry group A5×C2 both have
order120, but their centres differ. L(K4) is an octahedral graph; the polar
of the six-dimensional tournament cube has12 vertices, not6. These cheap
hostiles explain why the stronger geometric identification failed.

The March rapidity claim also needs its quantifier repaired. The numbers
logφ and logs of distinct rational primes are linearly independent over Q:
clear rational denominators, conjugate φ, and use its norm−1 to force the
rational product and the exponent ofφ to be trivial. This proves rational
linear independence, not algebraic independence of the logarithms.

## 4. Fifteen is several different structures, with some exact bridges

The [October18 cardinality atlas](../cardinality_bridges_20261018m.md)
already made the user's cross-field technique precise. It recovers the
June [icosahedral fifteen](../../../07-reflections/the-icosahedral-fifteen-s6.md)
while repairing its nonuniform commutator fibres. Its key maps should be
reused, not rediscovered as a new numerology:

    K6 perfect matchings
       ↔ Lagrangian planes in F2^4
       ↔ geometric genus-two Richelot kernels.

The matching-to-Lagrangian map uses even subsets modulo complement and
the intersection-parity pairing. Mark one of the six labels. Pairing it
with v and transposing the remaining two pairs gives an involution of A5
fixing v. This is an invertible S5-equivariant map to the fifteen
icosahedral half-turns, after choosing the usual rotation-group model.
It does not identify the unmarked S6 actions: a transposition fixes seven
duads but only three matchings. Fixed points of group elements already
reject that proposed action-preserving identification.

At eight labels there are105 matchings but135 Lagrangians; the missing30
come from Hamming-code structures. The existing ten-label continuation
keeps those missing primitives. This extension is much stronger evidence
for a mechanism than another unrelated list with fifteen entries.

The same atlas carefully distinguishes:

- fifteen *known* sporadic Apéry-like sequences in specified recurrence
  catalogues, with actual operator transformations needed for a bridge;
- fifteen convex pentagonal tile **families**, with moduli and intersections;
- fifteen rational elliptic torsion group types in Mazur's classification;
- fifteen nonidentity two-qubit Pauli classes, whose commutation pairing
  is symplectic but whose scalar product phases were quotiented out;
- fifteen Ogg primes, an equality of actual prime sets with Monster
  divisors, not just an equality of counts.

No new common bijection for all these lists is supplied. The present
[P5](PROOFS.md) adds a deliberately sharper comparison: index-p³ lattices
and symplectic four-space Lagrangians have the same polynomial count for
every prime p. The integral shear invisible modulo p proves that the
obvious action-compatible identification still fails. The missing
coordinate is congruence depth, not the cardinality.

The Session65 [fifteens report](../session65_cryptid_tournament_interfaces_20261026/session65-fifteens/REPORT.md)
also separates kernel counts from isomorphic codomains, compares Frobenius
orientation defects, and keeps the finite-sieve/integer-witness boundary.
Its historical/pending status is not upgraded by this atlas. The finite

## 5. Supersingularity, levels, Monster/Baby, and the repaired cycle

The prime predicate is that **all geometric supersingular j-values in
characteristic p are defined over F_p**. This is not the set of
supersingular primes of one fixed elliptic curve, and it does not kill
prime-to-p torsion. The old [supersingularity reflection](../../../07-reflections/supersingularity-triples.md)
is read through the corrected [divisor/level synthesis](../supersingular_divisor_shapes_qseries_20261022.md),
including MISTAKE-666.

[Aricheta's higher-level theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.07421)
relates nonrational supersingular pairs to the genus change on a specified
Atkin–Lehner quotient. Its level2 list gives the odd Baby Monster primes.
The repo's [level1|2|6 audit](../monster_levels_square_sum_7_21_20261022.md)
then organizes the requested sets as

    M minus B:       29,41,59,71;
    B minus Fi22:    17,19,23,31,47;
    Fi22 support:    2,3,5,7,11,13.

This is a level-sensitive geometric relationship; no genus computation
at X₀(N) can silently replace the quotient in that theorem.

The order ratio's small-prime part is

    R=2^5 3^7 5^3 7^4 11 13².

Define h_R(b)=max{k:b^k divides R} for b>=2, not just prime b. Then

    4→2→5→3→7→4,       13→2,       11→1.

This is the exact current interpretation in
[Session43's cycle proof](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/cycle-geometry/PROOF.md).
Its feasibility criterion for simultaneously prescribed composite-base
heights is stronger than a picture of the cycle. The identification13=4
does not descend modulo9:11 and2 have equal input residue but different
output residues. Also h_R(1) is not a finite height;1 is an explicitly
adjoined terminal convention. The actual involution centralizer is2.B,
so using its index divides R by2 and gives2↔4. Thus the normalization and
operation are indispensable parts of the phenomenon.

For{7,21}, there is a genuine group-action bridge in
[the Paley–Monster report](../session32_square_monster_20261023/paley/PALEY_MONSTER_BRIDGE.md):
`N_M(<7A>)/He ≅ AGL(1,7)`, whose square-slope subgroup is Aut(Paley7)
of order21. This quotient retains the signed seven-point action and
discards the Held kernel. Conjugation on C7 alone does not supply its
translation action. The actual Paley Hamilton count is189, not21.
The separately proved forbidden Hamilton counts7,21 in
**THM-1370 / [H-spectrum omission](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-1370-h-spectrum-omits-7-21-all-n.md)**
therefore remain a different statement.

## 7. The organizing theory that survives the comparison

The incoming guarded-context theorem is already the strongest general
quotient statement here: retain precisely the output and definedness of
every future operation. Its rank-two formula for coprime aliquot products
does **not** imply a two-state exact observer; the full source can be
reconstructed by a fresh-prime continuation.

The additional lesson of this session is to retain **weighted incidence
and integral depth before quotienting**. Examples now have different
mechanisms, not just repeated language:

| Exact source | Quotient | Information that must remain for the next task |
|---|---|---|
|Lattice chains|Endpoint lattice|Nonuniform intermediate-path multiplicities|
|Embedded lattice|Cyclic projective-line layer|Content and the omitted noncyclic layer|
|Quadratic ideal class|Genus characters|The square-subgroup kernel; uniformly nontrivial on the new ray|
|K6 matching|A5 involution|Chosen sixth label and induced S5 action|
|Integral congruence action|Reduction modulo p|Kernel action at deeper p-adic level|
|Squarefree prime support|Zero total signature|Its internal circuit/loop decomposition|
|Tournament or unit graph|Minor or unmarked chart|Actual boundary extension relation and shared ports|

This suggests a research strategy rather than an unproved universal
theorem: locate the first nontrivial kernel, construct a source-specific
witness there, and only then ask whether it is killed, paid for, or
retained under the next operation. The new quadratic-form witness does
that uniformly on one arithmetic ray. It does not yet produce the
weighted cancellation bound required by Collatz or a six-colour forcing
gadget. Those transfers have named missing maps in P4–P6, rather than an
asserted identification by matching numbers.
