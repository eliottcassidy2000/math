# The 7–8–9 corner: three equalities and the sign or carry they do not see

This packet separates rigid arithmetic matches from possible proof
transfers. Collatz and Chan–Pak remain open.

## 1. Three identities really single out 7, 8 and 9

For the principal modular curves X(N) take G_N=SL₂(Z/N)/{±I},
with **one global sign**. The [genus and cusp formula](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~arapura/preprints/shimura.pdf)
for N≥3 is μ=N³/2·∏_(p|N)(1−p^−2), with
(V,E,F)=(μ/N,μ/2,μ/3) and g=1+μ(N−6)/(12N).
This is the same curvature transition as in the prior
[5–6–7 flag and square-sum work](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/6ea3b2e587e0882cce1b2c63b5df822d02c55f1e/05-knowledge/results/fresh_unit_prime_gluing_20261116/geometry_phase_triple/README.md),
not the same graph.

| N | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| vertices | 12 | 12 | 24 | 24 | 36 |
| genus | 0 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 10 |
| 2g+1 | 1 | 3 | 7 | 11 | 21 |

[MODULAR.md](MODULAR.md) proves three uniqueness statements in the stated
families. A prime level and a power-of-two level ≥4 tie in cusp count
only at (7,8): the coprime consecutive factors (p−1)/2 and (p+1)/2 have
product 3 times a power of four, so their odd factor is 1 or 3.

A power-of-three hyperbolic level has twice the genus of a
power-of-two hyperbolic level only at (9,8). Put x=3^(a−1), y=2^(b−1),
z=y−1. The equation is z(z²−3)=2x²(x−2). It forces b odd and
v₃(b−1)=2a−4 by LTE. The absolute size inequality y<2x instead
gives b−1≤2a−2, excluding a≥3. This is a useful example of an
exact local-and-absolute proof, rather than a density argument.

Among the odd prime levels p≥7 and power-of-three levels ≥9,
g(3^a)=3g(p)+1 holds only at (9,7): the equivalent product
(p−3)(p−5)(p+2)=8x²(x−2) puts all the 3-part into one factor.
It must be at least x²; for x≥9 this forces all three factors to
exceed 2x, a contradiction. Neither identity is an unramified covering
relation: such a covering multiplies g−1, not g. In fact [there is no nonconstant holomorphic map X(9)→X(8)](WHEEL_AND_COVERS.md), even allowing branch points. The fixed-base
Catalan identity 9−8=1 is separate and elementary: if 3^a−2^b=1
with a≥2,b≥3, mod8 makes a even and the powers of two
3^(a/2)±1 differ by 2, hence are 2 and 4. The reverse sign is
impossible mod8.

### An exact 7-adic ladder, and a hostile value 63

Write C(N)=2g(X(N))+1, to avoid identifying it with tournament H.
For a≥2,
```
C(3^a) = f(x) = 2x²(x−2)+3,  x=3^(a−1).
```
Then C≡3 mod9 and C≡2x−1 mod8, so C≡21 mod72 for even a and
≡57 for odd a. In particular v₃(C)=1. The only root of f modulo 7
is x=3, and f'(3)=30 is nonzero mod7. There is therefore one
Hensel root mod7^k. Since 3 has order 6 mod7 and
v₇(3⁶−1)=1, it generates the units mod7^k. Consequently
```
7^k | C(3^a)  iff  a ≡ a_k (mod 6·7^(k−1))
a_1=2, a_2=26, a_3=236, a_4=236.
```
Exact v₇=k has density 7^(−k) among exponents for k≥1. All those
7-divisible values sit in the 21 class mod72. For prime levels
p≥5, the numerator 12 C(p)=(p²−1)(p−6)+36 has only the
root p=0 mod7, hence prime level 7 is the only prime level with
a 7-divisible readout. These are structural numerical matches,
not tournament exclusions.

**63 breaks any identification of the spectra:** an 8-vertex
transitive tournament with (0,3),(0,4),(0,7) reversed has H=63,
but no principal X(N) has C(N)=63. The product over primes is
≥∏_(m≥2)(1−m^−2)=1/2, so g≥1+N²(N−6)/48>31 for N≥14.
Levels 3..13 never give genus 31 by the explicit formula, and
levels 1 and 2 have genus zero.

## 2. Flat order 72, actual level 72, and arithmetic mod72

**Flat symmetry:** G₆≅S₃×A₄ has order 72, with 12 translations
and a rotation quotient of order 6. The flat triangular map is
Eisenstein modulo (2(1−ω)); see [MODULAR.md](MODULAR.md).
Sylow factors C₂³ and C₃² do give a set factorization of sizes 8
and 9, since they intersect trivially. They do not give cyclic
8- and 9-clocks: the group exponent is 6. This agrees numerically
with λ(72)=lcm(2,6)=6, but U(72) has only 24 elements and is Abelian.

**Relative sign at level 72:** G_72→G_8×G_9 has a central C₂ kernel
generated by 17I (positive mod8, negative mod9). This extension is
nonsplit, because an order-two element represented by S on the
G_8 factor has only order-four lifts. The sign acts freely on X(72),
being neither an elliptic nor a cusp stabilizer. Thus X(72) is
an unramified double cover of the normalized fiber product over X(1).
The genera are 9505 and 4753, and cusp counts 1728 and 864.
This is an actual missing sign, not a group of order 72.

**Arithmetic mod72:** alternating raw letters ax−1 then ax+1 gives
Sₐ(x)=a²x−(a−1). The marked coordinate z=(a+1)x−1 makes this
multiplication by a² at the enlarged conductor (a+1)M.
For M=72, a=3 resets mod9 and makes two 4-cycles; a=7 gives six
4-cycles and four 12-cycles; a=9 gives eight fixed points after
one step. In particular 49 has order 3 mod72 but S₇³ translates
by −18, of order four. The conductor 576 sees the missing factor.
The arithmetic proof and signed control are in [STITCHED.md](STITCHED.md). In
the older 63=7·9 wheel, three doublings multiply by 8, which is
(+1,−1) under (7,9) CRT: the Fano phase returns and the mod-9 phase
takes a half-turn. In the 72 wheel those same doublings erase the
mod-8 coordinate instead. The two wheels have the same unit exponent
six, while the multiplier 2 has very different dynamics.

### The order-six tournament hub is not the flat group

The earlier
[metagraph star](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/e76f1cda81c919ee099194d8ec37ce1ece075be4/05-knowledge/results/perpendicular_refinement_20261116/README.md)
has hub C₃[TT₂,TT₂,TT₂]. Its three twin pairs are intrinsic, and
every automorphism or converse map permutes them. Any cyclic pair
permutation preserves the arcs; reversing the cycle and all three
internal orders gives converse. No residual internal freedom remains:
its full automorphism-plus-converse group is S₃. Every onto map
G₆→this hub group factors through the S₃ coordinate: A₄ can
contribute only an Abelian image commuting with the S₃ image,
and every proper quotient of S₃ is Abelian.

Indeed the minimum faithful permutation degree of G₆ is **seven**.
The disjoint actions S₃ on three and A₄ on four give the upper bound.
In degree ≤6 the A₄ restriction needs a 4- or 6-orbit: orbits of size
one or three kill V₄, and there is no index-two subgroup. For a
4-orbit the order-three stabilizer is self-normalizing, so the
centralizer on it is trivial and the remaining ≤2 points add at
most C₂. For a 6-orbit the order-two stabilizer has normalizer V₄,
so the centralizer is C₂. Neither can accommodate a commuting
faithful S₃. Also no full G₆ acts via automorphisms and converse
on any tournament: automorphisms have odd order (an involution
reverses a transposed arc), and allowing converse adds at most one
factor two, while |G₆| contains 2³.

## 3. Even multiplication, negative Collatz, and the Paley sign

Modulo odd m the sign fold has only zero fixed and the central table
values are ±4^−1. Modulo 2h there is a second sign-fixed point h.
The extra sign-and-swap boundary has entries ih=0 for even i and h
for odd i: a literal parity channel (CRT idempotent for h odd,
nilpotent for h even). At odd h the signed raw letters can close
with partners x,h−x, not x,−x. No such two-step fixed point exists
when 4 divides the modulus. [STITCHED.md](STITCHED.md) spells out
which branch of this claim belongs to the raw letters.

For actual shortcut dynamics F₊(−x)=−F₋(x), so negative 3x+1
is positive 3x−1 reflected. The true shortcut never descends
modulo M>1. To read k steps mod M, use the lift mod 2^k M.
Indeed x and x+2^k have the same first k parity bits and their
k-th iterates differ by the odd integer 3^h, where h is the
number of odd steps. This inductively proves that every length-k
parity word occurs once among residues mod 2^k. CRT then realizes
every word on a unique lift of every given residue mod any odd q.
Arithmetic phase and parity legality coexist but do not give
absolute landing. The [finite-wheel construction](WHEEL_AND_COVERS.md)
makes this obstruction specific: any fixed modulus has arbitrarily
long primitive positive rational cycles above any prescribed height,
passing the entire wheel with no integer vertex.

[PALEY_COLLATZ.md](PALEY_COLLATZ.md) gives a different genuine
dynamical carrier. An odd valuation-marked branch
x↦2^(−k)(3x±1) reverses Paley arrows modulo seven: 2 is a
square and 3 is not. A word preserves the arrows exactly when
its odd-letter count L is even, and for even L its linear part
is one exactly when K≡L/2 mod3 (3²≡2), where K is total division
exponent. The native single-letter maps generate the entire
42-element affine group, and each prescribed finite valuation
word has sources in every mod7 class by CRT with its native
dyadic guard. The residue fixed-point sieve is exact, but
does not remove the integer carry. Example: (1,3),(2,2),(3,1)
all have (K,L)=(4,2) and linear part 1 mod7, while their
ordered carries are 5,7,11; only (2,2) returns mod7.

The [archived Catalan/cycle synthesis](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/05-knowledge/results/catalan_elliptic_20260921_catalan.md)
already emphasizes the exact cycle equation (2^K−3^L)x=±B
and the order-dependent carry B. [SIGNS.md](SIGNS.md) adds an
elementary restriction: the only absolute raw prime gap
≡7 mod24 is 7 at (K,L)=(4,2) (parity forces both exponents
even, then factor the difference of two squares). The one
compatible carry at ±1 repeats a fixed point, not a primitive
length-two cycle. Raw gaps 9 and 21 are impossible mod3.
This does not exclude other gaps or unbounded trajectories.

## 4. Octonions, Bott, and Carmichael

The earlier
[Paley–Fano–octonion archive](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/05-knowledge/results/collatz_mod6_20260922_paley_fano_octonion_design.md)
provides the actual signed observer. [SIGNS.md](SIGNS.md) verifies
nine normalized Hamilton-path orbits of Paley(7). Left-nested
octonion products give eight positive orbits and one negative
orbit (the arithmetic progression), each with 21 paths.
So 189=168+21 and their signed sum is 147=7·21. The 168 positive
paths do **not** admit an order-168 action induced by permuting the seven vertices:
their first arcs already exhaust the Paley arcs and any such
permutation must belong to the order-21 Paley group.

The Fano group of order 168 has eight signed monomial lifts
per element. This extension is nonsplit, with a short proof
on a Klein four-group of transvections: each of its three
nontrivial involutive lifts must negate one specific fixed
axis, but the product of two would fix that axis. Compare the
level-72 missing sign. Both extensions forbid a coherent choice
of lifts, but one kernel is eight sign characters, the other
a central C₂; they are not the same extension.

[Bott's period eight](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/bott4.pdf)
and Clifford stabilization are not arithmetic return periods.
The prior [marked Bott audit](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/05-knowledge/results/collatz_bott_marked_periodicity_20261007d.md)
keeps a marked tensor corner precisely so that the original
carry can be read after stabilization. Losing that corner can
identify a branch that grows and one that falls.

The earlier [Carmichael-number carry work](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/05-knowledge/results/carmichael_collatz_carry_20261019/INHERITANCE_AND_BRIDGES.md)
concerns marked Korselt conditions, not Carmichael's conjecture
on singleton totient fibers. A literal 7/9 connection to that conjecture
is φ(7m)=φ(9m)=6φ(m) whenever gcd(m,21)=1, including even m. The
coincidence is unique between positive powers of 7 and 3, since
6·7^(a−1)=2·3^(b−1) forces a=1,b=2. For possible singleton fibers there is an exact
local exchange: if p−1 divides the reservoir
R=∏_(q|n, q≠p) q^(v_q(n)−1), a singleton totient preimage n must
have p²|n. If p is absent replace n by np/(p−1); if it occurs
once replace n by n(p−1)/p. The other prime exponents stay positive
and the totient stays equal. Starting at p=2 forces squares of
2,3,7,43. The squarefree subset-product rule on those primes then
stalls: only 3,7,43 are prime among 1+ the nonempty subset products;
the full product plus one is 1807=13·139. The reservoir condition
is stronger than mere divisibility by φ(n); [Pomerance's theorem](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/PDF/carmichaelconjecture.pdf)
uses the bigger predicate in a sufficient singleton criterion.
The elementary chain is far weaker than the established lower bounds,
but it is a useful contrast between a real exchange and a residue.

## 5. The simultaneous constraints for Chan–Pak

The [strong-floor argument](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/01-canon/theorems/THM-1370-h-spectrum-omits-7-21-all-n.md)
excludes the actual holes by strong-order lower bounds and component
multiplication, not genus. [MODULAR.md](MODULAR.md) proves a new
hostile test for residual strategies: **every odd residue modulo
every 2^a3^b is a real tournament count modulo that modulus**, using
literal counts 3,5,11,13 and ordinal products. In particular
H=1375 looks like 7 mod72 and H=165 looks like 21 mod72. Even
arbitrary joint 2/3-adic precision on H alone cannot detect the
absolute holes. This does not exclude joint conditions using
endpoint, size, or deletion data.

The same example gives a quantitative veto on one naive absolute
selector. The grammar generated by 3,5,11,13 has at most
(1+log(X)/log(3))^4 distinct values up to X. For every sufficiently
large even modulus M=2^a3^b, all M/2 odd classes occur, but at least
one has no witness from this grammar below 2M. In that class the
representative N in [0,M) cannot be selected by a rule requiring
|H−N|<M. More generally the maximum, over odd classes, of the
least grammar witness is at least 3^((M/2)^(1/4)−1). Thus a family
can be complete at arbitrarily high joint residue precision and still
miss the absolute error bound by a large margin. A successful
reader needs new arithmetic freedom, not just a larger modulus.

For construction, ordinal products reduce the remaining arithmetic
to odd primes other than 7, 7p for odd primes p other than 3 and 7,
and the three values 49,63,343. Use powers of 49 and one factor 343
for valuations of seven ≥2, a 7p factor if available when v₇=1,
and 63 if the lone seven is accompanied only by at least two threes.
The weighted-tree proof could round an exact target because it
contained a uniform error bound narrower than one integer step.
None of the modular surjectivity, genus, or symmetry arguments yet
supplies that bound and a physical tournament reader. This makes
an absolute carry or an exact target-decreasing recursion the
objective, rather than another residue census.
