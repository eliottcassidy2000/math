# A cohesive mechanism: operation-closed observables and the cost of completion

Session35 working synthesis. This is a structural framework, not a claim that all open problems reduce to one theorem. The elementary observation below is standard contextual equivalence; applications here have separate proofs and audits.

## 1. The exact question behind a proposed unification

Given a state space X, allowed operations generating a monoid M, and a target observable f:X→Y, define

    x ~ y iff f(w·x)=f(w·y) for every w in M.

This is an equivalence relation stable under every allowed operation: appending v tests the words wv already present in M. It is the largest operation-stable equivalence on which f descends. Any proposed quotient that identifies a non-equivalent pair necessarily loses a future observable. Conversely this quotient is a sufficient deterministic state description for all those future observations.

Thus a structural analogy earns mathematical force only after specifying X, M and f. The same scalar invariant can be complete for one observable and insufficient for another.

## 2. A complete finite abelian case: Walsh support

Let X=F2^r with translations as operations, and f:X→C. Write

    f(x)=sum_(a in S) fhat(a)(−1)^(a·x),

where S is the nonzero-coefficient support. Then

    x~y iff a·(x−y)=0 for every a in S.

Indeed f(x+u)=f(y+u) for all u iff their Fourier coefficients agree, so every surviving character has equal phase. The number of deterministic contextual states is exactly2^rank(span S). The dimension of the complex linear span of translates of f is |S|: Fourier characters are eigenvectors and each supported character is recovered by averaging translates against that character.

These are different invariants. A small linear realization can require many deterministic states, and a scalar match need not preserve either.

Projective-line application: for odd q, v=q²+q+1 lines, every pair character e_L+e_M occurs in the directed-triangle-count observable. Those vectors span the even-weight subspace of dimension v−1. Hence its translation quotient has2^(v−1) states, while the linear span has dimension1+binomial(v,2). At q7 these are2^56 and1597. The zero character contributes to the span but not its rank. This does not make a statement about Hamiltonian counts, which are a different f.

Fano comparison: the separately inherited Hamiltonian observable in its special regular line-flip family has a much smaller supported-character space. The fact that the incidence pictures resemble each other does not preserve their observable Fourier support. The odd/even q parity gate is the decisive lost information.

## 3. The recursion carrier illustrates target dependence

For tournaments under source–sink extension E and skew doubling D, the pair(p,F) is closed under both operations and is equivalent to adjacency characteristic data. It therefore suffices for every future spectral observation generated by E,D.

It does not suffice for Hamiltonian counting. The audited six-vertex pair has equal(p,F), equal triangle and five-cycle counts, but H29 versus25 because one admits a pair of disjoint triangles and the other does not. Every E,D word preserves the spectral collision; E-only descendants preserve the Hamiltonian discrepancy. A stronger spectral algebra cannot repair this particular lost incidence unless it adds the missing observable.

The vertex-labelled square-zero port algebra adds exactly the local exclusion data needed to count simple paths. This repair has exponential label state. It is a correct completion, not a promise of faster general enumeration.

## 4. Completion is not free, and there are several different kinds

- **Combinatorial completion:** restore vertex labels and repeated-vertex exclusions. Cost: square-zero occupation state2^n.
- **Analytic completion:** replace a finite spectrum by all lattice vectors and its Fourier-dual form. Reward: theta inversion and Epstein continuation. Cost: a new infinite object, lost orientation, and a dimension-dependent reflection center. This does not prove RH.
- **Metric Lie completion:** retain E7's action and positive Heisenberg bracket, add opposite grades and an active grading direction. Under the stated homogeneous-metric hypotheses the minimum is248 and equality is simple E8. This is not a4-dimensional Yang–Mills construction.
- **Positive tensor realization:** retain all permutation-trace Gram constraints, not just fourth order. A formal noninteger t can pass the first floor(t)+1 orders and fail at the next. Interpolation exists without a positive realization.

They share a discipline: preserve the requested structure, identify what was discarded, and prove that the added data actually repairs that loss. They are not the same functor or the same theorem.

## 5. The arithmetic bridges survive only with their sidecars

The number57 by itself determines none of a projective plane, a symplectic representation, a Lie bracket or a rational VOA. With the cyclic action, a genuine57-point-to-Heisenberg construction exists; retaining that action exposes an E7 character obstruction. With CRT factors and a chosen square target, the4/2/1 root fibres become genuine sign-flip torsors. With a Virasoro parameter and its reciprocal involution, a rational discriminant controls the arithmetic type of associated mirror bases. Removing those sidecars leaves only numerical coincidences.

A useful next theorem should therefore say not merely 'these dimensions agree', but 'this map intertwines these operations and preserves this particular predicate'. The accumulated hostile examples explain why counts, dimensions and closed amplitudes alone repeatedly fail to reconstruct the full mathematical object.

## 6. Fixed-path metagraph boundary

The projective whole-line flips act on labelled tournaments and may reverse edges of a previously fixed Hamiltonian path. They are therefore not automatically edges of the original fixed-Hamiltonian-path tiling metagraph. For flips that reverse path edges, returning to that metagraph needs a new Hamiltonian-path choice/relabeling and its compatibility data. Restricting to unused lines preserves the path but defines an auxiliary move relation, not automatically the original metagraph adjacency. Likewise the Heisenberg lift forgets path labels. These constructions supply testable auxiliary lenses and new possible relations, not a complete realizability theorem for the original blue/black wiring.
