## Primary structural source pin

In §10, Theorem42 (printed pp19–20) identifies the full GL56 stabilizer of the standard quartic as the group generated by E7 and scalar fourth roots of unity. Corollary43 (printed p20) identifies the simultaneous stabilizer of the quartic and alternating form as exactly E7. Its proof explicitly explains that ±I already belong to E7, while the other component iE7 reverses the alternating form. Thus intersecting with Sp56 removes that extra component; this is a full stabilizer statement, not only an identity-component assertion. Here E7 is its simply connected minuscule56 realization, not its adjoint quotient. The paper's general hypotheses in §2 are characteristic different from2 and3 and a split group; specialization to C satisfies them. For a rational or real quartic, scalar extension to C suffices for the obstruction.

## Corollary audit

Let α:C57→Sp(W,Ω) denote the specified action, and let f:W→V be a symplectic isomorphism with quartic pullback F_W=f*F. Invariance of F_W is exactly the assertion fα(g)f⁻¹∈Stab_Sp(V)(F) for every g. The verified stabilizer theorem makes this a homomorphism into E7 whose minuscule restriction is W. The established order3 character obstruction forbids it. Consequently the proposed quartic cannot be invariant; invariance under the larger semilinear group also fails by restriction to C57.

As an independent arithmetic check on that dependency, enumeration of SL8 multiplicities a+b+c=8 and b+2c=0 mod3 gives fixed dimensions2,14,20,32,56 and traces−25,−7,2,20,56. The augmentation order3 element has19 cycles on57 points, hence fixed dimension18 after removing constants, and trace−1. Neither occurs in the allowed list.

The source can therefore replace the note's provisional stabilizer hypothesis with a cited standard theorem. No new classification is being proved. The symmetry obstruction is genuinely invisible to the alternating form: a C57-invariant nondegenerate alternating form exists, and produces the Heisenberg bracket, but the exceptional quartic structure cannot retain the action.

## Hostiles and optional strengthening

There are ordinary invariant quartics. An explicit example is Q(w)=(sum_i w_i²)² restricted to W. It is invariant under all coordinate permutations and nonzero (evaluate on e0−e1). Thus absence of all invariant quartics would be false. The correct exclusion is the GL orbit, or more narrowly the compatible symplectic orbit, of the standard exceptional quartic.

Indeed the same primary theorem supports a slightly stronger conclusion: no GL-equivalent standard Freudenthal quartic is C57-invariant, even without preselecting Ω. The full quartic stabilizer has quotient C2 by E7. Any homomorphism from odd-order C57 to this quotient is trivial, so the entire cyclic image would still lie in E7 and violate the character obstruction. This strengthening is optional; the original compatible-quartic claim needs only Corollary43.

No restriction on abstract E7 or E8 constructions after forgetting the cyclic action is inferred. Fourth order here means quartic polynomial degree, not physical dimension or differential order.
