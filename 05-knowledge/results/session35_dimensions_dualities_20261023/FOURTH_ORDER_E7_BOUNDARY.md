# The fourth-order boundary of the Singer57 Heisenberg bridge

Session35. **PROVED corollary of the audited representation obstruction**, with an explicit structural input. This is about an invariant quartic tensor, not spacetime dimension or differential order.

Let W be the56-dimensional characteristic-zero augmentation representation of C57, with any of the invariant symplectic forms constructed in this session. The order3 subgroup has trace−1. The audited character argument proves that this representation cannot be obtained by restriction of the minuscule E7 representation.

Let V be the standard symplectic56-dimensional E7 module and F its standard Freudenthal quartic, whose stabilizer inside Sp(V) is E7. This stabilizer description is the structural input; equivalently the following result applies to any quartic with precisely that specified stabilizer. Define an E7-type compatible quartic on W to mean a pullback of F by a symplectic isomorphism W→V.

**Corollary. No E7-type compatible quartic on W is invariant under the distinguished C57 action.**

If it were invariant, conjugating by the symplectic isomorphism would put C57 inside Stab_Sp(V)(F)=E7, with its minuscule restriction equivalent to W. That contradicts the trace−1 obstruction. The larger Singer semilinear action C57⋊C3 therefore cannot preserve such a quartic either.

The conclusion is not that W has no invariant quartic at all. Finite-group averaging produces many invariant polynomial tensors, and products of lower-degree invariants may exist. What fails is the specific E7-type nondegenerate exceptional quartic orbit, while retaining the prescribed cyclic action and compatible symplectic structure.

This supplies a precise hierarchy:

- Matching dimension56 is possible.
- A nondegenerate invariant alternating bilinear form is possible.
- The Heisenberg57 bracket is therefore possible.
- An invariant exceptional quartic compatible with the E7 minuscule structure is impossible for this action.

Thus “think fourth order” reveals a genuine obstruction invisible to the second-order symplectic structure. It does not assert a general obstruction to all E7 constructions, or to forgetting/replacing the finite action. The intermediate Lie algebra and its minimal E8 metric completion remain valid as abstract Lie-theoretic objects.

Source for the standard symplectic/quartic characterization should be read with the precise stabilizer group and characteristic-zero convention. The corollary itself is a one-line subgroup argument and remains valid under its explicitly stated stabilizer hypothesis without relying on a numerical coincidence.

## Verified source and stronger form

Fred W. Helenius, *Freudenthal triple systems by root system methods*, §10, Theorem42 and Corollary43, pp19–20:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1005.1275

Over C, Theorem42 identifies the GL56 stabilizer of the quartic with the group generated by E7_sc and scalar fourth roots of unity. Its identity component is E7_sc and component quotient is C2, since the scalar±1 already lie in E7_sc. Corollary43 identifies the simultaneous alternating/quartic stabilizer exactly with E7_sc; the extra scalar i negates the alternating form.

Therefore a stronger consequence holds: **no GL-equivalent Freudenthal quartic is invariant under this C57 augmentation action, even if compatibility with a prechosen symplectic form is dropped.** Any homomorphism from the odd-order group C57 to the component quotient C2 is trivial, so quartic invariance would again place its image in a conjugate E7_sc. The same minuscule character contradiction applies. For rational or real forms, extension of scalars to C gives this obstruction.

An explicit ordinary invariant quartic is (sum_i w_i²)^2 on the augmentation space. It is invariant under every point permutation, including C57⋊C3. Its existence makes the boundary concrete: invariant fourth-order tensors exist, but none lies in the Freudenthal quartic's GL orbit for this action.
