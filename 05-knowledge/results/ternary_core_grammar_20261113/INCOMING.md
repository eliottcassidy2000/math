Session299 proves the uniform substitution identity

    H(T[v←C3]) = 3H(T)+6(a2+a3),

where a_j counts exterior words with j separated root slots. Pure×3 holds
precisely when the old root is a singleton SCC. In contrast our shared-root
triangle gives3H+S+O, with an arbitrary cross matrix; it can produce targets
not divisible by3. Neither law substitutes for the other. A return-channel
matching controls which a_j can be positive in the uniform operation, while
our unrestricted operation retains every cross edge. This is a concrete
source/target map with a different preserved predicate and a different loss
of information, not a numerical analogy.

The incoming H17 chain work gives an all-k physical chart
C(q,s)=(q+3s,s,q,4q+2s), with odd positive q and even positive s, and

    s'=2^(k−1)(3q+2s),       q'=q+3s',        k≥2.

It preserves the chart and gcd, has an exact inverse on its generated
language, and counts addresses by(1−z)/(1−z−z^4). Its physical endpoint
entry and our graph-level atomic entry answer different questions. A future
selector must connect such charts according to the requested target, rather
than count their infinite legal words and infer density.

The incoming [neutral SCC analysis](../changing_core_returns_20261113/geometry/NEUTRAL_SCC.md)
proves that reversing an arc between different SCCs strictly increases H
unless it merely exchanges adjacent singleton components. Thus our neutral
macro cannot be justified by independently declaring each edited arc neutral.
It is a completed, independently recounted replacement, and its general
context claim uses the explicit SCC product law.

The precise next proof obligation is therefore an **exact-surplus selector**:
given an admissible target N, choose a realizable smaller rooted core, an
atomic ear and an actual cross matrix with

    N=H(parent)(2^(m−2)+1)+S+O,

and prove the selected parent lies in the recursively admitted language.
All terms are nonnegative, making inverse search finite under the order
bound, but this does not prove a solution exists. The small omissions and
the same-count15 obstruction show why both core changes and the surplus
sidecar must enter any such induction.
