# Work backward from an exact tournament target

Start from a hypothetical one-arc +2 witness and separate three questions: which source profile is forced, which contacts can realize its switch, and whether the source has to carry the switch at all.

* [Force the profile](arithmetic/PROOF.md). A tall original reader leaves at most one admissible source profile after the determinant-one inverse and the order/modular filters. For the native switch the source has even order at most H+11. All source choices with bounded H, including arbitrarily many singleton pads, retain the original thin counting exponent. A quantitative extension rules out even modestly growing H as a density-one strategy.
* [Recover the contacts](chart/PROOF.md). Delete the switched arc's endpoints. With their signed disagreements fixed, two exact linear equations control the repair and an independent quartic equation controls the absolute count. The switch must change the parity of near-spanning odd cycles. For a transitive remainder, one residue reads nearby contacts and the other reads opposite fringes; both can be programmed independently modulo every power of two with O(log² M) vertices. This does not control the high digits or the absolute count.
* [Change the physical source](physical/PROOF.md). A linked order-12 extension gives a literal 72,028,651 / 72,028,653 pair at the first reader height. Exhausting the stated two-vertex extension scheme finds no continuation to order 14; neither earlier successful two-block template can grow just by changing the two block widths. Every same-order source with the old signed profile has an unavoidable nonzero third-slot difference.
* [Move the switch to the reader](reader/PROOF.md). The desired gap selects an internal reader bit from the source's (H,P₂), with one free binary suffix left over. At any eligible fixed source there are Θ(√X) distinct starting counts up to X, and exact membership reduces to checking a bounded number of integers near a square root at each height. Large eligible heights can also choose every residue modulo H.

The [unknown-source inverse](reader/SOURCE_INVERSE.md) goes in the other direction: an address and desired N force both H and P₂ by exact division. The remaining constraint is that this forced profile be physically realized.

## A literal crossover of the two directions

The upper member of the original eight-vertex source pair has (H,P₂)=(197,3728). Keep that tournament unchanged on both sides, and use the new reader with j=25 and reversed suffix ρ=0: 2²⁵·3728 ≡ 2 mod 197, and the reader address is 42612739620732928. It gives counts 158860293255395167777 and 158860293255395167779 in 65-vertex strong tournaments differing only in a reader arc. The old switch has not been used. The next eligible j is 25+196; the same source admits the full free suffix at every eligible height. This links the two physical banks but is not an arbitrary target selector.

The constructive bottleneck is now precise: for the native route, choose a remainder whose two linear constraints admit contacts at the required quartic baseline; or choose the core from the target so the new reader's quadratic inverse returns a legal suffix. Neither modular lift establishes that absolute equality. Chan–Pak remains open.
