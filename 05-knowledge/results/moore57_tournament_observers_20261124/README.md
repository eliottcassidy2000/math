# Moore graphs as tournaments, and multirow completion

**2026-11-24.** The pentagon, Petersen and Hoffman–Singleton graphs are explicit inputs. The degree-57 Moore graph is open. Every degree-57 representation here is conditional; the remaining Gewirtz exact-cover search from [the previous session](../moore57_metagraph_seams_20261124/README.md) is not settled.

## Which tournament is the representation?

- The [strong blue observer tournament](moore-tournament-representations.md) has 10, 20, 100, or conditionally 6500 vertices. Its cross-block Gram recovers the Moore graph, and an appropriate order lets the plain tournament recover its own layers. This is a real self-converse tournament with a reflected Hamilton spine, not just an orientation of the undirected graph. The literal edit-metric representation separately uses fixed-spine charts on 5, 6, 12, or 83 vertices; Moore edges are nearest-distance contacts, not single-arc flips. The constant-Hamilton-count tapes give another exact edit model.
- [Native limits and witnesses](NATIVE-GRAM.md): the pentagon itself is recovered from the full skew Seidel Gram of its cyclic tournament. No two-value skew Gram on the original vertices can distinguish Moore adjacency at degrees 3, 7 or 57. The two-marker construction recovers the entire graph automorphism group whenever that group is odd. A three-role tournament records every actual middle label via prescribed directed triangles. [Palette bounds](moore-tournament-audit.md) explain why demanding that all local tournament palettes glue as tournament isomorphisms fails, even when a faithful global representation is possible.

## Many branch constraints at once

- [Early rejection](moore57-frame-moments.md): the collective residual Gram and component moments propagate equations to each unfilled branch. An explicit **eight-branch** partial fill obeys every triangle/square check and the spectral interval, yet an outside label would need exactly 27/7 contacts in one part. A **nine-branch** witness reaches the first possible raw spectral rejection.
- [Late inverse](SKELETON.md): at width 56, if 49 rows have been supplied, the old residual uniquely determines the incidence placements and row partition of the seven missing rows. It leaves at most seven labels per new cell and six possible new matching partners per other row after labels are chosen. After fixing the labels, the matching test needs only joint clauses of width at most six; the new/new quadratic constraints follow automatically. For three missing rows that last matching phase is 2-SAT. None of this establishes existence of the 49 rows or of their completion.
- [One- and two-row exact closure](moore_grid_pipeline.md): the last row can be read from residual cliques. The last two rows' line graph gives both color classes; a single initial binary choice propagates, so there is at most one actual completion up to exchanging the two rows. All 30 rooted Petersen tests of the one-row rule and all 350 rooted Hoffman–Singleton tests of both rules pass.

The common mechanism is to retain a *joint witness* before quotienting. In the [Lonely Runner filler work](../chanpak_lrc_two_edit_ports_20261123/FILLER-JOINT.md), two individually feasible phases were not necessarily attained by the same placement. In the Moore residual, a clique column is one placement across all known rows; taking independent pairwise marginals loses the requirement that they come from that same cell. Likewise the earlier [tournament switching quotient](../moore57_metagraph_seams_20261124/moore-tournament-holonomy.md) retained loop parity but lost the identity of each route. The observer and residual constructions keep those identities. These are mechanism-level comparisons, not reductions of the open conjectures to each other.

## Reproduce

Run each of moore-tournament-representations-check.py, moore57-frame-moments-check.py and recover-skeleton.py with Python 3. The three scripts native-check.py, moore_grid_pipeline.py and moore_grid_two_rows.py also use NumPy. The width-56 synthetic replay tests the incidence extraction only; the graph-completion replays at small order use genuine Moore graphs.
