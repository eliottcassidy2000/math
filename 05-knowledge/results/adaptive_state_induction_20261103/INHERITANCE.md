## Exact connection maps

**Source words -> matrix observer.** Process the reverse of the source SCC
word; concatenate the labelled atoms, toggle every nonspine pair and reverse
labels to the processing port. This preserves actual H and all future
same-ended bank observations. It forgets adjacency, other ports and much
source history. The literal word and labels restore the construction sidecar.
The actual `(37,18,19)` fibre failure is the cheap hostile to replacing this
orbit by its necessary positive cone.

**Actual paired states -> correction channel.** From actual words U,V with
`v(V)=3v(U)+m tau`, a common continuation w yields exactly
`v(Vw)-3v(Uw)=m tau P_w`. An eigenline makes the scalar correction constant
up to sign. This preserves the paired construction, not the physical
realizability of tau or a projected bulk by itself. The sign phase and two
actual source words are required sidecars.

**Correction channel -> integral bulk.** Quotient Z^3 by the primitive error
line, using a saturated annihilator basis B. This preserves the transverse
linear action and its prime-adic level. It forgets the charge coordinate and
H's correction term. The full actual state and source word remain necessary
for reconstruction. The q5 example, whose quotient determinant is divisible
by3, is the hostile control for the new p-unit hypothesis.

**Return bank -> affine induction.** A proved source operation with full-state
return identity and continuation-language closure can become an interface
edge. H'=aH+b alone loses those obligations. The repaired finite certificate
then needs seed coverage and incoming residue coverage for every type. The
prime/7p reduction does not supply arbitrary odd inverse parents.
