## Proof review and boundaries

The finite-ring annihilator identity, its root-grounded zero-sum specialization, the owner-marked Smith quotient and the bridge-path criterion have valid directions in their stated domains. The bridge criterion is for connected positive-integer-conductance multigraphs, with a weighted edge expanded into literal unit edges. It is not a new-priority claim. A bank separating the observed vertex classes, plus integer image witnesses for every remaining alias, proves the vertex equivalence without claiming that the bank generates all harmonic characters on arbitrary charges.

The exact order-eight source-conditioned target injectivity is a complete finite-atlas result, not an all-order statement. This audit reviewed its separate independent reconstruction and certificates; it does not redo the full2^21 owner-map census or tournament isomorphism classification. Twelve-bit vertex-class labels cannot be subtracted as coordinates of the3369-bit additive charge quotient.

The adaptive Bellman proof is a finite, source-known, precomputed-policy theorem. Actual indexed owners are query answers. Both lower bounds and attainable policies matter. Observation cost does not imply a legal reconfiguration or a native transition clock.

The shared-kernel obstruction is precisely an affine image-membership test. The torsor answer requires authenticated local cosets and the complete actual mismatch map. It does not turn arbitrary native attachment relations into cosets. Fixed shared pins and freely varying but identified owner variables remain different data. Alternating matching applies after shared owner values are fixed; dynamic owner constraints instead require the complete integer encoding. The conformal primitive-move argument connects a declared bounded integer fibre, not a promised small move bank or efficient algorithm.

The holonomy proof requires whole accepted torsor fibres and invertible full-domain transports, with reverses. Its conclusion is about exactly the supplied graph. A connected supplied subgraph can prove connectivity of a containing graph, whereas disconnected supplied components do not prove that the complete native graph is disconnected. Fixed sections, holonomy orbits and weighted spectral gaps remain different questions. No spectral gap is inferred from rank without transition probabilities.

The weighted-incidence compression identity is an exact trace/variance criterion, stronger than lumpability. The degree-seven and degree-five claims are finite order-five spectra. The Jacobsthal continuation is a particular native transfer with literal endpoints and remaining length, not an arbitrary electrical filter. Its Berggren address does not reproduce its native clock.

The regular group-algebra proof of the multivariable filtration is sound: exact tensor content valuations bound every surviving monomial's degree, one exhibited monomial attains the bound, and all top survivors reduce to the same signed full norm. Cancellations can increase valuations of sums, and a nonfaithful action can destroy sharpness. No change from the original mod8 electrical guard to a mod9 substitute is made.

The rectangle criterion preserves every original harmonic vector, equivalently inclusion of the old bank in the new one, not equality of banks. Paired-black coefficient2a is retained. Order4 charge certificates imply even amplitudes, while the two actual menus allow only absolute amplitude at most one. The general even-net condition is necessary but not sufficient: the source rows remain. The obstruction does not exclude other source pairs, larger capacities or incidence-neutral assignments.

## Independent stable-padding proof review

For R -> T -> S with transitive padding, all Hamiltonian paths have forced block order. Thus the core Hamiltonian count is preserved. Added prefix scores lie strictly above all shifted core scores and added suffix scores strictly below them; all new owners are individually fixed by automorphisms, and Aut(T') is exactly Aut(T).

Within the core, the given partner isomorphism supplies the required arc equality. Within each padding block the two partners are identical. Between blocks, all arcs are reversed except the actual Hamiltonian junctions. Fixing both common core endpoints is therefore precisely sufficient to extend the isomorphism by the identity on both padding blocks. An unpointed target-class equality alone would not supply this endpoint condition.

The authenticated map g=(4,0,2,3,1,5,6,7) fixes owners2 and3 and transports both source1238 target pairs. Consequently every r,s>=0 gives a genuine two-slot coupled move preserving all target-dependent potential differences at order8+r+s. This is an all-size family proved by the arc argument, not an extrapolation from finite tests.

A one-slot change cannot survive because the other slot fixes the entire shared core interior while all remaining positions are pinned. This argument does not assume that padded target-class fibres stay the same size. The existing two-slot replacement proves sharp support2; four slot-clock occurrences and two shared variables are separate support measures. Source rigidity rules out explaining the move as a source-automorphism lift. Nonempty padding makes the family decomposable; no claim about all regular or primitive tournaments follows. The original numerical phase values5 and4 are not asserted at larger orders, and no numerical higher-order electrical atlas is fabricated.

Converse transports every clock and arc. Global extension still needs distinct unoccupied literal resources or a complete injective reassignment. The theorem does not prove all guarded fibres connected or provide a complete native wiring classification.
