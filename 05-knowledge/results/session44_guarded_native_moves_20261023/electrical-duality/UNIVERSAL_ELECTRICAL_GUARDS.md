## 2. The exact all-modulus quotient and its converse

Let R=Z/mZ, for any m>=1. The dot-product character pairing on R^V is
perfect. For every matrix M over R,

    (ker M)^perp = im(M^t).                                      (1)

Containment is immediate. Equality follows from Smith reduction over the
integers (or the invariant factors of a finite abelian group): for a scalar
a, the annihilator of ker(a:R->R) is aR, and invertible coordinate changes
transport the statement. This includes composite m and nonunit entries.
No field-rank substitution is allowed.

For a symmetric Laplacian and any zero-sum vector d, grounding one root
does not change the test: every full harmonic x differs from a grounded
one by x_r times the constant vector, and d annihilates constants. Hence

    d.x=0 for every x in H_m  iff  d lies in im(L modulo m).      (2)

In particular, for a fixed actual source and two authenticated targets u,v,

    phi_x(s,u)=phi_x(s,v) for every x in H_m
        iff e_u-e_v lies in im(L modulo m).                    (3)

These are equivalences, not merely sufficient tests. Arbitrary source
changes require the full difference of charges
`(e_s-e_t)-(e_s'-e_t')`, not just the target difference.

For a connected graph let D^0 be the integer zero-sum divisors and let
K=D^0/LZ^V. The smallest additive universal guard quotient is

    C_m = D^0 / (LZ^V + mD^0) = K/mK.                           (4)

Its perfect dual is the grounded harmonic bank H_m. A directed port carries
the class of e_s-e_t in C_m. “Smallest” here means the coarsest quotient
retaining every character value, not a smallest coordinate bank in an
arbitrary observation menu. Every universal character observable factors
through it, and distinct classes are separated by a harmonic character.

For a root-reduced Laplacian A and integer Smith coordinates U A V=D,
write g_i=gcd(D_ii,m). A reduced charge c has exact signature

    ((Uc)_i modulo g_i)_i,                                    (5)

and two charges agree iff every coordinate agrees. Smith factors alone do
not provide (5): the owner-marked transformation U is indispensable.
The original order-eight 2-primary computation gives

    C_8 = (Z/2)^3358 + (Z/4)^4 + Z/8.

Thus arbitrary charge data have 3369 binary bits of additive ambiguity,
and a full module generating bank needs 3363 generators. This is not the
same problem as labelling only the 3528 vertex charges, for which the
coarsest global equivalence has 3525 states, or 12 bits as abstract labels.
Those abstract class labels cannot be subtracted to test aggregate moves.

## 3. Legal move criteria, with ports, kernel lifts, and converse retained

At a fixed source, a change of a labelled slot from actual path P to Q
preserves every per-slot electrical character iff their target difference
satisfies (3). One must separately check actual Hamiltonicity, the declared
shared owner-clock pins, endpoint pins, distinctness requirements, allowed
move menu, and any literal incidence condition. An electrical quotient
does not certify any of those omitted predicates.

For several slots owned by the same source, preservation only of their
aggregate phases is instead

    sum_old e_target - sum_new e_target in im(L modulo m).      (7)

Equal target multisets suffice simultaneously for every modulus and every
character. They do not imply per-slot target or phase preservation.
For multiple sources, retain these sums separately for every owner node;
a global unowned sum is a strictly weaker condition.

For a candidate network perturbation DeltaL with zero row sums, all original
harmonic characters remain harmonic precisely when every row of DeltaL
lies in im L modulo m. Equivalently, for some matrix B over R,

    DeltaL = B L modulo m.                                    (8)

Indeed each row annihilates H_m iff (2) holds. Symmetry of DeltaL does not
license assuming B symmetric. This checks all original marked characters;
it does not reconstruct the native wiring. Two distinct labelled trees
with degree vector (1,2,2,1) are an exact hostile: both reduced Laplacians
are unimodular and every grounded harmonic bank is trivial at every m.

The native automorphism identity remains
`K_(gP)(T)=g K_P(T)` for g in Aut(T), so source and target owners, hence

## 4. Why all-modulus vertex aliases are exactly bridge contractions

**Theorem.** For a connected undirected multigraph with positive integer
conductances (conductance w means w parallel unit edges), vertices u,v have
equal values for every grounded harmonic character at every modulus iff
they are joined by a path consisting only of literal bridges.

First, (4) implies equality at every modulus iff e_u-e_v is zero in the
finite group K: choose m to be its exponent. Equivalently, there exists an
integer f with Lf=e_u-e_v.

If a literal bridge joins a to b, the indicator of the component on its a
side has Laplacian e_a-e_b. Summing along a bridge path proves sufficiency.
Such a bridge has conductance one; a weighted edge of conductance greater
than one is not a bridge in the literal multigraph.

Conversely suppose Lf=e_u-e_v with f integral. By the maximum principle,
u can be chosen at a maximum level, v at a minimum, and f(u)>f(v).
For every integer k with f(v)<k<=f(u), put S_k={a:f(a)>=k}. Summing Lf on
S_k gives 1. Each edge crossing its boundary contributes a positive integer
`w_ab(f(a)-f(b))`. Therefore there is exactly one crossing edge, with
conductance one and potential drop one; it is a literal bridge. The
nonzero gradient edges form a directed acyclic integral flow from u to v
of total value one. Conservation at every other vertex forces a single
u-to-v path, all of whose edges are the bridges just identified. This
proves necessity. Constant plateaus do not introduce an unverified
path: the divergence equation forces the entering and leaving active
edges of each intermediate plateau to meet at the same vertex.

This reconstructs the standard bridge-contraction phenomenon by an
elementary proof; it is not a claim of a new abstract graph theorem.
No one fixed modulus is universally enough: a cycle of length q has a
critical quotient of order q, invisible to any coprime modulus.
