# Finite-edit recontact has an exact high-tail decoder

**PROVED for the retained-root binary grammar.** This generalizes the one-bit cut decoder in `landmark_selector/LANDMARK_CUT_SELECTOR.md` to any fixed finite coordinated edit. It neither establishes Chan–Pak nor excludes source-changing constructions.

## Setup and theorem

Take two physical rooted sources L=(hL,eL,tL,d) and R=(hR,eR,tR,d), sharing the odd deletion count d. The inherited retained-root transfers are

    0: (h,e,t,d) -> (h,0,2t,d)
    1: (h,e,t,d) -> (h+t-e,d,2t-d,d).

For a word whose chronological bits are read least significant first as W, its final count is

    F(h,e,t,d;W)=h+tW-dK(W)-e(W mod 2),
    K(2v)=K(v), K(2v+1)=K(v)+v+(v mod 2), K(0)=0.

Trailing zero letters have no effect on this count. Fix a nonempty signed edit z_j in {-1,0,1}, with highest nonzero index r. Require the R word's digit b_j to be zero at +1 edits and one at -1 edits. Then the L address is W+Z, where Z=sum z_j 2^j: the edit involves no hidden carries.

**Theorem.** All nonnegative word addresses satisfying

    F(L;W+Z)=F(R;W)

can be generated by testing only the at most 2^(r+2-|support(z)|) compatible low words through bit r+1. Each low word admits at most one high tail, even when the total word length is unbounded. The same assertion holds with any prescribed signed output gap by changing the constant on the right.

## Proof and decoder

Put D=tR-tL and

    A=2^(r+1)D+d sum_(j=0)^r z_j 2^(r-j).

A is odd: the first term is even and in the sum only z_r is odd. In particular A cannot vanish, regardless of how the sources' weighted starts were chosen.

Write W=u+2^(r+2)V, with 0<=u<2^(r+2). The quadratic expression

    K(W)=sum_(i<j) b_i b_j (2^(j-i-1)+[j=i+1])

shows that K(W+Z)-K(W) is linear in every unchanged bit. For i>=r+2 its coefficient is sum_j z_j 2^(i-j-1). There is no adjacency correction in this range, and unchanged-high/unchanged-high terms cancel. Consequently the exact equality becomes

    B(u)+2AV = C,
    B(u)=D u+d[K(u+Z)-K(u)]+(eL-eR)(u mod 2),
    C=hL-hR+tL Z-eL z_0.

The sole candidate is V=(C-B(u))/(2A). Accept it exactly when it is a nonnegative integer. Its binary digits give the entire upper continuation. This proves both completeness and uniqueness. No bound on the height or word length is assumed.

## Structural implication and constructive use

A fixed finite nonempty edit cannot cancel the response of all distant continuation bits. The obstruction is an odd leading numerator, not an empirical failure of a finite search. Any unbounded recontact scheme in this grammar must let the edit reach arbitrarily high bits, change the sources, change the shared-deletion assumption, or change the operation alphabet. This is scoped to a fixed pair of physical sources and a fixed mask; it says nothing against those alternatives.

The positive gain is a target-free complete recontact compiler. Instead of scanning longer and longer words for this fixed mask, enumerate the finitely many low patterns, solve a single exact linear equation, and then directly construct the output tournament from each accepted word. To test an absolute target, compare the resulting exact F with that target. Equal-output recontacts do not themselves guarantee a recontact at a prescribed height.

This offers a useful principle for the universal-selector effort: **a local edit has a finite collision spectrum unless its footprint or interface changes.** Therefore physical source changes and growing edit footprints are substantive mathematical resources, not optional search optimizations.

## Coordinated contacts produce new fibres beyond the single-contact chart

Use the two actual roots of the transitive three-vertex tournament, with profiles (1,0,2,1) and (1,0,4,1). The mask (-1,0,+1,-1,+1) gives addresses 22 and 11 and common count **37**. The mask (-1,-1,-1,0,+1) gives addresses 24 and 15 and common count **47**. Neither count belongs to the earlier single-contact chart, whose relevant chart contains only 5,19,23 before its next lower endpoint 63. (This excludes that specific chart, not every one-contact construction with other sources.) Independent literal adjacency-matrix path DP in the replay verifies both pairs. Thus the multi-edit decoder does supply additional physical collision heights; its odd-numerator obstruction explains why increasing a fixed mask's suffix length cannot then turn these into an unbounded family.
