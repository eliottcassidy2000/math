## Unifying result and limits

The operational principle now has two complete family solutions:

- In L1--L3, retain the whole top-window chart. A first-arrival function
  composes an arbitrary finite bank; maximum-preserving additions are exactly
  a modular acceptance set. The largest tied speed controls the mass germ.
- In E1--E4, retain the original source cylinder. A finite suffix polynomial
  composes all halving-cost histories, with a transported unit phase and exact
  geometric prefix weights. The full Gram bandwidth grows with retained depth,
  and product-compatible cross-multiplier transport has a sharp cutoff.

The preceding [prime-dilation packet](../prime_dilation_response_20261030/README.md)
separated compact formulas, linear memory and realized states. Here the same
separation produces exact geometric and analytic responses: a small recurrence
need not erase source bits, and an unchanged maximum need not retain mass.
This is a reusable framework with distinct proved maps, not a declaration
that all repository problems are isomorphic.
