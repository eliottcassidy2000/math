## Typed connection contracts

1. **Chosen `C5` action to four tournament orientations.** Map an inverse-pair
   half-set `S` to `sum_(s in S) P^s`. Preserves the exact directed relation
   and its affine transport. Passing to spectrum loses labels, orientation
   and, for general tournaments, SCC position. Sidecar: chosen`P`/`S` or
   ordered SCC word. Test: slope2 changes the graph but preserves its family.
2. **Complex representation to a C5 operator.** Map `(rho,g)` to
   `rho(g)+rho(g)^2`. Preserves the five eigenspace multiplicities because
   the polynomial has distinct values on fifth roots. Loses the rest of the
   group's action. Sidecar: module and element/class. Test: the cited5A/5B
   data give identical support and different multiplicities, traces42/-8.
3. **Adjacency spectrum to class identification.** No spectrum-only inverse
   exists even at order5: masks2,5,11 collide. Ordered SCC sizes restore all
   twelve classes in this finite universe; up to reversal they restore the
   ten converse classes. No larger-order completeness is asserted.
4. **Cyclotomic/Hecke analogy.** No literal field map to`Q(zeta17)` exists
   for the regular tournament eigenvalue field. The `X0(13)` function field
   has transcendence degree1. An alternative geometric or Hecke map remains
   unspecified, not quietly supplied by similar notation.

## Why the failed proposal was close

Its census and cyclic-order-five cue were real. It compressed two imaginary
magnitudes into two spectra, replaced a four-object family by one graph,
and confused character traces with eigenvalues. The strongest survivor is an
explicit polynomial operator with exact Fourier multiplicities. This is a
repair, not an assertion of new mathematical priority.

The applied META-PATTERNS cards are **Correct the object before sharpening the
technique**, **Type every analogy and every implication**, and **Controlled
forgetting and unlabeled quotients require a sidecar**. Their triggers and
counterindications are visible respectively in the operator replacement,
field/domain tests, and SCC/multiplicity collisions. No new universal card is
promoted from one session.
