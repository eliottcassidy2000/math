## 1. Exact statement and its scope

Let `R=Z[P]/(P^5-1)`, represented faithfully by integer circulant five-by-five
matrices, and let `A=P+P^2`. Write `S=Z[A]` for the **ordinary unital polynomial
subring**, without automatically adjoining the all-ones matrix, transpose,
or entrywise multiplication. Then:

1. `[R:S]=11`, with Smith factors `(1,1,1,1,11)`.
2. `R/S` is the **additive module** `Z/11Z`, generated by the class of `P`.
   This is not a quotient ring: the proper unital subring `S` is not an ideal.
3. For `r=Σ_(i=0)^4 c_i P^i`,

   `r in S iff c1-c2+3c3+6c4 = 0 mod11`

   `       iff r(1)=r(9) mod11`.

4. In `R_m=(Z/mZ)[P]/(P^5-1)`, the image of `(Z/mZ)[A]` has additive cokernel
   `Z/gZ`, where `g=gcd(m,11)`. Its size is `m^5/g`, and its coefficient map
   from `(Z/mZ)^5` has kernel size `g`. In particular, `A` generates all of
   `R_m` exactly when `11` is invertible modulo `m`.
5. If `11|m`, this cokernel has order **11**, not `11^v11(m)`.
6. Nevertheless, the **full labeled adjacency matrix does not lose P**:

   `P=5J-A^3-2A^2-3A-2I`,

   `J=I+A+A^T`,

   `P=A-A circ A^2`,

   where `J` is all ones and `circ` means entrywise/Hadamard multiplication.
   These are integral identities, valid over every commutative coefficient
   ring, including rings of characteristic 11. Thus
   `Z[A,J]=Z[A,A^T]=Z[P]`, and the analogous equalities hold modulo every `m`.

The defect is therefore precisely a restriction of **ordinary polynomial
functional calculus**, not a defect in the graph data or in reconstruction
when these natural extra operations/sidecars are permitted.

## 3. Why the cyclotomic factor itself has no defect

Put `zeta=zeta5`, `O=Z[zeta]`, and `lambda=zeta+zeta^2`. Its polynomial is

`q(x)=x^4+2x^3+4x^2+3x+1`.

The exact integral inverse is

`zeta=-lambda^3-2lambda^2-3lambda-2`.

Thus `Z[lambda]=Z[zeta]`: no index-11 order defect occurs in the cyclotomic
field. Independently, the field power-basis change has determinant a unit.
The field-order discriminant is 125 for both bases.

What changes is the **gluing of the trivial and cyclotomic factors**. Under

`R -> Z direct-sum O, f(P) -> (f(1),f(zeta))`,

`R` is the fiber product whose two entries agree in
`O/(zeta-1) ~= Z/5Z`. To see sufficiency explicitly, write `b=g(zeta)` with
`deg(g)<4`. One can adjust `g(1)` by any multiple of `Phi5(1)=5` without
changing `b`, by adding an integer multiple of `Phi5`.

Likewise, under `S -> Z direct-sum O`, with `A -> (2,lambda)`, the image is
the fiber product agreeing modulo `(lambda-2)`. Polynomial division by `q`
shows that this quotient is `Z/q(2)Z=Z/55Z`. The missing congruence is visible
in the factorization

`lambda-2=(zeta-1)(zeta+2)`,

with norms `5` and `11` respectively. The ideals are comaximal, so the second
fiber product imposes the old congruence modulo 5 and an additional congruence
at `zeta=-2=9 mod11`. This is exactly `f(1)=f(9) mod11` from Section 2.

Equivalently,

`[Z direct-sum O:R]=5`,

`[Z direct-sum O:S]=55`,

`[R:S]=55/5=11`.

Discriminants give a further check:

`disc(R)=5^5=3125`,

`disc(S)=q(2)^2 disc(q)=55^2*125=378125=11^2 disc(R)`.

These are statements about two integral orders in the same rational algebra
`Q direct-sum Q(zeta5)`, not different rational spectra.

## 4. Modulo 11 and the nonsemisimple hostile modulo 5

| P root | 1 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 9 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A value | 2 | 1 | 9 | 8 | 2 |

Hence the characteristic polynomial becomes

`chi_A=(x-2)^2(x-1)(x-8)(x-9) mod11`,

split algebra `F11^5`, `F11[A]` is exactly the four-dimensional subalgebra

**Hostile to careless eigenvalue counting.** Modulo 5, `P^5-1=(P-1)^5` and

has nilpotence index five. Eigenvalue counting diagnoses the mod-11 failure
because that algebra is split semisimple; it is not a general generation test
in a nonsemisimple coefficient algebra.

## 5. Sharp recovery from the original adjacency data

`q(A)=11J`,

`P=-A^3-2A^2-3A-2I+5J`.

`A^2=P^2+2P^3+P^4`,
