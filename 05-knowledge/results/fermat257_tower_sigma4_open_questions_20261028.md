## 2. The Collatz clock rotates the coset wheels (PROVED)

Fix a prime `p` and the cyclic group `U = (Z/p)^*`. For a subgroup `H <= U` of index `m`, the `m` coset graphs
`C_{uH} = Cay(Z_p, uH)` (one per coset `uH`) are pairwise isomorphic through multiplication by units, and they
partition the edge set of `K_p` into `m` isomorphic spanning subgraphs (a cyclotomic decomposition).

**Proposition.** The affine map `A(x) = 3x + 1` on `Z_p` maps `C_{uH}` isomorphically onto `C_{3uH}`. Hence `A`
permutes the `m` coset graphs of every level as a rotation of order `ord(3 modulo H)`, which at `p = F_n` prime is
`2^k` at level `k` (Pépin: 3 generates `U`). At level 1 of `F_n`, `A` is an isomorphism of the Paley graph onto its
complement; at level `k` it cycles through all `2^k` coset graphs before returning. Translation by 1 is invisible to
the coset graphs because every `C_{uH}` is a Cayley graph of `Z_p`.

*Proof.* `A(x) - A(y) = 3(x - y)`, so `x - y ∈ uH` iff `A(x) - A(y) ∈ 3uH`. The order statement is the order of
`3` in `U/H`, which is `2^k` when `3` generates the cyclic group `U` of order `2^(2^n)`. `QED`

**Reading.** The owner's two Collatz ingredients, "3" and "+1", separate cleanly on a Fermat modulus: the "+1" is a
translation, which every coset graph absorbs, and the "3" is the generator of the Galois tower, which rotates the
wheel of coset graphs at every level. The halving clock, by contrast, lives inside one coset (level 4) and never
rotates the wheel. This is the exact content behind THM-4472's finding that no local rule makes the two sheets
converse: the sheet sign `±1` is a translation, hence invisible to the cyclotomic structure, while the multiplier is
the rotation. Loss ledger: the integers are gone (everything is modulo `F_n`); the Collatz halving valuation is gone
(the clock uses the fixed `2^{-1}`); what survives is the orbit structure of `3` on the coset wheels, which is
Pépin's statement.

## 4. The four-vertex bridges and quantifier prefixes (typed)

- **Exact kernel.** A quantifier prefix of `r` blocks is a linear order of the blocks with a `∃/∀` colouring, i.e. a
  transitive tournament on `r` vertices with a 2-colouring; the `r!` labelled transitive tournaments are the Weyl
  chambers of `A_(r-1)` (Camion–Busch §6.1). On *alternating* prefixes of even length, dualizing (`∃ <-> ∀`) equals
  reversing the order; on odd length reversal is the identity. This is the same Klein group `{id, dualize, reverse,
  dualize∘reverse}` that THM-4472 found on the four-vertex diamonds (converse = negation ∘ time reversal) and that
  THM-584 found on the four classes. STRUCTURE for the group, COINCIDENCE for the number four: there are two
  alternating prefixes of length four (`Sigma^0_4`, `Pi^0_4`), not four.
- **Direction: intransitive quantifier dependence is fixed-point quantification.** Branching (Henkin) quantifiers
  realize partial orders of dependence; a dependence *tournament* with a 3-cycle (`x` depends on `y`, `y` on `z`,
  `z` on `x`) has no classical reading, but its Skolem form is coherent: functions `f_x(y), f_y(z), f_z(x)` and the
  matrix evaluated on the fixed points of `f_x ∘ f_y ∘ f_z`. The owner's "intransitivity as a fundamental aspect" has
  here a precise logical shape: the cyclic triangle is the smallest quantifier pattern whose semantics is a fixed
  point rather than a choice, exactly as the 3-cycle is the smallest tournament without a source. Cheap test: write
  the Collatz cycle half as a sentence with one cyclic triangle of dependence (`x` the start, `y` the word, `z` the
  return) and check whether the fixed-point reading reproduces the cycle equation `x(2^A - 3^j) = C_w`. DIRECTION.
- **What ETH's disproof contributes to the four-vertex program.** Nothing structural; the counting method
  (sparse-polynomial common zeros, `2^(o(v))` for independent sets) does not reach Hamiltonian-path counts, because
  the natural SAT encoding of a Hamiltonian path uses `n^2` variables and `2^(o(n^2))` is useless; the repository's
  `H` engines (THM-4532: `14.5·10^9` canonical subsets at `N = 38`) would need a linear-size encoding that does not
  exist. Recorded as a NEGATIVE with its reason.
