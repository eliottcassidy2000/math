## Result first

Put p=641 and q=6700417, so F5=2^32+1=pq. Every two-run source O^a Z^b, a,b>=1, has the following constraint:

    q divides its C1 Hamiltonian-path count  ==>  p divides that count.

This obstruction holds at **all word lengths**, not merely below a search cap. Nevertheless the full physical grammar realizes the infinite family

    H_t = 4909 + 6013 * 2^(20+64t),        t>=0,

with q|H_t and H_t=380 modulo p. The explicit recipe is

    prefix OOZJOZJJOJJOZ, followed by 20+64t letters O,
    followed by the C1-closing J.

The first member is H_0=6305092397=941*6700417, on 36 vertices. Thus none of these physically constructed targets can be realized by any two-run source. Root changes give an actual escape from a cyclotomic support constraint.

This does not prove that root changes are necessary for every realization of these targets: other O/Z words with more runs, other rooted descriptions, or other tournament constructions are not excluded.

## 2. Exact physical model

A source state is (D,E,U,d), with seed (1,0,2,1), and chronological births

    O(D,E,U,d)=(D,0,2U,d),
    Z(D,E,U,d)=(D-E+U,d,2U-d,d),
    J(D,E,U,d)=(D-E+U,D-E,D+U,D).

The C1 closure has Hamiltonian-path count F=D-E+U. Each letter is one vertex birth; the seed is the two-vertex transitive edge and closing adds one. We import this physical interpretation from the cited audited proof, not from a relaxed numerical cone.

For a,b>=1, write r=a+1 and s=b+1. The inherited compiler is

    H(O^a Z^b)=2+(2^r-1)(2^s-1).                 (1)

Here H denotes the Hamiltonian-path count after the closing J. The source word and the final tournament are distinct objects.

## 3. The complete Fermat-factor phase certificate

Trial division verifies that p and q are prime, and exact arithmetic verifies pq=2^32+1. For each factor, 2^32=-1, so the order of 2 is exactly 64. (Its order divides 64 but cannot divide 32.) The same order holds modulo pq without needing primality.

For a modulus m in {p,q,pq}, define

    Z_m={(r,s) in {0,...,63}²: 2+(2^r-1)(2^s-1)=0 mod m}.

The complete tables are

    Z_p = {(1,32),(5,11),(11,5),(25,63),
           (32,1),(44,49),(49,44),(63,25)},
    Z_q = Z_pq = {(1,32),(32,1)}.                (2)

These are finite, fully exhaustive facts, not sampled residue claims. `verify_fermat_grammar.py` checks all 4096 pairs using modular powers, then independently using exact integer powers and integer division. The explicit expected tables must match. Since every exponent reduces modulo 64, (2) proves the all-exponent implication q|H => p|H in (1). Restricting r,s>=2 removes no residue class: residues 0 and 1 have representatives 64 and 65.

The implication is strict. At (r,s)=(5,11),

    2+(2^5-1)(2^11-1)=63459=99*641,

not divisible by q. The physical source O^4 Z^10 closes on 17 vertices. More generally r=5+64u, s=11+64v, u,v>=0, gives a two-parameter physical family divisible by p and not q.

The shared zero phases are not accidental: for x=2^L,

    2+(x-1)(2x²-1)=(x+1)(2x²-4x+3).            (3)

Thus r=L, s=2L+1 gives a count divisible by 2^L+1. At L=32 this supplies both F5 factors simultaneously. Formula (2) says that, within the two-run compiler, every q-divisibility event has precisely these phases or their transpose. The small factor p admits additional phases; the large factor q does not.

## 4. Constructive escape and proof

Applying the thirteen-letter prefix w=OOZJOZJJOJJOZ to the seed yields

    s_w=(4909,859,6013,859).

This is checked directly by the three integer recurrence rules. For c>=1, O^c(s_w)=(4909,0,2^c*6013,859), hence closing gives

    F(O^c(s_w))=4909+6013*2^c.

At c=20, exact multiplication gives H_0=941q and H_0=380 mod p. Replacing c by c+64 preserves both residues, because 2 has order 64 at both primes. Consequently every t>=0 gives the claimed physical H_t, and none can lie in the two-run image by section 3.

The graph order is 2+13+(20+64t)+1=36+64t. The values are strictly increasing, so the family is genuinely infinite. No all-order enumeration is needed. The verifier checks six members only as regression; the proof covers every t by periodicity.

## 5. What this merges, and what it does not

**A genuine map:** the source is a physical O/Z/J recipe; target is its Hamiltonian-path count together with its pair of residues modulo (641,6700417). The map preserves physical realizability and the exact count. Reducing to residues discards magnitude and source state. The sidecar restoring a constructive continuation is the literal source recipe and (D,E,U,d), not a factorization alone.

The phases r,s come from the same Mersenne products used in Session218's selected Camion exponent analysis. Here they govern an entirely different expression, 2+M_r M_s. The Camion family uses 2^(2q)-1=3 M_q W_q, and its totient connection concerns the exponent 2q. No assertion transfers Camion membership or nontotient status to H_t.

The Fermat factors now do useful work: they identify a permanent deficiency of one construction sublanguage and produce a test target that a richer physical recipe repairs. This is relevant to Chan–Pak because a coverage proof must enlarge insufficient grammars by certified operations. It is not a coverage proof, a new forbidden global H-value, or an assertion that H=6700417 itself is unrealizable.

Prior total-tournament counting sees both F5 factors only through binary order. Prior strong-count Camion tests already distinguish F5 from a prime. Our target is neither of those counting functions: it is the number of Hamiltonian paths in an explicitly constructed tournament. Reusing the integers without retaining this type distinction would be invalid.

A concrete next experiment is to compute modular images of three-/four-run O/Z families and locate the least allowed source operation breaking each residual implication. The proved escape above is a positive control for that search. A finite modular obstruction is decisive for a sublanguage; absence of an obstruction is not a proof that every integer is constructed.
