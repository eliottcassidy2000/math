# A guarded sharp-exit bank around the two meanings of 27 modulo 64

2026-10-05. **PROVED:** the whole quarter-child cylinder, the threshold-guarded
two-parameter exit bank, and its density gain against the explicitly named

## 1. Inheritance and the distinction at entry

The closest mechanism is the original-source switch in
[paid portrait controllers, section 6](collatz_paid_portrait_controllers_20261004.md),
with its sharper boundary proposed in
[four-slot compression, section 7](collatz_four_slot_compression_20261004.md).
The general affine payment test and coarse/exact cylinder distinction are
inherited from the [boundary compiler](collatz_boundary_compiler_20261004.md).
The quarter-child identity is inherited from the
[checkpoint reroute](collatz_checkpoint_reroute_20261004.md).
This note applies these mechanisms to previously omitted exit guards;
it does not claim a new general affine identity.

The board is **literal source / exponent address / negative anchor /
exact terminal valuation / source-relative threshold / paid dependency**.
The anchor is guard coverage; the niche is a sharp exit with an explicit
height sidecar; the wildcard is its pullback to powers of three.

The canonical hostile is 27: its initial word 1211 ends at 71, above the
retained source. A local decrease from that checkpoint is not automatically
payment of 27. The corrected near miss is replacing a proved exit threshold
by its first coefficient-contractive value without checking the carry.

There are two different residue statements:

- **Exponent:** \(e\equiv27\pmod{64}\) implies
  \(3^e\equiv187\pmod{256}\). The inherited word 1213 already pays this class.
- **Integer source:** \(n\equiv27\pmod{64}\) forces word 1211, with
  \(x=(81n+85)/32\). This entire source class is not paid by that four-slot rule.

Inside the residual exponent domain \(e\equiv3\pmod8\), the first of
these additions lies on \(e\equiv11\pmod{16}\). The new family below lies
on the other branch, \(e\equiv3\pmod{16}\).

## 2. A whole source cylinder and its new exact half

For every \(t\ge0\), put
\[
n=219+256t,\qquad h=139+162t=\frac{81n+53}{128}.
\]
The exact first four valuations are 1211, and their endpoint is
\[
x=\frac{81n+85}{32}=557+648t=4h+1.
\]
Here \(h\) is positive and odd, \(h<n\), and
\(3x+1=4(3h+1)\). Consequently, if the actual next exponent at \(x\)
is \(a\), then \(a\ge3\), the next exponent at \(h\) is \(a-2\), and
\(U(x)=U(h)\). This proves a smaller common-future dependency at every
height. The initial four source checkpoints grow; none is an early root.
A root certificate for the child can be transported across this checked join.

The same whole source cylinder also has ordinary source-relative descent
along 1211 followed by its actual exponent \(a\ge3\), because
\[
U(x)\le\frac{243n+287}{256}<n\qquad(n\ge219).
\]
The final inequality is \(13n>287\). The lower bound on the exit is exact:
\(a=3\) if and only if \(t\) is even. Thus the previously omitted half is
\[
n=219+512s\;\xrightarrow{12113}\;209+486s,\qquad s\ge0.
\tag{1}
\]
The odd-parameter half has \(a\ge4\) and was already in the earlier switched
bank. The exact half (1) is new relative to the banks specified in section 4.

Since \(3^{83}\equiv219\pmod{512}\) and 3 has order 128 modulo 512,
(1) covers every power \(3^{83+128s}\). This is an additional \(1/16\) of
the domain \(e\equiv3\pmod8\), before the larger bank below is included.
It is not the previously covered exponent class \(27\pmod{64}\).

The concurrent [paid-guard controller](paid_guard_budget_20261005.md)
may strengthen the child by an additional guarded inverse step. Such a
strengthening has the same entry cylinder and is not another density gain.

## 3. An all-parameter sharp-exit compiler

Fix integers \(q,r\ge1\). Define
\[
\begin{split}
P&=3^{2q+r+1},\\
a_0&=\min\{a\ge2:2^{3q+r+a}>P\},\\
D&=2^{3q+r+a_0},\\
B&=5P-12\,3^r8^q-2^{3q+r+1}.
\end{split}
\tag{2}
\]
In fact \(a_0\ge3\). The word
\[
w=(12)^q1^r a_0
\]
has affine action \((Pn+B)/D\), verified independently by chronological
composition in the script. Its exact native cylinder is
\[
n\equiv c=(D-B)P^{-1}\pmod{2D},\quad 0<c<2D.
\tag{3}
\]
Final oddness reconstructs every preceding exact valuation, as in the
inherited cylinder theorem. All positive proper-prefix endpoints exceed
their starting source: each complete 12 block has slope \(9/8\), each
intermediate first step has slope \(3/2\), and the following ones grow.
Their carries are positive. Thus no proper prefix reaches 1.

**Threshold-guarded payment theorem.** Every positive source satisfying
(3) and
\[
(D-P)n>B
\tag{4}
\]
has an actual strict descent \(0<U^{2q+r+1}(n)<n\). Since its first
valuation is one, its incoming proper graph rank is also strictly lowered.
This is an exact iff payment test for this fixed actual word, not merely a
coefficient test. Positivity and integrality follow from the native guard;
(4) is precisely the inequality after clearing its positive denominator.

Every row contains infinitely many such sources. Its least paid member is
\[
c+2D\max\left(0,
1+\left\lfloor\frac{B-(D-P)c}{2D(D-P)}\right\rfloor\right).
\tag{5}
\]
This formula retains the original source throughout the switch from the
\(-5\) anchor of 12 to the \(-1\) anchor of the run of ones. A supplied
source is checked directly; the compiler never silently replaces it with
the least member of its residue class.

The earlier sufficient exit was \(a\ge a_0+1\). The new bank adds exactly
the \(a=a_0\) half, with (4) retained. The stronger claim that every row's
uncut least coarse or exact member already pays remains **OPEN** here.
The inherited four-slot artifact checked its coarse version on
\(1\le q,r\le128\). Our density calculation does not assume that observation
extends to all parameters: finitely many excluded sources in each row do
not affect its density, and section 4 supplies the required uniform tail
control across rows.

## 4. Disjointness and the quantified coverage gain

Compare against exactly the binary16 bank, the safe rational-anchor and
\(-17\) banks, the earlier switched bank, the whole checkpoint cylinder
155 modulo 2048, the four-slot bank, and its all-depth initial-ones
permutation extension. Also use the strengthened original-source ternary
bank when computing the fused residual. These are the named banks in the
two frozen JSON artifacts read by the script; other repository constructions
are not exhausted by this comparison.

Distinct \((q,r)\) specify disjoint actual prefixes: \(q\) is the number
of complete 12 blocks before the departure, and \(r\) is the following
run of ones. At \(r=1\), \(a_0\ge3\) differs from the next 12 block's
second exponent; at \(r\ge2\) the second consecutive one differs earlier.
The previous switched bank requires a strictly larger last exponent.
The rational 12 bank exits immediately after its full blocks, whereas
these new rows take another one; the other rational and \(-17\) banks
start with two ones. The permuted bank also starts with at least two ones.
The only compatible four-slot row is \((q,r)=(1,1)\), the already paid
1213 cell, which is omitted below.

For the checkpoint guard 155 modulo 2048, its initial word is 121112.
The cases \(q\ge2\), \((q,r)=(1,1),(1,2),(1,3)\), and \(q=1,r\ge4\)
each have a differing exponent within those six positions; at \((1,3)\)
the new terminal exponent is four, not two. Binary16 has debt index one
here and requires suffix 16 or 6 after the first reset two. Neither can
match: \(q\ge2\) gives suffix 12; \(q=1,r\ge2\) gives 11; the omitted
\((1,1)\) row gives 13. These are all-height word exclusions.

The additional bank, omitting \((1,1)\), therefore has odd-relative density
\[
\delta_* = \sum_{q,r\ge1}2^{-(3q+r+a_0(q,r))}-\frac1{128}
          =0.00689011741468514\ldots .
\tag{6}
\]
At finite \(Q,R\), the omitted numerical sum is at most
\[
\frac47\,2^{-3(Q+1)}+\frac{2^{-R}}{14}.
\tag{7}
\]
This follows by the weaker bound \(a_0\ge2\) and geometric summation.

Natural density needs more than formal summation. Rows with \(q>Q\)
lie inside the single native cylinder of \((12)^{Q+1}\). For each of the
finitely many \(q\le Q\), rows with \(r>R\) lie in the cylinder of
\((12)^q1^{R+1}\). Their odd-relative upper densities are respectively
\(2^{-3(Q+1)}\) and at most \(2^{-R}/14\) after summing over \(q\).
Both tend to zero. Finite row truncations have their AP densities even
after (5), so this sandwich proves existence of the infinite bank's
natural density and validates (6)-(7). It also controls all removed
finite initial segments without assuming a uniform height threshold.

CRT gives the same binary density inside every fixed ternary residue
class. Applying the controlled binary and ternary tails gives the gain
\(\delta_*(1-\delta_{\rm origin})\) outside the strengthened origin bank.
The exact rational enclosure in the script gives the new named residual
\[
0.10584246338775<\delta_{\rm residual}<0.10584246338776,
\]
down from the preceding 0.109660841806587. This is the residual of these
paid guard banks, not a density of hypothetical Collatz counterexamples.

## 5. Pullback to powers of three

Every new row starts with 121, hence lies in \(n\equiv27\pmod{32}\).
For a modulus \(2^b\), \(b\ge3\), the powers of 3 are exactly the units
1 or 3 modulo 8, with order \(2^{b-2}\). The elementary identity
\(v_2(3^{2^j}-1)=j+2\), \(j\ge1\), proves the order and binary lifting.
Thus each exact source cylinder modulo \(2D\) has a unique exponent
class modulo \(D/2\), inside \(e\equiv3\pmod8\).

Its relative exponent density is \(8/(D/2)=16/D\). The height guard
removes only finitely many exponents from that class. The same parent
cylinders used above pull back to exponent cylinders, whose relative
densities are multiplied by 16, establishing the exponent-density limit.
All distinct source guards remain disjoint under pullback.

Consequently the added relative coverage is \(16\delta_*\), and the
named coverage within \(e\equiv3\pmod8\) increases from 42.7206 percent to
\[
0.53744803446924<\delta_{\rm covered}<0.53744803446925.
\]
The power-phase API retains the least exponent satisfying (4), rather
than dropping that height condition. In the declared 1535-row truncation,
no binary or exponent initial member needs removal. That last statement
is finite-exact; the theorem and density proof do not depend on it.

## 6. Reproduction and next boundary

Run `python -B 04-computation/experiments/paid_guard_27_boundary_20261005.py`
and repeat with `-O`; stdout is saved in the matching `.out` file.
No prior experiment's main function is imported or run, and no historical
output is mutated. The script records the two frozen input hashes.

Controls comprise 576 literal source-word replays for \(q\le12,r\le16\)
and three lifts, 128 quarter-child instances, three explicit powers of
three, 204725 residue-disjointness checks against the saved earlier cells,
and exact density/phase calculations on 1535 rows. It rejects float/bool
parameters, a forged row carry, and sources outside the supplied guard.
These complement the all-parameter proofs; they do not establish universal
coverage or the uncut least-residue conjecture.

The reusable advance is to keep the signed carry threshold as part of a
guard, rather than spend an extra division solely to avoid storing it.
The next precise obligation is the set not admitted by the expanded guarded
bank, including unmatched prefixes and failed source inequalities. Merely switching
anchors, recognizing a modular phase, or reusing a proved local decrease
does not discharge that obligation.
