* Coded witness projections split exactly when a uniform computable selector
  exists. External bijections may fail to become internal epimorphisms in Eff.
* Same-clock predicates compose by intersection; their Boolean internal hom

These are precise maps with tested failure boundaries. We do not construct a
new realizability topos or identify the major open problems by analogy.
