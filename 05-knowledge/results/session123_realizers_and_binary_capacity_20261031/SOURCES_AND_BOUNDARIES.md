# Primary inputs, classical material, and boundaries

No literature-priority claim is made. The independently checked results are
proved in their specified models; finite controls do not settle a major
unrestricted conjecture.

## Realizability

Faber and van Oosten, *More on Geometric Morphisms between Realizability
Toposes*, §1.2, supplies the assembly/tracking and exact-completion framework:
https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~ooste110/realizability/geommorRTav.pdf .
Hyland, *The Effective Topos*, supplies the standard effective setting:
https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~jmeh1/Research/Oldpapers/hyland-effectivetopos.pdf .
The coded-section equivalence and halting-bit hostile are elementary deductions,
not new claims about the existence of a topos. A tracked map, an external
surjection, a regular epimorphism, and a section are kept distinct.

## Integer programming and continuous selection

Lenstra, *Integer Programming with a Fixed Number of Variables* (1983):
https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~lenstrahw/PUBLICATIONS/1983i/art.pdf .
The fixed-dimension algorithm is classical. The exact safe-time compilation
is given and checked here; the brute reference checker is not called a
Lenstra implementation. The separate conductor/family solver is implemented
and uses no integer-programming library.

Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, circle lifts and degree:
https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf .
The phase-family obstruction has an explicit elementary lift proof as well.
Failure of continuous selection over real phases does not prevent a rational-
input computable selector.

## Closed structure and circuit resources

The downset/convolution adjunction is proved directly. Its pattern is classical
Day convolution; primary background is Brian Day, *On closed categories of
functors*:
https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/DayReport.pdf .
No free pass from a numeric closed category to actual graph or circuit
realization is assumed. The full-profile internal hom's collapse and the
7-versus8 saturation example explicitly test the lost information.

Carlet's author-hosted *Boolean Functions for Cryptography and Coding Theory*,
§5.2/Theorem10, is the classical quadratic-function background:
https://www.math.univ-paris13.fr/~carlet/book-fcts-Bool-vect-crypt-codes.pdf .
Cambridge L118 notes §4.3/Theorem4.28 supply copying/deletion context:
https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/teaching/2324/L118/notes.pdf .
The shared-boundary kernel and scalar-cost obstruction are proved directly.
Forest gate savings require exclusive XOR intermediates and retain every
protected AND output; they are not unrestricted NP circuit lower bounds.

## Exact capacity and unresolved prior-art comparison

The all-budget binary capacity proof is self-contained from its graph grammar
and simultaneous parity lifting. Its actual constructors are also explicit.
The Boolean-incidence/Pascal and Kronecker identities are classical algebra;
their capacity consequences follow from the stated theorem, not a claim that
these identities were newly discovered.

A search found Exoo and Reynolds, *Ramsey numbers based on C5-decompositions*,
Discrete Mathematics71(2),119–127(1988):
https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(88)90065-9 .
The publisher abstract describes multicolor bounds for small dense graphs via
near-decompositions into disjoint pentagons. The full text was unavailable
through the inspected publisher route (HTTP403). Its relationship to this
exact recursively generated two-budget class remains unassessed; neither a
title match nor a search miss establishes novelty or equivalence.
