## Completed uniform classification

For the nonempty singleton/join/union/full-C5 graph class, the exact maximum
order with clique budget A and independence budget B is

    F(A,B) = sum_(0<=i<A,0<=j<B) 2^popcount(i & j).

[Complete all-budget proof and actual graph witnesses](monoidal_ramsey/ALL_BUDGET_KERNEL.md).
The crucial step is simultaneous five-layer parity rounding, including a
mixed-parity vertex-cover repair. Finite tables do not replace this proof.

[Tensor/Gram and minimal-generator classification](tensor/TENSOR_AND_GENERATORS.md)
proves that the necessary additive generators are exactly1 through the least
power of two at least A. Translation holds from the origin, the capacity
matrix is an integral determinant-one Gram matrix, and a binary tensor gives
the density kernel. [Independent audit](audit_ramsey/AUDIT.md).

[Closed profile category](monoidal_ramsey/CLOSED_PROFILE_STRUCTURE.md) supplies
a genuine convolution/internal-hom adjunction, but also proves that internal
hom between the full capacity rows collapses. The numerical abstraction is
not an equivalence of graph categories and is not a realizability topos.

Further verified witness-selection and circuit-resource interfaces are being
prepared in this session. This checkpoint claims only the included results.
No unrestricted Ramsey improvement or literature-priority claim is made.

  sections have different quantifiers. A coded category is not automatically a topos.

  extensional copying cannot be assigned a strict positive additive gate price.

of the underlying major open problems.
