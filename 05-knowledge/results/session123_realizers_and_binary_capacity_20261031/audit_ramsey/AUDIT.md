# Independent audit: binary capacity kernel and parity repair

Scope: the nonempty class generated by singleton, disjoint union, join and full nonempty C5 substitution. No unrestricted Ramsey extremal claim. This audit imports no producer code. It independently checks root's proposed proof, not originality in the literature.

## Main result: the all-budget upper-bound repair is valid

Write K(A,B)=sum_{0<=i<A,0<=j<B} 2^popcount(i&j), including K(0,B)=K(A,0)=0. Its mixed discrete derivative is exactly the positive summand. Splitting indices by parity gives

K(2a+e,2b+f)=5K(a,b)+2e*(K(a+1,b)-K(a,b))+2f*(K(a,b+1)-K(a,b))+ef*Delta_a Delta_b K(a,b),

for e,f in {0,1}. Five layers assigning two increment labels P to an odd first coordinate and two labels Q to an odd second coordinate therefore dominate the child kernel exactly when |P intersect Q|>=1 for a doubly odd child; intersection one gives equality.

For original C5 positions i modulo5 put P_i={i,i+2} and Q_i={i,i+1}. Adjacent P_i are disjoint; distance-two Q_i are disjoint; P_i intersect Q_i={i}. We prove the weighted inequality

sum_i K(a_i,b_i) <= K(A,B)

for nonnegative integer coordinates satisfying a_i+a_(i+1)<=A and b_i+b_(i+2)<=B, by induction on A+B.

Base A=0 or B=0 is immediate. If A=1, positive a-support is an independent set of the original C5, so has size at most2. The corresponding b's sum to at most B (or a single b<=B). Since K(1,b)=b this proves the base; B=1 is dual.

For A,B>=2 all five layer budgets below are strictly smaller in both coordinates, so induction applies even when a layer budget is zero.

* Both even: fixed P_i,Q_i give all layer budgets (A/2,B/2). This uses the fact that if an even-budget edge has one or two odd endpoints, its base half-sum is at most half-budget minus1; disjoint increment sets prevent a double increment.
* Both odd: use a common P={0,1}, Q={0,2} for all children. Layer budget pairs are (h,k) twice and (h+1,k), (h,k+1), (h+1,k+1) once, where A=2h+1,B=2k+1. Their kernel sum is exactly K(A,B).
* A odd, B even: let S be the positions with both child coordinates odd. If S is proper, the edges {Q_i:i in S} form a proper subgraph of C5. Deleting any absent edge leaves a four-edge path containing that subgraph; the second and fourth vertices along the path cover all its edges. Thus there is a two-vertex cover. Use this common cover P for all odd a-increments, retain fixed Q_i for b. Every double-odd child has overlap at least1. Three layers have budgets (h,k), two have budgets (h+1,k). Their kernel sum is K(2h+1,2k).
* Exception S=all five in the preceding case: retain fixed P_i,Q_i. All a_i are odd, so every adjacent base a half-sum is at most h-1; the disjoint P's imply layer a-budget at most h. Every b-layer has budget k. Thus the bound is 5K(h,k)<=3K(h,k)+2K(h+1,k)=K(A,B). Child overlap is exactly1.
* A even, B odd: interchange roles. The P_i edges form a pentagon in step-two order, so the same cover argument applies; the all-five exception is identical with roles reversed.

This checks the mixed-parity repair including its two essential issues: the odd-cycle vertex-cover obstruction is isolated exactly at S=all five, and that exception has a stronger half-budget bound. There is no appeal to finite numerical matching.

## Exactness for the graph class

Balanced substitution recursively realizes K(A,B). For odd clique budget choose two nonadjacent heavy positions; for odd independence budget choose two adjacent heavy positions. When both odd choose those pairs to intersect once. The resulting five child types implement the parity recurrence above. All children are nonempty when A,B>=2. The axes A=1 or B=1 are independent sets or complete graphs. Strong induction is valid because child clique and independence budgets are both strictly smaller.

Joins and unions are covered by the same weighted inequality with two nonzero positions: adjacent for joins and nonadjacent for unions. Although zeros are used in this inequality, no empty children are introduced into the generating graph class. Structural induction gives F(A,B)<=K(A,B), and the explicit balanced construction gives the reverse bound. This is a classification of extremal capacity values and explicit attainers, not all extremizing graph isomorphism types.

This equals the grammar obtained by adjoining the empty graph and permitting empty C5 substitution children. One direction restricts a singleton-leaf construction tree to its selected leaves. Conversely, replace every empty leaf of an empty-enabled construction by a fresh singleton; the result is a D construction, and deletion of all fresh vertices recovers the original graph as an induced subgraph. Joins/unions preserve this restriction identity.
