## What remains open

The full named carrier is already known to be faithful; its faithfulness is
not an open target here. A uniformly smaller closed representation for a
specified weaker attachment language, with an efficient transition algorithm,
remains a useful target. The existing fresh-reset minimum is different:
[unnamed dissection U2](../unnamed_carrier_dissection_20261026/README.md)
retains the two endpoint isomorphism classes of a complement line, within
its stated operation menu.

We do not claim a new record in scalar order, a complete order-ten class
generation in this packet, or a compressed classification of every strong
atom. The general graceful-tree and square-sum existence problems, and
LRC(14), remain open.
