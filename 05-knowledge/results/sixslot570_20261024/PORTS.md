    d_K(v) = n-1-d_T(v) + 1[v=first(P)] - 1[v=last(P)].

## A general new carrier which records the moving endpoints

For any tournament T on n owners, put S(T)=a -> T -> b, with a->b. Every
Hamiltonian path of S(T) is uniquely a,P,b. Thus this is a bijection of full
source/path records, with old owner clock i shifted exactly to i+1. Old fixed
internal clocks remain fixed, and the new first/last owners a,b are fixed.
The source automorphism group is unchanged, since its unique source and sink
fix a,b. No automorphism lift creates a spare resource.

Let H=K_(a,P,b)(S(T)). Then:

1. H restricted to the old owners is exactly K_P(T).
2. a has the unique outgoing neighbor first(P).
3. b has the unique incoming neighbor last(P).
4. Every old owner v has d_H(v)=n-d_T(v); d_H(a)=1 and d_H(b)=n.

All statements follow by listing which path arcs remain unreversed. This
turns the original endpoint correction into literal adjacency at the two
outer ports. All new targets have the same labelled degree vector; their
differences are divergence-free signed arc flows. The old endpoints have not
been erased: they are reconstructed from items2 and3. If T has neither a source
nor a sink, a and b are additionally the unique target owners of degrees1 and
n, so the marked inner restriction is recoverable from the target itself.

This is a source-record construction, not a map on bare target classes or
unmarked resource quotients. In particular

    K_(a,P,b)(S(T)) != S(K_P(T))

in general. Source suspension does not commute with path complement after
forgetting the boundary incidence. The actual enlarged complement and converse
square are retained and verified, rather than replacing this false identity
with an analogy. The chosen source orientation is also needed: suspending an
inner forward word and its complement separately need not give the same
enlarged normalized resource.
