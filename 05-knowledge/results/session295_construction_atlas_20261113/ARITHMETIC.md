# Context ideals as lower envelopes of evaluator valuations

**Proved.** Builds on Session293's exact uniform-module ideal and
Session294's odd-cycle-packing evaluator. These formulas concern uniform
attachments and generated ideals, not positive coverage.

Let f(t) be the odd-cycle contraction polynomial of a nonempty tournament
C: each disjoint packing of directed odd cycles contributes
2^(number of cycles) times t^(number of remaining atoms after contraction).
Thus f(0)=0; f(j)>0 for j≥1. Its ordered k-path-cover count is
F_k=Δ^k f(0). Let w_j=(j−1)! and d=degree f. The raw and context ideals are
generated by F_k and w_k F_k respectively, for 1≤k≤d.

## Weighted integer-valued change of basis

On vectors of length d the evaluation/finite-difference change of basis
has forward coefficients binomial(j,k) and inverse coefficients
(-1)^(k−j) binomial(k,j); both are integral triangular with diagonal one.
Scaling both vectors by w multiplies the (j,k)-coefficient by w_j/w_k
(j≥k), still an integer. Consequently

    raw = gcd_{j=1..d} f(j),
    context = gcd_{j=1..d} ((j−1)! f(j)).

Equivalently extend j to all positive integers: each f(j) uses only k≤j
in Newton's expansion, so every term w_j f(j) is a multiple of the same
context generator. This is equality of additive ideals. It does not
construct a positive tournament output w_j f(j).

## Ordinal products and first observation

Odd-cycle packings factor across ordinal sums. Fix core polynomials f_i,
multiplicities m_i≥0 (not all zero), and prime p. The product g=∏f_i^m_i
then obeys exactly

    v_p(raw(g))     = min_{j≥1} Σ_i m_i v_p(f_i(j)),
    v_p(context(g)) = min_{j≥1} [Σ_i m_i v_p(f_i(j))+v_p((j−1)!)].

The second is a finite minimum of affine forms uniformly for this fixed
list of cores: discard all Pareto-dominated points of the vectors
(v_p(f_1(j)),...,v_p(f_l(j)),v_p((j−1)!)) in N^(l+1).
Dickson's lemma leaves finitely many minimal vectors. This proves a finite
rational polyhedral chamber description. A constructive finite search
is also available: in a current union of p-adic residue classes with
some valuation coordinates already fixed, take its least positive
integer j0. Its factorial tariff is minimal within that region.
Every non-dominated point in this region either is represented by j0
or lowers at least one currently unfixed coordinate below its value
at j0. Branch on which coordinate is lowered and fix its exact
smaller valuation. Each branch condition v_p(f_i(j))=t is decided
modulo p^(t+1), so emptiness and its least positive representative
are effective. Each branch fixes one new coordinate; depth is at
most the number of core types. Repeated coverage of some regions is
harmless. This also constructs a uniform finite set for the raw
formula by dropping the already-minimal tariff.

Along a ray, write g=f^r for the product at the base of that ray. Put
a=min_j v_p(f(j)), let j* be the first minimizer, and c=v_p((j*−1)!).
No j>j* can beat j*. Thus the exact context valuation at r≥1 is the lower
envelope of at most j* lines, and stabilizes to ar+c once

    r ≥ max(1, max_{1≤j<j*} ceil[(c−v_p((j−1)!))/(v_p(f(j))−a)]).

An empty inner maximum is ignored. This is the least such integer onset.
The raw valuation is ar at every r. Thus the eventual difference is the
valuation of the factorial of the first place where the raw obstruction
is actually observed, minus one in the factorial argument. In particular
stabilization occurs by r=max(1,c), but often earlier. The slope a and
intercept c are distinct pieces of the state.

For example Session294's exceptional eight-vertex cores have first
minimum at j*=4 for p=3, giving slope 2 and intercept 1. Three-cycle
blocks have first minimum at j*=1. Mixed raw valuations need not add:
individual minimizers must be achievable at the **same integer j**.
They add exactly iff the positive-multiplicity core factors have a
common minimizing residue. For the context ideal, by contrast, only one
excursion tariff is charged for a common test; summing separate context
valuations is not a valid general prediction.

The correct analogy to Newton polygons is an exact lower envelope of
integer affine costs, not cancellation in coefficients: the gcd minimizes
among physical-probe combinations and cannot cancel those tests. The
construction bottleneck remains finding positive counts at an absolute
target with a renewable marked interface.
