## Proof audit: vertex lane

The directed triangle associator and constant-field locality commutator are correct. Commutativity plus commuting left multiplications forces associativity, so the stated winner-product construction gives a constant-field vertex algebra exactly for transitive tournaments.

The SCC quotient proof is valid for the ideal generated by associators. Sending vertices to SCCs gives one inclusion; the partial order on idempotents in any associative quotient gives the reverse. The explicit independent unit is decisive: A_T^+=C1 plus A_T has quotient C^(m+1), even if A_T already has a unit. Without adjoining a new unit the quotient would be C^m. The original A_T has a unit iff T has a sink, and then the sink basis element is that unit. This optional observation is not needed by the note's convention.
