## 1. The requested short proof

Let a finite group G act on a finite set X and let V=C[X]. Its permutation matrices satisfy tr rho(g)=|Fix_X(g)|. The Reynolds operator

P=(1/|G|) sum_g rho(g)

satisfies P^2=P and im P=V^G. The orbit sums are a basis of V^G. Taking traces gives

|X/G|=dim V^G=tr P=(1/|G|) sum_g |Fix_X(g)|.

This is the Cauchy-Frobenius orbit-counting lemma, not the different Burnside p^a q^b theorem. The complex field makes trace(rank) an ordinary integer. In characteristic dividing |G| the displayed averaging operator does not exist; in positive characteristic not dividing |G| its trace alone is an equality in that field and does not recover integer cardinalities without lifting.

More generally, for any commuting involution C and irreducible character chi_lambda,

m_{lambda,epsilon}=(1/(2|G|)) sum_g conjugate(chi_lambda(g)) [|Fix(g)|+epsilon |Fix(gC)|].

This is multiplicity, not dimension of the isotypic summand: multiply by d_lambda for that dimension. For lambda trivial, the sum/difference separates converse-symmetric and converse-antisymmetric functions on orbits. tr(C|V^G) counts self-converse G-orbits, not labelled self-converse objects.

## 2. What 'irreducibles are primes' preserves and destroys

Over C, every finite-dimensional representation is a direct sum of irreducibles with unique multiplicities. Thus the additive monoid of representation isomorphism classes is freely generated by irreducibles, like nonnegative integer coordinates. This is an additive atomic decomposition, not unique prime factorization under tensor product.

For S_2 the nontrivial sign representation s satisfies s tensor s=1: it is a unit in the representation ring, unlike an integer prime. For S_3, its two-dimensional standard representation W satisfies

W tensor W = 1 direct-sum sign direct-sum W.

Characters on the identity, transposition and 3-cycle classes are W=(2,0,-1); squaring gives (4,0,1)=(1,1,1)+(1,-1,1)+(2,0,-1). Tensoring two irreducibles can split. Fusion rules, not integer multiplication, govern the tensor structure.

Hostile modular example: for C_p over F_p, the matrix J=I+N with N=[[0,1],[0,0]] defines a 2-dimensional representation since J^p=I. It has an invariant line and two trivial composition factors but is not their direct sum (J is not I). Jordan-Hoelder composition factors do not remember the extension. Semisimplicity cannot be silently imported into modular or logarithmic vertex-algebra settings.

Primary background verified: Etingof et al., Introduction to Representation Theory, https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0827; MIT chapter on Maschke, characters and tensor multiplicities: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-712-introduction-to-representation-theory-fall-2010/resources/mit18_712f10_ch3/ . Proofs and examples here are explicit rather than dependent on a quoted theorem.

## 3. Constructive sign duality for every labelled tournament

Fix vertices [n], N=binom(n,2), and x_ij(T)=+1 for i->j, -1 otherwise, i<j. Put delta(T)=product_{i<j} x_ij(T). Let D e_T=delta(T)e_T on V_n=C[all labelled tournaments], rho(g)e_T=e_{gT}, and C e_T=e_{T^conv}.

Each inversion of g reverses the reference ordering of exactly one pair, hence

delta(gT)=sgn(g)delta(T), delta(T^conv)=(-1)^N delta(T).

Consequently

D rho(g)=sgn(g)rho(g)D,     DC=(-1)^N CD,     D^2=I.

The first identity is the precise orientation-sign coordinate inherited from the Vandermonde tournament expansion in THM-1805; it does not need any claim about a canonical cycle-cancelling involution.

Write V_n=sum_lambda S^lambda tensor M_lambda. Since S^lambda tensor sign=S^{lambda'}, D gives

m_lambda=m_lambda',
m_{lambda',epsilon}=m_{lambda,(-1)^N epsilon}.

Here lambda' is the transposed partition and (-1)^N=(-1)^floor(n/2). In particular the sign and trivial representations occur equally often, each T(n) times. For self-transpose lambda and n=2 or 3 mod 4, the converse trace on M_lambda is zero and its + and - dimensions coincide.

Immediate stabilizer checks: if gT=T then sgn(g)=1; if gT=T^conv then sgn(g)=(-1)^N. This avoids invoking classification, solvability, or a census.

## 4. Exact full-flip spectral refinement

Let A e_T=sum_e e_{flip_e T}, the N-regular labelled hypercube adjacency. A commutes with rho(S_n) and C, while DA=-AD because a single flip changes delta. Therefore D gives an isomorphism of joint sectors

(lambda, converse epsilon, A-eigenvalue theta)
<-> (lambda', converse (-1)^N epsilon, A-eigenvalue -theta).

This concerns the full single-arc-flip metagraph operator, NOT the tiling-line metagraph, nor a simple unweighted isomorphism-class graph. The trivial-isotypic restriction is the class transition matrix that counts labelled single-arc flips, including loops and multiplicities. Its matrix in normalized orbit sums is symmetric; in the ordinary class-value basis it is similar to that symmetric matrix.

For a computational character formula, use Walsh monomials x_F=product_{e in F}x_e. Degree k has adjacency eigenvalue N-2k and converse eigenvalue (-1)^k. Let O range over the cycles of g on unordered pairs and let s_O be the product of its endpoint-order signs around O. Then

sum_k chi_{n,k}(g)t^k = product_O (1+s_O t^{|O|}).

Proof: a basis monomial contributes to the trace iff its support is a union of whole pair-orbits; including an orbit contributes its length and sign. At t=1 this gives the usual fixed-tournament count; at t=-1 it gives |Fix(gC)|. Thus

m_{lambda,k}=sum_{mu partition n} chi_lambda(mu) [t^k] product_O(1+s_O t^{|O|})/z_mu.

Real characters of S_n remove the conjugation. Complementing support is D and proves m_{lambda,k}=m_{lambda',N-k}. Summing degree recovers section 3, so this is a single mechanism, not unrelated coincidences.

Small hostile to overclaiming: n=3 trivial-sector adjacency eigenvalues are 3 and -1. It is NOT symmetric about zero. The paired sign sector has 1 and -3. Therefore sign duality cannot be sold as bipartiteness of the ordinary class quotient.

n=4 trivial-sector spectrum is {6,2,0,-2}, each once. The standard (3,1) multiplicity is 8 with converse trace -2, hence (+,-)=(3,5); its transpose (2,1,1) has the same splitting because N=6 is even.

## 5. Hamiltonian marking gives a closed all-order character answer

Let Y_n={(T,p): p=(p_0,...,p_{n-1}) is a directed Hamiltonian path of T}, n>=1, with a full ordered vertex list, not paths modulo reversal or cyclic rotation. S_n relabels both entries. Define C(T,p)=(T^conv,reverse(p)). The S_n action is free because a fixed ordered path fixes every vertex.

Normalize p to (0,...,n-1). Its n-1 path edges are forced and the remaining

m=binom(n-1,2)

chord orientations are arbitrary. Thus Y_n is S_n times a Boolean m-cube as an S_n-set and C[Y_n] is 2^m copies of the regular representation.

In these coordinates C sends (g,b) to (g w_0,r b), where w_0 reverses the standard order and r reflects chords (i,j)->(n-1-j,n-1-i). There is NO bit complementation: path reversal and tournament converse cancel it. Exactly f=floor((n-1)/2) chords are individually fixed by r. The other chords are paired. Put q=(m+f)/2, an integer; r fixes 2^q masks.

The regular bimodule decomposition C[S_n]=sum_lambda S^lambda tensor (S^lambda)^* now gives

dim M_lambda=2^m d_lambda,
tr(C|M_lambda)=2^q chi_lambda(w_0),
dim M_{lambda,epsilon}=(2^m d_lambda+epsilon 2^q chi_lambda(w_0))/2.

For n=4, m=3,q=2. Standard lambda=(3,1) gives total multiplicity 24, C trace -4, (+,-)=(10,14). For the trivial representation the formulas give 2^m Hamiltonian presentations up to relabelling and 2^q converse-fixed presentations. These are not the numbers of unmarked tournament classes or THM-4731's kappa self-line endpoints. Forgetting the marked path loses the H(T)/|Aut(T)| presentation multiplicity; confusing these sets is exactly the kind of normalization error the inherited self-line hostile forbids.

## 6. Paley and Monster: genuine transported structure, not number matching

THM-214 proves that translation by a nonzero element of F_p fixes r=(p-1)/2 directed Hamiltonian cycles in the prime Paley tournament, counted modulo cyclic rotation. If hc is the total count, Fourier characters of C_p give

C[cycles] = ((hc-r)/p) C[C_p] direct-sum r times trivial.

Indeed the character is hc at identity and r at every nonidentity element. For p=7, hc=24,r=3: three regular representations plus three trivials. For Hamiltonian PATHS, no nonidentity automorphism fixes an ordered path: the 189 paths of P_7 form nine regular copies of Aut(P_7), order 21, or 27 copies under C_7. Cycles and paths must not be interchanged. Direct enumeration also gives 9 paths fixed by p -> -reverse(p) on P_7. Summing over all 21 affine anti-automorphisms gives 63 fixed pairs, so converse has trace 63/21=3 on the nine-dimensional Aut(P_7)-orbit space: its (+,-) dimensions are (6,3). This is a FINITE-EXACT witness, not an all-Paley formula.

The existing audited Monster/Paley note (05-knowledge/results/monster_levels_square_sum_7_21_20261022.md, section 7, inherited CITED structure) supplies N_M(7A)/He isomorphic to AGL(1,7), the orientation/converse group of P_7. An actual homomorphism permits inflation of the signed path representation to that normalizer, with He in the kernel. This is the correct representation-theoretic use of that bridge. It neither constructs a nontrivial action of the whole Monster nor a vertex operator algebra. Matching dimensions 7 and 21 alone would not permit inflation.

## 7. Relation to Yang-Mills / vertex algebra / simplices

The legitimate shared mechanism is projection onto invariant sectors and retention of nontrivial sectors as boundary data. For a finite-group lattice gauge model, a local group average is the same idempotent Reynolds operator; for compact groups it becomes normalized Haar integration (requiring the relevant analytic assumptions). Representation labels and intertwiner spaces organize gluing. This is a structural interface, not an equivalence between tournaments and Yang-Mills.

A tournament provides only an orientation choice on each edge. It does not by itself supply group-valued edge transports, face holonomy, a gauge-invariant action, an inner product satisfying positivity, or a scaling limit. An oriented simplex and an arbitrary tournament on its vertices are different data: cyclic triangles cannot come from a single total vertex ordering. A vertex algebra additionally requires specified fields, vacuum, translation and locality/Jacobi data. None follows from the Burnside average or from an irreducible dimension list. The new exact deliverable is the character-resolved finite symmetry interface above, suitable for testing proposed higher structures without calling those structures already constructed.
