d([x],0)=min_(b in B) |x+b|,                                  (D1)

where weight counts individual tournament edges, not Fano lines. This is exactly the shortest number of single-edge flips between the two gauge orbits. A sequence is NOT required to stay in the ground set; indeed no single-edge move connects ground fields.

## Geometric proof

The sixteen Fano line-code words are: empty, the seven pencils of three lines through one point, their seven four-line complements, and all seven lines. The normal-vector sum of a pencil is zero, so these are codewords; their distinctness and the known code dimension4 exhaust C.

For a point p, the union of the nine edges in its three pencil lines is

star(p) XOR M_p,                                              (D3)

where M_p is the near-perfect matching pairing the other six points along lines through p. The star is a gauge cut. Thus each weight-three line-code sector is represented by a THREE-EDGE MATCHING, not by a triangle or by its original nine-edge pencil.

For a matching M on six vertices, any cut delta(S) may be represented with k=|S|<=3. If r matching edges cross it, then

|M XOR delta(S)|=3+k(7-k)-2r.                                (D4)

For k=0 this is3. For k=1 it is>=7; k=2 gives>=9; k=3 gives>=9. Hence3 is the unique minimum. The maximum is15, attained exactly by three cuts: S contains the isolated point and both ends of one matching edge (k=3,r=0). Thus the complement sector has minimum21-15=6, with three minimizers. Finally the all-edge sector has weight21-|delta(S)|, minimized at k=3 to9, with binom(7,3)=35 minimizers. This proves(D2) and all asserted multiplicities without exhaustive state enumeration.

## Logical graph and kinetic boundary

The seven M_p partition the21 edges: an edge xy belongs to the unique M_p where p is the third point on its Fano line. At the logical level these are the seven odd weight-three words of C, with the eighth odd word being all seven lines. Thus the Cayley graph of C generated by the seven pencil sectors is the crown graph K_(8,8) minus a perfect matching. Its distance layers are1,7,7,1, and the edge-edit metric(D1) is exactly three times its graph distance. Translation invariance of quotient Hamming distance and(D2) show this for every pair of sectors, not only from the baseline.

This provides a concrete comparison of operation carriers: a single Fano line flip changes three particular edges and generally leaves the ground locus; a logical pencil move has a representative that flips three DISJOINT edges after a vertex gauge adjustment. They are not the same elementary kinetic operation. The fact that three individual edges suffice for a logical move is a code-distance statement. A tunneling coefficient or spectral splitting requires the specified intermediate energies and perturbation theory; none is inferred here.
