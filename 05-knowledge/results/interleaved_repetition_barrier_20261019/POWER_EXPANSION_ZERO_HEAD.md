# Endpoint realizability, repetition fuel, and a zero-head theorem

19 October 2026. **PROVED, with a CITED explicit logarithmic-form input.**
All statements concern the bank language below. General Collatz convergence,
arbitrary bank entry, and the full unbounded-episode boundary remain **OPEN**.

## Main result

Let U be any nonempty word over the expanding bank {K,V}, let k>=2, and let R
be any nonempty word over the reset bank {F,H}. Suppose the full word U^k R is
lawful at a positive integer x and its cumulative multiplier is below one.
Then its endpoint is **strictly below x**. There is no restriction on U, k,
the length or order of the reset word, or the total Terras time.

This is stronger than eventual repair on this sublanguage: positive overshoot
and exact return are both impossible at the stated endpoint. It is different
from the preceding contracting-square theorem, which assumes that the entire
contracting word repeats. Here only the initial expanding word repeats.

In particular K^L R and V^L R have zero excluded head for every L>=1. The
case L=1 follows from the inherited short-length exclusion, as detailed below.
The statement does not cover a general nonrepeated word over {K,V}, or a
later expansion episode after an explicit reset.

## 1. Inheritance and exact definitions

Use T(x)=x/2 for even x and T(x)=(3x+1)/2 for odd x. The chronological words are

    K=110110110110110101011011101010,
    F=10, H=110101000, V=K^4 F.

K,V are expansion letters; the internal F in V belongs to that expansion
letter. A lawful word of length n and h odd bits has branch (a x+b)/M, where

    a=3^h, M=2^n, b>=0.

Its unique lawful source class is x=-b a^(-1) modulo M. Composition retains
all parity guards; an integral endpoint at an integer source authenticates
the entire chronological word.

The following are inherited, with their actual scopes retained:

1. Every nonempty prefix of K,V expands. Every expansion word U has
   a>M and b/(a-M)<=C, where

       C=83829391903560839836419813590128166431
         /157489106280555890461927692448373459 < 533.

2. Every nonempty reset word is a composition of maps fixing or decreasing
   1, with individual multipliers at most 3/4 and lengths at most 9.
   In a contracting expansion-then-reset word the normalized carry e obeys
   0<e<E=C+1<534. See [the bank proof](../collatz_bank_reset_20261019.md).

3. Every lawful expansion-then-reset word of length

       N<N_*:=918644030413102796640970006656455

   and contracting multiplier already descends on every positive source.
   This is the proved [short-length obstruction](../collatz_zero_head_length_obstruction_20261019.md),
   not a numerical enumeration to N_*. Its neighboring-fraction/logarithm
   certificate is independently reproduced by the present checker.

4. A word W with data a,M,b has W^k lawful at x exactly when
   M^k divides (a-M)x+b. This is the inherited binary-fuel identity in
   [the pumping-memory note, Section 5](../collatz_guarded_pumping_memory_20261004.md).

The closest mechanism is therefore repetition fuel; the hostile is a lawful
contracting word which does overshoot because it has already descended
previously; the corrected near miss is replacing a formal infinite bank path
by a realized fixed-displacement source; and the useful sidecar is the
explicit power-gap estimate already used by the
[boundary compiler](../collatz_boundary_compiler_20261004.md).

## 2. Uniform logarithmic separation, with its external input stated

Set B=10^10. For integers 1<=h<=N and 3^h<2^N,

    1-3^h/2^N > exp[-B(1+log N)].                    (1)

**CITED input.** Matveev's positive-rational-number bound, in the form printed
as Theorem 2.1 in Languasco, Luca, Moree and Togbe,
[Sequences of integers generated by two fixed primes](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12188-025-00293-9#Sec2),
is applied to 3^h 2^(-N)-1. The numbers 3,2 are positive rationals, the
exponents h,-N are nonzero, their maximum absolute value is N, and unique
prime factorization excludes zero. Its coefficient is
1.4*30^5*2^(9/2)*log 3*log 2, less than
2*30^5*32*2 < 10^10. Thus (1) follows. This paper's displayed version, which
credits Matveev and Bugeaud--Mignotte--Siksek, was checked directly; no new
proof of the external theorem or priority for this estimate is claimed.

For any first-crossing bank word of total length N with non-descending
endpoint, write T^N(x)=s x+e. There are at most N expansion episodes, so the
inherited interleaved envelope gives e<534N. Since (1-s)x<=e, equation (1)
implies

    log x < log 534+B+(B+1)log N.                   (2)

For a single expansion-then-reset episode the stronger inequality is

    log x < log 534+B(1+log N).                     (3)

These are height bounds for hypothetical non-descending sources. They do
not assert that every source obtains a coefficient crossing.

## 3. A proper-power prefix forces exponentially large source height

Let U be an expansion-bank word of length n, with a,M,b as above, and suppose
U^k is lawful at x, k>=2. Put ell=kn. Since (a-M)x+b is a positive integer
multiple of M^k,

    x >= M^k/(a-M)-b/(a-M) > M^k/a-C.

Using a<=3^n and n<=ell/2 gives the uniform lower bound

    x > (4/3)^(ell/2)-C > exp(ell/8)-533.           (4)

The last inequality uses log(4/3)>1/4, which also follows by integrating
1/t strictly above 3/4 on [1,4/3]. No assumption that the endpoint repeats U
is used. This is a real-height consequence of the exact 2-adic fuel consumed
by the *initial* repetitions.

For ell>=8560, exp(ell/8)>1068 (already 1+ell/8>1068), so (4) yields

    log x > ell/8-1.                               (5)

The constants are deliberately loose. Their job is to join an all-length
argument to an already certified finite logarithmic bracket.

## 4. First reset crossing occurs in linearly many steps

First truncate R at the first complete reset for which the cumulative
multiplier drops below one. Let r be the number of reset letters retained.
The initial expansion multiplier is at most (3/2)^ell. Just before the
last reset it is at least one, whereas the product of the first r-1 reset
multipliers is at most (3/4)^(r-1). Hence

    (4/3)^(r-1) <= (3/2)^ell.

Because (3/2)^2<(4/3)^3, this gives r-1<3ell/2. Each reset has at most nine
bits. The expansion length is at least 60, so the first-crossing time obeys

    N < ell+9(3ell/2+1) <= 15ell.                  (6)

For completeness, every later lawful F/H reset is nonincreasing on positive
integers. Once the truncated endpoint is below x, the full endpoint is too.
Thus it suffices to prove descent at this first crossing.

## 5. Proof of the all-length zero-head theorem

Suppose that the first-crossing endpoint is not below x. Put L0=2^43.

If ell<L0, (6) gives N<15L0<N_*, contradicting the inherited short-length
theorem. The last comparison is checked as an exact integer inequality.

If ell>=L0, combine (3), (5) and N<=15ell:

    ell/8-1 < log 534+B[1+log(15ell)].              (7)

At ell=L0 the left side is 2^40-1. Since 534<2^10, 15L0<2^47 and log 2<1,
the right side is less than 10+48B. Exact arithmetic gives

    2^40-1 > 10+48B.

Moreover the derivative of the left side minus the right side of (7) is
1/8-B/ell>0 for ell>=L0, because L0>8B. Therefore (7) fails throughout
this entire range. Both cases contradict non-descent, proving the theorem.

For K^L or V^L with L>=2 apply the theorem with U=K or V. If L=1, their
expansion multiplier is less than 4/3, so the very first F or H contracts.
The truncated length is at most 131<N_*, and the same short-length theorem
applies. Arbitrarily many later reset blocks preserve the strict descent.

## 6. A quantitative restriction on the actual unbounded boundary

For d>0 let S_d have its preceding meaning: positive sources admitting a
fully guarded first-crossing bank word W with T^N(x)=x+d. No symbolic
source is added unless this endpoint equation holds.

### Theorem: logarithmic repetition budget at any realized endpoint

Suppose a realized first-crossing word begins with U^k, U in {K,V}+, k>=2,
and let ell=k|U|>=8560. Regardless of its later number of episodes,

    ell < 8[B+11+(B+1)log N].                      (8)

Indeed combine (2) and (5), then use log534<10. This includes d=0 as well,
although S_d here is defined for d>0.

In particular, if W begins with K^L, L>=2, the sharper anchor identity gives

    x >= (Q^L-C0)/D > Q^L/(2D),
    Q=2^30, C0=4912087879, D=88519643.

The strict second inequality follows from Q^L>2C0. Since log2>1/2 and
2D<2^28,

    log x >15L-28.

Consequently every such realized non-descending word satisfies

    15L < B+38+(B+1)log N,                         (9)
    N > exp[(15L-B-38)/(B+1)].                    (10)

Thus an unbounded family of endpoint realizations approaching the formal
ray K-infinity cannot have log N=o(L). It must eventually pay an exponential
completion length in its initial K repetition depth. The analogous conclusion
holds for every fixed expanding periodic bank ray by (8).

This is a restriction on *witnessed completions*, not a proof that
-c is absent from the full boundary Lambda_infinity(S_d). The latter may
still be approached by much longer interleaved completions. Such completions
are precisely the information discarded by a bare symbolic-path limit.

### An actual endpoint-sensitive exclusion, rather than a formal one

Let S_d^power be the subset defined using only words U^k R as in the main
theorem, with R consisting solely of resets. Then

    S_d^power is empty for every d>=0.

The corresponding symbolic prefix tree nevertheless contains K-infinity and
V-infinity, and its source cylinders do contain arbitrarily large positive
integers. Their exclusion from realized non-descending endpoints does not
require coefficient stopping of d-c or d-C. This is a concrete example where
endpoint filtering removes a formal boundary obligation, rather than assuming
that every formal path is realized.

## 7. Connections and the tractable remaining question

- **Binary pumping -> source height.** The map is U^k to the integer multiple
  ((a-M)x+b)/M^k. It preserves the exact guard. Passing to the multiplier alone
  loses the integrality lower bound. Equations (4)--(8) are the transfer.
- **Rational recurrence -> boundary ray.** The fixed point -b/(a-M) identifies
  the repeated 2-adic source, but its real negative value is not a positive
  endpoint realization. The endpoint equation and completion length are the
  required sidecars. The power/reset sublanguage supplies an exact exclusion.
- **Interval/carry certificates -> two-range proof.** A rational logarithm
  bracket excludes the entire short-length interval at once; the explicit
  logarithmic-form bound excludes the long range after the source-height
  lower bound is retained. Neither finite word enumeration nor floating
  Diophantine approximation supplies the universal quantifier.
- **Tournament insertion and ordered cancellation: a negative connection.**
  The [insertion-slot note](../tournament_insertion_slots_20260926.md) warns
  that deleting an inserted vertex need not preserve an actual path; the
  [ordered-context note](../collatz_context_cancellation_20261007d.md) permits
  cancellation only with a matched ordered middle word. Here a reset cannot
  simply be deleted from an interleaved bank witness: it changes both carry
  and source guard. No tournament or cancellation identity is used to promote
  the power/reset result to arbitrary interleaving.

A sharper endpoint-oriented target is now explicit: bound the shortest
realizing completion length along any surviving periodic-prefix boundary.
A bound log N=o(L) would exclude that boundary phase by (8) or (9). Such a
bound is **OPEN** for the full S_d language; it must be proved using the
endpoint divisibility, not supplied by compactness or regular-language
pumping. Even its proof would leave nonperiodic boundary paths and arbitrary
bank entry as separate obligations.
