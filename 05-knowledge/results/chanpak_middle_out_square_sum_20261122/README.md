# The 8–9 square-sum mirror and a shared-contact middle-out selector

**2026-11-22, euler.** This packet keeps two readings on one physical object. In the square-sum problem they are two ordered Collatz checkpoints along the same path. In the tournament metagraph they are two weighted cuts of the same contact set, including their signed difference and absolute midpoint.

The two graphs are not identified with each other. The square-sum result is a literal transport only on the stated sparse carriers. The tournament exact selectors decide a given source and target, but do not guarantee a jointly feasible mask at all exponents. A uniform construction must be able to re-encode the old seam or change the starting architecture, not insist on induced growth starting at exponent 3. Exact placement at exponent 61 remains open.
