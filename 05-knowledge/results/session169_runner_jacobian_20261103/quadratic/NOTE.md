# An arithmetic-optimal collision bank in the tournament guard language

**Status: proved by exact integer identities and induction, with independent finite controls.** Six actual guarded tournaments give a quadratic sampling lattice of index 64. This makes first-order collision tests complete over **every field of odd characteristic**, as well as characteristic zero. The bank attains the smallest possible nonzero integer determinant among all six-word banks, and the shortest possible maximum word length for a bank valid in every odd characteristic. This is a bridge between the earlier collision-period quotient and the arithmetic of literal guard states. It is not a Keller-map construction or a proof of a Jacobian conjecture.

## 1. The inherited interface

The [earlier quadratic-sidecar theorem](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/6b24421039fd2ffa820e9b56d107e458d75498ed/05-knowledge/results/planar_jc48_sep06_collision.md) identifies the obstruction left by scalar tangent-relation periods in a three-dimensional target: the homogeneous quadrics vanishing on the tangent directions. [S166's physical interpolation banks](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/2374ba81efc7ad585d723015630b546a3cf01ff1/05-knowledge/results/session166_planar_metagraph_20261103/collision_banks/NOTE.md) supply physically realized directions with optimal characteristic-zero test counts and word lengths. Its quadratic determinant is 192, so the chosen bank fails modulo 3. S166 explicitly leaves modular arithmetic separate.

We retain exactly its guarded-tournament semantics. For a strong tournament X, a marked vertex q with unique in-neighbor r, put

    v=(s,u,d),
    s = Hamilton paths of X starting at q,
    u = Hamilton paths of X not starting at q,
    d = Hamilton paths of X-q starting at r.

The directed-triangle seed has v0=(1,2,0). Add a vertex a with q→a and a→X-q; C moves the guard to a and D keeps q. These are literal strong-tournament extensions, with

    C(s,u,d)=(u,s+u+d,s),
    D(s,u,d)=(s+d,2u,0).

Words are temporal: DCC means apply D, then C, then C. A word of length k produces k+3 vertices. The map to affine-space branch tangents retains these three counts and physical realizability of the indexing tournament, but not its individual paths or isomorphism type. Affine-space branch motions are not asserted to be tournament deformations.

## 2. Six physical states generate the entire integral quadratic sampling lattice

Use the row of degree-two monomials

    φ(s,u,d)=(s²,u²,d²,su,sd,ud).

Let L be the subgroup of Z⁶ generated by φ(v(w)) for **all** finite C/D words w. Define the six-word bank

    W = (empty, C, D, CC, CCC, DCC),

whose states are

    (1,2,0), (2,3,1), (1,4,0),
    (3,6,2), (6,11,3), (5,10,4).

Its evaluation matrix, with rows in that order, is

    E = [[ 1,  4, 0, 2, 0, 0],
         [ 4,  9, 1, 6, 2, 3],
         [ 1, 16, 0, 4, 0, 0],
         [ 9, 36, 4,18, 6,12],
         [36,121, 9,66,18,33],
         [25,100,16,50,20,40]].

**Integral sampling theorem.** The six rows of E form a Z-basis of L. Moreover

    det E=64,
    Z⁶/L ≅ Z/2 ⊕ Z/2 ⊕ Z/2 ⊕ Z/8.

Thus every quadratic evaluation on every C/D state is an **integer linear combination** of its evaluations on these six states. This remains true for polynomial coefficients in any abelian group, with no division involved in passing from the bank values to other word values.

           [0,2,0,1,1,1],

The following integer matrices satisfy E T_A = B_A E:

           [ 9,  0, 0,   0,  0,  0],

           [ 2,-24, 5, -36,  8,  6],
           [ 0,-84,18,-126, 28, 21],
           [36,-48, 9, -72, 16, 12]].

Let L_E be the row lattice of E. These two identities make L_E stable under both quadratic transitions. It contains the seed row, so induction on word length puts every reachable evaluation row in L_E. Conversely each row of E is itself reachable, so L_E⊆L. Hence L=L_E. The determinant proves the index. The gcds of the k×k minors, for k=1,...,6, are

    1, 1, 2, 4, 8, 64.

Their successive ratios give Smith factors (1,1,2,2,2,8), proving the displayed quotient. These are exact finite matrix certificates, not an inference from a bounded word census.

For explicit propagation, begin with c_empty=(1,0,0,0,0,0) and set c_(wA)=c_w B_A. Then

    φ(v(w))=c_w E,
    P(v(w))=c_w (P(v(w_i)))_i

for every homogeneous quadratic P. The c_w may have negative entries; they are arithmetic combinations of observations, not additional physical operations.

### Determinant optimality over all words

For any six physical words, their evaluation matrix F has the form F=M E for an integer 6×6 matrix M. Therefore det F is divisible by 64. If F is nonsingular, |det F|≥64, and W attains equality. This is a global all-word optimality result, not just an exhaustive search through a chosen radius.

It also separates two issues: E is invertible over Z[1/2], but not over Z. Arbitrary six integer observations need not come from an integer-coefficient quadratic. Every genuine quadratic's further observations are nevertheless determined by integer combinations of the six bank observations.

## 3. Uniform odd-characteristic completeness, with sharp word radius

**Corollary.** Over every field of characteristic zero or odd characteristic, evaluation on W is an isomorphism on the six-dimensional space of homogeneous quadrics. The same holds over every commutative ring in which 2 is a unit. The six indexing tournaments have at most six vertices.

This follows immediately from det E=64. Six scalar evaluations are necessary by dimension over a field.

**Universal radius theorem.** Maximum word length three is minimal for a physical bank that works over every odd-characteristic field, even if more than six test states are allowed.

For the lower bound, reduce the entire radius-two word ball modulo 3. The distinct nonzero rows come from empty, C, D, CC, DC; DD repeats empty and CD becomes zero. The nonzero quadratic

    q(s,u,d)=s²-u²+sd+ud

vanishes on all seven radius-two states. Thus no bank confined to radius two can work modulo 3. The displayed W has radius three and attains the bound. This is a modular radius lower bound, different from S166's characteristic-zero optimum of two.

At characteristic two the obstruction is intrinsic to the seed language. Its entire orbit consists of

    (1,0,0), (0,1,1).

C swaps them and D sends both to (1,0,0). In particular u=d and every quadratic sampling matrix has rank at most two; no increase in word length repairs it. This is not a claim about all physically realizable guarded tournaments with arbitrary seeds.

Nor should the quadratic odd-prime result be extrapolated to arbitrary degree. In characteristic 3 the nonzero homogeneous polynomial

    s³u-su³

of degree four vanishes on every integer state reduced modulo 3. Multiplication by s^(δ-4) gives the same obstruction in every degree δ≥4. Hence no all-odd-characteristic scalar interpolation bank of integer states exists in those degrees, even if the entire C/D language is sampled. This argument leaves degree three open here.

## 4. The collision quotient works without dividing by two

The inherited collision theorem can be formulated over **any** field k, not only characteristic zero. Let V be three-dimensional, and let nonzero t_i span V. Put

    N=⊕_i V/k t_i,
    R={λ : Σ_i λ_i t_i=0},
    P(n)(λ)=Σ_i λ_i(t_i∧n_i).

The common-motion map is δ:V→N, w↦([w])_i. It is injective, since spanning tangents cannot share a common one-dimensional intersection. After choosing a volume form there is an exact sequence

    0 → V → ker P → I₂(t) → 0,

where I₂(t) is the vector space of homogeneous quadrics vanishing on all t_i.

**Proof without polarization denominators.** If periods vanish, t_i↦t_i∧n_i extends uniquely to A:V→∧²V. Let b(u,v)=vol(u∧A(v)) and q(v)=b(v,v). Then q(t_i)=0. If q is the zero polynomial, b is alternating, in every characteristic, and in dimension three it uniquely has the form b(u,v)=vol(u∧v∧w). Thus n_i-w is tangent, exactly the common-motion condition. Conversely every homogeneous quadratic is the diagonal of some bilinear form: assign its square coefficients to the diagonal entries and its mixed coefficients to one triangular half. No division by two is needed. If q(t_i)=0, the corresponding A(t_i) belongs to t_i∧V, so suitable n_i exist. This proves surjectivity and exactness. QED.

For the six tangents of W, the first three have determinant -2. Over every odd-characteristic field, the following three rows form a basis of R:

    (2,-2,-1,1,0,0),
    (1,-3,-1,0,1,0),
    (5,-4,-2,0,0,1).

Consequently the nine scalar wedge-coordinate tests supplied by these three rows are complete for common motion, after independent branch reparametrizations. On the original eighteen velocity coordinates their map has rank nine. The kernel is exactly three common-motion dimensions plus six independent tangential directions.

This applies to the actual affine-space germs

    f_i(z,ε)=z t_i+ε n_i, ε²=0.

It does not assert that the germs are physical tournament deformations or branches of a planar polynomial map. In characteristic two the displayed bank does not span V and is not a counterexample to the theorem's stated hypotheses.

## 5. An exact arithmetic determinant for every S166 homogeneous bank

There is also an all-degree description of exactly which primes spoil S166's particular radius-optimal bank. For δ≥0 let

    H_δ={D^j C^k : j,k≥0, j+k≤δ},
    v_(k,j)=C^k(1,2^(j+1),0).

Let Δ_δ be the absolute determinant of homogeneous degree-δ monomial evaluation on these binomial(δ+2,2) nodes. Define

    g_n(t) = third coordinate of C^n(1,t,0),
    g_0(t)=0, g_1(t)=1, g_2(t)=t,
    g_(n+3)=g_(n+2)+g_(n+1)+g_n.

Then

    Δ_δ = product_(0≤i<j≤δ) (2^(j+1)-2^(i+1))^(δ-j+1)
           × product_(1≤n≤δ, 0≤j≤δ-n)
                g_n(2^(j+1))^(δ-n-j+1).                 (1)

Empty products are one. Formula (1) is an exact integer determinant, not merely a sufficient list of bad primes. The first cases are

    Δ_1=2,
    Δ_2=2⁶·3,
    Δ_3=2¹⁹·3⁴·7,
    Δ_4=2⁴⁶·3¹⁰·5²·7³.

### Integral Newton-basis proof

Write (x_k,y_k,ℓ_k)=C^(-k)(s,u,d). Since det C=1, each is an integral unimodular coordinate system. For k=0,...,δ and j=0,...,δ-k define

    N_(k,j)=(product_(h<k)ℓ_h) x_k^(δ-k-j)y_k^j.

These form a Z-basis of the degree-δ homogeneous polynomial lattice. To prove it, restrict a polynomial to ℓ_0=0 and subtract its unique binary part in x_0,y_0. The remainder is integrally divisible by ℓ_0. Repeat on ℓ_1=0 in its unimodular coordinates, and continue. This gives integral decomposition and uniqueness, so the change from the monomial basis has determinant ±1.

At v_(h,i), N_(k,j)=0 if h<k. Thus evaluation is block lower triangular. The k-th diagonal block is a Vandermonde matrix in t_i=2^(i+1), multiplied rowwise by

    product_(a<k)ℓ_a(C^k(1,t_i,0))
      = product_(n=1)^k g_n(t_i).

Multiplying these block determinants and collecting repetitions yields (1). Positivity of every g_n(t), n≥1,t>0, makes its absolute value positive. QED.

For an odd prime p, this chosen bank is valid modulo p exactly when both conditions hold:

1. The multiplicative order of 2 modulo p is larger than δ.
2. Every g_n(2^(j+1)) is nonzero modulo p for 1≤n≤δ and 0≤j≤δ-n.

These are two different failure modes: repeated slopes on a reset plane, and a selected point meeting an earlier reset plane. They are failures of this sampling arrangement, not automatically failures of the entire physical language. Degree two modulo 3 illustrates the distinction: H₂ fails, while W is complete.

## 6. Reproduction and scope

Run `python3 check.py` and `python3 -O check.py`. The standard-library verifier uses always-active checks and produces byte-identical JSON. It independently counts Hamilton paths on all six literal tournament witnesses; checks the transition identities, determinant and all determinantal divisors; checks the collision ranks over Q and selected odd primes; verifies the radius-two hostile modulo 3 and the complete characteristic-two orbit; and compares the all-degree determinant product with exact Bareiss determinants through degree eight. Finite all-word controls additionally test the arithmetic propagation, but the two closure identities and induction prove the unbounded-word theorem.

No literature-priority claim is made. The inherited theorem supplies the collision interpretation and S166 supplies the physical guard language. The new result is the exact all-word integral quadratic observation lattice and its optimal six-state basis, together with its modular collision interpretation and the all-degree determinant factorization. No conclusion is drawn about the Jacobian conjecture, all guarded-tournament realizability, positive-word reachability beyond this language, or universal interpolation in higher degree.
