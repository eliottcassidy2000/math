## Universal proof, including the exact failure boundary

Use the six states (p,r)=(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,1), where p records empty P, a root-inneighbor endpoint, or a root-outneighbor endpoint. The second coordinate records whether R is already nonempty and starts at a root-outneighbor. A local nonempty ordered path cover has category (a,b) in {0,1,2} squared excluding (0,0). Here b=1 means R starts at a root-outneighbor and b=2 means it starts at a root-inneighbor. An old state with r=0 rejects b=2; otherwise update to (a if a is nonzero else p, bool(r or b)).

Let W=span(e1-e5,e3-e5), V=W+span(e2-e4), u=e3-e5, and rho=(1,0,1,0,1,0).

**PROVED:** Every nonempty block matrix M sends V into W, acts as H times identity on W, and satisfies rho M=H rho. Indeed, on W the covers with P nonempty forget p and cancel, while those with P empty contribute H times the original vector. On e2-e4 the P-nonempty covers again cancel. For P empty, rejection of b=2 leaves h_out times (e3-e5). The rho identity counts exactly the covers with R empty, of which there are H, for each old r=0 state; no old r=1 state contributes. These are statements about arbitrary nonempty blocks, not only singletons or binary-compatible blocks.

For two transfers with the same H,Q,alpha, their signed category difference has the form

(0,1):x; (0,2):-x; (1,0):y; (2,0):-y;
(1,1):a; (1,2):b; (2,1):-x-a; (2,2):x-b.

Here x is the change in full-block Hamilton paths starting at a root-outneighbor, and y the change in full-block Hamilton paths ending at a root-inneighbor. The category rule directly gives F(image) subset V, F(W)=0, F(e2-e4)=x u, and the e2 coordinate of Fz is y rho(z). Consequently, for ordered defects,

Three or more selected defects also vanish, including fully adjacent triples: two reach W, and the next defect kills W. Empty intervening products do NOT suffice; the rank-one product above can be nonzero.

It follows that the exact multi-affine scalar expansion of ell M_last ... M_first v has only constant, linear, and adjacent quadratic terms. Here v=e0 and ell=(1,1,1,1,0,0). At adjacent indices i,i+1 the coefficient is

## Carry theorem and coprime rigidity

Fix length t>=1 and common positive H,Q with Q>H. For physical nonempty tournament blocks Q>=2H and each alpha digit lies in [0,Q-H]: Q-H counts all ordered covers with R nonempty, of which alpha selects those whose first R vertex is a root-outneighbor.

Let g=gcd(H,Q), H=g h, Q=g q, and delta_i=alpha'_i-alpha_i. Equality of U for two ordered words is equivalent to

sum_(i=0)^(t-1) delta_i h^i q^(t-1-i)=0.

**PROVED:** This holds if and only if there are integer carries c_-1=c_(t-1)=0 with

delta_i=h c_i-q c_(i-1), and |c_i|<=g-1.

For necessity, reduce the weighted equality modulo h: coprimality of h,q implies h divides delta0. Set c0=delta0/h, divide by h, and repeat. This gives the integral recurrence c_i=(delta_i+q c_(i-1))/h and the final zero carry. For any i<t-1, the remaining suffix gives

c_i=-sum_(j=i+1)^(t-1) delta_j h^(j-i-1)/q^(j-i).

Because |delta_j|<=g(q-h), its absolute value is at most g*(1-(h/q)^(t-i-1))<g. Integrality gives the claimed bound. Sufficiency follows by substituting the recurrence into the weighted sum and telescoping. The bound is a necessary consequence of the physical digit interval, not an additional arbitrary restriction on equalities.

If g=1, all carries are zero and hence the ordered alpha words are identical. Equivalently, modulo-Q induction isolates the final alpha digit because each difference has absolute value less than Q. This is uniqueness of alpha digits, NOT uniqueness of root masks, rooted tournaments, or D. Masks24/38 already demonstrate that those stronger statements are false.

The checker exhausts all digit words of lengths1..4, H=1..4, 2H<=Q<=10, comparing each colliding word to a canonical representative in its fiber. It verifies 13,454 equalities and their integer bounded carries. The simplest recorded arithmetic hostile is H=2,Q=4, words(0,2)/(1,0), carry(1,0). The physical digit bound matters: for coprime H=19,Q=256, unrestricted words(19,0)/(0,256) collide, but digit256 lies outside [0,237].

## Additional audit of the final producer proof: divisibility and scalar entropy

**PROVED, specialized inherited divisibility:** for t repeated modules and g=gcd(H,Q), D is divisible by g^max(t-2,0), with or without singleton flanks. In a rooted Hamilton path P,r,R, mark the terminal block of P and initial block of R when these paths are nonempty. After fixing these at most two exceptional blocks and their constrained local covers, blocks to the left of the overlap are entirely P, blocks to the right entirely R, and blocks strictly inside the overlap have arbitrary ordered two-path covers. Their factors are H or Q. Impossible endpoint orders contribute zero. At least t-2 repeated blocks remain nonexceptional. Flanks may replace exceptional repeated blocks but do not reduce this lower bound. Thus each summand, and then the total, has the required divisibility. This proof requires forward interblock orientation; it is not a statement about arbitrary module insertions.

**PROVED, scalar entropy barrier:** with the strong flanks, each ordered two-path cover (L,R) of the middle deletion graph supplies the rooted Hamilton path L,q,r,p,R. Empty L or R is allowed.
