## Independent algorithm

The self-contained `audit.py` imports no producer, repository, sibling, or third-party module and reads no input. It derives full C3 profile coefficients directly from the quotient-walk series. Starting from each of three possible initial block labels, it generates every nonempty cyclic word of length≤13. Integer polynomial convolution forms W^d. For each positive content c, it multiplies [x^c]W^d by ∏c_i! and divides by d!, checking exact divisibility. Contracting this kernel with the three unordered path-cover profiles gives every output path-cover coordinate.

This is a different implementation from the producer's Gregory–Newton transform. Seed profiles come from endpoint subset Hamilton DP followed by unordered set-partition recursion. All64 triples of distinct physical seed profiles of orders≤3 are checked against literal full path-cover counts, not merely H. Ordered joins use pointwise Q multiplication and finite-difference reconstruction.

The full C3 kernel is symmetric in the three contents: its walk series is (x+y+z+xy+yz+zx+3xyz)/(1−xyz). Therefore sorting the three child orders does not lose any profile or scalar. The independent implementation retains all profile choices within each order rather than copying the producer's profile-order symmetry pruning.

All old and arbitrary ternary profiles of orders≤13 and H≤4095 are generated by increasing order, with minimum ternary nesting depth. Multiplicative closure of their H values handles arbitrarily long exterior joins. The all-order cap and completeness proof were audited in the first report. The implemented seed banks here have orders≤6, within the cap. This is exact membership only for the named grammar/seed bank, not all tournaments.
