# Session229 synthesis: what the combined constructions now achieve

**Chan–Pak completeness remains OPEN.** The target is every positive odd Hamilton-path count other than7 and21. This session supplies new physical repair interfaces, explicit strong families, exact recursive-language extensions, and rigorously scoped barriers. It does not supply an unrestricted coverage interval or all-target existence proof.

## 1. The most useful positive advance: repair the actual endpoint interface

Session228's H19 fibers retained the one-sided cover coordinate A. The new adjacent-defect calculus shows exactly how this freedom propagates, but the older arbitrary binary prefix/suffix construction also sees G,S,E. Changing A alone was therefore not the right repair interface.

Holding (H,Q,A,G,S,E) fixed and varying only the two-boundary coordinate T removes every context-dependent term. This produces a gap of exactly two under EVERY common legal binary prefix/suffix, with no bounded-padding assumption. A five-vertex core supplies a pair; two six-vertex core topologies supply a triple. The latter gives, for every b≥0, literal strong counts

    258·2^b+21, 258·2^b+23, 258·2^b+25.

These particular triples are explicit constructive families, not all odd triples. Complete labeled tournament/cut enumeration through six vertices proves the small-interface maxima1,2,3 through orders4,5,6, and does not bound larger cores.

The first pair's core is arithmetically saturated. Searching without symmetry then produces unsaturated H95/Q1276 and H389/Q5600 cores with the same full-interface gap-two property. Their star closures begin with681/683 and5267/5269. The H95 core yields strong adjacent-count pairs at every order≥9 through legal singleton padding.

**Keep the object carrying gcd one explicit.** It is the deletion core, not necessarily the final star closure. The first H95 closure has (H,Q)=(681,15600), gcd3; the corresponding H389 closure has (5269,111760), gcd11. Neither invalidates the unit-gap construction. The proof uses core unsaturation only where stated.

A different rigid H29/Q320 block supplies four distinct equal-A transfers. Concatenation gives4^k distinct full transfers at a common A with coprime H,Q. That statement concerns transfer information, not4^k distinct scalar values in every closure.

## 2. Why these repairs do not yet give arbitrarily long intervals

The full-interface T defect is a central rank-one operator whose square is zero. Any ordinary block context multiplies it by that context's Hamilton count. Hence k identical unit switches give only k+1 equally spaced scalar values, with spacing2H^(k−1), rather than2^k independently selectable unit increments. Singleton contexts preserve the unit gap because their Hamilton count is one.

The exact extension experiments test the cheapest proposed escape. All one-vertex extensions of the two H45 triple cores give no unsaturated gap-two interface. For the two unsaturated unit-switch cores, all384 one-vertex extensions are examined; after retaining the240 with gcd(H,Q)=1, no retained interface has a triple. Within that unsaturated extension subfamily, every surviving unequal-T fiber comes only from adding a source or sink. These are complete statements only for the named extension universes; they are not general obstructions to eight/nine-vertex cores or multi-vertex surgery.

Thus the next constructive question is precise: produce a growing fixed-interface T interval without the product scaling, or prove that fixed-width repair windows from varying contexts overlap every desired target. Neither follows from the present pairs/triples.

## 3. Adjacent interactions and carries are now exact

Every nonempty intervening deletion block, not only a singleton separator, maps the defect space into a subspace killed by the next equal-alpha defect. Therefore only adjacent quadratic interactions survive; all terms involving three or more defects vanish. A literal H19 mask pair has nonzero adjacent interaction20, while the original pair has none.

For a repeated block H=g h,Q=g q, equal-U alpha words are exactly related by bounded integral carries

    delta_i=h c_i−q c_(i−1),  |c_i|≤g−1,

with zero boundary carries. When g=1 the alpha word is rigid, though equal-alpha masks remain free. When g>1 carries can occur, but the inherited SCC divisibility forces g^max(t−2,0) into every scalar of t repeated modules. Explicit strong H9/Q84 carry witnesses have counts13149 and16719 at the same sidecar.

A separate path-cover injection gives D≥product Q_i for arbitrary masks between the fixed strong flanks. Consequently the ENTIRE all-mask H19 repeated-template family has O(X^(3/4)) values≤X. Even mixing the explicit H19 and H9 blocks remains O(X^(15/16)). These negative results encompass far more choices than one sparse binary fiber, but they leave variable singleton runs, richer banks, changing templates, and nonordinal operations available.

## 4. Recursive arity and physical inverse selection are different missing pieces

Allowing all three C3 blocks to vary preserves the sharp node bound H≥2^(n−2)+1. The complete target compiler therefore extends without worsening its order cap. Below4096, the old language has577 counts, arbitrary ternary closure has620, and adding the inherited H19 seed gives660. Independent polynomial-transform and quotient-walk implementations agree on the full profiles and minimum-depth distributions.

The operation C3[TT2,TT2,A] has H=4Q_A(3)+5Q_A(2)−11H(A); at A=TT3 it gives137, absent from the old arbitrary-depth language. This is a genuine grammar escape, not a newly discovered tournament count. Even the enlarged language still misses29 at EVERY depth by a structural size/value argument. The general cycle-certified finite-template compiler is exact but does not imply any finite template bank is complete.

For arbitrary proper grafts, the source root must be a nonsink. The all-gadget scalar inverse is now both finite and quantitatively decreasing in BOTH input Hamilton counts. For target N, gadget H,Q and source D,E,U,d satisfy

    H≤N/3,
    D≤floor((2N−2Q+3H)/(3H))<N,
    U≤floor(N/H)−1,
    gadget order≤floor(log2(N−1)).

The inherited physical four-state oracle then decides each candidate without assuming cone feasibility or matrix invertibility. Prime targets permit reachable-root normalization. A small complete image through31 validates this unrestricted-gadget inverse, not a new global cutoff.

**The remaining logical gap is existence:** prove that every admissible target has a physically realizable inverse branch carrying the required full state. Scalar descent alone cannot replace one source graph by any other graph with the same Hamilton count.

## 5. Provenance and research-method boundary

The session combines Sessions225–228 with the older Session216 arbitrary binary core formula, Session217 reversal/run corrections, inherited path-cover substitution theorems, the nonsink endpoint bound, and the cited Busch floor where explicitly needed. No general substitution theorem, known finite spectrum cutoff, or literature priority is reclaimed.

The useful research move was to turn a failed one-sided fiber into a complete endpoint interface, then search separately for arithmetic unsaturation. That produced actual universal repair families. The failed move was trying to iterate a central unit defect without accounting for its context multiplier. The exact inverse, template compiler, and entropy bounds identify where a future proof must add a new ingredient rather than repeat the same fixed menu.
