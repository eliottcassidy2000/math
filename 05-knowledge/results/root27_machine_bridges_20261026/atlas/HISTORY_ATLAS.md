## The fifteen primes and the genuine 4+5+6 decomposition

Let pi(G) mean the prime divisors of a finite group's order. The recovered
[October22 audited packet](../../monster_levels_square_sum_7_21_20261022.md)
identifies

```
pi(M) minus pi(B)       = {29,41,59,71};
pi(B) minus pi(Fi22)    = {17,19,23,31,47};
pi(Fi22)               = {2,3,5,7,11,13}.
```

These are actual prime sets, not just a partition of fifteen objects.
[Aricheta, SIGMA15 (2019), Theorem1.1 and AppendixA](https://www.emis.de/journals/SIGMA/2019/007/sigma19-007.pdf)
provide the arithmetic carrier: for p not dividing the level,
`Q_p(X)/2 = genus(X^p)-genus(X)`, where Q_p counts supersingular points
not rational over F_p. The levels1,2,6 give the three nested lists after
adjoining the primes of their levels. The
[Monster](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/M) and
[Baby Monster](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/B) orders agree
with the first two group prime sets; the Fi22 comparison is inherited
from the audited repository computation.

The useful mechanism is **an exact defect under adding level structure**.
Its missing-rationality count is twice a genus increase. A possible
Collatz analogue would need a proved defect identity for a precision
extension at the same arithmetic source. Neither genus-zero counts nor
the numbers4,5,6 supply that identity.

Two corrections are load-bearing. These are Ogg/moonshine primes, not
“all primes at which some elliptic curve is supersingular.” Supersingularity
does not annihilate all prime-to-p torsion. Also, the Monster centralizer
is `2.B`, not B itself: `|M|/|B|` is not the subgroup index `[M:2.B]`.
The latter is half the former. Both distinctions were lost in older
informal analogies and are preserved in the audited level packet.

## Negative controls that change the next construction

The old March claim identifying the full icosahedral symmetry group with
S5 fails: the former is A5 x C2 and has nontrivial centre, while S5 has
trivial centre. Equal order120 is insufficient. Likewise the six vertices
of L(K4) are edges of a tetrahedron, whereas the polar of its64-vertex
orientation cube has twelve vertices. The corrected
[fivefold boundary packet](../../fivefold_boundary_20261018g.md) retains
these as different carriers.

For the supplied sequence, a cubic interpolant through6,16,34,64 predicts
110 as the next term; the actual next term is114. Its exact affine branch
law eliminates this overfitting immediately. The coincidence of114 with
another Bernoulli denominator42 index, or564 with one of the Lehmer aliquot
seeds, has no known operation-preserving map. These are explicitly
**ANALOGY / NO TRANSFER ESTABLISHED**.

The notation “the first five Bernoulli numbers are prime” is not a usable
arithmetic statement: Bernoulli numbers are rational, with B1=-1/2 in the
standard convention. If the intended objects are the first five Fermat
numbers, their primality and constructible polygons have an exact
Gauss–Wantzel connection, but it is a different sequence. The B1 exception
can motivate a boundary correction; it does not identify that correction
with a missing Collatz rank.

## Three next constructions, with stopping rules

1. **Root-preserving excursion compression.** Store an affine word, its
   guard cylinder, exact source root and clock. Merge two words only when
   their required continuation and original threshold agree. The cheap
   rejection is the three14-step controls in README. A state-count decrease
   without the same literal target is not a success.
2. **Defect under refinement.** Seek an arithmetic analogue of the modular
   genus-difference identity: a nonnegative or signed exact quantity whose
   change measures the newly unresolved precision. Its type must include
   the same source; merely observing a 4/5/6 split does not define it.
3. **Prime-flow precision budget.** Use equation(3) as a schema for retaining
   valuation contributions separately. This is an architecture for a proof
   obligation, not an asserted conservation law for Collatz. The first test
   is an actual reset that regenerates precision and changes the affine
   intercept; all terms must be recomputed rather than charged to a fresh
   source.

These constructions leave Collatz, Sloane's general conjecture, and full
black-core connectivity open. The useful unification is a shared demand
for an exact operation plus its source/height sidecar, not a claimed
equivalence between famous open problems.
