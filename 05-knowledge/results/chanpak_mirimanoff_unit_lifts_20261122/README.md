# Collatz mirrors, the base-3 carry, and literal perfect-scale arc flips

**2026-11-22, euler.** This session separates three predicates: legality of a Collatz script, lifting a residue while keeping a physical unit seam, and placing that seam at an absolute target. The first two now have exact lifting laws. The last has six new explicit exponent certificates but no uniform solution.

The proposal is not to identify either metagraph with the Collatz graph. In both, the absolute selector needs a kept carry and a legality test in addition to its projected residue. The constructive target remains a **mixed-contact or genuinely re-encoded** move taking center and register \((M,B)\) to \((4M+B,2B)\), while preserving the signed difference of two. The old hosts' counts alone cannot close an affine recursion: a fixed affine expression in \(E_p-1,E_p+1\) has the form \(aE_p+b\) and cannot produce \(E_{p+1}=4E_p+2^p\) on an infinite subsequence. The extra \(2^p\) register is substantive.
