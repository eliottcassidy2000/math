# A fifteenth complete neighborhood in the degree-57 Moore chart

**Finite construction.** The partial graph now has **13,284 edges, 241 vertices of degree 57, and girth five** on the prescribed 3,250 vertices. The new complete site is **(1,1)**, with marker **(1,1)–(2,7)** retained. All fourteen earlier complete sites stay complete at their original addresses. Their contacts, and some of the three center matchings, can move. This is a new chart, not a completion sitting on top of all the old frozen edges.

This advances the [fourteen-site fixture](../moore57_quadratic_tensors_20261125/PLANTED.md), the [two-hole obstruction](../moore57_quadratic_tensors_20261125/55-13-33-SYNCHRONIZED-REPAIRS.md), and the [1,741-contact exact-cover board](../moore57_quadratic_tensors_20261125/HADWIGER-NELSON-PHYSICAL-SLOTS.md). The distinction is that we preserve *who sees which three physical routes*, without requiring every old target to keep its branch address.

## Factor the search into routes and branch labels

In the rooted chart call 56, 0 and 28 the centers. An exterior slot (h,y) carries a triple (y,p_h(y),q_h(y)) of literal center contacts. There are 3,024 distinct triples, and each branch is a parallel class: every coordinate is used once. At source (1,1), after the marker, choose 52 distinct triples covering the three unused coordinate sets; for this first step we do **not** require different branch labels. The recorded selection has 38 singly occupied, seven doubly occupied and seven empty eligible branches. The eligible triples were taken from the actual marked board, so no target triple of an old complete neighborhood is reused.

Here is the exact two-branch move. For branches h,k with p- and q-permutations, let
\[
\alpha=p_k^{-1}p_h,\qquad \beta=q_k^{-1}q_h
\]
on sheet indices. Any reassignment confined to these two branches which still has one triple per first coordinate in each branch must either swap both triples at a given sheet y, or leave both fixed. Swapping on a sheet set U preserves the second projection if and only if \(p_h(U)=p_k(U)\), and likewise for q. Equivalently, U is a union of orbits of the group generated by \(\alpha,\beta\). This characterizes all two-branch exchanges of these triples, not just some sufficient trades.

Now mark each old complete neighborhood by its source. The two branches each contain one chosen triple per old source. For a proposed orbit swap, record which old sources have their selected triple in the orbit in each branch. **The two source sets must agree.** This is exactly the condition that the old stars still use both branch labels once: either their two targets both swap, or neither does. The swap changes none of the physical routes incident with an old source. In this witness branches 1 and 2 are never traded, so the source addresses and marker remain fixed.

We searched three-coordinate covers near the previous two-hole references, then walked among these marked orbit switches to eliminate branch multiplicities. One can move an excess into an empty branch, shift which branch is empty, or rearrange a balanced intermediate pair. The greedy selection policy is only a discovery heuristic; the final certificate is independent of search order or stopping rules. In the switch log, a full 56-sheet swap is merely a permutation of the names of two branches. Carrying those permutations through the subsequent switch indices and dropping them gives a normalized **14-trade** witness: three orbits of size 14, nine of size 28, and two of size 42.

The normalized log changes 1,162 center edges and 161 of the 742 earlier exterior edges, with the corresponding replacement edges. The new site adds 53 exterior edges including the unchanged marker. Each of the fifteen source sites sends its neighbors' three center-contact coordinates onto all the required slots, exactly once including its pre-existing routes. All 795 exterior targets are distinct across the fifteen source lists.

The replay independently checks every branch matching and every path of length up to two in the resulting graph. There is a five-cycle through the root and any filled matching; the replay excludes triangles and squares. The degree distribution is:

The three center-pair route groups still generate \(S_{56}\), checked via the [full-cycle and isolated three-cycle certificates](../moore57_quadratic_tensors_20261125/THREE-STARS.md). These transport groups are not graph automorphism groups.

The [whole-chart Hall and radius screen](../moore57_quadratic_tensors_20261125/FROZEN-SPECTRUM-AND-ANCHORED-ACTION.md) passes: **3,595,449** optional edges, no failed matching, no candidate deleted by matching support, and **676,068** uncovered demands between a complete vertex and another leaf, each with at least one permissible final contact. Unlike the old 51-contact near-patch, the new chart survives these checks. This does **not** show that those contacts can be placed simultaneously. Forty-one sites of branch 1 are still incomplete; most exterior matchings are unfilled.
