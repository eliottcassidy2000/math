# The universal induced aliquot inverse already fails at 35

The [positive twin fork](README.md) is not a general existence theorem. At prime p=5, the aliquot predecessor of 7p=35 is 13, but **no tournament with 35 Hamiltonian paths contains any induced subtournament with 13 Hamiltonian paths**, even allowing arbitrarily many vertices in the extension.

Indeed, Hamiltonian counts multiply over ordered strong components. Since 35=5·7 and an H=7 tournament does not exist, every 35-witness has a single nontrivial component C carrying all 35 paths. All other components are singletons: any nontrivial strongly connected tournament has at least three Hamiltonian paths. By [Busch's lower bound](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v13i1n3), the strong component C has order at most seven, because 5^(7/3)>35. Singleton padding neither creates a factor in H(C) nor changes the count of an induced subset of C when padded singletons are included in it. We may thus check order seven, padding C to that order if necessary.

The standalone `inverse_obstruction.cpp` uses each of the 2^21 labelled tournaments exactly once. Its subset/end recurrence initializes one singleton path per end and extends along outgoing arcs to an unused vertex. Of the 10,080 labelled order-seven H=35 witnesses, **zero** have an induced subset of count 13. This finite proof applies to all sizes by the preceding bound, not a conjectured absence in higher layers.

Consequently the hoped-for induction “take any smaller aliquot count p+8 and extend it while preserving its whole tournament induced” cannot be valid for all prime p. This does not refute Chan–Pak: 35 itself occurs. It leaves an exception-and-restart induction, a different smaller target, or an operation that rewires old arcs as distinct possibilities. There is a sharp repair for this particular pair: take the H=13 rows (24,9,3,4,14,31), reverse old arc 0↔4 and append a new vertex with outgoing mask 39 on the six old vertices. This gives rows (8,9,3,68,79,31,39) of count 35 by direct subset DP. Thus one old reversal suffices when coupled to one added vertex; zero reversals cannot suffice. There is a separate source obstruction at p=13: its proposed ancestor p+8=21 is itself one of the forbidden counts.

Replay: `g++ -O3 inverse_obstruction.cpp -o obstruction && ./obstruction`. The previously exhibited induced returns 1057→7343 and 1059→7357 still hold.
