# Bounded primary-source provenance check

Checked 2026-10-27. This is a self-contained finite-ring proof closing the
repository's bounded gap, **not a claim of a historically new coding theorem**.

[Sidorov and Vershik, *Bijective arithmetic codings of hyperbolic
automorphisms of the 2-torus, and binary quadratic forms* (1998),
arXiv:math/9810085](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9810085), Proposition 1.3 and
Remark 1, PDF pp.9–10, discuss the symbolic identifications; for the golden
case their zero sequence is identified with the two alternating sequences.
The Fibonacci example on PDF p.11 also shows that normalization matters:
one natural homoclinic coding is five-to-one almost everywhere, while a
rescaled choice is bijective almost everywhere. These are direct prior
structural matches, not a pointwise substitute for the finite-ring proof
in RESULT.md. The exact identification with its fixed-period ideal quotient
is proved here rather than inferred from an almost-everywhere statement.

[Sidorov, *Bijective and general arithmetic codings for Pisot automorphisms
of the torus* (2000), arXiv:math/0006159](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0006159)
provides the broader Pisot-coding context. No classification or priority
conclusion is inferred from this bounded search.
