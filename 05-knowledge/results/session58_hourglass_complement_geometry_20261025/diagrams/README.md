# Session58 exact explanatory diagrams

Two original, accessible SVGs, generated by the standard-library `draw.py`. They are explanatory translations of the pinned Session58 proofs, not new mathematical claims.

## Figure 1: `hourglass-carrier.svg`

**Caption.** Two compatible seven-cell magic hourglasses share the four corners and center (gray). The horizontal-only pair is blue; the vertical-only pair is orange. Permuting the same nine values into the additive AP–K₃,₃ edge-value grid sends the five shared values to a spanning tree. Each hourglass adds two chords. The graph vertices are indexed potentials; the table entries are edge labels, not vertices. The additive grid is not itself the magic placement. The two AP constraints on its shared tree leave parameters E, x, z. Square membership remains an extra predicate: the structural equivalence neither constructs nor excludes distinct integer-square hourglasses or squares.

## Figure 2: `observer-profile.svg`

**Caption.** At n = 6, all 34 tournament converse classes have distinct individual order-averaged graph-pair profiles, but their first-moment matrix has rational rank 32. Retain 32 independent linear coordinates and append the two squared coordinates A_T(3)², A_T(15)² to obtain rank 34. These are same-latent second moments: draw T once, then choose two orders independently conditional on T. Fresh independent draws of T do not provide these moments. The nonzero displayed integer determinant certifies the finite rank repair. This is neither an individual-profile collision nor an all-n quadratic sufficiency claim.

Graph-pair keys follow lexicographic unordered pairs 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 12, 13, 14, 15, 23, 24, 25, 34, 35, 45. Thus key 3 is K₁,₂ plus three isolated vertices, and key 15 is K₁,₄ plus one isolated vertex, each paired with its complement.

## Reproduction and exact checks

Run:

    python3 draw.py
    python3 check.py
    python3 -O check.py

`check.py` uses explicit exceptions, never removable assertions. It checks all eight magic sums as integer coefficient vectors, the cell and line overlaps, exact label-to-grid mapping, connected five-edge spanning tree, chord colors and cycle counts, the two independent AP constraints, and the SVG's actual displayed cell labels and color categories. From the bundled pinned 34-by-78 integer profile matrix it recomputes rational rank 32 and 34 distinct rows. It reconstructs the selected augmented minor from the original matrix and checks its exact Bareiss determinant and prime residue. The original orbit-enumeration proofs remain in the source packet; these checks verify the explanatory artifact against its retained certificate rather than independently repeat that census.

`observer-data.json` contains the replayable matrix, selected columns, and augmented minor, copied from the two pinned observer certificates. `SOURCE_PINS.json` records SHA-256 hashes of all four read proofs and both source certificates. `SHA256SUMS.json` pins the diagram sources, SVGs, data, and source manifest. It deliberately excludes itself and run logs.

Both SVGs were rendered with Inkscape and their PNG previews visually inspected at native dimensions. All titles, cell expressions, subscripts, captions, and scope guards fit without clipping or overlap. The previews are review-only and MUST NOT be published. The first attempted ImageMagick render lacked its rsvg delegate; Inkscape completed both exports successfully.
