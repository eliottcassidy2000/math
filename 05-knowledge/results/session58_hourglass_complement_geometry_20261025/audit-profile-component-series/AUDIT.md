## Componentwise signed admissibility

For an ordinary graph X, Royle-even means that every automorphism has comparison-sign+1. If a connected component has a negative-sign automorphism, extend it by the identity to obtain one on X. Conversely, choose vertex labels in consecutive blocks for connected components, with identical internal labeling on isomorphic copies. The automorphism group is generated by automorphisms within copies and the maps interchanging two isomorphic copies vertex-for-corresponding-vertex. Each such block interchange preserves the relative order of the endpoints of every edge, since no edge crosses components. Thus it has comparison-sign+1 regardless of the component's vertex count or edge count. If the internal automorphisms all have sign+1, the full group does too. Comparison-sign on stabilizers is unchanged by relabeling, by the cocycle identity. This proves componentwise Royle-evenness without incorrectly importing the ordinary sign of a vertex permutation.

## Multiplicativity and parity

For an arbitrarily labeled graph, α_X is the expectation of the product of edge comparison signs under independent continuous uniform vertex coordinates. Factors in disjoint components depend on independent coordinate families. Consequently α_X is exactly the product of component α-values, using each component's inherited labeling. Independent relative orders on components can also be proved by counting their interleavings. A change of labeling may change the sign of α, but never its zero/nonzero status.

Reversing a component's entire order changes the product by(-1) to its number of edges. Every odd-edge component therefore has α=0. A graph has α≠0 precisely when every component has α≠0. Such components necessarily have even edge count. In the rank count retain Royle-even components as required by the signed-Walsh basis; ordinary even edge count alone is insufficient.

## Formal series

Let g_j count connected Royle-even graph types of order j with α≠0, z_j count those with even edge count and α=0, and o_j count those with odd edge count. These categories partition the connected Royle-even types. Define

R(t)=∏_{j≥1}(1−t^j)^(−g_j),
Z(t)=∏_{j≥1}(1−t^j)^(−z_j),
O(t,u)=∏_{j≥1}(1−u t^j)^(−o_j),
O_even(t)=(O(t,1)+O(t,−1))/2.

The parity variable counts multiplicity of odd-edge COMPONENTS, not vertex parity. Thus O(t,−1) has factors(1+t^j)^(−o_j), not(1−(−t)^j)^(−o_j). Repeated copies contribute parity by their multiplicity. The average extracts an even total number of odd-edge components, exactly the total edge-parity condition.

Unlabeled graphs are unique multisets of connected graph types. The profile rank criterion therefore gives R as its rank generating series. By the signed-Walsh basis, the dimension c_n of converse-invariant tournament functions counts Royle-even graph types with even total edge count. Their series is

C(t)=R(t) Z(t) O_even(t).

Consequently the kernel-dimension series is

K(t)=C(t)−R(t)=R(t)(Z(t)O_even(t)−1).

Every coefficient counts a finite set: only component sizes≤n contribute to degree n, with finitely many connected types at each such size. These are formal ordinary generating series, not analytic convergence claims. The division by2 in the parity projection yields integer nonnegative coefficients by its explicit combinatorial interpretation.

The empty graph has α=1, trivial stabilizer, and no components, so C(0)=R(0)=Z(0)=O_even(0)=1 and K(0)=0. A single isolated vertex is connected, Royle-even, even-edge, and α=1; it contributes the factor(1−t)^(−1) to R. Isolates are included, not silently excluded.

## Kernel lower bound

Let F be the connected six-vertex eight-edge zero mode with mask255. For every Royle-even even-edge graph X on n−6 vertices, X⊔F is Royle-even, has even edge count, and has α=0. Distinct graph types X yield distinct union types, because connected-component multisets admit cancellation of one copy of F, even when X itself contains F. Their signed-Walsh functions have disjoint orbit supports and are linearly independent. Therefore

dim ker_n ≥ c_(n−6) for every n≥6.

The empty source graph at n=6 yields one valid witness; the actual n6 kernel has dimension2. This lower bound concerns a vector-space kernel, not a pair of classes with identical profile. No new claim about individual collisions follows.
