## What is established

- Two seven-cell magic hourglasses share five labelled cells, not one. Those shared values have three independent coordinates. They glue to a full magic array, and injectivity in both compatible copies implies global injectivity. One scalar hourglass has a unique scalar completion; square membership of the two forced entries is the remaining arithmetic gate.
- The same overlap is the five-edge central AP spanning tree of the labelled K3,3 carrier. Each hourglass adds two chords. The seven-cell incidence is the classical **mitre**, not five Fano lines: its unused-pair graph is a six-cycle. Its minimal Steiner host has nine points. In AG(2,3), 54 same-center mitre pairs recover 432 labelled magic layouts, eight per pattern.
- Forgetting added square roots yields genuine finite locally free degree-four hourglass maps over the shared-root surface, and their global fiber product is the nine-root surface. Its rational-point count is a coupled fiber sum, not a product of marginals. A C2 symmetry splits this count into even energy minus odd energy.
- Graph complement is tournament converse in a fixed **same-size ordered observer** chart. It is a different operation in the **marked-Hamilton-path** chart on one fewer graph vertex. Averaging over all observers gives a lawful converse-invariant profile; individual separation is now checked through order six, despite a rank-32 first-moment matrix on 34 converse classes. Two shared-latent observer orders restore rank 34 at order six.
- The inherited frame formula admits a fixed-base punctured-line realization. Varying additive dimension is not automatically extension of one prime-field variety. Zeta rationality rules out the proposed eventually prescribed sequence over that base; Frobenius cycle data supplies the correct parity-sensitive repair. Converse cycles also have a deliberately limited finite étale realization.

- Gaussian allocation indices count square gaps exactly and reduce squarefree indexing to PG(k−1,3), but projective dependence is not numerical gap addition. Exact hereditary scaling, all-exponent repeated-maximum candidate counts and their primitive ancestry are proved. These enumerate necessary candidates, not actual hourglasses.
