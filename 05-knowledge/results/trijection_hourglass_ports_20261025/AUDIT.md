## Tournament proof review

The root lane independently checked both cyclic-prefix joins and all
anti-reversal witnesses, the two exceptional middle cases, and the
prefix/suffix rotation cycles. A separate trijection reviewer checked
the endpoint proofs in [INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md](endpoint/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md).
The terminal order-eleven failure is a real failure of its generic word;
the transitive cycle determines at most one labelled source, whose
alternative certificate is directly replayed. No isomorphism-class,
strong-partner or mixed-source filter was introduced.

One draft correction was caught before publication: N^+_(K_P T)(a)
also contains z. The cyclic-prefix reflection product fixes a and z,
so it preserves the four-point set obtained AFTER deleting z. The proof
survives with that explicit restriction. The corrected statement and
checker use the full five-point neighbourhood.

`audit.py` is a separate implementation. It constructs source arcs as
affine variables, adds exact reflection and port equations, and uses
integer bit-mask Gaussian elimination rather than importing the producer
constraint solver. Each legal join must reduce identically to zero in
the source system; adjoining each proposed blue system must be inconsistent.
The target is checked by a separate full edge mask. It covers:

The n11 exceptional detour is additionally decoded into an arc set and

one n11 generic failure occurs. The smaller orders are diagnostics and
do not lower the proved theorem's range. Larger producer tests are
explicitly labelled deterministic samples, not censuses.

The tail proof subsequently generalized to every k>=4,n>=2k+3. The
reviewer independently derived the first/last interval rotation product
and checked the uniform exceptional word's joins, XOR and black witnesses.

comes from the displayed formula and proof, not that finite range.

The affine compiler's finite-menu coverage theorem was checked in both
logical directions: an uncovered source chooses one blue failure in
every otherwise legal ledger, and every consistent such intersection
is an uncovered source. Legality must be uniform on each source cell.
The two-reflection sufficiency proof was checked on unordered-pair
stabilizers of the odd dihedral group; the parity-odd reflection coset
cannot fix both ends because a positional reversal has at most one
fixed vertex. Path joins are deliberately outside that existence theorem.

## Publication gates

Every mathematical checker uses explicit exceptions, which remain active
under `python -O`. Normal and optimized JSON receipts are compared by
`verify.py`. It also pins packet sources, verifies inherited proof bytes,
parses Python files, and checks local document links. The normal and
optimized outputs are evidence of completed runs, not substitutes for
the proofs above. The verifier is read-only unless explicitly invoked
with `--refresh` to regenerate publication pins.
