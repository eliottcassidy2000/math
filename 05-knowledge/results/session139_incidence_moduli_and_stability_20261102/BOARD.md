## Cross-comparison after the accepted results

- Stability vs spectrum: the new lower-bound proof keeps B and W, coordinates
  the cospectral quotient cannot reconstruct. This supports retaining them;
  it does not show that all spectral lower bounds are impossible.
- Stability vs arithmetic: exact rational endpoint arithmetic proves real
  signs before reduction. Congruence information cannot replace the signs.
- Spectrum vs history: the old binary disjoint-triangle obstruction becomes
  digon/triangle incidence in the weighted domain. The carrier explains the
  boundary shift 6 -> 5 without numerology.
- Spectrum vs metagraphs: the conjugacy fixes the all-one root, so simply
  adding that marked resolvent is insufficient. A vertex-exclusion sidecar
  is materially different from another unmarked walk statistic.
- History vs arithmetic: the 5/11 transfer discriminant, Paley-11 character
  sums and eleven order-seven skew spectra have different types. Preserve
  their actual maps; no prime-label universality is promoted.
