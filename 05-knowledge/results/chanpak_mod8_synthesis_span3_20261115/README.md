## [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md): the mod-8 dictionary (PROVED, elementary; verified)

1. **Multiplication tables.** By Zolotarev–Frobenius, the Collatz-side multipliers have signs given by Jacobi
   symbols: doubling (the inverse halving edge) has sign (2/m), negation (the mirror −(3(−x)+1) = 3x − 1) has
   sign (−1/m), and their composite has sign (−2/m). The affine Collatz step and its 3x−1 mirror are conjugate by
   negation, with sign (6/m).
2. **One Klein group.** (ℤ/8)* = ⟨−1⟩ × ⟨3⟩, generated by the mirror and the Collatz multiplier.
   - Its three characters are χ₋₁, χ₂ and χ₋₂.
   - **The kernel of χ₋₂ is ⟨3⟩.** So the primes x² + 2y² are exactly the primes congruent mod 8 to a power of the
     Collatz multiplier.
   - Diagonal joins, with norm (H + P₂)² + 2H² built from ordered pairs of disjoint paths, land in the class 3
     itself.
3. **Paley tournaments.** Multiplication by a is an automorphism iff (a/p) = 1, and a converse map otherwise.
   - So the Collatz mirror always reverses a Paley tournament.
   - Doubling reverses it exactly when p ≡ 3 (mod 8). In that case mirror ∘ doubling preserves it, and p = x² + 2y²
     is the diagonal-join class.
   - Measured: H(P₇) = 189, H(P₁₁) = 95,095 and H(P₁₉) = 1,172,695,746,915, each divisible by p(p−1)/2. Their
     diagonal joins all lie in 3 (mod 8), and none is prime.
4. **Collatz mod 8.**
   - v₂(3n ± 1) is fixed by n mod 8: 2, 1, ≥ 3, 1 for 3n+1 and 1, ≥ 3, 1, 2 for 3n−1, at n ≡ 1, 3, 5, 7.
   - The mirror swaps the tables. It keeps χ₂(n) and flips χ₋₂(n).
   - The 4n ± 1 merge laws hold.
5. **Artin.** 2 is a primitive root, so the doubling table is a single cycle, for 0.7406 of primes p ≡ 3 (mod 8)
   and 0.7488 of primes p ≡ 5 (mod 8) below 2·10⁵, against Artin's 2A = 0.7479.
6. **Metagraph.** The host operations act by explicit laws:
   - ordinal sums multiply path-cover polynomials;
   - cyclic joins are Re(β_A β̄_B) in ℤ[√−2], and diagonal joins are norms;
   - double cones are linear operators on P(q).

   The diagonal sub-family has a reciprocity obstruction, like Apollonian sub-families, but the full family is
   locally unobstructed.

## [SPAN3_LITERAL.md](SPAN3_LITERAL.md): span 3 with reachable cones

- **Exact facts.** In the 62-dimensional minimal transfer only the empty letter is a bottleneck: its cube has rank 1
  over ℚ. Runs of {1} give exactly the tribonacci numbers (growth 1.839), and the other letters grow at
  2.29–3.32.
- **Reduction (PROVED).** Bottleneck-free literal words are words over the 21-word prefix code 0^i a.
- **Periodic census (VERIFIED modulo two primes).** Of the 52,094 cyclic code words of up to 4 code units (periods
  up to 12 letters), 31,875 grow more slowly than 1.77 per vertex, and **every one is a bottleneck**. This extends
  THM-5202 (D) beyond period 6. Floating eigenvalue ratios misclassified 18 of them.
- **Program.** Uniform-memory reachable cones can only certify the floor 5^(1/3), because floor-syllable powers
  need about 12 letters to become rank one. Memory adaptive along syllables is needed; this is specified but not
  executed.
