## 3. A minimal carrier theorem for this declared language

**Lemma (fixed-marker transport).** Let a faithful finite permutation group
G act on a finite set Omega. A hidden frame is g in G. Fix a named subset
K of source labels. Allowed operations are common left multiplication
g->hg by known h, and observation of the ordered images g(K). Then the
minimal deterministic carrier has

    [G:G_(K)]

states, represented by g restricted to K. Here G_(K) fixes K pointwise.

**Proof.** The update is h(g(K)), so the restriction is closed. Two frames
have the same restriction iff they lie in the same right coset of G_(K).
The immediate observation distinguishes different cosets; no smaller exact
carrier can merge them. If an additional selector may name ANY x in Omega
and ask for g(x), every pair of distinct frames is separated, so the minimal
carrier becomes G itself. This is a classical action/observability argument,
stated to expose the exact operation assumptions. It is not a claim that
arbitrary response tensors are permutation frames. QED.
