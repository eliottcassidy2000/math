# General head-to-Mersenne phase compiler

**Status: PROVED** for the fixed-head phase, finite growth cutoff, guarded
common-future receipts and finite-bank relative densities below.
**FINITE-EXACT** for the listed script controls and the inherited finite head
bank. **CONDITIONAL** for ROOT completion: a paid child is still an obligation.

The inherited mechanisms are the audited clearing identities in
[the two-anchor specialization](collatz_completion_anchor_20261007b.md) and
the unique partner decoder in
[the general head package](collatz_twoanchor_head_decoder_20261007b.md).
The former independently checks the consumed affine part of
[THM-4601, two-anchor reduction](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4601-two-anchor-reduction-of-the-residual-first-reset-two-branch.md).
This package transfers every authenticated nonpadding head to actual positive
Mersenne sources. It does not infer integer coverage from Haar absorption.

The working objects are a native valuation cylinder, a binary exponent phase,
a shifted-anchor head identity, and an immutable-source height bound.
The map preserves the entire actual head word and the smaller child's common
future. It does not supply that child's ROOT suffix. The sidecars are the
source exponent, head cost, parameter cutoff, sibling gap and deletion depth.

## 1. Input contract

Let \(u,v\) be nonempty positive valuation words and let \(r\ge1\). Write

\[
F_w(x)=\frac{3^{|w|}x+B_w}{2^{A_w}},\qquad A_w=\sum w.
\]

An authenticated head packet satisfies

\[
|v|=|u|+3,\qquad A_v=A_u-2r,\qquad
F_v(1)=4^rF_u(1)+\frac{4^r-1}{3}. \tag{1}
\]

This is an exact affine identity test, not merely a comparison of slopes or
word lengths. Denote \(A=A_u\), \(P=3^{|u|}\), \(Q=2^A\), and \(B=B_u\).
The head's unique native odd-source cell is

\[
x\equiv x_u:=(Q-B)P^{-1}\pmod {2Q},
\qquad 1\le x_u<2Q. \tag{2}
\]

Final oddness forces all earlier valuations in a positive word to be exact:
an even intermediate makes the next state nonintegral, and a nonintegral
dyadic state can never recover integrality. First-hit ROOT safety needs an
additional guard; it is supplied in Section 3.

Require \(u_1\ne2\), so the head begins after the maximal two-run.
For \(u_1=1\), the native residue satisfies \(x_u\equiv3\pmod4\), hence
\(v_2(x_u-1)=1\). For \(u_1\ge3\), it satisfies \(x_u\equiv5\pmod8\), hence
\(v_2(x_u-1)=2\). Heads starting with \(2\) remain valid algebraic objects,
but this post-run interface rejects them; one must first normalize their
run placement.

## 2. Exact native exponent phase

Fix \(J\ge3\) and \(n=2^K-1\). After its \(K-1\) initial ones and \(J\)
twos, the formal endpoint is

\[
X=1+\frac{3^J(3^{K-1}-1)}{2^{2J-1}}.
\]

The native condition \(X\equiv x_u\pmod {2^{A+1}}\) is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{
3^{K-1}\equiv
1+2^{2J-1}(x_u-1)3^{-J}\pmod {2^{2J+A}}.
} \tag{3}
\]

All divisions here are by units or are performed with the explicitly retained
binary precision. The right side belongs to the subgroup generated by \(3\):
it is \(1\pmod8\). That subgroup has order \(2^{b-2}\) modulo \(2^b\), as
proved by \(v_2(3^e-1)=2+v_2(e)\) for positive even \(e\).
Consequently (3) gives one exact phase

\[
\boxed{K\equiv k_{u,J}\pmod {P_{u,J}},
\qquad P_{u,J}=2^{2J+A-2}.} \tag{4}
\]

The modulus is minimal. Moreover the target in (3) has exact difference
valuation \(2J-1+v_2(x_u-1)\). Thus it forces

\[
v_2(K-1)=
\begin{cases}
2J-2,&u_1=1,\\
2J-1,&u_1\ge3.
\end{cases} \tag{5}
\]

There really are exactly \(J\) twos before the head. This establishes the
native iff rather than assuming the run type separately.

The relative natural density of (4) in its exponent shell is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\begin{cases}
2^{1-A},&u_1=1,\\
2^{2-A},&u_1\ge3.
\end{cases}} \tag{6}
\]

These are densities of exponents \(K\), not of integer sources \(2^K-1\).
The old heads \(u=(1,10)\) and \(u=(10)\) recover the exact factors
\(1/1024\) and \(1/256\).

## 3. A uniform first-hit cutoff

The native phase alone is an affine-word guard. To ensure that the receipt
cannot contain ROOT padding, retain only parameters with

\[
\boxed{e=K-1\ge2A+J.} \tag{7}
\]

For a prefix of \(u\) of length \(i\), cost \(A_i\le A\), its slope after
the initial ones and twos is

\[
\frac{3^{e+J+i}}{2^{e+2J+A_i}}
\ \ge\ \frac{3^{e+J}}{2^{e+2J+A}}
\ \ge\ \left(\frac98\right)^{A+J}>1.
\]

The last inequality uses (7); at equality its middle ratio is exactly the
displayed power. Every nonempty source prefix has positive carry. Its
initial ones also grow, and its intervening two-run has an even larger
slope than this common lower bound. Hence every actual source prefix
through the head endpoint \(Z=F_u(X)\) stays strictly above the immutable
source \(n\).

For the least phase representative \(1\le k_{u,J}<P_{u,J}\), write
\(K=k_{u,J}+P_{u,J}t\). The exact retained parameter threshold is

\[
t\ge t_{\min}:=
\max\left(0,\left\lceil
\frac{2A+J+1-k_{u,J}}{P_{u,J}}
\right\rceil\right).
\]

No assumption about the size of the least representative is needed. This
removes finitely many parameters for a fixed head and \(J\), so (6) remains
the exact density. The cutoff is sufficient, not asserted necessary for
payment or optimal. Production APIs reject a parameter below it rather than
silently replacing the supplied source by a later member.

## 4. Actual deletion receipts for both clearing depths

Choose \(D=3\) or \(4\), with child \(h_D=2^{K-D}-1\). Put

\[
C_3=(4,1,1),\qquad C_4=(2,2,1,1).
\]

The inherited clearing calculation gives the anchored relation
\(X-1=27(Y-1)\) at the end of the source's two-run. From (1),

\[
F_v(Y)=S^r(F_u(X)),\qquad
S^r(z)=4^rz+\frac{4^r-1}{3}.
\]

Set \(c=v_2(3Z+1)\), where \(Z=F_u(X)>n>1\). The actual common-future words
are

\[
\begin{aligned}
w&=1^{K-1}2^J\,u\,(c),\\
v_D&=1^{K-D-1}C_D2^{J-3}\,v\,(c+2r).
\end{aligned} \tag{8}
\]

Indeed \(3S^r(Z)+1=4^r(3Z+1)\). Both sides have
\(K+J+|u|\) odd edges, and the source's valuation cost exceeds the child's
by exactly \(D\), independently of \(r\).

The child prefix ends at \(S^r(Z)>1\), an odd integer. Its positive affine
maps and final oddness force all its intermediate states to be positive odd
integers with exactly the prescribed valuations. It cannot have encountered
ROOT earlier, because the actual odd map stays at \(1\). The terminal step
in (8) is consequently the first ROOT hit if its endpoint is \(1\), and
otherwise both receipt prefixes remain ROOT-free.

This proves a strict smaller-child common-future receipt for every retained
parameter. A supplied first-hit certificate for \(h_D\) can be cut after
\(v_D\) and spliced after \(w\). No unprovided child ROOT certificate is
created by this compiler.

The same affine transfer also applies to \(n=2^Kt-1\) with odd \(t\ge1\),
\(K\ge5\), \(J\ge3\), provided its actual source prefix
\(1^{K-1}2^Ju\) remains above \(1\), including its endpoint. The child is
\(h_D=2^{K-D}t-1<n\); (8) again follows. If an earlier ROOT hit is observed,
its actual prefix is already a proof and must be returned without padding.
This is the inherited general-source lift with positive sibling gap, not a
new assertion that all such sources satisfy the native head guard.

## 5. A finite bank becomes an exact all-height family bank

The independently audited
[head decoder](collatz_twoanchor_head_decoder_20261007b.md) enumerates source
heads of lengths \(1,\ldots,4\), letters \(1,\ldots,12\), with every positive
gap allowed by the necessary cost bound. Its nonpadding, prefix-minimal
bank has \(225\) heads. This is a declared finite search universe, not a
classification of all head shapes.

Distinct heads in this antichain cannot begin the same actual orbit.
Their native cylinders are disjoint, and (3) pulls them back to disjoint
exponent cylinders within each fixed shell. The sum of (6) is therefore
the exact union density. Each head's finite cutoff leaves it unchanged.

For this particular finite bank, the cutoff actually removes **no positive
native member**. Its maximum head cost is \(25\). For \(J\ge4\), the least
possible shell value obeys
\(e\ge2^{2J-2}\ge50+J\ge2A+J\): check \(64\ge54\) at \(J=4\), then the
left side quadruples at each increment while the middle bound increases by
one. At \(J=3\), the only possible shell values below the maximum threshold
\(53\) are \(e=16,32,48\), i.e. \(K=17,33,49\). The complete exact check of
these three exponents against all \(225\) native guards finds no match.
This finite exceptional-set calculation strengthens the declared bank;
the general compiler still retains its sufficient cutoff for arbitrary heads.

The old baseline is **all nonempty words** over \(G_1,G_5,G_{17}\), with

It is not every historical selector. On Mersenne sources, the first letters
can start only at \(K\equiv5\pmod6\) or \(K\equiv733\pmod{1458}\).
In each dyadic exponent phase, these cover \(243+1\) of its \(729\)

This strictly enlarges the two single-head phases. For example, the new

\[

They also miss the old single head in the corresponding shell: their actual
head prefixes differ. The symbolic compiler proves both progressions without
materializing their enormous integer sources.

Every retained Mersenne phase has \(K\equiv1\pmod{16}\); its four-bit child
has \(v_2(K-5)=2\), hence a two-run of exactly two. This normalization is
specific to \(t=1\); it is not asserted for the general odd-\(t\) lift above.
The new bank handles more long-run phases, but still leaves the two-twos child
proof and uncovered source phases as real obligations. The last table counts
paid dependencies, not newly completed ROOT routes.
