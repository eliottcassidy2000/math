## 2. Exact state, guards, and clocks

For a positive odd source n and its exact valuation word `w=(a_1,...,a_m)`, let `K=sum a_i`, `A=3^m`, `Q=2^K`, and

    B = sum_i 3^(m-i) 2^(a_1+...+a_(i-1)).

The physical endpoint is `(A n+B)/Q`. In the source-owned coordinate `z=n-1`, retain the signed identity

    z_end = lambda_w z + P_w - D_w,
    lambda_w = A/Q,
    P_w = sum_(a_i=1) lambda_(i+1:m),
    D_w = sum_(a_i>=3) (1-4/2^a_i) lambda_(i+1:m).

Both P and D are nonnegative, but their **difference**, not their sum or an absolute-value surrogate, is the exact emitted charge. The positive envelope is `E_w(z)=lambda_w z+P_w`. At an actual endpoint h, it is paid exactly when `D_w<n-h`. This scalar signed identity is not an identity with the separate native signed-row/Gram ROOT transport, whose cross terms remain unpaid here.

Composition u then v gives

    lambda_uv=lambda_v lambda_u,
    P_uv=lambda_v P_u+P_v,
    D_uv=lambda_v D_u+D_v.                             (2.1)

The complete exact source guard is

    n = (Q-B) A^(-1) mod 2Q.                          (2.2)

The terminal odd bit is essential. All source claims below retain it; endpoint congruences without that bit are not exact valuation words. Extra guards compose by pullback to the original source.

There are three clocks. A word of m odd steps and valuation cost K takes K shortcut steps for `T(odd)=(3n+1)/2`, `T(even)=n/2`, and K+m ordinary unaccelerated steps. A first literal descent can occur inside the last division run, before the odd endpoint. ROOT always means the actual integer1 at its first arrival, never residue1 modulo105.

## 3. A separator-normalized cone with a sharp repair criterion

Fix an integer r>=0 and put `theta=(2/3)^r`. Let s be the word of r valuation-one letters. Its multiplier is `eta=1/theta` and its discarded debt is zero. Fix `c>=0` and define

    K_(theta,c) = { (lambda,D): lambda>=1,
                                  0<=D<=c(lambda-theta) }.

**Proposition S1 (PROVED).** This cone is closed under the separator product

    u star v := u s v.                               (3.1)

Indeed (2.1) gives

    D_(u s v) <= c[lambda_v eta(lambda_u-theta)
                             +(lambda_v-theta)]
               = c(lambda_(u s v)-theta).

The multiplier is at least1. More generally any separator consisting of at least r ones works, since `eta theta>=1` makes the same upper bound no larger. Generators and separator lengths may change at every join; the depth is unrestricted.

This is a useful normalization of the inherited cone, not a claim of new affine algebra. The auxiliary map `u -> u s` takes the displayed inequality exactly to `D_(u s)<=c(lambda_(u s)-1)` and intertwines the separator product with ordinary concatenation. It is an algebraic proof device. If s is not the actual next word at a source, it is **not executed or charged to that source**.

For a fixed nonzero-debt generator with multiplier lambda, the least c in this particular cone is

    c_min(theta)=D/(lambda-theta).                    (3.2)

A finite r permitting `c_min(theta)<=1` exists iff `D<lambda`. A finite r permitting strict `c_min(theta)<1` also exists iff `D<lambda`. This follows because theta decreases to zero through positive values. If `D>=lambda`, adding any finite all-one separator cannot repair this scheme. This is a sharp obstruction for the stated separator construction, not for every possible debt controller. For example `(3,1,1,1,1)` has `D>=lambda`. Even repeated growth cannot change the sign of `lambda-D`.

**Proposition S2 (PROVED, terminal payment).** Suppose a legal prefix u lies in `K_(theta,c)` with `0<theta<=1` and `0<=c<=1`. If its next actual odd step has valuation a and gives an actual strict descent h<n from the original positive odd n>1, the entire positive envelope is paid. If c<1, its absolute payment margin is strictly greater than `1-c`, uniformly over prefix length and reset count.

Proof. Set `mu=3/2^a`. The unshifted total word has positive carry, so `mu lambda_u<h/n<1`; hence a>=2. If c>0, composition and strictness give

    D_total < c(1-mu theta)+d_a,
    d_2=0; d_a=1-4/2^a for a>=3.

At a=2 this is at most c. At a>=3 it is

    1+c-(4+3c theta)/2^a < 1+c <=2.                  (3.3)

Oddness gives `n-h>=2`, proving payment and the stated strict margin. At c=0 the prefix debt vanishes, and `D_total=d_a<1`, so there is no unsupported strict multiplication by zero. No first-exit premise is needed for the implication, and no existence of the descending last step is inferred.

Dropping the separator can fail: the source63 generator below satisfies its cone with equality, while its ordinary square `u u` violates that same cone. The missing term is the positive `c(1-theta)lambda_u`. Thus this is not a fitted claim that arbitrary words compose for free.
