## Research method worth retaining

Ask three questions in order:

1. What exact future operations must this state support?
2. Which equality of responses is the coarsest safe quotient for those operations?
3. Does the quotient admit the required positive, integral, geometric or source-owned realization?

The session's new results answer these questions in several concrete carriers. They do not show that one answer transfers automatically to every mathematical problem.
