# What transfers from a 3–4–5 triangle to a tournament pair

The original [3–4–5 square-sum chain](squares/PROOF.md) traverses 1 through 15 from 8 to 9 using only sums 9, 16, 25. Its two reflections compose to rotation by a unit modulo 16. The inherited argument works for every primitive Pythagorean triple. The new scaled construction keeps the residue pairs that are lost in the primitive reduction: for (24,45,51), it gives a 224-vertex path and four 450-cycles on 1 through 2024, using only the three named squares. Their births and balanced edge-label word have exact modular formulas.

The [coprime square-edge birth tree](squares/BIRTHS.md) is a separate object. The marked 3–4–5 edge {9,16} is born at 16, and the first births under its three moves are 144, 441, 225. A priority queue enumerates the whole birth order without inspecting other square-sum edges. Even the sum 65² can come from edges born at different orders.

[Berggren's unique-parent mechanism transfers to a physical tournament tree](root/PROOF.md). Three strong rooted moves (each adding six vertices) land in disjoint intervals of a two-count ratio, with exact integer inverse and strict descent. A final read records both counts in one scalar, giving **3^d distinct Hamilton counts at even order 12d+4** for every d. Every odd dyadic residue occurs somewhere in this family, but the absolute count set is sparse. The seed-to-state address is exact; universal target placement and minimal order are separate problems.

The [determinant calculation](tournaments/PROOF.md) explains part of that distinction. Every nondegenerate migration has an odd lattice index at least 3 after removing dyadic padding. The padding can be moved across one interface by a literal isomorphism, but the odd index cannot be erased this way. Berggren has a primitive unimodular parent on its entire parity lattice; this tournament tree has extra image-lattice predicates.

The same [tournament migration admits a literal sum-of-two-squares read](tournaments/PROOF.md#an-exact-physical-sum-of-two-squares). Paired migrations followed by one physical reader give H=h²+R². An infinite subfamily makes this count the hypotenuse of a primitive Pythagorean triple. This is a new physical bridge and a sharp limitation: that specific norm construction cannot realize 7p for p an odd prime other than 7.

[The Fermat and Jacobian boundaries](boundaries/PROOF.md) are informative precisely because their hypotheses differ. Fermat changes the exponent, not the dimension of the same projective curve. For exact linear preservers of a diagonal form, the Hessian forces a finite integral symmetry group in degree ≥3, while quadratic Berggren symmetry can persist in higher dimension. The [formally verified](https://isa-afp.org/entries/Jacobian_Counterexample.html) complex constant-Jacobian counterexample in dimension three does not give one in dimension two by stabilization; an actual dimension-lowering splitting is needed. Real Pinchuk maps have nonconstant determinants and remain a different issue.

The common research question is to exhibit the actual inverse chambers, prove that legal inputs have legal predecessors, and track the extra information lost under projection. None of the analogies claims a proof of Chan–Pak or of the planar Jacobian conjecture.
