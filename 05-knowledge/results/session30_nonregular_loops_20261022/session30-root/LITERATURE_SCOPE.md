# Targeted literature check and priority boundary

Checked2026-10-22; this is a targeted check, not a systematic novelty review.

- N. C. Wormald, [Tournaments with many Hamilton cycles](https://users.monash.edu.au/~nwormald/papers/hamtourn.pdf): the author's PDF discusses expected Hamiltonian counts in random tournament families and blow-ups, and explicitly transfers conclusions to paths. Its introduction distinguishes random, random regular, and other structured ensembles. This supports the need to distinguish an ensemble average from an individual-tournament statement; it does not by itself certify this packet's uniform loop remainder.
- Zeina Ghazo Hanna and Amine El Sahili, [Counting Hamiltonian Paths in Transitive Tournaments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.11510): the abstract constructs a function counting oriented Hamiltonian paths of prescribed type in a transitive tournament. This is a relevant future comparison for the switched-transitive family. No equivalence with our signed-color endpoint constraints was proved in this session.
- The publisher page for McKay's1996 score-sequence enumeration paper was found but its full text returned403 during this check. No new theorem was imported from that inaccessible text.

The elementary expectation, score-flow and group-theoretic identities here are
proved directly, without claiming they are new in the literature. The prior
repository forest identities retain their explicit source/audit attribution.
The exact kernels and certificates establish the scoped results, not priority.
