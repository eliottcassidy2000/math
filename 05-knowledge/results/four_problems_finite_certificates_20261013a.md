## 4. BSD: two adjacent Selmer levels can certify the hypothesis

[The Selmer-growth packet](bsd_selmer_growth_20261013a.md) writes

    u_m = log_q #Sel_(q^m) - log_q #E(Q)[q^m]
        = ms + sum_i min(m,e_i),

where s is full q-infinity Selmer corank and the e_i describe its finite Sha
part. Therefore s<=1 is equivalent to success of either finite test:

    u_m < 2m,   or   u_(m+1)-u_m <= 1.

The second often detects the finite tail sooner. These tests require complete
Selmer data and the exact torsion correction. Finite-depth failure remains
inconclusive.

Using the cited Monsky matrix, exact computation certifies low corank for

already on the cyclic triangle. This is a precise arithmetic use of the

## 6. The common hypothesis and its limits

**OPEN methodological hypothesis.** A useful recursive proof system should
have a finite receipt whose success predicate is stable under the specific
extension operation, with an explicit record of every discarded relation.
This predicts which next operation is worth trying; it proves none of the
universal existence claims on its own.

There are now four different exact versions: continued orbit payment,
closed derivative action, compatible geometric increments, and bounded
Selmer growth. Their stopping conditions are different. The connection is
the certificate interface and the audit of what it forgets, not an asserted
equivalence of the mathematical problems.

The earlier [-7/4 and -29/16 return-thickness packet](marked_return_thickness_20261012a.md)
is a useful hostile comparison: repetition preserves a finite orbit pattern
without improving the missing multiplicity. Here prime-depth nilpotence,
repeated field reduction, and shallow Selmer dimensions have analogous
information limits, each with its own proved mechanism. No conclusion is
drawn from coincident small numbers alone.
