# Uniform triangle hypergraphs: a bounded moving core and an exact return observer

## 1. Turning a path count into a structural packing budget

For a tournament T let the 3-uniform hypergraph E(T) consist of the vertex sets of its directed triangles. If this hypergraph contains a matching of size b, then H(T)≥3^b: every subset of the b disjoint triangles contributes its weight 2^k in the odd-cycle packing formula, and the total of just those terms is (1+2)^b. This uses the complete odd-cycle formula [THM-002](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-002-ocf.md); a triangle packing gives a lower bound, not the exact count.

Take any inclusion-maximal disjoint triangle packing, and let S be its vertex union. S meets every triangle, so T−S is transitive. Thus **every** H-path tournament admits a transitive-spine description with at most 3 floor(log₃ H) exceptional vertices, including nonminimal realizations of arbitrarily large order. Conversely a specific triangle matching gives a positive certificate of the lower bound, and a specific hitting set gives a certificate of the spine. The two witnesses are retained, not replaced by a single estimated rank.

Each vertex in the transitive spine has a binary profile of incidences to the exceptional vertices. Group the spine into maximal consecutive runs of the same profile. Each is a transitive module of the whole tournament. With the exceptional vertices fixed, every spanning path visits a given run in at most |S|+1 pieces: between any two visits there must be at least one exceptional vertex, since all inter-spine arcs point forward. There is no implication that the number of runs is bounded independently of H.

A sharper **target-based bound** on active runs follows by considering one exceptional vertex at a time. For an exceptional vertex x, write a word on the spine with 1 when x beats the vertex. In any inversion 1…0 at distance d≥2, choose the intervening subset arbitrarily on the increasing segment between its endpoints; this gives 2^(d−1) distinct directed cycles through x. Exactly half of these subsets give odd cycles, because the cycle length is 3 plus the subset size. By the odd-cycle packing formula, H(T)≥1+2^(d−1); for d=1 it gives H(T)≥3. Therefore every 1-before-0 inversion spans at most 1+floor(log₂(H−1)) indices of difference, for H>1. Outside the interval from the first 1 to the last 0 (when nonempty and in that order), the word is monotone 0*1*. Consequently each coordinate changes at most O(log H) times, and the multi-profile spine has only O((log H)^2) uniform runs, regardless of the number of padded vertices.

This is an upper bound on the number of coordinates in a **search grammar**, not a finite bank of whole modules and not a proof that its arithmetic image contains all prime targets. Runs can grow or shrink, which is exactly what a changing-module rule would need to track.

## 2. Why the edge labels matter when joining those pieces

The older [endpoint-595 Lonely Runner hypergraph](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4309-lrc14-endpoint-595-support-threshold-residual-hypergraph-compression.md) distinguishes an edge that is disjoint from a failing body from one that is also active at that body's own clock row. Its certificate weights typed obligations, not just raw supports. The June 21 [refill law](../../../04-computation/lrc14_route2_refill_law_macmini_0621.py) likewise counts arrivals only on the correct sector's timeline; the [summary](../../../04-computation/lrc14_route2_SUMMARY_macmini_0621.py) exhibits an effective binder on a later pass and rejects a raw harmonic-sum exchange. The nearer October 11 [conditional-survival note](../lonely_runner_conditional_survival_20261011e.md) keeps a common time through all conditioning and retains weak witnesses even when positive measure disappears.

The neighboring [boundary-palette session](../boundary_palette_compression_20261011.md) provides the exact analogue: two edge sets with the same count of boundary colors can have disjoint equality partitions. The next day's [marked return thickness](../marked_return_thickness_20261012a.md) distinguishes returning three marked points from a genuine local return with the requisite derivatives. The [Stechkin tile split](../stechkin_refined_conflict_graph_20261012a.md) goes the other way: splitting a region can escape a quotient obstruction, but pairwise witnesses need not be simultaneous. Earlier [algebraic triangle packing](../finite_algebraic_witness_20261007.md) retains the conflict list as well as the packing. These are complementary design constraints, not statements about prime distribution.

For tournament refills, the appropriate typed data are the **same retained arc set and ports, the current intercept, and the ordered path-cover coordinates read at the next site**. One cannot combine an intercept-compatible witness with a different cover-compatible witness, even if both produce the same integer target. Session 306 makes the latter coordinate precise: when a port can be revisited r times, it can read r normalized ordered cover coordinates. The graph-level search should allow any maximal triangle packing and use its typed witness as an auxiliary coordinate; quotienting just by the number of triangles or the number of available sites is not justified.

## 3. Strong-component trimming gives a linear logarithmic grammar

There is an even stronger bound on *run count* if one uses the inherited [Moon–Busch floor](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4741-presentation-counts-single-view-classes-are-k1-c3-stacks-rotation-inequality-and-forbidden-fibre-sizes-7-and-21.md): a strong component of order d≥3 has H≥3^(d/3) (each of the three stated Busch expressions is at least this). Strong components are ordinally ordered, and their Hamilton counts multiply. Thus the total number of vertices in non-singleton strong components is at most 3 log₃ H. With the hitting set above, the only other spine vertices are singleton SCCs. Consecutive singleton SCCs form at most one run between two successive nontrivial SCCs and at the two ends, with constant incidence to S. The remaining spine vertices are at most 3 log₃ H individually. Hence at most 4 log₃ H+1 spine runs suffice. The first argument above has the merit of not needing the sharp strong-tournament floor; the latter is better for exact search.

For prime target p, there is exactly one nontrivial strong component and it has path count p. For 7p with p prime, there is likewise exactly one, since any multiplicative split into nontrivial counts would require a 7-path component, which is forbidden (when p=7 the only split is 7×7). Every other SCC is neutral singleton padding. A return using more nontrivial determinant modules must therefore couple them inside one strong component; merely inserting new ordinal factors can never fix the prime/7p bottleneck. The logarithmic grammar identifies where to look but does not certify the exact arithmetic image of its strong cores.
