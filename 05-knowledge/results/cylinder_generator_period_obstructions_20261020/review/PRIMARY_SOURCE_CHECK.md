1. S. S. Abhyankar and A. Sathaye, *Uniqueness of plane embeddings of special curves*, Proceedings of the AMS 124 (1996), 1061–1069. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6). The search index returned the original article text at pages 1061–1063 from this [journal-PDF mirror](https://scispace.com/pdf/uniqueness-of-plane-embeddings-of-special-curves-omujaliiec.pdf). Page 1061 states the algebraically closed characteristic-zero setting. Page 1062 gives Theorem (1.3) and the uniqueness convention; page 1063 supplies its proof. The theorem covers distinct prime leading exponents with strictly lower weighted terms, including singular curves and alternative abstractly isomorphic embeddings. The review needs ambient equivalence of curves/ideals, not literal equality of rescaled equations. This was an indexed primary-page inspection, not a complete PDF download.

2. J. Gwozdziewicz, *Injectivity on one line*, [primary arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9305008). The tool directly opened the two-page PDF. Theorem 1.1, page 1, states that a polynomial map of the affine plane with nonzero constant Jacobian is an automorphism if its restriction to one affine line is injective. This is precisely the common-zero-fiber use in the moving proof. It is not invoked for merely base-dependent nonconstant Jacobians.
