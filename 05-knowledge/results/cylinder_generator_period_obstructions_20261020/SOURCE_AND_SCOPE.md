## Hostile boundaries retained

- Global generation is stronger than membership or generation after
  localization.
- A unit on the finite singular-point cover need not be constant. Matching
  its values across every node is the extra datum used in scalar descent.
- A constant-\(g\) exceptional fiber invalidates the no-pole argument.
  It is handled separately only for constant nonzero Jacobian, using
  the cited one-line theorem.
- The principal-conductor hypothesis is essential to the elementary
  recognition proof. For \(I=(x,W)\), the algebra
  \(\mathbb C[x,W]\oplus yI\) contains \(y^2=W\), but its conductor
  \((x,y^2)\mathbb C[x,y]\) is not principal.
- The monic quadratic element is an extra actual-source hypothesis.
  The same conductor support can encode different gluing or tangent data;
  the divisor alone does not supply this element.
