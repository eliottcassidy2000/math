## 2. Infinite families with their own complete route

The [terminal-lift package](collatz_terminal_lifts_20261007.md) consumes those
grounded words. For a checked word w taking a>1 to1, let

    U_w(n)=(P n+B)/Q,  P=3^L, Q=2^A,  Pa+B=Q.

Its whole native cylinder has the smaller-child rule

    a+2Q t -> 1+2P t,    t>=0.

Arbitrary children on this ray still need proof. But the following explicit
subfamily is fully grounded, with no further discovery:

    J=1+P s,
    n_s=a+4Q(4^(P s)-1)/(3P),
    U_w(n_s)=(4^J-1)/3,
    U((4^J-1)/3)=1,      s>=1.

The divisibility4^(P s)=1 mod3P makes n_s an integer in the native cylinder.
The final valuation is exactly2J. At s=0 the base word w alone is used,
so ROOT is never padded. This is an explicit specialization of the existing
inverse-word completion grammar; it supplies infinitely many **completed**
sources inside each of the37 representative rays. It does not prove that
their union covers every positive integer.

The certificate stores(a,w,s) and its exact formula, rather than trying to
expand a binary string of astronomical length. Source residues modulo m
are read by modular exponentiation modulo3P m **before** dividing by3P.
The API also recognizes membership in each displayed family. This is
lossless arithmetic storage of a proof, with an explicit parameter decoder.

For wider conditional guards, the compiler trims w to its first descent
and keeps its exact native residue and strict initial cut. On the37
representatives the cylinder widens by factors between2^17 and2^129.
These are cylinder-measure ratios, not universal coverage or speedups.
