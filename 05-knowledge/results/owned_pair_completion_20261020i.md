## 2. Three exact ways to retain, rather than invent, credit

[Temporal opposite matching](owned_pair_blocks_20261020i/README.md)
pairs the kth positive and negative arrivals within each actual terminal
and equal-vector antipodal class. This single matching maximizes the
number of mature disjoint opposite pairs at every horizon simultaneously.
Let Z_d be their source norm, and C_d the residual coherence after their
zero vectors are removed. Then I_d=C_d-Z_d. The available temporal
compression improves, but I_d and the physical energy do not change.

At N4096 the final matching has179,418 pairs and Z=587,964, out of the
626,688 bank. Its residual source norm is38,724. At horizon19 it matures
119,460 more norm credit than source-ID matching. The residual coherence
rises by the same amount, so this is not an additional budget. At the
completed horizon, C=386,290 and I=-201,674. The inherited N7/depth8
deficit13 remains, as it must.

[Sibling interaction guards](sibling_annulus_guards_20261020i/README.md)
restrict the inherited inverse-parent chains to one new annulus. There
are at most two children per parent, and no such pair is a zero packet:
their norms are unequal. Three consecutive near-sibling pair addresses
instead have interaction values -3,0,+3. They pay together at every
horizon exactly when the negative term arrives no later than the positive
one. At N4096,11,624 nonzero triple blocks meet this guard and8,852 do not;
676 additional blocks are identically zero. Their checked selected
interaction reaches -14,274 at horizon16 and is zero after completion.
All interactions between those pairs remain charged.

These two mechanisms can coexist because the nonzero selected sibling
pair addresses have unequal source norms, while opposite-pair addresses
have equal norms. If D_d<=0 is the guarded sibling contribution, define
J_d=I_d+Z_d-D_d. The remaining exact target is

    J_d<=M_d+Z_d-D_d.                                  (1)

This is a sharper description of what remains to pay, not a proof of(1).

[The common-observer compiler](sunflower_owned_support_20261020i/README.md)
preserves all specified terminal-wise charge and source-norm observers
through a fixed horizon. It has minimal linear dimension on the stated
arbitrary-rational-coefficient test domain. Native coefficients remain1;
the linear source bill is not confused with the squared norm of a
reweighted source. Immutable IDs, multiplicity and routes remain sidecars.

## 4. What the two external inputs contribute

The supplied Bucić–He–Lee paper, *Proof of the Erdős–Szemerédi sunflower
conjecture*, arXiv:2610.26686v1, supplies a useful method: construct one
common low-degree space by tuning auxiliary tester amplitudes and then
use a diagonal/rank bound. We use that method as input, not a new audit
of its headline theorem. The detailed extraction and literal transfer
tests are in the [common-observer note](sunflower_owned_support_20261020i/README.md).

Its free tester amplitudes cannot replace fixed physical Gram coefficients.
A native four-petal example makes the ownership boundary tangible: one
common source33435 pairs to zero with each of32955,33891,33897,33921,
but their distinct union has energy9 against source norm5. It is a
subpacket obstruction, not failure of the full N4096 bank. Another six-source
example has three individually safe temporal pairs whose simultaneous
exposures have energy27 against their total norm18. Support compression
must retain both owner multiplicity and the arrival boundary.

The linked [quasi-RH hillclimb repository](https://github.com/rohanarun/quasi-rh-hillclimb)
provides a complementary workflow. Its [what-if explorer](https://github.com/rohanarun/quasi-rh-hillclimb/blob/main/auto-research/whatif.py)
separates changes to active constraints from parameter adjustment;
its [certificate code](https://github.com/rohanarun/quasi-rh-hillclimb/blob/main/certify.py)
uses exact polynomial/interval certificates. We transfer that distinction:
the one-annulus sibling norm table is a structural obstruction, while the

alone does not remove either obstruction. No analytic zero-free estimate

## 5. Incoming work changes the next target

The incoming [native phase lifts](session18_native_phase_lifts/README.md)
provide synchronized three-source zero packets along64s+35 or64s+49,
with explicit sparse unit defects. They span two annuli and are compatible
with our one-annulus no-zero-pair theorem. Such a three-lift packet
contributing a new member must be mixed old/new in a single annulus.
Two old phase vectors sum to minus the new one: the selected old/new
interaction is exactly -2Q(new), while the packet increment is -Q(new).
We authenticate two literal instances at N16384, with new sources4136049
and4159715, arriving at depths1 and2. Their remaining old-core interaction
is explicit, and the old norms are never counted as new budget. These
old/new pair addresses are distinct from both new/new mechanisms above.
Sparsity of the phase defects
does not bound the number of short unmatched inverse chains or their
mutual terminal coherence.

The incoming [finite-observer clock theorem](clock_channels_20261021/FINITE_OBSERVERS_INTEGER_CLOCKS.md)
supplies an all-index hostile to replacing full clocks with residue
registers. Our common-observer theorem is deliberately horizon-specific;
it cannot authenticate an unbounded source history from a static label.

The next precise obligation is to control J_d in(1) for the unmatched
sources, reversed-arrival sibling blocks and cross-block interactions,
retaining the sparse phase defects and mixed baseline. In the odd-word
lane, the next target is a source-specific bound on the necessary terminal

Closing supplied children and proving eventual existence of such children
are different quantifiers. Neither is replaced by the number of successful
