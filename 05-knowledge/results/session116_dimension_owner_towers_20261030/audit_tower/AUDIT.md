# Independent audit of the Session116 sedenion theorem

**Verdict: PASS for the stated real 16D spectral/classification theorem and integral bad-prime criterion.** This audit does not certify originality or a classification of higher-dimensional zero divisors. The producer's theorem is a consolidation/refinement of known sedenion geometry, with an explicit spectrum and arithmetic transport.

## Proof audit

1. **Quaternion splitting.** Octonion alternativity implies that the subalgebra generated by a,b is associative. It embeds in a quaternion subalgebra H even in the commuting/real/zero cases. A unit perpendicular ell gives O=H+H ell with the prescribed convention. This is a change of orthonormal coordinates, so it preserves the spectrum. In the perpendicular block the coordinates are right coefficients `(u ell,v ell)`; the multiplication formula is `(u a+v conjugate(b),v conjugate(a)-u b)`. In particular, left multiplication on O is not silently treated as associative.
2. **Adjoint and spectrum.** For quaternion right multiplication R_h, the adjoint is R_conjugate(h). Computing the two output contributions gives first off-diagonal coefficient `b a-a b`, second `a b-b a`. Thus T(u,v)=(-vK,uK), K=ab-ba, T squared is |K|²I and tr T=0. If Delta>0 this gives four eigenvalues of each sign; if Delta=0, self-adjointness or the displayed K=0 gives T=0. The canonical H+H block contributes N eight times. This proves the complete multiset including x=0 and commuting cases.
3. **Determinant sign.** Squaring the determinant gives the square of Q=N^4(N²-4Delta)^2. The polynomial ring in16 real coordinates has no zero divisors, so det=Q or det=-Q identically; x=1 fixes the positive sign. This argument avoids an invalid continuity argument across the singular locus.
4. **Equality and full kernel.** All inequalities in `2sqrt(Delta)<=2|Im a||Im b|<=|Im a|²+|Im b|²<=N` are simultaneous equalities exactly when the real parts vanish, the imaginary parts are orthogonal, and their norms coincide. For nonzero x the common norm is positive. Exactly four Gram eigenvalues vanish. In the perpendicular block, solving gives v=-uab/r with r=|a|². In octonion notation the resulting d is b(ca)/r; these parentheses are indispensable. The canonical block is positive definite and supplies no additional kernel.
5. **Defect sign.** The inner-product identity gives `<da,b conjugate(c)>=<(da)c,b>` and `<ac,conjugate(d)b>=<d(ac),b>`. Therefore the defect is **plus** 2<[d,a,c],b> for associator (uv)w-u(vw). This exact simplification uses composition in O, not an identity asserted without inherited terms in higher stages.
6. **Integral specialization.** N and Delta are integer-coordinate polynomials. The determinant equality on all real inputs is a polynomial identity over Z, hence holds modulo every prime including2. Singularity is exactly p dividing N(N²-4Delta). No ordering or positive-definiteness argument is transported into finite fields; no finite-field rank classification beyond nonsingularity is claimed.
7. **The exceptional eight-dimensional subalgebra W.** Its product formula closes in W but is not the standard octonion product. For nonzero factors all a,s,c,t must be nonzero. First equation gives t=sac/|s|², and the second gives ac conjugate(a)=-|s|²c. Quaternion norms force |a|²=|s|²; right multiplication by a gives ac=-ca. Nonzero anticommuting real quaternions are pure imaginary and orthogonal. These implications reverse, giving the stated full relation within W. Its annihilator dimension2 does not conflict with the ambient dimension4.
8. **Higher dimensions.** Conjugation norm and quadratic identity persist recursively. The unital span of1,x is closed and associative (equivalently a quotient of a quadratic real polynomial algebra), proving power-associativity. A two-sided inverse need not imply injective multiplication; the explicit associator `[x^-1,x,y]=y` accounts for the failed cancellation. Embedding established witnesses is legitimate; extrapolating the16D determinant/classification is not.

## Independent finite-exact implementation

`check_independent.py` imports no producer code and implements recursive Cayley–Dickson multiplication directly. It checks 75 matrices, including the zero element, a unit, a characteristic2 bad-reduction example, all42 ordered distinct positive imaginary basis pairs, five commuting/collinear cases with real parts, and25 seeded random integral inputs. Determinants use rational Gaussian elimination rather than producer Bareiss elimination. It separately checks Gram cubics and traces, six-prime modular ranks, the explicit kernel coordinates, 200 arbitrary octonionic associator norm defects, and quadratic/conjugation identities through64 dimensions. The exhaustive 9^4 W bank includes all signed quaternion basis elements and zero in each slot, verifies the product formula, and verifies both directions of the nonzero-factor criterion.

Run `python3 check_independent.py` and `python3 -O check_independent.py`. Gates are explicit exceptions, not assert statements. Normal and optimized receipts are recorded separately. Finite tests corroborate the proof but do not replace it.

## Addendum: integer shell corollaries

**PASS.** Within W, the full cone criterion says exactly that a is imaginary and N(a)=N(s)=m>0: the second octonion coordinate s ell is already imaginary and perpendicular to a. Distinct choices of a,s give distinct elements. Hence ordered signed integer shell counts are exactly r_3(m)r_4(m), and Jacobi's formula gives 8r_3(m) sum_{d|m,4 does not divide d} d. Positivity is exactly Legendre's three-square criterion; these are classical external dependencies, not newly proved theorems.

In the full standard integral sedenion lattice, the set of squared norms of nonzero zero-divisor elements is exactly the positive even integers. Necessity follows from N(a)=N(b)=m with integer coordinates. For sufficiency, Lagrange gives m=p²+q²+r²+s²; embed a=(p,q,r,s,0,0,0) and b=(-q,p,-s,r,0,0,0) in imaginary O. Both have squared norm m, and their dot product vanishes. The full real cone criterion therefore applies. The requirement concerns all integral elements, not primitive ones. An integral nonzero annihilator also exists because an integer singular matrix has a rational kernel vector, which can be cleared of denominators.

For m=7, a=(2,1,1,1,0,0,0), b=(-1,2,-1,1,0,0,0) explicitly provide squared norm14 in the full algebra; the W shell is empty. Thus the modulo-eight obstruction is a slice obstruction, not a full-sedenion restriction.
