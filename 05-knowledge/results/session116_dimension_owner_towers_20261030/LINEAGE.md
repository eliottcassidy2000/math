## Classical dependencies and corrected interpretations

Cayley–Dickson zero-divisor geometry is classical. The [tower proof](tower/TOWER_DEFECT.md) fixes its convention and distinguishes zero-divisor elements, their annihilators, and zero-product pairs. Two-sided conjugation inverses and power-associativity persist at every standard doubling. The raw historical output claiming power-associativity is lost at32 is false; reassociation, rather than the inverse formula, is what fails in cancellation.

Jacobi's four-square count, Legendre's three-square exclusion and Lagrange's four-square existence are cited classical inputs. Their exact use and primary references are recorded in the [synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md) and [integer audit](audit_tower/AUDIT.md). No local formal-proof build is claimed.

The canonical octonions have no zero divisors. A noncanonical eight-dimensional subalgebra inside the sedenions can have them. Thus neither dimension alone nor an unmarked algebra isomorphism determines the arithmetic statement under discussion. Likewise modulo-four norm composition does not restore real division or associativity, and the extreme-Walsh unit subgroup is not a sedenion zero-divisor stratum.
