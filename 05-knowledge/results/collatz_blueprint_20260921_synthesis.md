## 1. The geometric claims have exact algebraic replacements

Use the shortcut C(n)=n/2 for even n and (3n+1)/2 for odd n. Its inverse
branches on positive integers are

```text
D(x)=2x,
E(x)=(2x-1)/3, only when x=2 mod3.
```

The blueprint's third map is exactly E(D(x)). These are valid inverse
maps with guards. They are not three independent transformations on the
three odd residue classes: D leaves that set, and modulo 6 alone does
not determine the division branch's output modulo 6. Input modulo 9
suffices for E and E composed with D; modulo 18 additionally retains
the input parity.

Matrices for the displayed maps are

```text
A1 = [[2,0],[0,1]],  A2 = [[2,-1],[0,3]],  A3 = [[4,-1],[0,3]].
```

The projectively invariant quantity is trace(A)^2/det(A), not the raw
trace or determinant. For A1, A2*A1, and A3*A1 it equals respectively
9/2, 49/12, and 121/24, all greater than 4. Thus the advertised parabolic,
order-four, and order-three classifications fail.

There is a stronger obstruction. The unrestricted real group generated by
D and E contains their commutator x |-> x-1/3 and its conjugates
x |-> x-1/(3*2^m). These nonidentity transformations approach the identity.
The group is not discrete, hence this is not a Fuchsian triangle-group
representation with the asserted generators. The blueprint supplies no
other map, topology, or covering construction connecting these branches
to an S6 monodromy theorem.

The [affine companion](collatz_blueprint_20260921_affine.md) gives proofs,
the exact legal-word model, and the odd-perfect-number audit. The loss
ledger is explicit: forgetting integrality guards allows real/rational
group operations that are not legal integer inverse trajectories.

## 2. Carry, not trace, decides whether a word closes at an integer

For a chronological word of r letters D,E containing m occurrences of E,
its affine map has the form

```text
W(x)=(2^r*x-B)/3^m.
```

Start with r=m=B=0. Appending D replaces B by 2B. Appending E replaces
B by 2B+3^m and increases m by one. All intermediate integer guards
hold precisely when 2^r*n=B mod3^m. For a positive cycle with m>0,

```text
n=B/(2^r-3^m) must be a positive integer.
```

Two words can have the same matrix spectrum and different cycle answers:
(E composed with D)^2 has numerator 16x-7 and denominator 9, fixing 1;
E composed with E composed with D composed with D has numerator 16x-5
and denominator 9, fixing 5/7. Their traces, determinants, and slopes
agree. The integral return differs because the carry differs.

There is a genuine binary/ternary connection here: each guarded word
bijects one positive source progression modulo 3^m with one positive
endpoint progression modulo 2^r. The latter is
y=-3^(-m)*B modulo 2^r. The 2^r words of length r partition all 2^r
endpoint residues, as forward parity decoding shows. This proves a finite
counting law; it supplies no time-frequency law on a specified orbit.

Even positive rational odd cycles are not unique. With denominators odd
and the usual rational 2-adic valuation,

```text
5/7 --k=1--> 11/7 --k=3--> 5/7
```

is exact. It is not an integer cycle or a counterexample to Collatz. It
pinpoints the necessary integrality condition missing from a bare appeal
to 2^S-3^m or to bounds for linear forms in logarithms.

## 3. The submitted energy fails, including at every fixed lookahead

Let u={log_2(x)} and

```text
A(u)=3/2+(1/2)cos(pi*cos^2(pi*u/2)),   V(x)=A(u)*ln(x).
```

The integer multiple of 2*pi in the submitted cosine disappears. On
every open dyadic interval with x>1,

```text
A'(u)=(pi^2/4)sin(pi*cos^2(pi*u/2))*sin(pi*u)>0,
V'(x)=[A(u)+(ln(x)/ln2)*A'(u)]/x>0.
```

At x=2^m, m>=1, the left limit is 2m*ln2 while the value is m*ln2.
Thus V has downward jumps, not the claimed continuous differential
evolution. The genuine accelerated step 19 -> 29 stays in (16,32), so
V(29)>V(19) by the displayed monotonicity, without numerical approximation.

**Stronger PROVED obstruction.** For every L>=1 and every m>=2L+4, put

```text
n=2^m+2^(L+1)-1.
```

Its first L accelerated steps all have halving exponent 1, and

```text
V(T^j(n))>V(n) for every 1<=j<=L.
```

The proof starts near a dyadic minimum of A. Its initial excess above
ln(n) is less than 7/128, while every step's log growth exceeds ln(3/2),
which is greater than 1/3. The profile always remains at least 1.
The [energy companion](collatz_blueprint_20260921_energy.md) proves these
bounds and distinguishes exact arguments from numerical illustrations.

This rules out this proposed energy as a certificate with any uniform
finite lookahead. It does not rule out convergence, an adaptive unbounded
stopping time, or every conceivable Lyapunov function.

## 4. What the density and word ideas actually imply

The density 6/pi^2 comes from the prime-square sieve product
prod_p(1-1/p^2). Its numerator is not derived from counting negative
cycles. In fact the earlier proof gives row-conditioned densities
9/pi^2 in rows 1 and 5 modulo 6, and 6/pi^2 in row 3. After the first
accelerated step, an orbit avoids row 3 entirely. None of these ambient
counting statements implies equidistribution along each individual orbit.

The existing [squarefree-prefix theorem](arithmetic_braids2_20260917_squarefree_symmetry.md)
is a stronger hostile than a random sample: arbitrarily long increasing
Collatz prefixes can have every intermediate odd node squarefree. Thus
neither squarefreeness nor the two floor identities that detect it supplies
a fixed-time descent mechanism.

**New PROVED joint strengthening.** For every fixed L there are infinitely
many starts for which all L+1 odd nodes are squarefree, every one of the
L steps increases integer height, and every subsequent energy value
exceeds the initial proposed energy. This is a genuine intersection
argument, not a conjunction of separate existence statements.

For the linear forms n_j=3^j*2^(L+1-j)*q-1, a short-interval squarefree
sieve has a positive limiting density when H=O(X) and H/sqrt(X)->infinity.
Take q intervals placing n_0 between 2^m and 2^m*(1+1/m). Their lengths
are of order 2^(m-L-1)/m, so the sieve applies, while the initial energy
excess is O(m^(-3)). The fixed positive log growth then dominates that
excess. Details, exact controls, and the independent audit are in the
[energy companion, Section 5a](collatz_blueprint_20260921_energy.md).
The start varies with L; no single infinite growing orbit is asserted.

Every finite halving word is realizable, and it can be completed into
each of the three known negative basins. Therefore a finite symbolic
prefix cannot separate those basins. The displayed gap word also contains
both 22 and 44. After recoding its letters as zero and one, these two
length-two blocks violate the balance property of mechanical/Sturmian
words. Repairing the ninth entry to the actual Wythoff gap still would
require a specified Collatz observable and a proved coding map; an
unexplained phase angle supplies neither.

Even a valid negative expected drift under a chosen probability law
would leave a quantifier gap before an every-integer statement. For
comparison, [Tao's theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562) controls orbit
minima for almost all starts in logarithmic density, for every diverging
comparison function. Its quantifiers are explicit and do not assert that
all starts converge.

## 6. The repaired proof target and its Lean boundary

For a positive odd orbit segment with halving exponents k_1,...,k_L, set
K_j=sum_{i<=j}k_i, K=K_L, and

```text
B=sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^(L-1-j)*2^K_j.
```

The inherited exact identity is

```text
2^K*T^L(n)=3^L*n+B.
```

Consequently the integer descent certificate is

```text
T^L(n)<n  iff  3^L*n+B < 2^K*n.
```

If 2^K>3^L, all realizations of this fixed word above B/(2^K-3^L)
descend. If 2^K<=3^L, no positive realization descends at this endpoint.
The remaining obligation is to prove that each fixed positive odd n>1
eventually acquires a word with positive margin. Finite-word realizability
does not give that statement for a specified n.

The [Lean package](../../04-computation/lean/CollatzBlueprintAudit/README.md)
uses the standard unaccelerated Collatz map on Nat. It proves, by strong
induction, the following equivalence for any map preserving positivity:

```text
(for every n>0, some iterate is 1)
  iff
(for every n>1, some positive-time iterate is below n).
```

It specializes this theorem to Collatz, with the descent hypothesis still
explicit. It also proves the submitted all-integer odd-residue premise
impossible, the corrected odd-integer version, the zero fixed-point
obstruction, the affine-identity descent equivalence, a certificate-to-descent
interface, and the three displayed signed return examples. Returning
to -1, -5, and -17 is not a proof that there are no other cycles.

The supplied Lean template was not a formal proof: its premise claims
that every integer has an odd residue modulo 6 (already false at 0), its
arbitrary-matrix trace constraints are inconsistent, its trajectory axiom
is neither an orbit definition nor a squarefree-frequency statement, and
the convergence theorem ends in sorry. Replacing that input by a false
axiom would make checking meaningless. The new package supplies neither
custom axioms nor a placeholder for the open result.
