## 2. Four advances

### A. LRC: the high-height flow problem disappears, but the mass problem does not

[Proof and controls](synthesis_20260905_lrc_sparse_transport.md).

For two interval families whose gaps are at least twice their respective
maximum interval lengths, every contact graph is a star forest and

```text
maximum transport capacity = sum over contacts min(endpoint capacities).
```

Finite intersections preserve this separation. Ordinary danger combs with
radius at most `1/6`, in particular the `1/14` LRC combs, satisfy it at every
height and arbitrary phases. This promotes THM-4409's previously finite-only
flow equality to an all-height structural theorem. A costly optimization
routine can be replaced by an explicit sum in the degree-zero certificate.

The retained raw lattice gives the stronger explanation

```text
true mass = sum_C min_pair K_pair(C),
best network = min_pair sum_C K_pair(C).
```

The remaining loss is the order of the minimum and summation: one pair may
not be optimal on every carrier. The exact hostile `(1,19,79)` has gap
`44/10507`, despite having no flow competition. Arbitrary nonnegative
weights also destroy the explicit-flow identity; bounded distortion is a
proved survivor. Neither raising a flow solver's height nor dropping the
carrier selector addresses the remaining universal `6/77` inequality.

### B. Sparse moments: complete trinomial tunability and two hidden carry coordinates

[Proof and controls](synthesis_20260905_moments_trinomial.md).

For primitive nonzero charges `{-a,b,c}`, `0<b<c`, put

```text
g=gcd(a+b,a+c), A=(a+b)/g, B=(a+c)/g.
```

The first support return has more than one multiplicity channel exactly when

```text
a-A*B belongs to the numerical semigroup <A,B>.
```

In that case the first mass is exactly `g`, and `2g<=a+c`. This resolves an
old requested characterization by replacing its false bounded-resonance
proposal with a complete semigroup criterion.

More information survives at every later level. If the canonical
representation is `a=A(Bh+r)+Bz`, `0<=r<B`, `0<=z<A`, then

```text
number of channels at mass kg = kh+floor(kr/B)+floor(kz/A)+1.
```

The two floor terms count exactly the channels lost by generating from the
first slice alone. All slices are generated from that slice iff `a` is a
multiple of `AB`; the free two-ray entry into THM-2639 is exactly `a=AB`.

Two historical claims are refuted. `{-3,1,9}` is tunable but is outside the
old `{-a,1,a+2}` list. An explicit tunable family has shortest signed integer
relation of norm `2B`, unbounded even with three charges. This is a mechanism
correction, not a refutation of noncancellation. The all-trinomial two-rung
coprimality assertion remains **OPEN**, with exact evidence on 2,275 collided
supports in the stated finite box.

### C. Integral observers: the complete-residue prime boundary is closed

[Proof and controls](synthesis_20260905_wildcard_smith_boundary.md).

For `p` nodes `a+p^e*u_i`, with the `u_i` distinct modulo `p`, the complete
two-jet p-primary Smith exponents are

```text
0,0,e,2e,...,(p-2)e,(p-1)e+1,
(p+1)e,...,(2p-2)e,(2p-1)e-1.
```

Empty ranges are omitted. The single modular derivative-rank loss has
saturation index exactly `p`, so it costs one unit of p-adic valuation, not
one geometric scale `e`. This proves every intermediate determinantal
divisor, rather than just total determinant or rank. Combined with the
existing local CRT decomposition, it extends the complete consecutive-node
range from `n<=p(p-1)` to `n<=p^2`.

The next boundary is genuinely different: `n=p^2+1` creates a second scale
inside a residue cluster. The incoming dyadic law, independently accepted
below, now handles `p=2,n=5`; odd primes and higher jets need further analysis.

### D. The exceptional triple: formal integration and a second-order failure

[Proof and controls](synthesis_20260905_transgression.md).

The inherited first-order gate is

```text
5h(-1)-18h(0)+13h(1)=0.
```

This session constructs the full endpoint/common-target correction system.
Adding the polynomial coefficient of `x` gives a six-by-six Jacobian of
determinant `-288`. Consequently any prescribed formal perturbation of the
other coefficients has a unique formal correction retaining the labelled
triple, at every order. The local coefficient locus is a smooth formal
hypersurface.

But tangent directions need not remain on that locus along straight lines.
The polynomial `h=4x^2-9x` passes the first-order gate and fails at second
order unless one adds the explicit nonzero `s^2*x` compensator in the report.
This supplies a sharp higher-order hostile to identifying an infinitesimal
motion with a moving graph. Formal coefficient series, a polynomial family
in the deformation parameter, and a descended primitive remain distinct.
