# Prime pairs: local symmetry, additive surplus, and the missing correlation

Research date: 19 October 2026.

This is a text-first research packet. Read `prime-pairs-structural-note.md` first.
It develops a general finite-group identity and its twin-center specialization,
including proofs, counterexamples, an unconditional rough-number theorem, and
precisely stated conditional transfers. Neither twin primes nor binary Goldbach
is claimed to be solved.

Files:

- `prime-pairs-structural-note.md`: the self-contained mathematical deliverable.
- `prime_pair_checks.py`: supporting exact checks, with explicitly labelled
  floating-point experiments. Run `python prime_pair_checks.py`.
- `prime_pair_checks.json` and `prime_pair_checks.txt`: its saved output.
- `literature-and-inheritance.md`: sources, status, and relationship to prior work.

No theorem ID has been reserved and no global-novelty claim is made. The useful
contribution is the combination of an explicit sum/difference map, a sum-free
forbidden-set formula for the internal-target advantage, arithmetic moments of
the inherited local weight, and a sharp account of what they do and do not prove.

The work is staged outside the repository checkout for separate review.
