## Scope

This is an independent mathematical review of the sibling packet
`session11-jacobian/TRACE_HYPERBOLIC_PACKET.md`. The review covers its local
valuation theorem, sharp integral-lattice repair index, finite-root Newton
selector, hostile examples, and the boundary between those statements and a
polynomial Keller-map application. It does not independently recertify the
entire inherited theorem collection. No repository files or Git state were
modified by this reviewer.

## Proof findings

1. **Escaping-sheet rigidity: PASS.** If fewer than m roots attained the
   greatest pole order, their first r leading power sums would vanish for
   r equal to that smaller number. Newton identities over the characteristic
   zero residue field would then kill their nonzero product. Hence all m
   escaping roots have the same pole order; their leading polynomial is
   Z^m-c, c nonzero. Its m-th power sum is mc, so a=-v(T_m)>0 is an integer
   and each escaping valuation is -a/m. The single integral root is essential
   to the stated degree and to the intended inverse-sheet application.

2. **Higher traces and Smith invariants: PASS.** The first m Newton identities
   give c_1,...,c_(m-1) integral and v(c_m)=-a. Substituting the integral root
   gives v(c_(m+1))>=-a. For m<=k<=2m-2, a term containing c_m uses an integral
   trace of order at most m-2, and a term containing c_(m+1) uses one of order
   at most m-3, apart from the initial Newton terms k c_k. Every other
   coefficient is integral, so induction gives v(T_k)>=-a. The scaled Gram
   matrix B=t^aG is integral; reversing its columns makes its residue matrix
   triangular with nonzero diagonal t^aT_m. Thus B is unimodular, every Smith
   exponent is -a, and v(det G)=-a(m-1). This uses more than the determinant
   alone and is not an unsupported inference from a determinant valuation.

3. **Quartic congruence and index: PASS.** The primitive vector (1,0) of B is
   isotropic modulo t and pairs by a unit with (0,1). The stated one-variable
   Hensel argument produces a primitive isotropic vector over R. Subtracting
   a multiple of that vector from its partner makes the partner isotropic;
   a unit rescaling gives the hyperbolic plane. No residue square root is
   needed. For a sublattice of index length ell, the Gram determinant changes
   by 2ell in valuation, proving ell>=a. Scaling one hyperbolic generator by
   t^a attains equality. The scalar-repair parity cost is correct.

4. **All-rank sharp index: PASS, including the reviewed strengthening.** The
   original argument over C[[t]] was valid. In fact the residue form of this
   particular B has the totally isotropic subspace spanned by the first
   floor((m-1)/2) monomials: every corresponding sum i+j is less than m.
   Its dimension is the maximal possible Witt index. Split a hyperbolic
   plane through one such residue vector and Hensel-lift it as in rank two;
   the residual maximal isotropic space descends to the orthogonal
   complement with dimension one smaller. Induction gives hyperbolic planes
   and at most one unit line over the original complete DVR. Consequently
   the sharp budget is ceil(a(m-1)/2) in the full stated residue-characteristic
   zero scope. An arbitrary unimodular form over an arbitrary residue field
   need not split this way; the special anti-triangular structure is the
   indispensable justification for the strengthening.

5. **Rational selector and Newton rate: PASS.** Dividing by c_m gives
   f(Z)=Z-r+A(Z), with r=-c_(m+1)/c_m integral and A in t^a R[Z] having no
   terms of degree zero or one. Hence f' is a unit on R. Completeness and
   Hensel's lemma give the unique integral root in K itself; this also follows
   from Galois invariance of the unique bounded root. That point does not
   imply rationality over the original global target field. Taylor expansion
   gives E_next>=2E+a, starting at E>=a, so E_n>=(2^(n+1)-1)a. The centered
   quartic coefficient-ratio formula has the correct signs and constants.

6. **Multiplicative-closure boundary: PASS.** The rank-two hostile has
   integral unimodular repaired Gram matrix but retains the polar h^2.
   The stronger full-rank hostile proposed during review is also exact:
   in K[Z]/(Z(Z^3-t^(-a))), the lattice generated by
   1,h,t^a h^2,t^a h^3 has Gram matrix

       [[4,0,0,3], [0,0,3,0], [0,3,0,0], [3,0,0,3]],

   of determinant -27. It contains 1 and the original h while h remains
   polar. It is not stable under multiplication by h, because h^2 would
   require coefficient t^(-a) on the third basis element. This sharply
   isolates multiplication closure, not just inclusion of the observable,
   as the missing condition. A finite R-module containing 1 and stable under
   multiplication by h does imply h integral by Cayley-Hamilton. These
   facts do not realize any polynomial Keller map.

7. **Other boundaries: PASS.** The mixed-characteristic example has
   T_p=1 despite p escaping roots of pole order 1/p; integer divisions really
   are load-bearing. The complex bilinear trace form is not a positive
   Hermitian form. The polygon/circle comparison is the exact holomorphic
   moment identity through degree m-1, and fails at degree m. The source
   monicity and finite-sheet-survival assumptions used for the Keller
   application match the cited inverse-trace packet. Polynomiality of the
   low traces, polynomial source realization, global selector gluing, and
   multiplication closure remain unproved obligations; no JC(2), new A4
   exclusion, or unconditional properness statement follows from this packet.

8. **Global quartic literature correction: PASS as a cited input.** The final
   note correctly supersedes the inherited description of planar quartic A4
   as globally open. I independently opened the
   [primary publisher page for Domrina (2000)](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im273)
   on 20 October 2026. Its abstract expressly states the exclusion of
   four-sheeted polynomial self-maps of the complex plane with nonzero
   constant Jacobian. The repository's `CORE-PAPERS-PLANAR-JC.md` records an
   earlier full-PDF check of the generic-preimage degree definition and
   distinguishes the restricted 1998 paper from this general theorem. I did
   not independently reprove or reread that entire paper. The final note
   treats the quartic formulas as local controls and points to higher-degree
   conditional applications without claiming a first open generic degree.

## Independent computational design

`independent_controls.py` does not import or execute the producer's verifier.
It constructs multiplication matrices by polynomial remainder for 24
non-binomial families (Z-b)q(Z), with m=2,...,7, a=1,2 and b=1,2;
q(Z)=Z^m+sum_(j=1)^(m-1)(j+1)Z^(m-j)-t^(-a). Their Newton polygon gives m
escaping roots while b is an exact finite root. The checks compare matrix
traces with all needed Newton identities, test the scaled Gram matrix and
direct determinant, and test the finite-selector error.

Further controls compare exact rational Newton iterates with the known
finite root through n=2 in four quartic families, exhaust all diagonal
repair allocations for ranks 1,...,5 and a=1,...,4, verify the full-rank
hostile at a=1,...,5, and check the centered quartic ratio symbolically.
All assertion checks survive Python -O. Finite controls supplement the
uniform proof audit; they do not constitute a finite search for Keller maps.
