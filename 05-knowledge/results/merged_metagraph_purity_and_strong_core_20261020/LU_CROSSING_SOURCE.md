# Primary-source check: Lu's crossing theorem

Xiaoyun Lu, *On king–serf pair in tournaments*, Discrete Mathematics 343(7) (2020), 111903.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2020.111903
Primary indexed HTML: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0012365X20300959

Checked 2026-10-20. The primary indexed HTML exposes the abstract, Theorem7, and its proof. Direct opening returned HTTP403; search retrieval returned those sections. No full-PDF access is claimed.

Exact short excerpt: “T4 is the only strong X-hamiltonian crossing tournament.”

Mathematical restatement of Theorem7: let X and its complement partition V(T), and let V_1,...,V_k be the strong components, ordered with V_i dominating V_j for i<j. For T different from the order-four exception,

[every Hamiltonian path has one endpoint in each part]
iff
[(V_1 subset X and V_k subset V\\X) or (V_1 subset V\\X and V_k subset X)].

The exceptional construction has two vertices in each part and a directed alternating four-cycle. Thus a strong crossing tournament has order4. The application here only needs exclusion at order>=6, since order4 is excluded separately by the rotation2 automorphism.

The indexed proof ends its strong-case paragraph with a displayed non-equality involving T4 that appears inconsistent with its conclusion. The theorem, abstract, and introductory explicit uniqueness statement agree; the argument here cites that published theorem, not a repaired transcription of the proof.
