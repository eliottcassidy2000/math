# Preserve the predicate; escape the finite forbidden observations

**Status: proved research move in this packet; analogy elsewhere requires new hypotheses.**

Source: two labelled carriers with required edges. Target: a single carrier whose cycle language is the exact product. Map: two square injections, followed by deleting the ports and inserting exactly two cross-edges. Preserved predicates: square adjacency, source ownership, port-conditioned cycle extension, individual weights and branch clocks. Lost without a sidecar: original scale, owner partition and port orientation. Cheapest decisive test: check the two seam sums *and* intersections of the two scaled vertex sets.

The first algebraic solution failed ownership: it forced one port endpoint from each source to coincide. The repair is not to accept approximate or unmarked copies. It is to place that solution in a rational group, retain the exact square-class coset, prove an infinite sequence of distinct observations, and count how many observations the fixed sources can forbid. The binary valuation calculation supplies the infinite-order certificate. The finite forbidden-ratio set supplies the entry budget.

This move is useful when the target predicate is preserved by an explicit orbit and each source defect forbids only finitely many distinct observations. It is inapplicable when the source changes during the search without a paid defect budget, when orbit observations repeat, or when an infinite family of source defects has been silently treated as finite. This is precisely why neither a Collatz convergence claim nor universal tournament coverage follows from the present construction.

The Farey-rectangle compression is a second, more concrete lesson. Large numerical labels need not require expensive refactorization when their source charts and exact scales are retained. The representation compresses the collision predicate; it does not identify the owners or their weights. It complements packet h's tournament state reduction, where neutral padding can be forgotten for a restricted recurrence only if its position is retained for later re-marking.

This local card is not promoted to a general meta-theorem spanning unrelated targets. The preserved predicates and the missing hypotheses are recorded explicitly for future tests.
