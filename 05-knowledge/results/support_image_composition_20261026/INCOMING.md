## 2. A new explicit realization obstacle, with its repair

The [Walsh realization note](../session72_even_graph_unifying_carriers_20261026/session72-root/WALSH_REALIZABILITY.md)
transports positive tournament laws by `m=M^T p`. Their cone is
`K=M^T R_+^X`, generally not the standard coordinate orthant. At order four,
its explicit coordinates obey `|a|<=3m0+c` and `|b|<=m0−c` (normalized mass
sets `m0=1`). Full tournament converse sends `b` to `−b`, preserving this
cone although its coordinate matrix has a negative diagonal entry.
Independent basis-sign changes cannot remove that entry; returning to
point-mass coordinates can. This is full converse, not path complement.

Thus signed coordinates do not automatically supply probabilities on graph
types. In our card rule, the sum over an even child's root transports gives
orbit mass, while an odd child's signed witnesses cancel. In the Hamilton
defect rule, nonnegative coefficients make zero mean absence of new paths.
The incoming cone makes this distinction concrete: retain both the exact
operator and the admissible cone before interpreting a linear equivalence
as positive object dynamics. The full Walsh cone is simplicial; this note
does not import any separate projected-cone minimality theorem.
