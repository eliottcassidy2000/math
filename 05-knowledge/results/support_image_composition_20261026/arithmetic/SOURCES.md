# Source receipt and exact computation scope

Read 26 October 2026. All C1–C5 proofs are given in the note; no unpublished
asymptotic, spectral hypothesis, or global undecidability claim is needed.

## Classical mechanism

Jeffrey C. Lagarias, *The 3x+1 Problem and its Generalizations*, American
Mathematical Monthly 92 (1985), 3–23; DOI
[10.1080/00029890.1985.11971528](https://doi.org/10.1080/00029890.1985.11971528).
Read the author-linked CECM online version's
[stopping-time section, Theorem B](https://www.cecm.sfu.ca/organics/papers/lagarias/paper/html/node4.html),
and its [ergodic-theory section](https://www.cecm.sfu.ca/organics/papers/lagarias/paper/html/node10.html).
These credit the finite parity-vector mechanism to Terras and discuss the
unique residue class attached to a finite word. The latter section explicitly
separates a 2-adic statement from the positive-integer question. Some displayed
formulas are image-based in the HTML extraction; our proof and checker do not
depend on reconstructing an unread formula. The arbitrary-modulus relation,
path cylinder and odd-refinement criterion are proved directly here. No
priority claim is made for these elementary consequences.

The same source's [decidability section](https://www.cecm.sfu.ca/organics/papers/lagarias/paper/html/node12.html)
concerns generalized periodically piecewise-linear systems. We use it only
to keep that scope distinct from classical Collatz, not as a new reduction
or a proof of any halting assertion. A fetched 1990 Acta Arithmetica PDF was
unavailable (HTTP403) and is not a dependency.

## Finite universe and independent routes inside the producer

The standard-library checker uses explicit runtime checks, not assertions.

- Every clock modulus1..18 and path length0..7: graph-path enumeration is
  compared with all43605 source classes moduloM·2^L, generated by literal
  integer iteration. Each path also has a separately reconstructed one-bit
  lifting certificate and a checked affine evaluator.
- Every divisor pair M|N≤64 and every fine start: outgoing-arrow fibre
  sizes are checked against the exact odd-ratio criterion. This gives161
  covering pairs and119 noncovering pairs.
- Deterministic shrinking-precision maps are checked at M≤12,L≤6;
  every binary word of length≤7 is checked at every concatenation cut.
- Actual ordinary Collatz is replayed against12 shortcut steps from each
  positive source≤200. The modulo7 parallel-arrow/clock witness and generic
  quotient-composition failure are explicit hostile controls.
- The two signed boundary paths are checked at M≤13,L≤8. Exact positive
  interval counts are compared against direct enumeration at M≤8,L≤4 and
  seven named heights, including height equal to the class modulus.
- Three complete finite-height sieve/trace products use cutoffs3,5,7 and
  heights22,46,118, independently comparing direct trial-division twin tests.
- The recovered Berggren family is checked for M=1..100, including its
  Euclid guard and the full-matrix determinant distinction at odd M>1.
