Cheap probes included local compiler matrices through order6, face-preserving
products through four operations, exact unit-height cuts, and converse of
H-neutral sources. The larger matrix-product enumeration found no proof-level
extension of S134's short-bank spectral obstruction and is not a dependency.
The useful turn was replacing enumeration by actual transitive rays, which
force matrix positivity, then using SCC factorization to recover the arithmetic
hidden by that cone.

A dual/reversal repair was probed and not adopted as a four-state operation:
empty-cut transitive parents of orders1 and2 both have native state(1,1,0,0),
whereas converse sources have q=1 and3. This is consistent with S136's already
proved context-profile boundary; it is not promoted as a new finite-dimensional
impossibility theorem.

The resulting exact integer obstructions do not exclude unrestricted sources,
unbounded parent-height phases, positive L, re-marking arbitrary old vertices,
changing old arcs, or nonuniform old-parent attachments. Those are genuine
remaining escape routes. No universal acceptance proof was found. The fixed-height face then strengthened to a full fixed-(h,L) actual-source
normal form: internal singleton padding is paid by an even-path pair weight,
and unselected suffix blocks commute at the four-state level. This gives an
exact finite-base/dyadic target inverse for every fixed h,L. The packet
stops after universal proofs, independent literal audits, matching optimized
receipts and portable replay; exploratory matrix tables are excluded.
