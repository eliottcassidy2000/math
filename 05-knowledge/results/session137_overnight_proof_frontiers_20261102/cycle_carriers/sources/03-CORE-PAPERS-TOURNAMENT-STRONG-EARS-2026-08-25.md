## Exact imported role

Moon's Theorem 1 gives vertex-pancyclicity of strong tournaments. Theorem 2
proves `s(n,k)=n-k+1` for the minimum number of induced strong
`k`-subtournaments in a strong `n`-tournament. At `k=n-1`, every strong
tournament of order at least four has at least two strong one-vertex
deletions. That specialization is the cited input for all-order strong-ear
reducibility.
