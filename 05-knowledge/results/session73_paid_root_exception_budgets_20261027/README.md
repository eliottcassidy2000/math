## What is established

- **The native ROOT phase pays an all-depth bill.** The
  [ROOT theorem](session73-root-forcing/REPORT.md) proves
  `E = Σ W(m)/(6m+1) ≤ 13/57` under the original native source probability
  and actual branch-parity gain. Erasing that phase makes the first-step bill
  infinite. This applies to the eventual ROOT-supported profile and retained
  authenticated paths—not to arbitrary finite zero extensions, whose frontier
  bill is infinite. The same native probability has infinite expected literal
  first-hit time despite its inherited finite mean odd-step lifetime.
- **Beyond Paley7:** [shared-port grammar](session73-ports/REPORT.md) proves that
  one legal path complement cannot take a forest-framed tournament to Paley11.
  The feedback-arc tariff is `n−3` for `n≥4`; the forest one-step ceiling is
  `2n−4`, below Paley11's proved lower bound19 (exact finite minimum20).
- **Linear descriptions can have quadratic exception debt.** The C3 towers and
  inherited `P7[C3^k]` family have exact minimum edge debt `n(n−1)/6`; their
  nontrivial connected cases have cycle debt `(n²−7n+6)/6`. At vertex orders
  `7,21,63`, the latter debts are `1,50,589`. These are vertex orders, not raw-H
  values. The [compiler bound](session73-root/COMPILER_CLOCK_LOWER_BOUND.md)
  separately prices any realization in the narrower monotone J/star/complement
  language. It does not establish that a realization exists.
- **Changing moduli preserves neither discarded correlation nor free time.**
  The [precision/Gram theorems](session73-moduli/REPORT.md) give least exact
  guarded-affine precision, sharp same-source weighted point-evaluation bounds,
  CRT marginal rank and a two-debt split for aliases versus lost joint incidence.
  Actual source masses and shared endpoint tuples remain intact. Collatz P2 is
  for the general odd map U, not the killed first-hit ROOT process: first-hit
  exclusions are additional guards, and shortcut phase `S` is not literal time
  `h+S`. None of these observation formulas proves native ROOT positivity.
- **The two gap pairs do not identify image grammars.**
  [Arithmetic](session73-arithmetic/REPORT.md) proves that target-only aliquot
  compression fails under every common fresh-prime extension of unequal sources.
  The exact coprime carrier is `(n,s(n))`, not `s(n)` alone. Any fixed finite
  prime-extension core bank, even supplemented by all squarefull sources,
  reaches relative-density-zero prime targets. This limits that bank; it does
  not make the omitted targets untouchable. Raw-H attained values are closed
  under multiplication; aliquot-touchable values are not.

The weighted substitution law keeps pair-factor vertex costs distinct from
source probabilities. The shared-port cycle identity uses the component-incidence
**multigraph**: distinct parallel physical port pairs cannot be collapsed.
The arithmetic omission law prices discarded divisor mass, not just term count.
