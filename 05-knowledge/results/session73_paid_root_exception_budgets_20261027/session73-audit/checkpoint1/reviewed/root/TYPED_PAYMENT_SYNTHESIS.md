# Typed payment: what a compositional theorem must retain

Working synthesis for Session73. This organizing statement is an elementary storage-identity principle, not a claim of priority for dissipative systems or a universal solution of the repository's open problems. The concrete ROOT, graph and arithmetic instantiations are supplied and audited separately.

## A compositional payment lemma

Fix the original source set S and nonnegative source masses mu_s. For each source, follow its actual finite guarded word. At transition j suppose exact boundary states x_(s,j) and actual input/output ports u_(s,j),v_(s,j) satisfy

E_(s,j+1)(x_(s,j+1)) + Q_(s,j)(v_(s,j))
 = E_(s,j)(x_(s,j)) + P_(s,j)(u_(s,j)),

with nonnegative forms. The internal boundary state and its form must be the SAME object on the outgoing side of one transition and the incoming side of the next. Then multiplication by the ORIGINAL mu_s and summation telescopes exactly over every finite word. For a countable family of completed finite words, Tonelli sums the nonnegative initial/input and terminal/output sides, even when word lengths vary. For genuinely infinite trajectories, exact passage to a terminal identity additionally requires justified convergence of the terminal storage term; finite total initial/input budget always gives the corresponding output bound by nonnegativity and monotone convergence. No unjustified subtraction of two infinite sums is used. No replacement of source masses, independent resampling of ports, reinitialization of carries, or inserted dynamical microsteps is allowed by this lemma.

This statement supplies a checkable interface contract, not the missing identities or budgets. If two adjacent forms or states do not match, their discrepancy is an extra junction term; it cannot be silently canceled. For linear identifications B, the mismatch of storage forms is the quadratic form B*P_next B-P_previous. A sufficient upper budget must dominate its positive contribution. An exact signed or unitary gauge is allowed only when the transition and every observed port are transported together.

Integer clock increments form a separate additive ledger. A finite energy sum does not bound their sum unless an explicit inequality prices time in the energy terms. Observation precision likewise changes only the observation ledger unless the native dynamics actually performs a transition.

## Why all four coordinates are needed here

1. **Phase and source weight.** The actual signed ROOT profile becomes cheap under its native branch-parity gain, while forgetting that phase creates a divergent forcing bill under the same original source measure. The finite payment is a statement on the actual ROOT-supported profile. It supplies neither positive mass at an unproved source nor a bound for a zero-extended finite approximation.
2. **Boundary occurrence and connectivity.** For exception graphs, the correct junction debt is the cycle rank of the component-incidence MULTIGRAPH. Parallel physical port pairs cannot be merged. Pairwise visible components without their shared occurrence labels can miscount a cycle as a tree.
3. **Representation and operation language.** A constant-arity substitution tree has linear description size for families whose best total-rank exception graph has quadratic debt. This does not make their construction cheap in the smaller native J/star/complement language: the compiler-potential theorem imposes a separate growing complement bill whenever such a construction exists.
4. **Joint observation and realizability.** CRT identifies an integer residue from the JOINT tuple of residues. It does not reconstruct a joint source law from separately aggregated modular tables. The latter needs its exact Gram matrix and unresolved evaluation norm. A larger product modulus cannot retroactively supply discarded correlations.

These are not interchangeable notions of complexity. The decisive tests in this session deliberately hold some ledgers fixed while another changes: same source masses but phase erased; same child cycle counts but port edges merged; same tournament but a different representation baseline; same marginal observations but different source evaluation.

## The gap-pair question has a structural answer, not an isomorphism

There is a genuine7→21→63 tournament construction by P7[C3^k], with exact rank and cycle debts. This uses those numbers as VERTEX ORDERS, not as Hamilton-path counts. Separately, raw-H excludes7 and21, while the positive aliquot image contains7=s(8) and21=s(18).

The two scalar image grammars do not have the same multiplication law. Ordered tournament join makes H multiplicative. Coprime multiplication of integers requires (n,s(n)), with product (ab,a s(b)+b s(a)+s(a)s(b)); target s alone is not a congruence. Every common fresh-prime extension separates unequal sources in one aliquot fiber. Thus the source coordinate is the exact missing sidecar, not a numerical exception that can be repaired by matching the lists {7,21} and {2,5}.

A fixed finite bank of prime-extension cores, even with all squarefull sources, misses relative-density-one prime targets. This is a limitation of that bank, not a proof those targets are untouchable. A complete prime-target grammar would need genuinely growing cores or a new coverage mechanism, with its primality and coprimality guards retained.

## Remaining native gates

- Positivity at every named Collatz source, and the original spatial folded ROOT current estimate, are not consequences of a finite phase-aware pathwise forcing bill.
- Exact integer-clock integrability and finite-profile approximation require their own boundary budgets.
- A uniform linear relative-exception budget for the narrower reachable native tournament class remains open; the unrestricted substitution-family obstruction does not prove accessibility.
- The prime-untouchable uniqueness assertion and full raw-H spectrum classification remain separate. No reduction between these open assertions is claimed.

The proposed unifying research rule is precise: identify the actual source and allowed context, retain the boundary data that context reuses, and prove a payment inequality in the original measure before compressing or changing precision. Matching small numbers is useful for choosing a hostile test, not for bypassing this contract.
