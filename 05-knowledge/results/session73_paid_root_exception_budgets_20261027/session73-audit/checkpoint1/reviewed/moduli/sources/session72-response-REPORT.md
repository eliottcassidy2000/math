## 2. General reset–action marginal theorem

Let X be a nonempty finite set, q:X→Q a surjection, and M a monoid of
transformations of X, containing the identity. It is enough to take the finite
transformation monoid generated by the permitted deterministic operations.
Also permit any collection of reset kernels K for which the **entire row on
X** depends only on q(x):

    K(x,z) = κ(q(x),z).                                      (1)

For stochastic kernels, rows are probability distributions. For the linear
statement below, rows may instead be field-valued counted or weighted vectors.
Coefficients and linear ranks must all use the same declared field F. A normalized rational reset may be reduced to a positive-characteristic field only when every required denominator is invertible; the every-field incidence rank below does not assert that 1/deg remains defined in bad characteristic.
Define

    e(x) = (q(m x))_(m∈M),       E = e(X).

Let D be the observation-incidence matrix with columns indexed by E and rows
indexed by (m,a)∈M×Q:

    D[(m,a),e] = 1{e_m=a}.                                  (2)

### Theorem A: three exact carriers

**A1. Set carrier.** The coarsest exactly lumpable partition preserving q and
all allowed deterministic actions and resets is equality of e(x). This is the
monoid version of inherited U2; U2 itself is not reproved as a new discovery.

**A2. Terminal-only linear carrier.** Suppose an experiment chooses a finite
word of actions/resets in advance and observes only the final q. It does not
record intermediate observations, condition later actions on them, postselect,
or copy the same hidden state into two tests. For arbitrary finitely supported
signed input laws, the smallest exact linear response representation has

    dimension rank_F D.                                    (3)

This is the state-to-all-terminal-response map for all initial laws, not the
Hankel rank of one scalar process with one fixed initial law.

It is represented by Dν, where ν=e_*μ is the input law on E. Every allowed
operation descends to a linear update on this response. The terminal law is
recovered by selecting the identity-action rows. In particular, equality of
Dν means equality of every terminal response for every finite operation word,
not just equality of two present marginals.

**A3. Products and positive realization.** The unital pointwise algebra
generated by the rows of D is the full function algebra F^E. Over R, the exact
cone of realizable nonnegative responses is

    C = D(R_+^E),                                           (4)

and normalized responses form conv{Dδ_e:e∈E}. Every edge/signature column is a
distinct extreme ray of C and a distinct vertex of the normalized polytope.
Consequently C is linearly equivalent within its span to a standard orthant
if and only if rank D=|E|. The polytope has dimension rank D−1 and is a simplex
if and only if rank D=|E|.

### Proof

For A1, any stable partition refining q must preserve q(m x) for every m,
so it refines e. If e(x)=e(y), deterministic h sends them to states with equal
signatures, since q(m h x)=q(m h y). Reset rows at x,y are identical by (1),
so their pushforwards to any partition, in particular E, are identical.
Thus e is both necessary and sufficient. The argument concerns exact
lumpability, not all possible weaker stochastic trace equivalences.

For A2 let W be the span of functions 1{q(m x)=a}. Deterministic precomposition
preserves W because M is a monoid. For a reset and **any** function f on X,

    (Kf)(x) = Σ_z κ(q(x),z) f(z)

is a function of q(x), hence belongs to W. Thus W is invariant under the
pullbacks of every allowed operation. It contains every terminal observable,
so every terminal operation-word observable belongs to W. Conversely every
generator 1{q(m x)=a} is an actual terminal experiment using m alone. The
exact span is therefore W, whose dimension is rank D. Any linear summary
recovering every terminal response must contain these independent linear
functionals, proving the lower bound; Dν attains it and its kernel is stable
under forward law updates. No nonnegative-coordinate assertion is used.

For A3, the row functions separate E by its definition. The product over m
of 1{e_m is its specified value} is exactly the indicator of any specified
signature e. These point indicators span F^E, over any field. For the real
cone, a column has exactly one 1 in every m-block. A nonnegative combination
of other columns yielding that column must have zero entries outside its
support. Each summand would then match every coordinate of e and be the same
column. This proves extremality and distinctness. The identity-action block
sums to 1 on every column, so the cone is pointed and a probability section
has affine dimension rank D−1. A pointed cone of dimension d with exactly
s extreme rays is linearly isomorphic to R_+^d exactly when s=d. The same
argument gives the simplex statement. ∎

**Important scope.** The positivity claim concerns an exact faithful image
of *all* realizable responses, not a generic lower bound for every hidden
positive realization that may introduce spurious states. Abstract rank
compression may have signed matrix entries. The actual cone (4), not a
coordinate orthant chosen for convenience, is what those matrices preserve.

## 3. Involution specialization: cycles are exactly the lost joint law

Assume M={id,τ} with τ²=id. Then

    E={(q(x),q(τx)):x∈X}

is symmetric under swapping coordinates. Make the bipartite simple graph B
with vertex sets Q_L,Q_R and one edge a_L b_R for each (a,b)∈E. There are no
isolated vertices because q is onto. Let c(B) be its number of components.
The matrix D is its unsigned vertex-edge incidence matrix.

### Theorem B: sharp cycle repair and positivity boundary

Over every field,

    rank D = 2|Q|−c(B),
    dim ker D = β(B) = |E|−2|Q|+c(B).                        (5)

A spanning forest F of B gives an explicit optimal linear sidecar: retain
ν(e) for every chord e∈E\F. Its β(B) coordinates, together with Dν, recover ν
uniquely by leaf elimination on F. No lower-rank linear sidecar can recover
all ν. This last minimality is the inherited THM-840 §1a applied with
R=D and next observation N=id; the new calculation identifies its exact
kernel and a canonical combinatorial choice of repair coordinates here.

Every even cycle supplies two distinct probability laws with identical
terminal responses: put equal mass on alternate cycle edges. These laws
can nevertheless be distinguished by a two-time observation of the **same**
state, q before and q after τ. These joint observations give every edge
indicator 1{q=a}1{qτ=b}, and hence recover all ν.

The cone C is simplicial exactly when B is a forest. When β(B)>0, the minimal
linear response space is strictly smaller than its number of positive extreme
rays: no basis change in that smaller space turns exact realizability into
independent nonnegative coordinate constraints.

### Proof

Multiply all right-shore rows of D by −1 to obtain an oriented incidence
matrix (in characteristic two this is the same matrix). A dependence among
its rows has a coefficient constant along every graph edge, hence constant
on each component; the row-kernel has dimension c(B). This proves (5) over
all fields. In a forest, remove a leaf: the marginal at that vertex determines
the only remaining incident edge coefficient. Repeat. Thus the forest columns
are independent and every chord creates one independent alternating cycle
kernel vector, with its own chord coefficient 1 and other chord coefficients
0. This proves the repair and gives a basis of the entire lost space.
Alternating cycle edges have equal total mass and equal vertex marginals;
normalizing produces the stated probability laws. The cone assertion follows
from A3 and (5). ∎

### Exact probability feasibility, not just linear compatibility

For nonnegative left/right marginal vectors p,r with common total mass 1,
a probability ν supported on E exists exactly when

    p(A) ≤ r(N(A))       for every A⊆Q_L,                  (6)

where N(A) is the right neighborhood in B. Necessity follows by summing edge
mass leaving A. For sufficiency, use the finite network with capacities p_a
from source to a_L, r_b from b_R to sink, and capacity 2 on every allowed
middle edge. A cut of capacity less than 1 cannot cut a middle edge. For cuts
with left set A on the source side, its least possible capacity is
1−p(A)+r(N(A)), which is at least 1 by (6). The elementary max-flow/min-cut
criterion gives a unit flow, whose middle-edge weights are ν. This is the
standard finite transportation criterion, not a new flow theorem.

The full fiber over feasible marginals is

    (ν₀ + ker D) ∩ R_+^E.                                 (7)

If ν₀ is strictly positive on every edge, its relative dimension is β(B).
At boundary marginals it may be smaller. The normalized response affine
dimension is rank D−1; this removes the known total-mass coordinate but does
not remove any of the β(B) cycle directions.

A tiny symmetric forest already refutes “nonnegative marginal coordinates
and total mass are enough.” Let E={(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)} and p=r=(0,1). The unique
signed edge solution is (−1,1,1), so no probability realization exists. Its
Hall violation is A={1}: p(A)=1 but r(N(A))=r({0})=0. This illustrates why even
an injective linear decoder still needs its positivity predicate.

## 6. Reversible test-return upgrades guard separation to full terminal rank

There is a precise reason that named re-marking has more linear expressive
power than anonymous reset: it can **test a guard and restore the same hidden
state on success**. No intermediate observation is needed for this upgrade.

### Theorem C: test-return projection law

Let X be a finite set of valid states and ⊥ an absorbing failure state. For
each guard g_i in a finite Boolean guard family on X, suppose the permitted
operation alphabet contains
a partial test P_i and a recovery U_i satisfying

    U_i P_i(x) = x if g_i(x)=1, and ⊥ if g_i(x)=0.

Both operations fix ⊥. Use only the composites F_i=U_i P_i and observe
terminal alive/dead, starting with arbitrary laws on the **valid states X**.
Then the terminal-alive linear response space over any field is exactly the
unital pointwise algebra generated by the g_i. Its dimension is the number
of distinct realized guard signatures. If the guards separate X, its dimension
is |X|.

*Proof.* After a sequence F_i1,...,F_ir the original state survives unchanged
exactly when all r guards pass. Its terminal-alive function is therefore
∏_j g_ij. Repeated guards have no effect and the empty word gives 1. These
positive products span the generated algebra: each product containing
complements 1−g_i expands as a linear combination of positive products.
For any realized guard signature, the product of its true guards and the
complements of its false guards is exactly the indicator of that signature
cell. Thus the span has one independent indicator per cell, over every field.
No other terminal-alive function of the restricted test-return alphabet
splits such a cell. An infinite guard family can be reduced to a finite one
with the same partition, by choosing one distinguishing guard for each pair
of distinct signatures on finite X. ∎

This is a statement about **all initial laws**, not the scalar process from
one fixed state. It counts valid states only. If ⊥ is admitted as an additional
initial state, terminal-alive functions vanish there; observing its failure
mass as an independent coordinate requires a declared total-mass convention.
For unrestricted signed laws on X∪{⊥}, including both alive and dead outputs
adds the missing constant and gives one additional independent coordinate.
The restoration and absorbing-failure hypotheses are essential. Merely having
separating Boolean measurements does not supply their products as legal
terminal experiments.

### An all-order degree obstruction and a falsifiable sharpness question

A raw guard on X_n is a product of at most n−1 literals in the M free arc
bits: each required adjacency of p is either a fixed path arc, an impossible
reversed fixed arc, or one free bit/complement. A product of at most r guards
therefore has multilinear Boolean degree at most r(n−1). The degree-M monomial
is independent of every lower-degree multilinear polynomial on {0,1}^M,
over every field (the Boolean evaluation matrix is triangular with diagonal 1).
Consequently, for n≥2, full test-return response requires

    r ≥ ceil(M/(n−1)) = ceil((n−2)/2).                     (8)

This is a bound on the number of restored guard tests, not the number of
individual re-marking operations, and not the order of a block marginal in
the separate native zeta constructions. At n=1,2 the valid state set is a
singleton and the empty word suffices.

A portable additional order-six check reaches full rank 1024 over F₂ with
actual products of at most two guards. It records 1024 selected products by
lexicographic permutation indices and independently re-eliminates their
actual 0/1 rows using the opposite pivot order. Their determinant is odd,
hence nonzero over Q. Thus the bound (8) is sharp through n=6 over Q and F₂.
No every-field order-six two-test result is claimed without a unit determinant
or other suitable certificate. The all-order eventual full-rank theorem remains
every-field independently of this short-length experiment.

**CONJECTURE, not proved:** over Q, for every n≥3, products of at most
ceil((n−2)/2) named permutation guards span the full function space on X_n.
The finite data through six support it. A rank-deficient matrix at the
predicted depth would refute it; n=7 is the next case and requires at least
three tests. No rank growth extrapolation is used as evidence for a proof.

## 7. The broader compositional theorem, correctly attributed and typed

Here is the usable grand framework behind the examples, rather than a claim
that tournaments and dissection spaces are isomorphic.

Take finitely many sorts, finite-dimensional vector spaces V_s with declared
state bases, and a list of multilinear operation tensors

    A:V_s1 × ... × V_sk → V_t.

Include every legality/failure output one wants to preserve. For an initially
specified family of linear observations, let W_s be the span of all one-hole
context responses: compose operations, fill other holes by basis states, and
finally apply one observation. Put N_s=∩_{w∈W_s} ker w.

**Context factorization theorem (standard mechanism, proved here for this
setting).** The quotient family V_s/N_s is closed under every operation and
has minimum dimensions among linear compositional summaries preserving the
specified observations. A proposed rowspace family U_s is already sufficient
if and only if it contains the observations and is closed under contraction
of any operation against all fixed other inputs. If the operation tensors
are nonnegative in the state bases, they descend positively on the exact
quotient cones C_s=π_s(R_+^{states_s}), though not necessarily on coordinate
orthants in chosen quotient bases.

*Proof.* For v∈N in one input, any downstream context applied to A(...,v,...)
is itself a one-hole context applied to v, so it is zero. Multilinearity then
shows changing input representatives leaves the quotient output unchanged.
Any sufficient compositional summary evaluates every context by induction;
therefore its kernel is contained in N, proving dimension minimality and
factorization onto V/N. The contraction criterion is the same argument in
rowspaces. Nonnegative inputs have nonnegative state-basis expansions, hence
a nonnegative output before quotienting, proving cone preservation. ∎

The role of “one hole” is important: other inputs are independently specified
states/laws. Composing independent child laws uses their tensor product;
correlated child laws require the joint tensor. Multiplying marginal
expectations is not justified for a shared hidden alignment.

This is the weighted-automaton/Hankel and connection-matrix viewpoint, not a
new foundational invention. For primary context, Rabusseau–Balle–Cohen,
[*Low-Rank Approximation of Weighted Tree Automata*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v51/rabusseau16.pdf),
§2 defines one-hole contexts, multilinear transition tensors and context/tree
Hankel matrices, and recalls their exact minimal-rank characterization.
Lovász, [*The rank of connection matrices and the dimension of graph algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408232),
provides the graph-algebra counterpart. We use no approximate low-rank result
and infer no graph-homomorphism representation from rank alone.

The concrete contribution here is Theorems A–B: resetting makes the entire
infinite terminal experiment language close at explicit orbit marginals;
for an involution the exact missing response directions are graph cycles,
with an optimal forest sidecar and a sharp nonsimplicial-cone obstruction.
That is more information than an abstract “retain the missing coordinate.”

## 8. Falsifiable frontier, not an unsupported universal claim

the free-class pair graph above. If B_n is connected for every n≥4, then the
all-order terminal rank is exactly 2t_n−1, where t_n is the number of ordinary
