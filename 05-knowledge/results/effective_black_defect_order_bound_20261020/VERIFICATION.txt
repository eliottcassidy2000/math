INDEPENDENT FINITE VALIDATION
2026-10-20

The analytic proof establishes the theorem for every order. These programs
are independent checks of its sensitive ingredients, not a finite proof
of the general order bound. They were written independently for this audit
and do not import earlier research verifiers or datasets.

1. exhaustive_small.cpp

Enumerates every labelled tournament at orders n=3,4,5,6. Strongness and
vertex connectivity are computed by forward/backward reachability on
vertex subsets. Every vertex permutation is tested directly to count
automorphisms and Hamilton words. Blue words are determined by testing
whether their positional reversals reverse every edge. Every directed
Hamilton cycle modulo starting position is checked.

Assertions verify odd automorphism order; integer word-orbit counts;
exactly zero or gcd(2,q) blue cycle-cut orbits; q<=Delta+2; the stronger
q-blue_cuts<=Delta; the orbit-degree lower bound; and the two connectivity
bounds, including the separate q=1 case. The numerical order inequalities
are checked at d=Delta, although at these small orders they are very coarse.

TOTAL: 22,890 strong labelled tournaments and 62,234 Hamilton cycles.
All assertions passed. Includes non-self-converse tournaments, q=1 cycles
at orders 3 and 5, and q=2 cycles at order 6. Detailed histograms are in
exhaustive_small.out.

2. check_rotation_frames.cpp

Constructs every tournament invariant under x->x+q and containing the
fixed Hamilton cycle 0->1->...->qm-1->0 for the pairs (q,m):

(1,3), (1,5), (1,7), (1,9), (1,11), (1,13), (1,15),
(2,3), (2,5), (2,7), (3,3), (4,3).

It chooses orientations on unordered-pair orbits, forces the Hamilton
edges, verifies each size-m orbit is regular, and tests every vertex
deletion set smaller than ceil(m/3) for q=1, or ceil(m/2) for q>=2.
Strongness is checked by two-sided reachability. The specified rotation
subgroup need not be maximal, which is sufficient for this lemma.

TOTAL: 264,967 rotation frames and 4,546,185 deletion checks.
All passed. The checks explicitly include the q=1 case through order 15,
the q=2 boundary-matching case through order 14, and q=3,4 orbit systems.
Full details are in check_rotation_frames.out.

REPRODUCE FROM THIS DIRECTORY

g++ -O2 -std=c++17 exhaustive_small.cpp -o exhaustive_small
./exhaustive_small

g++ -O2 -std=c++17 check_rotation_frames.cpp -o check_rotation_frames
./check_rotation_frames

No external libraries are required. Compile without -DNDEBUG, because
assertions are part of the checks. The expected standard outputs are
included as plaintext; executable binaries are intentionally not included.
