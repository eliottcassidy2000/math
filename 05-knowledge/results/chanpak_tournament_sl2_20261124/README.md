# Three places where tournament-path symmetries live

The [H=23 Golay charts](../chanpak_h23_golay_chains_20261124/README.md) put a sporadic group on physical edit coordinates. This packet separates that finite metric action from operations that compose as a tournament grows, and from individual tournament automorphisms.

- [Ordinary strong strip CRT](STRIP-CRT.md): actual strip words realize the elementary shears modulo every odd modulus, and dyadically neutral commutators reach every admissible scalar residue for every mixed modulus.
- [Literal shears and weighted SL₂](SL2-TRANSFERS.md): triangle ports realize every real transfer, and on framed unweighted paths the two arc choices give the integral shears and all finite prime-field projective special linear quotients. Ordinary paths leak out of the frame.
- [Determinant-seven Farey transfer](FAREY-SEVEN.md): the native H=23 two-read transfer has a hyperbolic real completion and a 25-state positive-address machine. Not even a second step remains in the native physical output set.
- [The unweighted bare unimodular port](ZERO-GAP-PORT.md): computes the full ordinary path count through an odd-cycle partition, with an explicit determinant obstruction to composing its two-port blocks.
- [Projective switching](PROJECTIVE-SWITCHING.md): an actual faithful real action on signed projective points, a projective action on switching classes, and an endpoint covariance for signed paths. A one-vertex switch realizes a second, structurally different 23-path core.
- [The height-preserving envelope](SPINE-STABILIZER.md): identity and reflected converse are the only isometries of the full fixed-spine exact-height cube. Radius two around the transitive board already determines them.
- [Weighted cycle-to-path projection](WEIGHTED-COMPLEXITY.md): exact formal reduction to one fixed strong tournament, and an unconditional rank obstruction for cross-cut two-state reads. The circuit lower bound in the supplied paper is used only conditionally.
