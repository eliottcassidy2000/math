## Cancellation theorem

Write `F_w(x)=(3^len(w)x+B_w)/2^sum(w)` and `S(z)=4z+1`.
A macro `(D,u,v,r)` has D>=1, r>=1 and positive reduced heads such that

\[
 |v|-|u|=D,\quad A_u-A_v=2r,\quad
 F_v(y)=S^r(F_u(x))\quad\text{when }x+1=3^D(y+1).       \tag{1}
\]

The last equality is audited at the full affine anchor: slopes agree and
`F_v(-1)=4^r F_u(-1)+(4^r-1)/3`. For a source with e initial ones, the
macro's actual words are `1^e,u,c` and `1^(e-D),v,c+2r`, with c read from
the supplied source after u. Native guards and first-hit safety are separate.

Suppose an outer macro `(D_o,u_o,v_o,r_o)` and an inner macro
`(D_i,u_i,v_i,r_i)` satisfy the **ordered** containment

\[
 u_i=v_o\,b\,t,\qquad b>2r_o.                         \tag{2}
\]

Then their composition has macro

\[
 \boxed{(D_o+D_i,\ u_o\,(b-2r_o)\,t,\ v_i,\ r_i).}    \tag{3}
\]

Indeed

\[
 F_b(S^{r_o}(Z))=F_{b-2r_o}(Z).
\]

This follows by expanding `3S^r(Z)+1=4^r(3Z+1)`. Apply the remaining
ordered tail t, then use the inner identity (1). The checkpoint relations
multiply to `x+1=3^(D_o+D_i)(z+1)`, giving the claimed deletion. Positivity
of the adjusted letter in (2) is essential.

The source-head length and cost satisfy

\[
 |u_{new}|=|u_i|-D_o,\qquad A_{u_{new}}=A_{u_i}.          \tag{4}
\]

The cost cancellation uses `A_uo-A_vo=2r_o`; it is exact, not asymptotic.
This means more deletion can be represented by a shorter head without
requiring greater source precision. It does not imply the same head can
be reused indefinitely: the next ordered containment and guard must exist.

## Native integer families and the sixteen bit packet

For any audited macro let A=A_u, `(P,Q,B)` be its source carrier, and

\[
 r=(Q-B)P^{-1}\pmod{2Q},\qquad Q=2^A.
\]

Take `n=2^(e+1)t-1`, t positive odd, and require
`2*3^e*t-1=r mod2Q`. All ordinary sources at fixed sufficiently large e
are obtained from

\[
 t=t_0+2^A s,\quad t_0=((r+1)/2)3^{-e}\bmod2^A,
 \quad s\ge0.                                        \tag{5}
\]

For `e>=max(2A,D+1)`, every source prefix through u exceeds n:
the post-run coefficient is at least
`3^e/2^(e+A)>1`, and initial ones grow. The odd native endpoint and
identity (1) force positive exact child valuations. No earlier ROOT can
lead to the preterminal child endpoint `S^r(Z)>1`. The actual final
valuation therefore produces a strict common-future receipt whose child
is `(n+1)/2^D-1<n`. This proves the direct family's first-hit boundary.

For the new child packet, u_i begins with all15 letters of the old partner,
followed by3. The outer gap is1, so the replacement letter is1. Formula (3)
gives the source head

```text
(2,2,2,1,2,9,16,1,1,2,1,1,2,1,1,2,1,3,2,3,2,3,3,3,1,3,4,1,1,3,2).
```

Its length is31 and cost81; the partner has length47 and cost79. The exact
power-of-three congruence gives the minimal native Mersenne phase

\[
 \boxed{K\equiv924745905\pmod{2^{79}},\qquad
           M_K\rightsquigarrow M_{K-16}.}              \tag{6}
\]

The arrow is a strictly paid common-future implication, not a direct
Collatz edge. Every positive phase member exceeds the growth cutoff.
The final child's exponent is `924745889 mod2^79`. Its
`v2(E-1)=5` means the three-twos type has returned, but its phase misses
all225 inherited heads. Thus residual type is a lossy quotient of the
actual state. The successful new guard refines the old guard by47 bits;
it does not cover every old-phase parameter.

## Why the fifteen bit alternative adds no separate coverage

The depth7 child packet also gives a fifteen-bit original-parent receipt,
with the same source head and phase. Its final child has even exponent
`924745890 mod2^79`, and first reset4.
The already proved reset>=3 rule in
[checked switching, section3](checked_switch_phase19_20261004.md)
specializes to

\[
 1^q,4\quad\text{at }h,
 \qquad 1^{q-1},2,2\quad\text{at }(h-1)/2.
\]

The carrier identity is `F_4(3y+2)=F_(2,2)(y)`. In the present guarded
family q is positive and the paths are ROOT-safe. This bridges the
fifteen-bit child to the sixteen-bit child. Composing that bridge produces
exactly the sixteen-bit receipt, letter for letter. These are a commuting
triangle of proof obligations, not two disjoint sets of newly solved sources.
