## 4. Three bridges with actual maps

**A. Relative-frame gluing.** Fix separate orbit types with stabilizers H,K
under G. Simultaneous pair orbits are `H\G/K`, as proved in the D4 note.
For tournaments take G=S_n, source and rooted-target stabilizers, and
restrict to alignments whose agreement is the required forest. C1 is the
resulting exact coefficient formula. For cyclic decoration pairs the same
construction reduces to gcd(d,e) phases. The preserved predicate is joint
orbit identity; the discarded information is the independent-frame gauge.
The cheapest test is a fixed endpoint pair with two admissible alignments,
now supplied by the seven-vertex witness.

**B. Moments versus named events.** For a probability law on binary port
features, let `m(S)=Pr(all features in S equal1)`. Boolean-lattice inversion
recovers each complete configuration probability from **all named** m(S).
Summing m(S) by |S| instead recovers only the Hamming-weight law, by THM-561.
F3's two weight-three words of periods3 and9 show that even all weight
moments lose phase and surviving symmetry. This is a precise repair of
the old finite-observer language: specify the random variable, named
coordinates and support bound before claiming completeness or blindness.

**C. Quotient leakage as an operator.** In the finite presentation space,
let E average within an observation fibre and U permute by tau. Since
U is an involutive isometry, on the fibre-constant subspace the compressed
operator K=EUE satisfies

    I-K^2 = [(I-E)UE]^* [(I-E)UE].

This follows by inserting `I=E+(I-E)` between the two U factors. It is the
normalized version of the inherited [boundary-support leakage identity](../boundary_support_20261020_metagraph.md).
The quotient supports deterministic complement exactly when the leakage
vanishes. It is a positive quantitative defect for this finite Hilbert
space, not classical Vitali nonmeasurability. Re-marking requires its own
guard/action tests; zero leakage for tau alone does not close the process.
