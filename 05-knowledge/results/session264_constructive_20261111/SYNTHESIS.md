## 2. Several return edges have a complete address calculus

One return edge forced all new cycles to meet. Reversing an entire matching
between two ordinal shores permits compatible new cycles and cycles using
several returns. The new construction parses EVERY cross cycle into an exact
cyclic word of path legs, with all missing forward seams explicitly deleted.

Attach a commuting square-zero variable x_v to every literal vertex. The
k×k legal-leg matrix M then retains disjointness through multiplication. The
new odd-cycle inventory is

    Odd(Σ_(ell=1)^k trace(M^ell)/ell).

Its exponential gives all compatible packings. At the Hamiltonian evaluation,
the new factor is the finite determinant ratio

    det(I−M(−x))/det(I−M(x)).

This is a lawful positive cycle/address compiler, not a signed approximation.
One must extract coefficients in the square-zero algebra; setting x_v=1 would
illegally erase capacity. The algebra can still require exponential storage.

The first useful initial trade comes from two return matchings between two
triangles. It produces literal six-vertex modules A,B with

    Z_A=1+6tu³+4tu⁵+2t²u⁶,
    Z_B=1+6tu³+6tu⁵+t²u⁶.

They have different labelled selector graphs but the SAME complete cover
polynomial Q_r=r⁶+12r⁴+16r², hence H=29. At root0 they also have the same
complete root-deletion profile. Their outgoing/incoming rooted responses are

profiles. This supplies the physical input for the next mechanism.

## 3. Shared-root fusion turns that trade into a context-uniform sorting gate

Identify the roots of pointed A,B and orient every edge A−root → B−root.
This operation ⋆ is associative, preserves the distinguished root, and has an
exact EIGHT-integer carrier as a4×4 triangular matrix. It retains H, the deletion
count h, deletion two-cover count Q, two rooted responses U,V, their parity
responses d,e, and the empty-deletion switch k. Literal root paths and mixed
odd cycles prove M(A⋆B)=M(A)M(B).

Thus arbitrarily long fusion words compile without accumulating higher cover
orders. Repeating one module has a closed formula with recurrence polynomial
(λ−Q)(λ−h)². This is not a representation of arbitrary tournament contexts,
and its matrices are generally singular, not a physical inverse group.

For the two six-vertex trade modules above,

    M_A M_B−M_B M_A=150N,

where N has only three nonzero entries, all+1. Therefore EVERY actual rooted
left/right context L,R satisfies

    H(L⋆A⋆B⋆R)−H(L⋆B⋆A⋆R)
       =150[h_L V_R+U_L h_R−h_L h_R] >0.

This is an order-robust arithmetic switch. Surrounding material cannot reverse
its sign. With a copies of A and b copies of B, bubble-sorting the factors gives
ab+1 strictly increasing counts on1+5(a+b) vertices, all using the same inventory.
Sorted words are the minimum and maximum arrangements. We do not claim every
arrangement is distinct or that the intermediate integers are filled.

This is the most direct cross-topic composition in the session: a hard-core
cycle-address trade, invisible to all unmarked cover counts, acquires a usable
positive arithmetic action after passing through the rooted carry algebra.

## 4. Positive component merging and a retained-endpoint filter consumer

The triangle wrap W(A), with u→A→v→u, acts exactly on the multicolour cover
polynomial. In its odd-component basis it has a subtraction-free description:
keep the two new singleton components, or merge ANY nonempty odd number of
existing odd components together with the two new vertices. The scalar cover
specialization is

    Q_W(A)(r)=r²Q_A(r)+rQ_A(r+1)−rQ_A(r−1).

The apparent subtraction is a parity extraction with a positive component
interpretation. A skeleton compiler evaluates fixed wrappers without repeatedly
enumerating all induced path subsets. For a one-apex input its data reduce to
a finite cycle-length signature, giving an explicit affine filter search.
The bounded searches did not produce a new modulus16 filter; their exact scope

The positive output is an actual consumer for existing rooted length channels.

bijectively to

in W(A), beginning at the SAME old y. The position of v is |R|+1. Dually the
old endpoint y is retained and a gate suffix records length. Hence Start_W(A)(y)
=U_A(y) and End_W(A)(y)=V_A(y), with exact length-channel readout.

## 5. What has moved, and what still has to be built

- **Direct constructive progress:** positive-word modular source controllers;
  a hidden exact clock; a multi-return selector compiler; a finite shared-root
  algebra; a context-uniform sorting switch; and a literal length-channel consumer.
- **Niche progress:** a full-profile-neutral rooted trade, a positive odd-component
  merging operator, and a precise bounded-context signature rather than scalar
  cover-polynomial substitution where it is insufficient.
- **Actual connections:** the return trade drives both the sorting algebra and
  the endpoint consumer. The moving-mark clock is a native affine resonance,
  rather than a numerical resemblance to Pell or renewal dynamics.
- **Unchanged boundary:** modular witnesses may grow beyond the target; positive
  fusion offset H is not the moving-mark unit offset; a conditioned length
  channel is not a scalar projector; an exact matrix is not arbitrary physical
  realizability. The all-target inverse-cover controller is still missing.
