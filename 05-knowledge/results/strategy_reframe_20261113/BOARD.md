Euclidean-style recursion, rather than searching a larger context head.

Five live concepts:

1. Repair direction d=(0,1,2,1) in state (h,s,q,F).
2. Actual next target E=h+F-s, and the plane F+s=h+q.
3. Growth versus branching, with word counts distinguished from target counts.
4. Existential auxiliary coordinates: for each H choose some suitable E.
5. Odd-cycle conflict graphs: abstract partition functions versus tournament
   realizability.

are necessary for a complete scalar projection, or that an all-order
barrier for one repair direction obstructs Chan--Pak itself.
