# Chan–Pak: exact substitution interfaces and recursive tournament symmetry

This session studies which data a tournament module passes to its surrounding tournament. The results give exact recursive constructions and identify an obstruction that is invisible when moduli are checked separately. They do not prove unrestricted Chan–Pak.

- **[A constructive bypass of a joint congruence obstruction](COMPOSITION.md).** The entire directed-triangle composition grammar with one singleton and transitive seeds preserves a quadratic norm linking the mod-3 sign, the mod-5 slot readings, and order parity. It misses 25 modulo 30 at even orders and 5 at odd orders, even though separate mod-3 and mod-5 tests eventually see every class. Replace one transitive sleeve by a four-vertex strong block followed by a sink: a literal 12-vertex tournament has 18,955 paths and enters the previously missing class. The enlarged finite grammar attains every odd residue modulo 30 at each order n≥42, or n≥30 with one further transitive sleeve. This is fixed-order congruence coverage, not exact coverage.
- **[An exact arithmetic interface](OBSERVABLES.md).** If c_r counts ordered nonempty r-path covers, the complete integer reading by unmarked contexts is (r−1)!c_r, with physical triangular probes of unit diagonal, even using only strong outputs. For example, a module with 3 dividing H passes that divisor to *every* unmarked surrounding tournament, whether or not it has any graph symmetry. At fixed H>1, unmarked replacement cannot produce an exact shift of two. Permuting whole nonsingleton modules in a fixed quotient also preserves H modulo 4.
- **[Full count profiles and actual symmetry can separate](PORTS2.md).** For every n≥6 there are strong tournaments with identical full slot polynomials and different automorphism groups; from order eight there are exponentially many profile-equivalent classes with an exact group histogram. Ternary inflation gives a closed recursion for the automorphism exponent distribution, including the precise cost of changing one leaf from the most symmetric tower. A marked outlet distinguishes an order-seven same-profile pair by producing 273 and 275. The two earlier binary-return hosts agree in H but their full polynomial states differ; an explicit transfer exposes where continuation fails.
- **[Signed transfer and a self-converse selector](STRUCTURE.md).** Reversal-fixed path covers compose via the quotient anti-involution, and fixed presentations are counted by dividing by its automorphism centralizer. Equal cyclic inflation lets the fraction of mirror-fixed presentations decay factorially. In another family a fixed mirrored core and 16 bounded self-converse seeds hit every odd class modulo every power of two, yet produce only O(log X) distinct counts up to X.
- **[The cost of using only transitive sleeves](GROWTH.md).** Adding a vertex multiplies the k-slot reading by at least k, and a directed odd cycle through that vertex gives an exact positive correction. Padding alone therefore cannot synchronize the earlier two binary hosts. Even allowing both transitive sleeves to change arbitrarily at each cyclic wrapping step gives a power-sparse count set. The number of unoriented sleeve recipes is governed by walks on the reflection-folded six-vertex path, with Perron root 2 cos(π/7).

## How to reproduce

Run `python verify_observables.py`, `python verify_composition.py`, `python verify_modular.py`, `python cutoff_bypass.py`, `python verify_structure.py`, and `python replay_ports2.py`. The finite replays check probe coefficients, exact state recurrences, the small group/profile certificates, and the literal bypass. The unbounded statements use the composition proofs and, where indicated, exact repetition of a finite state rather than extrapolation.

The strongest constructive lead is to extend the sleeve bypass from a prescribed residue to a prescribed absolute count while preserving the marked continuation data. The new interface rules give exact tests of which substitutions can carry that data, not an unrestricted decoder.
