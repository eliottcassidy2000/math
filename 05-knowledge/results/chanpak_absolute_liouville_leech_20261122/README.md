# Absolute perfect-number targets, full word signs, and the Leech labels

## Absolute targets and the exponent-31 test

The concurrent [60- and 62-vertex certificates](../chanpak_e31_physical_20261122/README.md) straddle the exponent-31 perfect number: $E_{31}-1$ and $E_{31}+1$ are counts of two strong tournaments differing on a single arc. Their new nontransitive marked gate preserves the gap by demanding exactly one tagged starting path. A freely varying gate-and-suffix implementation also hits the exponent-23 Euclid shape. A straddling rule at every exponent stays **OPEN**.

This packet builds an independent exact selector for the raw unequal-flank operation. [Eliminating the binary read](UNEQUAL-FLANK-INVERSE.md) converts a prescribed pair $N,N+2$ to a signed divisor-pair search followed by one exact binary read, or to a finite affine-wall inverse in the degenerate cases. It also proves a positive linear drift for nonconstant repeated bit blocks, so fixed periodic words cannot repair the exact perfect-number residual in that chart. [Dyadic polynomial obstruction](DYADIC-PERFECT.md): with two growing flanks the two-slot mass must be a power of two, excluding the original mass 320. With one fixed flank, a bit-reversal valuation rules out a fixed dyadic polynomial address at any fixed offset from perfect height. The latter applies also to a fixed nontransitive gate in the concurrent construction; it does not obstruct its finite landings or choosing the gate anew at each target. For the growing gate, the [width law](UNEQUAL-FLANK-INVERSE.md#cross-exponent-width-rigidity-in-the-new-nontransitive-gate-family) shows that each active bit contributes at ternary, not dyadic, scale.

[Triangular offsets](TRIANGULAR-OFFSETS.md) construct seven fixed offsets uniformly, including the exact scalar $E_{31}-3 = 2,305,843,008,139,952,125$. [A strong offset and width obstructions](MODULE-OFFSETS.md) realizes $E_p-15$ strongly on $2p-1$ vertices for every $p\ge6$, and proves that just retuning the widths of the six inherited two-port carriers cannot hit either full neighbor at odd perfect exponents. A one-arc reversal cannot upgrade the ordinal-product witnesses into unit seams.

[The two missing pure-A Coxeter labels](LEECH-LABELS.md) complete the earlier six-label paired rails: both $a2^k+1$ and $7(a2^k+1)$ for $a=9,25$ have strong witnesses at every $k\ge1$.
