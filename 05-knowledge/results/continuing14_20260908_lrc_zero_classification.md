## 2. Zero budget forces both disjointness and attained marginals

Let d_i=gcd(t,u_i). The inherited sheet alphabet consists of 42 integers,
all coprime to seven. If t is divisible by seven and d_i is an allowed
divisor of t, then 7*d_i divides t. Each open danger set on a translated
t-grid has cardinality at most

    d_i*ceil(t/(7*d_i))=t/7.

Therefore the marginal excess E is exactly zero for every full profile
word in this entire clock slice. If there is no weak-safe point, the
seven danger sets cover all t grid points. Their cardinality bounds sum
to t. It follows that each set attains t/7 and that all seven sets are
pairwise disjoint.

The attainment statement supplies a strictly stronger pair gate. On a
quotient grid with n points, an individual p-danger set, with
a=gcd(n,p) and 7*a dividing n, contains n/7 points off its endpoint
phases. At an endpoint phase its count drops to n/7-a. This follows
by reducing to the n/a-point grid: its open interval has integral
length n/(7*a), so endpoint coincidence loses one point, repeated a
times. Hence an unsafe zero-budget grid cannot lie at any individual
danger endpoint.

Every endpoint of a component of the pair intersection is an endpoint
of at least one of the two individual danger sets. It is therefore
enough to minimize pair intersections over the **open chambers** between
their endpoints. A positive chamber minimum contradicts an unsafe grid,
even when the ordinary all-phase minimum is zero at a strict wall.
Chambers may include some other inadmissible individual endpoint phases;
that only weakens the lower bound and cannot create a false exclusion.

This is the first newly paid coordinate. It does not replace open dangers
by closed dangers or assert that every phase has positive intersection.

## 3. Native quotient banks and the zero-cut consumers

For an actual edge write its two speeds as D*p and D*q, with p,q coprime.
Put e=gcd(t,D)=gcd(d_i,d_j) and n=t/e. Multiplication by D maps the
translated t-grid to a translated n-grid with multiplicity e. Compatibility
with the margins a,b is exactly

    {e*gcd(n,p), e*gcd(n,q)}={a,b}.

All capacities and banks here use this actual n. No weight or zero bank
from 7,200, 7,560, or another nearby clock is reused.

There are two possible graphs on the seven labelled positions. The first
permits an edge if at least one compatible ratio has ordinary all-phase
intersection minimum zero. The second permits it if at least one compatible
ratio has chamber minimum zero. An unsafe grid with an actual connected
strict graph requires that graph to be a subgraph of either possible graph.
If either possible graph is disconnected, every actual connected graph
would need a positive-capacity crossing. This contradiction proves a safe
lift. No particular possible edge is forced to be actual.

The native classifier records the **complete number of oriented zero
ratios** for each quotient margin pair, separately for both graphs. At an
equal-margin pair p:q contributes both orientations; at unequal margins
the margin roles determine one orientation. These counts retain distinct
ratios, not phases or repeated witnesses for the same ratio.

All relevant quotient clocks belong to the same 1,085-clock set. Across
them there are 728 nonempty quotient-pair rows. Every strict atlas ratio
is considered. A sum of exact open-interval floor lower bounds may skip

that the ratio contributes to neither zero bank. There is no incumbent

## 4. Distinct speeds impose a local slot bound

Let a full word have m_a positions of margin a. Write R_ab for the
complete number of compatible **oriented chamber-zero ratios** u_a/u_b.
Suppose there is no chamber-zero edge within the a class. In an actual
connected zero graph every a vertex must have a neighbor outside that
class. Choose one such neighbor for each a vertex. For a fixed actual
neighbor speed u_b, the possible a speeds are among the R_ab distinct
numbers r*u_b. Two distinct a speeds cannot use the same neighbor and
the same ratio. Thus necessarily

    m_a <= sum_(b != a) m_b*R_ab.                         (1)

The right side may overcount equal speeds produced by different neighbors;
it remains a valid upper bound. Violating (1) therefore excludes the word.
This argument uses distinct actual speeds, not merely distinct graph
positions. It requires no simultaneous realizability of all possible
ratio slots.

The decisive first hostile to a graph-only carrier occurs at t=7,812.
Its only chamber-connected full-profile word is

    (4,4,4,9,9,12,18).

Each margin-4 position has the unique margin-12 position as its only
possible zero neighbor. At the actual quotient n=7,812/4=1,953 the
**entire zero bank** is p:q=33:320, with margins 12 and 4 respectively.
Equivalently u_4/u_12=320/33. The three distinct margin-4 speeds would
all equal (320/33)*u_12, a contradiction. In (1), this is 3>1.

The individual arm is not falsely declared impossible: the pair
(u_4,u_12)=(1,280,132) has the exact requested margins, lies in the strict
atlas, and has a genuine saturated zero chamber. Its quotient danger
cardinalities are both 279 and its intersection is zero. The missing
coordinate in the graph quotient was injectivity of actual speed values.

## 6. Lawful clock families and the precise stopping state

There is a useful divisibility transport, with a paid domain condition.
Suppose t divides T and both have the same allowed divisor alphabet.
For fixed margins a,b let e=gcd(a,b). A compatible ratio at T has the
same margins at t: its reduced margin divides t/e, so taking the gcd
with the smaller clock does not change it. A translated T/e-grid is
a union of T/t translated t/e-grids. If its intersection is zero,
every subgrid intersection is zero. If the refined phase is an open
chamber phase, so are the subgrid phases; a subgrid endpoint would also
be a refined-grid endpoint. Consequently both zero-ratio banks, and
their oriented slot counts, can only shrink under this divisibility
refinement. The full profile word domain is unchanged.

Thus all three exclusions transport upwards under **divisibility and
an unchanged alphabet**. The source checks every such pair within the
declared census. This is not monotonicity in clock size; allowing a new
margin state or changing a quotient without this argument would restore
the earlier invalid scale analogy.
