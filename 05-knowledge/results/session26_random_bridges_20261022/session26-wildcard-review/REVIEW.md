# Independent audit: Session 26 wildcard theorem

Verdict: PASS, subject to the stated finite-group, automorphism, output, and run-bound hypotheses. This is a residue-output Moore-machine theorem, not a claim about a single accepting language or canonical representations.

## General theorem

Let H be a finite abelian group, M an automorphism, v in H, and H generated by the orbit of v. Let ell:H -> A be a group homomorphism. On binary input the arithmetic update is s -> M s + d v, starting at zero. Legal words have no more than r consecutive ones, where r >= 1; all illegal words and extensions output a distinct INVALID symbol. Put K = intersection over t >= 0 of ker(ell M^t). The exact minimal Moore count is (r+1)|H/K|+1.

Because M has finite order, the intersection defining K is finite and M(K)=K. Thus arithmetic cosets support well-defined updates and output. The quotient product with counters 0,...,r and one sink gives the claimed upper bound.

For reachability, at counter zero the legal reset blocks 0 and 10 act as A(s)=Ms and B(s)=M^2s+Mv. They are permutations of H. The positive monoid they generate is a group: an inverse of each permutation is a nonnegative power of that same permutation. Therefore B A^{-2} is an attainable translation by Mv. Its conjugates by powers of A are translations by M^{j+1}v. These generate every translation of H, because M has finite order and the full orbit of v generates H. Every arithmetic state at counter zero is reachable by concatenated legal reset blocks. Appending 1^j for j<=r gives every arithmetic state at counter j, since its affine arithmetic map has invertible linear part M^j.

For minimality, unequal arithmetic cosets are distinguished by a suffix 0^t, using the definition of K. Both resulting prefixes are legal, including when t=0. For equal arithmetic cosets but different counters a<b, append 1^{r-b+1}. The b-state enters the sink and the a-state remains legal. The sink is distinguished from every legal state immediately by its output. These arguments also work if the counters and arithmetic cosets both differ.

## Quadratic specialization

For M=[[0,1],[1,1]], v=(a,b), and ell(x,y)=x, observations of the current state and after a zero recover x and y. Hence K={0}, even when H is a proper subgroup of (Z/mZ)^2.

Under (x,y) <-> x+y phi, with phi^2=phi+1, M is multiplication by phi. The reachable group is the ideal (a+b phi) in R_m, equivalently the image of the integer matrix [v,Mv] modulo m. This equivalence uses the quadratic relation, so every higher orbit vector lies in the span of v and Mv.

For an integer seed other than (0,0), write g=gcd(|a|,|b|) and D=a^2+ab-b^2. The determinant D is nonzero: a rational root of x^2+x-1 cannot arise from a/b, and the b=0 case is immediate. The gcd of all entries of [v,Mv] is g, so its Smith invariants are g and |D|/g. Unimodular Smith operations remain invertible modulo any positive m. Consequently

|H| = (m/gcd(m,g)) (m/gcd(m,|D|/g)).

For primitive seeds this is m^2/gcd(m,|D|). The seed (1,2) has D=-1; (2,1) has D=5. At r=1 their counts are respectively 2m^2+1 and 2m^2/gcd(m,5)+1. The Lucas loss at 5^k is a controllability loss of factor 5, not an observability loss or a factor 5^k. At modulus 5 the Lucas vector is an eigenvector, consistently with the rank-one reachable image.

## Edge cases and scope

- m must be a positive integer. The formulas include m=1.
- The zero seed has H={0}; for r>=1 its count is r+2, including the sink.
- At r=0 only all-zero words are legal. Starting from zero, the exact count is 2, regardless of the ambient orbit group. The positive-run hypothesis is indispensable to the general formula.
- INVALID must be outside the ordinary output alphabet. If it coincides with a valid output, the sink-separation argument fails.
- M must be an automorphism. The positive reset-permutation argument does not apply to a general endomorphism.
- Arbitrary leading zeros and unrestricted finite legal words are used in reachability. Canonical or fixed-length encodings require a separate theorem.
- The result is for outputting the residue at every prefix, not necessarily for a Boolean accepting language.
- No conclusion follows about actual Collatz transitions/clocks or universally compressing integer states.
