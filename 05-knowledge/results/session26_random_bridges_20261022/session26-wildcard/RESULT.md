# Session 26 wildcard: exact memory of a constrained arithmetic reader

Status: PROVED elementary, self-audited; FINITE-EXACT independent behavioral-minimization checks. No literature priority claim; no Collatz, LRC, or unit-distance chromatic conclusion. Main source: 05-knowledge/results/zeckendorf_guard_automaton_20261003.md, exact two-register digit arithmetic. ## Result

Let A be a finite abelian group, M an automorphism of A, v in A, and ell:A→B a homomorphism to a finite abelian output group. Let H be the subgroup generated by M^j v for j≥0. Let

K={h in H: ell(M^j h)=0 for every j≥0}.

Read binary words high-to-low, starting s=0 and updating s←Ms+d v. A word is legal when it contains no run of r+1 ones, where r≥1 is fixed. Leading zeroes are permitted. The function to compute is ell(s) on legal words and a separate INVALID symbol on every illegal word, including every extension of it. A deterministic Moore machine computes this entire function, not merely membership in one target residue class.

THEOREM. The exact minimal number of states is

                    (r+1)|H/K|+1.

The factors are the observable arithmetic module and the length 0,...,r of the final run of ones; the extra state is absorbing INVALID. Thus the grammar/arithmetic product is not merely a convenient upper bound. It is forced in this setting.

### Proof of reachability

It suffices first to reach every h in H with final run zero. Concatenations of blocks 0 and 10 are legal for every r≥1 and always end with zero. On arithmetic states their maps are

A(s)=Ms,   B(s)=M²s+Mv.

These maps permute the finite set H. Consequently each inverse is a positive power of its map, so the monoid generated by their legal blocks equals the group they generate. The composite B A^(-2) is translation by Mv. Conjugation by powers of A gives translations by M^j v. Since M has finite order, this includes translation by v; arbitrary sums and negatives generate all translations in H. Hence the orbit of zero contains every H state. This proof is about block concatenation and does not allow an illegal 11 at a block join.

For final run j, append 1^j to a run-zero word. This maps h to M^j h+sum_(i=0)^(j-1) M^i v, an affine bijection of H. Thus every (h,j) is reachable. INVALID is reached by 1^(r+1).

### Proof of quotient and minimality

K is invariant under M and M^(-1): M has finite order, so the conditions can be indexed cyclically. Two states with equal run length and arithmetic difference in K have equal outputs after every legal continuation, because their difference becomes M^t times the old difference. They have identical legality responses too. This constructs the stated upper bound.

If arithmetic cosets differ, some zero suffix 0^j distinguishes their outputs; zeros are legal from every non-sink state. If arithmetic cosets agree but run lengths a<b differ, suffix 1^(r-b+1) is illegal from b and legal from a. The sink differs from every valid state by its current output. These distinctions prove the lower bound. QED.

The role of invertibility is exact: it permits legal blocks to generate inverse moves and hence all translations. The role of observability is exact: unobservable state differences must be quotiented rather than counted.

## Fibonacci and Lucas consequences

Use M=[[0,1],[1,1]] on (Z/mZ)^2 and ell(X,Y)=X. The next zero outputs Y, so K=0 on every H. For Fibonacci weights f0=1,f1=2, take v=(1,2). The value and shifted-value reader is

                 (X,Y) ← (Y+d, X+Y+2d).

The columns v,Mv=(2,3) have determinant -1, so H is the entire module at every m≥1. The exact count is (r+1)m²+1; for the no-adjacent-one grammar with leading zeroes permitted, exactly 2m²+1.

Thus preserving modular value alone cannot compress the arithmetic reader to O(m) states. It must remember m² different observable contexts even restricted to prefixes ending zero. This is an information-theoretic statement about finite-state streaming, not a complexity lower bound on arbitrary algorithms or storage with unbounded integer registers.

For Lucas weights L0=2,L1=1, take v=(2,1), Mv=(1,3). The generating matrix has determinant 5 and an entry 1, so its integer Smith form is diag(1,5). Cayley-Hamilton M²=M+I means these two columns generate the full orbit subgroup. Therefore

                 |H|=m²/gcd(m,5),

and the exact machine size is (r+1)m²/gcd(m,5)+1. At m=5 the Lucas reader needs 11 states versus Fibonacci's 51 for r=1. At m=25 it needs 251 versus 1251. These are not equivalent numeral systems: the statement concerns the given recurrence-weight reading function on the same legal language. No uniqueness or completeness of Lucas representations is assumed.

### Discriminant-five mechanism and guard examples

Modulo5, the characteristic polynomial t²-t-1 is (t-3)². In the convention used here the Lucas INPUT vector v=(2,1) satisfies Mv=3v, so its reachable submodule collapses to one eigenline. The Fibonacci input(1,2) is not an eigenvector and sees the generalized/Jordan coordinate. The output row ell=(1,0) is not an eigenrow; confusing controllability with observability would misdescribe this collapse. Observation remains injective from the pair of empty and0 suffixes. For m=5^a, a≥1, the map from coefficient coordinates through the controllability matrix has kernel of size5, not5^a; this is precisely Smith diag(1,5). Its image has size5^(2a-1).

For no-adjacent-one input, the exact counts at moduli15,35,105 are:

- Fibonacci:451,2451,22051 states, respectively.
- Lucas:91,491,4411 states, respectively.

Reading residues modulo15 and35 simultaneously is equivalent by CRT to reading modulo105 (with the shared modulo5 consistency); it does NOT multiply the two automaton state counts. Both readers share a single grammar run-state and a single INVALID sink. At coprime a,b the arithmetic state group is the CRT product, but the legality control is synchronized, not duplicated. These are readers of finite digit-word residues only. They do not evolve a Collatz source or preserve an unprovided stopping clock.

More generally, weights g0=a,g1=b with Fibonacci recurrence give v=(a,b), Mv=(b,a+b). The full controllability determinant is a²+ab-b². A nonunit determinant can create genuine modular compression. The theorem needs the actual subgroup size, not determinant alone when entries have a common factor or determinant is zero. Smith normal form supplies the exact size at every modulus.

For odd m, reading both Fibonacci residue modulo m and the inherited two-bit charge (X mod2,Y mod2) is equivalent to the full pair modulo2m with observation (X modm,X mod2,Y mod2). Zero suffix reveals the remaining coordinate modulo m; the unobservable kernel is trivial. Consequently its exact count is 8m²+1 in the no-11 grammar. This confirms the cost of retaining the charge sidecar rather than treating it as a substitute for modular arithmetic.

## Hypothesis/test/refinement loops

1. Scalar compression temptation: retain value modulo m, last digit, and small color. Inherited hostile prefixes 10 and10000 represent 2 and8; they have equal value modulo3, equal last bit and equal modulo2 pair, but zero continuation yields3 and13, distinct modulo3. First failure: append-zero needs the shifted register. Repair: retain the exact pair.
2. Stronger hypothesis: the 2m² grammar/product reader is fully reachable and minimal. BFS and independent Moore partition refinement agree for every m=1,...,40. Proof above turns this into an all-modulus theorem. No assumption of statistical equidistribution or bounded numerical census is needed.
3. Overgeneralization: every two-register recurrence needs 2m² states. Lucas probe refutes it at prime5; the reachable group has index5. Repair: controllability subgroup H, then observability quotient K. Noninvertible M=0 at m5 has only3 reachable states rather than the putative11 from its generated v-subgroup, so invertibility is essential. Constant output with Fibonacci m5 has51 reachable raw states but only3 minimal states, so observability is essential. The exact theorem survives these hostile controls.
4. Extended grammar: replace no11 by no run of r+1 ones. The same legal-block controllability proof survives; exhaustive checks r=2,3,4 and m=1,...,20 verify both families and the formula. This is an actual operation-level generalization, not an analogy.

## Intrinsic quadratic ideal formulation and sharp boundaries

Identify (a,b) with alpha=a+b phi in R_m=(Z/mZ)[phi]/(phi²-phi-1). Multiplication by phi is exactly M. Hence the reachable subgroup is the additive group of the principal ideal alpha R_m, not an arbitrary analogy between recurrence and number theory. The integer determinant D=a²+ab-b² is Norm(alpha).

Fibonacci alpha=1+2phi=phi³ is a unit. Lucas alpha=2+phi=phi sqrt(5), where sqrt(5)=2phi-1; its ideal is the ramified discriminant ideal(sqrt(5)). Modulo5 this square-zero ideal is the eigenline, whereas the Fibonacci unit sees the full ring/Jordan coordinate.

For nonzero integer pair(a,b), g=gcd(|a|,|b|)>0 and D≠0. The latter follows because an integral root of a²+ab-b²=0 would give a rational golden ratio unless both entries vanish. Smith form is diag(g,|D|/g), and

 |H|=(m/gcd(m,g))*(m/gcd(m,|D|/g)).

Thus the exact validated-reader count is(r+1)|H|+1. For primitive input it reduces to(r+1)m²/gcd(m,|D|)+1. For v=0 handle separately: H=0, so the answer is r+2 grammar/sink states; no division by g is performed.

The theorem requires r≥1. At r=0 only zeroes are legal, reachability is trivial, and only two legal/sink states are needed. Finally, when input legality is promised externally rather than checked, the exact arithmetic-only memory is |H/K|, not(r+1)|H/K|+1: all cosets are reachable at run0 and distinguished by zero suffixes, while the quotient state alone computes every promised-valid output. The grammar factor must not be attributed to arithmetic itself.

Relation to the concurrent tournament lane: reachability and observability are separate tests in both exact finite observers. CRT combines arithmetic modules while retaining ONE shared legality controller and sink. This identifies a common proof architecture, not tournament dynamics with digit shifts or Collatz stopping clocks.
