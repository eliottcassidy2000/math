## 5. The old S-unit mechanism forces a change of template

The source-membership equation returns us to
[THM-4490, affine-word S-unit specialization](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4490-collatz-affine-word-sunit-specialization.md).
For any fixed middle head u, a Mersenne ROOT word `1^(E-1),u,c` requires

\[
                2^{A+c-1}-3^{E+|u|}=I/2\ne0.
\]

The [fixed-head theorem](collatz_terminal_head_finiteness_20261007f.md)
normalizes this to X+Y=1 in a group of rank at most3. The cited
Beukers–Schlickewei bound gives at most2^32 exponents per fixed head.
Therefore a finite dictionary of fixed middle heads cannot ground all
Mersennes through this form, even with variable initial runs and terminal
valuations. This gives a concrete stopping reason for that strategy.
It says nothing against recursive words, variable heads or ranked chains
of child substitutions, and supplies no maximum-exponent bound.
