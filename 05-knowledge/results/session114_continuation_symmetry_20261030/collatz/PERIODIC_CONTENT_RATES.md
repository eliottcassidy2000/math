# Exact marked periodic content rates from cyclic symmetry

**PROVED:** a closed rational formula for the ordinary, untruncated five-adic
content mean of every fixed positive valuation controller and entry word.
**PROVED:** the sharp asymptotic `5/8` clock bound for every controller whose
affine order grows by five from modulus 5 to modulus 25, with arbitrary entry.
**OPEN:** the all-layer finite-word `5/8` bound, the same asymptotic bound for
all controllers, and Collatz convergence.

This advances the fixed-controller divisor and boundary formulas in
[the predecessor](../../layer_lifts_cyclic_intersections_20261030/COLLATZ.md#4-a-fixed-controller-theorem-at-every-prime-with-the-entry-retained).
Those formulas and their one-unbounded-phase theorem are inherited, not new.
Here the former controller-dependent upper bound is replaced by an **exact**
all-layer rational mean. No precision-to-time limit is exchanged without proof.

## 1. Definitions and the exact answer

For a finite positive valuation word x, use

    F_x(X) = (P_x X+B_x)/Q_x,   L_x=P_x-Q_x,
    c(x)=v_5(gcd(|L_x|,B_x)).

Let w be a nonempty controller of length d and clock A_w. Fix any entry u,
including the empty word. Write v_j for the prefix of w of length j, for
1 <= j <= d. The charge during N complete controller blocks after entry is

    S(N;u,w) = sum_(k=0)^(N-1) sum_(j=1)^d c(u w^k v_j).

The finite charge inside u itself is omitted, not reset or reinterpreted.
Write M=F_w=(m,b) over the five-adic integers, and put t=ord_5(m), so
t belongs to {1,2,4}. Define

    if t>1: r=v_5(m^t-1),
    if t=1: s=v_5(m-1), h=v_5(b), r=min(s,h).

Here m is not 1 as a rational number, b is positive, and all these relevant
valuations are finite. When t=1, r can be zero. Define

    T_e = t * 5^max(0,e-r).

For every marked phase let H_j=F_(v_j u)=(n_j,c_j), and

    C_j = (m-1)c_j - b(n_j-1).

Power-of-two denominators are units. Its valuation can therefore be computed
from the integer `L_w B_(v_j u) - B_w L_(v_j u)`.

The depth D_j, possibly infinity, is given by the following formulas.

**Case t>1.** If n_j modulo 5 is not in the subgroup of order t, set D_j=0.
Otherwise put beta_j=v_5(n_j^t-1) and

    D_j = min(v_5(C_j), beta_j if beta_j<r else infinity).

**Case t=1.** Put alpha_j=v_5(n_j-1) when s<=h, and
alpha_j=v_5(c_j) when h<s. Then

    D_j = max(0, min(v_5(C_j)-r,
                    alpha_j if alpha_j<r else infinity)).

The convention v_5(0)=infinity applies. These formulas require only integer
arithmetic, valuations, and a residue modulo 5, not discrete logarithms or a
list of the cyclic subgroup.

Define

    f(t,r,D) = D/t                         if finite D<=r,
             r/t + (1-5^(-(D-r)))/(4t)    if finite D>r,
             (r+1/4)/t                    if D=infinity.

Then the exact ordinary Cesaro limit is

    lim_(N->infinity) S(N;u,w)/N = R(u,w)
                               = sum_(j=1)^d f(t,r,D_j).       (1)

In fact `S(N;u,w)=N R(u,w)+O_(u,w)(log(N+2))`. If every D_j is finite,
the error is O_(u,w)(1), with absolute discrepancy at most sum_j D_j,
and the per-block charge is periodic in k with period T_K, K=max_j D_j
(use period 1 when K=0).
The exact mean per original clock is R(u,w)/A_w.

## 2. Why the depth formula is exact

Modulo 5^e, the order of M is T_e. If t>1, m-1 is a unit, and conjugation by
its integral fixed point makes M a dilation. The subgroup generated by m is
precisely the subgroup with residue in the order-t group modulo 5 and
principal-unit depth at least min(r,e). This follows from LTE:

    ord_(5^e)(m) = t 5^max(0,e-r).

For H=(n,c), membership in the affine cyclic subgroup additionally says
`c=b(n-1)/(m-1)` modulo 5^e. These are exactly the two tests above.

If t=1 and s<=h, the same fixed-point conjugation is integral, although
m-1 is not a unit. Membership is exactly

    v_5(n-1)>=min(s,e),   v_5(C)>=e+s.

Indeed b/(m-1) is integral and the second test is the translation condition
after division by m-1. The first test is membership in the principal-unit
subgroup generated by m.

If t=1 and h<s, translation is the shallower coordinate. Set

    n_star = 1 + ((m-1)/b)c.

Then H is a power of M modulo 5^e exactly when
`m^k=n_star modulo 5^(e+s-h)` is solvable and n_star=n modulo 5^e.
These are equivalent to

    v_5(c)>=min(h,e),   v_5(C)>=e+h.

The same argument, or LTE on the translation geometric sum, gives the order
`5^max(0,e-h)`. Thus in both subcases the tests reduce to the stated cutoff
D. In particular,

    H_j belongs to <M> modulo 5^e  iff  e<=D_j.                  (2)

The actual prefix map is F_(v_j) M^k F_u. It is identity modulo 5^e iff
M^k=(F_u F_(v_j))^-1=H_j^-1. When (2) holds, exactly one residue class of k
modulo T_e succeeds. Otherwise none succeeds. Therefore its layer-e density
is exactly `[e<=D_j]/T_e`. Summing that geometric series gives f.

## 3. The all-layer limit, including possible high spikes

For finite D_j, every charge at that phase is bounded by D_j. Counting one
residue class modulo T_e makes the cumulative discrepancy at most D_j.
This case needs no infinite interchange.

For infinite D_j, C_j=0. The inherited faithful valuation-word representation
and commuting-word lemma imply

    w = a_word^a,   v_j u = a_word^b

for a common primitive nonempty word a_word and positive integers a,b.
(The marked word v_j u is nonempty.) Conjugating the actual prefix affine map
by F_(v_j) gives M^k H_j. Conjugation preserves the predicate of being
identity modulo each 5^e, so the content height at this phase is exactly
that of `F_(a_word)^(ak+b)`.

LTE and the geometric-sum formula now bound that height by

    C_(u,w) + log_5(ak+b).

For example, if the root multiplier is 1 modulo 5, its height is
`min(v_5(m_root-1),v_5(b_root))+v_5(ak+b)`. Otherwise it is zero unless
the residue order divides ak+b, and then is
`v_5(m_root^t-1)+v_5((ak+b)/t)`.

Choose K=C+ceil(log_5(aN+b)). No observed charge through N blocks exceeds K.
The difference from its truncated expected value is at most K, from one
residue-class counting error per layer. The infinite expected tail beyond K
is O(5^-K), whose product with N is O(1). This proves the O(log N) error and
(1). It specifically excludes the unjustified inference from a Haar mean to
an ordinary orbit mean for an arbitrary five-adic target.

A fixed exit word v may also be appended. Each of its finitely many prefixes
has the same commutator dichotomy, using H=F_(V u): its terminal charge is
bounded or O(log N). Consequently the **full** charge obeys

    S_infinity(u w^N v) = N R(u,w) + O_(u,w,v)(log(N+2)).

The exit does not change the mean; the entry generally does. Neither entry
nor exit is allowed to change with N in this statement.

## 4. A broad all-layer asymptotic 5/8 theorem

**Theorem.** If `ord_(25)(M)=5 ord_(5)(M)`, then for every entry u,

    lim_(N->infinity) S(N;u,w)/(N A_w) <= 5/8.                  (3)

The order condition is exactly r<=1 in the parameters above. It is checked
by the controller's affine coefficients modulo 25, and does not constrain
the entry. This includes a nonzero translation with multiplier 1 modulo 5,
and the usual unit controllers without an extra order-lifting cancellation.

**Proof.** For r<=1, T_e=5^(e-1) T_1 for all e>=1. Membership at level e
implies membership at level 1. Thus each layer-e mean is at most the
layer-1 mean divided by 5^(e-1), and

    R(u,w) <= (5/4) R_1(u,w).

The inherited sharp first-layer all-word theorem gives, on the actual word
u w^N, `S_1(u w^N)<= (A_u+N A_w)/2`. Subtract the fixed entry charge,
divide by N, and pass to the limit to obtain R_1<=A_w/2. This proves (3).
The coefficient is sharp for w=(1), u empty, whose mean is 5/8.

The same conclusion holds whenever the **primitive word root** of w has this
order-growth property: repeating w is merely repeating that root a fixed
number of times per period. This strictly enlarges the accepted class, for
example covering w=(1^10), whose own affine order stalls at low precision.

This is an all-layer **asymptotic fixed-controller theorem**, not a
pointwise finite-word bound and not a result about one positive integer
following an infinite periodic valuation word. Every finite word is
realizable by a positive source; those sources may change with N.

The order-lifting hypothesis cannot be omitted from the intermediate
inequality R <= (5/4)R_1: for w=(1^10,2), u empty, one has t=4,r=2,
R_1=3/2 and R=33/16 > 15/8. Its clock rate is only 11/64, so this is not a
counterexample to the desired 5/8 bound.

## 5. Controls and examples

Run `python check_periodic_rates.py` and the same command with `python -O`.
The checker independently enumerates affine cyclic subgroups modulo 5,25,125
for every controller of clock at most 7, testing 12,192 phase memberships.
A further 13,936 synthetic affine membership checks through modulus 625
exercise both translation-pivot branches, zero translations, and order stalls.
It also compares 372 finite-precision means against direct letter-by-letter
state walks, and checks all 4,095 nonempty controllers of clock at most 12.
Only all-one controllers achieve the maximum observed clock rate 5/8 in
that finite search. Of these controllers, 3,299 satisfy the primitive-root
order-growth criterion; 796 are outside that sufficient theorem. All 4,095
have their rates computed exactly by (1). This is evidence, not a universal
equality classification.

Examples:

- w=(1): R=5/8, clock rate 5/8.
- w=(1,2), empty entry: R=5/16, clock rate 5/48.
- w=(1,2), entry (1): R=1/4, clock rate 1/12; both phase depths are finite.
- w=(1,2), entry (2): R=5/16, clock rate 5/48; the unbounded phase moves.
- w=(5,5): R=5/4, clock rate 1/8.

The changed rate under entry (1) is a concrete reason not to erase the entry
when using the cyclic symmetry. A full arbitrary-word theorem still requires
uniform control when the controller changes, or another all-layer argument.

## Lineage boundary

The concurrent [marked single-ones-loop profile](../../phase_budget_shared_orbits_20261030/COLLATZ.md#6-exact-periodic-charge-with-a-fixed-entry-and-exit)
proves exact affine-periodic total charge as a middle ones-run length varies,
with fixed entry and exit and finite phase caps. The present theorem instead
repeats an arbitrary fixed controller, includes its genuinely unbounded
content phases, and proves an ordinary all-layer mean with a logarithmic
error. These are complementary quantifier patterns. Neither removes the
uniform changing-controller obligation.
