# Why the old physical binary–ternary return has no additive motion

The [Session 197 binary–ternary bridge](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/b49eb615b9238151032033db17bc0265c104a89b/session197/binary-ternary-bridge/PROOF.md)
is important positive evidence that **two real clocks** can coexist.
Its five retained physical ports support arbitrary interleavings;
the exact count is 60·2^t·3^m+1 for m,t≥1. This is not an example
of marginal residue compatibility: the same graph actually supports
both births. It has the opposite problem from synchronized padding.

The old derivation uses disjoint crossings with vertex covers of
sizes one and two. These bound the two maximal-run grades by two
and three *before* the finite calibration. The calibration then
coherently determines the entire tensor polynomial. One must not
infer a new reader from an extrapolation of six counts alone.

## The anchor test

For a directed graph of interfaces, suppose an exact move e from v
to w reads H' = λ_e H + b_e. If constants c_v can be chosen with

    b_e = c_w − λ_e c_v

on every allowed edge, then every composition satisfies

    H_final − c_final = (product of the λ_e) (H_initial − c_initial).

Proof: substitute the edge formula and telescope after shifting
each reading by its own c_v. In particular every closed walk at
its original interface with multiplier one is the identity on
the count, not a nonzero translation. The result also applies
if some actual inverse moves are permitted; the premise must be
checked on them. It does not grant any inverse physically.

This is an exact obstruction on the integer count, even when
local multiplicative orbits are large and both branches have
valid physical lifts. It is independent of how many alternate
words achieve the same multiplier.

## Apply it to the historical bridge, including its sign change

Use c=−1 at t=0 and c=+1 at t≥1. At t=0 the ternary birth is
3H+2, preserving c=−1. The first binary birth is 2H+3, carrying
c=−1 to c=+1. At t≥1 the two births are 2H−1 and 3H−2, both
fixing c=+1. Thus the **full** solution, not just the interior,
is exactly an anchored multiplicative action. It cannot implement
a repeatable positive additive shift by any closed scale-neutral
walk in those moves. The fixed ports and perfect commutation
are genuine advances, but their perfection erases precisely
the offset needed for the [integer return certificate](../chanpak_physical_routes_20261115/DIRECTED_RETURNS.md).

This also clarifies the link backward to
[Bass–Serre's two–three quotient](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/01-canon/theorems/THM-3145-bass-serre-two-three-tree-and-tetrahedral-congruence-quotient.md)
and the [Collatz collision algebra](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/01-canon/theorems/THM-4640-the-repair-algebra-at-minus-one-collisions-are-increasing-two-three-representations-ladders-and-gersonides-carries.md).
Two and three can describe branching or dilation exactly while
the integer carry still lives in an additional coordinate.
No assertion equating the actions on the three constructions is
needed. A sensible first test of any enlarged physical alphabet
is whether its affine constants are still all explained by one
set of interface anchors. If so, adding more words cannot create
the missing translation; if not, a positive fixed-scale return
and a uniform physical guard still have to be proved.
