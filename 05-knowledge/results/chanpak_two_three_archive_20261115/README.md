## What actually transfers

**An alphabet, a clock, and a physical return are different.**
Berggren and the square-sum absorbers have a genuine inverse
because the full address lies in one disjoint admissible chamber.
The Collatz and ternary-digit work shows why separate endpoint
guards do not select a whole integer. Session 197 gives the strongest
positive control: the *same* five exposed tournament ports support
both 2H−1 and 3H−2 in any order. But their common anchor 1 makes
them commute, and the initial sign gate is also an exact change
of anchor. Those literal independent clocks carry no additive
translation. [Historical bridge and anchor test](AFFINE_ANCHORS.md).

**The arithmetic sum-of-2^a3^b decoder already existed.** The
November inventory's antichain algorithm works for every integer.
This session identifies its canonical choice and proves an exact
complexity result: its first input requiring L terms is 3^L−2^L.
A target can therefore choose a whole list of smooth terms, not
just a residue. But the physical overlaps of three-run frames
remain unselected: the same arithmetic already supplies 7 and 21.
[Antichain decoder](ANTICHAIN.md).

## A new physical split, and the arithmetic price

A new exact module pair has the same H and Q₃ with Q₂ differing
by 3840. It gives a high-dyadic physical switch and survives all
three states of the previous port reader. Combining it with
the older Cayley pair (same H and Q₂, different Q₃) gives a literal
**2×2×3 box** with all cross-interactions zero. The three scopes
touch different arcs. This is not two marginal residue projections:
the two coordinate changes coexist on the same physical module.
Yet repeating within either class of copies gives geometric
increments, not a repeatable additive route.
[Physical pair](CHAN_HISTORY.md) · [Two independent axes](TWO_AXIS.md).

Three obstructions explain exactly where a simpler proof breaks:

1. **Freezing slots creates a prime sieve.** Freeze H and all
   higher slots 3,…,r. Any three equally spaced Q₂ readings must
   open a stride divisible by every odd prime through r+2, even
   if the source orders vary. At fixed order the paired difference
   has a stronger factorial divisor. This is why a third prime
   (already 5 at the first ternary lock) can be forced by the
   physical slot constraints. [Frozen-grade sieve](FROZEN_GRADES.md).
2. **Clock scale constrains actual step size.** The positive
   cycle-packing polynomial gives the sharp comparison
   Q_b≤Q_a^(log_a b), equality at transitive modules. A rank-three
   reader whose step is proportional to Q₂ cannot produce a modulus
   much smaller than N^log₃2, even by choosing richer modules.
   If instead the device grants a finite model A·2^u+B·3^v+C with
   independent exponents, S-unit rigidity says that deep bounded
   moves merely change offsets: both exponential magnitudes must
   stay fixed. These are different restrictions with different
   escape conditions. [Growth cone](SLOT_ENTROPY.md) ·
   [Independent-clock rigidity](INDEPENDENT_CLOCKS.md).
3. **One-port nesting cannot pay a repeatable return.** With a
   finite bank of marked readers, even arbitrarily scheduled
   one-port nesting along one chain gives O(log X) distinct
   observed counts ≤X. A cyclic mark forces two-slot exponential
   growth in carrier order; a singleton-component mark instead
   sees the factor-three growth of nontransitive births.
   Pooling small cores in the older synchronized reader still
   leaves widely separated height chambers. A fixed skeleton with
   many independent transitive padding lengths remains polylog
   by clipping each long module. [Nesting dichotomy](NESTING_DICHOTOMY.md)
   · [Chambers](NUMBER_HISTORY.md) · [Clipping](GRAPH_HISTORY.md).

## Where to push next

difference* of large slot charges, and carry it through a
target-selected change of reader. Do not freeze the entire
higher profile or simply nest the same module. In parallel,
