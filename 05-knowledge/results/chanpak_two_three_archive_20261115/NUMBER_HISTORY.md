# Two clocks, one budget: arithmetic trails and a separation theorem

This is a proof-oriented recovery of older work, not a new search for missing counts. The new result below is a limitation of one actual class of readers, not an impossibility theorem for tournaments.

## The ancestry worth keeping

1. **Möbius/Farey to carries, not just “2 and 3.”** October 19's [Farey–Mandelbrot dictionary](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/05-knowledge/results/farey_mandelbrot_20261019_collatz_angles_and_cross_conjecture_dictionary.md) leads through THM-4484 to [THM-4716](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/01-canon/theorems/THM-4716-balanced-collatz-cycles-are-three-smooth-ladders-and-are-free.md), THM-4717 and [THM-4719](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce55a202b73c80d56561ba56d00b503a6/01-canon/theorems/THM-4719-window-completeness-of-three-smooth-ladders.md). For the shortcut Collatz word with length q and p odd steps the affine equation is `(2^q − 3^p)x=dB_w`, where a 1 at position i contributes `2^i3^(number of 1s to its right)` to B_w. Moving the mechanical intercept swaps one adjacent 01 to 10: powers stay fixed, the carry changes by precisely one 3-smooth monomial. The denominator is coprime to 6. A prime dividing the denominator and one carry would propagate to every rotation, but it cannot divide the difference between adjacent carries. Thus only the denominator dividing d produces an integral balanced cycle. This is a rigidity argument, not evidence for arbitrary words. The smooth ladder's exact window criterion is also two-sided: its min and max m,M must satisfy both `M<3m` and `M<min(2^(q−1),3^p)`. A shared slope alone is not a window theorem. The corrected finite Collatz slope bound is `log2/log(3+1/m)≤p/q<log_3 2` for cycle minimum m, not equality at finite m. Transfer question: can one swap a local tournament motif at fixed left and right continuation data, changing only the carry? The old physical route below has the first feature (arcs really flip) but not the freedom to reposition the absolute baseline.

## New theorem: subpolynomial-budget chambers in the synchronized strip

Let H(D)=h denote the Hamiltonian count of the module D in the six-vertex physical route, and let Q_j(D) count covers in j numbered path slots (empty slots permitted). The previous result gives exactly the three displayed values
[
4Q_3+9Q_2-3h,quad 4Q_3+9Q_2-h,quad 4Q_3+9Q_2+h.
]
The actual consecutive arc flips change the count by 2h. Along the ordered strong components, each Q_j is multiplicative and a singleton contributes j. Thus every output is `B3^s+A2^s+d`, where s is the *same* singleton count for both, A=9Q₂(core), B=4Q₃(core), and d∈{−3h,−h,h}. Here “core” means the ordered concatenation of the nontrivial strong components; its empty profile has Q_j=1.

**Normalization.** Choose integer ℓ by `3^ℓ≤B<3^(ℓ+1)`. Put `b=B/3^ℓ∈[1,3)`, `a=A/2^ℓ`, and `n=s+ℓ`. The exact graph output is `b3^n+a2^n+d`. At fixed h there are finitely many (a,b), so for sufficiently large n distinct core pairs sort lexicographically by n,b,a before d. Different b at the same n split at scale 3^n; equal b and different a split at scale 2^n. The upper/lower ternary-scale boundary stays open because the finite largest b is strictly less than 3 and every b is at least 1. Two lanes can agree infinitely often (allowing a bounded shift in the singleton budget) only if the normalized triples agree at infinitely many n, and then the offsets must also agree. The middle target can be inverted exactly by monotone integer bisection in each physical core; no real rounding establishes a match.

**Uniform statement.** Set `K=exp(log X/(loglog X)^2)` and θ=log₃2. Pool *all* six-vertex strip counts in [X,2X] for modules with H(D)≤K. They fall into chambers indexed by (n,b,a). A chamber has diameter at most 6K; the distance between distinct chambers is at least `X^(θ−o(1))`. These are statements on all sizes of modules and do not presume canonical uniqueness of the nontrivial core.

**Proof.** A nontrivial strong component C has H(C)≥3 and, by the Busch bound used in the synchronized-port proof, order at most 1+3 log₅H(C). Since h multiplies along the condensation, the sum m of nontrivial component orders is at most `(1/log 3+3/log 5) log h`. For any t-vertex tournament, Q_j≤j^t t!: assign a slot to each vertex and an order within slots, bounded above by assigning any full permutation. Consequently A,B≤U=`X^(O(1/loglog X))` uniformly: `log(j^m m!)=O(m log(m+2))` and m≤O(log K). Enlarge U to include the constant coefficients 4 and 9. Now a≤U, 3^ℓ≤U and 2^ℓ≤U^θ. Also |d|≤3K and all coefficients are positive. If the output is in [X,2X], then `3^n≤2X+3K≤3X`; hence `a2^n≤U(3X)^θ=o(X)` and `3^n≥X/4` for X large.

Compare two outputs. If their main ternary terms differ, their difference in absolute value is at least `min(3^n,3^n')/U`: after removing the smaller power of 3 the rational denominators both divide the larger of the two denominator powers, at most U. This dominates twice the uniform upper bound on the dyadic terms and the offsets. If their ternary terms coincide, the normalization [1,3) forces n=n' and b=b'. Different a values then have difference at least `1/U^θ`, since their denominators are nested powers of 2. Multiplying by 2^n gives separation `X^(θ−o(1))` even after both offsets. If a also agrees, only d can differ, by at most 6K. This proves the claims.

**Consequence for the repair strategy.** For fixed ε>0, even granting switches between any physical witnesses in the pooled set, a chain staying in [X,2X] whose consecutive count differences are at most `X^(θ−ε)` cannot leave a chamber. This includes the literal 2h steps in that budget. A residual of polynomial width, for example the scale X^(3/8), cannot simply be traversed there from arbitrary starting positions. This does *not* show that a nearby target has no representation, rule out a larger budget, rule out a different reader, or rule out excursions beyond [X,2X]. In particular K is smaller than every fixed power X^δ, so this is not a negative theorem for polynomial-H routes.

## What to do with the obstruction

The useful transferable invariant is a **clock rank with fiber width**: after fixing the high-scale mantissa, which independent physical choices move lower coordinates, and how far can the fiber travel without changing the high observer? The synchronization in this route gives rank one and subpolynomial fiber width. Balanced Collatz exchanges keep both exponents fixed and move the affine carry; Berggren's inverse chooses an actual cone and descends; density methods solve neither. The most focused constructive request is therefore to change the physical reader to supply either (i) an operation fixing the normalized ternary term while changing the dyadic coefficient at finer-than-discrete scale, or (ii) a third output coordinate read with a genuinely independent, physically certified budget. Before searching target counts, test whether the proposed budget pair can vary independently on one actual family and whether the two-port continuation data survive that change. A cosmetic rewrite of the same shared s is excluded by the theorem.
