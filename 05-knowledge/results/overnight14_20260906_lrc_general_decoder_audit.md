height is at most 355<Q.

three and height at most Q. If W-perp had dimension at least three, a
matrix spanning W-perp would have three independent coordinate columns,
contradicting the relation on those columns. This is the exact instance of
**THM-2052**,
`01-canon/theorems/THM-2052-finite-height-forces-high-rank-bounded-relation-code.md`,
