## Inherited mechanisms and boundaries

Closest full-count engine: degree-local odd-factorial integral future-response
transfer (Session22), followed by Session239's factorial-gauge transfer
(THM-5097). The unit-tail and prime-tail ladder is THM-514. The gcd-field
Gaussian representation and meromorphic normalized expansion are THM-4845.
Session249 supplies connected interaction diagrams near q=1, not a fixed-jet
exact census at q=2.

Canonical hostile examples: scalar H is not composition-complete (Session268's
equal-H children separate under C3); a fixed q=1 interaction jet captures a
vanishing fraction of the total census; fixed-width representations need their
actual future interaction coordinate.

Corrected near miss: sparse multiplicative atom languages give enormous class
families but only exp(O((log X)^(2/3))) values below X. A target grammar must
retain boundary state rather than promise completeness from class growth.

Least-used sidecars: endpoint and two-path-fragment data, future divisor
responses, and precision-local coprime tuple counts. THM-5098 already proves
the normalized odd-order O(sqrt(b)) cycle cutoff and mod4/mod8 formulas.
These are inheritance, not Session270 discoveries.

No fixed finite polynomial-coefficient recurrence in n for the full A000568
sequence is claimed. Its output alone has Theta(n^2) bits. “Instantaneous”
means a specified practical query regime, never arbitrary exact output at
constant cost.
