# Next focused questions

1. **Full A000568:** can a dynamic residual-gcd component splitter choose
   convolution or transfer cheaply enough to beat the integrated counter at
   larger orders? Benchmark same output and arithmetic, and retain outside
   response charges. The present sector theorem does not imply a general
   polynomial state bound.
2. **Target inversion:** does the exact17-dimensional two-core semigroup have
   a returning cone with a digit algorithm? Find a decreasing target invariant
   or a finite covering certificate. The million-target census alone does not
   establish global completeness.
3. **Marked-to-unmarked descent:** characterize multiplicities when the core
   designation is forgotten. B(k,m) is a marked census; treating its words as
   distinct ordinary tournaments is invalid.
4. **Precision compiler:** compile even-order normalized residues modulo16
   and32 into divisor queries, retaining exceptional repeated-cycle strata.
   Factoring costs and query bit complexity must remain explicit.
5. **Tomography economy:** can the averaged-spine cover transform be recovered
   with fewer than all cross orientations, using exact weighted designs while
   preserving physical realizability? A smaller vertex count per query is
   not automatically fewer queries.
6. **Value-sensitive counting:** refine the common marked census by H with
   automorphism weights. A rational total-mass GF or large class count does
   not by itself imply any target H is reached.
