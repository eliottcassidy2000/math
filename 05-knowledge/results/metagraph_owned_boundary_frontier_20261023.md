## 3. An all-size rule once the actual source and target are aligned

For zero-one adjacency matrices A,B of a native complement port with path P,

    A+B-(J-I) = F_P-F_P^T.

Joint directed equitable refinement, using common actual cells in A and B,
is discrete within **floor(n/2) synchronous rounds**. The signed row sums
identify the start and end first; counts into their singleton cells identify
the next path vertices from both ends. Thus every non-singleton equitable
source cell must eventually split in the aligned partner information.

A stronger rigidity consequence is that the only nonnegative doubly stochastic
matrix commuting with both A and B is I. These are all-size proofs; controls
exhaust 33,867 fixed-path tournaments through n=7, and a second implementation
tests the signed-path refinement and clocks through n=256.

This is not a method for finding the unknown target or the correct alignment.
Indeed, many nonnative pairs also have discrete joint refinement. The inherited
agreement-path criterion remains necessary: endpoint degrees alone allow a
path plus disjoint cycles. With those separate degree guards retained,
det(I-D)=1, nilpotence of D, and an (n-1)-step start-to-end walk are equivalent
ways to reject the detached cycles. A two-vertex path plus a triangle is the
smallest hostile with distinct named endpoints.

Proof and independent audit: [aligned port refinement](aligned_port_refinement_20261023/PROOF.md).

## 4. Two exact Sierpinski connections, and what Hopf contributes

**Boundary recursion.** For a connected affine-F3 matching support with three
named boundary cells, the relation of compatible boundary-owner assignments
has exactly 64 possible states: empty, one of 27 singleton triples, or one of
36 affine three-point graphs. Every state is realizable. Every admissible
boundary tuple has a unique interior completion.

Three pieces on boundaries (a,x,z), (x,b,y), (z,y,c) compose by eliminating
the shared x,y,z. The result is again one of the 64 states, at every finite
recursion depth. All 64^3=262,144 compositions agree with direct propagation
on their literal supports. This gives a fixed six-bit compatibility interface
for the Sierpinski triangular overlap pattern.

Counts alone cannot replace it: three children each admitting three assignments
can glue to a parent admitting three, one, or zero. Independently quotienting
the child boundary phases also fails. The actual shared charts must agree.
This is a genuine compositional compression of one predicate, not of all
tournament data. Native tournaments with no parallel section remain valid.

The actual-port transfer supplies a decisive limit: all **420** reached
eight-triple systems have **zero global parallel sections**. In 168 cases the
21 based loops consist of nine fixed-point-free translations and twelve
reflections; in the other 252 all 21 are fixed-point-free translations.
Thus contracting the whole K8 support to its section relation gives the same
empty state every time. It cannot distinguish their wiring. The individual
negative loops still identify owners even though no owner satisfies all loops
at once. Smaller patch relations and the full loop tuple serve different
purposes and must not be substituted for one another.

The sharper diagnostic is **fixed points versus commuting symmetries**.
The 252 balanced systems have holonomy group C3; the other 168 have S3.
Both groups fix no owner, but their centralizers inside the allowed root
rotations have sizes three and one respectively. This explains why the same
empty section relation can coexist with either genuine residual symmetry or
an intrinsically resolvable source. A centralizer-size sidecar still does not
retain the full attachment phases or automatically compose under gluing.

The compactness does not persist automatically at other arities. Four named
boundaries need 298 states, and arbitrary seven-label permutation connections
with three boundaries need 185,036,888 states, hence at least 28 bits. General
regular seven-by-seven adjacency blocks are not permutations, so those blocks
need additional information even beyond that count.

**Actual path clock.** Over F2 an authenticated path matrix F satisfies

    (I+F)^t = product_(j: bit_j(t)=1) (I+F^(2^j)).

From the start row this is Pascal parity, with coordinate k present exactly
when k's binary digits are contained in t's. Its snapshot repeats with exact
period 2^ceil(log2 n); the integer time must remain a separate coordinate.
Thus a periodic compressed snapshot need not mean the original directed path
has a cycle. This is distinct from the 64-state gluing operation.

**Triangle/circle and Hopf.** The inherited map x -> exp(2*pi*i*x/3) extends
the actual affine transports to the dihedral subgroup of O(2). Existence of
a parallel section transfers exactly between the triangle and circle; its
number changes from 3/1/0 to a whole circle/2/0. Hopf-bundle charts supply a
useful model for retaining transition maps, with primary background in
[Baez's projective-line construction](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node9.html).
No graph-to-Hopf-bundle identification is asserted. A graph connection's
parallel-section obstruction and a Hopf bundle's topological section
obstruction are different. Shared powers of two also do not establish Bott
periodicity or identify this state code with the retained electrical guard.

receipt: [triangular boundary recursion](hopf_sierpinski_port_recovery_20261023/REPORT.md).

## 5. Square-sum edges have a birth context and a changing path role

Use Q_n on 1,...,n, with x--y when x+y is a square and x!=y. All Hamiltonian
paths and individual edge-use counts are independently checked through n=32.
The given sizes agree with the existing existence pattern:

| n | 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 | 22 | 23 | 24 | 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paths up to reversal | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 10 |

The user's first eight edges are exactly the edges born at the nontrivial
traceable arrivals 15,16,17,23,25 within this range. The second fourteen are
the degree-two births 13,14,19,20,21,22,24, where no Hamiltonian path exists
at arrival. Those are meaningful historical categories; they are not fixed
Hamiltonian-use colors at a later size.

At Q_25 the first group contains six forced edges, one optional edge 13--23
(nine of ten paths), and one unused edge 1--15. The second contains eight
forced and six optional edges. The listed 3--12 is not a square-sum edge
(its sum is 15); the user explicitly corrected it to **4--12**, which is used
in the third class. Absence of paths at a size is reported separately from edge roles,
avoiding vacuous claims that every edge there is forced.

The new obstruction experiment is especially informative. For any Hamiltonian
path P and proper vertex set S,

    |endpoints(P) intersect S| + |E(P[S])|
       <= |S|+1-c(Q_n-S).

At n=25, edge 1--15 has no unmarked tight-cut certificate containing both
ends. Even the complete endpoint-marked cut relaxation leaves three choices
for the other endpoint besides the mandatory leaf 18: 8,10,21. Explicit
degree propagation rejects 8 and forces the proper six-cycle
2--14--22--3--13--23--2 for 10 and 21. This supplies a finite hybrid proof
that 1--15 is never used, and a sharp warning that even all those cut budgets
are insufficient alone.

Birth is not monotone in path role: at Q_26 the only unused edge is instead
1--3. At Q_32 all 46 edges occur in some Hamiltonian path and none is forced.
There is an **all-size closure for these coarse single-edge roles**. In any
Hamiltonian undirected graph, every edge is both includable and omittable in
a Hamiltonian path. For a chord uv of an oriented spanning cycle, remove
u--succ(u) and v--succ(v), then insert uv; the two paths join into one spanning
path containing it. Cycle edges are included by cutting a different edge.
Omission follows by cutting the edge if it lies on the cycle, or by using any
cycle-cut path if it is a chord.

Using the existing **CITED** all-n>=32 cycle theorem, every square-sum edge is
therefore optional for every n>=32. Robert Gerbicz's own [OEIS A090461
comment](https://oeis.org/A090461) records that cycle result; its full gluing
construction is not re-audited here. Combined with the exact lower-size ledger,
this classifies coarse edge status for all n. It does not classify usage
frequencies or preserve prescribed endpoints or other simultaneously required
edges. The draft's incorrect contrary limitation is repaired and recorded in
the mistakes ledger.

Equivalently, every fixed distinct positive pair a,b with square a+b is used
by some Hamiltonian path and omitted by another at **every** size
n>=max(32,a,b). No square-sum edge stays globally unused or globally forced
through all later sizes. The small exceptional regimes are retained in the
finite edge timeline.

The two independent enumeration methods and the endpoint/path-cover arrival
recurrence retain both joining owners and both surviving endpoints.

The old summand comparison also has an exact boundary. Distinct-parent
additive closure of {2,3} misses {1,4,6}; it is a dependency hypergraph.
The square-sum graph's founders are {1,2,4}; it has three components only
from n=4 through n=12, merges at 13 and 14, and stays connected thereafter.
Vertex 6 belongs to the component founded at 1. Directed shadow reachability,
hypergraph generation, and undirected Hamiltonian paths preserve different
predicates.
