# Primary-source checks

20 October 2026. Theorems in the main note have explicit proofs; this file
records the bounded literature pass, not a novelty claim.

1. S. S. Abhyankar and A. Sathaye, *Uniqueness of plane embeddings of special
   curves*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 124 (1996), 1061–1069.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6.
   The journal PDF returned HTTP 403. Indexed original pages 1062–1063
   were inspected at the journal-PDF mirror:
   https://scispace.com/pdf/uniqueness-of-plane-embeddings-of-special-curves-omujaliiec.pdf.
   Theorem (1.3) requires distinct primes. Theorem (1.4) constructs
   nonuniqueness when one coprime exponent is composite. Page 1063 discusses
   reduction to nonprincipal characteristic sequences and cites the
   Abhyankar–Singh classification. We do not assume its general
   classification as a proved dependency: the main note proves its own
   inequivalence witness directly. The composite-exponent theorem alone
   does not assert that its examples are rational or immersed.

2. A. Sathaye and J. Stenerson, *On Plane Polynomial Curves*, author-hosted
   text dated 10 September 2014:
   https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/papnew.pdf.
   Full PDF downloaded and Section 3.1 read directly. Printed pp.8–12
   give the characteristic-sequence axioms, conductor, initial-entry bound,
   and primitive-generator property used only in the numerical
   degree-pattern observation. The published chapter is *Plane Polynomial
   Curves* (1994), pp.121–142,
   https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-2628-4_6.
   No byte-identical comparison of the 1994 and 2014 texts is asserted.

3. J. Gurycz, *Abhyankar-Moh Property and Unique Affine Embeddings*,
   Universitatis Iagellonicae Acta Mathematica 39 (2001), 99–107.
   Full original article downloaded through the EuDML archival PDF:
   https://eudml.org/serve/125639/accessibleLayeredPdf/0.
   The accessible text layer has OCR spacing and page-number defects;
   the original article is also available from the author's institution:
   https://ruj.uj.edu.pl/server/api/core/bitstreams/6c929a27-5fe2-4e7f-b7d4-ee8d1fc6e082/content.
   Section 7, especially Proposition 7.2, the following discussion,
   Definition 7.3 and Proposition 7.4, was read. It distinguishes
   equivalence of embedded images from extension of a prescribed
   isomorphism. No special <3,4> classification is attributed to this paper.

4. J. Gwoździewicz, *Injectivity on one line*, Theorem 1.1:
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9305008v1.
   Exact hypothesis and conclusion inherited from the frozen low-delta
   source check and independent review: a complex planar polynomial map
   with nonzero constant Jacobian, injective on one affine source line,
   is an automorphism. It is used only to exclude linear special-fiber
   elements in the actual noninvertible Keller corollaries. It is not used
   for the non-Keller hostile or for the inequivalence proof.
