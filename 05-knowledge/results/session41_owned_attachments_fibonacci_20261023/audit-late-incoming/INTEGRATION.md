## What the new source actually supplies

For a supplied named equitable partition into cells of sizes1,3,5,7 with actual
internal tournaments and actual cross blocks, informative components join cells
through nonuniform blocks. A sufficient actual-owner marking budget is
c3+c5+2c7, where isolated nonsingleton cells also count as components. This
bound is per component, not per cell, and is not asserted to be minimal for
every input. Additional intrinsic flags sharpen some cases further.

The one-sided chart-kernel lemma makes an informative attachment functional
on permissible internal charts even when binary rows or columns repeat. This
supports a source-only canonicalizer: choose fixed small-cell templates and a
deterministic informative spanning tree, minimize each child block with its
parent chart fixed, then retain every non-tree block and minimize over root
charts. A seven-cell component requires at most21 root charts. The resulting
code classifies the supplied named-cell source without a target inventory.
Its minimizing chart fibre records the remaining symmetry; a canonical code
does not intrinsically name vertices moved by that symmetry.

This advances the supplied-source problem beyond comparison of two already
supplied attachment arrays. It does not reconstruct arbitrary tournaments
from scalar margins, produce the required bounded partition in general, or
recover a literal Hamiltonian port without its path data.

## Exact joint-alignment information

For connected named-cell networks with FULL internal group C_p, fixed local
edge orbits give edge alignment torsors F_p^2/H_e. Shared vertex rotations
act by D on W=direct-sum_e F_p^2/H_e, and W/image(D) is the exact remaining
classifying coordinate. If beta is cycle rank and r the number of rigid edges,
its dimension is beta+r-1+a; here a=1 precisely for all-symmetric balanced
connections, otherwise0. Thus rigid attachments can leave relative alignment
information even on a tree. This is not the integer residual-flow quotient.

For all allowed degree-d_e blocks, the ordinary named-cell class count is
(product A_(p,d_e)+(p-1)^v product binom(p,d_e))/p^v. The common C_p^v action,
complete block inventory and retained uniform nonedge data justify Burnside
averaging. The formula includes neither converse nor cell permutation, and
prime cardinality alone does not supply its internal-group hypothesis.

## Boundaries and the natural next joint step

Connected networks of regular nine-cells with independent inner triples can
need3m actual owners across m cells. A regular prime-size eleven-cell example
also has a nonfunctional attachment kernel. These rule out extrapolating the
small-cell marking rule from coarse connectedness or primality alone.

The incoming packet explicitly credits Session41's af1ba9bc coupled integer
move criterion. The two results fit in sequence: authenticate shared charts
and retain the fixed-local-type alignment coordinates first; then compile the
actual compatible states, apply coupled-move uniqueness tests, or contract
owner-indexed messages over a certified decomposition. Neither stage permits
prematurely identifying independent local owners.

The unresolved frontier is a compact, lossless native-port observer or state
model at unrestricted orders, including larger-cell kernels and actual path,
converse, electrical mod8 and integer-clock data. The supplied-source advance
must be incorporated into that frontier rather than erased by saying all
source-only work remains open.
