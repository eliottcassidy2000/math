The seeded source pass used seed281 on tracked result Markdown paths. It selected, among others, bounded chain exterior compression, golden seam recurrences, clock channels, valuation frames and two-sheet boundary polarity. Those sources suggest mechanisms, not proof transfers by vocabulary.

## A. Conditional exterior compression versus arbitrary replacement

[Bounded chain compression](../boundary_chain_compression_20261019/BOUNDARY_CHAIN_COMPRESSION.md) preserves the entire core-extension response after conditioning on exterior orders. Its key warning is that every replacement having some favorable witness does not give a common witness for a mixture. Our corresponding question is whether one root receiver serves an entire count bank and all future contexts. The wildcard lane turns that obligation into a complete finite physical experiment basis; the receiver lane keeps the shared coordinates rather than just a favorable current scalar.

## B. Fixed transitive-module blowups are not a new escape

We independently considered proving that a fixed strong quotient with arbitrary transitive module lengths has only polylogarithmically many path-count values. Recovery found exactly this in [Session181](../session181_multiport_defect_20261104/branches/PROOF.md): induced-path insertion and a triangle blowup bound every module length. [Session191](../session191_tournament_growth_20261104/two_apex/SPARSITY.md) already closes the arbitrary ordinal-product loophole via a convergent Dirichlet product. No new theorem is claimed for that route. The useful implication is to change deletion SCC factorization or allow a genuinely growing interaction language, not just longer clocks on a fixed template.

## C. Residues are already known to lose the target

[Session237](../session237_chanpak_synthesis_20261109/README.md) already supplies full fixed-modulus reachability for another tail-state grammar and explicitly distinguishes it from absolute magnitude. Session281's selection lane therefore must not announce that general distinction as new. Its proposed advance is exact arithmetic control in one fixed tiny active-block alphabet, linked to a separately proved zero-density result for that SAME family, including the odd-prime and dyadic mechanisms.

## D. A complete quotient still depends on its operations

Session237 also has equal fixed-port-state rooted twins whose rerooting resources differ. Session281's finite experiment basis concerns the full seven-coordinate same-root fusion carrier, not merely the earlier tail state. Its stronger-coordinate hostile must be read as a scoped extension of this old warning, not discovery that root changes require sidecars.

The research direction remains positive: seek a renewable wider physical bank, exact scalar selection, or a genuinely different constructive gate. Neither local congruence saturation nor a complete observation quotient supplies those constructors by itself.
