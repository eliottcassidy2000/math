# Chan–Pak: moving the reader while keeping the same absolute count

The strongest result is a literal, repeatable change of source and reader
that preserves **all three** rooted coordinates (H,R+,R−). It can move
an arbitrarily long binary block across a fixed central tournament.
The move need not be periodic: a two-state hexadecimal digit law gives
all uniform shuttles in that chart, a boundary test gives exact neutrality,
and the moves have a unique right-normal form. At prescribed target
(H,R+) one can recover physical wings or reject them exactly.

This does **not** prove that every positive odd count except 7 and 21
occurs. The present inverses distinguish a legal target from a
nonexistent source inside the selected physical charts; they do not
prove that at least one chart accepts every target.

| Direction | Proven result |
|---|---|
| [Two-sided source and reader](bilateral.md) | Exact split readout and two literal neutral carries that change rooted responses at H=11,411 and H=1,982,811. Moving four contacts repeatedly preserves the full rooted profile; H=57,839 prime and H=57,841=7·8,263 each have two equivalent presentations. Exact bounded inverses work with just H or with both H and R+. |
| [Shuttle automaton and quotient](SHUTTLE_LANGUAGE.md) | Productive 12-state bit carry collapses to a two-state base-16 digit law. The count correction is purely a parity boundary. A nonperiodic 17-vertex pair gives (111,287,170,133,−15) in both literal graphs. |
| [Reciprocal reader](alternate.md) | Replace the entire strong source and reverse-complement the framed reader while preserving prescribed absolute output in an exact inverse chart. A separate fixed-reader source switch gives the actual adjacent pair 1,169=7·167 and 1,171 prime. Proved infinite framing holes. |
| [All single apex pivots](PIVOT_RETURN.md) | Exact four-branch inverse for prescribed count and signed gap, and infinite nonconverse moves with gap zero. Fixed-gap baselines only grow as Θ(√X), even over all pivots and orders in this class. |
| [Independent switch blocks](INDEPENDENT_RETURNS.md) | Modular selection and the remaining exact carry separate cleanly. Repeated matched components give a connected constant-count family of distinct strong tournaments, but their coarse gain lattice obstructs arbitrary baseline choice. |
| [Small endpoint bank](SMALL_ENDPOINT_BANK.md) | Exact all-order low endpoint supply. Endpoints 1 and 3 coexist at source H only at 9; endpoints 3 and 5 only at H=15. For any larger unit endpoint shift, at least one strong ready source has a singleton terminal component. |

## Replay

With Python 3 (standard library only), run `replay_bilateral.py`,
`replay_shuttle.py`, `alternate_replay.py`, `replay_pivot.py`,
and `replay_independent.py`. The scripts use literal subset path
counts for the small graphs, and separate exact recurrences for the
large address and carry checks.

The practical next proof target is a source-bank theorem, not another
fixed-gap census: the inverse must accept a prescribed N in at least
one chart, and that chart must have a **physical** way to close the
absolute carry. The new normal form helps distinguish redundant
presentations from genuine growth in the source bank.
