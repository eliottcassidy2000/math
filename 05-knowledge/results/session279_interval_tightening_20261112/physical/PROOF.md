# Pisot suffixes turn a scalar Hamilton count back into its full root interface

**Session279, 12 November 2026. PROVED from the inherited physical block law;
FINITE-EXACT controls below. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

This packet tightens a different interval from the A000568 interval: it bounds
missing **root coordinates conditional on an exact Hamilton count**. The
positive advance is an all-depth scalar-to-interface decoder for a concrete
infinite family. It does not assert that this family contains every odd count.

## 0. Inheritance, concept board, and the change of viewpoint

The physical input is the moving-root one-apex block law, now correctly
identified with the inherited O/Z/J grammar in
[`kps_s25_euler_field_bridge_law_20261111/README.md`, B4](../../kps_s25_euler_field_bridge_law_20261111/README.md),
and used in [Session278's physical correction proof](../../session278_signed_corrections_20261112/physical/PROOF.md).
For a rooted tournament let A be its Hamiltonian-path count. Let o and e be
sums, over simple paths starting at the root with respectively odd/even numbers
of vertices, of H on the complementary induced tournament; H(empty)=1.
Write W=o+e and d=o−e. The inherited nonempty-block grammar has

    0 <= d <= A <= W.                                      (C)

The three live concepts are:

1. **Exact resummation:** retain the full root-state operator instead of any
   fixed number of signed matching corrections. Session278 showed that those
   fixed corrections keep the wrong dominant growth pole.
2. **Pisot observation:** a dominant expanding direction can coexist with
   absolutely contracting transverse directions. That may recover an integer
   state from a scalar, rather than merely predict its ratios.
3. **Carry/ancestry:** closeness to an integer ray is not physical realizability;
   preserve congruences and the actual terminal seed throughout inverse steps.

The distant sources are the [Session128 triadic/Pisot packet](../../session128_triadic_pisot_feedback_20261031/README.md)
(complete observation windows versus scalar norms, and integral-order costs)
and [THM-2163, radix-relation carry descent](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2163-radix-relation-carry-descent.md)
(terminal-owner information is necessary even after exact digit recurrences).
THM-1360's figurate/Pisot atlas was also read as inspiration, not used as a
blanket theorem about all of its named polynomial families.

**Map and information ledger.** The source is a physical rooted tournament
followed by k specified triangle blocks. The target is its count N and a
rational cone slice. The count and suffix length are preserved. The two root
coordinates are initially forgotten. Their recovery uses a bound on the
initial scale W, followed by exact mod-4 inverse steps and membership in an
actual seed bank. This is not an equivalence between arbitrary integers and
tournaments. The cheap decisive test is whether both rational coordinate
intervals have width below one, and whether their integer candidate survives
the inverse and seed checks.

## 1. A physical positive-cone operator

A one-apex block has a transitive spine and a binary word: bit 1 means apex
beats that spine vertex. Compose blocks by ordinal sum and reverse the arc
between each consecutive pair of apexes; root at the last apex. Appending the
word 10 appends a directed triangle by this legal operation, adding three
vertices. It acts by

    x' = M x,   x=(A,W,d)^t,
    M = [[3,3,−1], [3,4,0], [1,0,0]].                       (1)

This is inherited. The minus sign does not make the physical operation signed:
in (A,o,e) it is the strictly positive matrix

    [[3,2,4], [2,2,2], [1,2,2]].                          (2)

The cone C in (C) is generated by

    r0=(1,1,0), r1=(1,1,1), r2=(0,1,0),
    x=(A−d)r0+d r1+(W−A)r2.                              (3)

All three coefficients are nonnegative and their sum is exactly W. The ray
r2 is a geometric bound, not an asserted physical tournament with A=0.
In the cone coefficients, (1) becomes the nonnegative matrix

    [[5,4,3], [1,1,0], [1,2,1]].                          (4)

Thus M preserves C; C_k=M^k C are nested. For k>=1 all three image rays
v_i(k)=M^k r_i have strictly positive first coordinates a_i(k).

## 2. Exact, simultaneous rational intervals

Let an actual output have first coordinate N>0 and promised triangle-suffix
length k>=1. Normalize the three image rays by their first coordinates. Every
point of C_k with first coordinate N is in the triangle spanned by
N v_i/a_i. Consequently

    N min_i(W_i/a_i) <= W <= N max_i(W_i/a_i),
    N min_i(d_i/a_i) <= d <= N max_i(d_i/a_i).              (5)

These extrema are attained on the cone rays, so the individual coordinate
intervals are tight for C_k. Their Cartesian product is not the entire
triangular slice; joint inverse checks below restore what the box loses.
They use only exact rational arithmetic and matrix exponentiation.

If the source has W<=B, equation (3) gives

    N <= B a_max(k).                                     (6)

Define, using ordinary exterior products in coordinate order (A,W,d),

    E_k = max_(i<j) ||v_i(k) wedge v_j(k)||_infinity,
    U_k(B) = B a_max(k) E_k / a_min(k)^2.                 (7)

The width of EACH interval in (5), for every source with W<=B, is at most
U_k(B). Indeed a ratio difference is one wedge minor divided by a_i a_j;
then use (6). A width below one gives at most one integer in each interval.
A true physical output has at least one, and so both coordinates are uniquely
recovered. No approximation or low-bit residue of N is being assumed: N is
supplied exactly.

An additional exact geometric identity is

    area of {(W/A,d/A): x in C_k, A>0}
      = 4^k / (2 a_0(k) a_1(k) a_2(k)).                 (8)

This follows by dividing the determinant of the three ray columns by their
first coordinates: |det M|=4 and |det[r0 r1 r2]|=1.
Area shrinkage alone would not bound both widths; (7) does.

## 3. Why absolute uncertainty eventually disappears

The characteristic polynomial, inherited from the triangle recurrence, is

    p(t)=t^3−7t^2+4t−4.                                  (9)

It has one real root lambda in (6,7) and two nonreal conjugates mu, conjugate(mu).
For example its discriminant is −3376; p(6)<0<p(7). Since its root product
is 4,

    |mu| = sqrt(4/lambda) < sqrt(2/3) < 1.               (10)

The rational-root test shows (9) is irreducible. Thus lambda is a cubic Pisot
number. We do not invoke general Pisot numeration or assert any canonical digit
system. We use only this explicitly derived spectral gap.

The positive matrix (2) has a positive Perron left vector. Every nonzero ray
in (3), expressed in (A,o,e), is nonnegative. Therefore

    M^k r_i = c_i lambda^k v + O(|mu|^k),  c_i>0,
    v=(1, 3/(lambda−4), 1/lambda).                       (11)

All eigenvalues are distinct. In wedge products the leading rank-one terms
cancel, leaving E_k=O(lambda^k |mu|^k). Also a_min and a_max are comparable to
lambda^k. Equation (7) gives

    U_k(B)=O(B |mu|^k) -> 0                              (12)

for each fixed B. Hence sufficiently deep triangle suffixes recover the
entire integer root packet from the scalar count and depth, UNIFORMLY over
all cone seeds with W<=B. The fixed scale bound is essential to this argument;
there is no uniform threshold here over arbitrarily large sources.

This is stronger than convergence of W/A and d/A. Their errors contract fast
enough to overcome the exponentially growing absolute count, because the two
transverse eigenvalues themselves have modulus below one.

## 4. A completely rational threshold: W<=96 and every k>=25

The preceding asymptotic theorem is made effective without floating roots.
In the pair-coordinate basis (A wedge W, A wedge d, W wedge d),

    T = wedge^2 M = [[3,3,4], [−3,1,0], [−4,0,0]].        (13)

For every vector in C_3, the one-step first-coordinate growth lies in

    g = 97/15 <= A'/A <= G = 797/123.                    (14)

To check this, evaluate A'/A on the three rays M^3 r_i. It is a weighted
average of those ray values. Since C_k is nested, (14) continues to hold on
every C_k for k>=3.

Direct exact matrix multiplication gives ||T^4||_infinity=992. Therefore

    U_(k+4)(B) <= rho U_k(B),  k>=3,
    rho=992 (G/g^2)^4 < 0.572 < 1.                      (15)

The proof retains all factors: a_max can grow by at most G^4, a_min by at least
g^4, and E by at most 992. For B=96 the four exact rational values in
`results.json` have the following decimal displays:

| k | U_k(96) |
|---|---:|
| 25 | 0.941036059132397 |
| 26 | 0.657036659097534 |
| 27 | 0.742115181143059 |
| 28 | 0.458249022998033 |

All four are strictly below one by rational comparison. Applying (15) to each
residue class modulo four proves the assertion for **every k>=25**. This is
not extrapolation from a finite table. Nor is 25 claimed optimal: the bound
in (7) is deliberately conservative.

The individual widths need not decrease at every step. Their oscillation is
expected from the nonreal conjugates. The four-step certificate is what turns
four finite computations into an infinite-depth statement.

**Injectivity corollary.** For every k>=25 the map x -> first(M^k x) is injective
on integer cone packets with A>=1 and W<=96. Equal counts recover equal W,d;
invertibility of M then recovers equal seeds. This separates packets, not
isomorphism classes: two different rooted tournaments with the same packet
are intentionally not distinguished.

The number 96 is an explicit workload scale, not a special universal constant.
For any chosen B, compute (7) until four consecutive bounds are below one;
(12) ensures this terminates, and (15) certifies all later depths.

## 5. Exact inverse, physical seed ownership, and prescribed targets

Given an integer output (A,W,d), its only possible predecessor under (1) is

    A_old=d,
    W_old=(W−3d)/4,
    d_old=3d+3W_old−A.                                  (16)

It exists in the integer cone iff W−3d is divisible by four and the resulting
triple satisfies 0<=d_old<=A_old<=W_old. Iterate exactly k times. The division
by four is a genuine ancestry/carry condition, not a harmless real rescaling.
The ambient lattice image of M^k has index 4^k; that fact alone does not count
physical packets, whose parity constraints and seed membership remain needed.

For a supplied finite bank of actual rooted tournaments, the following is a
certifying partial target selector, complete when the interval widths are below
one (in particular in the scale-bounded k>=25 regime):

1. Given prescribed N and depth k, compute the intervals (5).
2. If either has no integer, reject. If either has several integers, retain
   an ambiguity (or enumerate the finite box), never round silently.
3. For a unique pair, apply (16) k times, retaining its divisibility and cone
   checks. Reject a failure.
4. Accept only when the recovered packet belongs to the supplied physical
   bank; output that stored tournament followed by k actual triangle blocks.

Necessity follows from (5), (16). Sufficiency follows from the stored physical
seed, invertibility, and the actual block construction. A nonempty formal
cone preimage is not itself a realizability proof. Initial cases must remain
in the bank: no presumed empty tournament or erased root boundary is inserted.

The threshold proves uniqueness for true outputs at depths k>=25. For an
arbitrary proposed N one checks its own exact widths; (6) also rejects N above
the bank's scale bound. In the experiment, every depth-25 neighbor trial had
unique-or-empty intervals, so no ambiguous case was skipped.

If depth is not supplied, the search is still finite for N in this language:
A'=3A+3W−d>=5A on C, so a physical seed A>=1 requires 5^k<=N.
Test the finitely many possible depths, including the bank itself at k=0.
For completeness at short ambiguous depths, enumerate the finite coordinate
box or directly evaluate the finite bank; `recover_packet` instead reports
those ambiguities honestly. This is not claimed faster than directly evaluating a small known bank.
Its structural benefit is recovering the full reusable interface from one
absolute target count after a certified long suffix.

## 6. A hostile control: ratio convergence does not erase scalar memory

The two explicit physical seeds

    block words (01,00): (A,W,d)=(9,9,1), order 6;
    block words (10,0):  (A,W,d)=(9,9,3), order 5

have the same A and W but different d. Their first triangle extensions have
counts 53 and 51. For every k>=1 their counts differ by

    2 first(M^(k−1)(1,0,0)^t).                          (17)

Indeed their vector difference is −2e_d and M e_d=−e_A. The factor in (17)
is exactly the inherited k−1-triangle count with its formal initialization.
Thus their scalar difference grows exponentially even while their normalized
root coordinates converge to the same ray. The new decoder does NOT ignore
this memory: it conditions on the exact N, then reconstructs it.

The all-depth recovery theorem explains the right combination of perspectives:
**real cone squeezing determines a candidate, lattice carries test ancestry,
and physical endpoint ownership certifies a tournament.** None can replace
the other two.

## 7. What this achieves for Chan–Pak, and what remains missing

The program checks all 210 one/two-block words with nonempty block lengths at
most three by independent subset dynamic programming on their literal graphs.
They produce 84 distinct packets with W<=96. The threshold therefore supplies
an infinite, fully physical exact selector for this bank. All 84 packet
families are separated at every depth>=25. Short triangle extensions of the
two hostile seeds are also literally recounted, independently of the matrix.

A fixed finite bank followed only by triangle blocks contributes at most
O(log X) candidate counts <=X, because each block multiplies A by at least 5.
It cannot establish all-odd coverage. The wider task is to construct a physical
**digit/branch supply** that both covers the prescribed N and keeps a certified
suffix decoder. Pisot contraction is an observation mechanism, not a digit
coverage theorem. Branches can also destroy the uniform spectral gap; no
arbitrary-switching claim is made.

The useful next experiment is to combine such synchronizing suffixes with the
existing noninteracting physical digits: can choices be inserted before the
suffix with a scale bound that grows slowly enough for (7) to remain below
one, while their scalar target intervals overlap? The exact budget is
U_k(B), rather than a vague appeal to asymptotic similarity.

## 8. Classifying a larger supply of decoding blocks, and its first obstruction

The triangle is not isolated. Consider ANY one-apex word w which starts with
1 and ends with 0, so both memory indicators in the inherited block matrix
are one. Put n=|w|, t=2^n, P=the ordinary binary value of w, and
Q=sum_(j=0)^(n−1) (1−w_(j+1))2^j. The block law is

    M_w=[[h,Q+1,−1],[P+1,t,0],[1,0,0]], h=g(P),
    g(0)=1, g(2v+1)=g(v),
    g(2v)=g(v)+v+(v mod2), v>=1.                       (18)

This transfer, including the formula g, is inherited, not new. In (A,o,e)
its matrix has strictly positive entries (P,Q>=2 and n>=2). Its characteristic
polynomial is

    f_w(z)=z^3−(h+t)z^2+c z−t,
    c=h t−(P+1)(Q+1)+1.                                (19)

Let lambda_w be its Perron root. From the positive eigenvector's W equation,
(lambda_w−t)W=(P+1)A>0, hence lambda_w>t. The product of the two other roots
is t/lambda_w in (0,1).

**Exact synchronization criterion.** Both non-Perron eigenvalues have modulus
strictly below one if and only if

    f_w(1)<0 AND f_w(−1)<0.                             (20)

Proof: if the two roots are nonreal, their common squared modulus is their
product <1, and both endpoint inequalities hold. If they are real, their
product is positive and below one, so they have the same sign and cannot
both have magnitude >=1. Factoring f at +1 and −1 shows that the corresponding
endpoint inequality fails exactly when one of them reaches or crosses that
endpoint. This also retains equality at an eigenvalue +1 or −1. The Perron
factor at either endpoint is negative. These cases exhaust the possibilities.

Whenever (20) holds, the cubic is irreducible over the rationals: an integer
root could not be one of the roots strictly inside the unit circle, and the
Perron root, if integer, would have to divide t even though it exceeds t.
Thus lambda_w is a Pisot number. The spectral proof of Sections2–3 applies
to every such fixed block: bounded source packets are eventually recoverable
from their scalar Hamilton count after sufficiently many copies of the block.
The source cone is preserved: h<=P+1 follows inductively from g, Q<=t−1, and

    W'−A'=(P+1−h)A+(t−Q−1)W+d >=0,
    A'−d'=(h−1)A+(Q+1)W−d >=0.

A repeated non-Perron root, if present in a more general matrix problem,
would introduce a polynomial factor k but still decay when its modulus is
below one. In this irreducible characteristic-zero cubic the roots are distinct.
The specific uniform threshold25 belongs only to the triangle and B96; it is
not exported to other words.

The corresponding inverse retains its own radix rather than imposing mod4:

    A_old=d,
    W_old=(W−(P+1)d)/t,
    d_old=h d+(Q+1)W_old−A.                             (20a)

Thus divisibility by t=2^n, cone membership, and a physical seed owner give
the same exact ancestry test. The lattice image index is t, not one; a
real-cone classification without this radix sidecar would lose admissibility.

Exact enumeration of this criterion finds all63 eligible words of lengths
2 through7 pass. At length8 the unique failure is

    w=10101010, P=Q=170, t=256, h=117,
    f_w(z)=z^3−373z^2+712z−256,
    f_w(1)=84, f_w(−1)=−1342.                           (21)

This is the first failure BY WORD LENGTH in this declared class, not a minimal
failure over all tournament constructions. Since f_w(0)<0<f_w(1) and
f_w(2)<0, it has real roots in (0,1) and (1,2), besides its Perron root>256.
Its expanding transverse root invalidates the Pisot argument. Normalized
ratios can still converge, but uniformly shrinking absolute transverse
uncertainty is no longer supplied by this block. We do not infer that every
alternative decoding method fails for it.

The verifier gives the exact pass/fail table for all2047 active words through
length12. This classifies their spectral decoding suitability without
floating eigenvalues. It is NOT a count-coverage census. The new constructive
question is sharper: which mixtures of these certified block interfaces also
admit prescribed-target digit supply? Individual Pisot blocks do not imply
Pisot behavior for arbitrary switching products, so that composition question
remains open here.
