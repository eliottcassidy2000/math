connect a four-bit model of the sixteen good Fano codes with exact port work.
Their crossing-star action and the geometric D12 action have different orbits.
That distinction supports the same rule used here: a combinatorial symmetry
must be checked against its actual geometric carrier. It does not transport
all-order black-core connectivity or an A000568 recurrence automatically.

The broad unification attempt currently has a precise common operation:
pass to a quotient or representation, record the discarded information,
then test whether the target predicate survives. It organizes these examples
without asserting logical equivalence between unrelated open problems.
