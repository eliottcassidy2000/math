The two JSON files record actual choices, including unhelpful ones. A source
is an idea input at its stated scope, not an audited dependency merely
because it was randomly selected. These notes supplement the separate
constructor, bridge and wildcard ledgers.

## How the lanes rejoined

exactness. The universal domination slack becomes hereditary; its fixed

forbidden-basis synthesis using well-quasi-order and exact rational-series
