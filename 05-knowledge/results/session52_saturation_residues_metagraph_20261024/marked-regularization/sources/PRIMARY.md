# Primary source and construction attribution

Checked 24 October 2026 using the primary arXiv HTML source:

Omar Hatem, Sara Mohamed, Isabel Müller and Daoud Siniora,
*Cofinality of Regular Tournaments*, arXiv:2605.04846v1, Lemma 4.3.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04846v1#S4

The exact lemma statement is: “Every tournament is a subtournament of a Type-I
regular tournament.” The proof uses two isomorphic copies of the original
n-vertex tournament, opposite cross-copy outcomes, matching arcs from U to V,
and a vertex with all V incoming and all U outgoing. Its 2n+1-vertex
construction is exactly the D(T) used here. The paper alternates b/w for the
new vertex; our notation is z. The original vertices form V and their copied
vertices form U; both have the original orientation.

Attribution boundary: the construction, its regularity, the embedding of T,
and the Type-I property are known, not discoveries of this packet. The proofs
below independently extract rooted faithfulness, edge-block transport,
distinguished cycle transport, canonical-root recognition, and unmarked
collision witnesses. No literature-priority claim is made for those extractions.
