## 3. The odd-zeta comparison now isolates one explicit arithmetic gap

The recovered [Apéry framework](../reference/apery-style-irrationality-framework.md)
and [odd-zeta audit](odd_zeta_parallels_petersen_20261001.md) distinguish
nonvanishing, smallness, and denominator control. This matches the separate
requirements in [Brown, section1.2](https://www.ihes.fr/~brown/IrratModuliMotivesv8.pdf).
We transport those proof obligations, not a zeta theorem or an unreviewed
preprint's arithmetic estimates.

For x_d=4*2^d/(2^d+1)^2, the normalized shell polynomial has exact least
coefficient denominator

    D_N=product_(d=1)^N(2^d-1)^2.

[The denominator package](collatz_shell_denominator_budget_20261005.md)
proves that every rational polynomial which equals1 at the target and
vanishes at the first N shells must pay a multiple of D_N, even if it has
extra degree. Thus a coefficient norm below512 does not remove a quadratic
bit-height bill.

That obstruction has a constructive repair. Put

    A_N(h)=product_(d=1)^N[(2^d+1)^2 h-2^(d+2)],
    I_N(h)=h^((N+9)/2) A_N(h),        N>=3 odd.

I_N has integer coefficients. Away from the target it is zero on the first
N shells and negative on every later shell, with absolute value less than
2^((9-3N)/2). The actual law has known positive rooted leaves at arbitrarily
large distances. Therefore, under the hypothetical missing-atom condition
p_m=0, its readout J_N satisfies

    0 < -J_N < 2^((9-3N)/2).                              (2)

**Nonvanishing and coefficient-cleared decay are both paid.** A sufficient
remaining arithmetic lemma is: there are integers E_N>0 such that, under
the same hypothesis, E_N J_N is integral and

    E_N * 2^((9-3N)/2) -> 0.                              (3)

That would contradict(2) and prove p_m>0. Condition(3) is **unproved**.
Integer polynomial coefficients do not imply integer expectation: the
point mass at16/25 gives E[h^2(9h-8)]=-14336/15625. Nor do infinitely many
rational individual atom weights imply a rational total readout.

The raw localized moments cannot satisfy an Apéry-type finite polynomial
recurrence: their generating function has infinitely many uncancelled poles
from the explicitly rooted one-edge leaves. This excludes that particular
carrier, not other linear forms or auxiliary-variable constructions.

## 4. Two representation repairs, with different retained information

[Coefficient reversal](collatz_reciprocal_selector_20261005.md) gives

    R(z)=product_(d>=1)(1-x_d z)/(1-x_d),

an entire function with a controlled coefficient norm. In the reciprocal
coordinate z=(alpha+alpha^(-1)+2)/4 its dyadic shift law is exact. Squaring
alpha induces J->J^2-2 and z->(2z-1)^2; on shells this is
x_d->x_(2d)=x_d^2/(2-x_d)^2. The golden example alpha=phi^4 gives J=7.
These are exact algebraic identifications, with no asserted orbit conjugacy.

R(1/h_m(j)) is the exact atom indicator, but **all negative shell moments
diverge** on the actual known rooted leaves. Its power series cannot be
integrated term by term there. Multiplying the ordinary moment generating
function instead by(1-z)R(z) cancels its poles and yields an entire carrier
with coefficient norm below1024 and value p_m at1. That repairs the analytic
representation; it does not introduce a sign or arithmetic-lattice theorem.

[Spatial reconstruction](collatz_specialization_sidecar_20261005.md) varies
the observation address while keeping one fixed moment degree. For k>=7,
the field H_k=(I+T_k)p has an explicitly contracting off-diagonal operator.
It gives finite signed stencils, exact tail bills, noise control, and retained
bounds under refinement. Degree12 is substantially cheaper than the minimal
diagonal-dominance degree7 in the displayed plans. A synthetic law has a
positive atom certified by neighboring readings although its one target
reading admits a zero-target completion.

The [Fourier continuation](collatz_spatial_fourier_inverse_20261005.md)
then proves that even degree1 retains the full law. Its sampled kernel is
sech^2(d ln2/2), whose Fourier transform is a positive beta integral.
Periodization gives the half-line operator bounds

    (1/200000) I <= A_1 <= 9 I.

This supplies bounded l2 inversion and finite measurement plans, albeit with
very poor conservative cost compared with degrees8/12. The same beta
function appears in the counter weight2 B(K+1,L+2) and in the Fourier
transform at conjugate complex arguments. The common integral is exact;
their measures and positivity questions are different.

The universal hostile remains p=delta_0: every smoothed reading is positive,
yet all other atoms are zero. Identifiability and positivity are separate.
