## Why these connections work

The starting board had five objects: actual LRC entry, overlap profiles,
walk gcds, real-rooted common coefficient carriers, and integral jet
observers. For each, the operation had to preserve its target-bearing data.

* **Arithmetic filter plus two-coordinate overlap.** THM-4453 intersected an
  arithmetic atlas with a measure threshold. Reintroducing THM-4153's
  component length gives a sharp joint envelope. Two different estimates
  make the infinite parameter range finite: a product bound for measure
  and a largest-speed bound for width. Retaining their attaining pairs
  gives a sufficient physical entry criterion with inclusive boundaries.
* **Walk support plus divisibility.** The inherited exact cancellation is
  endpoint gcd divided by visited gcd. A walk's support sits inside one
  divisor-induced component, whose gcd must equal that visited gcd if the
  factor is exact. A spanning-tree walk proves the converse and provides
  a short certificate. Chronology can be forgotten only after retaining
  this component and its full gcd.
* **One preserved coefficient plus coupled differentiation.** Differentiating
  the common real-rooted carrier from both ends preserves the same zero.
  Centering at that coefficient turns the weights into polynomials in
  squared displacement. A triangular recurrence then reduces all windows
  to the minimal nonnegative cone. Arbitrary positive weights and arbitrary
  zero-preserving operators fail the explicit hostiles.
* **Homogenization plus primitive lattice coordinates.** Cardinal interpolation
  forms written with determinant brackets have exact primitive content.
  This computes attained denominators. Complete local jets transform
  unimodularly; residue-class splitting supplies an integral chart when
  one global affine chart is impossible. The inherited largest-factor law
  then transfers without pretending that all intermediate factors do.

No direct mathematical map is claimed from Laurent signs to LRC phases, or
from Smith precision to decoder safety. Their common methodological feature
is explicit retention of the coordinate needed by the next operation.
