## Live questions and decisive probes

1. **Geometric capacity.** For N=8,10,12, how many independent Mbar(0,5) factors can occur in a stable-tree boundary stratum? Count flags and internal edges. Keep Cartesian adjacency, full intersections, and dual complexes distinct.
2. **Quadratic orbit capacity.** Can an injective real vertex labeling make x -> x^2+c act as an order-three graph automorphism of K_5, K_{3,3}, or Petersen? Compare the automorphism's cycle type with the degree-six period-three polynomial. Repeat over Q only with an explicit rationality check.
3. **Square-sum triangle transport.** Interpolate the cyclic permutation of three distinct rational labels by a quadratic, normalize by affine conjugacy, and determine which parameter invariant survives. Square edge sums need their own side condition; affine conjugacy does not preserve them automatically.
4. **Constructive graceful continuation.** Recover the latest marked insertion and seam mechanisms, then seek a move or obstruction beyond the inherited bipartite gauge. Bounded checks must state their tree universe and endpoint constraints.
5. **E atlas.** Compare endpoint-reversal graphs, energy and elementary-symmetric quantities, and standard Euler/Eisenstein/Epstein interpretations. An odd-zeta connection needs a specified transform, series, integral, or period map, rather than a numerical resemblance.

## Failure controls

The Petersen graph is non-Hamiltonian but this does not obstruct every other graph problem that contains it. Planarity obstructions K_5 and K_{3,3} are not interchangeable with automorphism obstructions. A rational parameter does not imply rational periodic points. A boundary-period factorization does not supply the denominator estimates required for an irrationality proof.
