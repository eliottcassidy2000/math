# Tournament methods: open-problem routes and two recovered proofs

2026-10-29. **PROVED scoped deductions / CITED inputs / FINITE-EXACT tests /
OPEN research targets. Same-agent review; no external novelty claim.**

The strongest outcome of the recovery pass is concrete: two repository gaps
can be closed using the right representation.

1. **HYP-9375 is proved.** The Legendre-symbol tournament on increasing primes
   congruent to3 modulo4 is quasirandom. Heath-Brown's classical quadratic
   large sieve bounds its signed matrix norm by O_epsilon(X^(1/2+epsilon)).
   Dividing by the number of primes and applying a direct telescoping counting
   argument gives every fixed tournament class its random frequency. The
   inherited twelve-vertex27 class consequently occurs three times as often
   as the81 class. This preserves the hostile rather than merging it.
2. **THM-1450's VC=1 gap is closed at every size.** For open out-neighbourhoods
   the only families at n>=3 are TT_n and C3=>TT_(n-3). A single outside vertex
   beating any vertex of a cyclic triangle forces a shattered pair. There
   are exactly4n!/3 labelled members and two isomorphism classes.

The complete arguments, bounds and operation boundaries are in
[PROOFS.md](PROOFS.md). These are closures of recorded repository questions,
not claims to solve the general Erdős–Hajnal, Sumner or arithmetic conjectures.
The prime result uses a published theorem; it is not a discovery of a new
large sieve.
