# A proof-opportunity atlas, with actual maps and stopping conditions

**Status convention:** PROVED means the scoped argument given or inherited;
CITED means an external theorem; FINITE-EXACT means a complete stated finite
universe; OPEN includes proposed next lemmas. Rankings are research judgment.

## Inheritance and live board

Closest mechanisms: exact ordered path-component composition, SCC products,
integer response recovery and source-preserving transport. Hostile control:
the actual twelve-vertex81/27 pair, equal labelled cyclic triples but unequal
automorphism groups and joint response ranks. Corrected near misses: scalar
Hamilton counts as an EH proxy; a partial exchange digraph called a cyclic
tournament; uniform child sizes substituted for arbitrary weights. Least-used
sidecars recovered: open-neighbourhood VC witnesses and the numerical height
of a prime-indexed character matrix.

Board: **prime atoms; weighted joint incidence; realization; transport and
legal operations; arithmetic height; exact versus averaged observations.**
Anchor=spectrum/carrier; niche=VC classification; wildcard=Legendre prefix
frequencies. The niche and wildcard produced the two proofs in PROOFS.md.

## 1. Hamilton-path spectrum completeness — strongest direct external fit

**OPEN:** does every positive odd value except7,21 occur as H(T)? This is
Chan–Pak Conjecture6.2, attributed there to bof/Royle; not a newly proposed
problem. [Primary statement](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10214v2#S6.SS5).

**Recovered:** May `07-reflections/breakthrough-candidate-map.md` §1;
THM-4094 / `01-canon/theorems/THM-4094-hamiltonian-matching-deficit-and-two-prime-lane-completeness.md`;
[October feedback-word grammar](../feedback_word_hamilton_spectrum_20261020/README.md).
The last already proves the finite-state grammar and its one-apex limitations.

**Map/predicate:** an actual attachment word -> tournament -> ordinary
Hamilton-path count. SCC order-join multiplies counts. Full gap/port responses
retain orphan paths and repeated visits that scalar H loses. With the cited
carry atoms, the residual target is strong realization of p!=7 and7p with
p!=3. **Cheap test:** test a proposed grammar against the prime13, omitted by
the entire one-apex class despite being globally attainable. Then prove a
source-labelled unit-step interval or a new infinite arithmetic lane.
Connectedness of a move graph, a gcd of jumps, or another long prefix alone
does not prove numerical coverage.

## 2. Integral selected-path recovery — most direct metagraph target

**OPEN repository problem:** every strong atom admits an integral left inverse
for paired native three-run responses after at most one selected actual
Hamilton-path complement, possibly retaining a strong target.
**Recovered:** [COMPLEMENT_KERNEL](../overnight_rank_return_20261029/COMPLEMENT_KERNEL.md),
[RUN_RECURRENCE](../overnight_rank_return_20261029/RUN_RECURRENCE.md),
[DEFECT_TRANSFER](../overnight_rank_return_20261029/DEFECT_TRANSFER.md).

**Map/predicate:** (actual tournament, legal path, integral port weights) ->
two responses in one common transported frame; preserve recoverability over
every modulus. Rational rank loses torsion; an unframed multiset loses
alignment. **Known finite theorem:** all396 strong classes through seven,
with zero moves allowed for already invertible sources. **Cheap test:**
search prime atoms for a response-kernel vector invisible under every legal
path, then distinguish arbitrary integer weights from physically realizable
ones. T5's parity-defective paths and the nine-vertex alternate selector are
mandatory controls. No full twelve-vertex minimality theorem is inherited.
Incoming [Session105](../session105_metagraph_recursion_20261029/README.md)
shows that source/target class pairs already fail extension lumpability at
order4. The [full seam matrix](../seven_return_native_recursions_20261029/SEAM.md)
also fails native-complement closure at order5. These are separate operation
languages and concrete reasons to test a proposed smaller carrier before
extrapolating its useful single-operation recurrence.

## 3. H>=disc — a narrow strong-atom inequality with a finished outer induction

**OPEN repository problem:** for strong C, prove
H(C)>=max(1,s(C))*disc(C), with K=A-A^t,
s=1^t(I+K)^(-1)1 and disc=det(I+K)/2^(n-1).
**Recovered:** THM-1950 / `01-canon/theorems/THM-1950-h-ge-disc-reduced-to-strongly-connected.md`;
THM-3729 / `01-canon/theorems/THM-3729-rooted-pfaffian-response-and-sign-root-deletion-average.md`.

**Map/predicate:** tournament -> skew response/determinant, compared to exact
Hamilton counts. Schur composition and the max-kernel inequality already
transfer the strong base to all tournaments. Determinants forget path
incidences. **Cheap test:** express the residual in the actual joint-response
basis and test Paley7, with exact ratio27/8, before conjecturing monotone
growth. An averaged root identity is not a pointwise root inequality.
Use the exact Schur factor, not a blanket supermultiplicativity slogan.

## 4. Erdős–Hajnal and bounded VC — structural, with a concrete recovered proof

**OPEN general target:** for every fixed forbidden tournament F, every
F-free n-tournament has a transitive subset of size n^epsilon(F).
**CITED progress:** Nguyen–Scott–Seymour prove all two-colourable F, including
every six-vertex tournament. [Theorem6.3](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/scott/Papers/ehvc.pdf).
Do not nominate the old exceptional six-vertex Paley deletion as still open.

**Recovered:** July THM-1390 leverage audit, THM-1845 transitive sandwich and
THM-1450 / `01-canon/theorems/THM-1450-linearity-trivialises-vc-and-the-two-vc-one-tournaments.md`.
P2 now closes the latter's all-size VC=1 classification. P3 proves the exact
weighted transitive-subset substitution law and a sufficient norm certificate.

**Map/predicate:** a modular decomposition -> weighted transitive support
sets -> largest transitive size. Keep the child weights; discard path count
as a sufficient proxy. **Cheap test:** zero weights supported on an induced
C3 defeat the naive balanced T5 exponent. Next test a certified rational
polytope of weights for a specified prime palette. This proves an exponent
for that closure; extending to all F-free prime atoms is the hard missing
step. VC of named guard tests is a different set system from VC of vertex
neighbourhoods; a common terminology does not identify them.

## 5. Sumner and leaf-sensitive oriented-tree embedding

**OPEN general finite target:** every(2n-2)-tournament contains every oriented
n-tree. The sufficiently large case is CITED; Benford–Montgomery give
stronger asymptotic bounds when leaves or maximum degree are controlled.
See SOURCES.md. **Recovered:** [all6880 eight-vertex hosts versus27 five-trees](../sumner_t5_petersen_collatz_20261005.md)
and [guard reconstruction](../guard_reconstruction_20261026/CONNECTIONS.md).

**Map/predicate:** rooted tree fragments -> injective embeddings in a
tournament with a designated root image. Preserve directions, the complete
used-vertex set and agreement of shared roots. Multiplying marginal root
counts invents overlapping embeddings. LCA only reconciles genuinely nested
branch information; it cannot replace disjointness of different branches.
**Cheap test:** reproduce the five-tree degree obstruction, then give a
rooted Hall-type lemma for one additional branching pattern. This is a real
tree-embedding problem. The graceful-fiber exchange theorems cannot be
imported until the underlying tree shape is preserved.

## 6. Minimum cycle densities — an underused spectral frontier

**OPEN target in the checked literature:** the full Linial–Morgenstern
minimum four-cycle density at fixed triangle density, and related longer
cycle extremal problems. Ma–Tang explain why lengths2 mod4 need a different
candidate family. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14142).
No later general resolution was found in the targeted freshness searches.

**Recovered mechanisms:** July baby-Hodge cycle-count holes, HYP-3099 AppB,
and the recent12/22 exterior trace clocks. No earlier named Linial–Morgenstern
route was found in the targeted canon/result search: this is a new application
proposal, not a recovered theorem.
**Map/predicate:** a tournament adjacency kernel -> normalized cycle traces
and realizable spectral moments. Keep even cycles and normalization; the
odd-cycle formula for H discards the relevant fourth-cycle coordinate.
For long cycles, trace(A^l) counts closed walks, not injective simple cycles;
the difference must be bounded or retained. **Cheap test:** evaluate the
proposed inequality on transitive random block models, Paley and carousel
families, separating exact finite counts from asymptotic densities. A
positivity certificate on realizable moment regions is a plausible proof unit.

## 7. Skew-Hadamard matrices and symplectic equiangular frames

**OPEN existence direction:** skew-Hadamard matrices at all admissible orders.
Fallon's2025 paper gives an actual equivalence with a symplectic frame
existence conjecture, using tournament diamonds; see SOURCES.md.
**Recovered:** Paley design/code work in
`../oai3_two_orbits_twos_and_threes_20261007.md`, and the current prime-power
Paley pencil packet.

**Literal map:** a skew tournament sign matrix S of order q with S1=0 and
SS^t=qI-J gives the skew-Hadamard matrix

    [ 1   1^t ]
    [-1   I+S ].

Its product with its transpose is(q+1)I, and H+H^t=2I.
Conversely a normalized matrix of this form recovers the doubly regular
tournament. **Loss/guard:** a feasible spectrum or abelian cokernel does not
produce a{+1,-1} matrix with the required signs. Retain the integral lattice,
row gauge, transport and realization equations. **Cheap test:** filter a
small non-Paley candidate by exact local Smith layers, then solve the actual
sign equations. Do not transfer a claimed circulant-Hadamard result to this
different existence problem.

## 8. Directed triangle packing/covering — keep the correct incidence hypergraph

**Candidate sharp target:** improve the directed packing/covering ratio toward
3/2, with the precise graph class declared. The factor2 directed analogue
is already proved; a cited asymptotic result gives1.8 and examples near1.5.
This is different from the undirected Tuza conjecture.

**Recovered:** May breakthrough map §8, OCF conflict graphs, the H=21
intersection machinery and its later repairs. **Map:** directed triangles
become hyperedges on ARCS for arc packing, or on VERTICES for vertex packing.
These are different systems. The OCF odd-cycle conflict graph uses vertex
intersections and cannot be silently reused for arc-disjointness.
**Cheap test:** compute exact packing and hitting numbers on the intended
hypergraph for small prime atoms, retaining overlapping port identities.
Seek a restricted-family min-max theorem before a universal ratio claim.

## 9. Maximal Hamilton counts and exact tournament Ramsey problems

**OPEN extremal directions:** sharpen maximum Hamilton-path counts, or certify
particular unknown transitive-tournament Ramsey thresholds.
**Recovered:** May map §2, A038375 experiments, Paley versus interval
circulants, and the current A000568/root-extension machinery.
**Map/predicate:** exact class generation -> a forbidden-induced-pattern or
Hamilton-count oracle. Retain automorphism weights and actual orientation
choices. A000568's total class count does not reveal which classes survive a
Ramsey filter. **Cheap test:** add a transitive-subset blocker to a verified
small extension bank and publish a SAT/UNSAT certificate with an independently
checked universe. Sampling Paley/circulants does not certify global extrema.
For majority-order restrictions, the2026 Shapira–Yuster paper supplies a more
specialized Ramsey lane; its newer exponent supersedes the older one.

## 10. Reconstruction and finite model theory — repair an observer, not a false conjecture

Classical tournament reconstruction from vertex-deleted decks is **FALSE**.
[Session39's primary-source reconciliation](../session39_signed_phases_and_prime_cylinders_20261023/RECONSTRUCTION_PRECEDENTS.md)
distinguishes decks, rooted profiles, indexed hypomorphy and individualization.
**OPEN scoped target:** characterize the weakest selector/transport data
that restore a particular operation-closed observer.
**Map/predicate:** tournament -> observer response algebra. Equality is
preserved only for declared tests. Stockmeyer pairs and the81/27 pair test
different losses. **Cheap test:** compare all allowed continuations on a
minimal hostile, not just an extra unrooted motif count. A faithful graph
encoding must also commute with deletion before it says anything about the
ordinary graph Reconstruction Conjecture.

## 11. Graceful trees and square-sum attachments — transfer algorithms with guards

The Graceful Tree Conjecture remains a target in recent primary work; the
repo's [bounded-release theorem](../dynamics_labeling_entry_20261019/GRACEFUL_RELEASE_AND_MATCHING_SUPPORT.md)
reconfigures existing graceful fibers and explicitly allows tree shape to
change. [Square-sum ports](../square_sum_ports_20261023/README.md) retain actual
endpoints and future arrivals. **Map:** signed incidence and labelled edge
choices -> matching/contact constraints. Preserve label range, distinctness,
edge colors, connectivity, and the target tree shape when required.
**Cheap test:** restrict a two-color exchange to a shape-preserving fiber;
look for a Hall defect or a bounded buffer theorem. For square-sum Hamilton
questions formulate the exact endpoint/size variant first; this pass does
not authenticate the publication status of every folklore all-n formulation.
Neither graph is intrinsically a tournament merely because signs occur.

## 12. Hadwiger–Nelson — exact boundary relations can prove useful exclusions

**Recovered:** THM-4661 / `01-canon/theorems/THM-4661-odd-degree-carriers-a-failed-six-colour-exterior-has-at-least-twenty-vertices-and-equality-testers-are-seven-colour-certificates.md`;
[its actual exterior packet](../six_colour_odd_carrier_twenty_vertices_20261016.md).
Existing finite models already demonstrate Alon–Tarsi/list-coloring
certificates. **Map:** a geometric gadget -> its relation on endpoint colors.
Preserve joint color assignments AND a literal unit-distance realization.
**Open proof unit:** rule out another family of candidate exteriors, or
construct an actual realizable boundary relation. An equality tester here
would be an exceptionally strong object, not a routine six-color gadget.
The tournament contribution is exact response composition as a method;
arbitrarily orienting a unit-distance graph supplies no complete tournament.

## 13. Lonely Runner — preserve endpoint geometry, reject cosmetic tournaments

**Recovered:** May residue bridge, June proof-engine catalog/HYP-3099, the
current LRC guardrails. **OPEN repo anchor:** LRC(14), with external claims
still subject to the repository's source/audit policy.
Natural objects are interval contacts, simultaneous witnesses and labelled
cover debts. A score or H value does not preserve common intersection.
Strict scalar improvement moves form an acyclic partial graph, not a
tournament with cycles; the old deduction is repaired in this pass.
**Cheap test:** on a proposed tournament encoding, identify the intrinsic
pairwise relation, ties, and an actual implication from its certificate to
a common lonely time. If this first arrow is absent, use the interval or
hypergraph representation directly.

## 14. Planar Jacobian, Collatz and arithmetic clocks — analogy needs an operation

**Recovered:** July's fibre/dihedral reflection, August's source-normal
recurrences, October's CRT/valuation and return packets.
A monodromy action, a recurrence clock and a tournament automorphism are
different objects until an equivariant map is exhibited. An arbitrarily
chosen cyclic orientation on three roots is not a canonical arithmetic
orientation. Neither group order3 nor a ternary tree proves injectivity or
integer termination. **Useful proof unit:** a source-preserving local
transport obstruction, or an integral realization exclusion with the
valuation/normalization clock retained. No reduction of the planar Jacobian
or Collatz conjecture to the present tournament carrier was recovered.

## 15. Turing machines and density inequalities — a genuine limit on universality

**CITED:** Chen–Lin–Ma–Wei prove undecidability of polynomial inequalities
in tournament homomorphism densities; see SOURCES.md.
**Recovered:** TANGENTS and
[the operation/complexity atlas](../analogy_reduction_atlas_20261025/PROBLEM_CLASSIFICATION.md).
This is an actual computability bridge, unlike merely drawing an orbit as a
tournament. **Consequence:** an algorithm deciding every such universal
density inequality cannot exist. It does not prohibit a useful decidable
fragment or an exact finite-order recurrence.
**Open proof unit:** specify a fixed template/operation class and prove a
complete finite certificate calculus for that class. The language restriction
must exclude the undecidable universe; calling a carrier finite without
proving closure is not sufficient.

## The synthesis

Two different compression contracts are now visible. Exact composition
needs joint incidence, common transport and realization guards. Averaged
fixed-pattern questions can instead be controlled by a normalized operator
norm, as P1 shows. P2 supplies a fully classified low-complexity endpoint,
while its failure under converse/substitution marks the closure boundary.
The next unifying theorem should say which operations preserve each contract,
not assert that one scalar or one tree solves every target.
