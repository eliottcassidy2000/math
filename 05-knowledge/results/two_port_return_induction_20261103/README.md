## Why merely mixing the arithmetic return words does not close the proof

The old mod49 and mod100 gates are not the strongest obstruction for the
original H31/95/97 seeds. In saturated bulk coordinates beta=(Y,X), the
functional25X-34Y vanishes on those seeds and is strictly positive after
every possible last macro in{b,q2,s78,D}, on every nonzero physical bulk.
Thus **no nonempty mixed word in that alphabet returns exactly to these
interfaces**, at any length.

More generally set ell=min(v3(Y),v3(X)) and K=Y/3^ell. Seven calibrated
macros{b,q2,s78,D,L6,L45,L13} increase K in their forward matrix direction:
their real coordinate growth exceeds the largest3-content gain allowed by
their determinants. An exact ternary interface edge

    v_j P_word=3v_i+m(2,-1,0)

therefore lowers K, or for an empty word increases ell at fixed K. No finite
bank of fixed actual seed states can have a cycle of these exact returns.
This includes the singular channels that escaped the old3-unit hypothesis.
It does not cover changing correction channels, nonzero transverse carries,
native replacements, or an unbounded family of integer states.

A deterministic physical-bulk inverse uses four disjoint projective image
intervals. It rejects the original seeds and all compact repairs; only four
of the30 supplied deeper endpoints pass its first bulk test. Twelve selected
full-state candidates fail the exact inverse in the declared65+s78 bank.
These failures do not exclude sources in other banks. Positive actual
transport controls ensure the tests are nonvacuous.
[MIXED_RETURNS](MIXED_RETURNS.md) gives the all-word proofs and finite scopes.

## What remains for a complete induction

The finite grammar has unbounded integer states and a literal compiler;
the fixed-seed return-bank obstruction concerns a different state model.
A proof still needs a family of actual admissible states and a selector
that supplies every required prime/7p target, including admissible composite
inverse parents. Native resets must return to that declared family, retain
the ordered ports and choose a primitive next rooted interface. The all-k
reset returns to the full two-port language; membership in the particular
21-entry/28-map grammar is not claimed at every k.

The next decisive tests are therefore target-directed: characterize the
primitive four-coordinate images of the28 maps; test whether a missed
predecessor can be repaired by a source-preserving port orbit or the
internal-port reversal; and prove closure of the resulting parent domain.
The new formulas solve several transition and reentry obligations, but no
all-target source-existence lemma has yet been established.
