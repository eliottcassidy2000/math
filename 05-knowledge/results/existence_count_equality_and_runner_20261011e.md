# Existence/counting alternation: equality fragments and native runner clocks

**Status:** PROVED scoped elementary implications and implementations;
CITED geometric edge-bound inputs; FINITE-EXACT declared controls.
**OPEN:** six versus seven colours for the plane, a six-colour unit-separated
equality tester, LRC(14), and universal Collatz coverage. This session supplies
neither a seven-chromatic unit graph nor a missing universal positivity bound.

The useful alternation is precise: retain one actual positive witness,
count every remaining obstruction in a fixed source universe, and use its
integer spacing to turn a sufficiently strong bound into an exact decision.
Changing the universe or discarding its boundary invalidates that last step.

## 2. A source-specific counting threshold that can finish

For nonadjacent ports u,v let e count proper k-colour extensions of (0,0),
and d those of (0,1). A nonvacuous equality tester has e>0,d=0. If its ports
are actually unit-separated, adding their edge produces a (k+1)-chromatic
unit graph. The requested k=6 fragment is therefore equivalent to a finite
seven-chromatic plane witness, not an easier existence claim to assume.

The [quantized-guard packet](unit_equality_quantized_guards_20261011e.md)
proves that an independently authenticated chi(F)>=s implies

\[
 (k-1)_{s-1}\mid e,\qquad (k-2)_{s-2}\mid d.
\]

Consequently chi(F)>=5, an actual equal-port six-colouring, and an
authenticated absolute bound d<24 would finish the fragment. This is a
larger and correctly scoped zero threshold, not a construction of its
missing premises.

For edge additions on one fixed point set, let D_i be the actual surviving
separating assignments. A retained equal-port witness certifies existence
throughout, while the exact conditional factors count the obstruction:

\[
 d_r=d_0\prod_i\frac{|D_i|}{|D_{i-1}|}.
\]

If a source-specific bound gives d_0<=M and each actual factor is at most
sigma<1, then M sigma^r below the orbit quantum proves d_r=0. Appending
vertices changes the ambient count. An unconditioned marginal does not
bound its later conditional factor.

The exact Moser positive control deletes all 243 separating three-colour
assignments by ten edge additions. The same rule at six colours reduces
7776 to1056 and stops with genuine residual support. Its conditional factors
include 5/6 but are not all 5/6. This is a reproducible success/failure pair,
not an extrapolation from three colours to six.

## 3. Two search obstructions now have explicit exits

The [amplifier packet](existence_count_amplifiers_20261011e.md) proves that
finite two-terminal serial/parallel compositions preserve d>0 at k>=3.
Repeated independent common neighbours can make d/e arbitrarily small while
d remains positive. Moreover unit-separated ports have at most two common
unit neighbours. Repetition of this construction supplies neither a zero
certificate nor an unrestricted plane realization.

Connecting the interior constraints supplies an abstract escape: joining
both ports to an interior graph J gives

\[
                 (e,d)=(P_J(k-1),P_J(k-2)).
\]

Thus chi(J)=k-1 forces equality. The diamond exhibits the effect of one
dependent interior edge, and changing only its marked ports reverses the
interface. At k=6 the clique selector is abstractly correct and geometrically
impossible. The exit requires shared constraints with a simultaneous unit
realization; the interface algebra alone cannot provide that realization.

The [candidate packet](unit_equality_candidates_20261011e.md) implements
the inherited four-colouring of the full rational Moser field. Two residue
frames over F4 prove the unit-denominator property; a retained component
origin permits rational translations. Ten point sets, up to 81 vertices,
give 690 actual unit-edge omission cases, each explicitly separable in four
and six colours. The theorem excludes arbitrary repetitions remaining in
that field, not just the finite catalogue. A useful next candidate must
escape this field screen.

The inherited cherry bound plus an extreme-point equality obstruction
gives an elementary 17-vertex floor. Combining the cited
[Kostochka--Yancey critical-edge bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.1050)
with [Alexeev--Mixon--Parshall's Theorem1](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.11914v2)
raises the necessary floor for the requested tester to 27 vertices. At 26
the critical core requires 85 edges while a unit graph has at most 84.
These bounds stop excluding at 27; this is not an existence result or a
claim to the best published floor.

The [runner packet](lonely_runner_conditional_survival_20261011e.md) retains
the complete conditioned set on one time circle. It supplies an exact
positioned endpoint formula, a sufficient adaptive positive-mass criterion,
and a CRT rule that keeps the shared residue profile:

\[
 Z=\sum_{c\bmod\gcd(Q,q)}a_cb_c.
\]

Coprime periods yield an actual product on the common finite clock. With
shared factors, identical marginal counts can either survive or vanish,
depending on their occupied residue classes. A smaller common period is
not a replacement for the original time denominator.

For the tight 14-runner source with relative speeds 1,...,13, safe times are
exactly 1/14,3/14,5/14,9/14,11/14,13/14. Their interval measure is zero despite
existence. Thus (6/7)^13 cannot serve as a universal mass lower bound.

For a fixed finite integer source V and rational radius delta=p/d, the
clock N=d lcm(V) contains every boundary endpoint. Existence is equivalent
to positive exact survivor count on this complete clock. It can be expensive
and supplies no uniform LRC(14) proof. Equivalently, with M=max V, weak
existence at delta holds iff

\[
 |S(\delta-\varepsilon)|\ge2\varepsilon/M
       \quad\hbox{for every }0<\varepsilon<\delta.
\]

This replaces a false positive-mass demand at a critical boundary with a
correct source-dependent family of relaxed bounds. Compactness proves the
reverse implication; it does not supply those bounds for arbitrary speeds.

concrete versions of existence/counting alternation with different remaining
universal obligations. Shrinking error or probability alone discharges none.
