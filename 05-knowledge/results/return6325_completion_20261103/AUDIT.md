## Interpretation checks

Rational span permits signed linear combinations of measured counts. It
does not make a signed combination a physical source. Positive parity laws
witness the hidden directions without requiring negative source weights.
The observer in HB5 measures separate expected H responses, not arbitrary
products or the full distribution of a response. Those richer observations
can change both depth and completion numbers.

The even-subset/exterior indexing in HB6 is a graded vector-space map; its
products are different. The F49/charge-to-F343 codec is likewise only
F7-linear. Its two Frobenius operations have different orders. Integer
mod49 precision has characteristic49 and is not replaced by either field.
The older interval clock is frozen with its original source history.

The strongest finite recovery examples do not prove closure under arbitrary
actual complements: a complement may destroy the pair-module condition.
All native returns here instead preserve their complete target relations
and explicit paths. No reset to an arbitrary isomorphism class is free.

## Reproduce

From this packet directory:

    python -B -X utf8 reproduce.py --full

This compiles three small C++20 programs into the OS temporary directory,
regenerates the bounded target searches and literal return schedules,
then runs the independent audits and response controls in ordinary and
optimized Python. `reproduction.json` records output, script and direct
input SHA256 hashes. The checks use explicit exceptions, not removable
assert statements. Inherited Python dependencies include SymPy; g++ is
used for the exact finite scans. No binaries or temporary logs are committed.

Proofs were checked for empty-bin conventions, fixed versus moving labels,
individual states versus arbitrary weights, complete versus sampled
universes, integer versus rational inversion and baseline changes. During
drafting the TT4 statement was narrowed to its correct retained-fibre scope
before publication. No previously published claim is retracted here.
