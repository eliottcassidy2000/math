## The square-sum frontier we inherit

Ordinary interval Hamiltonian paths and cycles are already settled in the
repo's reconstructed Gerbicz construction: paths at1,15,16,17,23 and every
n>=25; cycles at every n>=32. The stronger unchanged seed, simultaneous
port, exponential multiplicity and exact closed-subgraph constructions
listed in the predecessor remain the starting point. Our20-object model
does not replace them with a weaker existence experiment.

Three older mechanisms directly informed this session:

1. [July edge-sum discriminants, THM-2761](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2761-graph-edge-sum-discriminant-codegree-factorization-and-graceful-sign-gauge.md)
   separate adjacent-edge collisions from disjoint-edge additive energy.
   This is the useful analogue of W versus matching terms: shared incidence
   changes the algebra. It is not an equality of the two polynomials.
2. [October19 graceful/square exchanges](../graceful_square_exchange_20261019.md)
   distinguish fixed-endpoint two-edge switches, impossible for graceful
   labels but present from46 for square-sum labels. Thus a switch diagram
   must carry its arithmetic predicate and actual endpoints. In the new
   model, six-bit rotation likewise preserves weight but destroys an enabled
   compatibility operation.
3. [Session32 named-port transfer](../session32_square_monster_20261023/ports/PORT_TRANSFER.md)
   and the [overnight cyclic-gluing correction](../square_sum_spectrum_overnight_20261031l/README.md)
   retain all spanning linear-forest states. Local Hamiltonian-cycle products
   can miss global cycles: the prism/C4 witness has3 rather than2. The same
   lesson prevents calling our twenty-object compatibility model a complete
   native continuation automaton.

The weighted-order11 proof uses these as design analogies, not mathematical
dependencies. Its actual proof dependencies are the signed cycle identities,
triangle variance bound and exact endpoint certificate stated in PROOF.md.

## Seven, fifteen, twenty-four and twenty-six

The [marked124 packet](../aliquot_power_124_20261026/tournaments/TOURNAMENTS_124.md)
already gives the strongest relevant warning. The literal divisor support
of8 is1+X+X^2; it is not a squarefree face complex, although its value at2
is7. Separately I(K3,2)=7 and I(P4,2)=21 are legal unrestricted graph
counts but cannot be WHOLE tournament odd-cycle conflict graphs. Ordinary
raw H=7,21 is excluded at all orders; H/Aut has a separate scope. We do not
re-prove these exclusions or infer spectrum completeness from stability.

The marked Collatz cycle1->4->2->1 is multiplication by4 modulo7 only on
that cycle. The inputs2 and9 show why this fails as a global residue
quotient. In the chosen F8 chart the exponent orbit1,2,4 has linear rank2;
another primitive generator gives rank3. Orbit length does not retain rank.
This directly motivates the six-slot rotation hostile in TWENTY_SHADOW.md.

The recovered finite-field decompositions15=1+2+3*4 and26=2+8*3 count
Frobenius orbits in F16 and F27;24 is the degree-three part of F27. They
are not the same operation as the square25 compatible pair's26 prescribed
arcs, or the radius26 triangular-lattice capacity failure. Both latter
clocks were recomputed exactly, with source labels retained.

[Session42's ancestry theorem](../session42_shared_lattices_square_colors_20261023/SYNTHESIS.md)
has founders1,2,4, but every n>=71 has all three ancestries. These are not
permanent disconnected components or a proper coloring. It also distinguishes
1024 parent-selector forests through25 from192 induced colorings. This is
an older precise example of why a summary must retain multiplicity.

The [polygon-ladder packet, THM-4663](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4663-polygon-ladder-of-the-finite-unit-distance-graphs-closed-walks-are-kloosterman-moments-pentagons-level-15-hexagons-level-6.md)
offers a less superficial15/24 connection: finite pentagon counts and

a closed-walk trace and a prescribed-endpoint propagator are different
consumers. Our compatibility carrier likewise must keep endpoints for any
future arithmetic gluing.

## Research moves that remain worth pursuing

- **Orientations after support geometry.** K4-freeness gives a real gain,
  but two unit-distance diamonds attain its relaxed bracket. A further gain
  must retain more than support realizability: test the actual oriented
  triangle surplus before spending it, with the full W budget subtracted.
- **A geometrically faithful tree with shared arithmetic tails.** The
  ternary-gap construction controls tree distance; native Collatz paths can
  still share even tails before their odd LCA. Glue those identified ports
  and retain ordinary-step lengths before asking for an embedding theorem.
- **A complete square25 consumer.** The20-object model preserves two
  operations on individual paths. The actual test in NATIVE_26.md now shows
  that the old orientation quotient needs38 active states when it must
  support old complement words followed by a26-extension count. Include
  dead sources and new complement commands next. A symmetry of the model
  cannot act on the rigid original graph by itself.

These are separate precise tests. No equal-number identification is used
as a proof step in the anchor theorem.
