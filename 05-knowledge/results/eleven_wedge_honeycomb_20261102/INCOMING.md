## The common reusable move

Begin with a total source partition, then refine only where a specified
future consumer can distinguish sources. In the order11 proof, every
endpoint graph receives either a proved positive lower bound or an exact
exception test; none is silently discarded. In the birth interface, the
otherwise hidden old-dead mass is retained and then split by actual new
paths. In the metric problem, the retained sibling gap supplies a lower
distance estimate that the triangle inequality never supplied.

These are typed constructions with different invariants. They suggest
future source-weighted arithmetic certificates, but give no Collatz entry
or HN forcing theorem. The exact next test is the26->27 birth interface
and composition of two such interfaces, not another fixed-order census.
