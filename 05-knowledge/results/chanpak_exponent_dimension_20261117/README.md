# Exponents, dimensions and tournament frames (2026-11-17)

**Constructive.** [Two-contact apex move](apex/PROOF.md) places every
signed even difference between equal-order strong tournaments, on
arbitrarily large baselines. At equal dimensions the counts are
4^(k+1)−1 and 4^(k+1)+1, including an actual first-birth host at 63.
[Dyadic decoder](apex/DYADIC.md): at a common order O(k²), place the
lower endpoint in any odd phase mod 2^k while preserving an actual
+2 mate. Yet the +2 chart has only Θ(√X) endpoints through X.
The gap from local control to absolute placement is explicit, not
just a failure to test enough sizes. The two edits here are ordinary
arc reversals in a shared frame, not a claimed native metagraph edge.

**All-order metagraph constraints.** [Two-reflection dimensions](metagraph/PROOF.md)
and [cycle-index corrections](metagraph/CYCLE_INDEX.md) calculate the
orientation bits that survive two reversals, prove typical rigidity
in blue frames and in uniformly sampled self-converse classes, and
isolate the first three signed-orbit corrections to the classical
self-converse count. [Degree boundary](metagraph/BOUNDARY.md) gives
a triangle-parity color on all odd-order blue edges. [Closed-frame
mean response](frames/PROOF.md) gives the exact all-line mean gain
of the closed end, with exceptions to pointwise dominance.

**Archive synthesis.** [Connections](CONNECTIONS.md) records the
bridges and the boundaries from Collatz cylinder measures, Berggren
cones, the ternary Smith law, Boolean Fourier repair, planar
Jacobian boundary ranks, and lonely runner tail domains.
