# A Hamilton-bounded profile-cone obstruction

**Proved:** membership in the positive same-parity Stirling cone, its leading coefficient one and even lower coefficients, positivity of every nonzero profile coordinate, factorial divisibility, and the Hamilton bound H<=n! do not suffice to prove higher ordered-cover domination. **Not claimed:** an actual tournament counterexample, an ambient collision, or an exact full named-response collision.

Let t^(r)_k=k!S(r,k), with coordinate zero at k=0. These are the ordered-cover profiles of transitive tournaments. The classical odd-cycle-cover expansion, cited and audited in `../profile_cone_obstruction/PROOF.md`, puts every actual order-n tournament profile in the nonnegative cone generated by t^(n),t^(n-2),..., with coefficient one on t^(n) and even coefficients on the lower generators.

For the equal-profile cyclic substitution and physical root query a=V1,c=V2, the companion `../covered_ambient/PROOF.md` gives the complete h-th ordered-cover contrast

    D_h(q) = (h/3) L_q[K E0^(h-1)/(1-XYZ)^(h+1)],

where

    E0=X+Y+Z+XY+YZ+ZX+3XYZ,
    K=X^2Y^2+X^2Z^2+Y^2Z^2
      +X^2Y+X^2Z+XY^2+XZ^2+Y^2Z+YZ^2
      -XYZ(X+Y+Z)-6XYZ.

Here L_q(X^iY^jZ^k)=q_i q_j q_k. This is an exact cover-coordinate identity, not a truncation of the named response polynomial.

Take the artificial order-eleven profile q(w)=t^(11)+w t^(5). Exact affine-polynomial expansion of the displayed functional gives

    D_9(q(w)) = -2756654916959793711894994556905685760
                +72943657112400893970868442880 w
                +533607768637358688000 w^2
                -10019483308800 w^3.

At the even weight w=35,000,000 the profile is

    (0,35000001,1050002046,5250171006,8403498000,4229607600,
       129230640,322494480,479001600,419126400,199584000,39916800).

Every coordinate after q0 is positive, every q_k is divisible by k!, the leading Stirling coefficient is one, the lower coefficient is even, and

    q1=35,000,001 <= 11!=39,916,800.

The two top coordinates have the tournament values q11=11! and q10=5·11!. Nevertheless,

    D_9(q)=20457251690201969885400943894314240 > 0.

Thus the strengthened proposed sufficient conditions still fail. The verifier also computes the exact first positive even weight on this ray. Its monotonicity certificate is explicit: if B=11!-1 and c_j are the polynomial coefficients, then c1-3|c3|B^2>0 and c2>0, so the derivative is positive throughout [0,B]. No minimal-order claim is made.

## Why this is still not a tournament

A word and a choice of k-1 cut positions determine an ordered k-path cover if all uncut adjacencies point forward. Consequently every order-n tournament satisfies

    q_k <= n! binom(n-1,k-1).

The artificial profile violates this bound at k=2,3,4. For example q2=1,050,002,046 exceeds 11!·10=399,168,000. It therefore cannot be realized by a tournament. Inverting the word-and-cut transform also gives negative counts for some numbers of backward adjacencies, another explicit impossibility.

These failures do not weaken the stated obstruction: the question here is whether H<=n! together with positivity, cone membership and divisibility alone repairs the cone argument. It does not. Adding all cover bounds or genuine realizability constraints is a separate, unresolved route. The unconditional scalar h=1 positive-square theorem remains intact.

## Reproduction

Run `python verify.py > receipt.json` and `python -O verify.py > receipt.optimized.json`. The verifier expands the three affine profile factors formally, rather than fitting a polynomial to sampled values. It checks the exact positive value, every asserted necessary condition, the first-even-weight threshold, and the explicit stronger-bound violations. All checks use exceptions that remain active under -O. Dependencies are the sibling `covered_response` and `response` primitives.
