# Critical-quotient synthesis

A separately sourced classification corollary: every critical prime quotient of order at least five, inflated by nontrivial tournaments with at least one pair child, is separated from quotient converse while interiors remain fixed. It depends on the independently proved cyclic, staircase and alternating-hub response theorems. The primary classification is Schmerl–Trotter Corollary 5.8(3), directly inspected in the author-hosted PDF. Exact label maps reconcile all three definitions.

Normal and optimized verifiers agree on all three family maps at orders 5 through 31. These checks validate conventions, not the external classification theorem. Arbitrary prime quotients, absence of any pair child, and arbitrary outer-ambient lifting remain outside scope.
