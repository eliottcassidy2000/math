# Simultaneous module reversals and uniform-port isolation

**Proved scoped all-order separation and incidence results, with independent audits.** These are exact named-tournament results. Arbitrary full-response injectivity and the global Hamilton spectrum remain open.

The original return-construction branch was redirected to simultaneous module reversals. The earlier contextual no-go is retained separately in `../returns/`.

## Results

- `PROOF.md` D1 separates every nonempty selection of disjoint nontrivial module reversals modulo four by an actual two-port query. It isolates one module while every other chosen reversal is exactly invisible to that query.
- D2 gives ordered path-cover domination when a transitive quotient is replaced by any tournament quotient, allowing internal block converses. D3 uses it to show that exactly two properly overlapping original modules strictly increase the scalar Hamilton count.
- D4 gives a direct cyclic-quotient separator for a nested pair whose complement has one common direction to the inner module. This special case is superseded in scope by `UNIFORM.md`, not asserted as the general nested proof.
- D5 proves every nontrivial tournament module is generated by a pair. The selected pair/module incidence minor is the inclusion-zeta matrix. Hence all distinct nontrivial module clique-edge vectors are independent over every field, and no nonempty simultaneous XOR family is graphically the identity. This alone does not prove response noncancellation.
- `UNIFORM.md` U1 proves a support-preserving initial-run switching involution. U2 shows that the incoming whole-converse parity identity survives uniform compression of disjoint modules if one singleton anchor remains exposed. U3 supplies an explicit compressed query battery. U4 separates every proper nested pair and every exposed-anchor laminar family modulo four, with ports individually uniform on the proper selected modules.

The companion `../module_decomposition/` proves hereditary Hamilton and ordered-cover monotonicity for arbitrary original-module XOR. It reduces every surviving full-response collision candidate to the legal current-module inversion class. This is stronger than D3's two-overlap scalar statement, but does not replace the named-port separation arguments. The companion `../simultaneous_response/ANCHORED.md` supplies an independent averaging proof of the general nested and exposed-anchor family scope.

Fully covered laminar families and general legal inversion sequences are not settled by this packet. `PROOF.md` includes a coarse-query hostile: for C3[TT2,TT2,TT2], all block-uniform converse contrasts vanish modulo four although actual integer contrasts and nonuniform named queries remain nonzero. No full-function collision is exhibited. Arbitrary internal-reversal cubic and quartic terms are retained through actual graph queries; only a whole-core converse is known to have degree at most three.

## Verification and review

Run `python verify.py` and `python -O verify.py`, then `python uniform.py` and `python -O uniform.py`. Checks use explicit exceptions. The first script checks all 1,099 labelled tournaments through order five, including 10,650 pair hulls, 3,830 modules, 824 disjoint pairs, 2,166 overlapping pairs, 3,580 uniformly split nested pairs and 308 quotient controls. The second checks 6,028 module/anchor alias sums, 11,908 compressed batteries and 61,252 actual uniform queries, including 716 compressed-order-two cases.

Independent proof reviews accepted D1–D5 and U1–U4 without mathematical corrections. The U1–U4 execution review is in `../nested_reversals_audit/UNIFORM_AUDIT.md`: 6,652 literal disjoint path pairs, 570 batteries, 80 compressed-order-two cases and 2,506 actual attachment queries, with normal/optimized agreement.

The files in this directory are the two proofs, two verifiers, shared literal Hamilton counter `probe.py`, their receipts, and matching normal/optimized stdout. The counter's standalone demonstration is not used as a theorem certificate. Exclude `__pycache__` from publication.
