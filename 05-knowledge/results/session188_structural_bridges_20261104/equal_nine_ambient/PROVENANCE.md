# Targeted provenance check

Checked on4 November2026 before making the order-nine extension claim.

The adjacent-position swap bound H<=n!/2^floor(n/2) is proved elementarily in this packet and is treated as a standard counting observation, not a new extremal result. A targeted search did not establish a precise first source for this exact weak finite bound. That absence is not evidence of novelty, and no attribution or priority claim is invented.

Primary literature confirms a much longer and stronger extremal theory. Noga Alon's [The Maximum Number of Hamiltonian Paths in Tournaments](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/hamilton.pdf), Introduction and Theorem1.1, records Szele's earlier exponential upper bound and proves an upper bound of order n^(3/2)n!/2^(n-1). The source's full introduction and theorem were inspected. This is historical context only: no unspecified asymptotic constant is used for our finite orders.

The recent primary preprint by Alexander Divoux and Shouda Wang, [The Maximum Number of Hamiltonian Cycles in Tournaments](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23755v1), Theorem1.4 and Section10, gives the asymptotic Hamilton-path maximum with constant cosh(1)/cos(1). It too is stronger asymptotic context, not an effective finite bound used by this certificate.

The profile cone is classical Grinberg--Stanley odd-cycle expansion, already cited precisely in the earlier cone packets. Nonpositive monomial coefficients on a nonnegative simplex are an elementary sufficient certificate, not a novel general positivity technique. This packet claims only its exact verified application at the stated child orders and the resulting named-response consequence.
