collision of rooted profiles would demonstrate why root transport needs a
richer state; it would not erase the usefulness of the fixed-root formula.

## 11. Three reasoning rounds and the reformulated next proof interfaces

### Round A: can parity plus multiplication already force all allowed odds?

**Hypothesis.** The only observed holes, parity, SCC multiplication and large
finite coverage force completeness.

**Cheap hostile.** The actual prefix-generated monoid in THM-4094 misses the
next prime. One-apex constructions miss13 forever, despite infinite size and
exact formulas. Positive integral observation lattices admit forbidden7.

**Reformulation.** Arithmetic reduces the task to two indecomposable lanes;
it does not realize them. A proof must give actual sources for those lanes.
The digital bank is therefore more informative than another support count:
it exposes exactly where a source-entry theorem could be stated.

### Round B: can a smooth or connected transition space force intermediate values?

**Hypothesis.** Exact insertion, a connected metagraph, gcd2 or favorable
moments make an odd intermediate-value argument possible.

**Cheap hostile.** Strong order5 ear images have gcd2 but holes; one-apex
adjacent exchanges have no unit odd steps at n>=6; continuous inverse points
can lie outside the Boolean domain; local Boolean energy has strict traps.

**Reformulation.** Require actual legal0/±2 moves plus connectedness, or prove
multi-parent image overlap, or find an energy-below4 point by a mechanism
that escapes the known traps. Each is a sufficient theorem with a visible
failure boundary. Moments and rank are useful only if they produce that
consequence object, not merely a large child or broad linear span.

### Round C: can adaptivity supply the missing source-entry mechanism?

**Hypothesis.** Root switching renews the branching resource that fixed-root
rays and scalar controllers lose.

**Cheap probes already positive.** The C3 adaptive language covers all allowed
odds<=199; actual singleton/D states admit a terminating inverse; source-order
moves change responses while retaining unmarked profiles. These show genuine
extra freedom. **Hostiles still present:** object reachability is restricted;
one-step profile data can collide; complete one-step response is not closed
under two births; many adaptive objects need not produce many new counts.

**Reformulated proof targets, in order of concrete inherited support:**

1. **Seven-parent digital cover.** Prove that the existing actual bank and its
   two digital address variables attain every odd>=249, or produce a certified
   counterexample beyond the finite100-million range. Preserve x-width,
   binary reversal/carry data and actual source guards. Cheapest useful work:
   derive residue/carry classes that are stable under digit descent, then
   test the proposed invariant on the finite exceptional sources. More large
   undirected parameter scans are not the missing argument.
2. **Adaptive target ancestry.** Given h in either prime lane, produce a
   legal predecessor graph/root with smaller H and prove that the process
   reaches C3. Its state must be closed under root changes. An exact
   recognition theorem for graphs already in the language is useful but is
   not this source-entry theorem. Cheapest test: locate the first pairs of
   equal proposed states with different next-root images and refine only the
   missing continuation coordinate.
3. **Stateful ear tiling.** Prove a recurrent bank of actual parent states
   has components of0/±2 moves whose image intervals overlap across orders.
   The scalar maxima are already unbounded; target the labelled overlap,
   not a better mean bound. Cheapest test: derive a structural overlap lemma
   on a narrow infinite family that includes the known gcd2 hostiles.
4. **Boolean concentration with repair.** Construct an actual source-supported
   probability law with `E[(H-h)^2]<4`, or a global surgery theorem escaping
   all non-target traps while retaining the source. Uniform-port laws and
   greedy one-edge descent have explicit no-gos, so change the law or permit
   bounded barrier crossings with a proved global invariant.

The map common to these routes is
`actual named constructor -> faithful continuation state -> integer H`.
The first arrow may be compressed, but not replaced by its rational span,
positive cone, unmarked cover profile or arbitrary integral solution. The
second arrow's image is the open problem. The strongest recovered work has
made both arrows exact separately; the remaining theorem must connect
**every target integer to an actual source**.
