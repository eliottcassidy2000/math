**PROVED scoped structural and transfer theorems / FINITE-EXACT small
controls / OPEN all-target realization.** The full Hamiltonian spectrum
conjecture and the unresolved parts of fixed-order metagraph geometry are
not claimed solved. No literature-priority claim is made.

The research target is still every positive odd Hamiltonian-path count
except7 and21. [THM-4094, two-prime-lane reduction](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4094-hamiltonian-matching-deficit-and-two-prime-lane-completeness.md)

with p an odd prime other than3. Actual source graphs, named roots, shared
cuts and mixed attachment information remain part of each construction.

## New structural results from the present exploration

**Canonical adaptive ancestry.** A strong tournament of order greater than
three has at most one indegree-one vertex whose deletion is strong. This
proves an intrinsic inverse on the adaptive image. Every strong tournament
has a unique oriented irreducible core and a chronological root word modulo
the core's automorphisms. For a core K, descendant classes at depth m number

    (1/|Aut(K)|) sum_g product_(i=0)^(m-1) (fix_K(g)+i).

All positive-depth C3 descendants are rigid, with exactly(n-1)!/6 ordinary
classes at order n>=4. This removes repeated histories and supplies an
object inverse. It does not assign an object to a target integer.
[Proof and reconstruction](ANCESTRY.md).

**An exact arithmetic inverse detector, with its missing coordinate.** For
an actual rooted source put z=(h,s,q,F), with h=H(T), s the root-start count,
q=H(T-root), and F the rooted two-slot-cover count. Keeping the root or
marking the newborn gives exact linear maps. The defect

    delta=F-h-q+s >= indegree(root)*(indegree(root)-1)

vanishes precisely at indegree at most one. On a strong source, the legal
last vertex is exactly delta=0 and q>s. Recovering its parent's complete
rooted state still needs the named predecessor and a two-deletion count.
[Exact formulas and proofs](ADAPTIVE.md).

**A sharp old-root switching obstruction.** Two actual adaptive order-six
sources have the same(n;h,s,q,F)=(6;37,5,13,71). Their marked first births
both give103, yet their next root choices offer different prime and7p
values: one reaches233 and287=7*41, the other307. Exact mixed two- and
three-path endpoint covers repair that next step. Four numbers at a single
root do not close arbitrary root switching.

**A finite weighted automaton for every fixed root-degree cap.** Restrict
choices to roots of indegree at most d. Once an old vertex exceeds the cap
it never returns, and every future vertex beats it. At most2d active named
ports survive after a birth. Retaining paired path endpoints gives a finite
nonnegative integer transfer, with no need to retain inactive names in the
future boundary. The degree-control graphs have1,6,24 states at d=1,2,3.
The exact weighted coordinates are more numerous; control states are not
the complete numerical state. [Construction and proof](ACTIVE_PORTS.md).

**The small experiment changes the proposed induction.** Degree cap2 covers
every allowed odd through99, then permanently misses prime127. The actual
history(0,0,3,3,0) reaches127 using root indegrees(1,1,2,2,3), so the minimum
cap for127 is exactly3. Cap3 realizes all allowed odds through199. This
motivates a precise finite-transfer image problem; it is not an all-target
coverage claim. [Experiment and independent audit](audit_root_degree.md).

**Metagraph structure and its limits.** A single temporary vertex and two
guarded native path-complement moves implement any specified old arc
reversal, with all other old arcs and names retained. Repeating gives exact
named reachability at peak order n+1. This uses births and deletions and
does not assert connectivity in the fixed-order metagraph.
[Compiler, existing complete carriers, and source weights](METAGRAPH.md).

**Duality creates a genuine critical-pair obstruction.** Allowing both
indegree-one and outdegree-one legal deletions is nonconfluent at every
order n>=6. A minimal source reduces to terminal cores C3 and C3[C3,1,1].
Moreover W(K)=K->y->b->K embeds every strong tournament K into a two-sided
irreducible core with two extra vertices, preserves Aut(K), and has
H(W)=3H(K)+c_2(K). Thus the primitive-core inventory already contains the
full strong-class problem. [Family, proof and smallest witness](TWO_SIDED_PEELING.md).

## Procedural exploration, rather than another large support scan

The assigned anchor was target-directed adaptive induction; the niche was
all-order metagraph structure; the wildcard was changing which vertices
may remain available as future roots. The live board was actual source
image / inverse ancestry / joint root response / active ports / duality /
native incidence. Each round changed more than one board item:

| Round | Possibility generated | Cheapest decisive test | Result and reformulation |
|---|---|---|---|
|1: recover| An exact recurrence or finite inverse might be the missing ingredient | Read prior digital inverse and100m certificate, including source holes | Those tools exist; universal actual-source entry is the missing theorem |
|2: compress| Keep the old root or select the newborn; close four coordinates | First three actual births, then exact reset | Misses11,13; reset can force a product incompatible with prime/7p targets |
|3: invert| Recognize the last birth intrinsically | Competing indegree-one vertices | Unique oriented inverse, rigidity and Burnside core-word classification |
|4: switch| Equal rooted profiles might permit the same older-root choices | Compare actual equal-profile order-six sources | Different prime/7p outputs; restore mixed endpoints and named deletions |
|5: bound memory| Indegree cap2 might suffice, giving four active ports | Exact count cutoffs99 then199 | First prime obstruction127; cap3 restores this prefix and has a finite weighted transfer |
|6: merge duals| Combine both unique inverse rules into one normal form | Their common two-deletion parent | Minimal nonconfluence at6, then an all-order critical family |
|7: change operations| Use the internal-insertion path defect to reverse an arc | Two native moves, with one temporary vertex | Exact stabilized compiler; fixed-order and H-control questions remain separate |

The useful generator was the product of object, representation, invariant,
operation, quotient and scale. Examples include replacing a scalar by a
rooted path-cover vector; replacing all old vertices by active ports;
replacing fixed-order complement by a birth/complement/delete program; and
replacing one oriented inverse by its two-sided rewrite system. Every
candidate kept its failed implication and strongest survivor.

No new million-range or billion-range scan was performed. The numerical
work was small: exact ordinary controls through order6, selected literal
eight-vertex path counts, three root-degree probes through99/199, finite
control/signature closure, and native-gadget controls through order5.

## Reformulated proof obligations

The most concrete new conjectural route is now:

    OPEN: the degree-cap3 adaptive weighted automaton reaches every
    odd prime p!=7 and every7p with odd prime p!=3.

If true, it proves the full spectrum via THM-4094. It is stronger than
unrestricted attainability, so a counterexample would reject this grammar
without refuting the spectrum conjecture. Cap2 is already refuted by127.
No claim is made that cap3 is sufficient or that root degrees must grow
without bound.

A constructive induction should specify a family of **reachable** weighted
states, indexed by the target and whatever additional integer coordinates
are needed. For each target branch it must supply a named legal root,
prove the transfer stays in that family, and give a decreasing inverse
measure or overlapping interval cover. Positivity and integrality alone
cannot replace reachable-state membership. The old digital source-hole
hostile applies directly to that temptation.

Three concrete parallel routes should remain live:

1. Prove a target-address cover or a source-preserving descent in the
   existing seven-parent digital bank. This has the strongest finite
   evidence and an existing exact source inverse.
2. Prove coverage of a reachable state family in the new cap3 transfer.
   This has a simpler temporal boundary and an intrinsic object inverse,
   but only the stated small finite target evidence.
3. Prove target entry in the incoming65-symbol single-bridge matrix bank.
   Its one-ended observer is genuinely three-dimensional, but exact
   membership in its positive matrix orbit remains an additional theorem.

The incoming ternary proposal makes a possible induction precise: an
actual bank covering odd bases23..67 and closed under3H,3H+2,3H+4 would
cover every larger odd. Neither the offset operations nor bank closure
has been proved. Fixed word wrappers are obstructed, and the first selected
single-ear children already fail the next ternary step. This is a useful
conditional proof target only when all three branches preserve the same
actual bank; an ordinal C3 product leaves the strong adaptive language.

The metagraph program has separate goals: preserve the complete named
incidence carrier; classify fixed-order components or sufficient smaller
observers under their actual move guards; and retain the two-deletion
critical-pair information when combining oriented reductions. The new
temporary-vertex compiler supplies object reachability, not a method to
select a source with prescribed H.

Three principles now have explicit mechanisms and hostiles here: an inverse
on objects is not surjectivity onto values; bounded future interfaces can
give finite transfer despite unbounded static graph complexity; and two
individually unique inverse rules can have incompatible terminal objects.
Their scopes and connection maps are recorded in the component notes. The
existing META-PATTERNS cards on controlled forgetting, actual images and
source-preserving transport were used; no broad new meta-pattern was
promoted merely from the present examples.
