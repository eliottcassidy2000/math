## 2. Exact small observer: a new concrete entry problem

The golden construction takes actual path-preserving complements of an
ordered source word. In reversed processing order, adjoining a block puts
all old-to-new cross arcs forward except one boundary bridge `b->a`.
For a marked old tournament `(T,a)`, retain

`(A,B,C)=(H(T)-End(T,a), End(T,a), C(T,a))`,

where C counts paths starting at a plus two-path covers whose marked path
starts at a. The general single-bridge identity is

`H(T star B)=H(T)H(B)-End(T,a)Start(B,b)+C(T,a)D(B,b)`.

A path using the reversed bridge has at most the runs `T1 B1 T2 B2`; this is
why a finite boundary description exists. A singleton sends

`(A,B,C) -> (C,A,A+B+C)`.

Current H, H after one singleton, and H after two singletons reconstruct
all three coordinates. Golden-Hamilton RECURRENCE G1–G4 proves exact
nonnegative integer3-by-3 transfer for **every supplied finite block**, not
just the checked alphabet. Its universal forest intertwining closes the
seam packet's earlier open arbitrary-block question. Compiling an arbitrary
block still requires its path/cover counts; no polynomial-time algorithm
for arbitrary tournament counting follows.

The seam coordinates are related by

`(h,x,y)=(A+B,(C-B)/2,(B+C)/2)`.

Their integrality belongs to actual words. The incoming reconciliation
transports all960 supplied charts and independently replays eight repair
witnesses. The two results are compatible coordinate descriptions, not
independent new spectrum classifications.

The operation scope is crucial. One-ended future-H observation has minimum
rational dimension3, but allowing both ends to grow needs dimension9,
retaining the full word matrix. Equal one-ended states can separate after
an extension at the other end. This directly supports our root-profile
collision diagnosis: **closure under one fixed operation does not imply
closure under changing the interface**. It does not provide a three-coordinate
observer for arbitrary adaptive root switching.

## 4. Cut carriers sharpen necessary conditions, not target entry

Session152 packages the known quadratic ear response into a nonnegative
integral directed cut:

`F(S)=H+2 sum_(i in S,j outside S)c_ij`.

The weights are obtained by integral max flow from singleton increments
and pair curvatures. They need not follow tournament arc directions and
are generally nonunique. A strong parent gives strong positive support.
This reformulates the older symmetric-cut-plus-field carrier; it does not
identify which arbitrary weighted digraphs are realizable by a tournament,
or give a closed rule for the next parent.

Its new stated strong-ear estimate is

`F(S)-H >= 2 ceil((n-1)/2)`

for every nontrivial cut of a strong order-n parent. A Hamilton cycle has
an S-to-complement crossing present in n−1 of its path rotations, forcing
that many extra legal insertions; parity rounds up. It applies to our root
births and strengthens the +2 pruning bound without changing any saved
search universe. Summing it along C3 births gives

`H(T_n) >= 1 + floor((n-1)^2/2)`.

This is a simple consequence, not a replacement for the stronger asymptotic
Busch floor. The identity was checked arithmetically at orders3..11; the
summation proof is elementary. No new search was needed.

Marked-break counting gives the exact total and complementary-cut constraints

`sum K_ij=(n-1)H+B`,
`F(S)+F(V-S)=2H+K(delta S)`,

where B counts old permutations with one backward adjacency. For strong
parents a crossing Hamilton cycle gives further lower bounds. The first
identity/averages connect to the older THM-4111/4123 one-defect and fixed-rank
program; do not claim them as first discoveries here. The supplied fake
weighted carrier meets elementary positivity, gcd and total conditions but
fails a symmetric-cut bound, showing that actual realizability remains an
additional coordinate.

The latest fixed-rank note sets `d(S)=(F(S)-H)/2` and
`P_r(q)=sum_(|S|=r)q^d(S)`. It proves equal constant/linear terms at
complementary ranks, hence `(q-1)^2 | P_r-P_(n-r)`, and an exact second-moment
asymmetry governed by

`D=sum_i b_i deg_i`,
`sum_|S|=r d(S)^2 - sum_|S|=n-r d(S)^2`
`= ((n-2r)/(n-2)) binom(n-2,r-1) D`.

Self-converse parents have stronger exact reciprocity. A general parent
can fail it already at order4; equal means are not equal distributions.
The ear generating-function recurrence eliminates vertices of a **fixed
carrier** with shifted fugacities. It is not a recurrence producing actual
child carriers. These results strengthen our distinction among scalar
moments, image support and recursively faithful source data.
