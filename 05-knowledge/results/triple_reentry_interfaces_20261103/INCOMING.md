# Incoming integration: closure exists, but its price changes the count

Incoming revision `89416555c4` was fetched from a clean committed tree and
rebased before this extension. These results strengthen several parts of
the initial survey. They do not establish the missing exact marked ternary
return. This note updates the handoff rather than leaving an obsolete
“try a second scalar offset” target live.

## 1. Repeated scalar offsets already have a new construction

S164's [complementary ears](../session164_emergent_20261103/complementary_guard/COMPLEMENTARY_OFFSETS.md)
change the attachment, not merely the old guard. They give the actual strong
chain `5 -> 17 -> 53 -> 161`, three steps `h -> 3h+2`, and two-sided
parametric families. The marked observer corrections still differ from the
required `j(2,-1,0)`. The analogous +4 two-sided attempt has count307 where
a third +4 step would require295. Thus our next test is repeated **marked**
returns with a joint source certificate, not another scalar chain.

The [ready-return packet](../ready_return_pentagon_20261103/RETURN.md) gives
both branches of an infinite singleton ladder actual graded returns, with
full named transport. Its plus output acquires a growing defect; its
polynomial degree is unbounded. It is an expanded source description,
not return to the old ready interface. This directly supports the atlas's
proposal to carry more memory, but that proposal is now partly implemented
and should not be advertised as a fresh empty starting point.

## 2. Every strong source enters a canonical bank at a measured price

The [universal guard lift](../session164_emergent_20261103/complementary_guard/UNIVERSAL_GUARD_LIFT.md)
counts `K(X,q)` ordered spanning path pairs with the first path ending at q
and the second possibly empty. Put `J(X,q)=K(X,q)-H(X)>=0`. The first ear
has exact count `3h+2J`; a second ear gives a canonical source with count

    9h+6J.                                                  (4)

Every old edge and vertex survives. Strongness is preserved. The source
entry question alone is therefore solved by this mechanism; preserving the
original count, or the desired affine observer offset, is not.

We apply the incoming theorem to the six **actual** witnesses in our forest.
`seed_lifts.py` computes every root slack by subset endpoint counts. The
independent audit counts the same pairs by cutting permutations and then
counts both literal ear outputs directly by subset DP.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|1|0|0|9|
|23|1|4|213|
|25|2|5|237|
|63|0|0|567|
|65|4|6|609|

The first ears at23 and25 yield71 and79 respectively, with exactly the
small scalar corrections we want. The first ears at65 and67 cost offsets8
and10. These are minima over ports of the **printed witnesses**, not over
all tournaments having those counts. Finding a different65/67 source with
small J is a well-defined next search; even success would still need a
repeatable marked return.

This calculation refines the six-root criterion: all six roots have a
finite canonical normalization, but the normalization moves them to new
counts. A source-normalization theorem must carry its arithmetic price.
Berggren inversion preserves the represented triple while changing its
address; this lift changes the represented count itself.

## 3. Canonical source returns versus canonical multipliers

The [canonical-return theorem](../session164_emergent_20261103/profile_algebras/CANONICAL_RETURNS.md)
classifies returns of the C/D guard language. It constructs canonical strong
sources with `H=3^(a+1)91^b` and proves coverage of every residue modulo every
power of seven. The multiplier91 comes from an actual eight-letter return;
its output count from C3 is273, not91. The distinction is essential.

This is stronger source control than S157's unrestricted one-apex residue
coverage in the seven-adic direction. Nevertheless every source in this
particular canonical family has H divisible by3. Exact realization of an
arbitrary target remains a different obligation. Even compatible p-adic
approximations do not by themselves provide a finite terminating word with
the exact target value.

The [closed Boolean-gate observer](../session164_emergent_20261103/boolean_gates/CLOSED_OBSERVER.md)
also gives genuine all-depth fixed-port induced extensions. Their maps on
`(P,R)` are diagonal `(9P,168R)` and `(11P,200R)`. This is a useful model of
closed source operations, but those are not the ternary affine maps.

## 4. Higher interfaces and the positive full-profile carrier

The [nonuniform operator theorem](../session164_emergent_20261103/nonuniform_operator/NOTE.md)
proves that s exceptional vertices give a Boolean response of degree at
most2s, with monomial support a linear forest. Combining this proved degree
bound with our P6 gives a source-preserving nonuniform bound of `2s*b`
reversed incidences whenever a unit exists and there are b distinct nonunit
output values. It does not turn determination by2s probes into a composite
unit witness at2s incidences: our s=1 hostiles remain applicable.

That operator also proves an infinite linear-rank obstruction for repeated
head insertions over all transitive tails. Thus a universal *fixed-dimensional
linear* profile is already ruled out for that operation family. The full
polynomial, a bounded-depth state, or a nonlinear representation remain
possible; an unrestricted finite linear compression is not a fresh target.
