# Verification and claim boundaries

The theorem and checker were read against the same git-pinned source archive. All executable mathematical checks use explicit exceptions rather than Python assertions.

The deterministic receipt records 221,409 checks before frozen-receipt comparison. Normal and optimized frozen runs each execute 221,410 checks and agree exactly.

Independent comparisons include:

- All 4,165 simple Z/3 voltage-graph catalogues on one through four labelled slots (absent edge or one of three voltages per unordered pair), plus parallel-edge and loop controls.
- All 4,164 optional phase-pin cases on one through three slots. Actual owner records are enumerated independently of the voltage compiler.
- 12,259 unpinned fibre pairs on up to four slots, with weighted direct Hamming minimization compared to overlay edge-deletion feasibility; another 3,393 pinned fibre pairs test transported-pin incompatibility as well as cycle frustration.
- 133 equality fibres, 635 full budget graphs, and 26,685 ordered endpoint routes checked for the exact distance enumerator, heavy-phase component count, accepted intermediate states, and no return to a departed full record.
- 1,344 abstract Z/4 charge-quotient cases. These test the conditional additive theorem and are not claimed to be native electrical current realizations.
- Full actual native matrices, paths, complete prefix-pinned C3 menus, source rotations, target partner isomorphisms, converse reflection, and normalized four-word reservoirs for one through four prefix blocks. The order-eight seed's trivial automorphism group is checked over all 8! permutations and its strong connectivity is checked directly.
- All 2,166 indexed-clock pair comparisons among four distinct resources of S_2, and all 1,444 indexed-owner pins on those slots.
- An actual frustrated triangle with no global section despite full edgewise phase support.
- A compact eighteen-state helper bridge on S_3, and a 162-state simple-cycle overlay bridge on S_4. Both retain actual literal source frames, same-target partner transports, continuously held distinct resources, exact slot costs, and exact owner-clock costs.

The all-size conclusions rest on the proofs, not extrapolation from these finite universes. The helper bridge controls use an explicitly supplied helper-conditioned higher-arity predicate. They do not establish that such a predicate follows from pairwise old/helper owner equalities. The affine-coordinate discussion deliberately rules out inferring native reflection holonomy from inconsistent edgewise relabeling.

Resource non-reuse means no reacquisition after release; all fixed-prefix routes here release no resources. A stronger prohibition on returning to a full record is established for the direct fixed-family component routes, not for arbitrary mode-quotient lifts. No normalized kernel lift is counted as extra capacity.
