## 2. Labelled paths, normalized words, and the sandwich square

For a fixed source and directed spanning path P, the partner is uniquely forced:
retain the path arcs and reverse every other arc. The agreement digraph is
exactly P, so no path witness is lost until labels or presentations are forgotten.
This construction is involutive on the pair and equivariant under relabelling.
Taking source converse and reversing P gives the partner converse.

A normalized word plus the forced consecutive arcs reconstructs the full
tournament in path-position labels. Equality of two such words is therefore
equivalent to an actual source automorphism carrying one ordered path to the
other. The automorphism action is free. Thus the number of words is exactly
H/Aut, not a guessed division or an unlabeled-path count.

For `S=R -> C3 -> R^op`, every path visits the summands in order: after crossing
an ordinal cut it cannot return. The ordered SCC list is unique, and an
automorphism fixes every position in it. This remains true when R is decomposable
or contains repeated C3 components. Consequently `H(S)=3H(R)^2` and
`Aut(S)=3Aut(R)^2`. Restricting an automorphism to the summands proves that the
ordered pair of R path orbits is a bijective coordinate system, not merely a
matching cardinality.

The literal source anti-automorphism exchanges the wings and takes `c_i` to
`c_-i`. Applied to the reversed path it exchanges the two coordinates and takes
phase k to `1-k`. The middle phases differ by an automorphism. Hence reflection
on normalized words is exactly `(x,y) -> (y,x)`, with no extra F complement.

For nonempty R, the source arc from the path's first owner to its last owner is
not a consecutive path arc. Its reversal in K closes the retained spanning path
to a directed cycle. Every target is strong and S is not; source and F-image
fibres are disjoint, including after converse merging. Therefore each resource
meets the source fibre in exactly `{(x,y),(y,x)}`. The complete profile is
`a^2` source words, `a` blue resources, `a(a-1)` weighted black incidences,
`a(a-1)/2` paired-black resources, and `a(a+1)/2` total resources. These numbers
do not count distinct neighboring classes. Empty R is outside the closing-arc
argument, and its C3 partner is transitive, as directly checked.
