## 3. Route theorem and actual costs

Let t be the free-core V count. For 8<=t<=9+e, normalize the receiver to
r 0=max(8,t-e), which is 8 or 9. Enter the band 8..9 by moving the required number
of selected slots between receiver and outside, refreshing R from its complete
section at every arrival. Then reduce the receiver grade to r 0, if needed.
Connect normalized outside states by Johnson exchanges, follow a Johnson path
of the free helper pair, refresh to the literal normalized final endpoint,
and reverse that endpoint's normalization. At every arrival, a section pays
exactly the required residual Q charge. The original core count, helper count
four, frozen records, owner 4, actual pins, and source resources are retained.
This construction terminates and restores the prescribed full endpoint,
not merely its charge representative.

For receiver subsets of grades r,s, their symmetric difference has size at
most min(r+s,34-r-s). The receiver costs are sixteen 2 s and one c in{3,4}.
At grade 8 or 9, a refresh costs at most 30+c and changes at most 16 slots.
A helper exchange adds at most 15 clocks and two slots. Therefore the interior
route has the uniform per-event budget

    18 changed slots and 45+c primary slot-clock occurrences,
    namely 18/48 for c=3 or 18/49 for c=4.

Consecutive grade 9->8 changes at most 17 receiver slots and one outside slot,
costing at most 36+c. Outside exchanges cost at most 38+c. The exact entry
maximum is 43: the auditor evaluates every free outside subset for each p and
every feasible t and receiver grade. Thus entry and other stages fit the
helper-stage bound. These estimates hold for every witnessing section, so
there is no unproved composition of independent minimum-cost representatives.

Outside the band, t<=7 or (17+e)-t<=7. Any two actual endpoints then differ
on at most 14 free-core slots and four free helper slots. A single simultaneous
accepted endpoint replacement is legal in this model. The 14 largest costs in
the entire original core sum to 39; the four largest free-helper costs sum to 29.
Hence every nonempty fibre has the uniform global bound

    18 slots /68 clocks.

This improves the originally proposed 85 bound; it is a safe upper bound, not
a claimed optimum. In the ten-frozen protected-pair case only the cost 4 and
cost 3 bank slots remain free outside the 16 cheap sources. The 14 largest free-
core costs sum to 31, so the specialized global bound is 18/60. Interior bounds
are unchanged. If p is first chosen from Good(F) and then all seven other
outside records are frozen, eleven core records are fixed and only the 17-slot
receiver remains free. Its 14 largest costs sum to 26+c, hence its global budget
is 18/(55+c), or 18/58 for c=3 and 18/59 for c=4. This is adaptive choice followed
by freezing, not a universal singleton protected before F is known. Interior
bounds remain 18/48 or 18/49 at t=8,9. These assertions do not promise charge
coverage outside their bands.

Core complementation is only a coordinate change. If C is the set of selected

    lambda(C)+eta(H) = lambda(all free core)-lambda(Cbar)+eta(H).

The checker evaluates this identity and the literal paired current at every

e=7, and the chosen-source case e=0. These are implementation controls; the complete section and migration
proof, not sampling, supply the universal endpoint quantifier.
