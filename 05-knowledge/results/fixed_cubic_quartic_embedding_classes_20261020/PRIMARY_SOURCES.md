  (1996), printed p.1062, Section 2, directly identifies the initial
  characteristic entries with the two coordinate pole orders and explains
  elementary reduction to a nonprincipal pair. Original indexed page:
  https://scispace.com/pdf/uniqueness-of-plane-embeddings-of-special-curves-omujaliiec.pdf.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6.
  Producer and independent boundary reviewer retrieved the original
  paragraph separately. Direct PDF retrieval failed, so no downloaded
  byte-identical copy is claimed.

- Sathaye–Stenerson, *On Plane Polynomial Curves*, Section 3.1(1)–(5),
  printed pp.8–12, author text dated 10 September 2014:
  https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/papnew.pdf.
  The full PDF was downloaded and read. The nonprincipal initial-degree
  bound is conductor+1; later entries are at most conductor−1. Together
  with the characteristic gcd and primitive-generator properties, this
  gives exactly the reduced patterns used in the proof.
