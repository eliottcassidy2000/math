## Inheritance and the actual new implementation

[Session87](../session87_four_vertices_enumeration_20261028/EXACT_GENERATION.md)
already proves parent-only canonical augmentation: enumerate root masks
modulo the parent's full automorphism group, restrict the chosen root to an
invariant locus, and break remaining ties by the complete aligned deletion
key. Its neighbor-degree locus is the relevant baseline, not the original
full root-switch matrix. [kernel125](../kernel125_closed_carriers_20261028/THEORY.md)
extends that locus through9 with24005 competing-root queries using its own
minimum convention. Its Paley gain-tree and native-complement hostiles are
also inherited, not reannounced as new theorems.

The new checker refines the degree partition repeatedly by counts of
outneighbors in every current ordered cell. In a tournament the incoming
counts are determined by these and the cell sizes. Cells retain their
previous order; subdivisions are ordered lexicographically by their exact
count vectors. Choose the last stable cell as the candidate-root locus.
A degree comparison can reject an impossible root before paying for stable
refinement, because refinement never changes the order of degree cells.

For parent canonization, if cells are not all singletons, individualize
every possible vertex of the first nonsingleton cell in turn and refine
again. At the leaves encode the whole tournament, choose the minimum code,
and retain **every** attaining order. No automorphism is replaced by its
order alone, and no tied root is accepted from the colour signature alone.

Why this is exact: every split and cell choice is isomorphism-invariant.
An isomorphism bijects the search trees and their leaf codes, making the
minimum a complete isomorphism invariant. Every attaining order is an
actual map from that canonical relation to the labelled input. Composing
with one inverse base order gives every actual source automorphism, and
none other. The original parent-only augmentation proof therefore applies.
The selected root and every comparison use the same canon convention.

1. arbitrary transport of the four named vertex labels;

## Frobenius and LCA: the positive instance and its boundary

The incoming kernel125 K4 section realizes Paley7 automorphisms as
`x -> alpha^b x^(2^a)` on F8*, and realizes those maps in actual14-vertex
strong matching attachments. A pinned owner removes b and leaves the three

products along the LCA path. Native complement leaves that restricted

normal-basis necklace correspondence, so we inherit it directly.

Our canonicalizer supplies a complementary test: it preserves the complete
transport set on C3,T5,Paley7 and both81/27 attachments. LCA can then locate

the choice of literal child versus converse. The new K3,3 bridge gives a
