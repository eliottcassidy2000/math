## Exactly what the two erased bits do

The original three field lines, listed in edge masks, are

    (1,4,5), (2,32,34), (8,16,24).

three additional triples have XOR zero:

    (1,16,34), (2,4,24), (5,8,32).

with the old triples on one side and the new triples on the other.

cut space of this K3,3. Its six star rows have one dependence (their total
sum is zero). The three old rows alone span the three-dimensional field

sigma(n)=product(p+1). This is a square exactly when the subset is a K3,3
