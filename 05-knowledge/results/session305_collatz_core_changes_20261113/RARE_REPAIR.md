# Sparse sign repair for ordinary Collatz and a warning for coverage

**Proved** for a modified dynamical system. The statement does not change the definition or solve ordinary Collatz. The relation to tournament readiness is a proof-design criterion, not a lift.

## Exact finite-horizon exceptional cylinders

Let T(x) = x/2 at even x and (3x+1)/2 at odd x. For every K≥2 there is a union B_K of dyadic cylinders and a finite set E_K with the following properties:

- Off B_K∪E_K and away from 1, an ordinary trajectory makes strict descent below its source in at most K half-steps.
- At every x>1 in B_K∪E_K (all odd), changing the sign on the first odd step and then halving once gives strict descent in two half-steps.
- B_K has exactly the natural density of points failing to descend under the ordinary map within K steps. Thus the density of repair sources is optimal among methods required to begin each block with an uninterrupted ordinary segment that already descends, or to declare a repair at that block's source. This is not optimality among arbitrary control policies or placements of sign changes.

Use the actual parity word w of the K ordinary half-steps; it is constant on x mod 2^K, and all binary words occur in exactly one residue class. Inductively the next two lifts of a length-j cylinder have x differing by 2^j, so their values at step j differ by 3^s with s the number of ones, and hence have different parity. A prefix of length j has affine map (3^s x + c)/2^j with c≥0. Put its cylinder in B_K exactly when no prefix slope 3^s/2^j is <1. On such a cylinder all prefixes stay at least x. On every other cylinder, strict descent holds for all x above the fixed point c/(2^j−3^s) of any contracting prefix. There are finitely many cylinders, so all ordinary failures outside B_K form a finite set E_K (excluding 1). For K≥2, an ordinary failure x>1 must satisfy x≡3 mod4: even x descends in one step, and x≡1 mod4 descends by (3x+1)/4 in two. Thus the minus repair yields (3x−1)/4<x in exactly two half-steps (one odd step and one even step), whether or not the result is odd. Restart the controller at that smaller positive integer. Strict descent at block boundaries proves reachability of 1; the finite normal segments are certified separately from the repair branches.

## How sparse is the repair?

Let p = log(2)/log(3)>1/2. Each prefix of a survivor satisfies s_j≥pj. Uniform binary words give the exact recurrence for its numerator N_K: start with C(0,0)=1, set C(j,s)=0 below ceil(pj), and otherwise C(j,s)=C(j−1,s)+C(j−1,s−1), taking absent terms as zero. Then density(B_K)=2^(−K) Σ_s C(K,s). To prove exponential decay, the terminal condition gives the Chernoff bound exp(−K D(p || 1/2)), where D=p log(2p)+(1−p)log(2(1−p))>0. The rate is exact: among words with s=ceil(pK), at least 1/K of the words have all prefixes above their own average (rotate at a minimum of the discrepancy). The lower bound K^(−1)2^(−K) binom(K,s) and Stirling have the same exponential rate. In particular the family B_K shrinks in density to zero, yet every finite K has the nonempty cylinder of K consecutive ones. There is no inference from small or vanishing natural density to absence of a fixed exceptional trajectory, nor any bound on the number of repairs along one orbit independent of its start.

The [source-aware Collatz compiler](../collatz_boundary_compiler_20261004.md) gives the exact fixed-point idea and sharper first-member bounds in the odd-valuation coding. The [fair-split necklace packet](../collatz_necklace_20260929_fair_splits_power_clocks_basins.md) isolates prefix discrepancy and rotation. Here those mechanisms specify an optimal *source-level* repair set for the particular K-horizon protocol. This is a scalar theorem: in a tournament compiler one must certify a physical reverse block on each surviving interface and count the failure **states**, not just project them to residues. A zero-density failure set, as in this model, need not be empty; a certified repair is a separate obligation.
