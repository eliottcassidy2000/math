## Load-bearing proofs

1. A block with distinct rows and columns induces a PARTIAL bijection on
   supplied internal chart sets. The domain may be empty. Fixing one chart
   fixes at most one neighboring chart; it does not ensure existence.
2. Root propagation is complete only when every chord and its domain are
   checked. The state-graph criterion additionally checks a lift over every
   incident base edge; merely covering all base vertices is insufficient.
3. Cell identities, marks and singleton attachments remain retained.
   Every candidate full permutation is checked on every source arc. The
   typed rule is not a reconstruction from anonymous row histograms.
4. Tournament automorphism groups have odd order; restricting to a moved
   root-cell point reduces a faithful stabilizer by an odd index at least3.
   This gives a group-relative owner bound, not a universal two-owner claim.
5. Residual potentials certify each authenticated chart branch separately.
   Projected baselines must agree between branches. The union of allowed
   cells can introduce combinations that do not share one chart.
6. Private-path suppression preserves domains and has a unique lift.
   A shared interior port cannot be eliminated independently on two paths.
7. The zero slot is never selected, but its atom index remains present.
   The complete fibre formula separates occurrence of indices0 and2.
   A zero length is not an instruction to identify adjacent boundaries.
8. Every preferred C leaf in a larger Fibonacci block is paired with A at
   one even-index expansion node. Substituting B there preserves value,
   nonadjacency and every other position. A terminal ordinary unit uses the
   corresponding row-boundary replacement. This proves coherent arbitrary
   C-to-B subsets, rather than inferring a law from a finite colour match.
9. The formal lift assigns nonzero charge to the zero-length slot. Each
   preferred split omits exactly that charge; counting internal expansion
   nodes and C-to-B replacements restores the inherited shifted register.
   The root register is taken before replacement.
10. Fibonacci macro products are noncommutative and keep their integer
   length. The finite group-pair periods cannot be read as time cycles of
   a Collatz orbit or as periodicity of the coloured word.
11. The eight-step return is derived over the integer Eisenstein ring,
   before reduction: I+3Q with Q^2=0, determinant zero and a unit entry.
   The prime3 is excluded, so its Smith factors over Z/(p^k) are a unit
   and zero. Translation valuation gives the exact8p^r periods and their
   counts. The separate identity-slope branch uses the Fibonacci matrix;
   its first seventh-power lift has nonzero correction, preventing an
   unproved Pisano-period assumption. An affine group model is not the
   full permutation carrier of matching-coupled prime cells.

  without an actual owner would discard symmetry.

  All420 reached triple systems are empty in that predicate. The relative
  chart-isomorphism carrier still has its identity, and is the appropriate
  carrier for comparing whole attachment systems.
- The old row35 obstruction is not retracted: its alphabet had adjacent
  A and B unit indices. The new zero-inserted alphabet makes them
  nonadjacent and supplies a previously forbidden expansion. The old
  statement is linked to its explicitly different extension.

These were pre-publication hypothesis/code probes or changes of grammar,
not retractions of previously proved canon. Their failure mechanisms and
strongest survivors are preserved here.
