# Famous conjectures, the 67/69 pair, and programmable nonzero determinants

- [What is and is not conditional on Goldbach](CONDITIONAL.md).
  Binary Goldbach **plus an unproved physical prime-gluing rule**
  would imply Chan–Pak. A three-offset selector avoids the forbidden
  prime 7. Disjoint induced inputs impose a product, not a sum;
  even preserving the larger witness alone fails for unbounded q
  and fixed small p. Collatz convergence does not supply inverse
  tournament witnesses, and a literal one-letter strong Zaremba
  compiler would fail on its Fibonacci branch.
- [An exact Artin/GRH bridge](ARTIN.md). The two literal 67/69 rays
  cover every residue of an odd modulus precisely for prime powers
  with 2 primitive and prime not 3 or 17. Infinite modular
  universality is exactly Artin(2), and Hooley's GRH implies positive
  density among prime moduli. Mod 67^k always works; mod 69 has
  only 32 residues. This is modular, not Chan–Pak.
- [Growing whole determinants at a fixed +2](DETERMINANTS.md).
  A fixed patch and four independent nibble choices give
  Ω(X^(log_3 2/4)) distinct nonzero signed determinants at count
  scale X. Cut determinants and whole-tape determinants are different
  objects, both controlled here by separate constructions.
- [Arbitrary prescribed gap up to X^(2/5)](ASYMMETRIC.md).
  An asymmetric digit/carry budget gives at least
  Ω(X^0.05773) distinct nonzero **whole** determinants for every
  such signed even gap with both physical counts at most X.
  This does not choose the baseline of the pair.
- [Vitali-style actual interval packing](PACKING.md).
  Independent digit changes separated by guards give disjoint
  physical +2 intervals, not just distinct projected determinants.
  The proof is not one that gaps between those windows disappear.
- [67/69 archive](ARCHIVE_67_69.md) and [binary carry decoder](DETERMINANT_CARRY.md).
  The original triple inflation has only polylogarithmically many
  contrasts through X; its inverse factors two Mersenne numbers.
  The new physical patch decodes the whole carry word from the
  signed determinant; the varying branch with alternating bits
  is Fibonacci. [The original gap-two pair itself](PAIR_67_69.md)
  cannot come from a mutual-arc flip over a transitive spine at
  any order. Vitali extraction after projecting to scores loses
  the physical distinction that 67 and 69 demonstrate.

**Status:** no implication between full Chan–Pak and a famous
open conjecture alone was established. The strongest output is
an actual joint supply of large determinant menus and exact gaps,
with the absolute baseline still uncontrolled.
