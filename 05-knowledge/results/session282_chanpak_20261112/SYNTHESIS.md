# Session282: exact receiver fibers, nine renewable counts, and carry-localized surgery

**Chan–Pak remains OPEN. Independently reviewed; final source receipt below.** This long
session pursued several genuinely different constructive routes. Its strongest
positive result is a nine-count renewable bank, coupled to an explicit infinite
family of eighteen consecutive odd counts. The unbounded target-selection
obligation is not hidden by those finite widths.

## 1. Replace an unbounded search with an exact receiver inverse

[Receiver fiber theorem](renewal/PROOF.md) fixes a deletion core D and an
actual receiver V, then solves every root cut of every
`TT_a -> D -> TT_b` extension by a logarithmic signed binary descent.
Acceptance gives the literal tournament, not only a congruence class.
The algorithm exhausts all padding orders in that specified universe.

Two small-core conclusions are global, not just chosen-template claims:

- At deletion count h=3, SCC factorization forces one directed triangle and
  singleton padding. A binary-defect formula proves that no fixed(h,V) fiber
  contains three consecutive odd counts, at any tournament size.
- At h=5, Camion's bound plus the complete small core certificate forces the
  unique strong four-vertex core and singleton padding. Thus the inverse
  classifies every fixed(h=5,V) scalar fiber. It finds a renewable four-bank
  from the unequal endpoint weights9,7,5,6; no universal width-four bound is claimed.

[Global compression](compression/PROOF.md) makes the broader boundary precise:
for V>h every(H,h,V,e) fiber has a representative of order at most

    b(h)+2+floor(log2((V-h)/h)),   b(1)=0, b(h)=h for h>1.

It deletes only neutral singleton SCCs after the last root-incoming component.
This gives an exact finite all-tournament decision universe for specified h,V,
not an efficient enumeration algorithm or a bound on the receiver required
for an arbitrary prescribed H.

Every common left unit-deletion fusion preserves all nine spacings and the
shared receiver; arbitrary left deletion count scales the spacings by that

## 3. Turn receiver equality into a finite carry automaton

[Carry-localized surgery](wildcard/PROOF.md) treats fixed(h,Q) physical module
alphabets as rational-base digits. With g=gcd(h,Q), each one-sided equality
uses at most2g-1 carry states. Two closed carries plus endpoint flags recognize
full-root-packet equality except H. Crucially, the remaining long-range H
interaction telescopes into an exact local integer action.

This produced a new literal CENTRAL +2 pair:

    H=2747/2749,       h=169,
    (Q,U,V,d,e)=(10816,4030,6955,104,65).

Its two-module identity is `M(B)M(C)=M(A)^2+2E14`. The net+2 comes from the
signed path-length channel after the other two channels cancel26−26.
Discarding that signed sidecar would miss the construction. Unit-deletion
contexts preserve this +2 forever; arbitrary contexts pay the exact multiplier.

The same alphabet has an exact monomer-dimer fiber: A and BC are interchangeable
receiver blocks. At length k with j dimers, the count is
`H(A^k)+2j*13^(k-2)`, and the program multiplicity is binomial(k-j,j), with
weighted generating function `1/(1-z-tz²)`. This is a genuine infinite
structural classification, not an A000568 enumeration.

Fixed-language arithmetic content still grows. A literal pair of16-vertex
packet twins has different rerooting resources; one reroot-and-triangle
construction gives914613, not divisible by13. More strongly, an exact
[18-state three-strand compiler](wildcard/ROOT_RESET.md) proves that rerooting
A^k at a specified last-block vertex and adding a triangle gives a count
congruent to4*3^(k-4) modulo13 for every k>=4. The original count is divisible
by13^(k-2). Thus one physical operation removes arbitrarily many powers of13,
not merely a bounded amount of the fixed-core obstruction. A uniformly
renewable marked-root return map and absolute-target control remain unproved.

## 4. Primitive mesh is not absolute ownership

[Central convolution](central/PROOF.md) gives the exact count law for independent
central banks: normalized differences are sums(D/h_i)d_i. Their ideal is
primitive only under the stated prime-ownership conditions. Repeating a
nonunit deletion type magnifies the mesh rather than filling it.

For pairwise coprime deletion types, a target determines unique residue digits;
an explicit bounded carry-sum condition is necessary AND sufficient for that
absolute target. Complete physical digit banks would yield a guaranteed interior
interval. Their supply is a separate condition, not granted by CRT.

The new h169 gate and inherited h15 gate give four literal counts
474053,474083,474391,474421. Their normalized weights169 and15 are coprime,
but474055 is absent from this fixed construction. The exact selector detects
that gap: a primitive difference gcd alone is not coverage.

## 5. Signed correction: a constructive escape and its interface price

[Template/Jordan lane](targeting/PROOF.md) proves an exact target-height bound
for fixed transitive-substitution templates. A finite template bank has only
polylogarithmic scalar support. When variable modules are acyclic, bounded
run counts yield a finite Stirling tensor and exact all-size identity tests.
Signed finite combinations along affine size schedules are constant or
exponentially large; they cannot leave a nonconstant polynomial correction.

Interleaved tapes escape that hypothesis. They physically realize

    9H((01)^(2m))-25H((0011)^m)=2m,    m>=1,

by cancelling the same exponential mode while retaining linear/Jordan memory.
This is not a new existence claim for every even gap; that was already known,
and the inherited gap construction has better height cost. Its value is the
explicit mechanism and a precise boundary for proposed signed-error designs.

An exact scalar-twin identity produces same-order, same-receiver families with
different sending coordinates. Their converses genuinely tune a receiver at
fixed scalar count. The [tuner addendum](targeting/RECEIVER_TUNER.md) proves
exactly when common two-sided fusion remains count-neutral, and why every
such neutral use retains factor9. It is a real adjustment resource, not a
free repair for prime targets or unit-spaced corridors.

## 6. Unifying lesson and remaining obligation

The useful common language is **finite experiments plus physical carries**:
retain the receiving state, compile exact digit compatibility, keep the signed
local action, and attach the absolute count baseline. This session turns those
requirements into concrete decoders, classified fibers and new gadgets.

Still missing is an operation supply that covers every prescribed allowed odd
count while preserving enough interface for the next step. Neither a finite
interval at infinitely many locations, a rational difference generating
function, a full residue orbit, nor same-count twins supplies that implication.
The next constructive routes are phase-compatible widening with rearmed
receivers, or a controlled reroot reset combined with the exact carry language.
