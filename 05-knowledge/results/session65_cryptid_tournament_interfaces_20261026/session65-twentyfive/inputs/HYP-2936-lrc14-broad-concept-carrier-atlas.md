## Broad Connections

- Unit-distance impairment spectroscopy says to damage a side channel and see
  which exact small witnesses fail.  For LRC14, the analogous impairment is to
  delete C27 shell lanes or K33 owners and measure which residual branches lose
  their certificate.
- Tournament deck derivatives say global scalar repairs are too weak; pair each
  projection card with the boundary/owner derivative.  For LRC14, this is the
  same reason a row needs visible/hidden relation labels after the Farey branch.
- Cauldron two-block games and Collatz-style residue analogies warn that
  density is blind to correlated residue streams.
- Vitali/Helly/Bonferroni work says finite moments can miss the decisive
  all-orders cancellation, so a conservative shell/probe basis is better than a
  low-order scalar moment.
- OCF and forbidden `H=7` supply the terminal packet category, but only after
  the LRC residual has been lifted into complete tournament-conflict structure.
