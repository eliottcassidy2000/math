# Session65: machines, tournament carriers, and typed problem transfers

The useful conclusion is not that the open problems are all isomorphic.
It is a collection of exact encodings, arithmetic bridges and obstruction
theorems, organized by what their maps preserve. `CONNECTIONS.md` states the
proposed framework and its limits; the separate reduction atlas gives the
principal repository problem families with explicit quantifiers.

## 1. The supplied sequence is an exact halting control

The sequence6,16,34,64,114,196,334,564,946,1584,2646,4416,7366,12284 is the
Marxen–Buntrock five-state machine's macro-orbit after its initial0. The next
macro is HALT. Independent native simulation reproduces47,176,870 steps,
4098 final ones, and the complete macro endpoint tapes. Arithmetic growth
alone is therefore not evidence for nonhalting.

The associated map's3-adic safe inverse branches form a binary Cantor full
shift. Exact residue counting proves density-one halting among integer
seeds and a sparse upper bound on possible exceptions, not all-seed halting.
Its induced signed ternary tournament is transitive after alternating the
code-bit order. This is a particularly clean example of a simple tournament
shadow failing to decide integer realization in a rich dynamical carrier.

## 2. The named morphology machines have exact tournament interfaces

The Spaghetti Sandwich, Bouncer and Bell, and typical Irregular Bell tables
are total four-state binary programs: none has a halting transition.
Their nonhalting is immediate; the interesting work is the morphology and
guarded native macro behavior. The packet supplies closed word macro systems,
exact tapes/heads/states, clocks and edit counts, with independent checks.

Binary tape windows embed isometrically into ordered joins of transitive
triples and directed triangles. Writes change a designated arc and may
merge/split actual transitive clone blocks. The decoder retains symbol
position; the scalar Hamiltonian count does not. Head/program/origin data
remain sidecars. This is not a simulator using Hamiltonian K moves.

A sweep family has endpoint tournament distance1 but native time2k+1.
Thus even an exact metric embedding cannot be used as a running-time bound.

## 3. Fifteen has a genuine arithmetic-action bridge

For the actual Frobenius permutation on supersingular j-values, the minimum
number of failed orientation equations over all tournaments equals the
number of nonrational conjugate pairs. The cited modular-curve theorem makes
this exactly g(X_0^+(p)). Hence the fifteen Ogg primes are the zero-defect
primes. At p=37, independent finite-field checks give{8,3+10t,3-10t}, t^2=2,
and the unavoidable defect is1. No canonical arithmetic tournament is asserted.

More generally, the complete defect polynomial factors over signed orbits
of unordered pairs. Its minimum is the number of even cycles of the vertex
permutation. This gives a precise common mechanism behind the arithmetic
involution and the familiar odd-order automorphism restriction for tournaments.

The earlier fifteen duads and fifteen Richelot synthemes are distinct S6
carriers. The new defect formula assigns minima4 and6 to the same labelled
transposition, quantitatively strengthening their recovered fixed-point
character distinction. Matching list lengths would have lost that information.

Sloane's exponent15 and the historical15-state Erdős machine are separate
facts. The all-ones tail family survives every finite ternary precision,
but its compatible profinite exponent is not an ordinary integer exponent.
This exposes a real missing realization condition, not a digit-conjecture proof.

## 4. Twenty-five: one actual isomorphism and one different equivalence

The historical S62c25+ cross-field connection list was recovered and its
claims source-ranked. It is a method atlas, not25 proved transports.

The Goldbach wiki table and the pinned Lean25-state table are literally
isomorphic by state renaming: all50 slots and49 instructions match.
The Goldbach statement itself is instead linked by “blank computation halts
iff there is a counterexample.” The2016 source had27 states; the formalized
25-state compression is later, and newer21-state claims have their own
verification status. Reachable two-state padding shows that program-state
count is not a semantic invariant. Source/upstream CI inspection is not a
local Lean rebuild, and this packet does not claim such a rebuild.

## 5. Multiply-perfect arithmetic was connected without forcing a reduction

For r>=2, the recovered tournament arc-family identity extends exactly to

    sigma(binomial(2^r,2))/binomial(2^r,2)
      = sigma(2^r-1)/2^(r-1).

It includes the triply-perfect arc dimensions120 and523776, as well as the
classical even-perfect examples. This family is even, so it cannot represent
hypothetical odd multiperfect witnesses. The separate odd valuation ledger
gives necessary conditions, not sufficient existence criteria. Only6 in
the supplied machine sequence is multiply perfect; the isolated equality
sigma(946)=1584 does not make the machine a divisor-sum recurrence.

## 6. The proposed completeness classification needs typed arrows

The atlas distinguishes finite decision problems, universal counterexample
search, totality/eventual-output statements, exact simulation and bounded
resource reductions. Sharing a target halting encoding gives no reverse
reduction. Under unrestricted computable reductions, nontrivial decidable
languages collapse for an uninformative reason; complexity comparisons need
their resource bounds. NP-complete languages are themselves decidable.

There are real directed implications, with their actual scopes retained:
full Collatz implies absence of nontrivial positive cycles; no odd
multiperfect numbers implies no odd perfect numbers; the cited algebraic
chain is JC_(2n) => DC_n => JC_n, not a fixed-rank equivalence. The atlas does
not invent converse arrows or a universal dependency among these conjectures.

Two structural no-go results explain why bare tournament pictures cannot
supply those arrows. A deterministic configuration graph with at least four
vertices is not a tournament. And an encoding that faithfully injects every
control-graph automorphism group into a tournament automorphism group cannot
exist, because general control graphs have involutions. This does not rule
out a generic isomorphism reduction or a labelled, symmetry-broken codec.

## Honest frontier

No Goldbach, full Collatz, all-seed Marxen–Buntrock, Sloane/Erdős ternary,
odd-multiperfect, global black-core-connectivity or NP-completeness theorem
is claimed. Formal proof sources are distinguished from local builds.
The mathematical gain is a set of proved interfaces and explicit reasons
why stronger purported transfers fail. A future genuine reduction needs
the exact state carrier, admissible operations, witnesses, converse and
clock/size bounds, not just a common machine encoding or a repeated integer.
