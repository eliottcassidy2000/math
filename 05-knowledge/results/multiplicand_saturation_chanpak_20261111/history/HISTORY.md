## 1. July support holes: keep the deleted diagonal in the native polynomial

**THM-2200**, `01-canon/theorems/THM-2200-convex-semigroup-and-finite-place-support-hole-trichotomy.md`,
proves for a positive-coefficient polynomial P that
`supp(P^n)=supp(P)^(+n)`. Convex-external holes stay absent on every
dilation, while an internal rational convex combination clears at a
finite denominator. It separately retains Frobenius divisibility of a
coefficient at prime dilation. Its tournament example repairs the absent
cycle profile (8,10) only after doubling to (16,20) by an actual ordered
join; it does not repair an unchanged target.

For the present strict vector growth, use the indicator polynomial
`F_t(z)=sum_(v∈S_t) z^v`. Its exact native update is

    F_(t+1)=Bool(F_t+(F_t(z)^2−F_t(z^2))/2),           (1)

where Bool replaces every positive coefficient by 1. The quotient counts
unordered unequal parent pairs: each ordered off-diagonal pair occurs
twice, and F_t(z^2) removes exactly one occurrence for each diagonal.
All coefficients before Bool are nonnegative integers.

Source→target: ordinary positive-support multiplication→strict parent
support. Map: subtract the actual diagonal before dividing by 2 and taking
support. Preserved: the complete next generation. Lost by plain Newton
support: equality of parent values. Required sidecar: the diagonal term.
Cheapest hostile: from seeds (1,0),(0,1), plain squaring incorrectly creates
(2,0) and (0,2). This is the same correction responsible for c_2=3 in
the new boundary recurrence, rather than the weak-pair value 2.

Equation (1) is an integer identity. Reducing its numerator modulo 2 first
erases it by Frobenius and cannot recover the divided pair count. This is
an actual use of the older distinction between support and finite-place
coefficient data, rather than a numerical analogy.

## 2. August Rule 30: a protected grade isolates a closed finite strip

**THM-3488**, `01-canon/theorems/THM-3488-rule30-inward-slack-monicity-and-parity-cartier-ramification.md`,
keeps ballistic weight `t−v=u+1+2d`. Its exact lowest face is
`Face_4=z^4/(1+zq)`, and its target slack coefficient is monic of degree
t−4. Setting q=1 can cancel the signal. The theorem's finite shallow
owner atlas is proved because competing higher grades cannot enter a
fixed lower grade, not because a large prefix looked stable.

The transfer used here is grade monotonicity. An integer face functional
ell is additive and nonnegative on every seed, so a target of grade≤K
cannot have a parent above grade K. Equation (1) can therefore be
truncated by grade before iterating, without approximation. With one
zero-grade seed A, at most K off-face seed leaves survive in that strip;
their finitely many cosets modulo ZA retain the transverse information.

Preserved: every actual fixed-strip construction and its synchronous time.
Destroyed by the scalar numeric maximum: which exposed face or grade
hosts a delayed value. Cheapest test: the complete k=1 row has only
t+1 points while balanced mixed products already grow on a much larger
exponent scale. The new theorem supplies every fixed k, including its
full interval and exact linear endpoint, not only the first layer.

## 3. July Gordian ideals: finite generators do not determine the clock

**THM-2220**, `01-canon/theorems/THM-2220-fixed-context-stable-response-and-catalyst-complexity.md`,
retains cost ideals `A_m(x)={y:rho_x(y)≤m}` and proves
`A_m(x)+A_n(x′)⊆A_(m+n)(x+x′)`. On a finite prime alphabet these upper
ideals have finite antichain bases. The theorem explicitly retains the
inclusion direction; it does not identify a static finite basis with an
exact continuation operator.

The multiplicand static closure likewise has a finite mixed-seed ideal
description, but it does not give generation times. The added coordinate
here is the height budget. For two independent seeds the constants c_k
are the minimum charge of binary construction trees, and the state is
the affine endpoint `kt−c_k`, not merely the eventual row ideal.

Source→target: static ideal→graded-time epigraph. Preserved: full closure
and exact late row membership. Added sidecar: construction height and the
equal-split tax. Cheapest hostile: (2,2) is permanently attainable but
absent at generation 2; the ideal alone cannot answer its time query.

## 4. July carry transducer: finite control requires its integer carry

**THM-2211**, `01-canon/theorems/THM-2211-carry-regime-root-transducer-and-infinite-autonomous-index.md`,
gives an exact finite LRC root state when floor-carry digits are supplied,
while proving infinite autonomous index for a stronger exact continuation
task. It carefully leaves weaker restricted recognizers open.

Our coset compiler has the same concrete need for representative carries:
`v_q+v_r=v_s+kappa(q,r)A`. Dropping kappa changes actual target values even
if all grades and coset names are retained. The integer clock also remains
unbounded: a finite set of row formulas is a finite description, not a
claim that every entire live set belongs to a finite state space.

Cheapest hostile: A=(2,0), off-face seed (1,1). Adding two odd transverse
representatives creates carry 1 in the first-coordinate quotient. The
literal controls use both even and odd representatives and verify the
resulting affine endpoints. This is a coefficient-level carry with an
explicit map; no LRC coverage conclusion is transported.

## 5. July image pumps: labelled supply is stronger than total capacity

**THM-2198**, `01-canon/theorems/THM-2198-scalar-five-plus-three-image-pump-and-first-depth-exclusion.md`,
disintegrates a cover under x↦13x. Divisible blockers become whole-fibre
bits; unit masks remain labelled singleton/chord movies. Its global
capacity improvement uses each named mask's own conditional capacity;
five times the largest mask capacity gives false negative margins on
explicit rows. The static deficiency quotient cannot update endpoint
deletions without owner multiplicity and winding.

The actionable transfer here is to retain each coset's supply interval.
Adding the largest maxima from two arbitrary rows is legal only when
their grades/cosets match the target and the actual parents differ. For
two equal rows the two largest *different* values are required. The
one-unit correction in (1) of BOUNDARY_COMPILER.md is the exact local
capacity loss. The general compiler keeps representatives, carries,
minimum values, and each row's own affine endpoint; one aggregate maximum
cannot replace this labelled menu.
