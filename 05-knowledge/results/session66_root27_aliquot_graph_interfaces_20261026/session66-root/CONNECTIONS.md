# Session66: what actually transports across the five carriers

**Independently audited synthesis.** This document is not
an additional proof of root-27 survival or an exact-density result. Proof

## The unifying proposal: retain the data needed for the next operation

The common mechanism is not a universal tournament encoding of all open
problems. It is a test for when compression remains valid under continuation:

1. Specify the original object, exact operation and desired predicate.
2. Specify a compressed representation and an exact decoder or loss ledger.
3. Test the next operation, not just the current statistic.
4. Where the operation fails to descend, retain its missing source, sheet,
   alignment, weight, or signed defect. If that coordinate grows without a
   proved bound, the compression is an interface rather than a solution.

This is a proposed organizing lens, supported here by several exact maps and
several explicit counterexamples. It is not a new general complexity theorem.

## A. Root27: source can be preserved while fuel still refills

The original guarded process has scalar source7 and pending count1; its third
macro cut is the prior odd-x source27 with credit6. A balanced tree block P
has affine map `(3^L z+C_P)/2^L` and periodic center `r_P=-C_P/(3^L-2^L)`.
Pulling each center back through the actual prefix retains the original
source exactly. This is valid for arbitrary shape changes.

But after legal P and a switch to Q, equal 2-adic depths permit additional
cancellation against `Delta=C_Q D_P-C_P D_Q`. On the marked ray at macro86,
the naive fuel goes5 ->2 ->9. That is not cured by merely retaining the
source label. The signed defect and the source-dependent odd units must
survive too. A common center occurs exactly for powers of a common primitive
word; even there different lengths are different clocked operations.

The independent center-bank appendix adds two exact limits. A finite nonempty
bank closed under a nonempty family of inverse block maps must be one
common fixed center. Thus closure under every balanced block is impossible,
since their centers differ.
The bank of all balanced-block centers has supremum depth equal to the exact
q=1 legal lifetime, including infinity. Showing this supremum is infinite
at z=7 is exactly the original survival question; the identity gives no
independent precision bound.

The actual open obligation is to bound all switch refills along this one
source while proving full itinerary coverage and native guards. Fixed-shape
certificates and arbitrary-source counterexamples do not settle it.

## B. Modular tables: a real alternating interface, not an entire orbit

The recovered theorem alternates signed inverse fibers as valuation exponent
increases: `(n,b)->(2n+b,-b)`. It does not identify every odd-modulus table
with one full Collatz graph. The lifted coordinate must retain its sheet,
integer carry and the extra binary bit enforcing *exact* valuation.

The concurrent odd-modulus interpretation supplies a different exact theorem:
for odd m>1, the k-indexed minus sheet with a shift t equals the plus sheet
read from negated sources exactly when 2^t=-1 modulo m. The common positive
2-adic order criterion in the independent appendix explains why mod3 and
mod5 each alternate but mod15 does not. This table equivalence keeps k as a
separate parameter; it does not recover the native valuation from x modulo m.

The factorization14*15=210 produces a specific signed-port permutation;
it has seven cycles of length6 and fourteen of length12. These numbers
are derived from CRT and doubling orders, not inferred from a visual match.
Reducing to the two residue tables without one common sheet introduces
false ports. A common successor with a different sheet can have a different
continuation.

## C. Frobenius-style defect and clone compression: a quantified loss

Session65's general signed-pair theorem transfers to the specified port
permutation without claiming it is arithmetic Frobenius. The minimum
orientation defect is its21 even vertex cycles. Restricting antipodal pairs
to genuine homogeneous clone modules raises that minimum to77. Cross-module
edges carry weight4 and their quotient retains a new even-cycle defect;
forgetting it erases56 unavoidable disagreements.

A different, explicitly distinct carrier uses the divisors of210. Its full
cube has16 vertices, the proper-divisor set15, and the complement-stable
interior14. The interior has seven complementary pairs and minimum defect7.
Its numerically increasing orientation has defect91, not7. Deleting only
the top boundary breaks the complement permutation. The selected products
15=3*5 and14=2*7 are its unique closest complementary pair.

Thus21 and7 here are minima of declared orientation-defect problems. They
are not Hamiltonian-path counts, nor explanations of why those two values
are missing from the Hamiltonian spectrum. Confusing the observables would
make a false theorem, despite sharing numbers and tournament language.

## D. Graph pairs: faithful coding is possible, but alignment is not free

The7n-vertex joint graph pair retains an asymmetric frame and directional
ports; one shared isomorphism reconstructs the tournament exactly. Independent
isomorphisms lose the alignment. A9n-vertex single-graph code restores it
intrinsically, with an explicitly known extra leaf-permutation kernel in its
automorphism group.

The odd-cycle conflict graph Omega exposes a different invariant:
`H(T)=I(Omega(T),2)`. Pairing it with the faithful code retains both class
and statistic, but Omega can be very large. The exclusions7 and21 constrain
the image of Omega; arbitrary graphs K3 and P4 realize those very values.
Mere existence of an encoding is easier than characterizing its image.

Under the prescribed lifted graph action, the edge symmetric-difference
count is exactly twice the tournament orientation defect:
`|E(G) Delta E(sigma G)|=2 D_sigma(T)`. This is an actual bridge,
not a claim that arbitrary graph-edit optimization equals the native rank.

## E. Aliquot fibers: finite certificates, infinite coverage

The last user sequence consists of inverse fibers of the proper-divisor sum.
Each target m>1 has all sources at most (m-1)^2, so a missing fiber has a
finite certificate. More efficient known prime-extension and squarefull
splits preserve every candidate with their primality/coprimality guards.
The complete100000 census is a check, not a limiting-density inference.

For density, finite collections of affine prime-core families cover only
a zero-density set of targets. The core coordinate must grow. The certified
Chen–Zhao instantiation gives liminf U(X)/X > 0.060452936134095, with
a divisibility-monotone parameter refinement. It is useful without claiming
a literature record, existence, or the exact value of the natural density. A model
constant and an actual target-density limit remain different statements.

The F1-compatible object is the multiplicative prime monoid and its weighted
proper-divisor polynomial. Evaluation at the primes produces the aliquot
sum; evaluation at1 counts proper divisors. Equal aliquot fibers do not
form a multiplicative congruence, so their labels do not inherit field or
monoid multiplication. Prime weights are essential additive data.
