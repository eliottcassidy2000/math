### Defect carriers, clones, and graph pairs

The new odd-modulus table criterion is a different operation from the
inverse-port lift. For a prime power p^a, a shift exists iff the order of2
is even, and permitted shifts are congruent to half that order modulo the
order. Lifting from p to p^a changes the order by an odd p-power, so its
2-adic valuation is unchanged. CRT permits one shift exactly when those
positive valuations agree for every prime divisor. Then half the least
common multiple of the prime-power orders is a common shift. This proves
the advertised mod3/mod5/mod15 contrast, not a deterministic native
valuation on an odd residue class.

The 210 signed ports, the210 integer itself, and its14 interior divisors
are three different objects. The modular cycle census determines21
negative pair-orbits. The module restriction imposes a new quotient with
14 even cycles and weight4, hence77. Divisor complementation instead has
seven swaps, hence minimum7. The actual increasing numerical tournament
has91 reversed arcs. None of21,77,7,91 in these calculations is `H(T)`.

The executable decoders have a valid-image contract, not an arbitrary-graph
image-membership validation contract. This distinction is consistent with
the proved codecs and must survive reuse. The independent graph audit
provides stricter malformed-input controls separately.

The 7n joint codec and9n single-graph codec genuinely reconstruct the
tournament, but under different isomorphism notions. The first retains

`Omega` statistic-bearing coordinate may be extremely large and is not

restrictions are correctly retained in the current synthesis.

### Aliquot bounds, density, and F1

The complete-fiber bound is inclusive and sharp. The core/squarefull split
retains primality and coprimality where appropriate; importantly,
`n=u²v³` with squarefree v does **not** require `gcd(u,v)=1`. It proves a
candidate-universe bound rather than a unit-cost runtime theorem.

The exact100000 census and the Chen–Zhao lower bound have separate
dependency chains. The latter is an integer-certified instantiation of a
cited fixed-parameter theorem, normalized among all positive integers.
It neither establishes convergence of `U(X)/X` nor licenses choosing
`M=M(X)` inside its nonuniform error term. The model-limit existence
statement concerns a different observable. No best-in-literature claim
is supported or made.

The F1 bridge is elementary monoid-ring bookkeeping plus a weighted
evaluation, supported only at that scope by Deitmar. Its polynomial has
nonnegative coefficients after deletion of the top monomial. At n1 the
polynomial and both evaluations are zero. Divisibility inequalities imply
antichain fibers but not finite fibers; the prime fiber is the required
infinite hostile. Equal outputs fail multiplicative congruence, so there
is no inherited quotient-monoid or field multiplication on fiber labels.
