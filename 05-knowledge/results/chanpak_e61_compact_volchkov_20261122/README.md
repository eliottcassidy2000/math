# Exponent 61: compact strong classes, exact suffix restrictions, and the Volchkov bridge

**2026-11-22, euler.** Exact target: \(E_{61}=2658455991569831744654692615953842176\). The question concerns a literal single reversal between \(E_{61}-1\) and \(E_{61}+1\), not isolated endpoint representations or agreement in any modulus.

| Result | Scope |
|---|---|
| [A new strong 90-vertex lower witness](STRONG-90.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** A two-coordinate product of six small strong components, 33 singletons and a five-vertex quotient realizes \(E_{61}-1\). Reordering the interior gives **2,349,088,560** distinct strong classes. An exact scan after reducing marked contacts to their prefix/suffix profiles, combined with uniform proofs for the other arc types, excludes +2 in this whole family. |
| [91 and 93 vertices, and an all-slot collision](STRONG-93.md) | **PROVED.** Two more exact product grammars use the cyclic triangle quotient. The 93-vertex grammar gives **237,918,897,216,000** classes, all with the same unmarked slot profiles, and no +2 arc in any permutation. This is an actual failure mode of the unmarked pointed-monoid observer, not a failure of the marked arc read. |
| [The three surviving order-123 charts](ORDER-123.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** The triangular receiver is ruled out for every suffix length, for every odd exponent. For the singleton and four-vertex charts, no suffix with at most **nine runs**, at least **40 equal terminal bits**, or at least **44 initial zeros after the leading one** hits \(E_{61}-1\). Exact congruences are retained. The two arbitrary-address searches are **not exhausted**. |
| [Broader Fibonacci suffix tests](SPARSE-SUFFIX.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** Among all 610 normalized receivers through order nine, no suffix with at most five runs works. No width-50 terminal run with at most three arbitrary bit defects works. At total order 122 all six-run suffixes are also excluded. The new first/last-run quadratic leaves only the middle run lengths to search. |
| [Other bounded inverses](variable_receiver/README.md) and [nearby two-port hosts](OFFSET-NEXT.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** A uniform Mersenne bound cuts trailing ordinal receiver padding; the checked branch bank has no exact pair. Neither offset \(E_{61}-5\) nor \(E_{61}-9\) yields either endpoint in one arc, and no tested radius-four backbone gives the lower target at any positive two-port widths. |
| [Decimal parity as a 5-adic fold](DECIMAL-FOLD.md) | **PROVED.** Four numerical digits have exponent period **500** but their parity word has least period **100**. The general trailing-\(d\)-digit parity period is \(4\cdot5^{d-2}\) on eventual odd exponents. At \(p=61\) the center is **2176** and the endpoints end **2175/2177**. |
| [Volchkov, the local F1 mass, and literal \(\mathbb F_1\) geometry](VOLCHKOV-F1.md) | **CITED + PROVED.** The kernel coefficients and its cosine transform give a precise corrected Volchkov identity for the normalized F1 local density and the E8 shell mass. A small toric carrier has the corresponding shifted zeta function and commuting Frobenius lifts. The formally certified RH implies square-root cancellation up to \(\epsilon\) in the signed inverse local mass, not for the actual tournament words. |

**Still open:** the two unrestricted order-123 addresses that survive the stated tests, the existence of a single arc across \(E_{61}\), and Chan–Pak. No claim of minimum order is made for the 90-vertex construction. The compact-fiber result shows why a new lower witness alone is not enough: billions of genuinely different lower classes can be excluded by the same exact marked-profile test.
