quotient genus/Niemeier/eta; five-core → endpoint tensors and receiver renewal;

`catalog.py` searches a pinned current tree, not inaccessible conversations.
Its JSON separates lexical seeds from expansion-only documents and records
Git blob identities. This is broad retrieval, not a claim to read every hit.
Commit-message history is a distinct provenance sample. The inventory cannot
establish exhaustive historical session recovery: deleted versions, binary
attachments and untracked chats are outside its declared universe.

The existing October28 atlas had already scanned 15,962 tracked prose paths
(14,886 blobs), finding 13,235 digit/spelled-five hits. Session252 recovered
329 named-theme candidates, and the November10 five-core pass connected many

This taxonomy is the first safeguard for the proposed plane-stress bridge:
identify *which* five the theorem supplies, then seek a law or representation
that transports it. A five-parameter family and five generators are not
interchangeable merely because both have five in their description.

## 4. Three genuinely stronger bridges

### A. Five is not the bridge: a marked operator is

The Session91 integer identity is
`(L,2I+Adj(C5)) ≅ (Z²,A²) ⊕ (Z²,A²)`,
where `L={x∈Z⁵:Σx=0}` and `A=[[0,1],[1,1]]`. Its frame is unimodular
inside L. Adding the constant vector makes determinant−5, not±1.
Modulo5 the ambient operator's shifted nilpotent blocks are3+2, while its
augmentation restriction has2+2. Thus five simultaneously denotes a cycle
length, a splitting obstruction and a ramification prime, with actual maps
joining those roles. It does *not* follow that any five-parameter stress
system has that module. The candidate stress bridge must exhibit a common
operator, bilinear form or continuation rule, not just a1+2+2 dimension split.

### B. A finite alphabet generates more than a finite list

The old ready atlas has five C=2 tails but infinitely many prefixed sources.
Its neutral prefix operation commutes with the five-step diagram. The
reassociation pentagon of Session252 instead preserves the entire interface
because matrix multiplication is associative. These two pentagons preserve
different predicates. A connection to compatibility equations should test
which version is flat after retaining root, endpoints and amplitude.

### C. One family, genuinely different growth constants

The Session252 stem T_n has cycle-packing series
`ΣZ_n(x)z^n=(1−z²)/(1−z−z²−(x−1)z³)`.
At x=1 one obtains Fibonacci growth; x=2 gives actual Hamiltonian counts
with tribonacci growth. T6 has triangle-overlap spectral radius φ and H=17.
This is a concrete common carrier for golden and Fermat observations, but
not a claim that H is Fibonacci or that all later stem counts are Fermat.
The transferable principle is to vary the observer on a fixed carrier,
then see which governing equation changes, rather than compare unrelated
lists of output numbers.

## 5. Proposition G: the constant-mode obstruction exists at every n

**PROVED, elementary; no external novelty claim.** Let
`L_n={x∈Z^n:Σx_i=0}`, `c=(1,...,1)`, and `M_n=Zc+L_n`. Then

    Z^n/M_n ≅ Z/nZ,        [Z^n:M_n]=n.                 (G1)

Proof: coordinate sum modulo n is onto Z/nZ, and its kernel is exactly M_n:
if Σx=nq, then x−qc∈L_n. The columns
`c,e1−en,...,e(n−1)−en` consequently have determinant±n.
Over a field K, `Kc⊕kerΣ=K^n` iff char(K) does not divide n. If char(K)|n,
c lies in kerΣ and the proposed sum fails to span K^n.

If an integral matrix B has every column sum d, then it preserves L_n and
induces multiplication by d on Z^n/M_n, provided it also preserves Zc
(for example, B has every row sum d). This is immediate from Σ(Bx)=dΣx.

The original C5 result uses much more than G1: cyclic action and its exact
Fibonacci-block identity. G1 explains only the constant/augmentation index.
It is therefore an honest extension from five to all numbers, without
pretending all n have golden eigenvalues. Its decisive tests are integral
index and reduction at primes dividing n, not a decimal coincidence.

## 6. Four hypotheses worth paying for

**H1: bounded-defect flat completion (OPEN).** Seek a specified family of
rooted tournament operations with a full interface state and finite local
rewrite relations, whose closed relation loops act identically on that
state. Require a terminating, height-controlled normal form. Hypothesis:
a bounded defect extension of the current ready-tail chart admits such a
presentation. The current holonomy lane now proves complete finite local relation schemas
for its marked partial-permutation family. This settles that restricted
coherence problem, not the bounded-defect ready-chart conjecture here. A local
coherence theorem without termination, guards and target height is not
Chan–Pak. Cheapest test: compare complete matrices on two competing
normalization paths, including seam coordinates.

**H2: rank-stratified tournament bridge (OPEN; stress premise now PROVED).**
The current stress theorem proves local dimension five and global dimensions
two through five under its explicit hypotheses. Fixing a tournament pattern
may analogously leave a finite
response space. Compute the latter's Hankel rank on actual legal continuations
and compare the rank-drop locus under deformation, not the number of seeds.
Prediction: any useful five-parameter bridge appears as matching operator
relations after quotienting unobservable directions, rather than a bijection
of five objects. The null test is two five-dimensional carriers with
incompatible minimal polynomials or symmetry characters.

**H3: geometric data separate the H9 collision (TESTABLE, NOT PROVED).**
Triangle-by-arc incidence plus endpoint markings may predict the first
continuation distinction between the two H9 cores more economically than
full adjacency. The known arc-overlap graphs A3 versus affineA2 separate
that pair, but may not separate larger examples. Group cores by the proposed
incidence invariant and compare their legal continuation languages; the first
collision determines the missing sidecar.

**H4: all-number mechanism profiles (RESEARCH PROGRAM).** For each integer n,
record the objects, natural equivalence relation, action, quotient map,
parameter dimension, integral index, birth threshold, and deformation law
where n occurs. Compare profiles by actual commuting diagrams. The prediction
is not that equal n has a common cause, but that verified bridges cluster by
mechanism (finite exception, Fourier mode, support overlap, extension index,
finite observation rank). `G1` supplies a universal index cluster; p^3-group
classification and Schläfli classification belong to different clusters.

The productive unification is **compatibility before scalarization**:
construct the constrained object, retain the response needed for its next
operation, and only then read the number. This is a method with precise
instances, not an equivalence of the open problems.

## 7. Current-session junctions: compare maps, not just dimensions

The [stress theorem](../stress/PROOF.md) identifies its five real parameters
as Hermitian 2-by-2 amplitude coefficients (four) plus a pressure constant
(one). The chosen nonvanishing holomorphic chart remains arbitrary: five
parameters do not mean the curves have only five kinds. The
[period theorem](../bridges/PERIOD_FIBERS.md) then removes zero through three
real amplitude directions by a period operator, leaving global dimensions
two through five. This gives a precise model of **fixed local carrier,
arbitrarily complicated background, finite-rank global obstruction**.

Compare the old C5 Fourier frame carefully: its rational dimensions also
split 1+4, but its integral constant/augmentation splitting has index five,
and characteristic five records extension data. The stress split is real
linear and has no implied integral lattice. An actual bridge would specify
an operator intertwiner and a chosen arithmetic structure; dimension alone
does not supply it. Likewise the tournament annihilator `(2,-5,2)` has roots
2 and 1/2, not the golden pair. The golden C5 frame cannot be substituted
for that recurrence merely because both contain five.

The [current tail construction](../holonomy/PROOF.md) yields partial
permutations, with counts 1,2,5,16,65,... and stellohedral rather than purely
associahedral growth. The first five-state pentagon is compatible with both
pictures, but the next count sixteen distinguishes it from the Catalan
fourteen. Thus **the first shared small polygon is not the recursive law**.
Squares, pentagons and hexagons carry different local commutation/coherence
relations. Test the next layer's marked incidence before assigning a
universal grammar.

These four sources unexpectedly converge on a sharper principle than
“everything is five”: **a low-dimensional observation becomes a reusable
construction only when its kernel is harmless for the next operation**.
The proposed research object is therefore a pair `(response space, legal
continuation congruence)`. Two witnesses are equivalent only if every legal
continuation has the same required readout. Search for a finite presentation
of this congruence, not merely a finite table of current counts. No finite
presentation or Chan–Pak completeness is claimed in this packet.
