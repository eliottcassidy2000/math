# Compatible-state fibers: a proposed common language

**Status: SYNTHESIS / RESEARCH PROGRAM.** The propositions cited below have
specified scopes. The vocabulary here does not prove a universal theorem
about all mathematics, and no numerical coincidence is promoted to a map.

## 1. The background, carrier and section must be separated

A useful research object is the quadruple

    (fixed background B, continuation carrier V_B,
     compatibility equations K_B, physical realization set P_B).

An observer π_B then reads a scalar or a coarser structure. Two different
questions follow:

- **Closure:** can a finite carrier describe every lawful continuation of the
  chosen family? A recurrence or a flat connection can answer this.
- **Selection:** does P_B meet the fiber π_B^{-1}(target), with the required
  initial and terminal conditions? Closure alone does not answer this.

The stress example fixes an arbitrary isothermal net h. Its compatibility
kernel has four amplitude modes, plus a trace anchor. The variable h is not
one of those five parameters. Allowing h to vary is an infinite-dimensional
union of fibers, not a single five-dimensional description of all stress.
The stress lane's two-net decomposition illustrates a productive escape:
change the background rather than attempt to enlarge one insufficient fiber.

The tournament example fixes a deletion tournament. Its root attachments
live in an affine-quadratic five-coordinate carrier after other quantities
are fixed. The physical realization set is a Boolean cube image. In a
transitive core, one continuation coordinate uniquely encodes every root
cut; continuous compatible directions therefore provide no nontrivial
physical moves. In a vertex-transitive core, symmetry gives equal-cardinality
cuts identical linear responses, so a genuine physical compatible fiber
exists. Its scalar count is still a quadratic energy, not an arbitrary digit.

**Proposed name: compatibility-generated memory.** It is a useful exact
mechanism when compatibility closes a finite jet or response state. It does
not mean that every combinatorial family has bounded memory.

## 2. The local-to-global bill is an operator, not a slogan

For stresses the bill is a real period map on three parameters. Its kernel
has the actual global solutions. For tail diagrams the bill is the defect
around square/pentagon relations. Those relations suffice for all loops of
the labelled partial-permutation graph; their quantitative filling law
bounds accumulated approximate defects. In each case loop information
restores what a locally valid formula alone forgets.

The two systems have different holonomy. Stress continuation acts by a
rank-one shear of the pressure anchor and factors through homology. A
tournament's scalar H can lose continuation information before a loop is
even formed: equal H at two vertices need not imply equal H after appending
the same tail letter. The full-state graph is indispensable.

**Proposed name: the closure-defect ledger.** For a proposed recursive
construction, record a finite generating set of local relations, their
full-state defects, and a proof that every global ambiguity is generated by
them. A pretty pentagon without that last proof is only an illustration.

## 3. How to study a number deeply without assigning it a universal essence

For each occurrence of n, record its mathematical type, generator, gauge,
and perturbation law. Useful questions are:

1. Is n a dimension, a cardinality, an index, a birth order, a cutoff or an
   observer choice?
2. Which theorem forces it, and which input can be varied?
3. Which action or polynomial makes two occurrences share a carrier?
4. What changes at n+1, at a larger modulus, or after forgetting the marking?
5. Can the shared object transport a construction rather than just a count?

The recovered C5 integral frame is a particularly good example: five is
simultaneously cycle length and the index obstructing an integral split of
constant and augmentation modes. That is a real bridge through an explicit
lattice. The stress dimension five has a different cause, Hermitian rank
four plus one integration anchor. The tail pentagon has five vertices only
at k=2; its next partial-permutation graph has sixteen. These distinctions
make the web richer, not weaker.

## 4. Concrete next hypotheses, with falsifiers

These are deliberately stronger research questions, not conclusions.

**P1 — physical relation completion.** Find a family of rooted tournament
moves with finite full-state relations, a decidable positive integral
realization set, and a target observer whose fibers have a constructive
section. The present tail graph supplies the relation side, not the target
section. A count quotient that fails under a common suffix falsifies a
candidate state representation immediately.

**P2 — orbit-energy interval growth.** Seek a sequence of symmetric deletion
cores and cardinality layers in which weighted cut energies contain growing
consecutive intervals, with continuation coordinates held fixed. The
vertex-transitive fiber theorem supplies the exact energy and response
constraints. Symmetry alone is not the conjecture: the tournament lane's
small interval test must distinguish actual gaps from sufficient extra
hypotheses. An interval that cannot be transported while keeping its
receiver is not a Chan–Pak construction.

**P3 — controlled background changes.** Instead of forcing one core family
to realize every target, seek finite rules changing the deletion core while
preserving a sufficiently rich continuation carrier and improving an
absolute-target potential. This is inspired by two-net stress decomposition,
not implied by it. The map must be a genuine tournament operation; formal
addition of count functions or convex mixtures does not qualify.

**P4 — integral obstruction modules.** For a candidate invariant subspace,
compute its integral saturation index, not just its real dimension. The
C5 frame's index5 and the stress period kernel suggest two different bills:
arithmetic lattice membership and global gluing. A rational solution that
lies outside the physical Boolean image is a decisive hostile control.

The near-term constructive goal is an operation that closes all four bills:
local state, global relation, integral realization, absolute target. None of
the present fives alone pays them all. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.
