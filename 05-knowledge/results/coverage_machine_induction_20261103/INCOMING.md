## 3 The polarized bin calculus is a quotient of the marked cover carrier

The concurrent [polarized calculus](../polarized_path_calculus_20261103/CALCULUS.md)
retains three polynomials P(m),E(m),S(m), with the first bin unrestricted,
ending at the root, or starting there respectively. It is an actual
one-root operation calculus, including a two-source reflection seam and
both newborn-root polarities. Its notes explicitly inherit the prior
graded-carrier mechanism and the S159 cyclic operator.

There is a direct projection from our two-port cover vector. Fix port0 as
the root. For a cover signature (s,d), put k=|s|+d and let c_(s,d) be its
coefficient. Then

    P(m)=sum_(s,d) c_(s,d) (m)_k,
    E(m)=sum_(s,d) c_(s,d) [port0 is an end] (m-1)_(k-1),
    S(m)=sum_(s,d) c_(s,d) [port0 is a start] (m-1)_(k-1).

This follows by assigning the k components injectively to bins. In E or S
the root component is forced to bin1, leaving k-1 assignments. It preserves
all three incoming polynomial observations and discards the second port's
joint endpoint information. We retain the two-port carrier for the present
operation language; no minimum claim follows for either
representation.

The accompanying [unique-start funnels](../polarized_path_calculus_20261103/README.md)
classify a genuine recursive source family with class series
x^3/(1-3x+x^2). These coefficients count rooted classes, not H values.
Their source condition supplies F-H=1 and hence a3H+2 return, while
two-start possibilities expose another mixed-port term. This is directly
useful source-selection structure. It does not assert reusable sources
of both types at every requested H.
