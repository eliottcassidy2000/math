# Scope and validity review

**Same-session proof audit, with independent computational implementations.**
This is not a claim of review by a separate agent or an external referee.

* T1 is an all-order congruence for same-size modules. The skeleton
  coefficients are integers and their one-run coefficient is H(Q), odd.
  Port vertices must copy the module's exterior incidences; arbitrary core
  attachments within the module remain allowed. No exact division of an
  integer response is asserted.
* T2 divides the actual even difference of two odd counts by two before
  reducing. The support-sector maps are linear, not evaluations at z=1.
  Singleton paths are retained. The paired-path product is present and its
  degree-four terms cancel only in the stated converse projection.
* T3 removes the flipped arc from each created/destroyed odd cycle. The
  remaining path has odd vertex count and cannot contain that mutual edge.
  Both path directions are required. This is equivalent to the old skew
  gate, not a stronger condition by itself.
* T4 concerns the whole named core and its converse, not arbitrary module
  reversals in a fixed asymmetric exterior. A port with all old attachment
  bits zero is not always a universal source: c=0 still leaves x->y. The
  proof handles that specialization algebraically. General pairs genuinely
  retain quartic differences: order-five codes1023 and1021 have coefficients
  4 and2 on a_0 a_1 c_1 c_4, checked by32 actual queries in audit.json.
* T5 tracks one distinguished endpoint slot and includes both possible
  skeletons. It descends the combined necessary conditions through C3[M,1,1];
  it is not an iff for full C symmetry or full response equality.
* The twelve-vertex prime/pair split uses all modules, including nested ones.
  The no-size4/8 step comes from complete inherited banks, and the no-size10
  step uses the prior complete combined census. The order-six survivor's
  isomorphism to the three-pair cyclic inflation is checked by a literal
  permutation. Thus a nested pair remains an actual homogeneous pair.

The five normal/optimized receipt pairs agree. Integer endpoint/cover DP,
literal Hamilton words and cuts, literal odd cycles, squarefree path
multiplication, and the compiled parity engine cover different failure modes.
The96 compiled-engine controls include both true and false cover tests,
the twelve-vertex endpoint-parity survivor, and the two shared-cubic-zero
Paley extensions. Finite controls corroborate proofs; they are not used
to extrapolate universality or independence of the new congruence.

The frozen verifier checks five receipt pairs, the complete packet inventory,
