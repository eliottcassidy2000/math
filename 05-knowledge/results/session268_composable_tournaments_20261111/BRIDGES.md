## 1. Behavioral equivalence has an exact physical test set

The modular compiler proves that equal full ordered path-cover profiles remain
equal under every uniform substitution context. The spectrum lane proves the
converse: H(T) and the contexts C3[TT_d,TT_d,T], d=1,...,n−1, recover every
coefficient by triangular inversion, with diagonal(d!)².

So this is not merely a convenient feature vector. It is exactly the
behavioral equivalence relation for integer Hamiltonian counts under the
specified class of operations. It can merge ordinary isomorphism classes,
provided their multiplicity and automorphism mass are retained. It is not a
fixed-dimensional state as order increases.

The two inherited five-vertex bits have equal full profile. No composition
of uniform substitutions can reveal their difference, regardless of context
size. The automata lane breaks one cross-module arc, so the operation leaves
that equivalence category. Rooted two-path covers then reveal a mixed term−8.
The successful new interaction is therefore explained by the precise
information the old interface forbids it from consuming.

## 2. Equal present values need not have equal futures

On the interacting CA family, present H is determined by the counts of
adjacent00,01,10,11 pairs. For a radius-r CA, the next pair depends on a
length2r+2 block; after t steps it depends on a length2rt+2 initial block.
Thus a sufficiently long block inventory predicts the horizon-t H exactly.
The needed window grows with the horizon, unless a specific rule has extra
closure. This is not a claim that a fixed pair histogram closes all rules.

The Rule110 words101000 and100100 have the same H88902740880625,
but their next H values are76567725723825 and77701668671025. This is an
explicit failure of scalar-state dynamics inside a fully classified infinite
family, not a conjectural chaos argument.

The full ordered SCC word remains an exact state at every time. A localized
change has a finite CA light cone in the ordered-component geometry even
though H is a global number and the underlying tournament is complete.

## 3. A literal one-dimensional partition function

The interacting-family weights form

    W = [215 179; 245 201].

For N>=1 the sum of H over all2^N ordinary classes on10N vertices is

    Z_N = trace(W^N).

This is the standard periodic one-dimensional nearest-neighbor transfer
construction applied to the **actual** tournament class family. It introduces
no Gibbs assumption into the unweighted class count. The pair factors come
from exact rooted-seam Hamiltonian enumeration, not fitted energies.

Here trace W=416 and det W=−640. Hence, setting Z_0=2 for the transfer identity,

    Z_1=416,
    Z_N=416 Z_(N−1)+640 Z_(N−2),
    Z_N=(208+56 sqrt(14))^N+(208−56 sqrt(14))^N.

The N=0 convention is an auxiliary matrix identity, not two empty tournament
classes. For example Z_2=174336. The ordinary uniform-class mean is Z_N/2^N.
More generally, replacing W_ab by W_ab^s gives the exact real moment sum
sum_[T] H(T)^s. Formal pair markers recover the closed multiplicities in the
automata proof. Thus the integer distribution, the transfer matrix, and the
class inventory are three representations of the same object.

The negative determinant supplies a signed alternating finite-size spectral
correction. The appearance of sqrt(14) is the discriminant of this specific
physical2×2 interaction, not a claim that the repository's other14-themed
problems share its dynamics.

Crucially this is a recurrence in **spatial size N after summing all classes**.
It is not a recurrence for the H-value of one chosen CA trajectory in time.
The previous section gives a concrete counterexample to that conflation.

## 4. Simple parity clocks and hard integer arithmetic can coexist

The dynamics lane splits regular odd lexicographic tournaments into internal
zero-sum modes and a parity-shifted quotient. Every C3/Paley7 word has only
fixed points and eventual three-cycles over F2, with explicit multiplicities.
This can be evaluated in word length while the graph grows multiplicatively.

It is a different quotient from the path-cover response. One preserves linear
parity trajectories; the other preserves integer Hamiltonian observations in
uniform contexts. Neither licenses merging classes in the other's inventory.
The supplied module word is a safe common carrier on which both can be computed.

## 5. Why this does not yet close the arithmetic spectrum

The spectrum lane provides actual strong families

    H(C3[1,TT_b,T])=(H(T)+q_2(T))2^b+H(T)−q_2(T),

and exact target membership tests for each fixed seed. It also proves that
arbitrary products of all singleton-transitive atoms
2+(2^b−1)(2^c−1) still cover only exp(O((log X)^(2/3))) values below X.

The CA family has exponentially many classes but its counts stay in a fixed
finitely generated multiplicative semigroup. It solves a structural counting
problem, not scalar density. Infinite families, enormous class populations,
modular controllability and fast evaluation do not supply target-directed
integer constructions by themselves.

The precise remaining goal is to find rooted operations with an invariant
physical state domain, an inverse/target-selection rule, and a decreasing
integer height or a proved finite covering certificate. The seam law's
subtractive endpoint term is a concrete place to search; its physical state
constraints cannot be replaced by freely chosen integers. That is a proposed
route, not an assumed missing lemma or a reformulated claim of success.
