# Session242 synthesis: descend the missing object, not its shadow

**Audited research synthesis. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

## Inheritance before novelty

The closest previous mechanisms were the exact Pythagorean/AP correspondence,
congruent square-gap theorem, THM-4094's prime/7p reduction, Session229's finite
all-gadget inverse and Session241's exact but sparse two-burst decoder. We
recovered those mechanisms before building on them. In particular the atom
classification and {3,5,7} closure are inherited; the numerical values used
as new suppliers were already known attainable. The present contribution is
a cleaner arithmetic map, an additional physical inverse obstruction and a
fully explicit finite-bank cost theorem. No historical priority is asserted.

Anchor: counterexample-preserving Chan–Pak descent. Niche: the exact roles of
12,31,41,49 in congruent class 5. Wildcard: physical normalization of gadget
content, as opposed to rational normalization of an elliptic witness.

## The live concepts compared after the tests

1. **Rational square AP.** The scale 12 is necessary and minimal. Congruence
   filters select a possible denominator; the explicit triple supplies
   existence. Necessary residue conditions are not alone a construction.
2. **Elliptic point.** The direct square map is a signed double of the triangle map.
   This matters: the same Q passes one chart's positivity test and fails the
   other. Q can be halved once but not twice. Halving preserves the curve but
   does not remain in the direct-square image locus.
3. **Primitive Pythagorean tree.** Parent height strictly decreases, yet its
   first move changes area squareclass 5→21 and leaves positive graph labels.
   It is a valid ambient descent and an invalid fixed-predicate descent.
4. **Missing tournament count.** There is no tournament to delete at a missing
   target. One must lower the existential target or an actual obstruction
   certificate. This is why a recursion on already supplied graphs is not a
   minimal-counterexample proof of spectrum completeness.
5. **Physical rooted response.** A graft needs more than the count H. The
   source state and gadget pair must be actual realizable data. At normalized
   cofactor 7 the pair is forced to the physical TT2 gadget, giving a genuine
   contradiction. At content 7, the surviving pair (7,116) is nonphysical, so
   blind gcd stripping is expressly forbidden.
6. **Universal ordinal join.** This constructor works for every witness of a
   smaller count. That fact closes the entire declared smooth sector by a
   genuine failure-preserving descent. Its finite supplier list is exactly
   what prevents a global conclusion. The cost minimization then has a
   separate and exact three-step seven-adic period.

## A sharply stated remaining proof program

The inherited reduction says that physical suppliers for every odd prime
p≠7 and every 7p with p≠3, together with fixed carries, would settle the
admissible odd spectrum. The finite smooth sector does not supply infinitely
many such atoms.

There are two honest next targets:

- **Universal lifting on an enlarged state.** Specify a predicate P(n,s) on
  count and rooted response, prove base states, show every target has a
  smaller admissible predecessor state, and prove its exact physical lift.
  A numeric predecessor satisfying inequalities is insufficient. If the
  stronger predicate loses a desired scalar, the induction has not covered
  Chan–Pak.
- **Restricted-lane predecessor existence.** On primes restrict to gcd-one
  gadgets. On 7p restrict to contents 1 or 7. Use the exact source-state
  oracle only as a feasibility test until an all-target nonempty-fiber
  theorem is proved. The strong examples 791 and 1673 show that neither permitted
  stratum can be discarded by this necessary filter. They do not prove that
  both strata are indispensable for covering all scalar targets.

The difference between these targets and a search slogan is explicit:
rank, base cases, failure preservation, domain closure and a witness-producing
lift must all be supplied. The current packet establishes all five on its
smooth sector and only necessary filters on the infinite prime lanes.

## Rejected shortcuts and strongest survivors

- **Repeated elliptic halving proves descent:** false for the proposed point;
  Q∈2E but Q∉4E. Survivor: exact membership and a finite halving obstruction.
- **Pythagorean parent stays in class 5:** false at its first move. Survivor:
  height descent in the larger Pythagorean tree, with the missing class/chamber
  coordinates named.
- **Divide tournament data by gcd:** false without a physical quotient gadget.
  Survivor: the legal normalization lemma and cofactor-seven theorem.
- **Strip any attained factor:** false at 35/5=7 and 63/3=21. Survivor:
  admissibility-preserving carries 35,49,63,217,287,343.
- **Greedy physical descent is size optimal:** false at 7^6. Survivor:
  a full fixed-bank minimization and exact eventual cost period.
- **A finite physical inverse implies coverage:** not proved. Survivor:
  a terminating membership test for that constructor's image.

The connection contract is therefore a transfer of proof architecture, with
one concrete application on the tournament side, not an unproved map from
congruent numbers to all Hamiltonian-path counts. No new unrestricted finite
coverage cutoff, all-order quotient-21 exclusion, or global minimum-order
claim is made. Session242 has ended.
