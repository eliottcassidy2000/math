# Next constructive obligations

All questions below are OPEN. Chan–Pak and Collatz remain open.

1. **Compress the binary controller.** F5 alone needs O(2^k) vertices to control
   k slope bits. Can a small positive alphabet keep the same exact-sign and
   lower-digit guarantees with O(k) or polynomial(k) vertices? Test physical
   F3/F5 words, not formal inverses or2-adic projections.
2. **Control ordinary height.** Can the source for a target residue be selected
   near its least positive representative? The explicit7 mod64 witness has
   slope1,570,759. Modular transitivity does not close this gap.
3. **Repeat the physical step2.** The common context of the Fano twins gives
   1769 and1771. Find a family of input states for which that same two-channel
   reading changes H by2 while preserving a state reusable by the next step.
   The current pair has different U,V after consumption, so iteration is not
   supplied by the scalar equality.
4. **Separate a length channel physically.** The positive packet2w²+w⁷ and its
   binomial amplifiers have real endpoint consumers. Can one isolate the unit
   seventh-length channel as an unconditioned scalar output? A Fourier
   coefficient or signed Bézout identity is not such a constructor.
5. **Make the shadow quotient compositional.** W is complete for the SCC
   skeleton but fails for selective apex insertion. Identify the least finite
   rooted sidecar for a useful larger operation grammar. The existing(U,d)
   suffices for one apex; the Session264 carrier suffices for shared-root words.
6. **Lift the exceptional primes.** The new proof excludes5 and761 from the
   all-prime-power full-state theorem. A finite mod5 orbit is not a5-adic
   argument. Seek a different affine clock or a direct first-digit lift.

The exact electrical inequalities are potential pruning tools for inverse
shadow searches, but a feasible first-moment matrix need not have a compatible
family of distinct directed paths. Keep that realization obligation explicit.
