# Unit-distance seams and exact planar contact folds

**2026-11-22, euler.** The common question is which data an inverse or a geometric drawing has to retain. Here two different notions of a unit edge, and two different notions of local invertibility, can be compared on actual tournaments instead of schematic examples.

| Result | Scope |
|---|---|
| [The literal planar fold](CONTACT-FOLDS.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** Every two-contact cut face has a complete bilinear normal form and an exact square-injectivity criterion. Two nonisomorphic **marked pairs** give the same 115/117 unit seam at opposite masks with nonzero Jacobians. A contact face's two Hamilton reads cannot be its planar equal-edge coordinates. |
| [The marked Jacobian identity](MARKED-JACOBIAN.md) | **PROVED.** Canonical pinned contacts give two determinants differing by \(-w^2\), where \(w\) is the invariant rooted weight of the reversed pair. This yields a formal prime-adic inverse and a positive area of expected joint readings, not arbitrary Boolean realizability. |
| [Unit-distance port gadgets from literal seams](UNIT-PORTS.md) | **PROVED.** Subdivided tournament cubes give rational planar, distance-two soft amplifiers with arbitrary independent arms and exact Jacobsthal color counts. Kempe switching fixes the sign of the bias. A strong seven-vertex height star defeats any planar unit-edge drawing that assigns Hamilton count by a linear coordinate. |
| [Shears, a physical seam cube, and an induced spindle](SPINDLE-CHARTS.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** Eight strong labelled tournaments give a literal ±2 cube with two rational planar frameworks related by a determinant-one polynomial shear, but their nonedge metric changes. The strong order-six unlabelled full flip graph contains an induced Moser spindle with 11 geometric edges, only four of them literal ±2. |
| [Relabelling holonomy](LABEL-COVER.md) | **PROVED.** The even-relabelling quotient is the canonical bipartite double cover of the ordinary flip quotient. Closing an odd unlabelled walk requires an odd relabelling; the independent Hamilton-height parity forces an odd number of zero-mod-four tolls. |
| [A new crossed-contact family](TWO-APEX.md) | **PROVED + FINITE-EXACT.** Adjacent opposite discordances reduce the signed ±2 condition to one comparison of weighted binary flanks. A strong nested construction attains every midpoint residue divisible by 32 at any specified dyadic precision, yet no member is an exact Euclid midpoint. The complete finite crossed-contact inverse finds no exact midpoint at exponents 3–20 in that architecture. |

The [Hadwiger–Nelson six-color lower bound](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-Euclidean-plane-is-not-five-colorable-September-23-2026/paper.pdf) leaves six versus seven. The [Isabelle verification of the complex Keller counterexample](https://isa-afp.org/entries/Jacobian_Counterexample.html) starts in dimension three; the planar complex conjecture remains open. The statements here concern specified cut maps or specified seam graphs, not solutions to either global problem. Neither Chan–Pak nor the exact exponent-61 flip is settled.
