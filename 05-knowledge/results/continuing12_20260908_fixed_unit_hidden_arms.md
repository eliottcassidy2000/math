# Identical distinguished Weyl modules hide arbitrarily many source torsion arms

**Status: PROVED ANALYTICALLY + FINITE-EXACT; INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED.**
On one fixed surface W2, the examples below have exactly the same pointed
Weyl module generated by the distinguished unit, but arbitrarily large
complete torsion modules. This is a limitation of an entire observable
module, not just of its highest pole order. It gives no polynomial mate
or general Jacobian-conjecture conclusion.

## 1. Inheritance, map and theorem

The closest mechanism is the coefficient-span lemma in
[the continuing11 quadratic family](continuing11_20260908_quadratic_family.md).
There scalar order two failed to distinguish a one-arm unit from a two-arm
unit. The present question retains ALL multiplication and canonical
connection operations on the unit, and asks whether the entire resulting
pointed module determines the ambient torsion. The answer is no.

The complete torsion and its canonical connection are inherited from
[the source torsion theorem](planar_jc48_sep06_torsion.md), using **THM-3412**,
`01-canon/theorems/THM-3412-hamiltonian-principal-part-differential-and-prufer-torsion-arms.md`,

Their hypotheses are paid below: a polynomial submersion, rational constant
field C(F), every source fibre component, and the original response ring.
No external novelty claim is made for the inherited torsion theory.

The earlier all-m linear family varied the surface to obtain unbounded

The corrected near miss is confusing the module generated by a marked

Fix nonzero b,d,h in C and an integer n>=3. On the original source put

    u=x-h, z=u-2h+u^3 t, y=u z, W=u z^2,
    K(W)=b+dW^n,
    F=g=u z K(W)=b y+d y^(2n+1)/u^n.                 (1)

The last expression is polynomial: its second term is d u^(n+1)z^(2n+1).
Its source-t degree is exactly2n+1. Define

    S_n(W)=sum_(m=0)^5 binom(5,m)d^m b^(5-m) W^(nm)/(nm+3),
    H_n(W)=W^3 S_n(W),
    G=S_n(W)/(u^3 K(W)^6)=H_n(W)/g^6.              (2)

**Theorem.** F is a global submersion on fixed W2 and J_(x,t)(F,G)=1.
Its zero fibre in the ORIGINAL affine source has n+2 reduced disjoint
irreducible components; every other source fibre is irreducible. In

    C_F=C[x,t]/D_F C[x,t],
    D_F=F_x partial_t-F_t partial_x,

the complete C[g]-torsion has n+1 principal-part arms. The unit theta=[1]
has exact scalar annihilator(g^6), generates exactly TWO full torsion arms
under the canonical Weyl algebra, and has the exact pointed presentation
given in section6. At fixed b,h, that presentation is independent of n AND d.

The map under examination takes (C_F,theta) to its generated submodule

## 3. Exact rational mate and every fibre

In the actual (u,z) chart, J_(x,t)(u,z)=u^3. A direct bracket calculation
gives J_(u,z)(g,W)=W K(W). The polynomial identity

    H_n'(W)=W^2 K(W)^5                              (3)

follows term by term from (2) and the binomial theorem. Therefore

    D_F G=g^-6 H_n'(W) D_F W
         =g^-6 W^2K^5 u^3 W K=1,

since g^6=u^6z^6K^6 and u^3W^3=u^6z^6. This proves the actual rational
mate without assuming formal integration algebraizes. Also
g^6G=u^3z^6 S_n(W) is an actual polynomial in C[x,t].

At g=0 the components are E_u={u=0}, E_z={z=0}, and one E_rho={W=rho}
for each of the n distinct nonzero roots rho of rho^n=-b/d. The line u=0
is irreducible. The z factor is primitive linear in t because h!=0.
For every rho, W-rho is primitive quadratic in t, with discriminant
4 rho u^7; the odd u-valuation makes it nonsquare in C(u), hence
irreducible. The components are pairwise disjoint: z at u=0 is -2h,
while W-rho is -rho on u=0 and on z=0; different rho factors cannot
vanish together. The distinct factors are reduced.

For c!=0 the fibre g=c lies wholly in the torus u*z*K!=0. Its exact
coordinate presentation is

    u=c^2/(W K(W)^2), z=W K(W)/c,
    O(F^-1(c) intersect U0)=C[W,W^-1,K(W)^-1].       (4)

This is the whole original-source fibre, not only a dense open subset:

Thus all nonzero fibres are irreducible, and each is P1 minus n+2 points.
Similarly C(x,t)=C(g)(W), with the same rational inverse, and D_F W is

The boundary maps nonconstantly to the target, so source and global fibres

## 6. Exact pointed Weyl presentation in arbitrary operator degree

Let D=C<g,partial>/(partial g-g partial-1), with partial the canonical
connection. In the two-arm model let delta_A=A/g and delta_B=B/g. For
0<=j<=5 define polynomials in T by

    A_j(T)=(-1)^(5-j) T^(5-j)/(5-j)!,
    B_0(T)=T^2/2-lambda T, B_1(T)=-T+lambda,
    B_2(T)=1, B_3=B_4=B_5=0.

Then g^j theta=A_j(partial)delta_A+B_j(partial)delta_B. Columns j=2 and5
form the matrix [[-T^3/6,1],[1,0]], of determinant-1. They are therefore
a basis over C[T] of the two free principal-part derivative towers.

For j in{0,1,3,4} set

    R_j=g^j-B_j(partial)g^2
           -(A_j(partial)+B_j(partial)partial^3/6)g^5.

The EXACT left annihilator of theta is

    I=D g^6 + sum_(j in {0,1,3,4}) D R_j.           (8)

Each generator annihilates theta by the displayed columns. Conversely,
PBW normal ordering modulo Dg^6 writes any operator as sum_(j=0)^5
p_j(partial)g^j. The four R_j reduce it to p(partial)g^2+q(partial)g^5.
If this acts as zero, its delta_B coefficient forces p=0, and its delta_A
coefficient then forces q=0. Derivatives of1/g are linearly independent
in characteristic zero. This proves both inclusions in (8), with no
degree bound. Thus (D theta,theta) is exactly(D/I,[1]) for every n,d at
fixed b,h, while the quotient of ambient torsion by D theta has n-1 arms.
