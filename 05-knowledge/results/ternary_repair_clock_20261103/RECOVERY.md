## Incoming Session154 strengthens the boundary

The source frontier advanced while the finite experiments ran. The following
incoming results are credited and not rederived as new results here:

- [Seven-adic lifting](../session154_gadgets_20261103/golden/SEVEN_ADIC.md)
  proves that the inherited pure-q2 family reaches every residue modulo
  every 7^e, with explicit regular phase lifts. Our residue-preserving repairs
  concern different integer sources and do not extend that congruence theorem.
- [Synchronized 7/19 clocks](../session154_gadgets_20261103/crt719/CRT.md)
  retain the common index modulo 3 and prove compatible all-power lifting.
- [The mixed-prefix orbit](../session154_gadgets_20261103/full_prefix/NOTE.md)
  certifies the complete primitive-pair orbit modulo 7*13^2, with triangle
  parity retained. It explicitly distinguishes congruence coverage from
  prescribed integer counts and from prime quotients.
- [The adaptive branching obstruction](../session154_gadgets_20261103/closure/NOTE.md)
  emphasizes a second necessary boundary: two branches growing by at least 3
  from finitely many seeds have only O(X^(log_3 2)) outputs up to X. Even
  an all-size closure under our two offsets alone would not complete the
  odd spectrum. The 3H branch must return to the same usable source family,
  alongside the seed range in the inherited ternary compiler target.

This is why more accurate clocks do not discharge the new source-existence
problem. The finite field can be completely understood while its integer
lift still needs a legal source and a return operation.

## A bounded test of the nonuniform triangle interface

Incoming [Session154's interface identity](../session154_gadgets_20261103/interfaces/README.md)
proves that uniform cyclic-triangle replacement has count 3H+6P2+6P3 and
cannot produce the two positive offsets. Nonuniform ports do produce both
offsets on its particular H13 example.

We tested a smaller local neighbourhood on the actual 95/97 sources: keep
boundary vertex 0, delete another root, replace it by a cyclic triangle,
and let each of its three old-neighbourhood masks differ from the removed
root by at most one bit. Old-old arcs are unchanged. Cyclic rotation of the
three new labels reduces the universe to (n-1)(n^3+2n)/3 cases.

The complete counts are 1232 cases at H95 and 1992 at H97, with no strong
output at either requested offset. A control using the inherited H13
code 41 parent has 180 cases and two strong H41 outputs; direct permutation
enumeration independently checks that control. The 95/97 negative probe
uses the primary subset DP, and is not promoted to an all-gadget theorem.
The stopping reason is architectural: this one-exception neighbourhood is
empty. More port exceptions, moving boundary 0, other representatives and
different replacement graphs remain outside this test.
