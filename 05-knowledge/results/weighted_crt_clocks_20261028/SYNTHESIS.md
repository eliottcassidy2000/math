# Relations as objects: retain the information that the next operation tests

The results fit a precise program, not a proposed equivalence of unrelated
mathematical objects. A representation is closed only when its declared
future operations and observations descend to it. On a finite source set,
(C4) constructs the coarsest such quotient. Its universal characterization
is elementary: two sources agree exactly when every allowed continuation
has the same observed output.

The counts identify three distinct situations. Cycle-index substitution gives
a positive all-order recursive closure for the specified weighted counts.
The CRT tests show an apparently compact quotient expanding to the whole
source under just two operations. The sampled unit trace admits a genuine
quotient for its restricted clock menu, but adding one primitive clock destroys
that saving. None of these conclusions can be inferred from the source's
homotopy type or from a coincidence in a small integer.

The source of ternary criticality is uniform sampling in an iterated wreath
product. Its shared two-copy action yields the variance4k(k-1), and the
weighted version(W4) identifies which sums of port weights contribute. This
can guide return-kernel bookkeeping in LRC investigations. It supplies no
uniform bound for arbitrary return operators, whose rank may grow even when
their failure complexes remain contractible. Likewise the affine obstruction
(A3) rules out one tempting literal group bridge without ruling out guarded
transfers or new source-marked relations.

For the original smallest-carrier question the next useful tests are explicit:

1. Run(C4) on actual strong-atom attachment transitions, with the supplied
   read-only scan as the initial observation and native complement/re-marking
   included only when their typed transitions are defined.
2. Extend the CRT universe to repeated odd prime factors; prime fields alone
   do not test higher-adic phase. The theorem(C1) already supplies the chart.
3. Extend the complete family-specific [canonical carrier](CANONICAL.md)
   to stabilizer actions of general strong atoms. Aggregate cycle indices
   can count while losing the incidence required to compose two independently
   supplied attachments; the explicit paired-colour recursion repairs that
   loss for the ternary family only.

These are OPEN tasks. No new blanket meta-pattern is promoted from a single
thread. The present evidence strengthens the existing operation-sensitive
representation pattern with an all-order recurrence and concrete minimal
finite observers, rather than a claim that every arithmetic dependence has
now been classified.
