# Proof and computational scope

**Same-agent review. No independent referee or subagent audit is claimed.**

The all-size claims have proofs in DIGITS_AND_TOWERS, MINIMAL_CLOCK and
SYMMETRY_AND_PORTS. No finite experiment is used to extrapolate a universal
absence or convergence statement.

* Digit formula and sequential carry lift: every n<100000. Complete
  block generating functions and moments: L=1,...,5. Dyadic locality:
  multipliers 2^a, a=1,...,8, and n<5000. Negative transition exponents
  are allowed in the Laurent transfer matrix; final counts are ordinary
  nonnegative polynomials. The controller has two states plus an
  unbounded accumulator, not two states for the entire integer source.
* Minimal clock: exact modules at k=1,...,4, dimensions 2,4,10,28;
  literal conjugacy to the old 27-coordinate radical chain at modulus 3.
  The dimension lower bound is a unipotent-order proof. No physical
  tournament realizability is inferred for arbitrary module vectors.
* Even charts: 80000 integer conjugacy checks. All 2016 unordered pairs
  in 1,...,64 have an explicit common suffix separating their halving
  clocks. The all-integer theorem is the valuation argument in D5.
* The 81/27 groups are reconstructed and counted. The complete affine
  54-group is enumerated and checked on the actual common triples. Its
  intersections with the actual automorphism lists are computed. The
  two distinct order-27 groups have different element-order spectra.
* The recursive families are checked through 108 vertices for common
  triples and their two generating permutations. Every affine candidate
  is checked through 36 vertices. Full high-level automorphism counts
  use the maximal-module proof, not a claimed high-order census.
* Attachment orbits use independent Burnside sums on actual groups;
  regular attachments use inclusion-exclusion over joined orbit
  partitions of order-three elements. These independently verify the
  recurrences for one, two and three named new ports. All 4096 binary
  masks confirm every stated stabilizer order at twelve vertices.
  Additional fixed old roots require a smaller source group.
* Native normalization is replayed for both signs through 36 vertices.
  Every supplied path is legal, every old edge change is the intended
  single arc, and both contractions retain the original-name fibres.
  Global minimum repair outside the prescribed bottom blocks is not
  claimed. The original family has no pair-module halving interface.
* Ready2107 is a bounded witness search through parent order 10. It
  returns two order-11 targets. All 725760 root/in-port-fixed target
  identifications are compared. Eight arc reversals are minimum only
  in this bank. Every completed repair is strong; temporary helper
  stages are not asserted strong. An independent endpoint DP recounts
  the final profile, and all five root/spare Q4 charts are saved.

The pinned driver checks inherited sources, local Python files and exact
receipt hashes. Normal and optimized modes have separate verification
receipts. Scripts use explicit exceptions; no critical check depends on
Python assert. The inherited failed 55-to-111 bank remains pinned and
was not rerun. No global theorem IDs or priority claims are made.
