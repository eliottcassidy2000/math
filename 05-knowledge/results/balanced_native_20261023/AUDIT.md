## Repaired development error: transporter direction

The first literal audit reused the forward partner transporter g=(4,0,2,3,1,5,6,7) for both orientations of a bit edge. Its order is three, so g is not its own inverse. A reverse native event correctly failed the target-isomorphism check. The repair uses g for0-to1 and g^-1 for1-to0, retaining actual clock direction. The inherited CUBES.md proof already used forward edges with x<y and was unaffected. No false theorem was published; the new checker and the two-direction audit were repaired before freezing receipts.

The missing coordinate was the direction of the chosen transporter. The survivor is undirected native connectivity with oriented witness maps, not an involution claim. This is a useful guard against mistaking a reversible relation for a self-inverse chosen representative.

## Remaining obligations

The uniform packet bound assumes globally available distinct resources and a fixed shared record. It does not solve compatibility of changing shared coordinates, produce rich supplies at unbounded tournament order, or make the charge group order-independent. The finite seed's cross-pointed bridges need a larger-host endpoint transporter. High-entropy refreshes cannot have depth-independent congestion in the stated chain. These are different missing assertions and cannot be exchanged for each other.
