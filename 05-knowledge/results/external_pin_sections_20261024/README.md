## What the native obstruction actually rules out

The complete2^21 normalized-word census finds120 source classes that can
vary their V choice while keeping the original independently owned source
row's dyadic current. Their pair sums cover only1988 charges even after
allowing repeated labels, dropping pins and ignoring collisions. Thus a
complete local helper section at occupancy four is impossible within this
one-unit-per-source architecture. The same actual bank is complete at helper
occupancy five, but changing that margin is a different comparison family.
[Exact obstruction and scope](coverage/README.md), [audit](coverage/AUDIT.md).

The obstruction is compatible with the positive main theorem: the latter
keeps helper occupancy four and lets the actual core change its own charge.
This is a coupled completion, not a repair of the impossible helper-only map.
