# What would advance the remaining existence proof

**Chan–Pak completeness remains OPEN.** The question is whether every positive odd integer other than 7 and 21 is an actual tournament Hamilton-path count. This session does not establish a new unrestricted coverage cutoff.

## 1. Choose a physical inverse, rather than merely a feasible vector

The exact cone and affine lattice are the complete homogeneous-linear and affine-subgroup tests on all rooted states. Their intersection still contains nonphysical points. A prescribed scalar target therefore needs an actual source recipe or a proved physical membership theorem after choosing the hidden coordinates.

The graph-module collision is a different issue. It disproves recognition of a given graph from its state, not the possibility of a state-based existence proof. A proof may choose any physical representative of a valid inverse state; uniqueness of its graph is unnecessary. The exponential collision family strengthens this distinction rather than supplying a general impossibility result.

## 2. Use the smaller edge graft adaptively

The source-rooted edge graft is the minimal escape from every nonempty O/Z/J terminal-face image. Full rank is not needed. Its image plane has an integral inverse for the restricted operator, but an integral inverse point still needs physical membership. The companion triangle gadget supplies a different root-start multiplier. These operations are reasonable candidates for a genuinely adaptive reconstruction, rather than a fixed list of scalar affine families. The two scalar-switch pairs examined here have injective sidecar maps and disjoint physical sidecar ranges, so even arbitrary mixtures of all four words leave at most two scalar values in a final sidecar fiber. A genuinely many-to-one sidecar return is an open design question for accumulating choices; the common-context gap theorem does not exclude branch-dependent reconstruction.

The required direction remains: for each prescribed odd target, at least one smaller, physically admissible predecessor exists. A descent that only removes letters from a representation already supplied proves a different statement.

## 3. Retain the limits of the negative results

The multi-root finite-menu theorem fixes its source bank and gadget types, then permits variable ordered gadget words and arbitrary outer ordered sums. It does not bound a recursive grammar whose outputs become new sources or new gadgets. The finite-line residue test requires a common missing odd residue. Parallel nonunit lines can have no such gap. Neither obstruction is a theorem against all adaptive constructions.

## Research-method record

The session used the repository's patterns of searching the statement before the method, retaining lost coordinates, separating support from bounded-height coverage, and testing the actual consequence object. The live concepts were physical four-states, graph modules, path-cover interfaces, positive cones, root-start complexity, and scalar existence. Existing inequality and substitution theorems were explicitly credited. The finite controls include singleton and sink boundaries, a cone/lattice false positive, a full-state graph collision, and a same-H collision between escaped and nonescaped states.

A proposed bounded-positive-defect sparsity shortcut was not promoted: a terminal J after a long O/Z block can have bounded positive E=d while the other coordinates grow. The correct bound uses bounded D before the closing chamber and a uniformly bounded U/D ratio after the last non-O operation. This stopping reason does not assert that every positive-defect sparsity statement is false.
