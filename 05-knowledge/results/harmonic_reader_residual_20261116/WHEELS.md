# The Riesel wheel: support, multiplicity, and phase are different

The classical Riesel cover for 509203 uses primes 3,5,7,13,17,241,
the prime support of 2^24−1. Its respective clock orders are
2,4,3,12,8,24. See the [ACL2 validation of Riesel numbers](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.4671)
and the [earlier exact wheel classification](../logarithmic_repairs_covers_20261116/CLOCKS.md).
There are two normalized patterns up to translation:

    A=(0,1,0,7,3,23),   B=(0,1,0,11,3,7).

Each covers all 24 slots, with 16 single and 8 double hits and
no triple hits. The reciprocals sum to 4/3, not one. Their
overlap functions (numbers of hits at e modulo 24) are explicitly

    m_A(e)=1+[3|e]−[e=15]+[e=19],
    m_B(e)=1+[3|e]−[e=15]+[e=11].

The fold modulo 8 agrees: the two replacement deltas both land
at 3 and the deleted slot at 7. Even every Fourier *magnitude*
agrees: e ↦ 6−e interchanges the two overlap functions. But
their complex phases and their folds modulo 3 differ. Thus
an observer that records either just the dyadic fold or just
Fourier magnitudes cannot choose between the two absolute
patterns. The labeled phase patterns themselves are related
by the affine map e ↦ 17e modulo 24, not by translation.
This is an exact test case for a harmonic decoder that throws
away phase.

## The two literal lists do not give wheels if read as periods

Interpret the user's multiset {4,2,5,7,7,13} as a list of
periods, with one chosen residue class per occurrence. The common
period is 1820. At least one residue modulo 4 avoids both
periods 2 and 4, four residues modulo 5 avoid the 5-phase,
five modulo 7 avoid the two 7-phases, and twelve modulo 13
avoid the 13-phase. These conditions are independent by CRT,
so at least 1·4·5·12=240 slots are uncovered, attained by
putting the 4-phase outside the 2-phase and using distinct
7-phases. The best possible coverage fraction is 79/91,
even though the reciprocal sum exceeds one.

For comparison the earlier repo list {4,2,5,3,7,13}, read
as periods, has at least 576 misses per period 5460, again
sharp by CRT. Neither is the Riesel cover as a period
multiset. If instead 7 is read as a prime divisor of k·2^e−1
for fixed k, it offers at most one phase (order three);
taking it twice does not give another independent phase.
The genuine precision lift is to 7², where 2 has order 21,
not to a second copy of the same clock. This makes different
roles for the same numerals explicit.

## Why the common 24 is not a coordinate identification with Golay

The Riesel phase rotation is a genuine 24-cycle on its slots.
The extended binary Golay code does not admit such a cyclic
coordinate ordering. If it did, it would be a binary cyclic
self-dual [24,12] code. In F₂[x],

    x^24−1=(x+1)^8(x²+x+1)^8.

A cyclic code is generated by a divisor; duality complements
the factor exponents and reverses the factors. Both displayed
irreducibles are self-reciprocal, so self-duality forces exponent
four on each, giving generator (x³+1)^4=x^12+1. This is a
weight-two word, contradicting the Golay minimum distance eight.
Thus the Riesel rotation is not a coordinate action on a Golay
frame of the Leech lattice. This does not exclude order-24
isometries of the Leech lattice in other representations.

A better analogy is exact cancellation in the Leech theta
series Θ_Λ=E₄³−720Δ: the norm-two coefficient vanishes by
a specified signed correction, not by small relative error.
The repo's [deep-hole theta theorem](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4758-leech-deep-hole-swap-theorem-and-the-deep-hole-theta-identity.md)
keeps the residue-class components of that comparison. What
can transfer to tournament repair is the distinction between
a formal cancellation, its actual phase, and the support
needed for a prescribed integer, not a shared dimension alone.

The two load spectra have the exact normalized Fourier form
`[k=0]+[8|k]/3+(e(−ak/24)−e(−15k/24))/24`, where
e(x)=exp(2πix) and a=19 or 11. The two eight-element
double-hit supports differ at just two coordinates,
{11,19}; both cannot be Golay octads in a fixed marking, since
their sum would be a forbidden weight-two codeword. This is
a second, local obstruction independent of the cyclic-code argument.

In the literal repeated-7 wheel at optimal coverage, the 240
holes form the full Cartesian product of its free classes.
Any single extra residue class containing every hole must have
period dividing four: their common lattice period forces the
candidate period to divide 1820; the multiple permitted residues
in each of the 5-, 7-, and 13-factors then rule out all those
prime divisors. Thus a one-clock repair must reuse a live
coordinate rather than just add another unrelated period.
