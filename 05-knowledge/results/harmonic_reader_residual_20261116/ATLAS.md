22. Fixed-carrier arithmetic factors versus exact support for Chan–Pak, the one-reader/both-half collision problem.
23. Tournament two-hub overlap decoder: select a sum of 2^α3^β terms *at a prescribed integer*, not merely obtain independent residue counts.
24. Multiplicative lonely runner: ×2×3 orbit visits across a reciprocal balance window; ordinary lonely runner is an adjacent but distinct problem.
25. Riesel/Sierpiński common-coefficient (Brier) coverage: odd order modulo 7 blocks the two signs in this wheel, not in others.
26. Tournament vs even-graph bijection compatible with odd-group refinements: 2-adic parity role versus smallest odd permutation cycle 3; equinumerosity is not the open part.
