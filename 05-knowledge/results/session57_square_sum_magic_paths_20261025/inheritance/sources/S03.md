## 2. The square-sum graph `Q_n` and its three chains

The neighbours of `m` in `Q_n` are the `k^2 - m` with `m < k^2 <= m + n`, `k^2 != 2m`. Equivalently every edge is a reflection `x -> k^2 - x`.
