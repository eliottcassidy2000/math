# Independent audit of two reduced magic-square frames

Verdict: PASS, with a reproducibility qualification about the AP producer scripts. No integer magic square of nine distinct squares is constructed, and bare Hamiltonian paths or square-sum bicliques do not imply the missing arithmetic decoration.

## Evidence and exact checks

The local `input` folder freezes the reviewed mathematical text and certificates. `audit.py` is self-contained relative to that folder, uses exact SymPy integer/rational algebra, makes no source writes, and implements all checks with explicit exceptions. `normal.json` and `optimized.json` are identical. Their outputs can be useful, but optimized runs of those producers must not be advertised as preserving all checks. This independent verifier does preserve its checks under -O.

## General real-layout proof audit

For nine distinct real magic entries, the center is the mean, hence determined by the set. In the normal form, after signs and interchange, x>y>0 and the four positive deviations are x+y, x, y, x-y; distinctness excludes x=2y. The largest two deviations are x+y and x, uniquely recovering x,y. Precisely eight signed ordered pairs remain; they give distinct arrays and the D4 orbit. Therefore the general eight-layout statement is valid, not merely an extrapolation of the finite test.

In characteristic three, any magic array still has the standard three-parameter normal form (the derivation needs characteristic unequal to two, not division by three). For a fixed affine F3² plane there are nine centers and 8*6 ordered independent directions, giving 432 layouts and 54 free D4 orbits. This does not assert that such a plane consists of squares.
