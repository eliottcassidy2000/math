## Proof audit

1. **Types and direction.** Each cell has one whole automorphism, after
   a fixed internal source-to-target identification. The comparison relation
   is a LEFT coset rH_A under function composition. Goursat kernels belong
   to the projections, not necessarily to the entire ambient groups.
2. **Integer rather than real compatibility.** The two edge congruences
   are equivalent to membership in the preimage lattice, including the
   divisibility needed before dividing by v. Every row is periodic on
   the stated residue box. The constructive update preserves the whole
   affine lattice, and the determinant count uses its full period lattice.
3. **Completeness of internal charts.** R_m^con has only rotations because
   its unique outgoing arc with maximal common-outneighbor count gives
   an intrinsic directed cycle. R9^lex is a different tournament with
   group order81. The scripts authenticate both at size9 independently.
4. **Source and ports.** The same cell variable occurs on all incident
   edges. A necklace alone is insufficient; its phase at an actual owner
   is retained. Named cells remain input data except in the explicitly
   degree-separated native examples. No unmarked reconstruction is inferred.
5. **Observation.** Exact Fourier coefficients are grouped AFTER restricting
   to the full solution subgroup and incorporating the source offset.
   The mean is not an extremal bound. An empty fibre is rejection, never
   a source-coverage certificate. The implementation tests linear integer
   observations; the proof covers supplied local finite tables more generally.
6. **Nonabelian extension.** The heap operation preserves left cosets in
   product groups; the tagged-domain extension satisfies both identities.
   Binary relation tables give the bounded-arity setting for the cited
   algorithm. The prefix bank consists of complete compatible tuples;
   quotient-image completeness is an explicit obligation. No arbitrary
   colour palette is silently substituted for a coset.
7. **Central lifts.** The cycle-charge lemma assumes a central kernel and
   a flat quotient connection. The UT3(F3) and Q8 examples detect the
   forgotten central coordinate. They do not assert that those groups
   occur as full automorphism groups of the native cells.
8. **Actual consequence.** These are compatibility and observation results.
   They do not supply the missing metagraph source inventory, an A000568
   recurrence with bounded native states, a six-colour forcing gadget, or
   Collatz descent. Zero-slot conservation does not bound its accumulated
   debt and does not change the actual integer clock.
