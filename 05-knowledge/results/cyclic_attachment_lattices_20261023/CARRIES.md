## 1. The same equation for representative carries and additive charge

For a in Z/m let sigma(a) be its least nonnegative integer representative.
Define

    kappa_m(a,b)=(sigma(a)+sigma(b)-sigma(a+b))/m.

Then kappa is0 or1 and

    kappa(a,b)+kappa(a+b,c)=kappa(b,c)+kappa(a,b+c).     (1)

Both sides equal [sigma(a)+sigma(b)+sigma(c)-sigma(a+b+c)]/m.
Thus every addition tree has the same total carry; associativity does not
mean that the carry vanishes. For the integer observation P=sum_i w_i sigma,

    P(x+y)=P(x)+P(y)-sum_i w_i m_i kappa_(m_i)(x_i,y_i). (2)

This is the exact failed homomorphism step in the generator-line hostile.

Incoming additive work retains b(n)=floor((n+1)/phi²) and
delta(a,b)=b(a)+b(b)-b(a+b). Its tree independence is the same telescoping
identity on the additive monoid of integers. The target, denominator and
possible values differ. This is a coboundary calculation, not an assertion
that the canonical Fibonacci charge is a residue-class homomorphism.
Incoming's example3+3 has zero delta despite overlapping digit supports,
so even an exact zero carry cannot replace shared-digit owners.

## 3. Prefix banks have kernel-valued carries

At one stage of the prefix bank let E=H^(i-1), K=H^(i), Q=K_i. For a
chosen section L:Q->E with L_1=1, retain

    kappa(a,b)=L_a L_b L_(ab)^-1 in K,
    alpha_a(k)=L_a k L_a^-1.

Associativity gives the exact, generally twisted identity

    kappa(a,b) kappa(ab,c)
        = alpha_a(kappa(b,c)) kappa(a,bc).             (4)

To prove it, expand (L_a L_b)L_c and L_a(L_b L_c), then cancel L_(abc)
on the right. Central K makes the action trivial and recovers the ordinary
cocycle equation. If K is not central, omitting alpha can change the answer.
Changing the section changes the carry; a nonzero carry for one section
does not alone prove that the extension is nonsplit.

This gives an exact bridge between the prefix bank, the cyclic carry,
and the central-lift lemma in CHARGE.md. The common operation is choosing
a quotient section and retaining its multiplication error. The Monster
double-cover discussion identifies a kernel action that must survive an
