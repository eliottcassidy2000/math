## What changes in the strategy

**The exterior alone can be insufficient, even when its size is unrestricted.**
Let Q be the strong four-vertex tournament and M_r its r-fold ordinal sum.
The incoming source has S=15*32^r-5^r. For every r>=3, no tournament
containing M_r as a uniform module has count3S+2 or3S+4. The proof first
uses K3 to exclude three or more visits, then the new two-run cone to
exclude every remaining frame. This is [C5](absorption/TWO_RUN_CONE.md),
not a claim that those integer targets are globally absent. A successful
selector must sometimes break, split, or replace the module itself.

The underlying [cone theorem, C1--C4](absorption/TWO_RUN_CONE.md) is elementary:
for a cyclic rooted frame with at most two visits,

    a_2+2 <= H(frame) <= 3a_2.

A recoverable path-pair injection proves it in all orders. This turns
the formerly empirical cone into a usable structural test. For example,
the proposed H9011 source frame (a1,a2)=(95,11) is impossible because95>33.

**Small error must be accompanied by enough distinct target placements.**
A fixed finite frame bank fed by ordinal words from a fixed finite strong
atom bank supplies only O(log^D X) distinct counts up to X. Even an ideal
repair radius O(X^alpha), alpha<1, cannot turn it into all-prime or all7p
supply. Nevertheless actual primitive modules give ternary debt O(S^epsilon)
for every prescribed epsilon>0. Both statements are proved in
[terminal capacity T1--T4](absorption/PROOF.md). Arbitrarily small relative
error does not by itself make an approximate witness useful near every target.

**One-shot source selection has a smaller obligation than a renewable core.**
The inherited Session193 prime-lane reduction needs certified operations
only for the relative prime/7p atoms and three finite carry atoms. The new
[factorization forest](arithmetic/PROOF.md) bounds the total number of
formal digit gates across all branches by log_3 n and optimizes the choice
of factors. It preserves the distinction between a supplied scalar count
and a physically applicable source witness. The remaining obligation is
uniform availability of such actual source certificates.
Allowing a3h-2 gate gives a choice of adjacent smaller parents for targets
congruent1 modulo3. The [alternate-parent theorem](arithmetic/ALTERNATIVE_PARENTS.md)
retains the total bound3^g*product(b_i-1)<=N-1. This weakens the universal
source obligation without pretending that the alternative gate is always
physically available.

## Full fibres and precise stopping reasons

The complete all-order response fibres are H11:P2=100*2^t and
H13:P2=104*2^t. Thus every H13 witness fails both incoming45P2±H prime
interfaces, while an H11 witness succeeds at9011. Neutral padding of any
fixed nonunit module admits only finitely many unit-digit matches with a
fixed finite frame bank, with an explicit exponential-zero bound.

Every nontrivial one- or two-port inflation of the specified H25 bridge
breaks neutrality, even with unequal arbitrary modules. The obstruction
uses positivity together with K2 congruent2 modulo4. A three-port change
escapes it at8179, and changing the source chart produces the infinite
induced family above. These are distinct operations with distinct scopes.

The unshifted target profiles81*2^m-49 and81*2^m-47 are impossible in all
orders: primitive strong-component reduction and Busch's bound reduce them
to the complete order-nine check. Shifting the parameter by two produces
the +2 target. The corresponding shifted +4 profile is absent only in the
declared finite and local searches; its existence remains OPEN.

The displayed77/79 children lose the marked preparation G-H=2. Different
cores of those same counts restore it at the base size, but their growing
response vectors do not match. This is a concrete finite source reselection,
not a proof of repeatable return.

The final [three-root theorem](absorption/NATURAL_ROOTS.md) explains part of
that failure in every order: after the generic +4 two-birth operation on a
G-H=2 source, neither the original marked vertex nor either newborn has
G-H=2. The other old vertices remain possible. This gives a precise reason
to search for a changed old root or a genuine core repair, rather than
assuming the new vertices automatically renew the preparation.

## Where the outside comparisons enter

The Collatz comparison motivates choosing among arithmetic parents rather
than demanding a lift of one fixed trajectory. The proof forest retains
actual tournament interfaces at its leaves and gates; ordinary or mixed-sign
Collatz paths do not supply them. Negative-digit source choice and its
shifted potential are documented with the arithmetic packet. No implication
to or from ordinary Collatz convergence is claimed.

The correspondence is deliberately typed in [BOARD](BOARD.md): path-cover
responses retain more than counts; exponential modes control constant
corrections; the physical integer cone is smaller than its rational
relaxation; and source placement has a capacity requirement. These outside
viewpoints have produced explicit constructions or obstructions here, rather
than numerical analogies alone.
