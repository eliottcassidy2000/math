## 3. Oddness is an action, not a shared label

The inherited [Lucas signed-copy calculation](../lucas_mirror_gap_spaces_20261028/SYNTHESIS.md)
has three separate facts: Lambda5 has15 edges and is Royle-sign-even;
its automorphism group is D5 of order10; its signed copy sum vanishes in
every self-converse eleven-vertex tournament. A positive Paley embedding
does not prevent cancellation. The copy count is11!/10, with exactly half
of either sign under an anti-involution.

In contrast, an automorphism group of a tournament has odd order because
an involution would reverse a swapped pair. A self-converse tournament adds
orientation-reversing maps; these are not automorphisms. The Lucas mirror
fixes three vertices, so it cannot be a tournament anti-involution either.

Fricke inversion, time reflection, word reflection, Frobenius, and tournament
converse can each yield traces and invariant/anti-invariant parts. They act
on different objects. [R2](RAMANUJAN.md) gives an actual shared modular
coefficient4372; it gives no map from Lucas copies to the Monster module.
The [odd-prime Walsh quotient theorem](../prime_local_walsh_bijection_20261020/QUOTIENT_COROLLARY.md)
also preserves a specified Sylow action, not every symmetry at once.
