## 1. The two-congruence idea now has an exact all-size solver

For authenticated cyclic chart groups C_m,C_n, each nonempty comparison
relation is an offset plus a lattice with basis(h,0),(s,v). The
[lattice proof](lattice/README.md) gives its two congruences, exact pair count,
and both one-sided kernel sizes, including multivalued attachments.

The stronger result is elimination at arbitrary degree. For a shared middle
chart, all incident possible-value sets are cosets in ONE cyclic group.
Generalized CRT makes pairwise compatibility sufficient for their common
intersection. Eliminate the chart by retaining every pairwise fill-in
relation and unary domain, and multiply the count by the gcd of the
incident kernel sizes. Repeated elimination uses at most cubically many
relation operations once the actual relations are supplied. Store the old
relations for actual-owner back-substitution.

Exact shared alignment classes are separately the integer cokernel

    Z^(2E) / (direct-sum_e L_e + image(D)),

where D uses one common variable for every named cell. Its Smith factors
retain composite torsion beyond the earlier prime-field dimension.
Counting a quotient is not choosing a literal port. General noncyclic
groups do not satisfy this cyclic Helly property; C3² already gives a
three-coset hostile. Extra guards outside binary affine-coset form must
remain as boundary queries or full joint factors.

## 2. Larger cells can be handled by their actual invisible kernels

[KERNELS.md](KERNELS.md) proves an exact reduction for arbitrary finite
internal groups. Intersect all incident one-sided kernels K_i, restrict to
the allowed projection P_i, then solve the quotient domains P_i/K_i.
Every compatible quotient tuple has exactly product|K_i| lifts. An
observation is quotient-safe only when it is invariant under those lifts.
Otherwise retain them or their full observation enumerator.

An [actual nine-cell control](lattice/NON_NORMAL_KERNEL.md) shows why the
allowed projection matters: the common kernel is not normal in the full
cell group. It also distinguishes source-kernel right multiplication from
target-kernel left multiplication and exhibits a nonconstant owner query
inside a nine-element lift fibre.

For R_d, the iterated lexicographic triangle on3^d vertices, label a block
by the prefix depth ell that it compares. Let c_k be the number of components
using edges of depth at least k. Then the full named-cell group order is

    3^(c_1+3c_2+...+3^(d-1)c_d),

and the exact minimum owner budget is **3^(d-1)c_d**. That budget also makes
ordinary refinement discrete. At nine vertices this is3c_2: fine matching
edges can lower the coarse-only3m cost. A three-cell path with depths(1,2)
needs exactly6 marks, rather than9.

The hierarchy is intrinsic, recovered from common-outneighbor counts.
This is an all-size theorem for the stated family, not a claim that every
large tournament has this hierarchy. Twisted prefix selectors need their
actual transports and further compatibility constraints.

## 4. The graph/complement proposal: repair the missing observer

The inherited same-size trijection uses an ordered graph(G,P): reverse its
graph pairs in the transitive tournament defined by P. Complementing G or
reversing P gives tournament converse. The pair{G,complement G} alone does
not retain P. Already one edge plus an isolate can produce either a
transitive triple or a directed triangle.

The [complement lane](complement/README.md) proves the sharp all-size
boundary: the tournament type is independent of P iff G is complete or
empty. More positively, the distribution over all observer orders obeys
an exact two-sided graph/tournament incidence balance. Its directed-triangle
histogram separates all522 graph/complement pairs through order7. General
reconstruction from this distribution remains OPEN.

There is an actual electrical connection. Inserting an isolated graph
vertex at an observer cut L|R adds exactly e_G(L,R) directed triangles;
a universal vertex adds the crossing nonedges. For a general new graph
neighborhood N, put O=(L intersect N) union(R minus N). The exact increment is

    sum_(u in O) outdegree_T(u) - binom(|O|,2).

Thus an addressed score vector has a complete insertion update for this
statistic. A score multiset or the unmarked cut size does not identify O.
This translates a particular predicate with its required coordinates;
it does not transfer all graph theorems to one unmarked tournament type.

The [incidence-flow extension](complement/INCIDENCE_FLOW.md) turns the exact
weights into a reversible chain with a literal electrical energy equal to
conditional variance. At order four, a star's tournament distribution equals
a mixture of one-quarter empty-graph and three-quarters two-disjoint-edge
sources. Distinct rows therefore do not provide lossless mixture decoding.

## 6. Shared integer clocks and geometric alignment

The incoming square-word decoder leads to an exact
[shared-clock compiler](square_sum/SHARED_CLOCKS.md). Each connected network
of indexed port identifications has one signed affine integer root, possibly
pinned by a cycle. Pulling every path's bounds and collision exclusions into
that root leaves an interval with finitely many excluded values. This gives
an exact count and actual back-substitution. Distinctness conditions between
different components remain joint factors; multiplying marginal counts
would lose them.

The [Gaussian extension](INCOMING.md) provides another complete decoder.
Separate D4 orbit coordinates and the ordinary dot product do not determine
the orbit of a sum: (2,1)+(-2,4) and (2,1)+(2,-4) have identical such input
data but land in different norm-25 orbits. Adding the mixed quartic
`x^3*u+y^3*v` completes a six-polynomial separator for every ordered pair of
real vectors. The common mechanism is a relative frame under one simultaneous
group action, not a numerical identification between Gaussian points and
tournaments.

The same integration exposes a sharp mod-eight boundary: an odd-order
tournament chart group has no nontrivial character to Z/8. Nonconstant
native mod-eight observers must retain their actual coordinate dependence.
