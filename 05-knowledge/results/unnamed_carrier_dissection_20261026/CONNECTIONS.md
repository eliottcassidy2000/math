## 3. An exact fivefold/sevenfold dictionary

The inherited [THM-4644 / Fermat-prime circulant rigidity](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4644-fermat-primes-rigid-circulant-tournaments-wieferich-orbit-explosion.md)
already supplies the multiplier-orbit mechanism. Here is its precise
specialization to binary Frobenius, with no novelty claim for that mechanism.

For an odd prime p, put d=ord_p(2). A connection set D in F_p* defines a
circulant tournament exactly when D and -D partition F_p*. It is invariant
under doubling exactly when it is a union of <2>-orbits. Such a D exists
iff d is odd, and then there are

    2^((p-1)/(2d))

choices. Proof: an even-order subgroup of F_p* contains -1 and forces a
forbidden inverse pair; an odd-order subgroup has its cosets freely paired
by negation, and each pair offers one choice. The checker exhausts the
actual inverse-pair selections for the listed primes through31.

At p=7, d=3 and the two choices are {1,2,4} and {3,5,6}: the converse
Paley tournaments. At p=5, d=4 and there is no doubling-invariant choice.
This does not forbid five-vertex tournaments or fivefold geometries. It
identifies a specific symmetry that cannot preserve one orientation.

The corresponding ring isomorphisms are literal:

    Z[zeta_7]/(2) ~= F2[X]/((X^3+X+1)(X^3+X^2+1))
                   ~= F8 x F8,
    Z[zeta_5]/(2) ~= F2[X]/(X^4+X^3+X^2+X+1) ~= F16.

The two cubic factors are reciprocal. Their roots form the doubling
orbits {1,2,4} and {-1,-2,-4} in exponent coordinates. Conjugation exchanges
the factors, whereas Frobenius stays inside each. In the fivefold quotient,
Frobenius squared sends a primitive fifth root to its inverse, so inversion
is already inside one orbit. This is a concrete reason the same chiral
selector construction works at seven and fails at five.

This connects directly to the repo's
[four-dimensional A4 fivefold carrier](../fivefold_boundary_20261018g.md),
whose two complex embeddings are zeta->zeta and zeta->zeta^2. The planar
projection alone is nondiscrete; retaining a bounded internal window gives
a uniformly discrete selected set. This is an exact lattice/window result,
not by itself a classification of Penrose kite-and-dart tilings. De Bruijn's
[published record](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/algebraic-theory-of-penroses-non-periodic-tilings-of-the-plane-i-/)
is the primary bibliographic route; its scanned theorem pages were not
audited here. No external tiling theorem is needed for the displayed finite
ring isomorphisms or the inherited A4 proof.

The incoming [16-state Paley cover](../growing_sieve_path_20261026/field/README.md)

## 4. Fermat, Dudeney and the genuinely relevant norm obstruction

**Source/version scope.** The requested [v3 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.03865v3)
has an [official v4 correction notice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03865v4): an
outdated file was compiled in v3. We read the no-flip theorem and matching
lemmas and checked the notice; we do not independently certify the full
case analysis. The [published ITCS2026 paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.47)
is the current primary reference. The cited result is the four-piece optimum for polygonal
pieces moved by rotations and translations. Its coupled edge/vertex
matching diagrams retain endpoint transport parity and length relations.
Fermat's theorem is not a stated dependency. The displayed cut-type ledger
has 44 square types and five triangle types, as reconstructed and qualified
by the incoming Session71 report. Flips and curved pieces are outside the
stated optimality theorem.

Fermat's two-square theorem is the Gaussian norm criterion: an integer N
is a sum of two integer squares iff every prime 3 mod4 occurs to even
exponent. [Conrad's Gaussian-integer notes](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/Zinotes.pdf)
give the classical arithmetic. In particular 5=2^2+1^2, whereas44=4*11
is not a two-square norm, even over Q. This cannot explain the piece
minimum: rescaling an equal-area triangle/square pair changes its area
to5 or44 without changing the minimum number of pieces.

There is a stronger, exact connection through norms **over another field**.
Normalize the triangle to vertices (0,0),(2,0),(1,sqrt(3)); its area is
sqrt(3). A square with coordinates in K=Q(sqrt(3)), in any orientation,
would have an edge (a,b) satisfying

    N_(K(i)/K)(a+ib)=a^2+b^2=sqrt(3).

Conjugating sqrt(3) to -sqrt(3) makes the right side negative and the
left side a sum of real squares. Thus no such square exists. Adjoining
theta=3^(1/4) removes this target-coordinate obstruction. This is the
incoming [norm/geometry theorem](../session71_dissection_norm_alignment_20261026/session71-norms/REPORT.md),
with the older [PS-D2 rigid-ear obstruction](../prime_shells_20260921_dissections.md)
as its geometric precursor. It excludes every piece count within the
restricted field, so it is not the argument separating three from four.

For golden geometry, phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2 has conjugate -1/phi. The same
conjugate-positivity test obstructs an equation z*conjugate(z)=phi inside
a finite abelian complex field: complex conjugation commutes with all
embeddings, so every conjugate of such a norm is nonnegative. The incoming
[bounded Penrose P5 audit](../session71_dissection_norm_alignment_20261026/session71-norm-audit/PENROSE_P5_REVIEW.md)
applies precisely this to equal-area similar kite/dart copies. Native
unequal-area templates remain valid; no Penrose dissection minimum follows.

## 6. The bold proposal, stated so it can fail

**PROPOSED research framework:** organize composition by a response object
containing jointly marked boundary occurrences, transport on their frames,
and a realization predicate over an explicit coefficient domain. Compose
by matching complete boundary assignments and summing their weights.
Minimize only relative to the allowed observations and operations.

This has three mathematically different layers:

1. **Incidence:** which endpoints, owners, runs or factors are identified.
   This is where tournament port alignment, graceful colour assignments,
   Petersen repeated visits and dissection piece ownership live.
2. **Transport:** permutations, reflections, Frobenius shifts, determinant
   characters. A path carries the ordered product, not only its endpoint.
3. **Realization:** orientation legality, positive lengths and nonoverlap,
   represented norms, or height-bounded primality tests. A valid transport
   state need not satisfy this extra predicate.

U2 proves when forgetting alignment is lawful: a genuine reset must make
the whole next-state law depend only on the retained observation. The blue
join hostile proves that adding a seemingly modest operation can invalidate
that compression. The earlier Vitali Boolean-conjunction guards prove why
bounded-order correlations fail when operations can address all coordinates.
The growing-sieve packet supplies the arithmetic counterpart: finite residue
information is exact for its finite observer, yet not for primality or the
squared-abc predicate; height and prime-equality exceptions are additional
realization data. The verified twin-prime abc collision remains intact.

An exact linear version is also available. For a finite state space, fixed
transition matrices K_a and observation columns o_j, form

    W = span{K_(a1)...K_(ar) o_j : r>=0}.

The minimum dimension of a linear carrier evaluating every such future
observation on arbitrary signed laws is dim(W): every realization must
separate all functionals in W, and a basis of W attains the bound because
W is invariant under each K_a. This is a linear-response theorem, not the
cardinality of a deterministic partition or a constant-size geometric
interface. It explains how the same proposal accommodates exponential
Boolean moment rank and much smaller reset quotients without contradiction.

The next falsifiable classification conjecture is narrow: do the sets,
or weighted histograms, of ordinary complement partners distinguish every
strong tournament? The through-eight census tests this; it does not prove
it. A counterexample would be a decisive new atom pair, not a failure of
the exact reconstruction and reset theorems already proved.

Research cards used: search the statement before the method; retain local
incidence through counting; type each map and its preserved predicate.
No universal meta-pattern or cross-domain isomorphism is canonized here.
