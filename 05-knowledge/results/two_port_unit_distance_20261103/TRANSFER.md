# Testing the reconstruction mechanism in planar unit-distance graphs

The tournament proof uses two operations: independently vary named
attachment arcs, and split a probe into adjacent twins with identical
external relations. Neither operation automatically belongs to a planar
unit-distance construction language. In particular two distinct points
have at most **two common unit neighbours**: every common neighbour lies
at an intersection of the two unit circles, and distinct circles meet in
at most two points. Three prescribed common neighbours are therefore an
exact metric obstruction to a literal twin-neighbour transfer. Opposite
corners of a unit square attain the two-neighbour limit. This elementary
circle fact is a realization gate, not a new chromatic lower bound.

The [geometry theorem](geometry/PROOF.md) supplies a different lawful
two-vertex operation: a private three-edge unit path between terminals at
distance<3. Its two scalar counts recover the full two-terminal colour
tensor. Joint star-forest contexts recover the entire boundary partition
table, with all zeros retained. The37-point realization demonstrates this
for the regular-hexagon wheel and all203 six-port partitions.

The two transfers must therefore be distinguished precisely:

| Lane | Actual queries | Algebraic readout | What is preserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tournament | Four arc assignments at split ordered ports | Signed sum equals twice a deleted-core response | Every named endpoint and attachment bit |
| Unit-distance colours | Unit ears on a star forest of boundary pairs | Integer forest expansion and partition inversion | Complete labelled colour weights and zero support |

Both inverses use signed combinations of measured counts. They are exact
readout procedures. Their signs are not additional physical edges or
negative colour multiplicities. For q=5, each ear multiplies an existing
colouring's extension count by12 or13; for q=6 by20 or21. All these numbers
are strictly positive, so every original boundary state survives every
allowed ear assembly. This proves a stopping theorem for this attempted
route to a six-chromatic witness, even though its observations are complete.

The distinction matters at the actual wheel: its all-six-distinct boundary
orbit is zero, yet40 other partition types survive and the complete probe
assembly is three-colourable. Full boundary recovery has found a zero entry,
not an empty relation. A genuine six-chromatic unit-distance graph needs a
certificate that **no five-colouring exists**. An obstruction to six-colouring
would prove the stronger seven-chromatic statement. Neither is produced here.

The next geometric construction must introduce an interaction outside this
positive product language: additional private edges, identified vertices,
or a realized gadget with a zero entry at the target palette. Its full
metric contacts and full boundary relation must then be checked anew.
