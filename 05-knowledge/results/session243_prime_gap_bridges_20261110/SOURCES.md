# Sources and status boundaries

Sources inspected on 10 November 2026. Definitions and limited contextual
claims only are imported below. The new elementary proofs are self-contained;
no search result claiming a major conjecture was accepted as a theorem.
No claim about the latest computational record is needed.

- [János Pintz, arXiv:1305.6289v1, Section2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.6289v1): distinguishes infinitely repeated consecutive-prime gaps (strong Polignac) from differences of arbitrary prime pairs (weak Polignac). We retain the strong meaning. Bounded-gap existence is not the assertion for every prescribed even gap.
- [Joel E. Cohen, JIS28 (2025), Article25.4.7, Section2.3](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL28/Cohen/cohen41.pdf): records prime-square Brocard with the adjusted starting prime p>2. This is distinct from the factorial equation. The full conjecture is not assumed here.
- [Berndt–Galway, Ramanujan Journal4 (2000),41–42](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1009873805276), [author institution record](https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/on-the-brocard-ramanujan-diophantine-equation-n-1-msup2sup/): identifies the factorial-square problem and the examples n=4,5,7. We independently check only the declared finite universe n<=60; this is not a new search record or a proof of completeness.
- [Čejchan–Křížek–Somer, JIS25 (2022), Article22.1.4](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL25/Krizek/krizek3.pdf): treats prime offsets near factorials and primorials and supplies short-window sufficient conditions. These do not prove the general Fortune conjectures. Session51's inherited wheel is explicitly credited.
- [Booker–Irvine, The Euclid–Mullin graph, arXiv:1508.03039v2, Introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1508.03039v2): the least-prime-factor sequence uses its own preceding terms, not the consecutive ordinary primes used in a primorial. Its graph is a separate object from a tournament realizing a specified Hamilton count.
- [PrimePages primorial-prime glossary](https://t5k.org/glossary/page.php?sort=PrimorialPrime): plus and minus conventions, with links to primary computational papers. We use definitions only and make no current-record claim.

Repository provenance is pinned by commit, blob and SHA256 in
`provenance/source_pins.json`. The strong tournament constructions depend on
proved physical graft identities, not the open prime conjectures. Exact
citations for those identities are in `constructions/PROOF.md`.
