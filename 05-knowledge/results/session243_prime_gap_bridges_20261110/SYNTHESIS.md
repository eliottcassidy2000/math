## The six concepts after the experiments

1. **Primorial offset supports.** Two primality bits at P_n±1 exactly restore
   the consecutive gap crossing P_n. The old 30030±17 pair has an intervening
   prime 30029. Its gap 34 is not a Polignac instance; the crossing gap is 18.
   At 9699690±23, both offset-one numbers are composite and the gap 46 is real.
2. **Growing versus fixed prime support.** A fixed offset d>1 eventually
   shares a prime factor with every primorial center, killing both endpoints.
   Dyadic padding of one physical gadget fixes its odd prime support, avoiding
   that particular obstruction. It still does not prove prime infinitude.
3. **Synchronized physical source states.** Four exact sources share slope 6
   and have intercepts -1,1,3,5. This gives the four strong counts 6Q+aH for
   every actual gadget. Common padding preserves differences 2H,4H,6H; the
   precise constant-gap criterion names which source coordinate must agree.
4. **Absolute placement and size.** The compact compiler gives a literal
   one-vertex increase 2N, with both counts at most 5N and logarithmic order.
   Its bound is sharp in that family. The endpoints are chosen together;
   no statement starts from an independently prescribed count or graph.
5. **Endpoint versus full count.** A fixed five-letter word comparison has
   difference 2E. Endpoint universality therefore gives another all-even gap
   mechanism, but its lower output is always composite. This explicitly
   blocks an otherwise tempting leap to Polignac prime pairs.
6. **Brown prime-power signs.** Whole prime powers partition into adjacent
   coprime factors of n!/4 for n>=4, with root their sum. A wheel loses exponents and
   signs; the complete sign packet still loses the equality quotient. The
   exact inverse restores it, only at a specified n. Physical witnesses for
   5,11,71 are supplied independently before their ordinal joins square them.

## What a Chan–Pak descent still needs

A least missing admissible count is inherited to reduce to the prime or 7p
lanes. For proper grafts the allowed gadget contents are 1 for prime targets
and {1,7} for 7p targets. This
session supplies new exact constructors and restrictions, but no theorem
that every target in either lane has a physical predecessor.

To invert the compact family at a specified target M, one would have to find
N with M=g(N)+2N or M=g(N)+4N. Selecting the difference 2N does not solve
either equation for arbitrary M. Likewise, inverting the synchronized family
requires a physical gadget with the requisite **joint** (H,Q) pair. Treating
these coordinates as independently chosen integers throws away the very
witness needed for the lift.

The remaining obligation is therefore target-dependent physical occupancy:
for each required M, specify an actual smaller state, its legal constructor,
a decreasing rank and an argument that the state exists. Finite residues,
prime roughness, gap coverage, scalar gcd division and square roots do not
supply that argument. The hostile 49-to-7 and the prime Euclid value 7 remain
mandatory controls.
