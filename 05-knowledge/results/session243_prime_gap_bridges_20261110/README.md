**ACTIVE RESEARCH, 10 November 2026. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

This session merges prior Brocard, primorial/Euclid and prime-gap work with
physical rooted tournament constructions. It does not assume any of these
open prime conjectures or infer paths from an arithmetic normalization.

1. Brocard has two distinct meanings in the repository: primes between
   consecutive prime squares, and Brown numbers n!+1=m². We recover both
   instead of silently choosing one from the misspelled name.
2. Polignac concerns infinitely recurring consecutive-prime gaps. It neither
   supplies arbitrary primes as path counts nor follows from a finite wheel.
3. Euclid numbers P_n+1, primorial offsets, and Euclid–Mullin factor iteration
   have different domains and output predicates.
4. Physical grafts retain full source state and gadget pair (H,Q). Session242
   excludes normalized gadget cofactor7, but does not prove inverse existence.
5. Prime/7p reduction and ordinal multiplication are inherited. An arbitrary
   divisor or square root of an attained count need not itself be attained.
6. Coupled affine/exponential outputs may retain exact gap or residue data.
   We will distinguish these actual families from formal integer solutions.

Closest proved mechanisms: THM-4094 prime/7p reduction, THM-5107 physical
content gate, Session51's prime-offset wheel. Canonical hostile:7 is a prime
