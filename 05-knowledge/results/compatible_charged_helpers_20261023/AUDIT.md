## Corrections made before publication

1. A controller draft incorrectly contrasted its finite charge code with
   preservation at every power of two. The original critical group's
   two-primary exponent256 makes this finite code equivalent to the ENTIRE
   original dyadic harmonic tower. Correct boundary: the printed integer X
   is not automatically a higher-modulus harmonic lift, and exact integer
   currents / odd-primary guards are not established. The corrected proof
   cites the inherited exact code theorem. No numerical certificate changed.
2. A draft said forty reserved resources where the count was forty helper
   SLOTS. Their two alternatives reserve eighty distinct normalized resources;
   each arrival occupies forty. The combined old/new counts544 reserved and
   272 occupied closed words were correct and independently checked.
3. The incoming reuse contract distinguishes simultaneous capacity-one from
   strict temporal nonreturn. Restored helper routes use the former. A helper
   forbidden to return to its original resource cannot vary and finish there;
   the primitive-atom obstruction survives that stronger rule.

## Validity boundaries

The lattice of signed integer moves does not enforce binary occupancy. The
margin4 hostile supplies the exact failed implication. Complete charge
coverage does not enforce shared-owner compatibility; the interval compiler
and native neutral-component control retain that lost coordinate. Kernel
multiplicity is not resource capacity. Simultaneous packet events are not
claimed to factor into legal one-slot microsteps. Aggregate full dyadic
currents are not all ordinary per-port phases; harmonic4eU modulo8 is an
explicit hostile. The complete canonical atlas is already occupied, so all
native packets require a separately compatible unoccupied option reservoir.

No cited incoming theorem or frozen incoming certificate was altered.
