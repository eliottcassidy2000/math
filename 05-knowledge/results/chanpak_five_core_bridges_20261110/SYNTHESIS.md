## 2. A general exact source test emerges from that obstruction

For a strong source S of order n and count h, every strong supertournament
with count at most N<(n+2)h is reachable by a chain of strong one-vertex
enlargements staying below N. If there is no such next vertex inside a
target, the relative strong-extension lemma supplies a cyclic two-ear,
whose count is H(S)+Q_S(2)>N. This is impossible by induced-count monotonicity.
The search terminates because a strong tournament of count<=N has order<=N.

Both ternary targets3h+2,3h+4 lie below this threshold for n,h>=3. The
theorem supplies an exact all-order rejection test for a specified source,
including proposed programs with arbitrarily many births. For nonstrong
targets, the proper-divisor condition must also be retained. The threshold
is sharp already for C3 and its two-ear of count15.
[General theorem and scope](algebra/LOW_RATIO_FRONTIER.md).
