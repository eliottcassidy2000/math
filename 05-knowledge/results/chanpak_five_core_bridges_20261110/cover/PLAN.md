## Inheritance that constrains the proof search

Session220 `selectors/PROOF.md` already proves that finite-state selectors
retaining an ordinary binary witness cannot cover a positive-density output
set for fixed one-variable join banks. Mixed repeated words produce an
unbounded linear drift in g. Session220 also inherits the identity
g(v)+f(v)=v+1-(v mod2). These are not new results of this lane.

The tempting five-core finite-state selector needs a new two-input audit:
an AB term changes pumping growth, but does not itself absorb the positive
linear drift of a mixed binary loop. Investigate this precise architecture,
without confusing rejection of a selector with rejection of all-height cover.

positive drift; bounded versus unbounded arithmetic memory.
