## 3. The sharp binary contrast: projection at seven, reversibility at eleven

**KNOWN / PROVED inherited, independently FINITE-EXACT here.** For a doubly regular tournament of order n=4t−1, its adjacency matrix has row degree 2t−1 and satisfies AAᵀ=tI+(t−1)J: the diagonal is 2t−1 and each off-diagonal entry is the common-outneighbor count t−1. Over F2 this yields A²=A when n≡7 mod8, whereas AAᵀ=I when n≡3 mod8. Thus P7 gives a rank-4 projection onto a Hamming [7,4,3] code, while P11 is an invertible 11-bit automaton. The parity-extended row spaces have parameters [8,4,4] and [12,11,2]. The latter is the whole even-weight code, not Type II or binary Golay.
