## 1. The correction and the stronger theorem

For three distinct positive ternary-unit tails define

    H_T(y)=max_(j=0,1,2) min_(w in T) ||w(y+j)/3||.

For either legal signed pair let d=|u+epsilon v|/3>0 and delta=||dy||.
The corrected exact uniform continuous lower profile is

    H_T(y) >= |delta-1/3|/2,          0<=delta<=1/2.       (A)

Here is an independent proof. Put alpha=|delta-1/3|/2. The zero case is
trivial. Otherwise 0<alpha<=1/6, and a strict danger arc of radius alpha
contains at most one point of an order-three fibre. Full spoil at this
threshold therefore requires three singleton masks with distinct owners.
An active tail has ||wy||<3alpha<=1/2 and a unique nearest integer n_w;
the equality/tie case is inactive. Its owner is -n_w w^(-1) modulo three.
Since u=-epsilon v modulo three, distinct pair owners imply
n_u+epsilon n_v is nonzero modulo three. The circle distance of its third
is exactly 1/3. Writing wy=n_w+e_w gives

    |(e_u+epsilon e_v)/3| < 2alpha.

The circle-distance function is one-Lipschitz, hence
|delta-1/3|<2alpha, impossible. Thus some common sheet has every tail
clearance at least alpha. This is a common-sheet max-min statement: individual
tail marginal measures cannot replace it.

The original addendum's bound max(0,(1/3-delta)/2) was valid, as were the
11/84 wall constant and its family extension. Its assertion that this bound
was globally sharp was false. Its equality examples only covered delta<1/3.
The first failed implication was extending sharpness from that interval to
the whole distance domain. The missing coordinate is the upper signed-distance
deviation from 1/3, retained by the other direction of the same Lipschitz
inequality. This is a strengthened repair, not a rejection of the owner proof.

Both branches of (A) are sharp, with primitive tails after rescaling:

* A<B, A and B congruent to 2 modulo three, T=(A,B,A+B), y=1/(A+B),
  d=(B-A)/3. For r=A/(A+B), the sheet minima are (r/3,r/3,0) and
  delta=(1-2r)/3. These ratios are dense in (0,1/2).
* A and C congruent to 2 modulo three, C>=4A, T=(A,C,A+C), y=1/C,
  d=(2A+C)/3. The clock uses the signed sum of A and A+C. For r=A/C,
  the sheet minima are (r/3,0,r/3) and delta=(1+2r)/3. These ratios
  are dense in (0,1/4).

If g is the common gcd, then 3 does not divide g. Dividing all tails and
d by g, replacing y by gy, and using the invertibility of g modulo three
permutes the sheets and preserves both H and delta. Thus the two dense
equality sets remain primitive. Continuity gives optimality at the limiting
points too; attainment at every individual real delta is not claimed.
The upper endpoint is attained by T=(1,4,5), y=1/4, d=2, H=1/12.
The explicit upper-half control T=(2,11,13), y=1/11, d=5 has
delta=5/11 and H=2/33, where the former bound was zero.

Full spoil at the original strict threshold means H<1/14, so (A) yields

    4/21 < delta < 10/21.                              (B)

Both endpoints are sharp. The primary lower sequence with even N is sound.
For the upper endpoint use T=(9N-1,42N-1,51N-2), y=1/(42N-1),
d=20N-1, N>=1 and N not congruent to 5 modulo eleven. The common gcd
divides eleven and equals eleven precisely in that excluded class. The
tails are ternary units, their exact H=(9N-1)/(3(42N-1))<1/14, and
10/21-delta=11/[21(42N-1)]. Thus actual spoiled primitive rows approach
the upper wall. Even all three inequalities (B) are only necessary:
T=(1,2,4), y=1/3 has every pair distance 1/3 but every tail is inactive.

## 2. Primary owner and wall proofs

The effective activity threshold is 3/14, and the physical threshold is 1/14.
The strict mask has one owner exactly when ||wy||<3/14. Equality is safe.
The raw/physical map is x=(y+j)/3, so 3C is safe exactly when C is safe at y.
Consequently a missing literal tail owner above one body point gives a full-row
witness. Replacing this existential coordinate by a body Haar mass or by
unlabelled marginal capacities would lose the needed implication.

The primary signed-pair implication delta>4/21 under full spoil is correct;
it is the lower half of (B). Its even-N sequence is genuinely primitive:
gcd(9N-1,24N)=gcd(9N-1,24)=1, since N is even and 9N-1 is a ternary unit.
Nearest teeth (0,1,1) give owners (0,1,2). The constant is approached from
above, never by an excluded equality point.

For a virtual wall y=(14k+1)/(14d), a body frequency w blocks exactly when
the signed residue of w(14k+1) modulo 14d lies strictly between -d and d.
Write g=gcd(w,d), q=d/g, h=w/g. The q possible reduced residues are exactly
those congruent to h modulo fourteen, each with multiplicity g, since h is
invertible modulo q. Counting -q<n<q yields

    g [ floor((q-1-h)/14)+floor((q-1+h)/14)+1 ].

This is at most g ceil(q/7). For q>=2 that bound is strictly less than gq;
for q=1 the only allowed representative is zero, which occurs iff 14|h.
Thus a single frequency covers the full code iff 14d divides w. The same
count holds at the reflected wall, without changing a strict inequality.
Multiples sd with 14 not dividing s block no point at all. Four such body
frequencies and at most six coprime residuals leave a survivor whenever
6ceil(d/7)<d. The seven-residue induction from 37 through 43 is correct;
31 also works, and 32 through 36 are outside gcd(d,42)=1. The bound at
36 fails, so it must not be rounded down universally.

For C=(1,14), the original fourteen safe intervals of frequency fourteen
lose exactly the first and last when frequency one is imposed. Every surviving
endpoint has frequency-one clearance strictly above 1/14. Nonetheless the
removed components were nonempty. The ten-pack C=(1,...,9,14) retains the
same deletion witness at 1/28 and a safe point at 1/11. This validates the
reported obstruction, without turning it into a non-lonely full row.

## 5. Incoming attachment result and the surviving contribution

During audit, current proved THM-4448 was read in full at
`01-canon/theorems/THM-4448-lrc14-general-shore-attachment-and-decoder-pair-cones.md`.
It gives a protected inverse sheet on a base-safe arc and attaches a scaled
shore; its filter-free pair cone is 7h>=45M and 7h>=15E, where M is the
base maximum and E is the tail maximum. In the explicit family M=14c and
E=c. Every h=1+mL satisfies this cone, so existence of that subfamily is
now independently covered by incoming canon. This is not a claim of first
existence closure after that incoming work.

The virtual method retains a different useful coordinate: a specified
wall, an exact owner word, two free labels, a sharp common-sheet margin,
and a construction that succeeds despite failure of every ordinary tail
event and, on a subfamily, every small denominator clock. The arbitrary-h
extension also covers some points outside the displayed uniform cone.
For example d=31 and h=1331 have M=1330 and eighteen surviving virtual
walls, with positive physical cells, whereas 7h=9317<45M=59850. This is
only non-subsumption by the displayed sufficient cone; the stronger
witness-dependent attachment criterion has not been disproved there. Section 4
strengthens this comparison to an atlas-admissible, explicitly addressed,
cofinal h=M+1 class. Its prescribed decoder split is nevertheless excluded
by (D), so being outside those sufficient cones is not a live-frontier claim.

THM-4448's cofinal hostile families show that cross height does not save an
arbitrary prescribed component. They reinforce the need for our existential
wall selection. The connection contract is: source is a labelled fibre above
a body wall, target is an actual physical safe interval, map is the retained
inverse branch, preserved predicate is simultaneous weak/strict safety, and
discarded information is the remainder of the body components. The wall code
and positive local interval are the necessary sidecars. No universal claim
that some such wall must survive is proved.
