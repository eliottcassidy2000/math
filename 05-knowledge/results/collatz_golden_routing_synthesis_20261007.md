## 4. The223/233 golden collision has an exact missing coordinate

Let P_n(z) be the ordinary binary digit polynomial of n. Then

\[
P_{233}(z)-P_{223}(z)=(z^2-z-1)(z^3+z),
\qquad P_{223}(\phi)=P_{233}(\phi)=18+28\phi.
\]

This concerns reading ordinary binary digits at phi, not the positional
base-phi expansion of the integer itself or the golden reading of its

The [golden-source package](golden_guard_arithmetic_20261007.md) repairs the
loss with an integral CRT identity. Since f(z)=z^2-z-1 has f(2)=1,

\[
\mathbb Z[z]/((z-2)f(z))\cong\mathbb Z[\phi]\times\mathbb Z.
\]

Three integer registers suffice: P(phi)=A+Bphi and D=P(2)-A-2B. Appending
a digit d updates them by

\[
(A,B,D)\longmapsto(B+d,A+B,2D+B).
\]

Their linear update has eigenvalues phi, -1/phi, and2. Discarding D erases
the growing binary mode. Addition, polynomial multiplication and exact
guarded receipts compose in this carrier, with normalization and ordered
history retained when the advertised predicate needs them.
