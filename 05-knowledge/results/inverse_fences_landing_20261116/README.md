The inverse problem is no longer just to find an input count with the right residue. For 53→105 not even arbitrary induced padding works: the replacement has to change an old arc or discard something. The packet gives a one-reversal construction attaining the lower bound. Explicit pointed families iterate reverse doubling indefinitely, and changing compensated cores gives three successive plus or four successive minus inverses on entire infinite families. The remaining obligation is an unbounded reservoir that replenishes the 3-adic coefficient, or an entirely different targeted interface. Full Chan–Pak coverage and Collatz convergence remain open.
