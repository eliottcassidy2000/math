# Arithmetic replay of 27 and an entropy cut on the heaviest landings

Use the shortcut Collatz convention T(n)=n/2 for even n and (3n+1)/2 for odd n. A step below the starting integer is not the same as reaching 1.

## Two different ways to reproduce 27

With this convention the first below-start return from 27 is at step 59, to 23; the full trip to 1 takes 70 steps. All starts n=27+2^59 j, j≥0, have the same first 59 parities. Write O_i for the number of odd letters in that prefix. At time i their value is T^i(27)+3^O_i·2^(59−i)j. For i<59 the slope 3^O_i/2^i is at least one; at i=59 it is 3^37/2^59<1. Therefore **every member** of this class first falls below its own start at step 59. It has exact natural density 2^(-59); no claim about its tail after time 59 is needed. The parity and slope claims at the base case are independently replayed in the script.

A second family is guaranteed to **join the same actual tail at 41**, not merely have the same parity word. For any m≥1, choose nonnegative e in the unique class modulo L_m=2·3^(m−1) solving 41·2^e≡−1 mod 3^m. The unit group is generated by 2: its order mod 3 is 2 and v₃(2²−1)=1, giving the order at every level by binomial lifting. Then
`n=2^m(41·2^e+1)/3^m−1`.
The first m steps are odd because T^i(n)+1=3^i·2^(m−i)(41·2^e+1)/3^m for 0≤i≤m, then e halvings hit 41. The least representatives for m=1,…,6 give 27,291,12439,2173995791,1449330527,966220351. For fixed m the number up to X is (log₂ X)/L_m+O_m(1). Each extra forced odd step costs a factor of three in this *one-tail geometric address* frequency. The relative initial rise is at least (3/2)^m. The two constructions here answer different questions: a residue class of exact stopping times, and a geometric family with a certified actual suffix.

The family is more abundant if the chosen join-to-41 can itself vary. By [Krasikov–Lagarias, Theorem 6.1](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/81918), for all large Y at least Y^0.84 starts below Y hit 41. These give at least Y^0.84/O((log Y)^2) distinct odd unit ancestors ≤1.5Y+1: strip powers of 2; if the odd part is divisible by 3, take its next odd iterate. Recovering a source after these two steps costs at most O(log²Y) possibilities, as each dyadic exponent has O(log Y) choices. For each odd unit ancestor u use the formula above with 41 replaced by u and least e<L_m. The construction is injective in u because u is the odd part of T^m(n). For m=floor((1−ε)log₃ log₂ X), with fixed 0<ε<1, the maximum inflation is exp(O(3^m))=X^o(1). Choosing Y=X^(1−o(1)) below that cost yields at least X^(0.84−o(1)) distinct starts below X with a growing prescribed initial ascent and a certified suffix through 41. This is a lower bound, not positive natural density or a record claim.

More generally a fixed shortcut parity word of length k with a odd letters has legal starts in one class mod 2^k, and its ending values form the corresponding increasing ray with stride 3^a. For a>0 every ending value is a 3-unit (since the last odd step made it one, and halving preserves it). Thus joining 41 via some 41·2^e selects a unique exponent class mod 2·3^(a−1). This is a small affine interface between the binary prefix and ternary landing phase. It does not imply that a finite-state machine can invert the complete dynamics: each exact inverse odd-edge step removes one factor of 3 from the modulus at which two potential landings are indistinguishable.

## Near-worst landing fibres are exponentially rarer than the ballot scale

Use [THM-4506](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4506-landing-multiplicity-exact-worst-case-and-recursion-saturation.md) for notation and the inherited shell lemma. Put k=floor(log₂ X), α=log₃2, h*=H₂(α). In this section the sign is + and depth D≥1 is o(k); landing always means first fall below 2^(-D) times the particular dipper. If a landing has m≥α(k−D)−.01k dippers, call it heavily loaded here. **The number of possible earliest dippers in all such fibres with x≤X is O(X^(h*−.001)) along this depth regime.** Consequently even charging O(k) starts per such fibre still has a power saving (with a slightly smaller exponent). This trims the top end of the multiplicity spectrum, not the whole averaged factor.

For a heavy fibre let t≤k be the distance from its earliest dipper to the landing, and O the count of odd letters in that prefix. The shell lemma puts all its dippers in a single factor-two interval, and there must be an odd step between successive ones. Charge every dipper but the earliest to the last intervening odd step. This injects into O letters. During a run of r consecutive odd steps, at most two internal arrivals can lie in the shell: after two odd steps the multiplier exceeds (3/2)^2>2. There can also be at most one charge after the run before another odd step. Thus at least max(0,r−3) odd letters of each run remain uncharged. This implies the number of occurrences of 1111, even allowing overlapping occurrences, is at most O−(m−1). The affine growth bound O<α(t−D) gives
`O−(m−1)≤.01k+1, t≥(1−.01/α)k−O(1)`.

Under independent Bernoulli(α) bits, the indicators of 1111 in disjoint aligned four-blocks are independent with probability α⁴. Put u=(1−.01/α)/4>.245; there are at least uk−O(1) blocks, with at most .01k+1 successes. Hoeffding bounds the probability by 2^(-(e+o(1))k), where e≥2·.245·(.158−.041)^2/ln2>.0096; α⁴>.158 and .01/u<.041. To convert this to counts, each admissible prefix has mass at least α^(m−1)(1−α)^(t−m+1), because O≥m−1 and α>1/2. There are at most 2^(k−t) extensions per prefix, and the resulting exponent increases with t (since −1−log₂(1−α)>0). At t=k and m≥(α−.01)k−o(k), the cost of using this smaller m relative to the ballot exponent is at most .01 log₂(α/(1−α))<.00775. Thus the net exponent is at most h*−.00185+o(1). A polynomial factor for selecting t is negligible. Legal k-letter parity words give one starting residue each modulo 2^k, and X<2^(k+1) gives at most two starts per residue.

The surviving problem is precisely orbit-coupled: fibres of smaller but still linear order can still produce the inherited logarithmic factor. This result does not give a sublinear averaged multiplicity exponent, change the recursion saturation in THM-4506, or move the min-max bounds for 7n±1 in [THM-4508](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4508-structure-of-optimal-sign-strategies-flip-calculus-and-7n-plus-minus-1-at-level-31.md). Finite-level sign-policy bounds and unbounded excursion families are not substitutes for either universal statement.

For arbitrary depths there is a weaker uniform variant: replace .01 in the definition by δ=10^(-5). The extended k-word has at most (1+1/α)δk+O(1) occurrences of 1111, by the lower bound on t and allowing arbitrary suffix. Flipping one 1 per occurrence breaks all, creating none. The language avoiding 1111 has growth root ρ=1.927561… of ρ⁴=ρ³+ρ²+ρ+1 (four final-run states), so the Hamming-ball bound gives count exponent log₂ρ+H₂((1+1/α)δ)=.94721…<h*−.001. This variant does not convert a finite set of locally hostile words into an orbit-coupled estimate.
