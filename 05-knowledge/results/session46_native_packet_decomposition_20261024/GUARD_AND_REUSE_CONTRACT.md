# Two meanings of preserving guards, and two meanings of not reusing a port

This note makes the session's conventions explicit instead of silently choosing
the interpretation under which a positive construction works.

## 1. Nodewise current guards versus every ordinary port phase

Every positive current-cancellation packet here preserves the original full
NODEWISE dyadic harmonic bank. The actual shared owner coordinates, actual
source/path presentations, chosen per-slot X-mod-8 phase and converse are
retained as separate records. No odd-primary claim is made.

This does not mean that every ordinary individual-port phase is unchanged.
There is an exact obstruction to that stronger reading for the target pair
U=1965,V=2234. In the original graph, the function chi=4e_U is harmonic modulo8:
every nonloop incidence at U has even weight, and direct multiplication by the
original Laplacian verifies L chi=0 modulo8. All sources used in these packets
are outside U and V. Thus the ordinary phase chi(source)-chi(target) is4 at a
U port and0 at a V port. Every actual U/V retargeting changes that marked phase.
Other slots cannot cancel a change in a separately marked port's own phase.

The black paired multiplicity2 annihilates this order-two contribution to the
aggregate current: 2 times4 is0 modulo8. Other harmonic characters impose the
nontrivial joint charge equations certified in the packets. Preserving full
current balance while changing some individual phase drops is therefore real,
not an omission in the current test.

If the required rule is instead preservation of EVERY ordinary individual-port
harmonic phase, these particular target-changing routes are forbidden already
by chi. The positive constructions answer the full nodewise-current version;
the stronger per-port version has this explicit impossibility certificate.
The two predicates should not be identified by using the same word 'guard'.

## 2. Unit-capacity injection versus strict temporal nonreturn

The native buffered routes use the standard capacity-one convention: at every
state each selected normalized F/R resource is occupied at most once, its full
closed port set is retained, and no hidden lift is counted as another credit.
An auxiliary slot may borrow its alternate resource and later return to its
original one. Every event and every changed owner clock is charged. Returning
later does not create a second simultaneous resource or erase the work done.

A stronger consumptive convention is also meaningful: once a port is released,
it may never be occupied again during the route. Under this rule, any auxiliary
slot required to finish at its exact original resource cannot change at all.
In a binary original slot, changing twice would likewise revisit its first
resource, so it can change at most once. Every such route is therefore conformal
to the endpoint difference.

Consequently a primitive16- or18-slot binary circuit cannot be decomposed into
smaller events under strict temporal nonreturn, even by adding buffers that
must return exactly. The endpoint-union primitive-atom theorem remains valid
under this stronger convention, but it returns the whole primitive atom.
The smaller buffered events are results about reversible borrowing without
simultaneous reuse, not a way around a consumptive no-return requirement.

## 3. What is needed for a complete wiring statement

These distinctions sit beside the immutable-remainder requirement. A selected
packet's distinct alternative resources must also avoid any fixed occupied
background, or a full compatible reassignment must be provided. Passing all
nodewise currents does not by itself authenticate that remainder. Likewise an
actual source extension must transport its original guard and owner records;
new abstract labels or spare multiplicities are not native ports.

The session therefore supplies positive constructions and precise obstructions
for both stronger interpretations, rather than presenting an unqualified
all-size rewiring theorem.
