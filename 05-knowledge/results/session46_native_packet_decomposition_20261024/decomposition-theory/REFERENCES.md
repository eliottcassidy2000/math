# References and provenance

Checked 24 October 2026. The all-size arguments in THEOREM.md are supplied
in full; no inference depends on access to a paywalled proof.

1. John E. Olson, *A combinatorial problem on finite Abelian groups, I*,
   Journal of Number Theory 1 (1969), 8–10.
   DOI: [10.1016/0022-314X(69)90021-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-314X(69)90021-3).
   [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022314X69900213).
   The indexed publisher abstract explicitly identifies the p-group zero-sum
   problem. Direct full-page retrieval returned HTTP 403 in this environment.
   The elementary augmentation-ideal proof in Lemma 2.1 independently proves
   the exact formula used here; it is the classical group-algebra mechanism,
   not a claimed new Davenport-constant theorem.

2. J. A. De Loera, R. Hemmecke, S. Onn, U. G. Rothblum and R. Weismantel,
   *Convex Integer Maximization via Graver Bases*.
   [Author-hosted primary research PDF](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~deloera/RECENT_WORK/nfold2.pdf).
   Section 2, pages 8–9 of the PDF, defines conformality and the Graver basis
   and proves the conformal decomposition used in Lemma 2.3. Theorem 4 here
   gives the direct finite-lattice/congruence argument, including its box
   consequence, rather than claiming the general integer-programming complexity
   results of that paper for native packet generation.
