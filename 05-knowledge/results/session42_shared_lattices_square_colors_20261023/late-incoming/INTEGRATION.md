## 3. Prefix banks need completeness, not merely valid samples

A nonempty full frame solution set is rH. The chain H^(i) fixing the first i coordinates has images K_i=pr_i H^(i−1). Choosing one full compatible lift L_(i,a) for every a in K_i yields the unique ordered product L_(1,a1)...L_(t,at). Successive left removal proves uniqueness without assuming commutativity. Hence |H|=product_i|K_i| and at most sum_i(|G_i|−1) nonidentity full tuples suffice.

The whole tuples and their multiplication order are essential. A bank containing only some successful witnesses certifies at most a subgroup unless every image K_i is certified complete. Feasibility-oracle extraction in the explicit domains supplies this missing coverage condition. These are standard chain/transversal tools applied in actual source/port coordinates, not independent local-edge choices.
