## A bookkeeping correction made before publication

The first draft of the compensator ledger labelled signed source-potential
sums as the separate old-block and helper physical currents. Their TOTAL was
correct, but each partial block temporarily changes its target count. The
diagonal contribution must therefore be included separately:

    old U row = 2[(old V sum - 1016) - 146*(old V count - 8)],
    helper U row = 2[helper V sum - 146*(helper V count)].

The first failed implication was identifying a partial source sum with a
Laplacian row while dropping that partial degree change. The repaired ledger
retains source sums, target counts, actual U/V rows and finite-character
residuals separately. The accepted-state universe, exact total cancellation,
connectedness thresholds and route remain unchanged. This was caught during
root review of an unpublished candidate; no prior proved statement was used
to justify the incorrect split.

## Scope gates

- Source counts16/18/28 are numbers of selected source CLASSES, all at
  tournament order8. They are not tournament orders.
- Every original dyadic AGGREGATE node-current guard is retained. The chosen
  per-slot X phase modulo8 is retained separately. Universal per-slot phase
  equality, odd-primary guards and arbitrary larger unscaled-X moduli are
  not claimed.
- A packet event is an atomic update of its supplied slots. No resource is
  double-occupied at an arrival, and no nonexistent kernel capacity is spent.
  A genuine port can be revisited at a later time. This is not a continuous
  schedule that first allocates every replacement before freeing anything.
- Ordinary owner-clock work76 for the selected five-event route becomes152
  if every reflected converse occurrence is counted separately. Shared
  variables, distinct owners and slot-clock occurrences are different costs.
- The complete closed resource reservoir is reserved against a fixed remainder.
  These partial-packet trades are not a replacement of the fully occupied
  canonical atlas, whose complement rule is already fixed.
- Extra native workspace changes the source by adjoining an actual block.
  It cannot be advertised as a new empty port in an unchanged atlas. Changing
  shared owners or aggregate charges are outside the independent one-slot
  theorem unless separately authenticated.
- The all-size endpoint-filter representation is exact for supplied endpoints
  and supplied full local records; it does not prove universal record supply.
