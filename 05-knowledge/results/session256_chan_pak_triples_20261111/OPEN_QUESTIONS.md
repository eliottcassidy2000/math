# Next constructive questions

These are research directions, not proved coverage or unrequested ongoing
work. Chan–Pak, five-core coverage and reusable ternary returns remain OPEN.

## 1. Turn finite contextual equivalence into a source-switch operation

Eight flank probes and c+1 cyclic probes determine their respective full
responses. Seek two actual, named source graphs with the same target H but
different useful branch availability, then give a legal move between them
that retains the exact interface needed by the next step.

The new Berggren H9 collision supplies a small training case: B,C,AA have
three different A-child responses. The old two H9 cores supply a stronger
five-core case. A switch must specify its source, cut, endpoint ports and
operation; an equality of eight signatures alone is a response equivalence,
not a physical edit recipe. Cheapest test: apply the candidate move to the
three H9 examples and recount the complete probe bank, not only H.

## 2. Add the smallest contextual repair that escapes the morphic holes

The complete modulo16 certificate gives concrete obstructions to the
single-spinor morphic grammar, including13 and15. Add one chosen core or
one actual repair operation and rebuild the positive-polynomial carrier.
Can the new grammar hit those values while keeping a finite-dimensional
positive representation and a usable return chart?

The goal is not another finite coverage record. A useful result is an
explicit repair rule with a proved domain and downstream response. Record
whether the rule changes the primitive-triple decoder or merely augments
it. Arbitrary adjacency edits do not automatically act polynomially on the
existing32-state carrier.

## 3. Prove a bound on one scalar target fibre, not every joint residue

The Pareto machine solves exact minima but gives no uniform bound. For each
allowed odd N, find a core/cut and a retained state in the class H≡N modM
with H<=N for some M>N. This would prove equality and five-core coverage.

Do not prescribe unnecessary auxiliary residues. Keep chart and target,
then minimize height or count over the remaining fibre. The incoming
bounded-suffix theorem rules out one especially tempting uniform policy;
a successful route must use an operation outside that exact hypothesis.

## 4. Use observer changes to transport a native norm

For each small fixed U, compute the integral positive form W_U and the
subset of vectors realized as truncated tournament path-cover profiles.
Which norm-preserving or norm-controlled transformations have actual graph
lifts? The K1 form has discriminant2, so Gaussian rotation is not a valid
operation on it without an explicit change of form and integrality data.

A successful result could be an infinite Pell-type family of source repairs
or an exact obstruction to a proposed primitive-Pythagorean transplant.
Search actual profiles, not arbitrary integer points on a quadratic form.

## 5. Extend controlled packing without losing positivity

The TT2 observer gives a complete packing-two family. Replace it by the
smallest strong observer, or by a source-sensitive bank, and retain the
sharp finite response interface. Seek new exact arithmetic images or
low-order recipes that the five-core one-apex grammar cannot express
compactly. Any claim of new coverage must compare with the inherited
one-apex and recursive grammars rather than with a naive baseline.

The incoming endpoint-fixer theorem supplies an additional source filter:
nonprime Paley bases and certain recursive prime-block bases retain a
coefficient-wise divisor under small extensions, even for asymmetric
children. Apply that obstruction before searching attachment masks; changing
an arithmetic label does not change the physical source.

## 6. Separate three different finiteness claims

For every new construction record explicitly which statement is proved:

1. finitely many residues/driver states;
2. finitely many Pareto-minimal integer carriers per residue (Dickson);
3. a uniform bounded integer or graph carrier for all targets.

The first two hold in this packet's positive systems. The third does not
follow from either, and would be the real new ingredient in a universal
Chan–Pak construction.
