## 2. Three ordered runs produce six labelings and two different lattices

An unordered partition into three nonempty directed paths has exactly3!=6
orders. Hence Q_3(S) is divisible by 6. The inherited ordered-run compiler

    E = [[1,0,0],[1,2,0],[1,6,6]],  SNF(E)=(1,2,6).

Its index is12. This is **not** the index 54 S3 Fourier-gluing lattice in
`overnight_rank_return_20261029/THREE_RUN.md`, whose Smith factors are
`1,1,1,3,3,6`. Both remember integral realization at 2 and3; their source
modules, maps and indices differ.

There is also a physical growth operation on arbitrary children: append one
universal sink, S->S▷{z}. Its first three nonempty ordered path counts obey

    Q' = G Q,  G=[[1,0,0],[2,2,0],[0,3,3]].              (S1)

The new vertex either ends one of r existing paths (r choices), or is its
own singleton path (r choices of slot). This proves
`Q'_r=r Q_r+r Q_(r-1)`. Passing to empty-allowed slots with the unimodular
matrix `P=[[1,0,0],[2,1,0],[3,3,1]]` gives

    P G P^(-1)=diag(1,2,3),
    SNF(G^t)=(1,1,6^t).                                  (S2)

Thus an inverse t-step padding has one cyclic integral obstruction of size
6^t, equivalently the two coprime guards that F_2 is divisible by 2^t and
F_3 by 3^t. These guards concern formal integer profiles; a valid profile
still needs an actual child relation. The diagonal ordinal-join mechanism
is inherited from **THM-3166**,
`01-canon/theorems/THM-3166-falling-factorial-order-join-path-colour-transform.md`;
S1/S2 provide its concrete use in the present
three-response grammar, not a new claim of the general diagonalization.
The universal complement square in G4 commutes with padding the child,
but its correction remains cH(S). Padding does not manufacture a unit-H

## 5. Catalan contributes the power-gap question; the carry remains essential

Mihailescu's published theorem classifies consecutive nontrivial perfect
powers. The primary publication is [Primary cyclotomic units and a proof of
Catalan's conjecture](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.2004.048), J. reine angew.
Math.572 (2004),167--195. The original publisher record was recovered; the
proof text was not available there. We also read the opening theorem and
method statement of the authors' later primary paper,
[Liu--Mihailescu, An alternative proof of the Catalan conjecture](https://webbut.unitbv.ro/index.php/Series_III/article/download/8768/6170/17457),
2025. Its argument extracts a character controlling Galois exponents of roots
of unity. We do not import or re-audit that proof as a tournament theorem.

The exact operational parallel is a **character plus a translation cocycle**:
the branch multiplier is3^k, while the ordered carry is C(w). Forgetting
C(w) is not licensed by multiplicative independence or by the unit-gap
theorem. The September
`catalan_elliptic_20260921_catalan.md` and
`decoder_mahler_catalan_20260925.md` already make this distinction for
Collatz/Mahler cylinders. Their hostile is the non-unit gap139 canceled by
an ordered carry, and their two same-clock words01/10 have different carries.

R2 uses only elementary multiplicative independence to exclude a finite
dyadic-ray bank. R1 retains the entire translation and solves every possible
intersection for the current families. R3 then retains the order of the
offset commands. These are actual uses of the old carry mechanism, rather
than treating Catalan's theorem as a convergence or spectrum theorem.
