## 1. Nonlinear return map at 19

The exact cycle modulo 19 is 2,3,7,5. With g=f^4 and h=f^12,

    g(2)−2 = 5·19²,       g'(2)=16575 ≡7 (mod 19),
    h(2)−2 ≡17·19³ (mod 19⁴),       h'(2)≡1 (mod 19).

The multiplier 7 has order 3. Taylor expansion of g on 2+19² Z
modulo 19³ gives displacement 5·19² Σ_(i<r)7^i after r returns.
This vanishes iff 3 divides r. Hence every return at level at least 3
has time divisible by 12; merely observing a square return would not
justify an ordinary factor-19 lift from level 2.

The stated lifting lemma is correct with its guards **p odd and s≥2**.
If H'(a)=1 mod p and v_p(H(a)−a)=s, the quadratic Taylor remainder
vanishes modulo p^(s+2). The p-term geometric sum of the derivative is
p mod p², since p is odd. This proves one exact valuation increment.
The derivative condition persists for H^p, so induction handles all p
powers. Applying the prime-to-p case to the resulting iterate handles
all t. This proves v_p(H^t(a)−a)=s+v_p(t), not just an upper bound.
Consequently the exact period is 4 for k≤2 and 12·19^(k−3) for k≥3.
A return to the initial seed proves pure periodicity without requiring f
to be a permutation of the entire residue ring.

The support corollary is also correct: reduction gives inclusion in the
full inverse image of the twelve mod-19³ points; equal cardinalities give
equality. This yields density of the integer orbit in exactly those twelve
19-adic balls and conditioned Haar frequencies on cylinders. It does not
identify the countable orbit with its uncountable closure. A fiber's Fourier
factor is Σ_t exp(2πi a t/19^(k−3)), zero whenever 19^(k−3) does not divide a.
The lower-level support obstruction and deterministic consecutive relation
remain. Finally q_19(2) is nonzero: this dynamical delay is not a binary

## 3. Quotient, carry, and affine identity tests

The Fermat quotient is well-defined for a unit residue modulo p² and
is an additive homomorphism from multiplication to F_p, including
negative integer powers. Thus q_p(3^J2^(−D))=Jq_p(3)−Dq_p(2) requires
p≠2,3. Under the essential antecedent α=1 mod p, writing α=1+pu
gives q_p(α)=−u mod p. Dropping that antecedent would make the slope
lift claim false: quotient zero also includes other prime-to-p torsion.

For a word already giving the identity modulo p, the carry condition
C=0 mod p² and quotient-zero condition are jointly necessary and
sufficient for the identity modulo p². The sign ε changes the additive
coefficient but not its divisibility. Iteration of the formal affine map
gives n+t p(un+v) mod p². Its fixed set has p points if u≠0, none if
u=0,v≠0, and p² if u=v=0; other point periods are p. These formal
iterates do not bypass exact 2-adic guards of repeated native words.

## 6. Focused follow-up: exact Fourier support (final sharpening)

**PASS.** The sharpened iff is valid: for k>3 the Fourier coefficient
vanishes exactly when 19^(k−3) does not divide the frequency. At a
surviving frequency the geometric factor leaves 19^(k−3) times a
12-term polynomial P evaluated at a root of 19-power order. For a
nontrivial root, its minimal polynomial is Φ_(19^t). Monic polynomial
division gives an integer quotient if it divides P over Q; evaluating
at 1 would force 19 to divide 12. The trivial root gives 12 directly.
The supplied Eisenstein proof of irreducibility is correct, including
t=1. Repeated exponents and reduction of exponents modulo the root
order cause no problem, since P(1) remains 12.

The producer's sparse cyclotomic remainder calculation is correct.

q/19 for every q=19^t arising from all 6,859 low-digit frequencies:
a degree less than q polynomial divisible by Φ_q must have equal
coefficients in each such 19-element coset. A selected occupied coset
has unequal coefficients in every nontrivial case. The trivial
frequency is checked separately. Thus all 6,859 coefficients are
nonzero using exact integer arithmetic, without floating point or
producer imports. The source manifest and normal/-O receipts were
refreshed after this proof/checker extension.
