## What transfers toward Collatz

A fixed exact valuation cylinder gives perfectly uniform source and endpoint
residues modulo any modulus coprime to 6, but the pair occupies an affine graph
with only m points out of m² possibilities. Its joint Fourier correlations are
explicitly nonzero on a whole line. This is the same information issue exposed
by tournament ports: averaging each port separately can erase a relation that
the next operation needs.

The useful next step is therefore to bound the remaining character or carry
obstructions under the **actual guarded dynamics**, not to assume that finite
residue uniformity supplies a random law for native trajectories. Original-source
payment and global Collatz convergence remain open. The equality
139=3^7−2^11 is retained as an exact arithmetic coincidence with its Sylvester
role; no causal mechanism linking those two occurrences was proved.
