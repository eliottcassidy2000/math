# A unifying mechanism: constraints first, cancellation on the remaining fibers

**PROVED standard finite Fourier identities with scoped applications.** This is
an organizing synthesis, not a new general theory claimed to supersede Fourier
analysis, character theory, or arithmetic dynamics. Distribution assumptions
are stated separately from algebraic constraints.

## 1. Uniform lifts kill exactly the non-quotient observables

Let G be a finite abelian group, pi:G→H a surjective homomorphism with kernel K,
and C a nonempty subset of H. Put uniform mass on pi^(-1)(C). For a character
chi of G, its expectation is zero if chi is nontrivial on K. Otherwise chi
factors as a character bar-chi of H and its expectation equals the average
of bar-chi on C.

Proof: each fiber is gK. Its character sum is chi(g) times sum_(h in K)chi(h).
If chi(h0)≠1, translation by h0 multiplies the latter sum by chi(h0) without
changing it, so it is zero. If chi is trivial on K, its value is constant on
each fiber and the equal fiber sizes cancel in normalization.

This identifies both the canceled observables and the surviving ones. It does
not say the surviving averages are small: C may be one point, a code, or an
arithmetic obstruction. A one-dimensional residue statistic can be perfectly
uniform while joint observations are completely constrained.

The analogous finite-group statement is also exact. If K is normal in a
finite group G and rho is a complex irreducible representation, averaging
rho over K projects onto its K-fixed vectors. That subspace is G-invariant,
so irreducibility makes the projector either zero or the identity. Uniform
lifting from G/K annihilates the former representations; the latter factor
through G/K. This is a quotient statement, not a statement about restricting
a representation to an arbitrary subgroup.

## 2. Four exact arithmetic manifestations

### 2.1 Squareclass colors

For fixed integer bases, multiplicative quadratic characters give sign bits
y=s+Vz. Uniform local residue choices make z uniform; the sign law is uniform
on the affine code s+im(V). Its binary Fourier coefficient at u is

    E(-1)^(u·y) = (-1)^(u·s)  if V^t u=0,
                  0          otherwise.

The surviving characters are precisely the exact squareclass relations.
For the requested bases(4,2,5,3,7,13), one port is a square and the other
five prime ports are independent squareclasses:32 possible colors, not64.
This explains one exact dependence; it does not explain why those labels
occur in a Monster valuation chain. The two constructions must be identified
before comparing them.

For fixed prime moduli, CRT gives equal residue counts and fixed-modulus PNT
transfers them to prime frequencies. The separate quadratic large sieve
controls moving prime-pair edges. Together these prove colored rooted
quasirandomness, with root-root relations fixed and growing root sets excluded
from the stated uniformity. See [the character proof](characters/CHARACTER_STRUCTURE.md).

### 2.2 The Sylvester orbit at19

For f(x)=x²−x+1 and seed2, the orbit modulo19^k for k>=3 is the full inverse
image of twelve classes C modulo19³. This follows from the proved period
12·19^(k−3), not from a random-digits hypothesis. Its uniform periodic law
therefore kills every frequency not divisible by19^(k−3). The low-digit
support remains highly constrained: Haar mass12/6859 in the19-adic integers.
Moreover the low-digit Fourier coefficients are all nonzero, since a sum
of12 roots of19-power order cannot vanish: the relevant cyclotomic polynomial
has value19 at1 and would force19 to divide12.

Thus the exact Fourier support is the annihilator of the lift kernel. High
digits are uniform on admissible fibers, but time-adjacent samples are not
independent because the next value is exactly f of the current value.
See [the all-level proof](recursions/RECURSIONS_AND_19.md).

### 2.3 Wieferich defects and the two-clock repair

At p=1093 or3511, base2 has no first-level period growth. The Fermat-quotient
character q_p is trivial on the subgroup generated by2 modulo p². This is a
specific surviving obstruction, not random failure of every observation.
Base3 has a nonzero Fermat quotient at both primes and supplies the missing
p-element kernel. Consequently H_k=<2,3> modulo p^k is the full inverse image
of H_1 for every k. Uniformity on H_k therefore kills all characters
nontrivial on that reduction kernel.

At1093 the remaining index is3; at3511 the joint subgroup is the entire unit
group. A formal uniform pair of exponent clocks maps uniformly to H_k because
all fibers of the homomorphism from a full exponent torus have equal size.
Actual Collatz clocks are not such a freely sampled torus. Exact source guards
and the additive carry are still required. See [orders and carries](orders/ORDERS_CARRIES_AND_CRT.md).

### 2.4 Fixed-word Collatz has uniform marginals and a thin joint law

An exact valuation cylinder n=r+2^(D+1)t has endpoint y=h+2·3^J t for the
usual3n+1 map. Modulo m coprime to6, t uniform modulo m gives uniform n and uniform y.
But the joint pair lies on the affine graph y=alpha n+beta modulo m, with
alpha=3^J 2^(-D) and beta=C2^(-D). Thus, with e_m(x)=exp(2πix/m),

    E e_m(u n+v y) = e_m(v beta) if u+alpha v=0 modulo m,
                    0          otherwise.

This follows by summing a geometric progression over n. Exactly m of the
m² frequencies survive, and the pair has only m possible values. Every
nontrivial one-coordinate Fourier coefficient vanishes, yet an entire line
of joint correlations is perfectly retained. This is the precise arithmetic
analogue of the tournament port warning: marginal responses do not determine
joint continuation data. The formula refers to a fixed-word source ensemble,
not a temporal equidistribution theorem for one Collatz trajectory.

## 3. Where the same algebra does not prove a distribution theorem

Euclid product recursions impose character syndromes on their factors:
Sylvester has offset1 and Fermat offset2. These are exact constraints but do
not prove the factors are uniformly distributed inside the compatible code.
Indeed the Sylvester terms that are themselves odd primes form a transitive
Legendre tournament. Assuming that a sparse recursively selected sample
inherits prime-prefix quasirandomness would contradict that elementary fact.
The existence of infinitely many such prime terms is not required or claimed.
See [factor-sign constraints](conditioning/EUCLID_OFFSET_SYNDROMES.md).

Similarly, a prime admitting roots of Phi6 need not be reached by its seed2
orbit. Prime19 admits roots, but the displayed four-cycle excludes zero.
Splitting, reachability, and equidistribution are different questions.

For the Monster and Baby Monster, large minimum nontrivial complex
representation degree bounds product correlations of dense subsets. This is
another Fourier mechanism, now involving matrix coefficients. Their local
Paley quotient has one-dimensional characters, so that lower bound does not
survive subgroup restriction. Nevertheless a particular Paley connection set
can have small Fourier coefficients inside an abelian additive group whose
minimum nontrivial degree is1. One mechanism constrains all subsets by the
representation theory of the group; the other constrains one chosen function.
See [the group proof and source ledger](groups/GROUPS_AND_RECOVERED_PRIMES.md).

## 4. Research consequences, not a convergence claim

A useful future program asks four separate questions for each proposed
quasirandom model:

1. What exact character relations or quotient coordinates force its support?
2. Which ensemble is demonstrably uniform or mixing on that support?
3. Which joint observables survive, even when every marginal looks uniform?
4. Which information is lost when changing clocks, roots, subgroup, or carrier?

The session supplies exact answers for the objects above. It does not supply
a random law for native Collatz clocks, a source-payment bound, a theorem on
all Sylvester prime factors, or a Monster explanation of every recurring
small number. The constructive gain is sharper: we can identify the exact
unmixed coordinates before spending effort on a cancellation estimate that
those coordinates make impossible.
