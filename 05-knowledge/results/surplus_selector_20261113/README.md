## The new all-size repair

[REPAIR23](REPAIR23.md) gives two strong six-vertex cores with the same
root star and profiles(23,2,9,60) and(23,2,11,60). One off-root arc exchanges
them. For **any nonempty prefix L** with h=H(L), f=P_L(2) and gcd(h,30f)=1,
both constructions have the same next target

    M=23h+60f.

Choosing the q9 core removes exactly the possible factor7 obstruction to
the inherited retained-chain entry; its q11 partner is obstructed precisely
when f≡h mod7. The marked ports and every later permitted chain step are
proved legal. A third23-core compensates a halved boundary response with
one extra singleton, yielding another primitive preparation at the same M.
Primitive entry is distinct from unit readiness; later chain steps change M.

Taking prefixes built from R6 and singletons gives

    M_(a,b)=23·17^b+60·224^b2^a,      a,b≥0, a+b>0.

This is an actual family with unbounded nontrivial core content, known
endpoint preparation and renewable entry. It is not contained in any
finite union of fixed dyadic rays. Its first transitive-prefix targets
are143,263,503,983,1943,3863,7703.

primitive chart coordinates and residue coverage do not supply this lift.
