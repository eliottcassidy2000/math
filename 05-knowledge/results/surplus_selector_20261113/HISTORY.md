## What failed, and what survived

1. **Common module inflation.** All six pure-q primitive repairs through7
   were aligned by the minimum root-fixing arc distance and tested at every
   nonroot vertex for transitive widths1,2,3, stopping at first failure.
   None survived. This excludes only these selected alignments/one-module
   probes. It led to whole-prefix multiplication and compensated core
   replacement, whose marked response has an all-size proof.
2. **One universally better core.** The q11 core improves the unprefixed
   first-birth gcd3→1, but q9 is the primitive choice in the guarded later
   prefix/chain context. A preference must specify its consumer.
3. **Formal fibre completeness.** The exact current/next kernel, with odd
   deletion counts, gives c+2d≡M+2 mod4. This is a lattice, not a realization
   theorem. The new response matrix records which actual contexts transport
   an available direction. Missing sources and missing consumers remain
   different obstructions.
4. **Every cyclic three-in-neighbourhood is a chain chart.** Refuted by
   rows(2,20,25,19,1), root4, profile(9,1,5,23). Its three predecessors form
   a cycle, but h-q-3s=1 and F-4q-2s=1. The missing data are their external
   incidences. This was an exploratory candidate, not promoted canon.
5. **A possible degree-sensitive insertion inequality.** The candidate
   H(T)-H(T-r)≥d^-(r)s_T(r) survived all2618 rooted strong classes through7
   and a12500-evaluation directed hill search at orders8..12. It remains
   **OPEN**, is not a dependency, and has no claimed literature priority.
   The insertion cut formula is known; the missing step is an injection
   paying d^- distinct paths per root-start path. A finite search does not
   supply that injection.

## Board at close and next cheap tests

The exact selector and 23-core repair join source existence with a proved
entry guard on a sparse language. The context matrix joins fibre geometry
to actual composition. The slope graph exposes unresolved consumers1/3,
4/9,6/11, while the original target problem still needs sources. The Collatz
lane supplies exact residue/carry coordinates and the cycle polynomial
explains a mod3 phase, without identifying the dynamics.

The next useful tests are targeted: an actual context accepting one of those
three slopes; a theorem excluding it at all orders; or a new target family
with a physical source and an independently verified endpoint terminal.
Merely enlarging scalar coverage or taking more formal integer combinations
does not settle those obligations. No new META-PATTERNS card is promoted:
the moves reuse existing observer-loss and physical-lifting principles.
