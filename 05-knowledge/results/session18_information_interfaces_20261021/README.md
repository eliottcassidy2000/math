# Session 18: information-preserving interfaces and the debts they leave

Status: reviewed research synthesis of thirteen independently audited, published result packets. Their proofs and audit scopes are authoritative. This document adds no theorem identifier or claim of global completion.

## 1. The useful meaning of information flow

A structural analogy earns its place here only after specifying the carrier, map, retained information, and obstruction. This session uses four such interfaces:

1. **Ordered graph to path-marked tournament.** A graph bit at (i,j) becomes the free tournament arc (i,j+1), while the consecutive Hamiltonian arcs are fixed. Graph complementation reverses exactly the free arcs. Vertex-order reflection becomes tournament converse followed by path reversal. These are exact maps on marked labelled objects, not automatic maps of unmarked isomorphism classes.
2. **Labelled description to isomorphism class.** Near the zero-heavy boundary, most descriptions have unique ownership. At the first collision, two P3 descriptions and one matching description describe one externally pointed triangle. The retained port, not merely the unpointed triangle, determines the correct correction.
3. **Original source to transported charge.** A source is never replaced by an arbitrary vector. Its actual primitive successor, first-arrival terminal, P-depth, full integer clock and joint mod105 register remain available. Zero charge at a chosen frequency is not the same as zero clock polynomial.
4. **Quadratic class to genus character.** Genus characters can lose information: the kernel is the square subgroup. The conductor is part of the carrier and cannot be discarded because the ambient quadratic field looks familiar.

These interfaces resemble lossless channels, quotient channels, and error syndromes. Those words describe the explicit algebra below; they are not new physical assumptions.

## 2. Tournament repairs: what is settled and what is not

The endpoint-three order-10 gap is closed by a finite symbolic certificate over 168 affine cells, containing 86,016 marked labelled source presentations. Of 504 blue-step systems, 501 are inconsistent and three singleton systems have literal repairs. Together with the inherited larger-order argument, this gives the unfiltered endpoint-(n-4,3) detours for n>=10.

The endpoint-four work provides structural families for n>=15, not a complete classification. The base, end-pair and odd-prefix addenda preserve actual ports and verify each proposed Hamiltonian ordering. The odd-prefix construction repairs all four recorded hostile examples of the prior template. A residual family remains, described in the frontier packet by closed-middle conditions, exceptional port positions and failure of all applicable odd-prefix guards. Failure of a template is not a bridge certificate.

The graph/tournament trijection is valuable here because it turns a proposed path repair into explicit bit equations. A reflection-product automorphism can then contradict a degree budget or an even-order action. No inference from counts alone to unique metagraph wiring is made. Global black-core connectivity and full metagraph realizability remain open.

## 3. The zero-heavy boundary and physical palette ports

The feedback-arc-budget parameter k is not a feedback-vertex parameter. In the near-maximal corridor the deficit decomposes as

    delta = (k+1)d + k h + U,

where d counts endpoint collisions, h missing gaps, and U weight deficit. Incoming work already established the deficit-k boundary and the first shared-endpoint onset at k+1; those facts are inherited, not claimed anew.

The checked extension gives the exact corridor through delta<=2k-1 for k>=3. At delta=2k, k>=4, the correction is L+K-2H. In the general count it simplifies to subtracting 3(k-1)(2k-3)!! from the corridor expression. The reason is an exact owner fibre of size three: C,C,D. The external marked port is fixed throughout the identification.

For the geometric palette gadget, N copies sharing only a root retain all 9N physical ports. If m_c is the number of prescribed palettes containing colour c, the exact minimum port-repair distance is N-max_c m_c; its maximum is floor(N/2). This is robust conditional palette forcing. The gadget remains four-colourable, so it is not unconditional six-colour forcing.

## 4. Source 63: a paid cone is not universal coverage

The separator-normalized cone has theta=2/3 and exact sharp parameter

    c = 14060731968178560 / 14425381885981321 < 1.

It covers the actual source-63 block without inserting a fictitious separator into that orbit. Composition supports arbitrarily deep finite block constructions with the stated terminal descent guard. A separate explicit infinite family grounds to 61 and then ROOT; its literal and odd-step exit clocks are distinguished.

The 63 retained variable-valuation families also admit a common positive-envelope barrier, approximately 8.9260112359. Named children are actually grounded. This does not ground every parameter in every half-family. An explicit infinite exponent progression remains outside the finite covering atlas and the retained guards. Thus paid scalar debt, coverage of source classes and grounding of all children are three different obligations.

## 5. The annulus budget: retain all cross terms

For the arrived-frontier observable, with original new sources retained, the exact increment is

    Delta = M_new + C_new,new + C_old,new,
    153N - Delta = R_moving - C_new,new - C_old,new.

This is not a fibrewise inequality. Some individual fibres have positive cross debt even where the total budget passes. Mixed old/new zero packets retain an old-baseline correction; removing their total zero does not make that correction disappear.

The contiguous finite range N=4096,...,16384 was checked at every finite P-depth arrival horizon, with additional exact rays and source recurrences. The audit independently rebuilt selected universes and checked the stored sweep consequences; it did not independently rerun the full contiguous producer sweep. Uniformity for unbounded N>=4096 remains open. The claim for every N>=1 is false: N=7 at depth8 has increment1084>153*7=1071. The full moving-endpoint Gram observable is a different unpaid target.

The independently reviewed phase-lift packet supplies a native cancellation mechanism rather than another census. The residue-guarded affine lifts 64s+35 or 64s+49 preserve the primitive successor and add exactly six clock ticks. Away from explicit band crossings their charges rotate by an Eisenstein unit. Each exceptional source has exactly one norm-one defect; there are only logarithmically many bad phase edges at a scale. Three good consecutive lifted sources outside the fixed frontier cancel synchronously at their common P-depth and already cancel after their first common-target step, without assuming eventual ROOT arrival.

However, there may be many short inverse chains. Sparse bad edges do not imply a small residual energy. A good pair pays its own new diagonal but leaves its interaction with the rest of the terminal vector. Mixed annulus membership still needs the old-baseline correction. Those are the next precise unpaid obligations.

## 6. Why actual clocks cannot be replaced by a finite static register

The exact family s_j=(32*64^j-5)/9 has primitive successor1 and native edge clock6j+3. Its fully odd map uses a different pair of valuation costs, with ROOT shortcut clock6j+5. After a finite frontier is passed, the first primitive arrival has depth1 but unbounded integer clock.

For any fixed modulus, suitable distinct members have the same residue and different clocks. In particular modulo840 the period-six parameter shift preserves the register while increasing the clock by36. No finite static observer can reconstruct all these exact clocks. This is not an obstruction to finite alphabets with unbounded words, counters, or parameterized rules, and not an impossibility theorem for convergence proofs.

Likewise a good three-source phase packet has clock polynomial

    z^t A (1 + omega^c z^6 + omega^(2c) z^12).

It vanishes at z=1 and z=-1 but is not identically zero when A is nonzero. A P-depth horizon sees the simultaneous packet; an integer-clock cutoff can expose a partial packet. “Every finite horizon” must name its clock.

## 7. The two reflections and the arithmetic conductor

For the divisor carrier of p^k q r with both 1 and p^k q r excluded, exponent complementation and exchange of q,r are commuting involutions. At k=2 their two views yield the 3-4-3 and 4-2-4 arrangements. The product has fixed points, whereas graph complementation followed by vertex-order reversal has none. Consequently no equivariant map from the original ten states can preserve both involutions. Splitting the two exceptional states gives a minimal twelve-state resolution and an explicit graph/tournament realization. This is a structural obstruction, not a failure to find a clever labelling.

The independently reviewed norm-ball packet offers a second exact realization. With omega^2+omega+1=0,

    (a,b,c) -> a-kc+k(b-c)omega

maps the divisor states onto all nonzero elements of norm at most k^2 in the order Z+kZomega. The two reflections become negation and complex conjugation. Only the six points in the conductor ideal k Z[omega] stay within this order under unit rotation. Thus the natural rotor does not preserve the entire ten-state arithmetic carrier. The obstruction is arithmetic and explicit.

The idoneal channel packet independently distinguishes d=63, whose class group is cyclic of order4 and whose genus loses one bit, from d=273, whose class group is elementary abelian of order8 and is exactly resolved by three genus characters. They have the same joint mod15/mod35 register but different class-group information and native successors/clocks. An eight-state graph code exists for the latter; it does not make the resulting tournaments regular.

For even k, the norm-ball order corresponds to d=3(k/2)^2. The elementary classification within this family is k=2,4,8, using explicit quadratic forms and the ring-class formula; it does not depend on completeness of the global 65-element list. The separate analytic completeness claim cited in prior repository work has not been re-proved in this session.

## 8. Next structural targets

1. Pay the residual terminal coherence after the no-reuse phase-packet compiler, including short chains, unit defects and mixed old/new baselines, without changing the153N budget.
2. Close or structurally obstruct the precisely retained endpoint-four residual, rather than treating failed templates as bridges.
3. Extend zero-heavy ownership beyond the first pointed-triangle collision by retaining boundary ports before quotienting.
4. Prove coverage and grounding for classes outside the current source-63 atlas, not just composition stability inside a cone.
5. Seek complete metagraph wiring criteria carrying isomorphism ownership and the two involutions, not only total node and edge counts.

No result here settles Collatz, universal annulus bounds, all bridges, global black-core connectivity, or complete tournament structure. The progress consists of exact interfaces, proved local repairs, explicit obstructions, and smaller, named remaining debts.
