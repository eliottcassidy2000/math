# FRACTRAN, physical register tapes, and why a positive reader cannot decode the target

**Status.** The physical compiler and output bound below are proved. They are not a proof of Chan–Pak or an undecidability reduction for its spectrum.

## An unconditional compiler for every finite FRACTRAN program

Let the program use reduced fractions supported on primes p_0,...,p_(s-1), including all register primes used in the input. Let F_i = 2^(2^i)+1. They are pairwise coprime, as the product of the earlier F_i equals F_j-2. No primality of later Fermat numbers is needed.

Put A_i = C3[TT_(2^i),1,1], taking a directed cycle among the three modules. These are strong tournaments with H(A_i)=F_i. Indeed, in the general C3[TT_m,b,c] there are three cyclic rotations of the three single-run blocks, and a path meeting TT_m twice has word A,b,c,A and exactly 2^m-2 ways to choose the nonempty first run; each part has the unique increasing internal order. No other block words can be walks covering all three modules. Thus H=2^m+1.

Encode x = product p_i^e_i by the ordinal sum, in fixed register order, of e_i copies of A_i. Prefix an isolated ordinal singleton if a nonempty witness is wanted even for x=1. The SCC decomposition identifies all tokens by their order (or their count). Ordinal sum multiplies Hamiltonian counts, so the physical register count is h = product F_i^e_i.

For each fraction, let a_i,b_i be its numerator and denominator prime exponents (disjoint supports). Its guard is exactly e_i >= b_i for each i, equivalently product F_i^b_i divides h. Take the first applicable instruction, delete b_i whole SCCs of type i, insert a_i whole SCCs at the corresponding register lands, and orient all inter-SCC arcs by the ordinal order as before. The physical count is multiplied by the corresponding ratio of F_i powers. Each program step inserts/deletes a bounded number of vertices, independent of the register values; this is not a bounded number of arc additions, since new vertices meet the tape. An external controller tests the register guards and stops when none fits. The same proof works for any bank of fixed strong tournaments with pairwise coprime H>1. An arbitrary carrier may be left untouched in a separate ordinal component; its count then multiplies all tape counts and is never changed.

As a concrete pin, standard PRIMEGAME started at 2 and mapped with F_0=3 gives pure-register checkpoints of count 3^p in increasing prime p. The included replay also substitutes ten **small** strong witnesses over orders 3–6 instead of the large Fermat blocks. A prime absent from the spectrum, such as 7, need not be physically represented: only the **semantics of the register**, not the original prime assigned to it, are preserved.

## Fixed-reader no-go: computation does not imply absolute density

**Setup.** Fix a k-vertex tournament R with marked ports. At each marked port substitute a nonempty ordinal sum D of arbitrarily many copies of tournaments drawn from a fixed finite bank. Different ports can have independent banks. Singleton vertices are used elsewhere. The bank may include transitive padding; call a bank member active when H>1. We allow arbitrary exponents, arbitrary register order within a port, and arbitrary cyclic feedback among ports.

**Conclusion.** There are O((1+log X)^B) distinct physical H<=X, where B may be taken as the total number of bank types across all ports. The optimum exponent is not claimed. In particular a finite bank cannot be made into an absolute selector by putting its universal tape under a single fixed reader, or under a finite menu of readers.

**Proof.** A directed Hamiltonian path in the substitution has a canonical visit word, taking maximal contiguous runs inside one module. It is a walk in R, covers every reader vertex, and uses each type i between 1 and n_i=|D_i| times. Conversely every such word contributes the product of the ordered nonempty path-cover counts P_(r_i)(D_i) where r_i is its multiplicity, and all coefficients are nonnegative. Every multiplicity 1<=r<=n is always feasible inside a tournament: split a Hamiltonian path. Thus feasibility at this level uses sizes alone; no hidden path-incidence conditions remain for homogeneous tournament substitution.

Fix a port j. If any legal visit word repeats j, then one witnessing that fact has length <= L=k(k+2). Read the walk with state (most recent vertex, occurrence counters capped at 1 everywhere except 2 at j). If two states repeat, erase the intervening part. This preserves adjacency, capped coverage and the repeat at j while decreasing every occurrence count; repeat until all states are distinct. The capped counters increase in aggregate at most k+1 times; during any plateau there are at most k distinct recent vertices. The legality and the existence of a repeat for any chosen j are therefore constant throughout each chamber of sizes clipped at L.

If j repeats, use a short witness with r visits, 2<=r<=L. Then P_r(D_j)>=2^n_j/(r+1): for every vertex subset S choose a Hamiltonian path of S and of its complement, allowing an empty side, and refine those paths into r nonempty ordered segments, which is possible since r<=n_j and initially there are <=2 nonempty paths. A given r-segment output refines at most r+1 of the original ordered pairs, one for each way to put the split between segments. In the witness all remaining port contributions are >=1, so n_j<=log_2(X(L+1)). Thus every active or padding multiplicity at a repeating port is O(log X), and one may discard all order information at repeated ports too: ordinal sums give P_r(D_j) invariant under reordering the summands. To see this, optional ordered covers satisfy Q_t(A -> B)=Q_t(A)Q_t(B) for every t by uniquely concatenating independently chosen per-slot paths; P_r recover from Q_t by triangular inversion. This proves order-independence for all path counts read by R.

If j never repeats in its chamber, every word uses it once and reads only H(D_j). At least one legal word exists and its contribution is >=H(D_j), so each active multiplicity is O(log X). Also H(D_j) is unaltered by transitive copies. If size is the small, un-clipped value, retain that finite size; if size is clipped, changing it within the chamber cannot change the allowable words having a single visit. Combining all ports, only finitely many chambers and at most logarithmically many choices per bank multiplicity matter. This proves the bound. Padding at a singleton/unmarked vertex is not assumed unless declared as a port.

**Growing-reader threshold.** Assume a global fixed token bank of b types. Allow all k-vertex tournament readers, all choices of marked port subsets, and all exponents. The proof gives at most
2^(k(k-1)/2+k) · (O(k^2 + log X + log(k+1)))^((b+1)k)
values up to X for one k, where an extra coordinate per port records the clipped size. With k <= (sqrt(2)-epsilon) sqrt(log_2 X), summing over such k still gives o(X). This is the information budget of a restricted architecture, not an order lower bound for arbitrary tournaments. With a sparse allowed-reader family, one may replace 2^(k(k-1)/2) by its actual reader count.

## What to lift next

The physical program above supplies arbitrary bounded batches of guarded **SCC register updates**, and therefore a rigorous test bed for carry scheduling or Turing discipline. It does not supply a decoder. On fixed seeds any raw FRACTRAN program keeps the part of the scalar count coprime to the rule support constant, and the count above already has only polylogarithmic size distribution. Proving termination of a register program cannot supply a missing physical readout. None of these observations imply independence or undecidability for the unrestricted spectrum.

A meaningful new instruction would decode one unit **while deleting it**, e.g. keeping a tape's unprocessed register factors external and advancing an exact count on a marked payload via a bounded operation that can perform the next unit with the same continuation data. That differs from leaving a fixed positive reader around the entire tape. It could alternatively use a reader whose contact graph grows and cannot be shrunk to bounded homogeneous ports. The hard checks are: effect on absolute H (rather than H mod m), exhaustion/zero guard, preservation of the payload interface, and exclusion of an exponential SCC product factor from the final output. Without a physical certificate for those checks, a universal language merely renames the missing construction.
