# Tournament tapes: programming a register is not yet programming the count

Research session, 2026-11-15. The absolute Chan–Pak conjecture remains open. The results below are all-order statements about specified physical constructions, except where a finite test is identified. No claim of external priority is made.

## Best new constructive step: a flat two-point repair

[The strong tape and head](HEAD.md) gives a single physical interface with a running mixed-radix register, a reverse reserve, and a nearest-neighbor correction. A cyclic five-vertex core has two masks with identical register, endpoint, and single-contact readings and different two-contact readings. The two literal strong outputs differ by exactly **2** for arbitrary left and right transitive guard lengths, starting at **485, 487**. The effect is independent of both guard lengths and comes from selecting a pair orbit, not from balancing two large numbers. Placing additional nontransitive live blocks generally multiplies the step by their Hamilton counts; the exact formula exposes this cost rather than hiding it.

The same construction writes every base-four address at depth d using d directed triangles. Replacing a triangle by a transitive pair at one site, and replacing a pair by a triangle at a parking site, preserves both register readings and the common host counts while changing the output by a known signed value. For the all-triangle tape, the least possible 3-adic valuation is d−2, detected precisely by a neighboring-digit product sum. A fully programmed address is thus not an independently settable absolute H. The one-head readout windows on an unchanged tape are strictly ordered and disjoint regardless of their masks.

## Back through the archive

- [Cellular automata / Rule 30](ca_bridge_ca.md): a physical cyclic wrap is a permutive linear tape shift after factorial rescaling. It gives exact temporal trace decoding and finite-state arithmetic at fixed modulus. Variant reservoirs control the full combined mod-3/mod-5 state from an explicit seed; large absolute unit repairs cannot be made simply by waiting for a hidden grade in a shared schedule. The source trails lead back to Rule 30 boundary selection, de Bruijn reset, the lossless Cartier tree, and the October Hasse/Gilbreath work.
- [Turing machine and continuation horizon](ca_bridge_tm.md) and [the weighted observation state](ca_bridge_state.md): a grade-j discrepancy becomes physically visible with exactly 2j−2 external vertices; two freely ordered families explain the factorial, and triangular matching probes recover every observable grade. Fixed-modulus state really is finite, while absolute continuation has infinite rank and forces the distinction between moving a head and writing a new state. The gcd of all physical reader outputs is invariant in the divisibility direction under uniform substitution; in particular a multiple of 3 cannot be cleared by any number of uniform-port steps. Selective contacts bypass this specific rule as the head construction shows. Sources include earlier residual/rank, corridor and planar-Jacobian Hankel work.
- [FRACTRAN / PRIMEGAME](ca_bridge_fractran.md): every finite program compiles to an exact program of SCC register updates using the realizable pairwise coprime Fermat counts, without assuming all Fermat numbers are prime or assuming Chan–Pak. But any fixed homogeneous reader on finitely many such block types has at most polylogarithmically many output counts up to X, even with arbitrary feedback among its ports. This separates general computation from the desired exact output geometry.

## The common principle and a falsifiable next goal

**Program the address, spend a contact to correct the value, then preserve just the continuation actually needed.** Full contextual equality forbids a changed uniform readout by definition; modular equality is too weak; and simple tape growth can multiply the very defect we need to keep at two. The flat switch works because first-order boundary data are held fixed and a two-contact term changes. Try to implement more independent two-point changes *inside the same nontransitive core*, or consume a live block before the next repair, so the factor from other nontransitive blocks does not amplify the step. The observable goal is a physical bounded-word move that decreases unprocessed address length, advances the absolute output by a prescribed step, and preserves the same marked inputs for the next move. Neither finite residue control nor the one-off flat switch supplies that repeatable return.

Individual standard-library scripts reproduce the finite fixtures. Their outputs are consistency checks for the symbolic theorems, not evidence replacing any infinite argument.
