## 2. Literal ancestors of27 and a useful common-tail family

The positive integers whose T-orbit hits27 are exactly

    {27*2^r:r>=0}.                                      (1)

Every predecessor of a multiple of3 must be its even double: an odd
predecessor x would require `3x+1=2y`, impossible modulo3. Iterating proves
(1). For X>=27 the count is `floor(log_2(X/27))+1`, so its natural density
is zero. Its2-adic closure is this countable family together with0, hence
has Haar measure zero and Hausdorff dimension zero.

A more informative family shares the next odd node41:

    n_r=(82*4^r-1)/3,
    n_0=27, n_(r+1)=4n_r+1,
    27,109,437,1749,6997,27989,111957,447829,... .        (2)

These are exactly the positive odd direct predecessors of41. Since
`3n_r+1=41*2^(2r+1)`, each has `U(n_r)=41` and therefore exactly the same
remaining odd-node tail. The shortcut total stopping times are `70+2r`;
the classical, unshortened3n+1 times are `111+2r`. These two conventions
are checked separately. For every r>=1, however,

    T^2(n_r)=(3n_r+1)/4<n_r.

Thus longer total duration here does not mean a longer initial no-descent
interval. The exact count in (2) is
`max(0,1+floor(log_4((3X+1)/82)))`. Its natural density is zero, and its
2-adic closure adds the single limit `-1/3`, so again has dimension zero.

There is a small exact branching rule: `n_r=r mod3`, since the recurrence
is `n -> n+1 mod3`. Exactly every third comb member is divisible by3 and
has no odd predecessors. This frequency is in the **index r**, not among
all positive integers. The same three-phase obstruction holds along any
integral odd inverse comb generated by `n ->4n+1`.

For any fixed d and fixed positive target y, the odd sources with `U^d(n)=y`
and n<=X number `O_d((log X)^d)`. The stepwise bound is
`n_j<=3^j(X+1)-1`, so
`3n_(j-1)+1<3^d(X+1)` for every j<=d. Thus every one of the d valuations is at most
`K=ceil(log_2(3^d(X+1)))`. Each valuation word determines at most one
source by its affine inverse, giving at most `K^d`. This controls fixed
depth only; it does not give a density for the union over all depths.

## 3. The basin of every target coprime to3 is dense in Z_2

Let y>0 with `3` not dividing y. For any length-L parity word w, write its
affine action as

    T_w(n)=(3^e n+C)/2^L.

If e>0, C is a unit modulo3: in the ordered carry sum the last odd letter
contributes a power of2, and all earlier terms have a factor3. The integer2
generates the units modulo `3^e`, with order `2*3^(e-1)`. One elementary
proof starts from `4=1+3` and observes inductively that cubing raises the
3-adic valuation of `4^(3^j)-1` by exactly one.

Choose r>=0 satisfying

    2^(L+r)y=C mod3^e,
    n=(2^(L+r)y-C)/3^e>0.                              (3)

Such r exist arbitrarily far out in a fixed residue class modulo
`2*3^(e-1)`. The integer n lies in the exact parity class of w, reaches
`2^r y` after L steps, and then reaches y after r even steps. When e=0,
the word is all even and `n=2^(L+r)y` suffices. Consequently the positive
basin of y meets every dyadic cylinder, at arbitrarily large heights.

Its closure is all of Z_2, of Haar measure1 and Hausdorff dimension1.
This supplies **no natural-density conclusion**. Every set of ordinary
integers, viewed without closure as a subset of Z_2, is countable and has
Hausdorff dimension zero. Moreover a natural-density-zero dense skeleton
can be constructed: enumerate all finite cylinders, choose a different
basin point in the i-th cylinder greater than `2^i`, and use (3) to do so.
There are at most `log_2 X` such selected points below X.

This is a genuine contrast with the basin of27, whose target is divisible
by3. The script constructs actual positive basin41 points in every parity
cylinder through depth10, then replays each prescribed prefix exactly.

## 4. Prime motifs persist on positive-density finite-horizon cylinders

Fix an actual odd source a and its odd nodes `m_i`, cumulative valuations
`S_i`, through a final half-step L. Put `ell=L+1`, so the parity information
includes the final node being odd. For every source `g=a+2^ell t`,

    m_i(g)=m_i(a)+3^i*2^(ell-S_i)t.                    (4)

Thus if t is a multiple of an odd prime product P, all listed nodes retain
their residues modulo P. Every designated prime has exactly the same
support within the chosen node family. In particular an exclusive-prime
odd cycle from THM-4497 persists. To retain exact prime exponents instead,
replace p in P by `p^(1+max_i v_p(m_i))`; this stronger modulus is optional.

Two useful bases are:

| a | L | ell | P | M=P*2^ell | retained motif |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
|27|52|53|455|4098275660907151360|selected odd indices8,13,35; edge primes7,5,13|
|4347|11|12|4669|19124224|odd indices0,3,6 within the first10 nodes; edge primes23,29,7|

For27 the selected values are91,175,325. The triangle is exclusive within
these three nodes, **not** throughout the full36-node prefix: several other
nodes also contain5 or7. The exact probe checks the selected scope.
For4347 the first ten odd nodes are

    4347,6521,4891,7337,5503,8255,12383,18575,27863,41795.

The three edge primes are pair-exclusive even within this complete ten-node
family. An arbitrary relation of its first slots must therefore have zero
coefficients at the three triangle vertices. Neither statement alone
certifies every node in the long prefix.

Both base words have every nonempty shortcut prefix slope above1; their
exact minimum is `9/8`. Hence all positive sources in

    G_a={a+Mt:t>=0}                                     (5)

have no descent during their L steps and retain the indicated motif. This
is a natural-density `1/M` arithmetic progression. It is a fixed-horizon
claim; it is not a common-tail assertion and not an infinite-hard family.

## 5. A recursive family with arbitrarily long no-dip prefixes and exact frequency

Both bases above satisfy `v_2(a+1)=2` and `gcd(3,M)=1`. For each k>=0 define

    r_k=(a+1)*3^(-k) mod M,
    F_k={2^k u-1 : u>=a+1, u=r_k mod M}.                (6)

The fixed cutoff `u>=a+1` is convenient for an exact scaling law below;
it removes only finitely many terms from each admissible progression.
The first k shortcut letters of any n in F_k are odd, with

    T^j(n)=3^j*2^(k-j)u-1, 0<=j<=k.

The landing point `g=3^k u-1` is at least a and belongs to G_a. Every
following base prefix stays above g, and every initial odd step stays
above n. Thus every n in F_k has **k+L steps of no descent**, followed at
the prescribed positions by the same prime-support motif. The selected
odd indices simply increase by k.

The families are disjoint: the residue condition implies `v_2(u)=2`, so

    v_2(n+1)=k+2.                                      (7)

Each F_k is one progression modulo `2^k M`, with finitely many small terms
omitted. Therefore for every fixed K>=0,

    density(union_(k>=K) F_k)=2^(1-K)/M.                 (8)

More precisely its count through X is

    [2^(1-K)/M] X + O_(a,M)(log X).                    (9)

To justify summing infinitely many densities, let u_k be the least allowed
u, so `a+1<=u_k<=a+M`. The exact k-th count is

    max(0,1+floor((X-(2^k u_k-1))/(2^k M))).             (10)

For `k<=J=floor(log_2((X+1)/(a+1)))`, its difference from
`(X+1)/(2^k M)` is bounded by `1+a/M`. All k>J give zero actual terms,
and their summed comparison tail is less than `2(a+1)/M`. This proves
(9), including the missing finite initial terms. It also gives a direct
tail bound from the shell `2^(J+2)` dividing n+1.

For the shorter4347 base, the union over k>=K consequently has exact
natural density

    2^(1-K)/19124224.

Examples are `1/9562112` for K=0 and `1/305987584` for K=5. Conditional on
membership in the tail union, the scale index has geometric frequencies
`Pr(k=K+j)=2^(-j-1)`. This is a frequency among an explicitly defined
set of integers, not a temporal randomness assumption about one orbit.

## 6. The finite residue clock and its exact scaling symmetry

The residues obey a finite arithmetic recurrence

    r_(k+1)=3^(-1)r_k mod M.                            (11)

Their least period is

    tau=ord_(M/gcd(a+1,M))(3).

For4347 the effective modulus is `2^10*7*23*29`, giving
`tau=lcm(256,6,11,28)=59136`. For27 it is `2^51*5*13`, giving
`tau=3*2^49`. These are verified by the full-order divisibility tests;
there is no claim that the ten-node graph itself contains this clock.

Because the cutoff on u in (6) is uniform, equality of residue phases gives
the exact family recursion

    F_(k+tau)={2^tau(n+1)-1:n in F_k}.                  (12)

This is a concrete self-similar family with a finite phase and an unbounded
dyadic scale. The phase alone does not retain source height. The recursion
does not identify all long Collatz trajectories, and its density decays
faster than the aggregate no-dip frequency supplied by THM-4495.

## 7. The measures and dimensions are different

In Z_2 the closure of F_k is the single cylinder

    C_k=2^k r_k-1+2^(k+ell) Z_2.

The odd factor P in M disappears because multiplication by P is a2-adic
unit. The C_k are disjoint by (7); their only new accumulation point as
k increases is -1. Hence

    closure_Z2(union_(k>=K)F_k)={-1} union union_(k>=K)C_k,
    Haar measure =2^(1-K-ell).                          (13)

This is P times the natural density in (8). The missing coordinate is the
odd-prime congruence condition, which is not a continuous residue condition
in the2-adic topology. Each nonempty closure in (13) has Hausdorff dimension1
because it contains a cylinder. But the intersection of these closed tail
families over all K is just `{-1}`, of dimension0. There is no positive
integer surviving all scales of this particular construction.

Thus the explicit recursion is real, but interpreting its nested pictures
as an infinite positive orbit would be a quantifier error. Natural density,
Haar measure of a closure, and Hausdorff dimension answer different questions.
