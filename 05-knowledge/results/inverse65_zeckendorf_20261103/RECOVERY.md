The recurring principle is **realization with retained operation data**:
an algebraic inverse becomes a constructive inverse only when its predecessor
is itself in the legal source image. A norm supplies termination, a state
supplies future tests, and a source word supplies actual realization. None of
these three substitutes for the others. This is a synthesis of the cited
mechanisms, not a theorem equating their different mathematical objects.
