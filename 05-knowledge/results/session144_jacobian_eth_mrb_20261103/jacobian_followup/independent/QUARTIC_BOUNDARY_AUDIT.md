# Additive independent audit: the invariant target hyperplane can lose sheets

Status: PROVED by exact algebra below; FINITE-EXACT companion verification. This is an additional correction to the generality of THM-4727 Corollary R', separate from the fixed cubic map and the composition tower. Source construction: `THM-3438-weighted-lift-keller-degree-spectrum.md`, §5, at source commit `5e1cedf452c7cc1ec447205939a9d6cf4c0e8743`.

Let `u=1+3xy`, `gamma=1-4xy-x^2z`, and define

`F=((2u+u^2-3u^4 gamma^2)/x^2, (1+u-2u^3 gamma^2)/x, x gamma)`.

The displayed quotients cancel, the coordinate degrees are `(12,11,4)`, and direct symbolic differentiation gives `det DF=-6`. The map is equivariant for source weights `(1,-1,-2)` and target weights `(-2,-1,1)`; reversing the first and third target coordinates puts it in the equal-weight convention of THM-4727.

## Generic degree four

Write target coordinates `(A,B,C)`, and take invariants `P=BC`, `Q=AC^2`. Put `w=u gamma`. Direct identities give

`P=gamma+w-2w^3`, `Q=2w gamma+w^2-3w^4`.

Eliminating `gamma` yields

`T(w)=w^4-w^2+2Pw-Q=0`.

This polynomial is irreducible over `C(P,Q)`: its ideal in `C[P,Q,w]` is prime because the coefficient of `Q` is a unit and the quotient is `C[P,w]`; it is primitive over `C[P,Q]`. On the generic open set `C gamma!=0`, a root reconstructs

`gamma=P-w+2w^3`, `x=C/gamma`, `u=w/gamma`,
`y=(u-1)/(3x)`, `z=(1-4xy-gamma)/x^2`.

The source field is therefore the degree-four simple extension generated by `w`. The extra independent target orbit coordinate `C` does not alter irreducibility.

## Exactly three finite points generically on C=0

The source plane `x=0` maps as

`(0,y,z) -> (87y^2+6z,y,0)`.

It contributes precisely one preimage to every target `(A,B,0)`.

At `x!=0`, the equation `C=x gamma=0` is equivalent to `gamma=0`. The remaining equations become

`B x=1+u`, `A x^2=2u+u^2`.

Eliminating `u` gives

`(B^2-A)x^2=1`.

Thus for a generic target of `C=0` (namely `B^2-A!=0`) there are exactly two source points with `x!=0`, each reconstructing uniquely. The total finite fiber size is three, even though the generic field degree is four.

For example, `(A,B,C)=(0,1,0)` has exactly the three points

- `(0,1,-29/2)`;
- `(1,-1/3,7/3)`;
- `(-1,1,5)`.

The derivative is invertible at all of them. One of the four generic sheets escapes toward infinity on approach to a generic target of `C=0`.

## Corrected boundary-degree statement

THM-4727's unqualified assertion that the orbit-zero surface covers the invariant target hyperplane with degree `d` minus the restricted source-plane degree is false. Here `d=4`, the restricted plane degree is one, and the orbit-zero degree is two, not three.

The identity is valid when a generic point of that target hyperplane is outside the nonproperness set: all `d` sheets then remain finite and can be partitioned by their source components. In general a further escaping-sheet term is required. This audit proves the missing term is nonzero in an explicit already-known weighted map; it does not assert a general classification of nonproperness boundaries.

The fixed cubic map used in the Session 144 composition theorem does have full generic three-point fibers along its invariant target hyperplane, so its specific `3=1+2` calculation remains correct. The failure concerns the blanket extrapolation to arbitrary equivariant Keller maps.
