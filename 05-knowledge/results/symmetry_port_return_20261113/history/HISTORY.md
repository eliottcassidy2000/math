## 1. July 24–25: contexts, gluing, and a genuine six-uniform construction

The local chronological neighborhood contains several different coefficient
algebras. It is important to keep them separate:

* [THM-2221, pinned context-cut transport](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2221-tournament-context-cut-metric-and-pinned-transport-response.md)
  (July24) retains the external incidence word and its multiplicity. Its
  exact cost is `D_mu(c,d)=sum_w mu(w)|w(c)-w(d)|`. The quotient applies to
  the specified pinned transport cost, not to Hamiltonian counts.
* [THM-2256, automorphism/contact dichotomy](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2256-automorphism-contact-dichotomy-for-quotient-frustration.md)
  (July25) decomposes a balanced transport into permutation layers. A
  zero-contact automorphism/nonautomorphism pair gives a constant defect;
  without such contact, the smallest nonfree defect grows at least `N-1`.
  This explains why replicated symmetry can make a local disturbance cheap
  or expensive, but does not predict a Hamiltonian-path change.
* [THM-2267, static owner coverage and transition holonomy](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2267-static-owner-coverage-is-flag-and-transition-holonomy-is-a-cut-kernel.md)
  proves that the zero-redundancy static complex is flag, while the
  transition problem needs eligibility projectors and the gluing kernel.
  The same static stalks can have different switching energies.
* Its immediate neighbor
  [THM-2268, private-owner trident](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2268-two-shell-private-owner-trident-and-raw-carry-cocycle-no-go.md)
  forces all three owner labels globally, but proves that the proposed raw
  relative carry is an exact periodic potential. A nonzero adjacent jump
  therefore need not supply holonomy or a return. The later
  [THM-2271 weighted owner/absorber cut](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2271-expiration-support-forces-a-weighted-owner-absorber-cut.md)
  and [THM-2273 common-time flow](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2273-shallow-owner-flow-and-deep-successor-gap-spread.md)
  retain exclusions and ancestry; positive escaping mass is still not a
  return to a useful blocker.
* [THM-2290, context-selected colored pair kernel](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2290-context-selected-colored-pair-kernel-is-hafnian-complete.md)
  (same dated neighborhood) gives a positive endpoint: the full selected
  pair tensor is sufficient and necessary for every matching-extension
  response. A differing coordinate has a forced-leaf separating context.
  This is stronger than merely warning that an uncolored shadow loses data.

**Typed transfer A.** The source object is a carrier together with its
declared legal contexts. The target is a tournament with named available
ports. The map is `T -> (C(T), port stars)`. What is preserved is every
path-cover response after any root-child program using those ports and
fresh vertices. What is lost includes ordinary isomorphism and global
automorphism order. The required sidecar is the set of old vertices that
may be selected as roots. The decisive update is exact:

```
birth at r creates precisely the cyclic triples {r,z,v} with v->r.
```

This proves a context congruence, rather than assuming that a static
hypergraph is dynamically adequate. At indegree one, even the full star
sidecar can be removed for the four-coordinate response: two possible
predecessors are twins in the common hypergraph. A successful same-root
unit rearm must consequently change that hypergraph. The inherited H9
triangle trade is an explicit escape, while the new ports construction
uses a different full-profile congruence to preserve an already primitive
port during symmetry erasure.

The less obvious positive neighbor is
[THM-2270, simultaneous balanced cuts and six-uniform orientation](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2270-simultaneous-balanced-cut-relation-and-six-uniform-orientation.md).
It does not orient runner pairs. A single integral relation produces
nonzero sums on all1716 six-subsets; the signs form a Gale monotone
six-uniform orientation. The proof combines nonzero linear factors and a
centered coefficient grid, yielding height504576. The key operation is
choosing **one** parameter which avoids **all** forbidden hyperplanes.

**Typed transfer B.** The target parameters are actual transitive module
orders, not signed formal relation coefficients. With proved run caps
`R_i`, the module response uses the ordered cover basis `c_r`. On `TT_n`,
`c_r=r!S(n,r)`, so orders1 through`R_i` give an invertible physical basis.
For several nonzero guards, multiply their exponential-polynomial
responses and use a tensor Vandermonde grid of distinct product bases.
This constructs a single physical substitution satisfying all nonzero
guards. It preserves exact integer inequality, loses any promise about
the size or residue of a nonzero difference, and needs the certified run
caps and exterior-mark scope. The smallest hostile is useful: two cap-two
guards can vanish at orders1 and2 separately, so neither point works
simultaneously; order3 does. The proof and the actual9/3/5 context example
are in TRANSITIVE_PROBES.

## 2. July 27 and July 29: phase gauges, heavy flags, and the missing interaction

The intermediate July27
[THM-2543 augmentation/norm relative phase local system](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2543-augmentation-norm-relative-phase-local-system-dichotomy.md)
keeps a relative phase under simultaneous row/phase shifts. Its rational
seven-phase carrier decomposes into the augmentation and cyclotomic
pieces; a nonconstant rational profile cannot silently retain only selected
nontrivial characters. This is relevant to the clock lane's exact residue
observer, but no identification of its normalization637 with a tournament
count is made. The reusable feature is retaining the phase on which a
transition acts, not treating a Fourier label as an arrival witness.

The July29 neighborhood was followed both before and after the main hit:

* [THM-2892 heavy-triangle closure](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2892-eight-body-five-slot-heavy-triangle-closure.md)
  leads into the general
  [THM-2893 finite-core flag lemma](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2893-complement-cap-finite-core-flag-lemma.md).
  If every `(k-s)`-union is at most`B`, every hypothetical`k`-cover induces
  a complete heavy`s`-hypergraph at threshold`h-B`. Thus at least
  `k-s+1` labels must lie in a high singleton core. A strict asymptotic
  margin makes this core finite. Ordered first-hit flags retain both the
  excluded prefix and the quota of later core hits.
* The next
  [THM-2894 residual semilattice no-go](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2894-unmarked-residual-semilattice-order-and-group-clutch-no-go.md)
  shows exactly what an unmarked residual forgets: deletion order and
  repetition. It factors through an idempotent commutative semilattice, so
  it cannot encode a nontrivial group-valued clutching operation. This is
  a proof about an operation algebra, not a ban on richer ordered states.
* [THM-2897 partition-cap convolution](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2897-partition-cap-tropical-convolution-and-alternating-pair-ladder.md)
  takes a cap bank to a min-plus composition rule. Its next neighbor
  [THM-2898 five-parity matching repair](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2898-unique-max-gate-five-parity-matching-closure.md)
  resolves a surviving apex using three actual heavy edges. Two attractive
  edge pairs share a vertex; the sole disjoint pair falls strictly below
  the required threshold. The matching sidecar is essential.
* [THM-2900 flag-conditioned rank selection](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2900-flag-conditioned-rank-selective-partition-closure.md)
  replaces a child`B2+q1` cap by`B2+q3` once the actual forced flag is
  removed. A fresh residual is not equivalent to an old cap with names
  erased; the forbidden prefix and remaining rank are retained.

**Typed transfer C, with a decisive failed implication.** One might use
these matching certificates to assemble a matching of neutral tournament
surgeries. The intrinsic support hypergraph is legitimate: vertices are
old physical vertices, and a surgery occupies the vertices incident to its
changed arcs. However, disjoint supports do not make Hamiltonian responses
additive. Here is the exact minimal-order hostile for two edge flips:

```
T=(6,16,50,7,41,11),              H(T)=37,
e={0,1}, f={2,4},                e intersection f=empty,
H(T^e)=H(T^f)=37,
T^(e,f)=(4,17,34,7,45,11),       H(T^(e,f))=31.
```

The mixed Boolean difference is`-6`. Every labelled tournament of orders
at most5 and every pair of vertex-disjoint individually neutral edge flips
was checked; none has this failure. The order6 four counts are independently
verified by all720 vertex permutations as well as subset DP. This is a
finite minimality statement in that exact universe, not a claim about
larger surgeries or graph distance.

The strongest survivor is positive and already used by the clock/ports
lanes: **full-profile neutral substitutions at uniform modules do compose**,
because the complete run contraction is multilinear in those profiles.
The support matching alone is insufficient; retain the joint response
tensor, or prove the full-profile module congruence. TRANSITIVE_PROBES gives
a finite physical basis for checking the latter whenever run caps exist.

## 4. What has actually advanced

The historical search produced three operational outputs, not a proposed
identification of unrelated conjectures:

1. a new all-program cyclic-hypergraph congruence, with an indegree-one
   corollary giving the exact data a unit-ready rearm must change;
2. a complete finite physical basis for bounded-run module identities and
   a constructive simultaneous separator theorem;
3. a minimal small disjoint-surgery interaction witness, identifying the
   missing coefficient when an attractive hypergraph matching is used as
   a neutral-repair plan.

The positive changing-module construction and primitive global-symmetry
erasure are in the sibling clock and ports proofs. They were independently
read here: named incidences, protected marks, primitive phase guards, and
the distinction between a neutral step and a count-changing growth cycle
all pass. The remaining Chan–Pak task is still an exact-target supply
theorem. Neither nonzero separation, global rigidity, nor an indefinite
physical growth rule alone supplies that quantifier.
