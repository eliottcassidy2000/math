# A connection is useful only if it preserves the next operation

## 1. The common mathematical object is a context-sensitive interface

A count is an observation, not a complete state. Let S be a collection of
physically realized marked tournament states, and let A be a specified family
of legal attachment operations. Include a terminal readout h. Define

    s ~_A t  iff h(w(s))=h(w(t)) for every legal continuation word w,

including domain/legality agreement. This definition is an exact continuation
congruence: if s~_A t and a is legal on both, then a(s)~_A a(t), since every
continuation after a is itself a continuation of s and t. Conversely any
congruence preserving legality and h refines ~_A. This is the standard residual
or observational-equivalence construction, not a new theorem about its index.

The point is operational. An endpoint profile may be sufficient for one
attachment alphabet and insufficient for another. Equality of total H, of
score sequences, or of a modular coefficient vector does not establish ~_A.
The square-zero endpoint carrier in the two analytic lanes is an exact finite
sufficient carrier before compression for attachments incident to the old graph
only at retained, addressable ports, with the original occupancy coordinates
retained. It costs exponentially many subset
coordinates and does not prove a finite all-order quotient.

**Concrete discovery program (OPEN):** begin with a proposed compressed
interface; find two physically realized states with identical interface but a
continuation distinguishing them; add the missing coordinate; repeat. Seek a
recursive symbolic description rather than a bounded numerical catalogue.
If the refinement never stabilizes, retain a growing graded family instead of
claiming a universal finite grammar. This is where prior cellular-automaton and
state-minimization ideas have a precise use.

## 2. The three lanes retain different information

- The port lattice retains an exponential coefficient vector. Its Smith index
  controls arithmetic information lost by reduction. All 4096 choice profiles
  can separate over integers while a small residue observation identifies many.
- The cluster lane retains the full marked path inventory while changing its
  elimination presentation. Such exchanges are exact but count-preserving by
  design; they need a separate map to physical count-changing constructions.
- The Witt lane retains vertex resources and endpoints before taking a scalar
  readout. Mixed terms are necessary. A formally lawful positive operation may
  nevertheless violate the parity required of a physical tournament count.

Their combination suggests a **three-part certificate for a proposed move**:
(1) an exact interface transport identity; (2) an actual tournament construction
realizing it; (3) a target rule and termination measure. Failure of one cannot be
repaired by proving the other two more accurately.

## 3. Arithmetic blindness is a diagnostic, not an obstruction for all sources

For this specific twelve-pair bank, let Delta_i(m) be its paired differences.
For m>=2 their gcd is G(m)=gcd(2^m-2,3^m-3). Every difference of two formal
aggregate selections is an integer combination of these Delta_i, hence divisible
by G(m). Thus a target correction outside the corresponding coset cannot be
made by toggling this fixed bank at that m. This is a genuine family obstruction.

The converse is false without more work: divisibility by the gcd does not mean
a binary selection attains the correction. Integer Bezout combinations permit
negative and repeated coefficients; actual selections allow only bounded signs.
Moreover the aggregate itself has not been compiled into one tournament. The
exact G(m) tells us when to change scale, bank or physical core; it is not a
coverage theorem. At primes p>=5 the order/LTE formula in ports/PROOF.md makes
this a predictable schedule rather than unexplained experimental irregularity.

This is a useful p-adic/cluster analogy with a strict limit: local charts can be
individually transparent yet need nontrivial transitions to cover a global
object. Here no atlas-covering theorem follows merely from calling them charts.

## 4. What would turn an interface grammar into Chan–Pak descent?

A sufficient theorem would give a physically realized interface family S and,
for each requested odd c outside a proved finite base set, a legal inverse rule
choosing c'<c and a state condition Q' such that:

1. the recursive target c' is not one of the excluded values;
2. every chosen predecessor satisfying Q' has a forward construction realizing
   **absolute** target c and the required output interface;
3. the base conditions are physically initialized, and the choice of inverse
   rule covers every c in its stated domain;
4. any additional state rank decreases lexicographically with c or is otherwise
   bounded, so core changes cannot cycle without progress.

The induction must range over target **and required interface predicate**, not
just the scalar target. The allowed predicates must be closed under inverse
branching, the recursive guarantee must supply a predecessor satisfying Q',
and every terminal predicate case must be initialized. With those hypotheses,
induction would give coverage. Neither existence of every even gap, dense
ratio limits, a coefficient gcd, nor finite successful repairs establishes item3.
The present results isolate tests for items2 and3 but do not prove them.

A promising concrete variant is to carry both a residue/Smith-class certificate
and the marked seam operator. Use the first to choose a candidate inverse branch,
and the second to certify legal continuation after switching cores. Restricting
to a class with a symbolic closed interface could make this tractable; the class
must also be proved to supply all required targets. This remains a proposed
construction program, not an asserted new theorem.

## 5. Why the 24 comparison remains useful after the naive analogy fails

A distance-eight code couples coordinates so strongly that independent local
swaps are forbidden. The twelve-pair observer currently exhibits independent
binary choices, so its natural code has distance two. This contrast points to a
specific missing object: a derived coupling law among ports, visible in actual
attachment or mutation monodromy, which forbids local changes but permits larger
coherent ones. Search for that law in endpoint contexts, not in a numerically
chosen 24-dimensional representation. A genuine coupling may yield a different
code or lattice, which should be accepted on its own terms rather than forced
to be Golay or Leech.

The inherited Paley/Golay map shows a real route via a cyclic quadratic-residue
structure. The inherited score/Leech map shows how a compelling lattice carrier
can forget Hamiltonian counts. Together they provide both inspiration and the
exact warning: representation is not preservation of the target invariant.
