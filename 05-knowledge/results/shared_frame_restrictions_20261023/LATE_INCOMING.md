# Integrating native branch width, guarded returns, and cyclotomic coordinates

23 October 2026. Incoming commits through **2489ccd2d9** were read before
this packet's final extension. This note distinguishes inherited results,
concurrent overlap, new deductions, and questions. It is not a new theorem
about universal Collatz entry or unrestricted tournament wiring.

## 1. Concurrent overlap and a carrier distinction that cannot be omitted

Session43's [restricted coupling theorem](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/coupling/THEOREM.md)
independently gives the fixed-path transitive singleton and the order-four
nontransitive matrix of RESTRICTIONS.md, with spectrum 1,8/15,0,0 and gap
7/15. These are **concurrent overlapping results**, not exclusive discoveries
of this packet. Its bounded-density reweighting and explicit Hamilton-count
mod-8 tests are further inherited restriction results. The contiguous-block
factorization, fixed-interior-port slow modes, and general one-edge-row
formula here are different extensions.

The other incoming [native connectedness proof](../hamiltonian_observer_restrictions_20261023/coupling/NATIVE_CONNECTEDNESS.md)
uses the COMPRESSED graph on n-1 positions: edge (i,j) denotes the native
backward chord (i,j+1). Our graph has n vertices and its actual backward
chord endpoints. Their graph-isomorphism partitions are incomparable from
order five, although they encode the same free bits. The incoming all-size
two-component theorem therefore does not prove the analogous open claim
for our partition. Likewise compressed free-bit complementation can join
the transitive component; full n-vertex graph complementation cannot do so
inside our fixed-path carrier. Matching small eigenvalues do not repair a
different partition or source measure.

## 2. Native composition wants shared-state transfer, not an explicit coset bank

The incoming [observer-bundle proof](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/native-ports/OBSERVER_BUNDLES_AND_NATIVE_PORTS.md)
distinguishes whole-source automorphism transport from assembly of local
path charts. Our C9 fixture authenticates the former, with 27 actual lifts.
The latter can be a noncoset union even for two C3 cells. These operations
must not be interchanged merely because both use cyclic coordinates.

The [branch-width theorem](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/native-branch-width/THEOREM.md)
gives regular hosts with a literal accepted phase family

    S_t = Delta^(-1)({0,1}^(t-1)) in F3^t,
    Delta(a)_i=a_(i+1)-a_i.

Its size is 3*2^(t-1). Every affine coset contained in it has at most three
points, so any exact coset union needs at least 2^(t-1) branches. Yet the
matrix M=I+Q on three shared phases counts every path with fixed endpoint
phases. The common-phase kernel has size three and remains an actual owner
choice. A guard can be added only with its authenticated update state.

This supplies a concrete rerouting rule: retain the shared separator phase
and count through it, rather than expand every permitted displacement word.
It does not assert that all guards have small state. The incoming
[native-template complexity theorem](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/native-complexity/THEOREM.md)
and its [independent audit](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/audit-native-complexity/AUDIT.md)
show NP-completeness for the specified general C3-template feasibility
problem, including the pinned regular-host version. Ordinary tournament
Hamilton-path existence is not this problem. Bounded interaction treewidth
is a positive regime for exact shared-phase dynamic programming.

The [conditional-prefix compiler](../hamiltonian_observer_restrictions_20261023/compiler/README.md)
supplies another positive regime: a guard depending on q authenticated
prefix coordinates leaves an explicit product of invisible kernel lifts.
This is stronger than merely observing a constant unfiltered kernel size.
An arbitrary labelled electrical predicate has no proved bounded footprint.

## 3. What survives a guard, and what its clock costs

The incoming [trace theorem](../hamiltonian_observer_restrictions_20261023/trace/TRACE_WITH_CLOCKS.md)
proves that watching returns to ANY nonempty accepted subset of a reversible
PSD chain preserves its spectral gap in return-event time. It keeps the
duration-generating matrix and stationary conditional mean 1/pi(S).

[RETURN_COST.md](RETURN_COST.md) adds a source-specific tail and logarithmic
source-weight expectation bound. For alpha=pi(S), gamma the ambient gap,
and actual initial state s,

    P_s(tau_S^+>t) <= min(1,A*(1-gamma*alpha)^(t-1)),
    A=sqrt((1-alpha)/pi(s)),
    E_s tau_S^+ <= 2+(1+log_+(A))/(gamma*alpha).

It applies componentwise to our cut-preserving block chain, with gamma=8/9.
The full word lift is important when a guard varies inside a graph class.
The complete-refresh control proves that the acceptance-mass dependence
cannot be removed in general. This is an explicit clock cost, not an
independent lower bound on alpha and not a proof that intermediate ambient
excursions are legal native moves.

This changes the next target from an unqualified spectral-gap transfer to
three separate obligations: authenticated nonempty acceptance, controlled
acceptance mass, and legal native realization with integer length/congestion.
For Collatz the same three quantities have to be supplied on the actual
source/terminal/charge carrier. The present tournament chain supplies no
such identification by itself.

## 4. The one-reversed-arc family now has two useful jobs

Our one-edge-row formula uses the strong core formed by reversing the two
extreme vertices' arc in a total order. Its path count and automorphism
factor determine a source-row return probability. The incoming
[cyclotomic affine-hull proof](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/cyclotomic-lattice/NOTE.md)
uses this same classical family to test every universal affine-linear
congruence of the forward-step polynomial. Its coefficient matrix is
unitriangular, so actual family members generate the entire affine lattice.
See the [independent audit](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/audit-cyclotomic-lattice/REPORT.md).

The map is precise: keep the actual core, then either take H/Aut for the
incidence column weight, or its forward-step polynomial for cycle-cover
coordinates. Neither observable recovers the other complete carrier. The
incoming nonrealizable polynomial A_n+3C_1 passes every affine-lattice
congruence, but its cycle-incidence constraints fail. This is a strong
hostile against treating complete linear congruence coverage as complete
realizability.

That distinction is especially useful for the mod-8/mod-9 program. The
[cyclotomic valuation note](../session43_observers_phase_guards_20261023/cyclotomic/NOTE.md)
provides literal prime-adic mechanisms, including factorial digit sums and
conjugation parity. Those are authenticated polynomial evaluations, not
automatic substitutes for the native electrical mod-8 guard. Each needs
its own observable and lifting map.

## 5. Refined live questions and cheapest decisive tests

| Question | Retained object | First decisive test / stopping boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Which source-preserving block moves realize accepted trace transitions? | actual owner tuple, cuts, kernel lift, guard and integer duration | two accepted states in one trace component but different legal-move components refute the proposed move set |
| Which native grammars have bounded separator state? | shared phases and repeated cell identities | measure interaction width, not just maximum degree; the incoming hardness construction has bounded degree |
| Does an electrical guard have a small proved footprint? | exact labelled guard value and prefix coordinates | search for two lifts identical on the proposed footprint with different guard values |
| Can acceptance mass be bounded independently of depth? | actual source weight and accepted set | complete refresh with alpha tending to zero defeats any gap-only argument |
| Can nonlinear incidence complement complete linear congruences? | cycle-intersection data beside the forward-step polynomial | A_n+3C_1 is the inherited positive-coefficient nonrealizable hostile |

The geometry/Gaussian lane fits this board through a genuine information
requirement: opposite characters must be paired before averaging, and
monodromy requires an actual sheet coordinate. It remains an analogy of
representation design until a map transports the requested predicate.
