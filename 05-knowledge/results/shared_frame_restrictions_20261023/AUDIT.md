## What was checked by a second route

1. Subgroup spectrum: signed inversion histograms and an integer Walsh
   transform are compared with literal ordinary/affine word orbits and
   rational incidence operators. The 633 eigenvector equations include
   arbitrary cyclic subgroups, fixed ports, contiguous blocks, and paired
   complement/converse carriers. The pairing rule retains even masks;
   the full-S_n claim that discarded modes are all zero is deliberately
   NOT extended to subgroups.
2. Contiguous blocks: all ordered block compositions through six vertices,
   1,065,509 mask equations. Fixed-port star formulas are independently
   counted by slot subsets. The n=4 port-2 hostile has even mask {12,13}
   and eigenvalue 4/9. Relative gap excludes the full eigenvalue-1 space;
   no global irreducibility is inferred.
3. Fixed-path restriction: actual word classes and weights are rebuilt
   after restriction. The transitive singleton is checked separately.
   The four-vertex nontransitive component has a literal matrix with
   spectrum 1,8/15,0,0. Source-row return probabilities are checked against
   the all-order two-apex counting formula. An independent actual-arc
   subset DP checks its H values through core order 12. The old tribonacci
   mirage is explicitly distinguished at order seven.
4. Native lifts: all 81 pairs of C9 translations are tested on all actual
   source arcs. Exactly 27 are compatible. Every indexed path, partner,
   endpoint pair and converse square is verified; strong connectivity
   uses literal reachability. The synthetic guard is checked by a second
   direct congruence join. A section-based count gives 9 instead of 2,
   supplying a genuine negative control.
5. Divisor heights: every nonunit divisor of N and N/2 is checked by both
   prime-exponent minima and repeated literal integer division. Exact
   tail and level counts agree. States 0,1 are terminal bookkeeping
   conventions, not an application of the maximum formula to base 1.
6. Projective incidence: normalized homogeneous coordinates and direct
   dot-product tests at q=2,3,5 verify MM^t=qI+J. The all-order claim is
   conditional on the projective-plane axioms, not existence at every q.
7. Poisson/source gates: SymPy derivatives check opposite-charge
   contraction, the invariant-carrier brackets, the punctured cotangent
   Jacobian, and the averaged-bracket hostile. These expose and repair
   two local statements in THM-1345; MISTAKES.md retains the lineage.
8. Guarded excursions: exact PSD certificates check the killed-kernel
   contraction on all 255 nonempty subsets of the eight-word order-three
   coupling, plus every nonempty subset of two nonuniform three-state
   controls. There are 12,576 source/time tests through time 12 and 1,048
   exact source expectation tests. The latter solve a linear system,
   independently of the tail iteration. Rational squaring determines the
   capped-tail cutoff without numerical logarithms. Complete refresh
   attains the killed contraction and exposes the necessary alpha cost.

All checks remain active under `python -O`. Receipts must be byte-identical
in normal and optimized modes. Python integer arithmetic is exact; SymPy
is used only by `algebra.py`. No numerical eigenvalue, sampled primality
test, or Lean certification is used.

## Proof boundary review

- Group restriction is not carrier conditioning. The sharp block gap
  applies to the full uniform cube with a smaller group; fixed-path
  and electrical filters require their own incidence measure.
- Complement and converse are coupled only through the declared full
  graph/tournament dictionary. Path-chart reflection and free-chord
  complementation are different operations.
- Native source automorphisms cover one path orbit. The 27 lifts do not
  exhaust all Hamiltonian paths of the source, all native partners, or
  a globally complete port grammar.
- The mod-8 clock in `native.py` is explicitly synthetic. The actual old
  electrical receipt is pinned as inherited data, not relabeled or
  certified on a new carrier without a transport map.
- Quotient mass must include actual kernel lifts. A constant fibre size
  alone does not make a nonconstant guard descend.
- Gaussian order signs are not polynomial Gaussian moments; finite
  angular symmetries are not arbitrary residue tests. An averaged
  determinant is not the full determinant.
- General Collatz entry, planar JC and six-colour forcing are unchanged
  open targets. No map from our auxiliary gap to their missing universal
  estimate has been supplied.
- Trace sampling preserves a return-event gap but can leave the accepted
  carrier during an excursion. RETURN_COST retains the actual source
  weight and acceptance mass, but does not establish a native lift or an
  independent lower bound for that mass. Its logarithmic source factor is
  obtained by summing the capped tail, not by changing the clock.
