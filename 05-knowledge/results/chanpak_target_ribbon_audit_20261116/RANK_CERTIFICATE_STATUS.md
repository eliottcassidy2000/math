**Update:** [the sharp growth bound](SHARP_GROWTH.md) proves the physical upper cap. The same-suffix rank obstruction still applies, and the lower-growth certificate on constant-rank stretches is still open.

## New exact obstruction for a literal rank-plus-suffix state

Use the minimal literal representation *after the initial vertex has been read*. Its dimension is 61 (62 when the empty-word initial state is included). The concrete words below encode bits of backward gaps 1,2,3 in the order used by the ordinary span-3 transfer:

| word | last three letters | rank before next letter 0 | rank after |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| (0,4,1,7,0,2) | (7,0,2) | 3 | 3 |
| (0,7,0,2) | (7,0,2) | 3 | 2 |

Both are physical prefix words. Thus the deterministic state (last three literal letters, current rank) cannot determine even whether the next letter drops rank. This is a statement about **image information**, not a no-go result for every cone representation or a longer suffix.

The verifier reconstructs the 119 raw fragment states; a modular sieve only chooses provisional observable rows O and reachable columns B. It then proves closure of each basis over the rationals, and computes the exact ranks of O M_w B. Selected nonzero minors are −4, 32, −8 and 8, respectively; exact full rank upper bounds follow from explicit factorizations through the chosen pivot columns. Rational calculations are mandatory; floating-point eigenvalue thresholds are not used. Replay `python verify_rank_alias.py` (NumPy and SymPy). Its receipt is `RANK_ALIAS_CERTIFICATE.json`.

## What is still open

- The 13-unit cap and lower-growth potential do not transfer to the full literal span-3 language by replacing the tags with the last three letters and a rank integer. A future proof needs rank-sensitive image data, longer-memory separation with proof, or inequalities verified over a sound nondeterministic over-approximation of transitions.
- A positive delayed output cures raw zero coordinates but still needs a uniform comparison to H on *physical* prefixes. The [envelope](POSITIVE_ENVELOPE.md) gives an exact reduction; it is not an all-prefix bound.
- [An audit of the counting argument](DENSITY_AUDIT.md) separates a finite eigenvalue-based lower bound on the joint spectral radius from the upper bound needed for a word-count estimate. A word-count estimate itself does not give distinct-value coverage. The [power-saving gate](DENSITY_GATE.md) states the separate absolute collision estimate that would suffice.
