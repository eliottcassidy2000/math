## Logical boundary audit

1. **Counts and classes:** H counts ordinary directed Hamiltonian vertex
   sequences. No automorphism quotient or A000568 class count is substituted.
2. **Difference and value:** A3 constructs every even difference, not
   every starting count. Full odd-spectrum coverage is still open.
3. **Graph and lattice:** Boolean words specify the graphs. A unimodular
   basis change of the response lattice is not automatically a graph move.
4. **Marked and unmarked:** the paired Smith group discards its source
   embedding. Keep the original basis, source row and named apexes.
5. **Linear and nonlinear:** six is minimal linear dimension. The actual
   word admits a nonlinear two-code representation; six-query optimality
   among nonlinear observers has not been proved.
6. **Operation language:** C1 closes common base append and jump queries.
   Arbitrary port attachments and native re-marking can require more data.
7. **Finite and all-size:** graph tests are finite. Contraction, cancellation,
   native-path identities, integral peeling and closed matrix recurrences
   provide the stated uniform quantifiers.
8. **Arithmetic scope:** an alternating response group and its paired
   discriminant form do not supply a Selmer realization or Sha finiteness.
9. **Novelty scope:** the gap automaton, actual-word codes, generic arc
   compiler and general alternating reduction are inherited. The contributions
   here are their explicit edge-difference constructor, six-dimensional
   derivative observer, size-dependent response lattice, and listed families.
