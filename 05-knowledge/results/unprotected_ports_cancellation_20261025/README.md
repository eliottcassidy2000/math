2026-10-25. Structural proofs remove the n-4 port from a substantial
endpoint-four family, extend the clone-excess recurrence by an adaptive
moment representation, and determine an exact characteristic spectrum
for a regular-action cut polynomial. Full black-core connectivity is
**OPEN**. The recurrence remains unrestricted but is not a closed scalar
formula or a proved faster enumeration algorithm.

The port theorem retains every literal vertex and every middle arc.
It constructs three black moves to the original marked target, rather
than replacing the target by an isomorphic tournament. In the inherited
large-order endpoint-four remainder, this packet leaves h=3 with 1->3
and h=4 with 3->1. Other endpoint layers and exceptional orders remain
outside this local conclusion. For arbitrary additional ports the main
ledger has two genuine critical degree loci; its hostile examples are
blue intermediate words, not disconnected black cores.

The counting theorem changes the representation of weighted banks of
histories. On a fixed defect fibre with remaining degree S and h cycle-count
values, it needs min(S,h) rational coordinates. Same-degree, same-cycle-count
histories have the same sign, so once a bank benefits from moments it never
needs its hidden sparse support reconstructed. Four independent coefficient
paths agree through order18. At that order the summed stage scalar counts
fall from94 to85. Bit cost and running time are not included in that claim.
The original unrestricted A000568 no-excess recurrence is its one-channel
restriction; the advance handles exact clone excess without separately
fixing final actual and excess degrees.

For q=p^r and h=q/p, the regular-action positive polynomial has exact depths
h in characteristic zero, q-p+1 in characteristic p, and q in any prime
characteristic dividing q-1. Other characteristics retain depth h. The
explicit 50-vertex tournament with q=25 therefore has one invariant toggle
and depths5,21,25 over Q,F5,F2 respectively. The quotient must retain integer
division before reduction; the finite logarithm in the valuation proof is
the exact residual object, not a numerical analogy.

## Inheritance and concept board

The anchor is the user's unprotected-port and unrestricted-counting target.
The niche is the older cyclic Hasse-coordinate mechanism, recovered from
[THM-2201](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2201-cyclic-root-fibre-hasse-jet-transition-carrier.md)
of July24. The wildcard is the way coefficient characteristic changes
the depth of an actual tournament's signed clone cancellation.

Closest proved mechanisms are the
[protected-port and clone-cut packet](../ports_regular_actions_hn_20261025/README.md).
The principal hostiles are a genuinely blue intermediate detour word,
a bank with moments0,0,192, the larger full automorphism group of C3[C3],
and a cyclic constant mode whose sum vanishes in the physical characteristic.
Corrected near misses are equating fewer keys with less storage, replacing
the full group by a chosen subgroup, and extracting an invariant mode by
an invalid division. The underused data are the two-endpoint degree defect,
one coordinate of a periodic invariant, and full cycle-product moments.

The live board has six objects: literal marked paths; canonical clone cuts;
finite-horizon responses; simultaneous group actions; integral cyclic
operators; and coefficient specialization. Each bridge keeps its own target:

The broad connection is an explicit comparison of observation kernels,
not an identification of the objects. Finite linear coordinates suffice
only for a specified future operation set. The moment horizon shrinks;
cyclic differences can hide increasing nilpotent depth; clone cancellation
uses individual elements of a full group rather than common fixed vectors.
None of these replaces the literal arc conditions in a black detour.
