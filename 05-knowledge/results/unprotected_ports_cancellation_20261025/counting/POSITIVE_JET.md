# Positive cancellation jets, one-toggle families, and correlated doubling

its exact rational cancellation depth, and the correlated doubling laws.
**CITED input:** Godsil's tournament regular representation theorem supplies

## 1. The cut polynomial has a nonnegative shifted form

Use the exact inflation fibre from
[CLONE_INVOLUTION.md](../../ports_regular_actions_hn_20261025/counting/CLONE_INVOLUTION.md).
For a fixed tournament T, let G be its set of canonical maximal-transitive-
module gaps, g=|G|, and A=Aut(T) acting on those gaps. Define

    P_T(t)=sum_(A-orbits [C] of subsets C of G) t^|C|.

Burnside's formula, applied to cut subsets, gives

    P_T(t)=1/|A| * sum_(alpha in A)
                      product_(cycles c of alpha on G) (1+t^|c|).  (J1)

Every cycle length is odd because A has odd order. Set

    Q_T(u)=(-1)^g P_T(-1-u).

For a cycle of odd length L,
1+(-1-u)^L=-((1+u)^L-1). The number of cycles of alpha has the same
parity as g. Therefore the signs cancel exactly and

    Q_T(u)=1/|A| * sum_(alpha in A)
                      product_(cycles c) ((1+u)^|c|-1).             (J2)

**Consequences.** Q_T has nonnegative integer coefficients. Positivity
comes termwise from (J2); integrality follows from its integral-polynomial
definition using P_T. Its exact lowest nonzero degree is

    nu(T)=min_(alpha in A) number_of_cycles(alpha on G).            (J3)

Indeed each factor in (J2) has lowest term L*u, so terms attaining the
minimum have strictly positive leading coefficients and cannot cancel.
More explicitly

    [u^nu]Q_T = 1/|A| * sum_(alpha: c(alpha)=nu)
                               product_(cycles c) |c| >0.         (J4)

The empty gap set has Q_T=1 and nu=0. This handles pair-free tournaments
and the empty tournament without a special limiting argument.

## 2. Depth and invariant toggle rank measure different things

A constant gap toggle sends C to C symmetric-difference F. It commutes
with every automorphism exactly when F is A-invariant. Such invariant
subsets form a binary vector space of dimension

    rho(T)=number of A-orbits on G.                                (J5)

Every orbit has odd size, so its basis toggle reverses the sign in the
clone-cut sum. Since a single alpha-cycle cannot cross an A-orbit,

    rho(T)<=nu(T).

The inequality can be arbitrarily strict. Here rho is the rank of
symmetry-commuting *constant XOR toggles*. It is not the number of all
possible nonlinear involutions, and nu does not count independent toggles.
Their definitions retain the distinction the user's cancellation theme
requires.

For example, the subgroup generated by a few convenient translations
cannot replace A in (J3). In Q=C3[C3], the regular C3^2 translation
subgroup has minimum cycle count3. But Q also has the automorphism
(i,j)->(i+1,j), with j incremented when i=2, which is a single9-cycle.
After uniform TT2 inflation, the full gap action therefore has nu=1,
not3. The tournament is vertex-transitive and its gap action remains
transitive, so rho=1. This is a concrete hostile to inferring depth from
an exhibited regular subgroup.

## 3. Genuine tournament families with rho=1 and unbounded depth

**CITED existence input.** Godsil's theorem states that every finite group
of odd order except C3^2 and C3^3 has a tournament regular representation:
a tournament whose **full** automorphism group is that group acting
regularly on its vertices. Primary source:
[C. D. Godsil, Tournaments with prescribed regular automorphism group,
Aequationes Mathematicae30 (1986),55--64](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02189910).
The original publisher abstract and the precise exception statement in
[Wong--Xu--Zhang--Zhao, introduction, arXiv:2501.13406v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13406v1)
were checked2026-10-25. The original probabilistic proof is cited, not
reproved here. The publisher's plain-text exponent/subscript formatting
is ambiguous; the latter primary paper states the two elementary abelian
exceptions explicitly.

For each r>=1 take a tournament regular representation R_r of C5^r,
of order q=5^r, and replace every vertex by TT2. Call the result T_r.
The base R_r is vertex-transitive and hence regular. A homogeneous pair
u->v would have outdegrees differing by one, so R_r has no homogeneous
pair. Consequently the TT2 blocks are exactly T_r's canonical maximal
transitive modules. Every automorphism permutes them, induces an
automorphism of R_r, and has a unique order-preserving lift. Thus

    Aut(T_r)=Aut(R_r)=C5^r,

with its regular action on the q gaps. This is a full-group statement,
not an inference from a Cayley subgroup. The action has one orbit, so
rho(T_r)=1. Identity has q cycles; every nonidentity element has q/5
cycles, all of length five. Hence

    P_(T_r)(t)=((1+t)^q+(q-1)*(1+t^5)^(q/5))/q,
    Q_(T_r)(u)=(u^q+(q-1)*((1+u)^5-1)^(q/5))/q,
    nu(T_r)=q/5=5^(r-1).                            (J6)

These are genuine tournaments on2*5^r vertices with a one-dimensional
space of invariant constant gap toggles and arbitrarily deep signed
cancellation. The existence assertion is all-r; an explicit adjacency
list for every r is not being claimed. Choosing the least Cayley
connection set that passes the full regular-representation test is a
finite definition, but not an efficient construction.

The same argument works for C_p^r at any fixed odd prime p whenever the
TRR theorem applies, giving depth p^(r-1). In particular the cases
C3^2 and C3^3 must not be silently included. The formal regular-action
polynomial still exists for those groups, but it does not by itself
provide the required full-automorphism tournament representation.

## 4. A concrete 50-vertex certificate

For r=2 the existence claim can be replaced by a small explicit certificate.
On V=F5^2 orient x->y exactly when y-x belongs to

    S={(0,3),(0,4),(1,0),
       (3,0),(3,1),(3,2),(3,3),(3,4),
       (4,1),(4,2),(4,3),(4,4)}.                    (J7)

The set chooses exactly one of every pair {v,-v}, v nonzero, so this is
a tournament. Translations are25 automorphisms. To certify that they
are all of them, distinguish vertex0 and refine colors by the current
color together with the incoming and outgoing counts to every color
class. The numbers of color classes are

    2,3,15,21,25.

Every automorphism fixing0 preserves every refinement class. The final
partition is discrete, so the stabilizer of0 is trivial. Orbit-stabilizer
then proves that the full automorphism group is exactly the25 translations.
This is a literal finite certificate checked by the companion script,
not a heuristic that the Cayley normalizer is probably small.

Uniform TT2 inflation gives50 vertices. Its homogeneous-pair graph is
checked directly to be exactly the25 internal TT2 edges, certifying the
canonical-block sidecar. Its gap polynomial has rho=1, nu=5 and leading
positive-jet coefficient3000. The next family member has rho=1 and
nu=25, using the cited all-r existence theorem.

## 5. The older F21 doubling tower exposes a different correlation

The relevant older sidecar is
[THM-4557, doubling a doubly regular tournament keeps its automorphism group](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4557-doubling-a-doubly-regular-tournament-keeps-its-automorphism-group-hyp-9162.md).
For the skew-Hadamard tower R_3=P7 and R_(k+1)=D(R_k), it proves the full
group remains F21 and acts diagonally on two old copies plus one fixed
new vertex. Each R_k is regular and therefore pair-free. Uniform TT2
inflation again transfers that exact action to the gaps.

For an automorphism alpha let

    F_alpha(t)=product_(cycles c on old vertices)(1+t^|c|).

Under doubling it becomes (1+t)*F_alpha(t)^2. Thus the new cut polynomial
is

    P_new(t)=(1+t)*average_alpha F_alpha(t)^2,         (J8)

not (1+t)*(average_alpha F_alpha(t))^2. The same automorphism acts on
both copies. If H_a(t)=average_alpha F_alpha(t)^a, the exact moment rule is

    H'_a(t)=(1+t)^a H_(2a)(t).                       (J9)

At P7 the full group has cycle types1^7 once,7 once per each of six
elements, and1*3^2 for fourteen elements. For u=2^(k-3), the inflated
tower's gap polynomial is consequently the three-term formula

    P_k(t)=(1+t)^(u-1)/21 *
           [(1+t)^(7u)+6*(1+t^7)^u
                         +14*(1+t)^u*(1+t^3)^(2u)]. (J10)

No enumeration of the tower's tournament classes is needed for this
family formula. Both gap-orbit rank and cancellation depth obey a'=2a+1,
starting from1, so

    rho_k=nu_k=2^(k-2)-1.

This tower has growing depth together with growing toggle rank; it is
different from the rank-one family in (J6). Preserving the abstract group
does not preserve the number of its orbits or its cancellation depth.

The smallest doubling hostile is exact: for the15-gap doubled P7 action,
the coefficient of t^2 in (J8) is7; the independent-copy expression gives5.
Their first-degree counts happen to agree. Retaining the shared group
element, or equivalently the cycle-product moments, is the necessary
correlation sidecar for doubling.

## 6. A positive interpolation between pair-free counts and A000568

Let q(T) be the number of maximal transitive modules of an N-vertex T,
so its gap count is N-q(T). Summing its weighted inflation fibre gives
t^q(T)*P_T(t). By the corrected meaning of b_(M,E), define

    R_N(u)=sum_(M=0)^N b_(M,N-M)*(1+u)^M
          =sum_([T] of order N) (1+u)^q(T)*Q_T(u).   (J11)

The second expression proves R_N has nonnegative integer coefficients.
Its constant coefficient is the pair-free count; its leading coefficient
is A000568(N), since every T contributes leading coefficient one.
The apparent signed sum on the left has therefore become a positive
polynomial on the right.

For N>0 let a_j=[u^j]R_N(u). There is also the exact monotonicity

    a_0 <= a_1/binom(N,1) <= ... <= a_N.             (J12)

For a fixed T and alpha, the coefficient of u^j in
(1+u)^q(T)*product_c((1+u)^|c|-1), divided by binom(N,j), is the
probability that a uniform j-subset of N positions meets every gap-cycle
of alpha. The q(T) free positions need not be met. This event is
monotone under adding a position; couple all uniform subset sizes by a
uniform random ordering. Averaging over alpha and summing over T proves
(J12). No choice of labelled representative affects this probability.

This is an exact positive interpolation, not a closed scalar recurrence
for A000568. Computing the action cycle indices or all bivariate
coefficients can still be expensive. In particular, a low jet sees no
T with nu(T) above its degree, and the rank-one family makes that blind
range unbounded. The [moment transfer](README.md) addresses a different
finite-horizon linear aggregation; neither theorem supplies the data
discarded by the other.
