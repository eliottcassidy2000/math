## 2. The low-credit actual graph is forced


Every edge has inherited minimum zero except `9--30`, whose minimum is102.

There are exactly three spanning trees of this graph. All contain the four

edges, and at least one of `8--48`, `32--48`. In particular it contains one
of the two actual paths

```

```

The edge9--30 is disjoint from the three labels of either path and has
uniform credit at least102. The pair credits of the two arms of (2) are

This isolates a complete zero-arm universe, rather than a chosen ratio-tree
assignment. The potential graph argument covers every actual topology with
