## One action explains all four numbers

The same 5000-element normalizer has signed kernel 25 and image 200. Forgetting orientation signs enlarges its kernel to 100 and reduces its image to 50:
5000=25·200=100·50.

The forgotten information is exactly two bits: global orientation reversal and reversal on odd-valuation layers. Two named probes restore them. This is an explicit quotient diagram, not numerology. See [SIGN_QUOTIENT](SIGN_QUOTIENT.md).

## Main advance: an all-depth symmetry recurrence

The useful new object is the actual action on the ramified tournament bank's orientation coordinates. At the first nontrivial level it splits into independent quotient actions; later levels retain a shared residue.

For a simply ramified metallic prime p and k≥2, the specified Sylow-p subgroup of the effective normalizer has Q-color orbit counts satisfying
B_(p,k+1)(Q)=[Q^(p−1)B_(p,k)(Q^p)+(p−1)Q^((p−1)(k+1))]/p.

The base is explicit. At p=5:

## Two different meanings of 200

At modulus25, Fibonacci multiplication, its square and its fourth power have full clocks100,50,25. But the 200-element group generated by multiplication and conjugation is
C4×D50,
whereas the effective full normalizer on bank choices is
C20×C10.

The first has a cyclic Sylow5 subgroup C25; the second has C5². Their exponents are100 and 20. Neither is the automorphism group of a single tournament: tournament automorphism groups have odd order.

The same abstract C5² also has different actions in this packet. On the old25 gaps it is regular; on the ramified24-bit bank it has four fixed coordinates and four five-point quotient orbits. Their cancellation depths differ. Group order alone is insufficient.

See [PISANO_GROUPS](PISANO_GROUPS.md). Its CRT hostile also explains why the signed clock at modulus100 is300, not150: the earlier scalar return has incompatible local phases.

The conservative constant200 in Session110's spectral bound is unrelated to either group order. No sharp-constant interpretation is asserted.

## A literal information interpretation

A legal fibre shift in the 625-vertex bank moves125 vertices in 25 five-cycles. One fair binary port imposes100 equality constraints. Two independent ports leave50 free bits out of 250, imposing200 constraints.

Correlated ports change that answer. Three pairwise-independent bits can still carry a parity relation: the symmetry-test probability changes from 2^(-300) to 2^(-200), despite identical pairwise marginals. See [PORT_INFORMATION](PORT_INFORMATION.md).

This is why joint port data, rather than separate summaries, matter to continued composition.

## Recovering the arithmetic thread

Gaussian multiplication gives an exact bijection
(a,b)↦(b−a,b+a),
transporting the complete square-sum shells25→50→100→200. Its second iterate doubles both coordinates. The Euclid lift preserves primitive triangle shape, so this is not a new Berggren child operation.

Aliquot sums do not follow the same transport: their values are6,43,117,265. Eisenstein shell sizes are6,0,6,0, because2 is inert there. These are precise boundaries to the analogy. See [ARITHMETIC](ARITHMETIC.md).

## What the synthesis suggests

The strongest common mechanism is composition through a shared quotient:
- retain the residue linking two cyclic actions;
- retain local scalar phases when using CRT;
- retain the joint palette law when testing ports;
- retain representation coordinates and scale when doubling norms;
- retain the odd-part divisor sum when transporting aliquot data.

These are explicit mathematical carriers, not a proposed identification of all underlying problems. The next substantial enumeration step would add ideal-cut conditions and full graph transporters to the bank recurrence. That remains open.
