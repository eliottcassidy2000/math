# The 4–2–1 choice quotient: shorting, Kron reduction, and integral information

**Status: elementary proofs and exact controls, awaiting independent audit.** This is a network on the balanced family's **choice states**, not an identification with its tournament metagraph. No literature-priority claim is made for the standard weighted-complete-graph identities.

## 1. Inheritance and exact carrier

The balanced two-return family has Hamiltonian paths indexed by X×X, where X consists of a Boolean subset set and one exceptional state infinity. Reflection swaps the two coordinates; diagonal paths are blue, and unordered off-diagonal pairs are the edges of the complete choice graph K_X. These facts and the literal deletion map are inherited from `zero_heavy_ports_20261020g/microcosm.md` and `balanced_fibre_endpoint_extensions_20261020/BALANCED_DELETION_EXTENSIONS.md`.

Let f:X'→X have q>=2 target states, m=q−1 finite states, s>=1 preimages over each finite state, and one preimage over infinity. Then Q=|X'|=sm+1. In actual d-level reflected balanced growth, s=2^d. The product deletion has fibre sizes s²,s,1 over finite/finite, mixed, and infinity/infinity parent states. For d=1 these are 4,2,1.

Write w_x=|f^(-1)(x)|. The present network construction retains the actual labelled partition and its exceptional state. The separate port-defect theorem shows that path deletion does not generally intertwine the physical metagraph flip. None of the network identities below removes that obstruction.

## 2. Exact contraction and its invisible internal mass

Start with K_(X') with unit edge conductances. Identify all vertices in each f-fibre, retaining parallel edge multiplicities and loops. Between distinct parent states x,y the conductance is

    c_xy=w_x w_y.

At x there are binom(w_x,2) collapsed internal edges, now loops. For a parent potential u, its constant-fibre lift has exactly the same Dirichlet energy:

    sum_{a<b in X'}(u(f(a))−u(f(b)))²
      =sum_{x<y in X}w_x w_y(u(x)−u(y))².                (1)

The loop terms vanish. With W=sum w_x=Q, the electrical Laplacian is

    L=Q diag(w)−w w^T.                                  (2)

This is shorting/contraction, not elimination of free internal potentials.

The lost loop mass is mathematically meaningful for the path problem. A diagonal parent (x,x) has w_x² lifts, of which w_x are blue and w_x(w_x−1) are ordered black paths. Modulo reflection, those black lifts are exactly the binom(w_x,2) collapsed choice edges. The electrical Laplacian is insensitive to arbitrary internal loop mass. Recovering the black-to-blue deletion correction therefore requires the loop counts or the fibre/source data that determine them. In this pointed complete-source family, the known exceptional state has weight one, so w_x=−L_(x,infinity) for finite x; the specified source provenance then reconstructs binom(w_x,2). This reconstruction is not available from an arbitrary looped network Laplacian without such provenance.

At s=2 the nonloop conductances are four between two finite states and two between a finite state and infinity. The exceptional one-point diagonal fibre is a **blue state**, not a conductance-one edge. It would be a type error to turn all three numbers 4,2,1 into ordinary edge weights without specifying this difference.

## 3. Resistance and spanning trees of the contracted choice network

For any distinct a,b, inject one unit at a and remove it at b. Equation (2) is solved, modulo an additive constant, by

    u_i=(delta_(i,a)−delta_(i,b))/(Q w_i).

Indeed sum_i w_i u_i=0. Hence

    R_ab=(1/w_a+1/w_b)/Q.                               (3)

For arbitrary positive weights, the weighted spanning-tree count is

    tau=Q^(q−2) product_x w_x.                          (4)

One proof is to delete a root r from (2) and apply the determinant lemma to the positive diagonal Q diag(w_other) and the rank-one subtraction w_other w_other^T. The factor left by the lemma is w_r/Q, giving (4). Loops contribute neither to this cofactor nor to a tree.

With the pointed weights s,...,s,1, this becomes

    tau=s^m Q^(m−1),
    R_finite,finite=2/(Qs),
    R_finite,infinity=(s+1)/(Qs).                        (5)

These are exact weighted-network quantities, not counts of tournament classes.

## 4. The full critical group, with an explicit presentation proof

Ground infinity. The integer reduced Laplacian is

    L_red=s[Q I_m−s J_m],       Q=sm+1.                  (6)

**Proposition.** Its critical group is

    C_s direct-sum (C_(sQ))^(m−1),                      (7)

where C_1 is trivial. Equivalently its Smith invariant factors are s followed by m−1 copies of sQ.

Proof. Let e_1,...,e_m generate Z^m and S=sum e_i. The relation lattice from (6) is generated by

    r_i=sQ e_i−s² S.

Summing these relations gives sum r_i=sS, since Q−sm=1. Consequently the same lattice is generated by sS and all sQ e_i: each is a combination of the r_i, and conversely each r_i is a combination of those generators. Replace the generator basis by

    e_1,...,e_(m−1),S.

This is unimodular. In that basis the relations reduce to sQ e_i for i<m and sS; the remaining sQ e_m follows from them. This gives (7), including m=1. QED.

For s=2^d with d>=1, Q is odd. Therefore the entire 2-primary subgroup is

    (C_(2^d))^m.                                        (8)

By contrast K_Q has critical group (C_Q)^(Q−2), of odd order. Contracting these fibres creates 2-primary critical-group information even though (1) preserves all constant-fibre energies. The integral firing lattice is not preserved by that energy restriction.

At q=3,s=2 the contracted critical group is C2 direct-sum C10, with 2-primary subgroup V4. The full group has order20, not4. At q=5,s=2 its 2-primary rank is four, not the arithmetic conductor tower's rank two. At q=3,s=4 the 2-primary subgroup is C4×C4, not the conductor16 group C4×C2. The minimal V4 coincidence is therefore a precisely bounded comparison, not an all-level identification with quadratic ideal classes.

## 5. Kron reduction is a different quotient

Instead keep one representative of each fibre as a terminal and eliminate the other Q−q vertices by the Schur complement. No vertices are shorted together. The original K_Q Laplacian is QI_Q−J_Q. If h=Q−q>0, its interior block is QI_h−J_h, and

    1_h^T (QI_h−J_h)^(-1)1_h=h/q.

The reduced boundary Laplacian is therefore

    L_Kron=Q I_q−(Q/q)J_q=(Q/q)L_(K_q).                 (9)

For h=0 the same formula is immediate. Every remaining edge has conductance Q/q and every terminal-pair resistance is 2/Q, exactly as in the original K_Q. This reduction does not depend on the fibre sizes or the choice of representatives.

For q=3,s=2, contraction gives finite/finite resistance1/5 and finite/infinity resistance3/10. Kron reduction gives2/5 for every pair. Thus the same endpoint labels and parent count do not specify the intended quotient. The operation—shorting versus elimination—is load-bearing information.

The rational Kron matrix does not have the same integral critical-group interpretation as (6) without an additional lattice/denominator convention. Multiplying it by a common denominator changes the network and its firing lattice; it is not an invariant-preserving cosmetic step.

## 6. This choice walk really is lumpable, including its holding probabilities

For the simple random walk on K_Q, a vertex in fibre x moves to fibre y!=x with probability

    P_xy=w_y/(Q−1),       P_xx=(w_x−1)/(Q−1).            (10)

These depend only on the current fibre, not its representative. Thus this partition is strongly lumpable for this particular walk. Its stationary distribution is pi_x=w_x/Q, and its off-diagonal stationary flow is

    pi_x P_xy=w_xw_y/[Q(Q−1)],

consistent with (1) after normalization. Collapsed loops encode the holding probability. Ignoring them and using the normalized loopless Laplacian walk changes the process.

This is a genuine positive quotient theorem for the complete **choice** walk. It is not a claim that the deterministic free-arc flip on physical tournament tilings is lumpable under deletion or isomorphism classes.

## 7. Lost bits and the blue correction under a specified probability law

Choose a child path uniformly from X'×X'. Its parent (x,y) has probability w_xw_y/Q², not the uniform law on X×X. Conditional on that parent the child is uniform in a fibre of size w_xw_y. For s=2^d the conditional Shannon entropy in bits is exactly

    H(child | parent)=2dm s/Q.                          (11)

This is twice the probability that one coordinate lies over a finite state, times d. It is not the same average as choosing a uniform parent first.

The child-blue probability is 1/Q. The probability that the parent is blue is

    (m s²+1)/Q².

Their difference is m s(s−1)/Q²: precisely the ordered internal black lifts erased by deletion. For q=3,s=2, the lost entropy is8/5 bits, child-blue probability5/25, parent-blue probability9/25, and the difference4/25 corresponds to two collapsed undirected black choice edges.

The bit count, loop mass, electrical energy, and integral critical group are different observables of the same labelled partition. Preserving one does not automatically preserve the others. This is the exact information-flow lesson relevant to the 4–2–1 fibres.
