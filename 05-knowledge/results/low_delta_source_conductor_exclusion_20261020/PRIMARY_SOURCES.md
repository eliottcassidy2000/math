# Primary-source checks

Checked 20 October 2026 by direct web retrieval. Mathematical derivations are in the main proof, not inferred from search snippets.

1. Janusz Gwoździewicz, *Injectivity on one line*, arXiv:alg-geom/9305008v1, 19 May 1993. Original two-page PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9305008v1. Theorem 1.1 on page 1 was read directly. It assumes an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field and a polynomial map k²→k² with nonzero constant Jacobian, and concludes polynomial invertibility from injectivity on one affine line. This is the exact external theorem used in relative globalization. No enlargement to arbitrary relative orders is made.

2. Zbigniew Jelonek, *A note on the Jacobian Conjecture*, arXiv:2011.03472v3, 1 February 2021. Versioned text: https://arxiv.org/html/2011.03472v3. Section 2, Theorem 2.7 independently records the preceding one-line theorem and cites Gwoździewicz in bibliography item [4]. The source contains an apparent ambient-line typesetting typo; the original Gwoździewicz Theorem 1.1 has the correct k² ambient space. The primary original, not that typo, fixes our scope.

3. Avinash Sathaye and Jon Stenerson, *On Plane Polynomial Curves*, author-hosted text dated 10 September 2014, https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/papnew.pdf. Section 3.1, printed page 9 (zero-based PDF page 8), item (3) states the symmetric-semigroup conductor property. The preceding page explains the relation with plane curves having one place at infinity. These are contextual checks of a classical theorem independently proved in our note. The published chapter is *Plane Polynomial Curves*, Algebraic Geometry and its Applications (1994), 121–142, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-2628-4_6; the DOI publisher fetch failed, so no byte-identical comparison with the 1994 printing is asserted.

4. Masakazu Suzuki, *Affine plane curves with one place at infinity*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 49(2) (1999), 375–404. Primary PDF: https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.1678.pdf. Its title, publication information, introduction, and account of the Abhyankar–Moh semigroup theorem were inspected. It is background only; our proof does not import an unstated low-delta classification from this paper.

No novelty claim is based on a failed search or on the lack of an identically phrased theorem title.
