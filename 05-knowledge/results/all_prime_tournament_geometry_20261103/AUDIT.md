## Proof gates

* AP1 fixes every old arc, assumes genuine old modules, and counts only
  modules incident to the q newborns along a Hamilton path. At most2q
  distinct modules are touched; a vertex touching twice does not buy two
  separate modules beyond that budget. Factorization is into integer
  ordered-cover counts, without division by automorphism factors.
* AP2 takes a gcd of all size-k products. Different primes may use different
  minimizing subsets; no common-subset assertion is made. The non-UFD
  version uses ideals, not element factorizations. Pair coordinates are
  truly an exterior linear map; the incoming Walsh grading is not an
  exterior algebra isomorphism.
* AP3's minimality argument explicitly handles the asymmetric5+3 split
  and the<=2-vertex factor case. The complete order<=5 census is needed;
  looking only at order4 profiles would not suffice. No valuation-increase
  minimality is claimed. Fixed slot and wrapper divisors remain separate.
* AP4 allows empty segments and both positions of c,z, rejects z->c, and
  handles two different runs of the same block incident to z without using
  the root twice. Internal weights include the factorial ordering factors.
* AP5 uses a cofinal nested modulus chain and a uniform finite height. The
  non-diagonal idempotent does not refute local-global solvability of its
  polynomial. Product-formula compatibility alone is explicitly rejected.
* AP6 inherits the stronger all-base digit and future-operation theorems;
  it does not redescribe a fixed-modulus summary as a source-specific
  Collatz proof. The all-odd family keeps shortcut and ordinary clocks apart.
* AP7 credits THM-4104 and the MRB sparse probe bank. Its exact ideal allows
  integer linear combinations, so ideal1 is not confused with an actual
  response1 or an actual ternary return. The source-specific missing values
  are tested over all512 cuts by an independent Hamilton recursion.
