## 1. The exact object and its physical witness map

Let C be a nonempty finite positive integer body and T a set of three
distinct positive integers prime to three. Define the actual closed safe set

```text
G_C={y mod1: ||cy||>=1/14 for every c in C}.
```

For a chosen tail w, its positive effective endpoint set is

```text
E_w^+={y_k=(14k+3)/(14w): k in Z/wZ}.
```

At such a phase, `||wy_k||=3/14`. The tail w has no **strictly** bad sheet;
its endpoint of clearance exactly `1/14` is weak-safe. Each of the other
two ternary-unit tails blocks at most one of the three lifts. Hence

```text
E_w^+ intersects G_C  ==>  G_(3C union T) is nonempty.       (1)
```

To extract a witness, compute the two other owner masks at y and choose a
missing label j. Then `x=(y+j)/3` is the physical safe phase. Every body
speed satisfies `||(3c)x||=||cy||`; every tail satisfies the weak target.
One must choose a missing owner label, not an arbitrary label associated
with the boundary tail. Reflection gives the same Boolean result for the
negative endpoint coset.

The exact body blocker set for frequency c is

```text
B_w(c)={k in Z/wZ : dist_(14w)(c(14k+3),0)<w}.
```

Thus the retained event deficit is

```text
D_w(C)=w-|union_(c in C) B_w(c)|.                         (2)
```

This is an exact finite cyclic code, not a scalar Haar estimate. The event
test succeeds iff `D_w(C)>0`. Evaluating it needs only body residues modulo
`14w`; it does not enumerate the teeth of the other two tails.

## 2. Exact marginal count and the single-owner boundary

Put `g=gcd(c,w)`, `q=w/g`, and `h=c/g`. Multiplication by h permutes the
q residue classes. Each resulting class has multiplicity g among the w
event labels. The bad values are precisely integers n with

```text
-q<n<q,  n=3h mod14.
```

Consequently the exact number of blocked event points is

```text
|B_w(c)|=g [ floor((q-1-3h)/14)
             +floor((q-1+3h)/14)+1 ].                    (3)
```

The endpoint inequalities are strict; replacing q-1 by q would include
weak-safe equality points. Counting a progression in this interval gives

```text
|B_w(c)|<=g ceil(q/7).                                   (4)
```

For `q>=2`, `ceil(q/7)<q`, so one body frequency cannot cover the whole event
set. For `q=1`, all event values are equal and are bad iff `3h=0 mod14`,
equivalently `14|h`. Therefore the exact single-owner statement is

```text
B_w(c)=Z/wZ  iff  14w divides c.                         (5)
```

The verifier independently compares (3)-(5) against literal residues for
all 4,920 pairs `1<=w<=61`, `3` not dividing w, `1<=c<=120`.

Marginal counts, gcds, and h modulo fourteen still lose phase. At w=5,

```text

```

All three speeds have the same `g=1`, `q=5`, and `h=1 mod14`, hence the same
count. But the two-body unions for `{1,71}` and `{1,29}` have sizes one and
two. The full frequency modulo `14w`, or an equivalent affine event word,

## 3. A cheap sufficient divisor gate

Suppose `|C|=10` and a chosen tail w satisfies:

* at least four body speeds are divisible by w;
* no body speed is divisible by `14w`;
* every body speed not divisible by w is coprime to w.

The divisible speeds block no event point by (5); each of the at most six
remaining speeds blocks at most `ceil(w/7)` points by (4). Thus the exact
sufficient numerical condition is

