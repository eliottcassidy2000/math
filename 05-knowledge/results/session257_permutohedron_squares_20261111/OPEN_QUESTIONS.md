# Next constructive gates

These are research questions, not asserted results or unrequested ongoing
work. Chan–Pak remains OPEN. General square-sum paths for n>=25 and cycles
for n>=32 are already cited inputs; the targets below demand extra structure.

## 1. Choose useful cut charts without knowing the destination path

The exact theorem gives r+1 common pieces AFTER two paths at surgery distance
r are known. The canonical Q25 forest freezes every elementary chart move,
even though two-edge surgery connects all solutions. Find a small adaptive
rule selecting cuts from the current source and target endpoint/color demand,
then prove a useful navigation or reachability statement. A valid result must
retain named seam edges and orientations. Ambient permutohedron connectivity
alone is insufficient.

Cheapest test: recover the two one-edge components of Q25 and the specific
cuts allowing their two-edge transitions, without listing destination paths
as input. Then test the same policy at the inherited Q28→Q29 obstruction.

## 2. Couple primitive reflection dynamics to a count operation

The square-target census recovers the triple and compiles it into actual
sorted-spinor tournaments. Its Berggren action is rebuild-and-compile, not a
local square-graph rewrite, and its count misses class1 modulo3. Can a
retained reflection itinerary select the UNSORTED Session256 word carrier
or a physical five-core repair with a controlled count change?

Specify source, operation, output triple, H law and returning boundary class.
A diagram that only preserves the spinor is already supplied here. The
stronger scalar transport H=2h+1 is globally impossible by Q23/Q25.

## 3. Identify the positive multi-seam sector

Two matching seam edges give an equality Gram kernel. Four expose the
indefinite connectivity matrix J−I. Classify natural restrictions on port
pairings or observables yielding a positive connection kernel while still
counting the intended connected object. Nonnegative entries are not enough,
and replacing connectedness by pairing equality changes the combinatorics.

A constructive target is a genuine planar or noncrossing boundary family
with a proved positive sector and sharp realized probes. No such broad
positivity statement is claimed here.

## 4. Compute subgroup marks behind the prime shells

The full/partial/zero prime-depth regimes depend only on the known group and
stabilizer orders. Their fixed-section dimensions and fixed attachment counts
require actual marks |(M/(2.B))^P|. Derive these from class fusion into2.B
for selected P, retaining the permitted port relation. Equal valuations
provably do not determine them.

The modular-level question becomes concrete: can the level1/2/6 correspondences
recover any of these marks, beyond their zero/nonzero prime support? This
would be a new bridge, whereas matching the same prime lists is inherited.

## 5. Make the normalization wall useful for an actual operation

The vector shift v→v−e_2 contracts360 edges and changes the artificial cycle
operator. Generalize to centralizer covers, tracking which weight walls are
hit and whether associated subgroup marks change. Keep normalization,
prime labels and action separate. The known S6 orbit polytope is not a
Monster Weyl group, and no modulo9 dynamics has been restored.

## 6. Use the new content lifetime without confusing it with reachability

Incoming C_(n−1)(T)=1 removes every common prime divisor under unrestricted
additions by a finite budget. The Session256 restricted positive grammar and
this packet's square/port constructions have smaller operation classes.
Determine which actual marked additions escape their fixed-budget obstructions
AND return to a useful source class. Gcd1 or residue coverage cannot replace
an exact target witness.

The useful interface comparison is explicit: Session211's apex flip uses
ordered possibly-empty TWO-path covers; square birth uses two components
with designated endpoints in different components; two-seam gluing uses
one path on each side. A proposed universal constructor must reconcile
those ownership/orientation/emptiness conventions, not merely identify the
number of pieces.
