## 1. Branch holes and deletion quantifiers

The same h_0 must serve every difference coordinate. Three pairwise-overlapping

the joint obstruction.

## 2. Relation-table quotas are not physical allocations

For fixed complete E and exact row/column counts, a q-avoiding table is an
integral unit-edge b-matching on E minus W_q. A cut with source-side row set U
and column set V has capacity

    M - sum_U d + |(E minus W_q) intersect (U x (Z minus V))| + sum_V e.

Its deficiency below M is exactly the producer's stated deficit. Thus a
positive deficit is equivalent to unavoidable q, provided the original quota
family is first shown nonempty. The converse produces an actual avoiding
table, not merely an inconclusive inequality. Our independent enumeration
uses complete tables rather than a max-flow implementation: every 2-by-3
compatibility graph and every equal-total quota were tested, with 4,288
infeasible families explicitly excluded and 1,408 feasible charge cases.

For complete binary-labelled n-by-n tables, the four cell counts determined by
t are t,a-t,b-t,n-a-b+t. Their factorial coefficient counts actual labelled
pairings. Direct named-permutation enumeration through n=6 verifies every
coefficient and the minima |n-a-b| and |a-b|. Universal retention of both
charges is therefore exactly a != b and a+b != n. The result does not require
an individually complete conditioned branch. It counts predicate tables and
their retained full-pair witnesses, never simultaneous resource units.

## 3. Square-zero coefficients and hyperedges

The occupancy proof is correct over nonnegative integer coefficients with
commuting square-zero resource variables. Expanding selects a labelled full

Nonnegative characteristic-zero coefficients prevent cancellation. Actual
within-witness injection must be checked before turning resource lists into
sets. Full tags or all future-visible separator records must survive until
every noncapacity relation has been applied. Total charge alone does not

This is not exterior algebra: (x_a+x_b)^2 has coefficient 2 at x_a x_b in the
commutative square-zero compiler, recording two valid assignments. Exterior
signs cancel those assignments, as does reduction modulo 2. Neither replacement
is a valid existence test here.

Hall is an iff only for independent single-resource choices at a sufficient
full boundary. For A={{0,1},{2,3}} and B={{0,2},{1,3}}, every A/B pair meets.
Each family has a disjoint two-option pack; even weighted union cardinalities
and uncoloured matching tests pass. There is no simultaneous allocation.
The independent check tests every pair of rank-two families on four resources.

The sufficient greedy criterion is correctly quantified over every prefix:
before demand i, at most K_i resources are occupied. If no K_i-resource set
hits its whole family, some option remains. Pack and degree estimates are
sufficient ways to establish this condition, not necessary allocation tests.
Empty options consume no resources; empty menus fail. Native realization uses
fixed slot counts per demand. Variable-size hyperedges require additional
unused-slot semantics rather than silently removing physical slots.

The final uniform repair-assignment corollary is also valid: private base

## 6. Additive-only Davenport bound and its exact sharpness scope

Population pairing is valid within cohorts having equal complete margin
columns: every 0-to-1 switch is paired with a 1-to-0 switch. The resulting
disjoint two-slot increments sum to zero in the remaining complete finite
guard Q. Partition them into zero-sum subsequences of length at most D(Q).
Every resulting event preserves each exact population, preserves Q and
switches each differing physical slot once. Any additional relation must be
separately closed under these intermediate events.

For Q=C128 x C2^4, D(Q)=132. Independently, the F2 group algebra is the truncated
polynomial algebra with generator exponents bounded by 127,1,1,1,1. Its maximum
nonzero total degree is 131, so the augmentation ideal's 132nd power is zero.
A zero-sum-free length-132 product of (1-X^g) would have identity coefficient
1, a contradiction. Conversely 127 cyclic generators and one of each binary
generator form a zero-sum-free sequence of length 131. The independent code
checks the full 2,048-dimensional sharp augmentation product and small p-groups.
This auxiliary characteristic-two proof does not replace the nonnegative
occupancy algebra of Section 3.

The conditional uniform event bound is consequently 264 slots. Actual clock
cost is the sum of selected per-slot endpoint costs, bounded by the largest
min(264,|S|) such costs; total route work equals exact endpoint work. It is not
an improvement over replacing an entire 82-slot endpoint directly, nor an
authentication of that group's physical meaning at a new order.

With 132 separate two-slot population classes, the increments consisting of
127 e0, e1,e2,e3,e4 and e0+e1+e2+e3+e4 form a minimal zero-sum sequence. Without
the last term only the empty subset vanishes; with it all other terms are
required. Each class has one V, forcing its two slots to flip together. Exactly
two accepted global states remain, separated by 264 slots. This proves abstract
sharpness with arbitrary numbers of population classes, not with just the
core/helper populations and not for an unconstructed native electrical bank.
