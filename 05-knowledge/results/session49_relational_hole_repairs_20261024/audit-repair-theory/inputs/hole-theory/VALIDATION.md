# Exact verification and limitations

The standalone checker executes 684,500 explicit conditions without Python
assert statements. Normal and optimized Python reproduce the identical frozen
receipt. The manifest-only relocated replay starts from just the listed packet
files and separately runs both interpreter modes.

Finite universes exhausted:

- Every one of 256 predicates on a declared eight-pair full-record universe,
  at all four grades, in C2 through C5: 4,096 graded predicate cases and
  3,968 literal witness reconstructions.
- 4,872 three-branch joint-hole cases in cyclic groups through C3, including
  14,344 weighted whole-branch deletion queries.
- All 512 bipartite 3-by-3 witness graphs: 1,536 weighted covers and
  8,192 separate left/right deletion-budget queries. The three-lift single
  resource hostile distinguishes record cover three from resource ban one.
- All 64 bipartite 2-by-3 compatibility graphs and all balanced bounded exact
  quotas: 4,992 quota cases, 704 nonempty relation families and 3,520 charge
  robustness tests against direct exhaustive relation-table enumeration.
  A separate 24-table positive fixture retains both C2 charges in every
  prescribed-marginal relation; the 2-by-2 full-projection hostile does not.
- All named bijections for every pair of binary charge multiplicities at
  orders one through seven: 203 marginal cases and 362,878 pairing
  instances. Exact attainable charge counts, sharp minima and the factorial
  coefficients of the marked relation-table polynomial all agree.
- All 256 accepted subsets of the binary three-slot universe under 32 pairs
  of simultaneous slot/clock budgets: 8,192 graphs, 147,456 endpoint tests
  and 83,608 fully reconstructed paid routes.
- All 64 three-vertex digraphs under 27 labelled boundary partitions:
  1,728 directed SCC quotients and 15,552 endpoint reachability tests.
- The inherited nine-state ternary-code nonreturn hostile: ordinary
  three-slot routing exists while full-record no-return fails; four slots
  permits the direct event and fixed normalized units never release.

The finite fixtures have declared abstract charges, complete assignment
identities, compatibility relations and occurrence costs. They are not falsely
presented as authenticated tournaments, native menus, physical potentials,
owner embeddings or global Hamiltonian observers. The native use contract
requires those inputs independently, including full paired dyadic equivalence,
source and target rows, diagonal terms, exact populations, kernel records,
inverse/converse closure and normalized-resource capacity.

The all-size implications are proved in `THEOREM.md`; finite exhaustive
controls supplement the proofs and do not extend native supply to other orders.
