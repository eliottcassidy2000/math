# Session49: holes, compatible witnesses, and non-reacquiring wiring

24 October 2026. This synthesis integrates the scoped theorems and certificates
linked below. It does not claim a complete all-order tournament wiring theorem.

## 1. The exact object is a witnessed conditional section

The inherited hole-difference rule remains correct for its declared compatible
binary repair records. A new relation may change the receiver domain differently
in each branch. One must first retain those actual branch domains.

The [relational hole theorem](hole-theory/THEOREM.md) writes their supports as
A_z at the requested full boundary and exact grade, and their repair charges as
c_z. The resulting holes are precisely the intersection of c_z+(Q minus A_z).
For several branches, the obstruction is a joint difference tuple, not separate
pairwise tests. Three translated hole sets can have every pair intersect while
their joint intersection is empty. Each successful section retains a complete
compatible record, not just its charge.

Arbitrary pins cannot universally preserve a nontrivial charge range: a relation
selecting one assignment retains only that assignment's charge. The constructive
question is which explicit relational families retain enough witnessed sections,
and at what exact cost those sections can be joined.

### Exact non-reacquiring thresholds with the exterior fixed

The [receiver-threshold theorem](receiver-thresholds/THEOREM.md) and
[independent threshold audit](audit-receiver-thresholds/AUDIT.md) sharpen the
finite receiver-only question. With triple1457=1841=3015 and every exterior
record fixed, every nonempty grade/charge fibre connects without resource
reacquisition using at most16 slots and34 clocks per event, simultaneously.
Both uniform bounds are sharp. The statement covers all grades0..19; it does
not assert that every charge occurs at every grade.

At grades9 and10, the exact ordinary uniform threshold is14 slots/30 clocks,
while strict non-reacquisition requires16/34. Thus the requirement has a genuine
cost even in the fully authenticated original charged carrier, not just in the
neutral prefix example below. At grades8 and11, ordinary routing already needs
16/34. No exterior helper movement or receiver refresh outside the endpoint
interval is used in the strict theorem.

A stronger endpoint-specific separation occurs at grade9/charge3: the retained
endpoint pair has exact ordinary optimum8 slots/18 clocks but strict optimum
16/34. Its certified three-event ordinary route reacquires released resources;
the strict route must pay the larger event. Pair-optimal thresholds are distinct
from the worst-case uniform thresholds at that grade.

The structural proof is broader than the finite census. In any finite accepted
binary family with disjoint alternative resources, a strict route stays in the
endpoint interval. An accepted pair with no internal accepted interval state
therefore forces a direct move. Conversely, recursively splitting every other
pair into accepted intermediate interval states gives disjoint, target-directed
changes. Uniform strict budgets are exactly the coordinatewise maxima of these
interval-indecomposable moves. This is a conformal primitive-move mechanism, not
a claim that arbitrary relational pins have uniformly small primitives.

The receiver census contains131,072 assignments,23,444 nonempty fibres, and
474,896 unordered endpoint pairs, with at most22 states in a fibre. Independent
endpoint-monotone dynamic programming and interval recursion agree; ordinary
path and spanning-tree computations agree separately. The actual event-cost
pairs form a coordinatewise chain, confirming that the reported slot/clock
thresholds are jointly attainable rather than incompatible separate minima.

The worst strict endpoints also have an exact counting explanation. There are
196 normalized primitive trades of support16 and cost34. Each changes the linked
triple and leaves three independent base bits untouched, so its common contexts
give the grade polynomial

    196 z^8 (1+z)^3.

Its coefficients196,588,588,196 count all1,568 strict34-clock endpoint pairs at
grades8..11. There are no extra decomposable critical pairs: every nonzero
primitive has support at least4, while any same-grade endpoint pair changes at
most18 slots. A decomposition into two or more primitives therefore uses only
supports at most14 and costs at most30. This is a structural consequence of the
audited primitive catalogue, not a conjecture extrapolated from the number196.
The [polynomial audit supplement](audit-receiver-polynomial/AUDIT.md) pins the
final generating-function revision separately from the earlier threshold audit.

## 3. Relational robustness has exact cut certificates

For one requested debt, regard each compatible receiver/repair full-record pair
as a witness. A declared resource or record ban removes every incident witness.
The [hole theorem](hole-theory/THEOREM.md) identifies the least destructive ban
as a weighted hitting-set problem. It becomes a bipartite vertex-cover/min-cut
problem only when the permitted bans are whole receiver or repair assignments.
One resource can remove many kernel lifts, so a raw-record cover number is not
automatically a resource resilience number.

A useful nontrivial class of arbitrary correlations consists of allowed pair
tables with fixed row and column counts. Coverage of a charge under every such
table is equivalent to a positive Hall-cut deficit after deleting that charge's
witness edges, provided the quota family itself is feasible. This is a relation
table, not simultaneous physical use of all its entries.

For n records on each side, all pairs compatible at one grade, and C2 charge-zero
counts a,b, every degree-one table retains both charges exactly when a differs
from b and a+b differs from n. The minimum counts of charge-zero and charge-one
witnesses are respectively |n-a-b| and |a-b|. The exact matching-table generating
polynomial in the theorem records all multiplicities, not just existence.

## 4. Several repaired debts still compete for actual ports

The [disjoint-section theorem](disjoint-sections/THEOREM.md) retains each demand's
complete witness resource set. It encodes allocations in a nonnegative,
commutative square-zero occupancy algebra: a repeated normalized resource kills
a term, and feasible terms cannot cancel. Witness tags preserve relational
distinctions and literal endpoint costs.

Hall's condition is exact for boundary-separated one-resource witnesses.
Several-resource witnesses require coloured hypergraph allocation instead.
The native two-demand example with options {0,1}/{2,3} and {0,2}/{1,3} has no
disjoint allocation, although each demand has two disjoint options and even
the uncoloured union-matching test passes. Extra kernel phases do not repair it.

Actual disjoint option packs or resource-incidence depletion bounds give useful
sufficient criteria. A separate conditional repair-assignment corollary applies
Hall to successful complete repair bundles, with private base receivers and
once-owned candidate records. These are explicit supply hypotheses, not copies
of an existing source manufactured on demand.

Before/after occupancy uses two alphabets, otherwise a continuously retained
resource would be falsely rejected. This enforces arrival capacity, not temporal
non-reacquisition. Exact boundary costs retain both endpoints and internally
connected budget components. For directed predicates use strongly connected
components and a directed quotient, not weak connectivity.

The native six-state control has a7-clock mode bridge, but budget7 leaves three
two-state components; changing the shared phase costs12 clocks and budget12
connects the six states. Contracting each mode before checking its internal
connectivity would give a false routing conclusion.

## 5. No-reacquisition is a directed endpoint problem

The [nonreturn theorem](nonreturn-repairs/THEOREM.md) considers authenticated
binary menus with distinct, disjointly reserved alternatives. A strict route
switches each endpoint-differing slot once and every endpoint-fixed slot never.
Thus its exact state space is the accepted endpoint interval, ordered by which
changes have already occurred. Conditional hole sections must retain increasing
compatible witnesses in this directed graph, not merely nonempty charge images.

A native paired-prefix-key carrier realizes the five accepted states
000,001,101,111,110. Ordinary one-slot/two-clock routing connects its endpoints,
but necessarily releases and reacquires the third slot's original resource.
Strict routing needs the direct two-slot/four-clock event. All literal source,
partner, converse, phase pins and normalized resources are retained. This carrier
has constant class-incidence guards; it is not a transport of the order-eight
numerical electrical bank.

The same paired-key construction embeds Boolean full-record predicates using
literal indexed owner values. Thus arbitrary relational pins can express general
Boolean constraint families even in a class-incidence-neutral carrier. This is
not an equality-only pin compiler or a global-observer construction; it explains
why an all-size rule needs structural assumptions on the relations themselves.

Disjoint synchronized blocks with an authenticated antimatroid of allowed block
states provide a positive all-size sufficient condition. Every pair of endpoints
then has a route changing each differing block exactly once; the largest changed
block's actual slot and clock costs give simultaneous sharp event thresholds in
that declared family. Arbitrary relations need not have this structure.

## 6. A conditional all-size dyadic bound from zero-sum theory

When only an authenticated additive charge in Q and disjoint population counts
remain, pair each endpoint's positive and negative changes within its population.
Each pair must have identical complete margin columns, so it preserves every
integer margin; arbitrary overlapping margins require a separately certified
neutral packetization. The sequence of pair charges sums to
zero. Partition it into minimal zero-sum subsequences. Every slot then changes
once, with no released port reacquired.

Classical Davenport theory bounds the subsequence length by D(Q). For
Q=C128 times C2^4, D(Q)=132, giving at most264 slots per event independently of
inventory size. Costs are the actual sum over the chosen slots, bounded by the
largest min(264, |S|) endpoint-differing slot costs, where S is the differing-slot
set. The [nonreturn proof](nonreturn-repairs/THEOREM.md)
includes a self-contained group-algebra argument and credits the classical
p-group theorem of [Olson](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022314X69900213).

The bound is sharp in a specified abstract model with132 separately fixed
two-slot population classes. Sharpness is not asserted with only core/helper
margins, nor for native tournament charge realizations. It is not an improvement
over the smaller finite82-slot bounds. Additional relational predicates can
forbid the intermediate neutral packets and must be certified separately.

There is a genuine algebraic connection, not a physical identification: the
nonnegative square-zero algebra in Section4 detects resource collisions without
cancellation; the characteristic-two augmentation algebra here deliberately
uses cancellation and nilpotence to force a zero-sum subset. Interchanging their
coefficient systems would invalidate their respective arguments.

## 7. What remains open

The [independent theory audit](audit-repair-theory/AUDIT.md) checks the logical
interfaces and finite controls separately from the native charged audit. Counts
and exact replay commands remain in each packet's receipts. All-size statements
rest on the displayed proofs and authenticated hypotheses, not finite extrapolation.

The remaining obstacles are:

1. All-order native supply for the complete requested physical guard bank.
2. Structural guarantees that arbitrary receiver-wide relations preserve usable
   charge sections and directed, non-reacquiring witness routes.
3. Compatible simultaneous supply without duplicating normalized ports.
4. Global-observer realization for arbitrary overlapping local cells, including
   source and partner flags, not merely a common host or target class.
5. Cross-order harmonic transport and any required microscopic realization of
   atomic full-record replacements.

The new rules expose and sometimes remove particular obstructions. They do not
identify charge coverage, port allocation, ordinary connectivity, strict temporal
routing and global-observer existence as the same property.
