# Why this composition works, and why it is not yet Chan–Pak

## The new mechanism

A single physical moving-core loop changes only the cut coordinate T; its
other path and endpoint coordinates remain fixed. Ordinal composition scales
that change by the Hamilton count of every other core. Repeating identical
cores therefore preserves a large common divisor, which was a real previous
obstruction.

The earlier search emphasized individual loops of unit gain. This session
instead recovers short nonunit loops and lets composition remove their
arithmetic obstruction. The h13 and h29 loops have half-step5 and3. Alongside
h45's half-step1, the complete weighted steps 6525,1755,377 have the right CRT
structure. The finite rectangle of actual positions, not just their gcd,
supplies every residue; the variable h45 position supplies the remaining
integer interval. The selector returns physical positions with all bounds
checked. This is a constructive gain, not a finite numerical pattern.

The preserved predicate is equality of(h,q,A,G,S,E) with controlled T.
The source objects are actual cut-labelled tournaments, the operation is
ordinal composition followed by the specified star closure, and the lost
position information is restored by the explicit bounded arithmetic selector.
Strongness follows from the protected first vertex and the two closing
vertices. Saturation remains present and does not invalidate the construction.

## Why the endpoint theorem is complementary

The binary near-transitive boundary supplies arbitrary positive endpoint
counts and trivial root stabilizers on logarithmic order. This is a genuine
extension of Session232's local 7/21 construction, with all integers now
covered, not just the old examples. It also demonstrates why no global
omission law can be transferred unchanged to a local endpoint.

Its exact response formula shows the missing coordinate. Binary digits use
terminal weights, while the rooted two-path response uses a different set
of weights. One-arc joins and grafts require the latter as well as H and the
endpoint. Hence this theorem provides a useful selector at the boundary but
not independently selectable full rooted states. The next constructive step
would need to synchronize those coordinates or cancel their variation by an
actual physical composition.

## Why packing-number runs are insufficient

THM-4951 controls how many disjoint cycles can be packed. Hamilton counts use
the entire weighted odd-cycle packing polynomial. The three-state bridge
transfer restores precisely that lost data for the complementary canonical
family. It also exposes an all-order obstruction: even after ordinal products,
the two canonical endpoint families miss the prime11. General noncanonical
tilings with the same triple word, broader ribbons and new seams remain
outside this obstruction.

The head-extension hostile explains the correct state law. A path crossing
one bridge need not grow only at its tail; later vertices can join its head
through backward arcs. Discarding this changes a count 13 into 11. Retaining
both extensions gives the exact polynomial transfer, independently checked
coefficient by coefficient rather than just at selected positive bases.

## The exact open obligation

The constructed windows have explicit, very large baselines. Their lengths
grow linearly with the variable repetition number and their baselines grow
exponentially. The results do not show overlap, eventual coverage, an
arbitrary-target placement algorithm, or any new complete global cutoff.
The h13/h29 loops enlarge the constructor beyond the single fixed core, but
that fact alone does not establish target occupancy.

A successful continuation needs one of:

1. A baseline-placement theorem for a stronger physical context language,
   with every candidate returning a real tournament.
2. A response-preserving composition that converts the complete endpoint
   selector into freely controlled global counts.
3. New seams or core families whose intervals overlap on every admissible
   target, with a uniform proof rather than a finite range.

Automorphism subgroup omissions, scalar gcd-one, arbitrary local endpoint
values and unbounded consecutive widths are useful inputs, not substitutes
for this existence step. No transfer between incompatible orbit measures or
base-three and base-two counts is used.
