# Predicate-restricted C5 descent for the two 5+3+3 embeddings

**Status: PROVED.** Reviewed separately from the complete degree-eleven/twelve census. Its finite counts are controls, not dependencies of this descent proof. The argument concerns only the two explicitly stated subgroup-presence predicates, not all objects with a C5 symmetry.

Let n∈{11,12}. Let K be a single 5-cycle on a five-set A, fixing all other vertices. Consider either

- H_d=C5×C3, with the C3 generator acting as two synchronized disjoint three-cycles on six vertices outside A;
- H_i=C5×C3², with the two C3 factors acting independently on those two triples.

There are n−11 additional fixed vertices. The claim is

    tau_T(H_d)=tau_E(H_d),   tau_T(H_i)=tau_E(H_i).

The key is **H-extendible copies of K**, not every copy of K in an object. A copy K' is H-extendible if it is the distinguished order-five subgroup of an ambient conjugate of the specified H inside the object's automorphism group.

## 1. Relative matching to be descended

N(K)/K≅C4×S_(n−5). For H_d let U_d be the quotient C3 generated by a permutation of cycle type 3+3+1^(n−11). For H_i let U_i be the quotient C3² generated by the two independent triple cycles.

The relative fixed-cube theorem in `../pronormal_quotient_bijections_20261020/PRONORMAL_QUOTIENT_THEOREM.md`, Theorem 1, supplies a bijection between N(K)-orbits of K-fixed tournaments and N(K)-orbits of relatively sign-even K-fixed graphs, preserving the presence of an N(K)/K-conjugate of each specified U. This works for arbitrary odd K and does not require K to be pronormal.

Restrict that bijection to the U_d predicate, or separately to U_i. A relative orbit satisfying that predicate is equivalently a full object with a marked H-extendible copy of K, modulo full isomorphism of the marked object. Forgetting the mark descends precisely if:

1. no relatively-even graph in this restricted sector is globally odd;
2. all H-extendible copies of K in each admitted full object are conjugate under its automorphism group.

We prove both. Nothing here asserts these properties on the unrestricted C5 sector.

## 2. The five-support must be independent twins

In any K-fixed graph the vertices of A have identical neighbors outside A. Its induced graph on A is one of the four C5-circulants: empty, the five-cycle, its complement, or K5.

Each nonempty option has an odd automorphism in N(K), extended by the identity outside A:

- x↦−x is an odd reflection for the five-cycle and its complementary five-cycle;
- x↦2x is a 4-cycle on A\{0}, and hence has odd edge-reversal sign on K5.

External edges contribute no sign because they occur uniformly and the outside vertices are fixed. Thus relative evenness forces A to be independent. All its vertices have the same open neighborhood, so A lies in a maximal independent-twin class C of size at least five.

Every permutation inside an independent-twin class is a graph automorphism of sign +1: a transposition reverses no edge between its moved vertices and all other edge contributions pair. These permutations conjugate any single-five-cycle subgroup supported in C to any other such subgroup.

If a graph automorphism g preserves C setwise, choose an internal permutation s of C with s(gKg^(-1))s^(-1)=K. Then sg∈N(K)∩Aut(G), and sign(sg)=sign(g). Consequently **every automorphism preserving C has sign +1** in a relatively-even K-fixed graph.

## 3. No sign escape in the double-triple sector

If a relatively-even graph in the restricted sector were globally odd, an odd automorphism would have to send C to a distinct independent-twin class C' of the same size w≥5. Because n≤12 there are only two such classes, and w is either five or six.

Choose an element h of the specified H with cycle type 3+3 and support disjoint from A. For H_d this is its C3 generator; for H_i take the product of the two independent generators. It commutes with K and hence fixes A pointwise.

- If w=5, then C=A. The order-three automorphism h cannot exchange the two size-five classes, and there are at most two other vertices. Thus h fixes C, acts on C' of size five, and fixes the remaining at-most-two vertices. It can have at most one three-cycle, a contradiction.
- If w=6, then n=12 and C,C' exhaust the vertices. They are independent-twin classes, so the graph is K_(6,6): the no-cross-edge alternative would merge them into one independent-twin class. All permutations within the parts have sign +1, and a part swap has sign (-1)^(6·6)=+1. Therefore the whole graph is sign-even, again a contradiction.

Every relatively-even graph satisfying the stated quotient predicate is therefore globally sign-even.

## 4. Graph fusion: only eligible C5 copies are compared

Now let G be a globally sign-even graph admitting the chosen H. Each H-extendible K' has its five-support inside an independent-twin class, by §2 applied to that K'. Copies inside one class are conjugate by even internal permutations.

If there is only one independent-twin class of size at least five, this already proves uniqueness of the eligible-copy orbit. Otherwise there are exactly two, say C_1,C_2, of weights w_1,w_2≥5, with at most two other vertices.

Suppose K' lies in C_1. An extending double-three-cycle h fixes its chosen five-support pointwise. It cannot permute the two large classes because it has order three, and each remaining twin class has size at most two. There are at most w_1−5≤2 unused vertices in C_1 and at most two vertices outside the two large classes. Therefore both three-cycles of h must lie in C_2. In particular **w_2≥6 is necessary for C_1 to be eligible**.

Similarly, eligibility of C_2 requires w_1≥6. Thus two eligible classes force n=12 and w_1=w_2=6. This is K_(6,6), where an even part swap conjugates the two classes and hence their five-cycle copies. Therefore all H-extendible K' form one Aut(G)-conjugacy class, even one Aut(G)^+-class.

This does not say that all copies of C5 are conjugate. For weights 5+6 or 5+7, a C5 in the size-five class can be extendible while a C5 in the larger class is not: the latter leaves fewer than three unused vertices there and only five in the other large class. The ineligible copies are outside the restricted matching predicate.

## 5. Tournament fusion uses the complete bounded classification

Every tournament automorphism group has odd order. If T admits either H, its automorphism group is an odd mixed-prime subgroup of S_n. The complete n=11,12 classification in [the degree-eleven/twelve census, §3](../mixed_prime_census_extension_20261020/README.md) shows that every such subgroup has 5-adic order exactly one whenever its order is divisible by five. This statement also follows directly by inspecting the structurally proved list; it is not inferred from object counts.

Thus every copy of K in Aut(T) is a Sylow-5 subgroup. All such copies are conjugate inside Aut(T), so in particular all H-extendible copies are conjugate. This proves tournament-side descent.

## 6. Conclusion and exact limits

The relative q=3 bijection restricted to U_d, or to U_i, has:

- exactly one relative source orbit per full tournament type with that H predicate;
- only globally sign-even graph targets, by §3;
- exactly one relative target orbit per full sign-even graph type with that H predicate, by §4.

It therefore descends to the two asserted full-type equalities. The finite counts 12=12 and 96=96 for H_d, and 4=4 and 32=32 for H_i, are consistent controls but not dependencies of this proof.

The exact degree-thirteen deficit of six for each specified embedding is proved separately in [SHARP_N13_BOUNDARY.md](SHARP_N13_BOUNDARY.md), so this equality range is sharp.

The single-three-cycle C15 predicate is deliberately excluded. With independent twin classes 5+5 and two residual vertices, the other five-class can support one three-cycle, and the K_(5,5) odd part swap escapes. That is exactly the inherited degree-ten counterexample. The preserved sidecar here is the **two-disjoint-three-cycle support demand**, not merely the abstract presence of prime three.

The proof does not claim a general object-relative descent theorem at arbitrary n, a classification beyond twelve, or that K is pronormal in S_n. It replaces ambient pronormality only for these two explicitly restricted predicates.
