# The double-triple descent range is sharp, with exact deficit six

**Status: PROVED.** Reviewed within this research session; the verification record distinguishes proof review from computational independence. The proof classifies only the anomaly mechanism for two specified subgroup predicates. It is not a complete subgroup census at degree thirteen. The separate fixed-cube controls are FINITE-EXACT.

Let n=13. Let H_d=C5×C3 act on vertex orbits 5+3+3+1+1, with its C3 generator a synchronized pair of three-cycles. Let H_i=C5×C3² have independent cycles on those two triples. Let K be the distinguished single-five-cycle subgroup.

**Claim.** For both specified embeddings,

    tau_T(H_d)−tau_E(H_d)=6,
    tau_T(H_i)−tau_E(H_i)=6.

Together with [the degree-eleven/twelve double-triple descent proof](DOUBLE_TRIPLE_DESCENT.md), this makes that bounded equality range sharp. It does not assert universal failure for every larger degree or any other embedding of the same abstract groups.

## 1. Match at K and retain the extension predicate

Use [the inherited relative fixed-cube theorem](../pronormal_quotient_bijections_20261020/PRONORMAL_QUOTIENT_THEOREM.md), Theorem 1, at K and q=3. Its normalizer quotient is C4×S8. Restrict the matching either to the quotient subgroup U_d generated by a double-three-cycle, or to U_i=C3² on two independent triples.

For a full object X, only **H-extendible copies of K** are counted: K' must be the distinguished five-subgroup of an ambient conjugate of the specified H inside Aut(X). Such pointed objects, modulo full isomorphism, are the corresponding N(K)-orbits. The relative target also imposes even sign on the K-normalizer stabilizer.

Let F_E^K be the sum of m_K−1 over the admitted full globally even graphs, where m_K is the number of automorphism-conjugacy classes of H-extendible copies of K. Let Z^K count the relatively-even eligible-copy orbits whose underlying graphs are globally odd. If the tournament side has one eligible-copy orbit per full type, relative matching gives

    tau_T(H)−tau_E(H)=F_E^K+Z^K.             (1)

These are **K-based, predicate-restricted defects**, not the H-normalizer defects tabulated by the ordinary census engine.

## 2. Tournament-side C5 copies remain Sylow at thirteen

Suppose an odd tournament automorphism group G≤S13 admitting H_d or H_i had 5² dividing its order. Since v_5(13!)=2, a Sylow-5 subgroup of G would be an ambient Sylow-5 subgroup, conjugate to independent five-cycles on disjoint five-sets. In particular G would contain a single 5-cycle.

If G were transitive, the triple-support lemma in `THREE_CYCLE_SUPPORT_LEMMA.md` would force 13≥15, a contradiction.

If G is intransitive, each vertex orbit has odd size. The elementary image classifications in [the degree-at-most-ten census](../odd_mixed_prime_census_20261020/README.md) and [the degree-eleven/twelve extension](../mixed_prime_census_extension_20261020/README.md) give:

- an eleven-orbit has image C11 or F55, with at most one factor five;
- a nine-orbit has a 3-group image;
- a seven-orbit has image C7 or F21;
- a five-orbit has image C5, and a three-orbit has image C3.

Thus two factors five require two distinct five-orbits. Only three vertices remain. Every order-three element acts trivially on both five-orbits, since their images are C5, so it has at most one three-cycle overall. But both specified H contain an element with two disjoint three-cycles. Contradiction.

Therefore v_5(|G|)=1. All copies of K in G are Sylow-5 subgroups and are conjugate, in particular all eligible copies. Tournament-side descent in (1) is proved without assuming a full degree-thirteen classification.

## 3. The graph carrier: maximal independent-twin classes

[The degree-eleven/twelve descent proof](DOUBLE_TRIPLE_DESCENT.md) supplies two elementary facts, repeated here to fix scope. Throughout this packet, graph sign means the edge-reversal character, not the ordinary parity of a vertex permutation.

1. In a relatively-even K-fixed graph, the K-support is an independent five-set with identical external neighbors. The three other C5-circulants are relatively odd: reflection is odd on either five-cycle, and multiplier 2 is odd on K5.
2. Every permutation within a maximal independent-twin class has sign +1. If an automorphism preserves the class containing the chosen K, correct it by such an internal permutation to normalize K. Consequently every class-preserving automorphism is even in a relatively-even marked graph.

Copies of K supported in one independent-twin class are conjugate. Hence an excess eligible-copy orbit or an escaped sign needs at least two independent-twin classes of size at least five. There are at most two at n=13.

For a marked five-support in a class of weight w, an extending element with two three-cycles fixes those five points. Its two cycles must fit in the other class, the w−5 unused points of its own class, or the residual vertices. The order-three element cannot exchange the two large classes.

The possible large-class weight patterns are:

    5+5+3 residual vertices;
    5+6+2 residual vertices;
    5+7+1 residual vertex;
    5+8;
    6+6+1 residual vertex;
    6+7.

Patterns 5+6+2 and 5+7+1 have only one eligible class: the weight-five class. They have no equal-weight class swap and contribute no anomaly.

When the two large weights are 5+5, the second three-cycle must use all three residual vertices. These vertices have equal adjacency to each large class, and their induced graph is empty or K3. K3 would give an odd transposition fixing the chosen five-support and is excluded by relative evenness. Thus the residual vertices are an independent triple. The anomaly analysis reduces to a three-vertex weighted quotient with weights (5,5,3).

For weights (6,6,1), the quotient has only three vertices. For (5,8) and (6,7), it has only two. These four weighted quotients give the exhaustive finite reduction below. A nonadjacent pair of classes with the same open neighborhood must be merged; retaining them separately would create spurious copies.

## 4. Exactly two escaping marked-graph types

An odd automorphism must exchange the class carrying K with a second class of the same weight. Equal weights can be five or six.

For 6+6+1, any class exchange fixes the residual vertex and reverses 36 cross edges, an even number. Internal independent-class permutations are even. Thus there is no sign escape.

For 5+5+3, the independent residual triple is preserved, with only even internal permutations. An exchange of the five-classes requires their adjacency to the residual triple to agree. They must be adjacent to each other, or equal neighborhoods would merge them. There are exactly two possibilities:

1. K_(5,5) disjoint union I3;
2. K_(5,5,3), the complete tripartite graph with independent parts 5,5,3.

The five-part swap has sign (-1)^25=−1 in each. The stabilizer of a chosen five-class has sign +1, so the marked graph is relatively even. Both five-classes are eligible and exchanged by that automorphism; hence each global odd type has exactly one relative eligible-copy orbit. Therefore

    Z^K=2.                                             (2)

An explicit realization of the first witness uses A={0,1,2,3,4}, B={5,6,7,11,12}, R={8,9,10}. K rotates A, while the two triples are {5,6,7} and R. Thus it uses exactly the original 5+3+3+1+1 embedding, not a different abstract C15 action.

## 5. Exactly four globally even fusion types

For each eligible class, all supported five-cycle subgroups are conjugate by its internal symmetric group. Thus m_K is the number of automorphism orbits of eligible large classes. It is at most two. Two nonconjugate eligible classes occur exactly in these four full graph types:

| graph | maximal independent-twin weights | why both classes are eligible and not conjugate |
|---|---|---|
| K_(5,3) disjoint union I5 | 5,3,5 | the residual triple supplies one cycle; either other five-class supplies the second; the two five-classes have different degrees |
| K_(5,8) | 5,8 | from the five-class use two cycles in the eight-class; from the eight-class use its three unused points and three points of the five-class; unequal weights prohibit exchange |
| K_(6,1) disjoint union I6 | 6,1,6 | the other six-class supplies both triple cycles; one class is isolated and one is adjacent to the singleton |
| K_(6,7) | 6,7 | either other class supplies two triple cycles; unequal weights prohibit exchange |

All four graphs are globally sign-even. Their maximal independent-twin classes are fixed individually by every automorphism, because of their distinct weights or degrees. Internal class permutations are sign-even.

To see exhaustiveness directly:

- On (5,5,3), the three quotient edges have eight settings. Equal empty neighborhoods merge classes. The asymmetric residual adjacency with no edge between the five-classes gives K_(5,3)⊔I5. With that edge present it merges the residual triple into one five-class, giving K_(5,8). Symmetric adjacency either merges the five-classes or gives one of the two odd escape types from §4.
- On (6,6,1), symmetric residual adjacency makes the two six-classes exchangeable by an even swap, or merges them. Asymmetric adjacency without their cross edge gives K_(6,1)⊔I6; with that cross edge it merges the singleton into one six-class, giving K_(6,7).
- The two-class cases (5,8) and (6,7) give those already listed complete bipartite graphs; absent cross edges merge the classes.

Each of the four types has m_K=2 and therefore contributes one. Thus

    F_E^K=4.                                           (3)

The eligibility constructions work for H_i as well as H_d: the two three-cycles can be selected independently inside independent-twin classes, so their full direct product is present. Conversely each H contains the required double-three-cycle, so the necessary support arguments apply to both.

## 6. Exact deficit and numerical controls

Combine (1), (2), and (3): the exact deficit is 4+2=6 for each predicate.

The separate cube computation finds:

| specified H at n=13 | H-normalizer R | tau_T | tau_E | F_T^H | F_E^H | Z^H |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| synchronized C15 on 5+3+3+1+1 | 1024 | 768 | 762 | 256 | 4 | 258 |
| C5×C3² on 5+3+3+1+1 | 256 | 256 | 250 | 0 | 4 | 2 |

For the synchronized group, 256 extra H-presentations and 256 extra H-relative odd graph orbits cancel before reaching the K-based description. In particular Z^H=258 must not be confused with the two K-based escape orbits used in the proof.
