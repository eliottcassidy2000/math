# Exact constructive obligations after Session275

Chan–Pak, an eventual odd threshold, and all-target source supply remain OPEN.

1. **Physical digit overlap.** Supply one-way letter banks at the needed response
   scales so their allowed weight intervals overlap. The proven square-zero law
   makes independence automatic, but neither digit1 nor enough digits is free.
   The h=1 binary-decoder obstruction prevents the most naive reset.
2. **Paid quadratic inverse.** Solve N=B+linear+positive bilinear for admissible
   sparse physical inputs, not arbitrary integers. Keep every linear cost and
   the correct root packet. Can the inherited11-offset bank be made compatible
   with its full Q(2) response, rather than just scalarH?
3. **Corridor escape.** Prove the count component containing23 is unbounded.
   The59→61→63→65 portal supplies a genuine initial extension, not a recurrence.
   Does every sufficiently large bounded corridor have a bounded-width portal
   to a larger count? Allow intermediate size growth and retain common frames.
4. **Attach renewable siblings.** The balanced bifan pair has gap2 at every
   length, but successive lengths are not connected. Match its low endpoint to
   a reachable corridor while preserving the next root menu.
5. **Mark-controlled content.** The normalized21 fiber is classified and its
   content29 can be escaped by moving the root. Find controls that vary the
   rigid fan intercept while retaining a lawful renewable packet and an inverse
   for an arbitrary prescribed odd target.
6. **Finite-state versus interaction depth.** Fusion allows exact degree≤2
   behavior for the new alphabet. A different operation may retain the same
   useful state while changing the quadratic form's digit capacity. A large
   support/counting exponent is useful evidence but not full integer support.

No result here treats a modular lift as an absolute target, a rational packet
as a tournament, a connected high-size family as unbounded inH, or a raw+2 pair
as an inductive successor operation.
