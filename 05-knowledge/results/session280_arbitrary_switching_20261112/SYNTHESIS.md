**Session280, 12 November2026. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

This session pursued five interacting directions rather than enlarging a
finite count census. It obtained a common physical language with both stable

## 1. Arbitrary switching can be proved, but individual spectra are insufficient

The chronological three-block schedule `(10,101010,1000)` consists of individually
Pisot blocks, yet its product has characteristic polynomial

    z³−15315z²−19568z−4096.

Its values at−2 and−1 have opposite signs, forcing a transverse root below−1.
Repeating that schedule defeats the blanket extension of the old real
contraction argument. This does not establish collisions between physical
integer seeds or rule out other decoding methods.

The positive repair is to retain the last block as a metric label. An exact
scalar-conditioned two-dimensional residual obeys a changing linear map.
Quadratic inequalities checked at three cone vertices imply the inequality
throughout the cone by convexity. This turns finite rational certificates into
an all-word theorem, without assuming a common Perron eigenvector.

The first certificate covers56 low-oscillation blocks. A further search found
an explicit151-block alphabet, with lengths at most9, for which68,403 rational

factor9991/10000. For source packets with W<=96, every KNOWN switching word
of at least15417 blocks has root-coordinate uncertainty below one, so its exact
scalar Hamilton count determines its full integer packet. The threshold is
conservative, not a speed claim; direct per-word intervals can be much tighter.
Radix divisibility and actual seed ownership are still required.

## 2. Hidden switching instructions are exactly recoverable without contraction

There is a complementary, entirely arithmetic mechanism. Every active block
maps a physical packet in the broad root cone into the thin strip

    0<=d<=A<=W<2A,  A odd, W=d mod2.

For a thin input, write the next packet as `(N,V,D)` and the block length as n.
Its law gives `D=A`, `V=aA+2^n W`, where `2^(n−1)<a<2^n` is odd. Therefore

    3·2^(n−1) < V/D < 3·2^n.

These intervals are disjoint: the real ratio identifies n. Because D is odd,
`a=V D^(-1) mod2^n` then identifies every bit of the block. Exact inversion
recovers the predecessor. Repeating this determines the entire unknown
switching itinerary from a fixed physical thin seed; the count drops by at
least a factor5 at each backward step.

No word labels, lengths, depth, Pisot condition or periodicity are supplied.
Distinct programs from a fixed seed give distinct ROOT PACKETS, even across
different word lengths. This is not an unrooted isomorphism-class injection.

Two equal-order physical probes, appending100 and110 separately, together
with the original count give a unimodular chart for the root packet after
parity normalization. Thus THREE exact physical counts recover the complete
hidden itinerary. One prescribed count alone does not provide those other two
observations. The broader scalar recurrence also works under arbitrary known
switching, including non-Pisot blocks.

## 3. Stable switching and sufficient branch capacity now coexist

For all63 active blocks through length7, a rational weighted-transition
certificate proves that any finite-seed scalar support is

    O(X^(399/400)).

It has zero density, even with arbitrarily long switching. Adding only a
sublogarithmic number of operations from any fixed exceptional one-apex
library still cannot overcome this scarcity. More numerical coverage inside
that restricted architecture would not repair its asymptotic deficit.

The151-letter contractive alphabet in Section1 escapes this obstruction:
an exact positive vector certifies lower reciprocal-growth pressure greater
than1069/1000. Ancestry injectivity upgrades the increased presentation supply
to genuinely distinct root packets. Thus **arbitrary-switch observability and
supercritical packet supply are simultaneously proved in one physical family**,
not in two incompatible constructions.

The scalar projection still loses information. In this explicit family its
fibers must become large: among targets at most X, some target has
Omega(X^(1/128)/log X) distinct root packets, and some has
Omega(X^(1/128)/(log X)^2) different unrooted tournament classes from the family.
These are maximal-fiber/subsequence statements, not lower bounds at every
odd target. Abundance forces collisions; it does not eliminate holes.

## 4. Repair the continuing interface, then combine a physical carry

The old41/43/45 corridor leaked a deletion coordinate. A universal sandwich

## 5. The complementary structures and the next proof obligations

The research now separates four jobs which previously looked too similar:

1. **Observation:** real contraction or extra physical measurements recovers
   a packet; neither asserts target existence.
2. **Ancestry:** a real scale interval plus a modular unit recovers the entire
   construction. A common exterior lattice separately imposes a sharp Smith
   carry bound under arbitrary switching, even when real directions expand.
3. **Supply:** branching must outrun actual absolute growth. The151-letter
   language passes that test, but large scalar fibers are unavoidable.
4. **Selection/renewal:** the prescribed target must own a reachable packet,
   or a width-growing bank must repair the state consumed by its next use.

The scalar-selection and renewable-width obligations remain OPEN. Two concrete
next targets are now available: control scalar fibers/holes in the simultaneous
151-letter language, and repair the receiving defect of the widened bank
without destroying its interval. Neither a joint contraction certificate,
a divergent reciprocal series, nor an infinite constant-width family is a
proof that all odd values except7,21 occur.
