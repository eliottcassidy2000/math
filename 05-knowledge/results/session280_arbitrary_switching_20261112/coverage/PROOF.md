# Switching capacity: a 63-letter scarcity theorem and an 83-letter escape

**Session280. PROVED in the stated physical language, with exact rational
certificates. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.** This is a target-supply theorem, not a
new larger finite spectrum census. It separates the number of available
construction programs from whether their outputs occupy every odd integer.

This turns a vague wish for more branches into a finite rational certificate.

## 1. Physical setup and a last-letter growth envelope

Write x=(A,W,d), with A the Hamiltonian-path count and W,d the inherited
rooted path-cover coordinates. Physical nonempty sources satisfy

    C = {0 <= d <= A <= W},   A>=1.

The cone is generated by r0=(1,1,0), r1=(1,1,1), r2=(0,1,0).
An active block is a binary word w starting with1 and ending with0. Put
n=|w|, t=2^n, P=int(w,2), Q=sum_(j=0)^(n-1)(1-w[j])2^j. Its legal transfer is

    M_w = [[h,Q+1,-1], [P+1,t,0], [1,0,0]],   h=g(P),
    g(0)=1; g(2v+1)=g(v); g(2v)=g(v)+v+(v mod2), v>=1.

All construction words below append actual blocks by the inherited reversed
apex-seam operation. No formal normalized state is used as a physical seed.

For each active letter u define exact rational numbers

    l_u = min_i (M_u r_i)_A / (M_u r_i)_W,
    b_u = max_i (M_u r_i)_A / (M_u r_i)_W.

The projective ratio A/W after u lies in [l_u,b_u], because it is a weighted
average of these ray ratios. For a following letter v define

    alpha_uv = t_v + (P_v+1) l_u,
    beta_uv  = t_v + (P_v+1) b_u.

Then **for every physical context**, not only repeated-letter contexts,

    alpha_uv <= W_after_v / W_before_v <= beta_uv.       (1)

This is immediate from W'=t_v W+(P_v+1)A. These are exact extremal bounds
on the one-letter cone, not heuristic Lyapunov estimates. A longer memory
cone could tighten them but is not needed below.

Useful uniform comparison: every active output satisfies

    W_out < 4 A_out.                                    (2)

Indeed Q>=t/2, h>=1 and d<=A give A'>=(Q+1)W>tW/2;
W'=(P+1)A+tW<=2tW. Also W'>=tW>=4W. Thus count cutoff
A<=X puts every nonempty active output at W<4X, and source W is no larger.

## 2. Every switching program in the 63-letter library is power-sparse

Let S7 be **all** active words of lengths2 through7. There are63, not a chosen
subfamily. Let K_uv=1/alpha_uv. In the length-then-lexicographic order in
`results.json`, the explicit positive integer vector c has63 entries and obeys

    K c < (973/1000)c.                                  (3)

Every comparison in (3) is exact rational arithmetic. For orientation only,
the largest displayed ratio is about0.971450387. The float was used to propose
a vector; the committed verifier proves the inequality with integer fractions.

Set s=399/400. Since alpha_uv<=256 and

    256 < (65/64)^400,

we have alpha_uv^(-s) <= (65/64)alpha_uv^(-1). Hence the true s-weighted
transition matrix K(s) satisfies

    K(s)c <= rho c,   rho=(65/64)(973/1000)=63245/64000<1. (4)

No fractional-power numerical evaluation is part of this certificate.
The initial letter increases W by at least t, irrespective of the source.
For any integer k>=0, (4) bounds the total s-weight of all k subsequent
letters by (c_u/min c)rho^k. Summing all lengths and first letters gives

    sum_(z in S7*) (W(M_z x)/W(x))^(-s) <= C0 <164,        (5)

uniformly for every nonzero x in C with W>0. The empty word contributes1.
For the explicit constant, t^(-s)<=(65/64)t^(-1), and
sum_(v in S7)1/t_v=6/4. Since max c=1000000 and min c=793469,

    C0 <= 1+(195/128)(1000000/793469)/(1-rho)
        =19619813819/119813819 <164.

**Theorem1 (absolute target scarcity).** From any finite bank B of physical
seeds, the set of Hamiltonian-path counts generated by arbitrary S7 switching
has at most

    |B| + 164 (4X)^s sum_(x in B) W(x)^(-s)              (6)

members not exceeding X. In particular its natural density among positive
odd integers is zero. It misses infinitely many allowed targets, no matter
how long one switches or how good a packet decoder one has.

**Proof.** Each nonempty output with A<=X has W<4X by(2), so its summand W^-s
is at least(4X)^-s. Apply(5) to each seed. Counting word presentations only
OVERcounts distinct outputs, which makes the upper bound valid even with
collisions. Empty words contribute at most|B|. QED.

This is stronger than the O(log X) obstruction for a fixed suffix. It applies
to all63 choices at every step and all lengths, with genuine target anchoring.
The exponent399/400 is a simple certified choice, not an optimal dimension.
It is not an assertion about all active blocks, all O/Z/J programs, or all
tournaments.

## 3. Bounded repairs, and even sublogarithmic repairs, cannot fix that library

Let E be any disjoint finite collection of other one-apex block words, allowing
the empty word J. They may reset the memory, fail the Pisot test, or preserve
no decoding cone. The full inherited transfer is

    A'=g(P)A+(Q+1)W-[Q even]d,
    W'=(P+1)A+2^n W,
    d'=[P even]A-[n=0]d.

It preserves C. Always W'>=W. If E has maximum word length L, every nonempty
program in S7 union E has terminal W<=kappa A, where one can take
kappa=2^(max(7,L)+1). For nonempty words, A'>=W and W'<=2^(n+1)W;
for J, A'=A+W-d>=W and W'=A+W<=2W. This proves the comparison uniformly.

Decompose a program with exactly r exceptional letters uniquely as

    z0 e1 z1 ... er zr,  zi in S7*, ei in E.

Use(5) on each active chunk. Since an exceptional step never decreases W,
its inverse-growth weight is at most1. Therefore the total s-weight of all
such programs from seed x is at most

    W(x)^(-s) 164^(r+1) |E|^r.                          (7)

**Theorem2 (repair budget).** For a fixed finite physical bank and fixed E,
programs with at most R(X) exceptional letters and scalar count<=X have at most

    O( X^s sum_(r=0)^R(X) (164|E|)^r )                  (8)

distinct counts. If R(X)=o(log X), their support is X^(s+o(1)) and still has
zero density. Consequently a positive-density target cover inside this fixed
library must allow a linear-in-log-X exceptional budget along some outputs:
a uniform cap R(X) below

    ((1-s)/log(164|E|) - epsilon) log X

cannot suffice, for any epsilon>0 and nonempty E. The bound is intentionally
conservative. It proves a necessary resource, not existence of successful
programs using that many repairs. It does not prevent unbounded block sizes,
a changing alphabet, or a different physical operation.

This explains why a fixed number of decoder-reset, rerooting, or repair
stages is insufficient **when those stages are from this fixed one-apex
library**. It does not cover arbitrary rerootings of a growing graph.

## 4. A countable seed bank must pay its scale bill

For any countable physically supplied bank B, if

    sum_(x in B) W(x)^(-s) < infinity,                    (9)

then its NONEMPTY S7 closure still has O(X^s) distinct outputs. This follows
by summing (6)'s nonempty part; repeated packets or isomorphic graphs may be
removed first, but counting them cannot invalidate an upper bound. The seed
values themselves are excluded here: infinitely many seeds with small A but
very large W could bypass the terminal comparison for the empty word.

More quantitatively, only seeds with W<=4X can contribute a nonempty output
<=X. Linear scalar coverage would force

    sum_(x in B: W(x)<=4X) W(x)^(-s) = Omega(X^(1-s)).      (10)

If the scale counting function B(Y)=#{x:W(x)<=Y} is O(Y^a), partial summation
gives the upper bound O(X^max(a,s)) when a!=s, and O(X^s log X) at a=s.
Thus any power-sparse seed supply with a<1 remains power-sparse after this
closure. A constructive proof cannot hide the whole target supply in an
unexplained seed bank. It must exhibit a sufficiently abundant, physically
initialized bank or change the switching regime.

## 5. A concrete capacity escape without introducing a non-Pisot letter

A negative capacity theorem is useful only if it points to a way past it.
Let S83 be S7 plus the first20 active length-eight words in lexicographic
order: the six middle bits run from000000 through010011. Each new letter
passes the exact individual Pisot test of Session279. In particular the first
non-Pisot word10101010, whose middle bits are010101, is NOT included.

Form the lower inverse-growth matrix L_uv=1/beta_uv on these83 letters.
`results.json` supplies a positive integer vector d with

    L d > (1003/1000)d.                                 (11)

The smallest exact ratio has decimal display about1.00383595. Again every
inequality is checked rationally. For a fixed physical source, the sum of
1/W over all length-m S83 presentations grows at least a positive constant
times(1003/1000)^(m-1): bound the first transition from above by t+P+1, and
use (1),(11) on the following transitions. Since A<=W,

    sum_(all nonempty S83 presentations) 1/A = infinity. (12)

**Theorem3 (capacity escape).** The explicit83-letter entirely individually
Pisot library escapes the convergent reciprocal-output budget which rules
out S7. It has supercritical weighted *presentation* supply at exponent1.
This remains true for the larger126-letter individually Pisot library through
length8 (all active words except10101010).

This theorem is a positive constructive ingredient, not a disguised coverage
claim: every presentation is a literal physical tournament and the increased
supply is at all depths. But many presentations can produce the same count.
A divergent reciprocal series over presentations neither proves divergent
reciprocal support nor positive density, much less an eventual interval.
`results.json` includes an explicit scalar collision. The old losses of
physical ancestry and absolute anchoring have not disappeared.

Nor does individual Pisot behavior imply uniform switching contraction:
the sibling lane's bad product already lies inside S7, hence inside S83.
The capacity escape and a synchronization theorem must be made compatible,
perhaps by an allowed-transition automaton or a richer observable. A permitted
transition graph simply zeros the corresponding entries of K or L, making
this pressure test immediately reusable for such proposed repairs.

### 5a. An alternative with a structural rather than lexicographic rule

Let S98 consist of every active word of length at most8 with at most3 bit

    L98 d > (101/100)d,

so the same presentation-divergence theorem applies. Its minimum ratio is

certificate must be recomputed on the actual permitted language.

## 6. What this supplies toward an actual proof

The requested arbitrary-switching program now has two distinct budgets:

* **Observation budget:** can exact scalar output recover the needed interface
  under the chosen switching language? The sibling lanes address this.
* **Supply budget:** do physically initialized branches survive absolute growth
  fast enough even to have the capacity for dense scalar coverage? S7 fails;
  S83 crosses this necessary capacity barrier.

A third, still OPEN obligation is **collision-controlled absolute selection**:
for every prescribed odd N outside7,21, obtain a legal initialized path whose
scalar output is N, not merely a large number of possible programs.
A supercritical pressure certificate is deliberately not promoted to that
statement. The new exact lower/upper transition envelopes let a proposed
constrained decoder language be assessed before another large census.
