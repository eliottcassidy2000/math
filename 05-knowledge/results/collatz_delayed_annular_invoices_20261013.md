## 2. A sharper all-height compiler

For a fixed head `w`, write `U_w(n)=(pn+b)/q`. With a lower bound `a0`
on the final valuation, set

\[
 P=3p,\quad B=3b+q,\quad M=q2^{a_0},\quad
 r=-BP^{-1}\pmod M,\quad c=o(w)+1.
\]

As in the inherited compiler, `n=r mod M` forces every head valuation
exactly and the last valuation `a>=a0`. Retain the height cutoff ensuring
`n>=3` and every proper head state is at least three. The terminal state is

\[
 h=(Pn+B)/(q2^a)\le (Pn+B)/M.                        \tag{1}
\]

Choose an integer drop `s>=1`. The new gate retains `e=E(n)` and requires

\[
 e\ge \max\left(s+1,\left\lceil\frac{c+3s^2}{6s}\right\rceil\right),
 \qquad Pn+B\le M(2^{e-s+1}-2).                     \tag{2}
\]

Then the actual odd endpoint has `E(h)<=e-s`, is smaller than `n`, and

\[
 o(w,a)\le c\le 3(e^2-(e-s)^2)
               \le3(E(n)^2-E(h)^2).                 \tag{3}
\]

These are all-height integer inequalities. They use neither the child's
ROOT status nor the ROOT status of any neighbouring source. Only an
authenticated child supplies a ROOT certificate, exactly as before.

The old compiler demanded the stronger relative inequality
`2^s(h+1)<=n+1` throughout a complete residue cylinder. The new one admits
an explicit portion of each magnitude band. On `E(n)=e`, its upper endpoint is

\[
 \min\left(2^{e+1}-2,
 \left\lfloor\frac{M(2^{e-s+1}-2)-B}{P}\right\rfloor\right).
\]

If `P/M < 2^(1-s)`, the limiting fraction of the native residue progression
accepted in each band is

\[
 \theta=\min(2,\;2^{1-s}M/P)-1>0.                  \tag{4}
\]

This is a fraction **within the guarded residue progression**, not a
density of all positive integers. The carry and cutoffs contribute bounded
endpoint errors. Formula (4) can be positive even when `M<=2^s P`, where
the old whole-cylinder slope test fails.

## 4. A total tester for the adaptive hypothesis

The repaired target is:

> **OPEN.** Every odd `n>1` has a strict prefix ending at an odd `h` with
> `2(h+1)<=n+1` and cumulative charge at most
> `3(E(n)^2-E(h)^2)`.

The endpoint is chosen by the source, rather than fixed to its first exit.
This remains a substantive stronger target than convergence alone.

It has a **total finite tester** without assuming convergence. Put
`B_n=3(E(n)^2-1)`. While a state `x>=3` has not reached ROOT, an odd
valuation letter multiplies `x-1` by at most two; an even one multiplies it
by at most `3/4`. Choose the least nonnegative integer `t` such that

\[
 (n-1)2^{B_n}3^t < 2\cdot4^t.
\]

By step `B_n+t`, either ROOT occurs or the `(B_n+1)`st odd letter occurs.
Otherwise the current positive odd state would have `x-1<2`.
Accept upon the first paid prefix; reject upon excess charge. At ROOT
within budget the prefix is necessarily paid. The tester always stops.
A rejection refutes this budget for that source; it is not nonconvergence.

Universal acceptance is equivalent to **universal ROOT together with**
the envelope `o(n)<=3(E(n)^2-1)`. In the forward direction, use strong
induction on the strictly smaller accepted child and telescope (3). In
the reverse direction, the complete ROOT path itself is an admissible
paid prefix. This equivalence identifies the remaining strength of the
proposal; it does not establish acceptance.

On a grounded certificate with independently verified charge cap `B`, the
inherited [positive ROOT receipt](collatz_charge_receipts_20261011.md) still gives

\[
