## All-order proof audit

**Exact cones.** All proposed extreme rays are actual orbit states. Thus their generated cones are contained in the respective conical hulls. The checked facets describe strictly convex normalized polygons; generator images remain in those polygons after positive-h normalization. Initial states are included. Induction gives the reverse containments for every word length. This establishes the ordinary conical hull exactly, with no closure or limiting-ray assumption. It is stronger than an outer enclosure and does not characterize integer reachability. The point `(10,4,5)` lies in both cones but is not physically realizable, using the inherited permanent gap theorem.

**Sharp growth and logarithmic certificates.** Every first row bounds h' below by h+3E. The nonempty cone gives h'>=(292/193)h, achieved at the stated word. For a word of length r>=1, only the first step is exempt: h>=(292/193)^(r-1), hence r<=1+floor(log base (292/193) of h). The weaker 3/2 version in the detailed proof remains valid. Each generator adds at most four vertices, giving the stated tournament-order bound. All intermediate states have O(log h) bit length by monotonicity and the sidecar bounds; an O(log h)-symbol word is a polynomially checkable certificate. Singular inverse branches do not imply polynomial-time search. The initial h=1 plateau must be handled explicitly; the proof does so, listing all five states. Subsequent integral steps increase h by at least 3.

**Fifteen-letter rigidity.** Every intermediate state has h>0 and nonnegative sidecars. The local inverse obstruction therefore forces the final generator to be T, and T^{-1} takes the positive target ray to itself. Removing the last letter gives induction to arbitrary length, both for input v and for the singleton seed. The h>0 hypothesis is essential and explicitly respected: M7 and M13 accept the boundary ray `(0,0,1)`.

**Complete-alphabet rigidity.** All 57 generators map the nonnegative positive-h cone into C={h>0,E>=0,O>=0,h<=3O}. Both v and the seed belong to C. In this invariant cone the unique extra preimage is impossible; consequently each last letter must be one of T,T^2,T^3,T^4. These are invertible, and backward induction proves the stated three-way equivalence for every word, including the empty word. It also proves that the seed orbit never reaches the target ray. The physical packing/path proof of h<=xO for real x>=1 is correct: removing the incoming-to-port edge from each port-covering odd cycle injects its complement-weighted contribution into the odd-path sum, and the singleton path contributes D.

**Finite parallel lines.** If infinitely many distinct points on one line parallel to v are mapped into finitely many such lines by a fixed word A, two images lie in the same target line. Their difference is a nonzero scalar times Av, so Av is parallel to v. Even if images could a priori coincide, Av cannot vanish because all generators preserve positive h on v. The scalar is positive for the same reason. Rigidity forces a tower power. This is a correct obstruction for the specified finite parallel-line ansatz, not for adaptive directions, arbitrary nonlinear sets, larger blocks, or first-coordinate coverage. The same geometric argument can use the 57-letter rigidity if that stronger alphabet is explicitly stated.
