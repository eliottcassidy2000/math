# Inverse mechanics: invisible modes, periods, and the missing probe

The [isothermal stress atlas](../five_stress_connection_atlas_20261112/STRESS.md)
starts from a prescribed regular holomorphic isothermal coordinate Z. Its
local normal form is q=(Z')²f, where
f=A|z|²+Bz+conj(B)conj(z)+C is real, and equilibrium recovers the pressure
as p=p0−2 Re integral (Z')²(Az+conj(B)) dz. The unordered axes of the
stress do not determine either f or the additive pressure. Even after
specifying the local coefficients, pressure is single-valued only if
the real periods of the displayed differential vanish. Requiring a
single-valued displacement is a further test. The older
[sampling result](../five_stress_connection_atlas_20261112/SAMPLING.md)
has no H13 in its direct image at any size: good native compatibility
does not make the image universal.

There is a literal discrete counterpart to the *period test*, not to the
elasticity PDE. Given the merged chiral block, let s=a+b. Its unseen
integer response d=a−b must satisfy |d|<=s and d congruent to s mod 2;
these bounds are necessary and sufficient to reconstruct nonnegative
integer a,b on that block. Thus the even observation leaves a bounded
integer inverse problem before signs are calibrated. Once magnitudes
and support have been measured, sheet re-naming acts by a vertex-sign
gauge. On a fixed connected support with v vertices and e nonzero
off-diagonal couplings, calibrate along a spanning tree: set its v−1
edge signs positive by propagating a sheet choice from the root.
There remain exactly e−v+1 independent binary sign invariants, one
product around each fundamental cycle. They give a complete gauge
classification. Diagonal signs are separate, and with several colors
the support is a multigraph retaining all parallel cycle tests.

For a proposed symmetry, subtract the two sign patterns in this binary
gauge. Zero difference on all fundamental cycles is exactly the
ordinary lift test, and adding edges to enforce squaring to the identity
can introduce new cycles. This is why testing an ordinary lift and
testing its order are distinct, just as stress and displacement have
different period obligations. Tree support has no sign-period
obstruction, but can still fail absolute magnitude or involution
constraints. No analogy makes arbitrary compatible block values
realizable as tournament darts. The actionable lesson for reservoir
changes is to look for a small set of **additional probes** that detects
all continuation obstructions before trying to integrate the move
globally; Q_2 is a first such probe, not a proved complete signature.
