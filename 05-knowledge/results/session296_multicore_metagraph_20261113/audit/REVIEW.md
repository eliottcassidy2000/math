# Separate cross-checks and scope

The two scopes are structural all-order identities on a specified language,
and finite exact entrance witnesses. The latter are not evidence of
readiness at untested heights. Published priorities for two-face frame
gluing, ordinary fiber mass, blue detection of self-converse nodes, and the
ready-source grammar are retained.

## Witness lane

A separately written forward subset-path recurrence reconstructs each
literal tournament from its funnel word. For each subset S and last
vertex v, extend a path on S ending at v to each out-neighbor outside S.
Set H(empty)=1. The ordered two-bin endpoint sum is then
Σ_S e_r(T[S]) H(T−S), with e_r=0 when r is absent. This direct computation
found (H,F−H)=(H,1) at all nine initial third-target entrances, both
881 realizations and the later 2645, 7937 and 23813 examples. For the
inherited ready Q(TT_k), k=1,...,6, a direct extension by two
sole-predecessor births gives the claimed H=405·2^k+71 and raw defect
108·2^k−3. In particular reaching that count and rearming gap 1 are
different tasks.

The matching used for a replacement is permitted to modify arbitrary
nonroot arcs. Preserving the root and its predecessor preserves all
retained root-star arcs, but does not certify a short native complement
word, invariant intermediate counts, or unchanged other old arcs.

## Metagraph lane

The paired-card proof uses an actual ordered lower frame, not its
isomorphism class. When the middle deletion omits j, the chord
(j−1,j+1) is a frozen shortcut in the surviving order; it selects the
unique good endpoint of the upper line. For adjacent interior j,k, the
arc (j,k) is frozen upstairs, so every upstairs free bit survives at
least one deletion. Normalize on common corner (1,n); comparison on
the full shared coordinate set is necessary and sufficient. This
proves existence as well as injectivity. All assertions about minimal
numbers of cards fix the chosen masks in advance.

For phase-only cards the potentially invisible common omitted vertex
has an incident free chord at every order n≥4. If omitted sets are
disjoint and every cross pair is a frozen edge, the path's lack of
K₂,₂ forces one omitted set to be a singleton. Toggling the singleton
interior shortcut changes the good phase but not the lower unoriented
line, and the other mask omits one endpoint of that chord. The boundary
endpoint case just exchanges the two masks. These are information
obstructions, not statements about reconstruction from a general
unlabelled deletion deck.

A separately implemented permutation canonicalization gives the end-
node hostile keys in READOUT.md in its specified gauge. The generic
non-Markovian weighted-span failure is already in THM-796; the hostile
here just locates the lost distinction with small literal examples.

## Scalar atlas lane

Every accumulating equality of positive dyadic rays has bounded
integer displacement in its exponents. Thus the no-cycle proof
does not assume a fixed shift from the start. In a finite bank the
non-accumulating edges have only finitely many input values; an
unbounded monotone orbit cannot continue using them. This is a
necessary arithmetic restriction only. It does not exclude a chart
with a ternary mode or a program generating new templates.
