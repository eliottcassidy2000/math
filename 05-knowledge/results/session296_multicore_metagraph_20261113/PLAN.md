# Session 296: multi-core handoffs and the growth of the merged metagraph

Research plan, not a result. A numerical inverse is not yet a tournament:
entry into each next chart must retain the marked endpoints and all guards
used by the subsequent construction. The first task is to locate the
precise scope of the mod-45 obstruction, then look for physical transitions
with a stated domain, preservation law, and exit.

On the metagraph side, distinguish framed paths, complement lines, and
converse-merged classes. Reuse the existing two-face and incidence atlas;
seek exact rules for more than one deletion slot, and isolate what the
quotient cannot reconstruct. These are separate tasks from proving that
a particular approximation recovers A000568 at every order.
