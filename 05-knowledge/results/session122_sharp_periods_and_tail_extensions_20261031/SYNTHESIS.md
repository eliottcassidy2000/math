## Reusable mechanism and its limits

The interval conductor replaces a full-period overestimate with the exact
residual-arc measure envelope. Connectedness of the closed interval prevents
an open covering at the equality boundary. In the small-radius sector the
coefficient2kr is optimal even for unshifted distinct integer speeds. This is
an interval-only optimum, not an optimum for a fixed core's full safe set.

The three lanes use different mathematical maps:

* Core geometry -> interval discrepancy: preserves a common safe interval,
  loses correlations and other components; exact endpoints and connectedness
  restore the equality case.
* Composition trees -> deficit-weighted residue paths: preserves capacity
  only after a proved finite-generator bound; it does not classify every
  attainable graph statistic or graph isomorphism type.
* Abstract content paths -> clock/length sets: supplies necessary arithmetic
  data, not word realizability; a failed necessary multiplier congruence is
  sufficient to reject the entire corresponding abstract path class.

Their common lesson is methodological, not an asserted equivalence between
LRC, Ramsey theory and Collatz: restore the exact information that the bound
forgets before trying to sharpen it.
