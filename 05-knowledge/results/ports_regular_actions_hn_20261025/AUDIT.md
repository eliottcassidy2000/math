## Repairs and negative controls

The draft removal/insertion pattern at n=11 moved its own predecessor;
the same error occurs in the protected pattern at h=4. The illegal final
join and failed target XOR are retained. Neither is asserted to be a
connectivity counterexample. The safer scopes are explicit in the proofs.

The first excess draft said its bivariate coefficients could be nonintegral.
Review disproved this and supplied the stronger statement: (-1)^E b_(M,E)
counts isomorphism classes of M-vertex source tournaments with nonnegative
integer vertex colours totaling E. This does not make M the size of a
particular resulting pair-free core. The repaired proof and literal
inventory checks retain that distinction. The live-coordinate theorem
also distinguishes observable equivalence from independent storage bits
on the subset of reachable states.

Other retained controls: one fixed point plus excess defeats the ordinary
ungraded gauge; palette counts do not determine weighted continuation
counts; independent successful local assignments need not agree; regular
conic-set action is not freeness on the whole K3; a noninjective K4,4
drawing and a tetrahedron in R3 each break a hypothesis of the square lemma.
