# An independent certificate for the thirteen-unit growth bound

**Status: PROVED (computer-assisted, exact). This is an independent replication of THM-5212 (E), not a new theorem.**

The concurrent packet [chanpak_cone_collision_placement_20261115](../chanpak_cone_collision_placement_20261115/SUFFIX_CONES.md)
proved first, with adaptive suffix cones, that every bottleneck-free word over the 13 span-3 floor units satisfies
H ≥ 1.7699^(n−13)/4000. This session found the same mechanism independently: images of the nonnegative orthant of
the ribbon profile transfer, indexed by three-unit suffixes. It used a different cone schedule, its own LP and
verifier, and obtains a much better constant.

**Theorem (replication).** Every bottleneck-free unit word W over the 13 units 0.2, 1.2, 0.123, 2.13, 0.1.3,
0.3.1, 0.13.2, 0.1.123, 0.3.12, 0.12.3, 1.2.3, 1.12.3, 0.1.23 satisfies

    H(W) ≥ 0.013402 · (17699/10000)^|W|,       |W| = number of vertices.

## Certificate

- **Realization.** The ribbon profile transfer (THM-4880) has 119 states, 108 of them reachable at unit
  boundaries, and nonnegative unit matrices A_u. Its reachable span V has dimension 81.
- **Hankel representation.** The 8-dimensional Hankel representation M_u of THM-5212 (D) satisfies O A_u = M_u O
  on V, where O consists of the eight suffix functionals. Its image O(V) is Q^8 and fin·M_(s_j) = e_j. So it is
  reachable and observable, hence minimal.
- **Units stay in V.** Every A_u maps every reachable state into V.
- **Language.** Bottleneck-free means rank ≥ 2 in this minimal representation. The verifier recomputes this
  exactly, using 2×2 minors of integer-scaled products. There are 146 pairs, 1,471 triples and 14,725 4-words that
  are bottleneck-free; every other 4-word has rank ≤ 1.
- **Cones of uniform depth 4.** For each triple s = (u1, u2, u3), take the cone K_s generated by the Hankel
  projections of A_(u3) A_(u2) A_(u1) A_(u0) e_j. Here u0 ranges over the allowed predecessors and j over the
  reachable states. Each cone has 17.7 primitive generators on average. Every state of a word with ≥ 4 units lies
  in K_s, and A_u K_s ⊆ K_t along every allowed edge, because A_(u0) ≥ 0.
- **Functionals.** For each triple there is a functional φ_s with integer coefficients over the denominator 2^34.
  It satisfies φ_t(M_u g) ≥ (17699/10000)^|u| φ_s(g) on all 262,196 (edge, generator) pairs (0 failures), and
  fin ≥ φ_s/6.25686 on the cones.
- **Short words.** Every bottleneck-free word with ≤ 3 units is checked directly.
- **Control.** A brute-force Hamiltonian-path count agrees with the realization, and obeys the bound, on 40 random
  bottleneck-free words.

## Comparison with the concurrent certificate

- *Depth 3 versus depth 4.* Uniform depth-3 cones (K_s = image of the orthant under three units) are LP-feasible
  up to about 1.7675. This session's exact depth-3 certificate is at 1.765 (constant 0.01416). That is consistent
  with the concurrent 20-row Farkas witness, which excludes depth 3 at 1.7699.
- *How the full rate is reached.* The concurrent proof adds one predecessor at twelve triples. This certificate
  adds one at all triples. That costs more generators per cone but needs no adaptive choice, and the constant
  improves from about 1.5·10⁻⁷ to 0.0134.
