## 2. A total source-owned adaptive return tester

Write \(U(n)=(3n+1)/2^{v_2(3n+1)}\), with strict first ROOT at 1, and
\(E(n)=\lfloor\log_2(n+1)\rfloor\). Fix an integer rate \(c\ge1\).
For the **original** source \(n>1\), continue its actual trajectory and retain
all accumulated odd valuation letters \(\delta\). A paid checkpoint is

\[
2(h+1)\le n+1,\qquad
\delta\le c\{E(n)^2-E(h)^2\}. \tag{1}
\]

An unpaid first half-band exit is retained as a checkpoint, not discarded or
declared fatal. Neither the source nor the debt is reset at that exit.
Stop at the first paid checkpoint, or when
\(\delta>B:=c(E(n)^2-1)\).

This is a total finite test. Inherit the ROOT-or-charge deadline from C2 of
the shadow-charge packet: let \(t\) be the least nonnegative integer with

\[
(n-1)2^B3^t<2\,4^t. \tag{2}
\]

By \(B+t\) actual odd steps, either ROOT has occurred or more than \(B\)
odd letters have occurred. An even letter contracts the displacement from 1
by at least \(3/4\); an odd letter expands it by at most 2. If no ROOT has
occurred and the charge is at most \(B\), (2) would force \(n_{B+t}-1<2\),
contradicting a positive odd nonroot state. If ROOT occurs within the budget,
it satisfies (1). If the budget is exceeded before any paid checkpoint, a
later checkpoint cannot pay (1), whose right side is at most \(B\).

The implementation finds \(t\) by exact integer bisection; the ceiling
\(4(B+\operatorname{bitlength}(n))\) is sufficient because
\((4/3)^4>2\). A user-specified smaller work cap returns `PENDING`, carrying
the authentic original source and complete actual word. `resume` reauthenticates
that prefix and its first decisive event, then continues the same debt.
`EXHAUSTED` means no paid return with this invoice, not nonconvergence.

There is an exact global equivalence:

\[
\begin{split}
&\text{every odd }n>1\text{ is accepted by (1)}\\
\Longleftrightarrow\quad&
\text{every odd }n\text{ reaches ROOT and }
o(n)\le c(E(n)^2-1). \tag{3}
\end{split}
\]

For the forward direction, each accepted dependency is strictly smaller.
Strong induction supplies its child's strict ROOT word, and the charge
inequality telescopes. Conversely, under the right side, the actual ROOT
endpoint itself is a permitted checkpoint, so budget exhaustion cannot
precede it. This is an all-source equivalence: a **single** accepted source
has only a smaller-child obligation until that child is grounded. The
universal right side of (3) at \(c=3\) is not established here and is not
implied by the finite experiment.

## 4. Exact return cylinders and a native-lattice repair

For a supplied paid word \(w\), write its carrier as
\(U_w(x)=(Px+B)/Q\), with \(P=3^{|w|}\), \(Q=2^{\sum w}\), and let
\(\delta\) be its odd charge. The complete exact valuation cylinder is

\[
x\equiv (Q-B)P^{-1}\pmod{2Q}. \tag{4}
\]

For a desired raw drop \(s\ge1\), require \(Q>2^sP\) and

\[
x\ge \left\lceil\frac{2^sB+(2^s-1)Q}{Q-2^sP}\right\rceil. \tag{5}
\]

Then \(2^s(U_w(x)+1)\le x+1\). Every proper prefix state is constrained
to be at least 3 by its own affine lower bound. This prevents ROOT padding;
final oddness in (4) forces all prescribed intermediate valuations exactly.
If additionally

\[
E(x)\ge\max\left(s+1,
\left\lceil\frac{\delta+cs^2}{2cs}\right\rceil\right), \tag{6}
\]

then \(\delta\le c(E(x)^2-E(U_w(x))^2)\). Round the maximum of these
cutoffs upward to the first native member \(x_*\) of (4). This recovers the
inherited all-height exit compiler for an **exact** last letter, so its
charge is exact; the coarser last-letter compiler uses a uniform parity cap.

A pointwise floor drop is not a uniform raw drop. Three of the nine first-paid
returns have \(E(n)-E(h)=2\) but only a one-bit multiplicative guarantee.
For them, the continuous cutoff \(x_*\) excludes the discovery source.
Silently claiming that source belongs to the generic tail would be false.

The native lattice supplies a precise repair. Put \(M=2Q\). If the supplied
point receipt is paid and

\[
x_*\le n+M, \tag{7}
\]

then **every** source \(n+Mt\), \(t\ge0\), is paid: the supplied receipt
handles \(t=0\); all \(t\ge1\) lie above the proved tail cutoff. No native
integer lies in the apparent gap. This is a one-point proof plus a symbolic
tail theorem, not extrapolation from samples.

All nine records satisfy (7), giving nine actual infinite paid families
containing their hostile source. Their modulus exponents are respectively
320,384,556,656,728,893,910,972,1009. The three that require the lattice repair
are 898,696,369,947; 2,081,751,768,559; and 2,602,714,556,700,227,743.
`apply_lift` preserves the supplied source class and returns its authenticated
smaller endpoint. These are paid **dependencies**, not uniformly rooted
families. The word need not be the first paid prefix at every larger member.

## 5. A charge-free absorption rule

Suppose a retained checkpoint has \(2^s(x+1)\le n+1\). If the following
\(r\) actual letters are all even, then their endpoint satisfies

\[
y+1\le2+(3/4)^r(x-1).
\]

They spend no odd charge. To acquire \(d\) additional raw bits, it suffices
that \(4^r>2^d3^r\) and

\[
n+1\ge
\frac{2^{s+d+1}(4^r-3^r)}{4^r-2^d3^r}. \tag{8}
\]

Substitution proves \(2^{s+d}(y+1)\le n+1\). If ROOT is reached sooner,
stop immediately; the same height bound implies the desired drop at ROOT,
so no padding is needed. In particular, **three even letters after a
half-band exit acquire another bit whenever \(n\ge29\)**. This is an exact
guarded mechanism, not an assertion that those three letters always occur.
At the least hostile source the actual two even letters already suffice
because its endpoint has extra height margin.

The exact carry is essential even when the leading slope contracts enough.
The legal word \((2,1,1,2,3)\) takes \(9\) to \(5\), with carrier
\((243x+373)/512\). Its leading slope is below \(1/2\), but
\(2(5+1)>9+1\). Formula (5) gives cutoff 49. This is a literal source-bound
hostile to dropping the carry, rather than a formal inadmissible word.

## 6. What transfers from the supplied papers

**CITED from the supplied OpenAI paper** *Ordinary two-point correlations of
multiplicative functions* (24 September 2026), Theorem 1.1 is accepted as
requested. The technique relevant here is Sections 3.1 and 4.3, particularly
Lemma 4.6: a truncated residue representation comes with a separately bounded
deletion cost, and that cost is transported back to the integer average under
explicit covariance, probability and circuit hypotheses. We do not have
those hypotheses for a deterministic Collatz return process. Ordinary
two-point Liouville cancellation is not an all-source charge bound.

**CITED from the supplied OpenAI paper** *The Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture*
(23 September 2026), Theorem 1.1 is likewise accepted. Sections 3–4 retain
the filtration while integral deletion tests certify the leading projection;
Lemma 4.2 kills a specified lower-count remainder under an exact count rule.
There is no identification of Collatz words with that Lie algebra here.
The useful design requirement is narrower: before eliminating a coordinate,
prove a test that accounts for the omitted contribution.

Here the checkable transfer is elementary and independent of either deep
theorem. The failed exit retains its original charge, the later return pays
that same debt, (5) keeps the affine remainder, and (7) checks every integer
parameter omitted by a continuous cutoff. The source-9 example shows exactly
why an unguarded leading projection is insufficient. Even a density-zero
collection of omitted sources could contain a prescribed unresolved source;
no average estimate is used to delete such obligations.

| Word | Leading multiplier | Large-height slope | Carry cutoff and ROOT boundary |
