## Mathematical validity review

- All count conventions are raw directed Hamiltonian vertex words.
- All re-markings retain a specified path and exact coordinate transport.
- Ordinal blocks cannot interleave in a Hamilton path. This proves the
  product count and isolates ordinary relocations; after complement a
  separate four-guard locality proof is required and is supplied.
- The interior three-command probe keeps both block endpoints fixed. This
  is why it can be used at every SCC position; the earlier endpoint probe
  C,R_(0,2),R_(0,4) cannot simply be shifted into an internal block.
- Reversing a successful word returns the actual source. Conjunctions do
  not use a class-valued reset or an arithmetic averaging operation.
- The2^r figure is a lower bound on any ambient closed observer, not a
  claim of closure of the selected source family.
- State cardinality, linear dimension for arbitrary integer weights,
  affine dimension at fixed mass and command depth are stated separately.
- The Boolean zeta inverse works integrally and modulo every modulus;
  probability normalization is over Q. Source nonnegativity is retained.
- The parity-law means are not converted into individual spectrum witnesses.
- Neither a new coverage interval nor a resolution of the7/21 conjecture,
  general strong-atom carrier, Jacobian conjecture or A000568 is claimed.
