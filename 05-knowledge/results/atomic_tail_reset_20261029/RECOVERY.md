## Later incoming: a normalization that really is integral

Commit `193329556d` adds Session105's
[integer projective recurrence](../session105_metagraph_recursion_20261029/enumeration/INTEGER_PROJECTIVE.md)
and [implementation route](../session105_metagraph_recursion_20261029/enumeration/README.md),
both read in full. Its full-factorial and smaller odd-local gauges prove
that negative binary shifts are exact even after compatible histories
merge. Its benchmark retains the stronger all-coefficients Session87
baseline; fewer states are not assumed to imply faster computation.

This changed the current wedge lane positively. Because the native
Collatz carrier has gcd exactly Q, dividing it by Q is legal **if** J,D
remain explicit. The resulting normalized update is an exact division
by2^a on the actual valuation guard. WEDGE_GEOMETRY now proves and tests
this operation. We do not ban projective compression in general: we
require a proof that the allowed operation remains integral and that the
lost scale is either unobservable for the target or separately retained.
The repayment sign survives this normalization, while its eventual
positivity remains unproved.
