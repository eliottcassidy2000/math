Lemma M (mixed characters). Let M >= 2 be even, k odd, c(w) = ceil(w/2), and on the states w in Z/2M let
T_k[w -> 2w] = 1, T_k[w -> 2w+1] = e(k c(w)/M).  Then T_k T_k^* = 2 I.
Proof. The rows w and w + M have the same two non-zero columns (2w, 2w+1 modulo 2M), with entries (1, om) and
(1, om'), om = e(k c(w)/M), om' = e(k c(w+M)/M).  M is even, so w and w + M have the same parity and
c(w + M) = c(w) + M/2; hence om' = om e(k/2) = -om (k odd) and the two rows are orthogonal.  Rows w, w' with
w' != w (mod M) have disjoint supports; every row has norm sqrt 2.  QED.
(This is the computation of THM-5128 (E), proof, first paragraph, with 2^j replaced by an even M.
 Numerical check: mixed_gap.out - "phase(w + M) = -phase(w): True" for M = 6, 12, 24, 10, 20, 40, 18, 36, 14, 28,
 30, 60, 90, and rho(T_k)/2 = 0.7071 for EVERY character of even order of these Z/M.)

Corollary (all non-trivial characters of Z/(2^(j-1) m)).  Let M' = 2^(j-1) m, k != 0 (mod M').
 - If 2^(j-1) does not divide k: k/M' = k'/M'' with k' odd and M'' = M'/2^a even; by Lemma M the cell sums of
   e(k q(h)/M') e(l h/m) are at most 2^(r/2) (2M'')^(1/2) (r free digits), for every l.
 - If 2^(j-1) | k: e(k q/M') is a character of q modulo m and THM-5128 (B1), (B2) apply.
 - k = 0, l != 0: geometric sum, as in THM-5128 (B2).
So on every cell with at least j - 1 prescribed bottom digits, (q mod 2^(j-1) m, h mod m) is equidistributed up to
O_(j,m)((2 rho)^r), rho = max(rho_m, 2^(-1/2)); the rate of the modulus 2^(j-1) m is the rate of m
