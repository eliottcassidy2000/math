The resulting experiment is [RESIDUE_SELECTOR](RESIDUE_SELECTOR.md): a
short digit lift with exact factor7 control inside an already composable
physical repair family. It uses an11-adic unit clock and retains the full
primitive root frame. Its all4-mod5 boundary is a transparent hostile to
the inference from local completeness to a universal target selector.

[History's incoming comparison](history/INCOMING_HISTORY.md) gives the
marked ordinal padding derivation and distinguishes the new shared
dictionary quantifier. The root-start adjacent-minor identity in
[CHIRALITY](CHIRALITY.md) also credits the older Session188 aggregate
identity; the scalar15p−h already appeared in earlier core-replacement
work, as documented in [selection](selection/PROOF.md). The new claim is
the named physical repair, arbitrary-module proof, and composable handoff,
not rediscovery of those scalar formulae.
