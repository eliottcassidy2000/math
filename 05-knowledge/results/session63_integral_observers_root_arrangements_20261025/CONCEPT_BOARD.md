recovered in concurrent mainline work. Their source and explicit negation map
are preserved in the cancellation notes and independently parsed-source audit.

Controls distinguish odd from even actions, diagonal from independent actions,
coefficient reduction from an invariant-space recount, and fixed extension
families from fieldwise averaging. The 64-twist mean is not an ordinary
geometric average motive. No identification is inferred merely from matching
dimensions, the number seven, or the value of a point-count polynomial at one.
