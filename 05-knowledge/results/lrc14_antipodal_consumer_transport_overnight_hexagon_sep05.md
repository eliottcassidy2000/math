## 2. Exact antiphase certificate and its quantifiers

Let `C` be a finite positive-integer body, let

```text
G_C={y in R/Z: ||cy||>=1/14 for every c in C},
```

and let `T` be three distinct positive integers, all prime to three. The
physical row is `3C union T`. At a body phase `y`, a tail speed `w` occupies
one strict dangerous sheet iff `||wy||<3/14`; when active its owner is
`-n w^(-1) mod3`, where `n` is the unique nearest integer to `wy`.

Suppose there are `y,z in G_C` and an **actual** `w in T` such that

```text
w(y-z)=1/2 mod1.                                      (1)
```

Then

```text
||wy||+||wz||=1/2,
max(||wy||,||wz||)>=1/4>3/14.                         (2)
```

At the maximizing phase, `w` is inactive. The other two ternary units can
occupy at most two of the three sheets. A missing sheet `j` therefore gives
the actual physical witness `x=(y+j)/3` or `x=(z+j)/3`, with every row
distance at least `1/14`. This is a sufficient certificate; no converse is
claimed.

In particular, if `G_C` contains `y` and `y+1/2`, the certificate holds for
**every** such `T` containing an odd speed. Every primitive tail triple has
an odd speed, but a primitive full row need not have a primitive tail.

More generally, let `v=min_(w in T) v_2(w)`. A body-safe pair separated by
`1/2^(v+1)` suffices: a tail attaining `v` maps that difference to a
half-integer. This keeps the actual valuation in the consumer rather than
rescaling the tail and silently changing its position relative to the body.

For a fixed actual tail speed `w`, all admissible antiphase translations are

```text
tau=(2k+1)/(2w),             k=0,...,w-1.              (3)
```

The exact condition is nonempty **closed** intersection
`G_C intersect (G_C+tau)`. If a point of that intersection is denoted `y`,
the paired point is `y-tau`; the translation sign is retained in the
implementation. A singleton intersection is sufficient.

The radius boundary is also explicit: for strict danger radius `rho`, an
antiphase pair forces one inactive phase iff this argument has
`rho<=1/4`. Thus it transfers directly to the scale-three effective radius
`3/14`, but not indiscriminately to larger sheet counts. At radius `4/14`,
the phases `1/4,3/4` are both strictly dangerous for speed one.
