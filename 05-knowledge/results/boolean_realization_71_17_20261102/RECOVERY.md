## Hostile probes and the surviving statements

The regular weighted value probe is positive in the wrong category:
both forbidden means are attained with nonzero derivative, even inside
the full open edge-probability cube. The repaired predicate is the
Boolean target energy. Its minimum is an exact feasibility test, but
there are strict local minima at non-solutions. The six-vertex target-17
sublevel census gives 768 isolated non-solutions at error four, alongside
a component containing solutions. Thus nonincreasing single-edge descent
cannot be a general inversion rule.

The weakest sufficient extra scalar for concentration of a given positive
law is its target second moment: mean h plus zero variance forces support
in the actual h-fibre. This is not a claim of a universal smallest carrier
under composition. Signed weights defeat the implication, and shared
ports require joint rather than separate moments.

twelve-vertex tournaments and computing their actual automorphisms.
They share the same 67 labelled cyclic triples but retain group orders
81 and 27; their regular binary subset counts are 1620 and 1728.
