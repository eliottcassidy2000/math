## 1. Statements and the coordinate that stops the branch

starts at a. Call a *scalar gap-b auxiliary* if `S_a(T,2)−H(T)=b`. Every

The CITED strong-order bound in §5 excludes all orders at least17 for a
strong tournament of H4289. Therefore:

**Retained-order theorem.** No strong tournament with H4289 and at least
fourteen vertices has a scalar gap2 auxiliary. This excludes all such
preparations regardless of old-arc changes, selected auxiliary, physical
root response, or how much larger the target is made.

**Sharp all-order minimum at retained order14 or more.** Every strong scalar-gap2-ready tournament with at least14 vertices has H>=4841, and this is attained at order14. The three exact ranks above and the cited bound H>=5625 for every order at least17 prove this without assuming monotonicity of the ready minima. In particular the same retained-order exclusion holds for H4291 as well as H4289.

**Two-step consequence.** Start with any eleven-name strong tournament of
H1429. At each step allow arbitrary H-neutral preparations by changing old
arcs and adding permanent names, retain every previous name, require the
prepared target to be strong, then use the standard two-permanent-birth C2
reset. Such a process cannot perform the digit sequence **(+2,+4)**.

Indeed a first +2 reset needs a gap1 auxiliary. An order11 preparation is
impossible by the987-class result, so it has order at least12. Its C2 child
has H4289 and order at least14. The next H-neutral strong preparation must
still have at least14 names and H4289, contradicting the theorem. This
argument does not require F-neutrality or a full joint guard: even the
weaker scalar condition is impossible. Arbitrary changing root defects do
not remove this particular obstruction.

The [actual weak second-round branch](CHILDREN.md) demonstrates the scope
boundary. It attains12871 after passing through a weak prepared target;
there is no conflict with the theorem. Direct non-C2 count changes,
preparations that change H, and deleting old permanent names are outside
the stated grammar. Nothing here closes the global Hamilton-path spectrum.

## 2. Why the finite universes are complete

Take converse, preserving H and strongness, so a start-gap auxiliary
becomes an end-gap root. The complete classification

1. For gap1, an indegree-one newborn attached to a strong parent having
   exactly one starting Hamilton path at its attachment root.
2. For gap2, an indegree-one newborn attached to a strong parent having
   exactly two starting Hamilton paths at its attachment root; or one of
   the five exceptional indegree-two word families.

Both scalar gap2 cases are retained. The more restrictive positive joint
guard may discard the exceptional case, but the present theorem does not
need that newer supplier.

The complete strong two-start parent grammar is
[TWO_ENTRY §§1–3](../../two_entry_funnels_20261103/TWO_ENTRY.md). Delete its
root, order the SCCs, and use the unique nontrivial first core followed by
a transitive tail. The root enters either one two-start vertex in that
strong core, or two unique-start vertices of a unique-Hamilton-cycle core.
Every other SCC is a singleton. Tail words are arbitrary binary words
ending in1 when nonempty. Empty tails are permitted because the entry set
is proper. These two entry cases are disjoint and exhaustive.

The unique-cycle cores are generated by the inherited complete one-start
rank/unrank grammar in
[recognize.py](../../polarized_path_calculus_20261103/recognize.py), whose
mathematical source is the same SCC decomposition. Every unique-cycle
tournament has a unique-start vertex. The rank labels follow its unique
Hamilton cycle; passing to the lexicographically least cyclic rotation is
exactly the ordinary-isomorphism quotient, since every isomorphism must
preserve that unique directed cycle. Converse is **not** quotiented.

The proved entry-set law says that the unique starts are the vertices of
outdegree1 together with their successors on the cycle. At orders at least4
these unique-cycle objects are rigid. Thus every unordered pair of entries
is retained. At order3, the three pairs form one rotation orbit; the source
chooses one representative and does not count that orbit three times.

For a two-start rooted source, its two actual directed Hamilton paths give
two rooted adjacency encodings; the lesser is the canonical rooted code.
The source retains both transported path words. The rooted stabilizer is
trivial: it acts freely on the two paths, and a tournament has no nontrivial
involution. Therefore this canonicalization loses no rooted class or
physical entry information. Constructing a new funnel transports these
actual paths by prefixing the new root and appending the uniquely ordered
tail. It is not a canonicalization by a count or signature.
