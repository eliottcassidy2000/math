## Hostiles and correction lineage

* The weighted cut must retain ordered component incidence. Equation(12)
  excludes using a single component in both distinguished bins.
* The old assumption that all2-ready sources are newborns of two-start
  parents is replaced by the five exceptional families. This repairs the
  specific incompleteness noted in the preceding packet's AUDIT; it does
  not retract that packet's sufficient construction.
* During development the two length-two exceptional suffix coefficients
  were initially assigned in reverse order. Literal DP gave H29 for
  `11,10,01`; the final table is13*2^k+3 for10,01 and14*2^k+3 for01,10.
  The displayed mixed state after10,01 retains D2=1 as well as G1=D1=1.
  The final word language and formulas are independently controlled.
* The standard-reset theorem excludes n=3. Its C3 boundary has gap list
  `(2,14,12,0,2)` in construction order, so a larger stated scope is false.
  In its old-root case the directed in-port order is y->a, making the
  first bit zero on the remaining old vertices. Swapping the two bit
  names leaves the language obstruction equivalent, but the final proof
  retains the actual directed port order.
* Equal H in the common source pair does not mean equal integer observer:
  their X difference is91*3^t+4*2^t-39. The mod-seven equality has the exact
  guard3|t; outside it a phase-neutral statement is false.
* The 81/27 pair is retained as an exclusion from the restricted low-start
  class, not a new classification result. Its inherited module ranks are
  not recalculated or promoted to an all-size invariant.
* Full complement closure of an eighteen-polynomial quotient is not
  asserted. The incoming counter example has equal complete forward
  observers but unequal deletion guards. Our literal source and path
  certificates supply the information required by the actual operations.
