# What two contacts recover: slot polynomials, F1 phases, and Rule 30

2026-11-13. **Chan–Pak remains open.** The key result is an exact
first-moment/second-moment divide, followed by a constructive use of
signed continuation data.

## Reconstruction and collisions

- [Strong shadow](STRONG_SHADOW.md): reversing a vertex-transitive
  module preserves the complete labelled induced slot-polynomial table,
  and every single-edge/start/end moment per slot on the full graph.
  A strong order-24 collision is explicit. At order 21k+3 a single
  such fibre contains at least 2^k classes even up to converse.
  [Ordinal shadow](ORDINAL_SHADOW.md) sharpens the order-22 example,
  gives its exact seam moments, and identifies the minimum order
  for the vertex-transitive asymmetric-block mechanism, not for
  arbitrary collisions.
- [Wedge reconstruction](WEDGE_RECONSTRUCTION.md): the Boolean
  co-usage of adjacent edges in ordinary Hamilton paths already
  recovers the tournament up to converse. It integrates signs on
  the connected used-edge relation, then applies the older directed
  support decoder to dark arcs. Two optional slots give a simpler
  local read and an optimal predetermined binary query budget.
- [Hereditary slots](HEREDITARY_SLOTS.md): a squarefree exponential
  links all labelled induced slot polynomials to odd-cycle cumulants.
  The entire unmarked table has exactly the information of the
  labelled cyclic triples. This uses the classical interval-inversion
  theorem and specializes the older unsigned curl result.

## Other native interfaces

- [F1 wedge jet](F1_WEDGE_JET.md): decorate each path by its own
  projective unit phase. In the finite-field point-count polynomial,
  the constant and linear terms at q=1 are blind to every orientation.
  The quadratic term with labels retained records the reconstructing
  wedge relation. This is a formal specialization, not a literal
  field with one element. [F1 slot geometry](F1_SLOT_GEOMETRY.md)
  gives the missing clutching factor when component phases are glued,
  and the split versus Frobenius-stable quotient count, including a
  nonzero exact correction on two triangles.
- [Rule 30](RULE30_CONTEXT.md): in spatial periodic predecessor counts,
  every pair of context-distinct nonempty words has an increasing,
  decreasing, and equalizing suffix at every fixed depth >=3.
  Four explicit probes distinguish the context classes and no three
  suffice. Same-depth balance is an all-depth identity; the reset
  and exact finite transition closure certify the rest. This says
  nothing about the temporal center from a single seed.
- [Signed tournament contexts](SIGNED_TOURNAMENT_CONTEXTS.md):
  one strong nine-vertex pair has opposite continuation signs at
  arbitrarily large common padding. A second pair with H=1047 has
  positive, zero and negative common strong continuations at the
  same output order 13. The exact shared ending is H=532623,
  although their Q(2) values still differ. This is a native return,
  but not a reusable readiness-preserving or arbitrary-target rule.

The recurring distinction is between a **scalar**, a **labelled joint
measurement**, and a **physical operation**. A joint measurement
can recover every arc without providing a physical way to modify an
arc to land on the target. The next constructive task is to make the new native equalizer
repeatable by recovering readiness at its exact ending, not just
to make richer numerical observations of the same tournament.
