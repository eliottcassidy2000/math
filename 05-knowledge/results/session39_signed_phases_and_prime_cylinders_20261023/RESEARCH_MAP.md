## 1. Actual tournament attachment phases

Source: a tournament equipped with an authenticated equitable partition into singleton and cyclic-three cells.

Map: normalize each regular mixed 3-by-3 block to a permutation matrix. Every permutation of three symbols is affine on F3, x↦εx+δ. Preserve the polarity of a block, the sign ε and the translation δ. Uniform blocks carry no matching constraint, but their orientations and the entire singleton-induced tournament are still retained.

Preserved: all arcs, up to the allowed rotations of the internally oriented cells. Spanning-tree normalization leaves simultaneous cycle holonomies. The construction is a representation of actual tournament data, not an arbitrary gauge field imposed after the fact.

Lost by retaining chirality alone: the translation part of the connection. Local cyclic-triangle incidence can remain unchanged when this translation changes around a long cycle.

Decisive tests: the complete three-cell family; the three phase choices on a five-cycle of cells; explicit Hamiltonian counts; motif-count invariance under forest gauge; and marked-vertex reconstruction.

## 2. Three different gluing predicates

Do not confuse the following questions.

1. **Classifying a connection:** nonidentity holonomy is allowed and may be the distinguishing invariant.
2. **Finding a parallel selected owner:** a root owner must be fixed by every fundamental-cycle holonomy. A reflection of F3 has one fixed owner, a nonzero translation has none, and the identity has three. Separately nonempty fixed-point sets need not have a common element.
3. **Trivializing complete frames:** for unrestricted affine frames, every cycle holonomy must be the identity. If charts must preserve each preassigned cyclic orientation, only rotations are allowed and reflection edge signs remain additional data; one cannot silently use the larger affine gauge group.

A valid nonflat tournament does not need a parallel owner section. A marked vertex can reconstruct the tournament even when an owner section does not exist. This is why a holonomy obstruction to one gluing predicate is not an obstruction to the tournament itself.

## 3. Signed Collatz is fixed-point transport, not flat-frame transport

For a prescribed odd valuation word, the signed maps x↦(3x+ε)/2^a compose to one affine rational map. A periodic orbit requires a fixed point of that map, not that the map be the identity. The sign of 2^A−3^r selects the positive sheet for ε=+1 or ε=−1; exact integrality supplies the arithmetic cycle condition.

There is no direct identification with the F3 permutation transport: the fixed Collatz branch has slope 3/2^a, which reduces to zero modulo three, not to an invertible ±1. Its inverse divides by three and therefore needs an arithmetic admissibility condition. The shared affine composition law does not supply a reduction functor preserving the two dynamics.

Finite valuation words determine dyadic cylinders. Their prime/composite ambiguity is an observer theorem: it says what a finite valuation prefix cannot identify. It is not a lower bound on primality algorithms that are allowed to read the integer, nor a Collatz convergence theorem.

The additional closing-step construction retains an actual exponent clock. It can place prescribed finite prefixes in known target basins, but its exponentially growing modulus is not a uniform source-budget estimate. Prime abundance in a cylinder and abundance of known-basin points in that cylinder must not be intersected without a further theorem.

## 4. Electrical torsion and physical twists need different extra data

For a connected graph, ordinary first integral homology is free. Its critical group is instead the discriminant of the integral cut lattice, equipped with a linking pairing. A quadratic refinement additionally needs an even lattice or a specified characteristic choice.

The full dyadic tower can preserve the 2-primary finite abelian group while missing an odd-unit squareclass of the linking form. Conversely, complete linking data still forgets the native ownership of individual path-labelled ports. Loops are invisible to the Laplacian alone, but known fibre degrees recover their multiplicities; that sidecar must not be dropped when stating the obstruction.

One can explicitly construct Abelian Chern–Simons or doubled BF matrices from graph/tournament data. This is a mathematical construction of candidate topological data. It does not establish a microscopic electronic phase, a physical realization, or a transfer of a physical conjecture to tournaments.

## 5. What the hidden-variable perspective actually contributes

The useful question is not whether two objects share a suggestive number. It is which observable forgets a coordinate, whether that coordinate can be restored, and which prediction changes when it is varied.

Here the hidden coordinates are explicit: a matching translation around a cycle; a common fixed owner rather than separate fixed owners; a valuation word's endpoint and exponent clock; an odd-unit linking class; and a spin/characteristic choice. Each has a different carrier and an explicit test. Their shared algebraic language is transport and quotient loss, not a claim that they describe astrophysical dark matter or one universal physical system.
