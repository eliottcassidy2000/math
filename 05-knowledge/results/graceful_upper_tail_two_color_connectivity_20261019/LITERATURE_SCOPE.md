# Literature scope

Checked 2026-10-19. This is a targeted primary-source check, not an exhaustive priority review.

Shandong University's [2024 lecture announcement](https://faculty-en.sdu.edu.cn/content.jsp?urltype=news.NewsContentUrl&wbnewsid=113951&wbtreeid=1091) describes Shun-ichi Maezawa and Yutaro Yamaguchi's work on rainbow spanning-tree reconfiguration under single-edge flips, including a sufficient connectivity condition. The announcement does not state that condition, so it cannot establish whether it subsumes the present rank-staircase theorem.

Maezawa's [author-maintained papers list](https://smaezawa.org/papers) was also inspected. This limited check did not locate a full text supplying the lecture's condition. It is not evidence that no related theorem exists.

Fundamental-cycle exchange and tree-cut reconnection are elementary established tools. The proof here uses matroid contraction, rank, parallel classes, and connected-cut reconnection. It gives a sufficient rank-staircase condition and verifies it for every integer-difference upper-tail fiber. No novelty claim is made for those tools, the general research area, or the particular theorem without further comparison. For an empty frozen tail the result does imply global two-color connectivity of existing gracefully labeled trees. It does not establish global one-color connectivity, does not preserve an unlabeled tree shape, and does not prove the Graceful Tree Conjecture.

A further targeted search on 2026-10-19 found the same Maezawa–Yamaguchi announcement and an [author's talks page](https://www.hojinchu.com/talks/) listing a presentation about rainbow spanning-tree reconfiguration. These confirm an active existing subject; they do not supply a full theorem with which to compare the present rank-staircase criterion. The criterion's historical novelty remains unestablished.
