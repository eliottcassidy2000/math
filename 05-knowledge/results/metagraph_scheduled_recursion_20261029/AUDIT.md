## Proof review

P2 uses termwise divisibility in Z_(2), then the inherited integrality of
the whole count. It does not round a rational sum. The odd/even k factorial
bounds are separate, and n=0,1,2 have explicit base cases. The large-n
claim is a proof using a monotone quadratic lower bound; finite checks
are not promoted to that quantifier.

P3 treats distinct triples, repeated nonunit parts, the repeated-unit
triple, and the all-one n=3 exception separately. The single leading
correction cannot cancel modulo its next power of2. n=1 is excluded
from the nonzero correction valuation. Larger r and s only increase the
higher-cycle gaps. The ordinary low-bit result does not imply the aligned
pair result by squaring; each is a separate channel.

P4 uses the actual root-reset trace and rooted-class interpretation. It
does not identify that reset matrix with the black/blue adjacency matrix.
At even orders, precision lost by division by n must be budgeted.

The scheduled recurrence contracts unique identity paths, and expires
exactly the coordinates that cannot be queried below the remaining
degree. It preserves positive color factors and odd-factorial weights.
It does not infer a smaller state quotient or a reduced pending-memory
bound from a smaller active work list.
