## Status discipline

**INHERITED** means an explicitly pinned repository input accepted here.
**PROVED** means a new statement with its proof in this packet, without a claim
of external novelty. **FINITE-EXACT** describes only the declared computation.
**CONDITIONAL** names its missing premises. **OPEN** is not a conclusion.
Unsupported snippet assertions never enter a proved dependency chain.
