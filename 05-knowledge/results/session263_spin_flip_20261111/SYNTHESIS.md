cone. Their inverse formulas recover the preceding state exactly. Starting

source states. Paired letters give a free four-symbol fixed-offset compiler,
with no prepaid finite queue to exhaust. The selected vertices move; the
original marks and original state are not restored.

## 2. Overlapping cycles become controlled addresses instead of leakage

Take pointed A,B, orient A→B, and reverse only beta→alpha. Every new odd

complete packing polynomial has a positive residual recursion: either no
new cycle, or one new cycle and independent packings on its two complements.
This remains exact under nested return joins if the induced-deletion sidecar
is kept.

## 3. Fresh-prime inspiration yields actual fixed-offset source codes

Place a single marked vertex between selected allocations of unmarked

    h_m=2^m+1, q_m=2·3^m+4·2^m-2.

## 4. A genuine Fourier projector can be a tournament, but only dyadically

Retain path length in the rooted response polynomial P. An unmarked ordinal
block C multiplies it by

    K_C(z)=sum_S H(C[S])H(C-S) z^|S|.

A universal exact m-residue equalizer exists exactly when S_m=1+...+z^(m-1)
divides K_C. Rédei parity gives K_C==(1+z)^|C| modulo2. Therefore:

**Every universal ordinal length filter has power-of-two modulus, and needs
at least m-1 vertices.** No amount of ordinary transitive padding erases
more than parity. This is a structural restriction on physical projectors,
not a numerical dimension analogy.

Positive initial filters are supplied: a singleton for2, a directed triangle
for4, and an explicit eight-vertex block F for8, with

    H(F)=231, K_F=231(1+z)S_8(z), Q_2(F)=3696.

After attaching F, every mod-eight length channel contains exactly462U
paths with complementary weight. The block is not an opaque random table:
it is a triangle wrap of the one-apex word10010, and the complete residual
recursion proves its length polynomial from that architecture. Two independent
finite path counters certify every induced subset of this explicit block.

The parity zero has another exact geometric meaning: its multiplicity is
the minimum number of odd directed cycles and singleton fixed points in a
spanning permutation cover. Its leading quotient is a positive weighted sum,
so minimum-order cancellation cannot occur. Fourier depth and parity-derivative
depth are different resources, even in the small explicit examples.
