# Constructive questions after Session263

**Chan–Pak remains OPEN:** the spectrum is conjectured to contain every
positive odd integer except7 and21. The following are specific next steps,
not conclusions inferred from the session's successful small constructors.

1. **From scalar recognition to scalar coverage.** The new one-count decoder
   recognizes an explicitly encoded binary-word family. Find a legal source
   operation whose inverse covers target residue classes and produces smaller
   predecessors, not only a separated subset of counts. Session262's finite
   controller/initial-bank certificate still applies. Supply its actual initial
   interfaces, not merely tournaments with the same H.
2. **Turn addressed return-join gates into a target arithmetic language.**
   Their predicate and every longer-cycle address are now known exactly.
   Find a family of such predicates whose signed-state counts hit the desired
   residual integers while preserving a usable protected source service.
   Arbitrary conflict graphs and arbitrary subset selection are not supplied.
3. **Higher dyadic physical filters.** Universal nondyadic ordinal filters are
   impossible. For2^a, search the exact triangle-wrap/return-join equations,
   beginning with the10010 apex word at a=3. Prove an all-a constructor or an
   additional obstruction. A degree bound of2^a-1 makes scale cost unavoidable.
4. **Select a channel rather than equalize every channel.** The present blocks
   realize positive Fourier annihilation, not a chosen residue projector with
   a retained active source. Build a literal length-channel consumer and track
   its endpoint/deletion sidecar and paid offset. Formal character averages
   alone cannot serve as physical tournament replacements.
5. **Reduce the price of lossless source encoding.** One-query radix separation
   uses O(k) extra vertices and increases count magnitudes. Can a bounded
   continuation distinguish all depth-k states, or can the constants be lowered
   without an unsupported claim that the original slopes are all distinct?
6. **Grow independent arithmetic species or control dependent ones.** The six
   valuation-independent species admit arbitrary multiplicities. An infinite
   species theorem needs actual new separating primes or another certificate.
   Alternatively exploit the full integral valuation kernel to build a useful
   nontrivial balance move. A truncated valuation matrix cannot certify this.
7. **Protect useful offset while combining returns.** Marked converse doubling
   forces D<=0; bilateral filtering preserves positive D but multiplies it by
   231². Find a nonordinal operation preserving the appropriate unit/paid offset
   and exact returned source interface, then test the finite scalar-cover gate.
