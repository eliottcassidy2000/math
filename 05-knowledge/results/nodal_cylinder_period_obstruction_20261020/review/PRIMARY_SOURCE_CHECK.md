## Pinpoints and imported scope

Page 1061 fixes an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Page 1062 defines equivalence via ambient polynomial automorphisms and isomorphism via coordinate rings. Its exact uniqueness sentence is:

> Finally, f has a unique embedding means every g which is isomorphic to f is equivalent to f.

Theorem (1.3), on that page, covers a leading form Y^n − X^m with distinct prime exponents and terms of weights strictly less than mn, using weights n on X and m on Y. It asserts unique embedding and one place at infinity. No smoothness, unibranchness, or bounded-alternate-degree assumption is attached to this theorem. Page 1063 proves uniqueness by the degree semigroup and nonprincipal generating sequences. The singularity hypotheses in Theorem (1.1) are not hypotheses of (1.3).

Application: h = Z² − W³ − W² has (n,m)=(2,3), and W² has weight 4<6. The needed conclusion concerns equivalent embedded curves/defining ideals; exact equality of arbitrarily rescaled equations is unnecessary.

The indexed definition's equation-level notation is not used to assert an exact polynomial identity up to no unit: the proof requires only ambient equivalence of the zero loci. The producer clarified this distinction after review. This is a citation-precision repair, not a gap in the period argument.
