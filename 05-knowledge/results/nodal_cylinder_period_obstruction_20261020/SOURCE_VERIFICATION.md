## Classical input actually inspected

S. S. Abhyankar and A. Sathaye, *Uniqueness of plane embeddings of special
curves*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. **124** (1996), 1061–1069.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6
- Original journal PDF mirror:
  https://scispace.com/pdf/uniqueness-of-plane-embeddings-of-special-curves-omujaliiec.pdf

The web index exposed the original article's complete relevant pages:
p. 1061 supplies the algebraically closed characteristic-zero field setup;
p. 1062 supplies the definition of uniqueness and Theorem (1.3);
p. 1063 supplies its proof and transition to the nonuniqueness section.
The theorem includes singular curves and arbitrary alternate embeddings.
It is used only for equivalence of embedded curves/defining ideals, not for
literal equality of rescaled defining polynomials.
