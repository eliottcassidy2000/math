## Audited results

### Exponential strong fibers with gcd one

For every t≥1, an explicit physical sidecar

    E=d=19^t,   U=(700·512^t−207·19^t)/493

has 2^t distinct Hamilton-path counts, all realized by strong tournaments on 7t+2 vertices, and the gcd of those displayed counts is 1. An order-six block with (H,Q2)=(19,256) admits two equal-alpha root masks. Exact superincreasing defect weights prove distinctness, while two selected differences and parity prove gcd one at every t. This removes the forced-common-factor weakness of the earlier exponential triangle family. [All-order construction](unsaturated_module/PROOF.md), [independent audit](unsaturated_audit/AUDIT.md).

The accompanying separator lemma is stronger than the binary-compatible version below: arbitrary block transfers preserve a three-dimensional defect-image space, and a singleton anywhere between defects sends it into their common two-dimensional kernel. Thus multi-defect terms vanish even across nonbinary baselines. The displayed fibers are still sparse; gcd one does not imply a prime, an interval, or arbitrary-target coverage.

### Finite physical-state membership at every deletion level

Every actual rooted four-state (D,E,U,d) has a bounded-order representative preserving all four coordinates. When d>1 and U>d, the elementary bound is d+floor(log2(U−d))+2; sink and d=1 boundaries have smaller explicit bounds. The proof removes only inactive singleton source components and uses the first root-path activation to bound the remaining suffix. Consequently physical four-state membership is decidable by a finite exhaustive search, and every specified invertible rational physical graft has a terminating numerical-state inverse test. This is an existence algorithm, not an efficient or size-decreasing induction, and not graph recognition. [Proof](physical_state/PROOF.md), [independent audit](physical_state_audit/AUDIT.md).

The earlier scalar-core decidability theorem and the special start-one source oracle are explicitly credited. The new extension preserves an arbitrary root's full state simultaneously. The optional logarithmic d refinement uses the cited Busch minimum theorem; the elementary result and verifier do not depend on it.

For each r>=2, an explicit mobile-triangle construction gives 2r−2 distinct Hamilton-path counts with exactly the same rooted sidecar (E,U,d)=(0,4^r+2,3), on 2r+2 vertices. The counts are B_r at the initial position and B_r+(-2)^p−2 for 1<=p<=2r−3, where B_r=(4^r+6r−1)/3. This proves unbounded fiber cardinalities already at deletion count three. It does not claim that one fixed numerical fiber is infinite, nor that the displayed counts fill an interval.

### An exact genuinely recursive target compiler

Arbitrary ordered joins and binary cyclic nodes from any finite physical seed bank admit a complete target-bounded membership and minimum-depth algorithm. Exterior singleton padding is handled by multiplicative closure, not an invalid global order cutoff. With every tournament through order five as seeds, the exact output through 4095 is 577 values; the first values requiring cyclic depths two and three are 241 and 4001. These are scoped grammar results, not new unrestricted coverage. [Proof](recursive/PROOF.md), [audit](recursive_audit/REPORT.md).

The general path-cover transform is inherited and explicitly credited; its low-moment quotient is not recursively sufficient.

### A coprime mobile module and universal separated-defect law

An explicit order-five module with (H,Q2)=(13,104) matches the binary word 010 in its normalized sidecar. Moving it through (01)^r gives r−1 distinct counts at deletion 13; for every r≥3 their gcd is 1, since the first two are odd and differ by 8. A trimmed subfamily is strong, including the audited gcd-one four-point fiber at r=7. These are sparse fibers, not interval or prime coverage. [Construction](coprime_module/RESULT.md), [independent audit](coprime_audit/REPORT.md).

The separation identity extends to every binary-compatible physical module, through an explicit four-parameter transfer-defect family and a common two-dimensional invariant space. Its exact additive formula also forces the divisor (product H_i)/lcm(H_i). Thus repeating a fixed finite bank of these modules retains an arithmetic obstruction. [All-order law](coprime_module/UNIVERSAL.md). The complete labeled census through order six is finite evidence only; the independent canonical verifier does not rely on the exploratory producer scripts.

### Universal SCC divisibility and a sharp triangle boundary

For any rooted tournament, let its deletion SCCs have g_i=gcd(H_i,Q_i(2)). Then H(A) is divisible by (product g_i)/lcm{product of g_i over at most two distinct indices}. At each prime, the forced valuation is the sum of the g_i valuations after removing the two largest. The endpoint-block proof applies to every ordered tournament-block decomposition, not just the construction families. [Proof](mobile_triangle/GENERAL_DIVISIBILITY.md), [independent audit](mobile_audit/divisibility/AUDIT.md).

For t triangle deletion blocks this gives 3^max(t−2,0), sharp for every t≥2 via an explicit 41-based family. The separated exponential family obeys the stronger divisor 3^(t−1). For prime target counts the universal theorem severely restricts repeated saturated deletion components, while blocks with gcd(H,Q2)=1 evade this particular obstruction. Passing it is not a physical-existence theorem.
