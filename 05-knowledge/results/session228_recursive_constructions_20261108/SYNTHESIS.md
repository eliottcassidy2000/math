The target is every positive odd Hamilton-path count except 7 and 21. This session produced constructive families, exact decision procedures, and universal obstructions. It did not establish a new unrestricted coverage interval.

## The main constructive advance

The strongest new construction is [exponential strong gcd-one fibers](unsaturated_module/PROOF.md). At every t≥1, the same physical sidecar supports 2^t distinct scalar values on strong tournaments of order 7t+2, and those values have gcd 1. Unlike the separated triangle family, this family does not acquire a growing common divisor.

The mechanism was found by following an obstruction rather than ignoring it. The initial exponential triangle construction had a large forced power of 3. A mobile H13 module gave unbounded gcd-one fibers, but the universal binary-compatible defect law still forced a product/lcm divisor when fixed modules were repeated. The general root-deletion SCC theorem identified gcd(H,Q2), rather than H alone, as the relevant obstruction. This pointed to modules with gcd(H,Q2)=1.

The first candidate, the strong order-four (H,Q2)=(5,32) block, failed: all root-neighborhood alpha subset sums are distinct. The order-six (19,256) block succeeds. Its two equal-alpha masks have different full transfers; their defects are killed by separating singletons even across nonbinary baselines. Exact geometric weights then give exponential distinctness and an all-order gcd-one proof.

This chain matters: the final construction escapes the proved arithmetic obstruction without assuming an unproved supply of fresh coprime modules. It still gives a sparse, rapidly spaced subset of each fiber.

## Other durable results

- [Physical four-state membership](physical_state/PROOF.md): every realized (D,E,U,d) has an explicit bounded-order representative. Hence exact membership, and numerical-state inversion for any specified invertible rational physical graft, are decidable. The procedure is not efficient and does not supply an all-target predecessor.
- [Exact deletion-one/deletion-three membership](mobile_triangle/MEMBERSHIP.md): canonical finite banks settle these levels. Deletion three is the first odd level with unbounded scalar fibers; its O(log U) size bound is attained in order of growth.
- [Physical binary-cyclic inversion](cyclic_inverse/PROOF.md): a divisor-based inverse and finite physical factor search replace numerical cone feasibility. The operation's finite image is explicitly distinguished from the unrestricted spectrum.
- [Recursive target compiler](recursive/PROOF.md): any finite physical seed bank under arbitrary joins and binary cyclic nodes has an exact target-bounded membership/minimum-depth algorithm. Full path-cover information is necessary for arbitrary recursive depth; low moments collide.
- [Universal SCC divisibility](mobile_triangle/GENERAL_DIVISIBILITY.md): every rooted tournament obeys the prime-valuation lower bound obtained by removing the two largest deletion-block gcd valuations. The triangle exponent is sharp at every number of triangle blocks.

The mobile, cyclic, recursive, state-compression, coprime, divisibility, and unsaturated branches all received independent audits. Their written proofs carry the all-order claims. Labeled censuses and literal graph computations are explicitly finite regression controls.

## Precise next questions

1. **Turn fiber freedom into target selection.** The H19 construction supplies many states with the same sidecar, but its binary defect weights are superincreasing. Find interacting or multi-choice equal-alpha modules whose attainable sums overlap enough to select a prescribed scalar, while retaining literal realizability.
2. **Use unsaturated deletion components.** Repeating components with H dividing Q is arithmetically costly. Components with gcd(H,Q)=1 evade that particular obstruction. Determine which equal-alpha transfer differences they support, rather than assuming a numerical cone is populated.
3. **Couple exact inverse branches to a decreasing measure.** Arbitrary four-state physical membership is now a terminating subroutine. What remains is an explicit finite or recursively specified family of physical graft inverses that covers every target and provably decreases a well-founded measure. Decidability alone supplies no such family.
4. **Increase the recursive grammar without truncating hidden moments.** The existing compiler can certify exact finite images and minimum depths of broader specified seed/gadget grammars. An all-odd theorem would require a structural coverage argument beyond a larger finite run.
5. **Test arithmetic obstructions root by root.** The universal SCC condition applies for every root. A prime-count construction must avoid repeated saturated deletion components at every chosen decomposition; satisfying the condition is necessary, not sufficient.
