# Composing genus-two transfers with the source still attached

2026-10-11. **PROVED, scoped and self-audited:** T1--T3 and the transfer
corollary in T5. **INHERITED:** the stronger carrier exclusions in T4--T5.
**FINITE-EXACT:** rational identities and control examples. **OPEN:** more
general source-normal compositions and planar JC(2). The named generator
group and polynomial carrier below do not exhaust birational symplectic
maps or all Keller maps.

## Inheritance and the change of question

The [Legendre-pencil note](legendre_pencil_normalizer_20261011.md) already
closes the full symplectic normalizer of its elliptic pencil and constructs
the cross-source map F_(a,b). The [cusp-ideal theorem](planar_jc48_sep08_cusp_ideal.md)
already excludes all nonzero rational scalar times in its polynomial
Hamiltonian carrier, including the elliptic boundary. We do not repeat a
monomial subcase of those stronger results.

Here we compose changing generators, classify which words genuinely
return to the polynomial correction family, and then change that family.
The hostile is a word whose maps are symplectic but whose intermediate
source coefficients do not match. The least-used sidecar is the coefficient
of t^-1 in the logarithmic chart. The connection to the
[Kakeya intake](kakeya_marked_context_intake_20261011.md) is the discipline of
retaining the shared object through a change of frame, not an imported
projection theorem.

Work over a characteristic-zero field K. Put

    v=xt,  p=t+v^2,  y=vp,  u=v^2/t,
    G_H=-u/2+H(p,y),              H in K[p,y].

The area form is dx wedge dt=dv wedge dt/t. For a nonzero linear
polynomial A(v)=a+bv, the inherited map is

    F_A(v,t)=(-v,-v^2/[2A(v)t]),
    F_(a,b)(x,t)=(2(a+bxt)/x,-x^2t/[2(a+bxt)]).

It satisfies G_(a,-b) composed with F_(a,b)=G_(a,b), where
G_(a,b)=-u/2+ap+by. Its inverse uses(a,-b), not an arbitrary next pair.

## T1. Exact normal forms close all words in these generators

Every even-length word has the form

    T_r(v,t)=(v,r(v)t),             r in K(v)^*;          (1)

every odd-length word has the form

    O_R(v,t)=(-v,R(v)/t),           R in K(v)^*.          (2)

All preserve the area form. In particular,

    F_(c,d) composed with F_(a,b)=T_r,
    r(v)=(a+bv)/(c-dv).                                (3)

These statements follow by substitution and induction. Over an
algebraically closed K every nonzero rational r is generated by the
linear-factor ratios in(3); over a general K these ratios generate only
the corresponding split-factor subgroup. No arbitrary factorization over
K is assumed.

**Source-return classification.** Among all these words, a map carrying
some G_H to some G_J is either the identity or one of the already supplied
F_(a,b). In the latter case both corrections must be affine-linear in
(p,y), with b changing sign and the additive constant unchanged.
This conclusion allows *arbitrary polynomial corrections* at the endpoints.

**Proof.** Write uniquely

    H(p,y)=sum_(d>=0) p^d h_d(v),     deg h_d<=d,

using y=vp. Under(1), the coefficient of t^-1 in G_H composed with T_r
is -v^2/(2r); no polynomial correction contributes a negative t power.
The required source coefficient is -v^2/2. Thus r=1, and the map is
the identity.

Under(2), the largest d with h_d nonzero gives the negative term
h_d(-v)R(v)^d t^-d. No lower degree or the transformed u term cancels it.
A target G_J has pole order exactly1, so d must equal1; d=0 would give
no t^-1 term. Thus H=c+ap+by. Matching the coefficients of t^-1, t,
and1 forces R=-v^2/[2(a-bv)] and J=c+ap-by. This is exactly the stated
F, with source and target labels in the appropriate order. QED.

Consequently a long compatible word inside this correction family cannot
produce an undiscovered source return. Nontrivial words can still reduce
to the same map; this is a classification of maps, not unique spellings.

## T2. A larger polynomial carrier makes factor transfers useful

Keep the logarithmic chart but enlarge to

    H_(P,Q,R)(v,t)=P(v)/t+Q(v)t+R(v),
    P,Q,R in K[v],             v divides P.             (4)

The divisibility is exactly what makes the expression polynomial in x,t.
Under T_r the coefficient triple changes by

    (P,Q,R) -> (P/r,Qr,R).                              (5)

For coprime A,B with r=A/B, the return remains polynomial in x,t **iff**

    A divides P,   B divides Q,   v divides PB/A.        (6)

Distinct Laurent powers of t cannot cancel a coefficient's denominator.
After(6), the only remaining negative power at v=0 comes from the constant
term of PB/A, proving necessity and sufficiency. The retained invariants
are PQ and R. They determine the same generic discriminant

    (R(v)-E)^2-4P(v)Q(v).                               (7)

This is a factor-allocation gadget, not a free simplification of an
arbitrary polynomial source. All poles and the final divisibility at0
are part of its state.

A second admissible operation actually changes v:

    L_c(v,t)=(v+c,t),     L_c(x,t)=(x+c/t,t).             (8)

It is symplectic. Its pullback in(4) is polynomial exactly when P(c)=0.
Thus a root of P is a legal centre for changing the product coordinate.
Moving a factor from Q into P by(5) can expose a new such centre.

For b nonzero, apply this to the original source with
P=-v^2/2, Q=a+bv, R=v^2(a+bv). First choose r=1/(a+bv), then centre at
c=-a/b. The combined map and its actual polynomial pullback are

    B_(a,b)(x,t)=(b x^2t-a x, 1/(b x)),
    G_(a,b) composed with B_(a,b)
      =t+b x(t-1/2)(xt-a/b)^2.                          (9)

Its Jacobian is1, its product coordinate is v-a/b, and its inverse is

    (X,T) -> (1/(bT), b X T^2+aT).

For a nonzero this escapes the signed-v normal forms in T1. The map is
birational with a pole, while its displayed Hamiltonian pullback is a
polynomial. Those are different regularity statements. Generic genus2
is retained from the original b-nonzero source by this birational transfer.

## T3. The persistent critical fibre explains why this reroute stops

The original coefficients satisfy R=-2PQ. Factor transfers and root-centred
translations preserve that relation. On the entire resulting carrier,

    1-2H=(1-2P/t)(1-2Qt).                               (10)

If PQ is nonconstant, over an algebraic closure choose v_0 with
P(v_0)Q(v_0)=1/4. Since v divides P, v_0 is nonzero; set t_0=2P(v_0),
which is also nonzero. Both factors in(10) vanish there, so dH=0.
The (v,t) chart is regular there. Hence every polynomial obtained by
these operations has an actual affine critical point and **cannot have
a polynomial Keller complement**.

For the original family the factorization reads

    1-2G_(a,b)=(1+x^2t)(1-2(a+bxt)t).                   (11)

In fact all G_H, even with nonlinear polynomial H, already have a simpler
critical-point test on t=0:

    G_H,x(x,0)=0,     G_H,t(x,0)=H_p(0,0)-x^2/2.        (12)

There is a root over the algebraic closure. This observation does not
invalidate their role as auxiliary source-normal Hamiltonians. It does
show that the earlier conditional suggestion “transport a Keller
complement if supplied” is vacuous for these particular polynomials.
The coefficient matching and source-flow questions are still legitimate;
identifying G_H itself with a candidate global Keller coordinate is not.

The stronger obstruction(10) survives the new polynomial models even
after their critical points on t=0 have moved away. Merely observing that
one boundary now has no critical point would miss the other fibre.

## T4. Break the factor relation, then test a different obstruction

**Inheritance update.** The entire monomial example below is already
excluded by the stronger
[THM-3716, monomial Broughton obstruction](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3716-monomial-broughton-hamiltonian-obstruction-family.md)
for all x+x^m t^n, m>=2,n>=1. The more general
[THM-3418, one-monomial nonlinear-fibre classification](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3418-one-monomial-nonlinear-fiber-keller-classification.md)
already treats f(x)+g(x)t^d. Its short weight computation below is a
control in the present coordinates, not a new exclusion theorem.

The enlarged carrier contains the explicit polynomial

    H_*=x+x^4t^5,
    (P,Q,R)=(v,v^4,0).                                 (13)

It is a genuine submersion: H_*,t=5x^4t^4, and at either x=0 or t=0
the derivative H_*,x equals1. Its generic fibre has discriminant
E^2-4v^5, a squarefree quintic over K(E), so has genus2.
This is a control showing that the old critical-fibre obstruction can be
escaped while retaining genus2; no novelty is claimed for the general
monomial-perturbation family.

This particular escape still has no polynomial Keller complement, for a
different exact reason. Assign weights wt(x)=5, wt(t)=-3. Then H_* has
weight5. If {H_*,J}=1, the weight-minus3 component of J must itself supply
the constant bracket, because bracketing with H_* raises weight by3.
Every monomial of weight-minus3 is t Z^k, k>=0, with Z=x^3t^5. Thus that
component is t A(Z), and its bracket is

    (1+4Z)A(Z)+5Z(1+Z)A'(Z).                           (14)

For a nonzero polynomial A of degree d, the coefficient of Z^(d+1)
is(5d+4) times its leading coefficient, and cannot vanish. Equation(14)
therefore cannot equal1. Other weight components cannot cancel this
weight-zero obstruction. QED.

This is the useful two-stage hostile: absence of critical points is
necessary but insufficient for a Keller complement. Generic genus alone
decides neither issue. The stronger recovered classification below rules
out the entire proposed enlarged carrier, not just this monomial boundary.

## T5. The archive already closes the whole three-charge carrier

Write P(v)=v A(v), Q(v)=B(v), R(v)=chi(v). Then(4) is exactly

    x A(xt)+t B(xt)+chi(xt).                            (15)

**THM-3772**, [variable-flank three-charge rational-exact classification](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3772-variable-flank-three-charge-rational-exact-classification.md),
already proves that a smooth polynomial of(15) has a polynomial
constant-Jacobian mate **iff it is linear**. This strictly subsumes the
candidate-component question suggested by T2, even when R is independent.
The two-charge predecessor is
[THM-3741, radial two-charge classification](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3741-radial-two-charge-keller-component-classification.md).
The intervening positive construction
[THM-3757, Pell--Chebyshev hyperelliptic tower](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3757-pell-chebyshev-three-charge-hyperelliptic-obstruction-tower.md)
already supplies arbitrarily deep smooth three-charge examples and excludes
even their rational mates. We do not reopen that tower by changing names.

The key retained datum is stronger than genus. Put

    Y=P(v)/t-Q(v)t,    D(v)=(E-R(v))^2-4P(v)Q(v).

Then {H,v}=Y, the generic fibre is Y^2=D(v), and the mate equation
{H,J}=1 forces

    dJ=dv/Y                                           (16)

on that fibre. If D is squarefree of degree at least3, dv/Y is a nonzero
holomorphic differential on the smooth projective hyperelliptic model.
It cannot be the derivative of a rational function: a pole of that
function would give a pole of its derivative, whereas a globally regular
function on a projective integral curve is constant. This is the
inherited generic-time-form obstruction, with sign matched to our bracket.

For G_(a,b), b!=0, let R=v^2(a+bv). Then

    D=(E-R)^2+2R,       D'=2(R+1-E)R'.                  (17)

D is squarefree over the generic energy field. A zero of R' is constant
over the algebraically closed coefficient field and cannot also make D
vanish for transcendental E. A zero with R+1-E=0 would make D=2E-1,
again nonzero. Its degree is6, so(16) is holomorphic of genus2. Therefore
**the original genus-two G_(a,b) has no rational constant-Jacobian mate**,
not merely no polynomial mate due to an affine critical point. The same
argument applies to the quintic control in T4.

Moreover this obstruction survives *every birational symplectic change*,
not only the words in T1 or the operations in T2. If H composed with F
had a rational mate J, then J composed with F^-1 would be a rational
mate for H, by the Jacobian chain rule. Equivalently a birational map
identifies the smooth projective generic curves and transports their
time differentials. Thus compositions cannot turn these particular
genus-two Hamiltonians into Keller coordinates, however complicated the
word. They remain valid auxiliary source-normal objects.

A genuinely different candidate target must leave the three-charge
carrier and, when its generic fibre has positive genus, change the time
form so that it is not a nonzero holomorphic differential. Meromorphicity
alone is insufficient: residues and all vertical principal parts still
need their inherited exactness tests. This is a precise change of
mathematical target, not a claim that the next carrier will succeed.
