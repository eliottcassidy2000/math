# Recovery map and a composition program

## Inheritance before synthesis

The **anchor** is the user's exact attachment/transport problem. The **niche**
is the Lucas divisor clock and its prime-value boundary. The **wildcard** is
Ramanujan's singular values and the Gaussian allocation model. LRC(14)
remains the repository's open anchor; the transfer here concerns exact
return operators and joint observations, not a new lonely-runner bound.

The closest proved mechanisms are the
[joint-port reconstruction](../joint_port_germs_20261028/README.md),
[closure under transitions and products](../session92_joint_order_carriers_20261029/algebra/JOINT_CLOSURE_ALGEBRA.md),
and [odd square-root transport](../session90_even_graphs_odd_descent_20261029/carriers/TRANSPORT_AND_HOLONOMY.md).
The canonical hostile remains the actual twelve-vertex 81/27 pair, not a
synthetic pair of groups with those orders. The corrected near miss is
equating a marginal count or spectrum with a composition carrier. The
least-used arithmetic sidecar is the modular function's level/multiplier,
alongside the older norm and selected-embedding data.

The six live concepts are: local fibers; relative frames; joint incidence;
loop holonomy; realization constraints; and arithmetic height. Every claimed
connection below is tested against all six. A count can preserve the first
while losing the next four; a finite congruence observer can preserve all its
declared predicates while losing the height-dependent primality target.

- The same packets classify the odd prime-Lucas reflection closure:
  for prime p=L_n, odd n>=7, it is S_p when n=11 modulo 12 and A_p otherwise.
  Their all-size proof credits primitive-group classification; short finite

  This is conditional on the value L_n being prime, not a prime-value test.
- [The endpoint/CM packet](../cursor_cm_phase_20261028/README.md) supplies the

## Four discovery waves, followed by targeted recovery

[recover.py](recover.py) records four differently seeded searches and ten
historical provenance samples per wave. [recovery.json](recovery.json) stores
the complete matched path lists, query expressions and sampled excerpts.
The waves cover quadratic/modular objects; Lucas/Pell/prime clocks;
equidecomposition and transport; and the exact constants in the quotation.
Thousands of file matches are discovery results, not thousands of audited
theorems. The sampled excerpts were inspected, and the sources listed below
were then read selectively around their actual mechanism. The search does
not claim an exhaustive review of every month or every hit.

### 1. Golden arithmetic supplies the scalable replacement for PSL(2,11)

[Golden prime clocks](../golden_prime_clocks_20261003.md) already retains
the two coordinates of Z[phi], conjugation, norm, prime-power lifting and
CRT compatibility. The new P2 observer takes the projective ratio phi/psi,
whose order is exactly the Lucas zero clock. In inert fields Frobenius
inverts this ratio. In the normal-basis tournament lane Frobenius rotates
binary decorations. These are two specified actions with analogous phase
bookkeeping, not an asserted isomorphism between their objects.

The [cat-map trace theorem THM-4763](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4763-the-cat-map-on-the-logarithmic-side-lucas-traces-and-spectral-radius-phi-squared.md)
provides the archimedean companion: the matrix M^2 has trace 3 and eigenvalues
phi^2,phi^(-2). Its powers yield L_(2n), while reduction modulo primes gives
finite clocks. The map preserves traces and powers; finite reduction loses
growth and height. This is why the same small matrix can have an elementary
formula and still require growing arithmetic certificates.

The [Lucas mirror packet](../lucas_mirror_gap_spaces_20261028/LUCAS_MIRRORS.md)
supplies the literal eleven-point biplane frames and the period-seven A29
control. P2's fixed-point calculation is the cheapest decisive test of a
proposed identification: 6/x and M^5 are not even in the same PGL conjugacy
class over F11. The scalable replacement is a family of split/nonsplit
tori, exact orders, valuations and index constraints. It does not predict
Lucas-prime density merely from quadratic splitting.

P2c adds a useful change of perspective. Candidate Lucas values are pairwise
coprime, so their prime supports are disjoint. A prime sieve is not repeatedly
filtering one stationary population: after the index gate, every fixed prime
has at most one opportunity to appear. The source-to-target map is index n
to the support of L_n; the preserved predicate is divisibility, while the
distribution of new factors is the missing information. This suggests
organizing future data by first-hit order and factor size, not only residues.

### 2. Ramanujan's constants recover three separate arithmetic mechanisms

The [reciprocal-power theorem](../session27_repunits_lattices_20261022/session27-recurrence/RECIPROCAL_POWER_THEOREM.md)
already compresses power observables into recurrences. P1 and M1 identify
the missing ingredient for *joint* multiplication: which conjugate of one
factor is aligned with the other. A short recurrence for each factor alone
does not determine the product trace. The 3,7 example separates 18 from 3.

The [half-period CM logarithm note](../halfperiod_twin_classes_cm_log_law_20261018.md)
contains a genuine reciprocal parameter m<->4096/m and classical cited CM
logarithm identities, with its earlier novelty claim corrected. Here 4096
arises concretely as 64^2 when inverting the q-product. The same reciprocal
structure is relevant, but the maps are typed differently: its isogenies
stay in one imaginary quadratic field, while n->2n in the present quotation
changes that field. A shared constant is not enough to identify the maps.

The [ring-versus-ideal-class grids](../two_grids_ring_or_ideal_class_13_37_20261027.md)
and [idoneal information channels](../idoneal_information_channels_20261021/IDONEAL_INFORMATION_CHANNELS.md)
show why genus characters can miss composition. The genus map A->A/A^2
preserves quadratic characters, loses A^2, and needs a class selector when
that kernel is nontrivial. Our C8 and the inherited C2^3 both have eight
classes, but only the latter is fully distinguished by its genus quotient.
The raw Weber counterexample g18 adds a separate level/multiplier guard:
class number two does not ensure a tower of square roots for the chosen
function value. These are two different losses, not one.

### 3. The 65 allocation square links the quotation to Pythagorean paths

The [Gaussian allocation/path bijection](../endpoint_pythagorean_fibers_20261019.md)
uses ordered prime powers and chosen Gaussian prime factors. Its underlying
fiber is F2^r modulo simultaneous conjugation; endpoint-tournament Hamilton
addresses supply an explicit codec. The complement of an address is an
action on the path set, not a vertex automorphism of that tournament.

M2a makes this concrete for the very primes 5 and 13 in g130 and G65:
the two conjugation orbits give (33,56,65) and (16,63,65), and correspond to
the main and cross-conjugate real traces. The map preserves allocation bits
and their quotient, while losing actual lengths, field multiplication and
angular positivity. The Gaussian factors and real embeddings are retained
as separate sidecars. This is stronger than equinumerosity but weaker than
an isomorphism of the number fields.

The [minus-port square-sum atlas](../square_sum_minus_bounded_section_contacts_20261019.md)
has another actual reciprocal relation, v_minus(t)=1/u_plus(1/t), with the
chosen sheet essential. It maps prescribed contact equations, not arbitrary
integer intervals. Its older
[Berggren ancestry obstruction](../berggren_ap_square_nonregular_20261019.md)
proves that quartic positivity walls generate infinitely many distinct
residual cuts. This is an important counterpart to P2b: modular Lucas
divisibility has a finite minimum, but that real cone language does not.
The minimal-state question is useful beyond finite-state answers. A finite
automaton obstruction is not a Turing-undecidability or time lower bound.

### 4. Equidecomposition explains why separate invariants do not compose

The [framed Dehn profile](../session29_framed_profiles_20261022/session29-dehn/REPORT.md)
already gives the clean hostile A=diag(1,-1), B=-A. Their separate spectra
agree, while tr((A+A)^2)=8 and tr((A+B)^2)=0. The additive tensor composes
in a common frame; the rotation-invariant scalarization does not.

That is the same *loss mechanism* as reciprocal product traces and atom
alignments: source is framed data, target is a quotient by independent local
symmetries, preserved predicate is the marginal invariant, lost data is the
relative frame, and the decisive test is a two-input composition. It is not
a claim that tournament path counts are classical scissors volume or that
metagraph Betti numbers are Dehn invariants. The historical
[equinumerosity/scissors output](../equinum_equidecomp_bridge_s617.out)
is retained as idea provenance; the framed report supplies the typed repair.

For tournaments and Royle-even graphs, the existing
[odd fixed-point/Walsh theorem THM-4721](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4721-twisted-burnside-on-odd-fixed-point-sets-sylow-refinement-of-even-graphs-and-tournaments-components-walsh-basis.md)
and [tree-compression packet](../session90_even_graphs_odd_descent_20261029/trijection/TREE_COMPRESSION.md)
answer different questions: an equality of orbit counts, and an explicit
marked encoding. Neither alone preserves all unmarked stabilizers. The
81/27 replay enforces exactly that distinction; named transporters remain
27 versus zero even when the cyclic-triple data agree.

### 5. Return Gram and Vitali memory separate linear and joint information

The [native forbidden-flip operator](../session91_packing_golden_carriers_20261029/operators/GUARDED_FLIP_LEAKAGE.md)
and [gap topology](../lucas_mirror_gap_spaces_20261028/GAP_TOPOLOGY.md)
give the star's stable S0 homotopy and exponential Gram rank. P3 identifies
the full three-point spectrum. Homotopy forgets boundary incidence; a short
spectral recurrence forgets how arbitrary sources occupy its eigenspaces.
Neither controls the dimension of an exact Gram factorization.

The [Vitali/strong-atom continuation packet](../strong_atom_continuation_vitali_20261026/README.md)
supplies the alignment-conjunction warning. The concurrent
[folded-observer calculation](../session92_joint_order_carriers_20261029/observers/FOLDED_OBSERVER_COSTS.md)
makes the failure explicit: closing two marginal tests and intersecting
their partitions need not close their joint test. Its native quadratic
observation and 16/35/64 costs are not identified with the star spectrum.
They explain why a useful general theory needs pointwise products as well
as linear responses and operation closure.

## A proposed unifying structure: composition before independent quotienting

Keep three layers distinct:

1. **Realizations and joint incidence:** which ports, words, classes or
   conjugates coexist in one object, with multiplicities where needed.
2. **Transport:** how a change of marking acts on the retained observations,
   including stabilizers and the action of closed transport loops.
3. **Realization guards:** consistency equations, admissible domains, norm,
   modular level, source height, and target-specific inequalities.

P6 is a precise theorem for the free-frame part. When G acts freely, a tree
stores all relative frames in |G|^(k-1) states. Known relations can reduce
this, exactly as shared CRT phases reduce a clock product to an LCM.
When the action has stabilizers, retain actual transporter incidence.
When natural transport has curvature, retain its observable holonomy or
explicitly choose a different transport rule. When constraints are non-graphic,
do not force them into graph cycles: the
[arithmetic square-class/Fano packet](../unifying_binary_dependence_free_bits_7_15_56_20261028/README.md)
already marks that boundary.

This proposal is broader than a decorated tree but more concrete than
"everything is a graph." It does not assert a universal finite carrier.
For each declared operation menu, the proposed minimum is the quotient of
realizations by agreement under all allowed continuations, with transport
and symmetry weights retained when those are observable. Determining a
finite presentation of that quotient is the substantive problem.

## Next problems and cheapest decisive probes

**O1 — Residual holonomy beyond the recursive family. OPEN.** For general
strong atoms and specified attachment menus, compute the image of the loop
group on joint responses and compare it with the folded counting kernel.
The recursive C3 tuple problem is solved by the credited folded-LCA theorem;
it already has the two-port hostile (3,6) versus (3,8) at order nine.
The next probe must therefore use another atom or compose with an actual
adjacency-changing complement. The twelve-vertex 81/27 pair must retain
unequal transporters. Test whether a proposed quotient survives that operation,
not merely whether it handles every loop in a fixed underlying tournament.

**O2 — First-hit factor atlas for Lucas candidates. OPEN research target.**
Organize divisors by exact order, split type, valuation and size relative to
L_n. Compare proof-producing modular-exponent/order tests with direct trial
sieving on prime and dyadic indices. The fixed-bank approach has the proved
stopping reason P2c; new work must grow the bank or use a distinct primality
certificate. Infinitely many Lucas prime values remains open, and BHV's
primitive-divisor theorem is not a substitute for it.

**O3 — The smallest modular-function selector. OPEN.** For the six doubled
radicands, compute the actual Weber multiplier action and specify the
normalizing power whose orbit is governed by the class group. Test g18 as
the hostile and retain the C4/C8 genus fibers. Separately certify upper
bounds on every unwanted conjugate in an integer-trace approximation.
The 130 example prevents accepting radical length as an error bound.

**O4 — Flat parts of attachment observers. OPEN beyond the known quotients.**
The credited blue-presentation quotient already has path-independent marking
transport, while a named full path does not. Classify additional attachment
menus that factor through holonomy or require a phase register after native
operations. Compare them with eigen-observers of the star Gram. Five vertex
cells, three spectral values, and exponentially many Gram directions are
different minima; the target must be declared before optimizing any of them.

**O5 — Shared guards beyond graphic dependence. OPEN.** Extend P6's free bits
to relation modules or finite constraint algebras, retaining a certificate
that the joint data are realizable. Use the regular and Fano arithmetic
examples to test where an LCA/tree implementation ceases to be sufficient.
For square-sum contacts keep the positivity sheet and label scale; for
graceful exchanges keep the prescribed shape/label target. No major graph
or arithmetic conjecture follows just by matching the carriers' sizes.

The four search waves did not establish a transfer from these results to
Collatz termination, Jacobian injectivity, Hadwiger–Nelson, or Lucas-prime
infinitude. Those are specific missing maps or predicates, not evidence
against the narrower exact bridges above.
