# Overnight tandem: independent responses and exact Moore contacts

2026-11-26. This is the landing page for the proofs and replays. Chan–Pak and the degree-57 Moore existence question remain open.

## Chan–Pak

- [Two disjoint physical edits](COMMUTING-UNIT-FORKS.md) give strongly connected four-corner counts $(N,N-2,N+2,N)$ at $\Theta(\sqrt X)$ distinct central heights up to $X$ in one explicit family. For the smallest displayed example the four counts are $557,555,559,557$. The second edge is independent because the first changes only the rooted endpoint count.
- [Prescribed responses](../independent_responses_and_two_holes_20261126/README.md) extend the two amplitudes beyond a pair of units. An endpoint-weight binary expansion realizes any first even amplitude; a second amplitude comes from an exact congruence. In particular both $(2g,2)$ for $1\le g\le512$ and $(2,2m)$ for $1\le m<15{,}828{,}366{,}895$ have literal disjoint-arc constructions. This does not prescribe the center.
- [Fibonacci grammar](UNIQUE-TERMINAL-GRAMMAR.md) classifies all rooted tournaments with a unique Hamilton path ending at their root. [Nested inverse](NESTED-FORK-INVERSE.md) and the [binary cover polynomial](BINARY-COVERS-AND-MOORE-PARITY.md) give exact negative exponent-61 results in the specified families, including 2,097,151 positive even binary words through order 23 and arbitrary ordered incoming prefixes. There is no overall exponent-61 conclusion.

## Degree-57 Moore graph

- [Pooled capacity and the mod-four exclusion](POOLED-QUARTET-CAPACITY.md) force **at least 129 changed whole branch matchings** between any two distinct hypothetical full completions of the actual three frozen stars. The pairing argument at 129 and [the parity replay](BINARY-COVERS-AND-MOORE-PARITY.md) leave **82 necessary tuples**, none known to lift to actual matchings.
- [Two unfilled branches](TIGHT-SHEET-SYNCHRONIZATION.md) are a separate partial-board problem. With the 50 reference contacts fixed, all 1,056 pairs put at least three squares on the board. All six two-square allocations are excluded through 11 reference edits, and the three concentrated allocations are excluded through 12. [Follow-up](../independent_responses_and_two_holes_20261126/README.md#4-the-two-unfilled-moore-branches-independent-repairs-stop-at-three) shows exactly why independent motions of both holes can reach three squares but cannot go below it while the other 50 contacts stay fixed.
- One reason coarse feasibility does not suffice: in an eliminated Moore case every one of 45 local fixed points has a [single seven-pair transport orbit](HALL-AND-PAIR-ORBITS.md). One cannot select the four or five pairs required by the exact count. The analogous need on the Chan–Pak side is to choose a particular absolute height, not simply obtain the right modular response.
