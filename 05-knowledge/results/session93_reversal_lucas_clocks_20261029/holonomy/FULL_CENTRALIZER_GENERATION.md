# A minimal triangle basis for the full recursive retained symmetry

**Status: PROVED**, with independent explicit-permutation checks through 243
vertices. Scope: the recursive family W_k=C3[W_(k−1)^3], W_0=1, and the
pair-local odd-square-root transport. This is not a claim about every
self-converse tournament, nor about every choice of flat transport.

Inherited sources: Session90 `carriers/ITERATED_TRIANGLE_FAMILY.md` and

`05-knowledge/results/session90_even_graphs_odd_descent_20261029/`.
These prove the full automorphism wreath recursion, the centralizer *order*,
and nonflatness; this note strengthens nonflatness to exact generation.

## 1. Statement

Let alpha_k negate all ternary digits. Let A_k=Aut(W_k) and
C_k=C_(A_k)(alpha_k). Define g(t,s)=sqrt(ts), and for anti-involutions s,t,u

    h(u,t,s)=g(u,s)^(-1) g(u,t) g(t,s).

All permutations compose right to left. Then:

1. The subgroup generated by all triangles based at alpha_k is **exactly C_k**.
2. An explicit set of **k(k−1)/2 individual based triangles** generates it.
3. This number is minimal, even among arbitrary group generators of C_k.
4. The same statement holds at every other anti-involution by conjugation.

In particular the result is generation, not merely normal generation, and
not merely generation of the commutator subgroup.

## 2. Coordinates and the centralizer as a group

Use ternary words, with top blocks 0,1,2. The inherited intrinsic-module
argument gives A_k=A_(k−1)^3 semidirect C3. Put B=A_(k−1), beta=alpha_(k−1).
An element centralizing alpha_k has trivial top rotation, since reflection
inverts the top C3. Its block coordinates are exactly

    (c,b,beta b beta),       c in C_(k−1), b in B.

Consequently this is a group isomorphism, not just an order identity:

    C_k ≅ C_(k−1) × A_(k−1) ≅ product_(j=0)^(k−1) A_j.

The factor embeddings have disjoint supports and commute. The paired factor
is a copy of B via psi(b)=(1,b,beta b beta).

## 3. Canonical automorphism generators

For 0≤j<k, let v_(k,j) rotate digit j by +1 precisely when all earlier digits
are zero, leaving all other digits unchanged. These have order three and

    alpha_k v_(k,j) alpha_k = v_(k,j)^(-1).

They generate A_k. Inductively, v_(k,0) is the top rotation; the remaining
ones generate the full child-0 group. Conjugating by the top rotation gives
the independent child-1 and child-2 groups. Thus all of the wreath product is
generated. Only k generators are required, although its order is
3^((3^k−1)/2).

## 4. Each paired generator is one literal triangle

Fix one canonical generator v=v_(k−1,j) of B. Write r for the top rotation
and y=(v,1,1), and put x=r. Both x and y are inverted by alpha=alpha_k.
Thus the following are anti-involutions:

    s=alpha,       t=x² alpha,       u=y² alpha.

Their rooted transports satisfy g(t,s)=x and g(u,s)=y. Since x has order3,

    z=ut=y² x^(-2)=y²r.

It has order9: z³=(v²,v²,v²). Therefore sqrt(z)=z^5. Direct block calculation
then gives

    h(u,t,s)=y^(-1) z^5 r=(1,v,v^(-1))=psi(v).

For clarity, z maps (i,a) to (i+1,a), applying v² if the new block is 0.
Five steps have target-block shift 2 and source-block actions (v²,v,v)
on starting blocks 0,1,2 respectively. Composing with r on the right and
y^(-1) on the left leaves block actions (1,v,v^(-1)). This also fixes the composition convention.

The k−1 such triangles generate the whole paired-child B factor.
For the child-0 C_(k−1) factor, inherit the previous-stage triangles: extend
each of their marks by alpha outside child0. Their transports and holonomies
are the corresponding child maps extended by identity, because products of
marks cancel outside child0. Hence they generate exactly (C_(k−1),1,1).

Induction proves all four claims except minimality. In particular it proves
full generation without enumerating any large automorphism group.

## 5. Minimality, including a concrete lower-bound invariant

Every tree automorphism is specified by local C3 shifts at internal ternary
tree nodes. For each depth d, sum these shifts modulo3 over all nodes at that
depth. Under composition, the nodes are merely permuted, so each level sum
is a group homomorphism. Together they give

    A_j -> (C3)^j,

with the canonical j generators mapping to the standard basis. This map is
surjective. Conversely A_j is generated by these j elements of order3, so its
abelianization is exactly (C3)^j. From the direct product decomposition,

    C_k^ab ≅ (C3)^(1+2+...+(k−1)).

Every generating set therefore has at least k(k−1)/2 elements. The triangle
basis constructed above attains this bound. This distinguishes a compact
*generating prescription* from a compact encoding of an arbitrary alignment:

    log_3 |C_k| = (3^k−2k−1)/4,
    d(C_k)     = k(k−1)/2.

An arbitrary centralizer element still has exponentially many possible
coordinates in k; a short list of generators does not give those coordinates
for free.

## 6. Port and marked-path consequences

Every retained centralizer alignment can be realized by a concatenation of
these based triangle loops. Thus forgetting all their errors discards the
entire C_k-action, rather than a small accidental cyclic subgroup. This is
an action-relative statement: a particular port can have a nontrivial
stabilizer and need not distinguish all centralizer elements.

For example, C_k has exactly 2k+1 vertex orbits: the zero word, and for each
possible position of the first nonzero digit, the two possibilities 1 and 2.
Every suffix after that digit is transitive under the relevant A-factor.
The sizes are 1 and, for each j=0,...,k−1, two copies of 3^(k−j−1).
Thus there are exactly three globally fixed vertices when k≥1. Ordered
multi-port data are not determined by these individual vertex orbits.

Let P_alpha be the set of Hamilton paths fixed by reversal followed by
alpha. A centralizer element preserves P_alpha. Its action on full ordered
paths is free, since a permutation fixing every position of a spanning path
fixes every vertex. Hence, whenever this path set is used, triangle-loop
orbits are exactly the C_k-orbits and all have size |C_k|. Combined with the
inherited coprime fixed-orbit descent, these are the marked representatives
of the blue ordinary-path orbits. This does **not** say that a triangle is a
black or blue metagraph edge, or that it changes any edge color. It operates
inside the relabelling/marking fibre. Native metagraph moves require their
separate definition and hypotheses.

## 7. Loss ledger and hostile boundaries

- Source: a named marked tournament and exact pair-local transport paths.
- Coarse target: mark endpoints with centralizer return errors forgotten.
- Preserved: tournament, base mark, and endpoint conjugacy condition.
- Lost: the C_k-valued return permutation, including joint named-port action.
- Repair: retain that permutation (or exact recursive group coordinates).
- Hostile: W_2 already realizes the nonidentity return
  `(0,1,2,4,5,3,8,6,7)` and moves port 3 to 4.
- Distinct hostile: the 12-vertex 81/27 same-cyclic-triples pair is not
  identified with W_2; no conclusion transfers solely from the number81.

Global-base transport f(t,s)=b(t)b(s)^(-1) remains flat. This theorem says the
pair-local square-root connection has full retained holonomy, not that flat
transport is impossible. For comparison, the inherited Paley7 example has
nontrivial C of order3 but flat pair-local transport; full generation is not
universal.
