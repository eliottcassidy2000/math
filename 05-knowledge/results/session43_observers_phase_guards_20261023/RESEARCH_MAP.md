# Session43 research map

1. Existence of a Hamiltonian path supplies an observer, not a unique observer or an unchanged measure. Analyze (T,P), its automorphism fibre, and the observer-conditioned incidence matrix separately.
2. A coarse observation and conditioning on its value are different operations. Preserve the stationary measure in spectral statements; retain common marked information rather than assuming ergodicity.
3. A finite phase is exact below an authenticated charge bandwidth. Powers increase that bandwidth; no fixed modular guard substitutes for unbounded integer grading.
4. Prime valuation data and composite-base divisor heights are related but distinct. Test whether a proposed quotient respects the actual transition; retain shared prime-exponent constraints.
5. A source Riemann sphere, a punctured covering, and a branch-order orbifold can have different geometry. A negative-real branch cut is a chart choice for a logarithm, not a singular seam in the complex plane itself.
6. Mod8 and mod9 must name their observables. Hamiltonian-count guards, forward-step evaluations, finite phase actions and Stern inverse-orbit residues are not interchangeable.

Positive repairs are preferred to mere rejection: a compatible observer bundle, a weighted-gap bound, a bounded-degree anti-aliasing theorem, a literal divisor-height extension, or a covering with explicitly added branches. None of these licenses transferring a conjecture without a structure-preserving map.
