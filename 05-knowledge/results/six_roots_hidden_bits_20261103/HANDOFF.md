   two-port E_n chart just enough to include its ear outputs, carrying the

   interface types stay bounded, even though arbitrary re-markings cannot?
   First tests are the actual195/197 outputs of E8, not abstract numbers.

   the desired ternary branches require a different attachment. The stated

   search, not a reason to assert an all-order impossibility.
3. **Projective operation test (OPEN).** Classify which path-set projective
   permutations preserve a specified endpoint or multi-path-cover response.
   All vertex-induced ones on E_n are trivial for n>=4. A richer path-level
   invariant or a chosen restricted family may survive without being a
   vertex action. Test the full incidence relation, not only orbit lengths.
4. **Square-sum transfer (OPEN).** Try source- and endpoint-preserving
   realizations of n→3n−35+j on the cofinal rank chart. Ordinary existence
   at the target is already proved; an actual extension preserving source
   edges is stronger. Existing native square-sum contexts can distinguish
   different boundary pairings, so such pairings cannot simply be erased.
