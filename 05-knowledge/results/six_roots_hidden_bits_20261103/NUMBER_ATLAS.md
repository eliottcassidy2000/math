## Synthesis: the common mechanism is a chosen quotient plus its boundary

The exact links found here retain different predicates: prime allocation,
path decoding, residue coverage, affine group action, source-preserving
growth, or rooted attachment response. They are not one unmarked numerical
isomorphism. The productive unifying question is:

> Can a particular family be encoded by an arithmetic clock and a bounded
> exceptional tag, with explicit transition rules for the operations we
> actually need?

There are positive examples: the golden-unit multiplicative carrier;
the even-period golden reader with a three-way tag at zero; the two
canonical E_n ports under native interior insertion. There are sharp
negative controls: all E_n marks need growing memory, fixed norm loses
the golden clock, zero-slot elimination cannot create a new adjacency,
and source normalization changes the path count. These determine where
the proposed theory should be strengthened rather than asserted.
