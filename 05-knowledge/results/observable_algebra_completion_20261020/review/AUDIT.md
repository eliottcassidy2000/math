## Proof findings

1. **Finite-state theorem is correct.** Total deterministic pullbacks preserve pointwise products. The algebra generated by all word-observables is therefore the least unital pullback-stable algebra containing the original observations. Its functions are constant on observational classes. Conversely, the displayed finite interpolation product is exactly each class indicator: the factor indexed by any other class vanishes there, while every factor equals one on the chosen class. These indicators span all functions on the quotient.

2. **The quotient and stopping condition are correct.** Observational equivalence is preserved by every chosen transition because future words can be prefixed by that transition. Any other observation-preserving transition quotient refines it. Successor-class refinement records all words through the current depth; one unchanged round proves stability. At most `|Omega|-1` rounds can strictly increase the number of classes. No extension to an infinite state space is claimed.

3. **State identification and law identification are properly separated.** W and A determine the same equivalence relation on individual states. A has additional linear coordinates that encode joint statistics over random initial states. Since `1 in W` and `dim A=q`, the exact number of additional arbitrary expectation coordinates is `q-dim W`. This follows by the earlier row-space argument applied to the quotient's q atoms; all quotient laws are available by assigning mass to representatives. Mean evolution closes on W, although arbitrary quotient-law recovery need not.

4. **The binary affine formula is correct under the stated hypothesis.** Initially observed characters are individually available. Pointwise products add their labels, and each affine pullback sends a label to `M_j^T a` with scalar factor `(-1)^(a dot b_j)`. The smallest additive transpose-stable label set is therefore L. Character independence gives real algebra dimension `|L|=2^r`; equality of their values is exactly membership of the state difference in `L-perp`. Invariance of `L-perp` under the original matrices proves well-defined quotient dynamics even for singular matrices. Affine shifts alter the signs and action, while leaving the state partition unchanged.

5. **The three dimensions and moment normalization are correct.** K includes zero and is closed under transpose words without additive closure, so the real linear statistic space has dimension `|K|`. Its binary label rank is r, and its product algebra has real dimension `2^r`. Fourier inversion divides by `2^r`, the number of quotient atoms, not by `2^n`. Full character PSD consequently has eigenvalues `2^r` times the quotient masses. The signed moment update is the expectation of the exact affine character-pullback identity.

6. **The cyclic-register hostile is decisive.** Three successive bit outputs identify all eight initial states. Their separate timewise means span only the constant plus three coordinate characters, a real space of dimension four. The even- and odd-parity laws each have four atoms, identical means at every time, and opposite triple-product moments. Three pair moments plus one triple moment fill the exact four-dimensional missing linear information. Multiplying separate means would not supply these products; the note explicitly requires measurements tied to the same initial random state.

7. **The Fourier-support caveat is necessary and present.** Under identity dynamics, a single observable `chi_e1+chi_e2` has only three values and generates a three-dimensional algebra. Treating its two labels as independently observed would falsely predict four classes. This reviewer raised that scope guard before the extension was frozen; the final note states the guard, supplies the hostile, and limits shift-independence to individually observed characters.
