# Validity gate and exact evidence

**PASS: S1-S3, with pairwise disjointness retained.** This is a same-session
logical review and independent-algorithm audit, not an external review.

## Types and proof directions

The objects are actual tournaments on fixed physical names. The quotient
orientation and the block partition are fixed in S1-S3. Two disjoint modules
see each other uniformly, so this includes every family of pairwise disjoint
modules, not merely a chosen construction syntax. Unselected vertices may
be singleton quotient vertices. Selected modules have at least two names;
reversing a singleton has no content and supplies no nonzero bit.

The two new ports always satisfy x->y. Each arbitrary query is a Boolean
attachment mask, and each response is its actual Hamilton-path count.
No isomorphism, port renaming, coefficient sorting, or response scaling
is used. A labelled self-converse isomorphism does not erase a reversal bit.

For exact S1, exterior block j stays a module after BOTH ports are added.
The complete ordered-cover profile, not its H coordinate alone, is fixed
by reversal. One can therefore change all exterior blocks sequentially
without affecting the chosen integer query. The target block plus ports
also forms a module in the lifted construction. Only then is T1 applied
to prove the residue of its nonzero difference. Its contracted quotient
is an actual tournament, hence has odd H. The sign does not matter modulo4.

The two-value formula proves separation between every pair of distinct
reversal vectors. It is not just a test against the all-zero vector.
The r fixed measurements recover r bits; no optimality claim is inferred
for unrestricted integer measurements. S3 requires equal complete q
profiles in every nuisance block and a modulo-four core separator in the
distinguished block. It supplies neither condition for arbitrary pairs.

three TT2 blocks; and Q=C3 with blocks TT2,C3,TT3. For each, both Q and

   retained cyclic three-pair cube, whose mixed a0*a2*c1*c4 coefficient
   is -2. The strongest survivor is exact independence on the lifted
   decoder queries. The missing coordinate is the mixed response term.
2. **Uniform quotient queries preserve a converse separator modulo4:**
   fails for C3[TT2,TT2,TT2] against C3^op[TT2,TT2,TT2]. All64 uniform

   *single-module* transfer cannot simply be applied to every inflated
   quotient vertex at once. The missing data are nonuniform block contacts.

   order-five source/triangle/sink hostile has identical quartics but
   quadratic and cubic separation31/29. Our complete tables keep all
   degrees, and no collision is inferred from a projected equality.

   order legally:** TT3 with modules{0,1},{1,2} refutes that premise.
   XOR reversal gives C3, with H changing1 to3. Nested/overlap semantics
