## 1. July: the response kernel is the correct gauge, not an arbitrary quotient

**THM-2230**, [planar Jacobian response fibre](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2230-planar-jacobian-response-fiber-and-exact-target-shear-quotient.md),
fixes P over a characteristic-zero field and assumes an actual mate B with
Jac(P,B) a nonzero constant. Its inherited centralizer input yields

    ker D_P=k[P],       D_P(Q)=Jac(P,Q),
    D_P^(-1)(R)=Q_R+k[P].

Thus target shears compute the *whole* response fibre. A degree-minimal
representative is not necessarily unique: the quartic degree14 locus still
has four lower shear parameters. A full Faber gauge selects one point only
after fixing the coordinate at infinity. The unit-response hypothesis is
essential; P=x² has a larger centralizer and no constant mate.

The transferred operation is to calculate the exact response kernel before
choosing a simplification. For tournaments the current and first-birth
observations are h and h−s+F. Their common kernel is exactly

    Delta=(0,v,w,v).

This is an elementary new calculation in a different category; no Keller
condition is transported. The old W_n root-position move has Delta s<0
and Delta F>0, so it cannot lie in this plane. That predicts its inability
to keep the next target fixed. Searching the actual plane instead finds
the two-arc pure-q point (0,0,2,0).

Source→target: a rooted tournament change→its current/next-count response.
Preserved: the two exact scalar targets. Lost: q and the common s/F shift.
Sidecar: actual adjacency rows and root, plus the subsequent R/N actions.
Decisive test: find two physical sources satisfying the kernel equations;
the finite order5 probe succeeds at H15 and first-child H33.

## 2. August: a quotient cycle still owes its connecting obstruction

**THM-3406**, [power jets and principal-part transgression](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3406-affine-modification-power-jets-and-principal-part-transgression.md),
studies P=f(x)+g(x)z, B=K[x,t], t=gz, R=B[t/g], S=R[g^-1], and the
additive principal-parts module Pi=S/R. Its exact coefficient test is

    {a in B : a/g^q in R}=(g,t)^q,
    Q_q=B/(g,t)^q,       Q_q→Q_(q+1):[a]→[ga].

The mixed generator gt at depth2 cannot be discarded. Nor can the filtered
module be replaced by its associated graded: the reduced support and its
multiplicity thickness form a generally nonsplit extension.

The particularly useful correction concerns a localized primitive h with
D(h)=m in R. The quotient class eta=[h] satisfies Dbar(eta)=0. It does
*not* map to the obstruction [m] by Dbar. The connecting homomorphism
delta([h])=[D(h)] in the two-term localization sequence performs that step.

Our finite-dimensional counterpart is explicitly different: a source
difference in the kernel of a current readout need not stay in that kernel
under the next physical move. The exact one-birth fibre above has

    Delta H_RR=2(v−w),       Delta H_NR=2v.

Both next responses vanish only at the zero profile difference. The
one-step-hidden repair therefore has a named continuation obstruction,
not an unspecified future effect. The two-arc witness is invisible at
H15 and H33, but a subsequent R/N birth after its first R reveals it.

Source→target: a current-neutral physical change→its response under a
chosen next command. Preserved: the already checked target. Lost by
scalarization: which kernel class was used. Sidecar: the linear state
maps and the literal marked source. Test: a pure deletion change is
killed by N, retained invisibly once by R, and detected by the next birth.
This is a transfer of the lifting discipline, not an isomorphism of
tournament data with relative de Rham cohomology.

## 3. August: the same finite layers can have opposite continuation types

**THM-3477**, [moving-numerator Jordan–Prüfer response](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3477-jacobian-effectivity-moving-numerator-jordan-prufer-response-normal-form.md),
uses the affine modification

    E_H=K[s,v,Y]/(sY−vH(v)) inside K[s,h],
    v=sh,       Y=hH(v).

Its additive quotient has grade-r term K[v]/(H^r), but its operation is
the additional map [f]→[vf] to the preceding grade. At a nonzero root of
H, v is a unit and a moving numerator conjugates the map to truncation;
this yields persistent Prüfer arms. At the zero root, v is a uniformizer
and the response decomposes into finite Jordan bars. Merging a nonzero
root with zero changes the operation type, even when a multiplicity-only
summary looks similar. The theorem does not classify arbitrary Keller
charts or solve planar JC.

For our pure-q repair, the corresponding exact transition distinction is

    N e_q=0,
    R^j e_q=(2−2^j,1,1,1−2^j),       j≥1.

The full-count equality language is exactly
`{empty,R} union N{R,N}*`. N erases the repair, R hides it for one scalar
observation, and further R-initial continuation exposes a strict signed
change. That is more informative than merely reporting the nonzero kernel
dimension or an improved gcd. These finite matrices are not themselves
the old infinite Jordan/Prüfer modules.

## 4. June: a closed quotient pattern still needs a physical lift

The older [HYP-2892 unital/Hodge carrier](../../../hypotheses/HYP-2892-lrc14-unital-pair-frame-hodge-refinement.md)
is **EVIDENCE / proof-carrier refinement**, not an LRC theorem. Its concrete
S4 adjacent-transposition graph has24 vertices,36 edges, six square faces
and eight braid-hexagon faces, with one spherical relation. The smaller
octahedral carrier has eight triangular faces and one relation. The proposed
use is to retain local signed changes and face identities before applying
a quotient; it does not manufacture a valid certificate from a symmetric
picture.

[HYP-3066 cycle lifting](../../../hypotheses/HYP-3066-hodge-cycle-lifting-carrier-lrc14.md)
is likewise a **SYNTHESIS / proof-interface sidecar**. It asks for a map
from named finite certificates to residual classes, with an explicit
missing generator when surjectivity is unknown. This is the useful part
of the Hodge prompt here. No assertion about rational algebraic cycles
on a complex projective variety is used as a tournament lemma.

On the physical resource-permutation graph, each continuation count H_w
is a vertex function. Its edge differences are exact coboundaries and
sum to zero around every actual closed lifted walk. Forgetting the root
can collapse several physical states to one unmarked graph, while those
states have different H_w. The apparent loop in the quotient therefore
does not supply a neutral marked return. The retained mark is the lift.

The same warning appears in June's **THM-509**,
[baby-Hodge moment boundary](../../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-509-the-two-layer-baby-hodge-dichotomy.md):
its automatic skew-moment positivity is constant on cospectral classes.
The transfer used here is only that a coarse feasible invariant need not
produce the specified finite object. We do not import the source's broader
metaphorical language as a Hodge-conjecture theorem or a general exclusion
of all nonlinear realizability tests.
