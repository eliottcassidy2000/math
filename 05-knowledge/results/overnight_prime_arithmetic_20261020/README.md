### Fixed/moving cylinder obstructions and relative quadratic descent

The [cylinder-generator packet](../cylinder_generator_period_obstructions_20261020/README.md) proves two separate **entire-algebra generation** obstructions. For E_g=C[x,y^2,y g(x,y^2)] with g(x,0) a nonzero constant, generic unique embedding plus surviving collision fibers forces the node scalars to agree and descend. A coefficient identity then excludes a nonzero base Jacobian. The symmetry statement is classical; the packet supplies a trace/residue proof, not a priority claim.

Base saturation and the one-line injectivity theorem globalize any generic monic quadratic over C(x) to one over C[x], without assuming constant fiber delta or conductor leading coefficient. Combining this with the cylinder obstruction gives the **current PROVED bound:** if the map is noninvertible, its generic source-conductor degree is at least six.
