# Complete finite field transport with the source charge retained

**PROVED algebraic classification with FINITE-EXACT group certificates over
F7.** These are actions of actual source-prefix words on residue data.
They are not exact integer resets, arbitrary-modulus results, or a spectrum
completeness theorem. The integer projective obstruction in PROJECTIVE
holds simultaneously with the complete residue-action theorem below.

## F1 Field maps rather than only multiplicative phases

Use F49=F7[theta], theta^2=4theta+2, and

    alpha=4X+Y theta,   g=h-y.

The encoding of v mod 7 by (alpha,g) is invertible. Every macro acts by

    alpha' = a alpha+b alpha^7,
    g'     = ell(alpha)+epsilon g,                    (F1)

where a,b are in F49, ell is an F7-linear form, and epsilon is its nonzero
tau multiplier mod 7. The receipt gives all coefficients. For example:

| Macro | a | b |
|---|---|---|
|B|6+6 theta|4+5 theta|
|Q|theta|0|
|S|4+3 theta|2+4 theta|
|L2|1+2 theta|3 theta|
|L6|4+3 theta|3+5 theta|
|L45|1+3 theta|6+theta|
|L13|theta|6|

These identities are checked on every field element. They follow by
matching the images of 1 and theta, using theta^7=4-theta. Thus an arbitrary
macro need not be multiplication by a field element. The two linearized
coefficients are unique and compose by

    (a,b) o (c,d) = (ac+bd^7, ad+bc^7).

The charge form and epsilon must also be composed; dropping them changes
the operation. Q alone is the inherited 48-phase multiplication clock.

## F2 The units generated by B and Q

Write a joint map in split row coordinates as

    R(A,t,e) = [[A00,A01,t0],[A10,A11,t1],[0,0,e]].

For B and Q the charge column t is zero; their e values are -1 and 1.
The exact generated group is

    {R(A,0,e): A in GL2(F7), e in {1,-1}},             (F2)

of order 4032. A Python closure builds all positive word addresses, compares
the result with every invertible 2 by 2 matrix and both signs, and checks
closure. A separately implemented C++ enumeration agrees. This is a full
matrix-action certificate, stronger than a single-vector orbit calculation.
It credits the earlier mixed-prefix orbit results and does not redo their
prime-power or four-prime lifting theorems.

One positive word implementing Frobenius with g unchanged is

    B B Q B B B B B Q B Q.                            (F3)

It costs 36 added vertices. On residues it gives alpha->alpha^7, g->g.
On the inherited nonzero clock based at alpha0=theta^38 this induces
k->7k+36 mod 48. Its actual integer source operation is expansive, not an
involution; two repetitions are not an integer return merely because the
field action squares to the identity.

## F3 Singular field transport retains a transverse charge

The full B,Q,S monoid consists exactly of the units in F2 and

    rank A=1, t not in the column space of A, e=+/-1;
    A=0, t nonzero, e=+/-1.                           (F4)

Its cardinality is

    4032 + 384*42*2 + 48*2 = 36384.

Here 384=(7^2-1)^2/(7-1) is the number of rank-one 2 by 2 matrices.

**Necessity.** Let P be the plane g=0 and L the charge axis. B and Q
preserve P and L. S maps P to a plane containing L: its bulk has rank one
and its charge column is transverse. Once a word contains S, the image of
P therefore contains L, and every later letter maps L invertibly to itself.
That image cannot vanish. For rank-one bulk this is exactly the first
condition in F4, and for zero bulk it is exactly the second.

**Sufficiency.** The block-diagonal group F2 acts transitively on pairs
(rank-one A, transverse t), with either sign. This follows by choosing a
basis consisting of a nonzero column of A and t and then choosing the
nonzero row functional of A. The product S B S has

    A=0, t=(3,3), e=-1;
    alpha'=0, g'=3Y+3X-g.                            (F5)

The group F2 sends its nonzero t to every nonzero column and adjusts e.
This realizes all zero-bulk cases. The whole-universe C++ test checks this
classification against all 705894 possible maps R(A,t,e), e nonzero.

This is a concrete loss ledger: annihilating the field part by these three
letters still retains a nonzero source-dependent charge. The zero-field
state alone does not describe the operation.

## F4 One extra actual loop yields every charge-preserving linear action

Adding L2 gives the entire monoid

    M = {R(A,t,e): A any 2 by 2 matrix over F7,
                   t any column, e in F7*}.           (F6)

Equivalently every map in (F1), with arbitrary a,b,ell and nonzero epsilon,
has an actual positive source-word implementation. Its size is

    7^4 * 7^2 * 6 = 705894.

**Group proof.** L2 has invertible bulk, nonzero charge column (5,2),
and e=5. Cancel its bulk using F2 to obtain h=R(I,t,5), t nonzero.
Conjugating by R(-I,0,1) reverses t. Multiplying h by the inverse of that
conjugate produces a nonzero pure shear R(I,2t/5,1). Conjugation by GL2
produces every pure shear. Removing t from h gives R(I,0,5), and 5
generates F7*. Therefore the generated unit group is every R(A,t,e) with
A invertible, of order 2016*49*6=592704.

The inverses used in this group argument have positive-word realizations:
each invertible residue map is a finite permutation, so its inverse is a
nonnegative power. No inverse source operation is being granted.

The unit group on both sides of S now produces every rank-one A, arbitrary
t and e. Applying it to the zero-bulk example F5 gives every A=0 case.
The three rank layers have sizes 592704,112896,294. They exhaust M.

An independent complete BFS verifies every element of M using only B,Q,S,L2.
Every action has a word of at most 16 macros in that certificate, hence at
most 128 added vertices. This is a supplied uniform bound, not a minimum
vertex claim. Repeating Q^48 preserves the entire residue action, giving
infinitely many distinct actual source-word lifts of every map.

## F5 Why this does not repair the ternary source bank

Over F7 the action monoid is complete subject to retaining the nonzero
correction line. Over positive rational bulk directions the eight macro
images are disjoint and decode the entire word. Both statements are exact.
The residue quotient forgets the integer address and its scale. The
all-length obstruction in PROJECTIVE therefore survives even this full
finite-field transport capability.

This is a useful operational separation: field Frobenius and all its
linearized companions can be implemented, while a prescribed exact integer
child still needs a realizable source and a return to the induction domain.
