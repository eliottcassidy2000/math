## Where a full proof could now enter

The inherited [THM-4094, two-prime-lane completeness](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4094-hamiltonian-matching-deficit-and-two-prime-lane-completeness.md)
still reduces the full odd spectrum to the specified strong prime and 7p
lanes. The new results suggest three precise tests:

1. Construct an actual reset between source interfaces whose descendant
   supports jointly supply all needed branches, and prove return to the
   same induction domain at every size.
2. Use a correction-changing Q4 phase with a retained channel and prove
   a legal return, rather than appending more same-channel macros.
3. Give an invariant actual-source set for the carry equation
   u=3v+j tau+7kappa; the residue action monoid is now completely classified,
   so the missing step is the positive integer source fibre.

These are open source-existence problems. No new A000568 coefficient,
global H coverage ceiling, Collatz theorem, or Jacobian theorem is claimed.
