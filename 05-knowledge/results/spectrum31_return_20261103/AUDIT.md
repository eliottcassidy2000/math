## Deliberately retained boundaries

- The projective decoder has a finite **seed ray** and eight specified
  macros. Another positive seed direction or a channel-changing operation
  can violate its hypotheses. No all-source obstruction is inferred.
- The integer return theorem is about affine state identities. Equal
  scalar H alone is weaker, and residue-preserving repairs with transverse
  carries are outside that theorem.
- The three-probe inverse is sufficient. Its minimality on this sparse
  family is not claimed; the Smith calculation concerns its integer image.
- The family generating function counts distinct constructed rooted
  isomorphism classes, not all rooted tournaments or distinct H values.
- F49 transport includes linearized maps, not only multiplication; the
  field-zero case retains source-dependent charge. Positive source-word
  implementations of inverse residue maps are not integer inverses.
- The H31 obstruction covers every source state *generated by the two-port
  language*. It says nothing about all tournaments with H31.
- The old 81/27 hostile remains inherited. No conclusion about arbitrary
  automorphism groups or full metagraph equivalence is drawn from the three
  coordinates or their modular action.
