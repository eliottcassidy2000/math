## Connections that changed the search

The live board had six interacting coordinates: prescribed invariants, actual
coefficient/source realization, relative scales, integral content, observation
clock, and joint incidence. Each successful connection kept a different
subset; the failures identify the missing coordinate rather than merely a
bad numerical sample.

**Cluster geometry to coefficient inversion.** Boundary spikes suggested
controlling root-gap differences to realize arbitrary strict words. Inverting
the Newton ratios directly went further: it gives exact prescribed values and
ties. The free first ratio pays for root separation while preserving the root
sum. Formula `R_1=S^2(d-1)/(d(S^2-Q))` exposes exactly why the fixed second
moment stops the transfer to the current anchored model. This is a genuine
source construction and a precise boundary, not a sign census extrapolation.

**Coin charts to source operators and observation order.** Primitive denominator
content survives every polynomial chart that remains nonconstant modulo the
relevant prime; the [independent coin audit](continuing8_20260906_coin_rational_handoff_audit.md)
proves that extension. But the same chart `x -> x^2` fails the actual Student
intertwining equation. Frobenius remains injective on polynomials while hiding
an arbitrarily long coefficient prefix. Thus preserved nonvanishing, preserved
operator response and bounded detection time are three separate predicates.
The incoming complete unit-triangular source image makes the last failure
exact against every alternative truncated source, not just one proposed lift.

**Finite Mahler phases to a probability law.** The map sends a uniformly chosen
unit residue `b mod3^j` to `floor(log2(3^j/oddpart(b)))`. Counting each odd part's
possible powers of two gives the exact finite distribution and its limit
`(k+1)/2^(k+2)`. The declared uniform-phase measure is the bridge to the
two-head waiting distribution. It supplies no fair-extractor algorithm and
no probabilistic assertion about a selected Mahler orbit.

**Known forests to an unknown connected graph.** Each actual edge has an
all-translation lower bound conditioned on its two sheet margins. Every
actual spanning tree therefore has weight at least the minimum over possible
trees. This changes the quantifier in the inherited forest consumer and lets
all six edges contribute. It retains seven positional vertices and all126
profiles. A maximum tree over possible edges would assert unproved edges;
the minimum is the correct lower bound for the information available.

One further type check links the ballot and coin lanes: a rational function
of the index giving adjacent sequence ratios is not a rational ordinary
generating function. Catalan numbers provide the elementary control:
their adjacent ratios are rational, while their generating function satisfies
`C=1+zC^2` and has the nonsquare discriminant `1-4z`. Therefore the Fuss repair
does not contradict, or automatically enter, the rational-orientation theorem.
