## 2. Variable-depth guard theorem

    U_v(n)=(p n+b)/2^A,      p=3^len(v).

For the next odd step put P=3p and B=3b+2^A. Choose c0>=1 with

    2(P n0+B) <= 2^(A+c0) n0,

and require that the next actual valuation at the anchor is at least c0.

    h_j=U^(len(v)+1)(125^j)=(P 125^j+B)/2^(A+c) <=125^j/2.

v2(125^j-125^k)=2+v2(j-k). Since P is odd, divisibility of
P125^j+B by2^(A+c0) holds exactly on the displayed exponent class.

after its final letter. All intermediate values are positive; their
affine expressions increase with n. The chosen exponent class has j>=j0.
Writing D=2^(A+c0)-2P, the anchor inequality gives D>0 and D n0>=2B.
Consequently D125^j>=2B. Increasing c only strengthens the half-descent.
No distributional or convergence assumption occurs.
