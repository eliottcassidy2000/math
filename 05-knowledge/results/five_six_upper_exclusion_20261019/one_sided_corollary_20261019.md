# All one-sided maximum-span roots are excluded in seven-thin posets

Date: 2026-10-19. Proved morphology reduction; no full seven-thin balance theorem or novelty claim.

In a hypothetical seven-thin poset without a balanced pair, choose a maximum-index-span incomparable pair x<y in the strict >2/3 majority order. The pair is critical. It must have at least one lower exclusive neighbor and at least one upper exclusive neighbor.

Indeed, suppose it has no lower exclusive neighbor, so D(x)=D(y), and let m=|U(x)\U(y)| and c=|I(x)∩I(y)|. Then 1+m+c≤7 by the incomparability degree of y. If m≤3, use the [all-size one-sided-three-slave theorem](../one_sided_three_slave_aggregate_20261019.md), including its separate c=0 endpoint. If m=4, use the [four-upper theorem](../four_upper_exclusion_20261019/four_upper_exclusion_20261019.md), which handles c≤2. If m=5 or6, use the [five/six-upper theorem](five_six_upper_exclusion_20261019.md), which handles c≤1 or c=0 respectively. These exhaust m≤6. The case with no upper exclusive neighbor is order-dual.

Thus every remaining maximum-span critical-root morphology must be genuinely two-sided. This reduction does not settle those mixed morphologies and does not prove the full 1/3–2/3 conjecture for seven-thin posets.

The constituent proofs include their exact computational scope, independent replays, external classical background, and priority disclaimers. No originality claim is made for the general methods or for this combined corollary.
