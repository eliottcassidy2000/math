# Session 335: Two switches, three forest slots, and Pythagorean addresses

**Chan–Pak remains open.** The new results separate three different kinds of exactness: choosing a path, assigning a switch to that path, and realizing a prescribed ordinary count.

- [Inventory of the 2–3 axis](inventory/TWO_THREE_OPEN_PROBLEMS.md): **23 documented entries**, with related assertions and restrictions labelled rather than counted as independent conjectures. Includes both Collatz sheets, Mahler, Erdős's ternary digit problem, Furstenberg, Markov unicity, perfect cuboid and the fixed-branch prime hypotenuse question. A fixed prefix and suffix in base 3 can both be prescribed on a positive-density set of exponents of 2; the uninspected middle remains the true condition. Solved statements such as Catalan and ternary Goldbach are not counted.
- [Bare forest seam chart](FOREST_SWITCH_CHART.md): after hub deletion there are three slots, some possibly empty. If we subsequently forget the slot names, the missing choices are counted by two tiny permanents. Seven gluings of one bare forest force at least **23** total paths, a sharp all-order result. All three nonempty components use 16 binary gauge charts; the 12 possible orders land in at most ten of them.
- [Binary seven-sheet strip in a ternary bulk](SEVEN_SHEETS.md): an unbounded strong family with $14\,2^k$ paths selected by exact local seven-gluings, yet ordinary counts grow as $3^k$. This is a concrete instance of many witnesses becoming a vanishing share of the count.
- [Berggren and Price charts](TRIPLES_AND_FORESTS.md): the actual ordered eligibility lists inject into classical triple addresses. Two binary survival profiles in Price's ternary tree recover each free vertex's eligible slots. One profile or a scalar $2^\alpha3^\beta$ need not. The Berggren slope supplies a different exact backwards rank with a reflection at the middle branch.

Run **python replay_forest.py**, **python replay_seven_sheets.py**, and **python replay_price.py** in this directory. A fixed class can have a unique chart for each prescribed path and still have many paths at one chart or none at another. The remaining inverse must start at an absolute target and choose the seam data, rather than trying to recover the seam data from a lone smooth weight.
