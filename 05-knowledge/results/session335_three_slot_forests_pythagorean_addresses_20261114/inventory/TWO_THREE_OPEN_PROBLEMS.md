# Open problems on the 2–3 axis: a repository inventory

**Scope.** Snapshot research ledger, not a claim that every mention in the archive was exhausted or that names are independent. A **direct** item puts both 2 and 3 in the equation, digit system, or dynamical model. A **structural** item uses a documented binary/ternary bridge or rank change inside a restriction of a larger problem. A **neighbor** is explicitly weaker. An open problem having a numeral in its name is not enough. Historical source links point to the mined checkout (commit 1b91742ce). The October [earlier 2–3 synthesis](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce/05-knowledge/results/two_three_axis_20261019_numbers_fixed_points_relations.md) and [sibling dimension ladder](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce/05-knowledge/results/collatz_procgen_20260922_sibling_dimension_ladder.md) are the trailheads, not the last word.

## Neighbor literature and false positives

- General Pillai asks for finiteness of representations of a fixed gap by two perfect powers when bases vary; fixed bases 2 and 3 are a solved S-unit finiteness problem (this particular gap even has [Bennett’s exact multiplicity bounds](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-314X%2802%2900049-5)). The special unit gap 8/9 is even more explicit: Catalan–Mihăilescu and the elementary Størmer classification settle these. The historical “fixed-base Pillai conjecture” gloss is not a valid additional open problem.
- Hall's conjecture lives on the square/cube gap abs(y²−x³) and is closer than loose 2/3 numerology, but use the [Collatz irrationality/measures notes](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce/05-knowledge/results/collatz_irrationality_measures_20261014.md) as background, not as a reduction without a demonstrated bridge. 
- The solved Boolean Pythagorean triples problem really does combine two colors with three vertices per constraint; the cutoff 7825 is not open and does not license analogizing away endpoint labels. See [the repo's worked Pythagorean/Artin seed audit](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce/05-knowledge/results/collatz_artin_corrections_20260927.md).
- Primitive triplet enumeration by Berggren/Price, ternary Goldbach, and the ×2×3 orbit-density theorem are solved. The topological ×2×3 density theorem is about **irrational circle orbits**, not an “odd-order” hypothesis. Do not confuse an explicit recursive enumeration with a universal constrained-target result.
- HYP-9339(3), the conjectural colored even-graph identity in the earliest 2–3 ledger, is already proved by [THM-4721 Theorem K](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/1b91742ce/01-canon/theorems/THM-4721-twisted-burnside-on-odd-fixed-point-sets-sylow-refinement-of-even-graphs-and-tournaments-components-walsh-basis.md). Counting equality is not an equivariant labelled bijection or a Hamiltonian spectrum.
- Odd perfect numbers have a genuine parity/divisor question, but no similarly strong operative role of 3 was identified here. General prime-gap conjectures should not be counted just because the mod-6 wheel is a sieve prefilter. A search by numerals would also retrieve the planar Jacobian conjecture, Erdős–Moser, Hadwiger–Nelson, Schur, Riemann, and much of the repo. They should enter a separate (weaker) comparison only with the operative binary/ternary object named.

## A useful obstruction that explains why the list is not numerology

**Fixed two-ended inspections cannot settle Erdős's digit problem.** Fix any finite leading base-3 word beginning with a nonzero digit and a trailing word of length r whose last digit is 1 or 2. There are infinitely many 2^n whose base-3 expansion has both prescribed words, in fact a natural density of exponents equal to

    (log_3(L+1) − log_3 L)/(2·3^(r−1)),

where L is the integer encoded by the leading word. The order of 2 mod 3^r is 2·3^(r−1), as follows from the even exponent requirement mod 3 and v₃(4^j−1)=1+v₃(j); so the suffix picks exactly one residue of n modulo that order. The leading word specifies the half-open mantissa interval [log_3 L−(d−1), log_3(L+1)−(d−1)) for the fractional part of n log_3 2. On the suffix progression this is an irrational rotation, hence equidistributed. In particular one can force both ends to avoid digit 2 on a positive-density subsequence for each fixed window size. The middle has not been selected. This complements the repo's fixed-window obstructions: the corresponding two-hub failure is that a perfect fixed frame or phase can leave the global baseline unselected.

The shared operational question is **how a chosen word gets validated globally**. A locked frame makes each ternary location independent and counts choices exactly. Primitive triple trees instead have a unique inverse branch based on a conic chamber. Collatz has a parity-word chart but must verify native descent or return of the actual integer, while Erdős fixes the source at 1 and requires its entire base-3 word to obey a restriction. Neither local branch richness nor an exact finite residue chart supplies that global certificate.
