## Proof scope

- THEORY I1--I3: the supplied finite65 alphabet, every word length and
  every vertex order. The inverse returns literal sources. Its finite
  descending search proves nonmembership of a full state, not absence
  of that scalar Hamilton count from all tournaments.
- I2 and I6 use explicit matrix inequalities and reverse-converse closure
  checked in the larger960-chart atlas through six. Their induction is
  over arbitrarily many supplied blocks, not over untested atom sizes.
- I4 classifies every left word attachment at h>=3. I5 reduces the entire
  d=1/2 source language to a finite grammar. I6's H25 panels are complete
  because the inverse enumerates every source word of that count.
- TRANSPORT T1--T4: literal suffix substitutions and arbitrary mixed
  prefixes, with matrix proofs for every length. No preservation of all
  old edges or single actual complement/re-marking operation is asserted.
- T5 rejects four specified affine states at every order in this bank.
  It does not reject their attained scalar counts or all possible future
  source choices.
- T6 minimizes an integer linear correction observer for all left-bank
  contexts. It is not a physical source-state or arbitrary-metagraph
  minimality theorem. The index-two lattice and parity congruence remain.
- T7 gives exact bounded outputs and an all-size sparsity upper bound.
  T8 classifies divisibility by7 for every pure q2 prefix. Neither is a
  proof of infinitely many prime quotients or complete prime lanes.

## Hostiles and corrected near misses

1. Positive/parity guards do not imply membership: state(25,0,25) fails.
2. Same scalar can have different extension behaviour: every H25 word
   fails the requested end wrappers, while77/79 are independently present.
3. Offset seeds do not close: the four287/289/293/295 triples in T5 fail.
4. One sign bit is not an all-phase carrier: q4/q5 corrections currently
   observe0, but the next singleton observes8/6.
5. Invertibility depends on the modulus: q2 fails at5; ramified17/19 clocks
   require nonzero nilpotent layers. Inverse acceptance still requires
   integer membership, even at an invertible modulus.
6. An additive ear lattice of step2 is not a solid interval; the recovered
   H9 parent has holes21,31,35,39.
7. The existing81/27 automorphism hostile is inherited with its full
   joint-response data. This packet does not compare it using H alone.
8. The pasted Ogg/Fermat proposal has an existing correction, retained in
   RECOVERY. Equal primes or discriminants do not authorize a field map.
