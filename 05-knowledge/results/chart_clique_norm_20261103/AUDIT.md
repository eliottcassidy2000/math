# Scope, hostile controls and reproducibility audit

**Same-agent audit, not an independent-agent review.** Proofs and finite
checks have separate statuses; no global literature-priority claim is made.

## Arithmetic proof and computation

- The norm argument handles every positive integer q. Its passage from
  two roots to three explicitly excludes repeated critical roots before
  dividing ordered-pair counts by6. Square q has exactly two exceptional
  two-point fibres. The Hasse-sized search interval is proved exhaustive
  for integer collisions, not merely chosen as an experimental cutoff.
- For each q=1..500 and q=1729, direct grouping of all integer traces in
  that interval verifies the fibre formulas. Independent enumeration of
  all Eisenstein norm pairs verifies r_E(q)=6 A(q). The number-theoretic
  formula itself is classical; the cubic-fibre application is derived.
- The precision theorem is tested for q=2,5,8,11,17,23 and source depths1..6.
  Every source residue is inverted, and each output image is independently
  compared with its three allowed modulo9 disks. The exact valuation law
  is checked on all unordered pairs in[-30,30] for q=2,5,11,17.
- q=1 and q=3 are hostile controls to unrestricted lifting. q=55 separates
  global integer injectivity from the stronger local three-adic unit condition.
- The supplied magic square and all nine indexed point counts are inherited
  from Session143. The new checks verify the digit-clock residue map. No new
  finite-field model census, magic-square discovery, frequency efficacy,
  or elliptic automorphism is claimed.
- Symbolic factorization verifies the quartic discriminant and the all-q
  normalization. The q=5 nine-state image avoids both t^2=q and t^2=3q.
  The inherited three rational points over(0,1,0) are evaluated in the fully
  cancelled polynomial map, including x=0; no singular displayed quotient
  is substituted directly.

## Clique-carrier proof and computation

- The universe is all complete r-coloured graphs; the observation is the
  separate forbidden-clique count vector. Allowed attachments fix all new
  edges independently and retain every old edge. No recolouring, adaptive
  choice, conditioning, shared-source copy, or native complement is added.
- The dimension includes one aggregate per terminal size/colour, not all
  named cliques of that terminal size. Promoting those individual terminal
  flags would overstate minimality. Proper flags have an actual isolating
  attachment; other-colour contributions are kept in different readout entries.
- Independence is proved in the full one-hot edge-coordinate function
  basis, not by treating dependent colour indicators as free variables.
  The last colour has a mixed leading monomial because r>=3. This is why
  the theorem is not silently extended to two colours.
- An independent coefficient routine constructs exact maximal minors of
  determinant+/-1 at n=3,4,5 for(3,3,5), n=6 for(3,3,6), n=5 for(3,4,5),
  and n=4 for four triangle-forbidding colours. The proof supplies all sizes.
- Complete edge-colour evaluation on27 n3 and729 n4 sources has ranks10
  and20 modulo2,3,101. At n5,512 seeded sources plus18 planted sources give
  rank39 in all three fields; that n5 source sample is not called exhaustive.
- The first random-only n5 sample missed rare clique coordinates and did
  not attain39. Adding explicit monochromatic and planted-clique sources
  fixes the lower-bound witness. No completeness statement was based on
  random coverage. This is a useful reason to retain positive controls.
- Every proper flag's actual isolating query is directly counted on the
  complete n3 and n4 source universes. All27 one-vertex cuts on all27 n3
  sources verify integer update matrices. Another100 seeded three-vertex
  attachments on n5 check larger updates.
- Modular minimality concerns arbitrary integer source weights. Positive
  law realizability is a different constraint. No Ramsey value follows
  merely from a small carrier or a unit minor.

## Geometry and corrected near miss

The product construction is reconstructed independently from six cyclic
vertices and a reflected copy. SymPy checks all ten outer unit chords,
the length-two common boundary split into two unit edges, endpoint closure,
and the exact shoelace product. The source gallery formula is credited.
The SVG is generated from those coordinates and XML-checked; it is an
illustration, not a numerical optimality certificate. Matplotlib was not
available, so no dependency was installed and the diagram uses plain SVG.

The first area-bound wording incorrectly applied the field-area cap to the
gross interior of every polygon component. A nested outer cycle can enclose
several capped faces and need not have gross area<=1. The repair associates
each cycle with the actual face immediately inside it, excluding nested
child interiors. Its cap and the triangle-area bound prove the same n/4
inequality for all nested and nonnested pseudoforests. This prepublication
repair is logged in MISTAKES.md; no accepted theorem is retracted.

Neither gallery's global optimum is proved. The product witness violates
the other problem's unit-area cap and is never used as a capped-area design.
Pseudoforest bounds require the declared straight, nonoverlapping embedded
graph model. Functional graphs collapse duplicate opposite arrows, unlike
the bare fraction-entry multigraph.
