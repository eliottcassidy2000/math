# Primitive sheet transport and the distance between degree-57 completions

2026-11-26. **PROVED conditional constraints**, not an existence or
nonexistence claim. Extends [the frozen spectral
and collision analysis](FROZEN-SPECTRUM-AND-ANCHORED-ACTION.md) and
[the Wilson chart](WILSON-ACTION-AND-RIGID-MOORE-CHARTS.md).

## 1. The Wilson group must be primitive

Use the 57 branches of 56 sheets. Gauge the links out of one branch to
the identity and let H be the group generated by the resulting
permutations on the common sheet labels. It is transitive because the
route lists in an exact chart visit every sheet. This is the based
holonomy group, **not** the automorphism group of the full graph.

For every H-invariant real subspace V of the centered 56-sheet
permutation module, the direct sum of 57 copies of V is invariant for
the leaf adjacency X. On it X satisfies X²+X=56I and has zero trace
(the diagonal branch blocks vanish). Its eigenvalues 7 and −8
imply that 15 divides 57 dim V. Hence **5 divides dim V**.
This test applies to each real constituent separately, not just to
the dimension of the whole module.

An H-invariant partition with b blocks contributes the centered
block-constant subspace of dimension b−1; also b divides 56.
The only divisors congruent to 1 mod 5 are 1 and 56, proving
primitivity. A solvable primitive group has an elementary abelian
minimal normal subgroup; it must act transitively, hence regularly
as a faithful abelian transitive group. Its degree would be a prime
power, so H here is nonsolvable.

### A more specific filter using the degree-56 database

The [GAP PrimGrp library](https://gap-packages.github.io/primgrp/doc/chap1.html)
lists [nine entries in degree 56](https://github.com/gap-packages/primgrp/blob/4ce779157c2a89b2ef513caf1efb2afb905baf2b/data/gps1.g).
Entries 6 and 7 are A8 and S8 on the triples of eight points.
For each point i take the indicator vector of triples containing i.
The 8×8 Gram matrix of these vectors has diagonal 21 and off-diagonal
6, so it is nonsingular. Their centered span therefore contains a
7-dimensional invariant space, forbidden by the divisibility test.
The other library entries are the five PSL(3,4)-type actions and the
natural A56 and S56 actions. This is only a necessary filter.
The five rank-three entries have point-stabilizer orbit sizes 1,10,45.
Their symmetric degree-10 orbital is strongly regular: primitivity
makes it connected, and 10(9−λ)=45μ forces λ=0, μ=2. Its centered
eigenspaces have dimensions 35 and 20, so this particular real
dimension test **does not eliminate them**. For the specific
[marked fixture](PLANTED.md), the three frozen center-pair transport
groups already generate S56; the general filter instead applies
before such a choice is made.

## 2. Support identity and a 1,408-edge separation bound

Let A,B be two distinct full Moore adjacencies in common chart labels,
agreeing on at least three full branch stars. Put D=B−A and U equal
to the vertices with a nonzero row in D. Let u=|U|, let r_x count
removed neighbors of x in U (equally the added neighbors), and let
b_x count its A-neighbors outside U. Let M denote the total number
of removed edges; then 2M=Σ r_x, and the same M edges are added.

For inactive w, the subtracted identities
DA+BD+D=0 give DA e_w=0. Consequently for a fixed x in U, any
inactive vertex adjacent in A to an old removed neighbor of x
is also adjacent to an added neighbor of x, and vice versa.
Two distinct vertices have at most one A-common neighbor.
Thus each vertex in either of those neighbor sets has at most
r_x neighbors outside U. On the other hand every active leaf has
its own head and at least three frozen exits outside U. It follows
that 4≤r_x≤53, and for a removed edge xy, b_y≤r_x.

Put a=min r_x and take x with r_x=a. Its 2a distinct removed and
added neighbors each have at least 57−a neighbors in U.
Among the a removed neighbors, the only A-common neighbor of each
pair is x, so their overlap loses exactly a−1 in the union bound.
Among the a added neighbors the loss is at most binom(a,2).
Across the two sets at least 4a pairs already have their unique
common neighbor outside U: each removed neighbor has at least
four inactive neighbors and each supplies an added witness. Pairs
cannot repeat, by the common-neighbor bound. Therefore

    u >= max(a²+1, 2a+1,
             2a(57−a) − (a−1) − binom(a,2) − (a²−4a)).

The first alternative uses the girth-five removed-edge graph
of minimum degree a, the second counts x and its two supports.
Summing b_y≤r_x on directed removed edges, and using
(r_x−a)(r_x−53)≤0, gives

    a Σ b_x ≤ Σ r_x b_x ≤ Σ r_x² ≤ 2(53+a)M − 53au.

The full-graph spectral cut bound alone would yield M≥1,407.
One more constraint comes from retaining the frozen boundary:
delete precisely three frozen branches (and all heads and the
root). The induced exterior leaf graph is 53-regular on 3024
vertices. Its Rayleigh quotient on centered vectors is at most 7
by extending the vectors by zero to the full graph. Each active
leaf also has exactly four edges to the deleted vertices.
Therefore

    Σ b_x ≥ 4u + 46u(1−u/3024).

Combining gives M≥a u (103·3024−46u) / [6048(53+a)].
The right hand side increases for 0≤u≤3024. Substituting the
lower bound on u and taking ceilings for a=4,…,53 yields minimum
**1,408**, at a=4 and u lower bound 415. These are actual edges,
not a count of which permutation links have changed. In particular
the symmetric difference is at least 2,816 edges.

## 3. If only 108 permutation links change, every entry moves

The [frozen-star link bound](WILSON-ACTION-AND-RIGID-MOORE-CHARTS.md)
shows that exactly 108 changed links requires exactly three untouched
branches and changed-link degree four at all other 54 branches.
Every active leaf then has r_x=4, again by the frozen-star argument.
The subtracted identity gives b_y≤4 for every removed neighbor y
of an active leaf, so b_y=4 at every active leaf. This means there
is no edge between active and inactive leaves inside the 53-regular
exterior graph. If both sets were nonempty, they would be two
53-regular girth-five graphs, each of order at least 53²+1=2,810.
There are only 3024 exterior leaves. Thus all are active, each
losing four neighbors, and all 56 matching entries move on every
one of the 108 links. In this equality case exactly **6,048**
edges are removed and 6,048 are added.
