# Degree 57 through matching supports and quadratic orbits

**OPEN.** A degree-57 diameter-two Moore graph would have 3,250 vertices. This packet builds explicit partial graphs, gives obstructions to completing them, and gives two equivalent ways to specify a full candidate. The supplied matrix-multiplication paper distinguishes three pairwise indices from one shared index, and tests whether lists have repeated labels. For a Moore chart, the corresponding exact test is whether two routes deliver the same *physical vertex*. Its validity uses elementary incidence counting and is independent of the claimed multiplication exponent. | How much can be filled explicitly? | **FINITE-EXACT.** Fix the 165 matchings incident with three of the 57 branches. | How far can a symmetric-looking partial graph extend? | **FINITE-EXACT.** There is a twenty-branch, 1,120-site matching graph that passes the necessary spectral test, yet admits no further single site seeing all twenty branches. All missing offsets occur separately in a different eighteen-branch candidate; an integer-weighted packing test excludes it too. [Partial arrays and exact exterior search](SEEDS.md) · [Packing certificate](SEED-BALANCE.md) |
| Can a shared index replace the root coordinates? | **PROVED.** Contract a perfect matching. A full graph is equivalent to a signed incidence test on a 112-regular quotient of order 1,625. It must have eigenvalue 12 with multiplicity at least 104, and cannot be strongly regular. [Tensor of routes and local reconstruction](PAPER.md) |
| Where does an actual quadratic iterate enter? | **PROVED.** In a proposed cyclic \(p\)-sheet cover of \(K_p\) with no short cycles, a Fourier matrix obeys a conjugacy to \((p-1)I-M-M^2\). For primes \(p\ge5\) this forces a square. For seven sheets any valid cover must generate \(A_7\) or \(S_7\) by transport, constraining the earlier seven-branch equality case. [Polynomial iteration and its obstruction](CONSTRUCTION.md) |
| Can the branch maps themselves be quadratics? | **PROVED.** Even with independently chosen real coordinates and constants, at most 386 of the 1,596 branch matches can have the one-way formula \(x^2+c_{ij}\). [Quadratic transport, field dependence, and Poonen](DYNAMICS.md) |
