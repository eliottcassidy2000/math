The three pairs among the selected branches have all 56 routes, each hitting every target sheet exactly once. The 56 permutations on **each** of these three Latin boards separately generate \(S_{56}\) in the normalization below. The full construction and tests use 28 recorded trades. ## A cyclic starting point and a trade that preserves all three projections

Use the sheet set \(\mathbb Z/56\), the pivot \(O=56\), and two other branches \(A=0,B=28\). All pivot matchings are identity and \(A\to B\) is translation by 28. Label the remaining branches by \(h\in D=\mathbb Z/56\setminus\{0,28\}\). Each exterior sheet \(x\) in branch \(h\) specifies its three contacts as \((x,p_h(x),q_h(x))\) on \(O,A,B\). Start with \(p_h(x)=x-h,\ q_h(x)=x-s(h)\).

The nine triples \((9,10,19),(8,12,20),(7,11,18),(6,15,21),(5,22,27),(4,13,17),(3,23,26),(2,14,16),(1,24,25)\) partition \(\{1,\ldots,27\}\) and have third entry equal to the sum of the first two. For each \((a,b,c)\), replace the third by \(-c\) and rotate its signed triple. Define \(s(u)=-v\) if \(v\) follows \(u\), and impose \(s(-u)=-s(u)\). Then both \(s(h)\) and \(h-s(h)\) permute \(D\), since \(u+v\) is minus the third signed entry. The three initial pair projections are the punctured translation tables. The additional two rows, identity and translation by 28, make all three complete. An entire cyclic row board already prevents a Moore completion by the additive quotient theorem, so this starting point itself is excluded. [Local Latin obstruction](LOCAL-LATIN.md)

For two exterior branches \(h,k\), choose one orbit of the permutations \(p_k^{-1}p_h,\ q_k^{-1}q_h\) on the sheets. For every \(x\) in that orbit exchange the complete triples in rows \(h,k\) at \(x\). The orbit's \(p\)-images in the two rows agree as sets, and the same is true of its \(q\)-images. Hence the altered lists are again bijections in each row and coordinate. The multiset of complete triples is unchanged, so all three two-coordinate projections remain exact. The specified orbit seeds in the executable make each trade deterministic. A direct check also evaluates all 1,596 branch pairs, including the pairs whose boards have only three rows.

For each of the three boards, a translation by a unit modulo 56 remains, so translation by one belongs to its group. The executable gives a product of three board rows with the indicated cycle lengths. Raising it to the lcm of all lengths other than three isolates a three-cycle. The differences of its support coordinates generate \(\mathbb Z/56\) in each row of the table. Thus the hypergraph of translated triples is connected. Three-cycles on the edges of a connected 3-uniform hypergraph generate the alternating group on its vertices: inductively add an edge meeting a previously generated alternating orbit. With two old vertices, conjugation by the old alternating group supplies all the needed triples with one new vertex. With one old vertex, conjugate the new triple to a different old vertex and multiply the two cycles in opposite orientations to introduce a triple with two old vertices. This reduces to the first case, twice if necessary. The 56-cycle translation is odd, giving all of \(S_{56}\).

For a displayed modulus \(p\), the defect minimum is over all independent shifts \(a_r\) for the prescribed function \(x\mapsto x\bmod p\); it counts cells where \(r(x)-x\ne a_r\bmod p\). For \(p=56\) the same minimum is 911 on every board. Without altering the row labels the number of cells changed from the original cyclic seed is 1,114 on each board. For all coordinate functions \(f\), the linear systems \(f(r(x))-f(x)-f(r(0))+f(0)=0\) have rank 55 on every board over both \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_7\). These exact-rank results exclude all nonconstant additive quotients. A lower bound on distance to **every** relabelled approximate quotient is a different question. A provisional 21-trade choice produced full symmetric groups but only 44 defects on one ordinary binary phase, which is why the certificate uses 28 trades.
