## 2. General gap-band pair certificate

For `0<lambda<=1/12`, an arbitrary circle shift `delta`, and three three-unit
tails, put

\[
 A_\delta=G_C^\lambda\cap(G_C^\lambda+\delta),\qquad
 h=\#\{w\in T:\|w\delta\|\ge6\lambda\}.
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{L_\lambda(3C\cup T)\ge {h\over6}\mu(A_\delta).}       \tag{2.1}
\]

Moreover, nonempty *closed* `A_delta` and `h>=1` suffice for an actual safe
point, even if its measure is zero. For `y in A_delta` use the six indexed
physical points `(y+j)/3,(y-delta+j)/3`, `j=0,1,2`. A three-unit tail sees two
three-grids. Within each, spacing is `1/3`; the least cross-grid spacing is
`||w delta||/3`. A counted tail therefore hits at most one indexed point in
its open danger arc of length `2lambda`; an uncounted tail hits at most two.
There are at least `h` surviving indices. Equality of the spacing and danger
arc length is safe because the danger arc is open. Counted grids are disjoint;
uncounted coincident points are correctly counted with multiplicity.

For the measure claim integrate the survivor count over `A_delta`. Each
three-sheet family has Jacobian three and covers each physical point at most
once almost everywhere. Thus the integral is at most `6 L_lambda`, proving
(2.1). This is the same integration operation as the earlier dyadic pair
packet; it does not require `delta`-periodicity of `A_delta`.

At `lambda=1/14` the band is `||w delta||>=3/7`. A closed body-safe interval
of width `W` and a tail `w` with `wW>=3/7` supply two such phases. This
recovers the component-width operation of
[THM-4442, bounded-ten-body-parity-free-scale-three-completion](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4442-lrc14-bounded-ten-body-parity-free-scale-three-completion.md),
without duplicating its complete bounded body/head census.

**Endpoint positive control.** For `C={1,...,13}`, outside the ten-body
universe, `y=9/14,z=1/14` are body-safe and `delta=4/7` is on the exact band
boundary. Tail `1` supplies escape for `T=(1,5,11)`, although the prior
antipodal note checks failure of every exact tail-antiphase choice on this
body. **Sharp individual cap hostile:** if `0<delta<3/7`, take tail `1` and
`y=delta/2`; the physical points `+delta/6,-delta/6` are both strictly bad.
This refutes a smaller universal one-tail cap threshold, not actual safety.

## 3. Three phases and nine physical points

Put

\[
 A_3=G_C^\lambda\cap(G_C^\lambda+1/3)
                      \cap(G_C^\lambda+2/3).
\]

For any three three-unit tails and `0<lambda<=1/9`,

\[
 \boxed{L_\lambda(3C\cup T)\ge\mu(A_3)/3.}                 \tag{3.1}
\]

Indeed `y in A_3` supplies the nine physical points `y/3+k/9`. A three-unit
is a unit modulo nine, so its observed packet is a simple nine-grid. An open
arc of length at most `2/9` hits at most two points; at least three of nine
survive the three tails. Integrating the three sheet families gives the upper
bound `9 L_lambda` for the survivor integral. This proves (3.1). The set
`A_3` is `1/3`-periodic. A nonempty packet with common body margin `alpha`
gives a point with full margin at least `min(alpha,1/9)`.

This is genuinely higher-order occupancy: for the selected phases
`y,y+1/3,y+2/3`, every three-unit tail has pairwise phase distance `1/3<3/7`.
The gap-band graph on these three vertices has no edge, yet their joint
packet succeeds. We make no claim that the *entire* body safe set has no
other qualifying pair. A cosmetic tournament would destroy this information.

The body supplier is nonautomatic: `C={1,...,10}` has empty `A_3` at `1/14`.
The all-odd body `{1,3,...,19}` has `mu(A_3)=86/2261`, but an elementary
clock-six argument already closes it. That positive control is not advertised
as a new hard family.

## 4. A mixed unbounded family, exact acceptance modulo 72

Let

\[
 P=120120,\quad B=(1,3,4,7,9,12,15,30,33,39),\quad
 c_i=B_i+Pn_i\quad(n_i\ge0).
\]

The ten lifts are independent. Put `C={c_i}` and `H=max C`, and fix physical
tails `8,14`. The phases

\[
 y=1/8,\quad y+1/3=11/24,\quad y+2/3=19/24
\]

have common body margin exactly `1/8`. Their ten individual minima are
`(1/8,3/8,1/6,1/8,1/8,1/2,1/8,1/4,1/8,1/8)`; `24|P` preserves them.
The corresponding physical nine-grid is `(3+8k)/72`. At margin `1/8`, tail
`8` kills exactly indices `{2,3,4}` and tail `14` kills exactly `{6,8}`.
The retained quartet is

\[
 \mathcal Q=\{3,11,43,59\}/72.                               \tag{4.1}
\]

For an arbitrary positive integer appender `u`,

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal Q\text{ contains a point safe for }u
                   \text{ at }1/8\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad72\nmid u.}
                                                                    \tag{4.2}
\]

If `9` does not divide `u`, its gcd with nine is one or three. The full
indexed nine-grid has at most three bad indices in an open arc of length
`1/4`: a simple nine-grid has at most three, and a three-grid repeated three
times has at most one bad distinct point. It cannot erase four retained
indices. If `u=9m`, every quartet numerator is `3 mod8`, so all four observed
phases equal `3m/8 mod1`; these are safe at `1/8` exactly when `8` does not
divide `m`. The endpoint residues `u=27,45 mod72` attain precisely `1/8`.

Consequently the thirteen-distinct-speed row `S=3C union {8,14,u}` has full
margin at least `1/8` whenever `72` does not divide `u`, subject to the
necessary distinctness restrictions `u notin 3C union {8,14}`. The family is
primitive: `c_1` is odd and `gcd(3c_1,8)=1`. The ten body residues modulo `P`
are distinct. Every clock denominator `2<=q<=14` is killed by a divisible
speed: tails `8,14` cover `2,4,8,7,14`; the lifted base entries `3,4,30,33,39`
give pins for `3,9`, `6,12`, `5,10`, `11`, `13`. The modulus preserves every
pin. Thus neither fixed small clocks nor bounded heights explain the result.

**Exact failure boundary:** a multiple of 72 kills the entire displayed
quartet. This is an iff for *this packet*, not a counterexample to LRC and not
an iff for full-row safety. Exact margin `1/8` alone implies no positive
`L_(1/8)` measure.

### 4.1 Tail-height-independent measure repair

The following stronger statement includes accepted appenders divisible by
nine:

\[
 \boxed{72\nmid u\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
         L_{1/14}(3C\cup\{8,14,u\})\ge {2\over91H}.}        \tag{4.3}
\]

At each of the four quartet points the twelve base speeds `3C union {8,14}`
have margin at least `1/8`. Their maximum is `3H` (`H>=39`). The four
disjoint intervals of radius `1/(56H)` are therefore base-safe at `1/14`.
Each has length `1/(28H)`. On an interval of length `ell`, one tail's strict
danger comb occupies at most

\[
 \ell/7+6/(49u),
\]

by the inherited one-period discrepancy lemma. Subtracting this on all four
intervals gives

\[
 L_{1/14}\ge {6\over49H}-{24\over49u}.                       \tag{4.4}
\]

The accepted full-margin point separately gives a safe interval of radius
`(1/8-1/14)/max(3H,u)`, hence

\[
 L_{1/14}\ge {3\over28\max(3H,u)}.                          \tag{4.5}
\]

For `u/H<=39/8`, (4.5) is at least `2/(91H)`; for `u/H>=39/8`, (4.4) is
at least that value. Both equal it at the crossover. This combines a local
point margin with a global interval discrepancy instead of incorrectly
applying a unit-nine count to an appender divisible by nine.

For three-unit `u`, the three disjoint phase neighborhoods in `A_3` have
total measure at least `6(1/8-lambda)/H`. Formula (3.1) improves the floors to
`L_(1/14)>=3/(28H)` and `L_(1/9)>=1/(36H)`. If `3|u` but `9` does not divide
`u`, the appender kills at most three indices, while tails `8,14` kill at
most two each at these margins. Two survivors give the respective floors
`1/(14H)` and `1/(54H)`. Those stronger formulas are not asserted for
appenders divisible by nine; (4.3) is the uniform repair.

## 5. AP8 plus two genuinely variable outliers

This supplier uses interval geometry and no residue-clone assumption on its
outliers. For an integer speed `v`, let

\[
 \omega(v)=\begin{cases}1&3\mid v,\\3&3\nmid v.\end{cases}
\]

The third-orbit danger set consists of points `y` for which at least one of
`y,y+1/3,y+2/3` is bad for `v`. At margin `lambda<=1/9` this is a periodic
comb with period `1/(omega(v)v)`, tooth length `2lambda/v`, and duty
`2lambda omega(v)`. Its primitive of the centered indicator has oscillation
`(2lambda/v)(1-2lambda omega(v))`. Therefore for every circle interval `I`,

\[
 \mu(I\cap U_v)\le2\lambda\omega(v)|I|
              +{2\lambda(1-2\lambda\omega(v))\over v}.      \tag{5.1}
\]

This is precisely the boundary-discrepancy operation of THM-4092, with the
new orbit multiplicity kept explicit. It remains valid at strict endpoints;
endpoint sets have zero measure.

For `0<=lambda<=1/9`, the AP8 body `{1,...,8}` has a complete third-orbit-safe
interval

\[
 J_\lambda=\left[{2+3\lambda\over21},{1-\lambda\over8}\right],
 \qquad |J_\lambda|={5(1-9\lambda)\over168}.                  \tag{5.2}
\]

The left endpoint is the tooth constraint from speed seven on phase
`y+1/3`, and the right one is speed eight on phase `y`. For all 24 addressed
teeth the lower and upper endpoint inequalities are affine in `lambda`;
checking `lambda=0,1/9` verifies the entire interval in (5.2). The exact
checker records these 48 rational inequalities, not a sampled-lambda claim.

At `lambda=1/14`, write `J=[31/294,13/112]`, `ell=25/2352`. For distinct
outliers `v,w>8`, set

\[
 b=\ell\left(1-{\omega(v)+\omega(w)\over7}\right)
       -{7-\omega(v)\over49v}-{7-\omega(w)\over49w}.         \tag{5.3}
\]

If `b>0`, the exact actual conclusion for **any three distinct three-unit
tails**, regardless of parity or height, is

\[
 \boxed{L_{1/14}(3(\{1,\ldots,8,v,w\})\cup T)\ge b.}       \tag{5.4}
\]

Indeed subtract both outlier dangers from `J` using (5.1). The residual has
measure at least `b`. Its translates by thirds are disjoint, because
`|J|<1/3`, and all lie in `A_3`. Thus `mu(A_3)>=3b`; (3.1) proves (5.4).
This accounts for the factor three exactly. In particular,

| Outlier types | Sufficient lower bound for both heights |
|---|---:|
| Both three-units | `109` |
| One divisible by three | `45` |
| Both divisible by three | `33` |

The raw strict thresholds are respectively `2688/25=107.52`, `224/5=44.8`,
and `4032/125=32.256`. The first eligible three-unit height above the first
threshold is 109. These are sufficient budgets, not optimal classifications.

The explicit variable-outlier family

\[
 v=110m,\quad w=130n,\quad m,n\ge1,\quad3\nmid mn,\quad v\ne w,
 \qquad T=\{14,a,b\}
\]

with arbitrary distinct three-unit `a,b,14` satisfies

\[
 \boxed{L_{1/14}\ge {1747\over11771760}>0.}                  \tag{5.5}
\]

This is an **absolute** lower bound independent of all outlier and tail
heights, not an inverse-height bound. The base body contains 1, so the full
row is primitive for any three-unit tail. The AP8 divisibility pins cover
every clock denominator through 14 except `10,11,13,14`; `3v` supplies
`10,11`, `3w` supplies `13`, and tail `14` supplies `14`. All speeds are
distinct after the explicitly stated restrictions; body/tail overlap is
impossible modulo three. This is an actual unbounded entry supplier, but
only for the specified AP8 core.

### 5.1 A scoped stronger margin

For two three-unit outliers and `0<lambda<1/12`, the same proof gives

\[
 b_\lambda={5(1-9\lambda)\over168}(1-12\lambda)
       -2\lambda(1-6\lambda)(1/v+1/w),\qquad L_\lambda\ge b_\lambda
 \quad\hbox{when }b_\lambda>0.                              \tag{5.6}
\]

At `lambda=1/13` this becomes

\[
 b_{1/13}={5\over7098}-{14\over169}(1/v+1/w).
\]

Both three-unit outliers at least 236 suffice: the strict common-height
threshold is `1176/5=235.2`. Therefore AP8 plus any such two outliers and any
three three-unit tails has a positive-measure set at margin `1/13`.
At the limiting `lambda=1/12` the crude two-comb density budget is one and
this proof stops; a pair-overlap or phase sidecar would be required.

## 6. Relation to incoming reductions and exact remaining scope

The new suppliers do not redo the incoming atlas or network ceilings.
[THM-4446, primitive-ten-pack-descent-and-dilation-rays](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4446-lrc14-primitive-ten-pack-descent-and-dilation-rays.md)
removes nonprimitive bodies. The AP8 family and the subfamily `n_1=0` of the
quartet supplier have primitive bodies because they contain 1; the latter
still has nine independently unbounded body heights. For comparison with the
exact ten-body/three-unit-tail clock-three frontier, additionally restrict
the quartet appender to `3` not dividing `u`. Accepted appenders divisible by
three remain covered by Section 4, but are a different body/tail entry type.
Other independent lifts
can have a nonprimitive body, although every full quartet row remains
primitive. For example the lift vector
`(9,10,2,12,13,6,16,15,8,11)` makes every body speed divisible by 17:
`120120=-2 mod17` and `B_i=2n_i mod17`. This independently supplied audit
hostile pinpoints the invalid implication “primitive full row implies
primitive body”; the final statements do not use that implication.
Thus the descent does not subsume the suppliers as a whole. The incoming
[THM-4447, composite-clock-capacity-and-small-clock-reduction](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4447-lrc14-composite-clock-capacity-and-small-clock-reduction.md)
removes arithmetic ten-pack clocks at least five, but on those primitive-body
subfamilies the physical body gcd is exactly three, not an arithmetic
nine-pack. The nine points come from a
geometrically supplied orbit of body phases. THM-4448 controls protected
shores with quantitative height hypotheses; (5.4) has no tail-height bound,
but it keeps the fixed AP8 core instead of claiming arbitrary shore entry.

Thus the missing coordinate is an actual body-safe phase configuration,
not a better global tail network constant. No statement here constructs that
configuration for arbitrary primitive clock-three ten-bodies. The strongest
survivors are (2.1), (3.1), the exact quartet acceptance (4.2), its uniform
measure repair (4.3), and the unrestricted-outlier AP8 budgets (5.4)–(5.6).
Packet rejection, empty `A_3`, or a failed density budget is not an LRC
counterexample. The next question is whether another core can supply a
three-phase interval with lower fragmentation, or an explicitly tracked
two-comb overlap at the `1/12` density boundary; no new census is needed to
state that missing operation.
