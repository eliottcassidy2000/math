# Verification scope

The standard-library runner `python3 verify_session.py` replays all nine
mathematical checkers normally and with Python optimization, suppresses
bytecode writes, and compares parsed JSON with the finite receipts committed
beside the sources. Failure raises an explicit exception. These are local
reproducibility checks, not a GitHub CI claim.

## Finite universes

- Modular observers: 1,487,200 kernel comparisons, 10,080 robust-code cases,
  odd cutoff through n=100 and a nonfree square-coloring Burnside control.
- Ordinary aliquot observers: 24,000 direct divisor comparisons, complete
  selected residue planes, 209,652 metric-pair checks, inverse compatibility,
  conjugacy/basins, shifted precision hostiles and step-semigroup tests.
- Weighted observers: 7,680 direct divisor comparisons, 1,053 power-fibre
  checks, 41,232 formal conjugacy checks and genuine prime-source ambiguities.
- Norm signs: all 131 line tables at dimensions 2, 4 and 8; all 1,024
  assignments in the five-equation obstruction; 128 arithmetic selections.
- Norm defects: all 128 seven-line tables and all 112 symbolic rectangles;
  independent coefficient expansions verify the exact mean-square defect.
- Random selectors: 89,418 gates, including anchored 625-square pin cases,
  1,510 independent clock cases and 1,700 one-pin repairs.
- Canonical cuts: 3,812 gates, including every cut on complete tournament
  cubes of orders 3, 4 and 5; selected affine-rank implementations through 4.
- Bank ranking: 4,694 gates, exhaustive small necklace thresholds, every
  canonical rank of the eight-coordinate model, and literal-action controls
  for 24- and 124-coordinate banks.
- Prime-bank counts: 796 gates, literal-ring construction and every acting element in
  four specified ring families, inclusion-exclusion over every proper ideal
  cut, aggregated-count comparisons and scalar recurrence checks. Its receipt
  specifies the final finite families and gate count.

## Proofs, inherited results and external reports

The all-depth conclusions use the proofs in the accompanying notes, not
finite extrapolation. The universal ramified group and canonical ideal-module
classification are explicitly inherited dependencies. Counts always retain
the intended action: specified bank orbits are not full graph classes.

Independent proof reviews covered the elementary modular interfaces,
selector formulas, norm/sign and arithmetic-code transport, canonical-cut
semantics, the bank threshold automaton and subgroup recurrence, and the
prime ideal-chain convolution. Where a result uses a finite symbolic identity,
the exact universe and equation certificate are displayed in its note.

The inspiration paper's registry reports external formal checking. Its Lean
build and general counting engine were not independently reproduced here.
The session proves no new general factoring bound, unrestricted tournament
classification or Collatz convergence theorem. No literature-priority claim
is inferred from successful checks.
