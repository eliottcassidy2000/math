    aba=bab,  bcb=cbc,  bdb=dbd,

This is an isomorphism of presentations with the same four marked
letters, not just a nonabelian quotient. No Coxeter relation is imposed.

## 2. Reversible reduction to D4

Write e=bcb^-1. A Hurwitz square fixes a pair exactly when that pair
commutes, and a Hurwitz cube fixes it exactly when the pair satisfies
the braid relation. For a literal chronological word P,core,P^-1, the
core acts on the tuple after P. Exact prefix calculations give:

| Name | Prefix | Core pair | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| cusp_plus | `[-1,-2]` | `(b,c)` | braid(b,c) |
| cusp_minus | `[2,1]` | `(b,d)` | braid(b,d) |
| cusp_two | `[2]` | `(a,e)` | braid(a,e) |
| node0 | `[2,-1]` | `(e^-1ae,b)` | commutation |
| node1 | `[2,1,2]` | `(a,d)` | commutation |
| node2 | `[2,3]` | `(e,bdb^-1)` | commutation |

The last relation is exactly [c,d]=1 after simultaneous conjugation
by b^-1. From braid(b,c) one obtains

    e b e^-1=c,               c e c^-1=b.                 (3)

Conjugating node0 by e therefore gives exactly [a,c]=1. Conjugating
the third cusp relation by c, and using this commutation and (3),
gives exactly braid(a,b). These transformations reverse: (1) implies
the original third cusp and first node after the indicated inverse
conjugations, and all other relations are unchanged. This proves (1).
In particular a quotient of the Artin group, rather than the group
itself, would describe any actual complement with further relations.

Although the reduced presentation is symmetric in its three leaves,
the actual cusp and node access pairs have not been relabeled. For
actual sheet counts we retain the original table. A simultaneous
conjugation of a node pair also transports both actual sets, and is
legitimate; replacing a pair by arbitrary D4 generators without that
transport would not be legitimate.

## 3. Actual retained sets and the three-cusp Euler identity

Write D>1 for mapping degree and k for the actual generic affine fibre
count on the entire smooth stratum. Thus 1<=k<D. The lower bound also
follows directly: the pullback of a nonconstant irreducible equation
of the target curve has a divisor, and quasi-finiteness prevents that
divisor from being contracted. The curve being a component of the
nonproperness set gives strictly positive missing generic degree.
The étale covering away from the whole nonproperness curve is connected,
so its monodromy on D labels is transitive. All positive meridians of
the irreducible curve are conjugate in the global group.

Let n_1,n_2,n_3 be the actual affine fibre counts at the cusps. For each
node p, let omega_p be the intersection size of the two actual deleted
sets, and put W=sum_p omega_p over all N nodes. The inherited actual
local theorem gives, at each ordinary cusp,

    sigma tau sigma=tau sigma tau,
    A subset Fix(sigma), B=(sigma tau)A subset Fix(tau),
    |A|=|B|=k,       n_i=|A intersect B|,
    2n_i >= 3k-D.                                         (4)

These sets are actual retained pages, not arbitrary inertia-fixed
letters. To recall the inequality, put U=A\B and O=Omega\(A union B).
The braid relation gives tau U subset O, so k-n_i<=D-2k+n_i.
If equality holds, the inherited equality proof shows

    Fix(sigma)=A and every nontrivial sigma-cycle is even. (5)

Because all global positive meridians are conjugate, equality at even
one cusp makes every smooth retained set the full fixed set. At every
node the deleted sets are then permutation supports. Its commuting
meridians preserve their support intersection, which is a union of
nontrivial even cycles. Consequently equality at any cusp implies

    every omega_p is even.                               (6)

At any node, without an equality hypothesis, the actual fibre count is
2k-D+omega_p. Thus

    omega_p >= max(0,D-2k).                               (7)

The normalized curve is A1. Removing the three cusp preimages and the
2N node preimages gives chi(S_reg)=-2-2N. Each singular target point
is then inserted once, so chi(S)=1-N and chi(A2\S)=N. Actual fibre
integration yields the different, three-cusp ledger

    1=DN+k(-2-2N)+sum_p(2k-D+omega_p)+sum_i n_i
     =-2k+n_1+n_2+n_3+W.                                 (8)

In particular W=2k+1-sum_i n_i<=2k+1. These are weighted actual
specialization identities inherited from the finite envelope; no
identification of deleted sheets with boundary-point cardinality is
being made.

## 4. A uniform transposition obstruction

In any permutation representation of (1) whose four generators are
transpositions, the total moved support has at most four labels.
Indeed fix b=(12). A leaf satisfying the braid relation with b is
either b itself or a transposition sharing exactly one endpoint with b.
Distinct disjoint transpositions commute, and commutation together
with braid would force equality, so no disjoint leaf is possible.

If any leaf equals b, its commutation with the other leaves and their
braid relations force all leaves to equal b. Otherwise pairwise
commuting leaves are identical or disjoint. At most two distinct such
leaves can exist, since each uses exactly one of the two endpoints of b.
The union of b and those at most two leaf edges uses at most four
vertices. This proves the support bound uniformly in ambient degree.
The transitive S4 control in §6 shows that four is sharp. In particular
no transitive degree>4 representation satisfying (1) can have positive
meridians that are transpositions.

## 5. Conditional exclusions in degrees five, six and seven

All four generator images are conjugate. If one is the identity, all
are identities and the generated action is not transitive for D>1.
Each nonidentity image fixes at least k labels.

For D=5, if k=1 then (7) gives W>=9 while (8) gives W<=3. If k=2,
(4) gives n_i>=1, so W<=5-3=2, while (7) gives W>=3. Hence k>=3.
A nonidentity permutation on five labels fixing at least three labels
is a transposition. Section 4 excludes transitivity.

For D=6, k=1 gives W>=12>3 and k=2 gives W>=6>5. Thus k>=3.
A nonidentity permutation fixing at least three labels is a
transposition or a single three-cycle. The former is excluded by §4;
in the latter case it has exactly three fixed labels, so k=3 and the
actual retained sets are full fixed sets. Equation (4) gives n_i>=2,
hence W=7-sum_i n_i<=1. Two commuting single three-cycles have disjoint
or identical supports: a commuting permutation preserves the first
three-cycle's support, and a second single three-cycle can meet that
support only by acting on all of it. Every node overlap is therefore
zero or three. It follows that W=0, so all node supports are disjoint.

In particular the original node2 pair, simultaneously conjugated by
b^-1, has disjoint supports for c and d. The original first and second
cusp pairs are (b,c) and (b,d). For full fixed sets on D labels,

    |Fix(sigma) intersect Fix(tau)|
       =D-6+|supp(sigma) intersect supp(tau)|              (9)

when both permutations are single three-cycles. At D=6 each of these
two cusp counts is at least two, so the support of b must meet each
of two disjoint three-element sets in at least two elements. Its own
support has only three elements, a contradiction.

For D=7, k<=2 is impossible by W>=3(7-2k)>2k+1. Suppose k=3.
Then n_i>=1 and 3<=W=7-sum_i n_i<=4. If any n_i=1, equality holds
in (4). By (6) every node overlap is even, and by (7) each is at least
one; thus each is at least two and W>=6, a contradiction. If none of
the n_i equals one, their sum is at least six and W<=1, again a
contradiction. Therefore k>=4.

A nonidentity permutation fixing at least four of seven labels is
again a transposition or a single three-cycle. The former is excluded;
the latter forces k=4 and full fixed retained sets. Now (4) gives
n_i>=3, so (8) forces W=0. The original node2 supports of c,d are
disjoint. Equation (9) gives n_bc=1+|supp(b) intersect supp(c)|,
and similarly for b,d. Both are at least three. The same impossible
packing of four required support labels into supp(b) finishes D=7.

The proofs hold for every N>=3. A finite table at N=3,4,5 is only a
control of the argument and is not the source of this quantifier.
