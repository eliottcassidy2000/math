## Verification universes and independent routes

`verify.py` uses explicit `need` exceptions; Python optimization cannot remove
the mathematical gates. Its normal and `-O` runs write identical receipts.

1. **Minimum costs:** forward Dijkstra from the actual seed, with every one
   of18 letters, in F<=512,q<=1024,h<=125. F,q,h are nondecreasing forward,
   so this window is hereditary for any terminal state inside it. All4,728
   reachable states agree with both the one-step Bellman inverse and the new
   suffix-jump inverse, including minimum cost and literal word replay.
2. **Rejections as well as positives:** every state with h in{1,5,25}, odd
   F<=63, q<=128 and allowed g phases that passes the cut/interior guards.
   There are17,836 candidates, exactly325 reachable. All three methods agree.
   This is a complete bounded state test, not a test of all tournament counts.
3. **Greedy hostiles:** four literal reconstructed graphs. Independent
   permutation covers check q,g,h and subset DP checks F. The first three
   greedy attempts reject reachable states; the fourth spends an extra vertex.
4. **Large numeric states:** two serialized words, with one or two D atoms
   and37 singleton letters, are inverted and replayed. No exponential-size
   adjacency DP is claimed for those controls.
5. **Integer images:** every minor of each of16 four-by-four lifted matrices
   computes its determinantal divisors and verifies (J5). The affine kernel
   is tested separately. All three points of the source-hole fibre are checked.
6. **Actual amplifier source:** literal permutation covers of the seven-vertex
   parent and independent adjacency DP of the eight-vertex marked tournament
   give(449,512,54,117). Both atoms are strongly connected; all their permutations
   and path cuts give(5,32) and(19,256).
7. **Bounded-atom classification:** both accepted31h bank files give the same
   three dyadic profile types and representative orders. Their hashes are
   recorded. Completeness is inherited from their prior independent nauty/ear
   routes and strong-floor proof; no fresh exhaustive graph generation is claimed.
8. **Infinite-family controls:** all a in0..2,b in0..1,r in0..2, excluding the
   zero triple:17 state/vertex-clock checks, nine adjacency-DP checks at order
   at most16. All16 cuts of D and64 cuts of B19 test dyadic-cut rigidity on
   the actual seed. The all-size conclusions have separate written proofs.

## Failed candidate and strongest survivor

The candidate "choose the predecessor of least rank F+q+h" is REFUTED by
the four witnesses in [DESCENT.md](DESCENT.md). This was a prepublication
candidate, not a previously accepted theorem. First failed implication:
smaller admissible rank does not imply actual source membership. Filtering
7/21 does not repair it because of(9,7,3,5). Its strongest survivor is exact
dynamic programming over all recursively legal branches, with the clock
lower bound used only for admissible pruning.

The missing coordinate is not merely a new scalar forbidden-value list: it
is the actual joint source condition, together with the stored overwritten
g when an ambient inverse is required. The new question is whether a finite
or recursively specified family of such actual-source branches has uniform
coverage of the two prime lanes from THM-4094.
