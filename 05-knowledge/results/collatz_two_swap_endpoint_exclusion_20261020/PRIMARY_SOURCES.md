# Primary source for the endpoint closure

Status: **CITED; application independently checked.** Checked 20 October 2026.

Y. Bugeaud, M. Mignotte, S. Siksek, *Classical and modular approaches to exponential Diophantine equations I. Fibonacci and Lucas perfect powers*, arXiv:math/0403046, Theorem 9.4, printed page 16 (PDF page index 15).

[Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0403046).

The real-field specialization bounds the logarithm of a nonzero multiplicative expression minus one in terms of the field degree, exponent maximum, and modified heights. For rational bases 2 and 3 and exponents q,-p, the field degree is one, exponent maximum is q, and admissible heights are 0.7 and 1.1. The resulting constant is less than 602,494,200. These precise hypotheses, the minus-one convention, and the factor 1+log B were inspected in the paper.

The paper derives this form from E. M. Matveev, Corollary 2.3. The original paper is identified by [DOI 10.1070/IM2000v064n06ABEH000314](https://doi.org/10.1070/IM2000v064n06ABEH000314). The explicit version actually used here is Bugeaud--Mignotte--Siksek's statement.

All endpoint-specific estimates, finite checks, and continued-fraction certificates are proved or supplied in this packet and its named arithmetic dependency. No historical novelty claim is made.
