# Grounded children and marked completion

**FINITE-EXACT:** four formerly open Mersenne child certificates, their four
parent certificates, and one further child by suffix transport are now
grounded. **PROVED:** new guarded one-bit and eight-bit deletions, a signed
ladder family outside the unchanged 225-head bank, and the marked-plan
grounding/substitution rules. **CONDITIONAL:** unsupplied terminal children
of these infinite families still need certificates. **OPEN:** universal
coverage and a well-founded completion rule for every positive integer.

Current continuation: [recursive child compression](collatz_child_compression_synthesis_20261007d.md)
adds guarded16/34-bit exits, exact parameter readers and marked four-/six-point gluing.

The concrete gain is threefold. Selected two-twos obligations now have
stored proofs to 1. A rule outside the finite head bank adds genuine guard
coverage. Composing a new child rule with an existing parent rule gives
eight-bit deletion, enough to meet previously unpaid seven-bit requests.
Marked proof plans keep every remaining defect attached to its actual source.

The same package proves a new one-bit exit

\]

Here and below a wavy arrow means a checked common-future implication with
a strictly smaller child. It is not necessarily a forward Collatz edge.
At E=18269, the already grounded source supplies a suffix that also grounds
`M_18268`, without another search. The infinite parent progression

\[

 \qquad M_K\rightsquigarrow M_{K-4}\rightsquigarrow M_{K-5}

stays outside the declared G1/G5/G17 entry bank. Its arbitrary final children

## Added coverage and deeper payment

The [signed-ladder package](collatz_complement_ladders_20261007c.md) keeps
the full state `X=3^kY+d`. A gap `-i` consumes two i extra bits of terminal
valuation: the actual final exponent must satisfy `c>=2i+1`.
This gives an exact guard for the inherited source-side head

\[
 u=(12,1,1),\quad v=(3,1,1,3,2,6),\quad r=-1.
\]

Its reserve cell `X=75093 mod131072` is disjoint from all 225 existing
head guards. At J=3 it yields

\[
 K\equiv485217\pmod{1048576}.
\]

Its relative added phase mass in the specified first-letter>=3 branch is
1/16384. This measures guarded paid coverage, not already grounded ROOT
coverage. Zero-gap matches are normalized to signed cores and common suffixes;
counting them again would inflate the apparent gain.

The [eight-bit package](collatz_eight_bit_completion_20261007c.md) finds a
different two-twos head, eight ones followed by22. Its partner intersects
the old parent head `(1,2,9)` at the exact same middle-source word. Their
composition proves

\[
 \boxed{K\equiv924745905\pmod{4294967296}
        \quad\Longrightarrow\quad M_K\rightsquigarrow M_{K-8}.}
\]

This has an ordinary-source version, permitting modest literal controls
instead of expanding `2^924745905-1`. Intersecting its native binary guard
with a legal inverse word's ternary guard pays the original inverse child.
Every nonempty inverse word of length at most13 fits the numerical eight-bit
budget. In particular `G5^36` and `G17^64`, whose exact uniform request is
seven bits, now have actual sufficient receipts on explicit joint guards.
Existence of a native Mersenne phase is checked separately.

The final eight-bit child is a new marked port. Its exponent is
`924745897 mod2^32`. All 271 signed J2 packets from nonpadding heads with
letters1..12 and lengths1..3 miss that phase. This is a precise stopping
reason for that finite bank, not a no-go theorem for deeper rules.

## Completion with marked defects

The [marked completion consumer](collatz_marked_completion_20261007c.md)
has three node types: a checked ROOT word, an authenticated common-future
transport, or a source-labelled hole. A finite selected plan yields exactly
one of GROUNDED, OPEN, or CYCLE for each listed source. A defect cut records
the portion with no path to a supplied ROOT anchor.

Replacing a hole with an authenticated finite plan preserves every previously
grounded export. It can expose a cycle, so replacement is not automatically
completion. The literal hostile `3 -> 5 -> 3` combines a valid forward edge
with a strictly paid common-future receipt; local payment does not make that
mixed dependency graph well-founded. Supplying the checked word `(4)` at5
breaks the cycle and exports `(1,4)` at3.

The consumer also fills the actual `M_6125` hole and exports the grounded
`M_6129` word. It never converts an assumed terminal into evidence. The exact
completion criterion for a finite selected plan is no holes plus a natural
rank strictly decreasing on every dependency. The rank is proof height,
distinct from the signed sum of actual path-length differences.

| Connection | Preserved object | Lost by compression | Retained sidecar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded word to parent receipt | Same integer and first-hit route | Terminal evidence | Supplied child word and prefix |
| Two deletions to eight bits | Actual middle orbit | Relative clocks and native guards | Ordered words and alignment |
| Signed ladder to guard | Full affine carry | Available division reserve | Signed gap and actual final valuation |
| Completed model to partial proof | Source-labelled obligation | Unsupported edges and circularity | Holes, defect cut, rank |

No intrinsic pairwise orientation is needed here, so no cosmetic tournament
is imposed. The useful graph is the dependency graph itself.

## What remains decisive

The next arithmetic target is a variable-depth exit for the specific
eight-bit child phase or a route from it to a separately grounded family.
The actual prefix and phase must remain attached to that child. The next
global target is a schema that returns, for every positive odd source,
either a ROOT certificate or a guarded dependency decreasing a proved
well-founded rank. The finite-plan theorem supplies the checking interface,
not the missing universal schema.

New first-hit boundary controls also separate a coarse native arithmetic
cell from strict certificate legality: source341 with head10 reaches ROOT.
Appending its formal valuation2 is arithmetic padding, not a permitted
first-hit word. The repair and survivor are logged in
[the mistakes ledger](../../01-canon/MISTAKES.md).
