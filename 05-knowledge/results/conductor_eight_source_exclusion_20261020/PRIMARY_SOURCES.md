# Primary-source scope

20 October 2026.

1. A. Sathaye and J. Stenerson, *On Plane Polynomial Curves*, author text
   dated 10 September 2014:
   https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/papnew.pdf .
   Section 2.2 gives the one-place and residually rational conventions.
   Section 3.1, printed pages 8--9, gives the characteristic gcd,
   semigroup-membership and strict-degree axioms, primitive-entry rule,
   and nonprincipal conductor bounds. The source was freshly opened and
   independently checked for this packet. Its introduction distinguishes
   plane realizability from polynomial realizability; the latter is proved
   here by explicit polynomial identities.
2. S. S. Abhyankar and A. Sathaye, *Uniqueness of plane embeddings of
   special curves* (1996), Section 2, printed p.1062:
   https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03254-6 .
   The identification of the initial characteristic entries with the
   actual coordinate pole orders, and elementary reduction to a
   nonprincipal pair, are inherited from the independently audited
   [fixed cubic/quartic source verification](../fixed_cubic_quartic_embedding_classes_20261020/PRIMARY_SOURCES.md).
   This packet does not claim a new downloaded copy of that article.
3. J. Gwozdziewicz, *Injectivity on one line*, Theorem 1.1:
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9305008v1 .
   The planar nonzero-constant-Jacobian map becomes an automorphism when
   injective on one affine line. The separate
   [all-quadratic-semigroup proof and source audit](../quadratic_semigroup_keller_obstruction_20261020/PRIMARY_SOURCES.md)
   freshly checked the original theorem. It is used in that dependency
   after a polynomial source shear, and in the synthesis's delta-zero
   denominator-clearing case.

The quartic/sextic and cubic/quintic Jacobian contradictions are direct
algebraic arguments. No general plane-embedding uniqueness theorem,
relative ambient lifting theorem, or historical priority claim is imported.
