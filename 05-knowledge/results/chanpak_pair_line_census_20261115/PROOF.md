# Physical pair lines: largest-order test and exact inverse reduction

Write `H(T)` for directed Hamilton-path count and `P₂(T)` for ordered
two-path covers with both paths nonempty. Use `Q=P₂+2H` (two slots allowing
empty paths). A reader line is a fixed affine slice through integer
pair coordinates: `r H+M Q=N`, equivalently `(r+2M) H+M P₂=N`.
A hit requires a single literal source realizing both coordinates, not
independent attainability or a residue hit.

## Exact arithmetic filters

For every nonempty tournament on n vertices,
`Q≥2ⁿ` (all 2-colorings of its vertices have at least one path per
nonempty color), and `Q≥(n+1)H` (split a Hamilton path at each
of its n−1 edges, then add its two one-empty placements).
For n≥2, `P₂≡2 mod 4`: complementary nonempty proper vertex
sets come in `2^(n−1)−1` unordered pairs, each product of path
counts is odd by Rédei, and ordering multiplies their sum by 2.
The repository's THM-4756 gives `Q≡(−1)^n H mod 3`; in particular
`N≡(r+(−1)^n M)H mod 3`. For `H+Q`, **all odd
source orders miss every target prime greater than 3**, irrespective
of size. More generally, fixed exact Q with a fixed reader leaves at
most two residues for N modulo 3 even if the source order varies,
because H modulo 3 belongs to {Q,−Q}. If 3 divides Q, just one
residue remains. Three consecutive odds require all three residues,
so a width-three window forces Q or the reader to change. These
are reader obstructions, not Chan–Pak obstructions.

Also `gcd(H,Q)|N`. For prime target N above H, the pair must be primitive.
When the reader outputs N>H, this rules out any source with a
nonprimitive SCC: both H and Q multiply through an ordinal join.
For target 7p where p is prime and H<p, `gcd(H,Q)` must divide 7.
For example if 3 divides H, the modulo-3 identity puts 3 in Q, ruling
out both prime targets >3 and 7p targets with p≠3.

## Delete only singleton SCCs before asking the target

Delete every singleton SCC and call the resulting core B. Its count h
is unchanged and its Q-value q gains a factor 2 for each singleton SCC
when they are restored. Allow the empty core with (h,q)=(1,1); to make
the source nonempty require at least one restored vertex in that case.
Consequently for **any fixed reader** with M>0,
`N=rh+Mq·2^s` is a necessary and sufficient inverse criterion:
choose an actual core with no singleton SCC, and a nonnegative s,
then `T=B⇒TT_s` is a literal witness. In particular the quotient
`(N−rh)/(Mq)` must be a positive integral power of 2, not merely
positive, approximately dyadic, or in a prescribed residue.
Singleton padding anywhere gives no other pair.

Using the Moon–Busch strong bound `H(C)≥3^{|C|/3}`, all nontrivial
SCCs of a core for h jointly use at most `3 log_3 h` vertices.
Thus for a fixed cap h≤B the core menu is finite. More quantitatively,
for any fixed finite bank of readers with M>0, the number of its outputs
≤X generated by sources with
`log₂ h ≤ (log₂ 3)/3 · sqrt(2η log₂ X)`, with 0<η<1,
is O(X^η log X). Indeed the number of labelled cores of order at most
`floor(sqrt(2η log₂ X))` is at most the sum of `2^(m choose 2)`
over those orders, hence O(X^η). For each core, outputs `rh+Mq2^s`
grow so that only O(log X) exponents fit N≤X (h itself is subpolynomial).
The bound holds for all physical sources and not just one-component
sources at those low heights. This explains why selecting only
fixed-count reservoirs cannot produce dense line coverage, even
if their low-order profile menu is exhaustive. Global coverage is
not addressed if h is allowed above the threshold, or if the reader
bank grows.

## Finite test and target-capped exactness

`census.cpp` enumerates every isomorphism class through order 10 by
adjoining a vertex in every direction assignment and canonizing by
degree and sorted out-neighbor-degree histogram, then permuting within
the tied bins. It is enough at the upper layers to delete a minimum
outdegree vertex. Each canonical pair has a literal witness.
The rows through 9 agree byte for byte with the previous kernel
catalogue. `capped.cpp` extends to high orders when Q is bounded.

Old directed odd-cycle packings survive when a vertex is adjoined
with twice their contribution to Q (the new isolated site in a packing
gets either color). Each directed triangle through that vertex gives
another disjoint *term of the sum*, with contribution `2^(n−1)`
from that single triangle packing on n vertices. Thus along every
deletion chain
`Q(T)≥2Q(T−v)+2^(n−1)·(# directed triangles through v)`.
Prune any partial source at layer k with Q>Q_cap/2^(n−k), and prune a
candidate before counting if the triangle-enhanced bound exceeds its
layer cap. Neither filter needs the deleted vertex to be chosen by a
particular structural rule. At every layer survivors are counted by
ordinary subset endpoint DP, certifying the exact output set.

The fixed readers tested include `3Q−H` and `6Q−H`, plus the
inherited reflected `1024Q−H`. The additional family uses every
nonempty mask on a transitive host of order at most six. In reverse
transitive order the mask is the integer M, and the reader is
`H+MQ−W(M)H`. To count W, choose the marked end of the first
path and the marked start of the second path. A pair of marks at
distance d has `2^(d−1)` ways when their orientations along the
host make their roles overlap, and one further way if d=1 from the
other role assignment. Thus
`W(M)=sum_{i<j both set} (2^(j−i−1)+[j−i=1])`.
Since W counts a subset of the M covers, `W≤M`.
When M=1 the reader equals H+Q; when M≥2 its output is at least
`H+M(Q−H)≥Q+1`, since Q≥2H. All tested nontrivial readers therefore
give N≥Q+1. There can be no source order ≥16 for N≤59,999, and the
uniform safe Q cap is 59,998. Thus the band census through order 15
is **all-order exact for the stated finite reader bank only**.
It does not subsume arbitrary nontransitive marked hosts, the entire
two-port kernel family, or the general tournament spectrum.

### Uncapped transitive host masks

The finite host restriction is avoidable without brute force in host
order. Appending zeros to the left of a binary mask does not change
(M) or (W), and thus does not change the line. Reversing the
transitive order for the mask, the exact recurrences are

    W(2m)=W(m),  W(2m+1)=W(m)+m+(m mod 2).

The new lowest bit contributes one distance term per existing bit
with total m, plus the adjacency indicator. On a fixed physical
source, the target leads to a bounded exact interval

    max(1, ceil((N−H)/Q)) ≤ M ≤ floor((N−H)/(Q−H)),

which follows from (0≤W≤M). Equivalently, write
(F_{q,h}(M)=h+Mq-hW(M)); then

    F_{q,h}(2m) = F_{2q,h}(m),
    F_{q,h}(2m+1) = q+F_{2q−h,h}(m)-h(m mod 2).

These give a one-bit-state absolute reader decoder. The new value
(2q-h) is a digit coefficient in the inverse recursion, not
automatically a physical new tournament profile. The decoder never
replaces the absolute test by a residue condition.

No order cap on transitive hosts is needed for fixed N. The upper
bound on M is at most N−1, since Q−H≥1. The argument that N≥Q+1 still
applies. Thus through 59,999 the only source pairs needed are the full
orders ≤10 and the exact Q-capped profiles at orders 11–15.
`all_marks.cpp` visits every possible mask and records an order
bitset per output; `targeted.py` returns literal witnesses when it
finds a pair on one of the few remaining target lines.

### Can padding perpetuate the order-ten window?

No: keeping the order-ten source and restoring s singleton SCCs
gives (H+2^sQ=2^s(H+Q)-(2^s-1)H). For odd s, **every**
output is 0 modulo 3 by (Q≡Hmod3) at order ten.
Even s dodges that formal obstruction but does not fix absolute
placement. With two pads and **all** order-ten source profiles (not
only ones in the original window), the dilated open interval
[147341,239995] contains 46,328 odd targets, only 42,533 of them
hit, with first misses 147343 and 147349. Keeping only sources
from the original window leaves 39,810 hits. See `dilate.json`
for s=1,...,6. This fails to lift an already perfect finite band,
rather than merely failing a count near the minimum of a reader.

## Reproduction

In this directory (the full order-10 sweep requires multicore time
and about a gigabyte of free memory):

    g++ -std=c++17 -O3 -fopenmp census.cpp -o census
    g++ -std=c++17 -O3 -fopenmp capped.cpp -o capped
    g++ -std=c++17 -O3 all_marks.cpp -o all_marks
    OMP_NUM_THREADS=6 ./census 10 > pairline10.tsv
    for n in 11 12 13 14 15; do
      OMP_NUM_THREADS=3 ./capped "$n" 59998 > "pairline_1_${n}.tsv"
    done
    ./all_marks pairline10.tsv pairline_1_{11,12,13,14,15}.tsv > all_mask_coverage.tsv
    python summarize.py all_mask_coverage.tsv > coverage.json
    python window.py pairline10.tsv > dilate.json
    python verify_witnesses.py

The profile rows are `order H P₂ code minimum_strong_code_or_dash`.
Coverage rows give `N all_TT_masks small_TT_masks anti_readers H_plus_Q`,
where each column after N is an order bitset. Capped classes are
complete independent of isomorphism tie breaking, because every
minimum-outdegree deletion satisfies the restriction and the inductive
catalogue contains its parent. No remote score data are required.
