## What the proof actually establishes

Each changed slot retains its source, full actual path, target owner,
endpoint pins, indexed arc and all unchanged owner clocks. The source1477
occurrence in the reversed second block is the reverse assignment of the
same two paths used in the first block. Its two shared coordinates force
these assignments to change together; literal injectivity excludes the
same one-slot changes as well. In the combined difference their electrical
source contributions cancel. This is legitimate circuit elimination with
retained paths, not subtraction of anonymous capacity counts.

For the surviving six-source vector `w` and target difference `v`, direct
selected-edge subtraction gives `Delta L=2(w v^t+v w^t)`. Because the
supports are disjoint and both charges have total zero, this changes
neither source nor target degree margins. The saved full vectors verify
`Lz=2w` and `Lz'=2v mod256` in the original network. Every row of `Delta L`
is therefore in its image modulo256, so it annihilates every old harmonic
vector. The root audit independently rebuilds each physical row from the
selected edge changes and checks that image membership by rowwise sums.
This verifies the sign and factor two without assuming the abstract
rectangle formula.

The extension to every dyadic modulus depends on the separate electrical
lane's proved original 2-primary exponent256. This audit does not replace
that dependency by the chosen scalar potential. The integer identity
`Delta L X=0` is valid, and is correctly distinguished from the false claim
that `X` itself is integer-harmonic. The original `4e1965 mod8` character
separates the individual target-changing slots; aggregate all-bank
preservation is not per-slot universal preservation.

## Exact synthesis with the assembly theorem

This is an application requiring the authenticated **full** eight-bit
truth table, not a reason to suppose electrical guards are generally
pairwise. For this one menu, its accepted states are exactly

```text
00000000, 01110111, 10001000, 11111111.
```

Taking every pairwise projection and enumerating all256 assignments
recovers exactly that table. Equivalently the whole predicate factors as

```text
b0=b4,       b1=b2=b3=b5=b6=b7.
```

Thus the complementary implication-component blocks of
[`BINARY_ASSEMBLY.md`](BINARY_ASSEMBLY.md) are precisely `{0,4}` and
`{1,2,3,5,6,7}`. With slot weights one their weights are2 and6, proving
budget6 by the assembly theorem. With the actual owner-clock costs in the
selected records, their weights are4 and13, proving budget13 for that
different additive cost. This exact correspondence explains the honest
eight-to-six simplification while retaining its two dynamic shared owners.
The retained component DAG happens to have no implication between these
two blocks; that independence is specific to this truth table.
