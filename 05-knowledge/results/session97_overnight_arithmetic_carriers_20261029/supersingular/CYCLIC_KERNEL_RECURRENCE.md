# A full cyclic Frobenius kernel formula: 7 versus 21, then Hamming to Golay

**Session97, 2026-10-29. Status:** PROVED elementary semisimple-module theorem;
FINITE-EXACT independent factorization and code certificates. This is an
application and synthesis of standard cyclic-code mechanisms, not a claim of
historical novelty for cyclic codes or Golay codes. It extends
[the six/eight-point carrier](RICHELOT_FROBENIUS_AND_FANO.md).

## 1. Statement with all types kept distinct

Let n>1 be odd. Let V be the even-subset space of n+1 points modulo complement,
with its intersection-parity symplectic form. Let F fix one named point and
cycle the other n points. In arithmetic, this is the two-torsion action for a
hyperelliptic curve over an odd finite field whose defining polynomial is
irreducible of degree n. The genus is `(n-1)/2`.

Write `ord_d(2)` for the multiplicative order of 2 modulo an odd integer d.
The exact number R(n) of F-stable Lagrangians is

```
R(n) = 0, if some prime p | n has even ord_p(2);
R(n) = 2^[ (1/2) sum_(d|n,d>1) phi(d)/ord_d(2) ], otherwise.
```

In the positive case the exponent is an integer. It counts reciprocal pairs
of binary cyclotomic factors. Choices in separate pairs are independent, so
this is also a source-labelled Boolean recursion: after r such pairs,
`R_(r+1)=2 R_r`, with a self-reciprocal component sending the count to zero.
It counts **all** stable Lagrangians, not merely those coming from branch
matchings.

Consequences include

| n | ord_n(2) | R(n) | Mechanism |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 2 | 0 | self-reciprocal component |
| 7 | 3 | 2 | one reciprocal pair |
| 11 | 10 | 0 | self-reciprocal component |
| 15 | 4 | 0 | prime3/prime5 obstructions |
| 21 | 6 | 0 | prime3 component obstructs despite prime7 |
| 23 | 11 | 2 | one reciprocal pair |
| 31 | 5 | 8 | three reciprocal pairs |
| 49 | 21 | 4 | one pair from order7, one from order49 |
| 71 | 35 | 2 | one reciprocal pair |
| 73 | 9 | 16 | four reciprocal pairs |
| 89 | 11 | 16 | four reciprocal pairs |
| 127 | 7 | 512 | nine reciprocal pairs |

Thus the seven/ twenty-one distinction has an exact explanation **in this
particular carrier**: adding the factor3 creates an unsplittable self-dual
summand. This is not an explanation of the tournament Hamilton-path
exclusions {7,21}, where both values have a different meaning.

## 2. Proof by reciprocal simple summands

Choose the representative of a subset class that omits the fixed point.
Then V identifies with the even-weight augmentation subspace of `F2^n`.
Let F act by coordinate rotation. It is the module

`F2[x]/(1+x+...+x^(n-1))`.

Since n is odd, x^n-1 is squarefree in characteristic two. The module is
semisimple and multiplicity-free, with one simple summand per nontrivial
irreducible factor of x^n-1. A stable subspace must therefore be a direct sum
of entire simple summands. Invariance of the symplectic pairing means that
eigenvalues lambda and mu can pair only if `lambda*mu=1`. Equivalently, a
factor f pairs with its reciprocal f*.

If f=f*, the corresponding simple summand is nondegenerate, so no nonzero
part of it can lie in an invariant isotropic subspace. Other components
cannot pay this dimension deficit. If f and f* differ, their individual
summands are isotropic and are in perfect duality; a Lagrangian must choose
exactly one. This proves the factor-by-factor Boolean recursion.

The factors correspond to orbits of doubling on nonzero residues modulo n.
The reciprocal of a coset C is -C. For a residue of additive order d, its
coset has size `ord_d(2)`, and it is self-negative exactly when `-1` belongs
to the subgroup generated by2 modulo d.

If a prime p|n has even order, the unique order-two element of the cyclic
subgroup of `(Z/p)^×` is -1. Residues of additive order p supply a forbidden
self-negative coset. Conversely, if every prime factor p has odd order,
`ord_(p^a)(2)` divides the odd integer `ord_p(2)*p^(a-1)` for every a.
Thus every `ord_d(2)` is odd and cannot contain -1, which has order two for
d>1. All nontrivial factors pair off. There are
`sum_(d|n,d>1) phi(d)/ord_d(2)` factors, proving the displayed exponent.

This uses all divisors, not only ord_n(2). A self-negative constituent cannot
be discarded by saying another constituent has a reciprocal partner.

## 3. Actual code generators and a transport to arithmetic kernels

For each reciprocal pair of irreducible factors, choose one factor. Let

`g(x)=(x+1) product_(chosen factors) f(x)`.

The cyclic binary code generated by g in length n has dimension `(n-1)/2`,
consists of even-weight words and is self-orthogonal. These are exactly the
augmentation-space Lagrangians. Its basis is the coefficient vectors of
`g,xg,...,x^((n-3)/2)g`. The generator retains which reciprocal factor was
chosen; the count R(n) forgets these labelled choices.

The corresponding length n+1 code is obtained by giving these words a zero
coordinate at the fixed point and then adjoining their all-one complements.
This is the full preimage of the Lagrangian under the subset/complement map.
The exact checker constructs every one of these kernels for
`n=7,23,31,49,71,73,89,127`; checks dimension, pairwise orthogonality and
rotation stability; and compares independent counts.

For n=7 the two even-code generators are bitmasks29 and23, i.e.
`x^4+x^3+x^2+1` and `x^4+x^2+x+1`.
For n=23 the two masks are7973 and5279, where bit i is the coefficient of x^i.
This convention, the cyclic coordinate order and the named fixed coordinate
are the complete source/port chart.

## 4. Verified Hamming → Golay extension

Exhausting all words gives the following extended-code weight enumerators:

```
n=7:  1 + 14 z^4 + z^8;
n=23: 1 + 759 z^8 + 2576 z^12 + 759 z^16 + z^24.
```

Both reciprocal choices give the displayed enumerator. The second code has
length24, dimension12 and minimum distance8. Puncturing at the named fixed
point gives a `[23,12,7]` binary code; the distance is exactly7 because some
weight-eight extended words contain the punctured point. Its radius-three
balls are disjoint and cover the full ambient space:

`2^12 * (C(23,0)+C(23,1)+C(23,2)+C(23,3)) = 2^23`.

This directly proves perfect triple-error correction, the classical binary
Golay parameters. Similarly the n=7 puncture has `[7,4,3]` parameters and
perfect single-error correction. Thus the Fano/Hamming carrier and the
Golay carrier arise from the **same reciprocal-factor selection mechanism**,
at Frobenius orbit lengths7 and23 and genera3 and11, respectively. The
Golay relation is not inferred merely from the dimension or the presence
of the prime23.

For n=31 all eight choices yield, by exact enumeration,

`1+620z^8+13888z^12+36518z^16+13888z^20+620z^24+z^32`.

They have the same weight enumerator; this alone does **not** prove that the eight
marked kernels, or even the underlying codes, are equivalent under arbitrary
coordinate permutations. The subsequent [four-port certificate](REVERSAL_AND_FOUR_PORT_HOSTILE.md) resolves this bank into exactly two coordinate-isomorphism classes. The marked source choices remain eight distinct
stable kernels. The n=23 perfect-ball identity does not extend to n=31.

The coding route is classical. Related primary literature on reciprocal
cyclotomic decompositions includes [Sloane–Thompson, Cyclic self-dual codes](https://neilsloane.com/doc/cyclic.pdf)
and [Consorte–Valdez, On Euclidean and Hermitian Self-Dual Cyclic Codes over
F_(2^r)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.03520). Our codes before extension are
**even-weight self-orthogonal codes of odd length**, not self-dual odd-length
codes (which cannot exist). The full self-dual code has length n+1 and a
fixed marked coordinate; its n-cycle fixes that coordinate, rather than
cycling all n+1 coordinates. This distinction is necessary when importing
cyclic-code results.

## 5. Quadratic and moonshine boundaries

When n≡3 mod4, the length n+1 subset model carries the canonical quadratic
refinement `q([S])=|S|/2 mod2`. On a stable Lagrangian, q restricts to a linear
F-invariant functional. The augmentation module has no trivial summand,
so neither does the Lagrangian or its dual. Hence that functional is zero:
every stable Lagrangian in this case is totally singular. In the present case n≡3 mod4, positive examples
necessarily have n≡7 mod8; products of primes of odd 2-order are congruent
1 or7 mod8. This explains why the Hamming and Golay extensions are doubly
even. For n≡1 mod4, this particular quadratic refinement does not descend
through complements; the symplectic theorem still holds.

Among the odd Ogg primes, exactly `7,23,31,47,71` have odd ord_p(2).
This partitions their **values as possible cycle lengths**, not their
characteristics. The branch-cycle length n and the characteristic of the
finite ground field are distinct parameters. The construction works also
at non-Ogg lengths73 and89, so it does not characterize moonshine primes.
Nor does it produce an action of a sporadic group on the fifteen Ogg primes.
The perfect Golay code gives a natural place to investigate Mathieu
symmetry next, but a claim about its automorphism group would require a
separate calculation or a precisely cited theorem. No such group computation
is included in this checkpoint.
