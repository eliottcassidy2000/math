# Independent audit: cyclic Frobenius recurrence and Golay extension

**PASS** proof review of `supersingular/CYCLIC_KERNEL_RECURRENCE.md`.
The statement is about odd cycle length n, not the characteristic of the
arithmetic ground field. The n=1 boundary is intentionally excluded.

The even-weight augmentation module of F2^n is multiplicity-free semisimple
because n is odd and x^n-1 is squarefree. Its simple factors therefore admit
no intermediate stable subspaces. Symplectic orthogonality pairs reciprocal
factors; each unequal pair contributes exactly one binary choice, while a
self-reciprocal factor is a nondegenerate simple block and creates an
unfillable isotropic dimension deficit. This establishes the all-order
count, not merely a small enumeration.

A self-negative doubling orbit of additive order p is forced when any prime
p dividing n has even ord_p(2). Conversely odd orders at every prime divisor
remain odd at prime powers and their least common multiples, so no divisor
has -1 in the subgroup generated by 2. The formula must use all divisors:
even ord_n(2) alone is not equivalent to a self-negative primitive-n orbit
(example n=15), though the prime3 and prime5 constituents still obstruct.

The quadratic refinement paragraph is correct. For n=3 modulo4,
q(S)=|S|/2 modulo2 descends through complement, and q restricted to an
isotropic subspace is linear. It is F-invariant. Since the augmentation
module and its dual have no trivial simple constituent, an invariant linear
functional on a stable Lagrangian must be zero. The extended code is doubly
even. Odd ord_p(2) implies 2 is a quadratic residue modulo p, hence p is
1 or7 modulo8; positive cases with n=3 modulo4 must consequently have
n=7 modulo8. This is compatible with, rather than an exception to, the
length-divisible-by-eight requirement for doubly-even self-dual codes.

checks the prime obstruction and divisor exponent against doubling orbits

all words. It recovers the Golay enumerator
