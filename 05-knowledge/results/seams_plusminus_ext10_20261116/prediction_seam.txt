Pre-registered predictions, klein-2026-11-16-seam, written 2026-11-16 before the runs below.  A seam is a pair of
tournaments differing by one port contact (T + v_X vs T + v_{X u x}) or by one arc flip, whose counts are c - 1 and c + 1;
c is the seam's centre.

S-1 (contact seams)  From bases of orders <= 8 and all contact sets, the even centres of one-contact seams cover every
     even number in [4, 2000] except 6, 8, 20, 22 (whose neighbours include the holes 7, 21) and at most 10 others.
S-2 (arc-flip lemma and seams)  H(T with the arc a->b reversed) = H(T) + M(b, a) - M(a, b) (0 mismatches on orders <= 7) and
     sum over arcs a->b of M(a, b) = (n - 1) h; one-flip seams from orders <= 8 cover every even centre in [4, 1000]
     except 6, 8, 20, 22 and at most 20 others; the flip digits Delta/2 take every value in [-30, 30] at orders <= 8.
S-3 (the +2 window by contacts)  For at least 80 % of the odd counts H <= 300 realised by extensions of bases of order <= 8,
     the window (3H + 2, 3H + 4) around 3(H + 1) is a one-contact seam from a base of order <= 8.
S-4 (changing the quotient)  Among the first-witness contact seams at centres <= 2000, at least 20 % join two
     tournaments with different numbers of strong components.
S-5 (contact digits)  The contact-flip digits |Delta|/2 over orders <= 8 take every value in [0, 100]; more than 5 % of all
     contact flips have digit 1.
S-6 (order-10 extension graph)  Contacts at order 10 lie between 9.4e7 and 9.7e7 (mean distinct parents >= 9.7 of 10); the

     childless in that tree.
