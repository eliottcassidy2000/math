## Inheritance and what changed the search

The previous [child-compression synthesis](collatz_child_compression_synthesis_20261007d.md)
proved deeper deletions on `t=0 mod2^47` and `t=0 mod2^519`. Those nested
guards buy depth without reaching the large parameter complement.
The closest mechanism is exact affine common-future substitution with its
native phase. The hostile is a repeated type on the wrong phase; the
corrected near miss is to equate a deeper deletion with wider coverage.
The least-used coordinates are the opposite sibling orientation and the
ternary address of the immutable original source.

The anchor is complement coverage, the niche is source-level inverse entry,
and the wildcard is reflection of a small commuting affine operation.
The concept board is source, ordered word, terminal reserve, two-base guard,
run length and ROOT obligation. Reversing one orientation adds cells;
varying a run turns one cell into a theorem; retaining ternary source data
supplies an independent address coordinate. Each operation changes a
different part of that board.

The old known source-level G5/G17 bank plus `t=0 mod2^47` has parameter
density approximately0.334705075445821. The new specified union improves
that declared comparison by approximately0.177451574802250, or17.7452
percentage points. This comparison is against named banks, not against
every certificate anywhere in the repository.

## Ternary source entries and the exact product

The [source-owned ternary packet](collatz_complement_guard_fusion_20261007e.md)
uses the word `1^k,a`, where a>=2 is even and k is the least integer with
`2^(k+a)<3^(k+1)`. On its exact native source guard it gives

\[
 h=\frac{2^{k+a}n-(3^{k+1}-2^{k+1})}{3^{k+1}},
 \qquad 0<h<n,\qquad F_{1^k,a}(h)=n.                              \tag{3}
\]

Minimality makes the positivity bound uniform over every positive native
source. The concrete a=4 and a=6 entries reach `t=84 mod243` and
`t=1007 mod6561`, disjoint from inherited G5/G17. Through a=12, retaining
G17 gives seven labelled alternatives and union `131325004/387420489`.
The [complete bounded inverse-word bank](collatz_bounded_inverse_cover_20261007e.md)
then enumerates every positive word of length at most8 with contracting
inverse slope:953 words,252 compatible Mersenne phases,12 counting cells.
Combining it with the reset-indexed bank leaves14 counting cells and gives

\[
                  \tau=\frac{150929272}{387420489}.                 \tag{4}
\]

The extra ternary mass is exactly332/6561. This is a complete declared
finite-word comparison, not a novelty claim for every inverse identity.
For instance its large added cell `t=3 mod27` comes from the already known
F91 word `(1,1,2,2)` and child `(64n-73)/81`. The progress is pulling known
and newly enumerated words into one authenticated source chart and counting
their exact union. All252 labelled children remain available.

A useful general positivity simplification emerged: full odd inverse
integrality forces every intermediate inverse value to be odd and integral;
each reverse step from a positive odd integer remains positive. Thus any
contracting positive valuation word already has `0<h<n` on every positive
native source. There is no additional lost finite native head. The theorem
does not cover all sources:7 has no smaller positive odd ancestor, although
its forward orbit reaches1. Complementary certificate constructions remain
necessary.

For example the formerly least binary hole t=4 is already paid by G5,
because4=1 mod3. The new child is `(8n-5)/9`, an actual odd integer; it is
not relabelled a Mersenne and its ROOT obligation remains open.

Finite binary and ternary guards combine by the Chinese remainder theorem.
For each all-run grammar, its uniform shrinking-prefix bound permits the
same law after passing to the limit. Hence the combined density is exactly

\[
               \beta+\tau-\beta\tau=1-(1-\beta)(1-\tau),           \tag{5}
\]

which gives (1). This is a congruence-counting theorem about the same t,
not a randomness assumption about orbit steps. Source-height cuts and
child identities remain part of pointwise receipt acceptance.

The placement of a ternary guard matters. After the old15-letter head,
the expanded endpoint Y has a locked ternary residue independent of t
through the stated depth. Every positive inverse word there of length at
most E-16 is still too large to pay the original `2^E-1`. Moving a useful
source guard to that endpoint would lose its payment predicate. The
source-owned entry (3) avoids this obstacle.

## A concrete uncovered family and preserved proof alternatives

The least parameter missed by all banks in (1), including both entire
infinite grammars, is23. More strongly the checker proves

\[
                 \boxed{t=23\pmod{13824}}                         \tag{6}
\]

is wholly uncovered by these rules. Nine binary digits and three ternary
digits suffice to separate this progression from every retained cell and
both all-run parents. The least source exponent is `E(23)=99708993705`.

A declared search for this supplied source finds no aligned sibling
collision with deletion1..128 and source depth0..1024, using8192 retained
bits. This is a finite template obstruction, not a proof that no later or
different certificate exists. The next precise target is a rule meeting
(6), preferably with a new word structure or source-level guard rather
than another refinement of the already covered zero phase.

The [guard-defect ledger](collatz_guard_cover_defects_20261007e.md) separates
two kinds of unfinished work: an uncovered parameter, and a covered
parameter whose available children are ungrounded. It retains every rule
label on a cell. A narrower rule can add zero coverage while supplying the
only available ROOT proof, so coverage antichains are counting tools only.
Actual common-future proofs propagate ROOT in both directions; only a
strictly smaller child carries a paid rank decrease.

The route toward completion therefore has two explicit obligations:
cover the residual source addresses with native paid rules, and ground
the resulting dependency graph without circular certificates. The present
work settles neither obligation universally.
