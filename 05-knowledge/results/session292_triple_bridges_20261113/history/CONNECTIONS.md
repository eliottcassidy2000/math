The packet reads the surrounding arguments, not merely matching keywords.
Detailed source locations are retained in each lane's proof/source files.

  cyclic-triple realization, prime duality and linear child permutations are

  automorphism/SCC-isotype assertions. New here: relative boundary equivalence,

  finite/global continuation and faithful operator transfer; neither the

These connections suggest retaining a finite, explicitly chosen interface
while allowing the invisible internal realization to vary. They do not
identify unrelated occurrences of three, five, 23 or 25 as isomorphic objects.
