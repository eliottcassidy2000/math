# A research synthesis: controlled loss, not universal frozen seams

The useful common principle is **a quotient is usable for construction only
when its continuation language is retained**. This session gives several
exact instances, not a claim that all open problems reduce to tournaments.

1. **Reflection word → partial affine action.** Composition forgets prefix
   feasibility and collisions. Restoring their interval and finite exception
   list turns an identity word into an exact infinite birth theorem.
2. **Prime congruences → conditional CRT product.** Independent factors exist
   only after conditioning on a shared exponent clock. The hidden-factor
   theorem shows why finite perfect filtering still cannot certify primality.
3. **Path networks → squarefree endpoint algebra.** Occupancy variables make
   vertex reuse vanish. Five terms in a two-pivot exchange come from ordered
   distinct visits, not from an unexplained universal significance of five.
4. **Cyclic triples → a modular realization fibre.** The inherited realization
   theorem preserves all induced path counts, but ports break some of its
   symmetries. Incidence colors identify exactly which symmetries survive.
5. **Sparse observation → comparison posets.** Relaxing selected guards is
   not uncontrolled guessing: it gives independent prime choices and linear
   extensions. This is the principal new constructive search space.
6. **Fixed spectral support → finite determination.** Eight observations
   determine an eight-mode exponential response; an omitted selected-gate
   coordinate can still distinguish physical continuations. A fixed observer
   theorem must not be silently promoted to all future operations.

## Three proof/experiment turns

The initial idea sought a full-support endpoint correction invisible on every
proper subset. Marked reciprocity first forces a paired reverse correction.
The module argument then rules out even that paired correction under the full
observer. This is a structural impossibility theorem, not a reason to abandon
endpoint constructions.

The second turn asks what the forbidden seam was trying to hide. Cubic
interior and quadratic port data already contain the entire relative boundary.
The colored modular formula reveals precisely where multiple actual
realizations remain. An engineered single port distinguishes all of them;
random ports require logarithmically many in the worst case. This separates
purposeful information placement from bulk measurements.

The third turn keeps only part of the distinguishing certificate. Its exact
solution is a product of linear-extension counts and unconstrained prime
reversals. Instead of searching for a non-existent invisible scalar seam,
search for two legal paths through this finite realization grammar whose
**changed** endpoint responses can be paid for by a genuine joining gadget.

## Next obligations toward Chan–Pak

- Choose a small, renewable endpoint interface, not the full frozen observer.
- Express retained quadratic constraints as the local comparison posets.
- Track the omitted response vector exactly along each permitted move.
- Produce a physical compensator and an absolute target equation, not only a
  difference gcd, residue cover, or dense limiting ratio.
- Prove target decrease and preservation of initialization/readiness. A
  family-wise descent must include initial cases and switching between cores.

A promising experiment compares linear extensions differing by one adjacent
incomparable swap. Such a swap is automatically a real tournament operation
and has localized LCA support; its full endpoint response can be computed
rather than assumed. Whether these responses admit uniformly renewable
compensation remains OPEN. The current results neither supply every odd seed
nor establish an eventual threshold.
