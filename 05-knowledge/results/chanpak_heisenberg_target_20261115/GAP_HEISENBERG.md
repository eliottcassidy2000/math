# A physical word monoid with simultaneous Heisenberg transfers

**Proved.** Strong one-apex tournaments already give a positive Heisenberg monoid in an exact triangle-count coordinate. Its natural weighted lift has an entire product of two finite Heisenberg groups in its reduced positive transfer monoid. In particular it controls the joint Hamiltonian path / two-cover profile, not just one scalar. This modular orbit does not by itself hit an absolute target.

## 1. The crossing polynomial is a weighted area

Use a transitive spine and one apex q. Write A at a spine vertex beaten by q and B at a vertex beating q. A word starting A and ending B makes the tournament strong: q reaches the first spine vertex, and every spine vertex can reach q through the last one. For a word w of length n put
```
a_w(t) = sum over A-positions i of t^(n-1-i)
b_w(t) = sum over B-positions j of t^j
c_w(t) = sum over i<j with (w_i,w_j)=(A,B) of t^(j-i-1)
M_w(t) = [[1, a_w(t), c_w(t)], [0,t^n,b_w(t)], [0,0,1]]
```
Positions start at zero. Directly splitting crossing pairs at the seam gives M_(uv)(t)=M_u(t)M_v(t): a_(uv)=a_u t^|v|+a_v, b_(uv)=b_u+t^|u| b_v, and c_(uv)=c_u+c_v+a_u b_v. At t=1 this is exactly the positive Heisenberg product, with abelian coordinates the two letter counts and central coordinate the directed triangle count. For each a,b≥0 it realizes every 0≤c≤ab: bubble-sort B^bA^a into A^aB^b, changing c by one at every step. With a,b≥1 and the strong boundary A…B imposed, the exact area interval is a+b−1≤c≤ab, by deleting the endpoints and applying the same argument inside. This is a full positive orbit in those stated intervals, not the full signed integer group.

Let h count Hamiltonian paths and Q count ordered two-slot covers with empty slots allowed. For an inversion A…B with gap g, a directed odd cycle through q uses its endpoints and an even number of middle vertices. There are no odd cycles outside q. By [the Rédei–Berge odd-cycle expansion](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10572v3#S3.SS4.Thmcorollary20), each such cycle contributes 2 to h and, when it adds k middle vertices, contributes 2^(n−k) to Q (choose its slot and those of unused vertices). Hence
```
h(w) = 1 + c_w(2) + c_w(0),
Q(w) = 2^(n+1) + 2^(n−1) [c_w(3/2) + c_w(1/2)].
```
This gives the same proof via all possible color assignments of unused singleton vertices and the cycle; the empty packing contributes 2^(n+1) to Q. All formulas read ordinary counts of a single physical tournament.

Exact central translation at t=2 is impossible in this word monoid at fixed horizontal coordinates and clock: diagonal 2^n fixes n and a_w(2) is the binary word read with an A bit set. Equal (2^n,a_w(2)) implies the very same word. Passing to t=1 loses more than an unobserved sign. The strong words `AABAABB` and `AAABBAB` have equal (a,b,c)=(4,3,10), but profiles (87,2424) and (81,2352). Put A^s before both and B^s after both, for arbitrary s≥0. Their profiles now differ by
```
Δh = 6·2^s,   ΔQ = 60·6^s + 12·2^s.
```
Indeed their raw differences (a,b,c) as polynomials are (−f,tf,f) with f=t(t−1)^2(t+1). On adding the prefix and suffix, the difference in the c polynomial is f[1+(t−1)(1+t+...+t^(s−1))]=t^s f. Substitution proves the formulas; the areas at t=1 still agree. In a fixed first-core readout N=LQ+(M−L)h with L,M>0 this changes the output by 6·2^s[M+L+10L·3^s], despite equal unweighted Heisenberg coordinates. This is an exact scale test, not just a modular collision.

## 2. Two full modular Heisenberg projections in a single physical semigroup

For every integer m>1 coprime to 2·3·5·7·13·19·37, restrict the words to concatenations of X=AAB and Y=ABB. Use simultaneous reductions of M at t=2,3/2,1/2 over Z/m, augmented by one additive counter modulo m for the number of blocks. Then the diagonal-one, zero-counter subgroup projects **onto the product** of the two full Heisenberg groups at 2 and 3/2. Moreover its center contains enough elements to control h and Q independently, without changing the block count modulo m or any diagonal clock.

Proof: the reduced matrices and counter give a finite group generated by the two physical blocks. Every inverse of a block in that group is a positive power of the same block. The two full Heisenberg factors here are genuine subgroups of the *projected reduced* monoid, not formal operations requiring physical deletions. Keep the third channel for the readout and the counter for adjacency.

Write V=XY^(-1) in the reduced joint group. At each t it lies in UT_3, with horizontal entries (t^(-2),−t). Conjugation by X acts on horizontal entries (a,b) by (t^(-3)a,t^3 b); central correction in a lift of a horizontal vector is harmless. Both V and each of its conjugates have zero counter and diagonal one. Use their products and powers to apply any polynomial in Ad_X to their horizontal coordinates: if f(z)=sum f_j z^j, take the product of (X^j V X^(-j))^f_j. This gives f(Ad_X) on the horizontal coordinates since those add in UT_3.

The four eigenvalues 1/8,8,8/27,27/8 are pairwise separated by units exactly under our coprimality assumption (a sufficient condition, not an exclusion proof for the omitted moduli). Lagrange polynomials isolate any one of them. At t=1/2 the two horizontal eigenvalues lie in the first pair, reversed. Thus obtain four vectors: a single unit horizontal coordinate in the first or second channel, none in the other channel; the third channel may also respond to the first pair. Taking the commutator of the two lifts in each pair gives central elements Z and T. The central coordinate in UT_3 of the commutator of (a,b) and (a',b') is ab'−a'b. Consequently Z has unit center at t=2 and zero center at t=3/2, while T has unit center at t=3/2 and zero centers at both t=2 and t=1/2. Centers and horizontals generate the full projected product, proving the orbit claim.

Every block has one adjacent AB and there is no adjacent AB across a BA block seam. Thus c_w(0) is the block count. Start with X and multiply by Z^i T^j. This freezes all diagonal clocks and the block count modulo m. In the h,Q readouts, h changes by a unit times i, and Q changes by some multiple of i plus a unit times j (the common factor 2^(n−1) is a unit). Solve this triangular system and replace inverses by positive words in the joint finite group, resulting in a nonempty strong physical core for every (h,Q) modulo m. These witnesses can also be required to have arbitrarily large order by appending as many copies of a suitable positive finite-group identity word as desired. There is no bound on their absolute h or Q implied by reduction.

This extends the project's [earlier mixed-depth Heisenberg idea](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-3452-unequal-depth-noncommuting-smooth-hensel-heisenberg-orbit-law.md) in a different direction: here evaluations of one word matrix supply the horizontal channels, and central commutators control two actual enumerative outputs. The carry loss in the exact coordinates is analogous to the [physical odometer versus its Heisenberg shadow](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2788-physical-modular-odometer-versus-heisenberg-bockstein-extension.md). Neither earlier theorem is needed for this proof.

## 3. The modular win still cannot replenish a physical progression

In the [two-core anchor inverse](../chanpak_quadratic_anchor_atlas_20261115/README.md), any fixed U and anchor lead to N=LQ(W)+(M−L)h(W), with L,M positive. The result above makes every residue in the image of this linear form attainable for every allowed modulus: in particular all residues if gcd(L,M,m)=1. The same W controls both coefficients, not two independently assembled modular gadgets.

For exact coverage, however, the W block grammar here has at most O(X^(1/3)) possible distinct outputs N≤X for fixed first core and anchor. With k blocks, the first and last spine vertices alone contribute 2^(3k−2) to h(W) (read their gap term in h); positive coordinates P(W), h(W) imply N≥M h(W). There are 2^k words at length k. The much sparser bound holds irrespective of modular distribution and even if many words collide on the same exact readout. So a second-core solver cannot simply take a finite-group inverse and pad it, or assume the unweighted area interval fills an absolute target interval. It must change the family or enforce an absolute-height bound as well as the residues.

Replay: `python heisenberg.py` checks 254 literal finite profiles independently by subset DP, full joint modular profile planes at six moduli (including a composite and prime powers), and the amplified collision identities. The infinite claims above are algebraic proofs, not inferences from these checks.
