1. [Delayed annular Collatz invoices](collatz_delayed_annular_invoices_20261013.md)
   retain the supplied source while changing the terminal. The new compiler
   uses the source's binary band as well as its residue. Eleven hostile
   prefixes extend to paid ones. Combining the concurrent native-tail repair
   with the new annular gates gives eleven complete paid progressions, each
   two or four times coarser than its exact-word modulus. The
   adaptive universal criterion, also proved in that concurrent packet,
   has a total finite tester and is equivalent
   to ROOT plus the specified global quadratic charge bound. Acceptance for
   every source is still the missing theorem.
2. [The fish's marked elliptic cover](fish_marked_elliptic_cover_20261013.md)
   turns a geometric norm constraint into a genus-one obstruction followed
   by a genus-nine necessary cover. Exact 2-descent and retained square
   roots exclude the entire field `Q(zeta11,sqrt5)` and any rational central
   parameter in `Q(zeta55)`. The genus-one existence question descends from
   the real degree-twenty field to its quartic subfield. Neither 36,518
   exact golden parameters nor 1,728 genuinely quartic parameters lift in
   the declared searches; this is not an emptiness proof. A placement in the full field, and then an
   attachment that changes the six-colour boundary relation, remain open.
3. [Keller transverse jet length](keller_transverse_jet_length_20261013.md)
   gives a canonical jet isomorphism and exact length `b*mu`. Changing a
   polynomial mate while fixing the component leaves the connection
   unchanged. A new argument must alter the admissible global carrier with
   a proved defect comparison; thickening the same one cannot force a
   contradiction.

The shared research move is concrete: a switch is useful only when the
original obligation is still identifiable after it. Here the identifiers
are a source and accumulated cost, a simultaneous square-root tuple, and
a global cokernel with its connection. This is a reusable design principle,
not an equivalence among the three famous problems.

## An arithmetic connection recovered from the concurrent work

The [new Selmer-growth packet](bsd_selmer_growth_20261013a.md) has the exact
shape `u_m=m*s+sum min(m,e_i)` after retaining the rational-torsion correction
and *complete* Selmer groups. The inherited Smith-bar formula has the same
shape. In our ordinary Keller carrier the connection makes the finite bars
disappear, leaving precisely `m*mu`. This explains both why comparing two
successive complete Selmer levels can detect low corank, and why repeating
ordinary Keller thickening cannot create a bounded competing length.

The same incoming arithmetic packet gives a legitimate tournament connection:
for congruent-number curves whose prime factors are all `3 mod 8`, quadratic
reciprocity orients the prime vertices, and a polynomial of the resulting
binary matrix controls the pure 2-Selmer dimension. The examples
`3*11*59` and `3*11*19` distinguish the transitive and cyclic triples.
This provides a structural use of familiar primes: the observable is a
Legendre symbol and the retained sidecar is the switch mask. It does not
give higher Selmer pairings or apply that congruent-number matrix to the
different elliptic curve forced by the fish. A useful next helper question
is which pairing data survives that exact tournament compression.
