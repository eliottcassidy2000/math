# Cocenter, minimal join failure, and free-Lie boundary audit

## Exact quotient

Unique ordered SCC decomposition identifies the rational ordered-join algebra with the free associative algebra on strong tournament types. The linear span of commutators is generated by differences of cyclic rotations, and no relation connects distinct cyclic orbits. Therefore its cocenter has the claimed cyclic-word basis, which the already proved metric-curl classification identifies with actual metric-curl types. This is an exact basis map, not merely equal counting series.

The distinction between a linear commutator subspace and a two-sided commutator ideal is essential and correctly made.

## Sharp eight-vertex join obstruction

Let a be the singleton letter and b the unique three-vertex strong letter. Words ab andba are rotations, whereas abab andbaab are not: the latter has cyclically adjacent equal letters and the former does not. Both expanded tournaments have8 vertices and H=9, with equal path-cover polynomials.

The claimed minimum is correct. Below total weight8, a multiset with at most one nontrivial strong letter has a single cyclic ordering. If there are two nontrivial letters, their weights are either3+3 with at most one singleton, or3+4 with no singleton. The size3 strong type is unique, so those possibilities also each have a single cyclic ordering. Three nontrivial letters require weight≥9. Hence all word multisets below weight8 have one necklace, making a join-congruence failure impossible there.

## PBW does not create a Lie embedding into the cocenter

The positive grading has finite-dimensional pieces, so the PBW Euler-product identity is well-defined degreewise. Its primitive/Lyndon counts agree with the connected-even graph counts already recovered in the October11 work. No new primitive-count theorem is needed.

Every Lie monomial of letter length≥2 is a commutator and maps to zero in the cocenter. The one-letter generators remain independent. Thus the image of the natural free-Lie projection is exactly V, not the space spanned by all primitive necklaces. Choosing a Lyndon basis permits a separate vector-space basis correspondence, but does not turn that projection into an embedding.

The Hamiltonian and full path-colour characters factor through the cocenter as linear maps and forget additional information; they do not supply a well-defined multiplication on the cocenter itself.
