The remaining inspection gives precisely p=2,3,11. More generally every pair
(k,p), k>=2, with p and kp in the same bracket is

    (2,2), (3,2), (4,2), (2,3), (3,3), (2,11).

Our fresh prime search through100000 checks this list; the inequality proves
its all-prime scope. Among these first doublings, mu(4)=0 while mu(6)=mu(22)=1;
lambda is+1 on all three. This is a particularly small geometric instance
where Liouville transports parity and Möbius detects self-divisibility.

**Leverage:** use this natural three-prime bank as a nine-state Möbius or
ten-state joint regression suite, including the E/O laws in THEORY D3. The
abstract minimal-carrier theorem works for any three distinct primes. The
bracket theorem explains the choice of this bank, not a special failure of
unique factorization or a theorem about future Collatz trajectories.

## E3. Möbius is the period guard that Liouville cannot replace (PROVED)

The number of primitive binary necklaces of length m is

    I_m=(1/m) sum_(d|m) mu(d) 2^(m/d).

There are2^m binary words of period dividing m; partitioning them by least
period and inverting divisor summation proves the numerator. Each primitive
orbit has m words. With a normal basis these are also Frobenius orbits of
field-degree-m elements, hence monic irreducible polynomials of degree m.

At m=11 the answer is186, and replacing mu by lambda accidentally succeeds:
every divisor of11 is squarefree. It likewise succeeds for m=66, whose
divisors use the whole {2,3,11} bank but never repeat a prime. At m=121 the
correct answer is `(2^121-2^11)/121`; the replacement adds2/121, not an integer.
The first such small failure is m=4:3 versus7/2. The checker enumerates all
binary words through length12, then performs exact arithmetic at121.

**Leverage:** degree11 is a positive control; degree121 is the hostile test
for repeated prime directions. Agreement on a squarefree divisor cube cannot
validate a purported all-size inverse. Here the squarefree guard distinguishes
exact period from repeated traversal while the normal-basis phase handles
transport. These are separate parts of the proposed composition theory.

## E5. A literal three-prime one-shot corner (PROVED)

[THM-2318 / one-shot-three-prime-mobius-amplifier](../../../01-canon/theorems/THM-2318-one-shot-three-prime-mobius-amplifier.md)
uses a fully minimal collision corner in Perron directions13,7,ell to isolate
a frequency13^(s-1)n with gcd(n,91ell)=1. Choosing ell=11 is legal, and gives
`n<=1001 J_A J_B-1` under its stated corner hypothesis. This does not assert
that a given native LRC source has that corner.

Here is an elementary unrestricted interval realization for ANY squarefree
Q>1, including66=2*3*11 and1001=7*11*13. On R/Z put

    A=1_[0,1/(2Q)),      B=1_[1/Q,3/(2Q)),
    P_d f(y)=(1/d)sum_(r=0)^(d-1) f((y+r)/d),
    I_d=integral (P_d A)(P_d B).

For a proper divisor d|Q, d<=Q/2: the projected supports are disjoint intervals
`[0,d/(2Q))` and `[d/Q,3d/(2Q))`, each of amplitude1/d. For d=Q the second
wraps to coincide with the first on[0,1/2); both have amplitude1/Q. Hence

    I_d=0 (proper d|Q),       I_Q=1/(2Q^2),
    sum_(d|Q) mu(d) I_d = mu(Q)/(2Q^2).

For Q66 the last value is -1/8712. Parseval, with
`Fourier(P_d f)(n)=Fourier(f)(dn)`, converts the left side to

    sum_(gcd(n,Q)=1) A_hat(n) conjugate(B_hat(n)).

The zero-frequency term cancels since sum_(d|Q)mu(d)=0. Absolute summability
of the cross products follows from Cauchy–Schwarz, so regrouping is valid.
This supplies a concrete finite-difference realization of the three-prime
guard, with exact rational interval controls at Q6,30,66,1001.

On these squarefree divisor cubes mu and lambda agree. If repeated directions
are introduced, their global selector identities differ:
`sum_(d|n)mu(d)=[n=1]`, while `sum_(d|n)lambda(d)=[n is a square]`.
The latter leaks repeated-prime frequencies. One-shot auxiliary primes can
therefore remove support without permanently enlarging the output clock;
forcing such a corner for an actual LRC current remains a separate problem.

## E6. Signed Collatz cycles: eleven counts operations, not prime factors

Their return equations have denominators `2^a-3^b`:1,-1,-139 for the first
three (a,b) pairs. Liouville gives an exact sign for the multiplicative factor

pairs(+1,+1) and(0,-1). Thus neither current arithmetic output suffices for
the next odd3x+1 operation. Support and affine carries need additional data.

types. Their defensible connection is that both describe transport histories
whose later observables need additional data. A useful next experiment would

no ordering-of-edges argument here proves unique or triple global basins.
