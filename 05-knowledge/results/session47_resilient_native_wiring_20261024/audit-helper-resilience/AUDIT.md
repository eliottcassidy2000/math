## Proof and causal-contract audit

For each charge q, pairwise disjoint r-subsets are a sufficient deletion certificate: a forbidden owner can meet at most one stored witness, so t+1 disjoint witnesses survive any t forced-U restrictions. No maximum-packing solver optimality is needed. At occupancy three the exact universal one-U test is that every complete triple fiber is nonempty and has empty total intersection.

For a fixed cardinality-r helper bank, any two assignments differ in at most 2r helper slots. Given an authenticated constant-margin core route and a total charge, choose at each destination arrival a currently allowed helper subset with the required residual charge. Every atomic event then has support at most the core-event support plus 2r. This is a simultaneous change between accepted arrivals, not a legal microscopic sequence of individual slot flips. The retained aggregate guard must be exactly the authenticated original paired charge after fixing target margins; extra integer-X, odd-primary, or changing shared-owner predicates require separate tests.

Time-varying restrictions may be handled causally if each destination restriction is revealed before committing its event, and every destination family has the certified coverage. They need not be cumulative. If restrictions instead mean instantaneous physical loss of a resource already occupied at the previous arrival, the arrival-indexed statement alone does not prove a legal response; that is a different temporal contract.

Prescribed initial and final helper records can be retained only when each prescribed endpoint is admissible under its own endpoint restriction. Arbitrary hostile final restrictions cannot guarantee an exact return to an incompatible initial helper assignment. Later reoccupation of a released own reserved resource is permitted under capacity-one reversible borrowing; strict temporal nonreturn would invalidate moving restored helpers.

No all-order native supply, complete-canonical-atlas rewiring, universal ordinary per-port phase preservation, or globally optimal helper cardinality is asserted.
