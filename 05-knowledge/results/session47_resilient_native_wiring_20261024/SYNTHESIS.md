# Session47: supply, compatibility, current, and the boundary between them

24 October2026. This session distinguishes several projections that previously
looked like one problem. A path record can retain its endpoints without
retaining a harmonic character. A complete charge bank can fail after a
restriction, or can fail to compensate the particular physical row that
needs help. A move alphabet can generate an integer lattice without connecting
a binary fibre. Nodewise dyadic current preservation does not imply that every
ordinary per-port harmonic phase is fixed. The useful unification is an exact feasibility problem with
explicit maps and separately checked obligations, not a numerical identity
between all its invariants.

## 4. Harmonic transfer is an extension equation

For a fixed old grounded matrix A and an enlarged matrix N, preserving
literal endpoint records does not prove preservation of any old harmonic.
The exact condition is that every requested old harmonic evaluation lie in
the image of the new harmonic readout. The transfer packet supplies image,
annihilator and Schur tests over finite rings, keeping the nonunit obstruction.

For coordinate inclusion, an order-respecting integer lift P can be certified
by an identity

    d N P = R A,   d odd, R integral.

General endpoint readouts have the corresponding stacked identity. The odd
multiplier is invertible at every dyadic modulus; it is not permission to claim
odd-primary transfer. The distinct positive two-adic Smith exponents suffice
for the all-level test. In the inherited order-eight bank the required moduli
are2,4,256. This reduces the number of precision levels, not the number of
actual row/character conditions or the need for an authenticated new carrier.

Pointwise coverage at one high modulus need not give an additive section or
coverage at lower moduli. The packet includes explicit small graph hostiles.
Likewise a valid harmonic lift does not automatically preserve a packet's
current after an old owner splits into several physical rows. Every new row
must be tested; summing them can hide equal and opposite failures.

## 6. Boundary memory yields exact generating functions, not automatic guards

The source suspension S(T)=a->T->b has a unique extension(a,P,b) of each old
Hamiltonian order. Its complemented target H remembers the moving old
endpoints as literal outer adjacencies. Incoming PORTS.md correctly keeps

Our explicit order-five example shows that isomorphic old targets can yield
nonisomorphic, nonconverse enlarged targets even when both outer ports are
uniquely identified by degree. The old target class alone does not determine
the enlarged one. This does not refute reverse restriction to the inner target;
it identifies the extra information needed in the forward construction.

There is a positive all-size combinatorial conservation law for n>=2. If c

    c(K_P(T))=c(T)+d_T(f)-d_T(l)-1,
    c(H(T,P))=c(T)+n.

series and an explicit fourth-cycle formula. At higher orders these are
closed walks, not necessarily simple cycles or Hamiltonian paths. This is a
precise bridge from endpoint incidence to generating functions; electrical
harmonic transfer remains a different predicate.

## 9. The cohesive framework and remaining obligations

The same pattern now has several exact forms:

- a cut versus a route certifies the weighted bottleneck;
- a helper hitting set versus weighted witness coverage certifies restriction
  resilience;
- a matrix-image witness versus an annihilator certifies harmonic transfer;
- shared-owner intervals and their boundary cost certify quotient lifting;
- directed blocks and actual clocks certify global-observer realization.

These are different dual certificates on different faithful state spaces.
The common principle is to retain the coordinate that makes a proposed lift
legal. Matching counts, integer spans or abstract charges is not enough.

[Bottleneck framework](bottleneck-theory/THEOREM.md) ·
[supply plus compatibility](bottleneck-theory/SUPPLY_TO_ROUTE.md).

Remaining concrete questions include the exact13-versus14 helper radius,
order-eight to order-ten readout transfer, and construction of sufficient
native compatible supply at every order. The all-size theorems here are
conditional compilers and obstructions, not a complete tournament wiring
classification. No Collatz, lonely-runner, Hadwiger–Nelson, or other major
open-conjecture consequence is asserted.
