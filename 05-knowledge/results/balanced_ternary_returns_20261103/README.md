# Balanced ternary branches need source changes and variable interfaces

**2026-11-03. PROVED scoped constructions and obstructions / FINITE-EXACT
certificates / CONDITIONAL induction reformulation.** The claim that every
positive odd Hamiltonian-path count except 7 and 21 occurs remains **OPEN**.

The strongest new result changes the induction's premise. The first C_m
output, with H=59, cannot reach the prime 181=3*59+4 by any sequence
of births retaining its old arcs, at any order. Interior insertion repairs
other branches, but a reusable construction must sometimes change the old
source relation. An explicit native excursion repairs this particular
obstruction while retaining the physical port names.

## Actual balanced branches and their limits

The prior source C_m has balanced pair

    (b,t)=(15*2^m-1,3*2^m+5),             m>=2.

In its actual two-apex presentation, two interior insertions transform

    202 0^(m-1)32 -> 202 0^(m-1)3332,
    (b,t) -> (3b+2,3t+2).

The old root and child, every old arc, and all physical names survive.
The new pair is primitive exactly when m is not 13 modulo 14; its guard
is a mod 43 condition, distinct from the prior mod 13 guard. The numerical
45*2^m-1 family was already known. The added result is the legal operation
on these sources and its named balanced output.

The concrete prime chain is

    59 -> 179 -> 541,
    (59,17) -> (179,53) -> (541,147).

Its first step uses the uniform two-insertion rule; its second inserts
one vertex at an interior cut. From the same H179 source a different
one-vertex attachment gives balanced H539, leaving the two-apex class.
This second branch is a positive operation control, but 539=49*11 is
outside the prime and 7p lanes with p prime.

The exact two-parameter word formula proves that extending the same
unsplit run cannot repeat the plus-two rule. The H541 source also has no
ternary successor after at most two arbitrary births; a three-birth
arbitrary extension is not excluded. [Interior proof and receipts](interior/NOTE.md).

## The prime obstruction has a sharp named repair

For the actual eight-vertex C_2, every one-vertex attachment and every
ordered pair of attachments is checked. No pair gives 181. An independent
literal path enumeration checks the full unpruned cube of 131072 pairs.
The SCC/divisor argument and the strong-order floor reduce every possible
extension of H181 to this finite universe. Thus this is an all-order
obstruction to retaining the old graph, even with unrestricted root choices.

Changing exactly three old arcs gives an H59 source whose original root
is two-ready. The inherited single-arc compiler realizes those changes
with three temporary births, six legal native moves, and three temporary
deletions, at peak order nine. All eight old names survive; the original
root-child edge is restored at the endpoint. Intermediate H values and
one intermediate reversal of that port edge are explicitly retained.

Two further births then give the balanced profile (181,59,59,181), with
fresh root and child names. Three old arc differences are minimal among
same-order H59 endpoint repairs that preserve the original root-child
edge and enable an induced H181 extension. This does not assert minimum
native-move distance or stepwise H preservation.

Full named rows, cuts, Hamilton guards, and all seventeen general two-port
birth queries are stored before and after the exchange. The H181 graph
retains an old global sink; its strong core has the same count.
[Relative extension theorem, sharp repair, and independent census](transport/NOTE.md).

The repair does not extend by resizing the same ready template. The five
exceptional two-ready families intersect 15*2^m-1 only at 59. At every
later m, a two-ready return would need the other classified mechanism:
an indegree-one newborn of a strong two-start parent.
[Exact intersection and remaining source condition](interfaces/EXCEPTIONAL.md).

## A seven-prime target forces a larger feedback interface

The actual C_4 has H=239. Its plus-four target is 721=7*103. Every interior
extension over every old feedback pair is checked through the strong-order
cutoff: 33948 insertion addresses, no H721 result. Two disjoint old
triangles force both apices of any final two-apex representation to be
old vertices, so fresh apex choices do not evade the test.

Therefore C_4 has **no induced H721 extension with feedback vertex number
at most two**, at any order. An extension retaining this source must use
at least three feedback vertices. A source-changing repair could avoid
that requirement. The independent audit uses a distinct 24-state transfer
and a greedy subsequence generator instead of insertion addresses.

## Old ports cannot recover the standard readiness interface by growth

For every m>=2, every vertex already in C_m remains neither one-ready
nor two-ready in **every induced extension**, including weak extensions
and arbitrary new attachments. A small named certificate proves this:
old in-neighbors, an undeletable directed triangle, and a fixed-start
path-count lower bound. The proof strengthens the inherited fact that the
immediate reset output has no ready root.

A fresh ready root can be constructed, but the tested one-birth repair
has count 26*3^m+23*2^m-27, above both ternary targets for every m>=2.
Its extra 3^m term comes from three possible runs through the old clone
block. It is a precise growth cost of that construction, not a lower bound
for every fresh-root strategy. [Persistent port obstruction](interfaces/NOTE.md).

## Assume the missing local property explicitly

Exact spectrum coverage does not supply arbitrary balanced representatives:
11 and 13 have no strong balanced root. More decisively, the inherited
H35 theorem excludes both standard ready interfaces at every order, even
though 35=7*5 and both desired successors 107 and 109 are prime.

The obstruction is to that reset mechanism: an actual common H35 source
does reach both 107 and 109 by other two-birth attachments. Its retained
old root has q=s throughout, while its defect changes. This suggests
allowing a graded interface instead of requiring balance or readiness at
every stage.

The [conditional reformulation](conditional/NOTE.md) separates three jobs:
select an actual lower-count source with an accepted target extension;
align the current source and its named ports with that source; and retain
the resulting interface for the next target. The generic native compiler
already handles alignment when excursions in H and explicit deletion of
nonport names are permitted. Universal acceptance of the local source
predicate is the unproved part. Assuming it gives a valid induction; exact
spectrum coverage alone does not establish it.

Variable readiness gives a second useful inverse equation,

    N=3h+2j,       gcd(N,h)=gcd(N,j) when 3 does not divide N.

It permits alternative predecessors when the j=1 or j=2 fibre is empty,
but arithmetic candidates still require actual source witnesses.

There is also an actual bridge back to the balanced method. From any strong
balanced source (b,t), a specified graded construction gives

    H=3b*2^k-t,    q=s=b,    F-H=b+t,             k>=1.

At a fixed k, a balanced source replacement (b,t)->(b',t') supplies the
desired graded step 3H+c exactly when

    3*2^k*(b'-3b) - (t'-3t) = c.

Our new balanced plus-two operation gives c=6*2^k-2, rather than 2 or 4.
This identifies an exact compensation the old methods still need. The
zero-prefix case follows a different formula and cannot be used to erase
that discrepancy. [Graded construction and structural rigidity](interior/GRADED.md).

The bridge has a concrete seven-prime control: the actual balanced pair
(2879,593), lifted at k=1, gives a named 17-vertex source with
H=16681=7*2383. Its family has a proved mod 49 clock. The graded chart also
retains two successive plus-two steps, 11->35->107. Its next required
parameter is excluded by the proved exterior rigidity, so these successes
are not promoted to unlimited iteration.
