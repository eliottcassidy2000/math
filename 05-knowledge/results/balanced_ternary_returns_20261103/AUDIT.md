## Proof obligations reviewed

The relative SCC reduction uses both strongness of the old source and the
proper-divisor inequality for 3h+2 and 3h+4. The weak two-flip mutant is
explicitly excluded from the guarded endpoint-distance proof. The H181
obstruction is for the actual named H59 source, not every source of that count.

The old-port theorem uses the zero-or-monotone fixed-start injection, the
positive rooted defect, and an old triangle that survives the required
deletions. The independent audit reconstructs the prior C_m directly in
chronological physical names and derives the five-run coefficients
(241,250,78,0,0). See [conditional/AUDIT](conditional/AUDIT.md).

The interior law is checked with the inherited 36-state transfer and an
independent 24-state representation. Exact matrix annihilation plus
Cayley–Hamilton proves the all-size recurrence; the sample grid is only
a control. The independent H721 menu generates distinct superwords by
greedy subsequence matching, whereas the producer enumerates insertion
addresses. Two disjoint old triangles ensure that every final two-apex
presentation uses two old apices, so the enumeration covers adaptive
re-markings as well.

The native repair is replayed without importing its compiler. Every
Hamilton guard, off-path complement, named deletion, intermediate count,
and all 68 saved general two-port query values are independently checked.
The all-3600 padded-template alignment scan verifies the positive endpoint.
The full radius-two exclusion with a fixed endpoint root-child edge makes
the three changed old arcs minimal in that stated metric. See
[interfaces/AUDIT-TRANSPORT](interfaces/AUDIT-TRANSPORT.md).

The conditional reduction keeps its unproved source-bank acceptance
premise explicit. The H35 empty ready fibres are inherited, while actual
common-source attachments to107 and109 are reconstructed from the inherited
finite bank. Neither branch is inferred from the absence of a ready root.
The small balanced gaps H11,H13 use a complete labelled universe plus the
strong-order bound, not just a sample of adaptive sources.

The graded exterior law is proved by a path-bin decomposition, with the
singleton and zero-prefix cases separated. The independent audit builds
the balanced-to-graded bridge directly from C3 and C_2 and checks both
the positive-prefix formula and its distinct k=0 boundary. The exceptional
ready-family intersection is proved by valuations; the finite grid only
checks its boundary cases. No arithmetic parameter is treated as an actual
source without a graph construction.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B 05-knowledge/results/balanced_ternary_returns_20261103/reproduce.py
```

The runner verifies direct source pins, runs every listed exact producer and
auditor normally and under `python3 -O`, and requires byte-identical output
against the saved receipts. Checks use explicit exceptions. The final
`verification.json` records scripts, receipts and source-manifest hashes;
`manifest.json` hashes the packet files. A replay confirms the stated finite
universes, not a larger one or the open universal hypothesis.

No Lean build, hosted CI result, literature priority, new A000568 record,
or full-spectrum proof is claimed.
