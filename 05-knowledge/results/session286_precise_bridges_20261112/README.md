# Session286: exact bridges beyond scalar coverage

**Status: proved family theorems and conditional constructive transfer; Chan–Pak OPEN.**

This session pursues the user's request for roundabout, precise connections to powerful mathematical tools rather than another large numerical coverage census. Four bridges turn actual tournament constructions into tractable objects, while retaining the information that prevents a family theorem from becoming a false completeness claim.

1. **Automata → Ising chains → numerical semigroups.** All32 constant-baseline full-central fibers over the inherited five-vertex core are classified. Four have a two-state grammar,28 are rigid. A complete nontrivial fiber has an exact gapless normalized interior and56 persistent boundary holes, governed by the semigroup generated by8 and17. Its actual count mesh still grows exponentially.
2. **Apéry normalization → an exact bounded-digit selector.** If suitable physical complete digit banks exist, target realization is equivalent to membership in a numerical semigroup and its reflected copy. The inverse constructs digits. The physical supply hypothesis is explicit, and a broad finite-alphabet obstruction shows why familiar banks do not supply it.
3. **Rerooting → long-range submodular Ising systems.** A literal reroot-and-triangle operation produces an all-order quadratic energy with exact coefficients. The unique extremizing words have a sharp position-dependent boundary. Minimum-cut methods optimize the family; a13-adic inverse reconstructs every spin from its reset count and proves injectivity. This decides membership only in the specified sparse family.
4. **Cyclic homometry → replica cancellation.** Old lonely-runner homometry creates actual tournament twins with identical interface data. Common toggles cancel all quadratic interactions in their difference, producing an exact constrained subset-sum polynomial. A concrete pair realizes a difference44 by cancellation of local differences−2390 and2434.
