## 1. Definitions (CITED unless marked)

### 1.1 Li filtration, singular support, associated scheme

For a vertex algebra `V`, Li's decreasing filtration is
`F^p V = span{a^1_(-n_1-1) ... a^r_(-n_r-1)|0> : sum n_i >= p}` (Li 2005). Then:
- `gr_F V` is a Poisson vertex algebra: a commutative `d`-algebra with a `lambda`-bracket. In Beilinson-Drinfeld
  language it is a **coisson algebra** on `A^1` (BD §1.4, §2.6), the classical limit of the chiral algebra `V_X`.
- `F^0/F^1 = R_V = V/C_2(V)` is Zhu's `C_2`-algebra. `X_V = Spec R_V` is the **associated scheme**, and its reduced
  variety is the **associated variety**.
- `SS(V) = Spec gr_F V` is the **singular support** (Arakawa-Linshaw 2019).
- `gr_F V` is generated by `R_V` as a differential algebra, so there is a canonical surjection
  `pi_V : C[J_infty X_V] -> gr_F V`, where `J_infty X_V` is the arc space (Arakawa-Linshaw; Li; Arakawa). Equivalently,
  `SS(V)` is a closed subscheme of `J_infty X_V`.
- `V` is **classically free** when `pi_V` is an isomorphism (van Ekeren-Heluani 2021).

For a strongly generated `V` with PBW basis, `F^p` is spanned by ordered PBW monomials of Li degree `>= p`. Here the
Li degree of `u^i_(-n_i - h_i)` monomials is `sum n_i`, and `gr_F V^univ` is the polynomial (super)algebra on the jets
`x_(t,j) <-> u^t_(-h_t-j)`, with `d x_(t,j) = (j+1) x_(t,j+1)`.

### 1.2 Arc spaces as mapping stacks

- `J_infty X = Map(D, X)`, with `D = Spf C[[t]]` the formal disc. For a curve `C`, the jets of `X` along `C` form a
  commutative chiral algebra (a commutative factorization algebra on `Ran C`).
- When `X` is derived, its Koszul model being the derived associated scheme, the same formula defines the **derived
  arc space** `DJ_infty X = Map(D, X^der)`. Its `pi_0` is the classical arc space.
- This is the "factorization algebra on a derived stack" in the owner's prompt, in its simplest instance: the
  factorization algebra of functions on a derived mapping stack.

### 1.3 OBSERVATION: Euler characteristic of the derived arc space of the fat point

Take `X = Spec C[x]/(x^N)` with `wt x = 2`. Then `C[DJ_infty X]` is the Koszul complex of
`(d^n x_0^N)_(n >= 0)` on `C[x_0, x_1, ...]`. Its graded Euler characteristic is
```
prod_(n>=0) (1 - q^(2N+n)) / prod_(j>=0) (1 - q^(j+2))  =  1 / prod_(m=2)^(2N-1) (1 - q^m),
```
a finite product.
- `H_0` is the classical arc space. Its Hilbert series is the Andrews-Gordon product, i.e. the character of
  `Vir_(2,2N+1)` (Bruschek-Mourtada-Schepers).
- So `AG_N(q) - 1/prod_(m=2)^(2N-1)(1-q^m)` is the alternating sum of higher Koszul homologies.
- `H_1` starts in weight `2N+3`. Its first class is the syzygy `x_0 d f - N x_1 f` used in THM-4671.

This is the precise sense in which the first non-arc relation of `Vir_(p,q)` (THM-4671) is the quantum image of a
`pi_1`-class of the derived arc space.

## 5. Supersymmetric QFT: what classical freeness means for 4d `N = 2` theories

For a 4d `N = 2` SCFT `T` with chiral algebra `V_T` (Beem et al. 2015):
- The Schur index is `char V_T`.
- The Higgs branch is conjecturally `(X_(V_T))_red` (Beem-Rastelli).
- The Macdonald index refines the Schur index by the `SU(2)_R` filtration.
- Andrews-Banerjee-Bhargava-Singh-Tao (arXiv:2507.06294v1, 2025) propose that the Macdonald index is the bigraded
  Hilbert series of `J_infty(R_(V_T))`. Its Schur limit is `char V_T = HS(J_infty R_(V_T))`, which is exactly
  classical freeness at the level of characters.

### 5.1 Audit of arXiv:2507.06294v1, Section 3

The paper states Theorem 3.1 (stated at the start of §3, proved in §3.2.3): for every strongly finitely generated
vertex algebra, the unflavoured refined character equals the Hilbert series of the arc space of `V/C_2(V)`.

**As stated, it is false.** At `t = q` it would make every such `V` classically free at the level of characters.
Two counterexamples:
- **Ising, `Vir_(3,4)`.** It is strongly generated by `T`, with `R = C[x]/(x^3)`. The arc space Hilbert series is the
  Andrews-Gordon product `prod_(n != 0, ±1 mod 7) (1-q^n)^(-1)`, with 6 states at weight 9; Ising has 5. This is
  van Ekeren-Heluani's theorem plus THM-4671.
- **The 4d example, `L_(-1/2)(sl2)`.** Weight 7: 324 vs 319.

**The gap.** The proof enumerates, after eq. (3.44), two sources of null states: top-degree images of singular
vectors, and normal ordered products of them. It omits a third: lifts of arc-ideal syzygies, whose top parts cancel and leave lower-degree
relations outside the arc ideal. THM-4671 computes the first such relation in closed form.

**What survives.** Their `(A_1, A_(2n))` statements, because these chiral algebras are classically free. Their
`(A_1, D_(2n+1))` conjecture, at the level of the Schur limit, is consistent with our boundary-level evidence
(HYP-9293), which is needed for it.

Typing: REFUTED for the general theorem as an external claim (CITED witness plus FINITE-EXACT). The repository's own
statements are THM-4671 and THM-4672; the latter is the exact certificate over `Q`.

**HYP-9298: arc-space `A_2` Rogers-Ramanujan identities.**
- The boundary `W_3` characters are products,

- **THM-4673 (PROVED, hand proof).** `V_(sqrt3 Z)` (`k = 1`) is not classically free. `G^+(z)G^+(w) = O((z-w)^3)`
  kills `G^+_(-5/2)G^+_(-3/2)|0>`. The arc ideal cannot contain its symbol `psi^+_1 psi^+_0`, because an odd square
  has no differential consequences.
- **HYP-9295.** No `N = 2` minimal model is classically free. The first failure is at weight `k+3`, Li 1, charge `±2`.
- **Contrast.** `N = 1` minimal models are classically free exactly for `(2,4k)` (Hao Li).
- **Reading.** The LG chiral algebra, the factorization algebra of the derived critical locus of `W` (S43, section
  2.1), is never the free quantization of the arc space of its `C_2`-superscheme.

## 6. Open problems and next tests

1. **Done.** The exact certificate for `L_(-1/2)(sl2)` is THM-4672.
2. **HYP-9293 falsifiers.**
   - `k = 3/2`: PASSED (spin 6 at weight 15).
   - `k = -4/3` to weight 16: free at 14; weight 15 and higher in progress at close-out.
   - `k = -6/5`: predicted failure at weight 18 (expensive).
3. **HYP-9294(a) falsifiers.** Predicted degree-`N` layers `{0,3,5,7}`, `{0,3,5,7}`, `{0,3,5}`, `{0,3,5,7,9}` and
   `{0,3,5,7}` for `(5,7)`, `(5,8)`, `(4,9)`, `(6,7)`, `(5,9)`. All PASSED.
4. **A proof of HYP-9293 at boundary levels.** The `L_(-4/3)` C_2 ideal is `(e, h, f)·Cas`, and its arc syzygies are
   the rotational ones. A THM-4671-style lift computation with symbolic `k` would show that their transfer
   coefficients vanish at `a = 2`.
5. **Physics.**
   - Does every interacting 4d `N = 2` SCFT have a classically free chiral algebra, with discrete gauging the source of
     failure (HYP-9296)? `(A_1, D_4)` and SU(2) `N_f = 4` are now done: free through weights 7 and 5.
   - Next: SU(2) `N_f = 4` at weight 6, `g2`/`f4` Deligne levels, and a `W`-algebra engine for `(A_2, A_3)` and `N = 4`
     SU(3).
   - Compare the Li-bigraded character of `L_(-1/2)(sl2)` with the Macdonald index of the gauged free hypermultiplet,
     the `Z_2`-even part of `prod (1 - T^(1/2) q^n y^(±1))^(-1)`. This uses the Gadde-Rastelli-Razamat-Yan `(q,t)`
     convention, not Song's or ABBST's `T = t/q`.
6. **Derived side.** Compute `H_1` of the Koszul complex of section 1.3 by weight, and test whether `ker pi` is always
   the image of `H_1` under a "quantum transfer" map. This would be a spectral-sequence formulation of classical
   freeness.
