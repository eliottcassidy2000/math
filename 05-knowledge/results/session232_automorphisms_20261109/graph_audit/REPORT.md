## 1. Endpoint quotient and minimum orders

**PROVED, passed.** For any finite group acting equivariantly on a set over its vertex set, the fibre of the quotient map over the orbit of v is the fibre over v modulo its stabilizer. In the tournament case an automorphism fixing an ordered spanning path fixes every vertex, so the stabilizer action is free. Therefore the exact coordinate is

    q(v) = e(v)/|G_v|,     H/|G| = sum over vertex-orbit representatives v of q(v).

There is no reason for each term to inherit the whole-space parity or gap theorem. In particular P7 has e(v)=27, |G_v|=3, q(v)=9, whereas dividing by the whole group gives 27/21=9/7. The separate code checks equality of actual orbit-set cardinalities, not only divisibility.

**FINITE-EXACT, passed.** Independently enumerating every vertex permutation as a candidate Hamiltonian path, over every labelled tournament of each order 1 through 5, gives maxima 1,1,3,5,15. This is independent of the producer's subset recurrence. Deleting the endpoint injects endpoint paths into paths of T-v. Thus q(v) <= e(v) <= 5 through order 5, and <=15 through order 6. The order-one case is immediate (its only endpoint count is 1), avoiding any dependence on an empty-tournament convention.

The following exact witnesses reproduce with full permutation enumeration of both paths and tournament automorphisms:

- n=6, mask26: endpoint counts [1,5,7,3,2,1], H=19, |Aut|=1, q(2)=7.
- n=7, mask290: endpoint counts [3,9,21,9,7,3,3], H=55, |Aut|=1, q(2)=21.

Mask bits are exactly the producer convention: lexicographic pairs i<j, bit1 means i→j. The minimum-order statements for raw and quotient endpoint counts therefore both follow.

## 2. Ordinal sums and action graphs

**PROVED, passed.** Every path in A=>B traverses A before B, so paths concatenate. The strongly connected components of a tournament are linearly ordered, and an automorphism cannot permute this chain nontrivially. This supplies the direct-product automorphism law even if the final asymmetric block is itself not strong. Consequently k initial C3 components multiply raw counts and group order by 3^k while preserving q at the fixed final root.

The free action of (C3)^k with the standard independent rotations has a Hamming graph H(k,3) on each orbit. For k=0 this is one isolated point. Endpoint-r paths form an invariant union of components because r is fixed by the whole group.

**FINITE-EXACT, passed.** The independent code explicitly builds edges from the generator rotating the first C3, then traverses graph components by BFS. It does not merely divide cardinalities or reuse the producer's group-orbit partition:

- C3=>B7: H=57, |Aut|=3, 19 whole triangles, 7 endpoint-r triangles.
- C3=>B21: H=165, |Aut|=3, 55 whole triangles, 21 endpoint-r triangles.

For the nine- and ten-vertex tournament automorphism calculation, the search exhausts all permutations within outdegree classes. Preserving outdegree is necessary, and every surviving candidate is checked on all ordered pairs. Paths are independently generated by a bitmask DFS.

The producer explicitly distinguishes the chosen action group from the full automorphism group of the disconnected action graph. This distinction is necessary and correctly retained.

## 3. Uncoloured simple-graph carrier

**PROVED, passed for every n>=3.** The core subdivision of K_n has minimum core degree at least2, including n=3. Leaf stripping removes exactly the attached chains, so the 2-core is intrinsic. A core vertex has one of three intrinsically distinguishable pendant attachments: length0, length1 or length2. These distinguish originals, L and R even when some full graph degrees coincide. The original-L-R-original path recovers each directed arc. Every graph automorphism restricts to a tournament automorphism; each tournament automorphism has a unique extension because its arc gadgets and attached rooted paths are rigid. The extension and restriction are inverse homomorphisms.

No hidden colours are supplied to either generic graph enumeration. The independent encoder also uses a different gadget ordering from the producer, reverses every graph vertex label, and then decodes the graph from its bare adjacency sets. It reconstructs the corresponding reversed-label tournament exactly and checks role counts.

Order, edge and leaf formulas are correct:

    |V(Gamma)| = n+5*C(n,2),
    |E(Gamma)| = 6*C(n,2),
    leaves(Gamma) = 2*C(n,2) = n(n-1).

At n=3 there are already six leaves. A simple undirected Hamiltonian path can have at most two leaves, so ordinary graph Hamiltonian-path counts are zero in every stated case. The admissible trace is spanning in the original vertices, not in all graph vertices. Its prescribed traversal original-L-R-original remembers direction, and the equivariant bijection with tournament Hamiltonian paths is exact. The producer correctly makes this predicate indispensable.

### Independent generic graph enumeration

The independent algorithm uses paired individualization and colour refinement on uncoloured adjacency alone. Refinement signatures comprise the current colour and multiset of neighbour colours. A mismatch of paired colour-class cardinalities rejects only impossible mappings. At each nondiscrete node it individualizes one source vertex against every target of the same class. At discrete leaves it checks the resulting adjacency bijection explicitly. Every actual automorphism follows exactly one branch, so this counts all graph automorphisms without assumptions about gadget roles.

Exact coverage is all72 labelled tournaments at n=3,4, plus B7, B21, both C3 towers, R7 and P7. For every graph the generic count equals the independently enumerated tournament automorphism count. ### Sign-even corollary

This additional claim is valid, although the audited producer note does not need it. An automorphism g of a tournament cannot have an even cycle of length2m: g^m would swap two opposite positions in that cycle and reverse their arc. Thus every cycle of every tournament automorphism has odd length, and every element has odd order. Faithful extension preserves element order, so every cycle of the corresponding graph permutation also has odd length. Hence every automorphism of Gamma(T) is even. The independent graph computation verifies the stronger all-cycles-odd statement in every finite control above.
