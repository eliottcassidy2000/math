## Meaning and stopping reason

The exact classification and a stronger unboundedness obstruction are complete in the abstract model. The next scientifically meaningful step would be to authenticate a native relation carrier and its complete physical guard, or prove a bounded packet-cycle coalescence invariant for a specific native class. Reusing an unrelated native dyadic charge label or declaring bounded treewidth sufficient would not establish such a transfer. No such authentication is claimed here, so this lane stops at the proved abstract boundary.
