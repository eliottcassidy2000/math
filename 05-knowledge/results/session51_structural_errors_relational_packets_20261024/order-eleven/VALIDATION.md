# Validation and provenance

The producer checker uses Python's standard library and explicit exceptions,
not assert statements. Separate normal and optimized processes must produce
byte-identical receipts. The copied packet must also replay from an unrelated
working directory without imports or files outside the packet.

The universes and independent checks are:

- Twelve odd partitions of11, generated recursively and compared with the
  explicit classification list.
- All2640 labelled regular sevens, generated by row-wise score pruning, then
  quotiented under all7! vertex permutations. A separate degree-pruned full
  arc-isomorphism search computes each full automorphism action.
- Fifteen regular nines reconstructed from pinned almost-regular-eight states.
  Full unrestricted automorphisms and all pairwise nonisomorphism are checked.
  Their completeness remains inherited from the certified Session50 input.
- All1024 labelled five-vertex quotients with one weighted root. Literal
  eleven-vertex refinement, strongness, S4 rooted orbit partitions, and full
  automorphisms on all66 surviving substitutions are checked. Sixteen strong
  quotient presentations are deliberately excluded as actual7+3+1 collisions.
- All120 degree-one and2040 degree-two C5 crossing matrices. Every one has all
  four singleton attachments checked for strongness and actual cells. The
  d2(1,0) regular-eleven collision is excluded. Literal C5^2 matrix orbits and
  unrestricted full actions on104 nonuniform class representatives are checked;
  the uniform weighted-cycle class is an additional zero-error control.
- All1024 three-triangle/two-singleton skeletons. An independent compressed
  five-block refinement is compared with literal eleven-vertex refinement.
  The analytic98-strong-attachments rule and exact collision criterion are
  tested for each skeleton. The780 actual skeletons form65 free S3-by-S2 orbits.
  On each representative, all216 sign/phase presentations are generated and
  literally partitioned under27 internal rotations. All1040 resulting full
  eleven-vertex automorphism groups are separately computed, recovering260
  rigid and780 zero-error classes.
- The exact known strong and total defect distributions and both first moments,
  using explicit inherited order9/order10 polynomials for SCC padding.

In total1232 nonregular strong order-eleven class representatives receive full
vertex automorphism checks, not just stabilizer lower bounds. The checker also
verifies15 regular-nine and3 regular-seven representatives, plus the standalone
C5 group. Some zero-error order-eleven families are covered only by the theorem's
coprimality and balanced-forest proof; the checker does not claim a full atlas.

The source archive preserves the Session50 regular-nine certificate and theorem,
and the audited Session51 order-ten theorem and receipt. SOURCE_PINS.json records
original paths, provenance, exact bytes, and SHA-256 digests. MANIFEST.json covers
all deliverable bytes except itself and explicitly named verification receipts.
No runtime network access, external packages, or parent directory is needed.

The source-completeness boundary is important: reconstructing15 nonisomorphic
regular nines with the expected groups does not alone establish that there are
no others. That completeness comes from the pinned Session50 authenticated
order-eight atlas and its proved unique-extension/reroot bridge. The new checker
independently validates the inherited representatives' local mathematical data.

Producer replay outcome: normal and python3 -O receipts are byte-identical.
The manifest verifier ignores interpreter-generated __pycache__ files and only
three named root receipt/manifest paths; every mathematical source and archived
input is covered. The relocated replay is required to reproduce normal.json
exactly, not merely its final coefficient.
