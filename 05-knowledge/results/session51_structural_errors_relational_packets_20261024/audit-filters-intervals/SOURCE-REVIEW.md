# Source review

Reviewed independently on 24 October 2026. This file records source scope, not a new literature-priority claim.

- [OEIS A005235](https://oeis.org/A005235): primorial-indexed positive offsets, starting at index 1. The retrieved definition and displayed first 19 values agree with the certificates. The page explicitly leaves universal primality conjectural.
- [OEIS A055211](https://oeis.org/A055211): lesser offsets start at index 2, with initial value 3. Index 1 is undefined. Therefore the user-supplied lower list starting with 7 begins at index 3, not index 1 or 2.
- [OEIS A046066](https://oeis.org/A046066): the increasing list removes repeated Fortunate values. [OEIS A045493](https://oeis.org/A045493) takes the complement within primes, including 2. Neither is the original index sequence.
- [Cejchan, Krizek and Somer, JIS 25 (2022), 22.1.4](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL25/Krizek/krizek3.pdf), Section 3: Conjectures 13 and 14 concern first prime endpoints on the two sides; the short-window theorems are sufficient conditions, not proofs of those conjectures. Example 19 explicitly records both 30030 +/- 17 and the two composite-offset endpoints at 17 squared. The audit does not rely on the paper's historical computational coverage claims.
- [Aricheta, Supersingular Elliptic Curves and Moonshine](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.07421v2), introduction and Theorem 1.1: the rationality condition is equivalent to vanishing genus for X_0^+(p), and the genus counts half the nonrational supersingular points at level one. The fifteen-prime list is a cited all-prime classification, not a consequence of checking only p <= 71. Appendix A and the inherited corrected note support the level-filtration distinction.
- [Delfs and Galbraith, Computing isogenies between supersingular elliptic curves over F_p](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.7789), Section 1: the total supersingular count and the piecewise rational count in terms of quadratic-field class numbers agree with the packet. The combined h(-4p), h(-p) formula uses primitive order class numbers when -4p is nonfundamental. The audit's independent reduced-form count verifies that convention and its conversion to the published piecewise expression throughout the stated finite range.
- [Booker and Irvine, The Euclid-Mullin graph](https://arxiv.org/html/1508.03039v2), introduction: least prime factor of one plus the product of previous outputs, not a first-prime offset from a primorial. The cited every-prime question is stated as open there; no new progress on it is inferred.
