## 6. Structural proof audit and non-consequences

The signed-equality theorem is correct for an authenticated product with only
the stated signed relations and complete additive guards, or with its asserted
closure separately proved. Given accepted endpoints, the change indicator must
be constant on every connected signed-relation component. Conversely updating
whole differing components preserves every signed edge; exactly the zero-sum
component unions preserve the complete additive guards. This proves the exact
accepted-interval formula in both directions.

After omitting zero-effect idle steps, strict routes are ordered partitions
into neutral component blocks. Binary alternatives have globally disjoint
reservations, so changing a source twice is exactly resource reacquisition;
changing each differing source once and every fixed source zero times is
sufficient for strict non-reacquisition. Splitting a neutral event into a
proper neutral subset and its complement produces accepted arrivals and cannot
increase any nonnegative additive event-cost coordinate. Thus minimal neutral
atoms suffice. Cycles test consistency, but do not limit component size.

The finite-group bound is explicitly CONDITIONAL on a supplied disjoint
packetization that is neutral for every integer margin and preserves full
relational closure. D(Q) then bounds the NUMBER of packets in a minimal
zero-sum event. The physical slots/clocks remain the sums of the actual packet
invoices. Bounding the largest D(Q) costs gives simultaneous upper bounds on
the same constructed route, not a sharp universal optimum. Without bounded
neutral packet sizes this does not give bounded physical locality.

The inherited D(C128 x C2^4)=132 derivation is valid. Over F2 the augmentation
ideal has maximal nonzero monomial degree127+1+1+1+1=131. A zero-sumfree
132-term sequence would give a vanished product with nonzero identity
coefficient, a contradiction. The standard131-term basis sequence supplies
the sharp lower bound. Independently, the audit enumerates all multiplicities
of subsets of the displayed132-term minimal zero-sum sequence and finds exactly
the empty and full zero sums, with2^132 total subsets counted.

The independent structural test examines all760 signed graphs on one through

The equality-path and alternating-path hostiles are correct abstract models.
The latter has two opposite assignments with the same exact population and

No unrestricted all-size repair theorem follows. All-order authenticated
