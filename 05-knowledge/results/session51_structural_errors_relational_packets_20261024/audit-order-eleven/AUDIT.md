## 5. Three triangles and two singletons:4680

Positive error forces all three triangle crossings to be nonuniform and the
product of their matching signs to be negative. Orient the majority quotient

beats s, beta_i=1 iff cell i beats t. The triangle core is already strong, and
whole-tournament strongness is equivalent to

    alpha !=000 and beta !=111.

There are49 named attachment pairs. Indeed the core must reach s, so some
alpha_i=1, and t must reach the core, so some beta_i=0. These conditions also
suffice, using s→t when either singleton lacks a direct needed core edge.

Actual-cell recognition does not assume the presentation blocks are canonical.
The singleton degrees are10−3|alpha| and9−3|beta|, distinct modulo3, hence they
cannot merge. A stable cell containing exactly one singleton has odd size and
can absorb only zero or two triangles. Absorbing two would form a seven-cell

Thus both singletons remain separate. The triangle cells can merge only all
three together. This occurs precisely when the majority quotient is cyclic
and both attachment vectors are constant: they then have equal core degrees
