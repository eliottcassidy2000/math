## 2. Frieze carries become a source-specific incidence test

An odd valuation word u has the exact matrix

\[
 G_u=\begin{pmatrix}3^{|u|}&B_u\\0&2^{A_u}\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad F_u(x)=\frac{3^{|u|}x+B_u}{2^{A_u}}.
\]

On authenticated native guards, the actual question is

\[
 \boxed{\det\bigl(G_u(x,1)^T,\;G_v(y,1)^T\bigr)=0.}
\]

Here the two displayed homogeneous images are the columns of the
determinant. This is equality of actual endpoints. Traces retain growth
data but forget B; the audited frieze minors retain carry data. The
universal carry exchange identity does not by itself make this determinant
zero. The new use is to test incidence with the source included.

For a fixed affine source relation, the determinant is linear in the
remaining source. Therefore the guarded solution set is **empty, the whole
native cylinder, or at most one source**. The
[debt-observer packet](collatz_debt_observer_integration_20261009.md)
implements this distinction with exact controls:

* x=y+16, words `(1,1,5)` and `(1,1,2,3)`: both denominators are128;
  numerator difference is `-54y+378`. The exceptional source y=7 gives
  the genuine join23/7 at5, even though the affine formulas differ.
* x=y+2, words `(1,3,3)` and `(2,4,1)`: numerator difference is identically
  zero. These exact odd words are legal for positive y=49 mod256;51/49
  meet at11. The seven-step Terras prefixes certify the larger cylinder
  y=49 mod128, whose endpoint may be even. At y=177 it is38, so one must
  not claim the same completed odd words on that larger cylinder.

The first case is Haar-null yet contains exactly the integer of interest.
The second is a reusable family. Both still require a terminal suffix to
ground a previously unresolved source. This trichotomy prevents a symbolic
identity detector from discarding valid pointwise certificates.

## 3. Adaptive blocks suggest a method, with the direction retained

THM-4611 succeeds by changing the unit of observation: one step need not

modulo d^k; updating consumes precision. One shared quadratic form and a

This is a useful construction pattern for our adaptive certificate

Applying the pattern to ordinary Collatz still owes the opposite target:
actual-source payment or a grounded terminal on every relevant leaf.
No covariance inequality alone provides that terminal.

The new checker also retains the precise hidden-state loss and the fact
that a coupling permutation and its inverse can have the same covariance
while their actions differ. A covariance quotient is useful for its
quadratic inequality; it is not a lossless orbit description.
