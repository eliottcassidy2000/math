## Approved scope

For every n>=15, let P=(a,b,x,...,y,c,z) be a closed blue Hamiltonian
word, with actual positional anti-reversal and endpoint outdegrees (n-5,4).
Then the literal labelled K_P(T) is reachable by three directed black K moves
in all open-middle cases; in all closed-middle cases with c outside I; in all
closed-middle cases with c in I and y outside I; and in the guarded closed
case I={y,c,h}, x->c, h not in {x,n-4}. Here N^-(a)={z} union I,
|I|=3, and J=r_P(I) gives N^+(z)={a} union J.

The statement does not classify orders below 15, impose a module, disjoint
ports, mixedness, strong-target or isomorphism filter, or prove global
black-core connectivity. The remaining closed end-pair boundary is not
silently removed from the universe. The companion addendum repairs one
further subbranch and has its own audit and publication target.

## 1. Actual port universe and operation coordinates

The endpoint hypothesis forces exactly three middle incoming ports. The
source path rules out b, and anti-reversal determines z's row from a's row.
There is no consistency condition requiring I and J to be disjoint. Internal
middle pairs range independently over reflection orbits, except adjacent
pairs, which are path arcs. Thus fixing physical I, middle status, and the
chord x,c leaves an affine family containing every allowed interior, not
merely selected examples.

The independent checker starts with one independent forward bit per unordered
pair and imposes exactly path, closing arc, P anti-reversal, actual endpoint
row, and the two stated branch bits. General GF(2) row elimination substitutes
these constraints. It does not import either producer checker or its union-find
or breadth-first solvers. Every directed join then reduces to the constant 1.
For each intermediate source the full anti-reversal equations of the actual
next word are inconsistent, proving that every member of that tested affine
family has a black word. K is implemented as complementation of precisely the
nonpath pairs. Both the undirected path XOR and the complete labelled final
matrix are compared to K_P(T).

For backward coordinates x_uv, the printed transport formula is correct:
a word-coordinate pair gains the time-complement bit, the cumulative
path-edge membership bit, and the label-order bit. Applying this to adjacent
pairs is exactly the directedness condition; applying it to reflected pairs
is exactly anti-reversal. The ordered-graph coordinate {u,v-1} has the correct
reflection and K-complement action. No abstract quotient path is being lifted.

## 2. Uniform structural proof review

The following is an audit of the displayed proofs, not an extrapolation from
the finite controls.

- Relocated-b final word. Direct composition r_F r_P fixes a,z and has the

  middle cycle of length n-2 for odd n and two cycles of length (n-2)/2 for
  even n. At B, N^+(a) in the middle is exactly {b} union I, of cardinality
  four. It must be invariant if F is blue. For odd n>=7 the single cycle is
  too large. For even n>=8 an even cycle length makes the automorphism order
  even, impossible in a tournament; if the cycle length is odd, neither one
  nor two such cycles has size four. This is an actual marked-row argument.

- Second-position movable cut. If x is a port but y is not, the reflected
  rows give x->z and z->y. The first outgoing port of z after x exists and
  has a predecessor beating z. The first word only changes the two joins
  around this insertion; the second gains exactly a->x, y->b, and b->c.
  The omitted old path pairs are restored after two complements. The first
  reversal compares c->b with y->a, and the second compares the retained
  a->b with complemented x->c. Each comparison has values 1 and 0. The
  last word is the relocated-b word above. If both x,y are ports, k=y works
  unless 3 is the third port. In that exceptional case k=n-4 works, and the
  first reflection product consists of two disjoint four-cycles. These
  are complete alternatives, not fixed-port guesses.

- Open adjacent end-run. Choose t=y except when the third port is y-1,
  when t=y-1. Then a beats the preceding vertex and t beats a, giving the

## 3. Six complementary-pair chains and the unbounded tail

Every printed pair in each chain is correctly ordered for n>=27. Directly
applying the two actual reversals gives the displayed next pair. In forward
pair coordinates the first Q2 link has right-hand side1; every other link
has right-hand side0. Thus each chain has total parity1. The endpoint-row
pairs are (4,n-6),(8,n-10),(12,n-14) in the open branch and
(5,n-3),(9,n-7),(13,n-11) in the closed branch. Each pair consumes an
incoming port. For n>=27 their six endpoints are distinct.

The open branch already requires y in I, distinct from these endpoints.
For the closed branch, Q2 reversal sends the known true S(y,b) to
S(n-5,z), and the exact neighborhood is N^-_S(z)={b} union J. Thus
n-5 belongs to J and4 belongs to I, outside the six endpoints. Both
branches would need four incoming ports, contradicting |I|=3.

These substitutions and disjointness inequalities establish the all-order
tail. The independent checker additionally tests each individual link
parity, not merely their sum, for n=27..260 and checks the printed final-word
permutation through n=260.

## 4. The 24 finite boundary systems, without hidden filters

The independent checker reconstructs the 24 raw edge systems for n=15..26.
They contain only P path, P-blue, closing arc, the explicitly stated branch
incidences, and Q2-blue at K_Q1(T). They contain no cardinality equation,
fixed I, mixedness condition, strongness condition, module assumption,
isomorphism representative or tournament enumeration.

General row elimination determines consistency and reduces each projected
T(a,v), 2<=v<=n-2, to either a constant or one free root bit plus a constant.
For a free group with p/q signs, the minimum number of zero row bits is
min(p,q). Forced zero bits add independently. Crucially, the checker also
constructs a full satisfying bit assignment attaining this lower bound,
checks every original equation, and checks its actual row weight. The cost
is therefore exact rather than merely a relaxation.

The independently recovered results agree with the frozen receipt:
- Open: consistent at n=15,17,19,21,23,25, with exact minima6,6,8,8,10,10;
  inconsistent at each even n from16 through26.
- Closed: consistent only at n=17,21,25, with exact minima6,8,10;
  all other orders in15..26 are inconsistent.

Thus no system admits the required three-port endpoint row. This finite
certificate fills exactly the boundary not covered by the six-chain proof.
