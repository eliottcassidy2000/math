## R1. Three charts remove artificial intermediate debt

Work in any real inner-product space, including the Eisenstein norm
Q(a,b)=a^2-ab+b^2. For four vectors z_0,...,z_3 put

    E = Q(z_0+z_1+z_2+z_3),       S = sum_i Q(z_i).

For each of the three perfect matchings p of four labels, let M_p be the
sum of Q of its two pair sums. Every unordered pair occurs in exactly one
matching, while each diagonal norm occurs three times. Polarization gives

    sum_p M_p = E+2S,       M_bar=(E+2S)/3.                 (R1)

Thus M_bar lies between E and S, and

    (E-M_bar)_+ + (M_bar-S)_+ = (E-S)_+.                   (R2)

This is a deterministic three-chart certificate; no random-source hypothesis
is used. Its coefficients 1/3 average descriptions of the same intermediate
partition, not the physical source masses. Both endpoint observations are
literally the same in every chart. The identity holds after summing over
all actual terminals and parent residue cells.

The average can be strictly better than every single chart. The allowed
Eisenstein vectors

    (1,0), (1,0), (1,2), (0,1)

have S=6, E=9 and middle energies 11,5,5. Every single chart pays at least
four units of positive two-step debt; the three-chart certificate pays
exactly three. This is an alphabet hostile, not a claim that those four
specific labels form an authenticated native first-arrival packet.

### Apply the identity to the actual source ledger

Use exactly the previous packet's primitive seeds, A_N(s), forward-closed
627-seed frontier, terminal u_s, exterior port p_s, full braid clock t_s,
depth d_s, sign v_s=(-1)^(t_s) A_N(s), and odd index m_s=(s-1)/2.
For any finite first-arrival cutoff, set

    E_k = sum_(u,r mod 2^k) Q(sum_(u_s=u, m_s=r mod 2^k) v_s).

At the first source-separating level K, E_K=S_arrived<=S_N. The inherited
binary debt is sum_k (E_k-E_(k+1))_+. Instead group two successive bits:

    D_4 = sum_(j) (E_(2j)-E_(min(2j+2,K)))_+.             (R3)

At each complete two-bit block, identify its four address children with
F_2^2 and use their three two-pair partitions. Equation (R2) shows that the
ensemble's intermediate positive debt is exactly the block debt, without
changing the signed terminal sum. Consequently

    E_0 <= S_N+D_4,          D_4 <= D_binary.              (R4)

The actual intermediate chart need not be a residue class modulo 2^(k+1);
its exact four-address partition is retained. Parent and child endpoints
are the genuine moduli 2^k and 2^(k+2). No physical time is skipped.

More generally choose an increasing sequence 0=k_0<...<k_j=K with gaps
at most three, and define

    B_3(E)=min sum_i (E_(k_i)-E_(k_(i+1)))_+.              (R5)

This is computed by a min-plus recurrence on levels, retaining the chosen
block word. Telescoping proves E_0<=S_N+B_3(E), and B_3 is no greater than
either the fixed two-bit or fixed three-bit debt. The word is an observation
controller; it is not a native trajectory or an accelerated physical clock.

**OPEN revised sufficient target:** for every N>=4096 and every finite
native first-arrival depth cutoff, prove B_3(E)<=S_N/2. This is weaker than
the preceding binary-debt target and still implies its intended ET energy
bound. The denominator always includes the original moving sources.
Universal entry is separate. The recurrence computes a finite certificate;
it is not an independent all-scale estimate of its cost.

### Why a spectral diagonalization alone does not finish the proof

Pad each terminal's source vector by zeros into M=2^K odd-index addresses.
Let P_k be orthogonal averaging on classes modulo 2^k. Then

    E_k = (M/2^k) ||P_k v||_Q^2.

These nested projections commute. The quadratic form E_k-E_(k+1) has
eigenvalue +M/2^(k+1) on P_k, eigenvalue -M/2^(k+1) on P_(k+1)-P_k, and
zero on the finer orthogonal complement. Thus an exact Haar basis already
diagonalizes all the debt forms. The missing ingredient is the **actual
source's mass on these modes**, not existence of an eigenbasis.

For M aligned unit charges with one terminal, E_k=M^2/2^k. Every block
controller pays M^2-M, including B_3. This hostile preserves the allowed
norm-one alphabet but not the special native source/terminal assignment.
Therefore palette membership, chart averaging and cheap gauged forcing
alone cannot establish the uniform estimate.

## L1. The three-chart action is an exact Eisenstein-lattice action

Let O=Z[omega], omega^2+omega+1=0. Its complex embedding is the triangular
lattice; a fundamental parallelogram for 2O is tiled by four translated
fundamental parallelograms for O. The quotient has four elements:

    O/2O = F_4,       since x^2+x+1 is irreducible over F_2.

Its three nonzero directions give exactly the three pair partitions of
four addresses. The inherited norm-seven element alpha=3+omega reduces
to 1+omega modulo 2, cycling those directions. In the address convention
0,1,omega,1+omega, multiplication is

    0 -> 0,       1 -> 1+omega -> omega -> 1.              (L1)

This provides a literal map from an arithmetic operation to the chart
family in R1. It does not identify a source's odd index with its charge.
Those are two distinct copies of the four-element module, linked by the
declared address convention.

The coefficient Haar map (x,y)->(x+y,x-y) has image

    {(u,v) in O^2 : u=v mod 2O}.                           (L2)

There is one four-valued residue guard (two coordinate parity equations),
and the image has index four over Z. Its exact
norm identity is Q(u)+Q(v)=2(Q(x)+Q(y)). Dropping u=v mod 2O permits
nonintegral children. This is the lattice information hidden by a real
orthogonal normalization of Haar coordinates.

One can act simultaneously on address and charge:

    {z_x}_{x in F4} -> {alpha z_(alpha^(-1)x)}_{x in F4}.  (L3)

The chart family is permuted, every energy scales by seven, and the marked
ROOT row transforms covariantly. If alpha z=(a',b'), then

    Q(alpha z)=7Q(z),       2a-b=(5a'-b')/7,
    2a'+b'=0 mod 7.

Dividing the new norm by seven preserves the original source weights.
The index-seven image guard and marked readout are indispensable; keeping
2a'-b' would silently change the observation. This does not pay real debt.

The same element has a controlled changing-modulus clock:

    alpha^3=19+18omega,
    ord_(O/2^r)(alpha)=3*2^(r-1),       r>=1.              (L4)

Proof: alpha mod 2 has order three. Alpha^3-1 has 2-adic valuation one,
alpha^3+1 also has valuation one, so alpha^6-1 has valuation two.
Subsequent squaring adds exactly one valuation because an element congruent
to 1 modulo 4 has its sum with 1 of valuation one. Raising to an odd power
does not change the valuation. Hence the asserted exact order.

The valuation is divisibility by the rational prime 2 in O; 2 is inert.
Modulo 2^r, the full unit group has 3*4^(r-1) elements. Alpha generates
3*2^(r-1) of them, a subgroup of index 2^(r-1). From r=2 onward, a
three-phase picture is therefore not full coverage of deeper coefficient
residues. Its return clock is arithmetic
precision, not the native braid clock. The numbers 18 and 19 arise here
from an explicit multiplication, not from a claimed cross-domain identity.

### The old C3 spectrum acts on the actual charge lattice

There is an exact representation map, not just a shared quadratic form.
Embed O in the centred three-coordinate lattice by

    iota(a+b omega)=(a,-b,b-a).

Its Euclidean squared norm is 2Q(a,b). If R(x,y,z)=(y,z,x), then R is
multiplication by omega in this embedding. The skew adjacency K=R-R^T
of the directed three-cycle is therefore multiplication by

    beta=omega-omega^2=1+2omega,       beta^2=-3.           (L5)

The actual three ROOT rows are exactly

    (2a-b,-a-b,-a+2b)=iota((2+omega)(a+b omega)).          (L6)

Thus their squared Euclidean norm is 6Q(a,b), explaining the inherited
trace normalization. C3's nonzero eigenvalues plus/minus i sqrt(3), the
Eisenstein norm, and the three-row embedding are manifestations of this
specific linear intertwiner. The first row is still the marked physical
readout; rotating it does not prove its fixed-row bound. The four-address
chart operation in R1 leaves that readout fixed instead.

## T1. A trace-norm bound for every bounded-arity substitution parse

Let G_b be tournaments built from singleton labelled leaves by substitution
into arbitrary tournament quotients on at most b vertices, b>=2. Arbitrary
ordered joins are allowed because they can be associated into binary joins.
Every original vertex and attached port occurs at exactly one leaf. For
b=3 this is precisely the ordered-join/C3 grammar.

Write K_B for skew adjacency, and ||K_B||_* for the sum of its singular
values. Put L=ceil(log_2 n). **PROVED for every B in G_b:**

    ||K_B||_* <= (2b-3) n L.                              (T1)

This also holds after any permutation, converse, or global Seidel switch
K_B -> D K_B D with diagonal D in {+1,-1}. It does not assert closure under
arbitrary local representation changes or native path complement.

### Proof: keep the heavy child out of the reversed rectangles

At each substitution node choose a child of maximum size, called heavy.
Order the other children that beat it first, then the heavy child, then
those it beats. Order recursively inside every child. All edges incident
to the heavy child agree with this observer order.

Every backward interface is therefore a complete rectangle between two
light children of sizes a_i,a_j. The skew rectangle has trace norm
2 sqrt(a_i a_j); reversing it costs at most

    4 sqrt(a_i a_j) <= 2(a_i+a_j).

At a node with q children, the total reversal invoice is at most
2(q-2) times its light-child population, because each light child occurs
in at most q-2 light pairs. Every light child has at most half the size
of its parent. Charge that population to its named leaves: each leaf is
charged at most floor(log_2 n) times. Thus all reversed rectangles cost
at most 2(b-2)nL.

The transitive staircase in this observer order has trace norm at most
nL. Indeed recursively split its order into two nearly equal intervals.
Their cross rectangle has trace norm 2 sqrt(ab)<=a+b; at any recursion
depth the total population is at most n, and there are at most L depths.
The triangle inequality now gives (T1). Every pair belongs to its unique
least common ancestor, so no pair is counted twice. Every port keeps its
original vertex. This is a representation theorem, not a claim of
capacity-disjoint physical wiring. QED.

The older cotangent formula sharpens the staircase constant, but is not
needed for this proof. The new point is uniformity over **all alternative
parse trees**, including extremely unbalanced ones. Summing a naive norm
bound at each heavy interface would lose this conclusion.

The order n log n cannot be improved to O(n) on this class. The inherited
regular-block spectral law applied to the k-fold C3 tower, n=3^k, gives

    ||K_(C3^[k])||_* = 2 k 3^(k-1) sqrt(3).              (T1a)

Indeed each positive singular magnitude 3^j sqrt(3), 0<=j<k, occurs with
total signed-pair multiplicity 2*3^(k-j-1). Every level contributes the
same trace mass. This is a direct consequence of THM-1960, not a new claim
for its spectrum. Arbitrarily deep reuse of these small atoms reaches
n log n spectral mass, still below Paley's order n^(3/2) mass.

## T2. Flat Paley spectra force quadratic exceptions after any such reparse

For a prime q=3 mod 4 let P_q have K_(x,y)=chi(y-x), with chi the quadratic
character and zero diagonal. Its familiar exact Gram identity is

    K K^T = q I-J,          ||K||_op=sqrt(q).              (T2)

For completeness, the off-diagonal character sum is -1: the number of
solutions v^2=z(z-1) is q-1, since
(2z-1-2v)(2z-1+2v)=1. This proves the quadratic character sum and (T2).
Thus no floating spectral estimate is needed.

If P_q differs in d unordered pairs from any B in G_b, then

    <K_P,K_B>_F=q(q-1)-4d.

Trace-norm duality and (T1) give

    d >= [q(q-1) - (2b-3) q sqrt(q) ceil(log_2 q)]/4.    (T3)

The same inequality allows arbitrary global switching of B. For fixed b
the lower bound is q^2/4-O_b(q^(3/2) log q). Hence neither a fixed cyclic
module bank nor **any fixed bounded-arity prime bank** admits O(q) exception
edges for all Paley tournaments. Since beta(D)>=d-q+1, changing the debt
from edges to cycles does not evade the obstruction.

The obstruction survives helper vertices if the original named Paley
vertices remain induced: restricting a G_b parse to them merely deletes
empty children and preserves the arity bound. The required exceptions
inside those vertices remain. A budget linear in an arbitrarily enlarged
helper population is a different resource claim, not a repair at fixed size.

An explicit exact consequence uses q=65519, which the checker verifies is
prime. With ceil(sqrt(q))=256, L=16 and b=3, every such parse has at least

    871,894,093 exception pairs.                          (T4)

This is a proved bound, not a search on a 65,519-vertex adjacency matrix.
The bound is conservative: square root was rounded upward.

Conversely, if an O(q)-exception representation with d<=Cq exists using a
maximum quotient size b, then necessarily

    2b-3 >= (q-1-4C)/(sqrt(q) ceil(log_2 q)).             (T5)

Thus the largest allowed quotient must grow at least on the order of
sqrt(q)/log q along this family. This is a concrete replacement for the
unqualified hope that a fixed collection of small modules always suffices.

### Native history and the scope of the obstruction

For a legal Hamilton path P, let A_P be its oriented skew path matrix.
The exact update and the exception tariff are

    K_(tau_P T)=-K_T+2A_P,
    dist(tau_P T,G_b) <= dist(T,G_b)+(n-1).               (T6)

The second inequality uses closure of G_b under converse; the reverse
inequality follows because the same path is legal after the move and the
move is an involution. Endpoints alone change the reflected score vector:

    outdeg_new(v)=n-1-outdeg_old(v)+1_(v=start)-1_(v=end).

These are the incoming exact native conventions. After r moves from a
zero-exception G_b baseline, d<=r(n-1). Combining with (T4), reaching
P_65519 from a G_3 baseline requires at least 13,308 moves **if such a path
exists**. The tournament move clock r is not a Collatz clock.

This is not an all-order reachability theorem or a refutation of a narrower
language that never reaches these targets. It is a uniform obstruction to
the proposed **representation** of those targets, even after optimal reparse.

## T3. An exact finite reparser that retains named ports

For every nonempty subset S of the original labels, store the least
disagreement cost f(S) with a G_3 baseline. Singletons cost zero. An ordered
two-part root contributes

    f(A)+f(B)+# {arcs of T from B to A}.

A cyclic three-part root contributes

    f(A)+f(B)+f(C)+back(A,B)+back(B,C)+back(C,A),

in both cycle orientations. Minimize over all nonempty disjoint partitions
of S, retaining the minimizer. Induction on |S| proves this is an exact
minimum over every labelled parse. This is an exponential reference
compiler, not a polynomial-time general parser. It neither discards shared
pins nor identifies equal-looking leaves.

The marked Hamilton path is legal in the decoded tournament T. It need not
be legal in the baseline B alone; any native move is checked against B plus
its exceptions. Reparsing changes the representation, never the actual arcs.

The checker gives minimum G_3 exception-edge costs 3 for Paley7 and 11 for
Paley11. These are different from their minimum total-order edge or cycle
costs. The C3[C3] control has cost zero. Along seven declared legal states
starting from C3[C3], the exact costs are 0,2,5,4,4,4,5. Every witness is
decoded back into the actual labelled tournament and its exception pairs.

It agrees with the subset-cost recurrence on every target: 760 have cost

## Rerouting after the obstruction

The spectral separation limits **module alphabets**, not every compact
description. Paley itself has the short arithmetic description
K_(x,y)=chi(y-x). A useful next grammar can therefore admit growing
character-defined prime nodes, with q, the field chart and all vertex/port
addresses retained, rather than expanding them into a fixed small bank.
Path complements still have (T6); compressing arbitrary repeated path sums
with a uniform budget remains OPEN. High trace norm does not imply a long
Kolmogorov description.

For ROOT, the corresponding repair is to change the intermediate chart
while keeping the endpoint observation fixed. The three-chart construction
does this exactly; rotating the actual charge or averaging away its marked
source direction would not. The remaining target is arithmetic control of
B_3(E) for the real source family, not diagonalization or a new coefficient norm.
