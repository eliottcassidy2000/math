## R4. Adaptive clipping on the same source-address tree — PROVED

Let F be a finite terminal-labelled source family and K a retained subfamily.
Keep the same address tree and zero removed leaves; do not renumber or shorten
the tree. At its levels0,...,L let E_F and E_K be the respective energies.
For a block i<j put

    d_F(i,j)=E_F(i)-E_F(j),   delta(i,j)=d_F(i,j)-d_K(i,j).

For every admissible partition pi with jumps1,2,3, the scalar positive-part
inequality gives

    cost_K(pi) <= cost_F(pi) + sum_(i,j in pi) (-delta(i,j))_+.

Therefore define the **boundary invoice controller**

    C(F,K)=min_pi [cost_F(pi)+sum_(i,j in pi)(-delta(i,j))_+].

Then

    B3(E_K) <= C(F,K)
             <= B3(E_F)+min_(pi optimal for F) sum_(i,j in pi)(-delta(i,j))_+.

Every quantity is a finite min-plus path cost on the same level DAG.
The proof is partitionwise, so it permits changing the optimizing partition
without pretending that the removed sources disappear without interaction.
Each block delta is the sum of the fine-level boundary deltas. The pair
allocation theorem from Session75 identifies these as signed interactions
touching a removed label, counting pairs of two removed labels once.
The original source denominator is unchanged. This is an exact upper
certificate, not yet a uniform small bound on its boundary invoice.

## The exact source cost of a catalytic escape — PROVED

For any binary vector-sum tree with quadratic energy Q, telescoping gives

    Q(root)-sum_leaves Q(leaf)=sum_merges beta(y,z),
    beta(y,z)=Q(y+z)-Q(y)-Q(z).

Thus its positive merge cost is at least
`max(0,Q(root)-sum_leaves Q(leaf))`. Suppose the original leaves have norm
budget S and neutral helpers of total norm H are added. Even if helpers
change the geometry to a free lattice, zero merge cost requires

    H >= max(0,Q(root)-S).

For M aligned norm-one original charges, Q(root)=M^2 and S=M. A zero-cost
catalytic representation therefore needs at least M^2-M added norm. This
is a generic hostile, not a native ROOT family or a criticism of THM-4824:
the theorem explicitly permits free addition of leaves, while our target
does not permit changing the source denominator. An isometric embedding
with the original leaves fixed preserves every merge cost of every fixed
tree; dimension alone cannot erase this invoice.

This has the same logical shape as the new aliquot example:20 admits a
shorter scalar expression, but its outer ports1,20 are not the original
inner divisor ports2,4,5,10. A valid compression must declare the changed
resources. There is no claimed conjugacy between the two dynamical systems.
