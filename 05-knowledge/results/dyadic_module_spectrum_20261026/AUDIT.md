# Internal adversarial review and reproduction

This is internal review with independent finite formulations, not an
independent-agent audit. No numbered canon theorem is promoted.

## Proof gates

1. **Three charts.** Each unordered pair of four charges occurs in exactly
   one matching; every diagonal occurs three times. This proves the average
   for every inner-product space, with no independence assumption. Positive
   parts are taken after averaging the intermediate energy. Averaging the
   positive parts instead gives a different, generally larger quantity.
2. **Fixed endpoints.** The source vectors, terminal vector, selected ROOT
   row and physical clocks do not rotate when chart partitions are averaged.
   Chart averaging is a proof operation, not three physical copies or three
   newly available ports. The mean is realized as a weighted direct-sum norm
   only as a representation of the same observations.
3. **Blocked target.** Finite telescoping bounds terminal energy by original
   source energy plus positive block debt. Optimizing blocks of at most
   three bits is legitimate, but computing that minimum is not an independent
   proof it is small. Universal entry is not inferred from arrived sources.
4. **Integral lattice.** The image of Haar lifting is u=v mod 2O. Alpha has
   index seven, so its transformed readout and normalization are necessary.
   The coefficient field and source address field are distinct objects with
   a declared map. The arithmetic twist order is not a native elapsed clock.
5. **Trace-norm proof.** Every backward quotient rectangle avoids the chosen
   heavy child. The light population shrinks by at least a factor of two
   along each charged leaf path. Cross rectangles are disjoint as owned
   pairs, although their vertex sets may overlap at different scales. The
   triangle inequality is applied to matrices; no disjointness of spectral
   eigenspaces is assumed. Ordered joins may be reassociated into binary
   nodes. The bound covers every parse, not just a particular decomposition.
6. **Paley and distance.** The quadratic-character Gram identity is proved
   directly. Each flipped unordered pair changes Frobenius correlation by
   four. Trace/operator norm duality gives a lower bound on all parses and
   their global switches. A large lower bound is not a reachability theorem.
7. **Native moves.** The path is checked in the actual decoded tournament,
   never just in its convenient baseline. A reparse preserves all vertex
   names and supplied external ports. The native tournament move count,
   Collatz braid clock and modular precision depth stay separate.

## Hostiles, first failed implications and repaired targets

- A single pairing need not avoid intermediate overshoot: the allowed
  four-vector example has energies E=9, S=6, and middles 11,5,5. The repair
  is an endpoint-preserving chart ensemble, not arbitrary selection of one
  supposedly favorable chart.
- Averaging arbitrary root directions is unsafe. THM-3729 and the previous
  ROOT trace examples retain the selected row. Here all three endpoint
  readouts coincide; this is the precise additional hypothesis.
- Aligned unit charges on M source addresses give debt M^2-M for every
  tested block controller. Thus all-scale control still needs a theorem
  about the actual transported source family, not its alphabet alone.
- A fixed collection of C3 modules cannot be made universal merely by
  reparsing optimally: the trace-norm lower bound gives quadratic Paley
  exceptions. The survivor is an exact finite reparser and a grammar with
  growing arithmetic prime nodes. Native all-order coverage remains OPEN.
- A large spectral norm or trace norm does not mean a long description:
  a quadratic-character formula describes Paley compactly. The obstruction
  is specific to bounded-arity substitution baselines with sparse edits.
- A global Seidel switch preserves the trace-norm obstruction. This does
  not license arbitrary internal switching as if it preserved all external
  module interfaces or the original rooted response.

## Explicit finite universes

- `check.py`: all 12^4=20,736 ordered quadruples from the inherited signed
  norm-one/norm-three alphabet; 1,800 make every single intermediate chart
  strictly more expensive than the endpoint-only debt. All 5^4=625 integer
  Haar controls on coordinates -2,...,2; twist orders through 2^12.
- Literal ROOT source construction at N=4096,11002,18275,65536,131072,262144.
  All primitive seeds s<4N, including zero charges, retain terminal, depth,
  exterior port and full clock. Each B-chain is summed directly. The fixed
  frontier has 627 entries and is checked forward closed with a decreasing
  supplied rank except at root 1. Loops or a 10,000-edge unresolved route
  cause failure; neither occurs in these finite universes.
- Every distinct native arrival-depth cutoff and, separately, clock cutoff
  at the first three listed scales. These are not all rectangular joint
  depth/clock cutoffs and not all-N prefix checks.
- `scan.py`: every integer 4096<=N<=65536, using a distinct three-event
  source-update construction. Source-separating energy is checked each time.
  Full profiles and all controller costs match the direct B-chain code at
  every shared control N. The declared S_N/2 threshold has zero violations.
- Module compiler: all 64 labelled order-four tournaments; seven declared
  states each at orders seven and nine, and three at order eleven. Every
  returned parse is decoded to the original labelled arcs plus its explicit
  exception set. Paths and score updates are checked literally. These are
  chosen walks, not exhaustive native depth balls.
- `audit.py`: independently generates all 760 labelled five-vertex J/C3
  baselines as actual bit matrices, then compares direct Hamming distance
  with the cost recurrence for every one of 1,024 target tournaments.
  Also checks 625 address/charge intertwining examples, marked-readout
  covariance, and aligned-charge hostiles for M=2,...,4096.
  It also checks 225 centred C3/Eisenstein intertwiners and the inherited
  tower spectral multiplicities through k=8 using integer coefficients.
- `spectral_check.py`: 96 deterministically seeded hierarchical examples
  with n in {3,4,7,11,19,31,63,127} and quotient arity at most 3,5,7.
  Checks owned rectangle reconstruction, heavy-child exclusion, exact leaf
  charges and the rational upper invoices used in the proof. It does not
  infer a theorem from numerical singular-value computations.
- Paley Gram matrices checked exactly at primes 3,7,11,19,31,43. The large
  prime 65519 is checked by trial division; its lower bound is evaluated
  using integer upward rounding of sqrt(q). No large adjacency enumeration.
