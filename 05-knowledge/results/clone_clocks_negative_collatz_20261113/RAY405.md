# The 405·2^k+71 ray: what a repeatable ready return would have to be

## 1. The arithmetic target

The inherited return [MODULE_RETURN](../cycle_opening_six_return_20261112/compiler/MODULE_RETURN.md) gives
`45·2^k+7 → 135·2^k+23` with readiness one. The next value is `405·2^k+71`. Its first member is 881, the old
ladder `97→293→881→…` at `k=1`. By the [finite-atlas theorem](../session296_multicore_metagraph_20261113/cores/FINITE_ATLAS.md),
an indefinitely repeated exact `3H+2` needs a ternary clock beside the dyadic one. The natural repeatable form is

    H(j,k) + 1 = 3^j (45·2^k + 8),        j ≥ 0, k ≥ 1,

which is the C2 line of [CLOCKS §2](CLOCKS.md) with `F(k)=45·2^k+8`. In `r` coordinates this is
`r=3^j(45·2^{k−1}+4)`. Its 2-adic anchors are `7, 23, 71, …`: the dyadic rays converge 2-adically to the
forbidden count 7 and its C2 images.

## 2. Clone realizations are closed or unobserved

* **Single dyadic module at fixed j (inherited, PROVED).** A fixed `Q[TT_k]` cannot be a ready `405·2^k+71`
  family (the [complete 881 fibre](../mrb_seven_cutoffs_20261113/README.md)). No homogeneous inflation of a
  ready 881 source reaches 1691, and the first six adjacent steps of the ray are likewise not inflations
  ([multicore FAMILIES](../multicore_metagraph_20261113/FAMILIES.md)). Readiness there means an in-degree-one
  root with defect 1.
* **Corollary (new, PROVED).** A ready repeatable return along the ray cannot be a double-cloning family. At
  `j=2` such a family is `Q[TT_k]` with `Q=S[u←TT_2]` fixed, which the 881 fibre excludes. This holds whether or
  not the anchor-asymmetry hypothesis is true.
* **Non-ready double cloning (FINITE-EXACT, hypothesis OPEN).** No two-clock in the complete census through
  order 9, nor in the near-transitive scans, has the C2 shape. None contains any ray of the 45 or 462 chain.
  The constant term `c_{11}=−1` never appears ([CLOCKS §5](CLOCKS.md)).
* **Single ternary clock at fixed k (FINITE).** The `j`-direction alone would be a digit-1 clock of amplitude
  `45·2^k+8 = 98, 188, 368, …`. The observed digit-1 amplitudes through order 10 are
  `60,100,156,180,260,300,468,540`, and none of the 416 screened digit-1 clocks is ready at its first member.

## 3. Why "selective ports" collapse back to modules in uniform constructions

Let a family grow one transitive block, and let each exterior vertex meet that block in a threshold.
Thresholds at bounded distance from an end split off a bounded part, so the middle is a module and the family
is a clone family on a larger fixed core: the first bullet. One threshold moving with `k` splits the block into
two modules of sizes `t, k−t`. Exact dyadic growth then forces the split-invariant relation
`a_{21}=a_{12}=2a_{22}`, hence `H=a_{22}2^k+(a_{11}−4a_{22})` for every split. Consecutive members still differ
by inflating one block, which the inherited adjacent-step theorem excludes for ready sources at `k≤6`. Its
all-`k` extension is the inherited OPEN hypothesis. Several moving thresholds give several modules with the
same conclusion, or non-dyadic rates (`4^k`, tribonacci) as in the eigenvalue table of
`scripts/eig3.py`/`scripts/twoclock_search.py`. In the ready (nested-funnel) language the only growing blocks
are tail runs, which are modules. Repeating a single funnel word gives eigenvalues such as `2^t+1` or the tribonacci
constant. None of the 944 blocks of one to three words (at most nine vertices) has eigenvalue exactly 2, or any
`3^a` with `a≤5`, in its second state block (`scripts/eig3.py`). This is exhaustive over
those blocks; it is not a theorem about all ready tournaments.

**Remaining escapes.** (i) Cores that change non-uniformly with `k`, like the
[Session296 entrances](../session296_multicore_metagraph_20261113/cores/ENTRANCES.md) for `k≤9`, now extended by the concurrent
[changing-core inversion](../changing_core_returns_20261113/README.md) to 29 of the first 30 values (see [INCOMING](INCOMING.md)). (ii)
`k`-automatic (numeration-driven) cores, which are not exponential polynomials in `k`. (iii) A change of
arithmetic. The certified Π family (CLOCKS §3) is an exact, physically repeatable two-clock return, forever, on
the +1 anchor (`H↦3H−2`, `H↦2H−1`, one-arc `H↦(3H−1)/2`). It is the mirror image under `x=−H` of what the C2
line asks for. The anchor-asymmetry hypothesis predicts that this mirror cannot be undone by cloning.

## 4. Reading for Chan–Pak

Exact clone clocks realize only odd digits (Theorem 2 of CLOCKS). In `r` coordinates they are `r↦3r+even`. The
balanced-ternary digits `±1`, the `3r±1` Collatz branches, are the scarce part of the full-digit sweep: they are
exactly the non-multiples of three, and they cannot come from any clone clock. They must come from births at
roots of digit 0 or 2, or from non-clone surgery. This is where the inherited
[full-digit sweep](../two_branch_rearming_sigma_sweep_20261104/README.md) places the difficulty. Nothing here
proves completeness.
