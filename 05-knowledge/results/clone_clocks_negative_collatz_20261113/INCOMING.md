read here as mathematical signal. Each item says how it meets this packet; no claim of theirs is re-proved.

* [changing_core_returns_20261113](../changing_core_returns_20261113/README.md) (opus). Exact arbitrary-tail
  inversion gives **unit-ready witnesses for 29 of the first 30 values `405·2^k+71`**. At `k=17` the
  26-vertex source has an in-degree-one root of defect 3. The fixed-balanced classification finds 62 count rays
  at `t=71`: `404·2^k+71` is physically repeatable and `405·2^k+71` is excluded. A one-birth ray step needs
  newborn defect ≥72. *Meeting point:* these are escape (i) of [RAY405 §3](RAY405.md), cores that change with
  `k`, now realized for almost every small `k`. They are not a uniform family, which matches the corollary that
  a ready repeatable return cannot be double cloning. Their 881 neutral-component analysis (single-arc
  H-neutral paths cannot repair) and this packet's one-arc reversal (not neutral: `H↦(3H−1)/2`) are two
  different uses of single arcs.
* [growing_t_returns_20261113](../growing_t_returns_20261113/BOARD.md). None of the three complete strong
