## 2. Self-lines without the cube: the twisted-Burnside engine

Fix `g in S_n`. The presentations `t` with `g . T_t = T_(kappa t)` (the tournament and its tile-complement isomorphic through `g`) satisfy, for every pair `e = {u < v}` with `a = g^-1 u`, `b = g^-1 v`,

`y_e + y_({a,b}) = [e is a tile] + [a > b]` over `F_2`, with `y_e = 1` pinned on the path pairs.

Following the cycles of `g` on unordered pairs, the system is consistent iff each cycle is (the twist bits sum to `0` on cycles without path pairs; pinned cycles agree with the pins), and then its solution set is an affine subspace whose dimension is the number of unpinned consistent cycles. So `Q_n`, the set of self-line endpoints (THM-849), is the union over `n!` permutations of small affine subspaces, and the number of `g` producing a given `t` is `|Aut(T_t)|` (THM-849's torsor). A parity union-find solves each system in `O(n^2)`; `n = 9` takes `14` s, `n = 10` three minutes, `n = 11` half an hour in Python. The same engine with the twist `[e is a path pair] + [a > b]` gives the presentations whose path is *reversal-neutral* (`T_t` isomorphic to `T_t` with its path arcs reversed), which are exactly the lines joining converse classes, since `(kappa T)^op` reverses precisely the path arcs; and the unpinned all-pairs twist reproduces the classical self-converse count as a control (`2, 8, 12, 88, 176, 2752, 8784`).

## 3. The sigma-twin theorem and the fundamental black graph

`sigma` fixes exactly the blue lines and acts freely on the black ones (THM-643; klein's L2). The new point is where the twin goes: `sigma t` represents the converse class of `t` (THM-644 (a)), so the twin `{sigma t, sigma kappa t}` of a black line joins the converse classes of the two endpoints, i.e. **the same pair of converse-merged nodes**. Hence every merged black multiplicity, loops included, is even, and the merged black multigraph is `2B` for a multigraph `B` with `2^(m-2) - 2^(e-2)` edges, the *fundamental black graph*: its degree is `tc(c)` (odd) at a merged non-self-converse node and `(tc(c) - bm(c))/2` at a self-converse node. Two corollaries the owner's "forced counts" program can use directly:

- **Handshake law:** `#{self-converse classes with (tc - bm)/2 odd} = (T(n) - SC(n))/2 (mod 2)`: `1, 2, 6, 40, 84` against `1, 2, 22, 184, 3352` non-self-converse merged nodes at `n = 4..8`, every sum even.
- **`SC(n)` is even for `n >= 3`:** the blue mass `sum_(SC) bm(c) = 2^e` is a sum of odd numbers.

Verified at `n = 4..8` by the C++ census: all `438,216` merged black multiplicities at `n = 8` are even, and every twin joins the predicted classes (`1,046,528` of `1,046,528` black lines). THM-849's `|Q^K| = 0 (mod 4)` is the twin statement restricted to self-lines.

## 4. Symmetric-core depth: a stratification of black lines by their blue core

Let `Delta_k` be the sub-staircase of the central path interval `[k, n-1-k]` (`C(n-1-2k, 2)` tiles, of which `floor((n-1)/2) - k` on the altitude). The *depth* of a tiling is the least `k` with `t|Delta_k` sigma-symmetric; depth `0` is blue. `kappa` (which toggles all bits) and `sigma` (which preserves `Delta_k`) preserve depth, so lines and class fibres have depth profiles, and

`#{t : depth(t) <= k} = 2^(m - (C(n-1-2k,2) - floor((n-1)/2) + k)/2)`.

At `n = 8`: `2^12, 2^17, 2^20, 2^21`, i.e. tilings by depth `4096, 126976, 917504, 1048576` and lines by depth `2048, 63488, 458752, 524288`. Under THM-793's descent `p` (strip the outer ring), `depth(t) <= k` iff `p^k(t)` is blue at level `n - 2k`, and `p` commutes with `kappa`: **every black line of depth `k` is a ring-decorated lift of a blue line at level `n - 2k`**, its two tournaments inducing on the central interval the two ends of that blue line. On a self-converse class the depth-`0` count is odd and every other depth count even (`sigma` pairs the non-blue tilings of the fibre). At `n = 8` the mixed classes have aggregate profile `(4093, 3576, 23428, 27612)`, the pure-black `(0, 123400, 894076, 1020964)`, the pure-blue `(3, 0, 0, 0)`; the numbers of classes touched by depth-`k` tilings are `176, 6628, 6870, 6863`. Remark on new line systems: for every nonzero `v in Fix(sigma)` the translation by `v` is a fixed-point-free involution commuting with `sigma`, so THM-643's T3-T5 hold verbatim for the `v`-lines with the same global counts; the owner's lines are `v = 1`, and specific members (mirror, diagonal, layer flips) are mac-mini's THM-4730.

## 5. The tribonacci partner of the transitive node

So the owner's starting node, the transitive class, has at every `n` a fully explicit neighbourhood in the line metagraph: one blue line to a rigid self-converse node of fibre `h(n)` and blue multiplicity `h(floor(n/2)+1)`.

## 7. New terminology fixed by this session

- *Twin lines* and the *fundamental black graph* `B`: black lines come in `sigma`-twins with identical merged endpoints; the merged black multigraph is `2B`.
- *Symmetric-core depth* of a tiling and of a line; *blue core* of a black line (the blue line at level `n - 2k` it lifts).
- *Reversal-neutral presentations* `Q'_n`: Hamiltonian paths whose reversal leaves the tournament's type unchanged; they are exactly the lines joining converse classes, and obey the leg law `s_0 = s_(n-1) + 1`.
- *Realisers* of a self-line: the coset `R_kappa(t)` of `Aut(T_t)`; the *realiser spectrum* (cycle type, dimension, path arcs kept).
- *The tribonacci node* `T*_n`: the transitive node's partner, with `(tc, bm) = (h(n), h(floor(n/2)+1))`.
