# Field-Paley operators: integral stability is not metric integrality

Let `p=3 mod 4` be prime, `q=p^3`, and use the finite field `F_q`, not the
ring of integers modulo `q`. Put `K_uv=chi(v-u)`, with quadratic character
`chi(0)=0`, and `L={x in Z^q:sum x=0}`. Character correlation gives

`K^T=-K`, `K1=0`, `K^2=J-qI`.

For example, the off-diagonal correlation is `sum_z chi(z(z-1))=-1`:
count `w^2=z(z-1)` by transforming it into `(2z-1-2w)(2z-1+2w)=1`.
Each nonzero first factor gives one solution, so there are `q-1` solutions.

## Minimal maximal-order-stable enlargement

On `V=L tensor Q`, identify `sqrt(-p)` with `K/p` and put

`gamma=(K/p-I)/2`, `O_p=Z[(-1+sqrt(-p))/2]`, `Lplus=L+gamma L`.

In the integral basis `b_i=e_i-e_*`, the matrix is
`M_ij=K_ij-K_i*`. Its diagonal entries are ±1 and its off-diagonal entries
are even. Thus `C=(M-pI)/2` is integral, `gamma=C/p`, and `content(M)=1`.
The equation

`gamma^2+gamma+(p+1)/4=0`

shows that `Lplus` is `O_p`-stable. Every stable lattice containing `L`
contains `gamma L`, proving that it is the unique least such enlargement.
The standard trace/norm integrality calculation identifies `O_p` as the
maximal quadratic order. The older order generated by `(I+K)/2` is
`Z+p O_p`, of order index `p`.

Since `L subset Lplus subset p^-1 L`, the additive quotient is the image
of `C mod p` in `L/pL`. Hence

`Lplus/L = (Z/pZ)^r`, where `r=rank_Fp(M)`.

This is an additive quotient, not an `O_p`-module quotient: the original
`L` is not stable under `gamma`.

## The exact rank, including the centering correction

More generally, for `q=p^t`, let `h=(p-1)/2` and `N=(q-1)/2`.
Frobenius expands

`(Y-X)^N = product_(j=0)^(t-1) (Y^(p^j)-X^(p^j))^h`.

There are exactly `(h+1)^t` nonzero monomials, indexed by exponents whose
base-`p` digits lie in `[0,h]`. Their coefficients are nonzero modulo `p`.
Evaluation on the field factors the character matrix into two polynomial
evaluation matrices and an invertible diagonal coefficient matrix. Each
evaluation matrix has independent columns: a polynomial of degree less
than `q` vanishing at all field elements is zero. Thus

`rank_Fp K = ((p+1)/2)^t`.

In characteristic `p`, `K^2=J`, with the **positive** sign. Therefore
`ker K subset H={sum x=0}`. The centered matrix is restriction to this
codimension-one space, so its rank is the full rank minus exactly one.
One must not split off the all-ones vector as a complementary summand:
it belongs to `H` because `p` divides `q`.

At `t=3` this proves

`r=((p+1)/2)^3-1`, `[Lplus:L]=p^r`.

In particular the indices are `3^7` at order 27 and `7^63` at order 343.
The rank has prior literature: Pantangi,
[Difference families, skew Hadamard matrices, and Critical groups of doubly-regular tournaments](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v26i3p59/pdf/),
Theorem 6 (2019). The monomial argument above gives the exact operator and
centering translation explicitly; no novelty assertion is made.

## Three distinctions that cannot be discarded

1. **Rational versus integral similarity.** The centered operator is
   rationally similar to `p^2+p+1` copies of the **p-scaled** prime-field
   operator: both give vector spaces of equal dimension over
   `Q[X]/(X^2+p^3)`. But the block matrix has content `p`, whereas `M` has
   content one. Content is invariant under unimodular conjugation, so
   integral similarity is impossible.
2. **Stable lattice versus integral metric.** In the basis `b_i`, Euclidean
   Gram is `I+J`, not identity. At `p=3` the enlarged lattice has a Gram
   entry `2/3` and Gram determinant `3^-11`; it is not metrically integral.
   The determinant follows from the original determinant 27 divided by
   the square of index `3^7`.
3. **An arithmetic intertwiner versus a native source map.** For
   `omega^2+omega+1=0`, the identification
   `iota(a+b omega)=(a,-b,b-a)` sends the C3 skew operator to multiplication
   by `1+2 omega`, whose square is `-3`, and has norm
   `||iota(z)||^2=2 Q(z)`. The actual ROOT row triple is
   `iota((2+omega)z)`, an index-three image, not all of the centered lattice.
   This does not preserve a marked observer, source weights, or native clocks
   unless those maps are specified separately.

The theorem is stated at exponent three. At higher odd powers the denominator
is a higher power of `p`, and a single mod-`p` rank cannot determine all
additive elementary divisors. No all-exponent saturation formula is inferred.
