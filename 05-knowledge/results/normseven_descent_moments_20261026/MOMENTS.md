# Twist mass cannot see missing sheets; a local second moment can

2026-10-26. **PROVED** M1-M5 by the arguments below; finite controls are
separate. Classical finite-group descent and elementary Kummer theory are
used, without a novelty claim. This is about the original finite root models,
not their smooth resolutions, and moments are taken **before summing over
base points**. Global count variance is a different statistic.

## M1. The actual stabilizer of the coarse product

Let G be a finite constant abelian group over F_q and G=H direct_sum K.
Fix a rational base point whose geometric fibre is one orbit O=G/I.
Frobenius commutes with G, hence acts as translation by a in G/I.
The two quotient fibres are G/(H+I), G/(K+I). Put

    J=(H+I) intersection (K+I), D=|G/I|, d=|G/J|.

The diagonal map from G/I onto their ordinary product is surjective and
has degree |J/I|=D/d as a map of geometric point sets. Indeed H+K=G,
so the usual two-congruence solvability criterion is automatic; its kernel
is J. Equivalently the product is the single G-orbit G/J.

For G=F_2^n write r=dim I, h=dim(I intersection H), k=dim(I intersection K).
The dimension formula gives

    dim J=2r-h-k,     e=dim(J/I)=r-h-k.                  (M1)

In particular the missing number of bits is r precisely when both kernels
are free on this stratum. Complementarity alone does not imply freeness.
When I is contained in H, e=0: coarse gluing loses no geometric sheets
there, although stack stabilizers can remain. This is a hostile to treating
the number of vanishing roots as the defect without checking the kernels.

## M2. All mixed moments and the conditional lift law

Twist the **same** G-action by every g in G with equal weight. The descent
on O is translation by a+g. Let x_g,y_g count rational points in the source
and coarse-product fibres. Then

    x_g=D [a+g in I],       y_g=d [a+g in J].          (M2)

Thus E x=E y=1, but for positive integers s,t,

    E x^s=D^(s-1), E y^t=d^(t-1),
    E (x^s y^t)=D^(s-1)d^t.                           (M3)

These follow by counting the |I| or |J| allowed translations among |G|.
In particular

    E (x-y)^2 = D-d,       E(xy)=E(y^2)=d.             (M4)

The entire difference distribution is

| Event | Probability | x-y |
|---|---:|---:|
| a+g in I | 1/D | D-d |
| a+g in J outside I | 1/d-1/D | -d |
| a+g outside J | 1-1/d | 0 |

Conditioning on a rational coarse fibre, a uniform twist lifts with
probability d/D. In that case every rational coarse point has D/d rational
lifts; otherwise none does. This probability concerns the declared finite
twist experiment, not the probability of a fixed rational point over Q.

The mean is inherited Frobenius-orbit mass (Session63 Theorem A). The added
object is its **nested-orbit coupling**, including the comparison map.
Forgetting the coupling would make the mixed-moment formula unjustified.
Even the full distribution forgets a: retain the labelled twist profile
and the original source point to decide which fixed twist lifts.

## M4. Arithmetic degree is not the number of geometric sheets

For G=F_2^n, once a+g lies in J, the rational coarse fibre has residual
torsor class a+g in J/I. Its order is one or two. All geometric source
points therefore become rational over F_(q^2), even when |J/I|=8 or more.
For this one fixed rational source P, the point counts over degree m are
D if m(a+g) lies in I and zero otherwise, and similarly d for J.
If a+g is in J outside I, the local zeta factors are

    Z_source(T)=(1-T^2)^(-D/2),
    Z_coarse(T)=(1-T)^(-d).                              (M6)

This is a statement about a finite fibre over the same P. It is neither
the global zeta function nor a fresh-twist average over each extension.

Over a general field of characteristic !=2 the residual constant binary
torsor can instead be described by e squareclasses c_1,...,c_e. Its splitting
field is k(sqrt(c_1),...,sqrt(c_e)), with degree 2^s where s is their F_2-rank
in k*/k*^2. This elementary Kummer statement follows by adjoining one
independent class at a time (a class becomes square in a multiquadratic
extension exactly when it belongs to the generated class span). Thus e
counts geometric ambiguity, while s measures arithmetic splitting degree.
Over finite fields s<=1; over Q the inherited triple example (2,3,5) has
s=3, by prime valuations. This is why “eight missing sheets” cannot be
translated into “a degree-eight extension is necessary” uniformly in k.

## Contract and boundary

Source: a marked cover orbit and two complementary quotient maps.
Target: the nested coset map G/I -> G/J and its twist-labelled fixed points.
Operation: coarse fibre product, then average translations and take local
moments. Preserved: actual point counts, sheet degree and conditional lift
criterion. Destroyed by moments: Frobenius phase and source location.
Required sidecar: I subset J, the labelled twist, the same P, residual
squareclasses. Cheapest tests: the two char-three triples and p=7 versus 11.

The energy is a nonnegative integer-valued statistic of finite-field fibres.
It is not the class of a torified variety, and its q=1 specialization has
no F1-point interpretation. F1 branch-map obstructions remain unchanged.
