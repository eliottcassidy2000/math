# Literature scope

Checked2026-10-19. This is a targeted primary-source check, not an exhaustive priority review.

Shandong University's [2024 lecture announcement](https://faculty-en.sdu.edu.cn/content.jsp?urltype=news.NewsContentUrl&wbnewsid=113951&wbtreeid=1091) describes Shun-ichi Maezawa and Yutaro Yamaguchi's work on rainbow spanning-tree reconfiguration under single-edge flips, including a sufficient connectivity condition. The announcement does not state that condition, so it cannot establish whether it subsumes the present width-two buffer criterion.

Maezawa's [author-maintained papers list](https://smaezawa.org/papers) was also inspected. This limited check did not locate a full text supplying the lecture's condition. It is not evidence that no related theorem exists.

Fundamental-cycle exchange and tree-cut reconnection are elementary established tools. The proof here applies them to the specific frozen-tail obstruction and states a sufficient abstract buffer/contact condition. No novelty claim is made for those tools, the general research area, or the particular theorem without further comparison. This result is not global graceful-tree exchange connectivity and does not preserve an unlabeled tree shape.
