## Reproduce

From the repository root run:

```sh
python3 -B 05-knowledge/results/orbit_spectrum_session_20261103/reproduce.py
```

The runner checks every direct source pin, executes eight producer/auditor
jobs in normal and optimized Python, and compares their output bytes with
the saved receipts. All mathematical checks use explicit exceptions rather
than removable assertions. The saved `verification.json` records the exact
script and receipt hashes. Benchmark timings are observations and are not
part of byte-identical replay.

`sources.json` pins direct dependencies and reviewed source notes, not a
transitive proof graph. For an incoming note repaired in this session, it
records the previously published bytes separately from the repaired bytes.
`manifest.json` hashes the final packet apart from the manifest itself.
