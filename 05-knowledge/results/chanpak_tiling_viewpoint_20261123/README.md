# Hamilton paths as viewpoints in the merged tournament metagraph

A board fixes the path \(0\to1\to\cdots\to n-1\) and specifies the remaining arcs. At each size, boards of a single ordinary class \(T\) number \(H(T)/|\mathrm{Aut}(T)|\), an odd integer. Reflection of the board takes the converse. A self-converse class keeps its odd mass; two distinct converse classes form one merged node of mass twice that odd integer. A blue board is fixed by reflection. Complementing every off-path tile sends a board to the other end of its blue or black line. Changing one tile is a single arc reversal.

## What paths remember about cycles and doubled nodes

* [Pair precedence and cycle supports](ORDER-PARITY.md) **PROVED.** The number of Hamilton words placing one vertex before another has parity equal to their arc indicator plus the number of directed simple cycles containing both. Boolean inversion on induced vertex sets recovers the parity of the number of Hamilton cycles using any chosen pair.
* [Distribution of odd and doubled masses](MASS.md) **PROVED / FINITE-EXACT.** An inclusion-exclusion formula counts the boards on doubled nodes at every size, and a positive interval expansion gives the asymptotic with its first parity-dependent term. At nine vertices, 263,927,596 of the 268,435,456 boards lie on doubled nodes.
* [Simultaneous selection at 2, 3 and 11](PRECEDENCE-FIBER.md) **PROVED.** Six rigid tournaments on at most nine vertices suffice to hit every odd residue modulo arbitrary powers of those three primes by ordinal joining, always with a transitive pair-precedence order. Eleven is the smallest order at which all odd residues modulo 24 occur with this same transitive order, by a census through ten and an explicit eleven-vertex witness. The exponent-61 upper neighbor is coprime to all six seed heights and so escapes this integer family.
* [Curvature of two physical flips](CURVATURE.md) **PROVED.** A square with two independent increments of magnitude two that persist on the opposite sides must use disjoint arcs. Above the square with counts 1, 3, 3 and 5, some outside vertex must distinguish the ends of at least one of them.

## Which changes of viewpoint are possible?

* [Viewpoint groups](VIEWPOINTS.md) **PROVED.** On a strong tournament, the permutations generated by comparing Hamilton words give the full symmetric group, except on the directed triangle and the five-vertex cyclic substitution \(Q_5\), where they give the alternating group. On an arbitrary tournament the group is the product over its strong components. An ordering is an actual viewpoint precisely when all its consecutive arcs pass the test on the original tournament. The [local relocation classifier](../chanpak_rooted_tiling_transfer_20261124/README.md) generates exactly the boards passing that test within a class.
* [Path signs and merged masses](FIBERS.md) **PROVED.** The complete sector in which every Hamilton word has the same permutation sign has a three-letter component grammar. Its odd and doubled masses, number of blue boards and class counts at every size follow explicitly. The one-backward-arc family separates cyclic cuts from all paths and gives signed counts for each. [Second proof and minority-sign bound](SECOND-PROOF.md).
* [Two free tiles](LINES.md) **PROVED.** Erase two arcs. A common path through all four completions exists exactly when each erased pair is comparable by reachability in the remaining graph. Any two distinct composable arc flips of magnitude two with all three counts at least five admit such a common board. The marked \(1\to3\to5\) example shows the boundary. At three holes endpoint compatibility inside a component can obstruct gluing.
* [Cuts of cycles and converse-paired squares](CYCLES.md) **PROVED / FINITE-EXACT.** Fixing the opposite chords under a half-turn parametrizes all even-order self-converse classes and all pairs of arcs exchanged by the specified reversing involution. Two flips at order eight give \(537,539,539,541\) around one physical square. The middle classes are distinct, rigid and converse to each other. The merged masses along either route are \(537,1078,541\).
* [Three coordinates for physical placement](PANELS.md) **PROVED.** For every \(k\ge1\) one rigid self-converse ordinary class of order \(128(k+1)\) reaches at least \(2^k\) distinct partner heights on blue lines and \(2^k-1\) on black lines. Two presentations of the same sequence of components encode each bit. The height at the opposite end is an exact integer matrix product.
* [Selecting an integer partner height](TARGET.md) **PROVED / FINITE-EXACT.** At fixed source height, a finite list of component words and independent tribonacci recurrences decides membership for any prescribed partner height. The admissible orders lie in an interval of bounded length depending on the source. No finite bank of source heights has single-line partners at \(2^{p-1}(2^p-1)\pm1\) for infinitely many \(p\). At exponent 61, all source heights through 23 are excluded on every literal line, at every order.

For the prescribed odd target problem, the existing multiplicative reduction leaves prime and seven-times-prime strong cores after three known composite anchors. Every outstanding core requires both signs of viewpoint. The two-free-tile theorem gives a common coordinate system for consecutive physical unit steps, and the placement matrices demonstrate how much target information can survive in the order and location of the tiles. Whether these constructions cover every required odd height, and whether one physical arc crosses the exponent-61 perfect number exactly, remain **OPEN**. [Atom reduction](../chanpak_two_holes_atoms_20261123/README.md) · [Current exponent-61 bracket](../chanpak_odd_square_spines_20261122/README.md) · [Chan and Pak, Conjecture 6.2](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~pak/papers/DominoPermanent16.pdf)

The quantitative comparisons use the ordinary height \(H\), the board mass, the signed path counts, the two endpoints of a marked edit, and the reflected/complemented board separately. In particular treating the doubled middle mass 1078 as a Hamilton count would lose the physical unit steps. The [signed Collatz and square-sum study](../square36_signed_collatz_20261123/README.md) exhibits the related obstruction at height 199, where individually realizable incident edges require incompatible middle cores. The color and sign tests here operate on tournament words with their placements.

```sh
python3 -B mass.py
python3 -B order_parity.py
python3 -B precedence_bank.py
python3 -B curvature.py
python3 -B viewpoints.py
python3 -B sign_balance.py
python3 -B lines.py

```
