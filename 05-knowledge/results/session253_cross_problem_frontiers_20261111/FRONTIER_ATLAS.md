## Reusable move and counterindication

Use a quotient's complete module or feasible fibre before exporting an
operation. Triggers: equal scalar counts with different repairs; equal
matrix determinant with different precision; universal local support with
missing integers. Successful evidence here comes from three distinct
objects: Laurent centralizers, jet/response lattices, and Sun/tournament
height fibres.

Counterindication: retaining a full algebraic invariant still does not
supply a physical constructor or a small integer representative. The
ternary liftability obstruction and the two H9 equal affine hulls make this
limitation explicit. This session records the research move locally rather
than modifying the global meta-pattern index without a separate review.
