## 1. The strongest cross-topic bridge also explains its own limit

A carried Laurent response degenerating at x=−r lives on an Eisenstein
small-root algebra of rank r. The inverse carry has valuation r−1. Therefore
its exact Smith partition is

    (0,1,...,1), with r−1 ones.

This holds at every height, not just in a computed range. It distinguishes
three observables that had been easy to conflate: determinant order r−1,
worst inverse precision loss1, and r−1 separate deficient directions.

At r=3 the special fibre is a rank-one square-zero operator with Jordan
type(2,1). Its centralizer is LITERALLY the five-dimensional Chan–Pak
interface algebra from Session252:

    [[d,o,j],[0,e,i],[0,0,d]].

The multiplication cross term oI is retained. This is not five dimensions
appearing by coincidence.

But only the three-dimensional subalgebra

    [[a,b,c],[0,a,b],[0,0,a]]

lifts to endomorphisms commuting with the full carried deformation.
The other two directions are created by the singular specialization. Thus
an algebra bridge does not automatically transport a positive constructor
or a lawful deformation. The missing information is now expressed by two
explicit equations, e=d and i=o, instead of a vague warning.

The same work closes four full-matrix strips: for h=r+ell and ell=0,1,2,3,
the complete h-dimensional partition is

    (0 repeated ell+1 times, 1 repeated r−1 times),

uniformly for r>=2. The nonzero complementary resultants have explicit
positive-coefficient residual factors of degrees2,10,24. This is native
Laurent progress, independent of its usefulness to Chan–Pak.

**Next small proof:** ell=4, or a structural proof of complementary
coprimality for arbitrary ell. **Large step still missing:** physical
positive-x noncancellation. Boundary module control does not supply it.
[Proof, liftability firewall and symbolic certificates](moments/PROOF.md).

## 3. Mahler safety gives height control, but can destroy coverage

For binary addresses with no adjacent ones,

    g(n)>=n/3.

The constant1/3 is sharp asymptotically. Combining this with the positive
bilinear core compiler gives

    (A+1)(B+1)<=3H+4.

So safe words solve a real height problem. However, their scarcity matters:
for ANY fixed finite core bank, requiring no11 on both flanks gives only

    O(X^(log_2 phi) log X)

different outputs up to X. Requiring strict finite Mahler safety sharpens
the exponent to log_2(3/2). Both are below1: either restricted grammar has
density zero and cannot cover all sufficiently large allowed odd targets.
The witness relation need not be finite-state. This is different from the
inherited rational-selector obstruction.

We then weakened the restriction and tried it rather than leaving an

The latter pair contains42=101010, which avoids11 but contains10101.

not merely adjacent ones, is essential in this concrete target fibre.

## 4. Substitution has a surprisingly small exact modulo-three interface

An arbitrary tournament factor has ordered path-cover coordinates
F(k)=k!p(k). Modulo p^a, factorial valuations kill all sufficiently deep
coordinates. The surviving jet is a true congruence for arbitrary iterated
tournament substitution, unlike scalar H over the integers.

At p=3 reciprocity collapses it further: only **H and order parity** remain.
For any quotient R and nonempty factors S_i,

    H(R[S_i]) ≡ H(R[TT_epsilon_i]) product H(S_i) (mod3),

where epsilon_i=1 or2 according as |S_i| is odd or even. Therefore a factor
with3|H is universally absorbing under substitution. For a triangle
quotient, equal parity makes the germ zero; mixed parity makes it2.
A singleton ordinal padding flips parity without changing H and therefore
repairs the latter quotient obstruction when the factors are3-units.

This property is special: the H5 four-vertex core becomes H37 in a triangle
substitution, so analogous divisibility by5 is not absorbing.

An exact constructor also emerged:

    H(C3[S▷TT_r,K1,K1])=H(S)+2^r Q_S(2).

The seeds K1 and S4 yield1+2^(r+1) and5+2^(r+5). Together they cover every
residue modulo every3^a. Their integer outputs remain exponentially costly,
and their actual support is only logarithmic. This is a new native
substitution route and a particularly transparent bounded-lift warning.

**Next small task:** costed modulo9/27 jet transitions, retaining actual
output and order. General fixed quotient constructors also have proved
uniform eventual modular periods, despite huge integer growth.
[Full compositional theorem and constructor](tournaments/PROOF.md).

## 5. Integral jets: an all-node first-band theorem

For n>=2 distinct nodes in one p-adic cluster of minimum pair depth e>=1,
with m Hasse jets at every node, there are exactly m zero Smith exponents.
The first positive exponent is

    e+v_p(m).

Its multiplicity is one, except when p=2,e=1,m is even, where it is exactly
two. This allows arbitrary n,m, nested higher pair depths, and unit lifts.
The dyadic exception comes from two binomial bands surviving at the same
valuation, with an explicit invertible2×2 residue block.

This closes a genuine all-scale first-band slice. It does not guess the
remaining partition. The recovered Deuring and p31 examples explain why
metric, determinant and largest loss together can miss intermediate data.

**Next small task:** eliminate the known first block and classify the next
band with its actual residue polynomial. This is closer than attempting an
all-node full Smith classification at once.
[Proof and192 exact new controls](niche/PROOF.md#2-all-node-uniform-jet-first-band-theorem).

## 6. Sun sums and two cubes contribute concrete height tools

For nonnegative monotone binomial atoms f_i(t), define the cost of a residue
as the MINIMUM INTEGER VALUE of the atom, not a modular support bit.
The proven input period compresses each minimum to one finite period.
Min-plus cyclic convolution then gives the minimum total C_M(r) exactly:

    C_M(r)=r+M kappa_M(r).

For0<=N<M, exact representation is equivalent to kappa_M(N)=0. Ordinary
modular support remembers only that the cost is finite. The costs also
refine exactly when the modulus is enlarged.

This is a lossless height-aware carrier for Sun-type binomial sums, not an
efficient solution of the known enormous hole or global leastness. Its
transfer to Chan–Pak is precise: optimize integer cost in a target residue
fibre, and retain the witness. The finite first-period compression is proved
for monotone binomial atoms, NOT automatically for binary tournament words.

A separate small native result gives an explicit forced divisor D(n) of
x+y for every positive distinct two-cube representation n=x^3+y^3. The
number of representations is bounded by the number of multiples d of D(n)
with n<d^3<4n. It includes nonprimitive representations and sharp inert/3-adic
valuation invoices. Candidates still need the divisor and discriminant
square tests; no support asymptotic or new infinite singleton family follows.
[Both tools and their next obligations](niche/PROOF.md).
