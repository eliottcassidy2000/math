# Proof and exact-computation audit

2026-10-29. **Same-agent audit; no independent human/agent review claimed.**

The proof audit checks J1 by literal block multiplication and tracks the
minus sign v^TA=-q^T. The exponent lower bound uses q_i^2 in an off-diagonal
block, so it requires at least two copies; the r=1 boundary is separate.
For J2, v not in im(M mod p) uses the elementary-exponent hypothesis.
Without it that implication is not asserted. The paired classification is
unmarked; the original matrix inverse supplies the retained marked data.

J3's two-port formula has factor 1-alpha*beta, checked against actual
Pfaffians. Its rank-one obstruction applies to a single attachment between
invertible even blocks, not arbitrary tournament modifications. New-prime
examples are genuine +/-1 cuts. Their groups are not called actual Sha.

F1 and F2 use different forms on the same coordinates. In characteristic
two, a quadratic graph still needs zero diagonal. Full quadratic counts
include both families of maximal isotropic subspaces. The derivative claim
concerns the displayed polynomials, not arbitrary representations.

Arithmetic checks authenticate the rational point, Legendre symbols and
binary kernels. Monsky's local-solubility theorem, finite Kummer exactness
and Nagell--Lutz remain cited inputs; brute-force matrix checks do not
re-prove them. No unverified supplied BSD theorem is used.

`check.json` and `check.optimized.json` record identical exact data (31,060
producer gates; 23,181 audit gates, many of them pivot-divisibility checks).
These counts are implementation checks, not counts of independent theorems. The
audit receipt replays both modes, checks those saved receipts, and then
uses a distinct path: prime-power Smith pivots on the original matrices.
No rational inverse or predicted cokernel is used to perform those pivots.
Precision p^3 separates all expected valuations 0,1,2; it would report an
unexpected valuation at least 3 rather than silently truncating to 2.

Controls include trivial and nonprimitive ports, coefficient prime two
in the abstract lattice tests, the C_3^2 self-join hostile, literal
five/eleven attachments, and the characteristic-two diagonal graph.
The 81/27 pair remains the inherited hostile for cyclic-triple carriers;
this packet makes no new cyclic-triple/class-separation claim and does not
reinterpret that pair's previously checked counts.

Universal proofs are J1--J3 and F1--F2. Exact enumeration is limited to the
universes in README. Neither establishes arithmetic realization, native
complement closure, or general Sha finiteness. No theorem namespace was
reserved, and no unreviewed stub was promoted to canon.
