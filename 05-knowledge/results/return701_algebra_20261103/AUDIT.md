# Proof and computation audit

**Same-agent review, not an independent researcher endorsement.**

* R1 uses inherited complete one/two-start rank grammars and the complete
  five-family exceptional classification. The cited Busch bound reduces
  the strong parent to orders 3--11. All 4,180 one-start and 7,896 two-start
  parents are evaluated, with one exact two-start hit. The SCC lemma
  supplies the all-size weak extension. No sampled census is substituted.
* R2's minimum six arc discrepancies is only within the 141,321,600-pair
  fixed bank: all 701 supplied source paths, five allowed deletions, and
  8! root-fixed target identifications. It is not a global move minimum.
  All native paths are actual Hamilton paths when used; helper deletion
  isolates precisely one old arc. Endpoint counts are independently
  recalculated. The old root, spare and three additional old vertices
  are protected. Intermediate counts and phases may change.
* The three one-birth ternary banks count strong labelled programs,
  not classes. Their defects do not establish recursive return readiness.
* A1--3 reconstruct the 95 word matrices over Q and check exact closure.
  The trace kernel is a two-sided ideal; its literal four-step module
  filtration proves nilpotence. Explicit simple representations have
  combined rank 26 and exact kernel J, proving a split quotient rather
  than assuming a rational center implies splitting. The matrix-derived
  multiplication table specifies the extension as well as the quotient.
* A4 is a block-triangular product identity. It bounds layer changes,
  not chronological word length. A6 multiplies the annihilating polynomials
  of the four semisimple layers, of degrees 2,4,12,6. Their component
  incidences are certified in semisimple.json; repeated copies on a layer
  do not require repeated factors. This proves the uniform degree-24 bound.
  A5 uses the displayed primitive matrix;
  an earlier trial matrix had cube zero mod 3 and was discarded before
  making the prime-3 statement. Positive-word singularity explicitly
  blocks the interpretation of its formal unipotent as a legal controller.
* H1--4 are proved by inverse valuation and grid recursion. Every finite
  source and clock through depth 7 is replayed literally. Addresses,
  geometric scan edges and native arithmetic edges have distinct roles.
  Local automata require marked compass ports. The infinite Hilbert curve
  is classical; only the specified arithmetic transport is developed here.
* The 12/13 tensor uses the primitive quadratic unit matrix and the
  explicit new unipotent matrix at modulus 13. It is formal, not a native
  attachment. The two-trace determinant 29 is an invertible selector here.
* The actual 81/27 pair is reconstructed and its automorphisms counted;
  it still has the same 67 labelled cyclic triples and distinct regular
  binary-mask counts 1620/1728. The 55-to-111 failure is pinned, inherited.
