# Metabelian global invariants and logarithms

19 October 2026. Exact classifications for the group generated by the scalar-time flows of x²y and x³ over any characteristic-zero field.

For a word with nonzero total Möbius time a:

- A rational first integral exists exactly when the normalized Hamiltonian logarithm is rational.
- Both occur exactly when the fourth-order partial-fraction coefficients sum to zero on each translation orbit t+aZ.
- A polynomial first integral exists exactly when the logarithm is polynomial, and exactly when the word is a quadratic-shear conjugate of the single Möbius flow M_a.
- The full rational invariant field and, where applicable, polynomial invariant ring are explicit.

Every finite jet of the Möbius flow is also shared by an exact word with no rational first integral or rational logarithm; a distinct-orbit finite-difference construction gives the exact first discrepancy.

The zero-total-time case is handled separately: every such rational vertical shear has a rational logarithm and preserves x, but its logarithm is polynomial only for a quadratic vertical shear.

The proof uses formal-log filtration, rational periodicity, and finite-support translation differences. It includes rational time maps with no rational Hamiltonian logarithm. This is a classification inside the stated generating family, not a general Keller-map reduction or a solution to the Jacobian conjecture. No historical-novelty claim is made.
