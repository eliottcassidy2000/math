# Chan–Pak: modular resets, hyperbolic windows, and offset admission

The starting point is [Wave 33, P1–P3](../chanpak_waves_20261113/README.md#wave-33-a-survey-of-tangential-prior-work-and-what-it-points-to). We tested two distinct notions of bottleneck and two distinct notions of upper-band coverage. The main gain is that both distinctions now have literal tournament examples and all-order proofs. Chan–Pak remains open.

## Twisting without rational bottlenecks

* **Removing floor runs does not remove all coherent frequencies.** [Modular resets](MODULAR_RESETS.md) gives strong rational-rank-two periodic families and equal-length binary sublanguages at (p=3,5,7,11,13). The two surviving eigenvalues of each periodic transfer have (p)-adic valuation (1/2). On the branching families the primitive conductor-(p^r) twist converges exponentially to 1 for every fixed (r), although no word has a rational rank-one factor in the thirteen-unit representation. This is not a Fourier estimate for the full ribbon ensemble.
* **The opposite behavior is just as real.** [The two cheapest alternating floor chains](TWISTED_FLOOR_CHAINS.md) have explicit depth-averaged rational twists. The two chains have means (1/4,-1/2) at conductor 3 and both (-1/24) at 5; each is coherent at 8, with opposite signs. High dyadic and ternary conductors cancel, and the remaining quinary branches reduce to quadratic Gauss sums. These are averages **across depths**, not contraction of a branching fixed-length ensemble. An exact Lucas factorization shows that one chain can have a prime count only at prime shifted depth.
* **A second port can see what (H) loses.** The certified (Pi_2) recurrences in [modular resets, §3](MODULAR_RESETS.md#3-the-hyperbolic-reader-separates-a-number-theoretic-coordinate) give infinitely many saturated (p)-adic ordinary counts whose hyperbolic readout (6Pi_2-H) is a unit at (p=5,7,11,13). The universal (Pi_2equiv(-1)^nHmod3) congruence blocks this bypass at 3, even if more unmarked slots are added: ordered covers of three or more pieces are divisible by 3.

## The upper half-band and the absolute target

* **Indefinite does not mean freely canceling.** [Fixed-core hyperbolic moves](HYPERBOLIC_MOVE.md) bounds the determinant cancellation of any physical two-visit kernel using (P_2ge(n-1)H). The relative deficit tends to zero when either core grows under a fixed kernel, so a finite bank with a forced dyadic factored baseline cannot fill a fixed-width fractional band below that baseline. There is an exact fixed-core inverse and a finite census: the first kernel with a singleton fixed core gives 44,760 distinct projections through module order 9, including 19,934 leading-(11) counts.
* **Non-dyadic placement reaches every fractional subband, with the residue controlled at the same time.** [Joint windows](JOINT_WINDOWS.md) uses literal profiles ((H,Pi_2)=(11,100),(5,32)) and the physical kernel (6Pi_2-H). In every sufficiently high dyadic band, every odd residue modulo a prescribed power of 2 occurs in every fixed fractional subinterval. One can let the modulus grow logarithmically in the band index. The entire family still has only (O((log X)^2)) absolute outputs up to (X). In addition the old anti-seed yields the genuine reflected rays (2^K-h) for modules with dyadic (Pi_2), but the exact offset inventory is controlled by those SCC profiles.
* **Changing readers changes differences more readily than it changes the absolute baseline.** [Row folds](ROW_FOLDS.md) finds an exact address collision in every row of the older two-tape carrier. Two literal repaints separate these pairs by (-810) and (900); every separating repaint in that fixed spine has a baseline displacement growing at least linearly in its address. The gcd of two changes is not an exact-target repair width.

## Seed induction

[Seed admission](SEED_ADMISSION.md) closes a narrower question and supplies a different construction. Offset 3 has no same-count dyadic seed at any order; the least paid witness has (H=9) and response 32. Any paid positive-offset seed still gives a leading-(100) output after a nonempty dyadic tail. In contrast, a pair of literal ports admits a joint signed update by any realized multiplier (c): the offsets are multiplied by (-c) while their responses stay synchronized with each other. This is a physical difference, not an arbitrary absolute count or a dyadic response.

## Where to push

1. **Analytic:** take the actual length-conditioned or tilted ribbon measure, split by the modular rank/content of the *visible profile*, and estimate centered endpoint characters in those strata. A rational rank test alone is not the analytic sieve. On a distribution explicitly restricted to units, even perfect equidistribution has a nonzero Ramanujan main term at primitive prime conductors.
2. **Constructive:** find literal modules sharing the profile coordinate used for multiplicative placement but varying the subtractive coordinate over a certified interval. The joint-window result already gives coarse real positioning and a finite residue decoder. The next task is to match the remaining integer error while retaining the surrounding interface, not to collect more approximate hits.
3. **Mod 3:** use genuinely selective contacts when trying to escape a module with (3mid H); adding unmarked ports cannot remove this factor. Port-specific divisibility at 3 is qualitatively different from the rank-two resets at the other tested primes.
