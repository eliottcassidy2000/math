## The useful periodicity connection is a marked coordinate

The [Bott and marked-periodicity packet](collatz_bott_marked_periodicity_20261007d.md)
recovers the repo's Clifford affine carrier and its earlier scope corrections.
The classical Bott/Clifford stabilization statements live in their own
categories. In our elementary matrix model, `M -> M tensor I16` preserves
the operator when a corner and frame are retained. Passing merely to a
homotopy class cannot distinguish any of the positive triangular word
matrices: all are connected to the identity. This explains why a periodic
type alone supplies no arithmetic descent predicate.

The constructive arithmetic coordinate is stronger. After the old child's
known15-letter head, normalize its endpoint Y by `j=(Y-5)/8`. Exactly,

\[
 j(t)=\frac{3^{924745911+2^{32}t}-8589800907}{2^{34}}.
\]

It is an isometric bijection of the binary parameter space:
`v2(j(t)-j(s))=v2(t-s)`. Every compatible finite continuation word therefore
pulls back to one exact parameter class. Parameter advance is an affine
map with a full cycle of length2^h modulo2^h. Raising precision doubles
that period; this is not an eight-step Collatz orbit.

There is an exact finite-degree readout: modulo2^h, j(t) is an
integer-valued binomial polynomial of minimal degree `ceil(h/34)`.
It is linear through34 bits, quadratic through68, and so on. The
new16-bit continuation's47-bit guard is therefore exactly quadratic.
The34-bit rule's519-bit guard has degree16. Independent power-modulus and

The loss boundary is explicit: at35 bits,

already fails at the next bit. Lossless reuse means carrying the extra
coefficient when the precision changes, not assuming the summary has
