## Demonstrated failures or narrowed claims

- Individual primewise counts cannot be combined into one all-prime-preserving bijection; five vertices already obstruct it.
- Normalizer-relative sign-evenness is weaker than global sign-evenness. False-twin merging can reveal an odd symmetry that the normalizer misses.
- An equivariant orbit-bijective map is not a labelled bijection. The prime-circulant labelled cardinalities differ already at three vertices.
- The maximal odd multiplier sector is not always a single type; the one-type forcing restriction is v_2(p-1)<=2.
- Odd denominators occur in primitive Eulerian coordinates despite a dyadic determinant. The original p-integral support argument cannot be transplanted unchanged.
- Taking involution-fixed multisets before inverse Euler introduces paired nonfixed atoms. The historical order-six connected self-converse oriented value is 726; 727 counted one extra paired atom.
