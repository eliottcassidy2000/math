# Tournament–even-graph bijections: structural maps and the exact symmetry boundary

Session 2026-10-20. “Even graph” here always means **automorphism-sign-even**: every automorphism reverses an even number of edges relative to a reference orientation. It does not mean even vertex degrees.

## What the session accomplished

The repository already had an all-order SCC/Lyndon codec with ranking at connected atoms. This session replaced ranking by stronger constructions in two general settings and removed full-catalogue matching entirely in the prime-circulant sector. It also found and proved the precise failure of the newly proposed general odd-symmetry refinement. These are scoped mathematical results; no major classical conjecture is claimed solved, and no historical priority claim is made.

### 1. General type bijections with prime-local structural information

For each chosen odd prime p, the signed Walsh matrix gives an explicit bijection from tournament types to sign-even graph types preserving the **entire S_n-conjugacy class of a Sylow p-subgroup action**. A nonzero coefficient modulo p forces a graph copy fixed by the tournament's p-group; integral matrix duality forces the p-orders to match. Thus every embedded p-group symmetry predicate is preserved simultaneously for that prime.

This proves the prime-power portion of HYP-9340(a), beyond the inherited ambient-Sylow theorem. The construction extends to invariant reversal quotients, including tournament switching classes versus graphs that are both sign-even and Eulerian. It can also respect converse pairs through graph edge parity.

[Walsh packet and explicit algorithms](../prime_local_walsh_bijection_20261020/README.md).

These are genuine finite inverse maps, using cofactor-guided matchings in nonzero signed-subgraph support. Given the matrix they use cubic field-operation time. Constructing the full atlas is expensive; this is not a local graph rewrite or a polynomial-in-vertex-count algorithm.

### 2. A local, atlas-free bijection for every prime-order circulant family

A circulant tournament becomes an antiperiodic binary word. Differentiation turns it into an odd-weight source necklace. Ordinary necklaces with an even-weight primitive root encode the sign-even circulant graphs. Cutting one incoming edge at each derivative branch pairs sources to branches, with an explicit integration inverse.

The Fitting stable subspace supplies a bounded cut rule: at most 2^{v_2((p-1)/2)} routing steps. The labelled forward map preserves the affine action and is a surjection; the inverse is on isomorphism types. For p congruent to 3 modulo 4, it reduces to a two-derivative formula. It preserves odd multiplier stabilizers.

[Complete prime-circulant construction](../circulant_necklace_bijection_20261020/CIRCULANT_NECKLACE_BIJECTION.md) · [General bounded-routing theorem](../bounded_fitting_routing_20261020/BOUNDED_FITTING_ROUTING.md).

The binary derivative and parity-necklace correspondences have classical and recent precedents, explicitly cited in the packet. The result here is a fully specified construction and its transport to the requested graph family, not a claim that binary differentiation was newly discovered.

### 3. Connected graphs become primitive Lie combinations

Normalized Walsh is a coalgebra isomorphism: connected graph orientation lines map precisely onto primitive tournament combinations. The tournament Hopf algebra is freely generated, as an algebra, by strong tournament types; Eulerian correction turns them into primitive generators. Its primitive algebra is the free Lie algebra on those corrected generators.

The resulting connected-graph/Lyndon-bracket matrix is nonsingular. A support matching therefore improves the previous connected-atom rank map while preserving the SCC/Lyndon-factor-to-component structure. It certifies each atom match by a nonzero Lie/Fourier coefficient.

[Primitive Hopf/Lie packet](../primitive_hopf_lie_bijection_20261020/PRIMITIVE_HOPF_LIE_BIJECTION.md).

Its determinant remains dyadic, but entries acquire odd denominators. Consequently the full Walsh p-integrality argument cannot simply be imported into this primitive basis. The packet proves connected graph edge-parity recurrences and produces exact enumeration through order sixty, without enumerating all graphs at those orders.

### 4. The general odd-order symmetry conjecture fails at ten vertices

For H=C15 generated by a disjoint 3-cycle and 5-cycle and fixing two vertices:

    tournament types with H symmetry = 32;
    sign-even graph types with H symmetry = 31.

The extra normalizer-even graph is K5,5. The three-vertex orbit and two fixed vertices merge into a false-twin class of size five; swapping it with the original five-vertex orbit is globally odd. The normalizer of H cannot see that swap. Prime strong tournament modules cannot undergo the corresponding merger.

[Elementary proof, full finite universe, and independent audit](../c15_symmetry_counterexample_20261020/C15_ORDER_TEN_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md).

The [infinite-family continuation](../c15_symmetry_counterexample_20261020/infinite-family/INFINITE_MIXED_PRIME_FAMILY.md) proves exact deficit one for affine-Paley product groups built from primes a<b<2a, both 3 modulo 4. The two counts are 1408 versus 1407 at order 22, and 874138624 versus 874138623 at order 38. The first has an independent quotient census; the second follows from the all-order formula. Classical reciprocal-prime divergence supplies infinitely many eligible prime pairs.

This refutes HYP-9340(a) in its general mixed-prime form while leaving the p-group theorem intact. A separate five-vertex obstruction already rules out one bijection preserving all odd-prime symmetry predicates simultaneously. Equality of separate symmetry counts would never by itself imply a joint coupling.

[Worked maps on three, four and seven vertices](WORKED_MAPS.md) show exactly what the algorithms do and why different valid bijections need not agree.

## Enumeration tools recovered and extended

The [sign-aware augmentation compiler](../sign_aware_augmentation_20261020/signed_root_augmentation.md) uses actual neighborhood choices and the sign character of the remaining stabilizer. For a fixed parent graph G, its count after k marked roots is

    b_G(k) = (1/|Aut G|) sum_g epsilon(g) 2^{k·cycles(g)}.

This has a nonnegative stabilizer automaton and a minimal signed exponential observer of order at most |V(G)|. It supports local rank/unrank without a terminal atlas. Complete graphs require exactly ceil(log_2 n) marked roots, linking parity repair to injective binary codes.

The crucial recovered warning becomes productive: rooted evenness is weaker than global evenness. An even-only parent compiler already loses a branch at parent order two. That missing root-moving symmetry is exactly the mechanism that led to the C15 counterexample.

Additional recovered instruments include signed cycle-index buckets, integer log-derivative “ghost” inversion, source-adjunction/Frobenius clocks, and the difference between positive transition systems and their smaller signed observers. Original source paths and the first failed implications are recorded in each packet.

## Further mathematical connections, with their maps specified

- **Integral lattices:** Walsh gives an explicit isomorphism between direct sums of cyclic groups formed from odd automorphism orders. This explains primewise agreement without a summandwise all-prime bijection.
- **Probability transport:** squares of normalized Walsh coefficients form a canonical doubly stochastic matrix. Its support has strict Hall expansion, and every permitted pair extends to a full matching. The tournament matched to the empty graph has the type distribution of a uniformly random labelled tournament under the associated coupling.
- **Inverse limits and algorithms:** a repeated x+1 primary factor controls the runtime of a concrete finite bijection; the invertible LFSR cycle lengths need not be traversed.
- **Finite fields and quadratic dynamics:** the [basis-free Artin–Schreier supplement](../artin_schreier_routing_20261020/ARTIN_SCHREIER_AND_CRITICAL_PERIODS.md) replaces cyclic differentiation by a↦a²+a on Galois orbits, with a relative-trace stable-space test. It also proves the characteristic-two critical period is d or 2d according to the minimal-field trace. The circulant packet separately composes with the cited real critical-parameter correspondence. Neither is a result about arbitrary rational periodic cycles.
- **Involutions and enumeration:** taking fixed multisets does not commute with inverse Euler transform. Paired nonfixed components contribute extra atoms; this repairs the historical connected self-converse oriented count at six from 727 to 726.

## What remains worth attacking

1. Replace the global primitive-support matching by a direct connected-graph construction from a Lyndon word, retaining its nonzero coefficient certificate.
2. Determine which additional structures can be preserved together with one Sylow action. The ten-vertex and five-vertex witnesses show that “preserve all odd symmetries” is not a viable universal target.
3. Use sign-aware augmentation to transport actual legal moves while retaining root-moving automorphisms. A recursion forgetting that character or transporter is already known to fail.
4. Seek faster implementations of the bounded necklace routing and its exact field-orbit variants, without confusing the cost of a local step with enumeration of an entire atlas.

The classical natural-unlabelled-bijection question needs an explicit criterion for “natural.” A selected type matching, a local circulant map, an equivariant orbit map, and a labelled bijection are distinct achievements. This session states which one each construction supplies.
