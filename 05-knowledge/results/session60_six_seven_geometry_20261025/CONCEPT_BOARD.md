Status: research board, not an assertion that every proposed link is proved.

User anchor: connect six-root K3 resolutions and seven-root hourglasses (p_g=7,K²=16) with other appearances of6 and7. Seek a shared family, map, invariant, or precise obstruction; numerical resemblance alone is not a bridge.

## Six live concepts

1. Root-count/adjunction threshold: extend to base dimension d and root exponent r; type canonical genus, polarization genus, and root count separately.
2. Deck characters: seven holomorphic two-form sectors and seven six-root quotient K3s; separate a single simultaneous flip from a subgroup generated by two flips.
3. Binary incidence: seven branch inertia labels, rank-six deck, rank-three Fano quotients, simplex codes, and free Campedelli kernels; never identify the branch matroid with Fano.
4. Six-plus-one symmetry: a distinguished seventh line and six hexagonal dual points; exact D12 action, tangent conic, arithmetic twists and Kummer/CM consequences.
5. Generic-line curve sections: the same weight-six characters yield genus-two quotients, with Jacobian/Richelot and marked pants correspondences.
6. Hostile degenerations: noncrepant multiple crossings, exceptional curves, quotient singularities and different minimal models. Retain base, branch, resolution and equivariance data.

Portfolio: deck/quotient geometry is the anchor; generalized thresholds and curve Jacobians are orthogonal niches; Fano-code quotients and hexagon CM are wildcards promoted only after exact proofs.

Every packet must state a map, preserved predicate, lost data, required marking, and cheapest decisive test. Classical models and classifications remain attributed; new-to-repository links do not imply historical priority. Integer distinct-square existence, RH, and the full tournament path problems are outside any claimed closure.
