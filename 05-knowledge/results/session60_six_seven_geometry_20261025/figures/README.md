# Six/seven correspondence map

`six_seven_map.svg` summarizes the audited complex-geometric comparisons, with geometric Picard ranks. Canonical models, minimal resolutions and quotient constructions are distinguished in the linked proofs; connecting strokes indicate correspondences, not a claim that every map between resolved models is finite.

The lower panel is a generic base-line pullback, a2H-section, not anH-section or a map from the surface to the curve. Jacobian decompositions are up to isogeny. Fano-code counts refer to the thirty specifically marked quotient kernels; the sixteen Campedelli kernels give two abstract complex isomorphism classes.

The vector was XML-parsed, rendered with Inkscape and visually inspected at1300×720. The review PNG is not a proof dependency. The final Picard-rank input is the separate audited elliptic-section and two-prime reduction argument.
