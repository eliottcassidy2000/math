# A genuine finite-clock mixing bound from the Collatz commutator

2026-10-19. **PROVED finite-state spectral estimates**, with explicitly separate numerical controls. This is a follow-on to the scalar-character blindness identified in the Carmichael/carry transfer. It is not a bound for fixed-composition Collatz words or integer trajectories.

## 1. Keep the affine action instead of abelianizing it

Let D>1 be an integer coprime to 6. On X=Z/DZ define two permutations

    E(x)=x/2,          O(x)=(3x+1)/2.

Inverses and fractions are taken modulo D. Let U_g act on complex functions on X by U_g f(x)=f(g^(-1)x). This is a unitary representation for the uniform inner product and satisfies U_g U_h=U_(gh).

A direct affine composition gives

    E O E^(-1) O^(-1)(x)=x−1/4.                         (1)

The translation −1/4 generates the entire additive group because D is odd. Thus the group generated by E,O acts transitively. This is exactly the coordinate erased by its one-dimensional characters, now retained in the permutation representation.

## 2. A commutator energy inequality

For arbitrary unitaries U,V, put W=UVU^(-1)V^(-1). Telescoping a product of unitaries gives

    ||(W−I)f|| ≤ 2(||(U−I)f||+||(V−I)f||),
    ||(W−I)f||² ≤ 8(||(U−I)f||²+||(V−I)f||²).         (2)

For example U1...Uk−I=sum_j U1...U_(j−1)(U_j−I); unitarity removes the prefix norms, and ||(U^(-1)−I)f||=||(U−I)f||. This proves (2) without assuming U,V commute.

A cyclic translation T by a unit modulo D has, on mean-zero functions,

    ||(T−I)f||² ≥ 4 sin²(pi/D)||f||².                   (3)

Indeed its additive Fourier eigenvalues are all D-th roots of unity, and every nonconstant character has |z−1|²≥4 sin²(pi/D). Apply (2),(3) to (1).

## 3. Explicit lazy symmetric mixing

Set

    S=(U_E+U_E^*+U_O+U_O^*)/4,
    L=(I+S)/2.

For every mean-zero f,

    <(I−S)f,f>=(||U_E f−f||²+||U_O f−f||²)/4
                ≥ sin²(pi/D)||f||²/8.

Hence the lazy operator L has spectral gap at least

    gamma_D=sin²(pi/D)/16,                            (4)

and all its eigenvalues lie in [0,1]. If mu_t is its law after t steps from any single residue,

    ||mu_t−Uniform||_TV
       ≤ (1/2)sqrt(D−1) (1−gamma_D)^t.                (5)

The square-root factor is the exact L2 norm of the initial point-mass density minus one. This is a quantitative finite-clock theorem obtained from the actual affine maps, with no prime-supply or random-carry assumption.

It allows E,O and their inverses, and a holding step. It is not the physical directed Collatz iteration. The deterioration gamma_D~pi²/(16D²) is explicit, not a uniform gap 1/2.

## 4. A forward eigenvalue bound, with its nonnormality boundary

For the forward random-choice operator

    P=(U_E+U_O)/2,

every eigenvalue lambda on the mean-zero subspace obeys

    |lambda| ≤ 1−sin²(pi/D)/8.                         (6)

To prove this, take Pf=lambda f. If lambda=0 the assertion is immediate. Otherwise write zeta=lambda/|lambda| and replace U_E,U_O in (2) by zeta^(-1)U_E,zeta^(-1)U_O. Scalar phases cancel in their commutator, while

    ||U_E f−zeta f||²+||U_O f−zeta f||²
        =4(1−|lambda|)||f||².

Combining with (2),(3) yields (6).

The eigenspace at one consists only of constants: equality in the average of the two unitaries forces a fixed vector to be fixed by both, hence by the generating translation. Since P is a contraction, its powers are bounded; a nontrivial Jordan block at one would make them unbounded. Thus one is also algebraically simple. Formula (6) also excludes every other unit-modulus eigenvalue.

P need not be normal. An eigenvalue bound is therefore not by itself the operator-norm estimate ||P^t−Pi||≤(1−gap)^t. A finite-time statement must control Jordan blocks or a singular-value/energy argument. Equation (5) is only claimed for the self-adjoint lazy symmetric chain.

## 5. Why this does not close the fixed-shape carry problem

For a binary word w of length q with p odd steps, its affine map has form

    x ↦ (3^p x+B_w)/2^q.

At the cycle clock D=|2^q−3^p|, its multiplier is one modulo D, so the target is the retained translation B_w. A fixed-shape word family conditions simultaneously on length and number of O steps. The random-choice operator P averages over all compositions with binomial weights; L averages over a larger inverse/holding alphabet. Neither law is the fixed-shape law.

Moreover, in any explicitly large-clock regime with D² log D much larger than q, the sufficient time supplied by (5) is far beyond q. Near-critical entropy alone does not supply a lower bound on |2^q−3^p|; the arithmetic approximation factor must be controlled separately. Thus even a correct finite-clock mixing theorem can be quantitatively irrelevant to a proposed period-q exclusion. The paper's reservoir proof succeeds because its selected-label supply and phase dispersion are proved at compatible scales; the clock theorem does not provide that compatibility for carries.

The exact remaining object is the coefficient of z^p in

    (U_E+z U_O)^q,

or the equivalent additive-frequency recurrence, retaining the order of noncommuting factors. The coefficient is a sum over fixed-composition words, not the product of independent scalar character factors used for Carmichael subsets.

## 6. A reusable commutator comparison principle

The proof applies whenever two permutations have a commutator that is one long cycle. More generally, if the commutator has a known gap on the relevant invariant subspace, (2) transfers a fraction of that gap to the symmetric generator walk. If the commutator has several orbits, its invariant functions must first be removed or controlled by additional generators.

This is an explicit representation-level bridge from affine dynamics to spectral comparison. It shows how to restore a lost nonabelian coordinate rather than assuming scalar Fourier analysis still sees it.
