# Session 54: replace a numerical resemblance with a testable carrier

25 October 2026 (UTC). Final research report. See the [compact synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md) and the
[exact explanatory diagrams](diagrams/README.md). The assignment is to revisit old
'numerology' verdicts boldly, without reviving a disproved equation.
The [recovered atlas](dismissed-atlas/REPORT.md) distinguishes ten old claims,
their actual correction status, and what information a stronger connection
would need to preserve. Absence of a map is different from proof that no

Our concept board is: symmetry actions; converse and other lost operations;
explicit digit languages; p-adic completion versus ordinary integers;
finite-budget transfer; and cyclotomic/modular observers. The anchor is the
old verdicts themselves, the coefficient fractal is the niche, and the
elliptic tangent family is the wildcard.

## A real fractal behind the shared-clock analogy

Session 53 proved exact coefficient digit laws for Cooper's normalized s7
root c(n). Its modulo 4 positive exceptions occur exactly at powers of 2.
Its modulo 3 nonzero indices have only base 9 digits 0,1,2; modulo 9 allows
at most one digit from 3,4,5 and none from 6,7,8.

The [cross-base result](cross-base-fractal/REPORT.md), with
[independent audit](audit-cross-base/REPORT.md), studies precisely which
exponents k make 2^k pass those digit conditions. At depth r the exponent
clock is M_r=6*9^(r-1), and the exact number of surviving residue classes is

    2*3^(r-1), or 2(r+1)*3^(r-1) with one allowed defect.

The inverse-limit exponent space has two parity sheets. The 3-adic
logarithm transports the corresponding digit sets by a similarity; each
completed survivor set has Hausdorff dimension exactly 1/2. General counting
and logarithmic mechanisms were already in the old foundry and clock notes;
the coefficient-specific extraction and its limits are explicitly credited.

For ordinary nonnegative exponents, the proved uniform bounds in ANY interval
of length K are O(sqrt(K)) and O(sqrt(K)*log(2K)). Thus the exponents have zero
upper Banach density. This neither proves finiteness nor classifies them.
The tempting guess that only 0 and 1 survive is false:

    2^13=8192=(12212)_9.

Through exponent 2000 the complete survivors are {0,1,13}, or
{0,1,2,7,13} with one defect. These lists remain finite observations.
For every fixed depth, moving exponent witnesses exist, but their p-adic
limit need not be an ordinary integer. This is a genuine fractal result,
not a picture-based Sierpinski identification or a Collatz coverage theorem.

## A CM selection theorem, not an MRB-defect formula

For integer m>4 set lambda_m=tan²(pi/m), and take the corresponding Legendre
elliptic curve. The [classification](tangent-cm/RESEARCH.md) and
[independent audit](audit-tangent-cm/AUDIT.md) prove:

    This curve has complex multiplication exactly when m=8.

At that point j=8000 and the CM discriminant is−8, already known from the
prior Bolza/MRB work. The new content is the all-integer parameter exclusion,
not rediscovery of that curve. In particular the old amplitude/elliptic
connection was worth pursuing beyond a numerical match.

The mechanism is finite and structural. j is a rational function of degree 3
of a real cyclotomic coordinate. If it is a singular modulus, its abelian
real Galois field must be multiquadratic. Therefore the square subgroup of
the ambient cyclotomic Galois group has size at most 3. This bounds every
prime-power conductor factor and yields a provably complete finite screen.
Exact field-degree and integrality checks leave m=8,16,24,48; small exact
CM-discriminant bounds eliminate the latter three.

The argument uses nonfundamental CM orders as well, but no complete idoneal
catalogue, GRH, approximate near-integers or uncontrolled parameter search.
The publication edition carries exact citations and source hashes, not
copies of third-party papers. Independent multiplication-matrix checks and
reduced-form enumeration verify the finite certificate.

This does NOT express A(8)-C_MRB as a theta term, explain why the greedy MRB
procedure selects 8, or identify Ogg's 15 primes with the curve's reduction
predicate. Those different questions remain separate.

## The 63 coincidence has a symmetry-preserving replacement

The [Paley blocker bridge](sixtythree-bridge/README.md) and
[converse extension](sixtythree-bridge/CONVERSE.md), with their
[independent audit](audit-sixtythree/REPORT.md), use the actual 63 minimum
six-arc Hamiltonian-path blockers of P7. There are no five-arc blockers.

There is an explicit Aut(P7)-equivariant bijection with the nonempty even
subsets of the seven vertices. Its formulas distinguish two-source,
two-sink, common-out/common-in Hall, and star types. It is not obtained
merely by listing 63 arbitrary objects on each side.

A stronger model retains converse: identify nonzero elements of F64 with
exponents e modulo 63. Translation of the Paley labels by b acts as
 e -> e+9b; multiplication of labels by 2 acts as e ->16e. The blocker
converse acts as e ->-e, or multiplicative inversion in the field.
The full group has orbits of sizes 7,14,42, corresponding to phase orders
1,3,9 in F64*/F8*. These are exactly 7*phi(1),7*phi(3),7*phi(9).

The required loss is now proved, not guessed: converse fixes exactly one
nonzero blocker, whereas any linear involution on a six-dimensional binary
space fixes at least seven nonzero vectors. No six-dimensional linear
converse extension can work. The even-subset module and additive F64 module
are also not linearly equivalent under the smaller group, despite their
isomorphic underlying permutation sets. Native arc XOR and metric are not
preserved. The old Sierpinski action has a different fixed-point profile and
still is not the desired carrier.

Thus the old shared count has a real replacement once the carrier and
operation are changed explicitly. It does not prove a forbidden Hamiltonian
count, an all-size metagraph law, or a linear code identity.

## A larger-order stress test explains the exception

The [P11 stress test](p11-stress/README.md),
[independently audited](audit-p11/REPORT.md), tests rather than assumes the
analogous F1024* model. Exactly one of the 55 order-five-invariant ten-arc
sets blocks a Hamiltonian path: star 0. Every other such set, and all 11
invariant five-arc sets, has a recorded surviving Hamiltonian path.
Frobenius x->x^4 on F1024* fixes three points. This fixed-point mismatch
refutes the proposed equivariant extension, including for the unknown
minimum-blocker family. It does not refute the bare number 1023 by itself.

A general theorem explains why the Hall part changes. Every doubly regular
tournament of order n>=11 has exactly n(n-1) Hall blockers of size n-1:
the two-source and two-sink pairs. The common-neighbor expansion inequality
is strict for every interior Hall size. At n=7 it has precisely the two
boundary equalities responsible for the extra two seven-element families.
Stars add n non-Hall examples, but no complete general HP-blocker census or
general inclusion-minimality theorem is claimed.

At P11 the exact Hall count is 110 and the stars contribute 11 more examples.
All 121 are independently verified inclusion-minimal. The stress certificates
alone left global beta and the full count unresolved. The later arguments
below now prove beta(P11)=10 and the complete count 121.

## Finite debt changes the pole, not the fractal dimension

The [budget transfer supplement](fractal-budget-transfer/REPORT.md) and
[audit](audit-budget-transfer/REPORT.md) generalize the exponent sieve to a
fixed number b of defective digits. Its state transition is 3(I+N), with N
the nilpotent operator advancing the defect count. The initial state is
(2,2,0,...), with the b=0 case treated separately. For r>=1,

    |S_(r,b)|=2*3^(r-1) sum_(j=0)^min(b,r) binom(r,j).

The generating function in r-1 is

    2/(1-3z) + 2 sum_(j=1)^b (3z)^(j-1)/(1-3z)^(j+1).

Its minimal denominator has the exact pole order b+1. The polynomial r^b
factor is an observable Jordan-block effect. For fixed b the completed
set still has Hausdorff dimension 1/2; the logarithmic counting factor does
not add a geometric dimension. Budgets b>=2 are abstract digit languages,
not newly proved coefficient congruences modulo 3^(b+1). No fictitious r=0
primitive-root clock is introduced.

## A reusable finite-conductor test for rational observers

The [rational CM observer lemma](rational-cm-observer/THEOREM.md) and
[audit](audit-rational-observer/AUDIT.md) broaden the mechanism behind m=8.
Let R=A/B be nonconstant over Q, of rational-map degree d>=1. If
R(2cos(2pi/n)) is a singular modulus, n>=3, and the denominator is nonzero,
then the square subgroup of the real cyclotomic Galois group has size<=d.
For each odd prime-power p^a dividing n this forces

    p^(a-1)*(p-1)/gcd(4,p-1) <= d;

for 2^a, a>=3, it forces 2^(a-3)<=d. Consequently n divides an explicit finite
integer N_d depending only on d, not on the coefficients of R. At d=3 one
may take N_3=2^4*3^2*5*7*13. This is a necessary finite screen, not a
sufficient CM criterion or an assertion that the bound is optimal.

Constants and poles are essential exclusions. Different rational observers
can realize CM at other conductors: an exact affine n=5 example gives
CM discriminant−15. Thus m=8 uniqueness belongs to the tangent-Legendre
family, not to every trigonometric or cyclotomic construction.

## Native boundary becomes projectivization, with a typed repair

The [boundary supplement](boundary-projectivization/README.md) and its
[independent audit](audit-boundary-polarity/REPORT.md) retain more than the
blocker count. A small adjustment of the field coding makes equality of the
native undirected vertex-incidence boundaries EXACTLY equality modulo 21 of
field exponents. The three-point fibers are the cosets of F4* in F64*.
They give 21 projective points of PG(2,4), not 21 projective lines disguised as
three-element sets. A genuine projective line has five points.

There is a sharp obstruction and a genuine repair. Bare inversion on those
21 points sends none of the 21 projective lines to a line. On two typed
sheets, however, the [trace-polarity construction](boundary-projectivization/TWO-SHEET.md)
realizes converse as a correlation interchanging points and lines. The base
has 42 typed objects and 105 incidences. Pulling that NEW relation back to
two 63-blocker sheets gives 126 objects and 945 incidences, degree 15. This is
a complete bipartite blow-up along each base incidence, not a graph covering
with local degree preserved. Its twins are precisely the native boundary
fibers, while its adjacency is not determined by native shared-arc counts.

The nonlinear correction is explicit: for x!=0 in F64,

    x^(-1)=x^8 / Norm_(F64/F8)(x), with Norm(x)=x^9.

Field conjugation x->x^8 alone fixes seven nonzero points; inversion fixes
only one. Forgetting the norm coordinate makes converse look linear but
loses its actual fixed points. This is a concrete missing-coordinate repair,
not permission to transfer every invariant between the carriers.

## Cycle packing and potentials close the native P11 classification

The [packing bound](cycle-packing-bound/README.md) and
[audit](audit-cycle-packing/AUDIT.md) observe that any HP blocker must delete
at least two arcs from each arc-disjoint Hamiltonian cycle. A tournament
with a Hamilton-cycle decomposition therefore has beta=n-1, since vertex
isolation supplies the opposite bound. Every odd-prime-order circulant has
such an explicit decomposition by nonzero differences. In particular
beta(P11)=10 is proved, not inferred from the 121 known examples. This does
not assert a decomposition for every regular tournament.

The [cycle-potential classification](cycle-potential-classification/README.md),
with [independent audit](audit-cycle-potential/AUDIT.md), then proves the
FULL minimum-blocker census:

    P7: 63;       P11: 121=55+55+11.

Arc-transitivity and orbit averaging make every minimum blocker meet every
Hamiltonian cycle in exactly two arcs. A small exact cycle-rank certificate
forces the blocker vector into the row-plus-column potential space. The
integer nature of those potentials comes from connected bipartite support
and edge values, not from assuming that a rational rank certificate is an
integral cycle-lattice basis. The selected P11 minor is−2, not a unit.

Threshold levels of these integer potentials give nested, Hall-compatible
cuts with positive integer deficiencies totaling 2. The doubly regular
expansion inequalities classify every possible level. Remaining mixed
row/column candidates admit explicit endpoint-compatible Hamiltonian paths.
Only 9 and 35 cycles are needed in the rank certificates; the final candidate
universes have 84 and 176 elements. This is an exhaustiveness proof, not an
enumeration of every ten-arc deletion subset.

Thus the earlier F1024* equivariant guess fails for two distinct established
reasons: its subgroup fixed-point count was wrong, and the complete native
count is 121 rather than 1023. The positive P7 bridge survives; the overly
broad extrapolation does not. Earlier frozen packets retain the chronology
of their former open boundaries, superseded here by the later proofs.

## The 1729 direction retains an actual arithmetic observer

The [endpoint observer](paley-endpoint-observer/RESULT.md) and
[independent audit](audit-paley-endpoint/AUDIT.md) revisit the corrected
blanket-nonmodularity verdict. For a Paley prime q=3 modulo 4, write r=(q-1)/2.
There are two endpoint counts a,b, and

    E=aA+bA^T, s=a+b, delta=a-b.

The open/closed values at 7 and 11 were already known. Independently verified
at 19, they are 3040229428 and 3817640437. Rédei parity forces s and delta odd,
so delta never vanishes. The rational algebra generated by E is
Q times Q(sqrt(-q)). Its generated augmentation order has conductor|delta|
and discriminant -q*delta^2. This is not a claim about the full integral
commutant or a modular form whose coefficients are s(q).

The whole integral algebra does not split into a direct product. With
lambda=(-s+delta*sqrt(-q))/2 and N=(s^2+q*delta^2)/4, its image in
Z times Z[lambda] consists exactly of

    (z,u+v*lambda), z=u+v*r*s  (mod M),
    M=(r*s)^2+s*r*s+N=q*(q*s^2+delta^2)/4.

The gluing index is M. Thus an actual integral congruence survives where a
rational decomposition would forget it. The recovered law b=1 modulo q
also gives a useful precise distinction: q divides M universally, while q
need not divide delta. Indeed v_q(M)=2 if q divides delta, and 1 otherwise.
At 19 the conductor is not divisible by 19, but the gluing index still is.

The old fixed-field prime-factorization pattern has not been repaired by
renaming this varying quadratic field. What survives is a specified
endpoint operator and its generated order, not a scalar modular-coefficient
law or a proof about the MRB constant.

## Endpoint markings separate what scalar incidence hides

The [joint response theorem](endpoint-blocker-response/PROOF.txt), with
[independent audit](audit-endpoint-response/AUDIT.txt), connects the two
operator lanes. A potential blocker b_uv=a_u-d_v with total deficiency 2
meets every Hamiltonian path from s to t in exactly 2-a_t+d_s arcs. It is the
actual endpoint marking, not the interior choice of path, that controls this
response.

All 121 P11 minimum blockers have the same scalar histogram:17290 paths
meet them once and 77805 twice. Yet they are different blockers and symmetry
types. P7's 49 pair/star minima have 54/135 in those two bins; its 14 additional
Hall minima have 57/129/3 in bins 1/2/3. The pathwise and endpoint-weighted
checks are independent.

For the stated n>=5 family consisting exactly of source pairs, sink pairs
and stars, a sharp marked-query result holds. Fix one observer vertex and
allow one Hamiltonian path for each ordered endpoint pair incident to it.
Within this 2(n-1)-query pool, exactly 2n-4 queries are necessary and sufficient
to distinguish every minimum blocker. Every optimal design omits both query
directions to the SAME other vertex; there are exactly n-1 such designs.

The n=4 failure is a formal signature countermodel, not a claim that a
four-vertex tournament realizes all the hypotheses. The optimum is only
within this fixed pool, not over arbitrary or adaptive path queries.
The certified larger Paley cases supply the needed hypotheses, giving
18,34,42,58 queries at n=11,19,23,31.

## Scope of the session's rehabilitation

Several directions once labelled numerology now have explicit maps,
operators, or classification proofs. Original false predicates remain
false: the Sierpinski action is not the blocker action, F1024 does not encode
the P11 minimum family, bare inversion is not a collineation, and a failed
scalar factorization is not a modular-form theorem. Positive structure is
recovered by specifying the carrier and the operation, not by overriding
counterexamples.

The remaining all-order cycle-rank problem, ordinary integer intersection
problem for the digit language, full native tournament metagraph wiring,
and independent A000568 predictions are not settled by this session.
