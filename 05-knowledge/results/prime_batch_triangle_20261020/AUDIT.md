# Audit and reproduction

**Review scope:** same-agent proof review plus separate exact algorithms.
No independent-agent audit was requested or performed in this session.
The propositions remain in the research-results route; no numbered canon
theorem or formal Lean proof is claimed.

## Proof gates

1. **Upper bound:** the exponent-box restriction is used on *part sizes*,
   not merely on the number of future coordinates. Both coloured parts are
   p-smooth and have combined mass at most N/p. Degree is separate. The
   Laplace/product estimate is uniform in s in [1/2,1), and the three ranges
   of log p cover intermediate batches. Polynomial bit overhead is absorbed
   by choosing an intermediate constant between c and 1/2. Finite timings
   and partition counts are not the asymptotic proof.
2. **Lower bound:** all rows and future words fit the same horizon and the
   row cell has common committed degree. Positivity is used only for the
   ordinary recurrence; the claim is not silently extended to signed core
   cancellation. The principal Gram matrix proves wordwise linear rank,
   not arbitrary computational complexity.
3. **Slack six:** the proof uses actual full-group orbits, not one chosen
   automorphism. Five-orbits are excluded by the charge/congruence condition;
   larger ones by charge. The two nonuniform triangle orbits consume the
   full external budget. Their union is forced to be a module by the
   three-edge cost of a separated ordering. Both relative orientations are
   kept; the matrix complement only exchanges the two degree triples.
4. **Four-endpoint grammar:** local triangle intersection tests detect
   weighted feedback one versus two. All weights are positive. A coloured
   automorphism orbit of size five would require feedback three; two
   nonuniform triangle orbits would require four. Nontrivial weight-three
   orbits would cost at least nine. Thus the second local test is sufficient
   for coloured rigidity. Two homogeneous directed triangles cannot overlap:
   overlap two immediately violates uniform external incidence; overlap one
   forces an outside vertex to see the other triangle in both directions.
   All accepted templates therefore have the claimed full group. Overlapping
   *presentations* are explicitly not counted as distinct classes.
5. **Prime powers:** factorial valuations give only a per-prime bound;
   different prime costs are not generally added. The mixed balance cost15
   is proved separately using the allowed orbit sizes and the regular-seven
   five-cycle obstruction. Group order p^a is distinguished from an actual
   element of that order. The carousel witness works at every odd order.
6. **Geometry:** exact Q(sqrt3) distances exclude unintended contacts at
   every translation offset. The list argument establishes the full boundary
   relation already at four colours. Six-colour lists, cubic edge-colourings
   and oriented tournament arcs remain different carriers. No branch in the
   older 375-branch geometry is claimed removed.
7. **Collatz:** the congruence is modulo 2^(K+1), not 2^K; the extra bit pins
   the final oddness and all earlier valuations. Sorting changes a formal
   envelope, never the physical word or original source. Refinement preserves
   the integer clock and shrinks the permutation set, making acceptance
   monotone. The DP minimizes over block cuts at a fixed prefix. A failure
   on a first-exit prefix says nothing about every later prefix.
8. **Concurrent claims:** the newer deficit-two completion and its approval
   record supersede the frozen manuscript's pending-audit heading. Its
   all-order classification is cited, not inferred from our n<=7 census.
   The exact center H/blue data are finite enumerations; SCC product laws
   supply the explicitly stated all-order wrapping consequence.

## Errors found and repaired before the first push

* The separate Collatz composition test refuted a draft formula for the
  one-block limiting anchor. The shift by two was incorrectly attached to n
  instead of z=n-1. The correct shift is T_1(n)+1=(3/2)(n+1), giving
  T_1^s(1)=2(3/2)^s-1. Source27 remains a hostile and the paid four-block
  endpoint is unchanged. The mechanism is recorded in MISTAKES.md.
* A manual import of the first checker exposed a module-name collision with
  the inherited file also named verify.py. The loader now uses explicit
  module objects and a scoped sibling binding. Standalone and imported use
  both work. This was a test-harness failure, not a mathematical correction.

## Reproduce

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/verify.py > /tmp/triangle-main.json
python3 -O 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/verify.py > /tmp/triangle-main-O.json
cmp /tmp/triangle-main.json /tmp/triangle-main-O.json
python3 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/separate_controls.py > /tmp/triangle-separate.json
python3 -O 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/separate_controls.py > /tmp/triangle-separate-O.json
cmp /tmp/triangle-separate.json /tmp/triangle-separate-O.json
python3 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/block_probe.py > /tmp/triangle-blocks.json
python3 -O 05-knowledge/results/prime_batch_triangle_20261020/block_probe.py > /tmp/triangle-blocks-O.json
cmp /tmp/triangle-blocks.json /tmp/triangle-blocks-O.json
```

The stored receipts and manifest bind the checked source versions. Normal
and optimized outputs must match byte for byte. Checks use explicit exceptions,
not Python assertions. No runtime package beyond the standard library is needed.

Final runs: `verify.py` completed **191,198** explicit checks and
`separate_controls.py` completed **167,737**, totaling **358,935**.
Each has byte-identical normal and optimized output; the 1,007-source probe
also matches byte for byte. `agents/check_docs.py` passes. These are finite
controls and same-agent review, not an independent proof audit. In particular,
the refinement theorem splits a fixed word; it gives no bound for arbitrary
appended future steps or the original uniform ROOT-current problem.
