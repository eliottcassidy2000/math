# Eulerian Fourier modes and commuting squares

The cut-aggregate spectrum is the signed Hamilton-path transform indexed by Eulerian edge sets. A linear-forest formula evaluates it exactly. Triangle modes equal −(n−2)!(n−4)(n−9), giving at least 168 zero modes on the full order-nine quotient and at least 84 on each parity component.
