## Reviewed claim and proof boundary

The reviewed proof is `TWISTED_PAIR_LIFTS.md`, with checker
`check_holonomy.py`. Its precise target is a section of an affine
endpoint-choice bundle on a finite graph. It is **not** trivialization of
all palette transports. Arbitrary edge transports are allowed; their
geometric realization by a unit-distance exterior is not asserted.

At each vertex, three pairs yield `V=F2^3`. An oriented edge has affine
transport `x -> omega + sigma*x`, with `sigma` a coordinate permutation.
The section equation is `b_v+sigma*b_u=omega`. This is exactly a
coboundary equation for the local system with linear transport `sigma`.
Changing the local orders or endpoint frames conjugates/gauges the
presentation but does not change existence of a section.

The parity map `p(x)=sum x_i` is invariant under all coordinate permutations.
Its kernel `E` has dimension two. The invariant vector `j=(1,1,1)` has
`p(j)=1`, so the splitting

```
V = E direct_sum F2*j
```

is equivariant, not just a chosen vector-space decomposition. Therefore it
splits the entire two-term graph cochain complex and its cohomology.
Projecting to completion parity forgets exactly `H^1(Gamma;E)`.

For a connected graph with `v` vertices, `e` edges and
`beta=e-v+1`, a spanning tree identifies flat sections with fixed vectors
of the cycle holonomy group `H`. If `f=dim E^H`, rank-nullity gives

```
dim H^1(Gamma;E) = 2e - (2v-f) = 2(beta-1)+f.
```

This proof works equally for loops or parallel edges; the candidate's
simple-graph census is evidence, not a restriction on the theorem. For a
disconnected graph one sums this expression componentwise.

The subgroup table is complete: identity fixes all of `E`; a transposition
fixes the one-dimensional line generated by its swapped-coordinate sum;
a three-cycle fixes only constant triples in `V`, hence only zero in `E`.
The possibilities `H=1,C2,C3,S3` consequently give `f=2,1,0,0`.
If the affine system is soluble, its solution set is a torsor under
`H^0(V)`, of dimension `1+f`. The odd invariant vector exchanges its two
parity classes, so each prescribed compatible completion has exactly `2^f`
lifts. These counts are conditional on full solvability.

## Independent Fano reconstruction

The audit did not assume that there are two completions. Label the six
slots `0,...,5` in pairs and add point `6`. The three lines through `6`
are forced. Each remaining line must choose one point of each pair; there
are eight such triples. Among all `binom(8,4)=70` choices of four additional
lines, literal pair incidence yields exactly two Steiner triple systems:
the four even and the four odd endpoint-choice triples.

For all 48 maps `x -> omega+sigma*x`, the audit constructs the actual
permutation of all seven Fano points and transports every line. The two
completions change by precisely `p(omega)`. Thus the completion equation
really is `eta_v+eta_u=p(omega_e)`, with constant scalar coefficients.

A marked conic-label torsor can carry the same abstract cube action once
three independent directions are chosen. The inherited geometric
translations agree with its translation subgroup. Neither this marking
nor the parity proof realizes every coordinate permutation by a surface
automorphism or planar isometry. The genus-two and surface interpretations
remain inherited sidecars, not new geometric theorems audited here.

## Independent finite universe and hostile controls

`check_independent.py` imports none of the candidate implementations. Its
holonomy path enumerates **all 259 ordered tuples of 0, 1, 2 or 3 elements
of S3**, on bouquet graphs with the corresponding cycle rank. For every
tuple it examines every affine gain assignment: **112,945 assignments**
in total. It solves the root equation directly over the eight triples,
without computing coboundary rank or a holonomy-group closure.

For each linear tuple it compares the literal root-image fibers with the
parity-compatible gains. The index is exactly `2^[2(beta-1)+f]`; every
nonempty fiber has `2^(1+f)` roots, balanced equally between the two
completion parities. Tree gauge explains why this is an independent
finite test of the same arbitrary-graph mechanism.

Hostiles and boundaries retained:

- Identity monodromy with nonzero even gain: completion exists, section
  does not; two forgotten bits on a single cycle.
- Transposition monodromy: only its one-dimensional image can be repaired;
  one forgotten bit remains.
- Three-cycle monodromy: `I+sigma` is invertible on `E`; on a single cycle,
  completion compatibility is sufficient.
- Two independent cycles with the same three-cycle: common invariants
  still vanish, but the lost dimension is two, not zero.
- Zero gain with a three-cycle has the sections `000` and `111`, while
  the prescribed transport is nontrivial. This explicitly separates a
  section from a full transport trivialization.
- At beta zero the only realizable holonomy is trivial; the formula is
  zero, not a negative-dimensional obstruction.

The candidate checker was separately replayed in ordinary and optimized

This tests every connected labeled simple graph on one through four
vertices with every S3 edge assignment, the affine one-cycle controls, and
all 48 affine cube maps. That is a replay, distinguished from the
