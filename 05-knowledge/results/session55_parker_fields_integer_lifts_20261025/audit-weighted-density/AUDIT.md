With finer partitions ordered below coarser ones, prescribed equalities mean summing above the test partition. The zeta transform is therefore multiplicative. 4. **Uniform odd-field asymptotic.** The exact admissible-v identity subtracts (1+chi(2))/2 at the two roots. Its error is at most 5/4; removing at most five exceptional values gives 25/4. Outside those exceptions the six roots are distinct in every odd characteristic, including 3. Product squarefreeness, not degree smaller than characteristic, is the required hypothesis. The zero-center term is not confused with the eight zero-cell lines at center one. This establishes the all-odd-prime-power asymptotic, not just the tested range.
