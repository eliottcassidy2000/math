## Seven-cell adversarial checks

- An automorphism-free tournament need not refine discretely. The proof
  uses the special classification of proper equitable partitions INSIDE
  a regular-seven cell, not a general implication from rigidity. The
  inherited order31 rigid but nondiscrete cases remain explicit hostiles.
- A nonuniform binary block can have repeated rows and columns. The
  deterministic bank contains literal such blocks. Functionality here is
  on internal tournament charts, a smaller set than arbitrary permutations.
- Equitability is used on both axes. A row invariant under a rotation of
  the two three-cycles is not excluded; a nonuniform biregular matrix of
  such rows is excluded.
- The rectangular rule propagates INTO a regular-seven cell. The reverse
  direction is not asserted for an arbitrary larger source cell.
- Two marks suffice PER connected nonuniform support component. Uniformly
  joined independent Paley modules disprove any claimed constant budget
  for arbitrarily many disconnected support components.
- Actual owner labels, cell names and path positions remain retained.
  The proof does not infer a literal Hamiltonian port from its endpoints
  or from an unaligned chart relation.
- The elementary proof covers every nonuniform biregular block; the442
  block bank is separately identified as a sample. All4184 automorphism-
  invariant row-regular matrices in the specified seven-cell test are
  exhausted; those are a different universe.

## Independent review of the five-cell packet

Read `five/README.md` in full. The following implications check out:

1. Two compatible chart pairs agreeing on the other cell differ by a
   one-sided internal automorphism. Every nonidentity regular C_p rotation
   is transitive, so a nonuniform equitable block has trivial one-sided
   kernel.
2. A subgroup of C_p times C_p with injective projections is trivial or
   a nonzero-slope line. Its nonempty compatibility coset is therefore a
   singleton or p-point affine relation. This needs the stated ACTUAL
   internal group C_p, not just a prime number of vertices.
3. All chart equations share one root variable. A closing gain fixes that
   variable or a closing offset rejects the comparison. They cannot be
   tested with separately chosen endpoint charts.
4. A proper nondiscrete equitable partition of regular R5 would have
   shape3+1+1; its singleton degree cannot be two. Thus one owner makes
   R5 discrete, and nonuniform equitable attachments propagate that fact.
5. The degree-two repeated-row family has a unique K2,2 component and
   complementary six-cycle, giving binom(5,2)^2 times3! =600 blocks.
   Their repeated pair cannot be stabilized by a nonidentity five-cycle.
6. The three actual five-cell examples have distinct cell outdegrees
   4,7,10, so their claimed nonisomorphism is not merely a named-cell
   artifact. The zero/one/five abstract relation census is clearly
   distinguished from realizability by a common pair of tournaments.

## Later five-cell intrinsic flags and all-prime refinement extension

The later Section4A was also read and checked. Constant row pair-type
places all selected two-sets on one intrinsic pentagon. The odd-cycle
equations m_i+m_(i+1)=2 force each edge once. Constant column pair-type
then makes the row-to-edge assignment an isomorphism between pentagons,
hence affine. Therefore every degree-two/three rigid block really has a
nonconstant actual-owner flag in at least one direction. The rooted
displacement flag handles degree-one/four. No outside owner is needed
when that intrinsic flag starts refinement.

For blocks with line stabilizers, the selected actual bijections have
the block's slope. Backward traversal correctly uses the inverse; a new
reverse winner would introduce a false translation. A nonunit cycle
gain yields a unique intrinsic fixed owner. Balanced nonflat cycles
retain C5 even though their parallel-owner section is empty. This is
consistent with the inherited order31 hostile, not a contradiction.

Read `five/PRIME-REFINEMENT.md` in full. Its rational spectral argument
is sound: equal nonconstant Fourier eigenvalues give a cyclotomic
polynomial multiple with zero constant coefficient, hence equal
connection sets. The stated FULL Aut=C_p hypothesis forces the
multiplier to be identity, so the conjugate orbit has degree p-1.
The zero-sum rational representation is an irreducible A-module.
Any proper equitable indicator span contains a nonzero vector in that
module and must therefore contain the whole module and constants,
forcing a discrete partition. Simple spectrum without this rational
irreducibility would not justify the inference; the text retains both.
