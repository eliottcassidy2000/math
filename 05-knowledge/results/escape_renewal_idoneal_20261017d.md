## 2. A lower bracket that survives every native refinement

Keep w(n)=3[n=2 mod3]-1, the ROOT-supported full-clock sign t(n), and
a_N=H(4N)-H(N). For a finite authenticated native graph, let

\[
 c_e(N)=\sum_{N<n\le4N,\ e(n)=e} w(n)(-1)^{d(n)},\quad
 R(N)=c_1(N),\quad U(N)=\sum_{e\ne1}|c_e(N)|.
\]

Every d(n) is the actual integer path length; e(n) is the literal odd
terminal. ROOT is stopped at1. These are the same coefficients used by
the native matching certificates, whose residual is B=|R|+U.

**D1 (PROVED).** Without assuming convergence,

\[
 R-U\le a_N\le R+U,\qquad
 L(N):=(|R|-U)_+\le |a_N|\le B(N).                    \tag{1}
\]

The signed intervals are nested under every native refinement. Thus L
can only increase and B can only decrease.

Proof: the exact stopped-path identity gives
a_N=R+sum_(e!=1)c_e t(e), with t(e) in {-1,0,1}. Under refinement the
nonROOT columns undergo signed mergers, possibly into ROOT. If their
total signed contribution entering ROOT is alpha, then R'=R+alpha and
U'<=U-|alpha|. This proves interval containment. No endpoint residue is
substituted for endpoint equality, and no original source weight is
renormalized. The lower bound concerns an **annular signed sum**, not
membership of each unresolved source in ROOT.

Let C be any current anchor-cone upper certificate from the preceding
packet, and let C' be any future valid certificate on the same physical
rows. With U_f(t)=#{N:f(N)>=t}, (1) gives the independently computable
unavoidable debt floor

\[
 \max_t\frac{t^2}{X^2}(U_{C'}(t)-U_C(t)/2)_+
 \ \ge\ \max_t\frac{t^2}{X^2}(U_L(t)-U_C(t)/2)_+.    \tag{2}
\]

This does not refute the desired subpower debt bound, which permits
positive bounded debt. It prevents trying to force debt to zero merely

## 4. Escaping a colouring place is a field operation, not a degree count

At an inherited conjugation-fixed19-place of E, the residue is F19^2 and
conjugation is Frobenius19. If a real unit d has nonsquare residue in
F19, adjoining sqrt(d) splits into two places exchanged by conjugation:
the two residue roots are Frobenius conjugates, whereas complex
conjugation must fix the real sqrt(d). Thus neither extension place is
fixed. In particular d=3 destroys every inherited19-place. A later
extension cannot restore a fixed place above it, since restriction of a
fixed place would already be fixed.

Adjoining zeta11 without sqrt3 behaves differently: ord_11(19)=10,

different escape mechanisms, not just different dimensions.

cubic root is1112. This is a convenient exact **edge sieve**, not a

## 6. Why1848 gives16, and why the next conductor fails

Under the repository's adopted #003 zero-free input, the65 idoneal

The relevant group for n is the proper class group of discriminant-4n.
[Watkins's two tables](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/~watkins/papers/IDONcorr.pdf)
distinguish the order for1848 from its maximal order:

|---:|---:|---:|---|
|462|-1848|1|C2^3|
|1848|-7392|2|C2^4|

The group structures through conductor64 are independently computed
by PARI. The first two are also proved by complete reduced-form lists.
The16 at1848 is one extra dyadic class bit over a maximal order with
eight classes. It is not the class number of that maximal order.

**D2 (classical conductor/genus specialization, PROVED).** More generally
let m be odd squarefree and D_k=-8m*4^k. The class-number formula gives
h(D_k)=2^k h(-8m). If g_k is the number of inversion-fixed classes,

\[
 g_0=2^{\omega(m)},\qquad g_k=2^{\omega(m)+1}\ (k\ge1).
\]

Thus these orders have exponent at most2 exactly when k is0 or1 and
h(-8m)=2^omega(m). In particular a dyadic conductor cannot indefinitely
create only self-inverse classes in any such field.

For the fixed-class count, ambiguous reduced forms lie on b=0,
b=a, or a=c. The b=0 forms are coprime factorizations of
2m*4^k, giving2^omega(m) unordered pairs. At k=0 the other boundaries
are impossible by their2-adic valuations. At k>=1 the other forms
are parametrized by coprime unordered factors s<t of2m*4^(k-1),
which have opposite parity: if t>=3s use (a,b,c)=(4s,4s,s+t);
if t<3s use (s+t,2(t-s),s+t). Equality is counted only once.
There are again2^omega(m) pairs. This proves the stated count.
Finally |Cl[2]|=|Cl/Cl^2| for a finite abelian class group, and exponent2
is equivalent to h=g. The class-number formula follows from conductor
2^k at a ramified prime and unchanged units. These are standard
quadratic-order mechanisms, not a new general class-number theorem.

For m=231 there is a particularly transparent all-order witness:

\[
 (a,b,c)=(16,8,1+462\cdot4^{k-2}),\qquad k\ge2.       \tag{4}
\]

It has discriminant-1848*4^k, is primitive, and satisfies0<b<a<c.
It is strictly inside the reduced-form chamber, so its inverse
(a,-b,c) is a different reduced class. Every level past1848 in this
conductor tower is therefore non-idoneal, without a large search.
The genus count stays16 while h doubles. This supplies an explicit
cutoff mechanism: growing total classes exceed a stabilized fixed sector.

**Prime ideals and the denominator sidecar.** In an imaginary quadratic
field, a prime element, a prime ideal, and a rational prime are different
objects. For j^2=-11, the maximal order has the norm-three element
(1+j)/2, whereas Z[j] does not. Its conductor-two order instead has
the inverse classes represented by (3,2,4) and (3,-2,4). For j^2=-19,
the corresponding missing half-integral element has norm5. These are
concrete explanations of the earlier C3 phenomena at11 and19.
They do not automatically become geometric obstructions: for any
quadratic order O_f=Z+f O_K,

\[
                         O_f[1/f]=O_K[1/f].           \tag{5}
\]

Indeed fa belongs to O_f for every a in O_K. Thus allowing the conductor
as a denominator erases this distinction of rings. The unit-distance
carrier is a field; an order-level obstruction needs an explicit
integrality/valuation condition before it can constrain those points.
This is exactly why the old19-place argument retained its valuation
ring and literal residue fibres. No nonmaximal-order class obstruction
is transported after silently inverting its conductor.

## 7. Inversion, the merged metagraph, and the16 comparison

For a finite involution set S, the merged count is
(|S|+|Fix|)/2. Tournament converse and quadratic-class inversion
both have this orbit law. At order four the two diamonds form the
paired orbit, and the transitive and strong classes are self-converse,
exactly as in the recovered metagraph theorem. A class group has an
additional operation: its genus quotient is Cl/Cl^2, not its set of
inversion orbits. At n=7392 these have sizes16 and24 respectively,
while the class group itself has32 elements. Confusing these three
counts would erase the first anomaly.

The16 rotational tournament classes at17 are all self-converse, by
vertex negation. Their256 presentations form16 free16-element
multiplier orbits. An inherited THM-871 sentence incorrectly called
the quotient a free torsor; it has been repaired and logged. The
abstract sixteen-point fixed involution set agrees with that of
Cl(-7392), but no canonical multiplication-preserving correspondence
is supplied.

The Cayley--Dickson comparison makes the missing data explicit. Its
sixteen basis addresses form F2^4 under XOR, just as an exponent-two
class group of order16 can be coordinatized after choosing generators.
The sedenion multiplication adds signs and loses associativity. In
[Baez's convention](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node5.html),
the exact check gives

\[
                   (e_1+e_{10})(e_4-e_{15})=0.
\]

Both factors have squared norm2. The address law alone does not
retain the norm or cancellation law. Likewise, a converse orbit
alone does not retain SCC chronology, a residue endpoint does not
retain a Collatz integer clock, and a polygon count does not retain
an eight-port colouring relation. These are typed losses, not a
claim that all four objects carry the same algebra.

## 8. What transfers, and what remains critical

Idoneal completeness uses an independent arithmetic lower bound and
a constrained genus upper count, followed by finite exclusion. The
local conductor family above makes both competing quantities explicit.
To use that pattern for Collatz one still needs an independently proved
source-current upper estimate and a uniform entry bound; no class-number
formula computes either. For the plane one needs either an authenticated
boundary obstruction or a universal extension theorem in the escaped
carrier; a finite residue graph's chromatic number supplies neither.

The successful neighboring move is the [marked cubic Fourier proof](keller_marked_cubic_fourier_20261017d.md):
one exact change of variables identifies the coefficient whose vanishing
creates the exceptional mass. It explains why one discriminant branch
contributes a large spectrum while another does not. The constructive
next questions are therefore: which source-marked coefficient controls
the coherent Collatz R current, and which mixed geometric edges remove
an actual boundary extension rather than just raise core degree?
Those are precise research targets, not asserted reductions to a solved
problem.
