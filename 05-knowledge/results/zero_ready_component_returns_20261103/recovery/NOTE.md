## 3 Three births produce an actual unit response dial

Let(D,w) be any nonempty rooted tournament, with profile(h,s,q,F).
Adjoin a beaten only by w, then b beaten only by a, then c beaten only
by b. Call the resulting graph X. The new vertices form the directed
triangle a->b->c->a. Both b,c beat every old vertex; a beats every old
vertex except w. All old vertices and arcs remain literal.

Three applications of the inherited birth identities give

    h_X=3h+3F-s.

The three possible marks have exact profiles

    at a: (h_X, h,       h,       3h+4F),
    at b: (h_X, h+F-s,   h+F-s,   h_X),
    at c: (h_X, h+F,     h+2F,    3h+4F-s).             (3)

For the first row, X rooted at a is exactly D diamond TT2 with the second
TT2 vertex marked before taking its converse: assign the seam root to a
and the two converse TT2 vertices to b,c. Polarized formula C1 gives
F_a=4F+3h. Deleting a leaves TT2->D, so q_a=s_a=h.
For b, use the retained-root R formula after the first two N births;
it gives F_b=2(2h+2F-s)-(h+F-s)=h_X. The last row is the direct N^3
calculation. If D is nontrivial and strong, every birth and X are strong.

Consequently switching the selected physical mark c->a changes F by

### The exact missing return condition is a joint cover count

For an old vertex v, define J_(v,w)(D) to count ordered two-bin path covers
of D in which the first nonempty bin starts at v and ends at w, and the
second may be empty. Then

    start_v(X)=J_(v,w)(D),       for every old v.           (5)

Indeed a Hamilton path of X starting in D can first enter the new triangle
only via w->a. It must then use a,b,c consecutively: leaving a for another
old vertex would make b,c unreachable without revisiting w or a. The old
prefix starts at v and ends at w; after c comes the arbitrary second old
path. Splitting and concatenating are inverse bijections.

In particular start_w(X)=H(D-w)=q, and deleting w gives

    X-w=C3->(D-w),       H(X-w)=3q.                        (6)

For nontrivial strong D the three new roots in(3) have at least three
starting paths.
Thus the output component has a unique-start reentry **iff some actual old
name v has J_(v,w)=1**. This is a joint same-bin endpoint response; neither
h,F nor their common divisor supplies it. Retaining the old w works once
exactly when q=1, but its next deletion count is3, so this condition does
not repeat at that same old mark.

    9h+11F-3,       9h+11F-1.                             (7)
