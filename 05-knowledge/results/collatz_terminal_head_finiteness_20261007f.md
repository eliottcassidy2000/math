# A finite fixed-head library cannot ground every Mersenne source

**PROVED application of a CITED theorem:** at most2^32 Mersenne exponents
per fixed middle head in the precise normal form below. **FINITE-EXACT:**
the algebra and first-hit controls. **OPEN:** universal Collatz; this
result does not obstruct variable heads or recursively generated words.

Artifacts: [program](../../04-computation/experiments/collatz_terminal_head_finiteness_20261007f.py)
and [output](collatz_terminal_head_finiteness_20261007f.out).

## 1. Recovering the relevant older mechanism

The source is the rank-controlled S-unit mechanism already used in
[THM-4490, affine-word S-unit specialization](../../01-canon/theorems/THM-4490-collatz-affine-word-sunit-specialization.md).
That theorem concerns growing-prefix multiplicative independence. Here the
target predicate is completely different: membership of an original
Mersenne source in a completed terminal family. The preserved object is
the full ordered affine carry, not just the slope of the word. The needed
sidecar is the equation fixing the cofactor to1. The cheap hostile is a
word whose endpoint is1 only after it has already visited ROOT.

The companion [grounded-port construction](collatz_grounded_child_ports_20261007f.md)
produces infinitely many completed ordinary sources for an authenticated
macro. Its additional source-identity equation becomes very restrictive
on the Mersenne line. This is a reason to change the completion schema,
not a reason to discard that valid construction.

## 2. The exact fixed-head theorem

Fix a finite positive valuation word u, either empty or beginning with a
letter at least2. Write l=len(u), A=cost(u), and

\[
 F_u(x)=\frac{3^l x+B}{2^A},\qquad
 I=3B-3^{l+1}+2^A,\qquad C=I/2.                              \tag{1}
\]

**Theorem.** There are at most2^32 exponents E>=2 for which the actual
first-hit ROOT word of `M_E=2^E-1` is of the form

\[
                         1^{E-1},u,c,                       \tag{2}
\]

where only E and the final valuation c may vary. Consequently a finite
dictionary of H fixed middle heads proves at most `H*2^32` Mersenne
exponents using exactly this certificate form.

**Proof.** The initial run reaches `x=2*3^(E-1)-1`. If the terminal letter
after u reaches1, then

\[
 2^{A+c}=2\,3^{E+l}+I,
 \qquad
 \boxed{2^{A+c-1}-3^{E+l}=C.}                                \tag{3}
\]

The invariant of the empty word is I=-2. Appending a positive letter a
updates it to `3I+2^(A+a)`. After a first letter>=2 it is2mod4, and every
later update preserves this congruence. Thus C is a nonzero odd integer
for every allowed head, including the empty head.

Put `a=A+c-1`, `b=E+l` and normalize

\[
                         X=2^a/C,\qquad Y=-3^b/C.
\]

Every solution lies in the subgroup of \((\mathbb C^*)^2\) generated by

\[
                  (2,1),\quad(1,3),\quad(1/C,-1/C),           \tag{4}
\]

which has rank at most3. The scalar C in (1) is the fixed rational constant.
**CITED:** Beukers–Schlickewei's
[Theorem1.1, author-hosted primary paper](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~beuke106/s-units.pdf)
bounds solutions of X+Y=1 in the rational closure of a rank-r subgroup by
`2^(8r+8)`. Applying it with r<=3 gives2^32 solutions. The map from (E,c)
to (X,Y) is injective, so the same bound applies to pairs and hence to E.
First-hit and native-word requirements only restrict this set further. ∎

In a genuine first-hit certificate the terminal c is even and at least4.
The argument deliberately bounds a larger set of exponential-equation
solutions, so it does not infer word legality from endpoint arithmetic.
For example E=2, u=(4,2), c=2 satisfies (3), but the formal full word
`(1,4,2,2)` visits1 after its second letter and is rejected as ROOT padding.
The positive control E=3, u=(2,3), c=4 is the actual ROOT word of7.

## 3. What this says about the next proof design

The result rules out exhausting the problem with a finite table of fixed
middle words, even though each entry may have arbitrarily long initial
runs and arbitrarily large final valuations. It provides no bound on the
largest exponent among the possible solutions, and the numerical2^32
bound is not proposed as sharp. A finite search without an independent
height bound is not a complete equation solver.

An infinite family of heads, a recursive grammar, a variable letter, or a
chain of ranked child substitutions can evade the fixed-head hypothesis.
None is proved universally successful here. The productive next target
is a source-preserving rule that changes the head while decreasing a
proved rank, with actual terminal leaves; the
[marked-completion interface](collatz_marked_completion_20261007c.md)
states exactly what such a rule would have to supply.

This also separates two useful sorts of reuse. A common suffix can be
stored once for many already authenticated child ports, as in the
[residual t23 packet](collatz_residual_t23_20261007f.md). A completed
ordinary-source family can be reused at every represented member. Neither
operation removes the source-membership equation when asked to prove a
particular new Mersenne integer.

## 4. Reproduction

The finite universe consists of the empty head and all heads of lengths1..4
over letters1..4 beginning with a letter>=2, exponents2..12 and terminal
letters1..24. Carrier evaluation and an independent invariant recurrence
agree. The checker compares the normalized equation with the unnormalized
endpoint equation and separately replays every strict first-hit solution.
This computation tests the specialization and its boundary; the cited
theorem supplies all-height finiteness.
