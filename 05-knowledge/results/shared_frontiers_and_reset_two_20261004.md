## 2. Reset two now has more than one guarded exit

Let U(n)=oddpart(3n+1), and let a word record the exact two-adic valuations
of successive odd steps. The [first reset-two switch](reset_two_debt_families_20261004.md)
proves

    source n:       (1^r,2,1,6,a),
    child (n-1)/2:  (1^(r-1),3,1,2,1,a+2),
    r,a>=1.

On the exact source cylinder both words are legal and meet at the same
endpoint. They have equal odd length; the source uses one additional
halving. A supplied child certificate therefore transfers with the same
odd rank and ordinary rank increased by one.

For a=1 and every r>=8, **every displayed actual source iterate exceeds n**.
For example, the whole family

    n=236031+524288t,
    child=118015+262144t,
    common endpoint=478505+1062882t, t>=0,

has this property. The improvement is a smaller proof dependency before
actual descent. It does not require the trajectory itself to have turned
downward yet, but still requires a home certificate for the child.

### A smaller necessary domain for a least counterexample

Immediate valuation at least two descends. A run of ones followed by a
reset at least three has the inherited smaller-child join. Thus a least
positive counterexample, if one exists, must first reset at two. This
necessary set has density1/4 among odd integers. The eight new selected
families are disjoint inside it. Removing them leaves a necessary domain
of odd-relative density

    1023535/4194304.

The density calculation has a vanishing-tail bound for arbitrarily long
initial runs. This is not the density of all currently unresolved inputs
or of nonconvergent integers. It describes the domain left by these
specified necessary tests only. Their universal exhaustion is open.

## 3. A learned frontier carries an infinite family

The [frontier-family compiler](frontier_family_compiler_20261004.md) keeps
the shared31-step segment from2287 to157 as the exact family

    2287+2^53*k -> oddpart(157+3^31*k), k>=0.

Every member descends. Pulling it back through already checked prefixes
gives two families from703 and937. A second learned segment gives a family
from871. The compiler proves payment relative to the original source and
retains the inherited tail's parameter threshold; the27->41->31 hostile
prevents dropping either requirement.

The important additional coordinate is

    h(k)=v2(e+3^j*k).

A coarse word fixes its last division only from below. Its actual total
valuation is A+h(k), so the mod19/mod13 observer needs (A+h(k))mod36.
For example k=0 and247 in the2287 family have the same source modulo247
but different extra divisions and different endpoint phases. The script
checks all247 phases and sixteen extra-valuation levels for each of five
families, giving19760 exact phase controls.

A supplied rooted hub z not divisible by three completes selected members:

    e+3^j*k=2^h*z.

Ternary digit lifting selects h, and an inverse AST stores the resulting
route without expanding its huge source. Sixty such symbolic certificates
are independently checked; the largest tested extra exponent exceeds10^25.
This constructs infinitely many completed members of a learned family.
It does not complete every freely chosen k. All discovery strategies in
the controlled experiment still need the same32 new literal edges; the
gain here is retained symbolic meaning and shared prefix reuse.

## 4. Return budgets become exact exit families

The [return-tail compiler](modular_return_debt_20261004.md) replaces the
first over-budget valuation by one exact tail constraint. If prefix v has
cost S, appending any k>=K is one original-source congruence modulo2^(S+K).
The bounded return leaves and first-overflow tails form an exact disjoint
partition, and the checked crossing edge is retained even when it reaches
the root. An over-budget observation is neither a failed trajectory nor
permission to discard its work.

For233, the descending marked-return mass increases from1/128 to25/2048
among odd233-multiples. Every newly counted family already descends within
one or two ordinary odd steps. This therefore adds marked-return coverage,
not new generic descent coverage. The distinction is essential to choosing
the next research target.

Two longer223 exit families genuinely have their first descent at odd

    6815+8192k -> oddpart(5459+6561k), k>=0.

These are applications of the existing first-crossing framework, without
a claim of new coverage beyond its atlas.

## 5. More inverse depth separates the joint addresses

The [joint-address theorem](joint_home_address_20261004.md) constructs,
for every a>=0,k>=1 and every pair of residues modulo3^a and19^k,
infinitely many positive integers at those addresses with supplied routes
to1 in at most four odd steps. Four is the sharp uniform bound here.

The variable exponent is placed after three inverse divisions, giving

    n_t=(A*2^(18t)-B)/27,
    v3(n_t-n_s)=v3(t-s),
    v19(n_t-n_s)=1+v19(t-s).

Consequently the ternary address uses t modulo3^a, while the chosen mod19
disk uses t modulo19^(k-1). Their periods are coprime. Nineteen explicitly
certified initial rays supply the nineteen first digits. All15200 joint
addresses at a=0..3,k=1..2 are checked, alongside literal and AST controls.

This addresses the earlier shared-digit obstruction by changing the
construction's inverse depth, not by contradicting the fixed-hub/row
theorem. The nineteen rays still have natural density zero among integers.
Topological coverage of every finite address is compatible with almost no
integer-density coverage, and cannot certify an independently supplied
integer merely because its residues match a constructed member.

## 6. What the next proof step must accomplish

The selector now has explicit interfaces for five different operations:

| Input evidence | Sound output | Obligation still retained |
|---|---|---|
| Checked frontier plus ordered word | Infinite guarded descent family | Rooted endpoint for any completed member |
| Exact source matching a reset-two rewrite | Smaller child and checked common future | Child's home certificate |
| Bounded return computation | Exact overflow tail and checked crossing edge | Further work if neither root nor descent is certified |
| Supplied rooted hub and completion parameter | Completed inverse AST | Exact constructed source identity |
| Requested finite joint address | Some completed sources at that address | No implication for another supplied source |

The next productive anchor is to adapt the word search to a supplied
remaining debt state and prove a well-founded rule for moving it into a
grounded dependency. Keep every successful candidate and the actual
frontier; do not replace them by a single smallest representative. The
bounded search already shows that a failed short ansatz can be repaired
by another word length. What remains unproved is that such extensions
always succeed, or that their failures admit another decreasing measure.

All five component packages were independently audited and reproduced
normally and under Python optimization. Source guards, root boundaries,
ordered carries, and the distinction between a dependency and a completed
certificate remain active in the saved checkers. No new literature or
historical-priority claim is made. LRC(14) is unchanged and open; no
certificate transport to that separate problem is asserted.
