# Exact carry overlaps, finite target cores, and the missing return map

**Session276, 12 November2026. Chan–Pak remains OPEN.**

This session produced actual gapless overlaps in infinite physical families,
not just a common gcd or more finite coverage. It also classified the precise
local limit of those overlaps, constructed an exact absolute-target decoder,
and found an all-size structural hierarchy for fixed-apex tournament words.
The remaining difficulty is now a return map that grows the interval width
or supplies a nonempty core for every prescribed target.

## 1. Controlled carries genuinely join physical digit ranges

A small symmetric deletion core supplies a central two-choice bank: its
counts differ by2 while every other rooted fusion coordinate agrees.
Generic central transport and the C5 scalar menu are inherited; this session
turns symmetric-core menus into explicit complete interfaces and consumers.
The more useful primitive C9 bank has counts15381/15383 and packet

    (h,Q,U,V,d,e)=(3357,50004,24089,29272,1865,1492).

Arbitrary nonnegative integers A,B are lawful inputs, encoded by actual
transitive-deletion rooted flanks. The lower count is exactly

    F(A,B)=15381+3357(g(A)+g(B))+50004AB
                     +25915A+20732B+1865(A mod2)+1492(B mod2),

where g(0)=0, g(2a+1)=g(a), and g(2a)=g(a)+a+(a mod2).
The second central choice adds2 without changing the continuing interface.
The digital corrections and the absolute baseline are paid, not subtracted.

A first carry construction with a C7 source joins two such pairs into three
consecutive odd counts. Its first literal outputs are2568295,2568297,2568299,
with four independently counted nineteen-vertex constructions accounting for
the repeated middle value. An explicit parameter formula gives the overlap
at every scale, not merely at those initial values.

The second wave compensates BOTH carries in the C9 consumer. For every j≥0,

    A_j=29527+1362656j,  B_j=29140+1344752j,
    F(A_j+1,B_j−1)=F(A_j,B_j)+4.

Thus four real tournaments realize N_j,N_j+2,N_j+4,N_j+6. The two central
pairs have different rooted interfaces: this is an output interval, not one
shared-interface four-way bank. At j=0 they begin
at43025739403961. The point is not the large number: fixed carry lengths turn
the exact nonlinear digital correction into a linear Diophantine equation.
A positive homogeneous solution direction then constructs infinitely many
nonnegative integer operands. This is stronger than passing a congruence test.

But the overlaps cannot simply be repeated. For this C9 consumer, the graph
joining nearest neighboring parameter pairs whenever their central banks
overlap or abut is a MATCHING plus isolated vertices. Axis moves never join;
the same-direction diagonals are too costly; the only possible compensating
diagonal has a fixed parity and exact difference4. Hence four consecutive
counts are the sharp local limit in this eight-neighbor geometry. Growing
width requires a nonlocal operand move, a different core, or a different
consumer. It is not supplied by these constant-width infinite families.

## 2. Prescribed absolute targets now have an exact positive inverse

The sandwich family has a closed seven-coefficient binary return map. Split
A=2a+r,B=2b+s. The same digital/quadratic form returns with exact updated
constant, linear coefficients, parity fees and product coefficient. Every
coefficient remains nonnegative; the linear costs at least double.

For a prescribed N, reject a branch when its constant exceeds N, and force
an operand tail to zero when its linear cost exceeds the remaining budget.
This yields a complete terminating inverse tree of logarithmic depth for
this physical family. Acceptance reconstructs the binary flank words and a
literal tournament. Rejection means only that this family misses N.

This settles neither universal branch existence nor interval overlap at all
scales. It does make the absolute-target obligation executable without
confusing it with residue coverage or with a formal normalized state.
The C9 bank reaches every odd residue modulo6714, yet its local overlap graph
is only a matching. Those are compatible facts, not a contradiction.

## 3. A core-changing corridor can grow, but its receiver does not return

Inside the fixed padded C5 deletion family, the41/43 central island is
isolated under one/two root-incidence flips at EVERY padding length. Its
smallest general exit jump is8; activating a nonneutral tail costs at least14.
This explains a failure of one particular continuation architecture.

Changing an old deletion-core edge genuinely escapes: an explicit43→45
single free-edge move has a common Hamiltonian frame. Together with41→43
it gives a three-count corridor with common receiving mass57. A one-emission
unit-deletion context transports it to

    57·2^t+41, 57·2^t+43, 57·2^t+45,    t≥1,

preserving the actual old free moves. This is more than unrelated high-count
siblings: the transported corridor includes a deletion-core-changing gate.

The full state exposes the remaining obstruction. The new receiving masses
are2^(t+1)·57−15,2^(t+1)·57−15,2^(t+1)·57−13. The gate creates a receiver
defect2. More generally ANY same-mark left/right unit-deletion fusion program
restores equality of the43/45 receivers only if it is count-neutral padding.

## 4. Infinite target fibres have a finite nonneutral core

For a fixed named pair of apexes and a transitive spine, the complete language
of words with count N has an exact finite target-sensitive automaton. It
retains every successful future suffix, rather than identifying prefixes
merely because they have the same current count. Positivity makes saturation
atN+1 exact; already-overflowing prefixes are dead by induced monotonicity.
This recognizes ALL word lengths, not just a search cutoff.

The stronger structural result is an unambiguous SCC grammar. Every word is
made from either one strong two-apex core and two neutral reservoirs, or two
one-apex cores and three reservoirs. Consequently

    F_N(z)=C_N(z)/(1−z)^2
             +(sum_(de=N) A_d(z)A_e(z))/(1−z)^3.

The core polynomials are finite under the inherited Busch bound. After the
core-length threshold, repetitions are exactly polynomial in spine length;
there is no hidden oscillating source of new strong structure in the neutral
reservoirs. This counts named word presentations, not isomorphism classes.

The extension to k fixed named apexes is particularly clean:

    F_N^A(z)=P_N^A(z)/(1−z)^(k+1),
    degree P_N^A ≤ floor(log_5(N^3)).

The numerator degree is bounded by the TARGET, independently of k. The
polynomial repetition degree is bounded by the APEX BUDGET. The proof keeps
the compatible ordered apex partition of the SCCs, including singleton-apex
empty cores. The numerator can be signed; it is not itself a physical bank.
The missing proof is positivity of an actual target core product, uniformly
in N. Pumping neutral reservoirs cannot create such a core.

## 5. Complete continuation obstructions suggest a different operation

The finite target automata permit all-length, rather than bounded-order,
source-menu tests. Among attained odd h≤63, only5 and9 have a single named
two-apex source whose appended suffixes can reach BOTH2h−1 and2h+1.
For example45 and47 each extend an H23 word, but no single H23 source at
any length supports both. For the tested h≤31 there is no two-level binary
fan in this append-only language. These are scalar-window statements with
unbounded source/suffix length, not all-h impossibility claims.

For H13 the obstruction is stronger: no H27 word contains any of its six
strong H13 core words as a subsequence. Even arbitrary interior insertions
preserving the named apexes and old arcs cannot make that branch. An explicit
old-arc repair does: the H13 word120 can reach25 by prepending1, or undergo
neutral padding and an old-edge repair followed by a new-edge change to27.
The upper result reframes back to the two-apex word2010. This is a concrete
architecture-changing fork, not a reusable all-target induction.

## 6. The principles made precise

- **Use carries as constructive resources.** Fix their actual lengths and
  parities; solve the resulting integer equation with a positive realization.
- **A count derivative belongs to its consumer.** Our C5+2 switch stays+2
  under unit-deletion fusion, but ordinary substitution amplifies it by
  2H(outer)+4a2. A C7+2 switch even reverses sign under a triangle consumer,
  and the reversal has an exact all-length law. Full cover responses explain
  the lost information. [Proof](selector/PROOF.md)
- **Separate target complexity from neutral repetition.** The finite core
  numerator carries existence; reservoirs carry multiplicity. Neither a large
  fibre nor a complete finite automaton alone proves every target has a core.
- **An infinite supply is not a return map.** The corridor and carry families
  are genuinely infinite, but their receiver leak and matching obstruction
  identify exactly why they do not yet iterate into a tail.

Distant archive work on Zeckendorf carries, summand graphs, recursive response
ideals, Stern–Brocot scale, positive versus signed compression and finite-core
descent supplied concrete changes of representation. Their use and limitations
are recorded in the lane notes. No blanket re-verification of old research or
numerical range expansion was substituted for the requested constructive work.

## 7. The next proof obligations

1. Join the locally optimal carry banks by a nonlocal, physically controlled
   move and make the number of overlapping banks unbounded.
2. Repair the receiver defect of a core-changing corridor without collapsing
   to neutral padding; rerooting or a genuinely new module is required by the
   proved same-mark unit-context obstruction.
3. Construct a recursion on the target-indexed strong-core polynomials which
   preserves a nonempty physical branch, rather than only a residue or a
   scalar count. The13→25/27 repair is an initial ingredient, not that theorem.
