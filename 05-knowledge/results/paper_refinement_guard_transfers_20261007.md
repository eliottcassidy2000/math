## 2. A finite guard compresses an unbounded final valuation

Let w be a finite positive valuation word, including the empty word. Write

    U_w(n)=(P n+B)/Q,   P=3^r, Q=2^A,

where r is length and A is total valuation. Fix k>=1 such that

    Q 4^k > P.                                             (1)

Set c_k=(4^k-1)/3 and S(h)=4h+1. We seek an actual retained frontier
x=S^k(h), with 0<h<n. The exact conditions on the original positive odd n
are

    Pn+B = Q(c_k+4^k h),
    h positive odd,  h<n,  n>1.                            (2)

Because P is odd, the parity/integrality part is the **single** native cell

    n = P^(-1)[Q(c_k+4^k)-B] mod (2Q4^k).                 (3)

The inverse is modular. The strict positivity/payment cuts are precisely

    n>1,
    Pn+B>Qc_k,
    (Q4^k-P)n>B-Qc_k.                                    (4)

They are effective lower cuts by(1). Let n0 be the least member of(3)
satisfying(4), and h0 its child. **Every** permitted source is then

    n=n0+2Q4^k t,    h=h0+2P t,    t>=0.                  (5)

There is no hidden finite search over t, no supplied child ROOT word, and
no assumption about later convergence. `compile_tail` computes the exact
least member using integer floor divisions of the strict inequalities.

**Proof of the actual-word guard.** The endpoint in(2) is an odd integer.
For a nonempty word, write its carry as
B=3^(r-1)+2^(a1)B_tail. The final affine identity first forces3n+1 to be
divisible by2^a1; its quotient is odd since the remaining word's carry is
odd, or directly since the final endpoint is odd if r=1. Repeat on the
remaining word. Thus all valuations are exact. Since x=S^k(h)>1, the prefix
cannot have reached ROOT earlier: actual U would then stay1.

Let b=v2(3h+1)>=1. The equality3S^k(h)+1=4^k(3h+1) gives the actual join

    n -- w,(2k+b) --> U(h) <-- (b) -- h.                  (6)

If h=1, the right word is empty and the left word ends at1. In particular,
ROOT is not padded by the formal valuation2. Otherwise, a supplied first-hit
ROOT word for h is authenticated, its first letter b is removed, and the
left prefix is prepended to the remaining suffix. `discharge` checks that
interface and replays the resulting source word.

The compiler is **iff within the chosen contracting coefficient family**:
an n is accepted exactly when w is actual, S^k has a positive odd inverse
there, and that inverse is smaller than the original n. It does not claim
that every possible paid sibling requires(1); rows whose coefficient is at
least1 can have a finite paid initial portion.

For any row(5), put z0=(3h0+1)/2. Then

    b(t)=1+v2(z0+3P t).                                  (9)

Since3P is odd, the parameter t maps bijectively onto residues modulo every
power of2. Consequently the cells b=j, j>=1, have relative natural density
2^-j in t>=0. A finite refinement through level J has exactly J leaves
and one retained tail b>=J+1, of densities

    1/2, 1/4, ..., 2^-J;    tail 2^-J.                  (10)

They partition every parameter; the tail is not dropped or assigned a
fictional completed certificate. Exact natural densities here follow from
individual residue classes, without a countable-union shortcut.

There are exactly floor((b-1)/2) further positive odd S-ancestors of h.
For j>=0, at least j such additional witnesses exist iff

    b>=2j+1
    iff t=-z0(3P)^(-1) mod 4^j.                         (11)

Their relative density is4^-j. Positivity is automatic for an integral odd
inverse: c_j<4^j and h>0 rule out a negative odd ancestor. Each is smaller
than h and hence than n. For fixed supplied n, however, the reserve is the
finite value determined by b(n); a distributional statement cannot supply
missing witnesses. No independence is asserted between multiple observations
of the same parameter.

This is a support-preserving refinement analogous to the useful interfaces
in the papers, not an identification of their probability spaces. The exact
datum contains t, its retained source, and the applicable receipt. Forgetting
b preserves the paid conclusion on(5), but not its exact final word; `apply_tail`
reconstructs b before exporting that word. A ROOT proof still requires a
grounded child certificate.

## 4. What this can and cannot finish

The production API adds a reusable way to compile any retained prefix into
a native all-height smaller-sibling guard, with a finite initial cut and an
arbitrary final valuation handled exactly. It can be combined with a finite
terminal bank or the parent's separate lifted-terminal descent rules. It
does not require injective assignment of certificates: one suffix may serve
many source receipts, as it already does for13 and53 through5.

It does not show that those guards cover every original source. The
[bounded-depth portfolio obstruction](collatz_partition_cover_20261004.md)
still applies: for any fixed source/child depth bounds R,S, the mixed cell
n=-1 mod2^(R+1), n=0 mod3^S has no smaller common-future child within those
bounds. Here the final exponent and sibling reserve can be unbounded, but
the source word length remains fixed and the child-to-join word has length
at most1. A finite list of such prefixes therefore remains subject to that
obstruction. Its escape route is a genuinely depth-growing rule, not a
rounding convention, matching theorem, or average-success estimate.
