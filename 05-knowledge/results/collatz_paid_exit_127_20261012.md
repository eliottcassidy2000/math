## 1. A compiler that pays an entire coarse cylinder

For a positive valuation word w, write

\[
 U_w(n)=(pn+b)/q,\quad p=3^{|w|},\quad q=2^{\sum w}.
\]

Choose a lower bound a0>=1 for the next valuation, and set

\[
 P=3p,\quad B=3b+q,\quad M=q2^{a_0},\quad
 r=-BP^{-1}\pmod M.
\]

Every positive source n=r mod M has the actual prefix w followed by a
valuation a>=a0, subject to the first-hit guard below. Its endpoint is

\[
 h=(Pn+B)/(q2^a).                                      \tag{1}
\]

This is a **coarse** last-letter guard, not an assertion that a=a0. For
completeness, divisibility of the final numerator by M forces every
proper prefix numerator to have exactly its prescribed 2-adic valuation:
express the final numerator in terms of that prefix and the remaining odd
carry, and reduce modulo one more bit than the prefix cost. Thus all
intermediate states are odd integers. The implementation additionally
replays the finite supplied head on the actual source.

For a desired bit drop s>=1, require M>2^s P and impose

\[
 n\ge \left\lceil
 \frac{2^s B+(2^s-1)M}{M-2^sP}\right\rceil.             \tag{2}
\]

Then (1) gives the all-height inequality

\[
                       2^s(h+1)\le n+1.               \tag{3}
\]

The compiler also imposes n>=3 and every head state >=3, using its affine
carrier, and n>=2^(s+1)-1. This excludes ROOT padding and makes every
remaining rank expression well typed. Round this height cutoff upward to
the first member n0 of r mod M. All larger members of that same progression
satisfy the guards, because each prefix has positive slope.

Let E(n)=floor(log2(n+1)), let e be either 1 or 2, and let
c=o(w)+1. If

\[
 c\le 3\{E(n_0)^e-[E(n_0)-s]^e\},                       \tag{4}
\]

then every source in the compiled domain pays

\[
 o(w)+(a\bmod 2)\le 3\{E(n)^e-E(h)^e\}.                 \tag{5}
\]

Indeed (3) gives E(h)<=E(n)-s, and the right-side difference is constant
in E(n) for e=1 and increasing for e=2. This is a finite integer
certificate for a quantified family. An unpaid plan is rejected; testing
individual heights is not the proof of (2)–(5). General descent cylinders
are inherited; the new consumer retains their odd-charge invoice.

The output stores w,a0,s,e,r,M,n0 and all carrier coefficients. It never
replaces n by another member of the progression. It proves a dependency
on h, not ROOT(h). A supplied smaller-child proof remains necessary.

## 2. Source127 has two useful paid family rules

For every n=127 mod 2^21, n>=127, put

\[
 a=v_2((177147n+571003)/2^{18})\ge 3.
\]

Then

\[
 n\xrightarrow{(1^6,2,4,3,3,a)}
 h=\frac{177147n+571003}{2^{18+a}},\qquad
 8(h+1)\le n+1,\qquad \delta=8+(a\bmod 2)\le 9.          \tag{6}
\]

Here 1^6 means six valuation-one letters. The affine height cutoff in(2)
is 29, below the least native source 127. Thus (6) pays the existing linear
rate-three rule at **every** height in this residue. At127, a=3 and h=11;
the supplied seed 11 has charge 2, giving the independent exact source
charge 11. No unknown trajectory is invoked by the production recognizer.

The earlier exit already supports the quadratic budget on the broader
cylinder n=127 mod 2^18:

\[
 a=v_2((59049n+102953)/2^{15})\ge 3,
 \quad n\xrightarrow{(1^6,2,4,3,a)}h,
 \quad 4(h+1)\le n+1,\quad\delta\le 8.                  \tag{7}
\]

It is the first half-band exit: before the last step, the smallest prefix
slope is 19683/32768>1/2 and its carry is positive. At127 it ends at 29 with
charge 8. Its rank drop is 3 at that one source, but only 2 is guaranteed
uniformly on the progression; the compiler does not silently promote the
pointwise gap. Equation(7) is paid by 3(E(n)^2-E(h)^2). Equation(6) takes one
more step and pays the linear budget uniformly.

These are cofactor/residue families. They do **not** cover all Mersenne

## 3. One unbounded two-run family has charge at most two

For integers r>=1 and t=1 mod 6, t>=1, define

\[
 n=\frac{2^{2r+3}t+1}{3},\quad
 a=2+v_2(1+3^{r+1}t),\quad
 h=\frac{1+3^{r+1}t}{2^{a-2}}.
\]

Then

\[
 n\xrightarrow{(1,2^r,a)}h,\qquad
 2(h+1)\le n+1,\qquad\delta=1+(a\bmod 2)\le 2.          \tag{8}
\]

Again 2^r denotes r repeated valuation-two letters inside the word. After
the initial 1 and r twos, the state is 1+4*3^r*t. Earlier valuations are
exactly 2 because the valuation of the displacement from 1 decreases by 2 at
each step and remains above 2 until the last such step. The final valuation
is the displayed a>=3. Positivity excludes earlier ROOT.

For payment, h<=(1+3^(r+1)t)/2, so (8) follows from

\[
                    (2^{2r+3}-3^{r+2})t\ge 5.
\]

Equality occurs at r=t=1, namely 11->5. The left coefficient is 5 at r=1
and increases thereafter. The initial run length is unbounded, but its
interior letters spend no odd charge. This is the useful distinction from
counting every step as debt.
