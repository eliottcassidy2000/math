# What a complete repair grammar must still do

**OPEN research obligations, following the scoped results in this packet.**
The completed work is in [PROOFS](PROOFS.md), with universe and replay details
in [AUDIT](AUDIT.md). No universal selector is hidden in the new birth count.

## 1. Carry the right state

A useful state must include actual adjacency rows, selected set, named ports,
the full continuation interface for the allowed operations, and the current
lower-head obligation. For a unit ray, retain `(c,j)` with `U=c*2^j`:
the existing construction pays only exponents `s>=j+1`. Equality of c,
equality of H, or an offset sign flip is insufficient. A direct H witness
for a head does not need a unit selection; requiring one loses valid repairs.

Stable-clone ancestry is not confluent. Keep sets of actual canonical terminal
ancestors, and scope identifiers by receipt. Positive transitive clocks have
no automatic length-zero deletion interpretation. Any contraction must be
proved on its actual graph, with the other lengths frozen.

## 2. Do not repeat searches already ruled out

| Candidate architecture | Exact boundary now known | What remains outside it |
|---|---|---|
| Any finite bank of affine TT or ordered-atom charts, with arbitrary parameter-wise choice | Cannot close126 old families on their specified valuation branches; repeated fixed cyclic atoms do not help | Nonlinear multiplicities, changing atom types or genuinely different module constructions |
| Fixed periodic65-seam words, including fixed finite AP branching | Rank/determinant obstruction for470 families | Adaptive words, new seams, different observer dimension |
| Fixed periodic O/Z/J words, including fixed finite AP branching | All478 have a required head progression excluded by the25-state parity closure | Aperiodic controllers, different graph operations |
| One-new-vertex repair on the same old source | Complete declared search found no whole head-family repair | Reselection, deletion, multiple vertices, different sources |
| Five-port source plus one unselected stable port | Every accepted six-port child of the explicit source is a contractible clone | Other sources, selected additions, coupled replacements |

These are representation or local-search barriers. They do not declare any
additional integer globally forbidden. The exact obstruction for periodic
O/Z/J is two distinct odd modes versus one odd eigenvalue slot, not the false
statement that a word containing J can never have eigenvalue1.

## 3. Three focused positive targets

**Head first.** Birth4/port0 has odd coefficient
`c(a)=-1+3*2^a+2*3^a`. The s=1 head and every s>=3 are constructed here;
the remaining family is `1+4*c(a)`, a>=1. Its first two affine starts are
excluded by G1, but a later-start chart with finite exceptions is not.
Search this precise physical target before adding another broad census.
Maintain the exact kernel; do not infer a family identity from a few matches.

**Operation first.** The formal positive transfer `[[1,4],[0,9]]` produces
`N_m=(9^m+1)/2`. It has two odd modes and a dyadic coefficient denominator,
so it crosses two distinct barriers at once. Actual tournaments are known
here for m=0,...,6; an all-m physical interface is not supplied. A new
constructor realizing this transfer on a nonempty invariant physical class
would be useful, but merely writing its integer recurrence is not progress
on realizability. Retain the full endpoint update and a literal base witness.

**Birth first.** Alternate core-changing extension with synchronized growth.
That composition already yielded46 infinite odd-order families beyond the24
even ones. The next structural target is an unbounded sequence of different
weighted quotients with a uniform growth certificate. Prove frozen-block
nonvanishing and family intersection equations at each step. Larger counts
at a few sizes do not establish an infinite quotient-changing mechanism.

## 4. The global proof obligation

A finite instruction set may carry unbounded integers, polynomial degree,
and graph size. The results here do not rule out such a program. A credible
surjectivity theorem needs a well-founded inverse: every allowed target
outside a finite witnessed base admits a legal smaller predecessor, together
with an actual graph lift and all endpoint guards. For a seed formulation,
it must also settle every unpaid head. For the inherited multiplicative
reduction, both the prime lane and the7p lane must be supplied.

The absent sets shrink pointwise under source/sink padding; exact strong-core
bounds decide a fixed target. Neither fact gives a uniform exit from every
allowed absent class. The limiting absent set `{7,21}` and the universal
finite endpoint-repair grammar remain OPEN. In the old even-axis bank,
five families are fully repaired and473 remain unclosed in this session.
