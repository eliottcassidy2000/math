targeted recovery of the nearest methods, not a claim to have classified every
metagraph. The earlier [structural atlas](../session271_predictive_tournaments_20261111/structures/ATLAS.md)

## 1. Four different meanings of birth

A first occurrence of an integer H, a substitution-prime tournament, a terminal
stable-clone seed, and a first word in a restricted construction language are
different objects. None implies the others. For example the inherited
[creator grammar](../session230_creator_grammar_20261109/README.md) generates
the H63 first creators by inflating W5 with three transitive pairs, while a
prime W7 can carry an already known count31. Our synchronized families are
stable-clone births with fixed nonprime weighted quotients. Their newness is
relative to contraction, not to H-value or substitution primeness.

For this session the intrinsic tournament vertices are actual graph vertices,
with their directed pairwise arcs. There are no forced ties. Marking a selected
set or port tuple refines the object. Ordinary marked isomorphism is the
equivalence; converse is not identified. Passing to H loses adjacency, marks,
endpoint response and continuation rights. Passing to the weighted twin
quotient preserves marked isomorphism only when all block lengths survive.

The one-vertex quadratic compiler is a specialization of the inherited
[degree-at-most-2r extension theorem](../return_channels_20261103/BIRTHS.md),
now also applied to selected two-path-cover defect. Each new vertex occurs
once and has at most two incident path arcs, so the same proof applies to
each cover indicator. It replaces 2^n costly path-table evaluations per
marking by 1+n+binom(n,2), followed by a cheap full Boolean-cube evaluation.
That is an exact practical improvement for this enumeration, not a new
polynomial-time algorithm for unrestricted tournament counting.

## 3. Connections revealed by putting the obstructions together

**Independent freedom and synchronized freedom are different.** The old
stable-face complex answers which coordinates move separately. The new
diagonal gate answers which unstable coordinates move together. Its map is
from a signed cover tensor to an exponential equation; it preserves the unit
predicate but forgets actual U and twin-block eligibility. The cheapest
decisive test is one off-diagonal length pair plus the full tensor. This is
why a catalogue of stable ports alone misses infinitely many births.

**Arithmetic division is a geometric demand.** Normalizing U by2^j is harmless
as an integer calculation, but realizing the quotient as a tournament count
requires another object. V2 turns that distinction into a coefficient
obstruction for affine transitive templates. V3 turns it into a rank and
prime-valuation cone for periodic seam matrices. Neither is a blanket
impossibility statement. Their intersection tells us exactly which familiar
construction languages should not be searched again for those heads.

V4 adds a different prime filter: reduce the *whole transfer matrix* modulo2
and count characteristic-root multiplicities. Two distinct odd exponential
modes both reduce to1 and require two slots. Mixed O/Z/J words have just one.
The source is a physical transfer word; the target is a finite matrix
semigroup; reduction preserves composition and a necessary mode-capacity
bound, while losing magnitudes, positivity and actual endpoints. The
required sidecar is the integer lift and its source/readout. Complete closure
of25 states is the cheapest decisive all-word test. Determinants alone
discard exactly the information that makes this new obstruction visible.

The stress family `(9^m+1)/2` isolates the distinction. A positive2x2 integer
transfer generates it; V2, V4 and a complete bounded-period65-bank test
exclude several familiar physical realizations. Finite tournaments are
provided through m=6, but an all-m tournament lift is OPEN. This is a
concrete target for richer endpoint rules, not an assertion that the formal
matrix can simply be declared to be a graph operation.

The ordered-product bridge makes the obstruction more robust. Passing from
an SCC word to Q_r turns ordered join into multiplication and discards the
word's order. Binomial inversion recovers C_r but cannot recover that order.
The fixed quotient's Hamilton count needs only the cover profile, whereas
named-port continuation may need more. Therefore rearranging repeated cyclic
atoms can create different graphs without gaining any head-repair capacity
in this representation class. The cheap hostile is the four-vertex pair
`C3->point` and `point->C3`; their degree multisets already distinguish them.

**The2-adic branches have nonperiodic clocks.** Of the66 unbounded families,
65 have two odd modes. Their unique lifting roots in Z2 are all irrational;
see [V1a](PROOFS.md) and `lifting_roots.json.gz`. Thus their binary root digits
are not eventually periodic. The uniform one-bit lifting algorithm still
describes all of them compactly. This is a rigorous connection to recursive
digit computation, without identifying these sequences with Collatz,
Zeckendorf digits or a cellular automaton. The one mixed3/5 branch has a
unique lifting rule too; its rationality is not classified here.

Two scales must remain separate: a large ordinary integer a makes the even
exponential modes vanish modulo a prescribed2-power, while closeness to the
2-adic root makes the odd modes cancel. The full expression containing2^a
has not thereby become an analytic function on all of Z2. The finite cutoff
is part of the certificate.

**Source equivalence is operation-dependent.** Equal H is adequate for a
final product but inadequate for a named-port continuation. Equal cover
response may suffice for one seam language but not for arbitrary re-marking.
The H55 source obstruction, the H31 neutral loop, and the new head graphs
without a unit selection are three concrete instances of the same loss.
The bridge preserves only the future operations it was proved to preserve.

**The Farey and sign-flip comparisons locate two different renewal failures.**
The Farey gate retains a determinant-one condition on actual cover profiles;
ordinal padding changes C2 but leaves H fixed, destroying that condition
unless both modules are transitive. Sign flips retain the offset predicate
but change the slope and ending-path count. Both are exact constructions;
neither supplies free renewal of the whole endpoint interface. The adjacent
powers8 and9 occur in the latter's first failed distance d=3. This uses an
elementary inequality, not Catalan's theorem and not a reduction of Chan–Pak
to Catalan. These bridges have explicit maps and failure coordinates; shared
small numbers alone would not establish the connection.

**Finite rules, finite states, and finite target alphabets must not be merged.**
A finite algorithm can carry unbounded integer valuations, polynomial degree,
or core size. The new synchronized rule is finite but has unbounded head
depth. The oldest-leaf controller has finitely many projective states but
unbounded actual source data. The graded-observer theorem rules out a fixed
linear dimension, not a finite program. These distinctions permit a realistic
finite-rule research target without promising a finite catalogue of all
tournament structure.

## 4. New enumeration coordinates and the next proof obligation

Keep the following sequences separate:

1. B_n: all strong marked stable-clone births at order n (complete only
   through10 in the inherited bank).
2. E_n: births reached by repeated one-vertex extensions from the complete
   order-ten birth layer. E_10=850, E_11=464, E_12=834, E_13=1414.
   This definition is recursively exact but does not equal B_n by assumption.
3. S_n: synchronized births outside the old base layer:24 at even n>=12,
   46 at odd n>=13, zero otherwise, using the extension-plus-synchronization
   alphabet. This entire infinite sequence and its rational generating
   function are proved. Keep its even and odd subgrammars separately.
4. E_(n,r): the rank-refined version, recording number r of stable singleton
   ports; retain a separate joint-stable rank. In particular E_(13,5)=8,
   while the full five-port joint tensor is certified for an explicit example.
5. A_n: distinct ancestry sets under a stated move alphabet. Stable
   contraction alone already has nonunique ancestry; adding extension and
   synchronized moves requires graph-key unions, not summing presentations.
6. R_(n,j): coefficient families born at size n whose minimum unpaid depth
   is j; keep the actual slope and head constructors. A finite maximum at
   one size is not a uniform bound, as B1 proves.

These are new statistics of specified marked construction graphs. They are
not replacement definitions or newly computed terms of A000568. The exact
ordinary isomorphism quotient and symmetry weights remain necessary before
relating marked production counts to an unmarked global sequence.

**OPEN proposed direction:** a controller with finitely many operation types,
an actual source interface, and unbounded integer state. It should combine
core-changing moves, synchronized growth and a head-repair routine. The
decisive theorem would be a target-decreasing inverse: every allowed odd
target outside a finite witnessed base admits a legal predecessor with a
strictly smaller well-founded measure and an explicit tournament lift.
For a seed formulation, it must construct every odd coefficient and pay
every exceptional lower head. For the inherited prime/7p formulation, it
must supply both remaining lanes. Neither the new census, the compact lifting
algorithm, nor the obstruction atlas proves that universal selection theorem.

The negative formulation remains useful: absent sets shrink pointwise with
size, and the inherited logarithmic core bound makes each fixed absence
decidable. What is missing is a uniform argument that every allowed target
eventually leaves the absent set. The results here narrow the permissible
grammar rather than establishing that the limiting set is {7,21}.
