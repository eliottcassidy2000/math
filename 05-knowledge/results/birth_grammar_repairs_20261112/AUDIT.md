## 2. Independent paths and hostile controls

- Full signed skeleton recursion and separate endpoint-table convolution
  agree on every retained synchronized free-port kernel and the full
  five-port kernel. The latter has exactly one defect monomial and108 U terms.
- `audit_math.py` checks every ordinary tournament through5:470 attachment
  counts,26920 selected-defect cube entries,85 full H and85 full U kernels,
  plus19 sparse/dense joint-kernel comparisons. The complete ordinary source
  counts through9 agree with1,1,2,4,12,56,456,6880,191536.
- Positive controls include nonuniform literal inflations,164 whole graphs
  from the five completed ray constructors, and literal mixed-family counts
  at orders13,15,17. These controls supplement complete tensor identities;
  they do not replace them.
- Hostiles include J1's defect-3 after simultaneous growth, C3's false
  length-zero deletion interpretation, and `OOZOZOJ`, whose characteristic
  polynomial is `x^2*(x-1)*(x-82)`. The last refutes the stronger claim that
  any J-containing word must lack eigenvalue1.
- The mod2 semigroup is independently recomputed by bit-packed multiplication
  and permutation expansion of polynomial determinants. All478 required head
  branches are checked to retain nonzero constant and3-mode coefficients.

Arithmetic is exact. Python integer tensors and rational profiles have no
fixed-width overflow. The compiled literal endpoint engine is used only
through order21. Pair disjoint adjacent positions in a permutation. At
most one of each swap orbit can satisfy the corresponding oriented arcs;
a fixed two-path cut removes at most one of these constraints. Thus H plus
all ordered nonempty two-path covers is bounded by
`(2n-1)*n!/2^floor(n/2)`. At n=21 this is2045633426796960000, below2^63.
The compiled one-port skeletons in this packet have at most19 total visits;
the looser `2*(19+1)*19!` bound also fits signed64. Exponential conversion
then uses Python integers. No floating-point equality decides a result.

## 3. Proof audit and repairs made before close-out

The proof audit checked necessary versus sufficient directions, positive
parameter domains, quotient symmetries, weighted chart intersections,
strongness, and the actual lower-head consequence. All28 frozen-block
certificates in the46 odd charts have positive Stirling coefficients; all20
same-quotient chart intersections occur only at formal length0. The code
gates now enforce those sharper facts rather than weaker discovery tests.

Three tempting claims were rejected: weak source/sink padding is not a
strong higher-rank birth; analytic length0 is not vertex deletion; a word
with J may have eigenvalue1. Their repaired statements and witnesses are in
[the mistakes ledger](../../../01-canon/MISTAKES.md).

V2's arbitrary finite-choice argument uses positive real exponential bases.
It was explicitly not exported to arbitrary periodic matrix sequences,
which may have negative or complex eigenvalues. V3/V4 are scoped to fixed
periods and fixed finite arithmetic-progression branching. General adaptive
words and finite programs with unbounded state remain outside these barriers.

G1 uses the classical strong-order bound from [Busch, Corollary1, p.4](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v13i1n3/pdf/),
checked12 November2026. The target all-odd-except7,21 statement is the one in
[Chan–Pak, Section6.5, Conjecture6.2](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~pak/papers/DominoPermanent16.pdf).
These primary sources were checked for the particular claims used; no
global literature-priority or exhaustive current-literature claim is made.

## 4. Reproduction

Run from the repository root. Python needs NumPy and Numba for the exact
compiled enumerators. The optional figure uses Matplotlib; the session used
an isolated temporary package directory without changing repository dependencies.

python3 -O -X utf8 05-knowledge/results/birth_grammar_repairs_20261112/audit_packet.py

`replay_fast.py` reruns27 producers and records the exact completed job list.

not a sampling mode. Normal receipts are checked in; remove `-O` to regenerate
them. The packet auditor verifies every normal/optimized pair, decisive
cross-receipt totals, completion flags, local document links and input hashes.
It does not replace the substantive producers or their symbolic proofs.
