# Session 113 — symmetry depends on its consumer

**Status: ACTIVE research session, 2026-10-30.** Elementary scoped proofs and finite exact controls; no major open problem is closed.

The organizing rule is to specify **the action, the consumer, and the retained state** before quotienting. The same orbit information can be sufficient for a binary relation and insufficient for a marked constraint, physical derivative, or nonlinear identity.

## Verified checkpoints

- [LRC boundary germ](lrc/boundary_germ.md): reflection and common dilation preserve the reciprocal-owner boundary coefficient. Modular witness orbits alone do not. A guarded finite relaxation gives an exact nonemptiness criterion, not its universal lower bound.
- [Composite cyclic parents](composite/COMPOSITE_PHASE.md): two generators produce a congruence kernel whose two-closure is a larger wreath group. The missing phase first appears at the number of fibres; exact native-chart intersections retain the inner and outer orientation coordinates.

- [Jacobian symmetry certificate](jacobian/README.md): actual finite equivariance above a sharp degree-aware order forces continuous equivariance and a diagonal Keller map. Reynolds projection can destroy the Keller condition; omitted character-bracket channels record the exact defect.

- [Prime-bank rank/unrank](prime_ranking/PRIME_RANKING.md): the named shared-residue action admits constructive indexing after retaining surviving ideal cuts and original block periods. Polynomial bit cost is in explicit bank dimension, not its logarithm; this is not full tournament-isomorphism ranking.

- [Collatz quotient certificates](collatz/THEORY.md): the closed state retains defect and square phase for an upper-bound consumer, proving sharp fixed layers E=6,7,8 after exact finite audits and an infinite-letter tail. It intentionally does not recognize exact future returns.
- [Envelope obstruction and repair](uniform_content/THEORY.md): explicit source-realizable words refute the old universal phase-gated budget, not the actual-content target. Retaining the multiplier's first-return offset gives a valid finer epoch upper bound; a universal payment certificate remains open.
