```bash

cmp normal.txt optimized.txt
python3 audit.py
python3 -O audit.py
python3 inventory.py --revision 4e88a9401adb3d1960053263d9254e8582149053
```

The output files normal.txt and optimized.txt are disposable local transcripts,
not tracked artifacts. receipts.json and audit.json are the stable evidence.
shift_search.json records exploratory search metadata; the audit rechecks its
positive witnesses directly. Capped negative rows make no nonexistence claim.
