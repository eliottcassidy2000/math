**Session267, 11 November2026. COMPLETE RESEARCH SYNTHESIS. JC(2) remains OPEN.**

This session treats the repository's NC2/GMC(2) theorem as an inherited
result and asks what useful structure can be built from it. The strongest
new connection is literal: the planar Student row functional is a
two-real-Gaussian expectation, and its repair operator is infinitesimal

The result is a constructive research program with proved pieces and

## 1. What the inherited Gaussian proof really gives

For a circular Gaussian Z and W=conjugate(Z), the functional is

    L(Z^a W^b)=a! when a=b, and zero otherwise.

Inherited NC2 classifies every nonzero polynomial with all positive
moments zero: every monomial has strictly positive charge a−b, or every
monomial has strictly negative charge. The repository calls this proved
and exposes an unconditional root Lean endpoint. This session did not
rebuild or independently re-audit it, and makes no external-consensus claim.

Three useful consequences are proved in the new note:

- A polynomial null family over an irreducible parameter space has ONE
  common charge orientation. The sign cannot change arbitrarily between
  fibres.
- If the smallest signed charge is delta>0 and a marked observer has
  opposite charge width B, then L(QP^m)=0 whenever m delta>B. In particular
  m>deg Q always suffices, independently of deg P.
- For a fixed finite support, the radical of the moment ideal is exactly
  generated by the neutral coefficients and every product of one positive
  and one negative coefficient. This supplies precise algebraic certificate
  targets and a finite-cutoff existence theorem, without claiming an
  effective universal cutoff.

These statements turn pointwise eventual vanishing into uniform
parameter-family control. The dimension boundary remains important:
ordinary GMC(2) is one complex Gaussian pair. The two-coordinate
special-image operator generally uses two pairs, or four real Gaussians.
The inherited unrestricted SIC(2) failure cannot be bypassed by relabeling.
Indeed a full degree-m two-pair contraction requires at least m+1
rank-one covariance samples. Fixed finite universal sampling is impossible.

## 2. The planar row operator is exactly Gaussian rotation

In the Session254 chart, the row-m operator is

    L_m theta = −x theta + (x²+6)theta'/(2m).

Homogenize by T_m(F)=H^(2m)F(sqrt(6)G/H), where G,H are independent
standard real Gaussians. Then

    (H partial_G − G partial_H) T_m
       = (2m/sqrt(6)) T_m L_m.

The inherited Student scalar is exactly E[T_m(F)]/E[H^(2m)]. In angular
coordinates U=H+iG,V=H−iG, the basis U^(m+k)V^(m−k) has rotation eigenvalue
2ik. The full rotational quotient has only one class, the k=0 channel.

Why do the planar rows have more than one debt? The allowable source
tangents occupy a **non-invariant degree flag** inside the full binary-form
space. Unrestricted rotation inversion can leave that flag. At row16,
x^11 has zero Gaussian expectation but is an unpaid rigid coefficient.
This isolates the missing bridge: Gaussian invariance is already present;
preservation of the actual source flag is not.

Products have exact angular convolution. Opposite one-sided factors can
produce a neutral parent: E[U²]=E[V²]=0, but E[U²V²]=8. A fixed scalar
summary therefore does not compose. Retaining every future product moment
on degree-2m forms requires at least 2m+1 linear state coordinates.

This joins the inherited warning that angular projection preserves the
expectation of an already-formed power but need not commute with powers.
The corresponding boundary warning is that a generic projective gradient
line loses the integral content needed for a unit Jacobian mate.

## 3. A finite certificate that handles every row at once

Let R(t,x)=P(t,x)/Q(t,x) be reduced, with Q(0,x)=1, and define

    w(t^r x^j)=2j−r.

The new rational-cone theorem proves, over every field:

    every coefficient monomial of R has weight≤B
       if and only if w(Q)≤0 and w(P)≤B.

The necessity uses the substitution x=u/t² and regularity of rational
functions in the local power-series ring. A positive-weight pole cannot
be concealed by infinitely many coefficient cancellations in a reduced
expression. The sufficiency is a geometric-series support argument.

For the complete tail R=sum_(m≥5)F_m(x)t^m of actual bracket discrepancies,
the inherited rigid-debt condition is exactly deg F_m≤floor((m+5)/2).
Thus, **if that full tail is represented rationally**, all rigid debts
vanish at every row precisely when w(Q)≤0 and w(P)≤5.

This is not extrapolation from a finite prefix. A forbidden pole can be
delayed beyond any chosen number of tests. The proposed next theorem is
to derive a lawful rational carrier from the actual normalized source
recursion and prove its operations preserve this weight cone. Rationality
alone is insufficient, and a finite-prefix rational fit supplies no proof.

The new row-shift intertwiners explain how Gaussian classes move between
rows, but explicit examples show they need not preserve the moving source
flag. The rational support cone targets that flag directly.

## 4. An actual recursive Gaussian compiler

For the specified shear word F_t=A_t composed with B_t, where

    A_t(x,y)=(x+t p(y),y),   B_t(x,y)=(x,y+t q(x)),

the forward polynomials construct one null base P=Z and observers

    Q_1=u−W p(v),   Q_2=v−W q(u−W p(v)).

Their marked Gaussian generating functions are exactly the two inverse
coordinates. The second marked sequence vanishes for m>deg q+1,
uniformly in parameters and independently of deg p for this ordering.

For an arbitrary specified triangular-shear word, the local rule is

    R_i <- R_i−W p(R_j),
    D_i <- max(D_i,1+(deg p)D_j).

This is a genuine recursive closure and a computable observer budget.
It uses the forward word and elementary legal moves. It does not first
assume an unknown inverse has terminated. Arbitrarily high degree and
arbitrary finite word length are allowed.

This does not prove that every Keller map has such a word. The
time-dependent shear displacement also must not be confused with the
single time-independent nilpotent tower used in a stable cubic reduction.
Transport between those descriptions remains an explicit open obligation.

## 5. The discrete recursion connection: what substitution hides

For a finite permutation group A, let N_fix(A) be generated by its
elements fixing at least one point. The inherited finite-survivor theorem
requires N_fix(G)=G for a Keller monodromy action.

For the full imprimitive wreath action, the new exact formula is

    N_fix(H wr K)=H^m semidirect N_fix(K),  m≥2.

The fixed-sheet gate sees the outer quotient and can be completely blind
to the inner group. C2 wr S3 passes the gate, while S3 wr C2 fails, despite
both acting on six sheets. The ordering of modules matters.

There is also a literal tournament model, using only odd-order groups:
the regular C3 action inside each vertex of the Paley7 tournament yields
an explicit C3 wr (F7 semidirect {1,2,4}) automorphism subgroup on21
vertices. Its inner regular action fails N_fix=C3, while the full
substituted action passes. This is a shared permutation-module mechanism,
not a realization of a Keller map by a tournament.

A positive terminal exclusion follows: no nontrivial nilpotent monodromy
action, nor nontrivial nilpotent outer block quotient, satisfies the
Keller fixed-sheet gate. Inner cyclic blocks can still be hidden. A
theorem recognizing them as actual polynomial Keller factors would pay
the missing algebraic debt; abstract intermediate fields are not enough.

The adjacent-transposition path generates S_d at every d≥3 while every
generator fixes a sheet. Even a path with one repeated local rule can
carry arbitrarily many sheets. Unlabelled recursion alone has no degree
bound.

## 6. Three honest routes toward the desired implication chain

**Gaussian inverse route.** Inherited NC2 gives uniform marked escape.
A forward-data finite null-packet compiler for every planar-origin stable
reduction would then force its formal inverse to terminate, hence JC(2).
Bare packet existence is equivalent to eventual inverse-coefficient
vanishing and therefore only repackages the problem. The constructive
local compiler, including its dimension-preserving transport, is the
substantive open claim. The shear-word sector above is established.

**Source-flag route.** A proved actual-source rational carrier with the
weight bounds would discharge every rigid bracket debt by Section3.
Scalar payment, inhomogeneous depth compatibility, global chart entry,
and a correct well-founded inverse-building termination rule would still
be required. None is silently supplied by Gaussianity or formal lifting.

**Boundary-descent route.** Suppose every Keller map has a realizable
grammar with N states, arity≤b, local sheet weight≤W, and every repeated
state yields a smaller noninvertible polynomial Keller map or a genuine
nonunit factorization. Then a least-generic-degree counterexample has

    d≤W^(1+b+...+b^(N−1)).

This implication is proved. Excluding the resulting terminal families
would finish JC(2). The bounded alphabet, bounded weights, actual affine
source recognition, and badness-preserving descent are open, separate
requirements. A bounded generic degree still permits continuous moduli;
it is not a finite coefficient census.

These are conditional architectures, not a proved ordered hierarchy of
strictly intermediate conjectures. A Gaussian-to-boundary cost is another
proposed bridge, but no such reconstruction theorem is claimed.

## 7. Why raw high degree and flat surfaces are the wrong risk labels

If F=(P,Q) is any Keller map, then `(P+Q^N,Q)` has degree N deg Q for
sufficiently large N. It has exactly the same generic degree,
invertibility status, and boundary-cover topology after a polynomial
coordinate change. Thus any counterexample, if one exists, already
produces arbitrarily high-degree representatives without a new anomaly.

JC(2) concerns complex dimension two, or real dimension four. A planar
drawing of its infinity divisor is not a classification of real flat
surfaces. The productive question is whether all genuine obstructions
reduce to controlled algebraic boundary states, not whether huge degree
forces an exotic new surface.

Nor can all the missing state be compressed into a few algebraic scalar
tests: after same-row scalar payment, the ambient row repair locus has
codimension floor(m/2)−2. Krull's height theorem requires at least that
many polynomial equations to recognize it universally. A marked Gaussian
polynomial can retain the full growing ledger exactly; unmarked moments
can be blind to every nonconstant ledger coordinate.
