# Open bridge dossier

These are precise research targets, not established implications from
GMC(2) alone. No logical strictness between the proposed conjectures and
JC(2) is claimed.

## A. Actual-source rational flag closure

Fix the inherited source-normal chart and a fully specified legal
normalization/repair procedure. Derive the COMPLETE actual bracket
discrepancy tail R(t,x)=sum_(m≥5)F_m(x)t^m as a rational function P/Q,
with coprime P,Q and Q(0,x)=1. Prove w(Q)≤0 and w(P)≤5, where
w(t^r x^j)=2j−r.

**Proved consequence:** every rigid coefficient debt in that chart
vanishes at every row. The proof is the rational-cone theorem, not a
finite-prefix inference.

**Still separate:** the source scalar, inhomogeneous depth compatibility,
coverage of all Keller maps by the chart, and a terminating correct
polynomial-inverse algorithm. Rationality by itself does not give the
weight bounds, and a finite-prefix fit does not identify R.

**First test:** evaluate the full rational carrier or at least the six
rigid row16 coordinates on the inherited actual G_m prefix family.

## B. Forward-data Gaussian packet compilation

For every planar-origin stable nilpotent reduction, construct a finite
derivation of its marked inverse-coefficient towers from null bases in
ONE complex Gaussian pair and fixed polynomial observers over the output
parameter ring. Derive each rule from the forward map; retain the output
parameters and the planar provenance. Packet number and observer support
may depend on the map but not on the power index m.

**Proved consequence:** inherited NC2 yields parameter-wide charge
orientation and uniform eventual marked vanishing. The formal inverse
identity then gives polynomial invertibility.

**Established sector:** every specified finite triangular-shear word
has an explicit one-base observer recursion and a computable degree
budget. Its t-dependent displacement still needs transport into the
single fixed-f stable-reduction tower.

**Important limit:** mere packet existence is equivalent to eventual
inverse-coefficient vanishing, since any finite sequence can be encoded
after the fact. The genuinely constructive target is the forward-data
compiler and its verified local operations, not this bare equivalence.

## C. Algebraically realized boundary contraction

Supply a finite decorated grammar for the full actual boundary passport,
with fixed state count N, arity bound b, sheet-weight bound W, and actual
affine-source gluing. Every repeated state in a surviving obstruction
must yield a polynomial Keller factorization into two nonunits or a
strictly smaller noninvertible polynomial Keller map.

**Proved consequence:** if JC(2) fails, a least-generic-degree
counterexample has degree at most W^(1+b+...+b^(N−1)). Uniform exclusion
of those terminal families would prove JC(2).

**Still separate:** finite alphabet, bounded labels, realization of a
contraction, strict descent, and terminal exclusion. Bounded generic
degree is not a finite complex-coefficient census.

**First test:** recognize the intermediate affine chart for one cyclic
inner block family. The exact wreath theorem shows why scalar
fixed-sheet normal generation cannot see that hidden factor, while a
genuine polynomial recognition would expose it to the Keller constraints.

## What would count as progress

- An all-parameter identity for one new source operation.
- An explicit conductor/chart obstruction to a proposed block contraction.
- A derived rational denominator with its exact weight and cancellation
  status, rather than more fitted row values.
- A new packet construction preserving the complete output polynomial.

More unmarked recursive pictures, a higher raw-degree cutoff, or more
vanishing scalar moments alone would not close any of these bridges.
