Here is the elementary support lemma needed to finish. A family of3-cycles whose support hypergraph is connected generates the alternating group on its union. Start with one triple, which generates A3. Suppose A_U has already been obtained, with |U|≥3. A new triple meeting U in two elements adds its remaining element: conjugating by A_U, and taking inverses if necessary, gives all3-cycles involving that new element and two old ones, and these together with A_U generate A_(U∪{z}). This uses transitivity of A_U on unordered pairs, valid even for |U|=3. If a new triple meets U only in x, write it as(x a b) and choose y∈U distinct from x. A_U conjugation also gives(y a b). Their product (x a b)(y a b)^−1=(x a y) adds a by the preceding case, then the original triple adds b. Connectedness allows this process to reach the full union. Finally every even permutation is a product of3-cycles (split a cycle decomposition into transposition pairs). This proves the lemma and the theorem.
