### M2. HYP-9680 (C), read literally, is false at order 12; THM-5154 (A) misses a symmetry

**Exact sentences.**
- HYP-9680 title: "(C) every coincidence between merged classes is generated by run redistribution, global
  converse, and seam-preserving core flips: S may be replaced by S^op whenever the multiset of its seam weights is
  unchanged".
- HYP-9680 body: "**(C)** All coincidences come from moving singletons between nonempty runs, global converse, and
  flipping cores whose seam-weight multiset the flip preserves."
- README: "So the general move set needs seam-preserving flips, which is HYP-9680 (C)."
- THM-5154 (A): "So the multiset depends only on four things: - the cores; …". The README has "the strong cores,
  in order".

**The problem.** Call a maximal chain of cores joined by empty runs a *core-block*, and call it interior when both
flanking runs are nonempty. By the same Session 319 product, an interior core-block [A ⇒ B] contributes:
- its internal weights;
- the cross seam e_A(u) s_B(v)·h/(H_A H_B);
- the entry weights s_A(u)·h/H_A;
- the exit weights e_B(v)·h/H_B.

Its block converse [B^op ⇒ A^op] contributes the same multiset. The cross seam becomes s_B·e_A, and entry and exit
swap. The multiset also ignores the **order** of interior core-blocks. Neither operation is a move (i)-(iii) or a
flip of a strong component.

**Counterexamples** (S4 = the strong 4-tournament; all cores are self-converse, so core flips do nothing).
- **Order 12, block reversal.**
  - P12a = C3 ⇒ v ⇒ C3 ⇒ S4 ⇒ w has out-masks `4090 4092 4089 4080 4000 4032 3984 2816 3584 3072 2176 0`.
  - P12b = C3 ⇒ v ⇒ S4 ⇒ C3 ⇒ w has out-masks `4090 4092 4089 4080 3936 4032 3968 3856 2560 3072 2304 0`.
  - Both have H = 45 and 32 zeros, with positive weights 36, 30^6, 27^3, 18, 15^6, 9^5, 6^3, 3^9.
  - The auditor's DFS and the packet's own `arcms_rows` agree.
  - The ordered SCC sequences (C3, 1, C3, S4, 1) and (C3, 1, S4, C3, 1) differ, and both converses start with a
    singleton. So these are distinct merged classes.
  - No move in (C) applies. Each nonempty run holds one singleton, the core flips are trivial, and converse turns
    the run pattern ENEN into NENE.
- **Order 13, block permutation.**
  - P13a = C3 ⇒ v ⇒ C3 ⇒ v ⇒ S4 ⇒ v and P13b = C3 ⇒ v ⇒ S4 ⇒ v ⇒ C3 ⇒ v.
  - Both have H = 45, 49 zeros and identical weights. They are distinct merged classes.
- **Order 24.** M1 also lies outside (C): a strong tournament has no runs.

A generous reading of "core" as "any segment of the SCC order flanked by nonempty runs" would cover P12 and P13. P13b
is P13a with the segment C3 ⇒ v ⇒ S4 flipped, and segment flips generate the block permutations. No such reading
covers M1.

**Fix.**
- Mark HYP-9680 (C) REFUTED as stated.
- If re-filed, add flips of segments flanked by nonempty runs, which generate interior core-block reversals and
  permutations. Add converse replacement of vertex-transitive modules (Session 320). Phrase it as "at least these

- In THM-5154 (A), add one sentence: the multiset also forgets the order of interior core-blocks and their block

## Proof check of THM-5154 (A)

Every bullet follows from the ordered SCC product: one path through each component, concatenated. Checks:
- **Inner run arcs.** Consecutive singletons have weight h. Non-consecutive singletons are non-adjacent SCCs and have
  weight 0.
- **Seams.** From a nonempty run into S_i, only the last singleton is adjacent, giving s(v)·h/H(S_i) on |S_i| arcs.
  From S_i out to a nonempty run: e(u)·h/H(S_i). An empty run gives the product seam. Core arcs get W_S·h/H(S_i),
  dark zeros included.
- **Move (i)** preserves every seam multiset and Σ(r_i − 1)_+. **Move (ii)** swaps a flanked core's entry multiset
  {s} and exit multiset {e} and reverses W_S. Neither changes H(S_i), so all other weights are unchanged.
- **Q.** t-slot covers of A ⇒ B are pairs of t-slot covers of A and B. So Q_(A⇒B) = Q_A·Q_B. Reversing every path
  gives Q(S^op) = Q(S).

No gap, beyond the missing sharpening in M2 and the nits in m9.
