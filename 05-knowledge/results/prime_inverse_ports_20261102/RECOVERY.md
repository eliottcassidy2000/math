## Three exact meanings of 'primes of the primes'

1. **Iterated prime indexing.** If p_n is the nth prime, p_(p_n) begins
   3,5,11,17,31,41,... . It is not the set of primes greater than2:7 already
   distinguishes them. A further iteration begins5,11,31,59,127,..., again
   different from simply removing2 and3.
2. **Successive localization / sieve.** In Z[1/2], the irreducibles up to
   units are the odd primes. In Z[1/6] they are exactly the primes greater
   than3. Equivalently, the multiplicative monoid of positive integers
   coprime to the first r primes has precisely the remaining primes as atoms.
   This gives an exact version of the proposed removal hierarchy.
3. **Atoms of an attained-value monoid, or prime orbit counts.** The inherited
   candidate spectrum monoid of positive odds except7,21 has atoms ordinary
   odd primes except7, the7p lane (p odd prime other than3),63 and343. That is not
   yet a proof that all its elements are attained. Separately H/Aut may be
   prime when H is composite: the classified seven-symmetry branch realizes
   exactly this distinction. Neither operation is prime indexing.

The useful transfer here is not an analogy based on removing small numbers.
It is the exact matrix action carrying valuations and the residue(6) of
SYMMETRY.md. Prime divisors constrain legal words, but congruence feasibility
