## A boundary hostile forces relative activity into any uniform theorem

Work in the charge abstraction with the same complete R bank, weights one,
and256 outside slots. Give128 outside slots label0 and128 label(0,1) of
order128 in G. Fix k=128 and q=0. Give every outside V choice weight T and
every outside U choice weight one. The two states with r=0 are exactly:

    A: all128 zero-labelled outside sources at V;
    B: all128 order128-labelled outside sources at V.

Both have mass T^128. Every other feasible full state has mass at most
T^127 times a fixed coefficient (R has at least one selected source).
Thus their combined stationary mass tends to one as T tends to infinity,
and each tends to mass1/2. A and B differ in256 actual slots. Any reversible
chain whose events change fewer than256 slots has no direct A--B edge.
Stationarity bounds the flow out of A by the total mass of the remaining
states, which is O(1/T). Applying the variational bound to1_A shows that its
gap tends to zero. This holds even if one optimizes all permitted rates.

The carrier is still connected by the complete-bank routing theorem on
interior total grades. What fails is weight-independent full-fibre mixing.
All outside odds here are EQUAL, so bounding their mutual ratio alone is
insufficient. Their activity relative to the reservoir is the missing
coordinate. This is an abstract label/weight hostile, not an unverified claim
that these256 records exist in the original native atlas.

The corrected survivor is twofold: arbitrary outside weights are allowed
with a uniform interior bound, and all accepted states are included with
the explicitly paid boundary factor H. A source-wide rescaling of all V
odds does not alter the conditioned law: Z[r,g] scales by t^r, outside odds
by t, and every summand defining H is unchanged. The obstruction concerns
relative activity, not a change of units.

## Do not turn the auxiliary bits into native capacity

Ten (or six) auxiliary exchange bits describe binomial multiplicity in a
reference measure. They do not create literal paths, shared owners, resources
or extra kernel lifts. The complete conditional native law replaces them at
each arrival. Likewise, an all-order harmonic-bank transfer needs the actual
new Laplacian readout conditions from
`../session47_resilient_native_wiring_20261024/carrier-transfer/TRANSFER.md`.
The frozen mod256 saturation result is not automatically a theorem for a
larger atlas.

There is a basic capacity bound: if s binary reservoir records must realize
every debt in a set D at a fixed grade a, then

    |D| <= binom(s,a).

Under prescribed pins replace the right side by the number of allowed
grade-a records. It is the required debt alphabet that matters; the ambient
group may be larger if its additional characters never occur as debts. A
fixed bank cannot cover unbounded independent debt alphabets. More banks,
conditional separator structure, or a proved smaller debt image are genuine
ways around this obstruction; renaming kernel lifts is not.

The resulting reusable move is to separate native supply, complete
conditional coefficients, local boundary mass and actual transition rates.
For Collatz a further source-preserving map and a comparison to its intended
ROOT transport are still missing. Reversible mixing is not a proof that a
deterministic trajectory enters a certified set. The theorem supplies a
quantitative ingredient, with its exact interface exposed.
