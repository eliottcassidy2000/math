   not physical supply. Quotient rates are lumpable under their permutations.
   Actual reservoir probabilities are restored by conditional refresh.
   Final proof review caught a wording overclaim: laziness belongs to the
   visible kernel; the full refresh has its spectrum plus zeros but can have
   diagonal probability below1/2. The gap bounds are unchanged. The four-state
   uniform heat bath (all entries1/4) is the minimal equal-two-fibre control.
2. **Weights:** source product weights are retained exactly. The elementary
   pair coupling is weight-independent; the Metropolis comparison loses one
   max/min potential ratio, not a global product ratio or its square.
3. **Boundary:** an interior restriction alone would miss most states at
   extreme activity. Full-fibre leaves receive legal paid edges and their
   entire mass is included in H. Every row is normalized and reversible.
4. **Quantifiers:** all native labels and full states in the declared supply
   are covered by the proof. Sampled event tests do not assert exhaustive
   endpoint enumeration. Uniformity in new slots is conditional on native
   supply; arbitrary correlations and additional pins are excluded.
5. **Pins and clocks:** the one- and twelve-pin families remain distinct.
   Twelve-control weights and charges precede projection. Full-record switch
   costs, including root changes, are bounded for every representative pair.
6. **Hostiles:** arbitrary conductance slowdown remains possible for another
   chain. Even optimized bounded-support chains have a relative-activity
   bottleneck on the cyclic-charge control. Global-observer and new-carrier
   harmonic conditions cannot be inferred from local host compatibility.
   Overlapping balanced admissions are treated as stochastic edge couplings,
   never as disjoint charts; their full incoming source-weighted mass is paid.

## Reproduction

Run `reproduce.py` from the repository root. It verifies the manifest, then
runs all five checkers with `python3 -B` and `python3 -O -B` and compares
every receipt. The native checker reads its frozen sibling dependencies from
Git into a temporary directory; it does not require a materialized sibling
in a sparse checkout. Other native receipts likewise come from explicitly
frozen Git revisions. No network request or optimizer is part of the replay.
