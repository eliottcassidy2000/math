# Source-weighted projected chains and conditional all-size coverage

24 October2026. **PROVED:** explicit weighted bounds for the declared
product-source laws and native interfaces. **OPEN:** all-order native supply,
harmonic transfer to larger carriers, and a comparison to Collatz ROOT.

The previous [85-source three-target theorem](../three_target570_20261024/README.md)
already supplies unrestricted20-source/45-clock routes and an exact
conditional-weight lifting identity. The new step is a quantitative bound
on the visible chain itself, with all accepted occupancy grades included.

- [Weighted theorem](PROOF.md): a complete twenty-source bank gives interior
  gap `1/(2 kappa binom(m+10,2))`, independent of arbitrary outside source
  odds. The full fibre pays an explicit boundary factor H. For bounded outside
  odds this gives an inverse-polynomial bound, with22-source/56-clock events
  covering all total grades. Actual source weights are never made uniform.
- [Balanced admission](BALANCED.md) spreads boundary mass over all legal
  nearest-band destinations. It improves the FULL-fibre bound to inverse
  quadratic in the bulk occupancy range. At m=61,k=40 with unit odds, the
  boundary factor falls from6,550,852 to65485/6327, approximately10.35.
- [Moving shared owners](PINS.md): the same proof retains the exact single-pin
  or twelve-pin systems, with their separate laws, conditional coefficients
  and integer clock costs. No pin or kernel lift is silently released.
- [Incoming resilient bank](ROBUST.md): Session47's thirteen-forced-U result
  now has a weighted chain bound uniform over every fixed such restriction.
  For unit helper odds and arbitrary core odds the gap is at least1/130788;
  each event costs at most ten sources/64 clocks. Adaptive changing
  restrictions require a further nonstationary argument.
- [Externally pinned receiver](RECEIVER.md): incoming fixed-owner4 routing
  now admits a weighted bound despite its helper charge holes, preserving
  both population margins. Interior events cost22/55;22/62 covers all grades.
  The bound is independent of the positive odds on all51 free helpers.
- [Extension and obstruction](EXTENSION.md): deterministic20/45 routing is
  stable under any compatible finite source extension satisfying the same
  interface. A heavy boundary shows why outside activity relative to the
  bank cannot be removed from a full-fibre bound. This is not an all-order
  tournament supply theorem.

Auxiliary binary bits are only a reference measure with known multiplicity.
Replacing them by complete conditional native fibres preserves source mass,
literal records, pins, original dyadic currents and clock accounting. Matching
their odds to the bank improves one explicit nonuniform test constant from
27440 to13048/115 without changing any actual source weight.
