# Cross-audit of the port-chain renewal result

**Status: VERIFIED.** Auditor: fibre-reconfiguration lane, 2026-11-13.
Object: [orbits/PROOF.md](../orbits/PROOF.md), Theorem 1 and Corollaries 2–3.
This accepts the scoped reset-removal result, not prime-lane coverage or
Chan–Pak.

The concrete block proof is sound. In R, link vertex 1 has exactly the
cross-arcs of the C singleton; vertex 0 is the new global sink and root.
All future cross-arc exceptions involve only the current root. Thus every
vertex in a closed prefix beats every later vertex permanently. Splitting at
each R gives the claimed rooted ordinal decomposition. The prescribed swap
of R's two born vertices gives the presented vertex order without changing
the other births.

The full-state scaling also follows at graph level: a path starting at the
final root cannot move into an earlier ordinal summand. Its complementary
Hamiltonian count gains exactly the product of the earlier counts. This
scales h,E,O together. A return factor ell·s(v) is itself supplied by the
reset-free word vC, so the equality of multiplicative closures follows.

Prime and 7p preservation is correctly scoped. A nontrivial proper split of
7p would need a factor 7, including p=7; p=3 is the forbidden value 21.
The analogous claim for 63 uses exclusion of both 7 and 21. Unit factors can
be removed for scalar membership but must remain in the graph/order identity,
as the proof states. Removing this one reset does not remove every singular
generator or establish any inverse-target theorem.

An independent direct-cut check used the literal compiler and inspected every
R occurrence in every word of length at most three. At an R born after offset
j, the closed set is all earlier vertices together with j+1; its complement
contains the new sink j and all later births. Every arc from the closed set to
its complement was forward: **534 cut checks across 2,380 words passed**.
This check did not use the split-join constructor, the claimed permutation,
matrix-state multiplication, or scalar count equality. The imported finite
orbit receipt was inspected but not regenerated by this auditor.

## Follow-on singular-word audit

**Status: VERIFIED.** The theorem in
[orbits/SINGULAR.md](../orbits/SINGULAR.md) correctly eliminates NN and X_aCN
from every critical-target word and removes a terminal X_aX_b by replacing
the last X_b with C.

The factor-realization sidecars are essential and are present. The return
rows are actual first rows of N and CX_a, hence their values on an actual
state are attained tournament counts. O≥1 follows already from the length-one
root path weighted by H(T−root); thus every return is at least five. The
output rays are actual rooted tournament states. A nonempty suffix on
(1,b,b) has H at least 1+2b≥3, by the common first-row lower bound, while any
suffix on (5,2,3) retains H≥5. These are two nonunit *attained* factors, which
exclude prime and seven-prime targets. The proof does not confuse an integer
divisor or a coordinate gcd with an attained factor.

A terminal XX replacement introduces no new forbidden suffix: its last
letter becomes C, and a longer XXX run would already have contained a
forbidden nonterminal XX. Full state need not be preserved; the statement is
correctly limited to scalar representation. Discarded unit factors belong
only to the prior R-removal membership argument.

An independent rerun over the 22,621 R-free words of length at most four
recovered 6,121 critical words and 471 terminal replacements, with every
claimed exclusion and scalar equality valid. Independent explicit enumeration
of *all simple root-starting paths*, weighted by complement Hamiltonian counts,
verified the literal strong states (25,10,15) and (1757,994,1372). This differs
from the author's rooted subset-DP implementation. Both literal strongness
checks also passed. The H=1757 witness correctly blocks any inference that
