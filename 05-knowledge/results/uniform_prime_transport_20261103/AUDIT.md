## Proof audit and failure boundaries

T1 uses homogeneous adjacency, actual nonnegative quotient coefficients,
Rédei oddness, SCC multiplication, THM-002 and the primary Busch minimum.
The polynomial-root argument uses degree strictly below p, established by
the strong-core bound; singleton padding is kept as the factor X^a.
The7p module exclusion treats p=7 separately. The finite-field test records
prime divisibility only, with prime-power precision explicitly excluded.

T2 keeps physical vertex names throughout. It is not the ordinary full
converse: only nonpath edges are reversed. The next path is checked in
that actual intermediate relation. Its homogeneous extension retains a
chosen full quotient frame; no extension of an arbitrary local frame is
assumed. Intermediate complements need not be strong.

T3 credits the earlier all-context affine exchange and C3 kernel. Its
novel use is their legal command implementation and explicit source
constructors. No signed count subtraction is accepted as a graph witness.

T4 needs arc transitivity, not just regularity or vertex transitivity.
The R7 singleton-ear formula uses vertex transitivity only; the full-cut
Paley formula is not transferred to arbitrary R7 cuts. The1259 certificate
proves the minimum added-vertex number2 by the all-cut one-ear obstruction,
not by optimizing only the supplied first cut.
