parity witness proves that the fixed-attachment recurrence alone is too
small; the81/27 pair prevents replacing joint incidence by run totals.

seed family's affine scale as exhaustive after composition. The relevant

and the retained divisor/frame before claiming classification. No map from
these tournaments to Keller maps, nor progress on the Jacobian conjecture,
is asserted by that comparison.
