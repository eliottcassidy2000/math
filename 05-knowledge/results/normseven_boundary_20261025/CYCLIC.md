# Cyclic carry conductors, numerical semigroups, and the cusp link

2026-10-25. **PROVED** with exact controls in `check_prime.py`. This
generalizes BOUNDARY.md. The numerical-semigroup and plane-branch examples
are recovered classical mechanisms, not priority claims. The proof is
self-contained. Internal audit, not an independent human/agent audit.

## C1. Keep coordinate images, not just whether they are nonzero

Let m>=2 and V=(Z/m)^n=C_1 direct_sum ... direct_sum C_r, with additive
subgroups C_j, not necessarily free Z/m modules. Represent coordinates by
integers 0,...,m-1. Over a characteristic-zero UFD R, take distinct primes
b_i times units and define

    A=R[u_1,...,u_n]/(u_i^m-b_i),
    A_(C_j)=direct_sum_(c in C_j) R u_c.

The fixed-group interpretation additionally uses appropriate roots of
unity; the free monic basis computation does not need that interpretation.
For z decompose it uniquely as sum_j c_j(z) in V and put

    T_i(z)=sum_j [c_j(z)]_i,    e_i(z)=(T_i(z)-z_i)/m.

Multiplication injects tensor_j A_(C_j) into A, with image

    B=direct_sum_z R b^e(z)u_z.                       (1)

For each coordinate set

    S_i=sum_j max{[c]_i:c in C_j},    a_i=S_i-m+1.     (2)

**C1.** All a_i are nonnegative and

    (B:A)=(product_i u_i^a_i) A.                     (3)

If A is a normal domain, it is the normalization of B in the common
fraction field. The universal exponent monoid has group completion Z^n,
saturation N^n, and conductor a+N^n.

**Proof.** Each tensor-basis vector has a distinct character and maps to
b^e(z)u_z, proving injectivity and (1). Inverting the b_i makes the two
algebras equal; every u_i is integral over B. This gives the normalization
assertion under the stated domain/normality hypothesis.

The least required exponent of b_i in a homogeneous conductor coefficient
f_z is, by componentwise divisibility in the UFD,

    c_i(z)=max_y [e_i(z+y mod m)-floor((z_i+y_i)/m)]_+.

Put w=z+y mod m. The expression in brackets is exactly

    floor((T_i(w)-z_i)/m),                           (4)

because the ordinary multiplication carry is one precisely when w_i<z_i.
Now T_i(w)<=S_i, and equality is attainable: independently choose a
maximizing coordinate value in every C_j and add the resulting vectors.
Since w ranges over all V, these choices occur. Hence

    c_i(z)=floor((S_i-z_i)/m).                       (5)

Full coverage of V includes a vector with coordinate m-1, so S_i>=m-1;
the right side is nonnegative. Multiplying u^a by u_(z-a mod m) has
coefficient exponent floor((a_i+m-1-z_i)/m), equal to (5). This proves
(3), including minimality.

Universally set t_i=u_i^m. The exponent monoid M is generated by mN^n
and the representatives of all C_j, with exact residue ideals

    M=disjoint_union_z (z+m e(z)+mN^n).

Its group completion contains mZ^n and surjects onto V, so is Z^n. Every
nonnegative vector has its m-fold multiple in M; no vector with a negative
coordinate does. This proves saturation. The proof of (3), with independent
t_i, proves the monoid-conductor assertion. QED.

No maximum is incorrectly required to occur simultaneously for every i:
the UFD tests each prime separately. Different coordinates may have
different hostile multiplication rows.

## C2. Prime packets and the composite correction

For m=p prime, each coordinate image is zero or all F_p. If t_i packets
have nonzero i-th coordinate image, then

    a_i=(t_i-1)(p-1).                                (6)

Two binary packets active in every coordinate give the norm-seven
conductor product u_i. Three ternary packets active in a coordinate give
exponent four. Repeated gluing increases conductor multiplicity even
though the elementary diagram remains the same.

For composite m, write the coordinate image in C_j as delta_(ij) Z/m,
where delta_(ij) divides m, with delta=m for zero image. Its largest
representative is m-delta_(ij), so the exact replacement is

    a_i=sum_j(m-delta_(ij))-m+1.                     (7)

Over Z/4 the subgroups generated by (1,2) and (0,1) are complementary.
Their conductor exponents are (0,2), not (0,3). At character (0,2) the
actual coefficient needs no b_2 factor; pretending every nonzero
coordinate map is surjective demands one. The missing information is
the coordinate-image index delta, not rank or support.

This is the same *kind of information loss* exposed by the inherited
integral wraparound Smith calculation: reducing before retaining indices
loses exceptional behavior. It is not an identification of the operators,
nor a claim about root-surface normalization in characteristic p.

## C3. A sixfold root recovers the ordinary cusp

Let a,b>=2 be coprime and m=ab. In Z/m use C=<a> and D=<b>, of orders b
and a, with trivial intersection and full sum. In the universal model,

    A=k[t],   B=k[t^a,t^b],
    conductor exponent=(m-a)+(m-b)-m+1
                      =ab-a-b+1=(a-1)(b-1).         (8)

For m=6, C={0,2,4}, D={0,3}, and B=k[t^2,t^3]. The normalizing parameter
t is omitted at the cusp; the conductor is t^2 k[t]. Thus the gluing of
degree-two and degree-three root covers recovers this classical plane
singularity. This is an exact structural six-related connection.

The additive semigroup <a,b> has exactly (a-1)(b-1)/2 gaps. In each
residue modulo b, its smallest element is ka for the unique 0<=k<b with
that residue. The number missing in this residue is floor(ka/b). Since
the remainders ka mod b permute 0,...,b-1,

    sum_(k=0)^(b-1) floor(ka/b)=(a-1)(b-1)/2.         (9)

Thus (8) is twice dim_k(k[t]/k[t^a,t^b]), the delta invariant of this
plane branch. This is a singularity invariant; the normalized curve has
genus zero.

A direct topological map supplies the torus-knot connection. Over C the
branch parametrization is (x,y)=(t^a,t^b). Intersect it with the small
sphere |x|^2+|y|^2=epsilon^2. Its |t|=r is constant since r^(2a)+r^(2b)
is strictly increasing. The link is therefore

    theta -> (r^a exp(i a theta), r^b exp(i b theta)),

literally the (a,b) torus knot on that product torus. Coprimality makes
this parametrization injective modulo 2pi. It preserves the two winding
numbers and the actual branch. We do not infer knot type from Hamiltonian
path counts or invoke a theorem about minimal Seifert genus.

## C4. Exact checks and remaining boundary

The checker covers all complementary pairs of projective lines in F_p^2
for p=2,3,5,7; all 234 unordered independent triples of projective lines
in F_3^3; a dimension-two plus dimension-one example over F_5; every
complementary pair of free cyclic lines in (Z/m)^2 for m=4,6,8 (12,36,48
pairs); the nonfree C2 plus C3 decomposition of Z/6; and all coprime
a<b in {2,...,12} for the numerical-semigroup consequence. The proof,
not the finite universe, supports all m and n.

Normality after additive specialization and the residual unit classes at
a rational boundary point remain separate obligations. Formula (7)
controls missing integral monomials; it does not manufacture ground-field
roots or erase valuations. The next extension is to nonprincipal
coefficient ideals on normal bases and specified arithmetic specializations,
not another numerical test of the now proved cyclic carry rule.
