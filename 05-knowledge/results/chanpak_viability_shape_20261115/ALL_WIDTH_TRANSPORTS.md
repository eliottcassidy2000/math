# Exact all-width displacement generating function

This classifies the **global suffix transports** of the fixed two-tape
carrier, in the sense of [the unit-transport note](SUFFIX_CHART_SWITCHES.md).
It enumerates physical choices of paired suffix addresses and their
exact signed displacements. A displacement is not an absolute count.

Let Q=5760,T=4034,S=3914. Width d>0 must be a multiple d=12k by the
previous coefficient conditions. Put A=2^d, n=d−7. Their tests are
`128V+rev_d(U)=C`, `128U+rev_d(V)=D`, where C=(A−1)4079/45
and D=(A−1)3959/45. Taking modulo 128 fixes the top seven bits of
U and V as the reversals of C mod 128 and D mod 128, respectively.
These are 87 and 89, independent of k (C mod 128=117, D mod 128=77).
Strip those top bits and call the n-bit remainders u and v. Then

```
v + rev_n(u) = C' = (74·2^n−73)/45
u + rev_n(v) = D' = (44·2^n−58)/45.
```

## The ternary word is unique

Pair the j-th bit of v from the low end with the (n−1−j)-th bit of
u. Their sum e_j is 0,1,2. The preceding two equations say that
the ordinary and reversed binary moments of e are C' and D'.
If two such words existed, their difference polynomial would vanish
at 2 and 1/2 and have integer coefficients in [−2,2]. Its highest
nonzero coefficient would be even, by pruning high zeros and reversing
the polynomial at 1/2. But its magnitude 2 would strictly dominate
all lower coefficients at 2, contradiction. There is also an explicit
decoder: C' gives the parity of the first low digit; if even, e_0
is 2 iff D'≥2^n, since the lower reversed tail is less than 2^n.
Then subtract that digit and shrink both moments.

In this carrier the unique word from low to high is
`P^(k−1) R`, with P=110222011212 and R=11022. The ordinary
and reversed moments of P are (6643,4004), of R (51,30).
Prepending one low tile P transforms the ordinary moment c to
4096c+6643 and the reversed moment b to b+4004·2^n. This proves
the displayed moment formulas by induction from k=1. Each 1 has
two independent assignments (v_j,u_(n−1−j))=(0,1),(1,0);
0 and 2 force the two bits. Hence there are exactly
4·32^(k−1) global transports at width 12k, and their order
enumerator is 4 t^12/(1−32 t^12).

## The exact directed displacement spectrum

Use X for the displacement generating variable. The polynomial
of choices, counting multiplicities, is the Laurent polynomial

```
D_k(X) = X^[16(k−2)] (1+X^(−32))^(2k−1)
                        (1+X^50)^(2k−1) (1+X^(−68))^(k−1).
```

Indeed the signed shift is Δ=SV−TU+45(p(V)−p(U)). From the exact
two-bit formula
`p(W)=sum_(i<j) omega_(j−i) W_j(1−W_i)`,
where omega_1=2 and omega_d=2^(d−1) otherwise, the interaction
between two free positions in p(V) cancels the mirrored
interaction in p(U): both are the same high bit times the
complement of the low bit. Thus Δ is affine in the free choices.

For a direct derivation of each coefficient, set A_0=73, B_0=58,
and read e forward for A and backward for B. The carries update
by c'=(c+45e)/2. At a free position j (low indexed) write
i=n−1−j. For j>0 and i>0 the increment when the bit goes to V
instead of U is

```
A_(j+1) + B_(i+1) − 45(e_(j−1)+e_(j+1)).
```

To see this, toggling position r of a d-bit word W from 0 to 1
changes p by
`2^r−1−rev_r(W mod 2^r)−floor(W/2^(r+1))
 + [r>0](1−W_(r−1))−W_(r+1)`,
with an out-of-range higher bit taken zero. Add this derivative
for V at j and U at i, since increasing v_j decreases u_i
and Δ uses a minus sign on p(U). Replacing each mirrored digit
pair by e, the large prefix terms cancel with S·2^j+T·2^i.
The remaining two rational expressions are exactly the
indicated A and B values by the formulas for C',D'.
At j=0 replace the neighbor deduction by 90+45e_1,
because U_n=1 and V lacks the lower neighbor. The last digit
has e=2, so i=0 is not free.

A starts at 73 and returns to it through each P. B reaches
44 from 58 through R read backward, and then returns to 44
through each reversed P. Using the displayed recurrence, the
five free digits of each P (positions 0,1,7,8,10) have increments
(−32,50,50,−32,−68), including at the exterior boundary; the
two free digits of R have (−32,50). Finally, concatenate k
copies of the known width-12 move (U,V)=(2811,2872), loss −16.
The corresponding V bits activate only position 8 in each P
and no free digit in R. Consequently the all-zero free-choice
baseline is −16k+32(k−1)=16(k−2). This proves the factorization.

## A complete bounded Frobenius support

Subtract the least displacement −116k+68 and divide by 2.
The support of the resulting polynomial is exactly the
bounded sumset with weights 16,25,34 and capacities
2k−1,2k−1,k−1 respectively. Its maximum is H_k=116k−75.
For every k≥9 it consists of:
- the unbounded semigroup generated by 16,25,34 on [0,247];
- every integer in [248,H_k−248];
- the reflection of the lower fringe about H_k/2.

For the central interval, verify k=9 by finite set addition.
Adding another period allows capacity increase (2,2,1),
so the next support includes the old support and its shift by
116. Their central intervals overlap and give the new one.
For the lower fringe no bound can affect the answer after k=9
since sums at most 247 use at most 15,9,7 of the respective
coins. Reflection gives the upper fringe. The Apéry minima
by residue modulo 16 are certified by the replay: 247 is a
gap, all x≥248 are in the unbounded semigroup, and the lower
fringe has 124 gaps. In particular there are exactly
116k−322 distinct signed displacements for k≥9.

The consecutive *shift* support is not a consecutive band at
one absolute location: changing a free choice generally moves
both transported addresses and their baseline. The result
identifies the complete exact local alphabet a successful
coarse selector may try to use; it is not a local-to-global
argument.

## All-width magnitude check

Alternatively the matched coefficients give directly
`Δ=((S+45)V−(T+45)U)/A+45(e_V−e_U)`,
where e_W=p(W)−W(1−rev_d(W)/A) has magnitude ≤d+1. Thus a
matched return of any allowed width changes the count by
O(d), never by an exponential amount, despite exponential
typical absolute centers. The explicit support sharpens this
qualitative bound.

Run `python replay_transport_spectrum.py` (also `-O`) for
the recurrence controls and exact finite support/Apéry certificates.

## Primitive transports and unique factorization

A factorization at width 12j, 1<=j<k, first *consumes* V's bottom
12j bits and then *writes* U's bottom 12(k−j) bits. In the j-th
P tile (counting V bits from the low end), the seven final positions
of that tile are positions 5 through 11. Their e-pattern is
2011212. To be the top seven bits of the first V block they must
equal 89, whose low-to-high pattern is 1001101. This forces the
three **V-ownership flags at positions 7,8,10** to be (0,1,0).
The same condition forces the complementary mirrored U bits to
be 1010111 read high-to-low, which is 87, the correct head of the
second U block. The remaining bits respectively see the two
correct ternary moment words, P^(j−1)R and P^(k−j−1)R; therefore
this condition is sufficient, not only a first-prefix test.
It is necessary from either V's first consumed suffix or U's last
written suffix. In particular primitive source V words are
suffix-free, and primitive destination U words are also suffix-free.

All markers identify factor boundaries, independently. Cutting
at all markers gives unique primitive factorization. For example,
the 36-bit unit return in the companion note is one of these
primitive returns. Each interior P of a primitive return has
4 choices of its first two ownership flags and 7 choices of
its last triple; the last R still has 4 choices. Thus
**exactly 4·28^(k−1) returns of width 12k are primitive**.

An exact primitive displacement polynomial is obtained from
the all-width factorization above by using the following factor
for each P instead of the five independent flags:

```
(1+X^(−32))(1+X^50) * [(1+X^50)(1+X^(−32))(1+X^(−68))−X^(−32)].
```

The subtracted term is the single forbidden marker assignment
(0,1,0) among the final three flags. This also makes the general
width-and-shift enumerators particularly simple. With t marking a
12-bit unit, put H=X^(−16)(1+X^(−32))(1+X^50) for the final R factor,
and J=X^16(1+X^(−32))²(1+X^50)²(1+X^(−68)) for the P factor.
Then the ordered return series (excluding the empty return) is
`T(t,X)=Ht/(1−Jt)`, and its primitive series is
`P(t,X)=Ht/(1−(J−H)t)`. Equivalently T=P/(1−P), as unique
factorization predicts. Specializing X=1 gives T=4t/(1−32t),
P=4t/(1−28t). These are generating functions of exact movement
choices; their coefficients do not count distinct absolute baselines.
