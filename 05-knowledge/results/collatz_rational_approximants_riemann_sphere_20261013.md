## 1. The transfer function as a rational function on P^1 x P^1

The physical transfer function is the restriction to the transcendental curve `Gamma_theta: s -> (x(s), y(s))`. For `Re s = 0` the curve lies on the torus `|x| = |y| = 1/2`, winds densely (`theta` irrational), and meets the polar line only at `(1/2, 1/2)`, the single point of the line on the torus, which it reaches exactly only for the trivial character at `s = 0`. ## 2. Rational approximants and their exact poles

`R_(P/Q)(w) = w^(P-Q) / p(w)`, `p(w) = w^(P-Q) - (1/2) zeta^alpha w^P - (1/2) zeta^(alpha+beta)`,
of degree `P` on the Riemann sphere, with poles at the `P` roots of `p`. A root `w` is a mode with `s = -Q log w`: frequency `f = -Q arg(w)/(2 pi)` modulo `Q` and decay `|w|^(-Q)` per octave.

## 4. The constant function infinity

## 5. The Moebius dictionary

A parity word with `b` odd steps and `a` halvings (`P = a + b` steps, `Q = b`) is the affine map `T_w(z) = (3^b z + c_w)/2^P`, an element of `PGL(2, Q)` fixing `infinity`; `infinity` is the common fixed point of the whole semigroup `<z/2, (3z+1)/2>`, repelling for halving (multiplier `2` at `infinity`) and attracting for the odd step (`2/3`). The word's multiplier at `infinity` is `2^P/3^Q` and at its finite fixed point `x_w = c_w/(2^P - 3^Q)` it is `lambda_w = 3^Q/2^P`, with `ln lambda_w = (Q theta - P) ln 2 = m theta_f ln 2`. The near-returns are exactly the nearly parabolic words (`lambda_w ~ 1`): the parabolicity defects are `5.2e-2` for `(5,8)`, `1.36e-2` for `(12,19)`, `2.09e-3` for `(53,84)`, `1.02e-3` for `(306,485)`, `4.4e-5` for `(665,1054)`, and THM-4629's decay per octave of the corresponding line is `exp(-4 pi^2 theta_f^2/(2 - theta)^3) = exp(-4 pi^2 (ln lambda_w)^2/(m^2 (ln 2)^2 (2 - theta)^3))`, a function of the multiplier alone. No word is exactly parabolic (`2^P != 3^Q`), which is why no line is exactly undamped. The finite fixed points `x_w` are the rational cycle points of the repository's shadow theorems (THM-4600, whose `c_w = B_w/(2^(sum w) - 3^|w|)` is the rational cycle point of a word, and the S6-S8 shadow notes `collatz_refinement_floor_shadows_20261005.md`, `collatz_weak_reset_family_20261005.md`: `x_w = c_w/(2^A - 3^l)`; the first draft cited THM-4514, which has no such object: audit); along the near-returns `2^P - 3^Q` is small relative to `2^P` and the fixed points move out towards `infinity`: the fixed-point map `w -> x_w` tends to the constant `infinity` exactly along the words that resonate. Divergent trajectories, if any, are the orbits attracted to the common fixed point `infinity` of the forward semigroup.
