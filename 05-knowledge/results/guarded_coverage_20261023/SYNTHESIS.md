# What these advances may be pointing toward

23 October 2026. This file separates proved mechanisms from research guesses.
It proposes a direction, not a claimed new foundation or a proof of a prize
problem. The substantive proved instances are in CUBES.md, PORTS.md and LIFTING.md.

## Inheritance and concept board

The anchor is guarded coverage and native cross-orbit transport. The niche
is the exact frozen part of heterogeneous matching. The wildcard is whether
a constant amount of genuinely new workspace turns large simultaneous
obstructions into sequential legal moves.

Closest mechanisms: Session44 endpoint-fixed partner maps; Session25's
single-junction correction to substitution; the paid carry family's two
full kernel coordinates. Canonical hostile: a fully occupied alternating
port cycle. Corrected near miss: phase equality or chart connectivity being
mistaken for compatible actual resources or complete coverage. Least-used
sidecar: the maximal tight Hall set, together with the actual conditional
source measure at a chart boundary.

## The broad guess: mathematical equivalence should remember continuations

My strongest guess is that many recurring obstructions here come from using
an equivalence relation appropriate for isolated objects on objects that
will later be composed. An isomorphism class, scalar charge or kernel residue
may be adequate until an external context asks which actual endpoint, port,
orientation, source weight or time is available.

A useful replacement is an object with a **continuation interface**: what
external contexts can attach, which actual states extend, what measure they
carry, and what it costs to realize an extension. Two objects are equivalent
for a problem only when this specified interface agrees. This resembles
contextual equivalence, bisimulation and boundary relations; those are
established ideas, not inventions of this packet. The research proposal is
to construct small *quantitative* interfaces for our actual arithmetic and
geometric objects, and prove their composition laws.

The strongest proved model here is four-layer stabilization: a large
simultaneous obstruction becomes sequential after a constant16-owner source
extension. The obstruction did not disappear; its cost moved from concurrent
support into additional resources and integer time. This suggests looking
for a three-way accounting law among boundary information, available
workspace and legal time, rather than a single scalar complexity invariant.
There is no established universal law asserted by that sentence.

## Three precise next hypotheses or targets

**OPEN — one-bit native completion conjecture.** For every r, every collection
of pin-defined cube faces E_i and every two injective assignments a,b,
the single-layer extension E_i x {0,1} connects(a,0) to(b,0) by legal
one-bit, one-slot moves. Two auxiliary bits suffice by PORTS.md. One bit
suffices for the Gray-cycle obstruction. It is not proved in general here.
The cheapest hostile is a collection of intersecting narrow faces whose
necessary horizontal routes all meet the temporarily parked slot.

**OPEN — bounded native reservoir target.** Find a broad class of original
guarded sources in which the four-layer inventory embeds inside the same
source, with endpoint transports and background disjointness authenticated.
An abstract copy or a weight multiplicity is not an embedding. If successful,
the stabilization theorem would become an internal controller rather than
a theorem about enlarged sources. The first test should be a small full
pointed fibre with explicit source, inverse and converse port maps.

**CONDITIONAL — quantitative continuation theorem.** Full coverage, balanced
cross-chart couplings and bounded native congestion imply a global spectral
estimate by LIFTING.md. The ambitious open step is a catalogue of local
interfaces for which these quantities stay polynomial under composition.
The frozen Gray family rules out any version that omits resource slack;
singleton charts rule out coverage-to-movement shortcuts; vanishing source
weights rule out a dimension-only gap bound. Thus the conditional theorem
is explicit, while the hoped-for universal catalogue is not yet a theorem.

rows are design constraints and proposed tests, not asserted reductions
between famous problems. In particular no common appearance of a small
integer substitutes for an authenticated map or a preserved predicate.
