- [Proof reading guide and the exact F₁ prime-power interface](RH-F1-READING.md): formal-verification status, the window argument, the multiplicity/slope mechanism, and the inverse of Riesz smoothing. The inert weight is the trace of a virtual character along scaling-site monodromy, not a positivity theorem.
- [A point-count lift of ordinary tournament heights](STRIP-F1.md): at field size two it recovers the Hamilton-path count; at the absolute specialization it gives Fibonacci tilings and a different unimodular port. The specialization discards information and the lift is noncanonical.
- [Exact Farey trace and a failed uniform Weil bound](FAREY-SPECTRUM.md): the determinant-seven filter has a sharp square-root scale, but the same construction at prime 433 violates that scale even on closed-orbit traces.

All statements about tournaments and finite transition matrices are scoped to their displayed hosts. This is not a new proof of RH or an exact Chan–Pak selector.
