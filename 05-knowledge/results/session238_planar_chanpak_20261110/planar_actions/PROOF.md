## 1. Physical plane and its positive maps

Fix real x>0, put k=x/2, and let h=I(Omega(T),x), d=I(Omega(T-u),x).
Let R=E+O be the rooted odd/even path-complement sums of THM-5085.
After a sink, Delta=E-O=0. Holding the port and d fixed, the two sink-ending
blocks act on this plane as

    A(h,R) = (h,2R),
    C(h,R) = (h+kR,4R-2d).                         (1)

This is obtained by applying B and then S in the inherited formulas. All
positive words in A,C are actual tournament constructions. In particular no
assertion that B alone preserves this plane is made.

The single-vertex rooted core has (h,R,d)=(1,1,1); after a sink it is (1,2,1).
The directed triangle has h=1+x, d=1, R=3; after a sink it is (1+x,6,1).
For the latter R statement there are exactly three rooted directed paths:
the singleton, its unique outgoing arc, and its unique spanning directed path;
each complement has packing count1. These are valid physical seeds for every x.

The closed region R>=2d>0 is positively invariant. Both generators strictly
increase R: A changes it by R, C by 3R-2d. Consequently no nonempty positive
word can be a horizontal translation on this region, or return its R value.
Every sink-extended physical seed lies in this region: before the sink the
singleton rooted path contributes d to R, with all other terms nonnegative.

## 2. Exact group commutator and Hamiltonian realization

For fixed d>0 let G be the subgroup of affine bijections of the real plane
generated by A,C and their inverses. This is a group generated by the maps,
not an assertion about a universal group completion of an abstract monoid.
Define

    D = C A^(-2),       K = A D A^(-1) D^(-1).

Direct substitution gives

    D(h,R) = (h+kR/4, R-2d),
    K(h,R) = (h-kR/8-kd/4, R-2d).

Their commutator is the pure translation

    J = D K D^(-1) K^(-1),
    J(h,R) = (h-3kd/4, R) = (h-3xd/8,R).           (2)

Proof: for maps U(a,b,c):(h,R)->(h+aR+c,R+b),

    [U(a,b,c),U(a',b',c')](h,R)
       = (h+ab'-a'b,R).

Insert (a,b)=(k/4,-2d) and (a',b')=(-k/8,-2d).
This proves (2) uniformly, including all real x,d; the positive interpretation
above only uses x,d>0.

There is a literal Hamiltonian interpretation, with bracket
{f,g}=f_h g_R-f_R g_h and flow derivative {-,F}. D and K are the time-one
flows of the polynomial Hamiltonians

    F_D = k R^2/8 + kd R/4 + 2dh,
    F_K = -k R^2/16 - 3kd R/8 + 2dh.

Indeed R'=-2d; integration of h'=partial_R F gives the displayed D,K.
Their bracket is {F_D,F_K}= -3kd R/4-5kd^2/4; its R derivative is
-3kd/4. The exact commutator formula, rather than an unqualified exponential
commutator rule, establishes (2). J itself is the time-one flow of
F_J=-3kd R/4. A,C have determinants2 and4, respectively, and are not
Hamiltonian maps for dh wedge dR. Cancellation restores determinant1 in D,K.
This is the precise planar bridge; none of these maps is identified with the
October source-adic boundary-fixing group.

## 3. False attainability of both permanent gaps

At d=1, formula(2) implies

    J^(-8)(1,2) = (1+3x,2),
    J^(-24)(1+x,6) = (1+10x,6).                    (3)

Thus the rational affine group orbit from physical seeds contains integral
states with first coordinates7 and21 at x=2, and10 and31 at x=3.
The all-order exclusions are inherited from THM-1370,
`01-canon/theorems/THM-1370-h-spectrum-omits-7-21-all-n.md`, and THM-4940
(the ternary exclusions cited in THM-5085). They are not reproved here.
Equation(3) is an unconditional algebraic construction; the nonrealizability
interpretation uses those inherited exclusion theorems.

The smallest displayed final points need not pass the inherited physical
inequality h<=xO=xR/2 on the sink sheet. That objection can be repaired without
repairing realizability: J is horizontal and commutes with A. Start with three
additional A letters on the singleton seed and two on the triangle seed. Then

    J^(-8) A^3(1,2) = (1+3x,16),
    J^(-24) A^2(1+x,6) = (1+10x,24).

At x=2 and x=3 these obey h<=xR/2, as well as d=1, Delta=0, R>=2d,
positive integral h,R, and h=1 mod x. Thus even this inherited invariant-cone
inequality does not repair a group-orbit argument. They are not asserted to
satisfy every physical-state inequality.
From each corresponding sink-padded seed, no nonempty positive A,C word
produces its displayed translated point, already because R strictly increases.
From the original unpadded seeds the global gap exclusions still rule them out;
monotonicity alone does not, because the larger R values can be reached. More strongly, no tournament at all produces their first
coordinates in the two stated bases. Chan–Pak completeness remains OPEN.

## 4. A faithful binary clock for the positive semigroup

There is an exact normal form that distinguishes a positive construction from
the spurious group words. Give A weight1 and C weight2, the numbers of added
vertices. A chronological positive word of total weight n acts on R by

    R -> 2^n R - 2d m,                              (4)

where m is a nonnegative integer whose binary1 positions are the numbers of
vertices added *after* each C. Therefore m has no adjacent1 bits and its
highest1 position is at most n-2 (m=0 is allowed).

Conversely every pair (n,m) with these properties specifies exactly one word:
read from the right end, use C for each marked position and its following
position, and fill all other positions with A. Equivalently, writing exactly n bits from high to low, the two binary
codewords are A=0 and C=01; decode from the low end. Disjoint marked
pairs are exactly the no-adjacent1 condition. Thus the positive semigroup of
these affine maps is free on A,C whenever d!=0: its R slope recovers n, and
its intercept recovers m, hence the whole word. This does not claim freeness
of all original S/B maps on four coordinates.

The crude but useful bound m<=(2^n-1)/3 follows by maximizing separated powers
in positions0 through n-2. For the seed R=2d, (4) lies in

    d[(4/3)2^n+2/3, 2^(n+1)].                     (5)

These intervals are disjoint for different nonnegative n. Therefore the final
R value alone determines the entire positive word from R=2d. Decode n via(5),
then m=(2^(n+1)d-R)/(2d), then its binary positions. This supplies an exact
sidecar test in this small alphabet. It does not classify arbitrary tournament
states, arbitrary S/B words, or the port-chain alphabet of THM-5080.
