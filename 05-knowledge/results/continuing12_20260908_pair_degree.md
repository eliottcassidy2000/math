# The same pointed unit module permits unbounded rational pair degree

**Status: PROVED analytic corollary; independently refereed.** Root derived the claim and `incoming_synthesis` independently checked the field identities, polynomial degree, irreducibility argument and all-mates conclusion below. This report adds no computational engine or mathematical claim based on a finite sample. It uses the fixed-family identities already accepted in the [hidden-arms primary](continuing12_20260908_fixed_unit_hidden_arms.md) and its [independent audit](continuing12_20260908_hidden_arms_audit.md), without altering those frozen reports.

## Statement

Fix nonzero complex parameters `b,d,h` and an integer `n>=3`. On the original affine source of the same surface `W2`, put

\[
u=x-h,\quad z=u-2h+u^3t,\quad W=uz^2,\quad
K(W)=b+dW^n,\quad F=g=uzK(W).
\]

The supplier proves that `F` is a global submersion on `W2`, that the original-source rational constants of `D_F=F_x partial_t−F_t partial_x` are exactly `C(g)`, and that

\[
G=\frac{H_n(W)}{g^6},\qquad
H_n(T)=\sum_{m=0}^{5}\binom5m
\frac{d^m b^{5-m}T^{nm+3}}{nm+3}
\]

satisfies `D_F G=1`. Then

\[
\boxed{[\mathbb C(x,t):\mathbb C(F,G)]=5n+3.}
\]

More strongly, for every rational `G' in C(x,t)` with `D_F G'=lambda` a nonzero complex constant,

\[
\boxed{\mathbb C(F,G')=\mathbb C(F,G)
\quad\text{as embedded subfields of }\mathbb C(x,t).}
\]

Thus the field degree is intrinsic to `F` among rational constant-Jacobian mates for this fixed original-source bracket. It is not the degree of a polynomial Keller map.

## Proof of the degree

The actual rational inverse from the supplier is

\[
u=\frac{g^2}{W K(W)^2},\qquad
z=\frac{W K(W)}g,\qquad
x=u+h,\qquad t=\frac{z-u+2h}{u^3}.
\]

Consequently `C(x,t)=C(g,W)`. Since the left side has transcendence degree two and the right side is generated by two elements, `g` and `W` are algebraically independent. In particular, with `k=C(g)`, the source field is the one-variable rational function field `k(W)`.

The exponents `nm+3` strictly increase with `m`. Therefore

\[
\deg H_n=5n+3,\qquad
[T^{5n+3}]H_n(T)=\frac{d^5}{5n+3}\ne0.
\]

There is no leading-term cancellation. Put `D=5n+3`. For an independent variable `Y`, the polynomial `H_n(T)−Y` is irreducible in `k[T,Y]`: the quotient by its principal ideal is `k[T]`, an integral domain, so that ideal is prime. Its leading coefficient as a polynomial in `T` is the nonzero field element `d^5/D`, making it primitive over `k[Y]`. Gauss's lemma therefore gives irreducibility in `k(Y)[T]`, of degree `D` in `T`.

The element `H_n(W)` is transcendental over `k`. Substitution `Y -> H_n(W)` identifies `k(Y)` with `k(H_n(W))`, and the preceding irreducible polynomial becomes the minimal polynomial of `W`. Hence

\[
[k(W):k(H_n(W))]=D.
\]

Since `g^6` is a unit in `k`, one has `k(G)=k(H_n(W))`. This proves the displayed source-to-pair field degree. Possible composition of `H_n` does not lower this degree: it factors the degree through intermediate fields rather than changing the degree of the complete extension. Characteristic zero also makes the extension separable.

## Every rational mate gives the same field

If `D_F G'=lambda`, then

`D_F(G'−lambda G)=0`.

The paid constant-field theorem gives `G'−lambda G=R(g)` for some rational `R in C(g)`. Thus `G'=lambda G+R(g)`, and either mate is recovered rationally from the other together with `g`. Because `lambda!=0`, the two generated subfields are identical. This conclusion uses the actual rational constant field, not a generic-fibre primitive chosen separately on each fibre.

## Consequence and scope

At fixed `b,h`, the supplier proves that the pointed module `(D theta,theta)` generated by the original-source unit is independent of both `n>=3` and nonzero `d`. Its exact scalar order remains six and it generates two complete principal-part arms. Nevertheless its ambient torsion has `n+1` arms and the rational Hamiltonian pair degree is `5n+3`, unbounded. Therefore even **all** Weyl relations of the distinguished unit impose no finite bound on either of these two quantities within this fixed-surface family. No isomorphism of the ambient modules, their labelled components, or source maps is asserted.

The [earlier all-m power-map example](continuing10_20260908_dg_unbounded_torsion.md), Section 4, already retained field degree separately from its actual affine chart and volume: its field degree and scalar unit order both happened to equal `2m−1`. The present family separates those two numbers, with scalar order six and field degree `5n+3`. This is a concrete failure of any universal equality inferred from that earlier example, not a new claim about its correctly scoped theorem.

The supplied `G` has genuine original-source poles; the nonzero unit class also rules out a polynomial mate of any degree. The field extension computed here is the degree of the rational pair, or equivalently its generic algebraic degree on a common regular domain. This report supplies no finite morphism on all of the original affine source or on `W2`, no polynomial/global second coordinate, and no counterexample or solution to the Jacobian conjecture. The same pointed module here is compared only after identifying the formal target parameter `g` and fixing `b,h`.
