# An integral lattice for the adaptive clone-excess recurrence

2026-10-25. **PROVED:** a state-determined valuation gauge turns every
adaptive moment transition into an integer multiplication and a
nonnegative binary shift. It preserves an explicit product lattice at
every stage, including exact signed clone excess and sparse/dense merges.
The moment state graph and rank are unchanged. **FINITE-EXACT:** the
accompanying checker records its tested bounds. No timing, bit-complexity,
positivity, or new unrestricted counting-state reduction is claimed.

## 1. Inheritance and the obstruction

The closest mechanisms are the
[degree-local odd-factorial transfer](../../degree_local_tournament_enumeration_20261021/session22-enumeration/README.md)
and the
[finite-horizon adaptive moment quotient](../../unprotected_ports_cancellation_20261025/counting/README.md).
The former made scalar response transfers integral; the latter reduced
the number of scalar weight coordinates, but its rational moment formula
could have negative powers of two. The canonical hostile is a genuine
prefix with a past 5-cycle, for which adding a 3-cycle reads half of a
moment. Simply scaling moments by the odd factorial does not make the
displayed transfer integral on arbitrary integer vectors.

The live board is committed degree / odd factorials / defect bounds /
finite moments / exact clone excess. The least-used relevant sidecar is
the elementary inequality gcd(c,d)>=1. It gives a lower bound on every
past cycle count from the already stored defect coordinates. Using that
bound as a valuation gauge repairs the operation itself.

The exact clone semantics are inherited from the
[clone-cut involution](../../ports_regular_actions_hn_20261025/counting/CLONE_INVOLUTION.md):
weighted quotient inflation is resolved into automorphism orbits of cuts
inside the canonical maximal transitive modules of the literal inflated
tournament. This packet changes coordinates in that same signed series;
it does not replace or approximate its clone mergers.

## 2. Coordinates and a lower bound already in the state

Fix total degree N. Immediately before processing odd length ell, let u
be the committed degree, including excess, and S=N-u. For a prefix with
m actual cycles define

    f(d)=sum_(past actual c) gcd(c,d),
    D(d)=d*m-f(d).

Retain the affordable odd coordinates d<=min(ell,S). In particular D(1)=0
whenever S>0. Since 1<=gcd(c,d)<=d, every reachable prefix satisfies

    0 <= D(d) <= (d-1)*m.                            (I1)

Define the state-determined nonnegative integer

    b(D)=max_{retained odd d>=3} ceil(D(d)/(d-1)),     (I2)

with b=0 when this set is empty. Thus m>=b for every prefix in the fibre.
No cycle-count coordinate is added to the dense state key.

Let O(u)=u!/2^v2(u!), with O(0)=1. Sparse storage uses

    x_m=O(u)*w_m.

Dense storage, for 1<=r<=S, uses

    Y_r=O(u)*2^(-r*b)*W_r
       =sum_m x_m * 2^[r*(m-b)],                    (I3)

where W_r=sum_m w_m*2^(r*m) is the previous moment coordinate. Terminal
S=0 has b=0 and the single coordinate Y_0=O(u)*W_0.

The original degree-local theorem makes every sparse x_m integral.
Equation (I1) then makes sparse-to-dense conversion integral, using only
left shifts. More strongly, the dense transition preserves the entire
product lattice

    Lambda_(u,D,S) = diag(2^(r*b)/O(u)) * Z^S        (I4)

in W coordinates, and O(u)^(-1)*Z at S=0. This is larger than the actual
moment lattice generated by realizable prefixes. The stronger statement
means dense arithmetic needs neither cycle-count reconstruction nor a
lattice-membership oracle.

## 3. Exact integral transfer

At odd length ell choose k actual cycles and e excess units, where
ell*(k+e)<=S. For k=0 only e=0 is allowed. Put

    beta(k,e)=(-1)^e binom(k+e-1,e) for k>0,
    beta(0,0)=1,
    A_ell(k)=k*(ell-1)/2+ell*binom(k,2),
    u'=u+ell*(k+e),       S'=N-u',
    D'(d)=D(d)+k*(d-gcd(ell,d)),
    v=v2(k!),            b'=b(D').

Let

    T_(u,ell,k,e) = beta(k,e)*O(u') /
                    [O(u)*ell^k*O(k)].              (I5)

The division in (I5) is exact before multiplying by beta. Indeed

    u'!/[u!*ell^k*k!]
      = binom(u',ell*k) * [(ell*k)!/(ell^k*k!)]
        * (u'-ell*k)!/u!

is an integer; its middle factor counts permutations with k cycles of
length ell. Taking odd parts proves that the denominator in (I5) divides
O(u'). This proof includes excess, k=0, and u=0.

For destination channel r=1,...,S', or r=0 when S'=0, the transfer is

    Y'_r += T_(u,ell,k,e) * 2^J * Y_(ell*k+r),      (I6)

    J = A_ell(k)-v
        + k*(ell*b-D(ell))
        + r*(k+b-b').                               (I7)

If ell>S then k=e=0 and D(ell) is not read. The formula is the identity
on the retained channels in that case. If S=0 the only source channel
is r=0. At ell=1 force k+e=S, as in the inherited recurrence.

**Theorem.** Every exponent J in (I7) is a nonnegative integer. Thus
(I6) preserves (I4) by a matrix with integer entries. Its only weight
divisions are the reusable, degree-dependent precomputations (I5), together with
the final exact removal of O(N); no state-dependent rational division
or right shift is required.

**Proof.** First v2(k!)<=binom(k,2), including k=0,1, while
A_ell(k)>=binom(k,2). Next, if ell>=3 and k>0, (I2) gives
D(ell)<=(ell-1)*b and hence ell*b-D(ell)>=b>=0. At ell=1 this term is
zero because b=0 and D(1)=0. If k=0 its whole contribution is zero.

For every retained destination d>=3,

    D'(d) <= (d-1)*b + k*(d-1)
           = (d-1)*(b+k),

since gcd(ell,d)>=1. Therefore

    b' <= b+k.                                      (I8)

This remains true when coordinates disappear because the remaining
degree shrinks: deleting coordinates can only weaken the maximum in
(I2). All three summands in (I7) are nonnegative. Finally substituting
W_a=2^(a*b)Y_a/O(u), a=ell*k+r, into the old rational moment transfer
and using k!=2^v*O(k) gives (I5)--(I7) exactly. QED.

In sparse storage the same batch adds

    x'_(m+k) += T_(u,ell,k,e)
                * 2^[A_ell(k)-v+k*(ell*m-D(ell))] * x_m. (I9)

The shift is nonnegative by the same argument. Converting a sparse
contribution to the destination dense gauge uses b' and (I3). Because
u' and b' are functions of the destination key and stage, all incoming
contributions use identical coordinates. Addition therefore commutes
with both sparse-to-dense conversion and merging.

## 4. Adaptive rank and clone information remain exact

Use sparse x_m while h<=S, where h is its support size; convert to the S
coordinates (I3) once h>S. Keep a dense state dense afterward. The old
proof still applies: sign at fixed u,m is (-1)^(u-m), so equal-m prefix
terms cannot cancel and every nonzero transition injectively shifts
support by m->m+k. Once h>S, every destination still has h>S>=S'.
The positive factors O(u) and 2^(-r*b) do not change that reasoning.

The rank is still min(S,h), since (I3) is an invertible rational diagonal
change of the prior moment coordinates. The adaptive scalar-coordinate
bound relative to the old f-state engine is therefore unchanged. The
gain is arithmetic: those same coordinates and transitions now live in
an explicit integer lattice. No new rank or state-count improvement is
claimed.

Retain even all-zero nonterminal dense vectors if using the inherited
semantic-support proof literally. A signed bank can have zero first S
moments and a nonzero later moment. The quotient only moves to decreasing
S. It cannot be reused at an increased horizon.

For ordinary A000568, set e=0. Only the future actual-degree channel r=S
is observable. The one-channel normalization is

    Y=O(u)*2^[S*(m-b)]*w,

summed over the fibre. It obeys (I6) with r=S'. This integrates the old
ordinary scalar defect quotient and its integer arithmetic, without
changing that quotient's graph. At the final boundary divide the integer
terminal coordinate by O(N), checking exact divisibility. The inherited
Burnside/core semantics identifies the quotient with the appropriate
integer number of isomorphism classes.

## 5. Hostile boundaries and the actual consequence

1. **Odd-factorial scaling alone is insufficient as a product lattice.**
   A prefix consisting of one 5-cycle has u=5, m=1, D(3)=2, and scaled
   sparse weight x_1=O(5)*4/5=12. At ell=3, k=1, e=0, S=3, the old
   scaled dense formula reads 7*X_3/2. It is exact on X_3=96, but sends
   the arbitrary integer X_3=1 to 7/2. The new b=1 gauge reads
   7*2^2*Y_3, with Y_3=12, giving the same integer336.

2. **Negative exponents occur after genuine adaptive compression too.**
   Process k=1,...,4 copies of a 9-cycle, with 4-k excess units there,
   and then one 5-cycle. All four prefixes have committed degree41 and
   D(3)=2; their actual cycle counts are2,...,5. At S=3 they form a
   legitimate dense fibre with h=4>S. Its 3-cycle completion has old
   exponent -1 and new exponent2. The checker constructs the exact
   signed prefix coefficients, rather than inserting an arbitrary bank.

3. **The valuation gauge does not imply positivity.** beta(k,e) can be
   negative, and so can normalized sparse or dense coordinates. This is
   a recurrence over Z, not over the nonnegative integers. The clone-cut
   involution and the separate positive cut-orbit polynomial are semantic
   statements; neither is used to replace the negative coefficients here.

4. **Global denominator removal is a different statement.** Merely
   observing an integer final count would not prove (I6). Here each
   transition matrix is integral on the full lattice (I4), and conversion
   and merging preserve that lattice before termination.

5. **More information can strengthen b, but is unnecessary.** The bound
   (I2) may be smaller than the minimum realizable m in the fibre. The
   41-degree hostile above has b=1 although every displayed m>=2. We
   claim a sufficient state-determined gauge, not the optimal valuation
   of every reachable moment or a complete description of its congruences.

Source: decorated odd-cycle prefixes and their signed weights. Target:
the same adaptive (S,D) keys, with integral sparse weights or integral
valuation-normalized moments. Preserved: every individual decorated
future's aggregate, exact clone excess, all stagewise mergers, and the
final ordinary/core count. Lost: individual cycle histories in a dense
bank, individual tournament objects, ports, and automorphism identities.
Sidecar: N, stage ell, O(u), the computable b(D), and the decreasing
continuation horizon. This is an operation-preserving coordinate change,
not an identification of extra individual histories.
