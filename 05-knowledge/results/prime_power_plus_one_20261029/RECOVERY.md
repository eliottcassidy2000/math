## Inheritance pass and concept board

Anchor: prime-power recursive tournament growth and exact attachment
counts. Niche: local neighborhoods of doubly regular tournaments and
their vertex-deletion margin. Wildcard: projective norm clocks and the
integral root-lattice operator. LRC(14) remains the wider open anchor;
none of these unweighted counts supplies its missing weighted bound.

The closest proved mechanism is
[regular-parent R1--R5](../regular_parent_symmetry_20261029/PROOFS.md):
the adjoined vertex is fixed by the full group, clone equivalences
correct unmarked counts, and complementary parity cuts remove symmetry
recursively. The canonical hostile is the same-cyclic-triples81/27 pair.
It remains live here, with different regular-mask counts1620/1728.

The corrected near miss is unconditional preservation of a forbidden
prime tournament under a new attachment: the earlier rigid10-child
creates R5. The repaired statement here keeps the deletion margin and,
for exact radii, the actual forbidden prime isotypes. Another hostile
is the non-distinguishing pair {0,1} in Paley27: Frobenius survives.

The least-used relevant sidecar is the integral Gram matrix I+J of the
root lattice. Its determinant q is coprime to the norm(q+1)/4, which
is exactly why the complementary finite groups pair perfectly.

The live board was: (1) ordered modular/LCA tree, (2) deletion margin
and prime obstruction type, (3) parity-color character, (4) Frobenius
and reciprocal phase, (5) integral Gram form, (6) positive weight on
the actual target. Every numerical coincidence was tested against
these coordinates. The main stopping boundary is coordinate6:
finite-group structure or generic symmetry rarity does not exclude an
exceptional arithmetic source.

  Our doubled15 control imports this known setting and verifies its

  [The simplicity index of tournaments](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/download/72680/56444),
  especially its introduction and Theorem1.2. Its simplicity index

  extremizers and treats arc-simplicity after up to three vertex

  a subtournament decomposable. Similar numerical thresholds do not
  identify these statements. No exhaustive priority search is claimed.

  A3 proves the exact signed determinant covariance being used.
  The full automorphism classifications of Hadamard matrices involve
  different row/column actions and exceptional cases; we do not import
