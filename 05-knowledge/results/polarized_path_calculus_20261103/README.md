## Why this helps the ternary problem

For an actual parent rooted at a vertex with b starting Hamilton paths,
an indegree-one birth marked at the newborn has F-H=b. The inherited
two-birth reset therefore produces 3H+2b. The b=1,2 source conditions
behind the desired 3H+2 and 3H+4 lanes are now structural: a one-entry
recursive core, or a two-start budget requiring either one entry of weight
two or two entries of weight one. The latter exposes a genuine joint
two-port polynomial in the compiler. The full target selector is still
missing; the grammar does not assert that every required H has both types.

The finite receipt includes an actual H31 child with F=32 from a parent
of start budget one. Its reset produces 95. An H31,F=33 control has
indegree two and profile (h,s,q,F)=(31,5,5,33), so its 97 reset uses a
different structural entry. Small-start parents give sufficient ternary
sources, not a classification of every F-H=2 source. These illustrate the
input conditions, not a new repair of the old 31 branching obstruction.

## Connection contracts

* **Natural recursion / Fibonacci:** delete a uniquely starting root;
  retain its first strong core, core entry and binary tail word. This is an
  invertible rooted-isomorphism decomposition, hence an exact enumeration.
  It retains more than the total path count.
* **Reflection / quadratic forms:** the diagonal sandwich is polarized to
  two actual sources. Its H pairing is a Lorentz-signature bilinear form
  after an integral coordinate change. This supplies a literal operation
  behind the older Pythagorean/Berggren inspiration; no Berggren matrix is
  asserted to preserve the realizable source cone.
* **Automata / FRACTRAN:** the tail coefficient has a six-state weighted
  word automaton. It compiles an arbitrary finite binary attachment word;
  it is not the actual tournament or a universal finite-state H carrier.
* **Q4 / F49:** extract the inherited integer observer from three polynomial
  evaluations, then apply its known transport. Bin shifts and tail insertion
  are different operations from field Frobenius.

to classify a forced starting path, rather than to identify unrelated
