## 1. Inherited definitions and the stronger question

Let W be a self-converse tournament, A=Aut(W), and I its anti-involutions.
Fix t0 in I. The group A has odd order. Put

    C=C_A(t0),   K={x in A : t0 x t0=x^(-1)},
    g(t,s)=sqrt(ts),   b(s)=g(s,t0),
    c(t,s)=b(t)^(-1) g(t,s) b(s) in C.

Products act right to left. Square roots are the unique odd-order roots.
The inherited result proves A=KC uniquely as a set factorization and that
pair-local transport can have nontrivial triangular error. The new question
is which part of C is actually generated by **all** such errors.

Define L=<K>. Define H as the subgroup of C generated by all c(t,s).
Equivalently H is the full holonomy at t0 of the complete marking graph
with edge s->t labelled g(t,s): the based triangle t0->s->t->t0 has
product c(t,s), and arbitrary loops factor into these based triangles.

**Theorem.**

    H = C intersect L.

Every g(t,s) is in L: ts=(t t0)(s t0)^(-1), both factors lie in K,
and its odd square root is a power of ts. Thus H is contained in C intersect L.

For the reverse inclusion, L acts transitively on I, with representatives
b(s) in K. These representatives cover every K element, since squaring
permutes K. Take a=b(t) in K and any s in I, and set v=a s a^(-1).
Naturality of square roots gives

    g(v,t)=a b(s) a^(-1),
    c(v,t)=b(v)^(-1) a b(s).

The second expression is precisely a Schreier stabilizer generator for the
action of L on I, using generators K and representatives b(s). To avoid
depending on terminology, write any element of L as a word in K. Insert
b(s_i)b(s_i)^(-1) at its successive conjugated marks s_i. If the word
returns to t0, the outer representatives cancel and the word is a product
of the displayed c(v,t). Hence C intersect L is contained in H. QED.

This strengthens a nonidentity witness into an exact group description.
It also sharpens the flatness test: H=1 iff K is abelian, by the inherited
flatness theorem. A nontrivial C alone need not produce holonomy; Paley7
has L=C7, C=C3 and H=1.

## 2. Full centralizer holonomy at every ternary depth

Let W_k be the tournament on length-k ternary strings, oriented by the
first differing digit around0->1->2->0. Let t_k negate every digit.
The inherited intrinsic-module proof gives

    A_k=A_(k-1)^3 semidirect C3,
    C_k is isomorphic to C_(k-1) times A_(k-1),
    |C_k|=3^((3^k-2k-1)/4).

**Theorem.** The square-root marking holonomy is exactly C_k for every k.

In fact K_k generates A_k. Take the k rotations which add1 to the next
digit inside each all-zero-prefix subtree (prefix lengths0,...,k-1).
Negation conjugates every such rotation to its inverse, so all lie in K_k.
Inductively, those below the root generate the full child group on block0.
The root rotation conjugates it onto blocks1 and2, generating the full
wreath product. Thus L_k=A_k; the preceding theorem gives H_k=C_k.

At order9 this recovers H=C3, and at order27 it gives the **nonabelian**
group C3 times (C3 wr C3), of order243. The previous embedded order9
witness established only that some error survives at every depth. This
result identifies every possible centralizer correction as a product of
actual marking-loop transports.

It does not assert every correction occurs on a single triangle. The following
construction does give a uniform scale-dependent length bound.

## 2A. A constructive loop bound with the transport clock charged

**Subsequent refinement:** the bound below remains valid. The
[linear repair construction](../crt_weighted_ports_20261028/LINEAR_REPAIR.md)
and concurrent Session94 improve it to3(3^k-2k-1)/4 legal re-marking edges,
using conjugated individual triangles and retaining full port permutations.

Put N=3^k, k>=1, and retain the zero-prefix generators g_0,...,g_(k-1)
used above, with their inverses. Every element of A_k is a product of at most

    B_k = sum_(d=0)^(k-1) 3^d(2d+1) = 1+(k-1)3^k

of these letters. Consequently **every c in C_k is the holonomy of a based
loop using at most3 B_k legal square-root transport edges**.

For the first bound, the usual wreath coordinates express an automorphism
as one local rotation, of exponent0,+1 or-1, at each internal tree node.
A rotation at prefix v=(v_0,...,v_(d-1)) is

    a_v g_d a_v^(-1),      a_v=g_0^(v_0)...g_(d-1)^(v_(d-1)).

Interpret exponent2 as-1. The map a_v sends the zero-prefix subtree to the
v-subtree and preserves its suffix, so this conjugacy is literal. It costs
at most2d+1 letters. There are3^d nodes at depth d; summing proves B_k.
The representation can be extracted recursively from the supplied permutation
by its macro rotation and its three child restrictions, without listing A_k.

For the second bound, read a word a_l...a_1 for c in execution order.
Let s_0=t0 and s_i=a_i s_(i-1) a_i^(-1); let t_i=a_i t0 a_i^(-1).
Because every a_i lies in K,

    b(s_i)^(-1) a_i b(s_(i-1)) = c(s_i,t_i).

Each factor is the actual triangle t0->t_i->s_i->t0. The product telescopes
to c, because s_l=t0. Execute the triangles in the corresponding order.
Each costs three transport edges; none is counted as a free reset. Identity
edges can be suppressed, so the bound remains valid if they are disallowed.

This is an upper bound, not an optimality claim. A triangle's endpoints are
full marks and may have long descriptions; the bound does not make their
storage or computation constant-cost. It also is not a bound for native
tournament **complement** moves: the legal moves here are the precisely
defined square-root re-marking transports. All named ports are moved by the
actual permutations, and no original source weights are overwritten.

## 3. Exactly which single-port observations forget the path safely

At the base mark, a single named vertex can be observed independently of
the marking path iff the observation is invariant under H_k=C_k. The
centralizer's vertex orbits have sizes

    1, 1,1, 3,3, 9,9, ..., 3^(k-1),3^(k-1).

There are exactly2k+1 orbits. Their coordinates are the zero string, or
the location and value(1 or2) of the first nonzero ternary digit. This is
a rooted longest-common-prefix coordinate, with the branch sign retained.

Proof: C_k preserves each top-level block. On block0 it acts as C_(k-1).
On each of blocks1 and2 it induces the transitive full A_(k-1) action,
with the two actions linked by negation. Apply induction inside block0.

This is the exact single-port quotient, not an exact quotient for several
shared ports. At k=2, write a port in block1 as (1,j) and in block2 as
(2,l). Centralizer action sends (j,l) to (j+a,l-a). Thus

    j+l mod3

is a joint invariant invisible to the two separate single-port orbits.
The nine ordered port pairs split into three orbits of size3. This is an
explicit arithmetic reason that independently correct port quotients need
a joint refinement. It connects directly to Session92's
[joint-closure algebra](../session92_joint_order_carriers_20261029/algebra/JOINT_CLOSURE_ALGEBRA.md),
without identifying its weighted transition operator with this group action.

An exact implementation has two choices: use a retained base mark and flat
transports b(t)b(s)^(-1), or accumulate the actual C_k correction. Retaining
only its order loses the action on ports. The arbitrary-depth correction
family is large, even though its recursive group description is short.

## 4. A precise, finite equidecomposition connection

Let a specified finite group G act on a finite set X. Two subsets A,B of X
admit a piecewise-G bijection iff

    |A intersect O| = |B intersect O| for every G-orbit O.

Necessity is immediate because every allowed move stays in its orbit.
For sufficiency, match points separately inside each orbit and choose an
element carrying each matched point to its partner; singleton pieces suffice.
No bound on the number of larger pieces is being asserted.

Thus with the **full** Lucas symmetry group of `REFLECTION_GROUPS.md`, the
point action is transitive and this finite equidecomposition test retains
only cardinality. With the marking-loop group C_k, it retains the2k+1
single-port orbit occupancies. On multiple ports it needs joint orbit
occupancies, including the preceding mod3 charge.

This is not a Euclidean scissors-congruence theorem. The old
[HYP-2187 fibre proposal](../../hypotheses/HYP-2187-equinumerosity-equidecomposability-fiber-bridge.md)
correctly motivates retaining more than cardinality, but its informal
"equidecomposition" vocabulary does not itself specify lawful pieces or
moves. Here both are specified, and changing the operation group changes
the invariant. The corrected Session29 warning also rules out transporting
a geometric Dehn invariant merely from a repeated numerical constant.

This finite theorem is also distinct from G-equivariant isomorphism of
G-sets, which retains stabilizer types. Piecewise action, global equivariant
action, and an arbitrary set bijection are three different tasks.

Target: its marking graph with permutation-valued edges.
Map: the exact square-root transports and their based centralizer correction.
