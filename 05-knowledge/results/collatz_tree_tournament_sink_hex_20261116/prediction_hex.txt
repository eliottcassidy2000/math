     share a common (X', g') still all have H <= 5.

     H(T_N) = 1 for N <= 8 and H(T_9) >= 3 (the first cycle 5 -> 32 -> 64 -> 5); at N = 20 the even-first and odd-first
     orders give different counts, both odd, and both exceed 100.

     that is not a power of two (the tree tournament is not a pure cone).
