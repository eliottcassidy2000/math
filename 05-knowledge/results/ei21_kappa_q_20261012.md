## 1. The 21-vertex Exoo-Ismailescu graph

EI21 (House of Graphs 51378, read 2026-10-12): 21 vertices, **38 = 2·21 − 4
edges**, so like the fish it is one edge short of minimal rigidity; degrees
`3^11 4^7 5^3`, triangle-free, `|Aut| = 2`, 10 unit 4-cycles. The direction
method ([EI19/fish note](ei19_fish_placement_q_zeta55_20261012.md)) gives 18
direction classes and 8 independent integer relations: 9 free angles against
8 equations, a one-dimensional realization family, confirmed by the local
degree of freedom 1 at every faithful sample (300 random starts converge
373 times; 61 faithful distinct samples). There is **no relation of support
three** (so no forced angle, unlike EI19) and exactly two of support four
(`-2u14 + u15 - 2u18 + u19 = 0` and `u15 + 2u16 - 2u17 - u19 = 0`, 1-indexed),
which are flexible. Placement in `L = Q(zeta55)` is therefore a Diophantine
question about the family's curve. Fixing one free direction to each of the
117 `L`-units of the bank (110 roots of unity, five Mersenne directions, the
thirds unit and its conjugate) and solving the remaining eight equations
gives 524 faithful realizations in all; LLL on the `zeta55` power basis at
200 digits finds no further direction in `L` at any of them (shortest vectors
at the random height `1e4`). **OPEN**, with the same decisive test as for
the fish: the genus and `L`-points of the family's curve.

So of the four smallest known triangle-free four-chromatic unit-distance
graphs, EI19 is excluded from `L` by a forced angle (PROVED), EI17 is
excluded numerically to coordinate height `1e63` (its field OPEN), and EI21
and the fish are one-parameter families with no forced angle (OPEN).

## 2. kappa(q): the Lovász LP and the first exact lower bounds

`G_q = Cay(F_(q^2), mu_(q+1))` is the finite unit-distance graph of
THM-4624 / HYP-9256; `kappa(q) = chi(G_q)` enters THM-4558's residue
colourings. For a Cayley graph on an abelian group the Lovász number is a
linear program over the orbit variables (`f(0) = 1`, `f = 0` on the unit
circle, `f` symmetric, Fourier transform non-negative), and for a
vertex-transitive graph `chi(G) >= |V|/theta(G)` and `alpha(G) <= theta(G)`:

| q | n = q^2 | degree q+1 | theta(G_q) | chi >= | known |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 4 | 4 (K_4) |
| 3 | 9 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 (K_3 x K_3) |
| 4 | 16 | 5 | 6 | 3 | 4 (Clebsch) |
| 5 | 25 | 6 | 7.5946 | 4 | |
| 7 | 49 | 8 | 17.6248 | 3 | |
| 8 | 64 | 9 | 16 | 4 | 4 |
| 9 | 81 | 10 | 27 | 3 | 3 |
| 11 | 121 | 12 | 34.5496 | 4 | |
| 13 | 169 | 14 | 52.4266 | 4 | **>= 5 (SAT below)** |
| 16 | 256 | 17 | 74.6667 | 4 | |
| 17 | 289 | 18 | 77.1985 | 4 | |
| 19 | 361 | 20 | 99.4980 | 4 | |
| 23 | 529 | 24 | 131.7620 | **5** | |
| 25 | 625 | 26 | 171.7743 | 4 | |

The LP is exact on the frame cases (`theta = 1, 3, 16, 27` at `q = 2, 3, 8,
9`, matching `kappa = 4, 3, 4, 3`), misses the Clebsch value (`3 < 4`), and
gives `kappa(23) >= 5` outright. It also bounds the independence numbers
whose CP-SAT runs the logic4 session had started: `alpha(G_13) <= 52`,
`alpha(G_17) <= 77`.

**SAT with lex-leader symmetry breaking.** The logic4 session's
`kappa_sat.py` fixed `colour(0) = 0`, `colour(1) = 1` and ran out of time at
one hour for `k = 5` at `q = 13` and `17`. Adding the standard lex-leader
constraint on colour classes (in a fixed vertex order, a vertex may take
colour `c >= 2` only if an earlier vertex took colour `c-1`) changes the
picture: with CaDiCaL 1.9.5,

    q = 13, k = 4:  UNSAT in 1.2 s   =>  kappa(13) >= 5,
    q = 17, k = 4:  UNSAT at once    =>  kappa(17) >= 5,
    q = 17, k = 6:  SAT in 92 s      =>  kappa(17) <= 6  (colouring verified, recorded).

So `5 <= kappa(17) <= 6`. The runs for `(13, 5)` and `(17, 5)` are recorded
in [the output file](gq_kappa_sat_20261012.out) as far as they went in this
session (SAT/UNSAT/undecided and times). Triangles exist in `G_q` iff
`3 | q+1`, so `G_17` has triangles (`omega >= 3`) while `G_13` is
triangle-free with `kappa(13) >= 5`: a triangle-free 5-chromatic finite
"unit-distance" graph on 169 points, the finite-field shadow of the plane
question of HYP-9247 (the plane field `Q(zeta55)` is triangle-free too, and
there the known four-chromatic triangle-free graphs do not even embed).
