## What a larger theory should retain

The candidate object is a relation presentation over a declared coefficient
system, with a transport action and a specified realization set. For linear
pieces one can store a kernel, image, cokernel and transport matrices;
nonlinear realization remains an actual predicate or relation. Composition
identifies common boundary variables and eliminates internal variables.
This is familiar from constraint satisfaction, algebraic relations and
categorical composition; the research problem is to find compact exact
presentations for these particular operations.

There are three constructive compression mechanisms, each with a test:

1. **Redundancy:** solve for a requested functional from retained equations
   (P1). Count missing independent equations, not independent summaries.
2. **Holonomy:** tree transport plus cycles fixes some or all root data
   (P3/P4). Use a cokernel or fixed-space obstruction, not just cycle length.
3. **Bounded lifting:** a residue determines an integer only after a proven
   range bound rules out aliases (P5/P6). The bound is part of the proof.

In this language the Fano obstruction says the relation module need not be
graphic. The square-sum parity obstruction says its coefficient ring
cannot always be reduced to F2. The Pauli comparison says an alternating
form can be additional structure even when addition is known. The prime
guards and planar Gram matrix say the realization set cannot be inferred
from the abstract relation module. The goal is thus more precise than
'everything is a graph' or 'everything is an observer'.

The claim that this framework will yield small carriers across all the
repository's areas is **OPEN**. Exact elimination can grow exponentially
or require unbounded memory. In particular the Vitali conjunction mechanism
continues to prohibit a fixed bound on correlation order whenever later
legal operations can test all those conjunctions.

## The hostile tournament pair remains active

The [Liouville/Mobius packet](../liouville_mobius_eleven_20261027/README.md)
provides the labelled12-vertex pair with identical cyclic triples and
automorphism orders81 and27. With A=C3->1, use C3[A,A,A] and
C3[A,A,A^op]. Both have462915 Hamilton paths, mu(H)=0 and lambda(H)=+1;
their counted legal path-complement responses differ. These counts are
inherited controls, not rerun as part of the present new finite universes.

Any candidate carrier for actual black/blue composition must recover that
difference and retain every ordered child path component, multiplicity and
complemented relation. A prime quotient can revisit a child, so two outer
endpoints do not suffice. Frobenius phase is transport data, and an unnamed
re-marking quotient must keep its action, not quietly replace it by a
named basis. The present arithmetic results do not classify strong atoms.

## Four concrete open problems

These are precise **OPEN workspace questions**, not claims of global
literature novelty or scheduled future work.

**O1 Arithmetic realization cost.** For rank r, minimize the largest source
prime in a bank whose successor square classes are all nonzero vectors of
an r-dimensional binary space. Distinguish a prescribed row basis from
optimization over square classes. The fixed basis (2,3,5,7) is solved here:
the minimum possible maximum is5669 because the class70 alone requires it.
The rank-three bank gives maximum89. First test: compare complete rank-four
banks using row-prime subsets of {2,3,5,7,11,13}, retaining failed quadratic
searches as unproved, and test whether a different basis lowers5669.
An all-rank existence theorem must confront the prime-value obstruction.

**O2 How little incidence pins transport?** In E=F_(2^(2m)), what is the
minimum number of marked K-lines whose isotropy, together with Frobenius
invariance, forces the trace form? Specify an actual marked field and
nondegenerate alternating forms. At m2, Frobenius leaves exactly two forms;
the single K-line with nonzero vectors {2,12,14}, corresponding to primes
{2,139,419}, distinguishes them and forces mask60. Zero lines do not, so
the exact minimum is one. First next test: linear algebra on alternating
forms at m3 and m4, then optimize the required line constraints. This asks
how much of the spread is redundant, extending P3's all-lines theorem.

**O3 Prescribed square-sum paths.** Characterize finite positive distinct
square-sum paths P that extend to Hamiltonian cycles on every sufficiently
large interval [1,N]. This is a prescribed-attachment strengthening, not
a claim that the usual interval existence problem is newly open. Start
with the Berggren insertion P=(99,1,3,97), with edge sums100,4,100,
and search exact hosts beginning at N=99, storing endpoint and connectivity
constraints. Then compare other norm-one branches. The first issue is
extension of a fixed legal path, not reconstruction of labels from squares.

**O4 Strong-atom joint responses.** For the actual black/blue moves, determine
whether the complete counted joint-port response, with complement and
re-marking transport, distinguishes strong atoms up to isomorphism. State
the allowed port patterns and counting convention before searching. First
test: existing canonical atoms through order7, followed by the12-vertex
81/27 hostile. If it fails, record the smallest pair and the first move
word that distinguishes it; if it passes, that is finite evidence only.
The proposed economy is to reconstruct redundant port coordinates by
relations analogous to P1/P4, not to assume bounded-order marginals suffice.

For Collatz and planar JC the immediate programme is deliberately narrower:
seek certified height/degree bounds that eliminate aliases in a specified
branch. A generic parity graph or a finite symmetry census cannot supply
those bounds. For Hadwiger–Nelson, preserve metric boundary relations and
palette transport together before testing whether any coordinate can be
reconstructed. These are applications of a tested method, not claimed
solutions to the conjectures.
