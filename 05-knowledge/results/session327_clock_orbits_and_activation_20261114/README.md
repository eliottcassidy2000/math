# Session 327: why the 30,000 neighborhood has structure

**Chan–Pak remains open.** The most useful split is among three independent constraints: the digit's arithmetic phase, the size grade of the physical source, and its absolute clock budget. The same-looking integers can signal any of these; treating them as one mechanism creates false predictions.

## What the old numbers predict

- [Optional-slot floor and activation](OPTIONAL_SLOT_FLOOR.md): the cost of matching the shared vertex's slot in the Busch merge gives a new all-order Q bound. In the actual 99 chart, 21,399 is the first six-vertex strong-component source entry, and 54,277 is the first seven-vertex one. The 30,000 neighborhood sits inside the six-component grade, not on a mysterious threshold. The exact activation table through eight is independently replayed.
- [Literal local comb](LOCAL_COMB_AND_STAR_GEOMETRY.md): from 29,327 to 30,119, the relaxed 99 chart has exactly 75 of 397 odd targets, arranged in three residue progressions. The physical one-return slope/intercept inequality eliminates an entire lane except where another lane overlaps it. The published phase bank hits exactly 43 of the 397. A common ×4 retiming still has a literal collision at 42,783; ×8 separates every distinct clock band in the complete low-clock atlas, leaving an explicit growing gap. None of those is a full-spectrum gap.
- [The exact primorial return](EXACT_PRIMORIAL_RETURN.md): at the source H=23 for 30,017, translating by 30,030 cycles the digits 31,97,163. But its only two physical core clocks are 272 and 296, up to powers of two. Among all these translates the next hit at that same H is after 3,996 steps, at 120,029,897. The exponent 12 is forced by the multiplicative order of 2 modulo 455, not a fit.
- [Raw Q scale](RAW_CLOCK_SCALE.md): the diameter-chain recurrence puts the order-eleven source (H,Q2)=(355,29780) just below 30,000. Under a *raw Q* budget between 29,780 and 41,472 the maximum possible strong-component size is exactly eleven. That is a separate clock scale, not the slope-99 output grade.
- [Prime-wheel comparison](PRIMORIAL_PHASE_PAIR.md): the 99 and 100 rails simultaneously avoid the first 25 primes on a fraction 187/10005 of exponents. Every surviving cylinder of any finite wheel nevertheless has infinitely many explicitly controlled simultaneous composite pairs. This is exact sieving, not a claim about prime pairs.

## What the machinery can do

[The clock lattice](CLOCK_LATTICE.md) treats the digit expansion as interleaved integer tiles. A fixed H makes Q unique, while the first physical restriction is a rotation mod 396 that generally allows only short runs of consecutive odds. Large positive digits consume a real extra phase by a counting inequality. [Four literal equal-clock switches](EQUAL_CLOCK_ORBITS.md) supply every unit residue for H at a common *exact* Q modulo arbitrary powers of 2,3,11. A two-switch witness gives H values 115 and 1501 at Q=303104; their gap 1386=7·198 flips complementary phases. The modulus-dependent clocks can be enormous, so this is not proof that the desired N can afford them.

[The strong odd-modulus joint classification](ODD_JOINT_BOUNDARY.md) independently removes virtually all odd-modulus mystery from the two-response problem: for fixed order parity, every pair occurs modulo every odd modulus, with only the necessary mod-three sign relation. The constructive family is still sparse in absolute height. It controls neither arbitrary even-modulus data nor an exact global target.

**Next proof obligation:** find target-dependent source grades and phases that simultaneously pay the actual Q budget. More residue widening, padding one finite alphabet, or choosing a locally cheaper H cannot replace that step.
