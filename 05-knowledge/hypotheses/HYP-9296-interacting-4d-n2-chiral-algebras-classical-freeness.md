# HYP-9296 — classical freeness of 4d `N = 2` chiral algebras

## Why it matters

The proposal "Macdonald index = bigraded Hilbert series of `J_infty R_V`" (Andrews-Banerjee-Bhargava-Singh-Tao 2025)
has `char V = HS(J_infty R_V)` as its Schur limit, which is classical freeness at the level of characters. Its
general form is false:
- Ising (THM-4671 / van Ekeren-Heluani);
- the `Z_2`-gauged free hypermultiplet (THM-4672).

HYP-9296 asks for the true domain.

## Evidence table

|---|---|---|---|
| `(A_1, A_(2n))` | `Vir_(2,2n+3)` | all (PROVED) | Andrews-Gordon |
| `(A_1, A_3)` | `L_(-4/3)(sl2)` | 15 | Kac-Wakimoto; `D(q^3)/D(q)` |

| `(A_1, D_4)` | `L_(-3/2)(sl3)` | 8 (weight 8 via zero-weight cone) | `D(q^2)/D(q)` |
| SU(2), `N_f = 4` | `L_(-2)(so8)` | 5 (zero-weight block, all of `ker pi` by root-lattice argument) | flavoured Schur index |
| `N = 4` SU(2) SYM | small `N = 4`, `c = -9` | 9 | gauge-theory Schur index |

## Falsifiers

- **For the positive list:** a non-arc generator at any weight. The decisive next weights are SU(2) `N_f = 4` at 6
  (zero-weight block of dimension about 18k) and `(A_1, D_4)` at 8.
- **For the organizing question:** a non-discretely-gauged 4d theory whose chiral algebra is not free. Candidates:
  - Deligne-series `L_(-h^vee/6-1)(g)` for `g = g2`, `f4`, `e6` (the `e6` MN theory);
  - `N = 4` SU(3) SYM (`W`-algebra, non-Lie);
  - `(A_2, A_3)`, i.e. `W_3(3,7)` (non-Lie).
