# HYP-9295 — `N = 2` minimal models are never classically free

## Statement

For every `k >= 1`, let `V_k` be the simple `N = 2` vertex superalgebra at `c = 3k/(k+2)` (NS sector).
1. The maximal ideal of the universal algebra is generated by one singular vector, of weight `k+1` and charge 0.
   CITED (Adamović).
2. `ker(pi_(V_k))` vanishes in weights `< k+3`. In weight `k+3` its minimal differential-ideal generators are exactly
   two, of Li degree 1 and `J_0`-charge `+2` and `-2`. OPEN.
3. In particular, `V_k` is not classically free. PROVED (THM-4673).

## Mechanism for part 2 (heuristic)

- **The charge-2 sector.** The charge-2 part of `V_k` is a parafermion module dressed by `U(1)`. Its lowest state has
  weight 4 for `k >= 2`, namely `G^+_(-5/2)G^+_(-3/2)|0>`. Its descendants fall short of the universal count first at
  weight `k+3`.
- **Example `k = 2` (corrected after audit 2).** The charge-2 sector is the Ising vacuum module tensored with a Fock
  module. Its dimensions at weights 4-10 are 1, 1, 3, 5, 10, 16, 28. The whole simple quotient matches
  `sum_m y^m q^(m^2) chi^Ising_(h(m)) / (q)_infty` through weight 10. In weight 5 the universal algebra has 2
  charge-2 states and 1 survives. An earlier version wrongly called this sector a pure Heisenberg module.
- **The arc space cannot see it.** The relation has Li degree 1, while the `C_2` relations live in Li degree 0 and
  propagate only through `d` and products.

## Tests

- **Falsifiers:** `k = 7, 8` to weight `k+4`; the second prime to weight 10.
- **Proof route for part 2:** the parafermion/`U(1)` decomposition of the charge-2 vacuum sector, compared with the
  universal count in weight `k+3`.
