# HYP-9295 — `N = 2` minimal models are never classically free

## Statement

For every `k >= 1`, let `V_k` be the simple `N = 2` vertex superalgebra at `c = 3k/(k+2)` (NS sector).
1. The maximal ideal of the universal algebra is generated by one singular vector, of weight `k+1` and charge 0.
2. `ker(pi_(V_k))` vanishes in weights `< k+3`. In weight `k+3` its minimal differential-ideal generators are exactly
   two, of Li degree 1 and `J_0`-charge `+2` and `-2`.
3. In particular, `V_k` is not classically free.

## Mechanism (heuristic)

- **The charge-2 sector.** The charge-2 part of `V_k` is the parafermion field `psi_2` dressed by `U(1)`. Its lowest
  state has weight 4 for `k >= 2`, namely `G^+_(-5/2)G^+_(-3/2)|0>`. Its `U(1)` x parafermion descendants fall
  short of the universal count first at weight `k+3`.
- **Example `k = 2`.** Here `psi_2 = 1`, and the charge-2 sector is a pure Heisenberg module. Weight 5 has 2 universal
  states and 1 surviving state.
- **The arc space cannot see it.** The relation has Li degree 1, while the `C_2`-algebra relations live in Li degree
  0 and their only differential consequences come from `d`. For odd generators these are blind to fermion-bilinear
  vanishing, as in THM-4673.

## Tests

- **Cheapest falsifiers:** `k = 5, 6` to weight `k+4`, and the second prime to weight 10.
- **Proof route for part 2:** the parafermion/`U(1)` decomposition of the charge-2 vacuum sector. The Gepner string
  functions give `char V_k` per charge; compare it with the universal count in weight `k+3`.
