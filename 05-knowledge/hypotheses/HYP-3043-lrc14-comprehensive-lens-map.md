# HYP-3043: LRC14 Comprehensive Lens Map And Controlled-Forgetting Center

**Status:** SYNTHESIS / coordination theorem target; not a proof.
**Session:** codex-2026-06-26-S206.
**Primary artifact:** `00-navigation/LRC-LENS-MAP.md`.

## Claim

All serious LRC14 proof lenses currently in the repo are best understood as
partial views of a labelled packet sheaf with controlled forgetting.  The
object common to the lenses is not a scalar, a raw runner tournament, a single
finite automaton, or a pure analytic estimate.  It is a packet whose status,
route, owner/topology, period/scale, and certificate/residual coordinates must
be retained, reconstructed, dual-annihilated, descended, boundary-stopped, or
named as residual debt.

Equivalently, a quotient produced by any LRC lens is legal only if:

```text
forgotten coordinate
  is fiber-constant
  or reconstructed
  or dual-annihilated
  or family-descended
  or stopped by AP/GW boundary topology
  or routed to named F7/THM-572/harmonic debt.
```

## Tournament Analysis

Vertices are lens families and proof obligations, not runners.  The considered
alternate vertex sets were runners, gaps, fixed circle sections, section
boundaries, wall-crossing events, residues, cover arcs, Fourier modes, Haar
tiles, matroid circuits/topes, sidecar fields, and proof obligations.

The observable records retained boundary/open status, route schedulability,
owner/topology, period/scale, dual-certificate strength, residual handoff,
compression, and formal checkability.  The switch orients toward the lens that
preserves more LRC predicate with fewer unnamed lost coordinates.  The tie path
is:

```text
packet_sheaf
  > boundary_topology
  > owner_stalk_barcode
  > period_arithmetic
  > harmonic_certificate
  > local_switch_decoy
  > automaton_sequence
  > external_analogy
  > raw_scalar.
```

Preserved by the quotient: boundary/open status, route schedulability, and
named residual handoff.  Destroyed by the quotient: row enumeration, runner
identity, and scalar ranking.

## Consequences

1. New LRC ideas should be entered as lenses with five fields:
   `lens_family`, `preserved_lrc_predicate`, `destroyed_coordinate`,
   `required_sidecar`, and `handoff_target`.
2. Pair-good decoys should be classified by generator grammar and active owner
   relation, not by count.
3. AP-tail residuals should be checked for hidden small-period puncture clocks
   before escalating to Fejer, THM-572, or a new residual sector.
4. Owner-strip filtrations should record the first surviving page before a
   residual is promoted to named F7/THM-572 debt.
5. Automata and sequence shadows should remain sidecars unless the exact
   magnitude/topology/owner/route packet is present.
6. Analytic and operator lenses should emit blindness reports and certificate
   keys before any scalar estimate is trusted.
7. The next comprehensive ledger should attach lens-family and destroyed-field
   tags to HYP-2963-style packets, then rerun status and route mixing.
