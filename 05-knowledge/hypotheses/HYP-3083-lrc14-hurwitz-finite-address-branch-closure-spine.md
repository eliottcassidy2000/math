# HYP-3083: LRC14 Hurwitz Finite-Address Branch-Closure Spine

This file completes the S252/S252b/S253 synthesis lane for the user's request
to integrate incoming work, past LRC14 proof work, Hurwitz's theorem, and the
q-Pochhammer/full-modular-group cusp cue into a sharper account of what remains
in the LRC14 proof, now tightened by the S31af level-7 lift sieve and the S31ag
polynomial-method / Conjecture 7.1 bridge.

The core rule is not that Hurwitz's approximation theorem directly proves
LRC14.  The transferable Hurwitz/Markov/Pell lesson is:

```text
an infinite tail, extremal scalar, or branch contraction is legal only after
the finite address that generates it has been retained or discharged
```

That same rule now applies to q-cusp tails, sixth-power equalities, median
scheduler centers, proof-graph kernels, endpoint owners, p-adic/Morita-gamma
discrepancy carriers, apex-periodic covering rows, and exact packet addresses.
Any quotient that forgets the coordinate making a row boundary/open
distinguishable must reconstruct it, dual-annihilate it, prove it
fiber-constant, or route it to named residual debt.

- Diophantine Hurwitz/Markov: a sharp approximation constant is explained by a
  finite continued-fraction or Markov address, not by the scalar alone.
- Complex-analytic Hurwitz: zeros/poles cannot appear silently under locally
  uniform holomorphic limits; a q-Pochhammer limit must retain zero/pole
  divisor data.
- Hurwitz/Vieta surfaces: an infinite Diophantine orbit is legal only after
  its finite seed and mutation word are retained.

For the full modular group, a modular function must be invariant under the
modular transformations and meromorphic at the cusp `i infinity`.  With
`q=exp(2*pi*i*z)`, this means its Laurent q-expansion has only finitely many
negative-power terms.  In LRC14 language, a quotient may use an infinite
product, partition, periodicity, or mutation tail only after its polar debt is
finite and every polar term has a named packet exit.

## Covering-Bound Priority

The incoming S59 redirect is correct in its main prioritization: the proof
should not put the AP/Goddyn-Wong census on the critical path.  For the
non-strict LRC14 theorem, if no runner is a multiple of `14`, then `t=1/14`
already gives every runner distance at least `1/14`.  The hard case is the
covering case where at least one runner is a multiple of `14`.

Past work refines the covering case:

- THM-523 gives the small-divisor / q-witness gate.
- THM-570 and THM-571 close the apex-majority branch with at least seven
  multiples of `14`.
- THM-573 sharpens the majority sieve: if at least seven speeds are divisible
  by `7`, then `M(S)>1/14` modulo accepted LRC<=13, so the live residual has
  at most six `7`-multiples.
- HYP-2906 peels a row when `v_max > 13 v_second`.
- HYP-2900 supplies the larger Node-3 equidistribution / induction picture.
- THM-566 rules out a naive fixed bounded-denominator witness atlas.
- HYP-3082 shows that raw scalar quotienting creates naked proof bridges, but
  protected sidecars remove them on the current HYP-2963 bank.

Thus the live residual is:

```text
primitive covering 13-row
  with at least one multiple of 14 but at most six multiples of 7,
  no small-q witness,
  no level-7 lift-sieve exit,
  no one-large-speed interval peel,
  no uniform 1/13 margin shortcut because aliasing/dilation can be tight,
  no uniform largest-lonely-arc floor for the direct 1/14 lonely set yet,
  and no legal quotient that forgets the finite address of its obstruction.
```

That is much smaller than the full census program, but still open.

## Improved Proof Spine

The improved argument should be stated as a finite-address exponential
covering spine:

```text
S primitive, |S|=13
  -> q-witness gate
  -> covering row with a multiple of 14
  -> level-7 lift sieve or <=6-multiple-of-7 residual
  -> apex-majority gamma descent or low-apex residue
  -> one-large-speed interval peeler or top-balanced residue
  -> finite-address packet P(S)
  -> protected branch graph Gamma(P)
  -> largest-lonely-arc floor or named obstruction to that floor
  -> strict witness / AP-GW boundary / C27 petal /
     covering moment / K33-THM-572 lift / named residual debt
  -> formal sidecar closure
```

The word "exponential" keeps the useful part of the S59 hyperoperation
reframe: a multiple of `14` is periodic on the `1/14` lift, and the gamma
trick exploits that periodicity.  The Hurwitz/Markov/q-cusp branch supplies
the guardrail: periodicity, q-series tails, route medians, power equalities,
local p-adic/discrepancy carriers, and scalar extrema are legal only after
the finite address that made them special is retained.

The S31af level-7 lift is the same principle in a clean finite form: the seven
lift phases are usable only because the divided-row address and the one-forbid
per `7`-coprime speed payload are retained.

The finite address should contain at least:

```text
P(S) = (exact scale, apex_divisible_by_14_flag,
        multiple-of-14 profile, multiple-of-7 profile,
        level7_lift_sieve_status, covering_margin_aliasing_status,
        low-apex/top-balanced status,
        grid_class, active_binder_owner_word, endpoint_owner_transition_word,
        owner/topology, route, primitive period deck, safe/open status,
        analytic certificate, endpoint shell, cusp/principal-part debt,
        relation/collision sidecars, p-adic/discrepancy sidecar,
        branch kernel, destroyed-coordinate exit, formal status).
```

The missing theorem is not "find one scalar separator."  It is:

```text
Every primitive low-apex, top-balanced covering residual with at most six
`7`-multiples admits P(S), and P(S) preserves the LRC predicate
M(S) >= 1/14 until a terminal witness, boundary equality, contradiction, or
named residual debt is reached.
```

## Theorem-Facing Gates

1. **Witness gate.**  If a row omits a multiple of some `q in {2,...,14}`,
   THM-523 gives the constructive `1/q` witness.  AP/GW equality atoms live on
   this easy side; they explain the tight floor but are not the hard covering
   proof.

2. **Level-7 lift-sieve gate.**  If at least seven speeds are divisible by
   `7`, THM-573 gives `M(S)>1/14`: divide the `7`-multiples, choose an
   LRC<=13 safe phase for that divided row, lift it to seven phases, and note
   that each remaining `7`-coprime speed forbids at most one lift.  This
   subsumes the old many-`14`-multiples residual and makes `<=6`
   `7`-multiples the live covering profile.

3. **Apex-majority gate.**  THM-570 and THM-571 remain useful as the older
   `14`-phase / gamma-descent route, especially for explaining the periodic
   mechanism.  They are no longer the sharpest residual split after THM-573.

4. **Scale-separation gate.**  If the top speed is larger than `13` times the
   second speed, HYP-2906 peels it by a connected seed-safe interval.  HYP-2900
   is the wider analytic Node-3 version: large committed speeds remove the
   expected `1/7` of a positive seed-safe set, pending effective
   Erdos-Turan/Weyl bounds.

5. **Finite-address packet gate.**  The remaining low-apex, top-balanced
   covering residue must emit a packet retaining exact `M`, q-threshold,
   `7`/`14` divisibility profiles, binding denominators, safe topology,
   endpoint owners, route labels, C27/K33 status, Ramanujan/q-cusp sidecars,
   sixth-power/relation sidecars, p-adic/discrepancy sidecars,
   covering-margin aliasing status, and destroyed-coordinate exits.  This is
   HYP-2963 plus the HYP-3075/HYP-3078/HYP-3079/HYP-3080/HYP-3084
   finite-address discipline.

6. **Protected branch graph.**  HYP-3082 changes the next theorem obligation
   from "find one scalar" to "prove every primitive residual reaches the
   protected graph and then discharge each protected exit."  A naked bridge is
   a missing sidecar/debt, not a usable theorem shortcut.

7. **Terminal exits.**  The exits are direct q-witness, strict safe component
   or covering-moment discharge, C27/petal discharge, AP/GW boundary equality,
   K33/THM-572 state-lift contradiction, or named formal residual debt.
   THM-572 is already Lean-formalized as a closure theorem once the missing
   LRC-to-tournament state lift is constructed.

Prove that every primitive low-apex, top-balanced covering row with at most
six `7`-multiples either reaches the HYP-2963/HYP-3082 packet bank or reaches
a declared outside-bank normal form with the same finite-address fields.  This

scalar, q-series tail, or covering-margin estimate unless the forgotten

Prove the strict safe-component exit for covering-family packets uniformly.
In older language this is OPEN-Q-108 / GAP G2 / bounded-core fattening.  In
the Lean formalization status it is the analytic side of `hmeasGP` plus the
finite-ruler Part A `hpartA` (or the p0/cap positive-margin route after its

exponential covering bound, not the additive census.

has margin `>1/13`.  Dilated and primitive aliasing examples can sit at or
near that scale, so the certificate must expose exact aliasing/debt rather

"find any cap law" but close the two deviation constants (`k=8,9`) and the

HYP-3091 adds the scissors/measure interface constraint: equinum, equidecomp,
and equidist are different shadows of the lonely set, and the proof ledger
must keep `Phi(S)` rather than a scalar count.  In the current hard family,
`D=41` records the bounded mod-41 core while `1/lmax=V*` records the apex
scale; the remaining normalized floor is the measure/scissors

The q-cusp analogy is useful here because it says the infinite covering tail is
legal only after its finite polar debt is named.  A covering proof should


## Candidate Branch-Closure Lemma

The theorem shape suggested by this synthesis is:

> **Finite-address branch-closure lemma.** Let `S` be a primitive 13-speed row.
> Suppose the q-witness, level-7 lift-sieve, apex/gamma, and one-large-speed
> gates reduce `S` to a low-apex, top-balanced covering row with at most six
> `7`-multiples.  Suppose this row admits a packet
> `P(S)` whose finite-address ledger retains exact scale, endpoint owner,
> `7`/`14` divisibility profile, aliasing status, route, residual section,
> grid class, active binder owner word, endpoint transition word,
> bridge-protection sidecar, and any q-cusp/Hurwitz/Morita arithmetic address
> used by the proof.  If `P(S)` has no illegal infinite polar tail, no naked
> protected-graph bridge, and every terminal branch is discharged by q-witness,
> boundary, owner-strip, nested-refinement covering-family certificate,
> cross-handoff THM-572 state-lift exit, or named missing sidecar, then
> `M(S) >= 1/14`.

This lemma is intentionally conditional.  It is a better target than another
scalar separator because each missing hypothesis is now a named proof
obligation.
