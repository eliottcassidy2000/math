# HYP-9315 — the Li spectral sequence of a Virasoro minimal model

## Setting

See THM-4695. The filtered surjection `Φ: ⊕_n U(Vir_(<=-2)) e_n -> I`, `e_n -> L_(-1)^n v`, has associated graded
the Koszul differential of the derived arc space of `Spec C[x]/(x^N)`.
- Its spectral sequence has `E_1 = (Z_1(K), C[x]/J)` and converges to `gr L`.
- So `ker π = grI/J` carries the filtration by transfer order: the `k`-th piece is `Im d_k`.

**Computation.** `vir_transfer_orders_20261018.py` processes the Koszul 1-cycles of each weight in increasing length
and reduces `Φ(z)` top-down against the tops already found. The step at which a new symbol first appears is its
transfer order.

## Statements

**(a) Cyclicity.** `H_1(K)` is generated over `C[x][d]` by the Euler syzygy `s_1 = x_0 e_1 - N x_1 e_0`, of weight
`2N+3`. Since `T_1` is `C[x][d]`-linear mod `J` (THM-4695(a)), `Im T_1 = C[x][d] T_1(s_1) = C[x][d] b_N` mod `J`.

**(b) Orders of the degree-`N` generators.** The minimal generators of `grI` in degree `N` are at Li degrees
`0, 3, 5, ..., 2p-3` (HYP-9294(a), kind-pasteur). The generator at Li degree `2k+1` is a transfer of order exactly `k`.
So the `p - 2` non-`J` degree-`N` generators are produced one by one by `d_1, d_2, ..., d_(p-2)`.

**(c) Higher degrees.** Generators of degree `N + i` have transfer order at most `p - 2 + i`, and order `p - 2 + i`
occurs.

**(d) Factorization.** `T_1` vanishes on Koszul boundaries mod `J`, so it factors through `H_1`.

## Reading

- **Classical freeness.** Classical freeness is the vanishing of every `d_k`. By THM-4695 and THM-4671, `d_1 = 0` iff
  `p = 2`. For `p >= 3`, (b) predicts nonzero `d_1, ..., d_(p-2)` in degree `N`.
- **Universal and model-specific parts.** The page `E_2` is universal for each `N`. The model-specific content of the
  singular support lies in `d_2, d_3, ...`.
- **Literature.** The cyclicity (a) is a statement about the jet (principal-subspace) algebra of `x^N`. It should be
  compared with the Calinescu-Lepowsky-Milas presentations of principal subspaces and the Feigin-Stoyanovsky
  functional models; it is not proved here.

## Falsifiers

- A bidegree where `H_1` is not generated by `s_1`.
- A degree-`N` generator at Li degree `2k+1` whose transfer order is not `k`.
- A transfer of order `> p - 2 + i` in degree `N + i`.
