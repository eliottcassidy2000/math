
## 2. The family, composed (the JC >= 3 infinite family)

Chain rule: `det J_{F o F} = 4` (companion-checked); in general
every composition of Keller maps is Keller, generic fiber
cardinality is multiplicative for dominant maps (classical), and
stable extension `F (+) id` preserves both. Hence, writing
`KDeg(n)` for the set of generic-fiber degrees of Keller
endomorphisms of `C^n`:

```text
KDeg(n) is a multiplicative monoid, and {3^k : k >= 0} subset
KDeg(n) for every n >= 3.                                       (1)
```

The JC >= 3 counterexample "infinite family" is therefore not a
list but a GRADED MONOID over the sporadic core: tame conjugates
and elementary extensions move within a degree grade (THM-2465's
W1, W2 are degree-3 members in new shapes, including 2-jet);
composition multiplies grades; the collision anatomy of `F^k` over
the branch tower is governed by k-fold iterates of the 1+2 branch
split -- a ternary orbit tree (SPECULATIVE link to the repo's other
ternary grammar, THM-2450's C3-towers: flagged, untested).

## 3. The reframe (the H-spectrum grammar, one universe over)

HYP-9029 reduced the H-spectrum to "the multiplicative semigroup
generated by strong atoms, with {7, 21} the non-atom-non-product
gaps". The same grammar here:

```text
KDeg(3) = multiplicative semigroup generated by the degrees of
COMPOSITION-IRREDUCIBLE Keller maps ("atoms").  Known atoms: 1 and
3 (the wild map).  Smith rule: 2 is not an atom (any dim).
Conjecture (semigroup form of order-{1,3}, all shapes not just
z-affine): the atom set is {1, 3}; hence KDeg(3) = {3^k}, and
G1's degree 4 = 2^2 is EXCLUDED as the first
non-atom-non-product value -- the "7" of the Keller world.     (2)
```

Under (2), THM-2465's hardness floor sharpens conceptually: proving
`4 notin KDeg(3)` is the Keller analogue of proving `7` is not an
H-value -- and the H-proof went through the STRONG-atom floor
(Busch crevasse). The parallel program: an atom-degree floor for
composition-irreducible wild maps. The Redei-parity lens (HYP-8160
lens A: fiber degree odd) would kill all even degrees at once; note
the honest gap: for real Keller maps, conjugation pairing gives
`#real preimages = d (mod 2)` at every real regular value, and F
witnesses its own oddness through the all-real collision fiber --
but a general theorem needs every wild map to admit an
odd-real-count value, which is NOT yet proved.
