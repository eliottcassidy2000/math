## Statement

Let `C = 2n−1`, runners `V = {1,…,(C−1)/2}` (a half-system of `ℤ/C`), cuts `ε∈{±1}^{n−1}` up to
global swap. Recall (THM-415) two cuts collide iff `Φ(ε)_t² = Φ(ε')_t²` for all `t`, where
`Φ(ε)_t = Σ_i ε_i sin(2π t i/C)`.

**(A) Mechanism — PROVED.** For each prime `q ∣ C`, let `H_q = {(C/q)·j : j=1,…,(q−1)/2}` be the
**half-system of the order-`q` cyclic subgroup** `(C/q)ℤ/C`. Flipping the signs of exactly the
runners in `H_q` is a "subgroup move" `σ_q`. It is **frequency-localized**: `σ_q` changes `Φ(ε)_t`
only at frequencies with `q ∤ t`, and leaves `Φ(ε)_t` unchanged whenever `q ∣ t`, because
`Σ_{i∈H_q} ±sin(2π t i/C) = Σ_j ±sin(2π t j/q) = 0` when `q ∣ t`. *(Proof: `(C/q)·t ≡ 0` makes each
sine argument an integer multiple of `2π`.)* The `q=3` case (`H_3 = {C/3}`, a single runner) is
exactly THM-413's order-3 silent flip.

**(B) Every collision is a subgroup move — VERIFIED (all composite `C ≤ 39`).** In every collision
pair, the symmetric difference of the two cuts (the flipped runner set, taken on the smaller side) is
the half-system `H_q` of a prime-order subgroup, or — for prime-power `C` — a symmetric-difference
combination of the half-systems of the subgroup *lattice* (e.g. `C=27`: moves generated by `H_{3}=
{9}` and `H_{9}={3,6,9,12}`, producing `{9}`, `{3,6,12}`, `{3,6,9,12}`).

**(C) Count law for `C = 3p`, `p > 3` prime — VERIFIED (p = 5,7,11,13):**
```
   deficiency(3p) = 2^{(p−1)/2},
```
split evenly: `2^{(p−3)/2}` Hamming-1 pairs (the `σ_3` flip of `x = p = C/3`) and `2^{(p−3)/2}`
Hamming-`(p−1)/2` pairs (the `σ_p` flip of the `(p−1)/2` multiples of 3). Data:
`C=15→4, 21→8, 33→32, 39→64`.

**(D) Other composite `C` — data (deficiency):** `9→1`, `25→4` (`q=5` only, all Hamming-2),
`35→16` (`8+8`, the `σ_5` and `σ_7` moves), `27→69` (prime-power, subgroup-lattice mixing).
Squarefree `C = qr` gives `deficiency = N_{(q)} + N_{(r)}` (no cross terms); prime powers add the
combined moves. A uniform closed form across all composite `C` is **open**.

## Why it matters / what it buys

1. **Converse of HYP-2270.** If (B) is upgraded to a theorem that **some** `H_q` flip is realized for
   at least one cut whenever `C` is composite, then composite `C ⟹` orbit `< 2^{n−2}`, which together
   with THM-415 **completes HYP-2270** (`orbit = 2^{n−2} ⟺ 2n−1 prime`). The obstruction is purely
   the existence of a complementary sign pattern `s` with `Φ(s)_t = 0` for all `q ∤ t` — a system of
   sine-vanishing conditions that is a power-of-2-sized solution set (hence the clean counts).

2. **Roots-of-unity dictionary.** The mechanism is the half-system face of vanishing sums of roots of
   unity (Lam–Leung / Conway–Jones): the order-`q` subgroup flip vanishes off the `q`-divisible
   frequencies precisely because `Σ_{j=1}^{q−1} ζ_q^{j} = −1` collapses on the subgroup. Composite
   modulus = nontrivial vanishing sums = collisions; prime modulus = no nontrivial vanishing (THM-415).
