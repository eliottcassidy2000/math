# HYP-3077: LRC14 Route-State Median-Hull Scheduler

## Claim

The final LRC14 proof interface should be tested as a medianization problem on
completed proof states:

```text
packet / route / certificate / sidecar / discharge
```

A serious route triple is legal only after each state carries the sidecars
needed by the coordinates it uses.  Once those sidecars are attached, the three
states should have a unique center.  If the center keeps only a typed
discharge coordinate but no specific terminal atom, then the proof has found a
scheduler center, not a terminal theorem exit.  The next move is to add a
separating sidecar or name the residual debt.

This is a synthesis target, not a proof of LRC14.  Its value is that it turns
the many recent sidecar rules into one finite check: does every proposed
triple have a legal median center, and does that center terminate or ask for a
refinement?

Read against the incoming S232-S239 layers, this is not a replacement for the
Hodge-cycle, Desargues-median, owner/root, medianized route-center,
route-triple center-control, cycle-class observability, or cross-carrier
pullback/polymer-Dirichlet sidecars.  It is the algebraic closure interface underneath them:
HYP-3066 asks whether residual cochains are generated by named certificate
cycles, HYP-3067 asks whether the proof graph has genuine median centers,
HYP-3068 makes owner/root objects first-class, HYP-3069 names Boolean
route-center completion obligations, HYP-3070 adds route-triple center-control
pages, HYP-3071 asks for cycle-class observability, HYP-3072 claims the
cross-carrier pullback resonance surface, HYP-3073 reserves the
polymer/Dirichlet bridge layer, and HYP-3077 asks whether the
completed coordinate ledger is closed under the legal sidecar rules used by
those median tests.

## Finite Scout

The S245 script models proof states as finite coordinate sets.  Sidecar
legality is encoded by unary Horn implications such as:

```text
route_label -> exact_M, endpoint_owner, safe_topology, magnitude_cocycle
observer_cut_payload -> value_origin_type, deletion_fiber_profile,
                        cross_sector_orientation_word
partial_cube_theta_word -> bit_position_phase, exact_M, endpoint_owner,
                           safe_topology
toeplitz_scale_gate -> ordered_quad_collapse_mode, exact_M, endpoint_owner,
                       safe_topology
desargues_girth6_residue -> observer_cut_payload,
                             rectangle_hourglass_residue,
                             beal_common_owner_gate
```

Because the current implications have one premise coordinate, the class of
legal states is closed under coordinatewise majority.  That gives the
medianization rule:

```text
median(A,B,C) = majority of retained coordinates,
                after closing under sidecar implications.
```

The finite audit has:

```text
named_features = 41
horn_rules = 34
max_premise_arity = 1
seed_states = 10
median_hull_states = 31
hull_triples_checked = 29791
raw_illegal_majorities = 0
closure_added_features_hist = {0: 29791}
interval_intersection_failures = 0
illegal_centers_after_closure = 0
```

Thus the chosen finite interface is median-closed, and in this finite hull the
closure step never repairs a bad raw majority.  This does not prove the real
packet bank is complete; it gives the schema and a consistency check for the
schema.  A future sidecar law that genuinely needs multiple premises must
either be compiled into a named coordinate or rechecked in the median hull,
since arbitrary Horn theories are not automatically majority-closed.

## Route Triples

The scout tests five serious triples.

1. `low_frontier_scheduler`

```text
AP/GW boundary route
C27 petal route
K33 state-lift route
```

The center keeps exact packet, route, certificate, and typed discharge data,
but drops the specific AP/GW, C27, and K33 terminal atoms.  This says the
low-frontier triple has a unique scheduler center.  It still needs a
separating sidecar before it can terminate.

2. `certificate_noncollapse_triangle`

```text
Fejer/Toeplitz interval route
Toeplitz positive-scale sidecar
Desargues/Beal girth-six finalizer
```

The center is a certificate/noncollapse scheduler.  The positive-scale and
girth-six data meet at certificate legality, but no single terminal atom wins.

3. `sequence_geometry_pressure`

```text
Moser/fibbinary partial-cube sidecar
hyperbolic reciprocal packet pressure
C27 shell transfer
```

The center keeps sidecar-completed route data.  This is the clean bridge from
S231 to S222/S225: partial-cube coordinates and hyperbolic geometry pressure
are proof-useful only after exact packet route data is retained.

4. `residual_repair_triangle`

```text
residual capacitor route
AP/GW boundary route
Fejer/Toeplitz certificate route
```

The center keeps owner/topology/certificate sidecars but drops the terminal
atoms.  This is a repair scheduler: attach zeta, endpoint-owner strip, or
interval-certificate refinement before claiming a discharge.

5. `finalizer_pressure_triangle`

```text
K33 state-lift route
Desargues/Beal finalizer
hyperbolic reciprocal pressure
```

The center again is unique but nonterminal.  The common-owner sidecar is the
load-bearing coordinate.

## Packet Fields

The medianization interface suggests adding or standardizing these packet
fields across HYP-2963/HYP-2987/HYP-3054 style ledgers:

```text
median_state_id
median_hull_id
packet_label
exact_M
endpoint_owner
safe_topology
magnitude_cocycle
route_label
certificate_payload
sidecar_payload
discharge_atom_typed
specific_discharge_atom
median_center_kind
median_dropped_atoms
median_required_refinement
```

A packet whose median center has `discharge_atom_typed` but no
`specific_discharge_atom` is not a failure.  It is a precise instruction:
refine the sidecar columns until a terminal atom appears, or name the residual
debt.

## Assumption Challenge

The chosen vertices are not runners, arcs, or scalar packet counts.  Candidate
vertices considered were runners, gaps, endpoint owners, route labels,
certificate atoms, sidecar columns, discharge modes, and proof obligations.
The useful vertices here are packet/route/certificate/sidecar/discharge
coordinates because they preserve boundary/open status plus exact route,
certificate, and discharge legality.

The challenged assumption is that a final proof should be a scalar route
ordering.  This carrier suggests a different target: every serious route
triple should admit a unique legal median center, and terminal proof work is
exactly the work of turning nonterminal scheduler centers into specific
discharge atoms.
