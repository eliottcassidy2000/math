# HYP-9175 -- which shadows the powers of three can cast, and the resisting share

**Proposition E (PROVED).** `Z_2^x = {±1} x (1 + 4Z_2)` and `-3` is a
topological generator of `1 + 4Z_2` (it is `5 mod 8`). Hence the closure of
`<3> = {3^a : a in Z}` in `Z_2^x` is `{(-1)^a (-3)^a : a in Z_2} = {u : u = 1
or 3 mod 8}` (the exponent `a` is read mod `2` for the sign). A rational
cycle point `x` (an odd 2-adic unit) is therefore a limit of powers of three
iff `x = 1, 3 mod 8`: `-5 = 3`, `-7 = 1`, `-37 = 3`, `-55 = 1`, `-61 = 3 mod 8`
qualify; `-1 = 7`, `-17 = 7`, `-25 = 7`, `-41 = 7`, `-91 = 5 mod 8` do not.
For the resisting class: `3^a = -5 mod 2^k` makes the first `k` letters of
`3^a` the letters of `-5` (word `(110)^inf`, no coefficient descent ever,
S13's `E_inf`), so `3^a` has no coefficient descent within `k` steps for
every `a` in the class `log_3(-5) mod 2^(k-2)`, where `log_3(-5) in Z_2` is
the unique 2-adic integer with `3^(log_3(-5)) = -5` (digits computed in the
script: `1, 3, 3, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 267, 267, 1291, 3339, ...`). ∎

**What is new against the coverage note of 2026-10-04.** That note found
the powers of three to be a hostile family for the early-reroute grammar
("`9^a >= 32 u^3`: a source-size versus ternary-valuation obstruction at all
depths") and left the exponent class `a = 3 mod 8` as the residual of its
three tests. Proposition E identifies that class: it is the `-5` shadow
(`3^a = 27 = -5 mod 32` for `a = 3 mod 8`), and it refines for ever
(`11 mod 128`, `267 mod 512`, ...). It also says the powers of three are
*gentler* than generic integers in one respect: they never shadow `-1`, the
only cycle point whose shadow buys the maximal growth `log_2 3 - 1` per bit.

**The share.** Among the no-descent residues, those in `<3>` are those whose
word begins `110` rather than `111`. Under the zero-drift critical tilt (odd
letters with probability `log_3 2`) a third letter `0` has probability
`1 - log_3 2 = 0.369`, but the no-descent words are a walk conditioned to
stay positive, which favours the higher start `111`; the measured limit
`0.1597` is that conditioning. With `h` the harmonic function of the killed
tilted walk (the renewal function of THM-4499's Lemma M), the limit should
be `(1-r) h(y_0) / ((1-r) h(y_0) + r h(y_1))`, `y_0 = 2 log_2 3 - 3 = 0.170`,
`y_1 = 3 log_2 3 - 3 = 1.755`, `r = log_3 2`; the data give `h(y_0)/h(y_1) =
0.3249`, i.e. `h(y) ~ y + 0.59` if `h` were affine at these heights.

**Non-consequences.** Nothing here certifies or refutes any orbit; the
resisting classes are no-descent classes, not divergent ones (THM-4512's
one-member bound still applies to each exact word class).
