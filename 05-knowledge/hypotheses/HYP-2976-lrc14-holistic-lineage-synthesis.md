# HYP-2976: LRC14 Holistic Lineage Synthesis

This hypothesis now fills the reserved S160 synthesis namespace with a mined
lineage map and proof-object convergence claim.  It is not a new scalar
certificate and not a proof of LRC14.  Its role is to explain how the project's
understanding changed from early tournament/observer quotients through the
current labelled-packet, boundary-moment, state-lift, and dual-certificate
cluster, and to state the present proof object precisely enough to drive the
next computation.

## Current Claim

The project has converged from runner-set scalarization toward a finite
proof-object sheaf:

```text
proof-object sheaf whose fibers retain exact scale, open-vs-boundary status,
endpoint owners, C27/unital/K33 labels, fixed-margin family data, and dual
certificates.  Failed proof routes are useful precisely when they identify a
quotient that destroys one of these fibers.
```

```text

all q/Farey/Haar fields, C27/unital/K33 labels, boundary-moment charts,
state-lift obligations, and dual-certificate cones are attached.
```

Equivalently, every primitive 13-speed row should route to a q witness, an
AP/Goddyn-Wong equality atom, a unit-petal/K33/state-lift exit, a positive
boundary/lift/moment packet, or a dual obstruction.

## Phase Ledger

### P0: Endpoint And Tournament Quotients

The first stable LRC14 picture was not "find the best scalar" but "a
counterexample must cover all witness intervals."  HYP-1802 and its endpoint
descendants already made the proof boundary finite and combinatorial: every
endpoint must be protected, and every gap has an owner.  This was the first
version of a boundary sheaf.

The A000568 and tournament line then tested whether raw tournament structure
could be the missing invariant.  HYP-1977 refuted the coarse version: raw and
even observer-pointed tournament classes mix safe and unsafe rows.  HYP-2486
kept the useful part by lifting to the observer-source fiber.  The lesson was
durable: a tournament quotient is useful only after threshold, source, gap,
endpoint, and scale labels have been attached.

### P1: Invariants, Sheaves, And Finite Endpoint Feasibility

HYP-2596 is the key negative result of this phase.  Direct finite endpoint
enumeration is not the proof: the shape count below useful bounds is enormous,
and naive `q=14V` endpoint checks already fail on small rows.  The finite
problem has to be structurally reduced before enumeration.

This phase also clarifies what "finite" should mean.  It is not a flat list of
endpoint patterns.  It is a finite atlas of typed packets: exact scale, endpoint
owner, boundary status, and local obstruction type.

### P2: AP Windows, Sector Routes, And Far-Tail Refinement

The AP and sector phase made two lasting contributions.

First, AP/Goddyn-Wong are equality atoms, not counterexamples.  Every later
classifier has to reserve a boundary atom bucket for them.  Second, the
wide/gK8 route showed that positive mass is often best proved by second-order
or higher-order structure, not by a raw pair count.

The sector/far-tail work also generated several guardrails.  Scalar additive
energy and low moments see pressure but lose packet ownership.  Same-frequency
and gK8 tails need depth labels.  Far-tail failures are not random discrepancy
events; the hard rows are aligned packet pockets.

### P3: Wide/gK8, Analytic Residuals, And The H=7 Guardrail

The wide/gK8 era separated analytic mass routes from low-frontier packet routes.
Positive p0/gK8 evidence remains essential, but it cannot classify AP/GW,
K33, unit petals, or exact boundary atoms by itself.

HYP-2908 is the main categorical guardrail: the number `7` is not enough.  A
bad atom must live in the tournament-state category, not in an arbitrary
digraph with the same scalar count.  This is why later K33/H=7 language is
state-lift language rather than numerology.

HYP-2901 also blocks a tempting shortcut: no fixed finite denominator ladder
can prove all of LRC14.  Any twist or Farey route must be dynamic or packet
conditioned.

### P4: Source-Spectrum, Farey, C27, Unital, And K33 Labels

HYP-2937, HYP-2947, HYP-2948, HYP-2949, HYP-2953, and HYP-2954 turned the low
frontier into a typed packet object.  The key map is:

```text
primitive reduced residual
  -> exact M/Farey branch
  -> Haar-Baire open/boundary front
  -> C27/unital/K33 owner address
  -> discharge, AP/GW atom, covering strictness, or state-lift.
```

The unital, Clebsch, half-cube, K33, octahedral, and C27 analogies are useful
only when they carry labels in this map.  Literal graph or design isomorphism
is too coarse.  The design object is a packet address; it is not the LRC
predicate.

### P5: Packet Classifiers, Families, Sporadics, And Boundary Moments

HYP-2956, HYP-2961, HYP-2962, and HYP-2963 promoted the user's "labelled packet
theorem" into an executable proof interface.  The important definition shift is
this:

```text
family = fixed-margin labelled packet class
sporadic = bounded singleton packet after family coordinates are removed
```

The HYP-2963 audit emits packets retaining exact `M`, binding denominators,
`q_threshold`, Farey excess, strict Haar/Baire mass, boundary debt, C27
transfer, K33/state-lift flag, and covering/source family.  Its bounded bank
has no unknown packets.

HYP-2965 through HYP-2969 then refined the live covering branch.  The boundary
bridge cannot be raw divergence: all 1187 audited covering rows in HYP-2965
have zero first endpoint current while still being positive-open.  The
boundary-moment theorem must therefore be labelled and multi-chart.  One
all-covered denominator chart is not an obstruction.

THM-571 removes the apex-majority branch.  The remaining Moon core is
few-apex, packet-labelled, and boundary-moment shaped.

### P6: Dual-Certificate Cluster

HYP-2970 through HYP-2974 are best read as projections of one
cover/noncover sheaf:

```text
HYP-2970 endpoint-credit potential:
  open danger-arc cover <-> winding cycle; dual potential forbids the cycle.

HYP-2971 multiplicity-moment dual:
  positive strict lonely mass is Pr[X=0]>0 for X(t)=#{v: ||vt||<1/14}.

HYP-2972 twist-ladder dual:
  rational twists are primal witnesses; failed ladders are blocker hypergraphs.

HYP-2973 danger-count moment dual:
  N(t)=#{s: ||s t||<1/14}; polynomial barriers certify safe mass.

HYP-2974 Fourier-Toeplitz dual:
  if C_S-1 is nonnegative, every Toeplitz moment matrix is PSD;
  negative eigenvalues are harmonic dual certificates.
```

These routes are not competing with the packet theorem.  They are certificates
available after a packet has forgotten some geometry.  The right proof should
glue them back to endpoint owners, packet labels, and state-lift exits.

HYP-2975 is the local bridge: a taut endpoint-owner graph between Haar/Baire
interval fronts and endpoint/Fourier dual routes.

## Counterexample-Family Squeeze

A strict counterexample now has to pass all of these gates:

```text
01. primitive normalization
    work with 13 nonzero speeds at total n=14.

02. q-clock witness
    qdiv < 14 gives a direct strict phase.

03. AP/GW boundary atoms
    qdiv = 14 and zero-open routes to known equality atoms.

04. positive Haar/Baire open front
    any strict interval front discharges the row.

05. unit petal / two-block discharge
    C27 unit-petal packets are positive-open.

06. K33 / state-lift obligation
    nonunit K33 packets are not scalar rows; route to THM-572.

07. THM-571 apex-majority discharge
    |14Z cap S| >= 7 leaves the live Moon core.

08. few-apex lift packet
    1 <= |14Z cap S| <= 6 must have positive lift mass or state-lift.

09. NORK pinch atlas
    non-AP/GW zero-open residuals have not appeared in the large local bank.

10. Ramanujan exact-period packet labels
    scalar divisor signatures must refine to primitive cyclotomic/Fourier
    endpoint labels.

11. boundary-moment ledger
    covering packets need a labelled multi-chart moment bridge.

12. dual certificate wall
    moment, twist, endpoint, and Toeplitz duals certify all positive audited
    hard rows.
```

The only live bad object is therefore highly constrained:

```text
qdiv >= 14,
not AP/GW,
zero strict Haar-open mass,
not unit-petal,
not K33/state-lift-owned in the current sense,
not apex-majority,
few-apex or covering-moment shaped,
not NORK in the audited local banks,
not merely a scalar divisor-signature collision,
and invisible to all known dual projections.
```

That object is so overdetermined that the strongest current conjecture is that
it is impossible unless it collapses back to AP/GW or to the named state-lift
boundary.

## Connections To Prompt Themes

The Farey-sequence variations, `n*2` and `n+2` recursion, and flower-angle
prompts all point to the same retained-coordinate issue: changing numerator,
denominator, or exponent produces a useful movie only when the proof remembers
which coordinate is being pulled back.  In the LRC14 proof, the corresponding
coordinates are exact denominator, Farey excess, qdiv, C27 shell, and endpoint
owner.  The raw scalar value is analogous to seeing only the petal count while
forgetting the turn angle.

The `22/7` and cube-root-of-31 prompts are useful as scale warnings rather than
as numeric claims.  Good approximations can be proof-relevant when they mark a
Farey boundary or a low-denominator packet.  They are dangerous if treated as
standalone numerology.  The repo's corrected version is: retain approximation
quality only as an address in a Farey/packet fiber.

The unital prompt split into two lessons.  The combinatorial unital is a design
address, useful for pair-slot and C27/K33 packet labels.  The algebraic unital
notion is a unit-preserving map warning: the proof functor has to send the
identity witness/boundary atom to the correct identity object in the target
packet category.  A non-unital quotient is exactly the kind of map that turns
AP/GW, petals, and K33 packets into indistinguishable scalar shadows.

The incoming HYP-2978/HYP-2979 Ramanujan lane sharpens the same guardrail:
divisor signatures, Ramanujan sums, cyclotomic packets, and exact-period
projectors are admissible proof quotients only when they retain the labels that
distinguish AP/GW boundary atoms, Toeplitz/Ramanujan exits, and K33/state-lift
debts.

The Paris-Harrington, Beurling-Selberg, elongated square gyrobicupola,
octahedral graph, Clebsch graph, half-cube, Paley-Zygmund, Borel/Baire/Haar,
and Beurling-Selberg-minorant themes all fit as carrier tests:

```text
Paris-Harrington:
  extension-rank and bad-child rank, not raw density.

Beurling-Selberg minorants:
  analytic dual witnesses must be glued to endpoint packets.

Elongated square gyrobicupola / octahedral / Clebsch / half-cube:
  finite incidence carriers are useful only as labelled local state spaces.

Paley-Zygmund:
  positive-mass arguments need moment information plus anti-concentration
  labels; moments alone are too coarse.

Borel/Baire/Haar:
  strict-open, closed-boundary, and measure-positive are distinct proof states.
```

```text
5. Dual completeness after labels.
   Endpoint-credit, multiplicity-moment, danger-count, twist, and
   Fourier-Toeplitz dual certificates cover every positive hard packet once
   AP/GW and named state-lift/petal exits are removed.

6. Sheaf gluing.
   The local discharges agree on overlaps: a row cannot evade qdiv, Haar,
   endpoint, packet, state-lift, and dual certificates by moving between
   quotient charts.
```

If these hold, the proof route is complete.  A disproof would now have to
produce a concrete packet violating one of these obligations, not merely a row
that fools a scalar quotient.

## Final Synthesis

The missing picture is not a better number.  It is a proof-object category in
which every quotient map is allowed only after declaring what LRC predicate it
preserves and what information it destroys.

The repo's history has made that category visible.  Endpoint covers supplied
the boundary sheaf.  A000568 supplied the quotient-refutation method.
Haar/Baire supplied the topology.  Farey/qdiv supplied the scale.  C27/unital
and K33 supplied typed low-frontier labels.  Fixed-margin packets supplied
family/sporadic language.  Boundary moments supplied the covering residual.
The dual cluster supplied Farkas-style projections of the same cover/noncover
obstruction.

The most fundamental object is therefore:

```text
labelled proof packet over a Farey/Haar endpoint sheaf.
```

That is the object HYP-2976 proposes to prove with, or to falsify by producing
a packet that survives every declared exit.
