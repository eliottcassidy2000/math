# HYP-9260 — the Collatz transfer law on the Riemann sphere

## Statements

1. **(Rational function and winding curve; PROVED.)** `R(s) = 1/(1 - x - y)`, `x = zeta^alpha e^(-s)/2`, `y = zeta^(alpha+beta) e^(s(theta-1))/2`, is the restriction of the rational function `1/(1 - x - y)` on `P^1 x P^1` (polar divisor the `(1,1)`-curve `x + y = 1`, zero divisor `{x = infinity} + {y = infinity}`, meeting at the indeterminacy point `(infinity, infinity)`; away from it the function is the constant `infinity` on the polar curve) to the curve `Gamma_theta`; on `Re s = 0` the curve winds densely on the torus `|x| = |y| = 1/2`, which meets the polar line only at `(1/2, 1/2)`; the lines of THM-4629 are the Diophantine near-approaches of the curve to that point.
2. **(Rational approximants; PROVED, FINITE-EXACT, NUMERICAL.)** For `theta' = P/Q` (a convergent or semiconvergent of `log2 3`; `(94,149)` below is the semiconvergent `(41,65) + (53,84)`, MISTAKE-602) the restriction is `R_(P/Q)(w) = w^(P-Q)/p(w)`, `p(w) = w^(P-Q) - zeta^alpha w^P/2 - zeta^(alpha+beta)/2`, `w = e^(-s/Q)`, a rational function of degree `P` on the sphere; `p(zeta^(-1)) = 0` iff `Q = alpha` and `P = -beta (mod m)` (the average of two unit vectors has modulus one only when they coincide), and more generally `p(zeta^j) = 0` iff `jQ = -alpha` and `jP = beta (mod m)` (for `m > 2` the circle pole of a convergent may sit at another root of unity, the conjugate character's line; for `m = 2` nothing is lost; audit) (an exact pole on the unit circle, verified: `p(-1) = 0` exactly for `tau` at `(5,8)`, `(53,84)`, `omega` at `(12,19)`, `(94,149)`, `C` at `(41,65)`; `+-1`, `+-2` for the other ten pairs), and the irrational law's pole is `zeta^(-1) e^(-w_THM/Q)`. The approximants' near-circle poles reproduce the exact lines (at `Q = 53`, `tau`: `14.4680/0.9048`, `2.4549/0.8082`, `9.5519/0.7370`, `21.5556/0.6821` against `14.4689/0.9097`, `2.4549/0.8088`, `9.5520/0.7353`, `21.5558/0.6789`) and carry poles inside the circle near the growing root `w = ln 2` of the root equation (NUMERICAL, rough at small `Q`).
3. **(Unfolding; the model's root equation at `j theta_f`, NUMERICAL for the integers.)** The approximant identifies `f` modulo `Q`; the exact law separates the multiples `(jQ, jP)` with defects `j theta_f`: for the Mercator pole the lines `26.5, 79.5, 132.5, 185.5, 238.5` decay at `0.9988, 0.9896, 0.9744, 0.9563, 0.9374` per octave, quadratic in `j` in the exponent to leading order only (the quadratic law gives `0.9035` at `j = 9`) (measured `0.9997`, `0.9892` for the first two; census amplitudes `0.354, 0.101, 0.036, 0.014`, twice the positive-frequency coefficients of the census output).
4. **(The constant infinity; PROVED, NUMERICAL, CITED.)** `|R(2 pi i Q/m)| = 1/(pi |theta_f|)(1 + O(theta_f^2))`: `8.49, 17.65, 211.3, 70.4, 10108` at `f = 2.5, 14.5, 26.5, 79.5, 332.5` for `(-1)^tau`; the maximum of `|R|` on the line near a convergent is at the drifted line frequency of THM-4629 and equals the inverse distance of the pole from the line, `sup |R| = 2/((2 - theta)|Re w_THM|)(1 + O(theta_f^2)) = (2 - theta)^2/(2 pi^2 theta_f^2)(1 + O(theta_f))` (measured `3861, 441, 8.80e6` at the `53`-, `159`-, `665`-lines against `3882, 462, 8.80e6`, while at the coarse convergents `(5,8)`, `(29,46)` the heights `12.3`, `36.5` are `0.54`, `0.72` of the pole formula, the `O(theta_f^2)` coefficient being large; peak positions at the THM-4629 drifts to `1e-5`). Line bound (PROVED): with `phi, psi` the principal arguments of `2x, 2y`, `|R(2 pi i f)| <= pi^2/(phi^2 + psi^2) <= theta^2/(4 theta_f(f)^2)`, `theta_f(f) = (Q theta - P)/m` for the integers `Q = alpha`, `P = -beta (mod m)` that `f` points at (`|Q - m f| <= m/2`); no violation on `4 000 001` frequencies in `(0, 400]`, minimum slack `2.0`. With Rhin's measure (CITED from memory in the Eliahou/Simons-de Weger form `|P ln 2 - Q ln 3| > Q^(-13.3)`, hypothesis on `Q` or on `max(P, Q)` not verified) the exact function of a nontrivial character on `Re s = 0` is finite everywhere, unbounded along the convergents, and polynomially bounded, `|R(2 pi i f)| <= C f^(26.6)`: a tempered distribution. THM-4632 (S31) makes the exponent exact for the trivial character: `limsup log|R|/log f = 2(mu(theta) - 1)`, so `26.6` is Rhin's `mu <= 14.3` and the conjectural value `mu = 2` is the exponent `2`.
5. **(Moebius dictionary; PROVED.)** The word with `Q` odd steps among `P` steps is `z -> (3^Q z + c_w)/2^P` in `PGL(2, Q)`, fixing `infinity` (the common fixed point of the semigroup) with finite fixed point `x_w = c_w/(2^P - 3^Q)` and multiplier `3^Q/2^P`, `ln(3^Q/2^P) = m theta_f ln 2`; near-returns are the nearly parabolic words, THM-4629's decay is `exp(-4 pi^2 (ln lambda)^2/(m^2 (ln 2)^2 (2 - theta)^3))`, no word is parabolic (`2^P != 3^Q`), and the fixed points of the resonant words tend to `infinity`.
6. **(The covariance Moebius map; PROVED model, NUMERICAL.)** THM-4630's equations give the generation update `rho -> (rho - 1)/(3 - rho)`, the Moebius map with matrix `[[1, -1], [-1, 3]]`, fixed points `1 -+ sqrt 2`, attracting multiplier `3 - 2 sqrt 2 = (sqrt 2 - 1)^2`; two generations compose to `[[3, 4], [2, 3]]`, the matrix of multiplication by the Pell unit `3 + 2 sqrt 2` on `X + Y sqrt 2` and of the square-triangular recursion, acting projectively on the covariance state (Codex first, section 6, with the variance law `V_k/V_0 = A ((2 + sqrt 2)/4)^k + B ((2 - sqrt 2)/4)^k`; a covariance state is not an integer Pell solution). 7. **(Conway constant; CITED/PROVED by Codex; correction.)** `sqrt(k) A_k -> C_* = 0.1380149023990716...` with the explicit eigenprofile `u = x g = phi_N(z)/2`, `x = 1 + Phi(z)` solving `u u'' = -1/4`; recomputed to thirty digits (NUMERICAL); measured `sqrt(k) A_k - C_* = c/k` with `c = 0.16-0.20`; the lagged continuum map of S27 is `0.9%` below the certified bracket at `k = 400` and about `3%` above it near `k = 128` (sign-changing, no fixed offset; its maximum position scatters around `x_*`), and the S27 inference "at most 0.137" was invalid (MISTAKE-600).

## Why

The owner asked for the field of rational functions on the Riemann sphere and the constant function `infinity`. The exact transfer law of THM-4629 is a transcendental limit of rational functions on the sphere, one per convergent of `log2 3`, each with a genuine pole on the unit circle where the exact law has a line; the covariance dynamics of the Hofstadter jitter (THM-4630) is itself a Moebius map with the silver fixed point and the square-triangular Pell matrix; and the words of the Collatz semigroup are affine Moebius maps whose only common fixed point, `infinity`, is where the fixed points of the resonant words go. The constant `infinity` is the value on the polar line, the pole of every approximant, and the tempered unboundedness of the exact function (peaks of height `(2 - theta)^2/(2 pi^2 theta_f^2)` at the drifted line frequencies, the inverse distances of the poles from the line).
