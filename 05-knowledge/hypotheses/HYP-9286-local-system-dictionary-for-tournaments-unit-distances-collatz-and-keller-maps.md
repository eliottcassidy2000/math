## (b) Conjecture

Every series-parallel pattern count of `G_p` is a sum over norm classes `c` of products of the radial walk functions
`R_k(c)` and codegree factors — so it is a moment, possibly twisted, of the Kloosterman and open-chain local systems.
The conjecture is that these moments are **modular**: for every series-parallel graph `H` with a root, `hom_0(H, G_p)`
is, for primes `p ≥ p_0(H)`, a polynomial in `p` and Legendre symbols, plus a `Z[p]`-combination of Fourier
coefficients of finitely many holomorphic newforms (and their Frobenius-power traces at prime powers) whose levels
involve only small primes (so far 2, 3, 5, 7: levels 15, 6, 525 = 3·5^2·7, 30). Verified so far: `W_k` for `k ≤ 8`
except `W_7` (range-tested only), the polynomial thetas, `θ(2,2,3)` (level 15 and 30a1), and `θ(3,3,3)` through the
third moment of the four-bar surface (weight 5, level 6, character `(-3/·)`; THM-4663 (10)). The untwisted four-bar
moments are modular by Eichler-Shimura for `X_1(6)`; only the twisted moments remain open. The content is modularity of every moment that occurs; a
level bound in terms of `H` is part of the problem.

**Falsifier.** A series-parallel `H` whose count needs a motive outside the span, e.g. if the third moment
`Σ_c a_c^3` of the four-bar surface (needed by `θ(3,3,3)`) is not a combination of the listed modular data and
polynomials.

**Status of the test (S39).** `θ(3,3,3) = Σ_d R_3(d)^3` reduces to the third moment of the four-bar surface, which the
audit identified: `Σ_c a_c^3 = -(1 + (p/3))(4p + 2) - tr(T_p | S_5(Γ_0(6), (-3/·)))` (PROVED by Eichler-Shimura-Deligne;
exact at 121 primes) — a positive case of (b). `θ(2,3,3) = Σ_d R_2(d) R_3(d)^2` reduces to the `η(c(c-4))`-twisted
second moment, which lives on a 6-dimensional weight-3 space (observed size up to `2.92 p^(3/2)`); OPEN. The twisted FIRST
moment is the pentagon form (THM-4663 (10)): the conic base change of the four-bar surface is a K3 with CM by
`Q(sqrt(-15))`, which is how the Kloosterman fifth moment acquires its CM form. A structural caveat for the twisted
second moment: the conic `s^2 = c(c - 4)` is branched at the cusp `c = 0` and at the ordinary point `c = 4` of
`X_1(6)`, so the base change does not come from a subgroup of `Γ_1(6)`; its first moment is still modular because the K3
has Picard number 20, but nothing forces small level for the second. **Test plan.** `θ(2,3,3)` and `θ(3,3,3)` at all primes below 1000 by the radial Kloosterman evaluation;
fit against polynomials and the listed forms; if a residual remains, identify it (a weight-5 or weight-6 form of level
dividing a power of 6?).

## (c) Row J prediction (CONJECTURE)

If a planar Keller pair `F = (P, Q) ∈ Z[x, y]^2` (`det JF = 1`) is not an automorphism, then for all large `p` its
exponential sums `S(a, b) = Σ_(x ∈ F_p^2) e_p(aP + bQ)` are of size `≍ p^(3/2)` or larger only on the finitely many lines
of frequencies dual to the points at infinity of its Jelonek curve, and `O(p)` elsewhere (the planar form of THM-4664;
the dimension-3 counterexample realizes the analogue with `|S| ≈ p^2` on the dual cone). Combined with the planar
criterion (the sums vanish identically iff `F` is an automorphism), the planar conjecture asks that these spectral
lines be empty. Status: CONJECTURE (no planar candidate exists to test).

The supporting dimension-3 calculation is now uniform: [K1-K3 of the marked cubic proof](../results/keller_marked_cubic_fourier_20261017d.md)
evaluate THM-4664's remainders exactly over odd finite fields and prove the dual-cone energy formula for
characteristic at least 5. This closes that auxiliary target, without promoting the planar prediction above.

## (d) What is not claimed

No colouring bound for `G_q` or `R^2`; no statement about Collatz convergence (the finite places carry no
obstruction); no planar Keller counterexample or proof (the finite-field criterion is standard).
