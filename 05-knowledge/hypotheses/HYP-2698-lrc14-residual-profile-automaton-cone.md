# HYP-2698 - Residual-Profile Automaton Cone

## Claim Stub

The residual profiles in HYP-2697 should be represented as generated words

```text
x -> w_x(R),    R subset {1,...,6},
```

not merely as arbitrary averaged vectors in `Q^64`.  At fixed slow coordinate
`x`, adding an independent carrier updates the residual mask distribution by a
finite OR/deletion automaton on the 64 sector masks.  The proof target is to
characterize the cone generated by these automaton words and prove the
consecutive-block compression inequality against that generated cone.

This is a namespace claim for the S63 scout.  The live computation will test
which coherent-context profiles are generated, how they differ from arbitrary
positive residual weights, and whether the S62 coordinatewise counterexamples
still lose against actual generated context words.

## Rule-110 Analogy

The analogy is structural, not literal: Rule 110 is useful because a simple
binary local update generates a constrained language of space-time words rather
than arbitrary binary arrays.  Here the local update is sector-mask OR
convolution / residual deletion; tournaments likewise evolve by local edge-bit
choices.  The intended transfer is "keep the generated word and update law
until the proof scalar is forced," not "replace LRC by Rule 110."
