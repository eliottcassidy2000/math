# HYP-9261 — graph carriers of the repository's threads

## Statements

1. **(Dictionary; ANALOGY, a classification.)** Every thread is a graph with one extra structure, and the open question is global: functional graphs with a measure (Collatz, base-`p`, Matthews-Watts; coalescence, Polya trichotomy), the Pascal graph with a measure or a code (Conway, prices, refuel bills, Landau), the Farey graph with a geodesic (the lines of THM-4629, the Moebius words, Pell), Cayley/de Bruijn graphs with a character (THM-4631, Terras, Paley, Kloosterman), graphs with a colouring or a certificate (Hadwiger-Nelson, chess, the carry cocycle), and polytopes/surfaces/line graphs (noble polyhedra, Petersen, glued boards). Section 1 of the note gives the table with sources and statuses.
2. **(The Cayley carrier; PROVED, THM-4631.)** For every odd prime `p`, `det(I - M_(j,k)(s)) = [1 - u/2 - v/2] prod_(i <= k) [1 - (u/2)^(L_i)]^(phi(p^i)/L_i)`, `L_i = ord_(p^i)(2)`: the fast modes are the doubling cycles on `(Z/p^i)^x`, with multiplicity the index of `<2>`; one at every level iff `2` is a primitive root modulo `p^2` (`3, 5, 11, 13, 19, 29, 37, 53, 59, 61, 67, 83` below `100`); the Wieferich primes `1093`, `3511` jump by the factor `p` at level 2; `p = 7` splits along `{1,2,4} | {3,5,6}`; the two-cycle primes are `7, 17, 23, 41, 47, 71, 79, 97` and the Paley ones among them `7, 23, 47, 71, 79`.
3. **(The Farey colouring; PROVED.)** The signed primitive pair `(Q, P) mod m` (`Q >= 0`) properly colours the Farey graph (Farey neighbours are unimodular pairs, hence distinct modulo `m`, even a basis of `(Z/m)^2`); for `m = 2` the three colours are the points of `P^1(F_2)` and the colouring is the classical one. A primitive line `(Q, P)` of THM-4629 is a Farey vertex and resonates with `(alpha, beta) = (Q, -P) mod m` and its conjugate, so the colour of the vertex determines the resonant pair through `P -> -P` (the character itself at `m = 2`); a non-primitive line `j(Q, P)` is the `j`-th unfolding of HYP-9260 and carries `j` times the colour. The record lines (minimal defect `|Q theta - P|` up to `Q`) are exactly the convergents `P_n/Q_n`, `n >= 1`, of `log2 3` (Lagrange, CITED; checked to `Q <= 20000`); the Farey triangles crossed by the geodesic have the convergents and semiconvergents as vertices (Series, CITED); but most census lines are neither: of the `80` lines of the `m = 2` census, `5` are convergents, `7` semiconvergents, `15` non-reduced, `53` other Farey vertices (`46` of them neighbours of a convergent or semiconvergent). Hence consecutive convergents, and consecutive vertices of every fan, never share a character (the fan around `84/53` alternates `C` and `omega`), while census lines consecutive in `Q` can (`(600,951), (624,989)`); for `m = 2` the three characters `tau, omega, C` are the three colour classes and the resonant character walks on `K_3` along the geodesic.
4. **(The census; NUMERICAL, OPEN as a theorem.)** Over `2599` convergents of `log2 3` (continued fraction validated at `dps 4500` and `5200`; `Q_2599` has `1324` digits) the residues `(Q_n, P_n) mod m` are consistent with equidistribution over the primitive pairs: `chi^2 = 0.2, 6.7, 5.4, 15.7, 31.4, 34.2` on `2, 7, 11, 23, 23, 47` degrees of freedom for `m = 2..7`; the three characters modulo `2` receive `0.3305, 0.3371, 0.3324`; the longest run of one character is `1`; the partial quotients follow Gauss-Kuzmin to `0.0044` (`0.4194, 0.1674, 0.0920, 0.0612` against `0.4150, 0.1699, 0.0931, 0.0589`). Equidistribution holds for almost every real number (Jager-Liardet, CITED from memory); for `log2 3` it is open.
5. **(The de Bruijn carrier; PROVED.)** The Collatz class graph modulo `2^j` is the de Bruijn graph `B(2, j)` (Terras); THM-4629's even-class averaging `E_j` has the single nonzero eigenvalue `1/2` and `rank(E_j^q) = 2^(j-q)` (`q <= j`): rank one plus nilpotent of index `j`. The 2-adic refinement is exact and spectrally empty at every depth; the `p`-adic refinement is a permutation at every depth and spectrally full (statement 2).
6. **(The observer lens is the symmetry axis; ANALOGY.)** Rigid carriers (the Collatz graph, THM-4523; the chess graph; the `Q` trees) have local views that identify the observer and global questions; transitive carriers (Farey, Cayley, Paley, the 2-shift) have uninformative local views and global answers (geodesics, spectra). THM-4629 and THM-4631 transport the Collatz question to transitive carriers; the `3`-adic closure is the step back, and THM-4523's `Aut = 1` suggests, as an inference, that no symmetry makes it.
7. **(Numerology watch; NUMEROLOGY.)** The `Q` resonator polynomial `2 z^2 + z + 1` is the reciprocal of `lam^2 + lam + 2`, the Paley-7 eigenvalue polynomial and the minimal polynomial of a prime above `2` in `Q(sqrt(-7))`; a doubly regular tournament on `n` vertices has `lam^2 + lam + (n+1)/4`, so the coincidence is `(n+1)/4 = 2`, `n = 7` only; no bijection.
