# HYP-9244 — the Polya trichotomy for Haar coalescence

## Setting

* **The map.** `d ≥ 2`. For each residue `i mod d` there is a branch `T(x) = (m_i x + r_i)/d` on `x ≡ i mod d`, where:
  * `m_i ≥ 1` is prime to `d`;
  * `d | m_i i + r_i`.
* **Haar digits.** Each branch maps its class onto `Z_d`. So under Haar measure the branch sequence of `y` is i.i.d. uniform: the d-adic Terras property.
* **The pair chain.** It is written `u = M v + e`.
  * If `v` takes branch `j` and `u` takes branch `i`, then
    `M' = M m_i/m_j` and `e' = (m_i e + r_i − (m_i/m_j) M r_j)/d`.
  * `j` is a fresh uniform digit, and `i ≡ M j + e (mod d)`.
  * The state `(M, e) = (1, 0)` is an equal-time merge. A merge with `M ≠ 1` is null, because it pins `v` to a fixed point.
* **The debt group.** `Γ ⊂ Q_(>0)^×` is generated by the ratios `m_i/m_j`, and `ρ = rank Γ ≤ d − 1`.
* **The contraction exponent.** `Λ = (1/d) Σ_i ln(m_i/d)`.
* **The debt walk.** `M_n` is a mean-zero walk on `Γ ≅ Z^ρ`. Both marginal digits are uniform, so `E[ln(m_i/m_j)] = 0`.
  * Its step law at each time is one of finitely many laws: the images of the uniform law under the coupling permutations `j ↦ M j + e (mod d)`.
  * Which law is used depends on `(M, e) mod d`, i.e. on the past.

## The obstruction lemma (PROVED; found by audit F)

**Statement.** Let `ℓ` be a prime not dividing `d·Π m_i`. Suppose some `c ∈ Z_ℓ` satisfies `(m_i − d)c + r_i ≡ 0 (mod ℓ)` for every branch `i`. Then along the chain from `(1, e_0)`,

    e_n − (1 − M_n)c ≡ (Π_(k<n) m_(i_k)/d) · e_0   (mod ℓ).

So if `ℓ ∤ e_0`, the chain never reaches `(1, 0)`: `y` and `y + e_0` never meet.

**Proof.**
* Substitute `r_i ≡ (d − m_i)c` and `r_j ≡ (d − m_j)c` into `e' − (1 − M')c`. It equals `(m_i/d)(e − (1 − M)c)` mod `ℓ`, and `m_i/d` is an `ℓ`-adic unit.
* By induction the claim holds. At `M_n = 1` it says `e_n ≢ 0`. ∎

**Examples (audit F; zero meetings in 400 direct samples to T = 4096 each).**

| map | rank | obstruction |
|---|---|---|
| `3x + 5` on `Z_2`, offset 1 | 1 | `ℓ = 5`, `c = 0` |
| `Z_3`: m = (1,1,5), r = (0,2,2) | 1 | parity |
| `Z_3`: m = (1,2,5), r = (0,7,14) | 2 | `ℓ = 7` |
| `Z_3`: m = (1,2,5), r = (9,1,5) | 2 | affine, `c = 1` |
| `Z_5`: m = (1,2,3,7,1), r ≡ 0 mod 11 | 3 | `ℓ = 11` |

**Consequences.**
* For `3x + r`: `y` and `y + e` merge almost surely iff `r/3^(v_3 r)` divides `e`. This uses THM-4581 3′ and the conjugacy `x ↦ rx`.
* Under `3x + 1` itself, `y` and `y + 1/5` never meet.
* None of the ten maps in the evidence table has an obstruction for any `ℓ < 2000` (audit F, `obstruction_scan.out`).

## Statement

* **Accessibility.** Restrict to starts `(1, e_0)` from which `(1, 0)` is accessible. That means no congruence obstruction of the lemma's kind (with `ℓ`-powers), and, in rank 0, a finite residual `e`-dynamics that reaches 0.
* **The trichotomy.**

  | regime | P(no equal-time meeting by T) |
  |---|---|
  | `Λ > 0` (expanding), any `ρ` | tends to a positive limit |
  | `Λ < 0`, `ρ = 0` | `≤ C e^(−cT)` |
  | `Λ < 0`, `ρ = 1` | `≍ T^(−1/2)` |
  | `Λ < 0`, `ρ = 2` | `≍ 1/log T` |
  | `Λ < 0`, `ρ ≥ 3` | tends to a positive limit |

* **The recurrence premise.** In the last two contracting rows, the mechanism needs the debt walk's coupling state `(M mod d, e mod d)` to equidistribute independently of the debt, so that the walk is effectively a non-degenerate `ρ`-dimensional walk.
  * Audit F's anisotropy test is consistent with this on five maps, and suggests it as a route to a proof.
* **Mechanism.**
  * The offset `e`, normalized by `max(M, 1)`, is multiplied at each step by a fresh uniform multiplier `m/d`, whose mean log is `Λ`.
  * Absorption needs the debt walk to return to `M = 1`, at an accessible residue.
  * So coalescence = accessibility × contraction × recurrence.
  * The tail is the first-return tail of the debt walk times a geometric number of returns. In rank 2 the Erdős–Taylor exponential law of the local time gives `1/log T`.

* **Rank 2 against rank 3.**
  * In rank 2 the visit window stays flat. That is the log-recurrence signature, and `q` keeps falling (`1/q` linear in `log T`).
  * In rank 3 the window decays like `T^(−1/2)` and `q` freezes.
  * Weakly contracting rank-2 maps can have nearly flat `q` at accessible `T`: `Z_3` (1,2,11) goes 0.757 → 0.735 between `T = 1024` and 65536, with its window flat at 0.55–0.66. So the window, not `q` alone, separates rank 2 from rank 3.
* **The expanding rows show the second factor alone.** Their debt walks are recurrent (visits `~√T` or `~log T`), but the offset expands.

## Reading

* **Collatz is rank one in its natural clocks** (DICTIONARY; HEURISTIC where noted).
  * In Terras time the equal-time debt group is `3^Z`. The powers of 2 are spent on the clock.
  * In the odd-step clock it is `2^Z`; that map has infinitely many branches, outside this class.
  * With free times, THM-4581 6(b) reduces grand orbits to the same rank-one case.
  * So, for offsets in `Z[1/3]`, Collatz coalescence is a one-dimensional null-recurrent debt walk with a contracting offset. Its signature is the `T^(−1/2)` tail; THM-4581's upper bound for that tail is at sketch level.
  * HEURISTIC: the orphan law's exponents (HYP-9242: 0.42–0.63 measured; 1/2 for a sketch-level upper bound) are this first-return exponent, modified by several pursuers. A rank-two analogue would have orphans decaying like `1/log`; a rank-three one, a positive orphan density.
* **P12** (ANALOGY). The atlas's rank-two principle (2 and 3 multiplicatively independent) splits here as clock + debt = 1 + 1. For maps on `Z_d` the debt rank is at most `d − 1`. A larger debt rank needs a larger digit base, not merely more primes in the multipliers: on `Z_2`, `15x + 1` still has rank one.
* **P1, corrected.**
  * Coalescence laws are invariant under affine conjugacy. `x ↦ rx` rescales offsets: THM-4606 statement 6, and `x ↦ −x` for `3x − 1`.
  * They depend on the sheet through the arithmetic of the offset: which starts are accessible, by the obstruction lemma. For example, `3x + 5` with offset 1 never coalesces, while `3x + 5` with offset 5 behaves exactly like `3x + 1` with offset 1.
  * So coalescence is not sheet-blind in general. It is sheet-blind for the conjugate pair `3x ± 1` (the orphan-law check in the results note, section 1.6).
* **Why this is not just Pólya.**
  * The step law of the debt walk is chosen from finitely many mean-zero laws by a rule that reads the past. Some of those laws are degenerate (the identity coupling gives zero steps; for `Z_5` (1,2,3,7,1) the coupling `j ↦ 4j` has a rank-2 covariance).
  * Peres, Popov and Sousi show that adaptive choice can change the type: two laws with invertible covariance and `2 + β` moments always give transience in `d = 3`, but three can give recurrence. Their theorems do not cover degenerate laws.
  * Georgiou, Menshikov, Mijatović and Wade show zero-drift walks in `d = 2` can be transient.
  * The conjecture is that the arithmetic rule `j ↦ Mj + e mod d` does not conspire. The equidistribution of the coupling state is the precise form to prove.

## What would refute it

* An unobstructed contracting rank-2 map whose visit window decays (transient).
* An unobstructed contracting rank-3 map with `q → 0`.
* An unobstructed rank-1 contracting map with a tail exponent other than 1/2.
* An accessible start, with no congruence obstruction, that is nevertheless never absorbed in a contracting rank ≤ 2 map.

**Structural facts used (PROVED).**
* For translation-only maps the coupling is a translation by `ē`. The debt step is uniform over the cyclic roots `v_(j+b) − v_j`.
* For independent multipliers its covariance is `(1/d)(2I − A_b)`, where `A_b` is the lag-`b` cycle with the unit positions deleted: a union of paths for prime `d`.
* Path spectra stay below 4, so rank ≥ 4 is balanced in the standard form.
* Rank 3 has two universal families (AP / non-AP positions), each with an explicit integer form.

**The minimal open requirements.**
1. **Rank 1, `d ≥ 3`.** Two-valued translation-only maps (multipliers 1 and `μ`) have an exactly fair SRW debt skeleton: along every lag cycle the transitions from unit positions to `μ`-positions equal those back.
   * Away from departures, the offset multiplier is `v`'s `m_j/d` at positive debt and `u`'s `m_i/d` at negative debt, so its conditional `θ`-moment is exactly `κ(θ) = (1/d)Σ(m_i/d)^θ < 1` whatever the lag. At departures it is `1/d`, and at zero debt the moment is at most `κ(θ)`.
   * So THM-4581's architecture should transfer: a level weight `s^|k|` with `s` close to 1; runs at level `h` of length at most `v_d(μ^h − 1)` plus a geometric; the SRW Green function. This is a sketch, not yet written.
2. **Rank 2.** A critical two-dimensional criterion. It needs the lag process to be asymptotically uncorrelated with the direction of the debt, or a common isotropizing form. The two families `{2I − E, 2I}` cannot share one.
3. **Rank ≥ 3, general couplings.** Involution couplings `j ↦ −j + b` have rank-≤ ⌊d/2⌋ covariances, so one-step balance fails. A block (multi-step) Lamperti form would need the coupling state to visit spread couplings often enough; audit F's anisotropy data suggest it does.
4. **Accessibility.** Does the absence of congruence obstructions imply that `(1, 0)` is reachable from every reachable state?
