# HYP-9679 - the 3-adic chart class omits infinitely many primes

C is the class of 3-adic determinant orientations, equivalently the switches of the grammar generated by ordinal and
cyclic C3 joins (Session 312).

**(A)** Infinitely many primes, and infinitely many 7p values, are never Hamiltonian-path counts of tournaments in
C. Stronger form: the attained primes have relative density strictly less than 1.

**(B)** C omits every Mersenne prime 2^p − 1 with p >= 3.

If (A) holds, no target selector that stays inside the address chart can prove Chan–Pak, and every complete selector
must leave the chart for infinitely many prime targets. (A) would follow from a structural reason that a strong
chart tournament's count avoids a positive proportion of prime residue patterns. No such reason is known. The
omitted primes below 375 meet every unit residue class mod 3, 4, 8 and 9, so no single congruence explains them.
