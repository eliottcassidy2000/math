# HYP-9680 - the arc-usage multiset determines strong tournaments up to converse, and the slot polynomial

The multiset of per-arc Hamiltonian-path counts forgets which arc carries which count. THM-5154 shows that through
order 9 it is nevertheless a complete invariant, up to three explicit moves.

**(A)** Two strong tournaments with the same arc-usage multiset are isomorphic or converse.

**(B)** Two tournaments with the same arc-usage multiset have the same full slot polynomial Q. By THM-5153's census,
the multiset is then a refinement of Q: it separates 21,199 of the 21,217 order-9 Q-classes that pair two or more
merged classes, and the remaining coincidences are exactly the moves (i)-(iii).

**(C)** All coincidences come from moving singletons between nonempty runs, global converse, and flipping cores
whose seam-weight multiset the flip preserves.
