# HYP-3617 -- forbidden-H-value proof certificates

The owner asked for creative ways to use tournament facts in a proof/disproof -- e.g. build a tournament
from a condition and show it would need an impossible H (7, 21, or a value impossible at its vertex count).
This develops the technique.

## The rigid invariant and its sparse image
`H(T) = I(Omega(T), 2)` (Hamiltonian-path count = independence polynomial of the odd-cycle conflict graph at
2) has a known, SPARSE image -- this is what makes it a certificate:
- **GLOBAL** (any n): the odd numbers MINUS `{7,21}`. A co-finite multiplicative numerical semigroup of
  genus 2, generated (via `H = prod H(strong components)`) by the strong-tournament H-values
  `{1,3,5,9,11,13,15,17,19,23,25,27,29,31,33,37,41,43,45,...}` (verified n<=6). `7` is absent (the bouquet
  obstruction), so `7` and `21=3*7` are unreachable.
- **PER-n**: `H` is always ODD (Redei) and `<= H_max(n) = 1,1,3,5,15,45,189,...` (the max Ham-path count).
  Beyond `{7,21}` there are EXTRA per-n gaps. **NEW (verified n=6): `H=35` and `H=39` are IMPOSSIBLE on 6
  vertices** (both `<= 45 = H_max(6)`, odd, achievable at `n>=7`), because `35=5*7` needs a `7`-factor
  (none) and `39=3*13` needs `3`(n=3)+`13`(n=5) = 8 vertices > 6, and no single strong 6-tournament has
  `H in {35,39}`. So vertex count alone forbids them.

## Three proof modes
**(a) FORBIDDEN-VALUE certificate (disproof / impossibility).** To show a condition `C` is impossible:
build a tournament `T_C` from `C`; if `C` forces `H(T_C) in {7,21}` (global) or into a per-n gap, then `C`
is contradictory.
- *Worked example (realizability):* **No tournament has odd-cycle conflict graph `Omega = K_3`** (three
  pairwise-conflicting odd cycles, no independent pair). Proof: `I(K_3,2) = 1 + 2*3 = 7`, forbidden. So a
  "bare conflict-triangle" of odd cycles cannot be realized -- the tournament's completeness forces a
  further cycle (why-seven). A general realizability obstruction: a graph `G` is `Omega(T)` for some `T`
  only if `I(G,2) not in {7,21}` (necessary).
- *Worked example (per-n):* **No 6-vertex tournament has `H=35` or `H=39`** (though `n>=7` do). So any
  condition forcing a 6-tournament with `H in {35,39}` is impossible.
- *Worked example (H_max):* **No 5-vertex tournament has `H>15`** (`H_max(5)=15`); forcing `H=17` on 5
  vertices is impossible.

**(b) EXISTENCE template (Redei).** `H(T)` is ODD, hence `>= 1`, hence **every tournament has a Hamiltonian
path** -- existence proved by a parity/counting argument (path-reversal involution leaves an odd count), NO
construction. TEMPLATE: realize a structure's count as a provably-NONZERO invariant (odd, positive, or
nonzero index) `=>` the structure exists.

**(c) FACTORING certificate.** `H = prod H(strong components)` constrains `H`'s factorization to products
of strong-tournament H-values. Force a factorization requiring an unavailable factor (e.g. an exact `7`)
`=>` contradiction. (This is the engine behind both the global `{7,21}` and the per-n gaps.)

## The LRC connection (honest)
- **Template (b) is ALREADY how the LRC small-measure regime works.** At the extremal the lonely set has
  measure 0 but the COUNT of touch-points is `phi(n)` (the units, HYP-3615), and at the LRC4 extremal the
  Borsuk-Ulam INDEX is `phi(n)/2 != 0` (HYP-3562) -- a nonzero topological count forcing a lonely point.
  Redei's "`H` odd `=> >=1`" is the tournament prototype; `SC = trace(R) > 0 => SC tournaments exist`
  (HYP-3562) is another instance. The LRC's "count, don't measure" IS the existence template.
- **The direct disproof mode (a) is implausible for LRC.** A would-be LRC14 counterexample lives on ~13
  speeds; any tournament one builds has `H` astronomically large, where forbidden values are vanishingly
  sparse -- forcing `H in {7,21}` or a specific large per-n gap is not realistic. The technique is
  strongest for SMALL / STRUCTURED tournaments (realizability of conflict graphs, score-constrained
  families).
- **But the meeting point is real and suggestive:** the LRC14 apex IS `7 = Phi_3(2)` = the forbidden-H
  prime = `|Fano|` (HYP-3616), with `ord_7(2)=3`, `<2>={1,2,4}` the octonion line. The disproof requires the
  full-`Z_7` core (the unique gap-0 cusp), and `7` is exactly the value a tournament's odd-cycle structure
  cannot realize "purely." Whether the full-`Z_7` disproof boundary can be tied to an honest forbidden-H
  certificate on a `7`-vertex apex object is an open creative target (the cleanest place to push mode (a)).

## What it buys
A concrete proof-strategy toolkit (forbidden-value / existence / factoring certificates), grounded by new
per-n data (`H=35,39` impossible on 6 vertices, explained by strong-component multiplicativity) and clean
worked examples (the bouquet, the per-n gaps, the `H_max` bound). It places Redei's classical
existence-by-parity and the LRC's existence-by-counting in one template, and identifies the apex-7 =
forbidden-H-7 meeting point as the one place a direct LRC disproof-via-forbidden-H might be pushed.
