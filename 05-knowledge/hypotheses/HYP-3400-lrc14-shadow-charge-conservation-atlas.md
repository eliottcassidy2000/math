# HYP-3400: LRC14 Shadow-Charge Conservation Atlas

## Claim

All of the current high-value LRC14 proof frames can be read as shadows of a
single conservation principle:

```text
proof charge is conserved across legal shadows.
```

A quotient, projection, scalar statistic, graph compression, root transform,
or finite-state lift is proof-grade only if it does one of three things:

```text
preserves witness-bearing charge
transfers that charge to a dual shadow
emits an explicit named debt
```

This is the HYP-3201 law-defect idea applied globally.  Failed compression of
commutativity, associativity, idempotence, or distributivity emits conditional
entropy sidecars.  Failed LRC compression emits proof-charge debt: endpoint
owner debt, odd-sign debt, Vitali bulk/core debt, root-radius debt, Green-current
debt, contact-holonomy curvature debt, tiling-fiber debt, or state-lift debt.

## Creative Frames

The point of the "charge" language is that several apparently different
proof routes become one network.

### Sheaf / Current Frame

Endpoint owners, Cech holes, Green currents, Fiedler cuts, autocorrelation lag
transport, and normal-fan slack are different boundary operators on the same
finite obstruction.  A trap should have zero unexplained boundary after all
sidecars are attached.

### Connection / Holonomy Frame

The HYP-3247 shell-lag commutator and HYP-3253 contact holonomy say that some
quotients fail like a non-flat connection, not like missing scalar data.  The
proof should either trivialize this curvature by exact `zeta_7` holonomy,
retain the endpoint cell, or name the residual curvature debt.

### Contact-Graph Frame

HYP-3265 upgrades the six-touch AP/GW equioscillation picture into a graph
case split.  The proof should first decide whether the unit-contact graph
survives globally, survives but is beaten by an off-unit peak, or is killed by
a `0 mod 14` speed and must pass through a promoted covering witness.

### Observability / Morse Frame

HYP-3300 turns the no-naked-quotient rule into a finite audit: after sidecars
are attached, an observability matrix should separate hidden coordinates, and
a vector-valued Morse energy should either descend to a named exit or expose
the first critical debt.

### Thermodynamic / Lee-Yang Frame

Circular Lee-Yang roots are the low-defect, low-temperature order parameter.
The phi4 off-circle dip is not noise; it is the charge that appears when the
circle-root compression fails.  Hermite-Biehler and Joukowski become legal
only when that off-circle debt is retained.

### Bulk/Core Vitali Frame

Measure information carries the bulk, then goes blind at the core.  At exactly
that wall, cyclotomic witness information turns on.  The conservation law says
there is no legal proof step where both vanish.

### Conductance Graph Frame

AP is not merely a row with large `lambda2`.  It is the row where positive
conductance, low effective resistance, Fejer autocorrelation, and Cech hole
charge align.  A non-AP row must create resistance excess, outward lag
transport, normal-fan slack, root collision, or a state-lift debt.

### Function / Information Frame

Commutativity, associativity, parity, positivity, evenness, root radius, and
scalar extremality are all quotient claims.  The proof should record
conditional entropy or typed debt whenever a quotient fails.
