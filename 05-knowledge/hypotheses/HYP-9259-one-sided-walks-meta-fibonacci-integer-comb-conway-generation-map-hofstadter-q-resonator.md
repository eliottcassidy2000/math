## Statements

1. **(Exact identities; PROVED.)** With `d(n) = x(n)/n - 1/2`:
   Conway (`a(1) = a(2) = 1`, `a(n) = a(a(n-1)) + a(n - a(n-1))`): `d(n) = (p/n) d(p) + (q/n) d(q)`, `p = a(n-1)`, `q = n - a(n-1)`, `p + q = n`.
   Hofstadter (`Q(1) = Q(2) = 1`, `Q(n) = Q(n - Q(n-1)) + Q(n - Q(n-2))`): `d(n) = (p/n) d(p) + (q/n) d(q) - (1/2)[(1 - 1/n) d(n-1) + (1 - 2/n) d(n-2)] + 3/(4n)`, `p = n - Q(n-1)`, `q = n - Q(n-2)`.
   In both, `p` and `q` are near `n/2`: `log2(n/p)` and `log2(n/q)` have means `0.948, 1.054` (Conway) and `1.0001, 1.0001` (Q) at `2^29` with standard deviations `0.02`, `0.014`; both Conway indices lie in the CLOSED previous arc `[2^(k-1), 2^k]` for every k>=1, with zero deviation at its upper boundary. The k=20 half-open generation has19 exceptions, so the former rounded-to100% universal claim is false. The `97%` Q value at k28 is finite numerical evidence.
2. **(Linearisation: the one-sided transfer law; PROVED for the model.)** Reading the identities as transfer laws for the deviation at scale `u = log2 n` with both steps equal to `-1`: Conway gives `d(u) = d(u - 1)`, whose modes `e^(su)` need `e^(-s) = 1`, `s = 2 pi i f` with `f` an integer: an undamped comb at every integer frequency, i.e. an exactly self-similar hump (generations = octaves), with no mechanism for decay except the nonlinearity; Q gives `d(u) = d(u-1) - d(u)` for slowly varying components, i.e. `d_k = d_(k-1)/2`: the smooth comb is damped by `1/2` per generation, and for a component `e^(i omega n)` within a generation the feedback acts as the filter `1/(1 + e^(-i omega)/2 + e^(-2 i omega)/2)`, a resonator with poles `z = (-1 +- i sqrt 7)/4`, `|z| = 1/sqrt 2`, period `2 pi/arg z = 3.253`, gain `2.1` at the resonance and `1/2` at `omega = 0`. Collatz, for comparison, has steps `-1` and `+0.585` (THM-4629): two-sided, lines at the irrational near-returns.
3. **(Conway: the continuum generation map; PROVED expansion, NUMERICAL agreement.)** In generation coordinates `x=n/2^k`, the exact identity uses selector `s_n=h_k(x)-(a(n)-a(n-1))/n` in both weights and displaced arguments on the closed previous arc. The implemented explicit model replaces this by the lagged `h=h_(k-1)(x)`; that further approximation has no proved `O(1/n)` bound. A formal Taylor expansion of the lagged model under smooth-profile assumptions gives `h_k - h_(k-1) = 2 h^2 (x^2 h')' + O(h^3)`: a nonlinear diffusion whose amplitude obeys `dA/dk ~ -c A^3`, `A_k ~ C/sqrt(k)`, with the shape a fixed point of the smoothing.
