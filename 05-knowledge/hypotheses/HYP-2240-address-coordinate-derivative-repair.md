# HYP-2240: Address-Coordinate Derivative Repair

## Claim

The S664 triune cycle sharpens into the operational pattern:

```text
find a scalar/product collision
attach the missing fraction/address coordinate
unroll it back into a derivative sum.
```

The address coordinate is the derivative variable.  It is the bit of quotient
state that makes a product/scalar shadow differentiable in a useful category:
carry word, deleted-card owner, branch sheet, Weyl `m`-function,
continued-fraction coefficient, generic/model state, activity mark, pin owner,
or point-deletion owner.  The derivative sum is the repaired ledger obtained by
taking a log derivative, deletion derivative, spectral derivative,
moment/continued-fraction expansion, owner-loss deck, or recursive parameter
sum.

## S665 Evidence

S665 searched the repo and web, then built a 20-lane repair atlas in
`04-computation/address_coordinate_derivative_repair_s665.py`, with stored
output in
`05-knowledge/results/address_coordinate_derivative_repair_s665.out`;
and reflection
`07-reflections/address-coordinate-derivative-repair-s665.md`.

Strongest exact repo lanes:

- **LRC14 Res27 carry:** keeping only the additive/product `Res_27` shadow mixes
  floor and strict rows; adding the carry-continuant/address state splits the
  collision and yields carry-deletion derivative ledgers.
- **Tournament deck paired owner:** full decks and global scalar repairs collide
  through `n=6`; paired `(card isomorphism type, deleted vertex outdegree)`
  separates all checked classes and turns deletion into a derivative sum.
- **Finite Vieta branch sheet:** over `F_17`, the `(sum, product)` map has
  maximum fiber `2`, while adding the branch coordinate makes every fiber
  singleton.  Newton power sums are the derivative-sum readout.
- **Goldbach/Lemoine pair plane:** the visible columns
  `E=p+q`, `O=p+2q` reconstruct the address by `q=O-E`, `p=2E-O`.
- **Finite-field Kakeya/Falconer:** distance support saturates while pinned
  minima and union sizes vary; pin/concurrency owners are the derivative data.
- **Union-closed frequency pressure:** frequency-vector collisions at `m=4`
  split after attaching pressure/loss coordinates.
- **A000568 marked observer fiber:** unmarked tournament classes forget the
  observer/threshold address needed by LRC-style predicates.

External theorem-transfer lanes:

- Graph reconstruction: decks collide unless address rows such as covering
  numbers or vertex-pair parameters are retained; Kocay-style equations are the
  derivative sums.
- Explicit formulae and Ihara/Selberg trace formulae: Euler or orbit products
  become prime-power, zero, or closed-geodesic sums after log differentiation.
- Stieltjes/J-fraction and Weyl `m`-function theory: moment or spectral scalar
  data needs continued-fraction/spectral-measure address coordinates before it
  reconstructs the underlying object.
- Tutte and matroid Hodge lenses: scalar polynomial coefficients become
  deletion-contraction or intersection/degree ledgers once activity/Chow-ring
  coordinates are retained.

## Tournament Analysis

Vertices are repair lanes, not objects in the underlying problems.  The
pairwise observable is
`(collision_evidence, address_clarity, derivative_sum_clarity,
finite_actionability, repo_leverage, source_confidence)`.  The switch is
majority, with curated source order as tie Hamiltonian path.

The nontrivial SCC is useful: it says the best transfer lanes are not linearly
ordered by "importance".  LRC carry, tournament decks, graph reconstruction,
OCF noncommutative addresses, explicit formulae, pi/e branch sheets, Weyl
`m`-functions, Goldbach/Lemoine, J-fractions, A000568, Ihara/Selberg, and Tutte
activities all beat each other along different criteria.

## Assumption Challenge

For LRC and Tournament Analysis, S665 did not assume vertices must be runners or
arcs.  Candidate vertices considered included formulas, domains, files,
invariants, graph cards, deleted vertices, residues, runners, roots,
eigenvalues, moments, bases, models, proof obligations, pins, sets, and
activity marks.  The selected quotient preserves the predicate "which missing
coordinate repairs a scalar/product collision and yields a derivative ledger?"
It destroys raw object identity on purpose.
