# HYP-9419: unifying candidates

1. **Three-layer composition.** Organize a response theory by jointly marked boundary occurrences (incidence), transport on their frames (permutations, reflections, Frobenius phases, no-flip role exchange), and a realization predicate (legality, positivity, metric identity, norm). Candidate theorem: responses compose iff all three layers are retained, and every composition failure in the repository is of one of four types, each witnessed once: joint-root collision (`H = 289` versus `253`, rooted lines R3), phase collision (the `81/27` pair, separated by the second response), metric contradiction (`3 sigma = 3 tau`, Dudeney Case B), insufficient test depth (one-step relocation guards collide at order 4; THM-4809 B bounds the depth needed). Falsified by a fifth failure type, or by a lawful compression that drops a layer other than U2's reset lemma.
2. **Sharp depth (session72's conjecture, CITED) and a support form.** Session72 conjectures `d_n = ceil((n - 2)/2)` for every `n >= 3`, verified through order 6, with three restored tests at order 7 as the first untested case. The support form proposed here: the rank of the span of `k`-fold products of named guards on `X_n` equals the dimension of the span of the monomials `x_S` over the free-pair sets `S` that are unions of at most `k` path supports of states, so that it is computable from Hamiltonian-path supports without elimination; it would explain `8, 44, 273`. Test: compute the support-generated monomial span at orders 5, 6 and compare; at order 7 the rational rank of single guards must be at least the GF(2) rank 1,934.
3. **Local obstructions for three-piece dissections.** For regular `n`- and `m`-gons of equal area, do the angle-charge congruence (session71) and the coordinate-field obstruction (session71, unnamed-carrier sec. 4) decide whether a three-piece dissection exists, in the cases where the paper's metric case analysis is otherwise required? The pentagon-square pair (charge group `Z/10`) is the first test.
4. **Pattern clocks — polynomial exclusion REFUTED; other clauses not decided here.** The earlier universal degree-at-least-three exclusion fails: `x^4 mod7` has nonzero image `{1,2,4}`, and `x^4 mod3` has nonzero image `{1}`, giving Paley7 and the directed triangle. Thus the squares/geometric-only claim also fails as an exclusion of other integer sequences. The separate Fibonacci and prime-sequence clauses are not decided by this counterexample. **Correction (Session73, 2026-10-27):** the source note's finite census tested cubes and other named sequences, not every polynomial of degree at least three. High polynomial degree is not a modular-image obstruction: `x^2 + m*x^D` has the square image modulo `m` for every `D>=3`. [Exact source-pinned counterexamples and corrected prime-monomial criterion](../results/session73_paid_root_exception_budgets_20261027/session73-moduli/REPORT.md). THM-4809 C's skew-half-set criterion is unchanged; the unrelated clauses above are not certified by this correction.
