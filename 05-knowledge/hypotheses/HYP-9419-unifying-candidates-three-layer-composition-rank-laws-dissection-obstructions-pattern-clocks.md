# HYP-9419: unifying candidates

1. **Three-layer composition.** Organize a response theory by jointly marked boundary occurrences (incidence), transport on their frames (permutations, reflections, Frobenius phases, no-flip role exchange), and a realization predicate (legality, positivity, metric identity, norm). Candidate theorem: responses compose iff all three layers are retained, and every composition failure in the repository is of one of four types, each witnessed once: joint-root collision (`H = 289` versus `253`, rooted lines R3), phase collision (the `81/27` pair, separated by the second response), metric contradiction (`3 sigma = 3 tau`, Dudeney Case B), insufficient test depth (one-step relocation guards collide at order 4; THM-4809 B bounds the depth needed). Falsified by a fifth failure type, or by a lawful compression that drops a layer other than U2's reset lemma.
2. **Sharp depth (session72's conjecture, CITED; order 7 settled here).** Session72 conjectures `d_n = ceil((n - 2)/2)` for every `n >= 3`, verified through order 6. This session settles the first untested case: the products of at most three named guards span all 32,768 functions on `X_7` (the 4,814,472 distinct nonzero products, i.e. the consistent unions of at most three path constraints, have GF(2) rank 32,768; `restored_products_exact_20261026.cpp`, basis certificate committed), so `d_7 = 3` (FINITE-EXACT) and the conjecture holds through order 7; pairs reach GF(2) rank 30,744 there. The first open case is order 8 (`2^21` states, beyond the elimination; the degree bound gives `d_8 >= 3`). The pre-audit draft proposed a support-generated rank formula (monomials over unions of path supports); the audit computed its counts 6 / 53 / 857 against the ranks 8 / 44 / 273 and it is withdrawn (MISTAKE-679). Session73 refuted it independently (at `n = 4`, `k = 1`: 6 full path supports against rank 8; the downward-closure repair fails at `n = 5`: 46 coefficient monomials against rank 44; [its guard-support report](../results/session73_paid_root_exception_budgets_20261027/session73-guard-support/REPORT.md) and its MISTAKES entry "polynomial supports are not independent guard coordinates" of 2026-10-27). Session73 did not re-audit the order-7 computation; the independent second check of the certificate is recorded in the note's section 12.
3. **Local obstructions for three-piece dissections.** For regular `n`- and `m`-gons of equal area, do the angle-charge congruence (session71) and the coordinate-field obstruction (session71, unnamed-carrier sec. 4) decide whether a three-piece dissection exists, in the cases where the paper's metric case analysis is otherwise required? The pentagon-square pair (charge group `Z/10`) is the first test.
4. **Pattern clocks.** Among the sequences tested, the squares (at the primes `3 mod 4`), the geometric sequences `a^k` (at index-2 primes without `-1`) and the triangular numbers (at 3 and 5) are the only ones whose patterns are tournaments to modulus 120; the cubes, Fibonacci numbers, primes and odd Collatz successors are excluded at every modulus by trivial negation symmetries (THM-4809 C). The pre-audit clause excluding every polynomial of degree at least 3 was false (`k^4` and `k^8` reproduce the squares' Paley tournaments, `k^3 + 2` modulo 7 has pattern `{1, 2, 3}`) and is withdrawn. Session73 (2026-10-27) refuted the same clause independently, with `x^4` modulo 3 (pattern `{1}`, the directed triangle) and modulo 7 (Paley7), the remark that `x^2 + m x^D` has the square image modulo `m` for every `D >= 3`, and a corrected monomial criterion (PROVED there): the nonzero values of `x^d` modulo an odd prime `p` form a tournament connection set iff `gcd(d, p - 1) = 2` and `p = 3 mod 4` ([session73's report](../results/session73_paid_root_exception_budgets_20261027/session73-moduli/REPORT.md); its MISTAKES entry of 2026-10-27, 'a cube census is not a polynomial-degree exclusion'). The open question is a characterization of the polynomials `f` and primes `p` with `f(F_p) \ {0}` a skew half-set beyond the monomial case. Session73 also proved the Fibonacci exclusion for every `m >= 2` by the reversible pair recurrence and cites Dirichlet for the primes ([closures](../results/session73_paid_root_exception_budgets_20261027/session73-moduli-supplement/INCOMING_FIBONACCI_PRIME_CLOSURES.md)); these are the trivial negation symmetries of the note's section 7.
