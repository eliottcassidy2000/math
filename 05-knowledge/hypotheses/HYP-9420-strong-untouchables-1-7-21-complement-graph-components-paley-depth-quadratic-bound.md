# Predictions after the spectrum atlas and the complement census

1. **Strong completeness.** The set of odd numbers that are not the Hamiltonian-path count of any strong tournament is exactly {1, 7, 21}. THM-1370's completeness conjecture (spectrum(infinity) = odd \ {7, 21}) is the all-tournament version; the strong version is stronger (joins are not needed) and is what the data show: exact below 200 (THM-4810 (B)), every odd value of [85, 2881] and 75, 81 witnessed at order 9, every odd value of [175, 6000] witnessed at order 10, so the statement holds for every odd v <= 6,000. Falsified by an odd v >= 23 shown, by an exhaustion of the orders n with f(n) <= v, to be attained by no strong tournament. Test: the augmentation method of THM-4810 (B) with a larger bound B, which is exact as long as the strong (n-1)-classes with H <= B can be enumerated.
2. **Components of the move graph.** For n = 0, 3 (mod 4), n >= 4, the native complement move graph on labelled n-tournaments is connected; for n = 1, 2 (mod 4), n >= 5, its components are exactly the two parity classes of THM-4810 (C). The eccentricity e(n) of the transitive tournament (7, 6, 7, 13 at n = 4..7) satisfies e(n) >= n for n = 0, 3 (mod 4) and the diameter is attained at the transitive tournament. Test: order 8 (connected, e(8) >= 8 predicted) by a BFS over 2^28 states; order 9 (two components) by sampling parity classes.
3. **Unique strong sources.** F_s(v) = 1 exactly for v = 3, 5, 11, 13; every other attained odd v has at least two strong sources. Complete for v <= 74; test: the fibres of 75 <= v <= 200 from the augmentation lists with canonical forms at order 9.
4. **The quadratic bound is nearly sharp.** THM-4810 (C) gives d(P_q, transitive) >= (q^2 - 1)/8 = 6, 15, 45, 66, 120 at q = 7, 11, 19, 23, 31 and d(P_11, forest frames) >= 5. Prediction: d(P_q, transitive) - (q^2 - 1)/8 stays bounded (it is 1 at q = 7), and the forest-frame distance of P_11 is exactly 5, i.e. there is a word of five complements from P_11 to a forest-framed tournament along which L decreases by exactly one at every step (the depth-two census shows the decrease is tight through depth two, and session73's depth-three witness with f = 12 shows it is tight at depth three). Falsified by an exhaustive depth-five search from P_11 without a forest frame, or by d(P_q) - (q^2 - 1)/8 growing with q.
5. **Feedback deficit.** q(q-1)/4 - f(P_q) = 3.5, 7.5, 21.5, 34.5, 52.5 at q = 7, 11, 19, 23, 31; the ratio to q^(3/2) is 0.19, 0.21, 0.26, 0.31, 0.30. Prediction: f(P_43) between 350 and 365 (the quasi-random order q^2/4 - c q^(3/2) with c near 0.3). Test: the subset DP at q = 43 is out of reach (2^43); a branch-and-bound or an exact ILP would decide it.
