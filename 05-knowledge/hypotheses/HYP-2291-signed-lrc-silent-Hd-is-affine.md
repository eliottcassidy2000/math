## Setup
`C=2n−1`, runners (magnitudes) `1..n−1`, gauge-fix sign of magnitude 1 to `+`, free signs on
`2..n−1`. A **cut** `ε∈{±1}^{n−1}`. Two cuts COLLIDE (homometric) iff equal difference
multiset over `ℤ/C`. `deficiency = 2^{n−2} − #classes`. Order-block lattice
`V = span_{F2}{H_d : d|C, 1<d<C}`, `H_d` = positive half of the order-d subgroup.

A **move** `D` (subset of magnitudes flipped) is **silent at ε** iff `ε^D ~ ε`. By the A·B
lemma (S708b): silent ⟺ `∀t  A_t·B_t = 0`, with `B_t=Σ_{i∈D} ε_i sin(2πti/C)` (depends on
`ε|_D=:σ`), `A_t=Σ_{i∈D^c} ε_i sin(2πti/C)` (depends on `ε|_{D^c}`).

## The claim
**(A) `silent(H_d)` is an affine F2-subspace** for every single subgroup-half `H_d`, with an
explicit THM-413-style **value-pairing basis** (for `H_3={C/3}`: the singleton `{C/3}` plus
triples `{a, C/3−a, C/3+a}`; general `H_d`: the cycle space of the value-multigraph).

**(B) σ-decomposition (non-brute count).**
`|silent(D)| = Σ_{σ∈{±1}^D} N(Req(σ))`, `Req(σ)={t : B_t(σ)≠0}`,
`N(R)=#{ε|_{D^c} : A_t=0 ∀t∈R}`. **For single `H_d`,** `N(R)=2^{|D^c|−rank_ℝ(Mat_R)}` exactly
(the ±1 solutions of the sine system fill the real null space — value-pairing). VERIFIED:
σ-decomp == brute for every single `H_d`, all composite `C≤39`
(`signed_lrc_sigma_decomp_s710b.py`).

**(C) Dimension / deficiency closed forms (structural re-derivation).**
- Squarefree `C=pq`: `dim silent(H_p)=dim silent(H_q)=(p+q)/2−2`; combined `H_p⊕H_q` is
  silent NOWHERE ⟹ `deficiency=2^{(p+q)/2−2}` (reproduces THM-417). Verified C=15,21,33,35,39.
- Prime square `C=p²`: single generator, `dim silent(H_p)=p−2` ⟹ `deficiency=2^{p−3}`.
  Verified C=9,25,49.

## The ISOLATED OBSTRUCTION (why prime-cube/mixed/3-prime stay open)
For **combined moves** `H_{d1}⊕H_{d2}` the silent set is **NON-affine** (e.g. `O_9={3,6,12}`
at C=27 has `|silent|=112=16·7`, not a power of 2). The σ-decomp's `2^{nullity}` formula
**OVERCOUNTS** them: the ±1 solutions of `A_t=0` do **not** fill the real null space.
Explicit witness: move `{3,5,6}` at C=15 — every σ yields real-nullity ≥0 but **zero**
homometric cuts (brute=0, naive 2^nullity=8). So the prime-cube (C=27→69, C=81=3⁴→?),
mixed (C=45→8728, 63,75,99), and 3-prime (C=105 size-4) closed forms reduce to **counting
±1 lattice points inside the non-affine combined silent sets** — a genuine open subproblem.

## What this unlocks (handoff)
- The deficiency Möbius framework: `deficiency = Σ_{W≤V} n_W(1−1/|W|)`, `n_W` from
  `f(W)=|⋂_{gens(W)} silent(H_d)|`. For W generated by SINGLE `H_d`, `f(W)` is an **affine
  intersection** (linear algebra, any C) — computable non-brutely. Only W generated by
  combined moves need the obstruction above.
- **Size-≥4 existence at C=81, 105** (S708b open Q) is decided by whether
  `silent(H_{d1})∩silent(H_{d2})≠∅` for two independent single moves — an affine-intersection
  test reachable at any C. (Next step of this session.)
