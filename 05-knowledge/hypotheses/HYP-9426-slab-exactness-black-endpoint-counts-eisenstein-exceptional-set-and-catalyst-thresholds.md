# HYP-9426 — slab exactness, the even prescribed core, Eisenstein exceptions, catalyst thresholds, W4-free tournaments

1. **Slab exactness.** Let n = 2m + 1. Every tournament with L + R = (n² − 1)/8 and R ≤ m − 1 is exactly L moves from the transitive class.
   - **What it implies.** Every such class lies on a geodesic to the regular set. This includes every regular class, so it implies HYP-9423 (7).
   - **Evidence.** THM-4819 (B), at orders 5, 7 and 9.
   - **Why the bound R ≤ m − 1.** A source or sink forces R ≥ m and blocks every L-descending move (THM-4819 (A4)). At order 7, strong λ = 1 failures already occur at R = m, so the bound cannot be relaxed to R ≤ m.
   - **Test.** At order 11, sample slab classes with R ≤ 4 by random R-descending chains from TT, then run the L-descending search on each.
2. **Even balanced orders, prescribed vertex.** Let n = 2m ≥ 8 and let T have all scores in {m − 1, m}. For every vertex v and both native roles, there is a black word of length m − 1 into C(v, ρ) whenever the dipole law permits it, that is, whenever |s(v) − (n−1)/2| can grow by one per move to (n−1)/2.
   - **What is known.** Session75 §4 proves the unprescribed version.
   - **The missing step.** The analogue of THM-4819 Lemma B for T − v, whose scores are not regular, is open.
   - **Test.** A full census at n = 8 and 10, with prescribed_isolation.c adapted to even n.
3. **Eisenstein debt is at most one above the floor.** The claim is g(x) ≤ ⌊Q(x)/3⌋ + 1 for every x ∈ Z[ω], and the
   exceptional set E = {x : g(x) = ⌊Q(x)/3⌋ + 1} has density zero.
   - **Evidence.** E meets 20 of the 328 norms ≡ 1 (mod 3) up to 2000, and no norm ≡ 0 there (THM-4820 (D)).
   - **Correction from the larger window.** E does contain points of norm ≡ 0: 51 (norm 2601) is exceptional in the
     W = 7500 window. So no residue restriction is conjectured.
4. **The rational exceptions (REFUTED as first drafted; corrected form).** The draft conjectured that, for odd n with
   3 ∤ n, n ∈ E iff n has only inert prime factors and (n² + 2)/3 is not of the form b² + 3t². "If" is THM-4820 (C).
   "Only if" is REFUTED by n = 59: the integer 59 is inert and (59² + 2)/3 = 3³·43, so 59 has a tight β = −1 split, yet
   59 is exceptional in the W = 7500 window (every tight split has an exceptional part).

   **Corrected conjecture.** E is generated by propagation from the window-free rational exceptions of THM-4820 (C):
   - **Odd exceptions to 86.** In the W = 7500 window they are 11, 23, 25, 29, 41, 47, 51, 59, 83, 85.
   - **Predictions that held.** Every predicted window-free exception (11, 23, 25, 29, 41, 47, 83, 85) is exceptional,
     and 5, 17, 53, 55, 71 are not.
   - **The two others.** 51 and 59 are the two non-window-free odd exceptions.
   - **Even exceptions to 86.** They are 22, 44, 46, 50, 58, 82, each 2^j times an odd exception. Prediction: 2^j times
     any odd exception is exceptional (next tests 92, 94, 102).
   - **Further predictions.** 101, 107, 113, 115 are exceptional (window-free).
5. **Catalyst thresholds.** k*(n)/n² tends to a constant c with 2/9 ≤ c ≤ 1/3.
   - **What is proved.** The lower constant 2/9 (THM-4820 (E)).
   - **Data.** k*(14) = 61, a ratio of 0.311.
   - **Test.** Run k*(n) for n ≤ 20 with K = 6n.
6. **W4-free tournaments — PROVED the same day (THM-4823 (C)).** Every tournament without a sink in which every
   out-neighbourhood is transitive is locally transitive. So W(n) = W(n−1) + LT(n) − 1, with LT agreeing with A000016
   at n = 3, …, 10.
7. **At most two blue rotations.** Let T be regular of order at least 7, let v be a vertex and let C be a Hamilton cycle of T − v. Then at most two of the m rotations of C closed at v are blue.
   - **What is proved.** Fewer than m (THM-4819 Lemma B).
   - **Evidence.** The maximum observed is 2 at orders 7, 9, 11 and 15, and 1 at order 13.
