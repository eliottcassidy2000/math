# HYP-3039: LRC14 Hidden-Coordinate Ledger Principle

## Claim

A quotient in the current LRC14 stack is proof-safe only after its next hidden
coordinate has been handled in one of three explicit ways:

```text
exposed as a sidecar,
annihilated by a dual/cut/cocycle,
or routed to named residual debt.
```

The recent residual chain from HYP-3024 through HYP-3038 is best understood as
a hidden-coordinate ladder, not as a sequence of better scalar estimates.  The
residual count becomes less important once the generator of each residual class
is known and every hidden coordinate has a declared repair tooth.

## Ladder

The current ladder is:

```text

  -> status is protected before route labels are compressed

  -> AP/GW equality atoms are owner-essential closed-H1 packets

```

This reframes the q=23 square: the obstruction is not raw squarefree analytic
mass.  It is a hidden mixed coordinate, followed by endpoint-owner data.

the residual stack.  The current proof route should keep address fields until
one of the ladder teeth has either separated, reconstructed, or killed them.

generator/cut class, not by repeated packet occurrences.
