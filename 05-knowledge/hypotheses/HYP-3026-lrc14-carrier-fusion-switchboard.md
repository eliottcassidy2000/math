# HYP-3026: LRC14 Carrier-Fusion Switchboard

## Claim

The next better LRC14 proof carrier is not a new scalar and not a standalone
sequence automaton.  It is a fused packet signature:

```text
strict-open status
lonely-profile barcode shape
large safe-stick / safe-body geometry
CRT first-chart witness
endpoint-current cocycle
magnitude/Farey cocycle
automatic-language and 2-adic doubling sidecars
danger-count dual distribution
active-bottleneck normal-fan slot
discrepancy-height trident slot
pair-good decoy generator / barcode-normal-fan slot
automatic fiber zipper slot
fiber-zipper convergence audit slot
closed arc-Cech / tope-cocircuit slot
```

On the named stress bank, automatic words still mix AP/Goddyn-Wong boundary
atoms with open rows.  Adding only the first chart denominator still leaks.
Adding barcode shape removes boundary/open mixing in this bank, and adding the
magnitude cocycle separates all named probes.  The full fusion signature has
no mixed boundary/open or route fibers in the tested rows.

This is not a proof of LRC14.  It is a proof-interface target: any future
finite-automaton, lacunary, 2-adic/Hurwitz, normal-fan, discrepancy-height,
pair-good decoy, automatic fiber zipper, arc-Cech, perfect-number, or
visibility argument should either retain an equivalent fusion packet or prove
why one of these coordinates is reconstructible, dual-annihilated, descended,
boundary equality, or named THM-572/F7 residual debt.
