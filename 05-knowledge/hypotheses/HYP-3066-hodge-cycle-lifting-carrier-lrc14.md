# HYP-3066: Hodge-cycle lifting carrier for LRC14

The Hodge conjecture should enter the LRC14 project as a disciplined
realizability template, not as a theorem being imported.  The true Hodge
conjecture says that rational cohomology classes of type `(p,p)` on a smooth
projective complex variety should be rational combinations of algebraic cycle
classes.  The LRC-facing analogue is:

```text
closed / positivity-feasible packet cochains
  should be generated by named certificate cycles,
  or else they must be emitted as named F7/THM-572 residual debt.
```

This turns the old "baby Hodge" metaphor into an operational packet sidecar:
every residual class is tested by a cycle-class map from concrete certificates
to the packet cohomology group where the quotient leak lives.

## Prior work being merged

The repo already has three Hodge-shaped layers.

1. **Baby Hodge / tournament realizability.**  THM-509 and HYP-2526..2528
   show that tournament invariant holes are moment-feasible but not realized.
   The flagship `(c3,c5)=(8,10)` is inside the continuous moment/flag body, so
   positivity does not cut it.  HYP-2521 and HYP-2530 sharpen the warning:
   missing values are missing compatibility or `alpha`-lattice points, not
   failed Hankel positivity.

2. **LRC octahedral Hodge current.**  HYP-2887 identifies the support-six
   repeated-packet residual as a current on the octahedron `L(K4)`.  Its graph
   is spherical: after low-height wall deletion, divergence and triangular
   face curl are the live objects, and there is no independent harmonic
   one-current.  HYP-2892 places that octahedron beside unital, Clebsch, and
   truncated-octahedral carriers as finite Hodge-style frame/curl ledgers.

3. **Cocycle normal form.**  HYP-2995 and HYP-2997 already define LRC14
   quotient safety by cochains on labelled packet fibers: a forgotten
   coordinate is legal only if it is fiber-constant, reconstructed, exact as a
   coboundary, killed by a dual certificate, descended to a smaller family,
   stopped at AP/GW boundary equality, or named as F7/THM-572 debt.

HYP-3066 combines these into a single Hodge-cycle lifting carrier.

## Dictionary

Use the following words literally inside the LRC proof stack.

| Hodge word | LRC14 packet meaning |
|---|---|
| Hodge class | A closed packet cochain whose type/positivity filters are satisfied: route/status compatible, endpoint-safe, Fejer/Ramanujan/Toeplitz allowed, and not killed by a known local test. |
| Algebraic cycle | A named finite certificate generator: AP/GW boundary atom, Fejer dual form, Ramanujan exact-period character, Haar zipper square, endpoint-owner potential, observer-cut boundary, C27/unital transfer, K33/state-lift incidence, octahedral face curl, partial-cube Theta class, Roth-Minkowski low-height wall, or family descent. |
| Cycle class map | The map from certificate generators to residual cohomology classes on the labelled packet complex. |
| Non-algebraic Hodge class | A residual closed cochain that survives positivity but is not in the span of named certificate classes.  In LRC language this is exactly what F7/THM-572 must mean, not an anonymous failure bucket. |

Thus the LRC theorem target is not "prove the Hodge conjecture."  It is a
finite or familywise cycle-class surjectivity statement:

```text
cl : Cert(P) -> H_res(P)

Every primitive non-AP/GW residual class in H_res(P) is in im(cl),
or maps to the forbidden THM-572 state-lift sector.
```

Here `P` is the labelled packet complex from HYP-2997, `Cert(P)` is generated
by the named proof carriers, and `H_res(P)` is the quotient of closed emitted
cochains by already discharged coboundaries and dual-annihilated directions.

## LRC proof order

The Hodge-cycle carrier suggests this proof order.

1. **Build the closed class.**  Start with the HYP-2997 cochain complex.  For a
   quotient or packet family, record the emitted cochain and verify the
   relevant closedness law: Haar square, endpoint winding, Ramanujan period,
   Farey carry, tope wall, octahedral face curl, or matrix observability.

2. **Apply positivity, but do not trust it alone.**  Fejer/Toeplitz PSD,
   skew-Hankel moment positivity, convex flag feasibility, and same-frequency
   additive-energy positivity are type filters.  HYP-2521/HYP-2530 warn that
   positive or convex-feasible shadows can still be non-realizable.

3. **Lift to certificate cycles.**  Try to write the residual cochain as a
   rational combination of named certificate cycles:
   AP/GW boundary atoms, local Haar zipper squares, endpoint-owner potentials,
   Ramanujan exact-period characters, Fejer dual forms, C27/unital blocks,
   K33/state-lift incidences, octahedral triangular face curls, partial-cube
   Theta classes, simplex face boundaries, Roth-Minkowski low-height walls,
   and observer-cut payload boundaries.

4. **Use finite Hodge carriers where available.**  On the support-six branch,
   HYP-2887 gives a strong template: after low-height wall deletion,
   `L(K4)` has no hidden harmonic one-current.  This means a surviving
   one-current should decompose into gradients and face curls, hence into
   certificate cycles plus Abel/HYP-2636 tail.

5. **Name the residual.**  If no decomposition exists, emit a specific
   `residual_hodge_class_id` and map it to THM-572/F7, or state the missing
   generator.  Do not call the proof complete while a phantom class remains.

## Integration with S227 partial cubes and simplices

HYP-3063 gives a useful extra source of certificate generators:

- Fibonacci-cube / partial-cube Theta classes are hyperplane coordinates.
- Gated subcubes and median intervals give canonical retractions and interval
  boundaries.
- Moser even-coordinate subcubes require bit-position phase before they become
  legal cycle generators.
- Simplex face boundaries explain why `n(n+1)` should be a directed-edge
  sidecar and not a scalar proof.

Thus a sequence/cube/simplex quotient is Hodge-safe only after its Theta
classes or face boundaries appear in the `certificate_cycle_generators` list,
or after the forgotten coordinate is routed as residual debt.

## Tournament Analysis

Vertices are not runners.  Candidate vertices considered: runners, residues,
gaps, Fourier modes, endpoint owners, proof obligations, cochains, cocycles,
certificate generators, octahedral faces, partial-cube Theta classes, simplex
faces, observer-cut payloads, and residual classes.

Chosen vertex set:

```text
certificate_cycle_generators
```

Pairwise observable:

```text
retained(predicate, closedness, type_filter, cycle_generation,
         formal_checkability, family_descent, residual_exit)
```

Gauge: orient toward the generator or sidecar that explains more of a residual
class without forgetting exact `M`, endpoint-owner, topology, route, or
certificate data.  Tie Hamiltonian path:

```text
labelled_packet_master_class
> fejer_toeplitz_dual_cycle
> haar_zipper_square_cycle
> endpoint_owner_boundary_cycle
> ramanujan_period_character
> observer_cut_boundary
> octahedral_face_curl
> partial_cube_theta_class
> simplex_face_boundary
> roth_minkowski_low_height_wall
> c27_unital_transfer
> k33_state_lift_incidence
> raw_moment_positive_shadow
```

This preserves the LRC predicate only as a labelled packet statement.  It
destroys raw runner identity and must therefore be paired with exact packet
sidecars before any quotient is promoted.
