# HYP-9293 — admissible `sl2`: classically free iff boundary or integrable

## Statement

Let `k = -2 + a/b` be admissible: coprime `a >= 2`, `b >= 1`. Let `V = L_k(sl2)`, and let `X_V`, `R_V` and `pi` be as
in THM-4671. The singular vector generating the maximal ideal has weight `D_0 = (a-1)b` and spin `a-1` (Kac-Wakimoto).
Its symbol is the `ad`-orbit of `e^(a-1) Cas^((a-1)(b-1)/2)`. This is consistent with the data at every tested level;
the exponent is always an integer because `a` and `b` are not both even.

**Conjecture.**
1. `V` is classically free iff `a = 2` (boundary admissible) or `b = 1` (integrable).
2. If `a >= 3` and `b >= 2`, then `ker pi` vanishes below weight `D_0 + 3`. In weight `D_0 + 3` its minimal
   differential-ideal generators lie in Li degree 2 and form one irreducible `sl2`-module of spin `a - 1`.

## Why it is plausible (mechanism)

- **Drinfeld-Sokolov parallel.** Reduction sends `L_(-2+a/b)(sl2)` to `Vir_(a,b)`, and THM-4671 shows that `Vir_(a,b)`
  acquires its first non-arc relation exactly when `a >= 3`. Its coefficient carries the factors `(a-2)` and
  `(b-1)`.
- **Same shape.** In both families, the first correction sits three weight units above the singular vector, as a
  quantum shadow of a first syzygy of the arc ideal.
- **Caveat.** The `sl2` statement is not a corollary of THM-4671: DS reduction does not obviously commute with taking
  singular supports, and for `(a, b) = (3, 2)` the reduction `Vir_(3,2)` is trivial while `L_(-1/2)` is not free.

## Physics reading

- **Boundary levels: classically free.** `L_(-4n/(2n+1))(sl2)` is the chiral algebra of the `(A_1, D_(2n+1))`
  Argyres-Douglas theory (`(A_1, D_3) = (A_1, A_3)`), and these levels are free. This supports the Schur limit of the
  Andrews-Banerjee-Bhargava-Singh-Tao (2025) arc-space formula for their Macdonald indices.
- **`k = -1/2`: not free.** `L_(-1/2)(sl2) = (beta gamma)^(Z_2)` is the chiral algebra of a free hypermultiplet with
  its central `Z_2` gauged. It is not free, so the Schur index is not the arc-space Hilbert series of `R_V`; see
  THM-4672.

## Falsifiers

- **Kills part 1:**
  - a positive-Li minimal generator at a boundary or integrable level, at any weight; or
  - a non-boundary, non-integrable admissible level that is classically free.
- **Kills part 2:** a first non-arc generator at a weight other than `D_0 + 3`, or not of spin `a - 1`.
- **Cheapest next tests:**
  - `k = -6/5` (`a/b = 4/5`, `D_0 = 15`): predicted first failure at weight 18, spin 3.
  - `k = -4/3` pushed to weight 16.
  - Exact (rational) certificates at a boundary level are out of reach; the cost is the size of `I`.
