# HYP-3118: LRC14 Coordinate-Resurrection Sheaf And Adjoint Repair Calculus

## Claim

HYP-3118/S267 follows the Lee-Yang/root-lattice-ear, approximation, carrier,
circuit missing-input, and proof-circuit past-work compiler frontier by
turning the question one level sideways.  Do not ask only which scalar,
sidecar, theorem, or analogy is strongest.  Ask:

```text
when a quotient destroys a proof coordinate, what is the smallest legal
sidecar, adjoint map, or sheaf section that resurrects exactly the coordinate
needed by the next proof obligation?
```

The preserved LRC predicate is progress toward a finite LRC14 certificate:
direct witness, AP/GW boundary, or finite-address plus observer-gluing plus
endpoint-owner plus uniformity plus one live sidecar.  In the executable
scout, those first four fields behave as the base stalk.  Root/ear,
relation-lattice, component, cocycle, state-lift, and PDE sidecars behave as
local sections.

A quotient may destroy raw time, runner labels, root phase, PGF root motion,
ear payload, lattice shape, endpoint owner, circuit input, PDE boundary data,
or approximation exception data only if the destroyed coordinate is
reconstructed, annihilated, descended, or routed to named residual debt.  The
gold object is therefore not a number.  It is the repair relation between a
quotient and the smallest legal section that restores the next coordinate.

## Generated Hypotheses

1. Coordinate resurrection: every failed scalar shortcut can become useful by
   naming the first destroyed coordinate and a minimal legal repair cover.
2. Proof sheaf: finite-address, observer-gluing, endpoint-owner, and
   uniformity form the base stalk; root/ear, lattice, component, cocycle,
   state-lift, and PDE carriers are sections.
3. Adjoint quotient: a quotient is legal exactly when it has a computable
   right-adjoint section on the coordinate demanded by the next operation.
4. Matroid debt: recurring missing-input vectors behave like circuits of a
   repair object; the four core coordinates behave like coloops in the current
   abstraction.
5. Energy-root duality: PDE weak forms and Ising energy become dual to
   Lee-Yang root confinement only after boundary and zero-mode coordinates are
   retained.
6. Approximation as section: irrational/transcendental approximation is a
   section from positive interval margin to finite grid address, not a separate
   proof route.
7. Concept-lattice atlas: stable intents should become packet schema fields
   before adding more analogies.
8. PGF curve over scalar: `p0` is a lossy quotient of the full PGF root curve;
   extremality should track zero trajectories, discriminant walls, and
   root-ear transfer.
9. Ear grammar as gluing: directed, odd, and nested ears are three possible
   observer-gluing grammars with different parity and nesting debts.
10. Bravais wall ownership: raw volume pressure becomes useful only after
    relation-lattice shape and low-height wall ownership are named.
11. Savitch proof compression: finite LRC routes may need recomputable
    midpoint certificate segments rather than stored global paths.
12. `phi4` stress discipline: quartic density/moment analogies are stress
    tests unless boundary/operator/zero-mode data survive.

be the adjunction between a quotient and the smallest sidecar that restores
