# HYP-2677 - LRC(14) Packet-Sign Tournament Atlas

This file reserves HYP-2677 / T917 for the next exact atlas around the six
one-missed-sector packet signs from HYP-2674/HYP-2676.

The point being tested is deliberately not "the six signs are the proof."  Past
coefficient-tiling work already warned that unmarked sign tournaments are too
lossy.  The sharper question is whether the six packet signs become useful when
viewed simultaneously as:

```text
six K4 edge orientations,
six sector vertices ranked by exact packet mass,
three opposite K4 edge-pair balances,
and a typed address carrying additive model, squarefree profile, and visibility.
```

The planned atlas will contrast the B14 near-speed leaders, the KPS third-pocket
row, dyadic/doubled-odd finite resonances, and the HYP-2675 boundary/true-wide
rows.  It will keep exact Fraction packet arithmetic and record Tournament
Analysis fingerprints for both the K4-edge quotient and the six-sector vertex
quotient.

No LRC(14) proof is claimed in this stub.  The immediate proof obligation is to
determine which tournament fingerprints are genuine separators and which are
only lossy shadows requiring the HYP-2648 state-word or HYP-2639 relation-shell
address to be reattached.
