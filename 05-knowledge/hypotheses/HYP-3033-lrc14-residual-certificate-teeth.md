# HYP-3033: LRC14 Residual Certificate Teeth

## Claim

After the HYP-3030 status-topology gate, the remaining coarse
ET+Henselian-unit route-mixed fibers can be scheduled by a small pair of
non-route teeth:

```text
topology bucket + unit-scale tooth.
```

The topology side records the compact arc-Cech data already emitted by S194:

```text
closed beta0, closed beta1, open beta0, open beta1, safe topes, quotient defect.
```

The compressed bucket keeps only:

```text
safe topes, quotient defect, open beta0 - closed beta0.
```

The scale tooth records only:

```text
unit-q<=13
unit-q>=14
nonunit-scale
```

No route label is used in the key.  This is a route scheduler for packets
already known to be strict-open, not a replacement for the boundary/open proof
gate.

Exact magnitude is not the cheapest residual proof carrier in this ledger:

is the first tested non-route carrier with `0` route mixing on the stored S194
residual ledger.

## Interpretation

HYP-3030 already says the coarse ET+unit quotient has `0` mixed boundary/open
fibers.  HYP-3033 pushes the next layer: once status is pure, we do not need a
large exact packet key to choose the proof route.  The residual rows split
cleanly by a topology tooth plus a tiny scale tooth.

The proof target is a residual certificate-teeth theorem:

```text
For each open coarse ET+unit residual family after the status gate,
the joined topology bucket and unit-scale tooth routes the packet to either
  q-witness,
  covering/Haar/nested refinement,
  or named state-lift/F7/THM-572 debt,
without using theorem route labels as input.
```

This should attach to the HYP-3031 Haar repair ladder.  In that language, the
joined tooth is a small way to decide whether the residual mixed coordinate is
handled by direct q-witness, nested covering refinement, an owner/topology
strip, or a residual state-lift handoff.

## Guardrail

This is not yet a full theorem carrier.  The S197 script parses stored S194


into packet sidecars and rerun on a direct packet ledger.  The family proof
must show that the joined tooth is generated by packet geometry, not by
accidental formatting or route-labelled sampling.
