## How This Augments The Current Proof Attempt

1. **Receiver first.**  HYP-3083/HYP-3091/LTI-232/LTI-234 already describe a
finite-address `Phi` tuple.  HYP-3120 says this should be the canonical target
for old certificates: endpoint circuit positivity, dual Farkas potentials,
normal-fan components, and twist-ladder denominators should all emit fields in
that receiver, not separate slogans.

2. **Observer gluing needs the first lost payload.**  HYP-3054/HYP-3056 and
the source-perspective/A000568 line explain why a perspective count can look
complete while the next extension is not legal.  The Lee-Yang ear payload in
HYP-3112 is exactly this phenomenon analytically: final roots are not enough;
the one-runner payload `A_t` is the observer-extension coordinate.

3. **Loaded denominator rows should be normalized by old resource ledgers.**
HYP-3114 showed direct-time intervals can be tiny or absent, and HYP-3115 found
a finite-bank `apex7_error<=5` classifier.  HYP-3120 routes both through the
older Q27/Q31 resource descent, twist-ladder dual certificates, and unit
endpoint sieve instead of treating the classifier as a theorem.

4. **Circuit certificates need all gate columns.**  The HYP-3116
`circuit_certificate_vector` says a row needs Phi/P, magnitude-cocycle route
purity, Horn closure, protected-bridge safety, uniform-family parameter, and a
terminal exit before a proof-circuit shortcut is theorem-facing.

5. **Endpoint circuit positivity is a proof compiler.**  HYP-2108/HYP-2112 can
compile circuit-complexity sidecars from HYP-3115, the HYP-3116
missing-input ledger, and the HYP-3117 past-work compiler into endpoint-`Phi`
gates.
The complementary endpoint-credit Farkas lane turns a proof-circuit predicate
into a dual certificate or a named directed cycle, preventing a fitted finite
classifier from masquerading as a uniform proof.

6. **Coordinate resurrection is the status language for destroyed fields.**
Completed HYP-3118 gives the repair schema: a quotient may destroy a coordinate
only if the next row has the base stalk, one live section, a named
`coordinate_resurrection_cover`, `repair_cover_rank`, `adjoint_section_status`,
and terminal debt or exit.  HYP-3120 supplies the older carriers to test
against those statuses.

7. **Approximation becomes legal only after interval normalization.**
Ostrowski/Beatty/Pell and irrational/transcendental approximation are useful
only when pulled back to normalized witness intervals with margin, denominator
shell, carry/Pell wall, and exceptional-approximant data retained.

8. **F7/THM-572 debt should not stay anonymous.**  The signed-polymer /
Dirichlet network and Ising domain-wall sidecars provide an energy/conductance
way to distinguish a legal state-lift exit from a phantom boundary atom.

9. **Sexy-prime material is a guardrail, not a proof route.**  The LRC
tournament machinery can organize local residue-channel admissibility for the
sexy prime conjecture, but it lacks the analytic prime-distribution input.  It
should remain a local-sieve analogy unless an explicit analytic sidecar is
added.
