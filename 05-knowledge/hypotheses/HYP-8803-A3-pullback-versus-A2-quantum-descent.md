# HYP-8803 -- descend the exact A3 certificate across the filtration wall

all defining relations for

```text
Phi:A_3 -> A_3,
Phi(x_i)=F_i,

```

and proves non-surjectivity from the collision of `F`.  It therefore gives an

This leaves a nonfiltered descent, not a direct reuse, as the honest target.

## 2. The exact Ore algebra hidden in the suspension

Use coefficient-left ordering in `A_2` and let `ell=L+g` be the THM-2044
polynomial.  Because `ell` is linear in the source momenta, its commutators
with base polynomials have no higher-order correction:

```text
[ell,x]=3x^2,
[ell,q]=2-6xq.                                      (2)
```

Thus the subalgebra generated by `x,q,ell` is

```text
O=Q[x,q][ell;delta],
delta=3x^2 partial_x+(2-6xq) partial_q.             (3)
```

The suspension coordinate

```text
R=x(2-3xq)                                          (4)
```

obeys `delta(R)=0`.  Hence `R` is not merely central to leading Poisson order:

```text
[ell,R]=0,          R in Z(O).                      (5)
```

Every polynomial in `x,q,ell`, including every ordering of the displayed
`T,S`, therefore preserves the full `R`-column exactly.  This isolates the
quantum obstruction to the other canonical pair and its `R`-connection.

There is an even sharper localization.  After inverting `x`,

```text
q=(2x-R)/(3x^2),          t=-1/(3x),
delta(t)=1.                                             (6)
```

Consequently

```text
O[x^(-1)] = Q[R,t,t^(-1)][ell; partial_t],
[ell,t]=1.                                           (7)
```

The difficult part of the descent is therefore not local Darboux form: on the
punctured chart it is already an ordinary one-pair differential-operator
algebra over `Q[R]`.  The difficult part is polynomial extension across the
missing divisor `x=0`, together with adjoining the exact `R`-dual `D`.

This is the main conceptual gain from the external formula.  The HYP-8802
`m -> m+6` cascade should now be read as a boundary-integrality or regular-
singular obstruction, not as failure to find adapted coordinates.

## 3. Two natural orderings fail differently

Write the sheared external positions as commutative polynomials

```text
T=T(x,q,ell),             S=S(x,q,ell),
deg_ell(T,S)=(2,3).                                  (8)
```

The script implements exact Ore multiplication

```text
(a ell^i)(b ell^j)
 = sum_(k=0)^i binom(i,k) a delta^k(b) ell^(i-k+j). (9)
```

No truncation in `hbar` is involved.

For commutative expansion followed by coefficient-left normal ordering,
`[R,T]=[R,S]=0` exactly, but `[S,T]-1` has `ell`-degree three.  Its top term is

```text
-x^9(3xq-2)^2 ell^3/3.                              (10)
```

Keeping the displayed factorization and multiplying its factors in their
written order also preserves the `R`-column, but makes the residual degree
four.  Thus the visually natural factorization is not the quantum structure;
noncommutative order is load-bearing.

## 4. Weyl order is uniquely optimal in the scalar PBW family

For a scalar parameter `theta`, define

```text
Q_theta(a ell^n)
 = sum_(k=0)^n binom(n,k) theta^k delta^k(a) ell^(n-k). (11)
```

Here `theta=0` is coefficient-left normal order and `theta=1/2` is Weyl order
for the Ore pair.  The exact leading residual is

```text
coeff_ell^3([Q_theta(S),Q_theta(T)]-1)
 = x^9(2theta-1)(3xq-2)^2/3.                        (12)
```

Therefore `theta=1/2` is the unique scalar ordering that drops the residual
below degree three.  It still leaves degree two, with top term

```text
x^10(3xq-2)^2 ell^2/2.                              (13)
```

This independently explains why HYP-8802's Weyl/Moyal calculation was the
right starting gauge, while proving that ordering choice alone cannot close
the relation.

## 5. Falsifiable next steps

The original alternatives can now be made more precise.

1. **Boundary-resummation route.**  Rewrite the HYP-8802 corrections in the
   localized coordinates `(R,t,ell)`.  Test whether the `+6` weight transfer is
   a finite regular-singular connection after allowing `T` to move.  A
   polynomial answer must have no pole when translated back across `x=0`.
2. **No-finite-extension route.**  Put the correction complex in the
   `x`-adic filtration.  Prove that killing the top `ell` layer forces a pole,
   or forces a nonzero class six weights higher, for every simultaneous
   correction of `T,D,S`.  This would upgrade HYP-8802's observed cascade to a
   theorem for the present witness.
3. **Quantum-reduction route.**  Start with the exact A3 endomorphism (1),
   partially Fourier-transform the third Weyl pair, and compute the commutant
   or Hamiltonian reduction of the `(R,D_R)` pair.  A valid descent must
   identify that reduced algebra with the standard `A_2`; merely finding an
   `A_2` subalgebra inside `A_3` is insufficient.

The live positive target remains a simultaneous polynomial quadruple in
`A_2`.  The exact negative result currently proved is THM-2046: no witness in
the external certificate's filtered pullback class can work with this `R`.
