# HYP-4071 — LRC safe-measure = weighted lattice-theta; the additive-combinatorics bridge

## The reformulation (EXACT, verified)
`μ(S) = meas{t: ||v_i t||>=1/14 ∀i} = Σ_{m ∈ L(S)} ∏_i c(m_i)`, `L(S)={m∈ℤ^13: Σ m_i v_i=0}` the RELATION
LATTICE, `c(0)=6/7`, `c(m)=-sin(πm/7)/(πm)` (`=0 iff 7|m`). A **lattice theta** `Θ_{L(S)}(c)`. Derived by
Fourier-expanding the danger indicators (opus HYP-4058, re-read as a lattice sum). Truncation `|m_i|<=K`
converges to `μ` slowly (`c~1/|m|`): AP/GW (`μ=0`) vs `{2..14}` (`μ=0.061`) at K=3 give `0.075,0.074` vs `0.093`.

## Two gaps as theta statements
- **GAP-A (tight ⟺ μ=0) = EXACT theta-cancellation** `Θ_{L(S)}(c)=0`. The AP relation lattice (the
  discrete-2nd-derivative lattice, generated by `(…,1,-2,1,…)`) is the densest in short vectors; its
  weighted theta cancels `(6/7)^13`. GAP-A = "the only relation lattices whose weighted theta cancels are
  AP's and GW's" — an inverse/rigidity theorem for a lattice theta (Freiman-flavoured, cf. GR extremizers=APs).
- **GAP-B (covering ⟹ μ>0) = theta POSITIVITY**: `Σ_{m≠0} ∏c(m_i) > -(6/7)^13`. Dominated by SHORT
  relations (2-term: `m_i v_i=-m_j v_j`, `|m|=(v_j/g, v_i/g)`), whose count = ADDITIVE ENERGY — the BDFKK
  product-set-sumset / Bourgain exponential-sum machinery; GR's hypercube `{-K..K}^13` = the bounded-relation
  domain. NAMED ANALYTIC TARGET: the weighted short-relation mass of a primitive covering family is `<(6/7)^13`.

## The honest negative (verified): additive energy is INSUFFICIENT
`E(S)=#{a+b=c+d}` is translation-invariant; `μ` is not. `{1..13}` (tight) and `{2..14}` (loose) have the
SAME `E=1469`. So no pure additive functional decides LRC-tightness; the **mod-7 radius arithmetic**
(`14=2·7`, `c(m)=0 iff 7|m`) is the theta WEIGHT, not the lattice. Pure sumset theory (GR) sees the lattice
only. => the covering-min needs a WEIGHTED (arithmetic) additive-combinatorics bound, not a plain one.
