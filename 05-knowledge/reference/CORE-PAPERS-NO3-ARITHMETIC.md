# Arithmetic source for reciprocal-grid obstruction families

Checked September6,2026 against the primary paper's opening theorem.

**CITED:** [Dirichlet1837, English translation0808.1408v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1408v2)
proves that an arithmetic progression whose first term and difference are
coprime contains infinitely many primes.

The first consumer is the
[native reciprocal packet theorem](../results/continuing6_20260906_reciprocal_native.md).
Only infinitude of its three reduced prime progressions is imported.
The displayed integer coordinates, reciprocal identities, native bounds,
primitive packet bijection and failure of the prescribed swap are proved
in that note and its independent audit. No new no-three-in-line literature
priority is claimed.

The separate [complementary norm theorem](../results/continuing6_20260906_norm_units.md)
uses an elementary proof of unboundedly many primes congruent1 modulo4;
it does not require Dirichlet's theorem.
