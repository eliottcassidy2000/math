# Core papers: classical limits of vertex algebras (singular support, arc spaces, classical freeness)

Checked 2026-10-18 (kind-pasteur-2026-10-18-S1); abstract or introduction level unless stated. Consumers:
THM-4671, THM-4672, THM-4673, HYP-9293 to HYP-9298, and
[the results note](../results/singular_support_arc_spaces_classical_freeness_20261018.md). For the definitions of
factorization and chiral algebras see S43's
[bridge note](../results/factorization_vertex_supersymmetric_bridge_20261018.md), section 1.

## Foundations

- **Li, *Abelianizing vertex algebras*, CMP 259 (2005).** Li's filtration; `gr_F V` is a Poisson vertex algebra.
  CITED for "Li filtration = PBW filtration" for strongly generated `V`.
- **Arakawa-Linshaw, *Singular support of a vertex algebra and the arc space of its associated scheme*,
  Progr. Math. 330 (2019), arXiv:1804.01287.**
  - `SS(V) ⊂ J_infty X_V` as a closed subscheme.
  - Example: not an isomorphism of schemes but an isomorphism of varieties (non-quasi-lisse, reduced `X_V`).
  - Poses the finite-generation question for `ker pi`.
- **van Ekeren-Heluani, *Chiral homology of elliptic curves and the Zhu algebra*, CMP (2021).** Defines classical
  freeness. `Vir_(p,q)` is classically free iff `p = 2`; `L_k(sl2)` is classically free for `k ∈ Z_(>=0)`. PROVED,
  CITED.
- **Andrews-van Ekeren-Heluani, *The singular support of the Ising model*, arXiv:2005.10769 (IMRN 2023).**
  - `ker pi` for `Vir_(3,4)` is generated by `b` (weight 9).
  - It gives a fermionic formula, and remarks that for `Vir_(3,5)` the analogous relation alone fails from
    weight 19.
  - Expects boundary admissible `sl2` to be classically free. Reproduced by our engine.

## Later classical-freeness results

- **Linshaw-Song, *Cosets of free field algebras via arc spaces*, IMRN 2024, arXiv:2109.09050.** `L_k(sp_(2n))`
  classically free; kernels finitely generated in further examples.
- **Hao Li.** `N = 1` minimal models are classically free iff `(p, q) = (2, 4k)`.
- **Creutzig-Linshaw-Song, Proc. AMS 2024.** Orthosymplectic affine superalgebras.
- **Salazar, *Boundary minimal models and the Rogers-Ramanujan identities*, JPAA 230 (2026), arXiv:2405.07126.**
  Classically free irreducible `Vir_(p,q)`-modules occur only for `p = 2`.
- **Song-Zeng, arXiv:2606.14407 (2026).** For classically free `V`, `R_V ≅ gr A(V)`; nontrivial holomorphic VOAs are
  not classically free.
- **Kanade, arXiv:2606.19234 (2026).** `L_1(sl_n)` is classically free.
- **Adamović-Möller, Math. Z. 313:87 (2026), arXiv:2511.20121v2.** Remark 6.3: boundary `L_(-2+2/p)(sl2)` is
  "expected" to be classically free. This is still unproved, so HYP-9293's boundary part is open in the literature.

## Arc spaces and q-series

- **Bruschek-Mourtada-Schepers, *Arc spaces and Rogers-Ramanujan identities*, Ramanujan J. 30 (2013).**
  `C[x]/(x^k)`: Andrews-Gordon. CITED.
- **Mourtada, *Hilbert meets Ramanujan*, Bull. AMS 62 (2025).**
  - Survey: fat points and the node `C[x,y]/(xy)`.
  - No weighted two-variable fat point with an `A_2` product. Searched for HYP-9298 by full-text grep of the
    author's PDF.

## Four-dimensional `N = 2`

- **Beem-Lemos-Liendo-Peelaers-Rastelli-van Rees, CMP 336 (2015).** Chiral algebras of 4d `N = 2` SCFTs; Schur index
  = vacuum character. **Beem-Rastelli, JHEP 2018:** Higgs branch = associated variety; MLDEs.
- **Deb-Meneghelli-Rastelli, *The nilpotency index for 4d N=2 SCFTs*, arXiv:2503.05975 (v2 2026).** Index of `T` in
  `R_V`. Kodaira theories and `N = 4` SU(2) have index 2, which our engine reproduces for `N = 4` SU(2).
- **Andrews-Banerjee-Bhargava-Singh-Tao, *Argyres-Douglas theories, Macdonald indices and arc space of Zhu
  algebra*, arXiv:2507.06294v1 (2025).**
  - §3.2.3 Theorem: for strongly finitely generated `V`, the refined character equals the arc-space Hilbert series.
  - **REFUTED as stated**, by Ising (van Ekeren-Heluani) and by `L_(-1/2)(sl2)` (THM-4672). The proof omits lifts of
    arc syzygies.
  - The `(A_1, A_(2n))` statements survive, since those chiral algebras are classically free. The
    `(A_1, D_(2n+1))` conjecture needs boundary `sl2` freeness (HYP-9293).
  - v1 only, as of 2026-10-18.
- **Argyres-Douglas chiral algebras.** `(A_(N-1), A_(k-1))` corresponds to the `W_N(N, N+k)` minimal model, and
  `(A_1, D_(2n+1))` to `L_(-4n/(2n+1))(sl2)` (Cordova-Shao; Creutzig; Song). CITED; used in HYP-9296 and HYP-9297.

## Not found (searches 2026-10-18; a search miss is not novelty)

Classical freeness or singular support of:
- `Vir_(p,q)` beyond `(3,4)`;
- non-boundary admissible `sl2`;
- `N = 2` minimal models or odd lattice vertex algebras;
- `W_3` minimal models;
- small `N = 4` at `c = -9`;
- `L_(-2)(so8)`.

Also not found: arc spaces of weighted two-variable fat points with `A_2` Rogers-Ramanujan products.
