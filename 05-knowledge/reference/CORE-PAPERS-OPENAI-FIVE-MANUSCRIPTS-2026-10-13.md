### Reconstruction of Function Fields from Mod-ℓ Milnor K-Theory (October 5, 2026; 36 pp.)
- **Claim.** For finitely generated `K/k`, `L/l` over algebraically closed constants of characteristic `!= ℓ` and transcendence degree `>= 2`, compatible isomorphisms `K_1^M(K)/ℓ -> K_1^M(L)/ℓ` (preserving the Steinberg relations, i.e. `K_2^M/ℓ` with its product) are exactly the maps induced by field isomorphisms of the perfect closures (with constants matched), up to one scalar in `F_ℓ^x` and Frobenius powers.
- **Technique.** Alternating pairs of characters (`f(x)g(1-x) = f(1-x)g(x)`) are valuative (Topaz's local theory); quasi-divisorial valuations and residue curves via Pop's density theorem; bounded support of the classes `Theta([t-a])` on a projective model by Riemann-Hurwitz on five pencil members; incidence variety and compositum descent to recover curve subfields; simultaneous values at a blowup give the field operations; scalar and Frobenius ambiguity last.
- **Repository role.** The alternating-pair rigidity is typed ANALOGY against the direction-class method for unit-distance graphs (S24) and against the independence of `v_2, v_3` in the Collatz thread; no proved consequence.

### The Deligne-Drinfeld conjecture (September 23, 2026; 41 pp.)
- **Claim.** `grt_1 = Lie_Q<e_3, e_5, e_7, ...>` (free on one generator in each odd weight `>= 3`), with the completed isomorphism; hence `H^0(GC_2)` of Kontsevich's graph complex is the completed free Lie algebra.
- **Technique.** Characteristic-two filtered degeneration of the pentagon (`A = x^2, C = [x,y], B = y`, filtered by the `B`-count); transfer of the leading relation to the level-two cyclotomic arrangement algebra by an ordering comparison with count-monotone corrections on both sides; integral deletion operators detect the leading part after `A = 0`; shift symmetry and Vandermonde confine the image to the free Lie algebra on `ad_C^k B`; lower bound by regularized braid holonomy values; dimension squeeze on saturated `Z_(2)`-lattices.
- **Repository role.** The squeeze needs linear equations and saturated lattices; it does not transfer to the Keller equation (bilinear, gains solutions modulo `p`) nor to colourings (THM-4628 shows the degenerate problem is harder). The ordering-comparison device names a test for the Weyl/Poisson lane of `JC_2` (PROPOSAL).

### Ordinary two-point correlations of multiplicative functions (September 24, 2026; 87 pp.)
- **Claim.** `sum_(n <= X) lambda(a_1 n + b_1) lambda(a_2 n + b_2) << X (log X)^(-c)` for fixed non-proportional affine forms (ordinary two-point Chowla with a logarithmic saving, every cutoff); binary corrected Elliott for ordinary averages under uniform nonpretentiousness of one factor.
- **Technique.** Multiplicative dilations and a divisibility graph; centered prime supplies (`1_(p|n) - 1/p`) with padding; comparison of integer averages with an independent residue model by Braverman's bounded-independence theorem after an exact `t`-wise-uniform correction (Lemma 3.1); closed walks, prohibited words, forests of repetition patterns; for general functions, the graph is fixed before the long average.
- **Repository role.** THM-4627: for Collatz the residue comparison is exact periodicity below the stopping time and the correlations are explicit and nonzero; beyond it coalescence gives correlation `+1`. The Chowla difficulty has no Collatz counterpart except the one-point equidistribution of `tau mod 2` (HYP-9258).

### The two-primary Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer formula in Selmer corank at most one (October 6, 2026; 164 pp.)
- **Claim.** For every `E/Q` with `2`-power Selmer corank `<= 1`: rank = analytic rank = corank, Sha finite, and `v_2(Q_E) = v_2(# Sha)` for the normalized leading term (total real period, full-lattice regulator, torsion squared, all Tamagawa numbers); any-prime input criterion via the unrestricted low-corank converse; density-one quadratic twists.
- **Technique.** Three comparisons (positive: `X(A) >= 0` for non-CM `A` of analytic rank `<= 1`; split-pair anchor `an(A_h) + an(A_hk) = 1`, `X(A_h) + X(A_hk) = 0`; CM); finite free cochain models with marked maps and homotopies; line and square switches with specified determinant volumes; horizontal divisibility at height-one primes plus residual concentration plus independent primitivity (Pfaffian cofactor lift, CM comparator, ray units, real-component detector).
- **Repository role.** The "integral versus unit" separation and the twist-pair squeeze (candidate pattern, section 5.2 of the digest).

### Exact Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer Formula from Low Selmer Corank (October 7, 2026; 94 pp.)
- **Claim.** If `s_q(E) in {0, 1}` for some prime `q`, then `r = a = s_q`, Sha is finite, and the full BSD leading-term formula holds at every prime, with no hypotheses on reduction, torsion, isogenies, CM or residual images. Inputs: the low-corank converse [23], the two-primary formula [24], the twist-density theorem [22] (Smith), Gross-Zagier-Kolyvagin, Burungale-Flach (CM rank zero).
- **Technique.** At an odd `p`: `X_p(E) >= 0` (Kato's element in finite cochain models over `Z_p[[t, u_1, ..., u_m]]`, tame variables `u_i`) and `X_p(E) + X_p(E^D) = 0` (Heegner index over `Q(sqrt D)`); quotient series `U_w = B_w/L_w` shown integral (high-character divisibility, Weierstrass division) and `p`-primitive (ordinary modular curve), then units by theta-to-cusp comparisons at characteristic-zero divisors and by adding tame variables until simultaneous jumps have codimension two.
- **Repository role.** If right, full BSD for every elliptic curve over `Q` of analytic rank at most one; the remaining wall is Selmer corank at least two at every prime. The repository's fruit curve (LMFDB 910.a4) and square curve (43.a1) satisfy the hypothesis. The codex note builds finite Selmer-growth receipts for the hypothesis.
