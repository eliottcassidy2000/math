Target: physical source profiles with a fixed Hamiltonian-path readout.
Map: swap the tags O/J above one physical source s.
Preserved predicate: both profiles are actual sources and have F=G(s).

K it uses the physical boundary b=(3,1,3,1) and the auxiliary boundary
