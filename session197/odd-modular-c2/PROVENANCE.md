This note leaves the independently reviewed odd-modulus packet unchanged. Its PROOF.md remains SHA2561e011326969c4dc8a3f4f26ed11f673f451e51b153ae714a3fbf48760e03cb4b. The packet supplies an alternative finite-gadget matrix-group construction; no novelty or stronger scalar-coverage claim over the following earlier results is intended.

1. **Earlier C1 universality is broader.** The reviewed modular-gadget proof, published at [48e10c6a4773de23f9ca19ef120b17012a0d7812](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/48e10c6a4773de23f9ca19ef120b17012a0d7812/session197/modular-gadget-universality/PROOF.md), SHA25674df1989d75ff743177937c108ac36dce133412e48701e4d412297d8c47b7d63, proves in Corollary3 that every parity-compatible residue modulo every positive integer occurs in infinitely many strong C1 one-apex tournaments. For modulus2^k it gives order at most4k^2+2k+3. The present odd-modulus proof does not improve that scalar scope, feedback bound, multiplicity statement or witness-order bound.

2. **Earlier joint profiles also imply C2 dyadic coverage.** The reviewed joint-profile proof, published at [33a63e57e68b818d2282721ef40b34aca420c8ab](https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/33a63e57e68b818d2282721ef40b34aca420c8ab/session197/dyadic-profile-universality/PROOF.md), SHA25649bfc941828bf680dcaca1d5e4ec448f3b689d0f72c71f164bcb8b6bf0088c64, gives in Corollary2 actual strong sources(D,1,U,1) with independently prescribed odd D,U modulo2^k and order at most4k^2+3k+2. Combining this with the reviewed N graft and a transitive two-vertex left source gives H=3D+6U-4 and C=2. For any odd target residue r, set U=1 and D=3^{-1}(r-2) modulo2^k. This is an odd D residue and yields H=r. The graft adds three vertices, giving order at most4k^2+3k+5. This C2 statement is a derived comparison from the two named inputs, not a claim that the earlier joint-profile packet separately stated it.

3. **What this packet records.** Its distinct content is the explicit corrected commutator T12(3/4), relative elementary action away from3, and compilation of modular inverse occurrences into positive physical gadget words. Its proof and audit cover odd moduli only. They do not establish exact-target coverage or cofiniteness. Later primitive-triple or prescribed-ray investigations are outside the frozen snapshot.
