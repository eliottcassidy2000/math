# Variable-leaf rigidity of the primitive cancellation bank

Session 197. **PROVED all-order extension of the fixed-leaf classification.** Keep the physical bank K,X,Y in `PRIMITIVE_CANCELLATION.md` and the singleton leaf P=K1, but allow the other leaf to be Tr_r for every integer r>=1. Binary-mode cancellation is possible if and only if r=7. Thus the entire variable-leaf language has exactly the rays already classified in that packet.

## 1. Ratio support and the two valuation constraints

For r=1 the two leaf signs are positive; for r=2 exactly one is zero. Both cases are excluded by the exact physical cancellation criterion. For r>=3 that criterion is

    rho_L/rho_M=kappa_r=(2^r-1)/(2*(2^(r-2)-1)).          (1)

The bank ratios are 2, 2^4*11*127/(31*37), and 2^5*11/37. Therefore any ratio generated by the bank is supported only on {2,11,31,37,127}, and its valuations obey

    v_127=-v_31, v_11=-v_37.                             (2)

These conditions are also sufficient for membership in the bank ratio group: set the X exponent to v_127, the Y exponent to -v_37-v_127, and choose the K exponent to correct v_2. This gives integer exponents and all required valuations. As usual, signed exponents give actual left/right ordered joins, with common padding if needed. At the successful leaf r=7, the explicit classification already guarantees nonempty modules without padding.

Let g=gcd(2^r-1,2^(r-2)-1). Subtraction and reduction modulo 3 give g=1 for odd r and g=3 for even r. Thus

    kappa_r=N/(2D),
    N=(2^r-1)/g, D=(2^(r-2)-1)/g,

where N,D are coprime odd positive integers and

    4 < N/D = 4+3/(2^(r-2)-1) <= 7.                     (3)

## 2. The order-36 obstruction removes the 11/37 mode

The multiplicative order of 2 modulo 37 is 36. Indeed 2^36=1 modulo 37, while 2^18=-1 and 2^12=26 modulo 37. Any proper divisor of 36 divides either 18 or 12, so these checks establish the order exactly.

Suppose 37 divides N. Then 37 divides 2^r-1, hence 36 divides r. In particular r is even, g=3 and 6 divides r. Since 2^6=1 modulo 9, the integer 2^r-1 is divisible by 9; consequently N=(2^r-1)/3 is divisible by 3. Coprimality of N,D means that this gives a nonzero 3-adic valuation in (1), impossible for the bank.

Similarly, if 37 divides D, then 36 divides r-2. Again r is even and g=3, and 9 divides 2^(r-2)-1, so 3 divides D and yields the same unsupported-prime contradiction. Therefore v_37(kappa_r)=0. Equation (2) then gives v_11(kappa_r)=0 as well.

This argument is exact for arbitrarily large r. No factoring of large Mersenne numbers and no finite leaf-length cutoff are required.

## 3. The remaining mode determines the leaf uniquely

After Section 2, conditions (2) and prime support force the odd ratio in (3) to be

    N/D=(127/31)^a for some integer a.

Since N/D>4, a must be positive. Since (127/31)^2>7, (3) forces a=1. Thus

    (2^r-1)/(2^(r-2)-1)=127/31.

Putting z=2^(r-2), cross multiplication gives 31(4z-1)=127(z-1), hence z=32 and r=7.

Conversely, at r=7 the ratio is 127/62=rho_X/rho_Y. Taking L=X and M=Y realizes cancellation physically. The complete allocation classification, primitive content, prime/7p witnesses and target-count inverse are therefore exactly those of `PRIMITIVE_CANCELLATION.md` and `PRIMITIVE_TARGET_INVERSE.md`. Nothing outside the specified bank and quotient is excluded.
