# An exact arithmetic spectrum for two-feedback, two-cycle tournaments

Session197. **PROVED, with a closed finite-state certificate.** This is a classification of the Hamilton-path counts of strong tournaments with exactly two Hamilton cycles and feedback vertex number at most two. It is not a classification of all strong C2 tournaments or a solution of the unrestricted spectrum problem.

## 1. The statement

Let g(1)=1, g(2l)=g(l), and g(2l-1)=g(l)+l-(l mod2) for l>=2. The three symmetric functions N,M,P and their literal two-port constructions are the frozen `session197/fixed-two-families` packet. Their domains are N,M with u,v>=2, and P with u,v>=1.

Add the fourth function, with u>=2 and v>=1, writing D=g(u) and p=u mod2:

    Q(u,v)=g(v)(D+3u-p-2)
            -(v mod2)(u-1)+v(2D+4u-p-1).          (1)

**Theorem.** The exact path-count set of strong tournaments T with C(T)=2 and feedback vertex number at most two is

    {15} union image(N) union image(M)
         union image(P) union image(Q).             (2)

Every parameter pair compiles into a physical fixed-two-port word. Conversely every tournament in the stated class has a value in (2). The converse concerns values: some of the reductions preserve a value rather than the original marked graph, and this distinction is explicit below.

A finite exact inverse algorithm follows from the parameter bounds. In particular 533,821 and1861 are not in (2). These are exclusions from this restricted class, not global path-count gaps.

## 2. Why fixed ports represent exactly feedback number at most two

Start with a->b. Append vertices beating any chosen subset of a,b and losing to every earlier nonport; retain a,b forever. Masks0,1,2,3 use bit0 for a and bit1 for b. Deleting the two ports leaves a transitive graph.

Conversely, if deleting at most two vertices of a tournament leaves a transitive graph, extend that deletion set to two distinct vertices if necessary. Order the two selected vertices so their arc is a->b, and order the remaining transitive tournament. Its successive incidences are exactly such a mask word. Strong C2 tournaments have enough vertices for this padding.

Converse the graph, interchange a,b, and reverse all nonports. At word level this is

    tau(W)=phi(reverse(W)),
    phi(0)=3, phi(1)=1, phi(2)=2, phi(3)=0.         (3)

Thus tau is an involution preserving H and C, realized by an explicit graph converse isomorphism.

## 4. A complete regular-language normal form

The following notation denotes ordinary regular languages; concatenation is physical word concatenation:

    A=1{1,3}*,       B=2{2,3}*,
    R={0,1}*1,       S={0,2}*2.

Let L0 be the union of the seven languages

    A 2 R;
    A 0 3 (empty or R);
    (empty or B) 0 2 3 (empty or S);
    B 1 3 (empty or S);
    A 0^k 3, k>=1;
    B 1 0^k 3, k>=1;
    the singleton word 0 1 3.                       (5)

The exact accepted language is

    L0 union tau(L0).                              (6)

This equality is certified at arbitrary length. The verifier constructs a Thompson epsilon-NFA for (5)-(6), with450 states, and explores its complete subset-product with the15-class edge-output controller, remembering the last output bit. The product closes after47 states. Every reachable product state has identical acceptance on both sides, and all four outgoing transitions are checked. This proves equality for every finite word, not only for tested words up to a maximum length.

The printed quotient also permits a direct case check. Its only nontrivial recurrent behavior is self-loops; the two flexible initial phases and the flexible final phases are separated by finitely many gates. The independent audit should verify the full closed product rather than rely on this qualitative description.

## 5. The fourth physical family

The fourth line of (5) is Q. Read w as the binary digits of u-1 from most significant first, map1,0 to masks2,3, append1,3, and append masks0,2 according to the reversed binary digits of v-1. The suffix is empty when v=1.

Its seed after the gate1,3, rooted at port b, has profile

    (H,e,R,d)=(3D+6u-2p-2,
                D+2u-p-1,
                3D+7u-2p-3,
                D+3u-p-2), with s_b=2 and C=2.     (7)

Here is an all-order graph derivation. Begin with V(u), the one-apex graph of profile(g(u),p,u) used in the N/M/P proof. Add two successive clones z->y, both beating only its apex. This is exactly N's first-clone seed, with z as its first clone. Append a new vertex b beating only z, and mark b. The ordinary singleton move J gives (7). Relabel the vertices as ports(apex,b), then the old transitive chain,z,y. The fixed-port word becomes B1 3. The marked root really is the new b, not one of the old clones.

Equivalently, begin from N's adjacent first-clone profile

    (D+3u-p-2, u-1, 2D+4u-p-1)

and apply the ordinary one-seam gadget indexed by v. Its scalar transfer is exactly (1). The actual fixed-port suffix and the retained-root identity produce the same graph/value description. This proves Q at arbitrary parameters and explains why it is not an arbitrary formal arithmetic state.

## 6. Reducing the remaining regular words arithmetically

The first four lines of (5) give N,M,P,Q, with two harmless boundaries:

- An empty suffix in A03 gives the scalar N(u,2), already included in (2).
- The allowed empty prefix or suffix in the P line corresponds exactly to parameter1.

For clarity, the two unary terminal languages have genuine scalar reductions, proved next. No claim that their port-marked graphs equal the replacement graphs is needed.

### A0^k3

The source A is U(u), with D=g(u), p=u mod2. At the two ports its response sums are

    h=D+u-p, R_a+R_b=D+3u-1,
    K_ab+K_ba=u+p.

The last identity is the adjacent outdegree-one identity. Each universal sink doubles each R, leaves h unchanged, makes the two endpoint counts zero, and leaves K_ab,K_ba unchanged: a cover ending at one selected port must append that sink to its other path. After k>=1 sinks, the final birth beating both ports therefore has count

    h+2^k(R_a+R_b)-(K_ab+K_ba)
      =(2^k+1)D+2^k(3u-1)-2p
      =N(u,2^k).                                   (8)

Both selected roots originally have start count1; sinks preserve these counts and force endpoints outside the selected pair. The final cycle readout is therefore2.

### B10^k3

The source B is V(u)->b. After mask1 it is V plus b->z with the reverse seam z->a. Its response sums at the retained old ports are

    h=D+2u,
    R_a+R_b=2D+6u-p-1,
    K_ab+K_ba=u+p.                                 (9)

For the R rows, retain the apex through O then Z, giving R_a=4u-1. Alternatively delete b and reinsert it as a singleton one-seam root at z, giving R_b=2D+2u-p.

For K_ab, the b-start path is either b alone or b,z; the other path ending at a contributes1 or p respectively. For K_ba, z must end the a-start slot and b must end the other slot. Removing b,z gives a reserved-a cover of V, of which there are u. The sole forbidden case is the reserved singleton a, which cannot be followed by z. Thus the two K values are1+p and u-1, proving (9).

The same sink and final pair-birth argument gives

    (2^(k+1)+1)D+(3*2^(k+1)+1)u
       -2^k(p+1)-p = M(u,2^k).                     (10)

These final graphs also have C=2. The isolated word013 is the regular five-tournament with rows[6,12,24,17,3], H=15 and C=2. Finally all converse words in (6) have the same scalar values. This proves the reverse inclusion in (2); the explicit physical families and regular5 give the forward inclusion.

## 7. A finite exact inverse, without a graph census

The already proved bounds are N>=3uv+1, M>=6uv+u+v+1, and P>=6uv+3u+3v+1. Symmetry permits u<=v in these three families.

For Q, its actual first-clone source satisfies

    H_source-e_source>=2u,
    R_source>=4u+1.

Since g(v)>=1 and v mod2<=1,

    Q(u,v)>=2u+v(4u+1).                            (11)

Consequently u<=(h-1)/6 and v<=(h-2u)/(4u+1) suffice for a target h. Q is not assumed symmetric.

The standard-library spectrum.py enumerates precisely these finite regions and records every matching parameter pair and physical word, plus regular5 at15. This is an exact decision procedure for the restricted class, not a claim that every odd h passes it. Both positive and negative answers have all-order force because of Theorem(2).

The verifier finds no parameter pair at533,821,1861. A physical unrestricted C2 witness at533 is

    [2,4,9,19,39,335,159,319,607,1279,511].

It has exactly533 Hamilton paths and two Hamilton cycles. Deleting vertices{2,5,8} leaves a transitive tournament, whereas no pair deletion does. Hence its feedback number is exactly3. Combined with the inverse exclusion, this proves that the restricted C2 value set is genuinely smaller than the full C2 value set. No independent C1 exclusion or least-gap claim is needed for this conclusion.
