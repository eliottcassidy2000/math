# Every congruence obstruction for a fixed rooted prefix

Session197. **Exact modular image and constructive local admissibility.** This short addendum upgrades the inherited necessary gcd filter to a complete description of congruence obstructions. It does not upgrade that filter to exact-value sufficiency.

## 1. Input and exact statement

Fix a physical rooted-grammar prefix (h,e,R). Its next-gadget count is

    F(L)=h g(L)+R L−e(L mod2),   L>=1.

Here h is odd and R=e mod2. Let d=gcd(h,R). The physical seam law and its necessary congruence filter are inherited from ARITHMETIC_INVERSE.md, SHA2564ac1727eb27c85f8a39f5598eb9f0e1b020bc4020cbca0a94e6288cb0676c3b7.

The new sufficiency input is the reviewed joint modular gadget theorem: for every even M, every pair L=u mod M and g(L)=z mod M with z odd has arbitrarily large actual physical gadget witnesses. Its proof is published at

https://github.com/eliottcassidy2000/math/blob/48e10c6a4773de23f9ca19ef120b17012a0d7812/session197/modular-gadget-universality/PROOF.md

with SHA25674df1989d75ff743177937c108ac36dce133412e48701e4d412297d8c47b7d63.

**Theorem.** For any positive modulus m, put D=gcd(d,m). The exact residue image of F is

    {r mod m: r is compatible with odd parity,
                 and r=0 or−e mod D}.                         (1)

Compatibility with odd parity means r is odd when m is even; it imposes no restriction when m is odd. Every residue in (1) has arbitrarily large physical witnesses L and unbounded actual counts F(L).

Consequently an integer N passes every congruence obstruction for this fixed prefix if and only if

    N is odd, and N=0 or−e mod d.                              (2)

This is a statement about the full congruence closure of the image, not membership in the image.

## 2. Both cosets are sufficient

Write L=2v+t, t in{0,1}, and g(L)=2w+1. Then

    F(L)=h+2hw+2Rv+t(R−e).                                    (3)

The joint theorem, applied with modulus2m, allows v,w to vary independently modulo m while t is fixed. The subgroup generated by2h and2R modulo2m is exactly the subgroup of multiples of2D. Thus the precise lifted image modulo2m is the union of the cosets

    h+2D Z,    h+R−e+2D Z.                                   (4)

Both constants are odd. Since D divides h and R and is odd, their reductions modulo D are0 and−e. Projecting (4) modulo m gives exactly (1). When m is odd, each residue has a unique odd lift modulo2m; when m is even, exactly the odd residues have such lifts. This checks the parity/normalization boundary rather than silently dropping the parity of L.

For an explicit construction, choose an odd representative N of the desired residue and choose t with D dividing N+t e. Put

    K=h+t(R−e),  b=(N−K)/2.

Then D divides b. Solve h w+R v=b mod m by the Euclidean algorithm. For example, if xh+yR=d, solve d s=b mod m and take w=xs, v=ys. The reduced modulus m/D may be1, in which case s=0 is valid. Set u=2v+t mod2m and z=2w+1 mod2m. Joint universality supplies a physical L with these two residues; (3) then gives F(L)=N mod m. Its arbitrary-large witnesses remain available, and F(L)>=LR for physical prefixes makes the attained counts unbounded.

Necessity of (1) follows already from (3); thus no congruence is being inferred from an abstract unphysical profile. For (2), necessity follows by testing moduli2 and d. Conversely, if (2) holds, then it holds after reduction modulo every D=gcd(d,m), so (1) supplies a witness modulo every m. The same parity branch can be chosen consistently, but the physical witness may depend on m.

## 3. Size and examples

The two cosets merge exactly when D divides e. Thus the residue image has size

    (m/D) * (1 if D divides e else2),       m odd,
    (m/(2D)) * (1 if D divides e else2),    m even.             (5)

In particular every fixed prefix has full odd image modulo every power of two, since d is odd. Odd-modulus restrictions can be stronger than the common content gcd(h,e,R).

- The physical prefix(3,1,3), obtained from the singleton by L2, has primitive common content1 but can produce only0 or2 modulo3.
- The physical prefix(33,1,33), obtained by L32, can produce only0 or32 modulo33. The integer143 satisfies the separate prime-modulus tests: it is−1 modulo3 and0 modulo11. It nevertheless fails modulo33. One cannot choose the parity branch independently at each prime factor and then combine the results.
- A prefix with d=1 has no congruence obstruction beyond odd parity. This includes the singleton prefix. Its known exact-value gaps are therefore invisible to every modulus.

The last observation does not assert that every locally admissible target is globally attainable, or that a finite bank has cofinite image. The exceptional counts7 and21, for example, are not repaired by modular witnesses of larger size. No infinite-primality claim follows.

## 4. Verification

The standalone verifier generates a small physical prefix bank by the exact integer-gadget transfer. For each prefix and each small modulus, it enumerates the complete independent residue variables in (3), compares their image with (1), and checks the cardinality formula. Separate tests execute the Euclidean construction and the actual joint-residue word compiler, then evaluate g on the resulting integer L. It checks the composite-modulus branch hostile above explicitly.

Run `python3 verify.py` and `python3 -O verify.py`. Neither writes files. The all-order completeness follows from the reviewed physical joint-residue theorem and the subgroup calculation, not from the finite bank.
