## Live concept board

The current board has seven objects.  Every new idea should be compared with
all seven rather than only with its home conjecture.

1. **Weak-order digraph:** remembers which literal inequalities a certificate
   proves; its cycle space controls assignment coverage.
2. **Divisor-death barcode:** remembers when each `phi(d)` grid layer is killed
   by speed insertion; it is a deletion process, not a transport process.
3. **Local Newton polygon:** forgets root residues but can separate algebraic
   factors by slopes at a chosen prime.
4. **Pole valuation fan:** forgets global chart entry but can isolate the unique
   top Faber face in polar, pure-`q`, and regular-`H` lanes.
5. **Endpoint jet:** remembers precisely the derivatives consumed by a
   substitution operation; its zeroth coordinate is the scalar `H`.
6. **Signed forest boundary:** reconstructs partition currents efficiently but
   carries no positivity or locality by itself.
7. **Upset dual / transverse selector:** detects whether a signed or commuting
   lift actually lies in the positive refinement cone.

Two relations are already exact.  A forest is optimal for independent signed
difference reconstruction, while a directed cycle is necessary for weak-order
coverage.  And both `p`-adic slopes and pole valuations are useful only when
the chosen local observer separates the faces that the target predicate
consumes.

## New exact mathematics

### 1. LRC fixed-grid response is inclusion--exclusion plus death

For `m|q`, define the principal response tail

```text
T_q(m)=sum_(m|d|q) phi(d).
```

If `q=prod p^a` and `m=prod p^b`, then THM-3130 proves

```text
T_q(m)=prod_(p^a||q) t(p,a,b),
t(p,a,0)=p^a,
t(p,a,b)=p^a-p^(b-1)  for b>=1.                              (1)
```

For an unhit-divisor upset with minimal antichain `M`, its complete grid
witness count is

```text
|W_q|=sum_(empty!=A subset M)(-1)^(|A|+1)T_q(lcm(A)).        (2)
```

Inserting a speed `v` kills exactly the part inside
`Div(gcd(v,q))`.  The count is decreasing and supermodular, and each divisor
layer is a bar of multiplicity `phi(d)` with death time
`min{i:d|v_i}`.  This is an exact operation law.  Its hostile boundary is
equally exact: after normalization, `q=6,S={1}->{1,2}` decreases the upset
mass of `{2,6}` from `3/5` to `1/2`.  A cemetery state can conserve the lost
mass, but that state is an added observer rather than an LRC witness.

### 2. LRC reflected assignment cover is cycle versus potential

For a directed edge define

```text
U_(u->v)={q>0:q_u<=q_v}.                                     (3)
```

THM-3135 proves

```text
union_(e in E)U_e covers all positive assignments
        iff E contains a directed cycle.                     (4)
```

Missing every lane means strict descent on every edge.  A directed cycle
makes this impossible; a DAG admits a strictly decreasing topological
potential.

The orientation is load-bearing.  For pair `(i,j)`, the tail ratio is
`Q/P=q_j/q_i`: low gives edge `j->i`, high gives edge `i->j`.  Keeping
`(0,1)` fixed and using both halves repairs

```text
H2=(1,3,4,6,8,12)
```

at caps `7/3` and `5/2`.  All `43+16` and `87+27` unequal-channel heads are
positive, and equality uses the inherited same-level mechanism.

For

```text
H=(1,2,3,4,6,12),
```

all `30` ordered pairs and all low/high/full sides yield only

```text
cap 2,7/3: 0->1->2->3,          cap 5/2: 0->1->2.            (5)
```

The admissible assignment `(9,8,7,6,10,11)` misses every edge.  The three
complementary reverse-lane asymptotic limits are negative, so merely moving
the tail threshold cannot create the missing cycle.  This is a precise
stopping reason for the saturated single-pair route, not a physical survivor.

### 3. The planar-JC simple-zero wall is empty

In the normalized nonsplit exact-square-prefix Faber bank, suppose a finite
point were a common simple zero of `V` and `M`.  THM-3133 splits by the source
orders `a=ord(A_src)`, `b=ord(B_src)`:

- `a=b=0`: the top polar row is strictly more singular than every retained
  lower row;
- `a=0,b>=1`: the unique pure-`q` face cycles through `phi`, `psi`, and `K`,
  and each congruence class of `R mod 3` contradicts a flux order;
- `a>=1`: the Faber polynomial ring is regular while the divisor identity
  requires the opposite order for `K`.

Thus common simple zeros do not occur for `R>=7`, without setting
`d_F=s_F=0`.  In the balanced response normal form this forces

```text

```

Equality forces the two-pole passport `(D,D)`.  The first formal cell is
`R=8,D=11,N=22,(11,11)`.  The odd-bipole family with `T=x^2-1` and `E` the

### 4. Prime factorial resonances separate by slopes

For `q(t)=a+bt+ct^2`, THM-3124 gives

```text
M_(n+1)=a^n(a+(n+1)b)
       +2(n+1)(2n+1)c M_n
       +n(n+1)(b^2-4ac)M_(n-1).                             (7)
```

Three consecutive zero moments beginning at `r` force
`b/a=-1/(r+2)`.  Put `p=r+2`, `v=p(c/a)`, and

```text
A_n(v)=L((p-t+vt^2)^n).
```

When `p` is prime, THM-3131 proves the exact lower Newton slopes

```text
A_(p-2): {0,1/(p-3)},       A_(p-1): {1/(p-1)}              (8)
```

with the evident `p=3` boundary.  The slope sets are disjoint, so the two
polynomials have no common factor.  This closes infinitely many starts and
leaves only

```text
r>=201,        r+2 composite,        b/a=-1/(r+2).           (9)
```

At composite resonances slope overlap is an observer failure, not evidence
of a common root: `d=4,6` already have overlapping slopes but rational gcd
one.  The next proof must add residual polynomials, unit classes, or another
valuation place.

### 5. Tournament profiles are endpoint jets

Let `F_T(d)=d!pc_T(d)` count ordered spanning path covers, and let `b_T(pi)`
count backward consecutive adjacencies of a permutation.  THM-3134 defines

```text
E_T(u)=sum_pi (u-1/2)^b_T(pi)(u+1/2)^(n-1-b_T(pi))           (10)
```

and proves

```text
sum_d F_T(d)z^d=z E_T(z+1/2),
pc_T(d)=E_T^(d-1)(1/2)/(d!(d-1)!).                          (11)
```

Thus the full substitution sidecar is exactly an endpoint Taylor jet; the
Hamiltonian count `H=E_T(1/2)` is only its value.  Masks `40` and `76` both
have `H=15` but different first derivatives, producing `C3` counts `123` and
`135` after substitution with two singleton factors.

For `C3[S_1,S_2,S_3]`, the THM-3121 quotient walk series is

```text
W=(U+2xyz)/(1-xyz),       U=(1+x)(1+y)(1+z)-1.              (12)
```

Expanding `(12)` and applying finite differences gives the exact transform

```text
F_C3(d)=sum_(j<=min(d,q)) 2^j binom(d,j)
        sum_(t<=q-j) binom(d+t-1,t) D_(t+j)^(d-j),           (13)
```

where `D_s^m` is the `m`th forward difference at zero of the product of three
binomial profile transforms.  Equal `N`-vertex factors cost `O(N^3)`
arithmetic and `O(N^2)` memory.  Iteration gives exact Hamiltonian counts
through `3^6=729` vertices; the canonical cyclic-wreath subgroup divides
every ordered path-cover count.  The profile dimension still grows, so this
is an efficient exact transfer rather than a fixed-order scalar closed form.

### 6. Signed lift is not positive lift

THM-3132 proves for the projection of the `(r+1)`-forest boundary onto
rank-`r` component partitions that

```text
image over Q = all partition currents,          r even;
image over Q = augmentation-zero currents,      r odd.       (14)
```

An endpoint swap realizes every one-point transfer, and parity supplies the
last direction exactly when `r` is even.  This explains earlier rank-four
surjectivity without a large matrix computation.

But the lift is signed.  In the active product-Gamma pole-prefix probe,
plethystic pole stripping commutes exactly with labelled deletion, yet the
first active coarsest upset has mass `-2324160` at
`N=5,(a,b)=(1,3),I2,j=5`.  The whole deletion fibre cannot repair it because
an invariant upset remains negative.  Commutation, boundary lifting, and
linear surjectivity are therefore preservers; positivity still needs a
transverse selector.

## Bold reframes that survived hostile tests

### A. Acyclicity has opposite values for different consumers

A forest has maximal independent boundary information, so it is ideal for
reconstructing signed differences.  The same absence of cycles leaves a
global potential, so it is fatal for covering all weak-order assignments.
The transferable lesson is not “use forests” or “use cycles”; it is to compute
the circuit rank seen by the named operation.

### B. Choose a portfolio of local observers

The factorial and JC arguments both win by making one face uniquely visible
at a local place.  In the factorial problem, the place is a prime and the
observer is the lower Newton polygon.  In the JC problem, the place is a
response zero and the observer is a valuation fan.  Their shared workflow is
legitimate:

```text
choose place -> normalize -> list face slopes/orders -> retain units or
coefficients -> prove separation -> test the first overlap.                  (15)
```

The objects themselves are not identified.  When slopes overlap, change the
place or add residue data; do not infer that the target objects coincide.

### C. A closed form should be an operation conjugacy

The useful sequence formulas here are not fitted scalar recurrences.  The
quadratic factorial recurrence comes from integration by parts, the tournament
transform from a rational quotient-walk kernel conjugated by finite
differences, and the LRC totient formula from divisor inclusion--exclusion.
Each formula names the state it must carry and the operation under which it
closes.  “Closed form” is therefore best treated as a finite or controlled
state conjugacy, with the first discarded derivative/profile as the hostile.

### D. Death, transport, and refinement are different operations

Speed insertion deletes LRC divisor layers.  Pole stripping is virtual-letter
subtraction.  Forest boundary is a signed lift.  None is automatically a
positive mass transport.  Before invoking monotonicity, add a cemetery state,
an upset dual, or a transverse selector explicitly and test whether that
addition preserves the original target predicate.

## Procedural exploration loop

For every new object or analogy:

1. name the source, target, map, and exact predicate consumed downstream;
2. list what the map forgets and the smallest plausible restoring sidecar;
3. run the cheapest hostile at an equality, composite, DAG, same-scalar, or
   coarsest-upset boundary;
4. if it passes, extend until a mechanism appears, not merely more data;
5. if it fails, record the minimal witness, first failed implication,
   strongest survivor, and a new typed question;
6. compare the result with every object on the live concept board.

This loop is the common research method behind all six new theorems.  It is
also the main defense against false cross-frontier synthesis: a shared
formula is interesting only after the operation and lost coordinate are
named.
