## Closed continued-fraction returns and future state

Source: `01-canon/theorems/THM-853-closed-cf-return-semigroup.md`, especially
Sections 2 and 4. The old theorem has 144 present equality partitions but
20,419 classes after future-word refinement. It is explicitly a fixed-size
return monoid, not an increasing-size continued-fraction theorem.

Target and map: source words are split at J; retain their entire numerical
response together with J cost, and minimize cost only within identical
response fibres. The map takes a word to `(D,E,U,d,minimal J cost)`.
Preserved predicate: every future word's response and extra J cost.
Destroyed information: graph isomorphism type and the particular birth word.
Sidecar: one predecessor word restores a physical witness when needed.
Decisive test: raw zero-one relaxation and independent positive-coordinate
layered closure agree at cap50,001. No scalar-only quotient is used.

This transfers the future-congruence criterion, not the old 46,126-map
monoid or its finite-state conclusion. The source matrices here generate
an infinite numerical orbit.

## Binary source guards rather than a growth statistic

Source: `05-knowledge/results/collatz_parity_cf_boundary_20261014a.md`,
Sections 2-3. That note gives an exact binary-address encoding while proving
that its common irrationality exponent2 carries no termination information.
Its inverse branches explicitly retain the native integer source guard.

Target and map: a finite epsilon word becomes the actual O/Z block.
Preserved predicate: its exact four-coordinate transfer, formula(1) of the
new proof, with a literal source construction for every block.
Destroyed information if compressed to length and digit count: bit order,
first and last bit, the bit-reversed number R, and pair correction C.
Sidecar: A,R,C and both endpoint bits, or simply the complete binary word.
Decisive hostile: OZ and ZO have the same two coarse statistics and different
physical readouts19 and15.

There is no asserted identification of a Collatz integer orbit, a continued
fraction, or an irrationality exponent with a tournament source. The useful
transfer is a proof-design constraint: keep the exact finite arithmetic
compiler rather than infer reachability from a growth proxy.

## Dyadic fibre counts and affine position

Source: `01-canon/theorems/THM-3435-dyadic-fibre-grid-decomposition-for-literal-half-twists.md`
(the filename can be located by theorem ID if renamed), and the reflection
`07-reflections/lrc-dyadic-fibre-grid-ternary-carry-and-boolean-quartet-codex-20260815.md`.
The corrected theorem retains affine fibre starts in addition to fibre counts.
The depth-three binary tree has a mod-seven carry cycle, not a literal
ternary tree or a tournament.

Target and map: the physical block keeps both binary readings A and R, and
its ordered pair correction C. The cover response U alone sees B,R, while
the Hamilton response D also sees A,C and the first endpoint bit.
Preserved predicate: literal finite word action on every input response.
Destroyed information under cover-only compression: the future D readout.
Sidecar: the complete block transfer.
Decisive test: equality on the four formal coordinate basis vectors for every
binary word through length10, plus exact unit source inversion through20,000.

This is a common retained-coordinate mechanism, not a map of LRC masks into
tournaments and not an LRC coverage implication.
