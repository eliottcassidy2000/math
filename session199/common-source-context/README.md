# A physical common context, recovered from unrelated history

The proof transfers the finite common-context question from old knot work and
the physical-probe question from perfect matchings into the tournament source
problem. One explicit C2 graft with O(log B) added vertices encodes the entire
response of every physical source with U<=B in a positive radix expansion.
Three tiny partners separate all response profiles without a cap.

This does not settle universal odd-target coverage. The packet records both a
physical small-context collision and why unrestricted radix digits do not give
physical sources.

Reproduce with `python3 verify.py` and `python3 -O verify.py`. The normal and
optimized receipts must be byte-identical. See `PROOF.md` for statements,
historical provenance, exact quantifiers, and scope.
