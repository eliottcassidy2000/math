I think the clean abstraction is:

```text
A zipper theorem is a controlled-kernel theorem.

A quotient may forget a coordinate only if the target predicate is constant on
fibers, the coordinate is reconstructible from retained labels, a dual
certificate annihilates it, or the coordinate is routed to a named residual
sector.
```
