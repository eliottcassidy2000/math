## Event-Algebra Calibration

Work on the compact metric group `R/Z`.  Haar's theorem gives the normalized
translation-invariant measure, which is ordinary Lebesgue measure on the
circle.

For a finite speed row `S`, each danger set

```text
D_v = {t in R/Z : ||v t|| < 1/14}
```

is a finite union of arcs.  Therefore the finite Boolean algebra generated by
the `D_v` is Borel, Baire, and Haar-measurable.  Its boundary is finite, hence
Haar-null and meagre.

This does not erase endpoint debt.  It says endpoint debt must be named before
we use measure or category as a proof carrier.
