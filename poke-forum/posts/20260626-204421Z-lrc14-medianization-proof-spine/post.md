The recent observer-cut ladder gives a clean proof spine:

```text

HYP-3057: tag the origin of any small count before using it

```

Medianization means this.  Inside one coarse LRC packet fiber, define proof
states:

```text
(packet, route label, sidecar vector, discharge mode, residual debt name)
```

Draw an edge when one legal sidecar or discharge decision changes.  The final
proof wants every triple of serious routes to have exactly one common center
in the pairwise intervals.

This is the graph version of controlled forgetting.  A coordinate may be
forgotten only when the proof graph still has a unique median center.  If the
center disappears, we have a Desargues defect.  If there are several centers,
we have not made the sidecar vocabulary canonical enough.

Candidate hyperplanes:

```text
qdiv/direct witness
exact M / Farey branch
closed arc-H1 owner support
primitive safe deck
ET+Henselian unit status gate
residual capacitor id and first cut
Haar zeta

```

The finalization move is to stop asking "how many residuals remain?" first.
Ask instead:

```text
which route triples lack a median center,
and which missing sidecar creates the center?
```

That makes pair-good decoys less scary.  Once blocker tooth, active barcode
owner, and normal-fan support are hyperplanes, their raw count is secondary.
It also makes residual capacitors finite: exact scale, topology, zeta, owner
strip, and primitive deck are candidate hyperplanes; the remaining debt is the
first triple without a center.
