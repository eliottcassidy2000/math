# Feasibility: one clean formalized LRC proof for ALL n ≤ 14 (the uniform pipeline)

**mac-mini-2026-07-02-S3 (HYP-3860).** Owner question: can our techniques prove LRC(≤13)
too, and what tricks make it easier? **Verdict: HIGH feasibility — the machinery is already
n-generic, the certificates shrink as n drops, and the recursion becomes the induction.**

## 1. What is already n-parametric (no new mathematics)
- Sieve/covering saturation (`sieve_frac` — Lean, takes n as a parameter today).
- Unit-residue rigidity (`LRCUnitResidue` — Lean, parameter q). Collapse law THM-597 (any q).
- Pair law THM-594, dangerous characterization THM-601 (`DangerousPatterns` — Lean, r free):
  dangerous ⟺ P+Q ≤ ⌊1/(2r)⌋ = ⌊n/2⌋. Truncation engine (`BonferroniTruncation` — c, D free).
- THM-602 decomposition: height threshold ⌊n/2⌋, HNF machinery n-free.

## 2. The two tricks
**(T1) The uniform e⁻² floor.** B₅(n−1, 2/n) ∈ [0.104, 0.132] for every n = 4..14 (exact
rationals: 1/8, 13/125, 32/243, 2223/16807, 135/1024, 7721/59049, 404/3125, 20547/161051,
163/1296, 46013/371293, 2052/16807). ONE lemma — "the resolved free-phase floor is ≥ 1/10
for all 4 ≤ n ≤ 14" — replaces eleven case analyses. Dangerous-pattern censuses shrink:
9 (n=14) → 6 → 6 → 5 → 5 → 3 → 3 → 2 → 2 → 1 → 1 (n=4).
**(T2) Recursion = induction.** Our tower renormalizes bounded clusters to shifted
smaller-n problems ("depth-1 residual ≈ shifted LRC(8)"). In the all-n pipeline this stops
being an external citation and becomes the INDUCTION STEP: prove LRC(n) for n = 4..14 by
strong induction (base n ≤ 3 classical/trivial); the renormalized base cases at level n
are instances of levels < n. The owner's ≤13-settled policy remains the pragmatic default
for n=14-only work; the self-contained induction is the *formalization* architecture.

## 3. The pipeline (one proof, eleven certificate packs)
Master theorems (Lean once, n-parametric): sieve ▸ tight/unit-residue ▸ dangerous
patterns ▸ forced independence + truncation floor ▸ lattice renormalization ▸ induction
wrapper. Per-n packs (generated by the existing engines, `decide`-checked): dangerous
list, exact minima, simplex census, lattice census, nest/cap tables, residual census.
Pack sizes at n < 14 are fractions of the n=14 sizes (already 30 patterns / few hundred
lattices there). The ≤13 published proofs (prime-product sieves + big finite checks) get
replaced by structured small censuses — likely SMALLER total computation than Rosenfeld-
style checking, and uniform.

## 4. Honest gaps for the all-n ideal
(i) The n-parametric restatement of the census specs (mechanical). (ii) The induction
wrapper's interface: the renormalized instance must match the induction hypothesis's
exact form (THM-602(B)'s effective pattern ↦ a genuine smaller-n speed system — one
bridging lemma to write). (iii) Small-n covering cases are tiny but must be generated
(n ≤ 7: covering families nearly forced by saturation pigeonhole). (iv) Everything in
the n=14 ledger's critical path, inherited.

## 5. Recommendation
Adopt the uniform pipeline as the formalization target: it is not harder than n=14 alone —
it is the same master theorems with smaller tables plus one induction wrapper, and it
upgrades the prize from "first open case" to "the complete theorem for n ≤ 14, one clean
formal proof." Sequencing: finish the n=14 critical path first (its packs are the largest);
the ≤13 packs then generate by rerunning the same engines with n as input.
