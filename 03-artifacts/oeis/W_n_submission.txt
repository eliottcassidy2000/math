%N A?????? Sum over permutations of {0,...,n-1} with no unit descents of 2^(number of unit ascents).
%C A?????? A unit ascent at position j means sigma(j+1) = sigma(j) + 1. A unit descent means sigma(j+1) = sigma(j) - 1. The sum is over permutations with no unit descents (NUD), weighted by 2^(number of unit ascents).
%C A?????? a(n)/n! = 1 + CV^2(H) where H is the number of Hamiltonian paths in a uniformly random tournament on n vertices and CV^2 = Var(H)/E[H]^2.
%C A?????? a(n)/n! = sum_{k=0}^{floor((n-1)/2)} [x^k] ((1+x)/(1-x))^{n-2k} / (n)_{2k} where (n)_{2k} is the falling factorial.
%C A?????? The generating function involves the x-tribonacci recurrence F(N+3,x) = F(N+2,x) + x*F(N+1,x) + x*F(N,x) with bivariate GF (1 + 2xy + xy^2)/(1 - y - xy^2 - xy^3).
%F A?????? a(n) = Sum_{sigma in S_n, no unit descent} 2^(adj1(sigma)) where adj1 counts unit ascents.
%F A?????? a(n)/n! = 1 + Sum_{k=1}^{floor((n-1)/2)} 2*g_k(n-2k)/(n)_{2k} where g_k(m) = Sum_{j=1}^{min(k,m)} C(k-1,j-1)*C(m,j)*2^(j-1) and (n)_{2k} = n!/(n-2k)!.
%F A?????? The Cayley transform identity: ((1+x)/(1-x))^m = 1 + 2*Sum_{k>=1} g_k(m)*x^k.
