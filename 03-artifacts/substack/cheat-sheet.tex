\textbf{Setup.} $n$ items compared pairwise. $H(T)$ = number of consistent total rankings.

\medskip
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Key Formulas}

Expected value (random):
\[ \mathbb{E}[H] = \frac{n!}{2^{n-1}} \]

Coefficient of variation:
\[ \mathrm{CV} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}} \]

Standard deviation:
\[ \sigma = \mathbb{E}[H] \cdot \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}} \]

Z-score of observed $H$:
\[ Z = \frac{H - \mathbb{E}[H]}{\sigma} \]
\end{minipage}
\hfill

\textbf{The Math.} \ $Q(x) = (1{+}x)/(1{-}x) = \exp(2\,\mathrm{arctanh}\,x)$. \ $Q^m = 1 + 2\sum g_k(m)\,x^k$ where $g_k$ are Delannoy weights. \ $\mathrm{CV}^2 = \sum 2g_k(n{-}2k)/(n)_{2k} = 2/n + O(1/n^3)$.

\medskip
