4. Remarks of the finisher (derived from formulas of PROOFS.txt)
---------------------------------------------------------------
R1  Sinks at intermediate bases give no new tower: by the level map of Lemma G, the sink rule
    (r, M, h, E) -> (r, 2M, h, 0) followed by the level (g, i, m) is the level (g, i + 1, m) (the sink is one more
    source of the tail), and a source of the base does not change the state.  So the G-towers of Theorem 4 are closed
    under the two universal base operations, and Theorem 4 bounds everything that the known unit-preserving
    operations generate from the transitive bases.
R2  The anti-seed analogue of Theorem 5 (d) is NOT available by the same argument: for r = -1 the set O can meet two
    strong components (transitive pairs), so THM-5130 (F2 (c)) does not reduce anti-seed slopes to strong
    tournaments.  The list of anti-seed slopes below 450 in b_small_slopes.out is a list for pairs of order <= 10.
R3  Not done by the lane: rules for the two-apex bases (only the exhaustive census to spine length 10); a lower bound
    for the number of distinct tower slopes; the unit neutral gadgets of order 10; the order-11 out-of-sample test.
