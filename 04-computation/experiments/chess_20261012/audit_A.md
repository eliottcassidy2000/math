Witness: `8/8/8/8/8/5K2/P7/6Bk b - - 0 1` (only move `Kxg1`, after which `8/8/8/8/8/5K2/P7/6k1 w` is a KPvK win; the session's own KPvK table says so). `kbpvk_sub.pkl` labels it 0. The full (T)+(B) re-check of the pickle with my move generator finds exactly these 37 Bellman violations and no other; the exact re-solve below gives the corrected counts (predecessors can inherit the error silently, so the Bellman check alone does not bound the damage). Repair: in `solve`, after the terminal loops add `for i in np.nonzero((term == 0) & (np.diff(indptr) == 0) & (bnd_best == -1))[0]: val[i] = -1; dtm[i] = 1; resolved[i] = True; frontier.append(i)`; re-run `traps.py` section 2 and replace the numbers in 5.2 and THM-4613 statement 5 by the corrected ones from my exact re-solve (`audit_A_kbpvk_resolve.py`, own enumeration/graph/solver, same flagged assumption): not-lost **357,171** instead of 357,209 (still 30.21%); wins 418,867 / draws 357,171 / losses 406,402 instead of 418,866 / 357,209 / 406,365; 38 labels change: 37 Black-to-move draws become losses (36 with the Black king on h1, one on c8) and one White-to-move draw becomes a win (the error propagated once through a predecessor: `2kB4/8/P1K5/8/8/8/8/8 w`, where a7! leaves Black only Kxd8 and a8=Q follows); in the by-square list only c8 changes, 16,193 -> 16,191.
