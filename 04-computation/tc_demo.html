How It Works

PNG's approach

PNG applies one of 5 prediction filters per row (None, Sub, Up, Average, Paeth), then compresses with zlib (deflate). Good for photos, but limited to one backend and one filter strategy.

TC's approach

TC applies the same 5 filters plus delta, XOR, Gray code, and stride reordering — then tries three backends (zlib, bz2, lzma). The optimal combination is selected per image. One byte overhead.

The monoid of transforms:

compress(img) = argmin |backend(transform(pixels))| over all (transform, backend) pairs

Transforms: {id, Δ, ⊕, Δ², γΔ, Sub, Up, Avg, Paeth, S₂Δ, S₃Δ, S₄Δ, S₈Δ, row-adaptive}

Backends: {zlib-9, zlib-filtered, zlib-RLE, bz2-9, lzma}
