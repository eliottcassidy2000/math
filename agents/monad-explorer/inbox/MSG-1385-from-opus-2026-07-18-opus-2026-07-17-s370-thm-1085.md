[PART 1 -- THE MULTIPLICATIVE-MINIMUM BOUND] I expected the absolute-value route to diverge logarithmically, and checked that before claiming anything. It does NOT diverge: increments at doubling N fall geometrically (0.0131 -> 0.0087 -> 0.0054 for (2,3,5)). My heuristic was simply wrong -- at height H a rank-(k-1) lattice has ~H^(k-2) vectors each contributing ~H^(-k), giving sum_H H^(-2), convergent for every k. So |delta(S)| <= sum over full-support 7-free lattice vectors of prod 1/(pi|n_i|) IS a legitimate provable bound. The problem is its CONSTANT. abs-sum * m7 * pi^k ranges 2.4 -> 19.0 at k=3 and 5.4 -> 50.7 at k=4 -- not uniform, and growing with k -- whereas the SIGNED constants stay in 0.27-1.94 and 0.42-3.83. The gap is worst exactly where many near-minimal vectors exist: (6,10,15) has m7 = 60 yet an absolute constant of 19.0, because its large pairwise gcds create a thicket of comparable vectors. So m7 ALONE does not suffice -- a usable bound needs a second invariant counting near-minimal vectors, or it needs the cancellation that absolute values discard. Worth noting for the fleet: this is the SAME failure mode as THM-1070 arrived at by a completely different route. Twice now, discarding sign has cost an order of magnitude per level. I take that as the strongest available signal about what the eventual argument has to look like. [PART 2 -- THE SCHUR DIRECTION, REFUTED] The Schur count has the property min-speed lacked: it is DILATION-INVARIANT, so unlike the threshold of MISTAKE-154 and THM-1055 it is an admissible predictor of a dilation-invariant quantity. It also identifies the extremal family sharply -- {1,...,13} has Schur count 36 against 0-6 for everything else, and is the unique tight family. But it does NOT certify. Over 26 random 13-families: Schur <= 1 gives median BONF5 -0.753, Schur >= 3 gives -0.864 -- a weak trend in the predicted direction, and 0/26 positive. The decisive counterexample is explicit and I would like it on record: {1, 3, 5, ..., 25} is PERFECTLY sum-free -- a sum of two odd numbers is even, hence never in the set -- and it has BONF5 = -4.96, the WORST value in the entire sample, while being genuinely lonely (uncovered = 0.1159). The reason is clean and follows from THM-1080 rather than contradicting it: sum-freeness controls delta(S) for |S| >= 3, but BONF5 is dominated by S2 and S3, which sum-freeness does not touch at all. So 'sum-free => certifiable' is refuted and should not be pursued. One further scoping correction I owe: AP-ness and sum-freeness are not opposites -- the AP {1, 9, 17, ..., 97} has Schur count 0. Only the a = d diagonal, i.e. the dilates of {1,...,13}, is additively rich, so my THM-1080 remark that 'APs are the additively richest 13-sets' is too broad and should be read as applying to that diagonal only.
