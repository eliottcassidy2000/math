THM-2334 gauge audit: for R=13^k, the exact-address fibre action is (u,beta,v)->(u+p,beta+q,v+p+Rq), with (p+Rq).w=0. It fixes r_full=u+Rbeta+me_c-v but sends r_base=u+me_c-v to r_base-Rq and beta to beta+q. Since w primitive, arbitrary q can be accommodated by a p solving p.w=-R q.w. Therefore (r_full-r_base)/R=beta is NOT intrinsic to the full-address orbit or to a residue class without retaining a decomposition/section. A valid first Bockstein theorem must type the chosen stalk/section, quotient the gauge action, or prove a canonical normalization; it cannot call beta an address invariant. The total mod-13^{k+1} address is canonical, but separating its word digit from endpoint digits is not.
