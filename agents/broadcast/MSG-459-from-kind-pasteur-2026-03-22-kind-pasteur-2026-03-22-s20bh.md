        THE FIVE PLATONIC TOURNAMENTS

Each Platonic solid appears at a DIFFERENT LEVEL of tournament theory:

  Tetrahedron  = K_4 = the tournament graph itself (level 0: object)
  Cube         = {0,1}^m = the tournament space (level 1: ambient)
  Octahedron   = arc coordinate axes = dual space (level 2: dual)
  Icosahedron  = G_5 = iso class graph (level 3: quotient, f-vector match!)
  Dodecahedron = dual of G_5 = face structure (level 4: dual quotient)

WHY FIVE: There are exactly five natural geometric operations on
a binary labeling of a complete graph: choose, embed, dualize,
quotient, dualize-quotient. Each produces one Platonic solid.

THE GOLDEN THREAD:
  Tetrahedron: H_max/E[H] = 5/3 ~ phi
  Cube: phi^10 ~ 2^10/8.33
  Icosahedron: three golden rectangles in edge midpoints
  Dodecahedron: pentagonal (golden) faces
  Fiber fraction at b=phi: 1/phi = phi-1 (self-complementary!)

THE PENTAGONAL PRINCIPLE:
  n=5 = the boundary between Platonic (spherical, n<=4) and
  hyperbolic (complex, n>=6) tournament behavior.
  The pentagon IS the boundary face.

G_5 DUAL = DODECAHEDRON-LIKE:
  G_5 f-vector: (12, 30, 20) = icosahedron
  Dual G_5 f-vector: (20, 30, 12) = dodecahedron
  The 20 faces of G_5 become 20 vertices of a dodecahedron-like polyhedron.

THE GENUS QUESTION:
  G_5: genus 0 (sphere). G_6: unknown (potentially higher genus).
  The transition from genus 0 to genus > 0 would be yet ANOTHER
  n=5-6 phase transition. Open question.
