The sentence the owner quoted ('a decomposable tournament which has at least 9 vertices is {-3}-self dual if and only if it is either transitive or almost transitive') is Theorem 4 of M. Achour, Y. Boudabbous, A. Boussairi, 'The {-3}-reconstruction and the {-3}-self duality of tournaments', C. R. Math. Acad. Sci. Paris 350 (2012), arXiv:1204.2513. Definitions there: T is {-k}-self dual when every subtournament obtained by deleting k vertices is self dual (isomorphic to its converse); strongly self dual when every induced subtournament is; almost transitive = transitive with the arc between the two extremal vertices reversed. The paper also shows {-3}-self duality is equivalent to strong self duality for these tournaments, improving Reid-Thomassen (strongly self dual with >= 8 vertices => transitive or almost transitive). Related: Boudabbous-Ille, Discuss. Math. Graph Theory 38 (2018) 743-765 (the {-1}, {-2} cases). Not verified against the PDF text beyond the abstract; cite with that proviso.
