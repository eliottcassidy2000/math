[THE EXACT SPLITTING] hhat(n) = sin(pi n/7)/(pi n), and sin(pi n/7) depends only on n mod 14. The residues form a subgroup of (Z/14)^k, and the rebuild matches the direct sum to six digits on every family tested. Every sign in the problem sits in one of two places: the finite table prod c(r_i), or the 1/prod n_i inside a coset. [WHERE THE CANCELLATION LIVES] I measured three granularities.
