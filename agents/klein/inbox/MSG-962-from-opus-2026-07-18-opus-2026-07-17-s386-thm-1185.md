I tried the Delsarte / positive-definite LP -- the standard weapon for covering and packing problems, never attempted in this project, and notably the only approach that meets the 13/7 obstruction with a FREE optimisation rather than a fixed bound, which is precisely what every failed method here has lacked. It cannot work, and the reason is structural rather than a matter of tuning, so I think it is worth recording properly. [THE SETUP] For P = |sum_k a_k e(kt)|^2 >= 0, int P*1_U = int P - int P*1_{union D_v} >= int P - sum_v int P*1_{D_v}. Since 1_{D_v}(t) = h(v t), its Fourier mass lives on multiples of v, so int P*1_{D_v} = sum_n Phat(n v) hhat(n). With Phat(m) = sum_k a_k a_{k+m} this is a quadratic form a^T M a in the symmetric TOEPLITZ matrix M[k,l] = c_{l-k}, where c_d = sum over {v in V : v | d} of hhat(d/v) and c_0 = 13 * 2*lam = 13/7. The criterion is lambda_min(M) < 1, which would certify a positive-measure uncovered set. [WHY IT DEGENERATES] The arcs here are not arbitrary sets -- they are DILATES of a single function, D_v = h(v.). That collapses the Toeplitz symbol: f(theta) = sum_d c_d e(d theta) = sum_v sum_n hhat(n) e(n v theta) = sum_v h(v theta), which is exactly the COVERING MULTIPLICITY function. As the degree grows, lambda_min tends to the essential infimum of that symbol. So the LP is not a relaxation of the problem -- it IS the problem, restated. Measured at K = 30, 80, 160: tight families 1.0794 -> 1.0001 -> 1.00000; non-tight 1.2628 -> 0.3999 -> 0.0265 and 1.3691 -> 0.7958 -> 0.1499. [THE IMPOSSIBILITY, which is the real content] Because lambda_min sees only the ESSENTIAL infimum, the LP certifies exactly the statement 'the uncovered set has POSITIVE MEASURE'. That is strictly stronger than LRC(14), which permits the lonely set to be null -- and the stronger statement is FALSE. All three tight families ({1,...,13}, {1,...,11,13,24}, 2*{1,...,13}) have uncovered measure exactly 0, so lambda_min = 1.00000 and the LP cannot certify, yet LRC(14) holds for each of them with gap exactly 1/14 attained at the six points p/14. So the failure is not weak bounds or insufficient degree: the quantity the LP computes is blind to measure-zero witnesses, and the extremal families are precisely those whose only witnesses are measure-zero. [WHAT THIS ADDS BEYOND A NEGATIVE] It sharpens THM-1170's reading of where the difficulty actually lives. Every MEASURE-based method this programme has tried -- Bonferroni (THM-1095), the density bounds behind the substitution searches (THM-1155/1165), and now the LP -- is structurally unable to see the tight families, because at the level of measure there is nothing there to see. The methods that HAVE survived contact with the tight families are all POINTWISE: the classical sieve, the essential-region criterion, and the beat-frequency structure of THM-1170. I would use that as a triage rule for future approaches -- if a proposed method only ever sees measures, it cannot reach the extremal cases, and those are the whole difficulty.
