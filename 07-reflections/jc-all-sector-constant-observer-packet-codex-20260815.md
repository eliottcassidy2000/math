## 1. What changed after the unit theorem

THM-3424 locates `[1]` inside the first generic character: it disappears only
at a resonant one-root divisor.  Applying the same question to every constant
monomial

```text
theta_sigma=[z^(sigma-1)],                 1<=sigma<=d,
```

reveals a more structural object.  Every sector is a polynomial differential
quotient

```text
F[x]/L_sigma(rad(g)F[x]),
L_sigma(q)=sigma q+(t-x)(sigma(g'/g)q-dq').
```

The regular Kummer packet of THM-3419 is therefore not only an abstract rank
statement.  It has a degree filtration with at most one missing diagonal.

## 3. Why the wrap repair matters

For `sigma<d`, an input of weight `sigma` is literally `qz^sigma`.  At
`sigma=d`, the input weight is zero and is an arbitrary `p in B`; treating it
as an arbitrary `qz^d` is not type-correct.  The repair is small but
load-bearing:

```text
p=p(t)+qz^d,
q=-g[p-p(t)]/(x-t).
```

The first term is in the Hamiltonian kernel, so the images agree.  This both
justifies the common `L_d` presentation and recovers the independent wrap
model

```text
B/(g partial_x B)=H^1_dR(A1 minus roots(g)).
```

The constant observer there is `dx/g`, whose residue rigidity is THM-3348.
The nonwrap infinity proof cannot simply be pasted into this sector because
its leading operator acquires the kernel generated by `g`.

## 4. The basis-swap interpretation

For a polynomial input of degree `m`, the leading diagonal of `L_sigma` is

```text
a_sigma(m)=dm+sigma(1-deg(g)).
```

It vanishes at most once.  If the zero is not an accessible input degree, the
usual low monomials `1,x,...,x^(N-1)` are a basis.  If it is accessible, one
high monomial at degree

```text
m_sigma=sigma(deg(g)-1)/d
```

survives, while the resonant column creates one relation among the `N` low
monomials.  Thus one high vector replaces one low vector without changing
THM-3419's rank.

For a one-root divisor, that low vector is exactly the constant.  The
replacement records the Prüfer endpoint, and the constant observer has exact
annihilator exponent

```text
q_sigma=sigma(e-1)/d.
```

For a multiroot divisor, an accessible degree hole may still exist, but the
relation removes another low combination.  The constant survives.  This is
the abstract regular-packet theme made concrete: equal sector ranks coexist
with character-dependent integral position.

## 5. What the theorem does and does not unlock

The positive result is global across all roots:

```text
theta_sigma is generically zero
iff g has one repeated root and d divides sigma(e-1).
```

Together with THM-3422, this gives its exact integral annihilator.  Therefore
all `d` constant-character observers are nontorsion for every multiroot `g`,
even though THM-3419 gives `N>1` response channels in each sector.

The next underexplored object is the reduced resonant defect polynomial
`R_sigma` for multiroot `g`.  It is a low-degree period coordinate that says
which low vector, rather than the constant, is traded for the high resonant
monomial.  Computing its root-interpolation or determinant formula may be the
right bridge toward local primary structure.  Nothing here yet proves that a
multiroot sector is torsion-free or decomposes into independent root arms.
