# Irrational and Transcendental Approximation Sidecars for LRC14

This note is a follow-up to HYP-3114/T1190/LTI-251/LTT-149.  It adds the
root-angle, Bravais-phase, and Baker/log-gap fields that became visible after
rebasing over the HYP-3112 ear-payload and HYP-3113 two-map root-lattice-ear
frontier, then folds in HYP-3110's De Moivre/Jacobi/theta sidecar as the finite
algebraic branch and theta-tail case.

The useful merge is not "try irrational times."  The LRC witness problem is
already controlled by finite endpoint partitions.  Irrational and
transcendental approximation become useful when they explain why a finite
endpoint, root, lattice, or resonance ledger is complete.

## Two Maps

Map 1 is finite and proof-facing:

```text
packet
  -> endpoint partition / exact M / Farey address
  -> finite-address branch or observer-gluing certificate
  -> legal LRC14 handoff
```

Map 2 is analytic and explanatory:

```text
packet
  -> PGF root angles / Bravais phases / q-lattice body
  -> continued-fraction, height, separation, or log-gap sidecar
  -> finite exception list or named residual debt
```

The second map is allowed to inspire the first, but it cannot replace it.

## What Changes

For Lee-Yang roots, the angle data are algebraic after the packet is fixed,
because `G_E(z)` has rational coefficients.  A root that appears close to a
`7`th-root direction needs coefficient height, isolating disks, and a separation
certificate before it can be used as proof data.

For Bravais or Minkowski lanes, a large reciprocal peak should be treated as a
low-denominator resonance wall.  The current bounded-bank evidence points the
other way for extremizers: high `p0` is reciprocal-flat and entropy-rich.  That
suggests adding a badly-approximable phase score and an exceptional peak list,
not replacing the proof with a crystallinity scalar.

For ear payloads, continued fractions and Ostrowski words can schedule the
finite localization of the next cut.  The exact sidecar remains `A_t(E,a)`.
Far ears such as `+21` should be audited as low-denominator resonance events:
they may keep roots complex while moving the root cloud toward the danger
interval.

For power and two-block resonance lanes, Baker-style linear forms in logs are
only relevant after a multiplicative relation has actually been extracted.
Then the certificate is a gap bound plus a low-height resonance list, not a
generic transcendence claim.

## Proof Frontier Use

The best next two tasks are paired.

First, extend the Lee-Yang ear-payload ledger so every root-motion row reports
its Farey parent interval, root isolation/separation certificate, and
low-denominator resonance status.  This tests whether danger-interval approach
is always paid for by high-mean payload, nonnested ear debt, component debt,
first-obstruction debt, K33/THM-572 debt, or AP/GW boundary status.

Second, extend the finite-address branch-closure ledger with the same
approximation fields.  This tests whether the approximation sidecar actually
helps route HYP-2963 packets: direct q-witness, level-7 lift, observer gluing,
root/ear exit, algebraic fold exit, Baker/log-gap exit, or named residual debt.

The conceptual warning is the same one learned from the pi/e rationality
threads: rationality, irrationality, algebraicity, and transcendence are not
enough.  The load-bearing datum is the field, height, dependence, exception,
and finite packet coordinate that survives the quotient.
