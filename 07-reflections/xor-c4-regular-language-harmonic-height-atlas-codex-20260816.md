## 2. The exact size-four object behind XOR

For subsets `A,B` of the natural numbers, each integer has one of four
membership states

```text
00, 10, 01, 11 = (not A or B), (A only), (B only), (A and B).       (1)
```

They form the group

```text
V4=(Z/2)^2.                                                        (2)
```

XOR selects the two off-diagonal cells `10` and `01`.  If the two standard
basis vectors are used as Cayley generators, the graph of `(2)` is a square:

```text
4 unordered generator pairs, all 4 bidirected, 2 diagonal pairs missing. (3)
```

Adding the third nonzero generator fills the diagonals, but every one of the
six pairs is then bidirected.  Neither relation is a tournament.  This is the
literal place where “missing edges and/or both-way edges” belongs.

There cannot be a translation-invariant tournament on `V4`.  In a Cayley
tournament one must choose exactly one of `g` and `-g` for every nonzero
difference, but every nonzero element of `V4` satisfies `g=-g`.  The same
half-turn obstruction kills rotation-invariant tournaments on `C4` and
`C6`.  Exact enumeration gives the hostile/control row

```text
translation-invariant tournaments:
V4: 0,     C3: 2,     C4: 0,     C5: 4,     C6: 0.                 (4)
```

Thus the user's size-four intuition has a precise survivor: XOR really does
have a canonical four-state carrier.  What fails is the word “tournament.”
The faithful object is a Boolean partition or a bidirected Cayley square.

## 3. Harmonic mass exposes why all four cells are needed

For a finite prefix define

```text
H_N(A)=sum_(n<=N, n in A) 1/n.                                     (5)
```

The pointwise Boolean identity gives

```text
H_N(A xor B)
 = H_N(A)+H_N(B)-2 H_N(A intersect B)
 = H_N(10)+H_N(01).                                                 (6)
```

This is exact for every `A,B,N`.  For infinite masses, the four-cell form on
the right is the safe statement; the first form may contain the meaningless
expression `infinity-infinity`.

Equation `(6)` says that harmonic mass is not additive under XOR unless the
intersection cell is retained.  The four-state Boolean carrier is therefore
not cosmetic: it is the smallest sidecar that makes the real-valued
pushforward exact.  Passing directly from supports to one harmonic scalar
destroys the overlap needed to reverse the operation.

## 4. A shell theorem for every subset generated by words

Let `L_d` be a set of words of length `d` over a `b`-letter alphabet and put
`a_d=|L_d|`.  Inject words into the natural numbers by the leading-marker
code

```text
nu_b(w)=b^d+value_b(w),        b^d <= nu_b(w) < 2b^d.                (7)
```

The image shells are disjoint.  Their reciprocal masses satisfy

```text
a_d/(2b^d) <= sum_(w in L_d) 1/nu_b(w) <= a_d/b^d.                  (8)
```

Consequently

```text
sum_(w in L) 1/nu_b(w) converges
iff sum_(d>=0) a_d/b^d converges.                                  (9)
```

This is an exact density-by-shell criterion.  It makes the phrase “every
subset of the natural numbers is a subset of the harmonic series” useful:
the Boolean support is primary, and the reciprocal series measures its
occupancy relative to exponentially growing radix shells.

For a regular language recognized by a deterministic finite automaton, let
`M` be the transition-count matrix on the accessible, coaccessible states
and let `rho` be its effective Perron radius.  Standard finite-matrix growth
together with `(9)` gives

```text
rho < b  => harmonic convergence,
rho = b  => harmonic divergence.                                  (10)
```

For the second line, an effective Perron component supplies a positive
multiple of `b^d` accepted words along one residue class of lengths.  For the
first, `a_d=O(d^k rho_1^d)` for some `rho<rho_1<b`.

The invariant is therefore entropy relative to the chosen radix, not the
mere existence of a recurrence.

## 5. Fibonacci and XOR are opposite sides of the threshold

Binary strings with no adjacent ones have counts

```text
a_d=F_(d+2),       sum_(d>=0) F_(d+2)/2^d=6.                         (11)
```

Their transition matrix has Perron root `phi<2`, so their marker-coded
binary image has a convergent harmonic subseries.

Even-parity binary strings are the XOR language.  For every `d>=1`,

```text
a_d=2^(d-1),       a_d/2^d=1/2.                                    (12)
```

They retain full binary entropy and their harmonic subseries diverges.  XOR
is a finite-state rule, but finite-state does not mean harmonic-thin.

Now change only the height.  Give the same no-adjacent-one words Fibonacci
place values `F_2,F_3,...`.  Induction on the last digit proves that the
length-`d` legal words fill exactly

```text
{0,1,...,F_(d+2)-1}.                                                (13)
```

The union is all of `N`, i.e. the elementary Zeckendorf coding, and its
harmonic series diverges.  The same abstract language is thin under binary
radix height and exhaustive under Fibonacci height.  Convergence is thus a
property of `(language,height)`, not of the language alone.

## 6. One Berggren branch has three harmonic answers

On THM-3334's `U`-spine at depth `n`, the exact heights are

```text
Farey denominator:   n+2,
hypotenuse:           c_n=2n^2+6n+5,
quadratic Q-height:   Q_n=(2n+3)^2+2.                                (14)
```

Therefore

```text
sum 1/(n+2) diverges,
sum 1/c_n converges,
sum 1/Q_n converges.                                               (15)
```

If the same branch is encoded by its ternary word in the full Berggren tree,
its marker height is at least `3^n`, so that reciprocal sum also converges.
The Fibonacci Farey ray has denominators `F_(2n+1)` and converges because
successive displayed denominators grow by at least a factor two.

Thus “the Berggren branch is divergent” is neither true nor false until its
height is named.  Its Farey-denominator shadow is divergent; its geometric
triple and ancestry-code shadows are convergent.

## 7. Tournaments, missing edges, and both-way edges as languages

Treat a loopless directed graph on `m` vertices as a language of vertex
walks, using the next vertex as an `m`-ary symbol.  A length-`d` walk has at
most

```text
m(m-1)^d                                                       (16)
```

choices.  Equations `(9)` and `(16)` show that every such walk language is
harmonic-thin under its own `m`-radix marker, whether a pair has one arc, no
arc, or both arcs.  The universal normalized upper sum is `m`.

For the successor cycles themselves the sharper sums are

```text
C4: sum_(ell>=1) 4/4^ell = 4/3,
C6: sum_(ell>=1) 6/6^ell = 6/5.                                  (17)
```

Loops are the boundary that can restore full radix entropy.  This gives
missing/bidirected edges a quantitative role without forcing them into a
tournament completion.
