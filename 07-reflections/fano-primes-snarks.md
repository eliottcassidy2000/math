# Fano Locus, Geometric Primes, Symbolic Snarks

## The Fano locus codimension = 5 (pentagon)

Lines on a cubic hypersurface in P^{n+1}:
Fano variety has codimension 5 in the Grassmannian (for n >= 3).
5 = the pentagon = the independent prime = the golden ratio's home.

The 27 lines on a cubic surface "shouldn't exist" by dimension counting
(expected dim = -1). They exist because of SPECIAL STRUCTURE.
27 = 3^3 = curvature cubed. The excess = ±1 = the Cassini atom.

## Primes as the branch locus of Z[omega] -> Z

The covering Z[omega] -> Z has three fiber types:
- INERT: one point (smooth, 1-to-1). Primes that pass through.
- SPLIT: two conjugate points (fold, 2-to-1). Primes that decompose with twist.
- RAMIFIED: double point (cusp, singular). The unique prime p=3.

Primes are the SPECIAL FIBER of the arithmetic covering.
Their type is determined LOCALLY: p mod 3. O(1) to check. Lossless.

## Snarks as the global topological obstruction

A 3-edge-coloring = a nowhere-zero Z/3Z-flow.
Snarks: bridgeless cubic graphs where the Z/3Z-flow is obstructed.
The obstruction is GLOBAL (topological), not local.
Checking requires examining the whole graph. Crystallization, not lossless.

## The three obstruction loci

| Locus | Ambient space | Obstruction | Codimension | Detection |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Lines on cubic | Grassmannian | degree-3 constraint | 5 (pentagon) | Algebraic |
| Primes in Z | Natural numbers | irreducibility | ~ln(n) (growing) | Local (mod p) |
| Snarks in cubic graphs | All cubic graphs | non-3-colorability | Growing fast | Global (topology) |

All three: special loci where a natural map FAILS.
Lines: the inclusion map into the cubic fails generically.
Primes: the factorization map fails (no proper factors).
Snarks: the coloring map fails (no proper edge coloring).

## The local-global distinction

Primes: LOCAL obstruction. p mod 3 determines type. Instant.
Snarks: GLOBAL obstruction. Must examine entire structure. Slow.
Lines on cubics: ALGEBRAIC obstruction. Degree conditions. Intermediate.

This IS the hierarchy: lossless (local) -> lossy (algebraic) -> crystallization (global).
The same three types, applied to obstruction detection.
