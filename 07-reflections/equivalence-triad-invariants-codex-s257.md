# Equivalence Triad Invariants, S257

The user asked for equidecomposability and equinumerosity to sit beside
equidistribution, and for hidden perspectives from old work.  The old notes
make a consistent warning:

```text
same count is not same structure;
same volume is not same scissors class;
same limiting distribution is not legal forgetting until resonances are named.
```

The synthesis is HYP-3091.  Its object is the forgetting-cost tuple:

```text
F_q(x; P) =
  (cardinal_shadow,
   scissors_fiber,
   observer_cut_orbit,
   distribution_law,
   interaction_order_defect,
   named_residual_debt).
```

This is a way to ask what an invariant must retain in order to capture the
fundamental nature of a mathematical situation.  Equinumerosity contributes the
count shadow.  Equidecomposability contributes the piece/fiber side channel.
Equidistribution contributes the limiting law that can make some side-channel
forgetting harmless after resonance debt is removed.

The past-work anchors line up unusually well:

- HYP-2187: Royle/even-graph equinumerosity is only a cardinal shadow until
  `H`, `beta1`, and packet data are preserved.
- HYP-2186: `H` is volume; strong-component multisets and `beta1`/packet
  refinements are Dehn-style scissors data.
- HYP-2232/HYP-2244: CH, dimension, and outer-extension examples say the
  model, embedding, deletion, or fiber profile is part of the object.
- HYP-2872/HYP-2883: even graphs are parity-dual cycle-space addresses, not
  `H`-complete obstruction closures.
- HYP-3053/HYP-3056: fixed-path presentations, rectangle/hourglass defects,
  and observer-cut orbits tell exactly what a quotient has forgotten.
- HYP-2949/HYP-2895/HYP-2899: Haar/Baire regular-open mass and Weyl tails are
  distribution laws, but endpoint and resonance debt remain separate.
- HYP-3090/THM-576: pairwise avoidance explains the cap side until explicit
  higher-order deviations appear at `k=8,9`.

The hidden perspective is that the first failure order is often the real
invariant.  A scalar may be wrong but close; a count may be right but useless;
a pairwise law may be exact until an order-3 correction.  The mathematical
object is the ladder of legal forgetting:

```text
count shadow
-> retained scissors fiber
-> observer-cut orbit under visible automorphisms
-> distribution law after resonance deletion
-> first interaction order where the shadow fails
-> named residual debt.
```

For LRC14 this reframes the current mainline.  The proof is not looking for
one final scalar or one universal tournament.  It is looking for a packet
invariant that says when a row is AP/GW boundary, when it has positive
regular-open mass, when pairwise co-emptiness suffices, when S3/S4 deviations
matter, and when remaining structure must be handed to Node-3, K33/THM-572,
or a finite-address branch packet.

First practical test: build a tiny ledger for three known collisions, not a
new broad search.

```text
1. Royle/even graph count vs (H,beta1,packet) fibers.
2. AP/GW endpoint-only boundary vs positive regular-open rows.
3. HYP-3090/THM-576 pairwise cap rows vs k=8,9 deviation constants.
```

If those three can be represented in one schema, the schema is probably the
right abstraction for future invariant probes.
