# LRC14 Packet Tournament Atlas

The useful outcome is not that "the six signs are a tournament proof."  The
useful outcome is a separation of three layers:

```text
sign chamber       = K4 edge orientation
mass address       = exact packet and opposite-pair sums
sector topology    = mod-7 cyclic pair-pressure tournament
```

The B14 near-speed leaders are the warning.  All top twelve are `++++++`, hence
all become the same transitive K4 tournament.  The K4 quotient is a locator for
the same-sign pocket, not a discriminator.  It says "finite chamber here" and
then stops.

The KPS third pocket shows where the extra address matters.  Its lex K4 gauge
can also look transitive, but one opposite K4 edge-pair balance is negative:

```text
3+4 = -23/6468.
```

That is the weighted positive/negative data the raw sign tournament hides.  The
six-sector `cyclic_pair_sum` quotient makes the same thing topological: B14
leaders have `c3=8`, `scc=(6,)`, `HP=41`, while the KPS third pocket drops to
`c3=4`, `scc=(5,1)`, `HP=15`.

Incoming THM-546 S2 matters here.  The rational signed Abel estimate

```text
|Delta_w| <= (6/49) V(E')/w
```

means the tournament atlas should not be asked to supply the analytic bound.
Its job is the tight ungapped classifier: identify which finite `++++++`
Ruzsa/Freiman models still need direct treatment, and identify which mixed-sign
or small-pair rows already expose cancellation before absolute values.

The next proof object should therefore be addressed:

```text
(finite additive model, packet sign word, opposite-pair balance vector,
 cyclic-pair tournament fingerprint, state-word/relation-shell address).
```

Dropping the last two fields is exactly how the quotient becomes too lossy.
