become free metaphors.  The useful move was to force every idea to emit exact
packet data:


The named-row audit supports the split.  AP and GW are zero-open controls with
six denominator-14 safe units and six zero-sum active pairs.  K33 and petal
rows still have denominator-14 boundary witnesses, but they are positive-open
and differ by component/slack data.  The covering row `12->84` is different
again: safe mass `563/105105`, eight positive components, no zero-sum active
pair current, and first CRT-unit witness `17/41`.

That last row is the most useful creative signal.  It says the covering branch
should not be attacked by a single denominator-14 skeleton.  A better proof
shape is:

```text

-> first CRT/solenoid chart at 41
-> lift/Fejer/moment certificate with endpoint labels retained
```

The proof-lens tournament has one directed 3-cycle and a size-3 SCC in the
middle layer.  This is good, not a problem: Cech/topology, tropical slack, and
CRT/solenoid arithmetic each preserve different coordinates.  The zipper proof
should carry them together.
