# LRC14 owner-cut chiral transcendence synthesis

This pass started from the prompt's wild scale and special-function cloud:
`exp(exp(exp(79)))`, Bring radicals, Schwarz-Christoffel maps,
Barban-Davenport-Halberstam, Menger cuts, chiral signatures,
Ramanujan-Soldner, Sophie Germain, Hermite-Lindemann-Weierstrass, Krasner, and
Meissel-Mertens.

The discipline was to ask which of these can become a packet coordinate after
HYP-3406 and the HYP-3412 special-function cut-recursion scout.
The answer is surprisingly concrete.  HYP-3406 showed that
residue+height still leaves an endpoint-owner leak.  HYP-3420 found that the
mixed residue fibers in the scanned enlarged banks have size-one owner cuts.
On the largest bank, the big `petal 13->26` versus positive-open
`26/40/54` family is separated by owner label `1:g1`; the `P10+GW` /
`GW-shell` / `12->48` fiber is separated by `5:g1`.

That makes the next theorem target look graph-theoretic rather than
transcendental: prove an endpoint-owner Menger cut statement for every
residue-mixed theorem-exit fiber, or find the first fiber where no such owner
cut exists.  The chiral owner class also kills the mixed fibers in the scanned
banks, so the mirror/chiral signature is a real compression candidate, not
just decoration.

The information-theory theme is still the right one.  Residue-only compression
has positive pair-disagreement variance.  Owner/chiral-owner support reduces
that variance to zero on the scanned banks.  A legal proof quotient is exactly
a zero-variance compression of the theorem-exit predicate, or a compression
with a named sidecar repair.

Assumption challenge: I considered runners, residues, speeds, the huge number,
named constants, and raw special functions as vertices.  They all lose the
preserved predicate.  The tournament vertices had to be proof carriers:
owner-cut certificates, chiral owner recursion, variance ledgers, stability
radii, contact-polygon accessory fields, quartic factor gates, and branch
guards.
