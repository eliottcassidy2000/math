## Session-level lessons

- Normalize the value group of the field actually generated by the chart
  before naming ramification.  The discarded divisible-by-three rows in the
  first THM-3383 draft were normalized index one, not `C3`.
- Full-field generation is a theorem hypothesis, not a synonym for
  algebraic independence.
- A finite depth ledger can be complete as differential bookkeeping and
  still be gauge-dependent under polynomial source automorphisms.
- Generic exactness should trigger an annihilator/intersection computation,
  not an integral conclusion.
- Collision data naturally ask for a minimal polynomial (lcm/max), while
  resultants naturally record a characteristic polynomial (product/sum).

These moves sharpen the local and response anatomy but do not close a
Jelonek component, construct a common `A4` lift, or prove `JC(2)`.
