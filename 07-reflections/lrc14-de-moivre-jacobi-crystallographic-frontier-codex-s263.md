## Synthesis

De Moivre's quintic is a solvable-normal-form detector.  The exact fold

```text
x = u - a/u
x^5 + 5a x^3 + 5a^2 x = u^5 - a^5/u^5
```

turns the special quintic into a quadratic in `u^5`.  That is a finite-depth
cancellation pattern, not the A5/Abel-Ruffini wall.  In LRC14 terms, it can
identify a degree-5 interaction that has already exposed its cancellation
sidecar.  If the coefficient pattern and substitution sidecar are absent, the
quintic analogy should be demoted.

Jacobi theta functions fit the repo's older signed residue-cusp thread.  The
theta tail belongs after the low-height wall deletion in the HYP-2614
support-six residue-cusp lane and next to the HYP-3073
Poisson-theta/Dirichlet bridge, not as a raw q-series shortcut.  The proof
version should be a tail certificate with deleted anti-cosets, named boundary
faces, and a route into observer gluing.

The 17 wallpaper groups and 230 three-dimensional space groups are finite
orbifold quotient audits.  They become useful only when the row quotient
records translation lattice, stabilizer word, glide/screw/torsion sidecar,
preserved LRC predicate, and destroyed coordinate.  The numbers `17` and
`230` are catalog budgets, not evidence by themselves.

HYP-3109 must stay upstream.  A crystallographic quotient that forgets the
miss-count PGF root locus, zero-collision curve, or zero-real ear sidecar has
discarded one of the best current fine-scale coverage signals.
