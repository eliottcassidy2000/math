# The Moment Shadow And The Compatibility Wall

The new baby-Hodge reframe tempted a familiar overreach: if a tournament
invariant vector survives the obvious moment or convex constraints, maybe the
hard part is already done.

The `n=6` hole `c5=10` is a clean correction.  It is not outside the spectral
shadow.  It sits *inside* it:

```text
(c3,c5) = (8,10) = (1/3)*(8,8) + (2/3)*(8,11).
```

So the one-particle moment cone permits the point.  The failure happens only
after the extra packet variable is remembered: THM-499's `D=alpha_2` layer.

The local Faulhaber packet says the same thing in a different language.  The
odd moments are not sick; they are perfectly healthy Stieltjes moments:

```text
S_{2r+1}(n) = sum_{i=1..n} i*(i^2)^r.
```

Their Hankel determinants are positive exactly as they should be.  The
breakdown at `p>=3` is not that the moment data stops being positive.  It is
that positivity alone does not force the Bernoulli/integrality address to land
on an exact tower.

That is the shared warning across the current packets:

```text
moment positivity is the one-particle shadow;
the real wall is compatibility.
```

On the tournament side, compatibility means conflict-graph / `alpha_2` packet
data.  On the Faulhaber side, it means the retained Bernoulli/support address.
On the repunit side, it means atomic prime-length supply rather than scalar
length alone.

This is a useful correction to the baby-Hodge slogan.  The moment problem is
real, but it is only the first layer.  The actual realizability problem starts
when the packet variables are put back.
