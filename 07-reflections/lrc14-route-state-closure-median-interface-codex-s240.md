# Route-state closure medians as the next LRC14 proof interface

The useful part of the median-graph idea is not that medians exist in a cube.
That is automatic.  The useful question is whether the legal LRC proof states
form something gated enough that the median of three serious routes is still a
legal proof state.

That makes this S240 note a legal-closure addendum to the S239 polymer /
Dirichlet bridge stub, the S238 cross-carrier pullback resonance stub, the S237
cycle-class observability matrix, the S236 route-triple center-control layer,
and the S235 medianized route-center gate: S239 contributes boundary-energy
and signed-polymer sidecar candidates, S238 names the carrier-portfolio vertex
candidates, S235 creates median obligations, S236 asks whether serious triples
have controlled centers, S237 names the certificate image side, and S240 tests
whether closed sidecars make those centers legal proof states.

That reframes several current carriers at once.  Moser/fibbinary/partial-cube
data should not be a sequence-shadow test.  It should be a route state whose
legal closure adds native transition, magnitude cocycle, Theta class, gated
subcube, and median-interval sidecars.  In the S240 scout the raw
automatic/partial-cube/q-witness median fails, but the closed median passes.
That is exactly the sort of proof carrier we want: a failure at the raw
quotient, followed by a precise repair.

The Hodge/Toeplitz/Fejer lane gives the opposite lesson.  A positive closed
cochain with analytic certificates is still not a proof center unless a
cycle-class image appears.  The phantom triple remains illegal after closure.
The repaired triple passes only when checked certificate generators force
`cycle_class_image_status` and `hodge_cycle_image`.  The debt triple passes
only by visibly carrying `residual_debt_exit`.

The observer-cut triple is the warning.  Three repair lanes can all touch the
same payload and still fail to medianize if no repair sidecar is majority
visible.  That says some triples should be split before a median proof
interface is applied.

Tournament Analysis also changes role.  The vertices are not runners; they are
proof states.  The pairwise observable is retained proof coordinates plus a
legality bonus, and the large number of edge flips after closure is evidence
that legal sidecars genuinely change the proof order.  Raw carrier dominance
is not reliable.

The next exact pass should build this over HYP-2963: every route witness emits
packet/route/certificate/sidecar/discharge fields, every closure rule is
audited, and every serious triple either has a legal closed median or names a
specific missing sidecar/debt exit.
