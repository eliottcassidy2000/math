# Formalization Pass S500

This pass turned the recent Tournament Analysis and n=14/n=18 LRC work into
canon-level finite statements.

## What Is Now Formal

- THM-372 proves the switchboard construction: one pairwise switch value plus a
  fixed Hamiltonian tie path per unordered pair gives a unique tournament, and
  finite wall sets give piecewise-constant tournament movies.
- THM-373 proves that integer runner phase clocks have exact wall times
  `m/(2|s_i-s_j|)` and form closed finite tournament walks.
- THM-374 proves the half-turn circular transitivity criterion: transitive iff
  all points lie in an open semicircle.
- THM-375 canonizes the n=14/n=18 local gate invoices: both have minimum lower
  cover size `8`; n=14 has a six-choice even bridge fiber, while n=18 has only
  `{6,12}`.
- THM-376 canonizes the first-even ladder gap-debt products: `5/11` for the
  n=14 rows and `1` for the n=18 rows.
- THM-377 canonizes the selected S492 pressure audit: k1, k2, and
  threshold-deficit pressure lifts have no strict SCC or directed triangle in
  the selected n=14/n=18 rows.

## What Remains Open

The main mathematical novelty is now better localized.  HYP-1942 still needs a
dual certificate turning bridge-fiber export into a global obstruction.  HYP-1950
still needs a pressure-peeling theorem beyond selected rows.  HYP-1951 has the
runner-clock wall theorem and semicircle endpoint proven, but the fixed circular
menu claim remains a classification problem.

The practical next move is to search for either a nontrivial pressure SCC after
private leaves are peeled, or a proof that every apparent SCC must violate the
endpoint-cycle arithmetic constraints.
