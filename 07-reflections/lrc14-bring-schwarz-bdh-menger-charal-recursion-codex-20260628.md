# LRC14 Bring/Schwarz/BDH/Menger Charal Recursion Reflection

This session was deliberately not a "proof by famous theorem" session.  The
useful move was to demote each outside object into a packet sidecar with a
preserved LRC predicate and a named lost coordinate.

The Bring radical is only a branch-normalization metaphor until it is tied to
the five theorem exits: q-witness, AP/GW equality, unit-petal, K33/H7, and
positive-Haar-open.  The thing to prove is not a quintic formula.  It is that
after legal sidecars are attached, every first-failure fiber has a single
theorem exit or emits named debt.

Schwarz-Christoffel is useful as a contact-polygon language, but the turning
word is not the invariant.  HYP-3406 shows that a polygon can forget exactly
the endpoint-owner coordinate that separates positive-open swaps from the
petal exit.  The accessory parameter is therefore the theorem-facing object:
owner support, off-unit endpoint address, height wall, or a named debt that
restores what the polygon turn sequence destroyed.

The Barban-Davenport-Halberstam analogy should stay finite.  I used it as
mean-square owner-channel discrepancy across the HYP-3406 mixed fibers, not as
an imported distribution theorem.  In that translation the high-variance labels
are exactly plausible separators: `5:g1` for the height leak and `1:g1` for
the persistent owner leak.  The later `(72,20)` frontier is different: its
top variance label is `13:g1`, but its actual minimum owner cut has size `3`.

Menger cuts were the strongest piece.  On the first two HYP-3406 mixed fibers,
a one-label owner cut separates theorem exits: `5:g1` for the height leak and
`1:g1` for the persistent owner leak.  On the new `10->20` drop/add frontier,
the minimum cut grows to size `3`, with first cuts such as
`('11:g1', '13:g1', '1:g1')`.  That is not a proof of LRC14, but it sharpens
the theorem shape: enlarge the bank and prove every mixed first-failure fiber
has a bounded owner cut, a dual/Farkas current, a Schwarz-Christoffel accessory
reconstruction, or a named residual exit.

"Charal" became the characteristic/chiral/arc-lift signature.  It records
replacement type, unit slot, turn word, owner groups, apex flag, `v2` span, and
owner labels.  The recursive signal is the `+14` ladder: `1/3/5 -> 26,40,54`
remain positive-open with nearly stable turn words; `12 -> 48,132` keeps an
apex-bearing turn word; `13 -> 26` is the petal exit; and the newer `10 -> 20`
petal has the same apex-bearing `(E,A)` turn but sits inside a harder size-3
owner-cut frontier.  The next theorem attempt should ask whether stable charal
recursion preserves exit status unless it crosses a forbidden owner cut.

Incoming work mattered.  HYP-3404 supplied the first-failure lead atlas and
put endpoint deletion cuts near the top.  HYP-3405 supplied the AP-collar
finite lemma and the warning that nonunit height misses unit-height flex.
HYP-3406 supplied the expanded-bank residue failure and the owner-support
repair, including the later `(72,20)` height-persistent owner leak.  Upstream
HYP-3407 now records the broader boundary-uniformization / special-function
cut-signature route; HYP-3410 is the exact Bring/Schwarz/BDH/Menger sidecar
synthesis over these leaks.

Assumption challenge: I considered runners, residues, owner labels, polygon
turns, BDH channels, Menger cuts, Bring branches, deletion events, and proof
obligations as tournament vertices.  The chosen vertices were proof carriers
and sidecar transformations because they preserve the LRC predicate under
controlled forgetting; raw runners or famous analogies destroy too much.

Tournament Analysis was transitive on this small carrier set.  The single
priority path was:

```text
menger_owner_cut_recursion
-> charal_recursive_signature
-> bdh_channel_discrepancy_packet
-> schwarz_christoffel_contact_polygon
-> tropical_height_wall_backend
-> bring_branch_resolvent
-> raw_cross_discipline_analogy
```
