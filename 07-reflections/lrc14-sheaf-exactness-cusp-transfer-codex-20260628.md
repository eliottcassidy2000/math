# LRC14 Sheaf Exactness And Cusp Transfer

This pass deliberately moved away from HYP-3300's two chosen routes.  I did
not ask for another sidecar observability matrix or another chamber Morse
energy.  The new synthesis asks for two theorem targets:

```text
1. first-obstruction sheaf exactness;
2. Farey-cusp renormalization transfer.
```

The first target says the current proof surface is a sheaf of packet charts.
Unit contacts, danger-cover holes, lag/residue curvature, signed address
changes, exact-period boundaries, endpoint cells, and state-lift exits are
not independent metaphors.  They are chart overlaps.  The first payload lost
by a quotient is a cocycle.  The proof should show it is exact, repaired by
`zeta_7` holonomy, lifted to an endpoint chamber, descended, stopped at AP/GW,
or named as K33/H7 debt.

The second target says the covering residual should be read as a cusp transfer
rather than a scalar floor chase:

```text
qdiv>14 exact-period cover
  -> boundary-moment image
  -> positive floor
     or forbidden AP/GW kernel
     or K33/H7 named kernel
     or genuinely new zero-open kernel.
```

This is the old boundary-moment bridge, but with the newer work attached:
HYP-3230 supplies the Farey/Stern-Brocot recursion, HYP-3231 supplies the
scale-normal ledger, HYP-3253 supplies holonomy as connection repair,
HYP-3255/HYP-3257 warn that residue symmetry is not magnitude rigidity, and
HYP-3265 turns the AP/GW unit contacts into an exact equality-core classifier.
