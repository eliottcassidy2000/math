## Chronological Proof Maturation

### 1. Tournament Shadows And Fixed Boundary Scaffolds

The early LRC work tried to read loneliness through tournament structure:
A000568 isomorphism classes, marked chambers, observer-source reachability,
pressure gauges, and fixed-boundary snapshots.  This gave vocabulary and
finite-state discipline, but it was too coarse as a proof invariant.

Durable lessons:

```text
observer-source matters;
wall switches matter;
the Hamiltonian-path tie convention is a real bookkeeping tool;
raw isomorphism class is not enough;
runner-level dominance is often transitive and uninformative.
```

The late HYP-2952 derived-boundary tournament confirms the same lesson in the
modern language.  AP, GW, K33 near-miss, petals, and S138 splices can all land
in the same transitive six-unit apex-pressure class.  That class is a necessary
front filter, not a final proof object.

### 2. Endpoint, Fold, Pincer, And Certificate Calculus

The next phase kept more geometry: endpoint owners, pair-sum folds, pincer
pinches, exposed cells, and boundary circuits.  This was the first place where
the tight locus began to look like endpoint arithmetic rather than only a
global max-gap problem.

The durable correction here is that total counts are usually too coarse:

```text
total visible folds can agree while target profiles differ;
raw additive energy points AP-facing but is not monotone;
raw endpoint divergence is zero on covering rows;
the useful datum is a typed endpoint/fold profile, not a scalar count.
```

HYP-2965 later sharpened this: all `1187` qdiv>14 covering rows in the audited
bank have zero first endpoint current, yet all are positive-open.  Thus raw
divergence cannot be the boundary proof.  The bridge must be localized
transition packets, second moments such as gK8/L_y, or state-lift.

### 3. q-Witness, Farey, And Exact Haar/Baire Fronts

The qdiv gate and exact interval engine changed the proof grammar.

For a primitive strict counterexample:

```text
qdiv(S)>14
```

because `qdiv(S)<=14` gives a denominator witness.  This recasts AP and
Goddyn-Wong:

```text
AP and GW are qdiv=14 equality atoms,
not strict counterexamples.
```

Haar/Baire then made the open-vs-boundary distinction exact.  AP and GW are
strict-Haar-zero, threshold-supported atoms.  Near/K33 `12->36`, petal `10->20`,
petal `13->26`, and the S138 splices are strict-Haar-positive.  Once this was
in place, a fake counterexample had to be not merely small in exact `M`, but
zero-open or covering in a much sharper sense.

### 4. C27, Unital, K33, And State-Lift Labels

The C27/unital/K33 layer explains why Haar/Baire boundary owners still do not
separate AP from GW.  AP and GW share the same six boundary owner pairs, so the
hidden `H12->D3@24` transfer is invisible unless C27/unital labels are attached.

Important refined points:


The q=3 unital prompt survived as a labelled pair-frame, not as a canonical

identification.  HYP-2942 then repaired the idea as branch-local pair completion:
use unital charts after AP/GW labels are attached.

This is a typical repo move: a literal analogy fails, but the labelled version
becomes useful.

### Irreducible Polynomials And Bunyakovsky

The prime/irreducible-polynomial bridge is analogous to the LRC packet endgame.
A reducible polynomial hides factor data in coefficient convolutions; a false
LRC quotient hides endpoint/source/owner labels in scalar shadows.  Bunyakovsky
asks for irreducible admissible polynomials to produce infinitely many primes.
The LRC analogue asks for irreducible admissible packets:

```text
after every known discharge/factor has been removed,
can a source-spectrum packet remain genuinely irreducible?
```

The current evidence says no in every bounded bank.  The proof would classify
irreducible packet atoms as AP/GW equality or K33/state-lift obstruction.

### Faulhaber Odd Moments

The Faulhaber anchor formula says only odd moments matter in the balance
equation after centering.  This is morally aligned with the odd-cycle and
danger-count moment work: signed boundary imbalances disappear in even
averages, while odd moments detect directed obstruction.

This suggests a useful refinement:

```text
build boundary-moment ledgers in an odd-moment basis,
then compare with danger-count factorial moments and Toeplitz Fourier moments.
```

If the common AP/GW kernel is exactly the simultaneous zero of these odd
boundary moments, the dual-certificate cluster may become one theorem rather
than five scouts.

### Pollock And Sierpinski

Pollock work moved from single residues to defect-pair carry ledgers.  That is


The Pollock tetrahedral descent, where one-back descent fails exactly when two
defect sets correlate across a triangular offset, is a close cousin of LRC
boundary-moment packets: the obstruction is not a point, but a labelled

### Pi, 22/7, Cube Root 31, Flowers, And Unital

The pi/unital/flower work is best read as a unit-preservation guardrail.

For LRC, the rule is:
