# LRC14 Spectral Shadow Dual Route

This pass intentionally stepped away from endpoint owners.  HYP-2965 and
HYP-2966 are local: a positive interval is a bridge or pinch with named owners.
HYP-2977 asks the complementary global question:

```text
what does the safe set look like as a Fourier object?
```

The answer is useful because it is not too pretty.  AP and Goddyn-Wong are
zero-shadow atoms in the audit, exactly as they should be.  Every other named
or few-apex row tested has positive exact Haar mass and a positive Fejer
midpoint shadow.  But the L2 energy is mostly not in the first few modes.  The
frequency-band tournament is transitive in the high-frequency direction:

```text
113-224 > 57-112 > 29-56 > 15-28 > 1-7 > 8-14.
```

So this does not license a naive "low Fourier modes prove LRC14" story.  It
suggests a sharper theorem:

```text
positive minorant packet + high-frequency relation-tail theorem.
```

That sits naturally beside the earlier Beurling-Selberg and support-6 work.
After rebasing over HYP-2970 through HYP-2976, the picture is cleaner:
endpoint-credit potentials, multiplicity-moment barriers, twist-ladder
blockers, danger-count moments, Toeplitz cover tests, taut-bridge curvature,
and safe-set Fourier shadows are related projections of the same cover problem;
HYP-2976 is the lineage map around them.  The old obstruction was that signed
singular integrals and AP-like extremizers do not yield termwise positive
floors.  HYP-2977 reframes the obstruction:
high bands are not noise; they are the labelled packet data in spectral form.
The dominant modes for the hard rows remember the denominators and relation
scales (`41`, `27`, multiples of `6`, and C27-style bands).  That is exactly
where HYP-2614/HYP-2617-style residue hyperplane cancellation should re-enter.

Assumption challenge:

```text
Considered:
  runners, gaps, fixed circle sections, section boundaries,
  wall-crossing events, residues, cover arcs, lift packets,
  Fourier modes, and proof obligations.

Chosen:
  Fourier bands and Fejer-dual packets.

Preserved:
  strict Haar mass, L2 mass of the safe indicator, and spectral detectability.

Destroyed:
  endpoint-owner labels and exact packet family identity.
```

The quotient is therefore not complete.  It is a bridge.  The local packet
route says which rows are possible; the spectral route should say that every
possible nonzero shadow has a positive one-sided certificate, while AP/GW are
the only rows with no strict-open shadow at all.

The most promising next computation is an LP/linear-program dual:

```text
choose a nonnegative trigonometric kernel K
such that integral K(t-x) 1_U(t) dt has a symbolic positive lower bound
on every labelled Moon-core packet,
with the uncovered high-frequency tail charged to relation-lattice ledgers.
```

If that can be done for the HYP-2968 few-apex bank using only packet labels
and not enumerated endpoints, it would be a real proof engine rather than
another finite audit.
