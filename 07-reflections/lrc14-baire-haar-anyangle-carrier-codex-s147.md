# LRC14 Baire-Haar Any-Angle Carrier Reflection

S147 reframes Borel/Baire/Haar language as proof infrastructure rather than
analogy.

For finite LRC14 rows, the danger sets are finite unions of arcs in `R/Z`.
That means they are Borel, have the Baire property, and are Haar-measurable.
Their boundaries are finite.  So endpoint strictness is a small but named
boundary debt: Haar-null and meagre, but still important for exact tight rows.

The exact row check is a useful calibration.  AP and Goddyn-Wong have no
positive open witness at threshold `1/14`; they are endpoint-only at this
level.  The near/K33 row and the two petal rows have positive open witness
measure.  That is exactly the split the Farey/C27 route needs.

The any-angle path-planning prompt then gives a good vocabulary:

```text
Theta*      direct witness shortcut
Lazy Theta* delayed owner/carry check
Block A*    finite AP/GW/petal/K33 atlas
ANYA        interval nodes and taut obstacle wraps
CWave       geometric wavefront arcs
```

The sixth carrier, Haar-Baire Taut Wave*, combines the useful parts but changes
the state space.  Nodes are not grid vertices; they are regular-open Baire
events with Haar mass, finite boundary debt, and C27/K33 owner labels.

The next test should be to attach each positive safe interval endpoint in the
near/K33 and petal rows to the C27 shell-transfer owner that creates it.  If
that owner map is stable under the known splices, it may be the event-level
counterpart of the HYP-2944 affine-depth path and the HYP-2946
minor-transitive K33 packet.
