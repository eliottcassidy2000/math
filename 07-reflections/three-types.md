# Three Types: Lossless, Lossy, Crystallization

## The three types of computation

**LOSSLESS (isomorphism):** Map = territory. Reversible. O(1) both directions.
Q, ln, formal group, unique factorization, CRT decomposition.
Eisenstein type: INERT. The prime passes through unchanged.
Cuboid axis: x (parity, mod 2). Always determined. Fixed by Rédei.

**LOSSY (projection):** Map misses territory. Irreversible. Kernel lost.
H from tournament, Eisenstein→Z, butterfly overlap, addition in rapidity.
Eisenstein type: RAMIFIED. The prime cracks at the singularity.
Cuboid axis: y (curvature, mod 3). Some combinations forbidden.

**CRYSTALLIZATION (irreversible transformation):** Map transforms territory.
Information preserved but form changed. COMPUTATIONALLY IRREDUCIBLE.
Kaprekar iteration, chain 3→7→47→2207, evolution, learning.
Eisenstein type: SPLIT. The prime decomposes into conjugate halves with twist.
Cuboid axis: z (position, mod 7). Must iterate step by step. No shortcut.

## Computational properties

Lossless: O(1) to compute AND reverse.
Lossy: O(1) to compute, impossible to fully reverse.
Crystallization: O(n) to compute (must iterate), determined but not shortcuttable.

Crystallization IS computational irreducibility.
Not lost (the orbit is deterministic). Not preserved in form (form changes).
Irreversibly transformed at each step. The only way to know = to do.
