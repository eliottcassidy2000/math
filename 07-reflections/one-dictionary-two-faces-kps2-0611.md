# One dictionary, two faces — and the census that stopped one vertex short

**Source:** kind-pasteur-2026-06-11-S2 (THM-481/482 session)

Two findings that look unrelated — "both Gleason Type II generators are
tournament-generated" and "the +2 sandwich for trans under doubling is false" —
are the two faces of the same object: HYP-2409's two-doubling dictionary.

On the CODE side, the skew doubling acts as pair-doubling + glue (THM-480's d⁺
mechanism), and the Paley/bordered face of the same gauge generates the
quadratic-residue ladder: ê₈ at p=7, g₂₄ at p=23 — the two Gleason generators,
with the proof a one-liner once you look at the rows (each row of (J−H)/2 IS a
QNR-translate plus its parity bit; THM-481 B). The ring of ALL Type II weight
enumerators is generated by two tournaments.

On the TRANS side, the same doubling acts on transitive chains as
two-blocks-plus-one-twin — and the exact law is the zigzag number (THM-483):
a transitive chain in D(T) is a shuffle of an ascending chain (the (b,b) block)
and a descending chain (the reversed copy) with one glue twin. The alternating
family A_l maximizes the shuffle and the "+2 sandwich" dies: δ = l, unbounded.

The meta-lesson is the census horizon. HYP-2360 had EXHAUSTIVE evidence — all
32768 tournaments at n = 6, δ never exceeding 2 — and the first counterexample
lives at n = 7, one vertex past the census, because the mechanism (A_l) needs
2l+1 ≥ 7 vertices to express its third rung. This is MISTAKE-018's lesson
("exhaustive at n ≤ 7 does not extend to n = 8") in its sharpest form yet: the
exception ALREADY SEEN at n=5 (A_2, the idx10 exception of THM-455) was the
general mechanism announcing itself, and the right response to a lone exception
is to PARAMETERIZE it (build A_l from A_2) rather than to conjecture it stays
rare. We had the counterexample's blueprint in hand for two days and read it as
a sporadic evasion.

Rule worth keeping: when an exhaustive census finds a single structured
exception, the next experiment is not a bigger census — it is the infinite
family the exception generates. One parameterization (ten minutes) did what no
n=7 census (2^21 tournaments, hours) would have shown as clearly: WHY.

Second lesson, from the other face: the adversarial verification round did more
than re-derive — it FOUND THE CITATION we missed (Kim–Solé 2008: the eQR
identity was a rediscovery; only the Gleason framing is ours). Verifiers should
always carry a literature mandate, not just a computation mandate: the cheapest
refutation of "this is new" is a DOI.
