The useful interpretation of the finite-automaton prompt is not "automatic
sequences prove LRC14."  It is narrower and more actionable: automatic
languages provide small state labels for packet fibers, and those labels can
be added to the current HYP-2963 classifier without violating the no-free-slider
rule.

The exact finite check is reassuring.  At threshold `1/14`, AP and Goddyn-Wong
remain zero-open boundary atoms.  The first 13 positive fibbinary speeds have
positive safe mass `66077/399840`; the first 13 positive Moser-de Bruijn speeds
have positive safe mass `4264747/40348854`.  So the obvious automatic/lacunary
stress rows are discharged by open safe components, not by new exceptional

The challenged assumption is that vertices should be runners or speeds.  In
this pass the Tournament Analysis vertices are proof carriers: DFA packet
