## Factorial: the exact missing operation is a diagonal, not a schedule

In the inherited carrier, all `69` active-face changes are

```text
F12 -> empty at Q12=(1,2,2,3,4,5).
```

There are no pair active-set changes and no face-acquisition edges.  Hence an
`F12` completion does not rebind a state register.  Retagging `Q12` would
replace the current shared ancestry rather than continue it.

The positive structural result is that every one of the `94` starts reaches a
row-independent unique shared blocker

```text
B(n)=C union (support(n) intersect {1,2}),
C={3,4,5,6,7,8},                                            (4)
```

with counts `47,16,24,7`.  The opposed reset requirements freeze the presence
bits of roots `1,2`, while roots `3,...,8` normalize to one.  This explains the
four states rather than merely listing them.

Now declare one new, rowless operation that copies the *current blocker state*
and its ancestry into two singleton `F13/F23` registers.  After this diagonal,
all moves are inherited row-labelled one-pole moves, and every start finishes
in exactly four total singleton steps, even with rows restricted to `{16,17}`.

This is a sharp conditional repair, not an inherited law.  Allowing it only on
the four generated blockers leaves `34/38` full-domain blockers; allowing the
same local continuation on all seven universal blockers still leaves `31`
row-specific ones.  The next mathematical question is therefore algebraic:

```text
does the product-Gamma / face-obligation carrier possess an ancestry-preserving
diagonal whose target predicate survives duplication?                     (5)
```

More scheduling cannot answer `(5)`.

## Jacobian: the blow-up produces a connected quadratic interface

THM-3306 leaves a transverse base ideal `(linear_a,b)` in each accessory
field, with `deg(linear_a)=36` and an exact degree-two common `y`-fibre.  The
preceding quadratic row has the distinct leading coefficient

```text
P2=2V'+8V^2,       deg(P2)=32.                              (6)
```

On the blow-up chart `u=linear_a`, `b=ut`, its exceptional equation is

```text
F_0(t)=P2 t^2-Q2 t+R2 in A_i[t],
A_i=K_i[x]/(linear_a).                                      (7)
```

The discriminant is nonzero and nonsquare in both `A_i`.  Exact norm
nonresidue certificates occur at `(p,alpha)=(13,6)` and `(23,16)`, with norm
residues `7` and `22`.  Hence

```text
B_i=A_i[t]/(F_0)=A_i(sqrt(delta))                            (8)
```

is a field of relative degree two.  The exceptional intersection is one
degree-72 closed point over `K_i`.  After fixing any of the `36` geometric
base embeddings, its relative fibre is two directions exchanged by the deck
involution; over an algebraic closure of `K_i` there are `72` points in total.
There is no `A_i`-rational direction and none at a coordinate infinity.

The fixed-normal first variation satisfies

```text
gcd(F_0,F_1)=1.                                              (9)
```

Both geometric directions therefore move, with distinct conjugate first
velocities.  The blow-up resolves the projective coefficient map but does not
choose a root.  The minimum lawful root-label sidecar is precisely `B_i`.

The most direct continuation is to base-change the critical inverse-graph and
cofactor/integrability sidecars to `B_i`, evaluate them on the two conjugate
branches, and descend only trace/norm data that is invariant under the deck
exchange.  A branchwise polynomial mate would need more than `(8)--(9)`.

## Sequences: minimal circuit arity controls the finite head

Closing the eleven missing static relation edges under all `2^11=2048`
subsets gives exactly six inclusion-minimal all-ones-visible zero-mode sets:

```text
one singleton: {(7,18)},
five triples,
no new minimal pairs.                                       (10)
```

The singleton primitive direction has `(1^T v,v^T v)=(3,15)`.  Every other
edge delta annihilates it, so it remains in every later kernel and forces

```text
mu(A)=1^T P_ker(A)1 >= 3/5                                  (11)
```

on all `1024` supersets.  The five triples are fragile.  For one of them, a
fourth edge can leave mass `12/35`, rotate it to `12/59` or `12/37`, replace
it by mass `14/15`, or annihilate it to zero.  Visibility is nonmonotone; the
primitive direction and its stabilizer are the real state.

Embedding every graph in the `26`-node full-core universe gives a fixed base
matrix `B`.  For active observer `w`, `b` new vertices and added adjacency
`Delta=UCU^T`, the exact scalar series update is

```text
G_new(x)-G_tree(x)
  = b + x s(x)^T(C-xK(x))^(-1)s(x),                         (12)

s=U^T(I-xB)^(-1)w,       K=U^T(I-xB)^(-1)U.
```

All `55` two-edge updates use local dimension `4` or `10`; a formal inverse
recurrence matches the first `20` direct coefficients in every case.  The
compiler keeps births and the finite zero-mode head instead of fitting only a
denominator.  This is the clearest current answer to the efficient-sequence
question: reuse base powers and update through the small changing interface.

The next structural task is to derive the six circuits from incidence columns

## Three cross-frontier connections that survive typing

### 1. Minimal joint supports precede scalar feasibility

The LRC status table, factorial blocker, and sequence circuits all reward
retaining joint support.  Only the factorial and sequence artifacts establish
a lower-order compatibility failure; the LRC artifact classifies four-bit
capacity cuts without testing pairwise sufficiency.  What transfers is the
search procedure:

```text
enumerate minimal joint supports -> derive their literal obstruction or repair
-> only then compress to a scalar count.                            (13)
```

The types do not transfer.  An LRC support is a Boolean capacity event, an FC
support carries reset ancestry, and a sequence support indexes adjacency
columns.  No theorem moves a certificate among them.

### 2. First variation classifies persistence

The new stabilizer ledger has four distinct outcomes:

| object | next operation | classification |
|---|---|---|
| singleton zero-mode direction | every remaining edge delta | persistent |
| arity-three zero-mode direction | a fourth edge | fragile or replaceable |
| exceptional quadratic direction | normal displacement | mobile conjugate pair |
| four-blocker copy repair | enlarge to full coloured domain | sectional, not global |

The operation must stay typed: `Delta v`, `F_1`, and an ancestry transition
are different first variations.  The transferable question is whether the
object survives its own next native operator.

### 3. Local interfaces compile large ambient systems

Each successful calculation factors into a fixed ambient engine and a small
changing interface:

```text
LRC:       ray screen       + four-bit capacity event,
factorial: reset DAG        + four blocker states + one proposed diagonal,
Jacobian:  degree-36 base   + rank-two algebra A[t]/(F_0),
sequence:  fixed base B     + 4x4 or 10x10 bordered formal inverse.     (14)
```

This is not a common algebra.  It is a research design: cache the ambient
propagation, expose the smallest target-bearing boundary, and spend exact work
there.  It explains why these probes made progress while additional scalar
fits did not.

## Tournament structure: the honest object is incidence, not orientation

There is no intrinsic tournament in the new sequence census.  A relation
“edge `e` breaks circuit `v`” is bipartite, has many ties, and is not a binary
orientation of one vertex set.  The same is true of “cut template closes
status state” in the LRC taxonomy.  Forcing either into a tournament would
erase multiplicity, compatibility and nonmonotonic replacement.

The useful tournament-adjacent question is operational: can the resulting
incidence hypergraph be composed under deletion, substitution or join?  Its
vertices should be circuit/support obligations, its arcs typed break or
preserve actions, and its target should be compiler size or proof closure.
Only if an intrinsic antisymmetric observable later appears should a genuine
tournament be extracted.

## Bold reframes now worth testing

1. **Certificate complexity before row complexity.**  In the LRC lane, rank
   families by physical invoice for execution but rank residual statuses by
   monotone-event proof complexity for theory.  Compile the complete four-bit
   event catalogue and see whether the eleven weighted states collapse under
   laminar/submodular templates.
2. **A root label is a field extension, not a chart choice.**  On the Jacobian
   base locus, work over `B_i`, keep the deck involution, and descend trace/norm
   statements.  Trying to select one root over `A_i` is now exactly ruled out.
3. **A factorial rebind is a comultiplication problem.**  Search for a lawful
   diagonal in the algebra of face-tagged proof obligations.  The four-state
   controller says response scheduling is no longer the first obstruction.
4. **Closed forms are bordered presentations.**  For sequence updates, cache
   base Krylov data and compile numerator, finite head, births and local
   determinant together.  A denominator-only recurrence is an incomplete
   closed form whenever a visible zero mode is present.
5. **Persistence is an invariant in its own right.**  Attach a stabilizer and
   breaking set to every newly found local object.  This separates global
   mechanisms from sectional successes before substantial generalization.
