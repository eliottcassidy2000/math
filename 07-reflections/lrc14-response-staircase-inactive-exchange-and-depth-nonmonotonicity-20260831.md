## Mechanism that mattered

The staircase initially looked like an accumulating-deck process. The
reframe was to price every addition against masks that are inert on the whole
new consequence family. If `R` is the new response set and `D` is an equally
large old set with strict negative activity margin on every target row, then

```text
Active_p((C union R)\D)=Active_p(C union R)
```

for every target `p`. This is stronger than merely preserving a scalar score:
it preserves the entire active witness family and therefore every body-cover
consequence. The direct 5.06-billion-instance replay then serves as a
structurally different audit.

This suggests an online invariant for later layers:

```text
exchange slack(K)=number of old nonjoint masks inactive on all rows of K
                  - number of new responses needed for K.
```

At `K_597` the slack is `76-69=7`. A future extension should update both sides
before growing the carrier. The seven unused inactive masks are a real buffer,
but only on the current prefix; they must be rescanned when endpoint 596 is
adjoined.

## What the wildcard changed

The four endpoint-628 bodies recur at endpoint 626 although their exact
deletion depths are only eight there. Thus a carrier failure is not evidence
for a deeper global obstruction. It may indicate only that a previously
selected response class has become inactive. The two endpoint-628 responders
show the exact sign word `(+,+,-)` over `628,627,626`.

Similarly, colex compression is not a lawful quotient of activity. The
counterexample `0000b28d -> 0000328f` changes only label `15 -> 1` but loses
enough labelled failure-atom mass to cross the wall. Any compression proposal
must therefore carry an atom-weight sidecar and prove the relevant label swap
is mass-nondecreasing; positional order alone is unusable.
