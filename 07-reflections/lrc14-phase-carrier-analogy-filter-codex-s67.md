# LRC14 Phase-Carrier Analogy Filter - Codex S67

The prompt was dense with good analogies, but the useful move was to make each
one pay rent in the repo's exact carriers.

What survived:

- Path integrals survive as the exact additive-character expansion of the
  mod-7 surjection indicator.
- Gibbs survives as the death-chain transfer matrix and spectral gap `1/7`.
- Hopfield survives after replacing the naive line-graph coupling by signed
  incidence couplings.
- Clifford/magic survives on the tournament side through HYP-2707's proved
  `c3 mod 2` Gauss-sum rank formula.
- Road coloring survives only as residual deletion/synchronizer structure.

What did not survive:

- The literal Arnold cat map: the mod-7 action is torsion or expanding, not a
  hyperbolic unimodular toral automorphism.
- Strict Cerny synchronization: LRC needs coverage/reachability, not state
  collapse under invertible maps.
- Convexity as a proof: convexity in beta is tautological, and `measS7` is not
  concave along natural offset deformations.
- Raw clock coherence as a metric: it gets some signs right, but not the
  quantitative margin.

The common structure is now clearer.  The proof should keep a generated phase
profile until the final cap comparison.  The free/decorrelated death-chain
quotient has already paid the HYP-2701 boundary margin after two far hits; the
open problem is the signed resonant deviation from that quotient.  This is the
LRC analogue of magic rank: low-degree relation defects go to finite atlases,
while the off-atlas branch needs a Fubini-Study/projective-angle bound.
