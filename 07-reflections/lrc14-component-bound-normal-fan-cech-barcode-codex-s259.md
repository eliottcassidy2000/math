# LRC14 Component Bound: Normal-Fan Cech Barcode

The remaining polynomial-method witness route has a very specific weak point:
positive lonely measure is not enough.  HYP-3096 needs a component bound, since
the denominator-net inference is

```text
mu(L) >= m0 and components(L) <= A0
  => largest_arc(L) >= m0/A0.
```

THM-575 explains why this cannot be phrased in raw time.  A huge apex can
subdivide direct time components while leaving a strict witness in a normalized
coordinate.  So the live object is not a denominator table and not a runner
tournament; it is the normalized topology of the direct lonely set.

The incoming S258 observer-gluing scout gives the first quantitative warning:
sample live residuals already range from `42` direct components in an `H7=6`
row to `860` direct components in the divisor-loaded `B=8` row, while the
largest direct arc falls to `1/82320`.  That does not refute the witness route;
it says exactly where the route must split.  Bounded-apex packets may admit a
direct component theorem, but large-apex packets need the normalized
slow/ruler component chart before any denominator-net claim is legal.

THM-577 also changes the surrounding pressure.  The cap values for `k=10,11`
now have symbolic closed-form overlap evaluations, and the `k=8,9` dips are
finite higher-order remainders.  The cap chart is therefore less speculative.
The unsolved part exposed here is topological: how the mass is distributed
among normalized components.

The old topology work is stronger than it looked.  HYP-3025 says AP/GW are
closed-cover `beta1=1` boundary atoms with owner-current balance, while the
positive rows have `beta1=0`.  HYP-3015 says positive rows have bars crossing
height `1/14`.  HYP-3018 says those bars have active bottleneck supports and
endpoint owner words.  HYP-3071 says the first residual teeth are arc topology
and coarse safe stalks, before exact magnitude.  Together they say the
component theorem should be a normal-fan chamber theorem.

The synthesis in HYP-3101 is to treat each component as a word:

```text

right endpoint owner

```

Then the desired component bound is a finite-chamber theorem after the
THM-573 level-7 sieve and finite-address branch normalizer.  If the chamber
word is not finite, the first missing word should be named: runner quotient
Betti defect, hidden owner current, normal-fan support loss, or F7 good-cover
defect.
