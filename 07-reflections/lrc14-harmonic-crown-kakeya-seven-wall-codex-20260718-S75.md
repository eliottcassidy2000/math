# LRC14 after the harmonic crown: the object is a weighted deletion lattice on a Kakeya needle

The long LRC14 program has repeatedly found the same obstruction in different
coordinates.  The useful advance of THM-1153 is not just another numerical
bound.  It identifies which earlier views were shadows of one object and
shows, by an all-`N` calculation, why every first-order density proof stops at
seven.

## What the accumulated viewpoints actually retain

1. **Small-modulus and divisor sieves** retain exact witnesses and carrier
   divisibility.  They quickly close generic packets, but the uniform
   `q<=25` hope is false.  A modulus tournament is a certificate scheduler,
   not a representation of the lonely set.

2. **AP/Farey ladders** retain regenerated maximizers.  THM-1149's toothpick
   blocker proves `13d|v` after an AP deletion has really been extracted.
   It does not extract that deletion, and therefore does not collapse the
   all-loose essential crown.

3. **The auxiliary `1/13` essential crown** retains private owner regions and
   multiplicity-three debt.  Its exact identity
   `mu_1=sum_(k>=3)(k-2)mu_k` says why naked pair telemetry is incomplete.
   It is stronger than the actual LRC14 target and must not be called an
   equivalent formulation.

4. **Component/tooth recursion** retains metric endpoint order.  It closes
   uniform two- and three-comb stages and large exact pieces of the four-comb
   stage.  THM-1148 corrected the tempting `4/3` actual-mean shortcut: pair
   edge descents do not count the components that remain after all combs
   coalesce.

5. **Fragmentation and density** retain bulk duty plus endpoint leakage.
   THM-1152 made the two-replacement AP neighbourhood finite.  Before
   THM-1153, however, the lower-core interval length and the multi-comb budget
   had not been kept symbolically in the same recurrence.

6. **Tournament analyses** consistently show what is lost.  Runner order is
   transitive; private-stalk order is transitive; ranked proof obligations are
   transitive.  Score histograms and Hamiltonian paths are useful regression
   fingerprints, but the theorem-facing data live in labelled incidence,
   owner, phase, and metric sidecars.

## The new unifying coordinate

Delete `r` speeds `S` from an `N`-runner counterexample, retain core `W`, and
write `m=max W`.  Lower-case LRC fattening produces a protected real interval
of length

```text
L >= 2(1/(N-r)-1/N)/m = 2r/(N(N-r)m).
```

The deleted danger combs must cover this interval.  Fragmentation therefore
forces

```text
sum_(s in S)1/s >= r(N-2r)/(N(N-r)m).
```

This is the multi-deletion harmonic crown.  It is simultaneously:

- a Kakeya-needle statement (every deleted comb must hit one protected
  interval);
- a weighted Boolean-lattice statement (every deletion cut has reciprocal
  capacity);
- a recursive scale bound (top cuts constrain the next order statistic);
- and an exact wall detector (`N-2r=0`).

For LRC14, retaining the full harmonic sums rather than replacing them by a
single minimum yields

```text
v13/v7 < 613466231/1350.
```

The scalar product bound is about 381 times worse.  The gain is the first
concrete payoff from treating the crown as a coupled object rather than
thirteen independent deletion inequalities.

## H-drift and the ladder's toothpick self-similarity

Let `x_i=v13/v_i` and let `T_r` be the cumulative normalized reciprocal mass
of the top `r` speeds.  The extremal triangular relaxation is

```text
new tooth = T_r/c_r,
T_(r+1)/T_r = 1+1/c_r = (98-r^2)/(r(7-r)).
```

That rational function is the functional form the H-drift discussion was
missing.  The numerator is a quadratic reservoir; the denominator has the
seventh-rung pole.  Each tooth is a magnified copy of the entire previous
stalk, so the self-similarity is cumulative, not geometric in the raw speed
ratios.  This also explains why multiplying independent ratio caps discards
so much information.

## Why the seventh wall is qualitatively different

At `r=7`, the protected core needle still exists, but seven comb densities
sum to exactly one.  The positive coefficient disappears.  Boundary leakage
can pay for any first-order discrepancy.  The missing currency is overlap:
if arithmetic forces pair or triple overlap inside the needle, an exact cover
must compensate it with endpoint excess, which again constrains scale.

This is where the still-open Fano/`chi_7` probe belongs.  Seven is not merely
the number of runner vertices.  It is the number of comb families in a
bulk-duty-one cover.  A Fano-shaped triple-incidence carrier can record
whether pair overlaps coincide in the same chambers; a tournament cannot.
HYP-7678 isolates a sufficient spanning-tree overlap inequality and marks
the triple-incidence sidecar that a valid proof may need.

The `j=4` flood tail and the uniform `r=5` component problem are a different
slice of this same geometry.  Their four inserted combs lie below the global
seven-density wall, so total mass is not the obstruction; endpoint deletion
and coalescence are.  THM-1148's exact cones and `Q4` gate solve large regions
of that slice but leave the ray `m(3,4,5,6)`.  The harmonic crown does not
close that ray.  It says how such local four-comb packets must sit inside any
global top-seven counterexample and supplies a finite projective ceiling for
the surrounding scales.

## What remains genuinely separate

- **Twelve-speed equality rigidity** asks whether every tight deletion is a
  dilated AP.  It remains open.
- **Crown collapse** asks whether a primitive compact Cover14 strict cover has
  any tight deletion.  It remains open even if equality rigidity is granted.
- **Uniform four-comb/r5 and r6 tails** remain open outside the proved cones,
  exact banks, and transfer gates.
- **The seventh-wall overlap debt** remains open and is now the exact place
  where Kakeya, Fano/`chi_7`, pair-floor, and private/triple ideas meet.

The challenged assumption is now explicit: neither runners nor whole combs
need be the vertices.  The smallest plausible carrier is a protected-needle
event complex with tooth addresses, owner sets, overlap lengths, and deletion
rank.  Any quotient must state which LRC predicate it preserves and route
phase or triple-incidence loss to an explicit residual bucket.

That is the current structural frontier.  THM-1153 makes meaningful progress
by compressing the top seven speeds and deriving the wall rather than merely
observing it; it does not pretend that naming the wall closes it.
