# The Hypotenuse Generates Everything

**Status:** CENTRAL INSIGHT — read this before any metagraph work  
**Session:** opus-2026-04-03-S27  
**Depends on:** THM-280, THM-281, THM-282  

## The One Sentence

The entire metagraph structure — SC/NS classification, blue/black edges, spine/ribs/sea, purity, self-loops, the merged quotient G_n/Z_2 — is generated by a single line of symmetry: **the hypotenuse of the staircase Young diagram**.

## The Object

The staircase delta_{n-2} is a right isosceles triangle with m = C(n-1,2) cells. Each cell (x,y) with 1 ≤ y < x ≤ n, x-y ≥ 2 holds one binary tile.

The triangle has three sides:
- **Vertical leg** (y=1): source arcs — who vertex n beats
- **Horizontal leg** (x=n): sink arcs — who beats vertex 1
- **Hypotenuse** (x+y=n+1): the axis of symmetry

## The Reflection

The grid reflection R: (x,y) → (n+1-y, n+1-x) is the unique involution that:
- **Fixes the hypotenuse** (tiles with x+y=n+1 map to themselves)
- **Swaps the two legs** (source column ↔ sink column)
- **Pairs all other tiles** into reflection orbits

This is the ONLY symmetry of the staircase that matters for isomorphism class structure.

## What R Generates (proved, THM-280)

At the tournament level, R encodes the vertex reversal σ(v) = n+1-v. For any tiling b, the reflected tiling satisfies T(R(b)) = σ(T(b)^op) ≅ T(b)^op. Therefore:

**R induces the complement involution T → T^op on isomorphism classes.**

## The Cascade

From R, everything follows:

### Level 1: Tilings
- **Grid-symmetric (GS) tilings**: fixed points of R (symmetric across hypotenuse)
- **Non-GS tilings**: free orbits of R (asymmetric across hypotenuse)  
- **Count**: 2^(fixed + pairs) GS tilings, where fixed = floor((n-1)/2)
- **GS fraction** = 2^{(floor((n-1)/2) - m)/2} → 0 as n → ∞

### Level 2: Isomorphism Classes
- **SC classes**: contain at least one GS tiling (straddle the hypotenuse)
- **NS classes**: contain zero GS tilings (live entirely on one side)
- **SC fiber size always odd** (THM-281: involution with odd fixed points)
- **NS classes pair up**: T ↔ T^op, combined tiling count always even

### Level 3: Complement Edges (d=m)
- **Blue lines**: from GS tilings (symmetric across hypotenuse, complement also GS)
- **Black lines**: from non-GS tilings (asymmetric, complement also asymmetric)
- **Blue ⊆ SC-SC** (THM-282: GS tilings can only reach SC classes)
- **SC-NS ⊆ Black** (THM-282: GS tilings can't cross the SC/NS boundary)
- **K ∪ SC² = ALL** (every edge is either black or on the spine)

### Level 4: The Merged Graph G_n/Z_2
- **Backbone (SC nodes)**: odd tiling count, the skeleton
- **Paired (NS nodes)**: even tiling count, the flesh
- **Spine (SC-SC edges)**: carries all color diversity (blue + mixed + some black)
- **Ribs (SC-NS edges)**: uniformly black, bipartite
- **Sea (NS-NS edges)**: uniformly black, dominant at large n

### Level 5: The Odd/Even Dichotomy
The hypotenuse tiles have skip = x-y-1. For x+y = n+1:
- **Odd n**: hypotenuse skips are 1, 3, 5, ... (odd)
- **Even n**: hypotenuse skips are 2, 4, 6, ... (even)

This propagates into: c3-parity bipartition at odd n, spine connectivity vs fragmentation at even n, zero-halo alternation, bilateral asymmetry.
