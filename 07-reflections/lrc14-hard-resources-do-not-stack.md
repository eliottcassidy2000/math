# LRC14 Hard Resources Do Not Stack

codex-2026-06-13

The new Q27 ledger changed the shape of the LRC14 target for me.

The HYP-2444 one-stranger rows looked like the kind of residue family that could become a many-runner wall: shell 27 misses one antipodal class, the stranger lives in that missing/zero class, and the 13-clock closes the trap.  That is the naive scalar view.

The replacement hull says the opposite.  Treat the hard residues as resource atoms and stack them into the 7-core frame.  There are `77520` exact primitive replacement rows, including the row where all eight hard residues are present.  Every one has a Q27 witness.  Plain `q<=27` misses shrink to ten rows: the eight original one-stranger rows plus two small stacked residues, caught immediately at `q=30` and `q=34`.  The only `q=91` rows remain the two original one-stranger packets.

So the packet is real but fragile.  It is not a direction one can keep walking.  It spends at least four currencies at once:

- shell-27 missing/zero antipodal class,
- the 13-clock,
- the 7-core support pattern,
- the marked owner/deletion channel.

Copying the packet forces the 7-core to lose support, and that loss opens lower clocks or divisor addresses faster than the shell obstruction can multiply.

This is the LRC version of the incoming unit-distance lesson.  The Moser/resonant-product work says `27=3^3` is not automatically rich; it is bonus-hostile because the necessary transverse packet cannot live inside the first factor shape.  Here `C=27` is not automatically a wall; the wall packet cannot self-stack inside the first core shape.

The proof now wants a compression theorem:

> If a primitive LRC14 row has no Q27 witness, compress its parity-typed ledger to a hard-replacement row without losing the blockedness data.

But HYP-2463 says there is no such hard-replacement row.  Therefore some step of the compression must fail, and every possible failure is useful: low clock, divisor-fiber witness, descent to AP/Vstar/2AP, or odd owner/Bprime opening.

The new mental picture is not "find the final missing denominator."  It is "prove the hard resources are linearly independent enough that a row cannot hide all their addresses at once."
