## The order-two objects are not the same bit

Three lanes now contain binary-looking data:

```text
LRC:       common overlap M versus right cofiber R along addresses;
FC:        a supplied face-origin colour for a static row selector;
backbone:  the sign cut of the response rows at Q+{4}.                  (2)
```

There is no map identifying the three coordinates.  Their sources are,
respectively, physical carrier pieces, named support faces, and response-row
evaluations.  Their targets and preserved predicates differ as well.  What
transfers is the research move: scalarization erased a transverse coordinate,
and restoring one bit exposed the exact obstruction.

The FC calculation makes the distinction especially sharp.  A genuine
three-way Helly defect does **not** force a three-valued controller.  The
local obstruction arity is three, but both the maximum local partition width
and the minimum fixed origin alphabet are two.  In other words,

```text
obstruction arity != sidecar alphabet size.                              (3)
```

The LRC horn has the analogous warning: three positive vertices make an
allocation word, not a four-state allocation square.  The JC calculation has
the infinitesimal version: two available affine jets eliminate two walls,
not an arbitrary amount of owner multiplicity.

## A genuine beach crossing: FC is an invariant Gaussian lane

THM-3300 supplies an actual map rather than an analogy.  For
independent standard complex Gaussians,

```text
x_i^a  |-->  |z_i|^(2a),       L_fac(x^alpha)=E prod_i |z_i|^(2alpha_i),
```

and the spherical moment map pushes to the uniform simplex measure.  Thus the
homogeneous factorial class is the `U(1)^n`-invariant subalgebra of the
Gaussian moment problem in `2n` real variables.  The map preserves every
factorial moment and homogeneous simplex average.  It destroys angular phase;
the needed sidecar is precisely the torus-weight decomposition.

That sidecar is hostile in two complementary ways.  On the invariant class,
the angular projection is the identity, so THM-3290's pole/coefficient
extraction has nothing to kill.  Off the invariant class, THM-3301 proves that
an infinite-order character kills both `L(P^m)` and every fixed `L(QP^m)`
eventually, so a pure eigenvector satisfies the Mathieu conclusion instead of
refuting it.  Finite simplex symmetry is weaker still: for the cyclic order-3
character it leaves all multiples of three, and THM-3300 finite-exactly
excludes the degree `1,2,3` cyclic families on `Delta_2`.

The resulting search rule is exact but narrow: a factorial counterexample
cannot be obtained by porting the known angular-pole mechanism, and a
Mathieu-style counterexample cannot be a single infinite-order isotypic
component.  The cheapest positive probe is to take a known mixed-weight GMC
family, solve exactly for its torus-invariant parameter locus, and then test
the surviving simplex moments.  Failure by an empty invariant locus is useful;
it does not touch `FC(3)`, `HFC(3)`, or `SFC(3)`.

## A literal analytic representative of the selector cut

The state

```text
B_*=Q+{4}
```

is simultaneously a maximizing witness on every THM-3277 backbone edge.  On
all twelve core rows its response signs are exactly THM-3278's selector
bipartition:

```text
positive={2,11,16,18,22},
negative={3,7,10,13,17,19,21}.                         (4)
```

Positive primitive integral row weights can normalize `(4)` to contribution
`+2M` on the positive class and `-M` on the negative class.  Every one of the
22 core edges then has the same positive margin `M`, and the full twelve-row
blend has margin `3M`.  This supplies an analytic representative of the cut,
not a phase character or physical walk.

The conceptual gain is that the selector bit is no longer only a combinatorial
colouring: it is visible as the sign of one exact physical response state.
The conceptual boundary is equally useful: every actual reset-link transition
from the witness states ends at `Q`, so the dominance arrows themselves are
not chronological transitions.

## Closed forms after retaining the witness

Projecting a lifted path to the row graph asks a nonlinear existence question.
Retaining the reset-link witness instead gives a small finite automaton on
active pairs `(row,state)`.  THM-3288 proves:

```text

phase backbone                 18                 32
selected tree                  23                 40
full core                      26                 68.                    (5)
```

If `a_n` counts witness-decorated walks of length `n`, the backbone sequence
begins

```text

```

and satisfies the exact parity recurrence

```text
a_n=16a_(n-2)-94a_(n-4)+244a_(n-6)-263a_(n-8)+93a_(n-10).               (6)
```

The selected-tree and full-core sequences have analogous lag-14 parity
recurrences.  Exact Hankel ranks prove minimal scalar orders `10,14,15`; the
extra full-core order is a zero-eigenvalue `n=0` atom, while its rational tail
has denominator degree `14`.  Thus the practical closed form is a rational
generating function or a sum over the roots of the displayed characteristic
factors, not a high-dimensional matrix power.

The finite-exact
[selector half-transfer scout](selector-paired-half-transfer-observer-quotient-codex-20260803.md)
now explains that parity structurally.  The exact selector splits are
`13+5`, `14+9`, and `14+12`, and in each case

```text
A=[0 X; X^T 0],                 A^2=diag(XX^T,X^TX).
```

The selector bit is only a parity carrier, not a two-state quotient: vertices
on the same side have different degrees.  Stable equitable refinement gives
`11/14/16` cells, while the all-ones observer orders remain `10/14/15`.
Their even/odd half-orders are `5+5`, `7+7`, and `8+7`.  In the full core the
order mismatch is the explicit kernel vector `h=p_C(A^2)1`, with

```text

zero spectral mass=87/113.
```

Thus the degree-14 rational tail and order-15 initial observer are two views
of one zero-mode head, not a recurrence-fitting anomaly.

Equation `(6)` counts static witness-decorated walks.  It does not repair the
chronological loss in `(1)`.

## Two squaring laws, one boundary debt

THM-3302 supplies an unrelated but illuminating closed-form doubling law.  Its
epoch coordinate satisfies

```text
p^R+q^R=L_R(pq),       L_(2R)=L_R^2-2(pq)^R,
```

and two exact witnesses close the `R=64` floor profile, attaining the stated
golden rate through `n=127`.  The all-`R` and `R=128` statements remain open.
Naive iteration fails because the doubling transport does not preserve witness
slimness; the explicit middle-pair term and a slimming sidecar are load-bearing.

The selector half-transfer and Lucas doubling are not the same objects, but
they expose the same procedural hazard.  Squaring makes the scalar closed form
shorter while hiding a boundary defect: the zero-mode head `h` for the walk
observer, and the `-2(pq)^R` correction plus witness slimness for the epoch.
The cheapest decisive test after any proposed doubling formula is therefore to
transport its boundary vector/correction class, not only verify the scalar
identity.  This is a two-thread synthesis rule, not yet a promoted meta-pattern.

## Ranked research board after the cheap probes

These are partial results and next tests, not theorem dependencies.  Each is
staged so that its first failure remains informative.

1. **First-normal endpoint horn.**  Map the THM-3285 horn into pairs
   `(endpoint origin, THM-2813 normal-lift label)`.  Preserve the six semantic
   gates, ancestry, and raw allocation square; discard higher normal jets and
   arbitrary new couplings.  Census `13*169` states in each exact field, first
   requiring outer co-support and a nonzero raw mixed face, and only then run
   affine covariance.  Normal label zero must reproduce the present hostile;
   `tau=12` and THM-2820's exclusive cells are additional controls.

2. **Jacobian section degeneracy.**  The two rational controls pass the first
   test: the raw linear row has exactly the owner boundary as content,
   `gcd(a,H)=1`, and `y=-b/a` kills both gradients modulo `H`.  Next compute
   the affine-parameter resultant of `a` with `H` and keep the homogeneous
   selector when `a=0`.  Owner content, repeated `H`, and PRS unit changes
   remain hostiles; no result for all affine parameters is being inferred.

3. **Close the move-decorated carrier.**  On the `94` inherited states the
   unique binary colouring survives and all internal edges are acyclic, but
   selected moves can exit the carrier.  Enlarge only to the exact closure of
   those exits while retaining active-face changes and the reset potential.
   The full-domain `(16,17)` failure and the minimal incompatible-reset state
   are controls.  Success would still be a finite controller, not `FC(3)`.

4. **Compile graph operations to rational series.**  The selector half-transfer
   now explains the three present sequences.  Next add one relation edge or
   one active witness fibre at a time and update the equitable/Krylov observer,
   carrying the active-start vector and zero-mode head explicitly.  The path
   `2-17-16` and selector-only lumping must remain negative controls.

5. **Torus-invariant mixed-weight sieve.**  Take one known GMC counterexample
   family with several weights, solve its exact parameter locus inside the
   `U(1)^n`-invariant subalgebra, and test the surviving simplex moments.  An
   empty locus is a structural stop; a pure eigenvector is rejected in advance
   by THM-3301.  Neither outcome alone decides `FC(3)` or `HFC(3)`.

The common procedural rule is now:

```text
before adding more parameters, identify the smallest typed compatibility
object that the current scalar quotient erased; then measure its Helly width,
section obstruction, or jet budget with a hostile control.               (7)
```

Only after `(7)` survives should one ask for dynamics, positivity, or a global
open-problem consequence.
