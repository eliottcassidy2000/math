# LRC14 Regular Solids and Tiling Recursion Carrier

Stub for S141.  The target is to test whether Platonic/Archimedean/Johnson
solids and the three Euclidean regular tilings contribute a useful labelled
recursion grammar for LRC14.

The main guardrail is inherited from HYP-2942: a geometric carrier may be
branch-local and still useful.  It should not be treated as a global atlas
unless its preserved unit is declared.
