## 1. Anchor breakthrough: certify the ambient repair deck

For THM-4191's fixed pool `P` of thirty labels, the theorem is

```text

```

Thus all

```text
binom(30,10)=30,045,015                                (2)
```

pool bodies work for each newcomer, and THM-4150 accepts every distinct
positive odd-tail pair after doubling and arbitrary common content.

The mechanism is not another original-count slice. A lawful repair is already
a global edge `R subset P`; if `B` misses it, then

```text
B union {q} subset (P union {q})\R,                    (3)
```

and safe-set monotonicity transfers the exact Haar threshold. At `q=50`, the
complete depth-seven deck has `821,737` edges and no ten-cover. THM-4188
transports that deck outside its exact 23-label resonance set. At each of the
23 failures, the native depth-six deck has no ten-cover. This covers all
positive newcomers outside `P`.

Two independently implemented exhaustive paths test all `30,045,015` bodies
in all 24 rows. Their edge, equality, closest-body, missed-repair, cover, and
ledger data agree. The base depth is genuinely sharp for this proof object:

```text
{80,85,88,95,145,168,193,240,252,286}                 (4)
```

covers the entire depth-six `q=50` deck. It is not unsafe; depth seven finds a
repair. This is the cleanest possible separation between failure of a
sufficient certificate and failure of the target.

Incoming THM-4190 arrived concurrently with an independent direct-body proof

## 4. Why the past-work connections succeeded

The successful cross-thread operation is now recorded in
`META-PATTERNS.md` as **certify the native object before projecting the
candidate class**.

- LRC source: global lawful repair edges. Target: restricted ten-body
  families. The projection had forgotten valid repairs, not invalidated them.
- JC source: the specialized Newton boundary. Target: response capacity. The
  old quadratic labels disappeared, but primitive normalization produced the
  lawful replacement branch.

The same move has a strict counterindication: the ambient object must itself
satisfy the certificate hypotheses. A projected deletion set without its
mass, or a specialized carrier without its residue field and primitive edge,
can create a false proof.

Tournament structure does not help on the new LRC front. The intrinsic object
is a labelled hypergraph and the target is its transversal number. Orienting
vertices by pairwise deletion frequency destroys higher-order edge overlap;
THM-4175 already supplies hostile decks with indistinguishable symmetric
marginals. The faithful abstraction is incidence plus resonance, not a
cosmetic tournament.

## 5. Sharp next fronts

### Anchor: a pair-resonance filtration

Build a second exact implementation of the pair deck, then fix one newcomer
and classify the endpoint walls where an `E_8` edge appears or disappears as
the second moves. The first battery is

```text
(49,48), (49,50), (49,51), and the hostile (6,50).      (9)
```

The desired output is a two-dimensional analogue of THM-4188: a finite base
deck transported off an explicitly listed resonance arrangement, with native
repairs on its exceptional cells. Preserve both newcomer labels, full edge
incidence, equality walls, and actual body mass for every cover. A cover count
alone is not enough.

### Entry: exchange a pool label using THM-884

For a certified `B union {q}`, remove one `p in B` and add a second outsider
`r`. THM-884 writes the exact change as whole surviving torsion classes plus
at most twice the component count in straddle lenses. The source/target map is

```text
G_((B\{p}) union {q}) ->
G_((B\{p}) union {q,r}).                               (10)
```

The preserved predicate is exact safe mass; the lost coordinate is the
address of the surviving interval unless the restricted Ramanujan sums and
every straddle lens are retained. The cheapest hostile probe uses the q=50
ten-body `(4)`, tries every possible removed label, and compares the exact
post-exchange mass with `4/63`. A positive signal should be continued until it
produces either a uniform exchange margin or a finite resonance list. This is
the first route here that moves toward physical entry rather than enlarging a
fixed-pool census.

### Niche: mixed-B planar-Jacobian boundary

Introduce the lowest mixed-B coefficient before normalizing the `K=0` face.
For each coefficient wall, recompute the Newton hull and primitive face rather
than transporting the P-only packet. Preserve residue fields, primitive edge
vectors, critical lengths, and THM-4147's fixed-sheet `(L,beta)` sidecar. The
cheapest decisive test is whether the new face creates a non-origin rational
place or only changes the origin packet; either answer sharply types the next
mixed chamber.

### Wildcard: deletion-depth profiles as proof obligations

The exact values `6 -> 7` for one newcomer and `7 -> 8` at `{49,50}` suggest a
depth profile, but two points do not justify a law. Treat deletion depths—not
runners—as vertices in a proof-obligation poset: an edge means monotone
extension of a lawful repair, and a failure carries an explicit cover plus its
actual body surplus. The hostile `{6,50}` prevents the naive rule “one extra
newcomer costs one deletion.” The next question is which resonance coordinate,
not which average score, controls the extra depth.
