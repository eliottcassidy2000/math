# The full circle: it was always the odd cycle

*mac-mini-2026-06-29-S33. The owner asked to look back at every frame we ever called fundamental and find the truth we were aiming at. klein-S14 found it as a number. This is the same truth read as an object — and the object turns out to be the one we started with.*

## Seventeen frames, one mountain

We kept declaring a new fundamental object and then superseding it. The odd-cycle collection formula. The covering reduction. The gap M-value. The floor R'. The continued-fraction cut. Anti-Littlewood. Γ₀(N) and the finite Siegel transform. CV(H)~2/n. The obstruction-as-measure. Relations-not-things. Essential × bounded. The Z₇ cyclotomic Gram. The cusp of X₀(14). The even-graph dual. The genus and the cusp form. Blue equals SC. The density of states. Seventeen lenses, each announced as the key, each refined or absorbed by the next.

klein-S14 named the convergence as a number: the floor is the positivity of one cyclotomic atom, `4cos²(3π/7) = 0.198`, sitting at the apex-7 cusp, surrounded by the bulk floor `3/π²`; the frames are a monotone zoom-in localizing that single atom. That is exactly right, and it is the value. But a value is a measurement of something, and the question the owner asked — *the truth we were aiming at* — is what the something **is**.

## The atom is an odd cycle

It is a cycle. Not metaphorically — literally. In S31 I found that `4cos²(3π/7) = 2 + λ_min(A(C₇))`: the binding number is the bottom of the seven-cycle's adjacency spectrum, shifted by two. The doublet that binds the cusp is a two-residue set, and a two-residue set is a single difference `d`, and a single difference is the Cayley graph `Cay(Z₇, ±d)` — the **7-cycle**. So the atom klein localized is the spectral floor of one odd-length cycle, `C₇`, the minimal even graph on the apex prime.

And once you see it as a cycle, every frame is the same cycle wearing a different coat. The metagraph's only invariant — the one quadratic mode that survives the quotient, THM-588's Fiedler eigenfunction — is the **3-cycle**, the minimal cyclicity. The relational frame's essentiality — the danger relation refusing to factor `f(v)g(t)` — is exactly **non-bipartiteness**, the presence of an **odd cycle** (the bilinear `vt`, the anti-Littlewood obstruction, is what closes the cycle). The genus, klein's "1-dimensional obstruction at N=14," is the **multiplicity** of the cycle: `genus(X₀(2p)) = 0,0,1,2,2`, the number of independent odd-cycle modes the boundary data cannot kill, and at N=14 it is exactly **one**. The cusp form `f_14` is that one odd cycle as a modular object. The blue/SC spine is where it sits. The density of states is its statistics. Every frame measures the one odd cycle, and the value of the measurement is `4cos²(3π/7)`.

## The full circle

Here is what makes it the *truth* and not just another frame: the object we converged on is the object we **began** with. This project is, at its root, the study of the **Odd-Cycle Collection** formula — `H(T) = I(Ω, 2)`, where `Ω` is the set of directed odd cycles of a tournament. Frame 1. The very first declared fundamental object was a collection of odd cycles. Seventeen frames and three months later, the Lonely Runner obstruction at n=14 collapsed to a *single* odd cycle, `C₇`, whose spectral floor is the whole question. We did not wander away from odd cycles and stumble onto something new. We took the parent theory's central object, watched it fragment across measure theory, modular curves, equivariant topology, cyclotomic spectra — and watched it reassemble, at the binding cusp, into one irreducible odd cycle. The journey was learning that the collection had collapsed to a singleton.

That is why N=14 is the first hard case. At smaller apex primes the genus is zero — no global odd cycle, the boundary (bulk, Eisenstein, `3/π²`, the Euler product) determines everything, and a local certificate closes the problem. At N=14 the genus jumps to one: there is, for the first time, **one** odd cycle that is genuinely global — not visible from the bulk, not a coboundary, an essential mode. LRC14 is the conjecture's first encounter with a single irreducible odd cycle, and the entire proof is two statements about it: it **exists** (essential — the relation is non-bipartite, the cycle is there) and its **spectrum is positive** (bounded — `4cos²(3π/7) > 0`, it does not sink to the `gap = 0` disproof boundary at the full `Z₇`). Essential times bounded is just: the odd cycle is present, and it is not degenerate.

## The small confirmations

Two open tasks closed this session, and both are the same `R` (the complement involution, the engine that splits cut from cycle, even from odd). The rib theorem — every self-complementary class has even SC-NS degree — is nothing but the fact that an involution-automorphism pairs the non-fixed neighbors of a fixed vertex; the SC classes are `R`-fixed, their NS-neighbors come in complement-pairs, so the count is even. It is `R` detecting the cycle space again, in miniature. And the honest non-result: the NS-side parity break at tournament-size n=6 is *not* the genus jump at LRC-modulus N=14 — different index variables, the n=4 metagraph that carries X₀(14)'s cusps is fixed across the whole genus family, so the genus cannot live there. They share only the root, `R`. I record that as a non-connection rather than force the pattern; the persistence test (klein's recurring discipline) kills it.

The truth we were aiming at was the simplest thing we knew at the start, seen finally as one object: an odd cycle. The collection became a singleton, the singleton became a number, and the number is the bottom of its spectrum. `4cos²(3π/7)`, and it is positive.
