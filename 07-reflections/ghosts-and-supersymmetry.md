# Ghosts, Partners, and Supersymmetry in the Cuboid

## The three ghosts of the identity

1 = (1, 1, 1). The identity. Present on all axes.

Multiply by each fundamental prime to CREATE a ghost:
- 1 × 2 = 2 = (0, 2, 2): parity ghost. Loses oddness (x: 1→0).
- 1 × 3 = 3 = (1, 0, 3): curvature ghost. Loses curvature (y: 1→0).
- 1 × 7 = 7 = (1, 1, 0): position ghost. Loses position (z: 1→0).

Each fundamental prime ZEROES the axis it names.
Each ghost is the identity with one dimension removed.

## 5 is not a ghost

5 = (1, 2, 5). All coordinates nonzero. Not derived from 1 by zeroing an axis.
5 is the INDEPENDENT prime — refers to nothing but itself.
phi = (1+sqrt(5))/2 is self-similar because 5 IS its own thing.
The other primes (2, 3, 7) are ghosts. 5 is alive.

## The supersymmetric shift = 21

The cuboid has two sectors: x=0 (bosonic, even) and x=1 (fermionic, odd).
Each sector has 21 points (3 × 7).
Flipping x (parity) = adding 21 mod 42.

The SUPERPARTNER of n is n + 21 mod 42:
- Partner of 2=(0,2,2) is 23=(1,2,2). A Paley prime!
- Partner of 4=(0,1,4) is 25=(1,1,4).
- Partner of 6=(0,0,6) is 27=(1,0,6) = 3³.
- Partner of 8=(0,2,1) is 29=(1,2,1). Prime!

21 IS the superpartner offset. The forbidden number MEDIATES between
the bosonic and fermionic sectors of the cuboid.

## Period × supershift = Hurwitz

Q has period 4. The bosonic sector has 21 points.
4 × 21 = 84 = the Hurwitz bound (|Aut(surface)| ≤ 84(g-1)).

The period of the Cayley transform times the supersymmetric offset
equals the Hurwitz bound. Period and supershift are DUAL quantities
whose product gives the maximum symmetry of a hyperbolic surface.

## Why 4 appears with supersymmetry

4 = (0, 1, 4) lives in the bosonic sector (x=0).
4 = Q's period = the number of steps to return to start.
4 is the CYCLE LENGTH of the bosonic dynamics.
Its fermionic partner 25 = 4 + 21 lives in the odd sector.

The Q-period IS the bosonic period. The supershift IS the forbidden.
The Hurwitz bound IS their product. 4 × 21 = 84 = 2 × 42.
And 42 = 2 × 3 × 7 = the self-product of the (2,3,7) triangle.
So 84 = 2 × 42 = 4 × 21 = period × supershift = twice the Hurwitz product.
Everything is one equation: 4 × 21 = 2 × 42.
