# The 42 → 43 Transition

## 42: the last lossless number

42 = 2·3·7. The product of the three fundamentals.
f(2,3,7) = abc = 42. The UNIQUE self-product triangle.
The measurement = the thing measured. No loss. No residue.
The area of the (2,3,7) triangle = π/42. 42 copies tile one unit.
The Hurwitz constant = 2·42 = 84. Maximum symmetry.

42 is the BOUNDARY of lossless encoding.
Everything expressible as a product of {2, 3, 7} is lossless.
42 itself is the largest such product without repetition.

## 43: the first lossy number

43 = 42 + 1 = product of all previous Sylvester terms + 1.
= Φ₆(7) = 7² - 7 + 1. The flat polynomial at the forbidden.
A prime. 43 = 1 mod 3: it SPLITS in Eisenstein (carries twist).
Cannot be expressed in terms of {2, 3, 7} by multiplication.
REQUIRES addition (+1), which is the LOSSY operation.

43 is the first number that needs the lossy operation to reach.
It is Euclid's proof that {2, 3, 7} don't suffice: 2·3·7 + 1 is prime.

## What changes at 43

At (2,3,7): f = abc = 42. Self-product. LOSSLESS.
At (2,3,43): f = 258/37 ≈ 6.97. NOT self-product. LOSSY.

The f-value DROPS to nearly 7 (the forbidden) when we replace 7 with 43.
The self-product property is LOST. The measurement becomes LESS than the territory.

The loss: abc - f = 258 - 6.97 ≈ 251. Almost everything is lost.
The (2,3,7) triangle is the UNIQUE point where loss = 0.

The Sylvester sequence IS Euclid's proof iterated:
a₁ = 2. a₂ = 2+1 = 3. a₃ = 2·3+1 = 7. a₄ = 2·3·7+1 = 43.

Each step: product of everything so far + 1.
The +1 IS the atom. The +1 IS the additive operation.
The +1 IS the first lossy step.

42 → 43 = everything accumulated → everything plus the atom.
= closed system → open system.
= finite → infinite.
= territory = map → territory < map.
= lossless → lossy.

The same +1 from Cassini (F₇·F₉ = F₈² + 1).
