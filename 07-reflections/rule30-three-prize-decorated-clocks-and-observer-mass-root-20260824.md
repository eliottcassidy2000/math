## 4. The decorated zero clock

For `ell_r(t)=a_t(-t+r)`, one period of the two lower drivers acts by

```text
x |-> Mx+C,
M=product(1+ell_(r-1)),                               (2)
```

so a nonzero preceding word resets, while a zero preceding word yields either
an odd-weight toggle or an even-weight identity. The physical bank gives

```text
z_n:       2, 7, 28, 399, 53207, 58286, 87866
successor: D, D,  D,   D,     I,     I,     D
phase:     0, 0,  0,   1,     0,     1,     0.       (3)
```

`D/I` mean doubling/identity. The initial `28=T(7)` coincidence is therefore
not a recurrence. The cheapest hostile extension found two identity events
before the next doubling and forced the missing phase bit into the state.
The honest natural-number representation is the rank `n`; the evaluator is
the decorated record `(z_n,type,phase)`. This is exactly the distinction
between calling the `n`th selected odd square “`n`” and retaining its square
value whenever a later operation consumes magnitude.

## 5. Three exact prize frontiers

### Nonperiodicity

The seeded `T`-periodic candidate at onset `N` compiles to a left-boundary
word. If `e` is the first failed relation `c_(e+T)!=c_e`, its first one is
exactly at depth `e+T`. Thus the prize becomes a uniform finite-witness
problem. Independently, an eventual period makes positive dyadic wrap lengths
bounded by `T` and constant on each late `m mod ord_u(2)` class. The missing
object in either route is uniform production, not another finite mismatch.

### Balance

Prefix balance is exactly

```text
A_m=max_(t<=2^m)|sum_(u<t)(2c_(2^m+u)-1)|=o(2^m).    (4)
```

For an observer, `V=length-2E` bounds signed discrepancy. This suggests one
concrete theorem. The forced word already gives `A_m>=r_m-1`, so balance
forces the wrap length to be sublinear; THM-3493's exact ledger then makes
wrapped depths density zero and hard depths density one. Delete the
target-visible prefix of length at most `r_m+K-1` and group the rest by the genuinely coarse owner
`U_m(r) mod 2^K`. It remains to prove sublinear maximal conditional variation
for one fixed `K>=2`, equivalently via the canonical label-aware causal
pairing. A label-blind physical pairing would explain the cancellation.
The exact center digit is `bit_L(B_m(r)+U_m(r))`, not `bit_L(U_m(r))`; the
minimal omitted-base carry is the inherited `n=6` hostile. Naive scale
induction then fails for a different reason: the Haar child divides a sibling
sum by `2^d_m`, so it needs the quotient carry, and a target-blind physical
collision already occurs at `m=3,K=2`. The square-root-like finite values
through `m=17` are signal only.

The incoming marked-cylinder observer gives another precise balance target:
`z_k>r` is exactly the occurrence of `1 0^(2r-2)` on the relevant half arc,
and radius `8` already fails at `k=79883`. Its Haar-random spatial frequency
does not control the deterministic temporal orbit. The next bridge is therefore
the single-seed cylinder discrepancy `D_r(N)`, jointly with the sign observable
`Q_k(-1)`, first at fixed `r` and then uniformly for growing `r`.

### Query complexity

The official wording does not select among no-`o(n)`, eventual `Omega(n)`, no
`O(n)`, and shell-worst-case formulations. The easy sparse-flip sequence

```text
x_n=(n mod 2) XOR 1_{n is a positive power of 2}      (5)
```

is balanced and `O(log n)`-computable but has right-special contexts of
length `P/2-1` before positions `P` and `2P-2`. Hence state count and history
depth cannot prove the prize. A valid attack needs a declared uniform model
and a locality, communication, perturbation, or crossing-sequence theorem.
