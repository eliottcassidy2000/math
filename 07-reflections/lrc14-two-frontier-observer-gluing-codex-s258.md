# LRC14 Two-Frontier Observer Gluing

The useful way to keep moving is to make two frontier obligations disappoint
each other productively.

First frontier: the polynomial-method witness route.  HYP-3096 says the
paper's `I(13,p,1)` enumeration should be replaced by a continuous witness
route: lower-bound lonely measure, upper-bound component count, then get a
largest-arc floor and a finite denominator-net budget.

Second frontier: the Pascal/equivalence/scissors route.  HYP-3097 says the
numbers `1001,2002,3003,4004` are not decoration; they are row-14/Pascal and
pair-mass shadows.  HYP-3092 makes the clean cap a pair-normalized Pascal
mass, while HYP-3093 warns that equal shadows are useless unless the scissors
fiber survives.

The S258 scout puts the two routes on the same table.

The witness route makes progress on fixed rows: `12->84`, `12->168`, K33
`12->36`, and `P10+K33` all have exact direct largest arcs and therefore
finite denominator-net thresholds.  But divisor-loaded `B=6` already pushes
the direct threshold to `5881`.  That is the old Conjecture 7.1 failure in a
new form: direct time is a chart, not an invariant.  The normalized ruler/apex
coordinate must be glued in.

The Pascal route makes progress in the opposite direction.  The cap chart is
pure pair mass through `j<=3`, then the first defect is exactly `1/4004`, one
affine pair-mass unit.  But pair mass does not know whether a positive packet
is nested-refinement covering work or K33 cross-handoff debt.  So the branch
chart must be glued in.

The hidden structure is the overlap map.  Each chart is allowed to forget
something only when another chart pays for that loss.  Direct arc charts forget
apex normalization.  Pascal charts forget branch handoff.  Branch charts forget
denominator-net topology.  Moment charts forget endpoint scissors unless the
pair/S3/S4 data is retained.  The proof is waiting for a small sheaf condition:
every local quotient has to be reconstructible, dual-annihilated,
fiber-constant, descended, or explicitly routed to named debt.

Next concrete move: build `lrc14_observer_gluing_ledger` rows over the
THM-573 residual.  The first rows should carry direct component data,
denominator threshold, cap defect, pair-mass unit, grid class, active binders,
endpoint owners, and a named overlap-failure chart.
