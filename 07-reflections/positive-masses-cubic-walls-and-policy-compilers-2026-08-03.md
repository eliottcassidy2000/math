## 1. Outcomes

Three precise objects emerged.

1. **Positive LRC mass is not the remaining rank obstruction.**
   [THM-3253](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3253-positive-owner-mass-newton-cyclicity-and-maximal-common-heisenberg-module.md)
   compactifies THM-3246's 168 strictly positive cell masses by zero on the
   Singer plane.  For every dilation g>=1, every one of the 8,064 primitive
   Singer relabellings gives a nonsingular 13 by 13 matrix.  The proof reduces
   to 84 degree-26 determinant polynomials, checks the exact head through
   g=57, and uses one-sign Newton differences from the sharp common base
   g=58.  The associated nonnegative central-slice packet spans the maximal
   common H_13 module of dimension 2,041.

2. **An ambiently inert Jacobian term becomes live on the first wall.**
   [THM-3257](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3257-degree-eight-tuned-cubic-infinity-wall-and-three-root-critical-escape.md)
   enlarges THM-3237's clutch from 1+t*x^9 to
   1+s*x^8+t*x^9.  Degree eight still cannot create the generic infinity
   face.  On the degree-nine wall, however, s linearly tunes the next
   strict-transform coefficient.  One exact value cancels it, exposes a
   nonzero cubic carry, drops the saturated critical resultant from degree
   55 to 52, and produces three cube-root branches with one C3 monodromy
   cycle.  Fifty-two squarefree boundary-disjoint critical points remain.

3. **A small FC chart policy coexists with a maximal operation trace.**
   [THM-3256](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3256-optimal-two-row-threshold-policy-injective-signed-trace-and-factored-distance-enumerator.md)
   compiles THM-3244's row-2/row-10 selector into one axis-threshold tree of
   optimal depth 5 and optimal leaf count 15.  Yet every Q-monotone compiler
   retaining signed coordinate edits has 4,319 distinct continuation words,
   because its signed Parikh vector is Q-n.  After commutative forgetting,
   the exact distance sequence has a short product formula.

The advances are complementary.  THM-3253 pays an algebraic rank bill but
leaves physical semantics.  THM-3257 creates a sharper local bifurcation but
leaves Keller chart entry.  THM-3256 makes one adaptive policy explicit but
proves that its physical trace cannot share the same compression.

Concurrent integration sharpens the FC boundary.  THM-3254 now proves that
all 31 covering pairs have a two-state fixed-blend clutch, with a graded
no-go for 23.  Promoted THM-3249 transports the adaptive
row-2/row-10 atlas to the neighboring support-(1,2) face and finds 24 common
covering pairs, while ruling out one constant positive cross-face gauge.
It does not yet transport the optimal threshold tree or compress the signed
continuation trace.

## 4. LRC: positivity and cyclic rank now coexist

For the THM-3246 lane, let I_(g,j)>0 be the 168 cell masses and place them on
the punctured Singer plane, with zero at the completion point.  THM-3253
proves

~~~text
det A_g^(a,b) != 0
for every g>=1 and every primitive (a,b),               (1)
~~~

not only for the signed second corrector.  This matters because three
suspected obstructions now separate cleanly:

~~~text
positive coefficients:                  passed;
charged multiplicity rank:              passed;
maximal common neutral participation:   passed;
physical equivariant realization:       open.           (2)
~~~

The determinant proof is itself reusable.  Clearing denominators makes every
matrix entry quadratic in g, hence every determinant has degree at most 26.
Symmetry reduces 8,064 gauges to 84 polynomials.  For a base r,

~~~text
P(r+n)=sum_(k=0)^26 Delta^k P(r) binom(n,k).             (3)
~~~

At r=58 all 27 differences have one strict sign for each polynomial.
The head g=1,...,57 is exact and load-bearing: sixteen classes genuinely
change sign, and one changes between 57 and 58.  Positivity of matrix entries
does not imply determinant positivity; the Newton basis certifies
nonvanishing without making that false inference.

## 5. Planar Jacobian: recompute minimality on every strict transform

THM-3237's ambient degree ledger says degree nine is first in
1+t*x^d.  The two-parameter calculation asks a different question:

~~~text
B_(s,t)=1+s*x^8+t*x^9.                                  (5)
~~~

Let a=2/Gamma and let r1,r2,r3 be the normalized top coefficients of V.
On the primitive wall t=a, the degree-98 coefficient vanishes for every s,
while the degree-97 coefficient is

~~~text
-2*a^11*(a*(4r2-3r1^2)+4s*r1).                         (6)
~~~

Thus the previously inert degree-eight term is the exact normal coordinate
on the exceptional divisor.  At its unique zero s=s_*, the next carry is

~~~text
-a^12*(r1^4+16r1*r3-16r2^2-8r1)/r1,                   (7)
~~~

which is nonzero in both accessory fields.  With w=1/x and delta=t-a,

~~~text
w^3 ~ -16*r1*delta/(a*Rho).                             (8)
~~~

The local monodromy is one 3-cycle.

This is not merely one more parameter calculation.  It changes the search
procedure:

~~~text

  does not imply
strict-transform face-minimality.                       (9)
~~~

A lower-degree term can be invisible before division and decisive after it.
The same procedure already appears in the factorial PRS tower of THM-3233:
retain the transverse parameter, divide the exceptional multiplicity, and
reclassify the surviving response state.

## 7. Two Newton methods, one procedural lesson

The word Newton appears in two genuinely different tools:

| use | LRC THM-3253 | Jacobian THM-3257 |
|---|---|---|
| basis | forward differences and binomial coefficients | lower hull of a reciprocal local polynomial |
| variable | integer dilation g | transverse wall parameter and w=1/x |
| conclusion | determinant nonvanishing for all g | number and monodromy of escaping roots |
| hostile | determinant sign changes before base 58 | 52 critical points remain after escape |

They should not be identified.  Their shared procedural lesson is narrower:
choose coordinates adapted to the operation after restriction.  In the LRC
tail, the shifted Newton basis makes every coefficient one-signed.  On the
Jacobian wall, the reciprocal Newton edge exposes the first nonzero normal
carry.  In both cases the original monomial basis hid the decisive
certificate.
