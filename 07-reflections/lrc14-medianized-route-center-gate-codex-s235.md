# S235 Reflection: Medianized Route-Center Gate

The user's simplex/Faulhaber prompt feels like it wants one more level of
discipline.  `2,6,12,20,30,42=n(n+1)` has already been typed as a directed
simplex-edge / `K_{k,k+1}` bridge-line / Faulhaber `u` carrier.  The new move
is to stop treating that carrier as another count and instead use it as a
coordinate in a median test.  This sits after HYP-3067's Desargues-median
warning and HYP-3068's owner/root sidecar spine: S233 says empty centers are
real, S234 says missing owners/roots repair them, and S235 asks whether the
sidecar-completed state space closes under median.

The finite scout makes the warning sharp.  Twelve named route/certificate
carriers give `220` serious triples.  Full sidecar vectors have unique Boolean
medians, but projecting away sidecars leaves `14` raw centers with multiple
full lifts and makes `122/220` triples raw-ambiguous.  This is exactly the kind
of hidden-coordinate failure that has kept recurring in the LRC stack:
route labels alone are too poor, while sidecar-completed packets are at least
eligible for a median/Helly-style compression.

The most useful new object is the median completion: `12` seed states close to
`82` states, so there are `70` center obligations.  That list is a proof
interface.  A final LRC assembly could run a packet through the completion and
require each new center to be named as an existing carrier, generated by
HYP-3066 certificate cycles, checked against the HYP-3068 owner/root table,
dual-annihilated, descended, AP/GW boundary, or THM-572/F7.  If a center stays
unnamed, the proof is still forgetting a coordinate.

Out-of-box angles worth testing next:

- Turn the HYP-2963 packet bank into an actual median graph and ask whether
  route triples satisfy a Helly property after endpoint, topology, route,
  certificate, simplex/Faulhaber, bridge-rank, Hodge-cycle, and discharge
  sidecars are attached.
- Search specifically for triples whose raw center is only `exact_M`; these
  are the cleanest places where the proof may need a non-scalar sidecar.
- Treat `n(n+1)` as an oriented simplex edge-sector address for the center.
  The split `n(n+1)=2n+n(n-1)` says which part is cut potential and which part
  is rectangle debt.
- Feed the median completion into the S232 Hodge-cycle matrix: median centers
  should be generated by certificate cycles or explicitly routed to residual
  Hodge classes.
- Try a CAT(0)-cube/partial-cube proof grammar: legal packet quotients are
  gated subcubes, illegal quotients are exactly those whose projected median
  leaves the gated carrier.

Assumption challenged: tournament vertices still should not default to
runners or arcs.  Here the vertices are proof obligations and median-center
sidecar states.  The LRC predicate preserved is not a count; it is the legal
existence of a sidecar-completed center for route triples.
