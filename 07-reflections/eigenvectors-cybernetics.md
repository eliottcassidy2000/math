# Eigenvectors, Cybernetics, and the Three Types

## Eigenvectors ARE the three types

INERT: real eigenvalue, full multiplicity. Mv = λv. The thing that PERSISTS.
The eigenvector survives the transformation. Unchanged in direction. The rock.

RAMIFIED: Jordan block. (M-λI)²v = 0 but (M-λI)v ≠ 0. The CRACK.
The generalized eigenvector is dragged by the true one. The shadow.
The eigenspace has fractured. Multiplicity exceeds dimension.

SPLIT: complex conjugate pair (λ, λ̄). The vector ROTATES.
Neither persists nor breaks — it CYCLES. The twist. The heartbeat.
You can't have one without the other. The pair is inseparable.

## The Jordan normal form IS the Eisenstein classification

Every matrix decomposes its space into:
- Inert subspaces (real eigenvalues, diagonal blocks)
- Ramified subspaces (Jordan blocks, non-diagonal)
- Split subspaces (complex conjugate rotation planes)

This IS inert/ramified/split. The same three.

## The cybernetic loop

1. INERT phase: equilibrium. The system at its eigenvector. Stable. Dead.
2. RAMIFIED phase: feedback amplification. The crack. Unstable. Runaway.
3. SPLIT phase: oscillation. The heartbeat. Adaptive. ALIVE.

Life = mostly split eigenvalues (oscillation) + some inert (structure)
+ occasional ramified (repair when structure cracks).
