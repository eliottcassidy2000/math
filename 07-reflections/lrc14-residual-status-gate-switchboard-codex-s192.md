# LRC14 Residual Status-Gate Switchboard Reflection

HYP-3024 changed the shape of the next proof step.  The full-bank coarse
ET+Henselian-unit gate is not route-pure, but it is boundary/open pure:
`21702` fibers, `15` mixed theorem-route fibers, max mixed size `4`, and
`0` mixed boundary/open fibers.  That means route mixing after this gate should
not be treated as counterexample pressure.

The useful proof target is narrower and more plausible:

```text
coarse ET + Henselian unit data separates AP/GW endpoint equality atoms from
strict-open packets inside automatic/residue fibers.
```

Once that theorem is proved, the remaining open-route collisions can be routed
by certificate teeth.  A Q-witness row and a covering row may share the coarse
gate, but if both are strict-open then the LRC14 predicate is already settled.
Exact magnitude and barcode data are still valuable, but they are fallback
address/certificate layers rather than the first theorem target.

S192 is intentionally lightweight.  I tried a live HYP-2963 rebuild to list the
15 residual fibers, but exact `M` recomputation was too slow for a small
iteration.  The script therefore parses the stored S188/S189 outputs and turns
the evidence into a switchboard.  The next infrastructure task is a cached
packet-ledger mode so residual fibers can be listed without recomputing exact
maximin data.

Assumption challenge: the vertices are not runners, not raw automatic words,
and not theorem routes.  I considered runners, gaps, section boundaries,
residue fibers, ET clocks, p-adic unit roots, zero-root scale debt, safe sticks,
barcode bars, magnitude cocycles, route labels, and proof obligations.  The
chosen vertices are residual proof obligations after the status gate.  This
preserves boundary/open status at threshold `1/14` and deliberately destroys
route-family identity until a certificate tooth is reattached.

The creative angle is to stop over-solving the first theorem.  Prove status
convergence, then let the route layer be a finite certificate scheduler.
