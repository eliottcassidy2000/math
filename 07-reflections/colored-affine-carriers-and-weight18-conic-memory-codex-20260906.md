## 2. What the natural-number model actually preserves

For complete rank-one support, [THM-4425](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4425-lrc14-all-height-rank-one-carrier-closure.md)
proves the lossless dictionary

```text
Lambda(w)={k u: |k|<B, 3 does not divide k}.
```

After choosing an orientation, the positive address is just an ordinary
natural number with every third value deleted. Replacing the `n`th admissible
integer, the `n`th odd square, or any other injective ordinal label by `n` is
harmless if the decoder and direction `u` remain attached. The proof does not
use the size of the label as a geometric magnitude.

The same encoding ceases to be a state in rank two. The exact width theorem
gives a primitive dual coordinate

```text
(coset sign, layer t, longitudinal address k),
|t|<(3/14)mu_1.
```

This tuple can of course be Cantor-paired into one natural number, but doing
so is only a renaming. Quotienting to `k`, `t`, or total carrier count loses
the additive circuit and changes the projection consumer. The first dense
example makes this visible:

```text
(19,23,29): Lambda=+/-{u,v,u+v}.
```

A tournament picture is legitimate only after declaring a pairwise
observable. Here the intrinsic observable between primitive directions is
the signed determinant multiplier in `u cross v=k w`. Parallel directions
are genuine ties, and central inversion is an orientation gauge. Forcing all
ties into arrows would erase the rank-one/multi-ray distinction. The useful
analogy with a rational edge and its reciprocal is therefore the sign reversal
`C -> -C`; no Stern--Brocot reflection is being asserted as an LRC symmetry.

## 4. No-three-in-line became a colored midpoint theorem

The incoming no-three-in-line probe succeeds only because the LRC dictionary
is a **complete** convex lattice section of two owner cosets. If two carriers
have the same owner color and the same class modulo `2 Lambda_w`, their
midpoint is integral, stays inside every strict roof, and keeps the owner
color. A complete dictionary with no collinear triple therefore has at most

```text
2 owner colors * 4 parity classes = 8 carriers.
```

The audited finite head and trivial large-height cap then give a sharp strict
network gap `204/5957` at `(23,29,37)`. A selected cap subset does not inherit
this theorem: `(1,11,23)` shows that deleting the compulsory midpoint changes
the source object.

For larger dictionaries the same argument forces same-color arithmetic
progressions. Extreme endpoint pairing gives an optimal disjoint Jensen
curvature payment, but an infinite two-line family `(1,m,2m+3)` has zero
curvature in every coordinate while retaining a positive affine deficit.
These rows are count-safe. Thus curvature is not the missing theorem; the
needed coordinate is the affine baseline along each colored line.

Three small circuits now prevent a cosmetic `A_2` model:

```text
(19,23,29): origin-based A_2 hexagon, dense;
(41,47,49): three positive directions in an affine AP, u+z=2v, sparse;
(85,97,107): two-shell A_2 equality, A=P union (-2P), sparse.
```

The correct rank-two object is a colored affine circuit with primitive
coefficients and offsets, not an unlabeled hexagon.

The colored-lattice descent now makes this precise for complete bodies with
exactly three directions: some two live rays form a basis of the index-three
owner lattice, and the third is, up to gauge, only `u+v` or `-2u+v`. The
reciprocal determinant bound then closes every such network at all heights.
The arithmetic-progression hostile was therefore signal: it supplied the
second normal form rather than refuting a finite classification.

The bare address still fails as a state. Address 37 also labels the

