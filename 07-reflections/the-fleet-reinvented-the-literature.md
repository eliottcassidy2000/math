# The fleet reinvented the literature — and where the exact beats the asymptotic

*klein-2026-07-11-S253, after the owner-directed concept search (Kravitz 1912.06034; Fan–Sun
2306.10417; Giri–Kravitz MPCPS 180 (2026) 343; Perarnau–Serra survey 2409.20160).*

Four of the LRC(14) program's central discoveries turn out to have direct published ancestors —
found independently, in each case with the fleet's version SHARPER on the axis that matters here:

1. **The pair-sum ruler theorem (mac-mini THM-668)** — "all witnesses live at times m/(vᵢ+vⱼ)" —
   is Kravitz's Proposition 2.1 (2019), used as the standard frame by Fan–Sun. The fleet's
   version adds what the literature never needed: the EXHAUSTIVE per-family certificate search
   over these times (the clean-ruler ledgers), because our goal is a per-instance decidable
   pipeline, not an asymptotic bound.

2. **The doubling-tight singleton (mac-mini THM-709)** — only 12→24 stays tight among
   AP[e→2e] at n=13 — is the m=2 slice of Goddyn–Wong's own acceleration criterion (their
   Theorem 12: r→mr tight iff r shares a factor with every element of [(n+1−r), m(n+1−r)−1];
   at n=13 only r=12, m=2 passes). The fleet's addition: the ESCAPE VALUES (1/11, 2/23, …
   exact) for every failing acceleration — the literature says "not tight", the ledger says
   by how much.

3. **The far-element decorrelation limits (THM-687/688/700/710)** — two-scale families
   converging to product/subgroup values with C/V rates — are the concrete instances of
   Giri–Kravitz's subtorus hierarchy: acc(S(n)) ⊆ S₂(n), with multiset-density EQUALITY
   den(S_mult(n)) = S₂(n) = S*(n−1). Their theorem says limits of n-runner values are
   2-dimensional-subgroup values; the fleet's transfer operators say it with explicit rates
   and eigenvalues ((7−r)/7 per factorial moment). Their "geometry of cubes" machinery is a
   candidate citation source for the limit lemmas in final write-ups.

4. **The m₃/triple instrument (the k=8 deg-3 tail)** — the one remaining analytic item of the
   moment ladder — is the same object as Tao's dimension-3 Bohr set estimates (his
   log n/(n² log log n) improvement runs through Pr(A_{v1}∩A_{v2}∩A_{v3})). The fleet
   needs it per-family at n=13 rather than asymptotically in n — but the technique transfer
   is the obvious next read.

And one thing the literature settles NEGATIVELY, validating a design decision made under fire:
**there is no citable margin above 1/14.** Kravitz's spectrum conjecture (which would give the
gap 2/27 − 1/14 = 1/378 above the tight value) is proven only for n ≤ 3 and is FALSE as
originally stated for n = 4, 6, 7 (Fan–Sun's k=2 families); the amended version remains open.
Every fleet route that tried to run a margin/averaging argument through the tight locus died
there (nine confirmations of the signed-cancellation law), and the eventual architecture —
exact τ = 1/14 dispatch for non-covering families, covering filter excluding the tight locus,
margin arguments only inside the covering case — is exactly what the state of the literature
FORCES. We did not choose the margin-free boundary treatment; the problem did.

The meta-pattern: a swarm doing exact, exhaustive, per-instance mathematics on a single n
reconstructs the field's structural theorems as side effects — and where it goes beyond them
is always the same direction: from asymptotic/qualitative to exact/quantitative/decidable.
The literature's role in the endgame is citations and technique transfer (Tao's Bohr-3 for
m₃), not new obstacles: nothing found contradicts or supersedes any fleet theorem.
