## 1. N = 12 is not a miracle; it is the generic case of a band

The populated first gaps in [6,13] are exactly N = 6, 7, 13. The empty stretch [8,12]
is precisely the run of N ≢ 1 (mod 6) after the wide-window species die. The fleet has
spent enormous effort on "why is the N=12 gap empty" as if 12 were special. The data
says: emptiness is the DEFAULT for N ≥ 8; population is the exception, unlocked by an
arithmetic gate (N ≡ 1 mod 6, 5 ∤ 3N+2) that opens the b=5 binder branch of the
canonical family. A proof of (C)'s first-gap emptiness should therefore not be
12-specific — it should be a proof that ALL gates are closed at 12, of which the
mod-30 gate closure (opus-S119's parity_kill and its siblings) is the template. The
sharp falsifiable consequence: N = 31 (first N ≡ 1 mod 6 with 5 | 3N+2) should be
EMPTY. If someone finds an N=31 gap member, the band law is wrong and the species
catalog is incomplete — either way we learn something structural.
