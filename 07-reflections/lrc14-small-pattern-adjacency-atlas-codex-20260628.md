## Pattern Notes

The `2-row skyline` pattern is structural: AP and doubled AP are the all-bank
equality face, and primitive normalization leaves AP.  This is not just a
sequence coincidence; it is a normal-form warning for every certificate.

The `11/12` trap pattern should become the next finite table.  The number 12
appears as local maxima, source witnesses, and chart sectors, but those are
different objects.  The right use is to label fibers: 11 non-AP traps plus AP,
then ask which dictionary coordinate first fails on each non-AP trap.

The `1/7` pattern is a named obstruction, not a scalar law.  It survives as
the apex-zero / analytic-equidistribution warning after HYP-3200 refuted the
exact associator ratio.

The `1,4,1` and `1,11,11,1` Worpitzky rows are useful because they carry
ordered edge-packet payload.  They should not be collapsed into a generic
Eulerian analogy.

The doubled triangular sequence `2,6,12,20,30,42` is still mostly a sidecar.
To make it proof-facing, the next user of it must specify the partial-cube,
simplex, or bridge-rank coordinate it preserves.

The `E/O legs` and `V7 double root` signals are among the best sidecars: they
connect Chebyshev, de Moivre, Hermite-Biehler, and Cohn-Elkies language to a
candidate dual certificate, but they still need the LRC off-circle defect and
analytic equidistribution guardrails attached.

## Tournament Analysis

I used motif families and payload atoms as tournament vertices.  Other
possible vertices were runners, arcs, edge tip-tail observers, fixed circle
sections, section boundaries, wall-crossing events, residues, cover arcs,
Fourier modes, matroid circuits, and proof obligations.  The chosen quotient
preserves the predicate "this motif preserves or repairs a named LRC payload."
It destroys source identity, raw incidence counts, and fine witness fibers.

The pairwise observable was payload-retention after quotienting.  The switch
oriented toward motifs that keep more of the HYP-3224/HYP-3205 dictionary while
still respecting HYP-3221's analytic-equidistribution obligation.  The induced
tournament was transitive: one Hamiltonian path and no directed 3-cycles.  So
the tournament is best read as a disciplined ledger order, not as a discovered
cycle obstruction.

## Assumption Challenge

The challenged assumption is that the small pattern itself is the insight.
The better rule is:

```text
small pattern + preserved coordinate + destroyed coordinate + repair sidecar
```

Controlled forgetting fits here.  A k-depth perspective, edge perspective,
cycle perspective, or cut payload is admissible when it makes the forgotten
coordinate explicit.  Otherwise it is just another scalarization.  This also
explains why the raw famous-problem motif is a sink: the name can carry energy
for brainstorming, but it carries no proof payload until it names its LRC
predicate and its loss function.
