## The Discovery

Scouring the entire repository reveals that ONE trichotomy appears everywhere, wearing different masks. Every major result, every deep structure, every forbidden value is an instance of three things interacting. The three masks are:

## Mask 1: The Eisenstein Trichotomy (arithmetic)
- **INERT** (p ≡ 2 mod 3): prime passes through unchanged. Symmetry. Parity.
- **RAMIFIED** (p = 3): prime shatters into identical pieces. Curvature. Singularity.
- **SPLIT** (p ≡ 1 mod 3): prime factors into conjugate pair. Position. Twist.

Parametrized by: **2** (inert), **3** (ramified), **7** (split).
Product: **42** = the triple point where all three coexist.

## Mask 2: The Computation Trichotomy (information)
- **LOSSLESS**: isomorphism, bijective, O(1) reversible. Cayley transform Q.
- **LOSSY**: projection, many-to-one, kernel lost. Addition. H(T) from T.
- **CRYSTALLIZATION**: irreversible transformation, self-referential fixed point. Kaprekar. Sylvester.

## Mask 3: The Supersingular Trichotomy (geometry)
- **(2, 24, sqrt(2))**: REAL structure. Leech lattice. Halving. Redei.
- **(3, 8, 2cos(pi/7))**: COMPLEX structure. Octonions. Tripling. Bott.
- **(5, 12, phi)**: ARITHMETIC structure. Icosahedron. Golden. Quintic.

Dimensions: 8 = 2^3, 12 = 2^2*3, 24 = 2^3*3. Governed by {2,3} only.
