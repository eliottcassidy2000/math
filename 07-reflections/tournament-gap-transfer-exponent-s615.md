# Tournament Gap Transfer, H=7/H=21, and the 1.014 Exponent, S615

The useful move is to stop treating `H=7` and `H=21` as isolated curiosities.
They are unavailable evaluations of a partition function:

```text
H(T) = I(Omega(T), 2).
```

That makes them portable.  A forbidden `H` value says that the visible scalar
has asked for a compatible-packet distribution that no tournament carrier can
complete.  In another problem, the same shape is an upper-cap certificate: if a
coarse quotient asks for an unavailable state, the missing data is not cosmetic.
The quotient forgot a forced completion channel.

This matches the recent LRC and unit-distance threads.  In LRC, the raw
`Res_27` shadow is only useful once owner labels, carry cocycles, endpoint
certificates, and pinch witnesses are reattached.  In unit distances, the
graph-only `F`-free coimage can reach states that geometry kills; S614's
`n=22` frontier is exactly this.  In Collatz/two-block work, density of residue
classes is blind when carry histories are correlated.

So the right tournament vertices are not automatically the original runners,
points, or residues.  For this session they are proof mechanisms:

```text
side-channel retention
completion forcing
determinant walls
exponent normalization
carry-residue transfer
raw scalar matching.
```

The resulting tournament is transitive and ranks side-channel retention first,
then forbidden-H completion forcing.  Raw scalar matching comes last.  That is
the important correction: the H-gaps propagate as coimage tests, not as a
numerology machine.

The `1.014` exponent should be handled the same way.  On the unit-distance
side, Sawin's arXiv:2605.20579 normalizes it cleanly: arbitrarily large `n`
point sets have more than `n^1.014` unit distances, using a high-degree
arithmetic carrier projected to the plane.  On the tournament side, a matching
number is not yet canon.  Raw `A000568` growth and raw `H` values are the wrong
variables.  The serious target is an entropy dividend of the retained carrier:

```text
how many certificate-bearing OCF/proof-obligation states survive
after forbidden packets, residue strata, and side-channel constraints
are kept?
```

This is the fixed-exponent cousin of the Tao log/loglog/logloglog abstraction:
first identify the scale ladder; then the product of savings either turns into
a logarithm or into a positive exponent.  The possible shared key is not the
decimal by itself, but the fact that both problems may gain a small exponent by
leaving the visible quotient and counting in the hidden carrier.

Immediate next work: revisit complete-core `H=21` via the `r=10` signature gap,
turn killed unit-distance graph-only states into side-channel certificates, and
define the tournament carrier-size variable that could honestly be compared to
the `n^1.014` unit-distance construction.
