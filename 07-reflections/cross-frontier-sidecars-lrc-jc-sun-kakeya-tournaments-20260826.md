canonical endpoints are:

  visible `j=0` quotient lattice has degree in `4Z`, while the two possible
  carrier responses are `34` and `42`. A counterexample in the stated gate
  must therefore lie on the proper countable hidden-`E_0` locus of the
  `Phi_12` fourfold and still meet the node-annihilation condition. This is a
  squeeze inside one seam, not an exclusion of that chamber or planar JC.

The shared lesson is precise: a quotient becomes useful only after naming
the coordinate it destroys and the sidecar that restores the target
predicate.

## 3. Exact companion observations

The maintained companion program proves the following finite statements.
They are audit sidecars, not dependencies for THM-4229, THM-4234, or THM-4230.

### 3.1 A compatible local Sun solution is still not an integer solution

THM-4027 implies a reflection-local corollary: for every prime `p`, the Sun
equation has a compatible solution in `Z_p^4`. At `p=2` use the permutation
`t -> C(2t,2)`; at `p=3,5,7` use the digit-permutation coordinate
`q -> C(pq+1,p+1)`; and at `p>8` lift a regular triangular coordinate with
`2w-1` a unit. Their product gives a solution in `hat(Z)^4`, although
THM-4026 proves that the prescribed positive integer fibre is empty.

The finite control constructs three levels at `2,3,5,7,11,13` and combines
them by the exact Pascal periods. It solves the equation modulo
`27081081027000`; its exact difference from the target is a nonzero multiple
whose quotient has `366` bits. This is a hostile for any claim that a
compatible inverse system automatically provides a height-bounded witness.
If this all-prime corollary is ever used as a proof dependency, it should be
promoted separately from this reflection.

### 3.2 Projectivizing the AP tail loses exactly one phase bit

For THM-4035's affine two-moment packet, the map

```text
r -> (A_1(r),A_2(r)) -> [A_1(r):A_2(r)]
```

has `59`, not `60`, images. Its unique collision is

```text
r=18: (21/10,24/5),
r=25: (5/2,40/7)=(25/21)(21/10,24/5).
```

Both have ratio `16/7`, while their full AP owner laws differ. Absolute
`A_1`, the full owner/track signature, or one exceptional-fibre bit repairs
this particular quotient. No claim is made that projectivization is the
native LRC dilation quotient.

### 3.3 Additive Fourier broadness and multiplicative CM primitivity are independent

For a character set `Phi`, the program compares its multiplicative unit
stabilizer with maximal Fourier minors at auxiliary primes:

- the nonprimitive order-seven type `{1,2,4}` has no zero maximal minor over
  `F_29`;
- the primitive `M=11` type `{4,5,8,9,10}` has respectively
  `22,11,0,0` zero minors at `23,67,89,199`;
- the primitive `M=13` type `{5,6,9,10,11,12}` has respectively
  `26,26,0,13` zero minors at `53,79,131,157`.

Thus neither condition implies the other, and Fourier broadness changes with
the auxiliary prime. This is a cheap degeneration screen, not a replacement
for the CM-simplicity, integral-Hom, component, or degree arguments in the JC
theorems.

### 3.4 The natural cyclotomic prime is a lift-sensitive bad-reduction diagnostic

For

```text
F_m(S,P)=1-A S P^m-B S^3 P^(m-1),
```

a torus critical point can occur only in characteristic dividing `2m+1`.
When `p=2m+1` is prime, every unit pair `(A,B)` has exactly one torus
critical point over `F_p`.

The residual census uses this mandatory convention:

> `A,B,S,P` are represented by their least nonnegative integers, the
> coefficients are held fixed at those lifts, and
> `rho=F_m(S,P)/p mod p` is computed from that integral polynomial.

Changing a coefficient lift can change `rho`; the residual histogram is not
an invariant of the bare `F_p` coefficient pair. Under the displayed
convention, `10` of the `100` unit pairs at `(m,p)=(5,11)` and `11` of the
`144` pairs at `(6,13)` have zero residual. On `A+B=0`, the lift/nonlift
splits are `1/9` and `0/12`. At `A=B=1`, the unique critical points are
`(4,7)` and `(8,3)`, with residuals `5` and `4`; neither lifts modulo `p^2`.

At a singular point, Taylor expansion makes all `p^2` variable lifts survive
or none survive. These rows diagnose an integral bad fibre. They do not
exclude a complex JC coefficient row, determine a Hom group, or replace the
good-reduction and regular-model ledgers.

## 5. The fixed-50 petal-pair graph is `K_12`; next is a typed hypergraph

Let

```text
O=P\C={10,15,20,30,60,63,132,170,176,190,264,290}.
```

For a pair `{p,p'}`, the first genuinely new bodies are

```text
{p,p'} union L,       L in binom(C,7).
```

There are `binom(18,7)=31,824` per pair and
`binom(12,2)*31,824=2,100,384` profiles in total. THM-4234 checks every edge:
the compatibility graph is exactly `K_12`. The relation is symmetric, with
no intrinsic orientation, so even after completion it is not a tournament.

At triple rank, every body containing at most two petals inherits a completed
edge. The new lane is

```text
{p,p',p''} union L,       L in binom(C,6).
```

All `220*18,564=4,084,080` limiting profiles are strict, uniformly closing
the tail `r>=589`. Only `{132,176,264}` has its finite remainder exhausted.
The live object is therefore a three-uniform hypergraph with two predicates:

```text
cofinal edge:     strict base reserve plus certified cutoff;
universal edge:   cofinal edge plus exhaustive finite remainder.
```

Its cofinal hypergraph is complete, while the proved universal subhypergraph
currently contains one displayed edge. Collapsing those predicates would
turn a tail certificate into a false finite-entry claim. Each hyperedge must
retain minimum surplus, component maximum, cutoff, and, when present, its
closest literal wall/body.
