# LRC(14) S73 synthesis: grid sampling, pair towers, and the parallel-class carrier

## What is already closed

The local-density block-gluing algebra is not the present gap.
`LRCLocalDensityBlockGluing.three_block_gluing` proves the product-minus-debt
recurrence, and the same module kernel-checks both the published and sharpened
three-block ledgers, their positive margins, the fixed-scale two-block ledger,
and the rational-circle component bounds.  What it does not supply is a
universal partition of every residual thirteen-speed packet into admissible
blocks.  The surviving block-side obligation is the partition trichotomy and
its inherited-window hypotheses, not the gluing inequality.

The consumer end of the live route is also closed.  `LRCLiveCountLonely` turns
one positive exact `liveCount` into a literal `Lonely 14` witness.  The incoming
`LRCLiveFloorSampling` layer inserts the missing conditional interface before
that consumer: a separated family of open intervals contained in the strict
safe set contributes at least

```text
q * (sum of interval lengths) - (number of intervals)
```

to the exact live count, and its `liveFloorModulus` chooses an explicit positive
modulus once an interval-length floor and endpoint budget are supplied.  This
does not manufacture the intervals.  The strict safe-component decomposition,
equality of its total length with the chosen safe measure, and the
`2*sum_i |v_i|` component cap remain the geometric producer in THM-984.

The consecutive-comb exits are proof-facing now: the c7 and c8 measure
theorems both yield literal `Fin 7` and `Fin 8` witnesses.  They are local
blocks, not an implicit completion of the other five or six speeds.

## Zarankiewicz values: where they help and where they do not

On the three-row parallel-class circle the static bound is already sharp:
`z(3,g;2,1)=g`.  Equality is the saturated class partition, so static overlap
has no slack with which to choose the required phase.  This explains why the
selected-witness crux is cyclic timing rather than another incidence count.

The repository kernel-proves

```text
z(m,13;2,2) = 27,30,33,37  for m=6,7,8,9.
```

The same collision identity gives the next exact values

```text
z(10,13;2,2)=40,
z(11,13;2,2)=44,
z(12,13;2,2)=48,
z(13,13;2,2)=52.
```

For right degrees `d_x`, K2,2-freeness gives

```text
sum_x choose(d_x,2) <= choose(m,2),
3*d_x <= 6 + choose(d_x,2).
```

Summing proves the upper bounds `44,48,52` for `m=11,12,13`.  They are
attained by restricting the thirteen-line incidence graph of the projective
plane of order three; the cyclic Singer blocks are the translates of
`{0,1,3,9}` modulo thirteen.

At `m=10`, the same inequality gives at most `41`.  Equality would force two
right degrees four, eleven degrees three, and codegree exactly one for every
left pair.  Let `A,B` be the two four-blocks.  They intersect in at most one
point.  The remaining eleven triples would triangle-decompose
`K_10 \ (K_A union K_B)`.  If `A,B` are disjoint, the two outside vertices
have odd residual degree nine.  If they meet once, their common vertex has
odd residual degree three and the three outside vertices have degree nine.
Both contradict the even vertex degrees of a triangle decomposition.  Hence
`41` is impossible, while ten projective-plane lines attain `40`.

These values bound distinct support incidences.  They do not count multiplier
activity and therefore do not by themselves pay the B5 depth ledger.

## Tau-five: the faithful rectangle carrier

The exact primitive-ratio cover at tau5 has 272 vertices and 861 edges.  Its
raw graph has 3,440 four-cycles and maximum common-neighbor count thirteen, so
a K2,2-free Zarankiewicz compression is false.  The reciprocal `<1`/`>1`
bipartition has 287 cross edges and 264 cross rectangles; even that cut is not
K2,2-free.

The useful compression keeps the two missing diagonals of each rectangle.
The 264 cross rectangles form 132 reciprocal orbits, with diagonal census

```text
neither diagonal: 240
left only:          12
right only:         12
both diagonals:      0.
```

Thus the exact finite statement needed by a median anchor is

```text
cross K2,2  ->  not (left diagonal and right diagonal).
```

A five-runner clique, anchored at its median absolute speed, would give two
ratios below one and two above one.  Its six quotient adjacencies would be a
cross rectangle together with both within-side diagonals, contradicting that
finite statement.  This carrier is smaller than a full local-neighborhood
K4 audit and is faithful to the target.  By contrast, orienting ratios by the
ordinary rational order produces a transitive tournament: its cycle and SCC
fingerprints are trivial and it forgets the allowed-ratio predicate.
