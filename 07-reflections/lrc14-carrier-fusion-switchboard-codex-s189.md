## Creative Carrier Upgrade

The largest new carrier is the safe-stick / safe-body field.  In the
one-dimensional LRC circle, the "large stick" is the largest strict safe
component and the "potato" is the full regular-open safe body.  This is useful
because it gives a geometric certificate anchor while preserving the exact
barcode and not pretending to replace arithmetic packet labels.

The 2-adic/Hurwitz and Moser/fibbinary data remain sidecars.  Their role is to
label fibers and transitions, not to certify loneliness by themselves.

After the S188 update, the most promising compression inside the fused packet
is the coarse ET/Henselian status gate: it may replace exact magnitude for the
boundary/open predicate while keeping the magnitude cocycle as the route-purity
fallback for the remaining open collisions.
