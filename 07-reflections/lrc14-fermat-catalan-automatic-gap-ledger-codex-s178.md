# LRC14, Automatic Gaps, And Power-Lift Guards

The useful synthesis is not "Fermat-Catalan proves LRC14."  It is stranger and
more practical: the Fermat-Catalan / Moser / fibbinary / gap-theorem cluster
tells us which coordinates a quotient is not allowed to forget.

Moser-de Bruijn is the most concrete piece.  Its numbers are exactly the
integers whose binary ones sit in even positions, and every integer has a
unique `x+2y` representation with `x,y` in the sequence.  That is a carry-free
square channel.  Fibbinary numbers are wider: no adjacent binary ones, matching
the Zeckendorf/path-independent-set normal form.  So Moser is not just another
sequence shadow; it is a strict sublanguage inside the Zeckendorf carrier.

The scout makes the warning visible.  AP13 and Goddyn-Wong `12->24` have the
same automatic word:

```text
MFCMMCCFFFCCC
```

So the automatic shadow alone cannot separate the two equality atoms.  But K33
`12->36`, C27 petals, covering tails, and a Res_27 probe do start to separate
by automaton word, lacunary tail ratio, and perfect-power status.  That is the
right level of use: not proof, but packet stratification.

The Fermat-Catalan side should be read negatively.  In the LRC unit-excess
family `p/(14p-1)`, the small stress point `p=2` has `q=27=3^3`; other payload
powers appear at `p=4` and `p=8`.  These are not magic shortcuts.  They are
places where a power lift might hide unless the cyclotomic and p-adic labels
are retained.  A Fermat-Catalan obstruction is a no-lift guard only after the
packet knows which factor, exponent, and residue class it is talking about.

The Ostrowski-Hadamard gap theorem plays the same role for analytic smoothing.
If a residual support becomes Hadamard-lacunary, a Fejer/Haar certificate should
not quietly smooth it away unless the lacunary address is preserved or proved
irrelevant on that packet fiber.  The theorem is not being imported literally;
it is a discipline for not erasing natural-frontier data.

The ACM DOI in the prompt was a useful correction: it is the polygon
"sticks and potatoes" approximation paper, not a Fermat-Catalan source.  For
this LRC pass it contributes only a visibility/approximation guardrail: do not
mistake an approximate largest-safe-region object for the exact lonely witness.

What I like about this angle is its humility.  It does not try to be the proof.
It tightens the proof machine: every sequence shadow must declare its finite
automaton, every gap shadow must declare its address coordinate, and every
power collision must declare whether it is a certified no-lift obstruction or
just a seductive scalar coincidence.
