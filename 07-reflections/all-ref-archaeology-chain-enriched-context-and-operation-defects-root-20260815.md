## 2. Admissible image, boundary compactification, reachable trace

The late-fetched S514 branch exposes a useful categorical distinction that

relabeling, be

```text
P_n^open = {
  p_i in (1/n,1-1/n),
  p_i != p_j,
  p_i-p_j != 1/2
} / S_(n-1).
```

The half-turn comparator gives a geometric image

```text
Phi_n : P_n^open -> A_n^open,
```

whose ordered representatives are Ferrers interval tournaments.  Adding

gamma_v(t) = (v_1 t,...,v_(n-1)t) mod 1,

together with its wall events.  There are therefore three different objects:

```text
formal open image  ->  wall compactification  <-  one arithmetic trace.
```

## 3. Operation-twisted kernel intersections

The all-branch comparison revealed that an exact current theorem already
contains a much broader research move.  Given dependency spaces `K_1,K_2`
and a native operation `T`, form

```text
B_T = K_1 cap T^(-1) K_2.
```

This does not ask merely whether an object has relations.  It asks which
relations survive the next operation.

In [THM-2850](../01-canon/theorems/THM-2850-paid-distinguished-coloop-round-budget-and-slope-grammar-rank-defect.md),
`K_1=K_2=ker(B^T)` and `T=D_rho`; the dimension of the intersection is the
exact slope-compatible bicirculation defect.  The `Cut cap Cycle` bicycle
space in
[THM-2467](../01-canon/theorems/THM-2467-bicycle-spaces-of-the-star-flip-split.md)
is another proved instance.  The proposed ports are:

- **FC:** with moment kernel `K=ker L` and multiplication `M_f`, adjacent
  vanishing means `f^m` lies in `K cap M_f^(-1)K`; all powers demand orbit
  containment, not isolated membership.
- **Rule 30:** use intersections of observer preimages under the actual
  nonlinear update and moving observer.  Do not linearize the cellular
  automaton by analogy.
- **LRC:** intersect phase/relation channels with the preimage under a lawful
  owner or chart transition, retaining the event-current sidecar.
- **Tournaments/Hadamard:** intersect response kernels before and after a
  deletion, join, or toggle.

Contract: the construction preserves simultaneous dependency before and
after `T`; it loses integrality, positivity, ownership, and later iterates.
Those require a coefficient lattice, effective cone, legal operation grammar,
and observer schedule.  Its advantage over the full continuation congruence
is cost: it is a quantitative one-operation obstruction that can kill a lane
before building an infinite automaton.

## 4. Topology / grammar / coefficient defect

THM-2850 proves, for its incidence compiler, an exact decomposition of the
form

```text
defect A(rho) = delta(B) + gamma_L + kappa(rho).
```

- `delta(B)` is a topology or matroid-union obstruction;
- `gamma_L` is generic loss forced by the legal coefficient-sharing language
  `L`; and
- `kappa(rho)` is exceptional cancellation at a special coefficient point.

This trichotomy is underrecognized because a raw rank, determinant, support,
or kernel dimension merges all three mechanisms.  The cheapest hostile is a
triangle: two independent slope coordinates can pass the topological
two-forest test, while one shared slope makes `[B | rho B]` rank deficient.
Topology passes and grammar fails.

Connection contract:

- **Source:** a support/incidence object plus its legal parameter variety.
- **Target:** a three-coordinate defect invoice rather than a scalar rank.
- **Map:** compute generic rank on the unrestricted topology, then on the
  legal coefficient grammar, then the special-point maximal-minor ideal.
- **Preserved:** the mechanism causing failure.
- **Destroyed:** positivity and physical realization unless added explicitly.
- **Needed sidecar:** legal parameter variety, restricted minor ideal, and
  effective cone.
- **Tests:** use the shared-slope triangle; then classify FC monomial support
  versus symmetry-forced coefficient ties versus exceptional cancellation;
  apply the same split to JC terminal monomials and Hadamard toggle supports.

For frontier management this changes the question from “why is the matrix
singular?” to “did topology, allowed syntax, or a special coefficient do it?”
The three answers require different next moves.

## 5. Two-axis response tower

Historical “first interaction order” work is partly present in
[HYP-3093](../05-knowledge/hypotheses/HYP-3093-equivalence-triad-forgetting-cost-invariant.md), and
current meta-patterns already recommend exteriorizing a lost relation.  The
missing synthesis is to track two independent axes.

For a finite operation set `E`, keep the Boolean response and its Möbius
coefficients

```text
mu(S) = sum_(T subset S) (-1)^(|S|-|T|) F(T).
```

This is the **interaction-arity axis**.  For sequential operations, also keep
iterated word responses or noncommutative marked tensors.  This is the
**operation-order axis**.

The need for both is exact:

- THM-3407 has examples with identical labelled pair data and different
  three-toggle determinants.  Pair truncation loses the first higher
  interaction.
- THM-3380's self-converse `ABBA/BAAB` collision kills the first skew current.
  One exterior order bit is not a complete chronology invariant.

Contract:

- **Source:** the full subset/word response functor for native local
  operations.
- **Target:** the first nonzero coordinate `(interaction arity, word order)`.
- **Map:** Boolean Möbius inversion in the commutative direction and iterated
  marked response in the ordered direction.
- **Preserved:** every response through the retained bidegree.
- **Destroyed:** all higher bidegrees and physical pointing.
- **Needed sidecar:** oriented cycle gain or the first higher signed minor,
  plus source/owner labels where physical realization matters.
- **Tests:** the pair-equal/triple-distinct Hadamard controls and
  `ABBA/BAAB`; for Rule 30 use the exact ternary-intersection controls; for
  LRC compare irreducibly aggregate inclusion-exclusion packets.

This gives tournament H-versus-discrepancy and Q4/non-P work a principled
escalation rule: do not add arbitrary statistics; move one step in the
bigrading until the hostile collision separates or a structural vanishing
law appears.

## 6. Codimension-one boundary descent

The strongest substantial unmerged proof candidate is
`ca7be21b6:01-canon/theorems/THM-3045-fc3-bridge-exact-audit-degree-one-transcendence-and-the-holomorphic-stokes-reduction.md`,
with audit repair at `b8d667fc8`.  Its reusable move is the homogeneous
holomorphic-slice identity

```text
integral_T F dA = (2i)^(-1) integral_(partial T) F(z) conjugate(z) dz,
```

which turns a triangle moment into three algebraic segment integrals.  This
must be scoped to the holomorphic/homogeneous slice.  MISTAKE-350 forbids
promoting it as full FC(3), where arbitrary polynomials are allowed.

Current deletion theorems supply a discrete analogue: a bulk response descends
to an oriented deletion deck.  The general research move is therefore a chain
map

```text
bulk moment or response  -->  signed codimension-one boundary data.
```

Contract: total moment/response is preserved; gluing, orientation, owner, and
chronology are destroyed unless explicitly marked.  The needed sidecars are
signed incidence, boundary parametrizations, initial phase, and face/event
owners.  Test the holomorphic formula against a nonholomorphic polynomial
that fails the primitive gate, and test the tournament deletion analogue on
`ABBA/BAAB` to see precisely which gluing bit is missing.

The immediate opportunities are a genuinely lower-dimensional homogeneous-FC
lane, deletion recurrences for tournament invariants, and an LRC automaton
whose alphabet is made of marked wall events rather than coarse safe/danger
letters.

## 7. Spacetime x valuation/unit-coordinate fibre product

The historical discrete-log program at
`8592b11381abe710565a4618c8534876584118be:01-canon/theorems/THM-445-lrc-momentum-twistor-discrete-log.md`
contains useful finite identities but an invalid global chart.  Discrete log
linearizes multiplication, inversion, and sign on cyclic unit factors; it
does not cover nonunits, and not every prime-power unit group is cyclic.  In
particular `(Z/2^k Z)^x` has no single primitive-root coordinate for `k>=3`.

The repair is a stratified coordinate packet

```text
ValLog_q(v) =
  (p-adic valuation strata,
   coordinates in the invariant factors of each unit group,
   physical event cell,
   owner/phase,
   source/arrival labels,
   exit packet).
```

CRT combines prime components only after their physical and endpoint labels
are retained.  Fibre this arithmetic packet with the finite-mode star/cochain
atlases of
[THM-3395](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3395-small-sheet-typed-cover-star-cochain.md),
[THM-3398](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3398-general-finite-mode-sheet-cover-cochain.md),
and [THM-3405](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3405-common-centre-gcd-gauge-and-boolean-half-twist.md).

This preserves ramification, multiplier action, owner, phase, and physical
entry.  It destroys only chart choice after recording transition data.  The
first controls are `q=27` for unit/nonunit strata, `q=6` for identical-mask
star compatibility, AP/lift for global exit, and the all-`q` denominator
escapes.  The same packet can organize JC signed valuations/effective cones,
unequal-depth Hensel carries, FC coefficient carries, and Rule 30's 2-adic
edge odometer.  Those are typed proposals, not consequences.

THM-3461 supplies a useful provisional gate rather than a solution: its exact
examples preserve literal half-twist masks while independently imposing
`7`- and `13`-adic decorations.  The wedge still vanishes, and the relation
residue, endpoint coefficients, delayed word, and nonconstant twist current
are absent.  The correct order is therefore relation-residue feasibility
first, CRT decoration second, and physical event/current compilation last.

### A. Large-owner CRT `D`-band automaton

Source:

```text

```

with shared `w`, all simultaneous components, owner-labelled endpoints, and

the current residual LRC rows and verify every survivor with the exact

### B. All-divisor margin/load Pareto atlas

Source:
`refs/remotes/fork/claudebox-S710-divisor-lattice@16a39213f77c02333b6d9ccddbb946bdb73c6489:01-canon/theorems/THM-421-lrc-divisor-clock-peeling.md`.

The exact survivor is elementary and strong: at clock `t=b/d`, danger is
exactly divisibility by `d`, and every nonmultiple is at distance at least
`1/d`.  The AP identity

```text
gcd(j,n)-1 = sum_(d|n, d>1) phi(d) [d|j]
```

is also exact.

The branch theorem's “largest divisor is best” conclusion is false.  The
margin `1/d` decreases as `d` grows, and no largest divisor uniformly
minimizes the number of multiples in an arbitrary row.  The repaired object
is the full family

```text
(S_d/d, margin_d, load_d),  d | n,
```

with nondominated margin/load points retained.  Per-runner slack, owner, and
common phase are required sidecars.  The cheapest test is an exact Pareto
atlas over every divisor for each current residual row, followed by a test of
whether one nondominated clock composes with a physical event current.

## Underrecognized current companions

Four mechanisms found in the old-root pass are already integrated on main,
so they are not promoted as forgotten board items.  They nevertheless make
the recovered architecture sharper.

1. **Equivariant fixed-state substitution.**  The map
   `forward/backward/bidirected -> forward/backward/absent` is an exact
   `S_n`-equivariant bijection from trierments to oriented graphs.  It
   preserves isomorphism, automorphism groups, converse monodromy, and the
   tie/absence grading, while destroying the semantic difference between
   equality and no interaction.  The per-orbit Burnside correction is
   recorded in
   [HYP-2078](../05-knowledge/hypotheses/HYP-2078-self-converse-oriented-digraph-burnside.md).
   This imports an exact finite state atlas into any genuine ternary
   comparator, but LRC must add THM-389's strict/boundary flag and tie owner.
2. **Parity can decode an involution's fixed locus.**  For complement-merged
   fixed-path tiling buckets, the current proof-backed
   [HYP-1772](../05-knowledge/hypotheses/HYP-1772-merged-tiling-bucket-parity.md)
   gives odd mass exactly on self-converse classes and twice an odd mass on a
   chiral pair.  Thus a quotient can forget chirality yet retain its fixed
   locus modulo two.  The lesson is constructive: before carrying a symmetry
   sidecar forever, test whether fibre parity or another character recovers
   it.
3. **Lift before symmetrizing.**  A tournament's raw symmetric adjacency part
   is constant, but the conflict graph on directed odd-cycle witnesses is
   nonconstant and satisfies the exact
   [THM-002 OCF](../01-canon/theorems/THM-002-ocf.md)
   identity `H(T)=I(Omega(T),2)`.  The portable move is
   `antisymmetric object -> symmetric witness-incidence lift -> partition
   evaluation`.  Candidate ports should use conflicts among proposed LRC
   escape certificates, Kakeya direction witnesses, or local JC obstruction
   packets, while retaining the witness-to-original incidence map.
4. **Use orbit-incidence mass, not a uniform class count.**  The proved fibre
   part of
   [THM-1430](../01-canon/theorems/THM-1430-the-tiling-class-metagraph-dictionary-and-which-tricks-pay.md)
   assigns tournament class `C` the natural mass
   `H(C)/|Aut(C)|` and gives a reversible quotient flip transport.  This is
   the correct measure for that tiling cube.  It must not be silently equated
   with an arithmetic LRC clock measure, a named Rule 30 orbit, or any other
   dynamics without a measure-preserving map.

## Recommended execution order

1. **Build the 84-cell event-current feasibility model.**  Encode physical
   source, arrival, later-root, owner, phase, endpoint residue, and mixed
   holonomy; implement the compactified exit packet at the same time.  A
   feasible chain gives a new carrier; an infeasible model should return a
   minimal cut certificate.
2. **Intersect the CRT `D`-band automaton with the current valid-configuration
   language.**  Do not count isolated CRT accepts.  Verify physical survivors
   with the exact pair-sum engine.
3. **Compute the divisor margin/load Pareto atlas on the live LRC rows.**  Ask
   whether a nondominated clock composes with the event current; do not select
   the largest divisor by fiat.
4. **Install the kernel-intersection and defect-trichotomy gates.**  Apply them
   first to the two-power FC moment lane and to every new AK/JC rank compiler.
   They are cheap enough to prevent expensive work on a topology-passing but
   grammar-failing model.
5. **Escalate tournament/Hadamard responses by bidegree.**  Start from the
   exact pair/triple and `ABBA/BAAB` hostiles, and compute only the next missing
   interaction/order coordinate.
6. **Audit the homogeneous FC boundary candidate as its own slice theorem.**
   Keep the full-FC scope barrier explicit and run a nonholomorphic hostile
   before any promotion.
7. **Prototype the stratified valuation bundle at `q=27`.**  Use invariant
   factors rather than a nonexistent universal discrete-log chart, then fibre
   it with the physical star/cochain and exit data.

The first three steps form the Anchor lane; steps 4--5 are the Niche; steps
6--7 are the Wildcard.  The board should be revisited after each exact result:
every new feasible carrier, violated cut, CRT survivor, or first nonzero
response coordinate changes which sidecar the other lanes need.

## Bottom line

The repo's forgotten work repeatedly reached the same boundary from different
directions: scalar observables and unpointed quotients are good at certifying
local compatibility, but they erase the physical event that must carry a
proof.  The productive reframe is therefore not “find one stronger scalar.”
It is:

```text
legal future contexts
  + operation-surviving relations
  + a typed physical event chain
  + compactified boundary/exit data
  + the first missing interaction/order layer.
```

That architecture is broad enough to connect the repository's main open
frontiers, yet concrete enough to fail cheaply on the current hostile bank.
Its first payoff should be either a finite LRC source-to-arrival carrier or an
exact obstruction explaining why every currently retained coordinate still
falls short.
