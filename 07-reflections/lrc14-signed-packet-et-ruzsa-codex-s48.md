# LRC14 Signed Packet ET And Ruzsa Models

The useful new distinction is between "packet sign" and "packet size."  THM-546
already says the far runner is controlled in the gapped regime by a convergent
one-dimensional BV/Erdos-Turan estimate.  The remaining rows are not gapped; in
those rows the packet terms can all point the same way.

The exact scout makes that concrete.  The B13 leader, the HYP-2671 dyadic block,
the HYP-2672 doubled-odd tail, and many B14 near-speed leaders all have
`++++++` sector packet words.  Their additive profiles are not random: they are
AP-like, near-AP, dyadic shell-full, or doubled-odd finite models.  Ruzsa
modeling is the right language because the issue is not "large energy implies
danger."  It is "large energy plus the right signed sector address can align
all six packets."

The third-pocket probe is the sanity check.  It has real additive structure,
but its sign word is `++-+--`, and its run-absolute cancellation ratio is
`1171/15473`.  That is the branch a signed Erdos-Turan estimate should be able
to exploit.

The proof target is now sharper:

```text
finite low-growth packet models
  -> classify ++++++

everything else
  -> signed packet cancellation / small mass
```

The Landau and Chebyshev analogies are guardrails.  Landau score sequences
solve low-degree tournament shadows but miss higher compatibility; Chebyshev
oscillation explains above/below-random bias but only after retaining the sign
mode.  LRC14 should do the same: keep packet signs, shell ownership, and
state-word address until the last possible step.

No proof is finished, but this does move the next obligation away from a vague
"discrepancy constant" and toward a finite-model inverse theorem plus a signed
packet estimate outside the inverse image.
