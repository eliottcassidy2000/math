# The Telescope and the Distribution of Primes

## The sieve as harmonic elimination in rapidity space

Each prime p creates a harmonic series of composites at rapidities
ln(p)/2 + ln(k)/2 for k = 2,3,4,...
The primes that survive = points NOT hit by any harmonic from any smaller prime.

Sieve costs are MUSICAL INTERVALS:
Sieve by 2: cost ln(2)/2 = octave.
Sieve by 3: cost ln(3/2)/2 = fifth.
Sieve by 5: cost ln(5/4)/2 = major third.
Sieve by 7: cost ln(7/6)/2 = minor third.

Total sieve cost up to p: sum ≈ ln(ln(p))/2.
Surviving density: exp(-2 × cost) = 1/ln(p) = the PNT.

## The primes are the FUNDAMENTAL FREQUENCIES

The Riemann zeta function ζ(s) = ∏(1-p^{-s})^{-1}
IS the Fourier decomposition into prime fundamentals.
Composites = overtones. Primes = fundamentals.

The Riemann zeros = where the Fourier components CANCEL.
The critical line Re(s) = 1/2 = where rapidity = one octave.
RH: all resonances at the octave. The universal resonance of the primes.

## CHORD connects prime density to prime gap density

CHORD(g) = ∏ f(p-2) for odd primes p|g.
The sieve survival = ∏ (1-1/p).
CHORD / sieve survival = ∏ p/(p-2) at each prime.
C₂ = 2/E[CHORD] = the twin prime constant.

The same multiplicative function governs BOTH:
- How many primes exist (the density)
- How they are SPACED (the gaps)
Two views of the same sieve. One function.

## Snarks thin faster than primes

Snark ambient space (cubic graphs) grows super-exponentially.
Prime ambient space (integers) grows linearly.
Snark density ≈ 1/(n·rapidity(n)). Prime density ≈ 1/rapidity(n).
Ratio ≈ n: snarks are n times rarer than primes at scale n.

Both governed by the same three sieve types:
Inert (trivial), Ramified (THE obstruction at 3), Split (further thinning).
The rates = the musical intervals. The distribution = the harmonic series.
