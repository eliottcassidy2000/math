## From "cannot tile" to "must miss this much, here"

Mirsky-Newman rigidity said distinct-speed danger systems cannot tile the circle -- one
surviving Fourier coefficient at the divisor-minimal frequency. That is a statement about
one global number. The local deviation lemma turns it into a statement about EVERY
neighborhood: test C - A against the divisor-minimal character localized by a Fejer
window narrower than the spectral gap, and the same surviving coefficient forces
int |C - A| K >= sin(2 pi r)/pi at every center. Rigidity becomes a floor with an
address. The corollary is the windowed uncovered bound hpartA's danger case wanted --
and the numerics show the hypothesis is sharp in the most satisfying way: the
consecutive cluster (spectral gap 1) actually DOES nearly cover a window, dropping below
the gap-cluster floor. The obstruction and the escape are the same object seen twice:
gap-less clusters are exactly the AP difference cores, the fixed points of the
renormalization flow, already owned by the tower. The analytic lemma and the dynamical
tower partition the danger case between them with no seam.

## The odd-covering conjecture, read through fair shares

The Erdos-Selfridge conjecture (no incongruent covering system with odd moduli > 1) and
its 22-prime-factor bound in the square-free case look, at first, like our covering
questions. The bridge (mac-mini HYP-3853 now owns its development) has one quantitative
moral worth stating plainly: LRC danger sets are FAIR -- every speed contributes measure
exactly 2r, so covering needs at least 1/(2r) = 7 participants and the fight is pure
overlap-geometry. Congruence classes are UNFAIR -- a class mod q has density 1/q, the
small moduli are rich, and the whole subsidy system leans on the even prime, the richest
of all. Take 2 away and distinct odd moduli must assemble unit density from 1/3 + 1/5 +
1/7 + ... with incongruence taxing every overlap: twenty-two primes before it is even
conceivable. Our own proof structure mirrors this dependency: the 2-adic parity descent
(THM-580) is the LRC proof "spending the even prime" -- and the polygon/DMNR theorem we
formalized is precisely the statement that WITHOUT repetition (congruent classes), exact
covering fails. One family of parity-and-subsidy obstructions: Redei's odd Hamiltonian
count, Kleitman's crossing parity, DMNR's repeated largest modulus, the missing even
prime. The prime 2 is not a number here; it is a resource.
