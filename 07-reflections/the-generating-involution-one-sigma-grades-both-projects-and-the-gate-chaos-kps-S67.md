# The generating involution: one σ grades both projects and the gate-chaos

**kind-pasteur-2026-07-07-S67 (HYP-5027).** The owner asked to abstract once more — to find the
broad structure in the supposed chaos and *the pattern that generates it*. The whole project,
both halves, is generated by **one order-reversing involution σ**, and the "chaos" — the
metagraph's family zoo, the LRC gate zoo — is the ±1 grading of σ. This is the pattern; it has
teeth, because it sorts the fleet's ~15 LRC obligations into two families and says which can
ever close.

## The one generator

**σ = "reverse the order."** On tournaments it is the *converse* (reverse every arc); on
runner families it is the *reversal* `E ↦ (max E + min E) − E` (my S62). It is an involution
on both configuration spaces, and each project is its ±1-graded quotient.

**Tournament side — σ is literally the generating function.** THM-587: the per-level signed
cycle index `P_n(x) = (1/n!) Σ_σ Π_cycles (1 + s_c x^{ℓ_c})` of the σ-action, with the two
antipodal evaluations
`P_n(1) = A000568(n)` (all tournaments) and `P_n(−1) = SC(n)` (σ-fixed = self-converse).
Everything downstream is this grading:
- **σ-odd (parity layer):** Rédei (`H` odd), and its σ-*twisted* refinement **anti-Rédei**
  (`g(C)`, the blue/grid-symmetric count, odd). The whole "blues odd, blacks even" structure
  (my S66, THM-643/644) is this parity.
- **σ-even (fixed layer):** the SC/blue spine — `P_n(−1)`, the transpose-self classes.

**LRC side — σ splits the difficulty.** Verified this session: `μ_{1/7}` and `E[maxgap]` are
σ-**even** (reversal-invariant), while the covering/residue predicates (multiple-of-`q`,
saturation) are σ-**odd** (algebraic). That is not decorative — it is the difficulty axis.

## Anti-Rédei, cleanly, in one involution (the σ-odd tournament instance)

The owner named "anti-Rédei via the ρ-twisted involution." Here is the crisp form (opus-S139
THM-644c and monad-S8 THM-647 reach the same parity by Sylow/fiber routes; this is the direct
single-involution statement, independently verified on all 24 self-converse classes n=3..6):

> Fix any **involutory anti-automorphism** `ρ₀` of a self-converse tournament `T` (it exists:
> `|Aut(T)|` is odd, so the order-2 element of the converse-automorphism group lands outside
> `Aut`). Define on the Hamiltonian paths of `T`
> `τ_{ρ₀}(p₁,…,p_n) = (ρ₀ p_n, ρ₀ p_{n−1}, …, ρ₀ p_1)` — **reverse, then apply ρ₀**. Because
> ρ₀ is an anti-automorphism, τ maps Ham paths to Ham paths, and (ρ₀ involutory) **τ is an
> involution**. Its fixed set is exactly `{P : π_P = ρ₀}` — the ρ₀-anti-symmetric paths —
> which is precisely the **`g(C)` grid-symmetric tilings**. By the involution parity,
> `#Fix(τ_{ρ₀}) ≡ #(all Ham paths) = H(T) ≡ 1 (mod 2)` (classical Rédei). **So `g(C)` is odd.**
> The full `H_anti = g(C)·|Aut|` (opus's fiber law) is then `odd × odd = odd`.

Verified with content: on the Paley `T₅`-type SC class, `H = 15`, `τ_{ρ₀}` is an involution on
the 15 paths with `#Fix = 3 = g(C)` (odd), `H_anti = 15`. One involution, one Rédei parity,
the blue count is odd. This is the *σ-odd* layer of the generator — the tournament twin of the
LRC *σ-odd* covering layer.

## Why the σ-even core is a discrepancy problem (the papers)

Aden-Ali's *Optimal Online Discrepancy Minimization in Linear Time* (2607.04388): assign signs
to keep prefix sums small; the achievable prefix sum is **the sum of three coupled Gaussians**,
discrepancy `O(√log T)`. The LRC σ-even core is exactly a discrepancy statement — `maxgap` is
the largest empty arc, the star-discrepancy complement of the phase set `{frac(e_i x)}` — and
the "**three** coupled" object is the discrete shadow of the **three-gap theorem** (≤ 3 distinct
gap lengths), which the fleet has circled from the start. The other paper (Hernandez et al.,
*Linearity of Relation Decoding*) is the abstraction lesson in miniature: a chaotic relational
computation is well-approximated by **one linear map**. Same shape as this note — a chaotic gate
zoo is generated by **one involution**. The σ-even core wants the discrepancy/three-gap
machinery; the σ-odd layer never will.
