## The Surviving Lemma: Road Coloring Is the Death Chain

For HYP-2702, the cleanest new proof handle is the road-coloring shadow.  Let
the states be residual masks `R subset {1,...,6}` and let each letter
`a in {0,...,6}` act by

```text
delta_a(R) = R \ {a},  if a in {1,...,6}
delta_0(R) = R.
```

This automaton is monotone, so it is not the strongly connected setting of the
road coloring theorem.  But its reset-word counts are exactly the all-singleton
death-chain kernel:

```text
#{length-r words resetting a fixed t-mask}
  = sum_{j=0}^t (-1)^j binom(t,j)(7-j)^r,
g_r(t) = 7^-r * that count.
```

Proof sketch: a word resets a fixed `t`-mask iff every required sector appears
at least once among the `r` letters.  Inclusion-exclusion over the missing
required sectors gives the displayed count.  Thus the singleton context in
HYP-2702 is not an arbitrary smoothing trick; it is the random reset
probability of the deficit automaton.

## What the Other Lenses Refute

The S65 cross-domain atlas makes several attractive proof routes visibly too
strong:

```text
Gibbs measure:
  beta=0 is the uniform signed aggregate.  beta>0 upweights negative-band debt.
  Every audited sparse-tail row has negative bands; smallest beta* is 183.619629.
  So a proof by arbitrary positive band reweighting is false.

Arnold cat map:
  A=[[2,1],[1,1]] over F7 has torus cycle lengths {1:1, 8:6}.
  Projective slopes split as [[0,4,2,6], [1,3,5,inf]].
  31/72 rows have a negative cat-cycle sum and 72/72 have a negative prefix.
  Hyperbolic mixing is structure, but not a local positivity proof.

Hopfield/Hebbian signs:
  The band-sign alphabet collapses to two binary patterns, -++-++- and -+++++-.
  That is a useful attractor picture for memory, but it stores the obstruction
  rather than proving the margin.

Fubini-Study geometry:
  Hit-count laws have projective distance correlated with death-chain margin
  (0.675197), but distance is not an order and cannot replace the kernel
  inequality.

Double slit:
  Measuring which band carried the debt destroys the interference.  The proof
  object must be the completed signed amplitude, not per-slit probabilities.

Clifford/T-gate:
  Incoming HYP-2707 says tournament quadratic/stabilizer quotients are
  tractable but the higher-degree magic layer carries H.  HYP-2702 has the same
  shape: raw hit-count or band quotients are tractable but too lossy; generated
  signed context is the magic resource.
```

## New Proof Hypotheses Inside HYP-2702

1. **Reset-kernel lemma (proved above):** the all-singleton context is exactly
   the random road-coloring reset kernel of the residual-mask automaton.

2. **Signed band-cone certificate:** the sparse-tail death-chain band vectors
   may have a finite cone description generated by the observed sign patterns
   `-++-++-` and `-+++++-`, plus zero-band degenerations.  A proof would bound
   positive fast-band mass against slow-band debt inside that cone.

3. **Context magic monotonicity:** merging singleton reset letters into coherent
   generated blocks should not decrease the HYP-2702 margin.  The right proof
   language is not stochastic dominance of hit counts; it should be monotonicity
   of the miss-zeta OR/deletion convolution.

4. **Gibbs no-go lemma:** any proof requiring positivity after all positive
   reweightings of the seven bands is impossible.  The target must be the
   uniform signed aggregate or a very special signed operator.

5. **Cat-prefix no-go lemma:** no proof can require positivity along the Arnold
   projective-cycle prefixes.  The completed orbit may be meaningful; its
   prefixes are not.

The net lesson is the same as HYP-2703/HYP-2704: LRC14's sector cover and
sparse tail are irreducibly aggregate.  The proof carrier is the generated
deficit language, not runners, bands, neural classes, or Clifford-stabilizer
coordinates alone.
