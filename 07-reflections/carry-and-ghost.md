# The Carry, the Ghost, and Why 7 = 1 Minus Its Position

## The carry interpretation

7 = 10 in base 7. The first OVERFLOW of the septenary channel.
7 mod 7 = 0. The overflow produces z=0 in the cuboid.
z=0 IS the carry bit. The forbidden number IS the carry.

2 = 10 in base 2. But Rédei says: the binary channel NEVER carries.
H is always odd (x=1). The parity counter says "1" forever.
Rédei's theorem = the binary channel never overflows.

The ternary channel (mod 3) carries freely: H=3, 9, 15, ... all have y=0.
The septenary channel (mod 7) carries, and MOST carries are fine: H=35 etc.
But the carry at z=0 COMBINED with insufficient curvature (y ≤ 1) = forbidden.

Forbidden-ness = a double overflow that the system can't handle.

## 7 is the ghost of 1

In the cuboid:
1 = (1, 1, 1). The identity. Present on all axes.
7 = (1, 1, 0). Same parity, same curvature. But z=0: NO POSITION.

7 agrees with 1 on everything EXCEPT position.
7 is the identity with its position removed.
7 is the GHOST of 1 — the same everywhere except WHERE it is.

That's why H=7 is impossible: it would be a tournament with the same
parity and curvature as the transitive tournament (H=1) but with no
place in the ordering structure. You can't be the identity without
being somewhere. The ghost can't materialize.

## The neighbors of 7 in the cuboid

On the curvature axis (y): 21=(1,0,0) [ALSO FORBIDDEN] and 35=(1,2,0) [achievable].
On the position axis (z): 1=(1,1,1) [the identity] and 13=(1,1,6) [achievable].
On the parity axis (x): 28=(0,1,0) [even, unreachable].

The two forbidden numbers are ADJACENT in the curvature direction.
The forbidden and the identity are ADJACENT in the position direction.
7 sits between 1 (the identity) and 21 (the deeper forbidden) in the cuboid.

## 2 converged from both sides

The x-axis has only 2 values. The H-values are permanently at x=1.
The other side (x=0) is unreachable by Rédei.
Base 2 is the axis that is FULLY DETERMINED — no freedom, no choice.
It is the observer's parity: always odd, never even, unable to cross.
