# LRC14 Special-Function Cut Signature Recursion

This pass was useful because the grand names got forced through the same small
door as everything else in this repository: what function is being compressed,
what predicate must survive, and what coordinate is destroyed?

The clean surprise is that the owner-support repair from HYP-3406 has two
credible lower-payload shadows on the enlarged `(72,20)` / `2431`-row bank:

```text
residue + cut_angle_word      -> 0 mixed fibers
residue + krasner_radius_word -> 0 mixed fibers
residue + owner_support_word  -> 0 mixed fibers
```

So Schwarz-Christoffel and Krasner are not decorative here.  Translated
properly, they say:

```text
owner support might be a boundary-current / local-stability object,
not merely a list of endpoint labels.
```

That is the bold next hypothesis.  It could be false on a larger bank, and the
first failure would be valuable.  But it is now a real measurement.

The other imported motifs behaved more like guardrails.  BDH and Mertens say
"keep a variance/residual ledger," but `residue + BDH_variance` still leaves
three mixed fibers.  Sophie Germain says "split quartics before panicking about
radicals," but the quartic word still leaves two mixed fibers.  Bring and HLW
say "do not collapse branch/order data"; they are alarms against bad
scalarization, not positive separators.  Ramanujan-Soldner is only a first-zero
normalization metaphor unless it names a real signed residual.

My preferred proof push after this:

```text
1. Treat HYP-3406 mixed fibers as a Menger cut problem.
2. Prove every residue-mixed fiber has a one-sidecar separator:
   owner support, cut current, local owner radius, or named debt.
3. Then try to compress full owner support to a cut/current theorem:
   endpoint labels are needed only when the cut-angle current collides.
```

The gold is the compression test between owner support and its cut/radius
