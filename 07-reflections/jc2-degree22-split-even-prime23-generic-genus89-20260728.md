This arithmetic irreducibility is geometric here.  The arithmetic monodromy
is transitive on the prime number `23` of sheets.  Orbits of the normal

loop around `t=0` acts by the normalization inertia word `4^5,1^3` from (7).

Consequently the all-one curve is geometrically integral.  Restrict to the

This is a positive-genus theorem for the generic prime-23 curve, but not a


The exact `v`-subresultant degrees are `3,2,1,0`; after removing its sole


Four exact resultants, each coprime to `Q69`, test respectively the
discriminant and leading coefficient of the quadratic first equation, the

Thus every `Q69` root gives one transverse `(t,q)` collision, two distinct

## 8. Scope boundary and next decisive work

Nothing here closes the split branch.  In particular:

1. all eleven odd seeds `E1,E3,...,E21` remain omitted;
2. special parameter strata can acquire extra singularities or components,
   so generic genus `89` is not a uniform Keller contradiction;
3. `A=0` reopens when `lambda!=0`, because the old `Phi=0` fixed-`E`
   argument gains a `1/q` term;
4. the third flux and the split `y=0` boundary still need their own equations.

There is, however, a uniform singularity-budget reformulation.  Every
integral flat member that retains (1) with `eta!=0` has arithmetic genus
`254`, and the uniform local calculation after (13) makes the five forced
cusps contribute delta `165`.  Such a member can have rational normalization
only if it gains at least

```text
254-165=89                                             (26)
```

additional delta.  Thus every hypothetical genus-zero survivor must either
become reducible/nonflat (or leave this chart) or lie on a proper
genus-drop/discriminant locus carrying at least `89` extra singularity units.
This is a precise next target, not a closure theorem.

The two sharp continuations are now:

- describe the proper coefficient discriminant on which at least `89` new
  intrinsic delta units can appear; and
- use the norm/trace form
  `S=C0*D^4-L*t^23*T+A^5*L^2*t^46` to Hensel-test every old/boundary factor
  type only through the decisive order `23`.

The second is the route most directly aimed at uniform component exclusion.
After that, the eleven odd Faber channels still have to be restored or killed
by the integral order-raising mechanism.
