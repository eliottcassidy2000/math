# LRC14 pi/unital/flower alias guardrail

The prompt is useful because it forces a normalization check before the new
imagery is allowed into the LRC14 proof stack.

The `22` in the flower story is real, but not under the literal phrase
`1/pi radians`.  A literal `1/pi` radian step has turn fraction
`1/(2*pi^2)` and about `19.739` steps per turn; `q=22` leaves a large angular
residual.  The `22` appears cleanly when the step is `1/pi` of a full turn,
i.e. `2` radians, because `1/pi ~= 7/22`.  Then `22` petals nearly close after
`7` full turns.  This is an aliasing/family-count signal, not a new exact
period.  If `pi=22/7` is substituted directly into the literal-radian
normalization, the turn fraction is exactly `49/968`; that is the compact
warning label for why the literal-radian model is not the 22-family model.

The two pi approximants have different roles.  `22/7` is the Farey/rational
carrier and can produce denominator labels such as `7/22` for a turn fraction.
`cuberoot(31)` is numerically better, but it is a cubic algebraic carrier:
`pi^3` is close to `31`.  It may resonate with Q31 fiber language, but only as
a scalar/cubic tag unless a rational-denominator statement is separately
proved.

The unital paragraph belongs as a semantics guardrail.  The `q=3` block-design
unital is the old `28=C(8,2)` pair-frame lead from HYP-2892/HYP-2894.
Algebraic or functional-analytic unitality is about identities and
identity-preserving maps.  The C=27 "unit-visible" shell language is about
gcd-one residue classes.  These are three different carriers; they should not
be collapsed just because the words are adjacent.  The concrete new experiment
is to test whether HYP-2937 marked C27 transfers lift into q=3 unital 4-point
blocks after category-1 AP/GW labels are attached.

The resulting proof-order instinct is conservative:

```text
exact angle normalization
> exact M/q/Farey branch
> C=27 unit/nonunit shell transfer
> pi approximant labels
> q=3 unital pair-frame after AP/GW labelling
> algebraic unital identity-map metaphor
> raw flower visual count.
```

This does not add a theorem.  It keeps a potentially useful analogy from
leaking into the exact proof layers before its labels are declared.
