## Exact advances

### 1. Four primitive outsider rays

[THM-4270](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4270-uniform-four-primitive-outsider-rays-common-deck-closure.md)
constructs one frozen 8,192-mask carrier and ratio-specific common decks for
the primitive rays `3:5`, `7:8`, `8:9`, and `11:12`. Literal joint-wall audits
cover every one of the `14,307,150` bodies at every declared scale. Exact
overlap accounting removes 146 then-current edges and, at that stage, leaves
residual `177,323` with unique top `(520,688)`.

The useful abstraction is not that a primitive ratio is an ordinal label.
It is that quotienting `(q,r)=g(a,b)` preserves the primitive wall geometry
only when the discarded scale `g` is retained as an activation sidecar. A
ratio name alone is not a common deck.

### 2. Historical one-atom control

[THM-4273](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4273-endpoint-542-732-minimum-one-atom-carrier-augmentation.md)
classifies the historical `(542,732)` boundary. Its frozen active deck misses
two bodies; all `125,970` common-disjoint candidates are checked, and exactly
`2,172` active masks complete the deck. Zero additions fail and one succeeds.

This control supplied two lessons used later. First, “minimum” must be typed:
it is minimum augmentation relative to a frozen deck and repair language, not
a globally minimum proof. Second, the whole completion family is more useful
than a canonical least witness because its support, transversals, exchange
graph, and transfer failures are new mathematical coordinates.

### 3. Complete observer calculus

[THM-4274](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4274-confined-confluent-observer-fibre-and-density-transport.md)
separates several operations that had repeatedly been conflated. For a finite
candidate set with complete observer image `W`, a linear quotient preserves
the complete fibre partition exactly when

```text
ker(lambda) intersect (W-W)={0}.
```

Only when zero belongs to the complete image does the quotient's zero test
have the intended meaning. Monic recurrence prefixes, cleared Bezout
identities, rectangular normal-Hasse samples, fixed-basis modular boxes,
total codomain capacity, and normalized fibre weights each receive their own
sharp gate. Uniform integrability is the exact sequential condition for
transporting every negligible target family.

The negative boundaries matter as much as the theorem:

- specialization vanishing is not coefficientwise vanishing;
- a bad resultant prime permits degeneration but does not force it;
- `q^r` is a capacity only when the full codomain really is the declared
  `r`-fold alphabet;
- bounded average fibre size does not replace uniform integrability;
- an observer with equal fibre sizes may still merge zero with nonzero data.

### 4. Opposite-parity JC attachment observers

[THM-4275](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4275-opposite-parity-attachment-observers-and-confluent-sample-matroids.md)
proves that, on all 1,512 inherited visible JC incidences, every pair of
attachment samples of opposite parity has exactly the same fibres as the full
twelve-edge word. All 36 unordered pairs work, including gaps three and nine;
parity orbits, not generation of `Z/12` by the gap, drive the proof.

The ambient `F4^3` sample matroid is controlled by represented residue classes
modulo four, while the visible `F4^2` matroid is controlled by represented
parities. A one-edge visible observer has four equal fibres but its zero fibre
contains three nonzero words, an exact warning that measure transport does not
imply predicate transport.

At THM-4272's fat contact, twelve generic Fourier point evaluations fall from
rank 12 to rank 1 when their sample points collide. Twelve Hasse coefficients
retain rank 12. The family

```text
D_N=t-t0,    E_N=t-t0-W^N
```

shows that simple roots on the special fibre impose no uniform transverse jet
depth. This identifies the next JC obligation: an effective normal
contact-order bound or an off-fibre dictionary, not another point-value
sample. No new incidence is excluded; JC(2) and DC(2) remain open.

### 6. Incoming minimum-six theorem as signal

During the session,
[THM-4271](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4271-fourth-round-learned-carrier-endpoint-descent.md)
arrived with the 2,419-edge descent to the two endpoint-671 hostiles. Then
[THM-4276](../01-canon/theorems/THM-4276-six-atom-endpoint-671-augmentation-and-one-layer-descent.md)
promoted the next step: its 27 row-labelled failure obligations admit 330
nonempty response patterns across all 5,852,925 repairs. A six-obligation
packing proves that no repair can pay for two selected obligations, while a
matching six-pattern cover proves sufficiency. The resulting carrier closes
both endpoint-671 rows and 161 of 163 endpoint-670 rows. The current exact
residual is `174,741`, topped by `(256,670),(384,670)`.

The important extension is conceptual and exact. Given a frozen active deck,
let `F` be its uncovered bodies and map every eligible repair `R` to the subset
`C(R)` of `F` that it covers. Minimum augmentation is exactly set cover on the
response quotient. THM-4278 is the case where 72 repairs have response `F`
and the optimum is one; THM-4276 is the case where a packing/cover dual pair
certifies optimum six. This quotient preserves the closure predicate but
forgets margins and pairwise transfer, so every selected mask must be rescanned
on every new arithmetic fibre.

## Cross-frontier synthesis

The strongest shared pattern is **complete incidence before quotient**:

| Source | Complete object | Quotient | Preserved target | Lost sidecar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LRC frozen deck | body-by-repair incidence | response pattern | deck closure | exact margin and pair identity |
| JC attachment word | twelve sample values | selected samples | full word fibre | parity orbit if chosen badly |
| fat contact | normal Hasse coefficients | coalesced point values | none at collision | multiplicity/jet order |
| finite map | candidate fibre partition | scalar density | negligible-family transport only with UI | zero predicate |

The table gives a procedural research rule:

1. build the complete finite observer or incidence object;
2. define the exact target equivalence on it;
3. quotient only after computing the pair-difference kernel or response
   fibres;
4. retain whatever margin, jet, weight, or arithmetic-fibre label the
   quotient destroys;
5. attack any apparent convexity or transfer with the cheapest hostile.

This is stronger than an analogy. Each row names the map and the preserved
predicate. It also says where the analogy stops: there is no known
predicate-preserving map from LRC repair incidence to the planar-JC normal
collar, so one cannot transport a theorem between them merely because both
use fibres.

## Negative signals that changed the search

- A frozen carrier's first failure is an address, not evidence of danger.
- A uniform fibre distribution can preserve density while destroying the
  zero/collapse predicate.
- A resultant identifies the only primes where rank may fall; it does not say
  that rank actually falls there.
- Generic point samples are the wrong observer at a collision; confluent
  Hasse data is the lawful replacement.
- Connected active-repair graphs need not remain connected under a change of
  arithmetic fibre.
- A canonical least witness is not a canonical mechanism; the complete
  response family and its set-cover dual expose the reusable proof.
- Tournament language is useful only when an intrinsic pairwise relation
  survives. These repair problems are naturally hypergraphs; forcing their
  multi-body obligations into cosmetic edge orientations would discard the
  very incidence needed for minimality.
