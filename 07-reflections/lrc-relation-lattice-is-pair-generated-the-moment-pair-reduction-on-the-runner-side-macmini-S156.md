## The one new structural fact: minimal relations are pairs

Computing the **minimal** nonzero relations `k` (`k·v = 0`, no proper sub-support relation) for
integer speeds:

| speeds | minimal relations |
|---|---|
| `{1,2,3}` | `(−2,1,0)`, `(−3,0,1)`, `(0,−3,2)` — all **pairs** |
| `{1,3,5}` | `(−3,1,0)` |
| `{1,2,4}` | `(−2,1,0)`, `(0,−2,1)` |

> **For integer speeds every minimal relation is a PAIR relation** `(v_j/g)·e_i − (v_i/g)·e_j`
> (`g = gcd(v_i,v_j)`) — because any pair `v_i, v_j` already has the relation `v_j·v_i − v_i·v_j
> = 0`, so no relation with support ≥ 3 is minimal. The triple `1+2−3=0` for `{1,2,3}` is **not**
> minimal: it contains the pair relation of `{1,2}`.

This is precisely the moment nullcone's **pair-reduction** (THM-1780), on the runner side. The
atoms — first-return events — are the pair realignments, and multi-runner coincidences are
generated by pair coincidences. So:

> **boxeph's "relation richness" is PAIR-relation richness**, and the pivot is the same as the
> moment nullcone's: the relations that matter are the `C(n,2)` pair realignments, and a tight
> instance is one where these pair returns pile up.
