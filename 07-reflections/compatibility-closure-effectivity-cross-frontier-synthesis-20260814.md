## The three gates

The common shape is

~~~text
local compatibility  -->  global closure  -->  target-category effectivity.
~~~

- **Compatibility** says the chosen local pieces may coexist on every pair or
  finite test.
- **Closure** says local differences integrate to one global object: a
  zero-circulation star, trivialized bundle class, all-iterate commuting
  family, or complete Walsh law.
- **Effectivity** says that object lives in the category the theorem needs:
  one physical phase, a polynomial coordinate ring, a positive correlation,
  or an explicit sign matrix.

None of the arrows is formal. A cohomology class can vanish without an
effective section, and a positive semidefinite shadow can close without an
integral or positive realization.

## What the sign puzzle contributed

The sign word separated three levels often conflated:

~~~text
pairwise column orthogonality
    < complete four-bit Walsh law
    < a load-bearing composite Hadamard template.
~~~

THM-3396 proves the middle level exactly. After the first and second Walsh
degrees vanish, four cubic moments and one quartic moment determine all
sixteen atom masses. The 896-row packet lies on the positivity boundary and
omits two atoms, although its Gram shadow is identical to every whitened
packet. THM-3394 shows that even this complete four-bit law is only a
sidecar: its unequal top block and fixed rewrite template remain essential
before the square Gram identity exists.

This is the same loss pattern as the other frontiers. A quotient can be
complete for one consumer and insufficient for the next.

## What q=8 changes in the LRC lane

THM-3395 is exact through seven sheets because a strict 1/14 danger arc then
meets at most one phase class per owner. The current q=8 finite-exact probe
shows the first new operation: an odd owner may block an adjacent two-sheet
domino. The right vertex is therefore not a speed and not even a single
coset; it is a cyclic consecutive block together with its centre lattice and
interval radius.

For one fixed selection of modes, the affine cochain remains the closure
gate. The new problem is finite mode-cover effectivity: select blocks that
cover every sheet while their source intervals have a common point. The
rank-four q=8 edge {1,3,5,7} is an anti-tournament control: four dominoes
partition the sheets with every pair gap zero. Orientation would add gauge,
not information.

The honest next theorem is a cyclic-block mode expansion, including
wraparound and duplicate-unwrapping rules. A larger tournament is not the
missing object.

## What the Banach analogy does and does not buy for JC(2)

The cited Banach preprint uses a positive-degree pullback to kill a finite
homotopy class and then obtains polynomial identities from a signed degree
formula. Only the first half has an immediate JC analogue: adjoining a root
can kill a finite residue torsor.

THM-3383 shows why that is insufficient. Its rational initial ring is an
A-type surface, but the oriented target-polynomial slice still imposes two
divisibilities, at the toric boundary and at xy=0. Killing the cyclic class
changes the cover; it does not make a rational principal part regular. The
missing JC sidecar is therefore an effective divisor ledger together with
control at infinity, not more monodromy data.

A useful next hostile should kill every visible finite class—preferably in
three symmetric views—while retaining a nonzero principal part on a boundary
divisor. Such a hostile would rule out topology-only JC transfers without
saying anything against JC(2).

## Five ranked next tests

1. **q=8 and beyond:** prove the cyclic consecutive-block mode theorem with a
   canonical wraparound convention; then seek a closed mode cochain that
   still fails the physical target.
2. **JC effectivity:** calculate the denominator filtration of each decoded
   target and test whether a symmetric cover kills divisor classes while
   leaving principal parts nonzero.
3. **Four-bit geometry:** identify the exact vertices and faces of the
   THM-3396 moment polytope, then use its extrema as finite cubic-chaos
   hostiles for Gaussian or factorial transfers.
4. **Operator completion:** seek a natural nontrivial source of commuting
   all-iterate defects. Ordinary compression is excluded by the first
   commutator.
5. **Hadamard structure:** factor the twelve inert contracts into lawful
   composition operations. Their finite existence is proved; extrapolation
   to the Hadamard conjecture is not.

The decisive question is always the same: which predicate survives the map,
and what exact datum must still pass through the final gate?
