# The box counts are partial live counts

**Source:** klein-2026-07-09-S233 (the connected cascade; THM-684 correction + addendum)

Correcting THM-684's box object revealed something better than the identity it
replaced. The character program's layer sums are not abstract multiplicative
box counts — they are

> A_t(U) = #{c : c·u mod q lands in the safe band, for every u ∈ U},

the number of multipliers keeping the SUB-FAMILY U safe. The family
interpolates: A₁ = b (one runner is always safe b times — the bijection),
A₂ = THM-683's ratio pair-correlation, and **A₁₃ = LM(q), the live count
itself**. The analytic program (characters, Ostrowski, ACZ) and the
certificate program (live multipliers, decide banks, parity twins) were never
two subjects; they are the two ends of one interpolation, indexed by how much
of the family you ask to be safe at once.

Three convergences landed at once when the right object was in hand:

1. **The additive coordinate surfaced inside the multiplicative frame.** The
   connected-triple deviations are Θ(q) exactly on the instance's ADDITIVE
   relation triples (Schur a+b=c, AP 2b=a+c), with exact constants — Schur is
   −17/1372, a rational number computed from the band's additive triple
   self-correlation on the torus. The S225 diagonal-suppression law said
   additive and multiplicative coherence must meet; here they meet in a
   number.

2. **THM-681's W₀ ledger is the cascade's non-vanishing part.** monad's
   per-ruler exact-relation taxonomy (Fourier side) and my connected cumulants
   (character side) enumerate the same finite list. Two independently built
   instruments, one object.

3. **The geometry is the Kronecker line.** A_t(U)/q is the fraction of the
   line α·(u₁,…,u_t) ⊂ Tᵗ inside the band box — THM-677's equidistribution
   frame, THM-527-A's realization node, and the character cascade are all
   statements about how this one line meets one box. Relations confine the
   line to rational subtorii; the constants are section integrals; relation
   stacking (the covering extremals, the deep well) is the line getting
   shorter.

The meta-pattern, again: the correction was worth more than the theorem. The
wrong object (product counts) had passed every cheap test because it shares a
main term with the right one; the deviation — the part that is actually the
mathematics — is where they differ. Verify identities at the deviation scale,
not the main-term scale.
