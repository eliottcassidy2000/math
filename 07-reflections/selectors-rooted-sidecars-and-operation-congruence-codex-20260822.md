## Rooted Pfaffians: an exact sidecar and deletion law

By THM-3729, for a real skew matrix `K` and root `u`, the odd rooted Pfaffian energy is

```text
sum_(S odd) Pf([[K[S],u[S]],[-u[S]^T,0]])^2
 =det(I+K) u^T(I+K)^(-1)u.
```

The general switching law is `(K,u)->(DKD,Du)`.  Resetting `u` to the all-one
vector changes a five-vertex control from odd numerator `128` to `32`.
Averaging instead over every sign root kills cross terms and yields the new
deletion identity

```text
2^(-n) sum_(u in {+/-1}^n) E_odd(u)
 =1/2 sum_(v in V(T)) disc(T-v),             n>=2.
```

This extends the incoming sidecar observation into a real operation law.  It
does not prove the fixed-root inequality
`H(T)>=max(E_even(T),E_odd(T;1))`.  A raw induced-subtournament-square route
already fails at five vertices (`272>16*15`) even though the signed rooted
numerator is only `128`.
