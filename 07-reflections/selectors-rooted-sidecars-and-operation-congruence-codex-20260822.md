## Rooted Pfaffians: an exact sidecar and deletion law

By THM-3729, for a real skew matrix `K` and root `u`, the odd rooted Pfaffian energy is

```text
sum_(S odd) Pf([[K[S],u[S]],[-u[S]^T,0]])^2
 =det(I+K) u^T(I+K)^(-1)u.
```

The general switching law is `(K,u)->(DKD,Du)`.  Resetting `u` to the all-one
vector changes a five-vertex control from odd numerator `128` to `32`.

deletion identity

```text
2^(-n) sum_(u in {+/-1}^n) E_odd(u)
 =1/2 sum_(v in V(T)) disc(T-v),             n>=2.
```

The older switching-class transversal sharpens this operation law.  Every
vertex ordering imposes path-graph sign equations that select exactly one
switched representative in which it is a Hamiltonian path.  Consequently

```text
avg_(T' in S(T)) H(T')=n!/2^(n-1),
avg_(T' in S(T)) E_odd(T';1)=1/2 sum_v disc(T-v).
```

Hadamard plus `m!>=m^(m/2)` makes the Hamiltonian mean dominate both
the constant even-energy mean and the odd-energy mean separately.  This is
a genuine class-level reservoir, but it does not control a fixed all-one root
in a fixed representative or the average of the maximum.  A raw
induced-subtournament-square route already fails at five vertices
