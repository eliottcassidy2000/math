# LRC14 Random031 Free-Hole Bracket Atlas

The useful correction here is that the `40` no-gate cells are not one blob.
HYP-3486 already said they form `14` legal mirror packets, but that still left
too much room to imagine a hidden wall.  The new atlas closes that loophole:
ten packets are individually ordinary-bracketed on both sheets, and the
remaining four packets occur only inside two same-branch doublets whose exposed
sides are still ordinary rank-2 boundaries.

That is the finite free-hole lemma the seam-complement story wanted.  The
coarse HYP-3510 one-component incidence graph says no-gate cells are interior
beads in the branch-order transport carrier.  HYP-3511 says the same thing
locally, in a proof-safe way: no-gate transport is confined to a tiny packet
grammar, not a global obstruction.

The puncture relation to HYP-3481 is subtler than I first expected.  The four
continuous dead islands are not literally the same as the fourteen q-grid
free-hole packets.  The common shadow is smaller: only two mirror-paired
half-open doublets carry same-branch free-hole adjacency at all.  That is the
place to localize any puncture sidecar or owner-boundary debt.

So the random031 terminal clause now looks like a three-way local split:
`242` routed rank-2 cells, `40` free-hole cells in a bracketed packet grammar,
and `12` pure bypass cells.  The forbidden seam remains owner-boundary debt,
but the phase transport object is no longer mysterious.
