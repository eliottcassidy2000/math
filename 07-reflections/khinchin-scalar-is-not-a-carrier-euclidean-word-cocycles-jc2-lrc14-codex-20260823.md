## 3. Exact no-go I: a finite Khinchin mean is not intrinsic on rationals

Every rational has the terminal ambiguity

```text
[0;a_1,...,a_r]=[0;a_1,...,a_r-1,1],       a_r>=2.    (1)
```

Consequently a finite digit geometric mean is not representation-invariant.
The LRC floor already supplies the minimal memorable example:

```text
1/14=[0;14]=[0;13,1].                               (2)
```

The finite digit products are respectively `14` and `13`, and the lengths
are `1` and `2`.  The almost-everywhere irrational theorem has no terminal
ambiguity because the expansion is infinite.  A rational LRC statistic must
first choose a canonical convention; it cannot simply import “the Khinchin
mean.”

The companion verifies `(1)` for all denominators `2..64`.  This bounded
replay is a control for the symbolic identity, not the proof.

## 4. Exact no-go II: canonicalization still does not recover LRC endpoint semantics

Fix an odd integer `m>=3`.  The two canonical rational continued fractions

```text
x_m=[0;1,2m]=2m/(2m+1),
y_m=[0;2,m] = m/(2m+1)                              (3)
```

have all of the following scalar data in common:

```text
length                 2,
digit product          2m,
finite geometric mean  sqrt(2m),
denominator            2m+1,
final midpoint phase   0.                            (4)
```

The last line follows from THM-778's recursion

```text
s_{j+1}=(a_j-s_j) mod 2,          s_0=1.             (5)
```

Modulo two, subtraction and addition agree, so after `r` digits

```text
s_r = 1 + sum_(j=1)^r a_j mod 2.                    (6)
```

Both digit sums in `(3)` are odd.

Nevertheless the centered endpoint words have different tie content.  For
the pair of speeds `(2m,2m+1)`, the reduced parities are even/odd, so THM-778
gives no simultaneous block.  For `(m,2m+1)`, both reduced speeds are odd, so
there is exactly one simultaneous block.  Thus:

> **PROVED infinite hostile.** Finite Khinchin length/product, the exact
> denominator, and even the final THM-778 midpoint phase do not determine the
> centered endpoint tie predicate.

The companion replays this family for every odd `3<=m<=101`.

## 6. Exact no-go III: literal continued-fraction tails are already closed in the Cohn constant orbit

A finite continued-fraction word with integer digits is a finite word in
constant elementary matrices.  For example,

```text
E_+(1)E_-(6) = [7 1;6 1],
E_+(2)E_-(3) = [7 2;3 1].                            (11)
```

Both matrices have determinant one and the same digit product `6`; they are
different ordered `SL_2(Z)` words.  The common upper-left entry `7` is the
length-two continuant `1+ab`, while the off-diagonal entries remember the
ordered factorization.

For the automorphic Cohn cell of THM-3736, however, every such finite integer
word collapses to one constant right matrix `R in SL_2(k)`.  THM-3736
classifies every closed row after one arbitrary polynomial exposure and
proves that no classified potential has a polynomial mate.  Therefore:

> **PROVED scoped corollary.** No literal finite positive-integer
> continued-fraction tail, regardless of its Khinchin statistic, escapes the
> THM-3736 constant-right Cohn orbit.

This is deliberately scoped.  It is not a statement about arbitrary long
left words, all non-elementary matrices, or JC(2).

## 7. Exact no-go IV: Khinchin magnitude does not see JC seam holonomy

