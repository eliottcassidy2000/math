## 3. THM-3818: the support-two address regains scale

For the finite atlas

```text
R={(p,q):0<p<q, gcd(p,q)=1, p+q<=356,
           every prime of p+q is 2 mod 3 with exponent at most 2},
```

there are `94` admissible sums and `5,855` ratios.  THM-3818 proves

```text
[p^3+q^3] in Q_(>0)^x/(Q_(>0)^x)^3
```

is injective on `R`.  Two independent primary algorithms agree: prime
exponents modulo three and removal of the largest integral cube divisor.  An
independent hostile audit instead generated the admissible sums
multiplicatively and tested all `17,137,585` unordered pairs after gcd
reduction.  It found no collision.

Consequently

```text
(g,p,q) |-> g^3(p^3+q^3)
```

is injective for positive `g` and `(p,q)` in `R`.  The proof is short but the
scope is load-bearing: equality identifies the primitive cube class, hence the
primitive pair, and only then identifies the scale.

If this pair is the **selected shortest support-two relation** and its labelled
speeds are `gp,gq`, the packet recovers

```text
q e_i-p e_j,
[(q-13p)/14,(13q-p)/14],
width=(6/7)(p+q),
W(gp,gq)=W(p,q)^g,
and g ties exactly in the reduced odd/odd case.
```

The selected qualifier was forced by audit.  In the ambient row
`{1,...,13}`, the admissible pair `(1,4)` has covector norm five, but `(1,2)`
has norm three.  A cube address can recover the former pair without proving it
is the first flatness pull.

The lawful next sidecar is ambient residue, not another scalar.  Put

```text
D=g(p+q),
G_ij={m mod D:14 d_D(m n_i)>=D},
B_l={m mod D:14 d_D(m n_l)<D}.
```

There is a witness on the pair-sum lattice exactly when

```text
G_ij is not contained in union_(l != i,j) B_l.
```

Thus a counterexample in this branch must satisfy a finite covering
obligation.  The statement is silent off that lattice.

## 4. THM-3819: the selector becomes a return cocycle

Let `g_n=G_n(2), h_n=H_n(2)` in THM-3757's canonical indexed polynomial Pell
tower, and define

```text
x_n=2g_n-h_n,          q_n=(h_n-g_n)/2,
m_n=(x_n+1)/2,         a_n=3g_n-2h_n,       b_n=g_n.
```

After evaluation, the coordinate changes are invertible.  For every `n>=1`,

```text
a_n=P_(2n-1),                 q_n=P_(2n)/2,
x_n=P_(2n)+P_(2n-1),         b_n=P_(2n+1),
h_n=P_(2n+1)+P_(2n),

h_n^2-2g_n^2=-1,             x_n^2-8q_n^2=1.
```

The first return law on THM-3756's boundary is

```text
(m_(n+1),1)=L^(h_n)(m_n,1),
h_n=7,41,239,1393,8119,... .
```

This is not a fixed Berggren substitution.  Once a positive word uses `M` or
`R`, the second ordinal coordinate becomes greater than one and can never
return to one.  Hence every boundary-to-boundary word is `L^c`, and the unequal
first two return times rule out a fixed word at all depths.

The same cocycle acts lawfully on two further shadows.

First, for the standardized THM-3745 module shape

```text
D_m=direct_sum_(i=1)^(m-1) k[X]/(X^i),
```

one return appends exactly `h_n` cyclic summands and increases total length by

```text
q_(n+1)^2-q_n^2
 =sum_(i=m_n)^(m_n+h_n-1)i.
```

This does not embed the actual branch rings: `k[t^9,t^10]` and
`k[t^50,t^51]` are incomparable.

Second, on the alternating THM-3744 scalar maxima,

```text
u_n^-=M(S_(4n-1))=a_n/x_n,
u_n^+=M(S_(4n+1))=x_n/(2b_n),
f(u)=1/(4-2u),
f(u_n^-)=u_n^+,       f(u_n^+)=u_(n+1)^-.
```

One return is observed by `f^2`.  This transports the optimum scalar, not its
runner-labelled carry and owner packet.

## 5. The merged row `(5,29)`

Under the flatness cap, the Pell/Markov ratios are exactly

```text
(1,5), (5,29), (29,169).
```

Only `(5,29)` has an all-inert cube-free sum.  At this row all four lanes meet:

```text

cube address              5^3+29^3=24514
Pell state                (x,q)=(17,6),       T_8=6^2=36
negative-Pell state       (g,h)=(29,41)
Berggren boundary         m=9, Xi=(17,144,145)
conductor packet          delta=36, exponent=8
loneliness scalars        5/17 -> 17/58
next boundary return      L^41:(9,1)->(50,1).
```

THM-3818 then gives the primitive pair-sum modulus `34`.  Multiplication by
five is invertible modulo `34`, with inverse seven, so

```text
G_(5,29)=Z/34Z minus {0,7,14,20,27}.
```

Any hypothetical counterexample whose selected minimal relation is a labelled
copy of `(5,29)` must use its other eleven runners to cover these `29` safe
residues.  This is the first concrete operation-level consequence of merging
the Pell selector with the cube address.  It is still only a lattice slice;
even a complete cover would not rule out an off-lattice witness.

## 6. Incoming JC signals and why they redirect the next search

THM-3814 and THM-3815 arrived while the same cells were being explored.  They
were treated as closures to verify and extend, not as prompts for duplicate
theorem files.

THM-3814 starts from the normalized nodal carrier and arbitrary polynomial
coefficients in the canonical `r`, `z^2`, and `rz` slots.  Three normal-form
buckets force an arm identity, a Wronskian relation, and a Kummer tower.  A
nonzero parameter root has incompatible orders `4m-1` and `5m-1`; if all roots
are zero, the origin has incompatible orders `3+4d` and `3+5d`; the constant
tower leaves residual `1/4`.  An independent audit rederived all three seams,
including the denominator boundary, and promoted it.

THM-3815 proves every carrier

```text
A=e^2-z/3+r(a_0+a_1e+a_2e^2)+h z^2
```

has a critical point on the cubic pseudo-plane.  A source-first slope
elimination and an independent `C_r`-first elimination reach the same closure
through different resultants.  A concurrent third derivation also reached the
same boundary, so the useful signal was not another certificate: the quadratic
mixed cell is saturated.

The next JC work should therefore change a native operation or coefficient
type.  The live lanes are nonconstant `z^2` profiles, cubic-and-higher mixed
`r` profiles, poles, or interactions between already nontrivial factors.  The
Pell evaluation cannot help with the missing polynomial geometry: replacing
`G_1=4z-3` by `5z-5` preserves the evaluated pair `(5,7)` but destroys the
polynomial Pell identity.  This hostile is exactly why THM-3819 calls only the
post-evaluation integer coordinate change lossless.

## 7. Khinchin content after the extension

The earlier synthesis separated Khinchin's metric continued-fraction theorem,
ordered continuants, and Khinchin's Flatness Theorem.  This session reinforces
that separation.

- The flatness lane advances: its support-two branch now has a reversible
  scale/facet/word packet and an exact residue-cover sidecar.
- The ordered-word lane advances: the Pell selector has a first-return cocycle
  and a Möbius semiconjugacy on its exact maximum.
- The metric digit-content lane does not advance.  The silver/Pell word is a
  deterministic quadratic orbit, not typical continued-fraction data, and no
  almost-everywhere scalar recovers the return, carry, or owner packets.

Thus “Khinchin content” remains plural.  The geometry-of-numbers theorem
produces a short relation; the continued-fraction word decodes a selected
pair; only the ambient residue sidecar tests a target LRC phase.

## 9. Hostiles that set the exact boundary

- `86^3+344^3=197^3+323^3` proves a raw cube sum is not an unrestricted
  two-cube decoder.  The two endpoint words even have different tie counts.
- Two thirteen-speed rows with the same unique norm-five relation
  `4(1)-1(4)=0` have minima `1/5` and `0` at the same phase.  No selected-pair
  sidecar decides global arrival.
- `{1,...,13}` shows that an occurring atlas pair need not be the shortest
  relation.  Minimality belongs to the selection, not the address.
- A perturbation in the kernel of evaluation preserves `(G(2),H(2))` but
  destroys the polynomial Pell identity.  Evaluation is not a JC functor.
- The actual conductor rings at degrees `9` and `50` are incomparable even
  though their standardized defect modules form an append staircase.
- `M({1,2})=M({1,2,5})=1/3` shows that the scalar optimum does not determine a
  carry or owner packet.

## 10. New research moves and next pulls

Three reusable ideas survived.

1. **Separated quotient plus compatible scale action can become a decoder.**
   A quotient invariant is not automatically lossy: if its primitive classes
   are separated on a declared atlas and physical scale acts by the same power,
   equality first identifies the primitive row and then the scale.  Outside the
   atlas, taxicab fibres return immediately.
2. **A selected family may be operation-compatible through first returns even
   when it is not edge-closed.**  Record the return cocycle and its update on
   every sidecar; do not force a subtree or fixed substitution.
3. **Audit injectivity twice.**  First ask whether the observer is injective on
   the selected section; then ask whether selection itself and the ambient
   object are recoverable.  THM-3818's minimality repair and THM-3819's
   evaluation repair are the two independent warnings.

The cheapest high-value next tests are:

- intersect actual rank-eleven star rows with the labelled `(5,29)` branch and
  test whether eleven danger residues can cover its explicit 29-class safe set;
- if that cover succeeds, search continuously between adjacent `1/34` phases
  before claiming a bad row;
- on the JC side, move directly to a nonconstant `z^2` profile or a cubic mixed
  `r` profile and retain the full polynomial tower—evaluation at `z=2` is only
  a hostile control;
- test whether the variable return cocycle acts on any owner-labelled Pell
  prefix packet beyond the scalar maximum; equality of maxima is not enough;
- look for other finite atlases where a power-class quotient and a native
  dilation satisfy the same decoder schema, always with an explicit
  out-of-atlas collision probe.

No current result excludes an LRC(14) row or constructs a planar Keller
counterexample.  The precise advance is a better packet, a lawful return
operation, two audited scope repairs, and one concrete 29-residue next target.
