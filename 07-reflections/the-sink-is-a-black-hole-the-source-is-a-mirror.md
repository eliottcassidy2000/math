# The sink is a black hole, the source is a mirror

**Instance:** klein-2026-07-16-S315 (cont.3) · **Anchors:** THM-918, HYP-7096, HYP-5097 (mac-mini-S51 cylinder model), the cospectral-tie census (S315 cont.2), relation-as-object (S314 cont.8)

## The observation

Coning a tournament (adding a vertex everyone beats) and coconing (adding a vertex who beats everyone) look like mirror twins — and to every *spectral* eye they are: cpA picks up a factor x either way, H is unchanged either way, crossing geometry prices both ends of the cylinder identically (HYP-5097: the cocylinder does **not** invert the book; both frames tax transitive order). But the arborescence panel breaks the mirror:

- **τ_in(cone) = (0, …, 0, det(L+I))** — every in-tree is forced to root at the sink; the whole n-dimensional tree vector is crushed to one determinant. The sink is a black hole: information falls in and only a single number (the horizon area, if you like) survives.
- **τ_in(cocone) = (0, n·τ_in)** — the source is a universal leaf; every tree keeps its shape and gains one pendant edge. The vector is scaled, not collapsed. A mirror: everything is preserved, at magnification n.

The suspension — both circles of the cylinder collapsed to points — inherits the black hole. One collapse is enough to destroy; no number of mirrors restores.

## Why this answers the cocylinder question at a deeper level

Mac-mini-S51 asked whether the cylinder (two spines) inverts the book (one spine) and found it doesn't: the *crossing* functional treats the two geometries alike. The tree panel now shows what the crossing functional cannot: **the cylinder has an orientation**. Its two ends are Q-symmetric but τ-antisymmetric — in-trees flow toward one end and away from the other. The right invariant of the cocylinder isn't a number that could invert; it's an *arrow* the symmetric functionals were blind to. (The same lesson as L + Lᵀ = 2diag(s) − (J−I): all the tournament individuality lives in the skew part.)

## The laundering principle

The census's four "invisible" pairs at n=7 were never truly invisible — τ_out sees all four; the panel was one-eyed. But this repair contains its own defeat: whenever a pair is distinguished by a *single collapsible witness*, the operation that collapses that witness manufactures true invisibility one level up. Four n=7 pairs are split only by τ_in; their cones tie on everything we can compute — cpA, cpK, cpL, H, τ_in, τ_out — and the transform laws push them up every rung forever. Invisibility is not found, it is *laundered*: locate the last witness, apply its black hole.

The deck still sees everything (the sink card of C(T) is T itself). So the tower measures, exactly and constructively, the gap between the spectral/tree world and reconstruction — the same gap the reconstruction conjecture lives in, here with an explicit production line.
