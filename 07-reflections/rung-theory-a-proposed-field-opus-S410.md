## 0. What the field is about, in one paragraph

Every object this campaign has touched — attained loneliness values, certificates,
gridmax accumulation sites, gates, ghosts, partitions, tournaments, deformations — lives
on one underlying structure: **the interaction of a finite speed set with the lattice of
rational phases, stratified by displacement**. Rung theory studies that structure
directly: its points are *rungs* (values D/s with their arithmetic (D, s, slack)); its
fibers are the three incarnations of each rung; its morphisms are the family moves
(dilation, translation, Hamming/Young-box flip, ghost swap, lift); its flows are the
θ-deformation; its conservation laws are duty budgets; its rigidity theorems are gates.

## 1. The RUNG STACK (three levels, one value line)

For each rational value v, define the fiber **F(v) = (A(v), C(v), G(v))**: families
ATTAINING v; CERTIFICATE classes guaranteeing ≥ v (bounded-denominator witnesses);
finite-subgroup GRIDMAX realizations of v (G-K's S*₀-land). The S403 discovery — the
same values 2/27, 3/41, 4/55, … populate all three levels — is the field's founding
observation ("one ladder, three roles"). The pinned G-K Theorem 1.4 is the stack's
**boundary properness**: value-accumulation in level A lands in level G — families
degenerate to subgroups. Escape/lift families are curves running to the boundary.
Morse-flavored reframing of the live wall: "(1/14, 3/41) finite" = "the value function
has finitely many interior critical values in the window" on the compactified stack.

## 2. The DUTY CALCULUS (conservation laws)

Duties are the currency: covering duties (k | some v), ghost duties (K·(m+1) ∈ V at
cost 1/K), spread duties (antipodal classes mod p). The fundamental constraint is the
**packing budget** (Σ gap-costs ≤ 1), and THM-1292's Φ₆(14)/14 = 13 + 1/14 is the first
ledger identity: thirteen core gaps + one ghost duty. **Extremality = ledger
saturation** — verified today (§5, T3) with the refinement that there are (at least)
two ledgers, ghost and sieve, and every tight family saturates exactly the one its
exclusion signature σ (S408 W3) names: σ = {(g,1)} ⟹ ghost ledger (deep well, slack 0
exactly); σ = ∅ ⟹ sieve ledger ((m+2)M = 1 pattern; AP, GW). boxeph's blocking-budget
heuristic becomes the field's basic counting conjecture: **duty capacity** — k speeds
serve at most f(k) independent duties; the tight locus is the duty-saturated locus.

## 3. The EXCLUSION CORRESPONDENCE (the Galois-flavored core)

THM-1291 + the ghost loop, restated as the field's first law: a family's tightness
mechanism is encoded by which convergents of its own maximizer it INCLUDES (the active
leg; D = its CF remainder) and which it EXCLUDES (σ(V); always the small-remainder ones,
provably; the extremal exclusion is the penultimate convergent, duty-paid by K·ghost).
**More exclusion ⟹ deeper maximizer ⟹ tighter value, bounded by the duty budget.** The
gate theorems are the correspondence's rigidity: an exclusion pattern is realizable only
on congruence classes of N (the trichotomy's phase-denial: the escape needs 3a ≡ ±2
(mod 2N+1), denied iff N ≡ 1 (mod 3)). Program: the exclusion lattice and its
fixed points = the tight locus.

## 4. The θ-FLOW (renormalization between two classical spectra)

M_θ = max min v^θ‖vt‖ deforms the rung world (θ=0: Farey, gates, AP-rigidity) into the
Markov–Lagrange world (θ=1: noble numbers, Fibonacci maximizers, Hurwitz). The repo's
oldest lore ("the AP is the unique fixed point") and Markov's chain become endpoint

entry ≈ 7/8, S409), the twin-separating middle band, and — today — the flow's law (§5,
