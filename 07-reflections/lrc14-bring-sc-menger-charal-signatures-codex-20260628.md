# LRC14 Bring/SC/Menger Charal Signatures

The useful synthesis in this pass is that the requested motifs split into two
very different classes.

This is now downstream of the HYP-3407 boundary-uniformization cut atlas,
HYP-3408 exotic guardrail atlas, HYP-3409 recursive sidecar pattern atlas,
HYP-3410 exact charal-recursion packet, HYP-3411 Galois reframe, HYP-3412
special-function sidecar scout, HYP-3413 GW gate, and HYP-3414 owner-cut
resurrection calculus.  After the rebase it is also downstream of HYP-3415's
critical-path map, HYP-3416's recursive quotient ladder, HYP-3417's margin-1
owner-current certificates, HYP-3418's 2-adic covering-floor correction,
HYP-3419's concrete charal owner-cut recursion prototype, and HYP-3420's
chiral-owner exactness audit.  After the final rebase it is also downstream of
HYP-3421's off-grid resonance transparency scout and HYP-3422's two-adic
relocation lemma, HYP-3423's q-uniform topology / q-specific arithmetic
guardrail, HYP-3424's covering-floor duality transfer, and the two incoming
HYP-3425 sidecars: finite-ruler Helly for branch relocation and
energy-plus-sheet packets for additive energy.  The final incoming pair,
HYP-3426 and HYP-3427, turns that into endpoint-support triples and exact
wall signatures.  The incoming HYP-3428 loss ledger then records the
controlled-forgetting debt for the same two-adic descent lane, and HYP-3429
compresses survivor windows to endpoint-spine certificates.  Those atlases
are the broader proof-carrier routers, while HYP-3440 isolates the smaller Bring/SC/Menger
endpoint-cut slice and tests which payload is indispensable.  The endpoint cut belongs to the
magnitude/owner layer where the `C6` orbit picture breaks, not to the residue
half that Galois symmetry already organizes.  HYP-3423 makes the rule explicit:
SC/Menger boundary language may certify order, owner legality, or quotient
purity, but it may not become a q-specific magnitude/floor proof unless the
arithmetic, owner-current, or two-adic floor coordinate is restored.

The rebase over HYP-3430-HYP-3437 changes the handoff.  The harmonic
Euler-Mascheroni layers are scalar firewalls and endpoint-budget priority
queues; HYP-3431 supplies the canonical `84m` corridor-fence base case;
HYP-3434 isolates the overlap-tax identity; HYP-3435 supplies finite
branch-cover endpoint-gate ledgers; and HYP-3437 reserves the graph-cut
overlap-tax certificate.  So the Menger part of HYP-3440 is no longer a vague
analogy.  It should become the incidence/cut language for the HYP-3435/HYP-3437
certificate stack.

The important correction is critical-path discipline.  HYP-3440 does not close
LRC14.  HYP-3415 says non-covering rows are handled by the q-witness and the
covering case needs one uniform decorrelation floor `R' > 0`; HYP-3418 says
that floor is governed by even speeds and 2-adic descent, not by the apex-7
census.  HYP-3421 and HYP-3422 sharpen the replacement: prove off-grid
transparency and two-adic branch relocation; HYP-3424 turns the same material
into a transfer ledger, and HYP-3425 Helly gives the finite interval target.
The Bring/SC/Menger packet is therefore useful only as a sidecar router: it can
clean owner/height impurities, build finite Menger/SC certificates, feed the
recursive quotient ladder, and name branch debt for the relocation lemma, but
it cannot replace the covering-floor theorem.

The live proof class is boundary/cut shaped.  HYP-3406 says residue plus
height still forgets the theorem exit, but residue plus owner support does
not.  HYP-3414 sharpens that into a clause calculus: the known leaks need
owner transversals of sizes `1`, `1`, and `3`, with the size-3 frontier having
empty core.  Schwarz-Christoffel language is useful if it turns owner supports
into ordered boundary sides.  Menger language is useful if it turns owner
deletion into a bounded cut-code theorem.  Together they suggest a real next
lemma: endpoint owner resurrection should be a bounded owner-transversal /
max-flow-min-cut certificate over the actual packet boundary, not a universal
singleton-owner rule.

The other motifs are guardrails or bulk sidecars.  Bring radical language
warns against flattening a branch packet into a scalar.  Krasner prevents
p-adic branch sliding.  HLW is a no-free-slider discipline, not a local owner
separator.  Sophie Germain factorization belongs to quartic height-wall
splitting.  BDH, Meissel-Mertens, and Ramanujan-Soldner belong to colored
bulk normalization and infinite-tail discipline.

The recursive Charal ladder is the cleanest output:

```text
coarse -> residue -> height/v2 -> unit slot
```

still leaks, while

```text
coarse -> residue -> endpoint cut
```

does not leak in the HYP-3406 model.  That is the route to formalize before
adding any more famous constants.  The HYP-3417 owner-current certificates
give the finite dual language for this formalization: `{5:g1}`, `{1:g1}`, and
`{2:g2,11:g1,13:g1}` all have margin `1`, so the next exact target is bounded
owner-current transversals, not a scalar analogy.

The new upstream owner-cut work sharpens the role.  HYP-3419 says the known
frontier is not one-label: the `10->20` family needs a size-3/depth-3 owner
decision tree.  HYP-3420 says adding owner/chiral-owner sidecars kills mixed
fibers on the scanned expanded banks.  Therefore HYP-3440 should be used as
the vocabulary layer around those certificates: Schwarz-Christoffel names the
boundary word, Menger names the theorem shape, and the other motifs are
guardrails or bulk-sidecar annotations.

The new off-grid/two-adic upstream work decides where that vocabulary can help
the proof.  If HYP-3422's branch-overlap interval fails in a larger bank, the
HYP-3440 payload should name whether the failure is endpoint-owner cut debt,
branch-sheet debt, Krasner local-stability debt, or BDH/exact-period debt.  If
the interval lemma succeeds uniformly, HYP-3440 remains a cleanup certificate
for the theorem-exit ledger rather than part of the main inequality.

The new HYP-3423 guardrail also blocks the tempting false compression.  A
Schwarz-Christoffel polygon or Menger cut can make the proof boundary finite
and checkable; it cannot turn a q-uniform topological charge into the
q-specific Goddyn-Wong magnitude switch.  The legal path is still: topology
for residue/order, owner currents for local mixed fibers, and arithmetic plus
two-adic floor data for magnitude.

The incoming HYP-3424/HYP-3425 work adds one more correction.  Additive or BDH
language is legal only as a packet sidecar: the additive-energy scout exhibits
raw scalar collisions, so energy must be paired with sheet data before it can
feed SPEC, phase debt, or terminal packet debt.  Likewise, a Menger cut is
legal only as endpoint-owner payload for the Helly/relocation or HYP-3424
transfer ledger, not as a separate scalar route to the floor.

The incoming HYP-3426/HYP-3427 work makes the target sharper still.  HYP-3426
removes the two-branch ambiguity by mirror symmetry and audits endpoint-owner
supports of size at most three; HYP-3427 says positive survivor windows should
be carried as exact wall words.  So the SC/Menger language should now present
endpoint-support triples and wall signatures, not replace them with geometric
analogy.

The incoming HYP-3428 loss ledger adds the floor-side version of the same
rule.  A halving quotient is legal only after odd blockers, the halved even
child, and named witness-loss classes are retained.  HYP-3440 can supply
endpoint cuts to that ledger, but it cannot turn them into an odd/coprime
shortcut around the even-speed descent.

The incoming HYP-3429 endpoint-spine certificate adds one more compression
rule: survivor components should carry the chosen endpoint labels and branch,
not just a positive window mass.  HYP-3440's geometric role is to present that
spine as a boundary/min-cut certificate without forgetting the endpoint
labels.

The newest branch-cover stack makes the next task concrete.  HYP-3435 already
certifies `135/135` stress rows with active endpoint roles such as `odd_unit`,
`even_R`, `seven_R`, and `14Q`; HYP-3434 identifies the rescue term
`overlap_tax` for negative naive slack; HYP-3437 is still a stub.  Therefore the
next useful HYP-3440-compatible computation is to replace the stub by a finite
incidence graph over `E_safe` components, odd branch-bad blockers, atomic
interval cells, endpoint labels, and proof obligations, then test whether small
Menger cut cores carry the overlap tax on every hard negative-slack row.
