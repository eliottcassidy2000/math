# LRC14 Euler-Mascheroni Harmonic Intercept

The useful Euler-Mascheroni reframing is negative control.  The constant is
the intercept left after subtracting the continuous logarithmic area from a
finite reciprocal tail.  That is exactly the kind of scalar that can look
structural while forgetting the proof object.

On the HYP-3429 endpoint-spine bank, `H_N - log N` tracks scale and window
length, but it does not choose the endpoint certificate.  The `N=84` rows
already collide: the same intercept gives both a mixed `B1+E` certificate and
an E-only branch-specific certificate.  That is the proof-facing signal.

So Euler-Mascheroni belongs next to Meissel-Mertens as a tail normalizer and
firewall.  It can price denominator entropy, compare towers, and warn when a
future analytic average has forgotten wall labels.  It cannot replace the
HYP-3427 wall word or the HYP-3429 endpoint spine.

The next theorem-shaped use is a sidecar rule:

```text
Any harmonic/loglog tail estimate used in the LRC14 covering floor must carry
an endpoint-owner, wall-signature, two-adic loss, sheet, exact-period, or
state-lift sidecar.
```

That rule is modest, but it protects the current proof route from collapsing
the exact finite certificate back into a plausible scalar.
