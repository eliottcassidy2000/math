## 1. The underlying object, as the history has sharpened it

Thirteen nonzero integer speeds; want `t` with all `‖v t‖ ≥ 1/14`. Six years of repo history
(compressed): the object is **not** a measure-theoretic blob and **not** a lattice-point
problem — it is a **self-tuned arithmetic resonance structure on the wall lattice**:

- The continuum problem restricts to the **wall lattice**: the finite set of rational
  breakpoints of `t ↦ min_v ‖vt‖` (denominators ≤ v_i + v_j). Everything extremal happens at
  rationals `a/q`; the (q,a) **witness frame** (safe-band residue systems) is the native chart.
- **Self-tuning**: the extremal directions of the problem are generated by its own modulus
  14 = 2·7. The tight locus of the AP {1..13} is the group μ6 = ⟨3⟩ ⊆ (Z/14)* (opus THM-873);
  equivalently `1/2 + {±1,±2,±3}/7` (opus-S321: Φ₁₄(z) = Φ₇(−z) — the half-turn times the
  heptagon star). The clock moduli are **{7, 13, 14}** = gap denominator / cluster size /
  runner count (THM-878): the only q where the mean Fejes-Tóth deficit vanishes.
- The global minimizer over covering sets is the **deep well {1..12, 182}** at
  `M = 14/183 = n/Φ₆(n)`, the Eisenstein norm resonance (183 = Φ₆(14), 14·13 ≡ −1 mod 183).
- Every extremal family found in three months of adversarial search lies in the **orbit of
  the AP {1..12}** under three moves: *dilate* (`c·AP`, coherent rays V_c, M = 1/13),
  *adjoin a resonant killer* (deep well, ladders `{1..12,182m}`), *renormalize* (difference
  flow: the AP is the fixed point of the two-scale tower, HYP-3901/THM-758). Nothing outside
  this orbit has ever gone below 1/13 while covering.

**The factor-2 reading of why LRC is hard.** At threshold 1/q the elementary union bound
tolerates j < q/2 runners; LRC(n) poses j = n−1 at threshold 1/n — off by exactly a factor 2.
Every successful route is a distinct way of winning that factor: pair/sign structure (Q_s,
THM-877/879/880), moment order 3 (LEM-020: pair data can NEVER certify — vacuity is a
theorem), rigidity (three-gap quantization: detuning jumps M up a CF rung), arithmetic sheets
(prime-13 tightness, divisor gates), or recursion into settled LRC(≤13) (safe-peel, escape).
