# LRC14 Holistic Lineage Synthesis - Codex S160

The central change in the repo's LRC14 work is that "tightness" stopped being
treated as a property visible in a single shadow.  The early shadows were
tempting:

```text
residues mod 14
apex-clock tournament class
additive energy
bounded denominator witnesses
finite q-ladders
single Haar mass
endpoint current
generic H=7 digraph analogy
```

Each one found signal, then failed in a way that preserved information for the
next route.  The residue liar `{1,...,11,13,26}` forced qdiv.  The lcm-tail
families forced adaptive exact periods.  AP versus loose tournament impostors
forced source-labelled spectra and state lifts.  Zero endpoint current in all
audited covering rows forced localized bridge/moment data.  Generic digraphs
realizing `H=7` forced the state lift to land in the tournament conflict
category.

The current object is best described as a source-spectrum packet:

```text
observer-source predicate
exact qdiv/Farey node
regular-open vs closed-boundary support
endpoint owners and taut bridges
C27/unital/K33 labels
fixed-margin family identity
moment/twist/danger-count/Fourier dual coordinates
```

This packet is the common refinement of the recent proof routes.  The labelled
packet classifier, NORK/pinch atlas, Moon-core reductions, boundary-gap bridge,
few-apex lift packet, endpoint-credit winding graph, twist ladder,
multiplicity moment barrier, danger-count dual, and Toeplitz PSD scout are not
rivals.  They are different projections of the same cover/no-cover negation.

The best tournament lesson is also narrower than the prompt's broad slogan.
Yes, any pairwise relation can be made into a tournament after a cutoff and tie
path are declared.  But usefulness depends on the quotient preserving the LRC
predicate.  Runner tournaments and residue tournaments are often too coarse.
The productive tournament vertices are proof carriers: packet families,
endpoint transitions, exact-period sectors, Fourier modes, state-lift packets,
and proof obligations.

The HYP-2976 mine made this visible at corpus scale:

```text
lrc_relevant_docs=6249
strongest co-occurrence:
  AP/GW tight locus <-> tournament/state-lift
  AP/GW tight locus <-> scalar guardrails
  scalar guardrails <-> tournament/state-lift
```

That is a good summary of the repo's history.  AP/GW are equality atoms; scalar
guardrails explain why they cannot be recognized by a scalar; tournament/state
lift remains the route for the nonunit obstruction once the right packet has
been retained.

The counterexample has become extremely constrained:

```text

no unit-petal exit
```

That remaining shape should be named without romance: an F6/F7 labelled
source-spectrum kernel.  If it exists, it should be possible to emit it with
all labels and attack it as a finite Johnson-harmonic sector.  If it does not,
the proof should show it state-lifts to HYP-2908/THM-572.

The practical next proof direction is therefore not another broad invariant.
It is an integration task:

```text
endpoint-owner labels for Toeplitz eigenvectors
endpoint transition graph over NORK rows
multi-chart boundary-moment feasible region
fixed-margin F7 sector definition
state-lift construction from zero-open packet schema
```

No proof was reached in this session.  The progress is that the proof target
has a stable type.  We are no longer asking "what scalar sees LRC14?"  We are
asking whether the labelled source-spectrum packet has any realizable
zero-open section besides AP/GW boundary and K33/H=7 state-lift debt.
