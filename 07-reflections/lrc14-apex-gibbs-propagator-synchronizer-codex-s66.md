# LRC14 Apex Gibbs-Propagator Synchronizer

**codex-2026-06-20-S66.**  The useful synthesis from this pass is that the
physics/neural prompts are not separate routes.  They are different names for
the same current proof split:

```text
free/decorrelated quotient + signed projective deviation + residual synchronizer.
```

The exact scout keeps the Fubini-Study idea rational.  If
`|psi_{t,r}>` is the amplitude vector with coordinates
`sqrt(Pr[t -> s after r iid color hits])`, then the squared projective overlap
with the middle-survival subspace is just the exact middle mass:

```text
cos^2(theta_M) = sum_{s=1}^4 Pr[t -> s after r hits].
```

From the fully missed state `t=6`, two decorrelated hits give
`cos^2(theta_M)=30/49` and expected survival currency `26/49`.  The Gibbs
obstruction ledger is cleaner still: the only negative depth is `t=6` for
zero or one hit, and there are no negative depth coefficients after two hits.
So the HYP-2701 two-far quotient is not itself the enemy.  The enemy is the
signed resonant deviation from that quotient.

Road coloring gives the automaton language.  A word synchronizes a known
missed set exactly when it contains every color in that set; an unknown missed
subset of the six inner colors needs a word containing all six colors.  LRC
does not need full synchronization before the cap comparison.  It needs
partial synchronization into the middle/cap cone, which explains why two hits
already matter even though full synchronization from depth `6` is impossible
that quickly.

The Hopfield/Hebbian and propagator prompts point to the same object: a
density matrix or kernel of obstruction profiles.  Co-firing of missed sectors,
far-runner endpoint ownership, and mod-7 relation packets are all phase
profiles.  Diagonalizing that profile should reveal which low-rank modes spend
the death-chain margin.

The Clifford plus `T` analogy is the most disciplined version of the same
thought.  Clifford/stabilizer is the cut-space/decorrelated quotient, the part
that is classically tractable.  The `T` resource is the relation-lattice or
cycle-space correction.  In LRC terms the missing invariant is a magic-rank
or projective-angle bound: high magic rank should decorrelate, low magic rank
should be finite and routed to AP, cube-root, Freiman, and squarefree-crossing
atlases.

The post-rebase HYP-2707 makes that paragraph much less speculative: on the
tournament side, score is degree `1`, `c3` is degree `2` and Clifford/Gauss-sum
tractable, while `c5`, higher OCF layers, and `H` are higher-degree magic.  The
right LRC analogue of magic rank should therefore measure degree/support of the
mod-7 phase correction, not raw relation count.  HYP-2706 gives the matching
death-chain warning: signed seven-band aggregate margins pass, while per-band
and FOSD quotients fail.  HYP-2705 should keep signed band and phase data until
the last margin comparison.
