# LRC14 L7 Tail45-Delsarte Bridge

Status: codex-2026-06-21-S72 reflection after exact bounded-ratio scout.

The useful correction is that `tail45` has two different lives.  In HYP-2728 it
is a normalized generated-atom separator: it proves that cheap abstract
q0-hiding directions are not generated miss-zeta words.  In L7 row laws,
`tail45=p5+5p6` is only a raw diagnostic.  The S72 tournament makes this
visible: channel risk and raw row `tail45` disagree on `27/91` pairwise edges.
That is a warning against forcing a scalar to carry the whole proof.

The handoff should be:

1. Generated words first: keep the miss-zeta word until compatibility kills the
   cheap atom directions.
2. Delsarte packets second: classify surviving row laws by Krawtchouk-positive
   dual observables and low-support relation packets.
3. Bounded-ratio L7 last: close a finite resonance atlas plus one
   non-resonant two-dimensional discrepancy lemma.

The exact scout was useful because it touched the KPS named row directly:
`(0,2,4,6,8,10,12,25,28)` has `p0=299/1050` and margin
`62911/300300`.  The broader exact bank had `3111` rows and no cap violations.
The worst sampled ratio channel is `5/3`, not `28/25`; that may just be a bank
artifact, but it says the finite atlas should not be designed around only the
named witness.

Several of the user's outside prompts fit this route as analogies with
potential proof value:

- **Gibbs measure.**  The Delsarte dual is an energy on the occupancy law
  `p_t`; Krawtchouk-nonnegative coefficients act like allowed interactions.
  Cheap atom directions are forbidden low-energy states only after the
  generated-word constraint is imposed.
- **Arnold cat map.**  The non-resonant L7 lemma is a torus-discrepancy
  statement for `x -> (f1 x, f2 x)`.  Rational ratios are periodic orbits and
  belong in a finite Markov/resonance atlas; irrational or high-denominator
  ratios should be handled by mixing/discrepancy.
- **Road coloring.**  Sector words form a deterministic finite automaton as
  `x` crosses breakpoints.  The finite atlas should look for synchronizing
  words in denominator/residue classes, not for runner-by-runner inequalities.
- **Fubini-Study metric.**  The atom vector `p_t` lives naturally in a simplex
  or projective cone.  A projective distance between generated atoms and cheap
  directions may be a cleaner compatibility certificate than coordinatewise
  dominance.
- **Hopfield / two-layer network.**  Generated-word compatibility is the hidden
  layer; row-level Delsarte packets are the readout.  Training only the readout
  against arbitrary atom moves overfits the wrong cone.
- **Clifford plus T.**  The cheap directions behave like a classically tractable
  stabilizer subcone; generated non-Clifford packets are the finite obstruction
  that must be certified before the scalar origin atom is evaluated.

Cell-automaton formulation for the next pass: states are `(base-template,
ratio-channel, denominator-residue, sector-word)`.  Transitions increment the
multiplier or merge/split near ratio one.  The proof obligation is to show that
every recurrent component either enters a finite atlas certificate or has a
uniform non-resonant discrepancy gap under the Delsarte readout.

The next computation should not merely add more rows.  It should construct the
residue-period cells for one ratio channel, probably `5/3` because it was the
sampled risk leader, and prove a periodic margin formula for all multipliers in
that channel.
