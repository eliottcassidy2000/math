# Selector sidecars, cycles, first mismatch, and operation saturation

**Status: CURRENT RESEARCH SYNTHESIS, 2026-08-23.**  This note compares proved
canon, cited imports, reproducible finite computations, provisional candidates,
and hostile controls.  It creates no object-level reduction.  LRC(14), JC(2),
DC(2), FC(3), HFC(3), the Arithmetic--Kakeya target, the strong tournament
inequality, and every Rule 30 prize remain **OPEN**.

## Outcome

Four mechanisms survived comparison across the active portfolio.

1. A **filtering selector** merely discards candidates.  A
   **mechanism-changing selector** makes its chosen object eligible for a new
   theorem or operation.  The distinction explains why another residue
   barcode did not finish the factorial frontier, while an LRC multiplier
   divisible by one coefficient can genuinely remove a runner and invoke an
   inherited lower-rank theorem.
2. **Cycles carry second-round debt.**  Forest structure makes the
   Arithmetic--Kakeya `k=1` floor easy; the first triangle changes the sound
   score.  In LRC interval atlases, orbit closure and connected components
   likewise matter after every pairwise width has been computed.  Pairwise
   data are not automatically composable.
3. The **first mismatch** is more informative than another positive replay.
   It identifies the missing coordinate: the prime `19` adaptive killer in a
   factorial row, the `(3,7,2)` LRC pair-only hostile, the polynomial/formal
   split in Jacobian normal strips, or the lower filtered bucket after a
   leading gauge cancellation.
4. A complete static universe should be **saturated before it is optimized**.
   Rule 30's positive-word monoid saturates the odd half of the address space;
   the two-cube elementary layers saturate a Bernoulli Euler product.  In both
   cases the exact complete mass is clearer than a sequence of local lower
   bounds.  Neither saturation theorem supplies the missing physical or
   arithmetic asymptotic by itself.

These are reusable research moves, not a common mathematical object.

## 1. Filtering is not the same as changing the proof mechanism

The exact extension of
[THM-3483](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3483-factorial-nondivisor-residue-digit-pair-compiler.md)
through `r<=10028` gives a clean hostile.  In the window `9980<=d<=10030`,
the final ledger is

```text
9 exit rows, 36 divisor rows, 6 rho rows, no survivors.
```

At `d=9996`, however, the inherited divisor barcodes still leave `3998`
coefficient candidates.  The first ordered adaptive specialization killing
the residual is `p=19`; it is not a unique magical prime.  This refutes only
barcode completeness.  A barcode filters the existing row, but it does not
change which theorem applies to a survivor.

The LRC support-two operation is different.  If a chosen multiplier is
divisible by coefficient `p` or `q`, the corresponding runner is absorbed and
the problem changes rank.  The selector has paid for a new implication.  Its
legitimacy therefore depends on the divisibility witness and the inherited
lower-rank theorem, not on whether the selector looks canonical.

This yields a practical test:

```text
selector output -> which new hypothesis becomes true?
```

If the answer is “none,” the selector is only a filter.  If it changes the
applicable theorem, record the exact source, target, and destroyed runners or
coordinates.

## 2. Cycles are the first place local certificates can acquire debt

[THM-3875](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3875-arithmetic-kakeya-k1-forest-one-round-score-floor.md)
proves that every normalized `k=1` forest certificate has sound score at least
`2`.  A five-row triangle reaches `5/3`, so cycles are necessary for the first
interesting improvement.  This is a structural boundary, not the
Arithmetic--Kakeya target: a finite forcing record is not automatically a
universal lower bound, and the previously used rule-(1) is unsound.

The closest LRC analogy is not “runners are graph vertices.”  It is that
connected obstruction intervals and their cyclic translates must glue after
pairwise widths have been computed.  A pair can be locally hard while a third
deletion closes the row, or locally easy while orbit chronology prevents the
same certificate from serving every translate.  The lawful vertices may be
gaps, sections, boundaries, or proof obligations rather than runners.

The cycle lesson is thus typed:

```text
local edge certificate + acyclic incidence -> composable by peeling;
cycle -> retain a second-round compatibility or circulation sidecar.
```

This suggests the next AK search should enumerate intrinsic minimal cycles at
`k>=2`, with every candidate replayed under the sound rule set, rather than
merely enlarging a forest bank.

## 3. First-mismatch hostiles locate the missing coordinate

Several apparently negative examples are method hostiles, not conjecture
counterexamples.

- The LRC packet `(p,q,s)=(3,7,2)` survives the pair-only absorption test.  It
  is not an LRC equality case; it says the pair statistic omitted another
  runner or scale coordinate.
- At factorial `d=9996`, the inherited barcodes stop before the polynomial
  row does.  The missing coordinate is the response to the next prime.
- In the Jacobian normal-strip program, a formal inverse can be unique while
  polynomial termination fails.  The polynomial/formal comparator must be
  carried explicitly.
- The Russell sextic scouts shear `(6,1)--(6,3)` into known quintic cells and
  kill the principal `(6,5)` branches, but the nonreduced `(6,4)` cell needs a
  strict transform.  The top pole cone forgot the lower principal part.

The correct record for a false method is:

```text
minimal witness;
first failed implication;
strongest surviving statement;
missing coordinate;
cheapest next test.
```

This prevents a method hostile from being advertised as a counterexample to
the underlying open conjecture.

## 5. Filtered kernels and principal parts are the same warning, not the same theorem

The strongest incoming connection is between the THM-3884/3886 residual

[sextic filtered-kernel scout](jc2-sextic-filtered-kernel-principal-parts-cross-frontier-scout-20260823.md).

```text
gr(ker F) -> ker(gr F)
```

need not be onto.  The source and target maps are different, but the failure
anatomy is identical: the associated graded object preserves the top
cancellation and destroys the lower residual/principal-part debt.  The needed
sidecar is the next filtered bucket, and the cheapest decisive test is to lift
one symbol by one layer.

The
[alternative Russell cubic-seed audit](jc2-russell-cubic-seed-quotient-density-cocycle-scout-20260823.md)
adds a determinant version of the same warning.  Constant density on a chosen
section is an artifact; the full source-addition quotient carries a
determinant cocycle, and the raw affine cubic cell is empty.  Rational
corrections remain open.  The determinant is not a decorative Jacobian scalar:
it is the predicate forgotten by the quotient.

The
[composite-depth ordinal synthesis](jc2-composite-depth-ordinals-filtered-liftability-and-hidden-coordinate-portfolio-root-20260823.md)

## 6. Saturating a complete static universe

The all-depth addendum to
[THM-3511](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3511-rule30-orbit-signalizer-gap-renormalization-and-shallow-portrait-hostile.md)
identifies the positive affine-word monoid exactly: ambient active words send
each even ray onto the entire odd half.  Consequently the least literal
two-stage bank for depth `D` and gap budget `B` has

```text
2^(D+B-1)+B+2
```

coordinates, giving `134/256` at `D=B=4`.  This proves an exponential static
tariff.  It does not prove bounded physical recursion, adaptive/projective
chronology, a minimal adaptive bank, or a Rule 30 prize.

The arithmetic analogue is
[THM-3793](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3793-inert-prime-sum-all-scale-two-cube-singleton.md).
Its exact finite inequality sums every elementary inert-prime layer.  The
native normalization uses independent Bernoulli variables with

```text
Prob(X_p=1)=1/(p+1),
E(Z)Prob(N_Z=j)=e_j(Z).
```

Williams' cited fixed-modulus Mertens theorem then gives the proved bound

```text
liminf H(X)sqrt(log log X)/sqrt(log X)
 >=(2/5)sqrt(2/3)E_32
 =0.25659588... .
```

The factor `2/3` is the exact `p=2` invoice.  This is a lower bound for the
deduplicated weighted mass, and trivially for the support count because
`C(X)>=H(X)`; it is not a support asymptotic, residue, or collision law.

Rule 30 and two cubes therefore share a research move:

```text
identify the complete finite operation monoid/product;
normalize its whole mass exactly;
only then optimize the cutoff.
```

They do not share states, probability, chronology, or a reduction.
