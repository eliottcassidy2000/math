## 1. The exact convex connection

THM-3327 and THM-3328 are opposite uses of a half-plane test.

- In THM-3327, a narrow coefficient phase arc sends every positive tensor
  summand into one closed half-plane.  A zero sum is impossible unless all
  terms hit the boundary, and strict mixed positivity then kills that boundary.
- In THM-3328, overlap of two derivative-image inward cones supplies target
  directions lying inside both inverse germs.  Rather than preventing a sum,
  cone intersection creates two simultaneous interior branches.

The shared datum is **orientation inside a cone**.  Support alone cannot see
it.  Rank alone cannot see it.  In the factorial lane the destroyed datum is
coefficient phase; in the Keller lane it is the inward side of a boundary
tangent.  This is a typed connection, not an identification of the objects.

```text
source:     positive tensor terms / labelled inverse directions;
map:        phase sum / derivative image;
preserved:  half-plane containment / cone intersection;
destroyed:  magnitudes and later moments / higher normal jets and winding;
sidecar:    covering arc / oriented inward cone;
test:       mixed boundary term / two inverse-chart expansions.
```

## 2. Gauge equality must name its locus

The THM-3320 cross-audit gives a small exact model of lawful gauge.  Its
half-affine Boolean certificate and zero-constant repeated-tail certificate
differ pointwise by `1-I_1`.  They become equal only after `H_1=q` forces the
empty-pattern mass to zero.  Thus the gauge statement has three required
fields:

```text
two representatives;       their difference;       the locus killing it.
```

The same type check repaired two incoming theorems.

- THM-3319's formal section parameterizes the base germ; adjoining the
  quadratic root creates a distinct rank-two cover over it.
- THM-3321 refutes covariance of one named Hesse torus; it does not classify
  every possible continuous automorphism of the moment ideal.

"Gauge-equivalent" therefore never means "the same physical object."  It
means that an explicit kernel vanishes on an explicit maintained locus.
