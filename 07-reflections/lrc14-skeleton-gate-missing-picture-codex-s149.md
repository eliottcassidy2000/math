# LRC14 Skeleton-Gate Missing Picture - Codex S149

The useful realization in this session is that the recent LRC14 artifacts are
not separate metaphors.  They are different failure detectors for the same
endpoint bottleneck.

After rebasing over the incoming HYP-2952 packet, the layer order became
cleaner: HYP-2952 supplies the transitive six-unit apex-pressure class as a
front filter, while HYP-2960 sits inside that class and asks which labelled
boundary/C27/unital/K33 data still separate AP, GW, petals, impostors, and
state-lift branches.

After the later proof-DAG / boundary-moment / source-spectrum rebase, the
global placement is sharper still.  This packet is not the whole LRC14 proof;
it is the executable `qdiv=14` boundary-source-core subclassifier inside the
HYP-2954 quotient-preserving bridge.  The `qdiv>14` covering residual should be
handled by the HYP-2953 source-spectrum pullback and the boundary-moment map
into gK8/L_y slack, with K33/state-lift packets retained as named kernel debts.
HYP-2955's packet-migration gauntlet is the complementary empirical motion:
most AP-neighborhood packets migrate to qdiv/Haar-positive fronts; this note
keeps the labelled skeleton for the boundary-only cases that remain.

Haar/Baire gives the outer switch: either a row has positive strict safe mass,
or it lives only on boundary support.  AP and Goddyn-Wong are the two local
boundary-only rows in the checked neighborhood.  But they have the same active
boundary owner skeleton, so this lens is intentionally blind to the
Goddyn-Wong transfer.  That blindness is not a flaw; it tells us exactly where
the next label must be carried.

The next labels are the Jacobsthal gate and C27/unital transfer.  The gate
says only `12` can be the hidden single acceleration.  The C27/unital label
then says which `12` branch we are in: `D3` is the tight GW branch, while `D9`
is the open K33/Farey-child branch.  The unit-petal branches are also open.

The new script made the "residues lie" warning concrete again: `12->26` has the
AP relative-profile hashes, but it fails the covering ledger and has positive
strict Haar mass `426/35035`.  So raw residues and apex-style tournaments
cannot be the final invariant.  They are evidence only after divisibility and
the exact witness layer survive.

The most interesting fingerprint is the proof-lens tournament.  It is not
transitive.  It has one strongly connected pocket:

```text
Jacobsthal_gate, derived_relative_profile, K33_state_lift
```

This looks like the missing packet.  The gate determines the legal site, the
derived profile remembers the first-fold class after coarse shells collapse,
and K33 says how to route the `D9` branch.  Trying to totally order these three
is probably the wrong move.  They need to travel together until the proof
chooses either the boundary branch or the state-lift branch.

Current best theorem shape:

```text
qdiv=14 and no strict Haar witness
  -> AP/GW boundary skeleton
  -> hidden single acceleration is 12
  -> C27/unital split D3 versus D9/unit
  -> D3 gives GW; D9/unit branches open or state-lift
```

What remains is global, not local: prove any primitive bad atom reaches this
skeleton-gate language, or prove that the complement always opens a witness
front or a HYP-2908/THM-572 state-lift packet.
