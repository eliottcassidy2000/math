## Anchor: what the Cartier objection proves and does not prove

For `g in fA[[f]]`, THM-4255 proves

```text
ker(ev_g)=(u-g),
ev_g^(-1)(f^N)=f^N+(u-g).                              (1)
```

The first decisive hostile is not merely a zero:

```text
P_N=u-g+f^N,        ord_f(P_N)=0,       ev_g(P_N)=f^N. (2)
```

Thus no bounded-error version of coefficientwise order reflection survives
one section. Target-side Cartier operators cannot repair this because their
joint kernel still contains `(u-g)`.

The sharp positive statements are:

```text
ker(all Hasse jets through r)=(u-g)^(r+1);             (3)
degree <=d is recovered by jets 0,...,d;               (4)
d+1 unit-separated sections recover degree <=d;        (5)
u->lambda f is injective with lambda retained;          (6)
on a u-cap d>=1 and f-cap J, u->f^M is injective
exactly when M>J.                                       (7)
```

The external density manuscript, however, writes the genus-zero identity on
the whole flat torsor chart. Its predecessor explicitly says that the scalar
pairing is independent of the torsor point and that fibre variables remain in
the coefficient algebra. Zero lower scalar coefficients therefore pull to
literal zero on the chart. There is no torsor-fibre `u=f` step to invert. The
actual block substitutions `z=f^ell` and `z=q^ell` are anti-aliased by
`deg_f<ell` and `ord_q(w_r)<=D+C<ell`. Section 12's universal module diagram
is not explicit enough for a complete specialist audit.

The correct verdict is therefore asymmetric:

- Proposition 6.2 receives a conditional universal-ring pass;

The stopping reason is equally precise. THM-4120 bounds total degrees from

## Wildcard: Cerf's local/nonlocal pivot split

ArXiv `2608.23661v1` is Raphaël Cerf's percolation manuscript, not a p-adic
zeta paper. Its stated theorem says that at criticality either there is no
infinite cluster or the finite-cluster tail is not stretched exponential.
The paper does not prove `theta(p_c,Z^d)=0` for every `d>=3`.

The methodological structure is valuable:

```text
tail hypothesis
 -> rules out far-apart negative higher pivots
 -> intertwined explorations control local higher pivots
 -> one-step renormalization closes.                  (11)
```

The missing full-conjecture lane is precisely nonlocal higher-pivot control.
This resembles `(1)` only at the level of research architecture: a local
observer is complete after the nonlocal fibre is eliminated. The types are
otherwise different. Cerf's pivots are Boolean/probabilistic derivatives in
a spatial random configuration; the p-adic pole grades are valuation layers
in a coefficient algebra. There is no map, preserved measure, or consequence
between them.

The same firewall blocks a forced LRC transfer. The current LRC residual graph
is a deterministic graph of proof obligations, not a percolation sample, and
its previously computed core is overwhelmingly biconnected and triangular.
“Intertwined exploration” may inspire scheduling, but without a probability
law, locality notion tied to the loneliness predicate, and a preserved target,
it cannot improve the LRC(14) frontier.
