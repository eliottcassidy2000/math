## 3. Why these are not one theorem

| Mechanism | Source to target | What it destroys | Why another lane is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pascal heredity | larger fixed label chart to its faces | arbitrary-row origin | affine entry must first reach the chart |
| projective entry | one anchored body to a scaled fixed-pool body | literal label identity | anchor and tails must remain fixed |
| literal implication | complete safe set to one new constraint | none, hence it is rigid | adaptive or measure maps must replace it |
| component escape | one addressed safe interval to physical odd tails | other components and total mass | global surplus can supply existence/cofinality |
| rank-three surplus | wall cells to a scalar lower certificate | address, owners, arrival, rank `>=4` mass | component and entry sidecars cannot be inferred |

This table is also the tournament boundary. At rank two the retained object is
a genuine undirected weighted graph. At rank three, triple overlaps enter with
their own inclusion--exclusion sign. No pair orientation can reconstruct that
term. A tournament model here would be cosmetic and would lose the exact
predicate being proved.

The same warning applies to ordinal compression. Raw tick numerators from
different wall grids are not comparable natural-number ranks. A valid discrete
address is at least `(pair, body mask, ticks/D, certificate type)`. One may
enumerate those records by a natural number, but the denominator and type are
load-bearing sidecars.
