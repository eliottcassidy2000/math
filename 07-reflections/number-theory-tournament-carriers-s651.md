# Number-Theory Tournament Carriers S651

Reserved for the S651 long session on how tournaments fit into number theory.

Initial claim: tournaments enter number theory as pairwise quotients of richer
arithmetic carriers.  The session will test this on prime-race residue
tournaments, local obstruction product ledgers, and proof-route tournaments for
hard number-theory problems.

The important guardrail is role typing.  Vertices need not be numbers or
primes; plausible vertices include residue classes, local moduli, zero modes,
Frobenius traces, divisor strata, height packets, and proof obligations.
