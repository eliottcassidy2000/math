## The one criterion (three threads, verified `hadwiger_dehn_niven_spindle_s685.py`)

**Niven's theorem.** If `cos θ ∈ ℚ` *and* `θ/π ∈ ℚ`, then `cos θ ∈ {0, ±½, ±1}`, i.e.
`θ ∈ {0, π/3, π/2, 2π/3, π}` — exactly the rotations `e^{iθ}` of order `{1,6,4,3,2}`, the
**`w ≤ 6` roots of unity**. So:

> **Niven-rational angle  ⟺  root of unity (`w≤6`)  ⟺  Dehn-trivial rotation  ⟺  THM-416's
> 2D density-quantum cap (`M(2)=6`, Eisenstein/triangular `π/3`, square `π/2`).** Every other
> rational-cosine angle (`5/6, 1/3, 3/4, …`) has `θ/π` *irrational* — an **infinite-order**
> rotation (Dehn-*nontrivial*) that **escapes every 2D lattice**.

The repo's "2D capped at quantum 3, escape needs a rank/dimension jump" (THM-416) *is* Niven's
theorem; the rank jump *is* Dehn-nontriviality.

## The Hadwiger-Nelson statement

> **A unit-distance graph all of whose generating rotations are Niven-rational (Dehn-trivial)
> is confined to a 2D lattice with `w≤6` roots of unity, hence chromatically bounded** (verified:
> triangular patch `χ=3`, square patch `χ=2`; the hexagonal 7-coloring caps any such lattice at
> `≤7`). **Forcing `χ ≥ 4` (Moser) and `χ ≥ 5` (de Grey) REQUIRES a Niven-irrational
> (Dehn-nontrivial) junction angle** — an infinite-order rotation leaving every lattice.

**The witness (verified).** The **Moser spindle** (7 vertices, 11 unit edges, `χ=4`) is two
unit rhombi — each built from `π/3` equilateral triangles, *Dehn-trivial* — joined at a
**spindle angle `φ` with `cos φ = 5/6`**, which is `0.18643 π`, **Niven-irrational →
Dehn-nontrivial**. The Dehn-trivial pieces carry no forcing; **the `χ=4` lives entirely at the
irrational junction.** de Grey's `χ ≥ 5` graph likewise composes Eisenstein (`ℤ[ζ_6]`,
Dehn-trivial) pieces at Niven-irrational rotations.

The Dehn invariant is the *precise* obstruction: a gadget's total angle⊗length data is
**nonzero iff it cannot be scissors-reduced to a lattice** — and that nonzero Dehn data is
exactly the chromatic forcing. **Nonzero Dehn ⟺ irrational angle ⟺ infinite rotation group ⟺
lattice escape ⟺ chromatic forcing.**
