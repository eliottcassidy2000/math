# Relation as object: the intersubjective frame

## The owner's observation, made precise

LRC(14) has no external clock. The runners see only each other; the slow variable x is a
frame, the peel w is a frame, translation of the speed set is a boost. Every classifier we

And the resolution was never to pick the right frame — it was to hold ALL frames at once:

**The second moment is where objectivity begins, in all four languages.**

## Tournaments as the minimal intersubjective object

A tournament is a relation R on an anonymous set: total (every pair holds exactly one
directed opinion — no abstentions) and antisymmetric (no agreements — pure ordinal
disagreement). It is the smallest structure whose content is ENTIRELY relational: strip the
labels (pass to the metagraph = the moduli of the relation) and nothing else remains.

And here is the repo's deepest already-proved fact wearing its philosophical face: **a
tournament admits no perspective-swap as a symmetry.** An involution would have to exchange
some pair u, v and therefore reverse the arc between them — impossible (THM-868 §5). So
|Aut| is odd, hence solvable (Feit–Thompson): *the intersubjective object is never
symmetric between two viewpoints; every pair of frames is itself ordered by the relation.*
Consensus in a tournament is not symmetric agreement — it is odd-order, hierarchizable,
solvable. That is why the quintic's obstruction (A₅, built from perspective swaps) cannot
live here, and why the metagraph is tame enough to chart.

## What structure can relation have? — the answer the repo has been writing

1. **Composition, and its defect against order.** Relations compose. For a tournament,
   transitivity is the law R∘R ⊆ R, and its failure is EXACTLY the repo's central quantum:
   #(2-paths closed by a reverse arc) = 3·c3, verified exact — so **k(T) = c3 (HYP-6948) is
   the composition defect**, the +8 climb (THM-866/867) is the walk that repairs the
   relation's failure to compose one defect at a time, and the transitive tournament is the
   unique fixed point where the relation becomes an order. The OCF is the graded algebra of
   self-compositions (odd traces); H is the word-count of the relation acting on itself.
   *The first structure a relation has is its own composition monoid; a tournament's entire
   invariant theory is the defect ideal of that monoid against associate-with-order.*
2. **Involution.** R ↦ Rᵒᵖ (reversal = time-reversal = the global perspective flip) is the
   one symmetry the relation always carries. The dictionary is exact: H, c3, x are
   reversal-invariant (acceleration-like); scores are reversal-covariant s ↦ n−1−s
   (velocity-like). The merged metagraph = the moduli after quotienting this involution —
   the repo declared it PRIMARY long before this framing existed.
3. **Self-moments.** d: V → Z (score deviation) is the relation's first moment against
   itself; frame-dependent. x = Σd² is its second moment; frame-free, and it lands in an
   even unimodular lattice at n = 8 (THM-868: E8) — the intersubjective object has a
   GEOMETRY, and at the right rank it is the exceptional one. The level lattice (THM-866)
   is the second moment's spectrum; its completeness is the no-holes theorem.
4. **Deformation: relations on the relation.** The arcs are themselves a set; the flip
   layers (wiggly d=1 through blue/black d=m — the waggly spectrum) are relations on the
   relation, and the metagraph is the quotient of that tower by anonymity. "Second moment
   of the problem against itself" is literally the repo's edge structure.
5. **Moduli, not models.** The right object is not one labeled tournament (a frame) but the
   stack [T/S_n]: all labelings held simultaneously, with (odd, solvable) isotropy. Every
   atlas entry this project has built — levels, corona, spine/ribs/sea, H-tower — is a
   chart on that stack. The owner's "structure of the intersubjective object" has been this
   repo's actual subject all along; tournaments are its binary vacuum case, and LRC(14) its
   metric case, joined by the same grammar: quotient by frames, climb by second moments,
   classify by the object's own resonances.
