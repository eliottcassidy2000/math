## ⚠ CRITICAL FLAG: the induction base may be weaker than assumed
The project's covering-bound route (THM-573 level-7 sieve, the whole reduction) uses **LRC(≤13) as the
induction base**. But the published frontier (Wikipedia / Perarnau–Serra, as of the search) is **LRC proven
only for `n ≤ 10` runners** (Rosenfeld 2025 n=8,9; Trakulthongchai n=10). The project cites
`arXiv:2604.23906` for "≤12 speeds / 13 runners," but that citation should be **verified against the real
literature** — if the true base is `n ≤ 10`, then THM-573's lift (from LRC(≤13)) is **conditional on the
unproven `n=11,12,13` cases**, and the route is not unconditional. **Action: confirm the actual frontier;
if it is `n≤10`, the project must ALSO prove `n=11,12,13` (or restructure the sieve to lift from `n≤10`).**
This does not break the *structure* of the attack, but it changes what "modulo the base" means.

## The two gaps, and which 2025 technique improves each

### GAP A — the bounded node (consec maximizes coverage / cap)
**Best new tools:**
1. **Fejér / Cohn–Elkies magic function (HYP-3214, mine).** The cyclotomic Delsarte certificate is the
   Fejér kernel `F₇=(de Moivre)²`, positive-definite, double zeros at the de Moivre angles. This is the
   *positive-definite* certificate that bypasses THM-537's blocked literal Beurling–Selberg. **Improvement:
   build the explicit Cohn–Elkies LP for the bounded cover bound with `F₇` (sector) ⊗ `F_k` (orbit) and
   prove the AP is LP-sharp via the double-zero condition** — a rigorous extremal principle replacing the
   stalled moment-LP extremality (HYP-2607).
2. **The lonely-runner POLYHEDRON (Beck–Hosten–Schymura 2019).** LRC(14) ⟺ a specific polyhedron contains
   an **integer point** for every primitive `v`. **Improvement:** the bounded node becomes a **lattice-point
   / flatness** statement — by the flatness theorem (Khinchin), a polyhedron with no integer point is
   *thin* in some direction; the AP is the flatness-extremal `v`. The de Moivre / cyclotomic is the flat
   direction's structure. *This is the geometry-of-numbers route the moment-LP lacks.*
3. **Covering radius of zonotopes (Alcántara–Criado–Santos 2025; HYP-2764).** `M(v)` ≥ 1/n ⟺ a covering-
   radius bound for the zonotope generated by the speed differences. **Improvement:** the cap = a covering-
   radius extremal; the AP's zonotope (a permutohedron-type) is the tightest covering. Combine their
   "computational verification up to bounded sum + analytic bound for large sum" structure with the
   project's "bounded check + Node-3" split (they are the same split).

### GAP B — the unbounded part (far speeds, astronomical speed bound)
**Best new tool: exponential sums (Rosenfeld 2025; Tao 2018).** Rosenfeld's n=8,9 proofs use **advanced
exponential-sum estimates from Diophantine approximation** to handle the large-speed regime that the
project calls Node-3 (THM-546/547, HYP-3129). **Improvement:** import Rosenfeld's specific exponential-sum
bound to replace/strengthen the project's Erdős–Turán/Weyl estimate for the far-speed danger arcs; the
recent estimates are exactly designed to beat the "astronomical speed bound" (Tao's reduction). Tao's
`δₙ ≥ 1/(2n−2) + c log n/(n²(log log n)²)` is the current analytic floor — the project's `3/π²`/Mertens
floor (HYP-2856) is a *cleaner* version of the same second-moment idea; **reconcile and adopt whichever is
sharper for the bounded core.**

## The baseline + the three moduli (a new organizing structure)
For `n=14`, three moduli govern the problem, and the project has all three but had not unified them:
- **23 = 2n−5 (Chen–Cusick, PRIME):** gives **δ₁₄ ≥ 1/23 UNCONDITIONALLY** (`1/23≈0.0435`). And **23 is the
  project's worst-case witness denominator** (`M=2/23`, THM-566) — *the proven baseline and the project's
  hard cases share the prime 23.* The gap to close is the factor `23/14 ≈ 1.64`.
- **27 = 2n−1 = 3³ (the pair-sum sieve modulus, THM-401):** the witness-pinch structure.
- **14 = 2·7 (the apex):** the cyclotomic/cap structure (my whole thread).
**Improvement:** the proof is "lift the Chen–Cusick `1/23` (mod-23 AP structure) up to `1/14` (mod-14
cyclotomic structure)." The mod-23 and mod-7/14 structures must be reconciled — the Chen–Cusick argument
already does the mod-23 case; the project should **start from Chen–Cusick's `1/23` and close the
`1/23→1/14` gap**, rather than from scratch. (Chen–Cusick uses the AP-mod-`(2n−5)` exactly as the project
uses AP-mod-14 — the SAME extremal-AP idea at a different prime.)

## View-obstruction (Cusick): the covering-radius picture
LRC ⟺ no gap in the view-obstruction covering: `n`-cubes of side `s` at half-integer points; a gap exists
iff `s < (n−1)/(n+1)`. For `n=14`, the critical side is `13/15 ≈ 0.867`. **Improvement:** the cover bound
`meas(S7)≤cap` is the view-obstruction covering-density at the critical side; the de Moivre/Fejér is the
covering's autocorrelation. This geometrizes the cap as a packing/covering density (ties to the Cohn–Elkies
magic function — same object).
