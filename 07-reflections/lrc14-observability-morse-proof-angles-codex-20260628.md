# LRC14 Observability And Morse Proof-Angle Reflection

The productive move this session was to stop treating the recent routes as
competing scalar attacks.  HYP-3243 already gave the finite chamber carrier
atlas.  HYP-3244 gave the lift/compress descent discipline.  HYP-3245 gave a
lag/autocorrelation projection.  The new synthesis is that these are not the
proof by themselves; they are the rows and columns of a forgetting audit.

Two proof targets now look clean.

First, build a sidecar observability matrix.  Rows should be actual residual
pairs where a quotient could change LRC status.  Columns should be retained
sidecars: endpoint owner, boundary cocircuit, Phi witness address, dilation
grid, Toeplitz slack, Green resistance, odd-negative payload, Lee-Yang root
word, tiling descent packet, lag transport, unit-equioscillation index,
binding complement-pair word, analytic/topological index equalizer, Gauss-sum
index word, Borsuk-Ulam forcing-gap flag, Roth-Halasz discrepancy,
Hensel-Krasner unit, state-lift H7, Cech hole, and ear payload.  A quotient is safe only when every
status-changing pair is separated or legally discharged.

Second, put a discrete Morse function on the finite chamber packet after the
observability columns are attached.  The energy should be vector-valued:
tope depth, Toeplitz deficit, Green excess, lag transport cost,
unit-equioscillation defect, index-equality defect, Gauss-sum word depth,
Borsuk-Ulam forcing-gap status, root collision depth, odd-negative debt,
chart-change depth, Hensel unit failure, and state-lift distance.  The target is an acyclic matching, not a prettier
potential.  Critical cells must be strict open cells, AP/Goddyn-Wong Phi14
equality, Phi14d dilation equality, state-lift H=7 contradiction, or named
debt.

The best cross-disciplinary connection is coding/control theory.  The proof
does not need a universal scalar; it needs an observability basis.  That
absorbs the Delsarte/autocorrelation work naturally: weight enumerators and
lag laws are powerful projections, but they are not legal unless the syndrome
columns they forget are either reconstructible or irrelevant to the row.

The second useful connection is discrete Morse theory joined to electrical
networks.  Toeplitz and Green quantities stop being just "signals" and become
descent coordinates.  HYP-3225/HYP-3236 already show AP uniqueness in these
coordinates on the bounded bank; the missing proof-style statement is that
nonterminal chambers have descending walls and no cyclic descent traps remain
after odd-negative and scale-lift debt are retained.

The incoming Chebyshev/index frame fits the same way.  HYP-3246/HYP-3247 give
the unit-equioscillation index and binding complement-pair word; HYP-3248/HYP-3249
add q-uniform Chebyshev and bold index-prediction sidecars.  Those are
excellent boundary/core columns, not permission to forget the chamber descent.
Incoming HYP-3253 makes the warning stricter: the index theorem describes the
tight saddle, but the proof still needs S-dependent floor or margin data,
contact holonomy, or a finite endpoint-chamber lift.
Incoming HYP-3255 says the same in arithmetic language: the `Q(sqrt-7)`
residue layer organizes binding pairs and QR/NQR signs, while the tight/loose
decision is magnitude-level exact-cover rigidity.
Incoming HYP-3257/HYP-3258 add the concrete missing columns: the unit active
gradient has rank `3`, so the observability matrix must retain the blind
residue/height ledger, strict safe-component atlas, covering `14`-multiple
kill switch, and binding/covering census split.
The incoming S289 discrepancy/lifting packet also fits only as columns.  The
Roth-Halasz bound may control bulk chambers, and Hensel-Krasner may stabilize
the scale-lift local unit.  Neither should be trusted as a terminal row until it
separates actual residual pairs.

The next concrete computation should instantiate HYP-3300 on real packet rows:
HYP-3202 traps, HYP-3225 trap sidecar classes, HYP-3236 Green decoys,
HYP-3244 lift/compress failures, HYP-3245 lag-transport residuals, and
HYP-3248/HYP-3249/HYP-3253/HYP-3255/HYP-3257/HYP-3258 index-prediction,
floor-margin, residue, nullspace, census, and magnitude boundaries.  For
each candidate column subset, list unresolved row collisions.  Those collisions
are exactly the next proof obligations.
