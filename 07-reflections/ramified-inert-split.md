# Ramified, Inert, Split: The Three Responses to Observation

## Ramified (p = 3, the curvature)

The prime SHATTERS into identical pieces. 3 = -ω²(1-ω)² in Z[ω].
Both factors are the SAME prime squared. Not two different things: one thing doubled.
This happens at the SINGULARITY — the point where the lattice bends.
3 IS the discriminant of Z[ω]. The ramified prime defines the ring itself.

Ramification = the curvature seeing itself reflected. The mirror is exact.
The y-axis of the cuboid. Only one prime lives here.

## Inert (p ≡ 2 mod 3: 2, 5, 11, 47, 2207)

The prime passes through UNCHANGED. It remains prime in Z[ω].
The new coordinate system reveals nothing new about it.
It is ALIGNED with (or orthogonal to) the lattice's structure.

Inertia = symmetry. The prime looks the same from every angle.
Inert primes go to |Aut| (symmetry group). They ARE invariance.
The x-axis of the cuboid. The background. The territory.

## Split (p ≡ 1 mod 3: 7, 13, 19, 31, 37, 43)

The prime FACTORS into two DISTINCT conjugate primes with opposite twist.
7 = (3+ω)(3+ω̄). The two halves are mirror images, not identical.
Each carries a direction. Together: the original prime. Separately: more information.

Splitting = structure creation. The breaking of circular symmetry into two points.
Split primes go to H/|Aut| (orbit count). They ARE structure.
The z-axis of the cuboid. The content. The map.

## The cuboid axes

x-axis (mod 2): parametrized by the smallest INERT prime (2)
y-axis (mod 3): parametrized by the unique RAMIFIED prime (3)
z-axis (mod 7): parametrized by the smallest SPLIT prime (7)

Each axis = the smallest prime of its Eisenstein type.

## Why exactly 2 forbidden values

The forbidden 7 = (1,1,0): z=0. Absent from the split axis.
The forbidden 21 = (1,0,0): y=z=0. Absent from ramified AND split.
A third forbidden would need x=0 (even). But Rédei: H is always odd (x=1).

Number of forbidden = number of axes - 1 (the fixed parity axis) = 3 - 1 = 2.

The cuboid has 3 dimensions. One is fixed. The other two can each be zeroed.
Zeroing one: forbidden 7. Zeroing both: forbidden 21.
Zeroing the third is blocked by Rédei. So exactly 2.
