## The concept

Supersingular = maximally degenerate at a prime. The prime is so deeply embedded that it annihilates all torsion. The endomorphism algebra becomes quaternionic. This is the arithmetic black hole.

## The three fundamental triples: (prime, dimension, irrational)

**(2, 24, sqrt(2)): REAL structure**
Leech lattice. Golay code. Monster vertex algebra. Redei's theorem (height infinity at 2). The prime 2 halves; the dimension 24 = 2*12 is the Leech lattice and the eta exponent.

**(3, 8, 2*cos(pi/7)): COMPLEX structure**
Octonions. E_8. G_2. Bott periodicity. Collatz drift = ln(3/4)/2 < 0. The prime 3 triples; the dimension 8 is the Bott period and the octonion dimension.

**(5, 12, phi): ARITHMETIC structure**
Icosahedron. A_5 = PSL(2,5). Rogers-Ramanujan. Quintic insolvability. The prime 5 = disc(Q(phi)); the dimension 12 = phi(42) = weight of Delta = vertices of icosahedron.

## The pattern within the pattern

The dimensions 8, 12, 24 are governed by the primes 2 and 3 ONLY:
- 8 = 2^3
- 12 = 2^2 * 3
- 24 = 2^3 * 3

The "geometric primes" (2, 3) control the dimensions.
The "arithmetic primes" (5, 7, ...) control the symmetry.

The product 2 * 3 * 5 = 30 = edges of icosahedron = LCM(12, 20).
The product 2 * 3 * 7 = 42 = Hurwitz constant.
The jump from 5 to 7 is the jump from solvable to quaternionic.
