## Sexy-Prime Connection

The sexy-prime conjecture connection remains local and structural.  The LRC
tournament machinery is good at organizing admissible residue channels and
detecting scalar false positives.  It does not supply the analytic
prime-distribution estimate needed for infinitely many prime pairs at gap six.
For this repo, the useful transfer is therefore one-way: sexy-prime local
sieve bookkeeping can be represented as a low-priority sidecar, while LRC14
closure still depends on finite packet/observer certificates.
