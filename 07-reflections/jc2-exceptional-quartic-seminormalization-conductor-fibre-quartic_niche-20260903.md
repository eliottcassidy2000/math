# Exceptional quartic: radicalizing the conductor exposes the missing graph class

**Session reflection, 2026-09-03.**  Current truth is THM-4381 and its cited
dependencies; this note records the search move and the remaining questions.

## Outcome

The productive operation was not another stable-order lift.  It was passage
from the degree-178 conductor

```text
c=L^2 h_172,                 L=x(x^2-1),
```

to its radical `r=Lh_172`, followed by the restriction map on the finite
conductor fibre.  The exact image rank is 87.  Since the radical has 175
geometric points, the known retained triple consumes one fibre and the other
172 points are forced into 86 pairs.  This yields one ordinary plane triple,
86 ordinary nodes, and the explicit seminormalization `S+K r`.

The important distinction is that the retained triple is ordinary but not
seminormal.  Its value equalizer allows a three-dimensional first-jet space;
the planar embedding supplies only the two-dimensional plane generated by the
`C` and `E` derivative rows.  The missing line is exactly the class of `r`.

## Why the rank collapses to a small certificate

The naive exact calculation row-reduced all 89 restrictions in 175
coordinates and returned rank 87.  The Apery filtration explains why only a
three-vector calculation is needed.  The 86 canonical basis elements below
degree 175 remain independent modulo monic `r`.  Only degrees 175, 176, and
177 wrap around.  Reducing those three modulo `r` and then through the exact
gap normal form leaves a rank-one residual.  This `86+1` certificate is both
faster and conceptually sharper than a dense rank computation.

This is an instance of two repository method cards:

1. classify the target over the exceptional stratum before closing by
   density; and
2. compare the full common-target algebra with the graph equalizer before
   trusting incidence.
