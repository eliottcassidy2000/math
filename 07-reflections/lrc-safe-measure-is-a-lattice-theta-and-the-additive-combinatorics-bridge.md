# The LRC safe-measure is a lattice theta-function — and where the additive-combinatorics bridge holds (and breaks)

*mac-mini-2026-07-03-S36. Prompted by the owner to explore connections to Gonçalves–Radchenko
"Sharp Lower Bounds for Sumsets in Hypercubes" (arXiv:2607.01458) and the Bourgain–Dilworth–Ford–
Konyagin–Kutzarova (BDFKK) RIP/additive-combinatorics circle.*

## The exact reformulation
For a speed set `S={v_1,…,v_13}`, the **safe measure** at radius `1/14` is a theta-sum over the
**relation lattice** `L(S) = {m∈ℤ^13 : Σ m_i v_i = 0}`:
```
   μ(S) = meas{t : ||v_i t|| ≥ 1/14 ∀i}  =  Σ_{m ∈ L(S)}  ∏_i c(m_i),
   c(0) = 6/7,   c(m) = −\hat{1_D}(m) = −sin(πm/7)/(πm)  (m≠0),   c(m)=0 iff 7|m.
```
*Derivation:* expand `∏(1−1_{D_i})` and Fourier-expand each danger indicator `1_{D_i}(t)=Σ_m \hat{1_D}(m)
e(m v_i t)`; the `t`-integral kills everything except `Σ m_i v_i = 0`. This is opus's HYP-4058 measure
form, now read as a **lattice theta function** `Θ_{L(S)}(c)` with the multiplicative weight `∏ c(m_i)`.

Verified (`additive_energy_lrc`): truncating `|m_i|≤K` gives `μ` slowly (c-weights decay like `1/|m|`):
AP/GW (tight, `μ=0`) vs `{2..14}` (loose, `μ=0.061`) at `K=3` read `0.075, 0.074` vs `0.093` — the right
order, slow convergence.

## The two gaps become lattice-theta statements
- **GAP-A (tight ⟺ `μ=0`)** is **exact theta-cancellation**: `Θ_{L(S)}(c)=0`. The AP `{1,…,13}` has the
  richest relation lattice — the "discrete second-derivative" lattice generated by `(…,1,−2,1,…)` — the
  densest in short vectors; its theta-sum cancels the main term `(6/7)^13` exactly. GAP-A = *the only
  relation lattices whose weighted theta cancels are the AP's and GW's.* This is an **inverse/rigidity
  theorem for a lattice theta** — Freiman-flavoured (the `μ`-extremizers are arithmetic progressions).
- **GAP-B (covering ⟹ `μ>0`)** is **theta positivity**: bound the correction `Σ_{m≠0}∏c(m_i)` above
  `−(6/7)^13`. The dominant corrections are the **short relations** (few nonzero coords, small `|m_i|`):
  a 2-term relation `m_i v_i = −m_j v_j` has `|m_i|=v_j/g,|m_j|=v_i/g` (`g=gcd`), contributing
  `(6/7)^{11}c(v_j/g)c(v_i/g)`. **Counting/【bounding】short relations is exactly additive energy** —
  the BDFKK / GR machinery.

## Where the bridge HOLDS and where it BREAKS (the honest part)
- **BREAKS — naive additive energy is the WRONG invariant.** `E(S)=#{a+b=c+d}` is **translation-
  invariant**, but `μ` is **not**: `{1,…,13}` (tight, `μ=0`) and `{2,…,14}` (loose, `μ=0.061`) have the
  *identical* additive energy `1469`. So no purely additive functional of `S` can decide LRC-tightness.
  The missing ingredient is **arithmetic**: the radius `1/14=1/(2·7)` puts the weight `c` on `ℤ`, with
  `c(m)=0 ⇔ 7|m` (the "heptagon" 7-Fourier-zeros). LRC-tightness = additive structure of `L(S)` **coupled
  to** the mod-7 arithmetic of the coordinates. Pure sumset theory (GR) sees only the former.
- **HOLDS — the weighted/relation-lattice version is the right home.** GAP-B is a genuine
  "bound-the-short-vectors-of-a-relation-lattice" problem, which is what BDFKK's product-set-sumset
  estimates and Bourgain-type exponential-sum bounds control; GR's **hypercube** `{−K,…,K}^13` is exactly
  the domain of the *bounded* relations `L(S)∩[−K,K]^13` that dominate the correction. Ford–Konyagin's
  **covering-systems** expertise is the natural lens for the covering constraint (multiples of each `q≤14`).

## The second obstruction (verified) — short relations do NOT dominate
I tested whether the additive-energy-controlled part (2-term relations) approximates `μ`. It does **not**:
the 2-term mass is `≈ +0.11` for AP/GW/loose alike, while the *total* correction for a tight family must be
`−(6/7)^13 = −0.135`. So the higher-order (3,4,…-term) relations carry `≈ −0.25` and **dominate with
essential cancellation** — `μ` is not short-relation-controlled. Hence even the *weighted* additive-energy
(short-relation) bound is insufficient by itself: the theta-sum's cancellation is genuinely all-orders.
This is a **second, independent obstruction** to the BDFKK/GR route, on top of translation-invariance.

## Net (what this buys — honest)
1. An **exact reformulation**: GAP-B (covering-min) = `Θ_{L(S)}(c) > 0` for every primitive covering `S`;
   GAP-A = `Θ_{L(S)}(c)=0 ⟹ S∈{AP,GW}` (theta-rigidity / inverse theorem, companion to GR's sumset-
   extremizers-are-APs).
2. **Two precise obstructions to the BDFKK/GR route, documented so nobody chases it in vain:**
   (i) additive energy is **translation-invariant** so cannot decide tightness (`{1..13}` vs `{2..14}`);
   the decisive ingredient is the **mod-7 radius arithmetic** (`14=2·7`, the apex-7 `c(m)=0⇔7|m` weight),
   not the lattice; (ii) short relations do **not** dominate — the theta cancellation is genuinely
   **all-order** (2-term mass `+0.11` vs. required `−0.135`), so even a *weighted* additive-energy bound
   is insufficient.
3. Verdict: the pure additive-combinatorics bridge (GR/BDFKK as-is) does **not** finish the covering-min.
   What survives is the **exact lattice-theta picture** and a sharp diagnosis of the two failure modes —
   the covering-min needs an *all-order, mod-7-weighted* theta-positivity argument (circle-method /
   singular-series flavour, which is where klein's `L_C`=singular-series THM-515 and opus's decorrelation
   THM-611 already live), not a sumset inequality.

So this exploration is largely a **well-characterized dead end for a direct additive-combinatorics proof**,
but it yields the exact theta reformulation and pinpoints why — useful negative knowledge for the measure
route. See HYP-4071; script `additive_energy_lrc_macmini_20260703.py`.
