# ABC, alien copies, and arithmetic task atlases

**Research synthesis -- 2026-08-23.**  This session merged the ABC conjecture
and ideas from inter-universal Teichmuller theory into the repository without
promoting a disputed claim.  The mathematical output is
[THM-3833](../01-canon/theorems/THM-3833-abc-conditional-cube-radical-and-hyperbolic-power-finiteness.md),
a **PROVED CONDITIONAL IMPLICATION** from ABC plus an unconditional exact
finite atlas.  ABC remains **OPEN**; the IUT-to-ABC implication remains
**CITED CLAIM / OPEN DISPUTE**.  IUT enters the synthesis as a source of typed
operations, copy-separation questions, and hostile audits, not as a proof
dependency.

The session's most useful compact rule is

```text
natural address
  + fibre/provenance sidecar
  + numerical evaluator
  + ordered operation word
  + quotient-loss ledger.                                      (1)
```

An object may be called task `n` once selected.  But `n` must not silently
replace the value, height, curvature, weight, frame, or transport path that a
later operation consumes.

## 4. Exact ABC transfer and its sharp kernel

For coprime `0<a<b` and `m=a^3+b^3`, ABC would give

```text
rad(m) >= K_epsilon^(-1/(1+epsilon))
          m^(1/(1+epsilon))/rad(ab),                  (3)
```

and AM--GM turns this into

```text
rad(m) >= 2^(2/3)K_epsilon^(-1/(1+epsilon))
          m^((1-2epsilon)/(3(1+epsilon))).             (4)
```

The exponent tends to `1/3`, so for every `delta<1/3` only finitely many
primitive two-cube values can have `rad(m)<=m^delta`.  This is a valuation-
excess budget, not a support asymptotic or decoder.

The infinite family in `(2)` makes the quotient kernel explicit:

```text
ambient state (2^k,3*2^k,28*8^k)
  --divide common cube--> primitive state (1,3,28)
  --radicalize-----------> support {2,7}.              (5)
```

THM-3825 runs in the other direction on its restricted carrier:

```text
coloured valuations of 28*8^k
  --> quotient k, primitive shell 4, cofactor 7
  --> pair (1,3).                                      (6)
```

Equation `(5)` is the exact information ABC chooses not to retain; equation
`(6)` is the exact sidecar the repo decoder restores.  Neither subsumes the
other.

For fixed `p,q,r` with `sigma=1/p+1/q+1/r<1`, ABC similarly leaves the height
exponent `kappa/2`, where `kappa=1-sigma`.  This yields conditional primitive
finiteness.  The signature ordinal and the curvature remain distinct:

```text
first shortlex signature: (3,3,4), kappa=1/12;
least-curvature signature: (2,3,7), kappa=1/42, rank 5. (7)
```

The Pythagorean side has a different, non-finiteness transfer.  Applying ABC
to a primitive `A^2+B^2=C^2` would give

```text
rad(ABC) >= K_epsilon^(-1/(1+epsilon))C^(2/(1+epsilon)). (7a)
```

In the odd-square chart the shell-mates `(5,12,13)` and `(15,8,17)` share
`B+C=25` but have radicals `390` and `510`.  Thus ABC would constrain the
whole node while the outer ordinal schedules only its shell; the complementary
odd-root coordinate is again load-bearing.

## 5. The triangular bridge to theta weights

The extended triangular polynomial gives

```text
T(z)=z(z+1)/2,
j^2=T(j)+T(j-1),
(j+1)^2-j^2=2j+1,
T(z+2)-T(z-2)=2(2z+1).                               (8)
```

There are two lawful encodings of a quadratic evaluator:

1. **absolute:** retain `w(j)=j^2`;
2. **procedural:** retain a start value and the ordered word of odd increments
   `1,3,5,...`.

The user can still call the `j`th selected square “`j`”.  But the evaluator
must remain attached to any consumer that adds, averages, exponentiates, or
takes a log-volume.  Passing to an order-indifferent label quotient destroys
the procedural integral unless the order/synchronization sidecar survives.

This sharpens the Pythagorean result:

```text
outer address:    r where B+C=(2r-1)^2=8T(r-1)+1;
fibre sidecar:    s where C-B=(2s-1)^2;
node evaluator:   Xi(r,s);
tree operation:   the affine Berggren L/M/R maps on (r,s).               (9)
```

The shell rank alone has fibre size `phi(2r-1)/2`.  It is an excellent task
index and a poor node decoder.  The same distinction is the safe part of the
IUT theta analogy: according to IUT II the label `j` accompanies a weight
`qbar^(j^2)`.  The analogy says to keep `(copy,j,j^2,path)` typed.  It says
nothing about the correctness of IUT III's disputed height step.

## 8. Why the major tempting transfers stop

### LRC(14)

THM-3743's support-two list is already the finite universe `a+b<=356`.
Unknown `K_epsilon` gives no effective pruning.  Cube scalarization destroys
the other eleven speeds, sign partition, relation owner, physical phase,
arrival, and loneliness.  Higher support is a multi-term signed partition,
not a three-term ABC equation.  The tight AP row is the cheap positive control:
it has many small exact relations and is safe, so arithmetic smallness is not
a danger predicate.

### Jacobian/Dixmier

ABC height is logarithmic arithmetic/projective height.  THM-3550's height is
polynomial degree.  The latter changes under a different class of operations,
and no canonical arithmetic specialization invariant under Keller equivalence
has been constructed.  Similar words such as height, discriminant, and
conductor do not supply a map.

### Sixth powers and additive energy

The first primitive three-versus-three collision in `(2)` is a six-term
equation.  A proposed generalized radical inequality would need its own
statement, exceptional subsums, normalization, and literature audit.  It
cannot inherit three-term ABC by deleting terms or grouping sums without
controlling new gcds and cancellations.

### Pythagorean tree

The exponent triple `(2,2,2)` lies on the spherical side
`1/2+1/2+1/2>1`.  ABC's hyperbolic Fermat--Catalan consequence therefore gives
no tree finiteness.  The odd-square ordinal remains a lawful infinite task
address.

## 9. Procedural task generator

For every incoming object, instantiate

```text
Task=(source object,
      representation,
      candidate invariant,
      next native operation,
      symmetry/quotient,
      scale,
      preserved predicate,
      lost coordinate,
      cheapest hostile).                              (19)
```

Enumerate the finite dictionaries for each field, then shortlex-rank only
well-typed tuples.  The resulting natural number is a stable task address.
Completion of a task generates successors by changing exactly one field:

```text
representation successor: prime support -> coloured valuations;
operation successor:       radicalize -> normalize -> scale -> branch;
quotient successor:        labelled -> orbit -> hull;
scale successor:           primitive -> one common prime -> all scales;
hostile successor:         equality fibre -> next-operation divergence.  (20)
```

This makes incoming ideas procedural without turning the ordinal into an
invariant.

## 11. Durable synthesis

ABC contributes a conditional *budget*: primitive height cannot outrun prime
support too severely.  THM-3825 contributes a proved *decoder*: on a special
carrier, coloured valuations recover the state that a radical forgets.
THM-3756 contributes a proved *address/fibre chart*: a selected odd square may
be called `r`, while a second ordinal restores the node.  IUT contributes a
challenging *transport design vocabulary*: keep copies, links, indeterminacy,
weights, and one-sided hull operations distinct.

Their honest merger is not “IUT proves ABC, therefore the repo's open problems
fall.”  It is the architecture `(1)`: rank selected things densely, retain the
evaluator and provenance needed by the next operation, expose every quotient
kernel, and turn each failed transport into the next exact task.
